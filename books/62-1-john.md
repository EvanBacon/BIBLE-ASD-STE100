# 1 John

## Chapter 1

**1** We write to you about the Word of life. The Word of life was there from the start. We heard him. We saw him with our eyes. We looked at him, and our hands touched him.

**2** The life appeared, and we saw it. We tell the truth about it. We tell you about the eternal life. This life was with the Father, and it appeared to us.

**3** We tell you what we saw and heard. Then you can have fellowship with us. Our fellowship is with the Father and with his Son, Jesus Christ.

**4** We write these things to you so that our joy is full.

**5** We heard this message from him, and we tell it to you: God is light. There is no darkness in him.

**6** We can say that we have fellowship with God. But if we live in darkness, then we lie. We do not obey the truth.

**7** But God is in the light. If we live in the light, then we have fellowship with each other. Also, the blood of his Son Jesus Christ makes us clean from all sin.

**8** If we say that we have no sin, then we deceive ourselves. The truth is not in us.

**9** If we confess our sins, God will forgive our sins. He will make us clean from all wrong actions. He does this because he is faithful and just.

**10** If we say that we did not sin, then we make God a liar. His word is not in us.

## Chapter 2

**1** My little children, I write these things to you so that you do not sin. But if a person sins, we have a helper who speaks to the Father for us. He is Jesus Christ, the righteous one.

**2** Jesus is the sacrifice that removes the anger of God against our sins. He removes it for our sins, and also for the sins of all the world.

**3** We know that we know God if we obey his commandments.

**4** A person can say, "I know God." But if that person does not obey God's commandments, then he is a liar. The truth is not in him.

**5** But if a person obeys God's word, then the love of God is truly complete in him. In this way, we know that we are in God.

**6** A person who says that he lives in God must live as Jesus lived.

**7** Brothers, I do not write a new commandment to you. I write an old commandment that you had from the start. This old commandment is the word that you heard from the start.

**8** But also, I write a new commandment to you. Its truth is in Jesus and in you. The darkness goes away, and the true light now shines.

**9** A person can say that he is in the light. But if he hates his brother, then he is still in darkness now.

**10** A person who loves his brother lives in the light. There is nothing in him that causes another person to fall.

**11** But a person who hates his brother is in darkness. He lives in darkness. He does not know where he goes, because the darkness made his eyes blind.

**12** Little children, I write to you because God forgave your sins. He forgave them because of the name of Jesus.

**13** Fathers, I write to you because you know him who was there from the start. Young men, I write to you because you won against the evil one. Little children, I write to you because you know the Father.

**14** Fathers, I wrote to you because you know him who was there from the start. Young men, I wrote to you because you are strong. The word of God lives in you. You won against the evil one.

**15** Do not love the world or the things in the world. If a person loves the world, then the love of the Father is not in him.

**16** All things in the world do not come from the Father. They come from the world. These things are the bad desires of the body, the bad desires of the eyes, and pride in possessions.

**17** The world and its desires go away. But the person who does the will of God lives for all time.

**18** Little children, this is the last time. You heard that the antichrist will come. Now many antichrists came. Thus we know that this is the last time.

**19** These antichrists went out from us, but they were not part of us. If they were part of us, then they would stay with us. But they went out. This showed that none of them were part of us.

**20** But the Holy One put his Spirit on you. Thus you all know the truth.

**21** I write to you because you know the truth. I do not write because you do not know it. You know that no lie comes from the truth.

**22** Who is the liar? The liar is the person who says that Jesus is not the Christ. This person is the antichrist. He refuses the Father and the Son.

**23** A person who refuses the Son does not have the Father. A person who confesses the Son also has the Father.

**24** Keep in your hearts the message that you heard from the start. If that message stays in you, then you will stay in the Son and in the Father.

**25** God gave us this promise: He promised us eternal life.

**26** I write these things to you about the people who try to deceive you.

**27** But God put his Spirit on you, and his Spirit stays in you. Thus you do not need a person to teach you. The Spirit teaches you about all things. His teaching is true. It is not a lie. Stay in Jesus, as the Spirit taught you.

**28** Now, little children, stay in Jesus. Then, when he appears, we will be confident. We will not be ashamed in front of him when he comes.

**29** You know that God is righteous. Thus you know that each person who does what is right is a child of God.

## Chapter 3

**1** Look at the great love that the Father gave us! He calls us the children of God, and we are his children. The world does not know us because it did not know God.

**2** Dear friends, now we are the children of God. God did not yet show what we will be. But we know that when Jesus appears, we will be like him. We will see him as he truly is.

**3** Each person who has this hope in Jesus makes himself pure, as Jesus is pure.

**4** Each person who sins also breaks the law. Sin is the act of breaking the law.

**5** You know that Jesus appeared to remove our sins. There is no sin in him.

**6** A person who lives in Jesus does not continue to sin. A person who continues to sin did not see Jesus and does not know him.

**7** Little children, do not let anyone deceive you. A person who does what is right is righteous, as Jesus is righteous.

**8** A person who continues to sin belongs to the devil. The devil sinned from the start. The Son of God appeared for this reason: to destroy the works of the devil.

**9** A person who is a child of God does not continue to sin. The life of God stays in him. He cannot continue to sin, because he is a child of God.

**10** In this way we see who the children of God are. We also see who the children of the devil are. A person who does not do what is right is not from God. Also, a person who does not love his brother is not from God.

**11** You heard this message from the start: We must love each other.

**12** Do not be like Cain. He belonged to the evil one, and he killed his brother. Why did Cain kill him? He killed him because his own actions were evil. But his brother's actions were righteous.

**13** My brothers, do not be surprised if the world hates you.

**14** We know that we went from death to life, because we love our brothers. A person who does not love his brother stays in death.

**15** A person who hates his brother is a murderer. You know that no murderer has eternal life in him.

**16** We know what love is because Jesus gave his life for us. Thus we must give our lives for our brothers.

**17** A person can have the goods of this world. He can see that his brother needs help. But if he does not help his brother, then the love of God cannot live in him.

**18** My little children, do not love only with words or with the tongue. Love with actions and in truth.

**19** In this way, we know that we belong to the truth. Thus our hearts can be calm in front of God.

**20** If our heart says that we are guilty, God is greater than our heart. He knows all things.

**21** Dear friends, if our heart does not say that we are guilty, then we are confident in front of God.

**22** We get from God all the things that we ask for. This is because we obey his commandments. We do the things that please him.

**23** This is his commandment: Believe in the name of his Son, Jesus Christ. Love each other, as Jesus told us to do.

**24** A person who obeys God's commandments lives in God, and God lives in him. We know that God lives in us because of the Spirit. God gave us the Spirit.

## Chapter 4

**1** Dear friends, do not believe each spirit. Test the spirits to know if they come from God. Many false prophets went out into the world.

**2** In this way you can know the Spirit of God: Each spirit that confesses that Jesus Christ came in a human body is from God.

**3** But each spirit that does not confess Jesus is not from God. That is the spirit of the antichrist. You heard that this spirit will come. Now it is already in the world.

**4** Little children, you are from God. You won against the false prophets. The one who is in you is greater than the one who is in the world.

**5** The false prophets are from the world. Thus they speak about the world, and the world listens to them.

**6** We are from God. A person who knows God listens to us. A person who is not from God does not listen to us. In this way we know the Spirit of truth and the spirit of error.

**7** Dear friends, we must love each other, because love comes from God. Each person who loves is a child of God and knows God.

**8** A person who does not love does not know God, because God is love.

**9** God showed his love to us in this way: He sent his only Son into the world. Thus we can live through his Son.

**10** This is love: We did not love God, but God loved us. He sent his Son as the sacrifice that removes his anger against our sins.

**11** Dear friends, God loved us that much. Thus we also must love each other.

**12** No person ever saw God. If we love each other, then God lives in us. His love is complete in us.

**13** We know that we live in God and God lives in us, because he gave us his Spirit.

**14** We saw the Son, and we tell the truth about him. The Father sent the Son to be the Savior of the world.

**15** If a person confesses that Jesus is the Son of God, then God lives in that person. That person lives in God.

**16** We know and believe the love that God has for us. God is love. A person who lives in love lives in God, and God lives in him.

**17** In this way, love is complete in us. Thus we can be confident on the day of judgment. We are like Jesus in this world.

**18** There is no fear in love. Complete love pushes fear out, because fear comes from punishment. A person who is afraid does not have complete love.

**19** We love because God loved us first.

**20** A person can say, "I love God." But if he hates his brother, then he is a liar. He sees his brother but does not love him. Thus he cannot love God, because he does not see God.

**21** God gave us this commandment: A person who loves God must also love his brother.

## Chapter 5

**1** Each person who believes that Jesus is the Christ is a child of God. Each person who loves the Father also loves the child of the Father.

**2** We know that we love the children of God in this way: We love God and obey his commandments.

**3** To love God means to obey his commandments. His commandments are not difficult.

**4** Each person who is a child of God wins against the world. Our faith is the victory that wins against the world.

**5** Who wins against the world? Only the person who believes that Jesus is the Son of God.

**6** Jesus Christ is the one who came by water and blood. He did not come by water only, but by water and blood. The Spirit tells the truth about this, because the Spirit is truth.

**7** There are 3 who tell the truth in heaven: the Father, the Word, and the Holy Spirit. These 3 are 1.

**8** There are 3 who tell the truth on earth: the Spirit, the water, and the blood. These 3 agree.

**9** We accept what men tell us. But what God tells us is greater. God told us the truth about his Son.

**10** A person who believes in the Son of God has this truth in himself. A person who does not believe God makes God a liar. That person does not believe what God told about his Son.

**11** This is what God told us: God gave us eternal life. This life is in his Son.

**12** A person who has the Son has life. A person who does not have the Son of God does not have life.

**13** I write these things to you who believe in the name of the Son of God. I want you to know that you have eternal life. I want you to continue to believe in the name of the Son of God.

**14** We are confident with God about this: If we ask for a thing that agrees with his will, he hears us.

**15** We know that he hears us when we ask for things. Thus we know that we have the things that we asked from him.

**16** A person can see his brother do a sin that does not cause death. Then he must pray, and God will give life to that brother. This is true for those whose sin does not cause death. There is a sin that causes death. I do not tell you to pray about that sin.

**17** All wrong actions are sin. But there is a sin that does not cause death.

**18** We know that a child of God does not continue to sin. The Son of God keeps him safe. The evil one cannot hurt him.

**19** We know that we are from God. We know that all the world is under the control of the evil one.

**20** We know that the Son of God came. He gave us understanding. Thus we can know God, who is true. We are in God, who is true, and in his Son Jesus Christ. He is the true God and eternal life.

**21** Little children, keep away from idols. Amen.
