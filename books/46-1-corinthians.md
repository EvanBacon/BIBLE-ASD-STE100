# 1 Corinthians

## Chapter 1

**1** This letter is from Paul and from our brother Sosthenes. God wanted Paul to be an apostle of Jesus Christ, and God called him for this work.

**2** We write to the church of God in Corinth. You are holy because you are in Christ Jesus. God called you to be his holy people. We also write to all the people in all places who call to the name of our Lord Jesus Christ. He is their Lord and our Lord.

**3** We ask God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ to give you grace and peace.

**4** I always thank my God for you. I thank him because he gave you his grace through Jesus Christ.

**5** Through Christ, God made you rich in all things. He gave you all types of speech and all types of knowledge.

**6** Thus, the message about Christ became strong and true in you.

**7** Thus, you have all the gifts from God. You wait for our Lord Jesus Christ to come again.

**8** Christ will also keep you strong until the end. Then no person can find fault in you on the day of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**9** God is faithful. He called you to share life with his Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.

**10** Brothers, I ask you strongly in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ: agree with each other. Do not let groups divide you. Be fully united in one mind and one opinion.

**11** My brothers, some people from the family of Chloe told me about you. They told me that you argue with each other.

**12** I tell you what I mean. Each of you says a different thing. One says, "I follow Paul." Another says, "I follow Apollos." Another says, "I follow Cephas." Another says, "I follow Christ."

**13** Is Christ divided into parts? Did the people crucify Paul for you? Did you get baptism in the name of Paul? No!

**14** I thank God that I did not baptize any of you, but Crispus and Gaius.

**15** Thus, no person can say that I baptized people in my own name.

**16** I also baptized the family of Stephanas. Other than these people, I do not remember that I baptized any person.

**17** Christ did not send me to baptize. He sent me to tell the gospel (the good news). I do not use clever human words for this, because those words take away the power of the cross of Christ.

**18** The message about the cross is foolish to the people who go to destruction. But to us whom God saves, the message is the power of God.

**19** The Scriptures say, "I will destroy the wisdom of the wise people. I will make the knowledge of the clever people useless."

**20** Where is the wise person? Where is the teacher of the law? Where is the person who argues about the things of this world? God made the wisdom of this world foolish.

**21** In the wisdom of God, the world did not know God through its own wisdom. Thus, God decided to save the people who believe. He used the message that the world thinks is foolish.

**22** The Jews ask for miracles as a sign. The Greeks look for wisdom.

**23** But we tell people about Christ, whom the people crucified. This message makes the Jews fall away. The Greeks think that the message is foolish.

**24** But some Jews and some Greeks hear the call of God. For these people, Christ is the power of God and the wisdom of God.

**25** The thing in God that people call foolish is wiser than all human wisdom. The thing in God that people call weak is stronger than all human strength.

**26** Brothers, think about what you were when God called you. By human standards, not many of you were wise. Not many of you were powerful. Not many of you came from important families.

**27** But God chose the things that the world thinks are foolish. He did this to make the wise people ashamed. God chose the things that the world thinks are weak. He did this to make the strong things ashamed.

**28** God chose the things of the world that are low and that people hate. He chose the things that are nothing. He did this to remove the power of the things that are important.

**29** Thus, no person can be proud in front of God.

**30** God put you in Christ Jesus. God made Christ our wisdom. Through Christ, God makes us right with him. Christ makes us holy and sets us free.

**31** Thus, it is as the Scriptures say: "If a person wants to be proud, he must be proud of what the Lord did."

## Chapter 2

**1** Brothers, when I came to you, I told you the truth about God. I did not use fine speech or high wisdom.

**2** I decided to think about only one thing while I was with you. That one thing was Jesus Christ, whom the people crucified.

**3** When I was with you, I was weak. I was afraid, and I shook with fear.

**4** My speech and my message did not use clever words of human wisdom to make you believe. But the Spirit showed his power through my message.

**5** I did this so that your faith does not stand on human wisdom. Your faith stands on the power of God.

**6** But we speak wisdom to the people who are mature. This wisdom is not the wisdom of this world. It is not the wisdom of the rulers of this world. These rulers will lose their power.

**7** We speak the wisdom of God. This wisdom is a secret that God kept hidden. Before he made the world, God planned this wisdom for our glory.

**8** None of the rulers of this world understood this wisdom. Because they did not understand it, they crucified the Lord of glory.

**9** But the Scriptures say, "No eye saw, and no ear heard. No human mind thought of the things that God made ready for the people who love him."

**10** But God showed these things to us through his Spirit. The Spirit looks into all things, and also into the deep things of God.

**11** No person knows the thoughts of another person. Only the spirit inside that person knows them. In the same way, no person knows the thoughts of God. Only the Spirit of God knows them.

**12** We did not receive the spirit of the world. We received the Spirit who comes from God. Thus, we can know the things that God freely gave to us.

**13** We speak about these things. We do not use words that human wisdom teaches. We use words that the Holy Spirit teaches. We explain spiritual truths with spiritual words.

**14** A person who does not have the Spirit does not accept the things from the Spirit of God. He thinks that these things are foolish. He cannot understand them, because only the Spirit can help a person to understand them.

**15** A person who has the Spirit can judge all things. But no other person can judge him.

**16** The Scriptures say, "Who knows the mind of the Lord? Who can teach him?" But we have the mind of Christ.

## Chapter 3

**1** Brothers, I could not speak to you as to spiritual people. I had to speak to you as to people who follow human desires. You were like babies in Christ.

**2** I gave you milk to drink. I did not give you solid food, because you were not ready for it. And you are not ready for it now.

**3** You still follow human desires. You are jealous of each other and you argue with each other. Thus, you follow human desires, and you live like people of the world.

**4** One of you says, "I follow Paul." Another says, "I follow Apollos." When you say these things, you act like people of the world.

**5** Who is Apollos? Who is Paul? We are only servants. Through us, you started to believe. Each of us did the work that the Lord gave to him.

**6** I put the seed in the ground. Apollos gave water to it. But God made it grow.

**7** Thus, the person who plants is not important. The person who gives water is not important. Only God is important, because he makes the plant grow.

**8** The person who plants and the person who gives water have one purpose. God will give each of them a reward for his own work.

**9** We work together with God. You are like a field that belongs to God. You are like a building that belongs to God.

**10** God gave me his grace. With this grace, I put down the foundation like a skilled builder. Now another person builds on the foundation. But each person must be careful how he builds on it.

**11** The foundation is already there, and it is Jesus Christ. No person can put down a different foundation.

**12** Some people build on this foundation with gold, silver, or valuable stones. Other people build with wood, grass, or straw.

**13** The work of each person will become clear. The day of judgment will show it. On that day, fire will show the work. The fire will test the quality of the work of each person.

**14** If the work that a person built stays after the fire, he will receive a reward.

**15** If the fire burns the work of a person, he will lose his reward. But God will save the person himself. He will be like a person who escapes through a fire.

**16** You know that you are the temple of God. The Spirit of God lives in you.

**17** If a person destroys the temple of God, God will destroy that person. The temple of God is holy, and you are that temple.

**18** Do not deceive yourselves. Some of you think that you are wise by the standards of this world. You must become foolish by these standards. Then you will become truly wise.

**19** The wisdom of this world is foolish to God. The Scriptures say, "God catches wise people with their own clever plans."

**20** The Scriptures also say, "The Lord knows the thoughts of the wise people. He knows that their thoughts are useless."

**21** Thus, do not be proud of human leaders. All things belong to you.

**22** Paul, Apollos, and Cephas belong to you. The world, life, and death belong to you. The things of now and the things that will come belong to you. All things belong to you.

**23** You belong to Christ, and Christ belongs to God.

## Chapter 4

**1** Think of us as servants of Christ. God gave us the responsibility to manage his secret truths.

**2** A person with such a responsibility must be faithful.

**3** It is not important to me if you judge me, or if a human court judges me. I do not even judge myself.

**4** I know of no wrong thing that I did. But that does not make me innocent. The Lord is the one who judges me.

**5** Thus, do not judge any person before the correct time. Wait until the Lord comes. He will bring to the light the things that people now hide in the dark. He will show the secret plans in the hearts of people. Then God will give each person the praise that he must get.

**6** Brothers, I used Apollos and myself as examples to help you. Learn from us what this rule means: "Do not go past what the Scriptures say." Then none of you will be proud of one leader and look down on another leader.

**7** Who makes you better than other people? You received all the things that you have. If you received them, why are you proud? Do not act as if you did not receive them.

**8** You think that you already have all that you need. You think that you are already rich. You think that you rule as kings, but without us. I wish that you truly ruled! Then we could rule with you.

**9** I think that God put us apostles at the end of the line. We are like people who must die in front of a crowd. The whole world, angels and people, look at us.

**10** For Christ, we are foolish, but you are wise in Christ. We are weak, but you are strong. People give you honor, but they hate us.

**11** Until this hour, we are hungry and thirsty. We do not have sufficient clothes. People hit us. We have no permanent home.

**12** We work hard with our own hands. When people curse us, we bless them. When people cause us to suffer, we endure it.

**13** When people say bad things about us, we answer with kind words. Until now, the world thinks that we are like dirt and garbage.

**14** I do not write these things to make you ashamed. I write them to warn you, because you are my dear children.

**15** You can have 10,000 teachers in Christ, but you do not have many fathers. I became your father in Christ Jesus when I told you the gospel.

**16** Thus, I ask you strongly: copy what I do.

**17** For this reason, I sent Timothy to you. He is my dear son, and he is faithful in the Lord. He will help you to remember how I live in Christ. I teach this way of life in all the churches in all places.

**18** Some of you became proud. You think that I will not come to you.

**19** But if the Lord wants it, I will come to you soon. Then I will find out what these proud people can do. I do not care about their words. I care about their power.

**20** The kingdom of God is not a matter of words. It is a matter of power.

**21** What do you want? Must I come to you with a stick to punish you? Or must I come with love and a gentle spirit?

## Chapter 5

**1** People tell me that there is sexual sin among you. This sin is so bad that even the people who do not know God do not do it. A man lives with the wife of his father.

**2** And you are proud! You must be sad about this sin. You must send away from your group the man who did this.

**3** My body is not with you, but my spirit is with you. I already judged the man who did this. I judged him as if I were with you.

**4** Meet together in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ. My spirit will be with you. The power of our Lord Jesus is also with you.

**5** Then give this man to Satan, so that Satan can destroy his sinful nature. Then God can save his spirit on the day of the Lord Jesus.

**6** It is not good that you are proud. You know that a small quantity of yeast makes all of the dough rise.

**7** Remove the old yeast, so that you are a new batch of dough without yeast. In truth, you are without yeast. Christ, our Passover lamb, died as a sacrifice for us.

**8** Thus, let us celebrate the festival. Let us not use the old yeast of hate and evil. Let us use the bread without yeast, which is honesty and truth.

**9** In my previous letter, I told you not to be friends with people who do sexual sins.

**10** I did not mean the people of this world who do sexual sins. I did not mean the greedy people, the people who steal, or the people who worship idols. If you avoid all of them, you must leave this world.

**11** But now I write to you about a different thing. Some people call themselves brothers. Do not be friends with such a person if he does sexual sins or if he is greedy. Do not be friends with him if he worships idols, says bad things about people, gets drunk, or steals. Do not even eat with such a person.

**12** It is not my work to judge the people outside the church. But you must judge the people inside the church.

**13** God will judge the people outside the church. The Scriptures say, "Remove the evil person from among you."

## Chapter 6

**1** Some of you have an argument with another believer. Why do you take the matter to judges who do not know God? Why do you not take it to God's people?

**2** You know that God's people will judge the world. If you will judge the world, then you can judge very small matters.

**3** You know that we will judge angels. Thus, we can surely judge the matters of this life.

**4** If you have arguments about the matters of this life, do not ask people who have no position in the church to judge them.

**5** I say this to make you ashamed. Surely there is one wise person among you. He can judge an argument between believers.

**6** But one brother takes another brother to court. And he does this in front of people who do not believe!

**7** When you take each other to court, you already lose. It is better to accept a wrong action against you. It is better to let other people cheat you.

**8** But you do wrong things, and you cheat other people. And you do these things to your own brothers!

**9** You know that bad people will not receive the kingdom of God. Do not deceive yourselves. These people will not receive it: people who do sexual sins and people who worship idols. People who are not faithful in marriage and men who have sexual relations with men will not receive it.

**10** Thieves and greedy people will not receive the kingdom of God. Drunkards, people who say bad things about others, and people who steal by force will not receive it.

**11** Some of you were such people. But God made you clean. He made you holy. He made you right with him. He did this through the name of the Lord Jesus and through the Spirit of our God.

**12** You say, "I am free to do all things." But not all things help me. "I am free to do all things." But I will not let any thing control me.

**13** You say, "Food is for the stomach, and the stomach is for food." But God will destroy both of them. The body is not for sexual sin. The body is for the Lord, and the Lord is for the body.

**14** God brought the Lord back to life. He will also bring us back to life with his power.

**15** You know that your bodies are parts of the body of Christ. Must I take a part of Christ and join it to a prostitute? No, never!

**16** You know that a man who joins himself to a prostitute becomes one body with her. The Scriptures say, "The 2 will become one body."

**17** But the person who joins himself to the Lord becomes one spirit with him.

**18** Run away from sexual sin. Other sins that a person does are outside his body. But the person who does sexual sin sins against his own body.

**19** You know that your body is the temple of the Holy Spirit. The Holy Spirit lives in you, and God gave him to you. You do not belong to yourselves.

**20** God paid a price to buy you. Thus, use your body and your spirit to give honor to God. They belong to God.

## Chapter 7

**1** Now I answer the questions in your letter. It is good for a man not to have sexual relations with a woman.

**2** But there is a danger of sexual sin. Thus, each man must have his own wife, and each woman must have her own husband.

**3** The husband must give his wife what is correct in marriage. The wife must do the same for her husband.

**4** The wife does not have control over her own body. Her husband has that control. In the same way, the husband does not have control over his own body. His wife has that control.

**5** Do not refuse each other. You can stop for a short time if you both agree, so that you can use the time to pray. Then come together again. If you do not, Satan can tempt you because you cannot control yourselves.

**6** I say this as a permission, not as a command.

**7** I wish that all people were like me. But God gives each person his own gift. He gives one gift to one person and a different gift to another person.

**8** I say this to the people who do not have a husband or a wife, and to the widows. It is good for them to stay as I am.

**9** But if they cannot control themselves, they must marry. It is better to marry than to burn with desire.

**10** I give a command to the married people. This command is not from me, but from the Lord. A wife must not leave her husband.

**11** But if she leaves him, she must stay unmarried. Or she must make peace with her husband again. And a husband must not divorce his wife.

**12** I say this to the other people. These words are from me, not from the Lord. A believer can have a wife who does not believe. If she agrees to live with him, he must not divorce her.

**13** A woman can have a husband who does not believe. If he agrees to live with her, she must not divorce him.

**14** The husband who does not believe becomes holy through his wife. The wife who does not believe becomes holy through her husband. If this is not true, your children are unclean. But now they are holy.

**15** But if the person who does not believe leaves, let him go. In such a situation, the believer, man or woman, is not tied to the marriage. God called us to live in peace.

**16** Wife, how do you know that you will save your husband? Husband, how do you know that you will save your wife?

**17** Each person must live the life that the Lord gave to him. He must continue as he was when God called him. I give this rule in all the churches.

**18** If a man had circumcision when God called him, he must not try to change it. If a man did not have circumcision when God called him, he must not get circumcision.

**19** Circumcision is not important, and no circumcision is not important. The important thing is to obey the commands of God.

**20** Each person must stay in the situation that he was in when God called him.

**21** Were you a slave when God called you? Do not worry about it. But if you can become free, use that chance.

**22** If a slave hears the call of the Lord, he is a free person in the Lord. In the same way, if a free person hears the call, he is a slave of Christ.

**23** God paid a price to buy you. Do not become slaves of people.

**24** Brothers, each person must stay with God in the situation that he was in when God called him.

**25** Now I write about the people who do not have a husband or a wife. I have no command from the Lord about them. But I give my opinion. Through the mercy of the Lord, people can trust me.

**26** There are troubles now. Thus, I think that it is good for a person to stay as he is.

**27** Are you married to a wife? Do not try to become free from her. Are you free from a wife? Do not look for a wife.

**28** But if you marry, you do not sin. If a girl who does not have a husband marries, she does not sin. But married people will have troubles in this life. I want to keep you away from these troubles.

**29** Brothers, I tell you this: the time is short. From now on, men who have wives must live as if they have no wives.

**30** People who cry must live as if they do not cry. People who are happy must live as if they are not happy. People who buy things must live as if they own nothing.

**31** People who use the things of this world must not depend on them. This world, as it is now, will soon end.

**32** I want you to be free from worry. A man who does not have a wife thinks about the work of the Lord. He wants to please the Lord.

**33** But a married man thinks about the things of this world. He wants to please his wife.

**34** His interests go in 2 directions. A woman who does not have a husband thinks about the work of the Lord. She wants to be holy in body and in spirit. But a married woman thinks about the things of this world. She wants to please her husband.

**35** I tell you this to help you. I do not want to limit your freedom. I want you to live in a correct way. I want you to serve the Lord fully, with nothing to stop you.

**36** A man can think that he does not act correctly toward the girl that he promised to marry. Maybe she is older, and he thinks that they must marry. Then he can do what he wants. He does not sin. They must marry.

**37** But another man can be firm in his mind. He does not feel a need to marry, and he has control over his desires. He decided in his mind not to marry the girl. This man does well.

**38** Thus, the man who marries the girl does well. But the man who does not marry her does better.

**39** The law joins a wife to her husband while her husband lives. If her husband dies, she is free to marry any man that she wants. But that man must belong to the Lord.

**40** But I think that she is happier if she stays unmarried. This is my opinion. And I think that I also have the Spirit of God.

## Chapter 8

**1** Now I write about the food that people offer to idols. We know that "we all have knowledge." Knowledge makes people proud, but love makes people strong.

**2** A person can think that he knows a thing. But he does not yet know as he must know.

**3** But if a person loves God, God knows that person.

**4** Thus, I write about food that people offer as a sacrifice to idols. We know that an idol is nothing in the world. We know that there is only one God.

**5** Many things in heaven and on earth have the name "god." There are many "gods" and many "lords."

**6** But for us, there is only one God, the Father. All things come from him, and we live for him. And there is only one Lord, Jesus Christ. God made all things through him, and we live through him.

**7** But not all people know this. Some people are used to idols. Until now, when they eat such food, they think that it is food for an idol. Their conscience is weak, and the food makes their conscience dirty.

**8** Food does not bring us near to God. If we do not eat it, we are not worse. If we eat it, we are not better.

**9** But be careful. Your freedom must not cause the weak people to sin.

**10** You have knowledge, and you eat in the temple of an idol. A person with a weak conscience can see you there. Then he will feel free to eat food that people offer to idols.

**11** Thus, your knowledge will destroy this weak brother. Christ died for this brother.

**12** When you sin against your brothers in this way, you hurt their weak conscience. Then you sin against Christ.

**13** Thus, if food causes my brother to sin, I will never eat meat again. I do not want to cause my brother to sin.

## Chapter 9

**1** Am I not free? Am I not an apostle? Did I not see Jesus our Lord? You are the result of my work in the Lord.

**2** Other people can think that I am not an apostle. But I am surely an apostle to you. You are the proof that I am an apostle of the Lord.

**3** This is my answer to the people who judge me.

**4** We have the right to receive food and drink.

**5** We have the right to take a wife who is a believer with us on our travels. The other apostles do this. The brothers of the Lord and Cephas also do this.

**6** Are Barnabas and I the only people who must work for money?

**7** No soldier pays for his own needs. A man who plants a vineyard eats its fruit. A man who keeps a flock drinks its milk.

**8** I do not say these things only as a human opinion. The law also says the same thing.

**9** The law of Moses says, "Do not put a cover on the mouth of an ox while it walks on the grain to separate it." Is God worried only about oxen?

**10** God surely says this for us. The Scriptures say this for us. The person who plows the field must hope to get a part of the harvest. The person who separates the grain must also hope to get a part of it.

**11** We put spiritual seed in you. Thus, it is not too much for us to get material things from you.

**12** Other people have this right to get help from you. Surely we have more right. But we did not use this right. We endure all things, so that we do not stop the gospel of Christ.

**13** You know that the people who work in the temple get their food from the temple. The people who serve at the altar get a part of the sacrifices on the altar.

**14** In the same way, the Lord gave a command. The people who tell the gospel must get their food and needs from the gospel.

**15** But I did not use any of these rights. And I do not write these things to get them now. It is better for me to die than to let a person take away my reason to be proud.

**16** When I tell the gospel, I have no reason to be proud. I must tell it. It will be very bad for me if I do not tell the gospel!

**17** If I do this work because I want to, I get a reward. If I do not want to do it, I still must do it. God gave me this responsibility.

**18** Thus, what is my reward? My reward is to tell the gospel without cost to the people. Thus, I do not use my rights in the gospel.

**19** I am free, and I belong to no person. But I made myself a slave to all people. I did this to bring as many people as possible to Christ.

**20** When I was with the Jews, I lived like a Jew to bring the Jews to Christ. I am not under the law of Moses. But when I was with the people under the law, I lived like a person under the law. I did this to bring them to Christ.

**21** When I was with the people who do not have the law, I lived like a person without the law. I did this to bring them to Christ. But I am not without the law of God. I obey the law of Christ.

**22** When I was with weak people, I became weak to bring them to Christ. I became all things to all people. I did this so that I can save some people in all possible ways.

**23** I do all these things for the gospel. I want to share in the blessings of the gospel.

**24** You know that in a race, all the runners run. But only one runner gets the prize. Thus, run so that you get the prize.

**25** All athletes in a contest control themselves in all things. They do it to get a prize that will not last. But we do it to get a prize that will last forever.

**26** Thus, I run toward a goal. I fight like a boxer who hits the target, not the air.

**27** I control my body, and I make it obey me. I preached to other people. I do not want God to reject me at the end.

## Chapter 10

**1** Brothers, I want you to know this. All our ancestors were under the cloud. All of them went through the sea.

**2** In the cloud and in the sea, all of them got a type of baptism. They became followers of Moses.

**3** All of them ate the same spiritual food.

**4** All of them drank the same spiritual drink. They drank from the spiritual rock that went with them. That rock was Christ.

**5** But God was not happy with most of them. Thus, their dead bodies fell in the desert.

**6** These things are examples for us. They show us that we must not want evil things, as those people did.

**7** Do not worship idols, as some of them did. The Scriptures say, "The people sat down to eat and drink. Then they stood up to dance and play."

**8** We must not do sexual sins, as some of them did. In one day, 23,000 of them died.

**9** We must not test Christ, as some of them did. Snakes killed those people.

**10** Do not complain, as some of them did. The angel of death killed those people.

**11** These things occurred to them as examples. The Scriptures tell about them to warn us. We live in the time near the end of the ages.

**12** Thus, if you think that you stand firm, be careful. You can fall.

**13** All the temptations that you have are the same as the temptations of all people. God is faithful. He will not let you have a temptation that is too strong for you. With each temptation, he will also give you a way out. Then you can endure it.

**14** Thus, my dear friends, run away from the worship of idols.

**15** I speak to you as to wise people. Judge for yourselves what I say.

**16** We bless the cup and we thank God for it. When we drink from it, we share in the blood of Christ. When we break the bread and eat it, we share in the body of Christ.

**17** There is one bread. Thus, we are one body, although we are many people. All of us eat from the one bread.

**18** Think about the people of Israel. The people who eat the sacrifices share in the altar.

**19** What do I mean? I do not say that an idol is a real thing. I do not say that the food for an idol is special.

**20** But I say this: the people who do not know God make their sacrifices to demons, not to God. I do not want you to share with demons.

**21** You cannot drink from the cup of the Lord and also from the cup of demons. You cannot eat at the table of the Lord and also at the table of demons.

**22** Do we want to make the Lord jealous? We are not stronger than he is.

**23** You say, "I am free to do all things." But not all things help. "I am free to do all things." But not all things make people strong.

**24** No person must look only for his own good. Each person must look for the good of other people.

**25** You can eat any meat that people sell in the market. Do not ask questions about it because of your conscience.

**26** The Scriptures say, "The earth and all things in it belong to the Lord."

**27** A person who does not believe can ask you to eat with him. If you want to go, eat all the food that he gives you. Do not ask questions about it because of your conscience.

**28** But a person can tell you, "Somebody offered this food as a sacrifice to an idol." Then do not eat it. Do this for the person who told you, and for conscience. The earth and all things in it belong to the Lord.

**29** I speak about the conscience of the other person, not your own conscience. Why must the conscience of another person limit my freedom?

**30** If I thank God for my food, I can eat it. Why do people say bad things about me because of food that I thank God for?

**31** Thus, when you eat or drink, or when you do any thing, do it all for the glory of God.

**32** Do not cause other people to sin. Do not cause the Jews, the Greeks, or the church of God to sin.

**33** I try to please all people in all things. I do not look for my own good. I look for the good of many people, so that God can save them.

## Chapter 11

**1** Copy what I do, as I copy what Christ does.

**2** Brothers, I praise you because you remember me in all things. You keep the teachings that I gave to you.

**3** But I want you to know this. Christ is the head of each man. The man is the head of the woman. God is the head of Christ.

**4** If a man prays or prophesies with his head covered, he brings shame on his head.

**5** If a woman prays or prophesies with her head not covered, she brings shame on her head. It is the same as if she cut off all her hair.

**6** If a woman does not cover her head, she must also cut her hair short. But it is shameful for a woman to cut her hair short or to cut it all off. Thus, she must cover her head.

**7** A man must not cover his head, because he is the image and the glory of God. But the woman is the glory of the man.

**8** God did not make the man from the woman. God made the woman from the man.

**9** God did not make the man for the woman. God made the woman for the man.

**10** For this reason, and because of the angels, a woman must have a sign of authority on her head.

**11** But in the Lord, the woman needs the man, and the man needs the woman.

**12** The woman came from the man. In the same way, each man comes into the world through a woman. And all things come from God.

**13** Judge for yourselves. Is it correct for a woman to pray to God with her head not covered?

**14** Nature itself teaches you that long hair is shameful for a man.

**15** But long hair is an honor for a woman. God gave her hair to cover her.

**16** A person can want to argue about this. But we do not have another custom, and the churches of God do not have another custom.

**17** I give you these instructions, but I do not praise you now. When you meet together, you cause more bad than good.

**18** First, I hear that there are groups among you when you meet as a church. I believe that this is partly true.

**19** There must be different groups among you. Then it will be clear which of you God approves.

**20** When you meet together, you do not truly eat the supper of the Lord.

**21** When you eat, each person eats his own food first. He does not wait for the other people. Thus, one person stays hungry, and another person becomes drunk.

**22** You have houses where you can eat and drink! Do you hate the church of God? Do you want to make the poor people ashamed? What must I tell you? Must I praise you? No, I do not praise you for this.

**23** I received this message from the Lord, and I gave it to you. On the night when a person betrayed the Lord Jesus, Jesus took bread.

**24** He thanked God for the bread and broke it. He said, "Take this and eat it. This is my body, which I give for you. Do this to remember me."

**25** In the same way, after the supper, he took the cup. He said, "This cup is the new covenant (agreement), which my blood makes. Each time you drink from it, do this to remember me."

**26** Each time you eat this bread and drink from this cup, you tell about the death of the Lord. Do this until he comes again.

**27** Thus, a person can eat the bread or drink from the cup of the Lord in a wrong way. Then he sins against the body and the blood of the Lord.

**28** Each person must examine himself before he eats the bread and drinks from the cup.

**29** If a person eats and drinks without respect for the body of the Lord, he brings judgment on himself.

**30** For this reason, many of you are weak and sick, and some of you died.

**31** If we judge ourselves correctly, God will not judge us.

**32** But when the Lord judges us, he corrects us. Then God will not condemn us with the world.

**33** Thus, my brothers, when you meet together to eat, wait for each other.

**34** If a person is hungry, he must eat at home. Then God will not judge you when you meet together. I will give instructions about the other matters when I come.

## Chapter 12

**1** Brothers, now I write about spiritual gifts. I want you to know the truth about them.

**2** You know that before you believed, you did not know God. Other people led you to worship idols that cannot speak.

**3** Thus, I tell you this. A person who speaks through the Spirit of God never says, "God curses Jesus." And no person can say, "Jesus is Lord," but through the Holy Spirit.

**4** There are different types of gifts, but the same Spirit gives them.

**5** There are different types of service, but we serve the same Lord.

**6** There are different types of work, but the same God does all the work in all people.

**7** The Spirit shows himself in each person for the good of all.

**8** The Spirit gives one person the ability to speak with wisdom. The same Spirit gives another person the ability to speak with knowledge.

**9** The same Spirit gives faith to another person. The one Spirit gives the gift to heal people to another person.

**10** He gives another person the power to do miracles. He gives another person the ability to prophesy. He gives another person the ability to know which spirits come from God. He gives another person the ability to speak in different languages. He gives another person the ability to explain these languages.

**11** One and the same Spirit does all these things. He gives a gift to each person as he decides.

**12** The body is one unit, but it has many parts. All the parts make one body. Christ is the same.

**13** All of us got baptism by one Spirit into one body. Some of us are Jews and some are Greeks. Some of us are slaves and some are free. God gave all of us the one Spirit to drink.

**14** The body does not have only one part. It has many parts.

**15** The foot can say, "I am not a hand. Thus, I am not a part of the body." But it is still a part of the body.

**16** The ear can say, "I am not an eye. Thus, I am not a part of the body." But it is still a part of the body.

**17** If all of the body were an eye, how could it hear? If all of the body were an ear, how could it smell?

**18** But God put each part in the body as he wanted.

**19** If all the parts are one part, there is no body.

**20** But there are many parts, and only one body.

**21** The eye cannot tell the hand, "I do not need you." And the head cannot tell the feet, "I do not need you."

**22** No, the parts of the body that seem weaker are very necessary.

**23** We give more honor to the parts of the body that we think are less important. We give more care to the parts that we do not show.

**24** The parts that we show do not need this care. But God put the body together. He gave more honor to the parts that did not have it.

**25** He did this so that the body does not divide. All the parts must have the same care for each other.

**26** If one part of the body suffers, all the parts suffer with it. If one part gets honor, all the parts are happy with it.

**27** You are the body of Christ, and each of you is a part of it.

**28** God put people in the church in this order. First, there are apostles. Second, there are prophets. Third, there are teachers. Then there are people who do miracles and people who heal. There are people who help, people who lead, and people who speak in different languages.

**29** Not all people are apostles. Not all people are prophets. Not all people are teachers. Not all people do miracles.

**30** Not all people have gifts to heal people. Not all people speak in different languages. Not all people explain these languages.

**31** But try hard to get the best gifts. Now I will show you a way that is better than all of them.

## Chapter 13

**1** I can speak the languages of people and of angels. But if I do not have love, I am only a noisy gong or a loud cymbal.

**2** I can have the gift of prophecy. I can understand all secret truths and have all knowledge. I can have faith that is sufficient to move mountains. But if I do not have love, I am nothing.

**3** I can give all that I own to feed the poor people. I can give my body to the fire. But if I do not have love, these things do not help me.

**4** Love is patient. Love is kind. Love is not jealous. Love does not boast, and it is not proud.

**5** Love is not rude. Love does not look for its own good. Love does not become angry quickly. Love does not think about evil.

**6** Love is not happy with sin, but it is happy with the truth.

**7** Love endures all things. Love always trusts. Love always hopes. Love continues through all things.

**8** Love never ends. But the gift of prophecy will end. The gift of languages will stop. The gift of knowledge will disappear.

**9** Now we know only a part. And we prophesy only a part.

**10** But when the complete thing comes, the partial things will end.

**11** When I was a child, I spoke as a child. I understood as a child, and I thought as a child. When I became a man, I stopped the actions of a child.

**12** Now we see only a dark picture, as in a bad mirror. But then we will see face to face. Now I know only a part. But then I will know fully, as God knows me fully.

**13** Now these 3 things stay: faith, hope, and love. But the most important of these is love.

## Chapter 14

**1** Try hard to have love. Also, have a strong wish for the gifts of the Spirit. Most of all, wish that you can prophesy.

**2** A person who speaks in an unknown language does not speak to people. That person speaks to God, because no person understands him. He speaks secret things by the Spirit.

**3** But a person who prophesies speaks to people. His words make them stronger, give them encouragement, and give them comfort.

**4** A person who speaks in an unknown language makes himself stronger. But a person who prophesies makes the church stronger.

**5** I want all of you to speak in other languages. But I want you to prophesy more. The person who prophesies is greater than the person who speaks in languages. But if the speaker tells the meaning, then the church becomes stronger.

**6** Brothers, if I come to you and speak in languages, how will I help you? I will help you only if I speak a revelation, knowledge, a prophecy, or a lesson.

**7** Things without life also make sounds, for example a flute or a harp. If the notes are not clear, how will a person know what tune the musician plays?

**8** If the trumpet does not make a clear sound, no soldier will prepare for the battle.

**9** It is the same with you. If you do not speak words that people can easily understand, how will they know what you say? You will speak only into the air.

**10** There are possibly many types of languages in the world. Each language has a meaning.

**11** If I do not know the meaning of the language, I will be a foreigner to the speaker. And the speaker will be a foreigner to me.

**12** It is the same with you. You have a strong wish for the gifts of the Spirit. Thus, try to have many gifts that make the church stronger.

**13** Thus, a person who speaks in an unknown language must pray for the ability to tell the meaning.

**14** If I pray in an unknown language, my spirit prays. But my mind does not get a result.

**15** So, what will I do? I will pray with my spirit, and I will also pray with my mind. I will sing with my spirit, and I will also sing with my mind.

**16** You can praise God with your spirit only. But then an ordinary person who listens cannot say "Amen" when you give thanks. That person does not understand what you say.

**17** You give thanks well. But the other person does not become stronger.

**18** I thank my God that I speak in languages more than all of you.

**19** But in the church I prefer to speak 5 words with my mind, to teach other people. I do not want to speak 10,000 words in an unknown language.

**20** Brothers, do not think like children. Be like babies in evil things. But in your thoughts, be adults.

**21** The law says this: "The Lord says, 'I will speak to these people through men of other languages and through the lips of foreigners. But even then, the people will not listen to me.'"

**22** Thus, languages are a sign for people who do not believe. They are not a sign for believers. But prophecy is for believers, not for people who do not believe.

**23** Possibly the whole church comes together, and all the people speak in languages. Then some ordinary people or some unbelievers come in. Those people will say that you are crazy.

**24** But possibly all the people prophesy, and an unbeliever or an ordinary person comes in. All the people will show that person his sin. All of them will examine him.

**25** The secrets of his heart will become known. Then he will bow down with his face to the ground and worship God. He will say, "It is true that God is among you!"

**26** Brothers, what is the conclusion? When you come together, each person has a song, a lesson, a revelation, a language, or the meaning of a language. You must do all these things to make the church stronger.

**27** If people speak in an unknown language, then only 2 or at most 3 must speak. They must speak one at a time. And 1 person must tell the meaning.

**28** If no person can tell the meaning, then the speaker must be quiet in the church. He must speak only to himself and to God.

**29** Let 2 or 3 prophets speak. The other people must examine what the prophets say.

**30** If God gives a revelation to another person who sits there, then the first speaker must stop.

**31** All of you can prophesy, one at a time. Then all the people will learn, and all the people will get encouragement.

**32** The prophets control their own spirits.

**33** God does not cause confusion. God causes peace. This is true in all the churches of the saints.

**34** Your women must be quiet in the churches. They do not have permission to speak. They must obey, as the law also says.

**35** If the women want to learn something, they must ask their husbands at home. It is shameful for a woman to speak in the church.

**36** Did the word of God start with you? Or did the word of God come only to you?

**37** A person can think that he is a prophet or a spiritual person. Then that person must accept that the things which I write to you are commands of the Lord.

**38** But if a person does not accept this, do not accept that person.

**39** Thus, my brothers, have a strong wish to prophesy. Do not stop people who speak in languages.

**40** But do all things correctly and in the correct order.

## Chapter 15

**1** Brothers, now I will remind you about the gospel. I told you the gospel, and you accepted it. You stand firmly in the gospel.

**2** God saves you through the gospel, if you hold firmly to the message that I told you. If you do not, then you believed for no result.

**3** I gave you the most important message, as I received it. Christ died for our sins, as the scriptures say.

**4** People buried him. Then God raised him to life on the third day, as the scriptures say.

**5** Christ showed himself to Cephas, and then to the 12 apostles.

**6** After that, Christ showed himself to more than 500 brothers at one time. Most of those brothers are still alive now. But some of them died.

**7** Then Christ showed himself to James, and then to all the apostles.

**8** Last of all, Christ showed himself to me also. I was like a baby born at the wrong time.

**9** I am the least important of the apostles. I am not good enough to have the name "apostle," because I attacked the church of God.

**10** But I am what I am because of the grace of God. And his grace to me had a good result. I worked harder than all the other apostles. But it was not I. It was the grace of God which was with me.

**11** Thus, it is not important if I or the other apostles preached. We all preach the same message, and you believed that message.

**12** We preach that Christ rose from the dead. So, why do some of you say that the dead do not rise again?

**13** If the dead do not rise again, then Christ did not rise.

**14** And if Christ did not rise, then our message has no value. Also, your faith has no value.

**15** Also, we are false witnesses about God. We said that God raised Christ. But if the dead do not rise, then God did not raise Christ.

**16** If the dead do not rise, then Christ did not rise.

**17** And if Christ did not rise, your faith has no value. You are still guilty of your sins.

**18** Also, the believers in Christ who died are dead forever.

**19** If our hope in Christ is only for this life, then people must pity us more than all other people.

**20** But Christ truly rose from the dead. He is the first of all the dead people who will rise. He is like the first part of the harvest.

**21** Death came through a man. Thus, the resurrection of the dead also comes through a man.

**22** All people die because of Adam. In the same way, God will make all people alive because of Christ.

**23** But each person will rise in the correct order. Christ rose first. Then, when Christ comes again, the people who belong to him will rise.

**24** Then the end will come. Christ will give the kingdom to God the Father. Christ will destroy all rulers, all authority, and all power.

**25** Christ must rule until God puts all enemies under the feet of Christ.

**26** The last enemy that God will destroy is death.

**27** The scripture says, "God put all things under his feet." But "all things" does not include God, because God put all things under Christ.

**28** God will put all things under the control of the Son. Then the Son will put himself under the control of God. God put all things under the Son. Thus, God will be all in all.

**29** Some people receive baptism for the dead. If the dead do not rise, what will those people do? Why do they receive baptism for the dead?

**30** And why are we in danger each hour?

**31** Brothers, I am proud of you in Christ Jesus our Lord. Because of this, I tell you that I am near death each day.

**32** At Ephesus I fought with wild animals, for human reasons only. If the dead do not rise, what did I get from that fight? Then "Let us eat and drink, because tomorrow we will die."

**33** Do not let people trick you. "Bad friends cause good habits to become bad."

**34** Think correctly, as righteous people must. Stop your sins. Some of you do not know God. I say this to make you ashamed.

**35** But a person will possibly ask, "How do the dead rise? What type of body will they have when they come?"

**36** You are foolish! When you put a seed in the ground, the seed must die before it becomes alive.

**37** The thing that you put in the ground is not the plant that will grow. It is only a seed, possibly of wheat or of a different grain.

**38** But God gives the seed a body, as he wants. He gives each type of seed its own body.

**39** All bodies are not the same. People have one type of body. Animals have a different type. Fish have a different type, and birds have a different type.

**40** There are bodies in the sky, and there are bodies on the earth. The glory of the bodies in the sky is of one type. The glory of the bodies on the earth is of a different type.

**41** The sun has one type of glory. The moon has a different glory, and the stars have a different glory. Also, each star has a glory that is different from the other stars.

**42** It is the same with the resurrection of the dead. The body that people bury will decay. The body that God raises will never decay.

**43** The body that people bury has no honor. The body that God raises has glory. The body that people bury is weak. The body that God raises has power.

**44** People bury a natural body. God raises a spiritual body. There is a natural body, and there is also a spiritual body.

**45** The scripture says, "The first man, Adam, became a person with life." The last Adam became a spirit that gives life.

**46** The spiritual body did not come first. The natural body came first. Then the spiritual body came.

**47** The first man came from the dust of the earth. The second man is the Lord from heaven.

**48** The people of the earth are like the man who came from the earth. The people of heaven are like the man who came from heaven.

**49** We are like the man from the earth. In the same way, we will be like the man from heaven.

**50** Brothers, I tell you this: Bodies of flesh and blood cannot receive the kingdom of God. A thing that decays cannot receive a thing that never decays.

**51** Listen, I tell you a secret. Not all of us will die. But God will change all of us.

**52** God will change us very quickly, as fast as the blink of an eye. This will occur when the last trumpet sounds. The trumpet will sound, and God will raise the dead. They will never decay, and God will change us.

**53** Our bodies that can decay must become bodies that never decay. Our bodies that can die must become bodies that never die.

**54** This will occur. Then the words of the scripture will become true: "Victory destroyed death completely."

**55** "Death, where is your victory? Death, where is your power to hurt?"

**56** Sin gives death its power to hurt. And the law gives sin its power.

**57** But we thank God! He gives us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ.

**58** Thus, my dear brothers, stand firmly. Do not let anything move you. Always do much work for the Lord. You know that your work for the Lord has value.

## Chapter 16

**1** Now I will write about the money for the saints. Do the same things that I told the churches in Galatia to do.

**2** On the first day of each week, each of you must put some money aside. Put aside an amount that agrees with your income. Then you will not have to collect money when I come.

**3** When I come, I will send the people whom you choose. I will give them letters. They will take your gift to Jerusalem.

**4** If it is good for me to go also, then those people will go with me.

**5** I will go through Macedonia. After that, I will come to you.

**6** Possibly I will stay with you for some time, or for the whole winter. Then you can help me continue my journey to the place where I will go.

**7** I do not want to see you now only for a short time as I go past. I hope to stay with you for some time, if the Lord permits.

**8** But I will stay in Ephesus until Pentecost.

**9** God gave me a large opportunity for good work here. But many people are against me.

**10** If Timothy comes, make sure that he has no fear when he is with you. He does the work of the Lord, as I do.

**11** Thus, no person must think that Timothy is not important. Help him to continue his journey in peace, so that he can come back to me. I wait for him with the brothers.

**12** Now about our brother Apollos. I asked him many times to go to you with the brothers. But he did not want to go now. He will go when he has an opportunity.

**13** Be alert. Stand firmly in the faith. Be brave. Be strong.

**14** Do all your work with love.

**15** You know the family of Stephanas. They were the first believers in Achaia. They give their lives to serve the saints. Brothers, I ask you this:

**16** Obey such people. Also obey each person who works with us and works hard.

**17** I am happy that Stephanas, Fortunatus, and Achaicus came to me. They gave me the help that you could not give me.

**18** They gave new strength to my spirit and to your spirit. Thus, give honor to such people.

**19** The churches in Asia send you their greetings. Aquila and Priscilla send you many greetings in the Lord. The church that meets in their house also sends greetings.

**20** All the brothers here send you their greetings. Greet each other with a holy kiss.

**21** I, Paul, write these words to greet you with my own hand.

**22** If a person does not love the Lord, let God curse that person. Our Lord, come!

**23** I pray that the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ will be with you.

**24** I give my love to all of you in Christ Jesus. Amen.
