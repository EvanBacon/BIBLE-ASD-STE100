# Philemon

## Chapter 1

**1** I am Paul, a prisoner for Jesus Christ. Our brother Timothy is with me. We write to Philemon, our dear friend who works with us.

**2** We also write to our dear sister Apphia, and to Archippus, who fights with us like a soldier. We also write to the church that meets in your house.

**3** I pray that God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ will give you grace and peace.

**4** I always thank my God when I remember you in my prayers.

**5** I hear about your love and your faith. You have faith in the Lord Jesus, and you love all the saints.

**6** I pray that the faith that you share with other people will be effective. Then you will know fully each good thing that we have in Christ Jesus.

**7** Brother, your love gives me much joy and comfort. You gave new strength to the hearts of the saints.

**8** Because I am in Christ, I can boldly order you to do what is correct.

**9** But because of love, I ask you instead. I am Paul, an old man, and now also a prisoner for Jesus Christ.

**10** I ask you to help my son Onesimus. He became my son in the faith while I was in chains.

**11** In the past, he was not useful to you. But now he is useful to you and to me.

**12** I send him back to you. Accept him, because he is very dear to me, like my own heart.

**13** I wanted to keep him with me. Then he could help me in your place while I am in chains for the gospel.

**14** But I did not want to do anything if you did not agree. I want your good act to come from your own choice, not because you must do it.

**15** Possibly he went away from you for a short time for this reason: then you can have him back for all time.

**16** Now he is not only a slave. He is more than a slave. He is a dear brother. He is very dear to me, but he is even more dear to you. He is dear to you as a man and as a brother in the Lord.

**17** If you think of me as a partner, accept him as you will accept me.

**18** If he did something wrong to you, or if he owes you money, put that cost on my account.

**19** I, Paul, write this with my own hand: I will pay it back. I do not tell you that you owe me your own life.

**20** Yes, brother, I want to get some benefit from you in the Lord. Give my heart new strength in Christ.

**21** I write to you because I am sure that you will obey. I know that you will do more than I ask.

**22** Also, prepare a room for me. I hope that God will answer your prayers and let me come to you.

**23** Epaphras says hello to you. He is in prison with me for Christ Jesus.

**24** Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, and Luke also say hello. They work with me.

**25** I pray that the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ will be with your spirit. Amen.
