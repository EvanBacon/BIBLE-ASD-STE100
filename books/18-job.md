# Job

## Chapter 1

**1** A man named Job lived in the land of Uz. He was a good and honest man. He feared God, and he kept away from evil.

**2** Job had 7 sons and 3 daughters.

**3** He had 7,000 sheep, 3,000 camels, 500 pairs of oxen, and 500 female donkeys. He also had very many servants. He was the most important man of all the people in the east.

**4** The sons of Job usually gave feasts in their houses. Each son had his own day for a feast. They invited their 3 sisters to eat and drink with them.

**5** After each period of feasts, Job sent for his children and made them clean for God. He got up early in the morning. He gave a burnt offering for each of his children. Job thought, "Possibly my sons sinned and cursed God in their hearts." Job always did this.

**6** One day the angels (sons of God) came to stand in front of the LORD. Satan also came with them.

**7** The LORD said to Satan, "Where did you come from?" Satan answered the LORD, "I came from the earth. I went everywhere on the earth."

**8** Then the LORD said to Satan, "Did you look at my servant Job? There is no other person on the earth like him. He is a good and honest man. He fears God, and he keeps away from evil."

**9** Satan answered the LORD, "Job fears God because he gets something for it.

**10** You always protect him, his family, and all his property. You bless all the work that he does. His herds become larger and larger in the land.

**11** But hold out your hand now and take away all that he has. Then he will curse you to your face."

**12** The LORD said to Satan, "All that Job has is now in your power. But do not touch Job himself." Then Satan went away from the LORD.

**13** One day the sons and daughters of Job ate food and drank wine in the house of their oldest brother.

**14** A messenger came to Job and said, "The oxen pulled the plows, and the donkeys ate grass near them.

**15** Then the Sabeans attacked and took the animals. They killed the servants with swords. Only I escaped to tell you."

**16** While he spoke, another messenger came and said, "Fire from God fell from the sky. The fire burned and killed the sheep and the servants. Only I escaped to tell you."

**17** While he spoke, another messenger came and said, "The Chaldeans made 3 groups and attacked the camels. They took the camels away and killed the servants with swords. Only I escaped to tell you."

**18** While he spoke, another messenger came and said, "Your sons and daughters ate food and drank wine in the house of their oldest brother.

**19** Then a strong wind came from the desert. The wind hit the 4 corners of the house. The house fell on the young people, and they died. Only I escaped to tell you."

**20** Then Job stood up and tore his robe. He shaved his head. He fell down on the ground and worshipped God.

**21** He said, "I had no clothes when I came from the body of my mother. I will have no clothes when I go back to the earth. The LORD gave, and the LORD took away. Praise the name of the LORD."

**22** In all these things, Job did not sin. He did not say that God did wrong.

## Chapter 2

**1** On another day the angels (sons of God) came to stand in front of the LORD. Satan also came with them to stand in front of the LORD.

**2** The LORD said to Satan, "Where did you come from?" Satan answered the LORD, "I came from the earth. I went everywhere on the earth."

**3** Then the LORD said to Satan, "Did you look at my servant Job? There is no other person on the earth like him. He is a good and honest man. He fears God, and he keeps away from evil. He continues to be honest. But you caused me to attack him and to destroy him for no reason."

**4** Satan answered the LORD, "A man will give all that he has to save his own life.

**5** But hold out your hand now and hurt his bones and his body. Then he will curse you to your face."

**6** The LORD said to Satan, "Job is in your power. But do not kill him."

**7** Thus Satan went away from the LORD. Satan put painful sores on Job, from the bottom of his feet to the top of his head.

**8** Job took a piece of a broken pot to scratch his skin. He sat in the ashes.

**9** Then his wife said to him, "Do you still continue to be honest? Curse God and die!"

**10** But Job said to her, "You speak like a foolish woman. We accept good things from God. We must also accept trouble from God." In all these things, Job did not sin with his words.

**11** Job had 3 friends: Eliphaz from Teman, Bildad from Shuah, and Zophar from Naamah. They heard about all the trouble that came to Job. Each man came from his own home. They agreed to meet and to go to Job. They wanted to show him their sorrow and to comfort him.

**12** They saw Job from far away, but they did not recognize him. They cried loudly. Each man tore his robe and threw dust in the air over his head.

**13** Then they sat on the ground with Job for 7 days and 7 nights. None of them said a word to him, because they saw that his pain was very great.

## Chapter 3

**1** After this, Job spoke. He cursed the day when he was born.

**2** Job said,

**3** "Let the day when I was born disappear. Let the night disappear when someone said, 'A boy is in the womb.'

**4** Let that day become dark. Let God above forget it. Do not let light shine on it.

**5** Let darkness and deep shadow take it back. Let a cloud stay over it. Let black darkness frighten it.

**6** Let thick darkness take that night. Do not include it in the days of the year. Do not count it in the months.

**7** Let that night have no children born in it. Let no person shout with joy in it.

**8** Let the people who curse days curse that day. These people know how to wake up Leviathan (the sea monster).

**9** Let the stars of its early morning be dark. Let that night wait for light, but get none. Do not let it see the first light of the morning.

**10** That night did not close the womb of my mother. Thus it did not keep trouble away from my eyes.

**11** Why did I not die at birth? Why did I not die when I came out of the body of my mother?

**12** Why did a mother take me on her knees? Why did she give me milk from her breasts?

**13** If I died then, I would now lie down in peace. I would sleep and rest.

**14** I would be with the kings and advisers of the earth. They built cities for themselves that are now ruins.

**15** I would be with rulers who had gold and filled their houses with silver.

**16** I would be like a baby that died before birth and that people buried secretly. I would be like a baby that never saw light.

**17** In the grave, evil people stop their trouble. In the grave, tired people rest.

**18** In the grave, prisoners rest together. They do not hear the shout of the man who forces them to work.

**19** Common people and important people are there. The slave is free from his master.

**20** Why does God give light to people who suffer? Why does he give life to people who are very sad?

**21** These people want to die, but death does not come. They look for death more than for hidden treasure.

**22** They are very happy when they find the grave.

**23** Why does God give life to a man who cannot see his way? God put a wall around that man.

**24** I cry before I eat. My painful cries flow like water.

**25** The thing that I feared most occurred to me. The thing that frightened me came to me.

**26** I have no peace. I have no quiet. I have no rest. Only trouble comes."

## Chapter 4

**1** Then Eliphaz from Teman answered,

**2** "If someone speaks to you, will you become angry? But no person can be silent now.

**3** You taught many people. You gave strength to weak people.

**4** Your words helped people who started to fall. You gave strength to people whose knees were weak.

**5** But now trouble comes to you, and you become weak. Trouble hits you, and you are afraid.

**6** You fear God. Is that not your confidence? You live an honest life. Is that not your hope?

**7** Think about this: Did an innocent person ever die like this? Did God ever destroy good people?

**8** I see this: People who plan evil and do wrong things get evil and trouble in return.

**9** The breath of God destroys them. The strong wind of his anger kills them.

**10** The lion roars, and the young lion shouts. But God breaks the teeth of the strong lions.

**11** The strong lion dies because it cannot find food. The young lions go away in all directions.

**12** A message came to me secretly. I heard only a quiet voice.

**13** It came in a dream at night, when people are in a deep sleep.

**14** I became afraid and shook. All my bones shook.

**15** A spirit went past my face. The hair on my body stood up.

**16** The spirit stopped, but I could not see its shape. A form was in front of my eyes. There was silence. Then I heard a voice that said,

**17** 'Can a man be more just than God? Can a man be more pure than his Maker?

**18** God does not trust his servants. He finds errors in his angels.

**19** Thus he trusts men much less. Men live in bodies of clay. Their bodies come from the dust. A moth can easily crush them.

**20** Between morning and evening, men are destroyed. They die forever, and no person sees it.

**21** Their life ends like a tent when someone pulls out its cords. They die without wisdom.'

## Chapter 5

**1** "Call for help if you want, but no person will answer you. None of the holy angels will help you.

**2** Anger kills a foolish man. Jealousy kills a person who is not wise.

**3** I saw a foolish man who was successful. But suddenly I cursed his home.

**4** His children are not safe. Other people defeat them in court. No person helps them.

**5** Hungry people eat his harvest. They take it even from among the thorns. Thieves take his riches.

**6** Trouble does not grow from the dust. Problems do not grow out of the ground.

**7** But man is born to have trouble. This is as certain as the sparks that fly up from a fire.

**8** If I were you, I would ask God for help. I would give my problem to God.

**9** He does great things that no person can understand. He does more wonderful things than any person can count.

**10** He gives rain to the earth. He sends water to the fields.

**11** He puts humble people in a high position. He keeps sad people safe.

**12** He stops the plans of clever people. Thus they cannot be successful.

**13** He catches wise people in their own tricks. He quickly stops the plans of dishonest people.

**14** These people find darkness in the day. At noon they feel their way as if it is night.

**15** But God saves poor people from the cruel words of their enemies. He saves them from the power of strong people.

**16** Thus poor people have hope, and evil people become silent.

**17** The man that God corrects is fortunate. Thus do not refuse the correction of the Almighty (God).

**18** God hurts, but he also puts bandages on the wounds. He causes injury, but his hands also heal.

**19** He will save you from 6 troubles. In 7 troubles, no evil will touch you.

**20** When there is no food, he will save you from death. In war, he will save you from the sword.

**21** He will protect you from the cruel words of people. You will not be afraid when destruction comes.

**22** You will laugh at destruction and hunger. You will not be afraid of the wild animals.

**23** The stones in the fields will not cause problems for you. The wild animals will be at peace with you.

**24** You will know that your home is safe. You will look at your property, and you will find nothing missing.

**25** You will know that you will have many children. Your descendants will be as many as the grass on the earth.

**26** You will be very old when you die. You will be like grain that people harvest at the correct time.

**27** We examined these things, and they are true. Listen to this advice and use it for your good."

## Chapter 6

**1** Then Job answered,

**2** "I wish that someone could weigh my pain. I wish that someone could put all my trouble on a scale.

**3** It would be heavier than the sand of the sea. That is why my words were wild.

**4** The arrows of the Almighty are in me. My spirit drinks their poison. The terrors of God attack me like an army.

**5** A wild donkey does not make a noise when it has grass. An ox does not make a noise when it has food. Thus I complain because I have a reason.

**6** No person eats food with no taste without salt. The white of an egg has no taste.

**7** I refuse to touch such food. It makes me sick.

**8** I wish that God would give me what I ask. I wish that he would give me what I want.

**9** I want God to destroy me. I want him to use his power to kill me.

**10** Then I would still have comfort. I would be happy, although I have pain that does not stop. I did not refuse the words of the Holy God.

**11** I do not have the strength to continue to hope. I have no future. Thus I have no reason to be patient.

**12** I am not strong like a stone. My body is not made of bronze.

**13** I have no power to help myself. All my success is gone.

**14** A person who has troubles must get kindness from his friends. If not, he can stop his fear of the Almighty.

**15** But my friends are not loyal to me. They are like streams in the desert that become dry.

**16** In winter the streams are dark because of the ice and the melted snow.

**17** But in the dry season, the water disappears. When it is hot, the water dries up.

**18** Groups of traders leave their path to find water. They go into the desert and die.

**19** The traders from Tema look for water. The travelers from Sheba hope to find water.

**20** They are sad because they trusted the streams. They arrive there, but they find no water.

**21** Now you are like those streams. You see my trouble, and you are afraid.

**22** I did not say, 'Give me something.' I did not say, 'Pay money for me from your riches.'

**23** I did not say, 'Save me from my enemy.' I did not say, 'Pay to rescue me from cruel people.'

**24** Teach me, and I will be silent. Show me how I did wrong.

**25** Honest words have power. But your arguments prove nothing.

**26** You want to correct my words. But the words of a man without hope are only wind.

**27** You would even throw dice for an orphan. You would sell your friend.

**28** Now please look at me. I will not tell a lie to your face.

**29** Stop, and do not be unfair. Think again, because I am not guilty.

**30** I do not say evil things. I can know when something is wrong.

## Chapter 7

**1** "Life on earth is hard like the life of a soldier. The days of a man are like the days of a worker who works for pay.

**2** A man is like a slave who wants the shade of evening. He is like a worker who waits for his pay.

**3** I get months of no value. I get nights of trouble.

**4** When I lie down, I think, 'When will I get up?' The night is long. I turn from side to side until the morning.

**5** My body is covered with worms and dirt. My skin breaks open and becomes infected.

**6** My days go faster than the shuttle of a weaver. They come to an end without hope.

**7** God, remember that my life is only a breath. I will not see good things again.

**8** The people who see me now will not see me again. You will look for me, but I will not be there.

**9** A cloud disappears and goes away. In the same way, a person who goes down to the grave does not come back.

**10** He will not come back to his house. The people of his home will not know him again.

**11** Thus I will not be silent. I will speak about the pain of my spirit. I will complain because I am very sad.

**12** Am I the sea or a sea monster? Why do you put a guard over me?

**13** I think, 'My bed will comfort me. My couch will make my complaint easier.'

**14** But then you frighten me with dreams. You make me afraid with visions.

**15** Thus I prefer to choke and die. I prefer death to this body.

**16** I hate my life. I do not want to live forever. Let me be alone, because my days have no meaning.

**17** Man is not important. Why do you think he is so important? Why do you pay so much attention to him?

**18** Why do you examine him each morning? Why do you test him at all times?

**19** You never look away from me. You do not let me be alone for even a short time.

**20** If I sinned, what did I do to you? You watch all men. Why do you make me your target? Why am I a burden to you?

**21** Why do you not forgive my sins? Why do you not take away my wrong acts? Soon I will lie in the dust. You will look for me, but I will not be there."

## Chapter 8

**1** Then Bildad from Shuah answered,

**2** "How long will you speak like this? Your words are like a strong wind.

**3** God is never unfair. The Almighty never does wrong to justice.

**4** Your children sinned against God. Thus he punished them for their sins.

**5** But you must look for God. Ask the Almighty for mercy.

**6** If you are pure and honest, he will quickly help you. He will give you back the home that you deserve.

**7** At first you had little. But in the future you will have very much.

**8** Ask the old people. Learn what their ancestors found out.

**9** We were born only a short time ago, and we know nothing. Our days on earth are like a shadow.

**10** The old people will teach you and tell you. They will tell you what they understand.

**11** Papyrus cannot grow without mud. Reeds cannot grow without water.

**12** While they are still green and nobody cuts them, they dry up faster than other plants.

**13** People who forget God are like these plants. The hope of evil people will disappear.

**14** The things that they trust are weak. Their trust is like the web of a spider.

**15** They lean against the web, but it breaks. They hold it tightly, but it does not stay.

**16** An evil person is like a plant with much water in the sun. Its branches grow over the garden.

**17** Its roots go around a pile of rocks. They hold the stones.

**18** But when someone pulls the plant from its place, that place will forget it. The place will say, 'I never saw you.'

**19** That is the end of the happiness of an evil person. Then other plants will grow from the same ground.

**20** God does not refuse an honest man. And God does not help evil people.

**21** God will again cause you to laugh. He will again cause you to shout with joy.

**22** The people who hate you will be ashamed. The homes of evil people will disappear."

## Chapter 9

**1** Then Job answered,

**2** "I know that this is true. But how can a man be right with God?

**3** If a man wants to argue with God, he cannot answer 1 question in 1,000.

**4** God is wise and very strong. No person can fight against him and be safe.

**5** He moves mountains, and they do not know it. In his anger he turns them over.

**6** He shakes the earth from its place. He causes its foundations to shake.

**7** He tells the sun not to shine, and it does not shine. He hides the stars.

**8** Only he spread out the skies. He walks on the waves of the sea.

**9** He made the Bear, Orion, and the Pleiades (groups of stars). He made the groups of stars in the south.

**10** He does great things that no person can understand. He does more wonderful things than any person can count.

**11** He goes past me, but I cannot see him. He moves near me, but I do not know it.

**12** He takes things away, and no person can stop him. No person can say to him, 'What do you do?'

**13** God does not stop his anger. Even the helpers of Rahab (the sea monster) bowed down at his feet.

**14** Thus I cannot answer God. I cannot find the words to argue with him.

**15** Although I am innocent, I cannot answer him. I can only ask my Judge for mercy.

**16** If I called him and he answered, I would not believe that he listened to me.

**17** He attacks me with a storm. He gives me many wounds for no reason.

**18** He does not let me get my breath. He fills me with bitter troubles.

**19** If it is a matter of strength, God is strong. If it is a matter of justice, who will call him to court?

**20** Although I am innocent, my own words would make me guilty. Although I am honest, my words would prove that I am bad.

**21** I am innocent, but I do not care about myself. I hate my life.

**22** It is all the same. Thus I say, 'God destroys innocent people and evil people.'

**23** When a disaster suddenly kills people, God laughs at the pain of innocent people.

**24** God gives the earth into the power of evil people. He covers the eyes of the judges. If God does not do this, who does?

**25** My days go faster than a man who runs. They go away quickly, and they see no happiness.

**26** They go past like fast boats. They are like an eagle that flies down to catch its food.

**27** I can say, 'I will forget my complaint. I will change my sad face and smile.'

**28** But I am still afraid of all my pain. I know that you will not say that I am innocent.

**29** You will say that I am guilty. Thus why must I try for no reason?

**30** I can wash myself with soap. I can make my hands very clean.

**31** But you will push me into a hole of mud. Then even my clothes will hate me.

**32** God is not a man like me. I cannot answer him. We cannot go to court together.

**33** There is no judge between us. There is no person who can put his hand on both of us.

**34** I want God to take away his rod from me. Then his power will not frighten me.

**35** Then I would speak and not fear him. But now I cannot do that.

## Chapter 10

**1** "I hate my life. Thus I will speak freely about my complaint. I will speak about my bitter feelings.

**2** I will say to God, 'Do not say that I am guilty. Tell me why you argue with me.

**3** Is it good for you to be cruel to me? Why do you refuse me, whom your hands made? Why do you make the plans of evil people successful?

**4** You do not have the eyes of a man. You do not see as a man sees.

**5** Your life is not short like the life of a man. Your years are not like the years of a man.

**6** Thus why do you look for my wrong acts? Why do you search for my sins?

**7** You know that I am not guilty. No person can save me from your power.

**8** Your hands shaped me and made me. But now you destroy me.

**9** Remember that you made me from clay. Will you change me back into dust?

**10** You poured me out like milk. You made me solid like cheese.

**11** You put skin and flesh on me. You joined my bones and muscles together.

**12** You gave me life and showed me kindness. Your care kept my spirit alive.

**13** But you hid a different plan in your heart. I know that this was in your mind.

**14** If I sinned, you would watch me. You would not forgive my wrong acts.

**15** If I am guilty, I will have much trouble. If I am innocent, I still cannot lift my head with honor. I am full of shame. Look at my trouble.

**16** If I lift my head, you hunt me like a lion. You show your great power against me again.

**17** You bring new witnesses against me. Your anger against me becomes larger. You send new armies against me.

**18** Why did you let me be born? I wish that I died before any person saw me.

**19** I wish that I never lived. I wish that I went from the womb to the grave.

**20** My life is almost at an end. Let me be alone, so that I can have a little comfort.

**21** Soon I will go to the land of darkness and deep shadow. I will not come back from that place.

**22** It is a land of very deep darkness, and it has no order. In that land, even the light is like darkness.'"

## Chapter 11

**1** Then Zophar from Naamah answered,

**2** "Someone must answer all these words. A man who speaks much is not right because of his many words.

**3** Your foolish talk will not make men silent. When you insult God, someone must make you ashamed.

**4** You say to God, 'My teaching is correct. I am pure in your eyes.'

**5** But I wish that God would speak. I wish that he would answer you.

**6** I wish that he would show you the secrets of wisdom. True wisdom has 2 sides. Know this: God punishes you less than your sins deserve.

**7** You cannot know all the secrets of God. You cannot learn the limits of the Almighty.

**8** His wisdom is higher than the skies. What can you do? It is deeper than the grave. What can you know?

**9** It is longer than the earth. It is wider than the sea.

**10** If God puts a person in prison or brings him to court, no person can stop God.

**11** God knows which people are false. When he sees evil, he pays attention to it.

**12** A foolish man will become wise only when a wild donkey is born as a man.

**13** But you must make your heart ready. Lift your hands to God in prayer.

**14** If you do wrong, stop it. Do not let evil live in your house.

**15** Then you can lift up your face without shame. You will be strong and not afraid.

**16** You will forget your trouble. You will remember it only as water that flowed away.

**17** Your life will be brighter than the sun at noon. The darkness will become like the morning.

**18** You will feel safe, because you will have hope. You will look around you and rest in safety.

**19** You will lie down, and no person will frighten you. Many people will ask you for help.

**20** But the eyes of evil people will become weak. They will not escape. Their only hope is death."

## Chapter 12

**1** Then Job answered,

**2** "Surely you think that you are the only wise people. You think that wisdom will die when you die.

**3** But I have understanding as you do. I am not less than you. All people know these things.

**4** My friends laugh at me. I called to God, and he answered me. But people laugh at me, although I am good and honest.

**5** People who live in comfort hate the people who have trouble. They think that God hurts only the people who are ready to fall.

**6** But the tents of robbers have peace. People who make God angry are safe. They think that their power is their god.

**7** But ask the animals, and they will teach you. Ask the birds of the sky, and they will tell you.

**8** Or speak to the earth, and it will teach you. The fish of the sea will tell you.

**9** All these creatures know that the LORD made them with his hand.

**10** The life of each creature and the breath of all people are in the power of God.

**11** The ear tests words as the mouth tastes food.

**12** Old people have wisdom. A long life gives understanding.

**13** But God has true wisdom and power. He has good advice and understanding.

**14** If God breaks something down, no person can build it again. If he puts a man in prison, no person can let him out.

**15** If God stops the rain, the land becomes dry. If he lets the water go, it floods the land.

**16** God has strength and wisdom. He controls the person who tricks others. He also controls the person whom others trick.

**17** God takes away the wisdom of advisers. He makes judges foolish.

**18** He removes the authority of kings. He puts a cloth around their bodies like prisoners.

**19** He takes away the power of priests. He defeats strong people.

**20** He makes trusted advisers silent. He takes away the wisdom of old people.

**21** He makes rulers ashamed. He takes away the strength of powerful people.

**22** He shows the secrets that are hidden in darkness. He brings deep shadows into the light.

**23** He makes nations great, and then he destroys them. He makes nations larger, and then he sends them away.

**24** He takes away the understanding of the leaders of the earth. He makes them go in all directions in a desert with no path.

**25** They feel their way in the dark without light. He makes them go from side to side like drunk men.

## Chapter 13

**1** "I saw all this with my eyes. I heard it with my ears and understood it.

**2** I know what you know. I am not less than you.

**3** But I want to speak to the Almighty. I want to argue my case with God.

**4** You hide the truth with lies. You are all like doctors who cannot heal.

**5** I wish that you would be silent. That would be wise for you.

**6** Now listen to my argument. Hear the words that I say in my defense.

**7** Will you say wrong things for God? Will you tell lies for him?

**8** Will you be on his side? Will you argue for God?

**9** If God examines you, will he find good things? Can you trick God as you trick a man?

**10** If you secretly take the side of God, he will surely punish you.

**11** His power will frighten you. You will be very afraid of him.

**12** Your wise words have no more value than ashes. Your arguments are weak like clay.

**13** Be silent and let me speak. Let anything occur to me.

**14** I will put my life in danger. I will take this risk.

**15** If God kills me, I will still trust him. But I will argue my case in front of him.

**16** This possibly will save me, because an evil person cannot come in front of God.

**17** Listen carefully to my words. Hear what I tell you.

**18** I made my case ready. I know that God will say that I am innocent.

**19** Can any person show that I am wrong? If someone can, I will be silent and die.

**20** God, give me only 2 things. Then I will not hide from you.

**21** Take your hand far away from me. Do not frighten me with your power.

**22** Then call me, and I will answer. Or let me speak, and then answer me.

**23** How many wrong acts and sins did I do? Show me my wrong acts and my sin.

**24** Why do you hide your face from me? Why do you think that I am your enemy?

**25** I am like a leaf that the wind blows. Will you frighten me? I am like dry grass. Will you chase me?

**26** You write bitter charges against me. You punish me for the sins that I did when I was young.

**27** You put my feet in chains. You watch all my paths. You put marks around the bottoms of my feet.

**28** Thus I become weak like rotten wood. I am like a coat that moths eat."

## Chapter 14

**1** "A man that a woman gives birth to lives only a short time. His life is full of trouble.

**2** He grows like a flower, and then he dies. He goes away like a shadow, and he does not stay.

**3** Do you watch such a weak person? Do you bring me to court in front of you?

**4** No person can make a clean thing from a thing that is not clean.

**5** You decide how long a man will live. You know the number of his months. You set limits that he cannot go past.

**6** Thus look away from him and let him rest. Let him have peace, like a worker at the end of his day.

**7** If a person cuts down a tree, the tree has hope. It can grow again. New branches will continue to grow from it.

**8** Its roots can become old in the ground. Its stump can die in the soil.

**9** But when the tree gets water, it will grow again. It will make branches like a young plant.

**10** But a man dies and becomes weak. He takes his last breath, and then he is gone.

**11** Water disappears from a lake. A river becomes dry.

**12** In the same way, a man lies down and does not get up again. Until the skies disappear, dead people will not wake up from their sleep.

**13** I wish that you would hide me in the grave. Hide me until your anger stops. Then choose a time and remember me.

**14** If a man dies, will he live again? I will wait during all my hard days until my relief comes.

**15** You will call, and I will answer you. You will want to see me again, because you made me.

**16** Then you will count my steps, but you will not look for my sins.

**17** You will close my wrong acts in a bag and seal it. You will cover my sins.

**18** But a mountain falls and breaks into pieces. A rock moves from its place.

**19** Water wears away stones. Floods wash away the soil. In the same way, you destroy the hope of man.

**20** You always defeat a man, and he goes away. You change his face in death and send him away.

**21** If people give honor to his sons, he does not know it. If people bring shame to his sons, he does not see it.

**22** He feels only the pain of his own body. He mourns only for himself."

**8** The proud animals did not walk on it. The fierce lion did not go by it.

**9** The miner puts his hand on the hard rock. He turns the mountains over at their base.

**10** He cuts channels through the rocks. His eye sees all the valuable things.

**11** He stops the streams so that they do not flow. He brings hidden things into the light.

**9** Will the wild ox agree to serve you? Will it stay at night near your feed box?

**10** Can you tie the wild ox with a rope to plow a furrow? Will it break up the soil in the valleys for you?

**11** Will you trust the wild ox because its strength is great? Will you give your heavy work to it?

**12** Can you trust it to bring your grain home? Will it collect the grain to your threshing floor?

**13** The wings of the ostrich move with joy. But its wings and feathers do not show love like the wings of the stork.

**14** The ostrich puts her eggs on the ground. She lets the sand keep them warm.

**15** She does not think that a foot can crush the eggs. She does not think that a wild animal can break them.

**16** She is cruel to her young, as if they are not hers. She is not afraid that her work is for no purpose.

**17** God did not give her wisdom. He did not give her understanding.

**18** But when she runs fast, she laughs at the horse and its rider.

**19** Do you give the horse its strength? Do you put the mane on its neck?

**20** Can you cause the horse to jump like a locust? Its proud snort causes fear.

**21** It paws the ground in the valley and is glad in its strength. It runs out to meet the weapons.

**22** It laughs at fear and is not afraid. It does not turn back from the sword.

**23** The quiver of arrows rattles on its side. The spear and the javelin shine there.

**24** It runs quickly over the ground with fierce excitement. It cannot stand still when the trumpet sounds.

**25** When the trumpet sounds, it says, 'Aha!' It smells the battle from far away. It hears the shouts of the commanders and the battle cry.

**26** Does the hawk fly because of your wisdom? Does it spread its wings to the south because you know how?

**27** Does the eagle fly high because you command it? Does it make its nest high in the mountains because of you?

**28** The eagle lives on the high rock. It stays at night on the cliff, in a strong place.

**29** From there, it looks for food. Its eyes see the food from far away.

**30** Its young birds drink blood. Where there are dead bodies, the eagle is there."

## Chapter 40

**1** The LORD continued to speak to Job. He said,

**2** "Will the man who argues with the Almighty correct him? The man who says that God is wrong must answer."

**3** Then Job answered the LORD. He said,

**4** "Look, I am not important. What can I say to you? I put my hand over my mouth.

**5** I spoke one time, but I will not answer again. I spoke 2 times, but I will say nothing more."

**6** Then the LORD answered Job from the storm. He said,

**7** "Prepare yourself like a man. I will ask you questions, and you must answer me.

**8** Will you say that my justice is wrong? Will you say that I am guilty so that you can be right?

**9** Is your arm as strong as God's arm? Can you thunder with a voice like his voice?

**10** If so, put on majesty and honor. Put on glory and splendor as clothing.

**11** Pour out the power of your anger. Look at every proud person and make him humble.

**12** Look at every proud person and bring him down. Crush the wicked where they stand.

**13** Bury all of them together in the dust. Lock them in the grave.

**14** Then I will also agree that your own right hand can save you.

**15** Look at Behemoth. I made him as I made you. He eats grass like an ox.

**16** Look, his strength is in his hips. His power is in the muscles of his stomach.

**17** His tail is as stiff as a cedar tree. The muscles of his thighs are strong and tight.

**18** His bones are like tubes of bronze. His legs are like bars of iron.

**19** He is the first among the works of God. Only his Maker can come near him with a sword.

**20** The mountains give him food, where all the wild animals play.

**21** He lies under the lotus plants. He hides among the reeds in the marsh.

**22** The lotus plants cover him with their shade. The willows near the stream are around him.

**23** Look, if the river floods, he is not afraid. He is calm even when the Jordan River rushes against his mouth.

**24** Can a person catch him while he watches? Can a person put a ring through his nose with a trap?"

## Chapter 41

**1** "Can you pull out Leviathan with a fishhook? Can you tie down his tongue with a rope?

**2** Can you put a rope through his nose? Can you put a hook through his jaw?

**3** Will he ask you many times for mercy? Will he speak gentle words to you?

**4** Will he make an agreement (covenant) with you? Will you make him your slave for all time?

**5** Will you play with him as with a bird? Will you put him on a leash for your young girls?

**6** Will traders buy and sell him? Will they divide him among the merchants?

**7** Can you fill his skin with harpoons? Can you fill his head with fish spears?

**8** Put your hand on him one time. You will remember the fight, and you will not do it again.

**9** Look, any hope to defeat him is false. Only the sight of him makes a person fall down.

**10** No person is fierce enough to wake him up. Thus who can stand in front of me?

**11** Who gave something to me first, so that I must pay him back? Everything under the whole sky is mine.

**12** I will speak about the parts of Leviathan's body. I will tell about his great strength and his good form.

**13** Who can pull off his outer skin? Who can come near him with a bridle?

**14** Who can open the doors of his mouth? His teeth cause terror all around.

**15** His back has rows of shields. They are closed tightly together, as with a seal.

**16** Each one is so near to the next one that no air can go between them.

**17** They join one to another. They hold together, and no person can separate them.

**18** When he sneezes, light flashes. His eyes are like the first light of the morning.

**19** Burning torches come out of his mouth. Sparks of fire jump out.

**20** Smoke comes out of his nose, as from a pot that boils over a fire of reeds.

**21** His breath sets coals on fire. A flame comes out of his mouth.

**22** His neck is very strong. Terror goes in front of him.

**23** The folds of his skin join tightly together. They are firm, and no person can move them.

**24** His heart is as hard as a stone. It is as hard as the lower millstone.

**25** When he stands up, the mighty are afraid. They step back because of his attack.

**26** A sword that hits him has no effect. Spears, darts and javelins also have no effect.

**27** He thinks that iron is like straw. He thinks that bronze is like rotten wood.

**28** Arrows do not cause him to run away. Stones from a sling are like dry grass to him.

**29** He thinks that clubs are like dry grass. He laughs at the noise of a spear.

**30** His lower parts are like sharp pieces of broken pots. He leaves marks in the mud like a threshing sledge.

**31** He causes the deep water to boil like a pot. He causes the sea to bubble like a pot of perfume.

**32** He leaves a shining path behind him. A person thinks that the deep water has white hair.

**33** Nothing on earth is equal to him. He is a creature without fear.

**34** He looks down on all the proud animals. He is king over all the proud creatures."

## Chapter 42

**1** Then Job answered the LORD. He said,

**2** "I know that you can do all things. No person can stop your plans.

**3** You asked, 'Who is this man who hides my plan without knowledge?' I spoke about things that I did not understand. These things were too wonderful for me to know.

**4** You said, 'Listen now, and I will speak. I will ask you questions, and you must answer me.'

**5** Before, I only heard about you with my ears. But now I see you with my eyes.

**6** Thus I hate what I said. I sit in dust and ashes, and I change my mind (repent)."

**7** The LORD finished speaking these words to Job. Then the LORD said to Eliphaz the Temanite, "I am angry with you and with your 2 friends. You did not speak the truth about me, as my servant Job did.

**8** Thus take 7 bulls and 7 rams now. Go to my servant Job. Give them as a burnt offering for yourselves. My servant Job will pray for you, and I will accept his prayer. Then I will not punish you for your foolishness. You did not speak the truth about me, as my servant Job did."

**9** So Eliphaz the Temanite, Bildad the Shuhite and Zophar the Naamathite went. They did what the LORD commanded them. The LORD accepted the prayer of Job.

**10** Job prayed for his friends. Then the LORD gave back to Job all his prosperity. The LORD gave Job 2 times as much as he had before.

**11** Then all the brothers and sisters of Job came to him. All the people who knew him before also came. They ate food with him in his house. They showed him sympathy. They comforted him for all the trouble that the LORD brought on him. Each person gave him a piece of silver and a gold ring.

**12** The LORD blessed the last part of Job's life more than the first part. Job had 14,000 sheep, 6,000 camels, 1,000 pairs of oxen and 1,000 female donkeys.

**13** He also had 7 sons and 3 daughters.

**14** He gave the name Jemimah to the first daughter. He gave the name Keziah to the second daughter. He gave the name Keren-Happuch to the third daughter.

**15** In all the land, no women were as beautiful as the daughters of Job. Their father gave them an inheritance with their brothers.

**16** After this, Job lived 140 years. He saw his children and his grandchildren for 4 generations.

**17** Then Job died. He was very old, and he had a long and full life.
