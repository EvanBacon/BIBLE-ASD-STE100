# Acts

## Chapter 1

**1** Theophilus, in my first book I wrote about all the things that Jesus started to do and teach.

**2** I wrote until the day when God took him up into heaven. Before that day, Jesus gave orders through the Holy Spirit to the apostles that he chose.

**3** After he suffered, he showed himself alive to the apostles. He gave them many sure proofs. They saw him during 40 days. He spoke to them about the kingdom of God.

**4** He met with them and gave them an order. He said, "Do not go away from Jerusalem. Wait for the gift that the Father promised. You heard me speak about this gift.

**5** John baptized with water. But after a few days, God will baptize you with the Holy Spirit."

**6** When the apostles were together, they asked him, "Lord, will you now give the kingdom back to Israel?"

**7** He said to them, "You do not need to know the times or the dates. The Father decides these by his own authority.

**8** But you will receive power when the Holy Spirit comes on you. You will speak about me in Jerusalem, in all Judea, and in Samaria. You will speak about me to the most distant parts of the earth."

**9** After he said these things, God took him up while they looked. A cloud took him away, and they could not see him.

**10** They looked up into the sky while he went up. Then 2 men in white clothes stood near them.

**11** The men said, "Men of Galilee, why do you stand and look up into the sky? Jesus went up from you into heaven. He will come back in the same way that you saw him go into heaven."

**12** Then the apostles went back to Jerusalem from the Mount of Olives. This mountain is near Jerusalem, about 1 kilometer away.

**13** When they came into the city, they went up into an upstairs room where they stayed. Peter, James, John, and Andrew were there. Philip, Thomas, Bartholomew, and Matthew were there. James the son of Alphaeus, Simon the Zealot, and Judas the brother of James were also there.

**14** All of them prayed together continuously with 1 purpose. Some women, Mary the mother of Jesus, and the brothers of Jesus were with them.

**15** In those days, Peter stood up among the disciples. There were about 120 people together. Peter said,

**16** "Men and brothers, the scripture had to come true. The Holy Spirit spoke through David a long time ago about Judas. Judas guided the men who seized Jesus.

**17** He was one of us, and he had a part in this work."

**18** Judas bought a field with the money that he got for his evil act. He fell head first, and his body broke open in the middle. All his intestines came out.

**19** All the people who lived in Jerusalem knew about this. Thus they called that field Akeldama in their own language. This name means "Field of Blood."

**20** Peter said, "The Book of Psalms says, 'Let his house become empty. Let no person live in it.' It also says, 'Let a different man take his position.'

**21** Thus we must choose 1 of the men who were with us during all the time that the Lord Jesus went in and out among us.

**22** That time started when John baptized Jesus. It continued until the day when God took Jesus up from us. The man that we choose must speak with us about the resurrection of Jesus."

**23** They named 2 men. One was Joseph, who had the name Barsabbas. Joseph also had the name Justus. The other was Matthias.

**24** Then they prayed. They said, "Lord, you know the hearts of all men. Show us which of these 2 men you chose.

**25** Show us who will take the position in this work as an apostle. Judas left this position and went to his own place."

**26** Then they threw lots for the 2 men. The lot chose Matthias. Thus Matthias became an apostle with the 11 apostles.

## Chapter 2

**1** When the day of Pentecost came, all the believers were together in 1 place.

**2** Suddenly a sound came from heaven. It was like the sound of a strong wind. The sound filled all the house where they sat.

**3** They saw tongues that looked like fire. The tongues divided, and 1 tongue sat on each of them.

**4** The Holy Spirit filled all of them. They started to speak in other languages. The Spirit gave them the ability to speak.

**5** Jews lived in Jerusalem at that time. They were religious men from each nation of the world.

**6** When they heard this sound, a large crowd came together. The people were confused, because each man heard them speak in his own language.

**7** All of them were very surprised. They said to each other, "Look, are all these men who speak not from Galilee?

**8** Then how does each of us hear them speak in the language of the country where we were born?

**9** We are Parthians, Medes, and Elamites. We are people who live in Mesopotamia, Judea, Cappadocia, Pontus, and Asia.

**10** We are from Phrygia, Pamphylia, Egypt, and parts of Libya near Cyrene. We are visitors from Rome, Jews and converts to the Jewish religion.

**11** We are Cretans and Arabians. We hear them speak in our own languages about the great works of God."

**12** All of them were surprised and confused. They said to each other, "What does this mean?"

**13** Other people laughed at them and said, "These men drank too much new wine."

**14** But Peter stood up with the 11 apostles. He spoke loudly to the people. He said, "Men of Judea and all the people who live in Jerusalem, know this. Listen to my words.

**15** These men are not drunk as you think. It is only the third hour of the day.

**16** But this is the thing that the prophet Joel spoke about.

**17** God says, 'In the last days, I will put my Spirit on all people. Your sons and your daughters will speak as prophets. Your young men will see visions. Your old men will have dreams.

**18** In those days I will put my Spirit on my servants, men and women. They will speak as prophets.

**19** I will show wonders in the sky above. I will show signs on the earth below: blood, fire, and clouds of smoke.

**20** The sun will become dark. The moon will become red like blood. These things will occur before the great and famous day of the Lord comes.

**21** God will save each person who calls on the name of the Lord.'

**22** Men of Israel, listen to these words. Jesus of Nazareth was a man whom God showed to you. God did miracles, wonders, and signs through him among you. You know this.

**23** God planned and knew before that Jesus was to be given to you. You took him. With the help of evil men, you crucified him and killed him.

**24** But God brought him back to life. God made him free from the pain of death. Death could not keep him.

**25** David speaks about him: 'I always saw the Lord in front of me. He is at my right hand, so that I do not fall.

**26** Thus my heart was glad and my tongue was happy. My body will also live in hope.

**27** You will not leave my soul in the place of the dead. You will not let your Holy One decay.

**28** You showed me the ways of life. You will fill me with joy when I am with you.'

**29** Men and brothers, I can speak openly to you about the patriarch David. He died and they buried him. His tomb is with us today.

**30** David was a prophet. He knew that God made a promise to him with an oath. God promised to put one of the descendants of David on his throne. That descendant was the Christ.

**31** David saw this before it occurred. He spoke about the resurrection of the Christ. He said that God did not leave the Christ in the place of the dead, and that his body did not decay.

**32** God brought this Jesus back to life. All of us saw it.

**33** God lifted Jesus up to his right hand. Jesus received the promised Holy Spirit from the Father. He gave the Spirit to us. You now see and hear this.

**34** David did not go up into heaven. But he says, 'The Lord said to my Lord, "Sit at my right hand

**35** until I put your enemies under your feet."'

**36** Thus all the people of Israel must know this as sure: God made this Jesus both Lord and Christ. You crucified him."

**37** When the people heard this, they felt great pain in their hearts. They said to Peter and the other apostles, "Men and brothers, what must we do?"

**38** Peter said to them, "Turn away from your sins. Each of you, get baptism in the name of Jesus Christ so that God forgives your sins. Then you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.

**39** The promise is for you and for your children. It is for all the people who are far away. It is for all the people whom the Lord our God calls."

**40** Peter spoke many other words to them and gave them strong advice. He said, "Make yourselves safe from these evil people."

**41** Then the people who received his message got baptism. About 3,000 people joined them on that day.

**42** They continued in the teaching of the apostles and in their fellowship. They continued to break bread together and to pray.

**43** All the people felt fear. The apostles did many wonders and signs.

**44** All the believers were together. They shared all their things.

**45** They sold their land and their things. They gave the money to all the people, as each person had a need.

**46** Each day they continued to meet together in the temple with 1 purpose. They broke bread in their houses. They ate their food with joy and with sincere hearts.

**47** They praised God, and all the people liked them. Each day the Lord added to the church the people whom he saved.

## Chapter 3

**1** Peter and John went up together into the temple at the time of prayer. It was the ninth hour of the day.

**2** People carried a man there who could not walk from birth. Each day they put him at the gate of the temple with the name Beautiful. He asked for money from the people who went into the temple.

**3** He saw Peter and John when they were about to go into the temple. He asked them for money.

**4** Peter and John looked at him. Peter said, "Look at us."

**5** The man looked at them, because he thought that he would get something from them.

**6** Then Peter said, "I have no silver or gold. But I give you the thing that I have. In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, get up and walk."

**7** Peter took him by the right hand and lifted him up. Immediately his feet and ankle bones became strong.

**8** He jumped up, stood, and walked. He went with them into the temple. He walked, jumped, and praised God.

**9** All the people saw him walk and praise God.

**10** They knew that he was the man who sat and asked for money at the Beautiful Gate of the temple. They were very surprised because of the thing that occurred to him.

**11** The healed man held Peter and John. All the people ran together to them in the porch of Solomon. The people were very surprised.

**12** When Peter saw this, he spoke to the people. He said, "Men of Israel, why are you surprised at this? Why do you look at us? We did not make this man walk by our own power or holiness.

**13** The God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, the God of our fathers, gave glory to his Servant Jesus. You gave Jesus to Pilate. Pilate decided to release him, but you rejected Jesus in front of Pilate.

**14** You rejected the Holy and Righteous One. You asked Pilate to give you a killer.

**15** You killed the Author of life. But God brought him back to life from the dead. We saw this.

**16** This man is a man whom you see and know. Faith in the name of Jesus made him strong. The faith that comes through Jesus made him fully healthy in front of all of you.

**17** Now, brothers, I know that you did this because you did not know. Your leaders also did not know.

**18** But in this way God did the things that he told before through all his prophets. He said that his Christ was to suffer.

**19** Thus turn away from your sins and turn to God. Then God will remove your sins. Then times of new strength will come from the Lord.

**20** He will send Jesus Christ, whom he chose for you before.

**21** Heaven must keep Jesus until the time when God makes all things new. God spoke about this time through his holy prophets since the start of the world.

**22** Moses said to the fathers, 'The Lord your God will cause a prophet like me to come from among your brothers. Listen to all the things that he tells you.

**23** God will completely destroy from among the people each person who does not listen to that prophet.'

**24** All the prophets, from Samuel and the prophets after him, also spoke about these days.

**25** You are the children of the prophets. You are the children of the covenant that God made with our fathers. God said to Abraham, 'Through your descendants I will bless all the families of the earth.'

**26** God brought his Servant Jesus back to life. He sent him to you first to bless you. He sent him to turn each of you away from your evil acts."

## Chapter 4

**1** While Peter and John spoke to the people, the priests came to them. The captain of the temple guard and the Sadducees also came.

**2** They were very angry because Peter and John taught the people. They taught that through Jesus the dead come back to life.

**3** They seized Peter and John and put them in jail until the next day, because it was already evening.

**4** But many of the people who heard the message believed. The number of the men who believed was about 5,000.

**5** On the next day, the rulers, elders, and scribes met together in Jerusalem.

**6** Annas the high priest was there. Caiaphas, John, Alexander, and all the family of the high priest were also there.

**7** They put Peter and John in the middle of them and asked, "By what power or by what name did you do this?"

**8** Then the Holy Spirit filled Peter. He said to them, "Rulers of the people and elders of Israel,

**9** today you ask us about a good act that we did for a sick man. You ask how we healed him.

**10** All of you and all the people of Israel must know this. We healed this man by the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth. You crucified Jesus, but God brought him back to life from the dead. Through Jesus, this man stands here healthy in front of you.

**11** Jesus is the stone that you builders rejected. He became the most important stone of the building.

**12** No other person can save us. There is no other name under heaven that God gave to people to save us."

**13** The leaders saw that Peter and John were brave. They knew that Peter and John did not go to school and were ordinary men. The leaders were surprised. They knew that these men were with Jesus.

**14** They saw the healed man stand with Peter and John. Thus they could say nothing against them.

**15** They told the apostles to go out of the council. Then they spoke among themselves.

**16** They said, "What will we do to these men? All the people who live in Jerusalem know that they did a famous miracle. We cannot say that it did not occur.

**17** But we must stop this news so that it does not go farther among the people. We will tell them strongly not to speak to any man in this name again."

**18** They called Peter and John back. They told them not to speak or teach in the name of Jesus at all.

**19** But Peter and John answered them, "Is it correct in the eyes of God to obey you more than God? You decide.

**20** We cannot stop. We must speak about the things that we saw and heard."

**21** The leaders threatened them more, and then they released them. They found no way to punish them because of the people. All the people praised God for the thing that occurred.

**22** The man whom they healed by this miracle was more than 40 years old.

**23** After they were released, Peter and John went to their own people. They told them all the things that the chief priests and elders said to them.

**24** When the people heard this, they spoke to God together with 1 purpose. They said, "Lord, you are God. You made heaven, earth, the sea, and all the things in them.

**25** Through the Holy Spirit you spoke by the mouth of our father David, your servant. You said, 'Why were the nations angry? Why did the people make plans that are of no use?

**26** The kings of the earth stood together. The rulers came together against the Lord and against his Christ.'

**27** Truly, Herod and Pontius Pilate came together in this city against your holy Servant Jesus, whom you chose. The Gentiles and the people of Israel were with them.

**28** They did the things that you decided before by your power and your plan.

**29** Now, Lord, look at their threats. Give your servants the ability to speak your word bravely.

**30** Put out your hand to heal. Let signs and wonders occur through the name of your holy Servant Jesus."

**31** After they prayed, the place where they met shook. The Holy Spirit filled all of them. They spoke the word of God bravely.

**32** All the believers had 1 heart and 1 soul. No person said that his things were his own. They shared all things.

**33** The apostles spoke with great power about the resurrection of the Lord Jesus. God gave great grace to all of them.

**34** None of them had a need. The people who had land or houses sold them. They brought the money from the sale.

**35** They put the money at the feet of the apostles. The apostles gave money to each person as he had a need.

**36** Joseph was a Levite from the country of Cyprus. The apostles gave him the name Barnabas. This name means "Son of Encouragement."

**37** Joseph had a field. He sold it and brought the money. He put it at the feet of the apostles.

## Chapter 5

**1** But a man with the name Ananias and his wife Sapphira sold some land.

**2** Ananias kept part of the money for himself. His wife also knew this. He brought only a part of the money and put it at the feet of the apostles.

**3** But Peter said, "Ananias, why did Satan fill your heart? You lied to the Holy Spirit. You kept part of the money from the land for yourself.

**4** Before you sold the land, it was yours. After you sold it, the money was yours to control. Why did you plan this in your heart? You did not lie to men but to God."

**5** When Ananias heard these words, he fell down and died. All the people who heard these things felt great fear.

**6** The young men got up and put cloth around his body. They carried him out and buried him.

**7** About 3 hours later, his wife came in. She did not know what occurred.

**8** Peter said to her, "Tell me, did you sell the land for this much money?" She said, "Yes, for that much."

**9** Then Peter said to her, "Why did you and your husband agree to test the Spirit of the Lord? Look, the feet of the men who buried your husband are at the door. They will carry you out."

**10** Immediately she fell down at his feet and died. The young men came in and found her dead. They carried her out and buried her next to her husband.

**11** All the church and all the people who heard these things felt great fear.

**12** The apostles did many signs and wonders among the people. All the believers were together with 1 purpose in the porch of Solomon.

**13** No other person was brave enough to join them. But the people praised them.

**14** More and more men and women believed in the Lord. They joined the believers.

**15** People carried the sick people into the streets. They put them on beds and mats. They wanted the shadow of Peter to fall on some of them when he went by.

**16** Many people also came from the towns around Jerusalem. They brought sick people and people whom unclean spirits troubled. The apostles healed all of them.

**17** Then the high priest and all the people with him became angry and jealous. These people were the group of the Sadducees.

**18** They seized the apostles and put them in the public jail.

**19** But at night an angel of the Lord opened the doors of the jail. He brought them out and said,

**20** "Go, stand in the temple, and speak to the people. Tell them all the words of this new life."

**21** When they heard this, they went into the temple early in the morning and taught. The high priest and the people with him came. They called together the council and all the elders of the people of Israel. They sent officers to the jail to bring the apostles.

**22** But when the officers came, they did not find the apostles in the jail. They went back and told the council.

**23** They said, "We found the jail fully locked. The guards stood outside at the doors. But when we opened the doors, we found no person inside."

**24** The high priest, the captain of the temple, and the chief priests heard these words. They were confused about the apostles. They did not know what was to occur.

**25** Then a man came and told them, "Look, the men that you put in jail stand in the temple. They teach the people."

**26** Then the captain went with the officers and brought the apostles. They did not use force, because they were afraid that the people would throw stones at them.

**27** They brought the apostles and put them in front of the council. The high priest asked them questions.

**28** He said, "We gave you a strong order not to teach in this name. But you filled Jerusalem with your teaching. You want to make us responsible for the death of this man."

**29** Then Peter and the other apostles answered, "We must obey God more than men.

**30** The God of our fathers brought Jesus back to life. You killed Jesus when you hung him on a tree.

**31** God lifted Jesus up to his right hand as Prince and Savior. He did this to cause Israel to turn away from sin and to forgive their sins.

**32** We speak about these things. The Holy Spirit also speaks about them. God gives the Holy Spirit to the people who obey him."

**33** When the council heard this, they became very angry. They made plans to kill the apostles.

**34** But a Pharisee with the name Gamaliel stood up in the council. He was a teacher of the law, and all the people respected him. He told the officers to take the apostles out for a short time.

**35** He said to the council, "Men of Israel, be careful about what you will do to these men.

**36** Before these days, a man with the name Theudas came. He said that he was an important person. About 400 men joined him. People killed him, and all his followers went in all directions. They came to nothing.

**37** After him, Judas from Galilee came in the days of the census. He caused many people to follow him. He also died, and all his followers went in all directions.

**38** Now I tell you this: Stay away from these men and leave them alone. If this plan or this work is from men, it will fail.

**39** But if it is from God, you cannot stop it. If you try, you will fight against God."

**40** The council agreed with Gamaliel. They called the apostles and caused people to beat them. They told the apostles not to speak in the name of Jesus. Then they released them.

**41** The apostles went away from the council. They were glad because God thought that they were worthy to suffer shame for the name of Jesus.

**42** Each day they continued to teach in the temple and in houses. They told people the gospel that Jesus is the Christ.

## Chapter 6

**1** In those days the number of the disciples became larger. Then the Greek-speaking Jews complained against the Hebrew-speaking Jews. They complained because the believers did not give food to their widows each day.

**2** Then the 12 apostles called all the disciples together. They said, "It is not correct for us to stop teaching the word of God to serve food.

**3** Thus, brothers, choose 7 men from among you. These men must have a good reputation and be full of the Holy Spirit and wisdom. We will give them this work.

**4** But we will continue to pray and to teach the word."

**5** All the people liked this plan. They chose Stephen, a man full of faith and of the Holy Spirit. They also chose Philip, Prochorus, Nicanor, Timon, Parmenas, and Nicolas. Nicolas was from Antioch and was a convert to the Jewish religion.

**6** They put these men in front of the apostles. The apostles prayed and put their hands on them.

**7** The word of God spread. The number of the disciples in Jerusalem became much larger. Many of the priests also obeyed the faith.

**8** Stephen was full of faith and power. He did great wonders and miracles among the people.

**9** Then some men from the synagogue with the name "Synagogue of the Freedmen" argued with Stephen. These men were from Cyrene, Alexandria, Cilicia, and Asia.

**10** But they could not win against the wisdom and the Spirit by which Stephen spoke.

**11** Then they secretly paid some men to lie. These men said, "We heard Stephen speak words against Moses and against God."

**12** They caused the people, the elders, and the scribes to become angry. They came to Stephen, seized him, and brought him to the council.

**13** They brought men who told lies. These men said, "This man always speaks words against this holy place and against the law.

**14** We heard him say that this Jesus of Nazareth will destroy this place. He also said that Jesus will change the customs that Moses gave us."

**15** All the men who sat in the council looked at Stephen. They saw that his face was like the face of an angel.

## Chapter 7

**1** Then the high priest said, "Are these things true?"

**2** Stephen said, "Men, brothers, and fathers, listen. The God of glory came to our father Abraham when he lived in Mesopotamia. This was before Abraham lived in Haran.

**3** God said to him, 'Leave your country and your relatives. Go to the land that I will show you.'

**4** Then Abraham left the land of the Chaldeans and lived in Haran. After his father died, God moved him from there into this land, where you live now.

**5** God gave him no land here as his own, not even enough to put his foot on. But God promised to give the land to him and to his descendants after him. At that time Abraham had no child.

**6** God said to him, 'Your descendants will live as foreigners in a different country. The people of that country will make them slaves. They will treat them badly for 400 years.

**7** But I will judge the nation that makes them slaves.' God also said, 'After that, your descendants will come out and worship me in this place.'

**8** God gave Abraham the covenant of circumcision. Then Abraham became the father of Isaac. He circumcised Isaac on the eighth day. Isaac became the father of Jacob. Jacob became the father of the 12 patriarchs.

**9** The patriarchs were jealous of Joseph, and they sold him as a slave into Egypt. But God was with Joseph.

**10** God saved Joseph from all his troubles. God gave him favor and wisdom in front of Pharaoh, king of Egypt. Pharaoh made Joseph governor over Egypt and over all his house.

**11** Then there was a time with no food in all the land of Egypt and Canaan. There was great trouble, and our fathers found no food.

**12** But Jacob heard that there was grain in Egypt. He sent our fathers there the first time.

**13** The second time, Joseph told his brothers who he was. Then Pharaoh knew the family of Joseph.

**14** Then Joseph sent a message and called his father Jacob and all his relatives. There were 75 people.

**15** Thus Jacob went down into Egypt. He died there, and our fathers also died.

**16** People carried their bodies to Shechem. They put them in the tomb that Abraham bought for money from the sons of Hamor in Shechem.

**17** The time came near when God was to do what he promised to Abraham. The number of the people in Egypt became very large.

**18** Then a different king started to rule over Egypt. He did not know Joseph.

**19** This king tricked our people and treated our fathers badly. He forced them to throw out their babies so that the babies would die.

**20** At that time Moses was born. He was a very beautiful child in the eyes of God. His parents cared for him in the house of his father for 3 months.

**21** Then they put him outside. The daughter of Pharaoh took him and cared for him as her own son.

**22** The Egyptians taught Moses all their wisdom. He was powerful in his words and in his acts.

**23** When Moses was 40 years old, he decided to visit his brothers, the people of Israel.

**24** He saw an Egyptian treat one of them badly. Moses defended the man. He killed the Egyptian to punish him for the man.

**25** Moses thought that his brothers would understand that God used him to save them. But they did not understand.

**26** On the next day, Moses saw 2 of them fight. He tried to make peace between them. He said, 'Men, you are brothers. Why do you hurt each other?'

**27** But the man who hurt the other man pushed Moses away. He said, 'Who made you a ruler and a judge over us?

**28** Do you want to kill me as you killed the Egyptian yesterday?'

**29** When Moses heard this, he ran away. He lived as a foreigner in the land of Midian. There he became the father of 2 sons.

**30** After 40 years, an angel of the Lord came to him in the desert near Mount Sinai. The angel was in a fire in a bush.

**31** When Moses saw this, he was surprised. He went near to look at it. Then the voice of the Lord came to him.

**32** The voice said, 'I am the God of your fathers, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.' Moses shook with fear. He was too afraid to look.

**33** Then the Lord said to him, 'Take off your shoes. The place where you stand is holy ground.

**34** I truly saw the suffering of my people in Egypt. I heard their cries. I came down to save them. Now come, I will send you into Egypt.'

**35** The people of Israel rejected this Moses. They said, 'Who made you a ruler and a judge?' But God sent him to be a ruler and a deliverer. God sent him through the angel that he saw in the bush.

**36** Moses brought the people out. He did wonders and signs in Egypt, at the Red Sea, and in the desert for 40 years.

**37** This is the Moses who said to the people of Israel, 'The Lord your God will cause a prophet like me to come from among your brothers. Listen to him.'

**38** Moses was with the congregation in the desert. He was with the angel who spoke to him on Mount Sinai. He was with our fathers. He received living words to give to us.

**39** But our fathers did not want to obey him. They pushed him away. In their hearts they turned back to Egypt.

**40** They said to Aaron, 'Make gods for us that will go in front of us. We do not know what occurred to this Moses, who brought us out of Egypt.'

**41** In those days they made an idol in the shape of a calf. They gave sacrifices to the idol. They were glad about the thing that they made with their hands.

**42** Then God turned away from them. He let them worship the stars of the sky. The book of the prophets says, 'People of Israel, you did not give me animal sacrifices and offerings during the 40 years in the desert.

**43** You carried the tent of Moloch and the star of your god Remphan. These were idols that you made to worship. Thus I will send you away, farther than Babylon.'

**44** Our fathers had the tent of the testimony in the desert. God told Moses to make it. Moses made it as the model that he saw.

**45** Our fathers received the tent, and they brought it with Joshua into the land. They took the land from the Gentiles, whom God pushed out in front of our fathers. The tent stayed there until the days of David.

**46** God gave favor to David. David asked to make a house for the God of Jacob.

**47** But Solomon built a house for God.

**48** But the Most High does not live in temples that men make with their hands. The prophet says,

**49** 'Heaven is my throne, and the earth is the place for my feet. What type of house will you build for me? says the Lord. What place is my place of rest?

**50** Did my hand not make all these things?'

**51** You are stubborn people. Your hearts and ears are like the hearts and ears of the people who do not know God. You always resist the Holy Spirit. You do the same as your fathers did.

**52** Your fathers caused all the prophets to suffer. They killed the prophets who told before about the coming of the Righteous One. Now you betrayed him and killed him.

**53** You received the law through angels, but you did not obey it."

**54** When the council heard these things, they became very angry with Stephen. They ground their teeth at him.

**55** But the Holy Spirit filled Stephen. He looked up into heaven. He saw the glory of God, and he saw Jesus. Jesus stood at the right hand of God.

**56** Stephen said, "Look, I see heaven open. I see the Son of Man. He stands at the right hand of God."

**57** Then they shouted with a loud voice and covered their ears. All of them ran at him together.

**58** They threw him out of the city and threw stones at him. The men who spoke against him put their coats at the feet of a young man with the name Saul.

**59** They threw stones at Stephen while he prayed. He said, "Lord Jesus, receive my spirit."

**60** Then he went down on his knees and called out with a loud voice, "Lord, do not hold this sin against them." After he said this, he died.

## Chapter 8

**1** Saul agreed with the killing of Stephen. On that day the people started to attack the church in Jerusalem very strongly. All the believers except the apostles went to different places in Judea and Samaria.

**2** Religious men buried Stephen. They cried very much for him.

**3** Saul tried to destroy the church. He went into each house. He pulled out men and women and put them in jail.

**4** The believers who went to different places told the word of God everywhere they went.

**5** Philip went down to the city of Samaria. He told the people there about the Christ.

**6** The crowds heard Philip and saw the miracles that he did. All of them listened carefully to what he said.

**7** Unclean spirits came out of many people. The spirits shouted with a loud voice. Many people who were paralyzed or could not walk got their health again.

**8** There was much joy in that city.

**9** But a man with the name Simon lived in that city. Before Philip came, Simon used magic. He surprised the people of Samaria. He said that he was an important person.

**10** All the people, from the least important to the most important, listened to him. They said, "This man is the great power of God."

**11** They listened to him because he surprised them with his magic for a long time.

**12** But Philip told them the gospel about the kingdom of God and the name of Jesus Christ. They believed Philip. Then men and women got baptism.

**13** Then Simon also believed. After he got baptism, he stayed with Philip. He saw the miracles and signs that Philip did, and he was very surprised.

**14** The apostles in Jerusalem heard that the people of Samaria received the word of God. They sent Peter and John to them.

**15** When Peter and John came, they prayed for the people. They prayed that the people would receive the Holy Spirit.

**16** The Holy Spirit did not come on any of them yet. They only got baptism in the name of the Lord Jesus.

**17** Then Peter and John put their hands on them, and they received the Holy Spirit.

**18** Simon saw that the apostles gave the Holy Spirit when they put their hands on people. Then he offered money to them.

**19** He said, "Give me this power also. Then each person on whom I put my hands will receive the Holy Spirit."

**20** But Peter said to him, "Let your money be destroyed with you. You thought that you could buy the gift of God with money.

**21** You have no part in this work, because your heart is not correct in the eyes of God.

**22** Thus turn away from this evil thing. Pray to God. Then perhaps he will forgive the plan of your heart.

**23** I see that you are full of bitterness. Sin controls you."

**24** Then Simon answered, "Pray to the Lord for me. Then none of the things that you said will occur to me."

**25** Peter and John told the people about the Lord, and they spoke the word of the Lord. Then they went back to Jerusalem. On the way they told the gospel in many villages of the Samaritans.

**26** An angel of the Lord spoke to Philip. The angel said, "Get up and go south. Go to the road that goes down from Jerusalem to Gaza. That road goes through the desert."

**27** Philip got up and went. On the road he saw a man from Ethiopia. The man was a eunuch and an important official of Candace, queen of the Ethiopians. He controlled all her treasure. He came to Jerusalem to worship.

**28** He went back home. He sat in his chariot and read the book of the prophet Isaiah.

**29** Then the Spirit said to Philip, "Go near and stay near this chariot."

**30** Philip ran to the chariot. He heard the man read the book of the prophet Isaiah. Philip said, "Do you understand the words that you read?"

**31** The man said, "How can I understand if no man guides me?" He asked Philip to come up and sit with him.

**32** The man read this part of the scripture: "People led him like a sheep to be killed. A lamb is silent when a man cuts its wool. In the same way he did not open his mouth.

**33** People humiliated him and did not give him justice. Who will tell about his descendants? People took his life from the earth."

**34** The eunuch said to Philip, "Please tell me, about whom does the prophet speak? Does he speak about himself or about a different man?"

**35** Then Philip started to speak. He started with this scripture and told him the gospel about Jesus.

**36** They continued on the road and came to some water. The eunuch said, "Look, here is water. Is there a reason that I cannot get baptism?"

**37** Philip said, "If you believe with all your heart, you can." The eunuch answered, "I believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God."

**38** He told the driver to stop the chariot. Philip and the eunuch went down into the water, and Philip baptized him.

**39** When they came up out of the water, the Spirit of the Lord took Philip away. The eunuch did not see him again. He went on his way with joy.

**40** People found Philip at Azotus. He went through all the towns and told the gospel in each of them until he came to Caesarea.

## Chapter 9

**1** Saul continued to threaten the disciples of the Lord. He wanted to kill them. He went to the high priest.

**2** He asked the high priest for letters to the synagogues in Damascus. With these letters, Saul could arrest men and women who followed the Way. Then he could bring them as prisoners to Jerusalem.

**3** Saul traveled and came near Damascus. Suddenly a light from heaven shone around him.

**4** He fell to the ground. He heard a voice that said to him, "Saul, Saul, why do you attack me?"

**5** Saul said, "Who are you, Lord?" The Lord said, "I am Jesus. You attack me. When you fight against me, you only hurt yourself."

**6** Saul shook and was very surprised. He said, "Lord, what do you want me to do?" The Lord said to him, "Get up and go into the city. Someone will tell you what you must do."

**7** The men who traveled with Saul stood and did not speak. They heard the voice, but they did not see a person.

**8** Saul got up from the ground. He opened his eyes, but he could not see. The men held his hand and took him into Damascus.

**9** For 3 days Saul could not see. He did not eat or drink during these days.

**10** In Damascus there was a disciple with the name Ananias. The Lord spoke to him in a vision and said, "Ananias." Ananias said, "I am here, Lord."

**11** The Lord said to him, "Get up. Go to the street with the name Straight. At the house of Judas, ask for a man from Tarsus with the name Saul. He prays now.

**12** In a vision, Saul saw a man with the name Ananias. The man came in and put his hands on Saul. Then Saul could see again."

**13** Ananias answered, "Lord, many people told me about this man. He did much harm to your holy people in Jerusalem.

**14** The chief priests gave him authority to come here. He can arrest all the people who call on your name."

**15** But the Lord said to Ananias, "Go. I chose this man to work for me. He will tell the Gentiles, kings and the people of Israel about me.

**16** I will show him how much he must suffer for my name."

**17** Ananias went and entered the house. He put his hands on Saul and said, "Brother Saul, the Lord Jesus sent me. Jesus appeared to you on the road when you came here. He sent me so that you can see again. He also sent me so that the Holy Spirit will fill you."

**18** Immediately something like scales fell from the eyes of Saul. He could see again. He got up and was baptized.

**19** Saul ate food and became strong again. Saul stayed for some days with the disciples in Damascus.

**20** Immediately Saul started to preach about Jesus in the synagogues. He said, "Jesus is the Son of God."

**21** All the people who heard Saul were very surprised. They said, "This is the man who attacked the followers of Jesus in Jerusalem. He came here to arrest them and take them to the chief priests."

**22** But Saul became stronger and stronger. He proved that Jesus is the Christ. The Jews who lived in Damascus could not answer him.

**23** After many days, the Jews made a plan to kill Saul.

**24** But Saul learned about their plan. Day and night, the Jews watched the city gates so that they could kill him.

**25** But the disciples took Saul at night. They put him in a basket and lowered him through an opening in the city wall.

**26** Saul came to Jerusalem. He tried to join the disciples. But they were all afraid of him. They did not believe that he was a disciple.

**27** But Barnabas helped Saul and took him to the apostles. Barnabas told them that Saul saw the Lord on the road. He told them that the Lord spoke to Saul. He also told them that Saul preached boldly in the name of Jesus in Damascus.

**28** So Saul stayed with the apostles. He went freely everywhere in Jerusalem.

**29** He spoke boldly in the name of the Lord Jesus. He argued with the Jews who spoke Greek. But these Jews tried to kill him.

**30** The brothers learned about this. They took Saul to Caesarea and sent him to Tarsus.

**31** Then the church in all of Judea, Galilee and Samaria had peace. The church became strong. The people respected the Lord, and the Holy Spirit helped them. The number of believers increased.

**32** Peter traveled through all the area. He also went to the holy people who lived in Lydda.

**33** In Lydda, Peter found a man with the name Aeneas. Aeneas could not move his body. He stayed in his bed for 8 years.

**34** Peter said to him, "Aeneas, Jesus Christ makes you well. Get up and make your bed." Immediately Aeneas got up.

**35** All the people who lived in Lydda and Sharon saw Aeneas. They turned to the Lord.

**36** In Joppa there was a disciple with the name Tabitha. In Greek, her name is Dorcas. She always did good things and gave much help to poor people.

**37** At that time, Tabitha became sick and died. The people washed her body and put it in a room upstairs.

**38** Lydda was near Joppa. The disciples heard that Peter was in Lydda. So they sent 2 men to him. The men said, "Please come to us quickly."

**39** Peter got up and went with them. When he arrived, they took him to the room upstairs. All the widows stood near Peter and cried. They showed him the coats and clothes that Dorcas made when she was alive.

**40** Peter told all the people to go out of the room. He kneeled down and prayed. Then he turned to the body and said, "Tabitha, get up." She opened her eyes. She saw Peter and sat up.

**41** Peter gave her his hand and helped her to stand. Then he called the holy people and the widows. He showed them that Tabitha was alive.

**42** All the people in Joppa heard about this. Many people believed in the Lord.

**43** Peter stayed in Joppa for many days. He stayed with a man with the name Simon. Simon made leather from the skins of animals.

## Chapter 10

**1** In Caesarea there was a man with the name Cornelius. He was a centurion (an officer with 100 soldiers) in the Italian Regiment.

**2** Cornelius and all his family were devout. They respected God. Cornelius gave much money to poor people. He always prayed to God.

**3** One day at about 3 o'clock in the afternoon, Cornelius had a vision. He clearly saw an angel of God. The angel came to him and said, "Cornelius."

**4** Cornelius looked at the angel and was afraid. He said, "What is it, Lord?" The angel said, "God heard your prayers. God saw your gifts to the poor. God remembers you.

**5** Now send men to Joppa. Tell them to bring back a man with the name Simon. His other name is Peter.

**6** Peter stays with a man who makes leather. This man is also named Simon. His house is near the sea."

**7** The angel who spoke to Cornelius went away. Then Cornelius called 2 of his servants. He also called a devout soldier from his personal guard.

**8** He told them all these things. Then he sent them to Joppa.

**9** On the next day, the men traveled and came near Joppa. At about noon, Peter went up on the roof of the house to pray.

**10** Peter became very hungry and wanted to eat. While the people prepared the food, Peter had a vision.

**11** He saw heaven open. Something like a large sheet came down to the earth. Someone held the sheet by its 4 corners.

**12** In the sheet were all types of animals with 4 feet. There were also animals that crawl on the ground, and there were birds.

**13** A voice said to Peter, "Get up, Peter. Kill and eat."

**14** But Peter said, "No, Lord! I never ate food that is not pure or not clean."

**15** The voice spoke to Peter a second time. It said, "God made these things clean. Do not say that they are not pure."

**16** This occurred 3 times. Then the sheet went back up into heaven.

**17** Peter did not understand the meaning of the vision. At that time, the men from Cornelius found the house of Simon. They stood at the gate.

**18** They called and asked, "Is Simon Peter here?"

**19** Peter continued to think about the vision. The Spirit said to him, "3 men look for you.

**20** Get up and go down. Go with them and do not doubt. I sent them."

**21** Peter went down to the men. He said, "I am the man that you look for. Why did you come?"

**22** The men said, "Cornelius the centurion sent us. He is a good man and respects God. All the Jewish people speak well of him. A holy angel told him to bring you to his house. He wants to hear what you will say."

**23** Peter asked the men to come in and stay with him. On the next day, Peter went with them. Some of the brothers from Joppa went with him.

**24** On the day after that, they arrived in Caesarea. Cornelius waited for them. He asked his relatives and close friends to come to his house.

**25** When Peter came in, Cornelius met him. Cornelius fell down at the feet of Peter and worshipped him.

**26** But Peter lifted him up and said, "Stand up. I am only a man."

**27** Peter talked with Cornelius and went into the house. Peter found many people there.

**28** He said to them, "You know that the law does not let a Jew be with a person from another nation. A Jew must not visit such a person. But God showed me that I must not say that any person is not pure or not clean.

**29** Thus, when you sent for me, I came and did not argue. Now I ask you, why did you send for me?"

**30** Cornelius said, "4 days ago at this time, I fasted. At 3 o'clock in the afternoon, I prayed in my house. Suddenly a man in bright clothes stood in front of me.

**31** He said, 'Cornelius, God heard your prayer. God remembers your gifts to the poor.

**32** Send men to Joppa. Ask Simon Peter to come here. He stays in the house of Simon, a man who makes leather. The house is near the sea. When Peter comes, he will speak to you.'

**33** So I immediately sent men to you. You did well because you came. Now we are all here in the presence of God. We want to hear all the things that the Lord told you to say."

**34** Then Peter started to speak. He said, "Now I truly understand that God does not prefer some people to other people.

**35** God accepts the people of every nation who respect him and do what is right.

**36** You know the message that God sent to the people of Israel. It is the good news of peace through Jesus Christ. Jesus is Lord of all people.

**37** You know what occurred in all of Judea. It started in Galilee, after John preached about baptism.

**38** God gave the Holy Spirit and power to Jesus of Nazareth. Jesus went to many places and did good things. He healed all the people who were under the control of the devil. He did these things because God was with him.

**39** We saw all the things that Jesus did in the land of the Jews and in Jerusalem. The people killed him. They hung him on a cross of wood.

**40** But on the third day, God raised Jesus from the dead. God caused people to see him.

**41** All the people did not see him. Only the witnesses that God chose before saw him. We are those witnesses. We ate and drank with him after he rose from the dead.

**42** Jesus told us to preach to the people. He told us to tell them this: God chose Jesus to be the Judge of the living and the dead.

**43** All the prophets speak about Jesus. They say that God will forgive the sins of each person who believes in him. God will do this through the name of Jesus."

**44** While Peter spoke these words, the Holy Spirit came on all the people who heard the message.

**45** The Jewish believers who came with Peter were very surprised. God also gave the gift of the Holy Spirit to the Gentiles.

**46** These believers heard the Gentiles speak in other languages and praise God. Then Peter said,

**47** "These people received the Holy Spirit as we did. Nobody can stop them from baptism with water."

**48** So Peter told them to be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ. Then the people asked Peter to stay with them for some days.

## Chapter 11

**1** The apostles and the brothers in Judea heard that the Gentiles also received the word of God.

**2** Peter went up to Jerusalem. There, the Jewish believers who wanted circumcision for all believers criticized him.

**3** They said, "You went into the house of men who are not circumcised. You ate with them."

**4** Then Peter told them all that occurred, from the start. He said,

**5** "I was in the city of Joppa, and I prayed. I had a vision. I saw something like a large sheet that came down from heaven. Someone held it by its 4 corners. It came down to me.

**6** I looked carefully into it. I saw animals with 4 feet, wild animals, animals that crawl, and birds.

**7** I heard a voice that said to me, 'Get up, Peter. Kill and eat.'

**8** But I said, 'No, Lord! I never ate food that is not pure or not clean.'

**9** The voice from heaven spoke again. It said, 'God made these things clean. Do not say that they are not pure.'

**10** This occurred 3 times. Then all of it went back up into heaven.

**11** At that time, 3 men came to the house where I stayed. They came from Caesarea to get me.

**12** The Spirit told me to go with them and not to doubt. These 6 brothers also went with me. We went into the house of the man.

**13** The man told us that he saw an angel in his house. The angel said, 'Send men to Joppa. Bring back Simon Peter.

**14** He will tell you a message. Through this message, God will save you and all your family.'

**15** I started to speak. Then the Holy Spirit came on them, as the Holy Spirit came on us at the start.

**16** Then I remembered the words of the Lord. He said, 'John baptized with water, but God will baptize you with the Holy Spirit.'

**17** God gave them the same gift that he gave us when we believed in the Lord Jesus Christ. Thus, I could not try to stop God."

**18** The believers heard these things and stopped their arguments. They praised God and said, "Thus, God also lets the Gentiles repent and have life."

**19** After the death of Stephen, people attacked the believers. Thus, many believers went to other areas. They went as far as Phoenicia, Cyprus and Antioch. They told the message only to Jews.

**20** But some of these believers were men from Cyprus and Cyrene. They went to Antioch and spoke also to the Greeks. They told the Greeks the good news about the Lord Jesus.

**21** The power of the Lord was with them. A large number of people believed and turned to the Lord.

**22** The church in Jerusalem heard about this. They sent Barnabas to Antioch.

**23** Barnabas came and saw that God gave grace to the people. He was glad. He told them all to stay loyal to the Lord with all their hearts.

**24** Barnabas was a good man. He was full of the Holy Spirit and faith. Many people came to the Lord.

**25** Then Barnabas went to Tarsus to look for Saul.

**26** He found Saul and took him to Antioch. For a full year, Barnabas and Saul met with the church and taught many people. The people in Antioch first called the disciples "Christians."

**27** At that time, some prophets went from Jerusalem to Antioch.

**28** One of them, Agabus, stood up. Through the Spirit, he said that a large famine (a time with no food) will come on all the world. This famine occurred when Claudius was emperor.

**29** The disciples decided to send help to the brothers who lived in Judea. Each disciple gave as much as he could.

**30** The disciples did this. They sent the money to the elders through Barnabas and Saul.

## Chapter 12

**1** At that time, King Herod started to attack some members of the church.

**2** He killed James, the brother of John, with a sword.

**3** Herod saw that this pleased the Jews. So he also arrested Peter. This occurred during the Festival of Unleavened Bread.

**4** Herod arrested Peter and put him in prison. He gave Peter to 4 groups of soldiers to guard him. Each group had 4 soldiers. Herod wanted to bring Peter out to the people after the Passover.

**5** So the soldiers kept Peter in prison. But the church prayed to God for Peter without stopping.

**6** The night before Herod wanted to bring Peter out, Peter slept between 2 soldiers. 2 chains held him. Guards in front of the door watched the prison.

**7** Suddenly an angel of the Lord came. A light shone in the prison cell. The angel hit Peter on the side and woke him. The angel said, "Get up quickly." The chains fell off the hands of Peter.

**8** The angel said to Peter, "Put on your belt and your sandals." Peter did this. Then the angel said, "Put on your coat and follow me."

**9** Peter followed the angel out of the prison. He did not know that the actions of the angel were real. He thought that he saw a vision.

**10** They went past the first guard and the second guard. They came to the iron gate to the city. The gate opened for them without help. They went out and walked along one street. Then suddenly the angel left Peter.

**11** Then Peter understood what occurred. He said, "Now I am sure that the Lord sent his angel. The Lord saved me from Herod. He saved me from all the things that the Jewish people wanted to occur."

**12** Peter thought about this. Then he went to the house of Mary, the mother of John Mark. Many people were there, and they prayed.

**13** Peter knocked at the door of the gate. A servant girl with the name Rhoda came to answer.

**14** She knew the voice of Peter. She was so happy that she did not open the gate. She ran inside and said, "Peter is at the gate!"

**15** The people said to her, "You are crazy." But she continued to say that it was true. Then they said, "It is his angel."

**16** But Peter continued to knock. When they opened the door and saw him, they were very surprised.

**17** Peter moved his hand to tell them to be quiet. He told them how the Lord took him out of the prison. He said, "Tell James and the brothers about this." Then Peter left and went to another place.

**18** In the morning, the soldiers were very troubled. They did not know what occurred to Peter.

**19** Herod looked for Peter, but he did not find him. Herod questioned the guards. Then he gave an order to kill them. After this, Herod went from Judea to Caesarea and stayed there.

**20** Herod was very angry with the people of Tyre and Sidon. These people came together to him. They made Blastus their friend. Blastus was an important servant of the king. The people asked Herod for peace because their country got its food from the country of the king.

**21** On a specified day, Herod put on his royal clothes. He sat on his throne and made a speech to the people.

**22** The people shouted, "This is the voice of a god, not of a man!"

**23** Immediately an angel of the Lord hit Herod with a disease. This occurred because Herod did not give the glory to God. Worms ate Herod, and he died.

**24** But the word of God continued to spread to more and more people.

**25** Barnabas and Saul completed their work in Jerusalem. Then they went back to Antioch. They took John Mark with them.

## Chapter 13

**1** In the church at Antioch there were some prophets and teachers. They were Barnabas, Simeon (also called Niger), Lucius from Cyrene, Manaen and Saul. Manaen grew up with Herod the ruler.

**2** These men worshipped the Lord and fasted. The Holy Spirit said, "Put Barnabas and Saul apart for me. I chose them for a special work."

**3** The men fasted and prayed. They put their hands on Barnabas and Saul. Then they sent them away.

**4** The Holy Spirit sent Barnabas and Saul. They went to Seleucia. From there, they sailed to Cyprus.

**5** They arrived in Salamis. There they preached the word of God in the Jewish synagogues. John Mark was with them to help them.

**6** They went across all the island to Paphos. There they found a Jewish man with the name Bar-Jesus. He used magic and was a false prophet.

**7** He was with the governor, Sergius Paulus. The governor was an intelligent man. He sent for Barnabas and Saul because he wanted to hear the word of God.

**8** But the magician fought against Barnabas and Saul. In Greek, his name is Elymas. He tried to stop the governor from believing.

**9** But Saul, who is also called Paul, was full of the Holy Spirit. He looked directly at Elymas.

**10** Paul said, "You are full of lies and evil. You are a son of the devil. You are an enemy of all that is right. You must stop changing the true ways of the Lord into false ways!

**11** Now the Lord will punish you. You will be blind. For some time, you will not see the sun." Immediately a dark mist came over Elymas. He went around and looked for a person to hold his hand and lead him.

**12** The governor saw what occurred and believed. He was very surprised at the teaching about the Lord.

**13** Paul and his group sailed from Paphos and came to Perga in Pamphylia. There, John Mark left them and went back to Jerusalem.

**14** Paul and Barnabas went from Perga to Antioch in Pisidia. On the Sabbath day, they went into the synagogue and sat down.

**15** Someone read from the law and the prophets. Then the leaders of the synagogue sent a message to Paul and Barnabas. They said, "Brothers, if you have a message to encourage the people, please speak."

**16** Paul stood up and moved his hand. He said, "Men of Israel, and you other people who respect God, listen to me.

**17** The God of the people of Israel chose our ancestors. He made the people great while they lived as foreigners in Egypt. With his great power, he brought them out of Egypt.

**18** For about 40 years, God was patient with their behavior in the desert.

**19** God destroyed 7 nations in the land of Canaan. He gave their land to his people.

**20** All of this took about 450 years. After that, God gave them judges until the time of Samuel the prophet.

**21** Then the people asked for a king. God gave them Saul, the son of Kish. Saul was from the tribe of Benjamin. He was king for 40 years.

**22** God removed Saul. Then God made David their king. God said about David, 'I found David, the son of Jesse. He is a man who loves what I love. He will do all that I want.'

**23** God promised to send a Savior to Israel from the descendants of David. God did this. The Savior is Jesus.

**24** Before Jesus came, John preached to all the people of Israel. He told them to repent and be baptized.

**25** When John almost completed his work, he said, 'Who do you think that I am? I am not the Christ. But a man will come after me. I am not good enough to untie his sandals.'

**26** Brothers, you are the children of Abraham. Some of you are other people who respect God. God sent this message of salvation to us.

**27** The people in Jerusalem and their leaders did not know who Jesus was. Every Sabbath day they read the words of the prophets. But they did not understand them. When they condemned Jesus, they made the words of the prophets come true.

**28** They did not find a reason to kill Jesus. But they asked Pilate to kill him.

**29** They did all the things that the prophets wrote about Jesus. Then they took him down from the cross and put him in a tomb.

**30** But God raised him from the dead.

**31** For many days, Jesus appeared to the people who went with him from Galilee to Jerusalem. Now these people are his witnesses to the people.

**32** We tell you the good news. God made a promise to our ancestors.

**33** God did what he promised for us, their children. He raised Jesus from the dead. The second psalm says this: 'You are my Son. Today I became your Father.'

**34** God raised Jesus from the dead, and his body will never decay. God said, 'I will give you the holy and sure blessings that I promised to David.'

**35** In another psalm God also says, 'You will not let your Holy One decay.'

**36** David did what God wanted during his life. Then David died and was buried with his ancestors. His body decayed.

**37** But God raised Jesus from the dead, and his body did not decay.

**38** Brothers, you must know this. Through Jesus, we tell you that God forgives your sins.

**39** The law of Moses could not make you free from your sins. But through Jesus, God makes each person who believes free from all sins.

**40** Be careful! Do not let the words of the prophets come true for you. They said,

**41** 'Look, you people who laugh at God! Be surprised and die! In your time I will do something. You will not believe it, even if someone tells you about it.'"

**42** Paul and Barnabas went out of the synagogue. The people asked them to speak about these things again on the next Sabbath.

**43** The meeting ended. Many Jews and many converts to the Jewish religion followed Paul and Barnabas. Paul and Barnabas spoke to them. They told them to continue in the grace of God.

**44** On the next Sabbath day, almost all the city came to hear the word of the Lord.

**45** The Jews saw the large crowds and became very jealous. They spoke against what Paul said. They insulted him.

**46** But Paul and Barnabas spoke boldly. They said, "We had to tell the word of God to you first. But you refuse it. You show that you are not good enough for eternal life. Thus, we will now go to the Gentiles.

**47** The Lord gave us this command: 'I made you a light for the Gentiles. You will bring salvation to all the earth.'"

**48** The Gentiles heard this and were glad. They praised the word of the Lord. All the people that God chose for eternal life believed.

**49** The word of the Lord spread through all the area.

**50** But the Jews caused trouble. They spoke to the devout women of high position and to the leaders of the city. These people attacked Paul and Barnabas. They forced Paul and Barnabas out of their area.

**51** So Paul and Barnabas shook the dust from their feet as a warning to these people. Then they went to Iconium.

**52** The disciples were full of joy and full of the Holy Spirit.

## Chapter 14

**1** In Iconium, Paul and Barnabas went into the Jewish synagogue. They spoke so well that a large number of Jews and Greeks believed.

**2** But the Jews who did not believe caused trouble. They made the Gentiles angry with the brothers.

**3** Paul and Barnabas stayed there for a long time. They spoke boldly about the Lord. The Lord showed that their message about his grace was true. He gave them power to do miracles and wonders.

**4** But the people of the city did not agree. Some of the people supported the Jews. Other people supported the apostles.

**5** Some Gentiles and Jews and their leaders made a plan. They wanted to hurt the apostles and kill them with stones.

**6** But the apostles learned about the plan. They escaped to Lystra and Derbe, cities in Lycaonia, and to the area around them.

**7** There they continued to preach the gospel.

**8** In Lystra there was a man who could not use his feet. He was born that way and never walked. He sat there.

**9** The man listened to Paul when Paul spoke. Paul looked directly at him. Paul saw that the man had faith that God could heal him.

**10** Paul said with a loud voice, "Stand up on your feet!" The man jumped up and started to walk.

**11** The crowd saw what Paul did. They shouted in the Lycaonian language, "The gods became like men and came down to us!"

**12** They called Barnabas "Zeus." They called Paul "Hermes" because Paul was the main speaker.

**13** The temple of Zeus was near the city. The priest of Zeus brought bulls and flowers to the city gates. He and the people wanted to make a sacrifice to Paul and Barnabas.

**14** The apostles Barnabas and Paul heard about this. They tore their clothes. They ran into the crowd and shouted,

**15** "Men, why do you do these things? We are only men, as you are. We bring you good news. We tell you to turn away from these useless things. Turn to the living God. He made the heavens, the earth, the sea and all things in them.

**16** In the past, God let all nations go their own ways.

**17** But God always showed that he is real. He does good things for you. He gives you rain from the sky and crops in their seasons. He gives you much food and makes you happy."

**18** Paul and Barnabas said these things. But it was very difficult to stop the people from making a sacrifice to them.

**19** Then some Jews came from Antioch and Iconium. They changed the minds of the crowd. The people threw stones at Paul. They pulled him out of the city because they thought that he was dead.

**20** But the disciples stood around Paul. He got up and went back into the city. On the next day, Paul and Barnabas went to Derbe.

**21** They preached the gospel in Derbe and taught many people. Then they went back to Lystra, Iconium and Antioch.

**22** In those cities, they made the disciples strong. They encouraged them to stay in the faith. They said, "We must have many troubles before we can go into the kingdom of God."

**23** Paul and Barnabas chose elders for each church. They prayed and fasted. Then they gave the elders to the care of the Lord. The elders believed in the Lord.

**24** Paul and Barnabas went through Pisidia and came to Pamphylia.

**25** They preached the word in Perga. Then they went down to Attalia.

**26** From Attalia they sailed back to Antioch. In Antioch the believers gave them to the grace of God for the work. Now Paul and Barnabas completed this work.

**27** They arrived in Antioch and brought the church together. They told the people all that God did through them. They told how God opened a way for the Gentiles to believe.

**28** They stayed there with the disciples for a long time.

## Chapter 15

**1** Some men came down from Judea to Antioch. They taught the brothers, "God will not save you if you are not circumcised. You must obey the law of Moses about circumcision."

**2** Paul and Barnabas argued strongly with these men. Thus, the church decided to send Paul, Barnabas and some other believers to Jerusalem. They had to speak with the apostles and elders about this question.

**3** The church sent them on their journey. They went through Phoenicia and Samaria. They told how the Gentiles turned to God. This news made all the brothers very happy.

**4** They arrived in Jerusalem. The church, the apostles and the elders received them. Paul and Barnabas told them all that God did through them.

**5** But some believers from the group of the Pharisees stood up. They said, "The Gentile believers must be circumcised. They must obey the law of Moses."

**6** The apostles and elders met to think about this question.

**7** They argued for a long time. Then Peter stood up and said to them, "Brothers, you know that some time ago God chose me from among you. He chose me to tell the gospel to the Gentiles. Thus, they could hear it and believe.

**8** God knows the hearts of all people. He showed that he accepts the Gentiles. He gave them the Holy Spirit, as he did to us.

**9** God did not make a difference between us and them. He made their hearts pure because they believed.

**10** So why do you test God now? Do not put a heavy load on the disciples. Our ancestors could not carry that load, and we cannot carry it.

**11** We believe that God saves us through the grace of the Lord Jesus. He saves the Gentiles in the same way."

**12** All the people became quiet. They listened to Barnabas and Paul. Barnabas and Paul told them about the miracles and wonders that God did through them among the Gentiles.

**13** Barnabas and Paul stopped speaking. Then James said, "Brothers, listen to me.

**14** Simon Peter told us how God first showed his care for the Gentiles. God took from the Gentiles a people for himself.

**15** The words of the prophets agree with this. It is written:

**16** 'After this I will come back. I will build again the house of David, which fell down. I will build again its ruins, and I will make it stand again.

**17** Then the other people will look for the Lord. All the Gentiles who have my name will look for the Lord. The Lord says this. He does all these things.

**18** He made these things known a long time ago.'

**19** Thus, I decide this: We must not make it difficult for the Gentiles who turn to God.

**20** But we must write a letter to them. We must tell them not to eat food that people gave to idols. They must not commit sexual sin. They must not eat the meat of animals that people killed by strangling. They must not eat blood.

**21** For a long time, people in every city preached the law of Moses. They read it in the synagogues every Sabbath day."

**22** Then the apostles and elders and all the church decided to choose some men from among them. They decided to send these men to Antioch with Paul and Barnabas. They chose Judas, who was also called Barsabbas, and Silas. These men were leaders among the brothers.

**23** They sent this letter with them: "From the apostles and elders, your brothers. To the Gentile brothers in Antioch, Syria and Cilicia. Greetings.

**24** We heard that some men from our group came to you. We did not send them. Their words caused you trouble and confused your minds.

**25** So we met and all agreed to choose some men. We decided to send them to you with our dear friends Barnabas and Paul.

**26** Barnabas and Paul put their lives in danger for the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**27** Thus, we send Judas and Silas. They will also tell you the same things with their own words.

**28** The Holy Spirit and we decided this: We will not put a heavy load on you. You must only obey these necessary rules.

**29** Do not eat food that people gave to idols. Do not eat blood. Do not eat the meat of animals that people killed by strangling. Do not commit sexual sin. If you obey these rules, you will do well. Goodbye."

**30** The church sent the men away, and they went to Antioch. There they brought the believers together and gave them the letter.

**31** The people read the letter. They were happy because the letter encouraged them.

**32** Judas and Silas were also prophets. They spoke for a long time to the brothers. They encouraged them and made them strong.

**33** Judas and Silas stayed there for some time. Then the brothers sent them back in peace to the apostles.

**34** But Silas decided to stay there.

**35** Paul and Barnabas stayed in Antioch. With many other people, they taught and preached the word of the Lord.

**36** After some days, Paul said to Barnabas, "We must go back and visit the brothers in all the cities where we preached the word of the Lord. We will see how they are."

**37** Barnabas wanted to take John Mark with them.

**38** But Paul did not think that it was good to take John Mark. In Pamphylia, John Mark left them. He did not continue with them in the work.

**39** Paul and Barnabas had a very strong disagreement. So they separated. Barnabas took Mark and sailed to Cyprus.

**40** Paul chose Silas and left. The brothers gave Paul to the grace of the Lord.

**41** Paul went through Syria and Cilicia and made the churches strong.

## Chapter 16

**1** Paul came to Derbe and then to Lystra. A disciple with the name Timothy lived there. His mother was a Jewish believer, but his father was a Greek.

**2** The brothers in Lystra and Iconium said good things about Timothy.

**3** Paul wanted Timothy to travel with him. So Paul circumcised Timothy because of the Jews in that area. All the Jews knew that the father of Timothy was a Greek.

**4** Paul and his group went through the cities. They gave the believers the rules from the apostles and elders in Jerusalem. They told the believers to obey these rules.

**5** So the churches became stronger in the faith. Each day the number of believers increased.

**6** Paul and his group went through the area of Phrygia and Galatia. The Holy Spirit did not let them preach the word in Asia.

**7** They came to the border of Mysia. They tried to go into Bithynia, but the Spirit of Jesus did not let them.

**8** So they went past Mysia and came down to Troas.

**9** During the night, Paul had a vision. A man from Macedonia stood and asked Paul for help. The man said, "Come over to Macedonia and help us."

**10** Paul saw the vision. Then we immediately prepared to go to Macedonia. We were sure that God told us to preach the gospel to the people there.

**11** We sailed from Troas directly to Samothrace. On the next day we went to Neapolis.

**12** From there we went to Philippi. Philippi is a Roman colony and the most important city in that part of Macedonia. We stayed in that city for some days.

**13** On the Sabbath day, we went out of the city gate to a river. We thought that there was a place for prayer there. We sat down and spoke to the women who met there.

**14** One of the women was Lydia, from the city of Thyatira. She sold purple cloth. She worshipped God. She listened to us. The Lord opened her heart so that she accepted the words of Paul.

**15** Lydia and the people in her house were baptized. Then she asked us to come to her house. She said, "If you think that I am a true believer in the Lord, come and stay in my house." She made us agree to go.

**16** One day we went to the place for prayer. A slave girl met us. She had an evil spirit that let her tell the future. She got much money for her owners when she told the future.

**17** The girl followed Paul and us. She shouted, "These men are servants of the Most High God! They tell you the way of salvation!"

**18** She did this for many days. Paul became very troubled. He turned and said to the spirit, "In the name of Jesus Christ, I command you to come out of her!" Immediately the spirit came out of her.

**19** Her owners saw that they could not get more money from her. They took hold of Paul and Silas. They pulled them to the leaders in the marketplace.

**20** They brought Paul and Silas to the magistrates (the city judges). They said, "These men are Jews. They cause much trouble in our city.

**21** They teach customs that we must not accept or do. We are Romans."

**22** The crowd joined the attack on Paul and Silas. The magistrates tore the clothes off Paul and Silas. They told men to beat them with sticks.

**23** The men hit Paul and Silas many times. Then they threw them into prison. They told the jailer to guard them carefully.

**24** The jailer received this order. He put Paul and Silas into the inner prison. He put their feet in blocks of wood.

**25** At about midnight, Paul and Silas prayed and sang songs to God. The other prisoners listened to them.

**26** Suddenly there was a large earthquake. It shook the foundations of the prison. Immediately all the doors opened. The chains fell off all the prisoners.

**27** The jailer woke up. He saw that the prison doors were open. He thought that the prisoners escaped. So he took out his sword to kill himself.

**28** But Paul shouted, "Do not hurt yourself! We are all here!"

**29** The jailer asked for a light and ran in. He shook with fear. He fell down in front of Paul and Silas.

**30** Then he brought them out and said, "Sirs, what must I do to be saved?"

**31** They said, "Believe in the Lord Jesus. Then God will save you and the people in your house."

**32** Paul and Silas told the word of the Lord to the jailer and to all the people in his house.

**33** At that time of the night, the jailer took Paul and Silas and washed their wounds. Immediately the jailer and all his family were baptized.

**34** The jailer took Paul and Silas into his house and gave them food. He and all his family were very happy because they believed in God.

**35** In the morning, the magistrates sent officers to the jailer. They said, "Let those men go."

**36** The jailer told Paul, "The magistrates sent an order to let you go. Now you can leave. Go in peace."

**37** But Paul said to the officers, "We are Roman citizens. But they beat us in public without a trial. They threw us into prison. Now they want to send us away secretly? No! The magistrates must come here themselves and take us out."

**38** The officers told these words to the magistrates. The magistrates were afraid when they heard that Paul and Silas were Roman citizens.

**39** So the magistrates came and apologized to them. They took Paul and Silas out of the prison. They asked them to leave the city.

**40** Paul and Silas went out of the prison and went to the house of Lydia. There they saw the brothers and encouraged them. Then Paul and Silas left.

## Chapter 17

**1** Paul and Silas went through Amphipolis and Apollonia. They came to Thessalonica. There was a Jewish synagogue in that city.

**2** As usual, Paul went into the synagogue. On 3 Sabbath days, he discussed the Scriptures with the Jews.

**3** He explained the Scriptures and proved that the Christ had to suffer and rise from the dead. He said, "This Jesus that I tell you about is the Christ."

**4** Some of the Jews believed and joined Paul and Silas. A large number of devout Greeks also believed. Many important women also believed.

**5** But the Jews who did not believe became jealous. They got some evil men from the marketplace and made a crowd. They caused trouble in all the city. They attacked the house of Jason. They looked for Paul and Silas so that they could bring them out to the crowd.

**6** They did not find Paul and Silas. So they pulled Jason and some other brothers to the city leaders. They shouted, "These men caused trouble everywhere in the world. Now they came here too.

**7** Jason let them stay in his house. All these men disobey the laws of Caesar. They say that there is another king, Jesus."

**8** The crowd and the city leaders heard these things and became very troubled.

**9** The leaders made Jason and the other believers pay money as a guarantee. Then they let them go.

**10** That night, the brothers immediately sent Paul and Silas to Berea. When Paul and Silas arrived there, they went to the Jewish synagogue.

**11** The Jews in Berea were better people than the Jews in Thessalonica. They were very willing to receive the message. Each day they studied the Scriptures. They wanted to know if the words of Paul were true.

**12** Thus, many of them believed. Many important Greek women and many Greek men also believed.

**13** The Jews in Thessalonica learned that Paul preached the word of God in Berea. So they went there too. They caused trouble and made the crowds angry.

**14** Immediately the brothers sent Paul to the coast. But Silas and Timothy stayed in Berea.

**15** The men who went with Paul took him to Athens. Paul gave them a message for Silas and Timothy: "Come to me as quickly as possible." Then the men left.

**16** Paul waited for Silas and Timothy in Athens. He saw that the city was full of idols. This made him very troubled.

**17** So he discussed with the Jews and the devout Greeks in the synagogue. Each day he also discussed with the people in the marketplace.

**18** Some Epicurean and Stoic philosophers argued with Paul. Some of them said, "This man talks too much. What does he try to say?" Other people said, "He seems to tell us about foreign gods." They said this because Paul preached about Jesus and the resurrection.

**19** They took Paul to a meeting of the Areopagus (the city council). They said, "Tell us about this new teaching.

**20** You tell us strange things. We want to know the meaning of these things."

**21** All the people of Athens and the foreigners who lived there used all their time in one way. They only wanted to tell or hear the newest ideas.

**22** Paul stood in front of the council of the Areopagus. He said, "Men of Athens, I see that you are very religious in all things.

**23** I walked through your city and looked at the things that you worship. I found an altar with these words on it: 'TO AN UNKNOWN GOD.' You worship this God, but you do not know him. I tell you about him now.

**24** God made the world and all things in it. He is the Lord of heaven and earth. He does not live in temples that people built.

**25** People do not serve him with their hands, because he does not need anything. He gives life and breath and all things to all people.

**26** From one man, God made all the nations of people. He made them so that they live on all the earth. He decided their times in history and the borders of their lands.

**27** God did this so that people will look for him. Maybe they will try to find him and will find him. But God is not far from each of us.

**28** Through God we live and move and exist. Some of your own poets said this: 'We are his children.'

**29** We are the children of God. Thus, we must not think that God is like an image of gold, silver or stone. People make images with their skill and their ideas.

**30** In the past, people did not know about God, and God did not punish them for this. But now God tells all people everywhere to repent.

**31** God chose a day when he will judge the world fairly. He will judge through the man that he chose. God proved this to all people when he raised this man from the dead."

**32** Some of the people heard Paul speak about the resurrection of the dead. They laughed at him. But other people said, "We want to hear you speak about this again."

**33** Then Paul left the council.

**34** But some men joined Paul and believed. One of them was Dionysius, a member of the council of the Areopagus. A woman with the name Damaris and some other people also believed.

## Chapter 18

**1** After this, Paul left Athens and went to Corinth.

**2** In Corinth, Paul met a Jew with the name Aquila. Aquila was born in Pontus. Aquila and his wife Priscilla came from Italy a short time before. They left Italy because Claudius ordered all the Jews to leave Rome. Paul went to visit them.

**3** Aquila and Priscilla made tents, and Paul did the same work. So he stayed with them and worked with them.

**4** On every Sabbath day, Paul discussed the Scriptures in the synagogue. He tried to make Jews and Greeks believe.

**5** Silas and Timothy came from Macedonia. Then Paul used all his time to preach. He told the Jews that Jesus is the Christ.

**6** But the Jews spoke against Paul and insulted him. So he shook the dust from his clothes as a warning. He said to them, "If God punishes you, it is your own fault. I am not responsible. From now on I will go to the Gentiles."

**7** Paul left the synagogue and went to the house of a man with the name Titius Justus. Justus worshipped God. His house was next to the synagogue.

**8** Crispus was the leader of the synagogue. He and all his family believed in the Lord. Many people in Corinth heard Paul. They believed and were baptized.

**9** One night the Lord spoke to Paul in a vision. The Lord said, "Do not be afraid. Continue to speak and do not stop.

**10** I am with you. Nobody will attack you or hurt you. Many of my people live in this city."

**11** So Paul stayed there for 1 year and 6 months. He taught the word of God to the people.

**12** At that time, Gallio was the governor of Achaia. The Jews joined together and attacked Paul. They took him to the court.

**13** They said, "This man tells people to worship God in ways that are against the law."

**14** Paul was ready to speak. But Gallio said to the Jews, "If this man did a wrong act or a serious crime, I will listen to you.

**15** But this is a question about words, names and your own law. You must solve this problem yourselves. I will not be a judge of these things."

**16** Then Gallio made them leave the court.

**17** Then all the people took Sosthenes, the leader of the synagogue. They beat him in front of the court. But Gallio did not care about these things.

**18** Paul stayed in Corinth for many more days. Then he said goodbye to the brothers and sailed to Syria. Priscilla and Aquila went with him. In Cenchreae, Paul cut his hair because he made a promise to God.

**19** They came to Ephesus. There Paul left Priscilla and Aquila. Paul went into the synagogue and discussed with the Jews.

**20** The Jews asked Paul to stay with them for a longer time. But he did not agree.

**21** He said goodbye to them. He said, "I must keep the festival that will come in Jerusalem. But if God wants, I will come back to you." Then he sailed from Ephesus.

**22** Paul arrived in Caesarea. He went up to Jerusalem and greeted the church. Then he went down to Antioch.

**23** Paul stayed in Antioch for some time. Then he left and went from place to place through Galatia and Phrygia. He made all the disciples strong.

**24** A Jew with the name Apollos came to Ephesus. He was born in Alexandria. He was a good speaker and knew the Scriptures very well.

**25** Apollos received teaching about the way of the Lord. He spoke with much enthusiasm. He taught correctly about Jesus. But he knew only about the baptism of John.

**26** Apollos started to speak boldly in the synagogue. Priscilla and Aquila heard him. They took him aside. They explained the way of God to him more correctly.

**27** Apollos wanted to go to Achaia. The brothers encouraged him. They wrote to the disciples in Achaia and told them to receive him. When Apollos arrived, he gave much help to the people who believed through the grace of God.

**28** In public, Apollos argued strongly against the Jews and proved that they were wrong. He used the Scriptures to show that Jesus is the Christ.

## Chapter 19

**1** While Apollos was in Corinth, Paul went through the inland areas and came to Ephesus. There he found some disciples.

**2** Paul said to them, "Did you receive the Holy Spirit when you believed?" They said, "No. We did not hear that there is a Holy Spirit."

**3** Paul said, "Then what baptism did you receive?" They said, "We received the baptism of John."

**4** Paul said, "John baptized people who repented. He told the people to believe in the man who will come after him. That man is Jesus."

**5** The disciples heard this. Then they were baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus.

**6** Paul put his hands on them, and the Holy Spirit came on them. They spoke in other languages and prophesied.

**7** There were about 12 men in the group.

**8** Paul went into the synagogue and spoke boldly for 3 months. He discussed with the people and tried to make them believe about the kingdom of God.

**9** But some of the Jews refused to believe. In front of the crowd, they said bad things about the Way. So Paul left them and took the disciples with him. Each day he discussed in the lecture hall of Tyrannus.

**10** Paul did this for 2 years. Thus, all the Jews and Greeks who lived in Asia heard the word of the Lord.

**11** God did special miracles through Paul.

**12** People took cloths that touched Paul to the sick people. Then their diseases went away, and the evil spirits left them.

**13** Some Jews went from place to place and forced evil spirits out of people. They tried to use the name of the Lord Jesus on people with evil spirits. They said, "In the name of Jesus, whom Paul preaches, I command you to come out!"

**14** 7 sons of Sceva did this. Sceva was a Jewish chief priest.

**15** But the evil spirit said to them, "I know Jesus, and I know about Paul. But who are you?"

**16** Then the man with the evil spirit jumped on them. He was stronger than all of them. He beat them so badly that they ran out of the house without clothes and with wounds.

**17** All the Jews and Greeks who lived in Ephesus heard about this. They were all afraid. They gave much honor to the name of the Lord Jesus.

**18** Many believers came and told the people about the bad things that they did.

**19** Many of the believers used magic before. They brought their books of magic and burned them in front of all the people. They calculated the value of the books. The value was 50,000 silver coins.

**20** In this way, the word of the Lord spread to more people and became very strong.

**21** After these things, Paul decided to go through Macedonia and Achaia and then go to Jerusalem. He said, "After I go there, I must also visit Rome."

**22** Paul sent 2 of his helpers, Timothy and Erastus, to Macedonia. But Paul stayed in Asia for some more time.

**23** At that time, there was much trouble about the Way.

**24** A silversmith with the name Demetrius made small silver models of the temple of Artemis. This work gave much money to the workers.

**25** Demetrius called these workers and other workers with similar work. He said, "Men, you know that we get much money from this work.

**26** You see and hear what this Paul does. He says that the gods that people make with their hands are not gods. He changed the minds of many people in Ephesus and in almost all of Asia.

**27** There is a danger that people will think that our work is bad. There is also a danger that people will not respect the temple of the great goddess Artemis. All Asia and all the world worship her. But she will lose her greatness."

**28** The men heard this and became very angry. They shouted, "Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!"

**29** All the city became confused. The people took Gaius and Aristarchus, 2 men from Macedonia who traveled with Paul. All the people ran together into the theater.

**30** Paul wanted to go into the crowd, but the disciples did not let him.

**31** Some officials of the province of Asia were friends of Paul. They sent him a message. They asked him not to go into the theater.

**32** Some people in the crowd shouted one thing, and other people shouted a different thing. The crowd was confused. Most of the people did not know why they came together.

**33** The Jews pushed a man with the name Alexander to the front. Some people in the crowd told him what to say. Alexander moved his hand because he wanted to make a defense to the people.

**34** But the crowd learned that he was a Jew. Then all the people shouted together for about 2 hours. They shouted, "Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!"

**35** Then the city clerk made the crowd quiet. He said, "Men of Ephesus, all people know that Ephesus guards the temple of the great Artemis. All people know that our city also guards her sacred stone that fell from the sky.

**36** Nobody can say that these things are not true. Thus, you must be calm. Do not do anything quickly without thinking.

**37** You brought these men here. But they did not rob temples. They did not insult our goddess.

**38** Demetrius and his workers may have a complaint against someone. If so, the courts are open, and there are governors. They can take their complaints to the courts.

**39** If you want something more, the official meeting of the citizens must decide it.

**40** There is a danger that the government will accuse us of a riot because of today. We have no reason for this disorder. We cannot explain this crowd."

**41** After he said these things, he sent the people away.

## Chapter 20

**1** The trouble stopped. Then Paul sent for the disciples and encouraged them. He said goodbye and left for Macedonia.

**2** He went through those areas and encouraged the believers with many words. Then he came to Greece.

**3** He stayed there for 3 months. He was ready to sail to Syria. But the Jews made a plan against him. So he decided to go back through Macedonia.

**4** Some men went with Paul. They were Sopater from Berea, and Aristarchus and Secundus from Thessalonica. Gaius from Derbe and Timothy also went. Tychicus and Trophimus from Asia also went.

**5** These men went before us and waited for us in Troas.

**6** After the Festival of Unleavened Bread, we sailed from Philippi. After 5 days we joined them in Troas. We stayed there for 7 days.

**7** On the first day of the week, the disciples met to break bread. Paul spoke to them. He wanted to leave on the next day. So he continued to speak until midnight.

**8** There were many lamps in the room upstairs where they met.

**9** A young man with the name Eutychus sat in a window. Paul spoke for a long time, and Eutychus became very sleepy. He fell asleep and fell from the third floor to the ground. The people picked him up, and he was dead.

**10** Paul went down and lay on the young man. He held him and said, "Do not be troubled. He is alive."

**11** Paul went upstairs again. He broke the bread and ate. He talked with them for a long time, until the sun came up. Then he left.

**12** The people took the young man home alive. They were very comforted.

**13** We went to the ship before Paul and sailed to Assos. We planned to get Paul there. Paul told us to do this because he wanted to walk to Assos.

**14** Paul met us in Assos. We took him on the ship and went to Mitylene.

**15** We sailed from there and on the next day arrived near Chios. On the day after that, we went to Samos. On the next day, we came to Miletus.

**16** Paul decided not to stop at Ephesus. He did not want to use much time in Asia. He went quickly because he wanted to be in Jerusalem on the day of Pentecost, if possible.

**17** From Miletus, Paul sent a message to Ephesus. He asked the elders of the church to come to him.

## Chapter 23

## Chapter 24

**31** They went out of the room and talked together. They said, "This man did nothing that deserves death or prison."

## Chapter 27

**1** The officials decided that we must sail to Italy. They gave Paul and some other prisoners to a centurion with the name Julius. Julius was an officer in the Imperial Regiment.

**2** We went on a ship from Adramyttium. The ship was ready to sail to the ports along the coast of Asia. Then we sailed. Aristarchus, a man from Thessalonica in Macedonia, was with us.

**3** On the next day, we arrived in Sidon. Julius was kind to Paul. He let Paul go to his friends so that they could give him the things that he needed.

**4** We sailed from Sidon. The wind blew against us. So we sailed near the island of Cyprus, on the side away from the wind.

**5** We sailed across the sea near Cilicia and Pamphylia. Then we arrived at Myra in Lycia.

**6** There the centurion found a ship from Alexandria that sailed to Italy. He put us on that ship.

**7** We sailed slowly for many days. With much difficulty we arrived near Cnidus. The wind did not let us go farther. So we sailed near Crete, on the side away from the wind. We went past Salmone.

**8** We sailed along the coast of Crete with much difficulty. We came to a place with the name Fair Havens. It was near the city of Lasea.

**9** We lost much time. Sailing was now dangerous because the Day of Atonement (the Fast) was already past. So Paul warned the people on the ship.

**10** He said, "Men, I see that our journey will be dangerous. We will lose the cargo and the ship. We can also lose our lives."

**11** But the centurion believed the captain and the owner of the ship. He did not believe what Paul said.

**12** The harbor was not a good place to stay for the winter. So most of the men decided to sail again. They hoped to arrive at Phoenix and stay there for the winter. Phoenix is a harbor in Crete. It opens to the southwest and to the northwest.

**13** A gentle wind started to blow from the south. The men thought that they could do what they wanted. So they pulled up the anchor and sailed along the coast of Crete.

**14** But soon a very strong wind came down from the island. People call this wind "the Northeaster."

**15** The wind caught the ship. The ship could not sail against the wind. So we let the wind push the ship.

**16** We went past a small island with the name Cauda. The island gave us some protection from the wind. There we got control of the small boat with much difficulty.

**17** The men pulled the small boat up onto the ship. Then they put ropes under the ship to hold it together. They were afraid that the ship will hit the sandbanks near Syrtis. So they lowered the sails and let the wind push the ship.

**18** The storm hit us very strongly. On the next day, the men started to throw the cargo into the sea.

**19** On the third day, they threw the equipment of the ship into the sea with their own hands.

**20** For many days we did not see the sun or the stars. The storm continued to be very strong. At last we lost all hope that we will live.

**21** The men did not eat for a long time. Then Paul stood up among them and said, "Men, you did not listen to me. You sailed from Crete. Thus, you have this damage and loss.

**22** But now I tell you to be brave. None of you will die. Only the ship will be lost.

**23** Last night an angel of God stood near me. I am his servant, and I worship him.

**24** The angel said, 'Paul, do not be afraid. You must stand in front of Caesar. God will save the lives of all the people who sail with you.'

**25** So, men, be brave! I believe God. All will occur as he told me.

**26** But the storm will push our ship onto an island."

**27** On the fourteenth night, the storm pushed us across the Adriatic Sea. At about midnight, the sailors thought that we were near land.

**28** They measured the depth of the water. It was 20 fathoms (about 37 meters) deep. After a short time, they measured it again. It was 15 fathoms (about 28 meters) deep.

**29** The sailors were afraid that the ship will hit the rocks. So they lowered 4 anchors from the back of the ship. They prayed for the morning to come.

**30** The sailors tried to escape from the ship. They lowered the small boat into the sea. They said that they wanted to lower anchors from the front of the ship.

**31** But Paul said to the centurion and the soldiers, "If these men do not stay in the ship, you cannot live."

**32** So the soldiers cut the ropes of the small boat and let it fall into the sea.

**33** Before the morning, Paul told all the people to eat. He said, "For 14 days you waited and did not eat. You ate nothing.

**34** So I ask you to eat some food. You need food to live. None of you will lose even 1 hair from your head."

**35** After Paul said this, he took some bread. He thanked God in front of all of them. He broke the bread and started to eat.

**36** Then all the people were encouraged, and they also ate some food.

**37** There were 276 people on the ship.

**38** They ate as much as they wanted. Then they threw the wheat into the sea to make the ship lighter.

**39** In the morning, the sailors did not know the land. But they saw a bay with a beach. They decided to try to sail the ship onto the beach.

**40** They cut the anchors and left them in the sea. They also untied the ropes that held the rudders. Then they lifted the front sail to the wind and moved toward the beach.

**41** But the ship hit a sandbank where 2 currents met. The front of the ship stuck in the sand and could not move. The large waves broke the back of the ship into pieces.

**42** The soldiers wanted to kill the prisoners. They did not want any prisoner to swim away and escape.

**43** But the centurion wanted to save Paul. So he did not let the soldiers do this. He told the people who could swim to jump into the sea first and go to land.

**44** He told the other people to follow on boards or on pieces of the ship. In this way, all the people arrived safely on land.

## Chapter 28

**1** We arrived safely on land. Then we learned that the name of the island was Malta.

**2** The people of the island were very kind to us. It rained and it was cold. So they made a fire and welcomed all of us.

**3** Paul got some sticks and put them on the fire. The heat caused a poisonous snake to come out. The snake bit the hand of Paul and held on.

**4** The people of the island saw the snake on his hand. They said to each other, "This man must be a murderer. He escaped from the sea, but Justice does not let him live."

**5** But Paul shook the snake off into the fire. He was not hurt.

**6** The people waited for Paul to swell up or suddenly fall down dead. They waited for a long time. But they saw that nothing bad occurred to him. So they changed their minds and said, "He is a god."

**7** Near that place there was land that the chief official of the island owned. His name was Publius. He welcomed us into his house. For 3 days he was kind to us.

**8** The father of Publius was sick in bed. He had a fever and dysentery (a disease of the intestines). Paul went to see him. Paul prayed and put his hands on him. Then Paul healed him.

**9** After this, the other sick people on the island came to Paul. He healed them too.

**10** The people gave us many gifts to honor us. When we were ready to sail, they put on the ship all the things that we needed.

**11** After 3 months, we sailed on a ship from Alexandria. The ship stayed at the island during the winter. On the front of the ship were the images of the twin gods Castor and Pollux.

**12** We arrived at Syracuse and stayed there for 3 days.

**13** From there we sailed around the coast and arrived at Rhegium. After 1 day, a wind started to blow from the south. On the next day, we arrived at Puteoli.

**14** There we found some brothers. They asked us to stay with them for 7 days. Then we went to Rome.

**15** The brothers in Rome heard about us. They came to meet us at the Market of Appius and at the Three Taverns. Paul saw them. He thanked God and was encouraged.

**16** We arrived in Rome. The centurion gave the prisoners to the captain of the guard. But the officials let Paul live by himself with a soldier who guarded him.

**17** After 3 days, Paul called the Jewish leaders together. When they came, he said to them, "Brothers, I did nothing against our people or against the customs of our ancestors. But the Jews in Jerusalem arrested me. They gave me to the Romans.

**18** The Romans questioned me. They wanted to let me go because I did nothing that deserves death.

**19** But the Jews spoke against this. So I had to appeal to Caesar. But I do not accuse my own nation of anything.

**20** For this reason, I asked to see you and speak with you. I wear this chain because I believe in the hope of Israel."

**21** The Jewish leaders said to Paul, "We did not receive letters about you from Judea. None of the brothers who came from there told us anything bad about you.

**22** But we want to hear what you think. We know that people everywhere speak against this group."

**23** The Jewish leaders chose a day to meet with Paul. On that day, many of them came to the house where Paul stayed. From morning until evening, Paul explained the kingdom of God to them. He used the law of Moses and the books of the prophets. He tried to make them believe in Jesus.

**24** Some of them believed the things that Paul said. But other people did not believe.

**25** They did not agree with each other, and they started to leave. Paul told them this last message: "The Holy Spirit spoke the truth to your ancestors through the prophet Isaiah. He said,

**26** 'Go to this people and tell them: You will listen and listen, but you will not understand. You will look and look, but you will not see.

**27** The hearts of this people became hard. They do not hear well with their ears. They closed their eyes. They do not want to see with their eyes or hear with their ears. They do not want to understand with their hearts. They do not want to turn to me so that I heal them.'

**28** Thus, you must know this: God sent his salvation to the Gentiles. They will listen!"

**29** After Paul said these words, the Jews left. They argued strongly among themselves.

**30** For 2 full years, Paul lived in a house that he rented. He welcomed all the people who came to see him.

**31** He preached about the kingdom of God. He taught about the Lord Jesus Christ. He spoke very boldly, and nobody stopped him.
