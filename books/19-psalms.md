# Psalms

## Chapter 1

**1** A man who does not follow the advice of wicked people is blessed. He does not stand in the path of sinners. He does not sit with people who mock.

**2** But he is glad to obey the law of the LORD. He thinks about the law of the LORD day and night.

**3** He is like a tree that grows near streams of water. The tree gives its fruit at the correct time. Its leaves do not become dry. All that this man does is successful.

**4** The wicked are not like that. They are like chaff that the wind blows away.

**5** Thus the wicked will not stand firm on the day of judgment. Sinners will not stand in the assembly of the righteous.

**6** The LORD knows and protects the way of the righteous. But the way of the wicked will lead to destruction.

## Chapter 2

**1** Why do the nations become angry? Why do the peoples make useless plans?

**2** The kings of the earth prepare for war. The rulers make plans together against the LORD and against his anointed king. They say,

**3** "We must break their chains. We must throw off their ropes."

**4** God sits on his throne in heaven and laughs. The Lord laughs at them with contempt.

**5** Then he speaks to them in his anger. He frightens them in his great anger.

**6** He says, "I put my king on Zion, my holy hill."

**7** I will tell about the decree of the LORD. The LORD said to me, "You are my Son. Today I became your father.

**8** Ask me, and I will give you the nations as your inheritance. I will give you the farthest parts of the earth as your property.

**9** You will break them with an iron rod. You will break them into pieces like a clay pot."

**10** Thus, you kings, be wise. Learn from this, you rulers of the earth.

**11** Serve the LORD with fear. Be glad, but shake with fear.

**12** Kiss the Son. If you do not, he will become angry, and you will die on your way. His anger can start quickly. All people who trust in him are blessed.

## Chapter 3

**1** LORD, I have very many enemies! Many people attack me.

**2** Many people say about me, "God will not save him." Selah.

**3** But you, LORD, are a shield around me. You give me honor. You give me courage again.

**4** I cry out to the LORD with my voice. He answers me from his holy hill. Selah.

**5** I lie down and sleep. I wake up again, because the LORD keeps me safe.

**6** I will not be afraid of 10,000 people who attack me from all sides.

**7** Stand up, LORD! Save me, my God! You hit all my enemies on the face. You break the teeth of the wicked.

**8** Salvation comes from the LORD. Bless your people. Selah.

## Chapter 4

**1** Answer me when I call, God of my righteousness. You gave me relief when I was in trouble. Be kind to me and hear my prayer.

**2** You men, how long will you bring shame on my honor? How long will you love empty things and look for lies? Selah.

**3** But know that the LORD chose the godly man for himself. The LORD will hear me when I call to him.

**4** Shake with fear and do not sin. Think deeply in your heart on your bed, and be quiet. Selah.

**5** Give the correct sacrifices. Put your trust in the LORD.

**6** Many people say, "Who will show us good things?" LORD, let the light of your face shine on us.

**7** You put more joy in my heart than people have when they have much grain and new wine.

**8** I will lie down and sleep in peace. Only you, LORD, keep me safe.

## Chapter 5

**1** Hear my words, LORD. Think about my sad thoughts.

**2** Listen to my cry for help, my King and my God. I pray to you.

**3** LORD, you will hear my voice in the morning. In the morning, I will put my requests in front of you, and I will wait.

**4** You are not a God who likes evil. Evil people cannot live with you.

**5** Proud people will not stand in front of you. You hate all people who do evil.

**6** You destroy people who tell lies. The LORD hates people who are violent and dishonest.

**7** But because of your great love, I will come into your house. With respect, I will bow down toward your holy temple.

**8** Lead me, LORD, in your righteousness, because I have enemies. Make your path straight in front of me.

**9** My enemies do not say anything true. Their hearts are full of destruction. Their throat is like an open grave. They use their tongues to lie with false praise.

**10** God, show that they are guilty. Let their own plans cause them to fall. Throw them out because of their many sins. They rebelled against you.

**11** But let all people who trust in you be glad. Let them always sing with joy, because you protect them. Let people who love your name be happy in you.

**12** LORD, you bless the righteous. You cover them with your kindness as with a shield.

## Chapter 6

**1** LORD, do not correct me in your anger. Do not punish me in your great anger.

**2** Be kind to me, LORD, because I am weak. Heal me, LORD, because my bones shake.

**3** My soul also has much trouble. LORD, how long will this continue?

**4** Come back, LORD, and save my life. Save me because of your faithful love.

**5** Dead people do not remember you. In the grave, no person thanks you.

**6** I am weak because I cry with pain. All night my bed is wet with my tears. I make my bed wet when I cry.

**7** My eyes become weak because of sorrow. They become weak because of all my enemies.

**8** Go away from me, all you people who do evil! The LORD hears the sound when I cry.

**9** The LORD hears my request for help. The LORD accepts my prayer.

**10** All my enemies will have shame and much trouble. They will turn back with sudden shame.

## Chapter 7

**1** LORD my God, I go to you for protection. Save me from all the people who chase me. Rescue me.

**2** If you do not, they will tear me apart like a lion. They will tear me into pieces, and no person will save me.

**3** LORD my God, maybe I did this wrong thing. Maybe my hands did evil.

**4** Maybe I did evil to my friend. Maybe I robbed my enemy without a reason.

**5** If so, let my enemy chase me and catch me. Let him crush my life into the ground. Let him put my honor in the dust. Selah.

**6** Stand up in your anger, LORD! Fight against the anger of my enemies. Wake up, my God, and give the justice that you command.

**7** Let the people of the nations come around you. Rule over them from your high throne.

**8** The LORD judges the peoples. Judge me, LORD, because I am righteous and innocent.

**9** Stop the evil of the wicked. Make the righteous safe. You are a righteous God. You examine the minds and hearts of people.

**10** God is my shield. He saves the people who have honest hearts.

**11** God is a righteous judge. Every day, God is angry with the wicked.

**12** If a person does not change, then God will make his sword sharp. God bends his bow and prepares it.

**13** God prepares his deadly weapons. He makes his arrows into arrows of fire.

**14** Look, the wicked man plans evil. He thinks of trouble and causes lies.

**15** He digs a pit to trap other people. But he falls into the pit that he made.

**16** The trouble that he causes comes back on him. His violence falls on his own head.

**17** I will thank the LORD because he is righteous. I will sing praise to the name of the LORD Most High.

## Chapter 8

**1** LORD, our Lord, your name is very great in all the earth! You put your glory above the sky.

**2** You cause small children and babies to praise your strength. You do this because of your enemies. Thus you stop the enemy and the person who wants revenge.

**3** I look at the sky, which you made with your fingers. I see the moon and the stars, which you put in their places.

**4** Then I ask, "What is man, that you think about him? What is a human, that you take care of him?"

**5** You made man a little lower than the angels. You gave him glory and honor like a crown.

**6** You made man the ruler over all the things that you made. You put all things under his control.

**7** You put all the sheep and the oxen under his control. You also put the wild animals under him.

**8** You put the birds of the sky and the fish of the sea under him. You put all things that swim in the sea under him.

**9** LORD, our Lord, your name is very great in all the earth!

## Chapter 9

**1** I will thank you, LORD, with all my heart. I will tell about all your wonderful works.

**2** I will be happy and glad in you. I will sing praise to your name, Most High God.

**3** My enemies turn back. They fall and die in front of you.

**4** You gave me justice and supported my case. You sit on your throne as a righteous judge.

**5** You punished the nations. You destroyed the wicked. You removed their names for all time.

**6** The enemy is completely destroyed for all time. You destroyed their cities. No person remembers them.

**7** But the LORD rules for all time. He prepares his throne for judgment.

**8** He judges the world in righteousness. He judges the peoples with honesty.

**9** The LORD is a safe place for people who suffer. He is a safe place in times of trouble.

**10** People who know your name trust in you. LORD, you do not leave the people who look for you.

**11** Sing praises to the LORD, who lives in Zion. Tell the nations what he did.

**12** God punishes murderers, and he remembers their victims. He does not forget the cry of the people who suffer.

**13** Be kind to me, LORD. See how my enemies cause me to suffer. You can lift me up from the gates of death.

**14** Then I will praise you at the gates of Zion. I will be glad because you save me.

**15** The nations fell into the pit that they dug. The net that they hid catches their feet.

**16** The LORD shows who he is by his justice. The work of the wicked traps them. Higgaion. Selah.

**17** The wicked will go to the place of the dead (Sheol). All the nations that forget God will go there.

**18** God will not always forget the people in need. The hope of the poor will not go away for all time.

**19** Stand up, LORD! Do not let man win. Judge the nations in front of you.

**20** Make the nations afraid, LORD. Let them know that they are only men. Selah.

## Chapter 10

**1** LORD, why do you stand far away? Why do you hide in times of trouble?

**2** In his pride, the wicked man chases the poor. Let the plans that the wicked make catch them.

**3** The wicked man boasts about the things that his heart wants. He blesses greedy people, but the LORD hates them.

**4** In his pride, the wicked man does not look for God. In all his thoughts, he says, "There is no God."

**5** His ways are always successful. Your laws are too high for him to see. He laughs at all his enemies.

**6** He says in his heart, "Nothing will move me. I will never have trouble."

**7** His mouth is full of curses, lies and threats. His tongue speaks trouble and evil.

**8** He waits in hidden places near the villages. He kills innocent people in secret places. His eyes secretly watch for helpless people.

**9** He waits in a hidden place like a lion in its den. He waits to catch the poor man. He catches the poor man and pulls him into his net.

**10** The wicked man bends low and waits. Helpless people fall under his strength.

**11** He says in his heart, "God forgets. God hides his face. He will never see what I do."

**12** Stand up, LORD! God, lift up your hand to punish. Do not forget the helpless.

**13** Why does the wicked man hate God? He says in his heart, "God will not ask me to explain."

**14** But you see trouble and sorrow. You look at it, and you take it into your hand. The helpless man gives himself to you. You help the orphan.

**15** Break the arm of the wicked and evil man. Punish his evil until there is no more evil.

**16** The LORD is King for all time. The nations will disappear from his land.

**17** LORD, you hear the desire of the humble. You give them courage. You listen to them.

**18** You give justice to the orphan and the oppressed. Then man, who comes from the earth, will not cause terror again.

## Chapter 11

**1** I go to the LORD for protection. How can you say to me, "Fly like a bird to your mountain"?

**2** Look, the wicked bend their bows. They put their arrows on the string. They prepare to shoot from the darkness at people with honest hearts.

**3** If someone destroys the foundations, what can the righteous do?

**4** The LORD is in his holy temple. The throne of the LORD is in heaven. His eyes watch all people. He examines them.

**5** The LORD examines the righteous and the wicked. He hates the person who loves violence.

**6** He will send hot coals, fire and sulfur on the wicked like rain. A hot wind will be their punishment.

**7** The LORD is righteous, and he loves righteous actions. Honest people will see his face.

## Chapter 12

**1** Help, LORD! There are no more godly men. The faithful people disappear from among men.

**2** Each person tells lies to his neighbor. They speak with false praise and with a dishonest heart.

**3** Let the LORD cut off all lips that use false praise. Let him cut off the tongue that boasts.

**4** These people say, "Our tongues will make us win. Our lips are our own. No person is our master."

**5** The LORD says, "The helpless suffer, and the poor cry. Thus now I will stand up. I will keep them safe from the people who attack them."

**6** The words of the LORD are pure. They are like silver that a person melts in a clay furnace 7 times.

**7** LORD, you will protect the poor. You will keep them safe from these evil people for all time.

**8** The wicked walk around everywhere when people give honor to evil things.

## Chapter 13

**1** How long, LORD? Will you forget me for all time? How long will you hide your face from me?

**2** How long must I fight against my thoughts? How long must I have sorrow in my heart every day? How long will my enemy win over me?

**3** Look at me and answer me, LORD my God. Give light to my eyes, or I will die.

**4** If you do not, my enemy will say, "I defeated him." My enemies will be glad when I fall.

**5** But I trust in your faithful love. My heart will be glad because you save me.

**6** I will sing to the LORD, because he is very good to me.

## Chapter 14

**1** The fool says in his heart, "There is no God." Fools do evil and terrible things. No person does good.

**2** The LORD looks down from heaven at all people. He looks for any person who understands and looks for God.

**3** All people turn away from God. All of them are bad. No person does good, not even one person.

**4** Do the people who do evil not know anything? They destroy my people as they eat bread. They do not pray to the LORD.

**5** There they are in great fear, because God is with the righteous people.

**6** You stop the plans of the poor, but the LORD is the safe place of the poor.

**7** I want the salvation of Israel to come from Zion! The LORD will make his people prosperous again. Then Jacob will be glad, and Israel will be happy.

## Chapter 15

**1** LORD, who can stay in your tent (tabernacle)? Who can live on your holy hill?

**2** The person who lives an honest life can live there. He does what is right. He speaks the truth from his heart.

**3** He does not tell lies about other people. He does not do evil to his neighbor. He does not say bad things about his friends.

**4** He hates evil people. He gives honor to people who respect the LORD with fear. He keeps his promise even when it hurts him.

**5** He lends money without interest. He does not take money to hurt innocent people. The person who does these things will always stand firm.

## Chapter 16

**1** Keep me safe, God, because I go to you for protection.

**2** I say to the LORD, "You are my Lord. I have no good thing without you."

**3** The holy people in the land are wonderful. I am very glad with them.

**4** People who run after other gods will have many troubles. I will not give their drink offerings of blood. I will not speak the names of their gods.

**5** The LORD gives me all that I have and need. You keep my future safe.

**6** You gave me good land. I have a beautiful inheritance.

**7** I will praise the LORD, who gives me advice. My heart also teaches me at night.

**8** I always keep the LORD in front of me. He is at my right hand, thus nothing will move me.

**9** Thus my heart is glad, and I am happy. My body also will live in safety.

**10** You will not leave my soul in the place of the dead (Sheol). You will not let your Holy One decay.

**11** You show me the path of life. With you there is complete joy. At your right hand there are pleasures for all time.

## Chapter 17

**1** Hear my just case, LORD. Listen to my cry for help. Listen to my prayer, which comes from honest lips.

**2** Let my judgment come from you. Let your eyes see what is right.

**3** You examine my heart. You come to me at night. You test me, and you find nothing wrong. I decide that my mouth will not sin.

**4** Other people do evil things. But I obey your words, and I do not go on the paths of violent people.

**5** My steps stay on your paths. My feet do not slip.

**6** I call to you, God, because you will answer me. Turn your ear to me, and hear my words.

**7** Show your wonderful love. With your right hand you save the people who go to you for protection from their enemies.

**8** Protect me as you protect the pupil of your eye. Hide me under the shadow of your wings.

**9** Hide me from the wicked people who attack me. Hide me from my deadly enemies who are all around me.

**10** They have no mercy in their hearts. Their mouths speak with pride.

**11** They follow my steps and are now all around me. They watch for a chance to throw me to the ground.

**12** They are like a lion that wants to tear its food. They are like a young lion that waits in a hidden place.

**13** Stand up, LORD! Fight against them and throw them down. Save my life from the wicked with your sword.

**14** LORD, save me with your hand from such men. Their reward is only in this life. You fill their stomachs with your treasure. Their sons have much food, and they leave their wealth to their children.

**15** But I will see your face because I am righteous. When I wake up, I will be happy because I see you.

## Chapter 18

**1** I love you, LORD. You are my strength.

**2** The LORD is my rock, my fortress and my rescuer. My God is my rock, and I go to him for protection. He is my shield and my strong salvation. He is my high tower.

**3** I call to the LORD, who deserves praise. Thus he saves me from my enemies.

**4** The ropes of death were around me. The floods of evil frightened me.

**5** The ropes of the grave were around me. The traps of death were in front of me.

**6** In my trouble, I called to the LORD. I cried to my God for help. He heard my voice from his temple. My cry for help came into his ears.

**7** Then the earth shook. The foundations of the mountains moved, because God was angry.

**8** Smoke went up from his nose. Fire came out of his mouth and burned. Hot coals burned in front of him.

**9** He opened the sky and came down. Dark clouds were under his feet.

**10** He flew on a cherub. He flew quickly on the wings of the wind.

**11** He covered himself in darkness. He put dark rain clouds around him like a tent.

**12** From the brightness in front of him, his thick clouds came out with hail and coals of fire.

**13** The LORD thundered in the sky. The Most High made his voice loud among hail and coals of fire.

**14** He shot his arrows and scattered his enemies. He sent much lightning and made them confused.

**15** Then people saw the bottom of the sea. The foundations of the world were open to view. This happened when you shouted, LORD, and when you blew the breath from your nose.

**16** He reached down from above and took me. He pulled me out of the deep waters.

**17** He saved me from my strong enemy. He saved me from the people who hated me. They were too strong for me.

**18** They attacked me on the day of my trouble. But the LORD supported me.

**19** He brought me out into an open place. He saved me because he was happy with me.

**20** The LORD gave me a reward because I did what was right. He rewarded me because my hands were clean.

**21** I obeyed the ways of the LORD. I did not turn away from my God to do evil.

**22** I kept all his laws in front of me. I did not refuse his rules.

**23** I was without fault in front of him. I kept myself from sin.

**24** Thus the LORD rewarded me because I did what was right. He saw that my hands were clean.

**25** You are faithful to the faithful. You are honest with the honest man.

**26** You are pure with the pure. But you are clever with the dishonest.

**27** You save humble people. But you bring down people with proud eyes.

**28** You keep my lamp bright. The LORD my God makes my darkness light.

**29** With your help I can attack an army. With my God I can climb over a wall.

**30** The way of God is perfect. The word of the LORD is true. He is a shield for all people who go to him for protection.

**31** Only the LORD is God. Only our God is a rock.

**32** God makes me strong. He makes my way perfect.

**33** He makes my feet like the feet of a deer. He causes me to stand on high places.

**34** He trains my hands for battle. My arms can bend a bow of bronze.

**35** You give me your shield of salvation. Your right hand supports me. Your help makes me great.

**36** You make a wide path for my feet. Thus my feet do not slip.

**37** I chased my enemies and caught them. I did not turn back until I destroyed them.

**38** I hit them, and they could not stand up. They fell under my feet.

**39** You gave me strength for the battle. You put my attackers under my control.

**40** You caused my enemies to turn and run from me. I destroyed the people who hated me.

**41** They cried for help, but no person saved them. They cried to the LORD, but he did not answer them.

**42** I beat them into small pieces like dust in the wind. I threw them out like mud in the streets.

**43** You saved me from the attacks of the people. You made me the leader of the nations. People who I did not know serve me.

**44** When they hear about me, they obey me. Foreigners bow down to me.

**45** The foreigners lose their courage. They come out of their strong places and shake with fear.

**46** The LORD is alive! Praise my rock! Give honor to the God who saves me!

**47** God gives me revenge. He puts the nations under my control.

**48** He saves me from my enemies. You lift me up above my attackers. You save me from violent men.

**49** Thus I will thank you among the nations, LORD. I will sing praises to your name.

**50** God gives great victories to his king. He shows faithful love to his anointed king, to David and to his descendants for all time.

## Chapter 19

**1** The sky shows the glory of God. The sky shows the work of his hands.

**2** Each day tells about him to the next day. Each night gives knowledge to the next night.

**3** The sky does not speak. It has no words. People do not hear its voice.

**4** But its message goes out through all the earth. Its words go to the farthest parts of the world. God put a tent in the sky for the sun.

**5** The sun comes out like a husband from his room after the wedding. The sun is glad like a strong man who runs a race.

**6** The sun goes out from one end of the sky. It moves across to the other end. Nothing can hide from its heat.

**7** The law of the LORD is perfect. It gives new life to the soul. The rules of the LORD are true. They make simple people wise.

**8** The commands of the LORD are right. They make the heart glad. The commands of the LORD are pure. They give light to the eyes.

**9** The respect for the LORD is clean. It continues for all time. The judgments of the LORD are true. All of them are righteous.

**10** They are more valuable than gold, more than much pure gold. They are sweeter than honey, sweeter than honey from the comb.

**11** Your laws warn your servant. A person who obeys them gets a great reward.

**12** No person can see all of his own errors. Forgive me for my hidden faults.

**13** Keep your servant from sins of pride. Do not let these sins control me. Then I will be without fault. I will be innocent of great sin.

**14** LORD, let the words of my mouth and the thoughts of my heart please you. You are my rock and my rescuer.

## Chapter 20

**1** I pray that the LORD answers you in the day of trouble. I pray that the name of the God of Jacob protects you.

**2** I pray that he sends you help from the holy place. I pray that he gives you support from Zion.

**3** I pray that he remembers all your offerings. I pray that he accepts your burnt sacrifices. Selah.

**4** I pray that he gives you what your heart wants. I pray that he makes all your plans successful.

**5** We will shout with joy when you win. We will raise our flags in the name of our God. I pray that the LORD gives you all that you ask.

**6** Now I know that the LORD saves his anointed king. God answers him from his holy heaven. God saves him with the great strength of his right hand.

**7** Some people trust in chariots, and some people trust in horses. But we trust in the name of the LORD our God.

**8** Those people fall down. But we stand up and stay firm.

**9** LORD, save the king! Answer us when we call.

## Chapter 21

**1** LORD, the king is glad in your strength. He is very happy because you give him victory!

**2** You give him what his heart wants. You do not refuse the request of his lips. Selah.

**3** You come to him with many good blessings. You put a crown of pure gold on his head.

**4** He asked you for life, and you gave it to him. You gave him a long life for all time.

**5** You make him very famous because you give him victory. You give him honor and majesty.

**6** You give him blessings for all time. You make him very glad because you are with him.

**7** The king trusts in the LORD. The faithful love of the Most High keeps him safe.

**8** Your hand will find all your enemies. Your right hand will find the people who hate you.

**9** When you come, you will burn them as in a hot oven. In his anger, the LORD will destroy them. The fire will burn them up.

**10** You will destroy their children from the earth. You will remove their descendants from among all people.

**11** They plan evil against you. They make bad plans, but they cannot be successful.

**12** You will cause them to turn and run away. You will aim your arrows at their faces.

**13** Be great, LORD, in your strength! We will sing and praise your power.

## Chapter 22

**1** My God, my God, why did you leave me? Why are you so far from my help? Why are you far from my cries of pain?

**2** My God, I cry out during the day, but you do not answer. I cry out at night, and I do not stop.

**3** But you are holy. You sit as King on the praises of Israel.

**4** Our fathers trusted in you. They trusted, and you saved them.

**5** They cried to you, and you saved them. They trusted in you, and they did not have shame.

**6** But I am a worm and not a man. People insult me and hate me.

**7** All people who see me laugh at me. They make faces at me and shake their heads. They say,

**8** "He trusts in the LORD. Let the LORD save him. Let the LORD rescue him, because the LORD likes him."

**9** But you brought me out of the womb. You caused me to trust you when I was a baby with my mother.

**10** I depended on you from my birth. You were my God from the time that my mother gave birth to me.

**11** Do not be far from me, because trouble is near. No person is here to help me.

**12** My enemies are around me like many bulls. They are like the strong bulls of Bashan.

**13** They open their mouths wide against me. They are like lions that roar and tear their food.

**14** I am like water that a person pours out. All my bones are out of their joints. My heart is like wax. It melts inside me.

**15** My strength is dry like a piece of broken pot. My tongue sticks to the top of my mouth. You put me in the dust of death.

**16** My enemies are around me like dogs. A group of evil people is around me. They made holes through my hands and my feet.

**17** I can count all my bones. People look at me and stare.

**18** They divide my clothes among them. They throw lots for my clothing.

**19** But you, LORD, do not be far away. You are my strength. Come quickly to help me.

**20** Save my life from the sword. Save my precious life from the power of the dogs.

**21** Save me from the mouth of the lion. Save me from the horns of the wild oxen. You answer me.

**22** I will tell my brothers about your name. I will praise you in the middle of the assembly.

**23** You people who respect the LORD with fear, praise him! All you descendants of Jacob, give him honor! All you descendants of Israel, respect him with fear!

**24** He does not hate or ignore the pain of the person who suffers. He does not hide his face from him. When that person cries to him for help, the LORD hears him.

**25** I will praise you in the great assembly. I will keep my promises in front of the people who respect him with fear.

**26** The poor will eat and be full. The people who look for the LORD will praise him. Let your hearts live for all time.

**27** All the farthest parts of the earth will remember and turn to the LORD. All the families of the nations will bow down in front of you.

**28** The kingdom belongs to the LORD. He rules over the nations.

**29** All the rich people of the earth will eat and worship. All people who go down to the dust will bow down in front of him. No person can keep himself alive.

**30** Future descendants will serve him. People will tell the next generation about the Lord.

**31** They will come and tell about his righteousness to people who are not yet born. They will say that the LORD did this.

## Chapter 23

**1** The LORD is my shepherd. I will have all that I need.

**2** He causes me to lie down in green fields. He leads me near calm waters.
