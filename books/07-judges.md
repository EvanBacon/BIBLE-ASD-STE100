# Judges

## Chapter 1

**1** After Joshua died, the people of Israel asked the LORD a question. They said, "Which tribe will go first to fight against the Canaanites for us?"

**2** The LORD said, "Judah will go. I give the land into the control of Judah."

**3** The men of Judah spoke to the men of Simeon, their brothers. They said, "Come with us into the land that we got by lot. Then we will fight the Canaanites together. Then we will go with you into your land." So the men of Simeon went with them.

**4** The men of Judah attacked. The LORD gave the Canaanites and the Perizzites into their control. They killed 10,000 men at Bezek.

**5** At Bezek they found Adoni-bezek and fought against him. They defeated the Canaanites and the Perizzites.

**6** But Adoni-bezek ran away. They chased him and caught him. They cut off his thumbs and his big toes.

**7** Adoni-bezek said, "70 kings with no thumbs and no big toes got their food from below my table. Now God paid me back for what I did to them." They took him to Jerusalem, and he died there.

**8** The men of Judah fought against Jerusalem and took the city. They killed the people with the sword and burned the city.

**9** After that, the men of Judah went down to fight against the Canaanites. These Canaanites lived in the hill country, in the Negev, and in the low hills.

**10** Judah attacked the Canaanites who lived in Hebron. Before, the name of Hebron was Kirjath-arba. They killed Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai.

**11** From there they attacked the people of Debir. Before, the name of Debir was Kirjath-sepher.

**12** Caleb said, "I will give my daughter Achsah as a wife to the man who attacks Kirjath-sepher and takes it."

**13** Othniel, the son of Kenaz, took the city. Kenaz was the younger brother of Caleb. Caleb gave his daughter Achsah to Othniel as his wife.

**14** When Achsah came to Othniel, she told him to ask her father for a field. She got down from her donkey. Caleb said to her, "What do you want?"

**15** She said to him, "Give me a gift. You gave me land in the dry south. Thus, also give me springs of water." So Caleb gave her the upper springs and the lower springs.

**16** The descendants of the Kenite, the father-in-law of Moses, went up from the city of palm trees. They went with the people of Judah into the wilderness of Judah, south of Arad. They went and lived among the people there.

**17** The men of Judah went with the men of Simeon, their brothers. They killed the Canaanites who lived in Zephath and fully destroyed the city. Then people called the city Hormah.

**18** Judah also took Gaza, Askelon, and Ekron, and the land around each city.

**19** The LORD was with Judah. The men of Judah forced out the people of the hill country. But they could not force out the people of the valley, because those people had iron chariots.

**20** As Moses said, the Israelites gave Hebron to Caleb. Caleb forced out the 3 sons of Anak from there.

**21** But the people of Benjamin did not force out the Jebusites who lived in Jerusalem. The Jebusites live with the people of Benjamin in Jerusalem to this day.

**22** The tribe of Joseph also attacked Beth-el, and the LORD was with them.

**23** The tribe of Joseph sent men to look at Beth-el in secret. Before, the name of the city was Luz.

**24** The spies saw a man who came out of the city. They said to him, "Please show us the way into the city. Then we will be kind to you."

**25** The man showed them the way into the city. They killed the people of the city with the sword. But they let the man and all his family go.

**26** The man went into the land of the Hittites and built a city. He called it Luz, and that is its name to this day.

**27** Manasseh did not force out the people of Beth-shean, Taanach, Dor, Ibleam, or Megiddo, or of their villages. The Canaanites continued to live in that land.

**28** When Israel became strong, they forced the Canaanites to do hard work for them. But they did not fully force them out.

**29** Ephraim did not force out the Canaanites who lived in Gezer. The Canaanites continued to live in Gezer among them.

**30** Zebulun did not force out the people of Kitron or Nahalol. The Canaanites lived among them and did forced work for them.

**31** Asher did not force out the people of Accho, Zidon, Ahlab, Achzib, Helbah, Aphik, or Rehob.

**32** The people of Asher lived among the Canaanites, who lived in the land, because they did not force them out.

**33** Naphtali did not force out the people of Beth-shemesh or Beth-anath. The people of Naphtali lived among the Canaanites in the land. But the people of Beth-shemesh and Beth-anath did forced work for them.

**34** The Amorites forced the people of Dan into the hill country. The Amorites did not let them come down into the valley.

**35** The Amorites continued to live in Mount Heres, Aijalon, and Shaalbim. But the tribe of Joseph became stronger, and the Amorites did forced work for them.

**36** The border of the Amorites went from the Pass of Akrabbim, from Sela, and to the north.

## Chapter 2

**1** The angel of the LORD went up from Gilgal to Bochim. He said, "I brought you up out of Egypt. I brought you into the land that I promised to your fathers. I said, 'I will never break my covenant with you.'

**2** I told you, 'Do not make an agreement with the people of this land. Break down their altars.' But you did not obey me. Why did you do this?

**3** Thus I now tell you, 'I will not force these people out in front of you. They will be like thorns in your sides. Their gods will be a trap for you.'"

**4** The angel of the LORD spoke these words to all the people of Israel. Then the people cried loudly.

**5** They called that place Bochim. They made sacrifices to the LORD there.

**6** Joshua sent the people away. The people of Israel went, each man to his own land, to take control of the land.

**7** The people served the LORD during all the life of Joshua. They also served the LORD during the lives of the elders who lived after Joshua. These elders saw all the great things that the LORD did for Israel.

**8** Joshua, the son of Nun, the servant of the LORD, died at the age of 110 years.

**9** They buried him in his own land at Timnath-heres. This place is in the hill country of Ephraim, to the north of Mount Gaash.

**10** All the people of that generation also died. Then a new generation came after them. These people did not know the LORD. They did not know the things that the LORD did for Israel.

**11** The people of Israel did evil in the sight of the LORD. They served the Baals.

**12** They left the LORD, the God of their fathers, who brought them out of the land of Egypt. They followed other gods, the gods of the nations around them. They bowed down to these gods. Thus they made the LORD angry.

**13** They left the LORD and served Baal and the Ashtaroth.

**14** The LORD became very angry with Israel. He gave them into the control of robbers, who robbed them. He sold them to their enemies around them. The Israelites could not stand against their enemies after that.

**15** Each time the Israelites went out to fight, the LORD was against them and caused them to lose. The LORD said this before, and he made a promise about it. The people had very much trouble.

**16** Then the LORD gave them judges. The judges saved them from the control of the robbers.

**17** But the people did not listen to their judges. They were not loyal to the LORD and served other gods. They bowed down to these gods. Their fathers obeyed the commands of the LORD. But this generation quickly left that way and did not obey.

**18** Each time the LORD gave them a judge, the LORD was with the judge. The LORD saved them from their enemies during all the life of the judge. The LORD felt pity for them, because their enemies hurt them and made them cry out.

**19** But when the judge died, the people went back to evil ways. They did worse things than their fathers. They followed other gods, served them, and bowed down to them. They did not stop their evil acts and their stubborn ways.

**20** Thus the LORD became very angry with Israel. He said, "This nation broke my covenant that I gave to their fathers. They did not obey me.

**21** Thus I will no longer force out any of the nations that Joshua left when he died.

**22** I will use these nations to test Israel. I will see if the Israelites will obey the LORD as their fathers did."

**23** So the LORD let those nations stay. He did not quickly force them out. He did not give them into the control of Joshua.

## Chapter 3

**1** The LORD let these nations stay in the land to test Israel. He tested the Israelites who did not know about the wars in Canaan.

**2** He did this to teach war to the new generations of Israel who did not know war before.

**3** These are the nations: the 5 rulers of the Philistines, all the Canaanites, the Sidonians, and the Hivites. The Hivites lived in the Lebanon mountains, from Mount Baal-hermon to the entrance of Hamath.

**4** The LORD used these nations to test Israel. He wanted to know this: will the Israelites obey his commands? He gave these commands to their fathers through Moses.

**5** The people of Israel lived among the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites.

**6** The Israelites took the daughters of these nations as their wives. They gave their own daughters to the sons of these nations. They also served the gods of these nations.

**7** The people of Israel did evil in the sight of the LORD. They forgot the LORD their God. They served the Baals and the Asherah poles.

**8** Thus the LORD became very angry with Israel. He sold them to Chushan-rishathaim, the king of Mesopotamia. The people of Israel served Chushan-rishathaim for 8 years.

**9** Then the people of Israel cried to the LORD for help. The LORD gave them a man to save them. This man was Othniel, the son of Kenaz, the younger brother of Caleb. Othniel saved them.

**10** The Spirit of the LORD came on Othniel, and he judged Israel. He went to war. The LORD gave Chushan-rishathaim, the king of Mesopotamia, into his control. Othniel defeated Chushan-rishathaim.

**11** Then the land had peace for 40 years. Then Othniel, the son of Kenaz, died.

**12** Again the people of Israel did evil in the sight of the LORD. Thus the LORD made Eglon, the king of Moab, strong against Israel.

**13** Eglon got the Ammonites and the Amalekites to join him. He attacked Israel and took the city of palm trees.

**14** The people of Israel served Eglon, the king of Moab, for 18 years.

**15** Then the people of Israel cried to the LORD for help. The LORD gave them a man to save them. This man was Ehud, the son of Gera, from the tribe of Benjamin. Ehud used his left hand. The people of Israel sent him with a payment for Eglon, the king of Moab.

**16** Ehud made a dagger with 2 sharp edges. The dagger was approximately 45 centimeters long. He tied it below his clothes on his right thigh.

**17** He gave the payment to Eglon, the king of Moab. Eglon was a very fat man.

**18** After Ehud gave the payment, he sent away the men who carried it.

**19** But Ehud himself turned back at the stone idols near Gilgal. He went to Eglon and said, "O king, I have a secret message for you." The king said, "Be quiet!" All his servants went out of the room.

**20** Ehud came to the king. The king sat alone in his cool upper room. Ehud said, "I have a message from God for you." The king stood up from his seat.

**21** Ehud reached with his left hand. He took the dagger from his right thigh and pushed it into the stomach of the king.

**22** The handle also went in after the blade. The fat closed over the blade, and Ehud did not pull the dagger out. Waste came out of the body.

**23** Then Ehud went out through the porch. He closed the doors of the upper room behind him and locked them.

**24** After Ehud left, the servants of the king came. They saw that someone locked the doors of the upper room. They said, "The king must be in the toilet in his cool room."

**25** The servants waited a long time and became worried. The king did not open the doors of the room. So they took a key and opened the doors. They saw their master dead on the floor.

**26** While the servants waited, Ehud escaped. He went past the stone idols and escaped to Seirath.

**27** When Ehud arrived, he blew a trumpet in the hill country of Ephraim. The people of Israel came down with him from the hills, and he led them.

**28** He said to them, "Follow me, because the LORD gives your enemies, the Moabites, into your control." So they followed him down. They took control of the places where people crossed the Jordan River to Moab. They did not let any man cross.

**29** At that time they killed approximately 10,000 Moabites. All of these men were strong and brave. Not one man escaped.

**30** So on that day Israel defeated Moab. Then the land had peace for 80 years.

**31** After Ehud came Shamgar, the son of Anath. He killed 600 Philistines with a stick that people used to drive oxen. He also saved Israel.

## Chapter 4

**1** After Ehud died, the people of Israel again did evil in the sight of the LORD.

**2** So the LORD sold them to Jabin, a king of Canaan, who ruled in Hazor. The commander of his army was Sisera. Sisera lived in Harosheth of the Gentiles.

**3** Jabin had 900 iron chariots. He was very cruel to the people of Israel for 20 years. So the people of Israel cried to the LORD for help.

**4** At that time a prophetess, Deborah, the wife of Lapidoth, judged Israel.

**5** She sat below the palm tree of Deborah, between Ramah and Beth-el in the hill country of Ephraim. The people of Israel came to her for decisions.

**6** Deborah sent a message to Barak, the son of Abinoam, at Kedesh in Naphtali. She told him to come. She said to him, "The LORD, the God of Israel, gives you a command. 'Go to Mount Tabor. Take 10,000 men from Naphtali and Zebulun with you.

**7** I will bring Sisera, the commander of the army of Jabin, to you at the Kishon River. He will come with his chariots and his army. I will give him into your control.'"

**8** Barak said to her, "If you go with me, I will go. But if you do not go with me, I will not go."

**9** Deborah said, "I will go with you. But you will not get the honor in this fight. The LORD will give Sisera into the control of a woman." Then Deborah went with Barak to Kedesh.

**10** Barak called the men of Zebulun and Naphtali to Kedesh. 10,000 men followed him. Deborah also went with him.

**11** Heber the Kenite separated from the other Kenites. The Kenites were the descendants of Hobab, the father-in-law of Moses. Heber put up his tent near the large tree at Zaanaim, near Kedesh.

**12** People told Sisera that Barak, the son of Abinoam, went up to Mount Tabor.

**13** So Sisera gathered all his 900 iron chariots and all his men. He brought them from Harosheth of the Gentiles to the Kishon River.

**14** Deborah said to Barak, "Go! Today the LORD gives Sisera into your control. The LORD goes in front of you." So Barak went down from Mount Tabor with 10,000 men behind him.

**15** When Barak attacked, the LORD caused confusion in Sisera, all his chariots, and all his army. Barak killed them with the sword. Sisera got down from his chariot and ran away on foot.

**16** Barak chased the chariots and the army to Harosheth of the Gentiles. The Israelites killed all the army of Sisera with the sword. Not one man stayed alive.

**17** But Sisera ran away on foot to the tent of Jael, the wife of Heber the Kenite. He went there because Jabin, the king of Hazor, and the family of Heber had peace.

**18** Jael went out to meet Sisera. She said to him, "Come in, my lord. Come into my tent. Do not be afraid." So he went into her tent, and she put a blanket over him.

**19** Sisera said to her, "Please give me some water. I am thirsty." She opened a skin bag of milk and gave him a drink. Then she put the blanket over him again.

**20** He said to her, "Stand at the door of the tent. If a man comes and asks you, 'Is a man here?' then say, 'No.'"

**21** But Sisera was very tired and fell into a deep sleep. Then Jael took a tent peg and a hammer. She went quietly to him. She hit the peg through the side of his head and into the ground. So he died.

**22** At that time Barak came after Sisera. Jael went out to meet Barak. She said to him, "Come. I will show you the man that you look for." Barak went into her tent. He saw Sisera dead, with the tent peg through his head.

**23** On that day God defeated Jabin, the king of Canaan, in front of the people of Israel.

**24** The Israelites became stronger and stronger against Jabin, the king of Canaan. At last they destroyed him.

## Chapter 5

**1** On that day Deborah and Barak, the son of Abinoam, sang this song:

**2** "The leaders of Israel led, and the people gave themselves willingly. Thus praise the LORD!

**3** Listen, you kings! Hear, you rulers! I will sing to the LORD. I will sing praise to the LORD, the God of Israel.

**4** LORD, you went out from Seir. You marched out from the land of Edom. Then the earth shook. The sky poured rain, and the clouds poured water.

**5** The mountains shook in front of the LORD, the God of Sinai. They shook in front of the LORD, the God of Israel.

**6** In the days of Shamgar, the son of Anath, and in the days of Jael, people did not use the main roads. Travelers used small paths.

**7** The people of the villages stopped their work in Israel. Then I, Deborah, came. I came as a mother to Israel.

**8** The people chose new gods. Then enemies fought at the gates of the cities. No one saw a shield or a spear among 40,000 men in Israel.

**9** My heart is with the commanders of Israel. They gave themselves willingly with the people. Praise the LORD!

**10** You who ride on white donkeys, think about this. You who sit on rich carpets, and you who walk on the road, think about this.

**11** Near the wells, where people get water, they tell about the righteous acts of the LORD. They tell about his righteous acts for his villages in Israel. Then the people of the LORD went down to the gates.

**12** Wake up, wake up, Deborah! Wake up and sing a song! Get up, Barak, son of Abinoam! Take your prisoners away.

**13** Then the people who stayed alive came down against the leaders. The people of the LORD came down for me against the strong men.

**14** Men came from Ephraim, whose land was once the land of Amalek. Benjamin came after you with his people. Commanders came down from Machir. Officials with staffs came from Zebulun.

**15** The leaders of Issachar were with Deborah. Issachar was loyal to Barak. They followed him quickly into the valley. But the clans of Reuben could not decide what to do.

**16** Why did you stay among the sheep pens? Did you want to hear the shepherds call the flocks? The clans of Reuben thought much but did not decide.

**17** Gilead stayed on the east side of the Jordan River. Why did Dan stay near the ships? Asher stayed at the coast and lived near the harbors.

**18** The people of Zebulun put their lives in danger of death. Naphtali did the same on the high fields.

**19** The kings came and fought. The kings of Canaan fought at Taanach, near the waters of Megiddo. But they took no silver as plunder.

**20** The stars fought from the sky. In their paths, the stars fought against Sisera.

**21** The Kishon River swept the enemies away. It is an old river, the Kishon River. My soul, march on with strength!

**22** Then the hooves of the horses hit the ground loudly. The strong horses of the enemy galloped and galloped away.

**23** 'Curse Meroz,' said the angel of the LORD. 'Strongly curse the people of Meroz. They did not come to help the LORD. They did not help the LORD against the strong enemies.'

**24** Jael, the wife of Heber the Kenite, will be the most blessed of women. She will be the most blessed of the women who live in tents.

**25** Sisera asked for water, and she gave him milk. She brought him cream in a fine bowl.

**26** She put her left hand on the tent peg and her right hand on the hammer of the workmen. She hit Sisera and broke his head. She pushed the peg through the side of his head.

**27** He fell down at her feet. He fell and lay still. At her feet he fell down. Where he fell, he died.

**28** The mother of Sisera looked out through the window. She cried out through the window, 'Why is his chariot so late? Why do I not hear the wheels of his chariots?'

**29** Her wisest women answered her. She also said the same words to herself:

**30** 'Surely they found plunder and now divide it. Each man gets a girl or 2 girls. Sisera gets colored cloth. He gets colored cloth with needlework, 2 pieces of colored needlework for my neck. All this is plunder.'

**31** LORD, let all your enemies die like Sisera! But let the people who love you be like the sun when it rises in its strength." Then the land had peace for 40 years.

## Chapter 6

**1** The people of Israel did evil in the sight of the LORD. So the LORD gave them into the control of Midian for 7 years.

**2** The Midianites were stronger than Israel. Because of the Midianites, the people of Israel made places to hide in the mountains. They used caves and strong places.

**3** Each time the Israelites planted their seeds, the Midianites attacked them. The Amalekites and the people of the east also attacked them.

**4** The enemies made camps in the land. They destroyed the crops as far as Gaza. They left no food for Israel. They left no sheep, no cattle, and no donkeys.

**5** They came with their animals and their tents. There were as many of them as there are locusts. No one could count the men or their camels. They came into the land to destroy it.

**6** The Midianites made Israel very poor. So the people of Israel cried to the LORD for help.

**7** The people of Israel cried to the LORD because of the Midianites.

**8** Then the LORD sent a prophet to them. The prophet said, "The LORD, the God of Israel, says this: 'I brought you up from Egypt. I brought you out of the land where you were slaves.

**9** I saved you from the Egyptians and from all your enemies. I forced your enemies out in front of you, and I gave you their land.

**10** I told you, "I am the LORD your God. Do not worship the gods of the Amorites, in whose land you live." But you did not obey me.'"

**11** The angel of the LORD came and sat below the oak tree at Ophrah. This tree was the property of Joash the Abiezrite. Gideon, the son of Joash, beat wheat in a winepress. He did this to hide the wheat from the Midianites.

**12** The angel of the LORD showed himself to Gideon. He said, "The LORD is with you, strong and brave man."

**13** Gideon said to him, "Please, my lord, if the LORD is with us, why did all these bad things occur to us? Our fathers told us about all his miracles. They said, 'The LORD brought us up from Egypt.' Where are those miracles now? Now the LORD left us. He gave us into the control of the Midianites."

**14** The LORD looked at Gideon and said, "Go with the strength that you have. Save Israel from the Midianites. I send you."

**15** Gideon said to him, "Please, Lord, how can I save Israel? My clan is the weakest in Manasseh. I am the least important person in my family."

**16** The LORD said to him, "I will be with you. You will defeat the Midianites as easily as you defeat 1 man."

**17** Gideon said, "If you are happy with me, show me a sign. Show me that it is really you who speaks with me.

**18** Please do not go away from here until I come back to you. I will bring my offering and put it in front of you." The LORD said, "I will wait until you come back."

**19** Gideon went into his house and cooked a young goat. He also made bread without yeast from 1 ephah (approximately 22 liters) of flour. He put the meat in a basket and the soup in a pot. He brought them out to the angel below the oak tree and gave them to him.

**20** The angel of God said to him, "Put the meat and the bread on this rock. Then pour out the soup." Gideon did this.

**21** The angel of the LORD touched the meat and the bread with the end of his staff. Fire came up from the rock and burned the meat and the bread. Then the angel of the LORD went away, and Gideon did not see him again.

**22** Then Gideon knew that he was the angel of the LORD. Gideon said, "Oh, Lord GOD! I saw the angel of the LORD face to face!"

**23** The LORD said to him, "Peace to you. Do not be afraid. You will not die."

**24** Then Gideon built an altar to the LORD there. He called it "The LORD Is Peace." The altar is still in Ophrah of the Abiezrites to this day.

**25** On that same night the LORD said to Gideon, "Take the young bull of your father, the second bull, which is 7 years old. Pull down the altar of Baal that your father has. Cut down the Asherah pole that is near the altar.

**26** Then build a correct altar to the LORD your God on the top of this hill. Take the second bull and offer it as a burnt offering. Use the wood of the Asherah pole that you cut down."

**27** So Gideon took 10 of his servants and did as the LORD told him. But Gideon was afraid of his family and of the men of the city. Thus he did not do it during the day. He did it at night.

**28** The men of the city got up early in the morning. They saw that someone broke down the altar of Baal. Someone cut down the Asherah pole near it. Someone offered the second bull on the new altar.

**29** The men asked each other, "Who did this?" They looked for the answer. Then they said, "Gideon, the son of Joash, did this."

**30** The men of the city said to Joash, "Bring out your son. He must die. He broke down the altar of Baal and cut down the Asherah pole near it."

**31** But Joash said to all the people who stood against him, "Will you argue for Baal? Will you save him? The person who argues for Baal will die before morning. If Baal is a god, he can argue for himself, because someone broke down his altar."

**32** So on that day Joash called Gideon "Jerubbaal." This name means "Let Baal argue against him," because Gideon broke down the altar of Baal.

**33** Then all the Midianites, the Amalekites, and the people of the east came together. They crossed the Jordan River and made a camp in the valley of Jezreel.

**34** But the Spirit of the LORD came on Gideon. Gideon blew a trumpet, and the Abiezrites came to follow him.

**35** He sent messengers through all Manasseh, and the men of Manasseh also came to follow him. He sent messengers to Asher, Zebulun, and Naphtali. The men of these tribes also came up to meet him.

**36** Gideon said to God, "You said that you will save Israel through me. If this is true,

**37** then I will put a piece of wool on the floor where we beat grain. Possibly dew will be only on the wool, and all the ground will be dry. If this occurs, then I will know that you will save Israel through me, as you said."

**38** This is what occurred. Gideon got up early the next morning. He pressed the wool and got the dew out of it. The water filled a bowl.

**39** Then Gideon said to God, "Do not be angry with me. Let me speak only 1 more time. Please let me do 1 more test with the wool. This time, make the wool dry and make dew on all the ground."

**40** That night God did this. The wool was dry, and there was dew on all the ground.

## Chapter 7

**1** Early in the morning, Jerubbaal (Gideon) and all his men made a camp near the spring of Harod. The camp of the Midianites was to the north of them, near the hill of Moreh, in the valley.

**2** The LORD said to Gideon, "You have too many men for me to give the Midianites into their control. Israel could say proudly against me, 'We saved ourselves with our own strength.'

**3** Thus tell the men, 'All the men who are afraid can leave Mount Gilead and go home.'" So 22,000 men went home. 10,000 men stayed.

**4** The LORD said to Gideon, "There are still too many men. Take them down to the water, and I will test them for you there. If I say, 'This man will go with you,' he will go. If I say, 'This man will not go with you,' he will not go."

**5** So Gideon took the men down to the water. The LORD said to him, "Put the men who drink the water with their tongues like a dog in one group. Put the men who get down on their knees to drink in a different group."

**6** 300 men put the water to their mouths with their hands and drank like a dog. All the other men got down on their knees to drink.

**7** The LORD said to Gideon, "I will save you with the 300 men who drank like a dog. I will give the Midianites into your control. Let all the other men go home."

**8** So the 300 men took the food and the trumpets of the other men. Gideon sent all the other Israelites to their tents, but he kept the 300 men. The camp of Midian was below him in the valley.

**9** That night the LORD said to Gideon, "Get up and attack the camp, because I give it into your control.

**10** But if you are afraid to attack, go down to the camp with your servant Purah.

**11** Listen to what the enemies say. Then you will have the courage to attack the camp." So Gideon went down with his servant Purah to the edge of the camp, where the soldiers were.

**12** The Midianites, the Amalekites, and all the people of the east were in the valley. There were as many of them as there are locusts. Their camels were as many as the sand on the seashore. No one could count them.

**13** Gideon arrived and heard a man who told his friend about a dream. The man said, "I had a dream. A round loaf of barley bread rolled into the camp of Midian. It hit a tent so hard that the tent fell over. The tent fell flat."

**14** His friend answered, "This can only mean the sword of Gideon, the son of Joash, the man of Israel. God gives Midian and all the army into his control."

**15** Gideon heard the dream and its meaning. Then he bowed down and worshipped God. He went back to the camp of Israel and said, "Get up! The LORD gives the army of Midian into your control."

**16** Gideon divided the 300 men into 3 groups. He gave a trumpet and an empty jar to each man. There was a torch in each jar.

**17** He told them, "Look at me, and do what I do. When I come to the edge of the camp, do what I do.

**18** I and all the men with me will blow our trumpets. Then you also blow your trumpets on all sides of the camp. Shout, 'For the LORD and for Gideon!'"

**19** Gideon and the 100 men with him came to the edge of the camp at the start of the middle watch of the night. The enemy guards just changed. Then Gideon and his men blew the trumpets and broke the jars in their hands.

**20** All 3 groups blew the trumpets and broke the jars. They held the torches in their left hands and the trumpets in their right hands. They shouted, "A sword for the LORD and for Gideon!"

**21** Each man stood in his place around the camp. All the enemy soldiers ran. They cried out and ran away.

**22** The 300 men blew the trumpets. Then the LORD caused the enemy soldiers to fight each other with their swords in all the camp. The army ran to Beth-shittah toward Zererath, as far as the border of Abel-meholah, near Tabbath.

**23** The men of Israel came together from Naphtali, Asher, and all Manasseh. They chased the Midianites.

**24** Gideon sent messengers through all the hill country of Ephraim. They said, "Come down and attack the Midianites. Take control of the waters before the Midianites get there. Take control of the Jordan River as far as Beth-barah." So all the men of Ephraim came together. They took control of the waters as far as Beth-barah and the Jordan River.

**25** They caught 2 leaders of the Midianites, Oreb and Zeeb. They killed Oreb at the rock of Oreb. They killed Zeeb at the winepress of Zeeb. They chased the Midianites. They brought the heads of Oreb and Zeeb to Gideon, who was on the other side of the Jordan River.

## Chapter 8

**1** The men of Ephraim said to Gideon, "Why did you do this to us? Why did you not call us when you went to fight the Midianites?" They argued with him angrily.

**2** Gideon said to them, "What did I do, compared to you? The last grapes of Ephraim are better than all the grapes of Abiezer.

**3** God gave Oreb and Zeeb, the leaders of Midian, into your control. What did I do, compared to you?" When he said this, their anger stopped.

**4** Gideon and his 300 men came to the Jordan River and crossed it. They were very tired, but they continued to chase the enemy.

**5** Gideon said to the men of Succoth, "Please give bread to my men. They are very tired. I chase Zebah and Zalmunna, the kings of Midian."

**6** But the officials of Succoth said, "You do not have Zebah and Zalmunna in your control now. Why must we give bread to your army?"

**7** Gideon said, "When the LORD gives Zebah and Zalmunna into my control, I will come back. Then I will beat your bodies with thorns and briers from the wilderness."

**8** Gideon went from there to Penuel and asked the same thing. The men of Penuel gave him the same answer as the men of Succoth.

**9** So he said to the men of Penuel, "When I come back safely, I will break down this tower."

**10** Zebah and Zalmunna were in Karkor with their army of approximately 15,000 men. These men were all that stayed alive from the army of the people of the east. 120,000 soldiers with swords died.

**11** Gideon went up by the road of the people who live in tents, to the east of Nobah and Jogbehah. He attacked the army when the army did not expect an attack.

**12** Zebah and Zalmunna, the 2 kings of Midian, ran away. Gideon chased them and caught them. He caused confusion in all their army.

**13** Then Gideon, the son of Joash, came back from the battle by the Pass of Heres.

**14** He caught a young man from Succoth and asked him questions. The young man wrote down for him the names of the 77 officials and elders of Succoth.

**15** Then Gideon came to the men of Succoth. He said, "Look, here are Zebah and Zalmunna. You laughed at me because of them. You said, 'You do not have Zebah and Zalmunna in your control now. Why must we give bread to your tired men?'"

**16** He took the elders of the city. He took thorns and briers from the wilderness and punished the men of Succoth with them.

**17** He also broke down the tower of Penuel and killed the men of that city.

**18** Then Gideon said to Zebah and Zalmunna, "What were the men that you killed at Tabor like?" They answered, "They were like you. Each of them looked like the son of a king."

**19** Gideon said, "They were my brothers, the sons of my mother. As surely as the LORD lives, you killed them. So now I will kill you."

**20** Then he said to Jether, his oldest son, "Get up and kill them." But the young man did not pull out his sword. He was afraid, because he was still young.

**21** Then Zebah and Zalmunna said, "You get up and kill us. A man has the strength of a man." So Gideon got up and killed Zebah and Zalmunna. He took the decorations from the necks of their camels.

**22** Then the men of Israel said to Gideon, "Rule over us, you, your son, and your grandson. You saved us from the Midianites."

**23** But Gideon said to them, "I will not rule over you. My son will not rule over you. The LORD will rule over you."

**24** Then Gideon said, "I have 1 request. Each of you, give me an ear ring from your plunder." The enemies wore gold rings in their ears, because they were Ishmaelites.

**25** The men answered, "We will gladly give them." So they put a cloth on the ground. Each man put an ear ring from his plunder on it.

**26** The gold earrings that Gideon asked for weighed 1,700 shekels (approximately 19 kilograms). The men also gave him the decorations, the necklaces, and the purple clothes of the kings of Midian. They also gave him the chains from the necks of the camels.

**27** Gideon used the gold to make an ephod (a special garment). He put it in his city, Ophrah. All Israel stopped their loyalty to God and worshipped the ephod there. The ephod became a trap for Gideon and his family.

**28** So the people of Israel defeated Midian. The Midianites did not become strong again. The land had peace for 40 years, during the life of Gideon.

**29** Jerubbaal, the son of Joash, went back to his home and lived there.

**30** Gideon had 70 sons of his own, because he had many wives.

**31** His concubine in Shechem also gave birth to a son. Gideon called him Abimelech.

**32** Gideon, the son of Joash, died when he was very old. They buried him in the tomb of his father Joash, in Ophrah of the Abiezrites.

**33** After Gideon died, the people of Israel again stopped their loyalty to God and worshipped the Baals. They made Baal-berith their god.

**34** The people of Israel did not remember the LORD their God. The LORD saved them from all their enemies around them.

**35** They also were not kind to the family of Jerubbaal (Gideon). They did not thank him for all the good things that he did for Israel.

## Chapter 9

**1** Abimelech, the son of Jerubbaal, went to Shechem, to the brothers of his mother. He spoke to them and to all the clan of the family of his mother. He said,

**2** "Please ask all the leaders of Shechem this question: 'Which is better for you? Do you want all 70 sons of Jerubbaal to rule over you? Or do you want 1 man to rule over you?' Also remember that I am your own relative."

**3** The brothers of his mother told all these words to the leaders of Shechem. The leaders decided to follow Abimelech. They said, "He is our brother."

**4** They gave him 70 pieces of silver from the temple of Baal-berith. Abimelech used the silver to pay worthless and careless men, who followed him.

**5** He went to the house of his father at Ophrah. On 1 stone he killed his 70 brothers, the sons of Jerubbaal. But Jotham, the youngest son of Jerubbaal, escaped, because he hid.

**6** Then all the leaders of Shechem and all the family of Millo came together. They made Abimelech king near the large tree and the stone pillar at Shechem.

**7** People told Jotham about this. He went and stood on the top of Mount Gerizim. He shouted to the people, "Listen to me, leaders of Shechem! Then God will listen to you.

**8** One day the trees went out to choose a king for themselves. They said to the olive tree, 'Be our king.'

**9** But the olive tree said to them, 'People use my oil to honor gods and men. Must I stop to make my oil and go to rule over the trees?'

**10** Then the trees said to the fig tree, 'Come and be our king.'

**11** But the fig tree said to them, 'Must I stop to make my sweet, good fruit and go to rule over the trees?'

**12** Then the trees said to the vine, 'Come and be our king.'

**13** But the vine said to them, 'My wine makes gods and men happy. Must I stop to make my wine and go to rule over the trees?'

**14** Then all the trees said to the thorn bush, 'Come and be our king.'

**15** The thorn bush said to the trees, 'If you really want me as your king, then come and rest in my shade. If you do not, then let fire come out of the thorn bush. Let the fire burn the cedars of Lebanon.'

**16** Now, did you act honestly and correctly when you made Abimelech king? Were you good to Jerubbaal and his family? Did you give him what he deserved?

**17** My father fought for you. He put his life in danger and saved you from the Midianites.

**18** But today you attacked the family of my father. You killed his 70 sons on 1 stone. You made Abimelech, the son of his female slave, king over the leaders of Shechem, because he is your relative.

**19** If you acted honestly and correctly with Jerubbaal and his family today, then be happy with Abimelech. Let him also be happy with you.

**20** But if you did not, then let fire come out from Abimelech. Let the fire burn the leaders of Shechem and the family of Millo. And let fire come out from the leaders of Shechem and the family of Millo. Let that fire burn Abimelech."

**21** Then Jotham ran away to Beer. He lived there, because he was afraid of his brother Abimelech.

**22** Abimelech ruled over Israel for 3 years.

**23** Then God sent an evil spirit between Abimelech and the leaders of Shechem. The leaders of Shechem stopped their loyalty to Abimelech.

**24** God did this to punish the cruel murder of the 70 sons of Jerubbaal. God made Abimelech, their brother, responsible for their blood, because he killed them. God also made the leaders of Shechem responsible, because they helped him kill his brothers.

**25** The leaders of Shechem put men on the tops of the hills to attack Abimelech in secret. These men robbed all the people who went past them. Someone told Abimelech about this.

**26** Gaal, the son of Ebed, came with his brothers to Shechem. The leaders of Shechem trusted him.

**27** The people went out into the fields and gathered the grapes from their vineyards. They pressed the grapes and had a celebration. They went into the temple of their god. They ate and drank, and they cursed Abimelech.

**28** Gaal, the son of Ebed, said, "Who is Abimelech? Who are we in Shechem, that we must serve him? He is the son of Jerubbaal, and Zebul is his officer. Serve the men of Hamor, the father of Shechem! Why must we serve Abimelech?

**29** I wish that these people were under my control! Then I will remove Abimelech." He said to Abimelech, "Make your army bigger and come out to fight!"

**30** Zebul, the ruler of the city, heard the words of Gaal, the son of Ebed. Zebul became very angry.

**31** He secretly sent messengers to Abimelech. They said, "Gaal, the son of Ebed, and his brothers came to Shechem. They cause the city to turn against you.

**32** So, at night, go with your men and hide in the fields.

**33** In the morning, when the sun comes up, get up early and attack the city. When Gaal and his men come out against you, do to them what you can."

**34** So Abimelech and all his men got up at night. They hid near Shechem in 4 groups.

**35** Gaal, the son of Ebed, went out and stood at the entrance of the city gate. Then Abimelech and his men came out from the places where they hid.

**36** Gaal saw the men and said to Zebul, "Look, men come down from the tops of the hills." Zebul said to him, "You see the shadows of the hills. They look like men to you."

**37** But Gaal spoke again. He said, "Look, men come down from the center of the land. Another group comes on the road from the tree of the fortune-tellers."

**38** Then Zebul said to him, "Where are your proud words now? You said, 'Who is Abimelech, that we must serve him?' These are the men that you hated. Go out now and fight them!"

**39** So Gaal led the leaders of Shechem out and fought against Abimelech.

**40** Abimelech chased Gaal, and Gaal ran away from him. Many men fell wounded, as far as the entrance of the gate.

**41** Then Abimelech stayed at Arumah. Zebul forced Gaal and his brothers out of Shechem. They could not live there after that.

**42** The next day the people of Shechem went out into the fields. Someone told Abimelech about this.

**43** So Abimelech took his men and divided them into 3 groups. They hid in the fields. When he saw the people come out of the city, he attacked them and killed them.

**44** Abimelech and the group with him ran forward and stood at the entrance of the city gate. The 2 other groups attacked all the people in the fields and killed them.

**45** Abimelech fought against the city all that day. He took the city and killed the people in it. He broke down the city and put salt on the ground.

**46** All the leaders of the tower of Shechem heard about this. They went into the strong room of the temple of El-berith.

**47** Someone told Abimelech that all the leaders of the tower of Shechem were together there.

**48** Abimelech and all his men went up Mount Zalmon. Abimelech took an axe and cut a branch from a tree. He put the branch on his shoulder. He said to his men, "Quickly, do what you saw me do!"

**49** So each man cut a branch and followed Abimelech. They put the branches against the strong room and set it on fire. So all the people of the tower of Shechem died, approximately 1,000 men and women.

**50** Then Abimelech went to Thebez. He made a camp near Thebez and took the city.

**51** But there was a strong tower in the city. All the men and women, and all the leaders of the city, ran into the tower. They locked the door and went up to the roof of the tower.

**52** Abimelech came to the tower and attacked it. He went near the door of the tower to burn it.

**53** But a woman dropped an upper millstone on the head of Abimelech. The stone broke his skull.

**54** Quickly, Abimelech called the young man who carried his weapons. He said to him, "Take your sword and kill me. I do not want people to say, 'A woman killed him.'" So the young man pushed his sword through Abimelech, and Abimelech died.

**55** The men of Israel saw that Abimelech was dead. Then each man went home.

**56** In this way God punished Abimelech for the evil that he did to his father, when he killed his 70 brothers.

**57** God also punished the men of Shechem for all their evil. The curse of Jotham, the son of Jerubbaal, came on them.

## Chapter 10

**1** After Abimelech, Tola came to save Israel. Tola was the son of Puah, the son of Dodo, from the tribe of Issachar. He lived in Shamir in the hill country of Ephraim.

**4** He had 30 sons, who rode on 30 donkeys. They had 30 cities in the land of Gilead. People call these cities Havoth-jair to this day.

**6** Again the people of Israel did evil in the sight of the LORD. They served the Baals and the Ashtaroth. They served the gods of Syria, Zidon, and Moab. They served the gods of the Ammonites and the Philistines. They left the LORD and did not serve him.

**7** So the LORD became very angry with Israel. He sold them to the Philistines and the Ammonites.

**17** Then the Ammonites came together and made a camp in Gilead. The people of Israel came together and made a camp at Mizpeh.

**18** The leaders of the people of Gilead said to each other, "Which man will start the fight against the Ammonites? That man will be the leader of all the people of Gilead."

## Chapter 11

**1** Jephthah from Gilead was a strong and brave soldier. His mother was a prostitute. Gilead was the father of Jephthah.

**2** The wife of Gilead also gave birth to sons. When these sons became adults, they forced Jephthah out. They said to him, "You will not get property from our father, because you are the son of a different woman."

**3** So Jephthah ran away from his brothers and lived in the land of Tob. Worthless men came to Jephthah and went out with him.

**4** After some time, the Ammonites started a war against Israel.

**5** When the Ammonites made war against Israel, the elders of Gilead went to get Jephthah from the land of Tob.

**6** They said to Jephthah, "Come and be our commander. Then we can fight against the Ammonites."

**7** Jephthah said to the elders of Gilead, "You hated me and forced me out of the house of my father. Why do you come to me now, when you are in trouble?"

**8** The elders of Gilead said to Jephthah, "That is why we come to you now. Come with us and fight against the Ammonites. Then you will be the leader over all the people of Gilead."

**9** Jephthah said to the elders of Gilead, "You ask me to come back and fight against the Ammonites. If the LORD gives them to me, will I be your leader?"

**10** The elders of Gilead said to Jephthah, "The LORD hears what we say. We will surely do what you say."

**11** So Jephthah went with the elders of Gilead. The people made him their leader and commander. At Mizpeh, Jephthah said all his words again in front of the LORD.

**12** Then Jephthah sent messengers to the king of the Ammonites. They said, "What is your problem with me? Why do you come to attack my land?"

**13** The king of the Ammonites answered the messengers of Jephthah. He said, "When Israel came up from Egypt, they took my land. They took it from the Arnon River to the Jabbok River and to the Jordan River. Now give this land back to me peacefully."

**14** Jephthah again sent messengers to the king of the Ammonites.

**15** They said to him, "Jephthah says this: 'Israel did not take the land of Moab or the land of the Ammonites.

**16** When Israel came up from Egypt, they went through the wilderness to the Red Sea. Then they came to Kadesh.

**17** Israel sent messengers to the king of Edom. They said, "Please let us go through your land." But the king of Edom did not listen. Israel also sent messengers to the king of Moab, but he did not agree. So Israel stayed at Kadesh.

**18** Then they went through the wilderness. They went around the land of Edom and the land of Moab. They came to the east side of the land of Moab. They made a camp on the other side of the Arnon River. They did not go into the land of Moab, because the Arnon River was the border of Moab.

**19** Then Israel sent messengers to Sihon, the king of the Amorites, who ruled in Heshbon. Israel said to him, "Please let us go through your land to our own place."

**20** But Sihon did not trust Israel to go through his land. Sihon gathered all his people and made a camp at Jahaz. He fought against Israel.

**21** The LORD, the God of Israel, gave Sihon and all his people into the control of Israel. Israel defeated them. So Israel took all the land of the Amorites, who lived in that country.

**22** They took all the land of the Amorites from the Arnon River to the Jabbok River. They took it from the wilderness to the Jordan River.

**23** The LORD, the God of Israel, forced out the Amorites in front of his people Israel. Thus you have no right to take the land.

**24** You keep the land that your god Chemosh gives you. So we will keep all the land that the LORD our God gives us.

**25** Are you better than Balak, the son of Zippor, the king of Moab? Did he ever argue with Israel? Did he ever fight against them?

**26** For 300 years Israel lived in Heshbon, Aroer, and their villages. Israel lived in all the cities along the Arnon River. Why did you not take them back during that time?

**27** I did not do wrong to you. But you do wrong to me when you attack me. Let the LORD, the Judge, decide today between the people of Israel and the Ammonites.'"

**30** Jephthah made a promise to the LORD. He said, "Give the Ammonites into my control.

**31** If you do this, then I will give you the first thing that comes out of the door of my house to meet me. I will give it when I come back safely from the Ammonites. It will be the LORD's. I will offer it as a burnt offering."

**32** Then Jephthah went to fight against the Ammonites. The LORD gave them into his control.

**34** Jephthah came home to Mizpeh. His daughter came out to meet him. She played a tambourine and danced. She was his only child. He had no other son or daughter.

**40** Each year the young women of Israel go out for 4 days. They remember the daughter of Jephthah from Gilead.

## Chapter 13

**1** Again the people of Israel did evil in the sight of the LORD. So the LORD gave them into the control of the Philistines for 40 years.

**2** There was a man from Zorah, from the clan of Dan. His name was Manoah. His wife could not have children.

**3** The angel of the LORD showed himself to the woman. He said to her, "You cannot have children. But you will become pregnant and give birth to a son.

**4** Thus be careful. Do not drink wine or strong drink. Do not eat any food that is unclean.

**5** You will become pregnant and give birth to a son. Do not cut his hair. The boy will be a Nazirite (a person set apart for God) from his birth. He will start to save Israel from the Philistines."

**6** Then the woman went to her husband. She said, "A man of God came to me. He looked like an angel of God and made me very afraid. I did not ask him where he came from. He did not tell me his name.

**7** But he said to me, 'You will become pregnant and give birth to a son. Do not drink wine or strong drink. Do not eat any food that is unclean. The boy will be a Nazirite for God from his birth to the day of his death.'"

**8** Then Manoah prayed to the LORD. He said, "Please, Lord, let the man of God that you sent come to us again. Let him teach us what to do for the boy that my wife will give birth to."

**9** God heard Manoah. The angel of God came again to the woman while she sat in the field. But Manoah, her husband, was not with her.

**10** The woman quickly ran to her husband. She said to him, "Look, the man who came to me the other day showed himself to me again!"

**11** Manoah got up and followed his wife. He came to the man and said, "Are you the man who spoke to my wife?" The man said, "I am."

**12** Then Manoah said, "When your words come true, what will be the rules for the life of the boy? What will his work be?"

**13** The angel of the LORD said to Manoah, "Your wife must do all that I told her.

**14** She must not eat anything that comes from the grapevine. She must not drink wine or strong drink. She must not eat any unclean food. She must obey all that I commanded her."

**15** Manoah said to the angel of the LORD, "Please stay here. We will cook a young goat for you."

**16** The angel of the LORD said to Manoah, "If I stay, I will not eat your food. But if you make a burnt offering, offer it to the LORD." Manoah did not know that he was the angel of the LORD.

**17** Then Manoah said to the angel of the LORD, "What is your name? When your words come true, we want to honor you."

**18** The angel of the LORD said to him, "Why do you ask my name? It is too wonderful for you to understand."

**19** So Manoah took a young goat and a grain offering. He offered them on a rock to the LORD. Then the angel did a wonderful thing while Manoah and his wife watched.

**20** The flame went up from the altar toward the sky. Then the angel of the LORD went up in the flame of the altar. Manoah and his wife saw this. They fell with their faces to the ground.

**21** The angel of the LORD did not show himself again to Manoah and his wife. Then Manoah knew that he was the angel of the LORD.

**22** Manoah said to his wife, "We will surely die, because we saw God."

**23** But his wife said to him, "The LORD does not want to kill us. He accepted our burnt offering and grain offering. He showed us all these things. He told us these things now."

**24** The woman gave birth to a son and called him Samson. The boy grew, and the LORD blessed him.

**25** The Spirit of the LORD started to move Samson at Mahaneh-dan, between Zorah and Eshtaol.

## Chapter 14

**1** Samson went down to Timnath. There he saw a young Philistine woman.

**2** He came back home and told his father and mother. He said, "I saw a young Philistine woman at Timnath. Get her for me as my wife."

**3** His father and mother said to him, "Is there no woman among your relatives or among all our people? Why must you take a wife from the Philistines, who do not have circumcision?" But Samson said to his father, "Get her for me. She pleases me."

**4** His father and mother did not know that this was from the LORD. The LORD looked for an opportunity to attack the Philistines. At that time the Philistines ruled over Israel.

**5** Samson went down to Timnath with his father and mother. When they came to the vineyards of Timnath, a young lion attacked Samson and roared at him.

**6** The Spirit of the LORD came strongly on Samson. He tore the lion apart with his hands, as easily as a man tears a young goat. He had no weapon in his hand. But he did not tell his father or mother what he did.

**7** Then Samson went down and spoke with the woman. She pleased him.

**8** Some time later he went back to marry her. He went off the road to look at the dead body of the lion. He saw a group of bees and honey in the body of the lion.

**9** He took some honey in his hands and ate it as he walked. He came to his father and mother and gave them some honey, and they ate it. But he did not tell them that he took the honey from the body of the lion.

**10** His father went down to see the woman. Samson gave a large meal there, because this was the custom of young men.

**11** When the people saw Samson, they brought 30 young men to be with him.

**12** Samson said to them, "Let me tell you a riddle. The celebration is 7 days long. If you find the answer during that time, I will give you 30 linen shirts and 30 sets of good clothes.

**13** But if you cannot tell me the answer, you will give me 30 linen shirts and 30 sets of good clothes." They said to him, "Tell us your riddle. We want to hear it."

**14** He said to them, "Out of the eater came something to eat. Out of the strong came something sweet." For 3 days they could not find the answer to the riddle.

**15** On the seventh day they said to the wife of Samson, "Trick your husband so that he tells us the answer to the riddle. If you do not, we will burn you and the family of your father. Did you invite us here to make us poor?"

**16** The wife of Samson cried in front of him. She said, "You hate me! You do not love me! You told my people a riddle, but you did not tell me the answer." He said to her, "I did not tell the answer to my father or my mother. Why must I tell it to you?"

**17** She cried in front of him for the rest of the 7 days of the celebration. On the seventh day he told her the answer, because she continued to ask him. Then she told the answer to her people.

**18** On the seventh day, before sunset, the men of the city said to Samson, "What is sweeter than honey? What is stronger than a lion?" Samson said to them, "You used my young cow to plow. That is how you found the answer to my riddle."

**19** Then the Spirit of the LORD came strongly on Samson. He went down to Ashkelon and killed 30 men there. He took their clothes and gave them to the men who told the answer to the riddle. He was very angry, and he went back to the house of his father.

**20** The father of the woman gave her to the friend who was the best man at the wedding of Samson.

## Chapter 15

**1** Some time later, at the time of the wheat harvest, Samson went to visit his wife. He took a young goat with him. He said, "I will go into the room of my wife." But her father did not let him go in.

**2** Her father said, "I was sure that you hated her. So I gave her to your friend. Her younger sister is more beautiful than she. Please take her younger sister instead."

**3** Samson said to them, "This time I will hurt the Philistines, and no one can say that I am wrong."

**4** So Samson went and caught 300 foxes. He tied the foxes in pairs by their tails. He put a torch between the tails of each pair.

**5** He lit the torches and let the foxes go into the grain fields of the Philistines. The fire burned the cut grain and the grain in the fields. It also burned the vineyards and the olive trees.

**6** The Philistines asked, "Who did this?" Someone said, "Samson did this, the son-in-law of the man from Timnath. The father of his wife gave her to his friend." So the Philistines went up and burned the woman and her father.

**7** Samson said to the Philistines, "Because you did this, I will punish you. After that, I will stop."

**8** He attacked them very violently and killed many of them. Then he went down and lived in a cave in the rock of Etam.

**9** Then the Philistines went up and made a camp in Judah. They spread out near Lehi.

**10** The men of Judah said, "Why do you attack us?" The Philistines answered, "We came to tie up Samson. We will do to him what he did to us."

**11** Then 3,000 men of Judah went to the cave in the rock of Etam. They said to Samson, "You know that the Philistines rule over us. Why did you do this to us?" He said to them, "I only did to them what they did to me."

**12** The men of Judah said to him, "We came to tie you up. We will give you to the Philistines." Samson said to them, "Promise me that you yourselves will not kill me."

**13** They said to him, "We promise. We will only tie you up and give you to the Philistines. We will not kill you." So they tied him with 2 new ropes and brought him up from the rock.

**14** When Samson came to Lehi, the Philistines shouted and ran to him. Then the Spirit of the LORD came strongly on him. The ropes on his arms became like burned flax. The ropes fell off his hands.

**15** He found a fresh jawbone of a donkey. He took it in his hand and killed 1,000 men with it.

**16** Then Samson said, "With the jawbone of a donkey, I made heaps of dead men. With the jawbone of a donkey, I killed 1,000 men."

**17** When he finished his words, he threw away the jawbone. He called that place Ramath-lehi.

**18** Then Samson was very thirsty. He called to the LORD and said, "You gave your servant this great victory. Must I now die of thirst? Must I fall into the control of these men, who do not have circumcision?"

**19** So God opened a hollow place at Lehi, and water came out of it. Samson drank, and his strength came back. Thus he called the spring En-hakkore. It is still in Lehi to this day.

**20** Samson judged Israel for 20 years during the time of the Philistines.

## Chapter 16

**1** One day Samson went to Gaza. There he saw a prostitute, and he went into her house to sleep with her.

**2** Someone told the people of Gaza, "Samson is here." So they went around the place. They waited for him all night at the city gate. They were quiet all night. They said, "At sunrise we will kill him."

**3** But Samson stayed in bed only until midnight. At midnight he got up. He took the doors of the city gate and the 2 posts. He pulled them up with the bar. He put them on his shoulders. He carried them to the top of the hill that is across from Hebron.

**4** After this, Samson loved a woman in the valley of Sorek. Her name was Delilah.

**5** The rulers of the Philistines came to her. They said to her, "Trick Samson. Find out what makes him so strong. Find out how we can defeat him and tie him up. Then we can make him weak. Each of us will give you 1,100 pieces of silver."

**6** So Delilah said to Samson, "Please tell me what makes you so strong. How can someone tie you up and make you weak?"

**7** Samson said to her, "Tie me with 7 new bowstrings that are not dry. Then I will become as weak as other men."

**8** The rulers of the Philistines brought her 7 new bowstrings that were not dry. She tied Samson with them.

**9** Men hid in the room and waited. She said to him, "Samson, the Philistines attack you!" But he broke the bowstrings as easily as fire breaks a piece of string. So no one found out the secret of his strength.

**10** Then Delilah said to Samson, "You made me look foolish. You told me lies. Now please tell me how someone can tie you up."

**11** He said to her, "Tie me tightly with new ropes that no one used before. Then I will become as weak as other men."

**12** So Delilah took new ropes and tied him with them. She said to him, "Samson, the Philistines attack you!" Men hid in the room and waited. But he broke the ropes off his arms like string.

**13** Then Delilah said to Samson, "Until now you made me look foolish. You told me lies. Tell me how someone can tie you up." He said to her, "Weave the 7 braids of my hair into the cloth on the loom."

**14** So she did this and made it tight with a peg. Then she said to him, "Samson, the Philistines attack you!" He woke up from his sleep. He pulled out the peg of the loom and the cloth.

**15** Then she said to him, "How can you say, 'I love you,' when you do not trust me? 3 times you made me look foolish. You did not tell me what makes you so strong."

**16** Each day she continued to ask him with her words. She caused him so much trouble that he wanted to die.

**17** Then he told her the full truth. He said to her, "No one ever cut my hair. I am a Nazirite for God from my birth. If someone cuts my hair, my strength will go away. I will become as weak as other men."

**18** Delilah saw that he told her the full truth. So she sent a message to the rulers of the Philistines. She said, "Come back 1 more time. He told me the full truth." So the rulers of the Philistines came to her with the silver in their hands.

**19** She made Samson go to sleep with his head on her knees. She called a man to cut off the 7 braids of his hair. Then she started to make Samson weak, and his strength went away.

**20** She said, "Samson, the Philistines attack you!" He woke up from his sleep. He said, "I will go out as at other times and get free." But he did not know that the LORD left him.

**21** Then the Philistines caught him and removed his eyes. They took him down to Gaza. They tied him with bronze chains. He had to grind grain in the prison.

**22** But after they cut his hair, his hair started to grow again.

**23** The rulers of the Philistines came together to offer a large sacrifice to Dagon, their god, and to have a celebration. They said, "Our god gave Samson, our enemy, into our control."

**24** When the people saw Samson, they praised their god. They said, "Our god gave our enemy into our control. This man destroyed our land and killed many of our people."

**25** The people were very happy. They said, "Bring Samson here. Let him make us laugh." So they brought Samson out of the prison, and he made them laugh. They made him stand between the pillars.

**26** Samson said to the young man who held his hand, "Let me touch the pillars that hold up the building. I want to put my weight on them."

**27** The building was full of men and women. All the rulers of the Philistines were there. On the roof there were approximately 3,000 men and women. They watched while Samson made them laugh.

**28** Then Samson called to the LORD. He said, "Lord GOD, please remember me. Please make me strong only 1 more time, O God. Let me punish the Philistines for my 2 eyes."

**29** Samson put his hands on the 2 center pillars that held up the building. He pushed against the pillars, with his right hand on one and his left hand on the other.

**30** Samson said, "Let me die with the Philistines!" He pushed with all his strength. The building fell on the rulers and on all the people in it. Thus Samson killed more people when he died than during his life.

**31** Then his brothers and all his family came down and took his body. They brought him back and buried him between Zorah and Eshtaol, in the tomb of his father Manoah. Samson judged Israel for 20 years.

## Chapter 17

**1** There was a man from the hill country of Ephraim. His name was Micah.

**2** He said to his mother, "Someone took 1,100 pieces of silver from you. I heard you curse the person who took them. Look, I have the silver. I took it." His mother said, "May the LORD bless you, my son."

**3** Micah gave the 1,100 pieces of silver back to his mother. His mother said, "I give this silver to the LORD for my son. He can use it to make a carved idol and a metal idol. So now I give it back to you."

**4** But Micah gave the silver back to his mother. She took 200 pieces of silver and gave them to a metalworker. The metalworker made a carved idol and a metal idol from the silver. The idols were in the house of Micah.

**5** Micah had a shrine. He made an ephod and household idols. He made one of his sons his priest.

**6** At that time Israel had no king. Each man did what he thought was correct.

**7** There was a young man from Beth-lehem in Judah, from the clan of Judah. He was a Levite, and he lived there for a time.

**8** This man left the city of Beth-lehem in Judah to find a different place to live. As he traveled, he came to the house of Micah in the hill country of Ephraim.

**9** Micah said to him, "Where do you come from?" The man said, "I am a Levite from Beth-lehem in Judah. I look for a place to live."

**10** Micah said to him, "Live with me. Be a father and a priest to me. Each year I will give you 10 pieces of silver, a set of clothes, and your food." So the Levite went into the house.

**11** The Levite agreed to live with Micah. The young man became like one of the sons of Micah.

**12** Micah made the Levite his priest. The young man lived in the house of Micah.

**13** Then Micah said, "Now I know that the LORD will be good to me, because a Levite is my priest."

## Chapter 18

**1** At that time Israel had no king. The tribe of Dan looked for land where they could live. Until then, they did not get their land among the tribes of Israel.

**2** So the people of Dan chose 5 brave men from their clans. They sent them from Zorah and Eshtaol to look carefully at the land. They said to them, "Go and look at the land." The men came to the hill country of Ephraim, to the house of Micah. They stayed there for the night.

**3** When they were near the house of Micah, they knew the voice of the young Levite. So they went to him and said, "Who brought you here? What do you do in this place? Why are you here?"

**4** He said to them, "Micah did some things for me. He pays me, and I am his priest."

**5** They said to him, "Please ask God for us. We want to know if our journey will be successful."

**6** The priest said to them, "Go in peace. The LORD watches over your journey."

**7** Then the 5 men left and came to Laish. They saw that the people there lived safely, like the Sidonians. The people were quiet and safe. They had everything they needed. They lived far from the Sidonians and had no agreement with other people.

**8** The 5 men went back to their brothers at Zorah and Eshtaol. Their brothers said to them, "What did you find?"

**9** They said, "Come, we must attack them! We saw the land, and it is very good. Do not wait! Go quickly and take control of the land.

**10** When you go, you will find people who feel safe. The land is large. God gives it to you. That place has everything on the earth that people need."

**11** So 600 men of the clan of Dan went from Zorah and Eshtaol. They had weapons for war.

**12** They went up and made a camp at Kirjath-jearim in Judah. Thus people call that place Mahaneh-dan to this day. It is to the west of Kirjath-jearim.

**13** From there they went to the hill country of Ephraim and came to the house of Micah.

**14** The 5 men who looked at the land of Laish spoke to their brothers. They said, "Do you know that there is an ephod, household idols, a carved idol, and a metal idol in these houses? Now decide what to do."

**15** So they went there to the house of the young Levite, at the house of Micah. They said hello to him.

**16** The 600 men of Dan, with their weapons for war, stood at the entrance of the gate.

**17** The 5 men who looked at the land went into the house. They took the carved idol, the ephod, the household idols, and the metal idol. The priest stood at the entrance of the gate with the 600 armed men.

**18** The men went into the house of Micah and took the carved idol, the ephod, the household idols, and the metal idol. The priest said to them, "What do you do?"

**19** They said to him, "Be quiet! Do not speak. Come with us. Be a father and a priest to us. Is it better to be a priest for the family of 1 man? Or is it better to be a priest for a tribe and a clan in Israel?"

**20** The priest was happy. He took the ephod, the household idols, and the carved idol. He went with the people.

**21** They turned and left. They put their children, their animals, and their property in front of them.

**22** When they were some distance from the house of Micah, the men who lived near Micah came together. They caught up with the people of Dan.

**23** They shouted at the people of Dan. The people of Dan turned around and said to Micah, "What is your problem? Why did you bring these men?"

**24** Micah said, "You took my gods that I made, and you took my priest. Then you went away. What do I have now? How can you ask me, 'What is your problem?'"

**25** The people of Dan said to him, "Do not argue with us. If you do, some angry men will attack you. Then you and your family will die."

**26** Then the people of Dan continued on their way. Micah saw that they were too strong for him. So he turned and went back home.

**27** The people of Dan took the things that Micah made and the priest of Micah. They came to Laish, to a quiet people who felt safe. They killed the people with the sword and burned the city.

**28** No one came to save the people of Laish. The city was far from Zidon, and the people had no agreement with other people. The city was in the valley near Beth-rehob. The people of Dan built the city again and lived in it.

**29** They called the city Dan, after the name of their ancestor Dan, a son of Israel. But before, the name of the city was Laish.

**30** The people of Dan put up the carved idol for themselves. Jonathan, the son of Gershom, the son of Manasseh, and his sons were priests for the tribe of Dan. They were priests until the enemies took the people of the land away as prisoners.

**31** The people of Dan kept the carved idol of Micah. They kept it during all the time that the house of God was at Shiloh.

## Chapter 19

**2** But his concubine was not loyal to him. She left him and went back to the house of her father in Beth-lehem in Judah. She stayed there for 4 months.

**3** Then her husband went to her to speak kindly to her and bring her back. He took his servant and 2 donkeys with him. She brought him into the house of her father. When her father saw the Levite, he was happy to meet him.

**4** The father of the young woman made the Levite stay. The Levite stayed with him for 3 days. They ate and drank and slept there.

**5** On the fourth day they got up early in the morning, and the Levite prepared to leave. But the father of the young woman said to his son-in-law, "Eat some food to make you strong. Then go."

**7** The man got up to leave, but his father-in-law asked him strongly to stay. So he stayed there again that night.

**8** On the fifth day he got up early in the morning to leave. The father of the young woman said, "Please eat some food to make you strong. Wait until the afternoon." So the 2 men ate together.

**9** Then the man got up to leave with his concubine and his servant. But his father-in-law, the father of the young woman, said to him, "Look, it is almost evening. Please stay tonight. The day is almost at its end. Stay here and be happy. Tomorrow you can get up early and go home."

**37** The men who hid quickly attacked Gibeah. They spread out and killed all the people of the city with the sword.

**38** The men of Israel and the men who hid agreed on a signal. The signal was this: the men who hid will make a large cloud of smoke rise from the city.

**39** Then the men of Israel turned back in the battle. The people of Benjamin started to kill the Israelites. They killed approximately 30 men. They said, "Surely we defeat them, as in the first battle."

**40** But then the column of smoke started to rise from the city. The people of Benjamin looked behind them. They saw the smoke of all the city as it went up into the sky.

**41** Then the men of Israel turned around, and the men of Benjamin were very afraid. They saw that disaster came on them.

**42** So the men of Benjamin ran away from the men of Israel toward the wilderness. But they could not escape the battle. The Israelites who came out of the city killed them there.

**43** The Israelites went around the men of Benjamin and chased them. They easily defeated them to the east of Gibeah.

**44** 18,000 men of Benjamin died. All of them were brave soldiers.

**45** The other men of Benjamin turned and ran toward the wilderness, to the rock of Rimmon. The Israelites killed 5,000 of them on the roads. They chased them to Gidom and killed 2,000 more men.

**46** On that day a total of 25,000 soldiers of Benjamin with swords died. All of them were brave soldiers.

**47** But 600 men turned and ran into the wilderness to the rock of Rimmon. They stayed at the rock of Rimmon for 4 months.

**48** The men of Israel went back to the land of Benjamin. They killed all the people in each city, and also the animals and all that they found. They burned all the cities that they came to.

## Chapter 21

**1** At Mizpeh the men of Israel made a promise. They said, "None of us will give his daughter as a wife to a man of Benjamin."

**2** The people went to Beth-el and sat there in front of God until evening. They cried loudly.

**3** They said, "O LORD, the God of Israel, why did this occur in Israel? Why does Israel lose 1 tribe today?"

**4** Early the next day the people built an altar there. They offered burnt offerings and peace offerings.

**5** Then the people of Israel said, "Which group from the tribes of Israel did not come to the assembly in front of the LORD?" They asked this because they made a strong promise. They promised to kill any person who did not come to the LORD at Mizpeh.

**6** The people of Israel felt sorry for their brothers, the people of Benjamin. They said, "Today Israel loses 1 tribe.

**7** How can we get wives for the men of Benjamin who are still alive? We promised in the name of the LORD not to give them our daughters as wives."

**8** Then they said, "Which tribe of Israel did not come to the LORD at Mizpeh?" They found that no one from Jabesh-gilead came to the camp for the assembly.

**9** When they counted the people, they saw that no one from Jabesh-gilead was there.

**10** So the people sent 12,000 of their bravest soldiers to Jabesh-gilead. They commanded them, "Go and kill the people of Jabesh-gilead with the sword. Also kill the women and the children.

**11** This is what you must do. Kill every male and every woman who had sexual relations with a man."

**12** Among the people of Jabesh-gilead they found 400 young women who were virgins. These young women never had sexual relations with a man. The soldiers brought them to the camp at Shiloh in the land of Canaan.

**13** Then all the people sent a message to the men of Benjamin at the rock of Rimmon. They offered peace to them.

**14** So the men of Benjamin came back at that time. The Israelites gave them the women of Jabesh-gilead who were still alive. But there were not enough women for all the men.

**15** The people felt sorry for Benjamin, because the LORD made a gap in the tribes of Israel.

**16** The elders of the people said, "The women of Benjamin are dead. How can we get wives for the men of Benjamin who are still alive?"

**17** They said, "The men of Benjamin who are still alive must have descendants. Then no tribe of Israel will disappear.

**18** But we cannot give them our daughters as wives. The people of Israel made this promise: 'A curse is on any man who gives a wife to Benjamin.'"

**19** Then they said, "Each year there is a festival of the LORD at Shiloh. Shiloh is to the north of Beth-el and to the south of Lebonah. It is to the east of the road that goes from Beth-el to Shechem."

**20** So they gave a command to the men of Benjamin. They said, "Go and hide in the vineyards.

**21** Watch. The young women of Shiloh will come out to dance. When they come, run out of the vineyards. Each of you, catch a wife from the young women of Shiloh. Then go back to the land of Benjamin.

**22** Possibly the fathers or brothers of the young women will come to us to complain. Then we will say to them, 'Please be kind to the men of Benjamin for us. We did not get wives for each of them in the war. You did not give your daughters to them. Thus you did not break your promise.'"

**23** The men of Benjamin did this. Each man caught 1 of the young women who danced and took her as his wife. They went back to their own land. They built their cities again and lived in them.

**24** Then the people of Israel left that place. Each man went back to his tribe and his clan. Each man went back to his own land.

**25** At that time Israel had no king. Each man did what he thought was correct.
