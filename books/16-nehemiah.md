# Nehemiah

## Chapter 1

**1** These are the words of Nehemiah the son of Hachaliah. In the month Chislev (Chisleu), in the 20th year, I was in the palace at Susa (Shushan).

**2** Hanani, one of my brothers, came with some men from Judah. I asked them about the Jews who escaped and came back from captivity. I also asked them about Jerusalem.

**3** They told me, "The people who came back from captivity live in the province. They have much trouble and shame. The wall of Jerusalem is broken down. Fire burned its gates."

**4** When I heard these words, I sat down and cried. For some days I was sad. I fasted and prayed to the God of heaven.

**5** I said, "LORD God of heaven, please hear me. You are the great and powerful God. You keep your agreement (covenant) and show your love to the people who love you and obey your commands.

**6** Please listen and watch. Hear the prayer of your servant. Day and night I pray to you for the people of Israel, your servants. I confess the sins that we, the people of Israel, did against you. I and the family of my father also sinned.

**7** We did very bad things against you. We did not obey the commands, rules and laws that you gave to your servant Moses.

**8** Please remember what you told your servant Moses. You said, 'If you are not loyal to me, I will send you to many nations.

**9** But if you come back to me and obey my commands, I will gather you. Some of you will be in the most distant places under the sky. But I will bring you back from there. I will bring you to the place that I chose for my name.'

**10** These people are your servants and your people. You saved them with your great power and your strong hand.

**11** Lord, please hear the prayer of your servant. Hear the prayers of your servants who want to respect your name. Please help your servant today. Make this man kind to me." At that time I was the cupbearer of the king.

## Chapter 2

**1** In the month Nisan, in the 20th year of King Artaxerxes, there was wine in front of the king. I took the wine and gave it to the king. Before that time I was never sad in front of him.

**2** Thus the king asked me, "Why is your face sad? You are not sick. Your heart must be sad." Then I was very afraid.

**3** I said to the king, "Let the king live for all time! My face is sad because the city of my fathers is a ruin. Their graves are in that city. Fire destroyed its gates."

**4** The king asked me, "What do you want?" Then I prayed to the God of heaven.

**5** I said to the king, "If it pleases the king, and if you like your servant, send me to Judah. Send me to the city where my fathers have their graves. Let me build the city again."

**6** The queen sat near the king. The king asked me, "How long will your journey be? When will you come back?" The king agreed to send me. I told him a time.

**7** I also said to the king, "If it pleases the king, give me letters to the governors west of the river. Then they will let me go through their land until I come to Judah.

**8** Also give me a letter to Asaph, who controls the forest of the king. Then he will give me wood to make beams for the gates of the fortress near the temple. I also need wood for the wall of the city and for the house where I will live." The good hand of my God was on me. Thus the king gave me these things.

**9** Then I came to the governors west of the river. I gave them the letters of the king. The king also sent army officers and horsemen with me.

**10** Sanballat the Horonite and Tobiah the Ammonite official heard about this. They were very angry that a man came to help the people of Israel.

**11** I came to Jerusalem and stayed there for 3 days.

**12** Then I got up in the night with a few men. I did not tell any person what my God told me to do for Jerusalem. There were no animals with me, only the animal that I rode.

**13** At night I went out through the Valley Gate. I went toward the Dragon Well and to the Waste Gate (Dung Gate). I examined the walls of Jerusalem. The walls were broken down, and fire destroyed the gates.

**14** Then I continued to the Fountain Gate and to the Pool of the King. But there was no space for my animal to go through.

**15** Thus I went up the valley in the night and examined the wall. Then I turned back and went in through the Valley Gate. Thus I came back.

**16** The officials did not know where I went or what I did. I did not yet tell the Jews, the priests, the nobles, the officials or the other workers.

**17** Then I told them, "You see our trouble. Jerusalem is a ruin, and fire burned its gates. Come, let us build the wall of Jerusalem again. Then we will not be ashamed."

**18** I told them that my God helped me. I also told them what the king said to me. They said, "Let us start to build." Thus they prepared for this good work.

**19** But Sanballat the Horonite, Tobiah the Ammonite official and Geshem the Arab heard about it. They laughed at us and insulted us. They asked, "What do you do? Do you fight against the king?"

**20** I answered them, "The God of heaven will give us success. We are his servants. We will start to build. But you have no share, no right and no history in Jerusalem."

## Chapter 3

**1** Then Eliashib the high priest and his brothers the priests started to work. They built the Sheep Gate. They made it holy and put up its doors. They built the wall to the Tower of the Hundred (Meah). They made it holy. They built the wall to the Tower of Hananeel.

**2** The men of Jericho built the next part. Zaccur the son of Imri built the part next to them.

**3** The sons of Hassenaah built the Fish Gate. They put in its beams and put up its doors, locks and bars.

**4** Meremoth the son of Urijah, the son of Koz, repaired the next part. Meshullam the son of Berechiah, the son of Meshezabeel, repaired the part next to them. Zadok the son of Baana repaired the part next to them.

**5** The men of Tekoa repaired the next part. But their nobles did not agree to work for their supervisors.

**6** Jehoiada the son of Paseah and Meshullam the son of Besodeiah repaired the Old Gate. They put in its beams and put up its doors, locks and bars.

**7** Melatiah from Gibeon and Jadon from Meronoth repaired the next part. They were men of Gibeon and Mizpah. These places were under the control of the governor west of the river.

**8** Uzziel the son of Harhaiah, one of the goldsmiths, repaired the next part. Hananiah, one of the perfume makers, repaired the part next to him. They repaired Jerusalem as far as the Broad Wall.

**9** Rephaiah the son of Hur repaired the next part. He was the ruler of half of the district of Jerusalem.

**10** Jedaiah the son of Harumaph repaired the next part, opposite his house. Hattush the son of Hashabniah repaired the part next to him.

**11** Malchijah the son of Harim and Hashub the son of Pahath-moab repaired another part. They also repaired the Tower of the Ovens.

**12** Shallum the son of Halohesh repaired the next part. He was the ruler of the other half of the district of Jerusalem. His daughters helped him.

**13** Hanun and the people of Zanoah repaired the Valley Gate. They built it and put up its doors, locks and bars. They also repaired 1,000 cubits of the wall, as far as the Dung Gate.

**14** Malchiah the son of Rechab repaired the Dung Gate. He was the ruler of the district of Beth-haccerem. He built it and put up its doors, locks and bars.

**15** Shallun the son of Col-hozeh repaired the Fountain Gate. He was the ruler of the district of Mizpah. He built the gate and put a roof on it. He put up its doors, locks and bars. He also repaired the wall of the Pool of Siloah (Siloam) near the garden of the king. He repaired the wall as far as the stairs that go down from the City of David.

**16** Nehemiah the son of Azbuk repaired the next part. He was the ruler of half of the district of Beth-zur. He repaired the wall to a point opposite the graves of David. He repaired it as far as the man-made pool and the House of the Soldiers.

**17** The Levites repaired the next part, with Rehum the son of Bani. Hashabiah repaired the part next to him for his district. Hashabiah was the ruler of half of the district of Keilah.

**18** Their brothers repaired the next part, with Bavai the son of Henadad. Bavai was the ruler of the other half of the district of Keilah.

**19** Ezer the son of Jeshua repaired the next part. Ezer was the ruler of Mizpah. He repaired another part opposite the path up to the armory, at the corner of the wall.

**20** Baruch the son of Zabbai worked hard to repair the next part. This part went from the corner to the door of the house of Eliashib the high priest.

**21** Meremoth the son of Urijah, the son of Koz, repaired the next part. This part went from the door of the house of Eliashib to the end of that house.

**22** The priests from the area around Jerusalem repaired the next part.

**23** Benjamin and Hashub repaired the next part, opposite their house. Azariah the son of Maaseiah, the son of Ananiah, repaired the part next to his house.

**24** Binnui the son of Henadad repaired the next part. This part went from the house of Azariah to the corner of the wall.

**25** Palal the son of Uzai repaired the part opposite the corner of the wall. He also repaired the tower that comes out from the upper palace of the king, near the courtyard of the guard. Pedaiah the son of Parosh repaired the next part.

**26** The Nethinims lived on the hill of Ophel. They repaired the wall to a point opposite the Water Gate on the east, and the tower that comes out.

**27** The men of Tekoa repaired another part. This part went from a point opposite the great tower that comes out, to the wall of Ophel.

**28** The priests repaired the wall above the Horse Gate. Each priest repaired the part opposite his own house.

**29** Zadok the son of Immer repaired the next part, opposite his house. Shemaiah the son of Shechaniah repaired the part next to him. Shemaiah was the guard of the East Gate.

**30** Hananiah the son of Shelemiah and Hanun, the sixth son of Zalaph, repaired another part. Meshullam the son of Berechiah repaired the next part, opposite his room.

**31** Malchiah, one of the goldsmiths, repaired the next part. He repaired the wall to the house of the Nethinims and the merchants. This part was opposite the Inspection Gate (Miphkad). He repaired the wall to the upper room at the corner.

**32** The goldsmiths and the merchants repaired the part between the upper room at the corner and the Sheep Gate.

## Chapter 4

**1** Sanballat heard that we built the wall. He became very angry. He laughed at the Jews.

**2** He spoke in front of his friends and the army of Samaria. He said, "What do these weak Jews do? Will they make their city strong again? Will they offer sacrifices? Will they complete the work in 1 day? Will they make new stones from the piles of burned rubbish?"

**3** Tobiah the Ammonite stood near him. He said, "Look at what they build! If a fox walks on their stone wall, the wall will fall down!"

**4** Hear us, our God, because people insult us. Let their insults come back on their own heads. Let enemies take them as captives to a foreign land.

**5** Do not forgive their guilt. Do not remove their sin from your records. They made you angry, because they insulted the builders.

**6** Thus we built the wall. We joined all the wall together to half its height. The people worked with all their hearts.

**7** Sanballat, Tobiah, the Arabs, the Ammonites and the people of Ashdod heard about the work on the walls of Jerusalem. They heard that we started to close the holes in the walls. Then they became very angry.

**8** All of them made a plan together. They planned to come and fight against Jerusalem and cause trouble there.

**9** But we prayed to our God. We put guards day and night to protect us from them.

**10** The people of Judah said, "The workers who carry loads are tired. There is too much rubbish. We cannot build the wall."

**11** Our enemies said, "The Jews will not know or see us until we come among them. Then we will kill them and stop the work."

**12** The Jews who lived near the enemies came to us. They told us 10 times, "From all directions, the enemies will attack you."

**13** Thus I put people behind the lowest parts of the wall, in the open places. I put them in groups by families. They had their swords, spears and bows.

**14** I looked at the situation. Then I stood up and spoke to the nobles, the officials and the other people. I said, "Do not be afraid of them. Remember the Lord. He is great and powerful. Fight for your brothers, your sons, your daughters, your wives and your houses."

**15** Our enemies heard that we knew about their plan. God caused their plan to fail. Then all of us went back to the wall. Each person went back to his work.

**16** From that time on, half of my men did the work. The other half held spears, shields, bows and armor. The officials stood behind all the people of Judah.

**17** The people who built the wall and the people who carried loads worked with 1 hand. They held a weapon with the other hand.

**18** Each builder had a sword on his side while he built. The man who blew the trumpet stayed near me.

**19** I said to the nobles, the officials and the other people, "The work is very large. We are far apart from each other on the wall.

**20** When you hear the sound of the trumpet, come to us at that place. Our God will fight for us."

**21** Thus we did the work. Half of the men held spears from sunrise until the stars came out.

**22** At that time I also said to the people, "Each man and his servant must stay in Jerusalem at night. Then they can guard us at night and work during the day."

**23** My brothers, my servants, the guards who followed me and I did not take off our clothes. Each person took off his clothes only to wash them.

## Chapter 5

**1** Then the men and their wives complained loudly against their Jewish brothers.

**2** Some people said, "We have many sons and daughters. We must get grain so that we can eat and stay alive."

**3** Other people said, "We give our fields, our vineyards and our houses as security for loans. We need grain because there is a famine."

**4** Other people said, "We borrowed money to pay the tax of the king on our fields and vineyards.

**5** We are the same as our Jewish brothers. Our children are as good as their children. But we must sell our sons and daughters as slaves. Some of our daughters are already slaves. We cannot buy them back, because other men own our fields and vineyards."

**6** When I heard their complaint and these words, I was very angry.

**7** I thought carefully about the problem. Then I spoke strongly to the nobles and the officials. I told them, "You charge interest to your own brothers!" I called a large meeting against them.

**8** I told them, "As much as we can, we buy back our Jewish brothers who were sold to foreign nations. But now you sell your own brothers! Then we must buy them back again!" They were quiet. They had no answer.

**9** I also said, "What you do is not good. You must obey our God and respect him. Then our foreign enemies will not insult us.

**10** My brothers, my servants and I also lend money and grain to the people. Let us stop this practice of charging interest.

**11** Today give back to them their fields, vineyards, olive gardens and houses. Also give back the interest that you charge them on the money, grain, new wine and oil."

**12** They said, "We will give these things back. We will not ask them for more. We will do as you say." Then I called the priests. I made the nobles and officials promise in front of the priests to do this.

**13** I also shook out the folds of my robe. I said, "If a man does not keep this promise, God will shake him out in this way. God will take away his house and his property. He will become empty." All the people said, "Amen," and praised the LORD. Then the people did as they promised.

**14** The king made me governor in the land of Judah from his 20th year until his 32nd year. During these 12 years, my brothers and I did not eat the food that the governor can get.

**15** The governors before me put heavy loads on the people. They took food and wine from the people, and 40 shekels of silver in addition. Even their servants ruled harshly over the people. But I did not do that, because I feared God.

**16** I also worked on this wall. We did not buy any land. All my servants came together there to do the work.

**17** 150 Jews and officials ate at my table. Other people from the nations around us also ate there.

**18** Each day my servants prepared 1 ox, 6 good sheep and some birds for me. Every 10 days they gave a large supply of all types of wine. But I did not ask for the food that the governor can get. The people already had a heavy load of work.

**19** My God, remember me and be good to me because of all that I did for these people.

## Chapter 6

**1** Sanballat, Tobiah, Geshem the Arab and our other enemies heard that I completed the wall. They heard that there were no more holes in it. But at that time I did not yet put the doors in the gates.

**2** Then Sanballat and Geshem sent me this message: "Come, let us meet in one of the villages on the plain of Ono." But they planned to hurt me.

**3** I sent messengers to them with this answer: "I do a great work. I cannot come down. Why must the work stop while I leave it to come to you?"

**4** They sent me the same message 4 times. Each time I gave them the same answer.

**5** Then, the fifth time, Sanballat sent his servant to me with the same message. The servant held an open letter in his hand.

**6** The letter said: "The nations talk about this, and Gashmu (Geshem) also says it: You and the Jews plan to revolt. That is why you build the wall. They say that you will become their king.

**7** They also say that you chose prophets to announce in Jerusalem, 'There is a king in Judah!' The king will hear this report. Thus come now, and let us talk about this together."

**8** I sent this answer to him: "None of these things that you say are true. You make them up in your own mind."

**9** All of them tried to make us afraid. They thought, "Their hands will become too weak for the work. Then they will not complete it." But I prayed, "God, make my hands strong."

**10** Then I went to the house of Shemaiah the son of Delaiah, the son of Mehetabeel. Shemaiah stayed in his house. He said, "Let us meet in the house of God, inside the temple. Let us close the doors of the temple. Men will come to kill you. They will come at night to kill you."

**11** But I said, "A man like me must not run away. A man like me must not go into the temple to save his life. I will not go in."

**12** I understood that God did not send Shemaiah. Tobiah and Sanballat paid him to give this false prophecy against me.

**13** They paid him to make me afraid. They wanted me to do this and to sin. Then they could give me a bad name and insult me.

**14** My God, remember what Tobiah and Sanballat did. Also remember the prophetess Noadiah and the other prophets who tried to make me afraid.

**15** We completed the wall on the 25th day of the month Elul. The work took 52 days.

**16** All our enemies heard about this. All the nations around us saw it. They lost their confidence. They knew that our God helped us to do this work.

**17** In those days the nobles of Judah sent many letters to Tobiah. Tobiah also sent letters to them.

**18** Many people in Judah made a promise to support Tobiah. He was the son-in-law of Shechaniah the son of Arah. His son Johanan married the daughter of Meshullam the son of Berechiah.

**19** The nobles also told me about the good things that Tobiah did. Then they told Tobiah what I said. Tobiah sent letters to make me afraid.

## Chapter 8

**1** All the people came together as one person in the square in front of the Water Gate. They told Ezra the scribe to bring the book of the law of Moses. The LORD gave this law to Israel.

**2** On the first day of the seventh month, Ezra the priest brought the law to the assembly. Men, women and all the people who could understand were there.

**3** He read the book from early morning until midday. He read it in the square in front of the Water Gate. The men, the women and all who could understand listened. All the people listened carefully to the book of the law.

**4** Ezra the scribe stood on a high wood platform. The people made the platform for this purpose. Mattithiah, Shema, Anaiah, Urijah, Hilkiah and Maaseiah stood at his right side. Pedaiah, Mishael, Malchiah, Hashum, Hashbadana, Zechariah and Meshullam stood at his left side.

**5** Ezra opened the book where all the people could see him. He stood higher than all the people. When he opened the book, all the people stood up.

**6** Ezra praised the LORD, the great God. All the people lifted up their hands and answered, "Amen! Amen!" Then they bent down and worshipped the LORD with their faces to the ground.

**7** The Levites helped the people to understand the law. These Levites were Jeshua, Bani, Sherebiah, Jamin, Akkub, Shabbethai, Hodijah, Maaseiah, Kelita, Azariah, Jozabad, Hanan and Pelaiah. The people stayed in their places.

**8** The Levites read the book of the law of God clearly. They explained the meaning. Thus the people understood what they read.

**9** Then Nehemiah the governor, Ezra the priest and scribe, and the Levites who taught the people spoke to all the people. They said, "This day is holy to the LORD your God. Do not be sad or cry." All the people cried when they heard the words of the law.

**10** Nehemiah said, "Go and eat good food and drink sweet drinks. Send some food to the people who have nothing. This day is holy to our Lord. Do not be sad. The joy of the LORD makes you strong."

**11** The Levites made all the people calm. They said, "Be quiet, because this day is holy. Do not be sad."

**12** Then all the people went away to eat and drink. They sent food to other people and celebrated with great joy. They understood the words that the Levites explained to them.

**13** On the second day, the family leaders of all the people came together with Ezra the scribe. The priests and the Levites came too. They wanted to study the words of the law.

**14** In the law they found what the LORD commanded through Moses. The people of Israel must live in shelters during the festival of the seventh month.

**15** The law also said that they must announce this message in all their cities and in Jerusalem: "Go out to the hills. Get branches from olive trees, wild olive trees, myrtle trees, palm trees and other leafy trees. Make shelters with them, as the law says."

**16** Thus the people went out and brought branches. They made shelters for themselves on the roofs of their houses and in their courtyards. They made shelters in the courtyards of the house of God. They also made shelters in the square at the Water Gate and in the square at the Gate of Ephraim.

**17** All the people who came back from captivity made shelters and lived in them. The Israelites did not do this from the time of Joshua (Jeshua) the son of Nun until that day. There was very great joy.

**18** Each day, from the first day to the last day, Ezra read from the book of the law of God. They kept the festival for 7 days. On the eighth day they had a special assembly, as the law told them.

## Chapter 9

**1** On the 24th day of this month, the people of Israel came together. They fasted and wore sackcloth. They put dust on their heads.

**2** The descendants of Israel separated themselves from all foreigners. They stood and confessed their sins and the sins of their fathers.

**3** They stood in their places. For 3 hours they read from the book of the law of the LORD their God. For another 3 hours they confessed their sins and worshipped the LORD their God.

**4** Some Levites stood on the stairs. These Levites were Jeshua, Bani, Kadmiel, Shebaniah, Bunni, Sherebiah, Bani and Chenani. They called loudly to the LORD their God.

**5** Then the Levites Jeshua, Kadmiel, Bani, Hashabniah, Sherebiah, Hodijah, Shebaniah and Pethahiah spoke. They said, "Stand up and praise the LORD your God for all time!" They prayed, "Let all people praise your glorious name. Your name is higher than all praise and honor.

**6** You alone are the LORD. You made the heavens, the highest heavens and all the stars. You made the earth and all that is on it. You made the seas and all that is in them. You keep all of them alive. The armies of heaven worship you.

## Chapter 13

**1** On that day the leaders read the book of Moses to the people. In the book they found this law: No Ammonite or Moabite can ever come into the assembly of God.

**2** The Ammonites and the Moabites did not give food and water to the Israelites. Instead, they paid Balaam to curse the Israelites. But our God changed the curse into a blessing.

**3** When the people heard this law, they separated all foreigners from Israel.

**4** Before this, Eliashib the priest was in control of the storerooms of the house of our God. He was a relative of Tobiah.

**5** Eliashib prepared a large room for Tobiah. Before that time, the priests kept the grain offerings, the frankincense and the containers in that room. They also kept the tenths of the grain, the new wine and the oil there. The law gave these things to the Levites, the singers and the gatekeepers. The room also held the offerings for the priests.

**6** During all this time I was not in Jerusalem. In the 32nd year of Artaxerxes king of Babylon, I went back to the king. After some time I asked the king for permission to leave.

**7** Then I came back to Jerusalem. I found out about the evil thing that Eliashib did for Tobiah. Eliashib gave Tobiah a room in the courtyards of the house of God.

**8** I was very angry. I threw all the furniture of Tobiah out of the room.

**9** Then I gave an order, and the people made the rooms clean. Then I brought back the containers of the house of God into the rooms. I also brought back the grain offerings and the frankincense.

**10** I also found out that the people did not give the Levites their shares. Thus the Levites and the singers who did the work went back to their own fields.

**11** Thus I spoke strongly to the officials. I asked them, "Why do you not take care of the house of God?" Then I gathered the Levites and the singers. I put them back at their posts.

**12** Then all the people of Judah brought the tenths of the grain, the new wine and the oil to the storerooms.

**13** I put men in control of the storerooms. I chose Shelemiah the priest, Zadok the scribe and Pedaiah, one of the Levites. Hanan the son of Zaccur, the son of Mattaniah, helped them. People knew that these men were honest. Their work was to give the shares to their brothers.

**14** My God, remember me for this. Do not forget the good things that I did for the house of my God and for its services.

**15** In those days I saw people in Judah who pressed grapes on the Sabbath. They brought in bundles of grain and put them on donkeys. They also brought wine, grapes, figs and all types of loads into Jerusalem on the Sabbath day. I warned them not to sell food on that day.

**17** Then I spoke strongly to the nobles of Judah. I said, "You do an evil thing. You make the Sabbath day unholy.

**18** Your fathers did the same things. Thus our God brought all this trouble on us and on this city. Now you make God more angry with Israel, because you make the Sabbath unholy."

**22** Then I told the Levites to make themselves clean. I told them to come and guard the gates to keep the Sabbath day holy. My God, remember me for this also. Show me kindness because of your great love.

**23** In those days I also saw Jews who married women from Ashdod, Ammon and Moab.

**24** Half of their children spoke the language of Ashdod or the language of another nation. They could not speak the language of the Jews.
