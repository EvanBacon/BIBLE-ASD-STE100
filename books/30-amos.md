# Amos

## Chapter 1

**1** These are the words of Amos. He was one of the shepherds from Tekoa. He saw these things about Israel during the rule of Uzziah, the king of Judah. It was also during the rule of Jeroboam, the son of Joash, the king of Israel. It was 2 years before the earthquake.

**2** Amos said, "The LORD roars from Zion. He shouts from Jerusalem. The fields of the shepherds become dry. The top of Mount Carmel becomes dry."

**3** The LORD says this: "The people of Damascus sinned again and again. Thus I will not stop their punishment. They crushed the people of Gilead with iron tools, as farmers crush grain.

**4** I will send a fire on the house of Hazael. The fire will destroy the strong buildings of Ben-hadad.

**5** I will break the gate of Damascus. I will destroy the people who live in the Valley of Aven. I will destroy the ruler of Beth-eden. The people of Aram will go as captives to Kir," the LORD says.

**6** The LORD says this: "The people of Gaza sinned again and again. Thus I will not stop their punishment. They took all the people of a region as captives. They sold the captives to Edom.

**7** I will send a fire on the walls of Gaza. The fire will destroy its strong buildings.

**8** I will destroy the people who live in Ashdod. I will destroy the ruler of Ashkelon. I will use my power against Ekron. The Philistines who remain will die," the Lord GOD says.

**9** The LORD says this: "The people of Tyre sinned again and again. Thus I will not stop their punishment. They sold all the people of a region as captives to Edom. They did not remember their agreement of friendship (covenant) with their brothers.

**10** I will send a fire on the walls of Tyre. The fire will destroy its strong buildings."

**11** The LORD says this: "The people of Edom sinned again and again. Thus I will not stop their punishment. Edom chased his brother with the sword. Edom showed no pity. His anger did not stop. He kept his anger for all time.

**12** I will send a fire on Teman. The fire will destroy the strong buildings of Bozrah."

**13** The LORD says this: "The people of Ammon sinned again and again. Thus I will not stop their punishment. They cut open the pregnant women of Gilead. They did this to make their own country larger.

**14** I will start a fire on the walls of Rabbah. The fire will destroy its strong buildings. There will be shouts on the day of battle. The attack will be like a storm on a day of strong wind.

**15** Their king will go into captivity. He and his leaders will go together," the LORD says.

## Chapter 2

**1** The LORD says this: "The people of Moab sinned again and again. Thus I will not stop their punishment. They burned the bones of the king of Edom until the bones became lime.

**2** I will send a fire on Moab. The fire will destroy the strong buildings of Kerioth. Moab will die in a loud battle. There will be shouts and the sound of the trumpet.

**3** I will destroy the ruler of Moab. I will kill all its leaders with him," the LORD says.

**4** The LORD says this: "The people of Judah sinned again and again. Thus I will not stop their punishment. They refused the law of the LORD. They did not obey his commands. Their ancestors followed false gods. Those same false gods caused the people of Judah to go the wrong way.

**5** I will send a fire on Judah. The fire will destroy the strong buildings of Jerusalem."

**6** The LORD says this: "The people of Israel sinned again and again. Thus I will not stop their punishment. They sell innocent people for silver. They sell poor people for a pair of sandals.

**7** They push the heads of poor people into the dust. They do not let humble people get justice. A man and his father have sex with the same young woman. Thus they make my holy name not holy.

**8** They lie down next to every altar on clothes that they took from the poor as payment for a debt. In the house of their god, they drink wine that they took from people as fines.

**9** But I destroyed the Amorites in front of my people. The Amorites were as tall as cedar trees. They were as strong as oak trees. But I destroyed them completely, from the fruit above to the roots below.

**10** I brought you up from the land of Egypt. I led you through the desert for 40 years. I gave you the land of the Amorites.

**11** I chose some of your sons to be prophets. I chose some of your young men to be Nazirites. People of Israel, this is true," the LORD says.

**12** "But you made the Nazirites drink wine. You commanded the prophets, 'Do not prophesy.'

**13** Thus I will crush you, as a cart that is full of grain crushes the ground.

**14** The fast runner will not escape. The strong man will not have strength. The soldier will not save his life.

**15** The man with the bow will not stand. The fast runner will not escape. The man on the horse will not save his life.

**16** On that day even the bravest soldiers will run away without their clothes," the LORD says.

## Chapter 3

**1** People of Israel, hear this word that the LORD speaks against you. He speaks against all the families that he brought up from the land of Egypt. He says,

**2** "Of all the families of the earth, I chose only you. Thus I will punish you for all your sins."

**3** Can 2 people walk together if they do not agree to meet?

**4** Does a lion roar in the forest if it has no animal to eat? Does a young lion growl in its cave if it caught nothing?

**5** Does a bird fall into a trap on the ground if there is no bait? Does a trap close if it caught nothing?

**6** If a trumpet sounds in a city, the people become afraid. If a disaster comes to a city, the LORD caused it.

**7** The Lord GOD does nothing until he tells his plan to his servants, the prophets.

**8** The lion roared. Who will not be afraid? The Lord GOD spoke. Who can stop himself from prophesying?

**9** Announce this to the strong buildings of Ashdod and the strong buildings of the land of Egypt. Say, "Gather on the mountains around Samaria. Look at the great confusion in Samaria. Look at how the people there hurt each other."

**10** "The people do not know how to do right," the LORD says. "They store violence and stolen things in their strong buildings."

**11** Thus the Lord GOD says this: "An enemy will surround the land. The enemy will destroy your defenses. The enemy will take everything from your strong buildings."

**12** The LORD says this: "A shepherd tries to save his sheep from a lion. But he saves only 2 leg bones or a piece of an ear. In the same way, only a small part of the people of Israel will be saved. Those people live in Samaria and sit on the corners of beds and on the cushions of couches."

**13** "Hear this and warn the family of Jacob," the Lord GOD, the God of armies, says.

**14** "On the day when I punish Israel for its sins, I will also destroy the altars of Bethel. The horns of the altar will be cut off and fall to the ground.

**15** I will destroy the winter houses and the summer houses. The houses decorated with ivory will be destroyed. The large houses will be destroyed," the LORD says.

## Chapter 4

**1** Hear this word, you women of Samaria. You are like the fat cows of Bashan on the mountain of Samaria. You hurt the poor people. You crush the needy people. You say to your husbands, "Bring us drinks."

**2** The Lord GOD made a promise by his holiness. He said, "The time will come when the enemy will take you away with hooks. The enemy will take the last of you with fishhooks.

**3** You will go out through the holes in the walls, each woman straight ahead. The enemy will throw you out toward Harmon," the LORD says.

**4** "Go to Bethel and sin. Go to Gilgal and sin more. Bring your sacrifices every morning. Bring your tithes every 3 days.

**5** Offer bread with yeast as a sacrifice of thanks. Announce your free offerings loudly, so that all people know. You love to do this, people of Israel," the Lord GOD says.

**6** "I caused hunger in all your cities. You had no bread in all your towns. But you did not come back to me," the LORD says.

**7** "I also stopped the rain when the harvest was still 3 months away. I sent rain on 1 city, but I did not send rain on another city. Rain fell on 1 field. Another field did not get rain, and it became dry.

**8** The people of 2 or 3 cities went to 1 city to get water to drink. But they did not get sufficient water. But you did not come back to me," the LORD says.

**9** "I destroyed your crops with hot winds and with disease. The locusts ate your many gardens, vineyards, fig trees, and olive trees. But you did not come back to me," the LORD says.

**10** "I sent diseases among you, as I did in Egypt. I killed your young men with the sword. I let the enemy take away your horses. I filled your noses with the smell of the dead bodies in your camps. But you did not come back to me," the LORD says.

**11** "I destroyed some of you, as I destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah. You were like a burning stick that someone pulled out of a fire. But you did not come back to me," the LORD says.

**12** "Thus I will punish you, Israel. Because I will do this to you, prepare to meet your God, Israel."

**13** God made the mountains. He makes the wind. He tells people his thoughts. He changes the dawn into darkness. He walks on the high places of the earth. His name is the LORD, the God of armies.

## Chapter 5

**1** People of Israel, hear this word. I sing this sad song about you:

**2** "The young woman Israel fell. She will not get up again. She lies alone on her land. Nobody lifts her up."

**3** The Lord GOD says this: "A city in Israel sends out 1000 soldiers, but only 100 will come back. A town sends out 100 soldiers, but only 10 will come back."

**4** The LORD says this to the people of Israel: "Come to me, and you will live.

**5** Do not go to Bethel to worship. Do not go into Gilgal. Do not go to Beersheba. The people of Gilgal will surely go into captivity. Bethel will become nothing."

**6** Come to the LORD, and you will live. If you do not come to him, he will attack the people of Joseph like a fire. The fire will destroy Bethel, and nobody will stop the fire.

**7** You change justice into something bitter. You throw righteousness to the ground.

**8** God made the stars of the Pleiades and Orion. He changes the deep darkness into morning. He makes the day dark like night. He calls the water from the sea. He pours the water on the land. His name is the LORD.

**9** He quickly destroys the strong people. He destroys their strong cities.

**10** You hate the person who tells the truth in the court. You hate the person who speaks honestly.

**11** You hurt the poor people. You take their grain as taxes. Thus you will not live in the stone houses that you built. You will not drink the wine from the good vineyards that you planted.

**12** I know your many crimes and your large sins. You attack innocent people. You take bribes. You refuse justice to the poor people in the court.

**13** Thus wise people stay silent at that time, because it is an evil time.

**14** Look for good, not evil, so that you will live. Then the LORD, the God of armies, will be with you, as you say.

**15** Hate evil and love good. Make sure that there is justice in the court. Maybe the LORD God of armies will show kindness to the people of Joseph who remain.

**16** Thus the LORD, the God of armies, the Lord, says this: "People will cry in all the public places. They will say in all the streets, 'We are sad! We are sad!' They will call the farmers to cry. They will call the professional mourners to cry loudly.

**17** People will cry in all the vineyards, because I will come through your land to punish you," the LORD says.

**18** You want the day of the LORD to come, but you will have much trouble. Why do you want the day of the LORD? That day will be darkness, not light.

**19** It will be like a man who runs away from a lion, but a bear meets him. It will be like a man who goes into his house and puts his hand on the wall, but a snake bites him.

**20** The day of the LORD will be darkness, not light. It will be very dark, with no light.

**21** "I hate your festivals. I refuse them. I do not like your special assemblies.

**22** You offer me burnt offerings and grain offerings, but I will not accept them. You offer peace offerings of fat animals, but I will not look at them.

**23** Take away the noise of your songs. I will not listen to the music of your harps.

**24** But let justice flow like a river. Let righteousness flow like a stream that never becomes dry.

**25** People of Israel, did you bring me sacrifices and offerings during the 40 years in the desert?

**26** You carried the shrine of your king Moloch. You carried Chiun, your idols. You carried the star of your god. You made these things for yourselves.

**27** Thus I will send you into captivity, to a place farther than Damascus," the LORD says. His name is the God of armies.

## Chapter 6

**1** You will have much trouble, you people who feel safe in Zion. You will have much trouble, you people who trust in the mountain of Samaria. You are the important leaders of the first nation. The people of Israel come to you for help.

**2** Go to Calneh and look. Go from there to the great city of Hamath. Then go down to Gath of the Philistines. Are you better than those kingdoms? Is their land larger than your land?

**3** You refuse to think about the day of disaster. But you bring near a time of violence.

**4** You lie on beds decorated with ivory. You relax on your couches. You eat the best lambs from the flock. You eat the best calves from the barn.

**5** You sing songs with the music of harps. Like David, you make musical instruments for yourselves.

**6** You drink wine from large bowls. You put the best oils on your bodies. But you are not sad about the destruction of Joseph.

**7** Thus you will be the first people to go into captivity. Your parties, where you relax, will stop.

**8** The Lord GOD made a promise by himself. The LORD, the God of armies, says this: "I hate the pride of Jacob. I hate his strong buildings. I will give the city and all its people to the enemy."

**9** If 10 men remain in 1 house, they will all die.

**10** A relative, who must burn the dead bodies, will come to take the bodies out of the house. He will call to a person who hides in the house, "Is there another person with you?" The person will say, "No." Then the relative will say, "Be quiet. We must not say the name of the LORD."

**11** The LORD gives the command. He will break the large houses into pieces. He will break the small houses into small pieces.

**12** Horses do not run on rocks. People do not plow the sea with oxen. But you changed justice into poison. You changed the fruit of righteousness into a bitter plant.

**13** You are happy about Lo-debar, which means "Nothing." You say, "We took Karnaim with our own strength."

**14** "People of Israel, I will send a nation against you," the LORD, the God of armies, says. "That nation will hurt you from Lebo-hamath to the stream of the Arabah."

## Chapter 7

**1** The Lord GOD showed me a vision. He made a swarm of locusts. This occurred when the second crop started to grow. That crop came after the king took his share of the harvest.

**2** The locusts ate all the plants in the land. Then I said, "Lord GOD, please forgive your people. How can Jacob survive? He is very small."

**3** So the LORD changed his mind. "This will not occur," the LORD said.

**4** The Lord GOD showed me another vision. The Lord GOD called for a fire to punish the people. The fire dried up the deep water and started to destroy the land.

**5** Then I said, "Lord GOD, please stop. How can Jacob survive? He is very small."

**6** So the LORD changed his mind. "This also will not occur," the Lord GOD said.

**7** The LORD showed me another vision. The Lord stood next to a wall that a builder made straight with a plumb line. The Lord held a plumb line in his hand.

**8** The LORD asked me, "Amos, what do you see?" I said, "A plumb line." Then the Lord said, "I will use a plumb line to examine my people Israel. I will not forgive them again.

**9** The high places of Isaac will be destroyed. The holy places of Israel will become ruins. I will attack the family of Jeroboam with the sword."

**10** Then Amaziah, the priest of Bethel, sent a message to Jeroboam, the king of Israel. He said, "Amos makes a plan against you among the people of Israel. The land cannot accept all his words.

**11** Amos says, 'Jeroboam will die by the sword. The people of Israel will surely go as captives away from their land.'"

**12** Then Amaziah said to Amos, "You prophet, go away. Go back to the land of Judah. Earn your living there. Prophesy there.

**13** But do not prophesy at Bethel again. Bethel is the holy place of the king. It is the temple of the kingdom."

**14** Amos answered Amaziah, "I was not a prophet. I was not the son of a prophet. I was a shepherd. I also took care of fig trees.

**15** But the LORD took me away from my work with the flock. The LORD said to me, 'Go and prophesy to my people Israel.'

**16** Now hear the word of the LORD. You say, 'Do not prophesy against Israel. Do not preach against the family of Isaac.'

**17** Thus the LORD says this: 'Your wife will become a prostitute in the city. Your sons and your daughters will die by the sword. Other people will measure and divide your land. You will die in a foreign land that is not clean. The people of Israel will surely go as captives away from their land.'"

## Chapter 8

**1** The Lord GOD showed me another vision. I saw a basket of ripe summer fruit.

**2** He asked, "Amos, what do you see?" I said, "A basket of ripe summer fruit." Then the LORD said to me, "The end comes for my people Israel. I will not forgive them again.

**3** On that day the songs in the temple will change into loud crying," the Lord GOD says. "There will be many dead bodies in all places. People will throw the bodies out in silence."

**4** Hear this, you people who walk on the needy. You try to destroy the poor people of the land.

**5** You say, "When will the new moon festival end? Then we can sell grain. When will the Sabbath end? Then we can sell wheat. We will make the measure smaller. We will make the price larger. We will cheat with false scales.

**6** We will buy poor people with silver. We will buy needy people for a pair of sandals. We will even sell the bad parts of the wheat."

**7** The LORD made a promise by the pride of Jacob. He said, "I will never forget anything that they did.

**8** Because of these sins, the land will shake. All the people who live in the land will be sad. The whole land will rise like the Nile River. It will move up and down, and then go down again like the river of Egypt.

**9** On that day," the Lord GOD says, "I will make the sun go down at noon. I will make the earth dark during the day.

**10** I will change your festivals into times of sadness. I will change all your songs into sad songs. I will make all of you wear sackcloth. I will make you shave your heads. It will be like the sadness for an only son who died. The end of that day will be very bitter.

**11** The days will come," the Lord GOD says, "when I will send a famine on the land. It will not be a famine of bread. It will not be a thirst for water. It will be a famine of hearing the words of the LORD.

**12** People will wander from sea to sea. They will go from the north to the east. They will run here and there to look for the word of the LORD. But they will not find it.

**13** On that day the beautiful young women and the young men will become weak from thirst.

**14** Some people make promises by the shameful idol of Samaria. They say, 'Dan, we promise by your living god.' They say, 'We promise by the living god of Beersheba.' Those people will fall and never get up again."

## Chapter 9

**1** I saw the Lord. He stood next to the altar. He said, "Hit the tops of the columns, so that the foundations shake. Make the building fall on the heads of all the people. I will kill with the sword the people who remain. Not one of them will run away. Not one of them will escape.

**2** If they dig down into the grave, I will take them out from there. If they climb up into the sky, I will bring them down from there.

**3** If they hide on the top of Mount Carmel, I will find them and take them from there. If they hide from me at the bottom of the sea, I will command the sea snake to bite them there.

**4** If their enemies take them into captivity, I will command the sword to kill them there. I will watch them to cause them harm, not to help them."

**5** The Lord GOD of armies touches the land, and the land melts. All the people who live in the land are sad. The whole land rises like the Nile River. Then it goes down again like the river of Egypt.

**6** He builds his rooms in the sky. He puts the base of the sky on the earth. He calls the water from the sea. He pours the water on the land. His name is the LORD.

**7** "People of Israel, to me you are the same as the people of Cush," the LORD says. "I brought Israel up from the land of Egypt. I also brought the Philistines from Caphtor. I also brought the people of Aram from Kir.

**8** I, the Lord GOD, watch the sinful kingdom. I will destroy it from the earth. But I will not destroy all the family of Jacob," the LORD says.

**9** "I will give the command. I will shake the people of Israel among all the nations, as a person shakes grain in a sieve. But not one small stone will fall to the ground.

**10** All the sinners among my people will die by the sword. They say, 'Disaster will not come near us. Disaster will not catch us.'

**11** On that day I will build again the fallen house of David. I will repair the holes in its walls. I will build again its ruins. I will make it as it was in the past.

**12** Then my people will take what remains of Edom. They will take all the nations that belong to my name," the LORD says. The LORD will do this.

**13** "The days will come," the LORD says, "when the harvest will be very large. The plowman will plow before the harvester finishes the harvest. The person who crushes grapes will work before the farmer finishes the planting. Sweet wine will flow from the mountains. Wine will flow from all the hills.

**14** I will bring my people Israel back from captivity. They will build again the destroyed cities and live in them. They will plant vineyards and drink their wine. They will make gardens and eat their fruit.

**15** I will plant my people in their land. Nobody will pull them out of the land that I gave them again," the LORD your God says.
