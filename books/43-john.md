# John

## Chapter 1

**1** At the start, the Word was there. The Word was with God, and the Word was God.

**2** At the start, the Word was with God.

**3** God made all things through the Word. Nothing that exists came into existence without the Word.

**4** Life was in the Word. That life was the light for all people.

**5** The light shines in the darkness. The darkness did not defeat the light.

**6** God sent a man with the name John.

**7** John came as a witness. He came to tell people about the light. He came so that all people will believe through him.

**8** John was not the light. He came to tell people about the light.

**9** The true light, which gives light to all people, was about to come into the world.

**10** The Word was in the world. God made the world through him. But the world did not know him.

**11** He came to his own people, but his own people did not accept him.

**12** But some people accepted him and believed in his name. He gave these people the right to become children of God.

**13** They did not become children of God by a human birth. A human father did not decide this. God made them his children.

**14** The Word became a human and lived among us. We saw his glory. It was the glory of the only Son of the Father. He was full of grace and truth.

**15** John told people about him. John shouted, "This is the man about whom I spoke. I said, 'The man who comes after me is more important than I. He existed before me.'"

**16** We all received from his fullness. He gave us grace after grace.

**17** God gave the law through Moses. Grace and truth came through Jesus Christ.

**18** No person ever saw God. But the only Son, who is very near to the Father, showed us who God is.

**19** The Jewish leaders in Jerusalem sent priests and Levites to John. They asked him, "Who are you?" This is what John told them.

**20** John did not refuse to answer. He said clearly, "I am not the Christ."

**21** They asked him, "Then who are you? Are you Elijah?" John said, "I am not." They asked, "Are you the Prophet?" He answered, "No."

**22** Then they said to him, "Who are you? We must give an answer to the people who sent us. What do you say about yourself?"

**23** John said, "I am the voice of a person who shouts in the wilderness: 'Make a straight road for the Lord.' The prophet Isaiah said this."

**24** The Pharisees sent these men.

**25** They asked John, "You are not the Christ, Elijah, or the Prophet. Then why do you baptize people?"

**26** John answered them, "I baptize with water. But a man stands among you whom you do not know.

**27** He comes after me, but he is more important than I. I am not good enough to untie his sandals."

**28** These things occurred in Bethabara on the other side of the Jordan River. John baptized people there.

**29** On the next day, John saw Jesus come to him. John said, "Look, here is the Lamb of God! He takes away the sin of the world.

**30** This is the man about whom I spoke. I said, 'A man who comes after me is more important than I. He existed before me.'

**31** I did not know him. But I came to baptize with water so that the people of Israel will know him."

**32** Then John said, "I saw the Spirit come down from heaven like a dove. The Spirit stayed on him.

**33** I did not know him. But God sent me to baptize with water. God told me, 'You will see the Spirit come down and stay on a man. That man will baptize with the Holy Spirit.'

**34** I saw this. I tell you that this man is the Son of God."

**35** On the next day, John was there again with 2 of his disciples.

**36** John saw Jesus walk past. John said, "Look, here is the Lamb of God!"

**37** The 2 disciples heard John say this, and they followed Jesus.

**38** Jesus turned and saw them follow him. He said to them, "What do you want?" They said to him, "Rabbi, where do you stay?" ("Rabbi" means "Teacher.")

**39** Jesus said to them, "Come and see." So they went and saw where he stayed. They stayed with him that day. It was about 4 o'clock in the afternoon.

**40** One of the 2 disciples who heard John and followed Jesus was Andrew. Andrew was the brother of Simon Peter.

**41** First, Andrew found his brother Simon. He said to Simon, "We found the Messiah." ("Messiah" means "Christ.")

**42** Andrew brought Simon to Jesus. Jesus looked at him and said, "You are Simon, the son of John. You will have the name Cephas." ("Cephas" means "a rock.")

**43** On the next day, Jesus decided to go to Galilee. He found Philip and said to him, "Follow me."

**44** Philip was from the town of Bethsaida. Andrew and Peter also came from that town.

**45** Philip found Nathanael and said to him, "Moses wrote about a man in the law. The prophets also wrote about him. We found this man. He is Jesus of Nazareth, the son of Joseph."

**46** Nathanael said to Philip, "Can anything good come from Nazareth?" Philip said to him, "Come and see."

**47** Jesus saw Nathanael come to him. Jesus said about him, "Look, here is a true Israelite. He does not lie."

**48** Nathanael said to Jesus, "How do you know me?" Jesus answered, "I saw you under the fig tree before Philip called you."

**49** Nathanael answered, "Rabbi, you are the Son of God! You are the King of Israel!"

**50** Jesus said to him, "Do you believe because I said that I saw you under the fig tree? You will see greater things than this."

**51** Then Jesus said to him, "I tell you the truth: You will see heaven open. You will see the angels of God go up and come down on the Son of Man."

## Chapter 2

**1** On the third day, there was a wedding in Cana in Galilee. The mother of Jesus was there.

**2** People also invited Jesus and his disciples to the wedding.

**3** When all the wine was gone, the mother of Jesus said to him, "They have no more wine."

**4** Jesus said to her, "Woman, why do you tell me this? My time did not come yet."

**5** His mother said to the servants, "Do what he tells you."

**6** There were 6 stone water jars there. The Jews used these jars for their washing ceremonies. Each jar held 80 to 120 liters.

**7** Jesus said to the servants, "Fill the jars with water." So they filled the jars to the top.

**8** Then he said to them, "Now take out some water and give it to the man who controls the feast." So they did.

**9** The man who controlled the feast tasted the water. The water became wine. He did not know where the wine came from. But the servants who took out the water knew. Then the man called the bridegroom.

**10** He said to the bridegroom, "All people serve the good wine first. When the guests drink much, then they serve the cheaper wine. But you kept the good wine until now!"

**11** This was the first of the miraculous signs of Jesus. He did it in Cana in Galilee. Thus he showed his glory, and his disciples believed in him.

**12** After this, Jesus went down to Capernaum with his mother, his brothers, and his disciples. They stayed there for a few days.

**13** It was almost time for the Jewish Passover. So Jesus went up to Jerusalem.

**14** In the temple, he found people who sold cattle, sheep, and doves. He also found men who sat at tables and exchanged money.

**15** Jesus made a whip from ropes. He pushed all the people and the sheep and cattle out of the temple. He threw the coins of the money changers on the ground and turned over their tables.

**16** He said to the people who sold doves, "Take these things out of here! Do not make the house of my Father into a market!"

**17** His disciples remembered that the scriptures say, "My strong love for your house will burn in me like a fire."

**18** The Jewish leaders said to Jesus, "You do these things. What miraculous sign can you show us to prove your authority?"

**19** Jesus answered them, "Destroy this temple, and in 3 days I will build it again."

**20** The Jewish leaders said, "People worked for 46 years to build this temple. Will you build it again in 3 days?"

**21** But the temple about which Jesus spoke was his body.

**22** Later Jesus came back to life from the dead. Then his disciples remembered that he said this. They believed the scriptures and the words that Jesus said.

**23** Jesus was in Jerusalem during the Passover feast. Many people saw the miraculous signs that he did, and they believed in his name.

**24** But Jesus did not trust them, because he knew all people.

**25** He did not need a person to tell him about people. He knew what was in their hearts.

## Chapter 3

**1** There was a Pharisee with the name Nicodemus. He was a leader of the Jews.

**2** Nicodemus came to Jesus at night. He said to him, "Rabbi, we know that you are a teacher who came from God. No person can do the miraculous signs that you do if God is not with him."

**3** Jesus answered him, "I tell you the truth: If a person is not born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God."

**4** Nicodemus said to him, "How can a man be born when he is old? He cannot go into the body of his mother a second time and be born!"

**5** Jesus answered, "I tell you the truth: If a person is not born of water and the Spirit, he cannot go into the kingdom of God.

**6** Human parents give birth to a human body. But the Spirit gives birth to a spirit.

**7** Do not be surprised that I tell you, 'You must be born again.'

**8** The wind blows where it wants to blow. You hear its sound, but you do not know where it comes from or where it goes. All people who are born of the Spirit are like that."

**9** Nicodemus said to him, "How can these things be possible?"

**10** Jesus answered him, "You are a teacher of Israel. Do you not understand these things?

**11** I tell you the truth: We speak about what we know. We tell people about what we saw. But you people do not accept what we tell you.

**12** I told you about things on earth, and you do not believe. Then you will not believe if I tell you about things in heaven.

**13** No person ever went up to heaven except the Son of Man. He came down from heaven.

**14** Moses lifted up the bronze snake on a pole in the wilderness. In the same way, people must lift up the Son of Man.

**15** Then all people who believe in him will have eternal life.

**16** God loved the world so much that he gave his only Son. All people who believe in him will not die, but they will have eternal life.

**17** God did not send his Son into the world to judge the world as guilty. God sent him to save the world.

**18** God does not judge as guilty the person who believes in the Son. But the person who does not believe is already guilty. He is guilty because he does not believe in the name of the only Son of God.

**19** This is the reason for the judgment: The light came into the world. But people loved the darkness more than the light, because their actions were evil.

**20** All people who do evil hate the light. They do not come to the light, because they do not want people to see their evil actions.

**21** But the person who obeys the truth comes to the light. Then all people will see that he does his actions through God."

**22** After this, Jesus and his disciples went into the land of Judea. He stayed there with them and baptized people.

**23** John also baptized people at Aenon near Salim, because there was much water there. People came to John, and he baptized them.

**24** This was before people put John in prison.

**25** Some disciples of John argued with a Jew about the ceremonies for washing.

**26** They came to John and said to him, "Rabbi, you told people about a man who was with you on the other side of the Jordan River. Look, now he baptizes people, and all people go to him."

**27** John answered, "A person can get only what God gives him from heaven.

**28** You heard me say, 'I am not the Christ. God sent me ahead of him.'

**29** The bride belongs to the bridegroom. The friend of the bridegroom stands near and listens to him. The friend is very happy when he hears the voice of the bridegroom. In the same way, my joy is now complete.

**30** He must become more important, and I must become less important."

**31** The man who comes from above is more important than all people. A person who comes from the earth belongs to the earth, and he speaks about things on the earth. The man who comes from heaven is more important than all people.

**32** He tells about what he saw and heard. But people do not accept what he tells them.

**33** The person who accepts what he tells shows that God is true.

**34** God sent him, and he speaks the words of God. God gives him the Spirit with no limit.

**35** The Father loves the Son. He put all things under the control of the Son.

**36** The person who believes in the Son has eternal life. The person who does not obey the Son will not see life. The anger of God stays on that person.

## Chapter 4

**1** The Pharisees heard that Jesus made and baptized more disciples than John. The Lord knew that the Pharisees heard this.

**2** But Jesus himself did not baptize people. His disciples baptized them.

**3** So Jesus left Judea and went back to Galilee.

**4** He had to go through Samaria.

**5** He came to a town in Samaria with the name Sychar. This town was near the field that Jacob gave to his son Joseph.

**6** The well of Jacob was there. Jesus was tired from his journey, so he sat down next to the well. It was about 12 noon.

**7** A woman of Samaria came to get water. Jesus said to her, "Give me some water to drink."

**8** His disciples went into the town to buy food.

**9** The woman of Samaria said to him, "You are a Jew, and I am a woman of Samaria. Why do you ask me for a drink?" She said this because the Jews do not associate with the Samaritans.

**10** Jesus answered her, "You do not know the gift of God. You do not know who asks you for a drink. If you knew, you would ask him. Then he would give you living water."

**11** The woman said to him, "Sir, you have no bucket, and the well is deep. Where can you get that living water?

**12** Our father Jacob gave us this well. He drank from it, and his sons and his animals also drank from it. Are you more important than Jacob?"

**13** Jesus answered her, "All people who drink this water will be thirsty again.

**14** But the people who drink the water that I give will never be thirsty again. The water that I give will become a spring of water in them. That spring will give them eternal life."

**15** The woman said to him, "Sir, give me this water. Then I will not be thirsty, and I will not have to come here to get water."

**16** Jesus said to her, "Go, call your husband, and come back here."

**17** The woman answered, "I have no husband." Jesus said to her, "You speak correctly when you say that you have no husband.

**18** You had 5 husbands. The man with whom you live now is not your husband. What you said is true."

**19** The woman said to him, "Sir, I see that you are a prophet.

**20** Our ancestors worshiped on this mountain. But you Jews say that people must worship in Jerusalem."

**21** Jesus said to her, "Woman, believe me. A time will come when you will not worship the Father on this mountain or in Jerusalem.

**22** You Samaritans worship what you do not know. We Jews worship what we know, because salvation comes from the Jews.

**23** But a time will come, and it is here now, when the true worshipers will worship the Father in spirit and truth. The Father wants people who worship him like that.

**24** God is spirit. The people who worship him must worship in spirit and truth."

**25** The woman said to him, "I know that the Messiah will come. People call him the Christ. When he comes, he will explain all things to us."

**26** Jesus said to her, "I am the Messiah. I am the man who speaks to you now."

**27** At that time, his disciples came back. They were surprised that Jesus talked with a woman. But no person asked, "What do you want?" or "Why do you talk with her?"

**28** Then the woman left her water jar. She went back into the town and said to the people,

**29** "Come and see a man who told me all the things that I ever did. Can he be the Christ?"

**30** The people went out of the town and came to Jesus.

**31** While this occurred, the disciples asked Jesus strongly, "Rabbi, eat something."

**32** But he said to them, "I have food to eat that you do not know about."

**33** So the disciples said to each other, "Did a person bring him food?"

**34** Jesus said to them, "My food is to do what God, who sent me, wants. My food is to complete his work.

**35** You say, 'There are 4 more months until the harvest.' But I tell you, look at the fields! The crops are ready for harvest now.

**36** The person who harvests gets his pay now. He gathers crops for eternal life. Thus the person who plants and the person who harvests can be happy together.

**37** These words are true: 'One person plants, and another person harvests.'

**38** I sent you to harvest crops for which you did not work. Other people did the hard work, and you get the result of their work."

**39** Many Samaritans from that town believed in Jesus because of the words of the woman. She said, "He told me all the things that I ever did."

**40** So the Samaritans came to Jesus and asked him to stay with them. He stayed there for 2 days.

**41** Many more people believed because of the words that Jesus spoke.

**42** They said to the woman, "Now we believe, but not because of what you told us. We heard him ourselves. Now we know that this man is truly the Savior of the world."

**43** After 2 days, Jesus left that place and went to Galilee.

**44** Jesus himself said that people do not respect a prophet in his own country.

**45** When Jesus came to Galilee, the people of Galilee accepted him. They saw all the things that he did in Jerusalem at the Passover feast, because they also went to the feast.

**46** Jesus went again to Cana in Galilee, where he changed the water into wine. There was an officer of the king in Capernaum. The son of this officer was ill.

**47** The officer heard that Jesus came from Judea to Galilee. He went to Jesus and asked him to come down and heal his son. His son was about to die.

**48** Jesus said to him, "If you people do not see miraculous signs and wonders, you will not believe."

**49** The officer said to him, "Sir, come down before my child dies."

**50** Jesus said to him, "Go home. Your son will live." The man believed the words of Jesus, and he went home.

**51** While he was on his way home, his servants came to meet him. They told him, "Your son is alive and well."

**52** He asked them at what time his son started to become better. They said to him, "The fever left him yesterday at 1 o'clock in the afternoon."

**53** The father knew that this was the same time when Jesus told him, "Your son will live." So he and all his family believed in Jesus.

**54** This was the second miraculous sign that Jesus did after he came from Judea to Galilee.

## Chapter 5

**1** After this, there was a Jewish feast. Jesus went up to Jerusalem.

**2** In Jerusalem, near the Sheep Gate, there is a pool. In the Hebrew language, its name is Bethesda. The pool has 5 covered porches.

**3** Many ill people lay in these porches. Some were blind, some were lame, and some were paralyzed. They waited for the water to move.

**4** At certain times an angel came down into the pool and moved the water. After the water moved, the first person who went into the pool became well. He became well from all his diseases.

**5** A man was there who was ill for 38 years.

**6** Jesus saw the man lie there. Jesus knew that the man was ill for a long time. Jesus said to him, "Do you want to become well?"

**7** The ill man answered him, "Sir, when the water moves, I have no person to put me into the pool. While I try to go into the pool, another person goes in before me."

**8** Jesus said to him, "Get up, pick up your mat, and walk."

**9** Immediately the man became well. He picked up his mat and walked. That day was the Sabbath.

**10** So the Jewish leaders said to the man whom Jesus healed, "Today is the Sabbath. The law does not let you carry your mat."

**11** He answered them, "The man who made me well told me, 'Pick up your mat and walk.'"

**12** They asked him, "Who is the man who told you, 'Pick up your mat and walk'?"

**13** But the man whom Jesus healed did not know who Jesus was. Jesus went away into the crowd in that place.

**14** Later, Jesus found the man in the temple. He said to him, "Look, you are well now. Stop sinning. If you do not stop, something worse will occur to you."

**15** The man went away. He told the Jewish leaders that Jesus was the man who made him well.

**16** Jesus did these things on the Sabbath day. So the Jewish leaders persecuted Jesus and wanted to kill him.

**17** But Jesus answered them, "My Father always works until now, and I also work."

**18** Because of this, the Jewish leaders tried more to kill him. Jesus did not obey their rules for the Sabbath. Also, he said that God was his own Father. Thus he made himself equal with God.

**19** Jesus answered them, "I tell you the truth: The Son can do nothing by himself. He can do only what he sees the Father do. The Son does the same things that the Father does.

**20** The Father loves the Son and shows him all the things that he does. The Father will show the Son greater works than these. Then you will be surprised.

**21** The Father brings dead people back to life and gives them life. In the same way, the Son gives life to the people whom he chooses.

**22** The Father does not judge any person. He gave all judgment to the Son.

**23** He did this so that all people will honor the Son as they honor the Father. If a person does not honor the Son, he does not honor the Father who sent the Son.

**24** I tell you the truth: A person who hears my words and believes God, who sent me, has eternal life. God will not judge him as guilty. He left death and came into life.

**25** I tell you the truth: A time will come, and it is here now, when the dead will hear the voice of the Son of God. The people who hear his voice will live.

**26** The Father has life in himself. In the same way, he gave the Son life in himself.

**27** The Father also gave the Son authority to judge, because he is the Son of Man.

**28** Do not be surprised at this. A time will come when all the dead people in the graves will hear his voice.

**29** They will come out of the graves. The people who did good things will come back to life and live. The people who did evil things will come back to life and get judgment.

**30** I can do nothing by myself. I judge only as the Father tells me. My judgment is fair, because I do not try to do what I want. I do what the Father, who sent me, wants.

**31** If I alone tell about myself, people cannot accept my words as true.

**32** But another person tells about me. I know that the things that he says about me are true.

**33** You sent messengers to John, and he told them the truth.

**34** I do not need the words of a human to prove who I am. But I tell you these things so that you can be saved.

**35** John was like a lamp that burned and gave light. For a short time, you were happy to enjoy his light.

**36** But I have a better proof than the words of John. The Father gave me works to complete. These works that I do show that the Father sent me.

**37** The Father who sent me also told about me. You never heard his voice, and you never saw his shape.

**38** His word does not stay in you, because you do not believe the Son whom he sent.

**39** You study the scriptures carefully, because you think that they give you eternal life. The scriptures tell about me.

**40** But you refuse to come to me to get life.

**41** I do not accept praise from people.

**42** But I know you. I know that you do not have love for God in your hearts.

**43** I came with the authority of my Father, but you do not accept me. If another person comes with his own authority, you will accept him.

**44** You accept praise from each other. But you do not try to get the praise that comes from the only God. So how can you believe?

**45** Do not think that I will accuse you in front of the Father. Moses will accuse you. You hope in Moses.

**46** If you believed Moses, you would believe me, because Moses wrote about me.

**47** But you do not believe what Moses wrote. So you will not believe my words."

## Chapter 6

**1** After these things, Jesus went across the Sea of Galilee. People also call this lake the Sea of Tiberias.

**2** A large crowd followed him, because they saw the miraculous signs that he did for ill people.

**3** Jesus went up on a hill and sat down there with his disciples.

**4** It was almost time for the Jewish feast of Passover.

**5** Jesus looked up and saw a large crowd come to him. He said to Philip, "Where can we buy bread for these people to eat?"

**6** Jesus said this to test Philip, because Jesus already knew what he will do.

**7** Philip answered him, "200 silver coins will not buy sufficient bread for each person to have a small piece."

**8** Another of his disciples, Andrew, spoke to Jesus. Andrew was the brother of Simon Peter. He said,

**9** "A boy here has 5 small loaves of barley bread and 2 small fish. But that is not sufficient for so many people."

**10** Jesus said, "Tell the people to sit down." There was much grass in that place. So the people sat down. There were about 5,000 men.

**11** Then Jesus took the loaves and gave thanks. He gave the bread to the disciples, and the disciples gave it to the people who sat there. He did the same with the fish. All the people ate as much as they wanted.

**12** When all the people had sufficient food, Jesus said to his disciples, "Gather the pieces that are left. Do not waste any food."

**13** So they gathered the pieces. They filled 12 baskets with the pieces of the 5 barley loaves that the people did not eat.

**14** The people saw the miraculous sign that Jesus did. They said, "This man is truly the Prophet who must come into the world."

**15** Jesus knew that the people wanted to come and take him by force. They wanted to make him king. So he went away again to the hill alone.

**16** In the evening, his disciples went down to the lake.

**17** They got into a boat and started to go across the lake to Capernaum. It was dark now, and Jesus did not come to them yet.

**18** A strong wind blew, and the waves on the lake became high.

**19** The disciples rowed about 5 or 6 kilometers. Then they saw Jesus walk on the water. He came near to the boat, and they were afraid.

**20** But Jesus said to them, "It is I. Do not be afraid."

**21** Then they were happy to take him into the boat. Immediately the boat came to the land where they went.

**22** On the next day, the crowd stood on the other side of the lake. They knew that there was only 1 boat there. They knew that Jesus did not go into the boat with his disciples. The disciples went away alone.

**23** But other boats came from Tiberias. They came near the place where the people ate the bread after the Lord gave thanks.

**24** The crowd saw that Jesus and his disciples were not there. So the people got into the boats and went to Capernaum. They looked for Jesus.

**25** They found Jesus on the other side of the lake. They said to him, "Rabbi, when did you come here?"

**26** Jesus answered them, "I tell you the truth: You look for me, but not because you saw the miraculous signs. You look for me because you ate the bread and had sufficient food.

**27** Do not work for food that spoils. Work for the food that lasts and gives eternal life. The Son of Man will give you this food. God the Father put his mark of approval on the Son of Man."

**28** Then they asked him, "What must we do to do the works that God wants?"

**29** Jesus answered them, "This is the work that God wants: Believe in the man whom God sent."

**30** So they said to him, "What miraculous sign will you do? Then we will see it and believe you. What will you do?

**31** Our ancestors ate manna in the wilderness. The scriptures say, 'He gave them bread from heaven to eat.'"

**32** Jesus said to them, "I tell you the truth: Moses did not give you the bread from heaven. My Father gives you the true bread from heaven.

**33** The bread of God is the man who comes down from heaven. He gives life to the world."

**34** They said to him, "Lord, always give us this bread."

**35** Jesus said to them, "I am the bread of life. The person who comes to me will never be hungry. The person who believes in me will never be thirsty.

**36** But I told you that you saw me, and still you do not believe.

**37** All the people whom the Father gives me will come to me. I will never send away a person who comes to me.

**38** I came down from heaven to do what God, who sent me, wants. I did not come to do what I want.

**39** The Father sent me, and this is what he wants: I must not lose any of the people whom he gave me. I must bring them back to life on the last day.

**40** This is what my Father wants: All people who see the Son and believe in him will have eternal life. I will bring them back to life on the last day."

**41** The Jewish people complained about Jesus because he said, "I am the bread that came down from heaven."

**42** They said, "This is Jesus, the son of Joseph. We know his father and mother. How can he now say, 'I came down from heaven'?"

**43** Jesus answered them, "Stop your complaints among yourselves.

**44** No person can come to me if the Father, who sent me, does not bring him. I will bring that person back to life on the last day.

**45** The prophets wrote, 'God will teach all the people.' All people who listen to the Father and learn from him come to me.

**46** No person ever saw the Father except the man who comes from God. Only he saw the Father.

**47** I tell you the truth: The person who believes in me has eternal life.

**48** I am the bread of life.

**49** Your ancestors ate manna in the wilderness, but they died.

**50** But here is the bread that comes down from heaven. If a person eats this bread, he will not die.

**51** I am the living bread that came down from heaven. If a person eats this bread, he will live for all time. The bread that I will give is my body. I will give my body so that the world can have life."

**52** Then the Jewish people argued among themselves. They said, "How can this man give us his body to eat?"

**53** Jesus said to them, "I tell you the truth: You must eat the body of the Son of Man and drink his blood. If you do not, you do not have life in you.

**54** The person who eats my body and drinks my blood has eternal life. I will bring him back to life on the last day.

**55** My body is true food, and my blood is true drink.

**56** The person who eats my body and drinks my blood stays in me, and I stay in him.

**57** The living Father sent me, and I live because of the Father. In the same way, the person who eats me will live because of me.

**59** Jesus said these things while he taught in the synagogue in Capernaum.

**60** Many of his disciples heard this. They said, "This teaching is difficult. Who can accept it?"

**61** Jesus knew that his disciples complained about this. He said to them, "Does this teaching upset you?

**62** Then what will you think when you see the Son of Man go up to the place where he was before?

**63** The Spirit gives life. The human body cannot give life. The words that I told you are spirit, and they are life.

**64** But some of you do not believe." Jesus knew from the start who did not believe. He also knew who will betray him.

**66** After this, many of his disciples went away. They did not follow him any longer.

**68** Simon Peter answered him, "Lord, to whom will we go? You have the words of eternal life.

**69** We believe and we know that you are the Christ, the Son of the living God."

**71** Jesus spoke about Judas, the son of Simon Iscariot. Judas was one of the 12 apostles, but he was about to betray Jesus.

**6** Then Jesus said to them, "My time did not come yet. But your time is always ready.

**22** Moses gave you circumcision. Circumcision did not come from Moses but from the fathers. And you circumcise a man on the Sabbath.

**37** On the last day, the great day of the feast, Jesus stood and called out loudly. He said, "If a man is thirsty, let him come to me and drink.

**38** The scripture says this about the man who believes in me: 'Rivers of living water will flow from inside him.'"

**39** Jesus said this about the Spirit. The people who believed in him were to receive the Spirit later. The Holy Spirit was not given yet, because Jesus did not get his glory yet.

**40** Many people in the crowd heard these words. They said, "Truly this man is the Prophet."

**41** Other people said, "This man is the Christ." But some said, "The Christ will not come from Galilee.

**42** The scripture says that the Christ comes from the family of David. He comes from Bethlehem, the town where David lived."

**43** Thus the crowd divided because of Jesus.

**44** Some of them wanted to seize him, but no man put his hands on him.

**45** Then the officers came back to the chief priests and the Pharisees. The leaders said to the officers, "Why did you not bring him?"

**46** The officers answered, "No man ever spoke like this man."

**47** Then the Pharisees answered them, "Did he also deceive you?

**48** Did any of the leaders or of the Pharisees believe in him?

**49** No. But this crowd does not know the law. God curses them."

**50** Nicodemus was one of the Pharisees. He came to Jesus at night before. Nicodemus said to them,

**51** "Our law does not judge a man before it hears him and knows what he does."

**52** They answered him, "Do you also come from Galilee? Search the scriptures and look. No prophet comes from Galilee."

**53** Then each man went to his house.

**5** In the law, Moses told us to throw stones at such a woman until she dies. What do you say?"

**6** They said this to test him, because they wanted a reason to accuse him. But Jesus bent down and wrote on the ground with his finger. He acted as if he did not hear them.

**7** They continued to ask him. Then he stood up and said to them, "Let the man among you who has no sin throw the first stone at her."

**8** Then he bent down again and wrote on the ground.

**10** Jesus stood up and saw only the woman. He said to her, "Woman, where are the men who accused you? Did no man condemn you?"

**12** Then Jesus spoke to the people again. He said, "I am the light of the world. The man who follows me will not walk in darkness. He will have the light of life."

**13** Then the Pharisees said to him, "You speak about yourself. Thus what you say is not true."

**14** Jesus answered them, "I speak about myself, but what I say is true. I know where I come from and where I go. But you do not know where I come from or where I go.

**15** You judge by human standards. I judge no man.

**16** But if I judge, my judgment is true. I am not alone. The Father who sent me is with me.

**17** Your law also says that the words of 2 men are true.

**18** I speak about myself, and the Father who sent me also speaks about me."

**19** Then they said to him, "Where is your Father?" Jesus answered, "You do not know me or my Father. If you knew me, you would also know my Father."

**20** Jesus spoke these words in the treasury while he taught in the temple. No man seized him, because his time did not come yet.

**21** Then Jesus said to them again, "I will go away. You will look for me, and you will die in your sin. You cannot come to the place where I go."

**22** Then the Jews said, "Will he kill himself? He says, 'You cannot come to the place where I go.'"

**23** Jesus said to them, "You are from below. I am from above. You are from this world. I am not from this world.

**24** Thus I told you that you will die in your sins. If you do not believe that I am he, you will die in your sins."

**25** Then they said to him, "Who are you?" Jesus said to them, "I am the one that I told you about from the start.

**26** I have many things to say about you and to judge in you. But the one who sent me is true. I tell the world the things that I heard from him."

**27** They did not understand that he spoke to them about the Father.

**28** Then Jesus said to them, "When you lift up the Son of Man, then you will know that I am he. You will know that I do nothing from myself. I speak these things as my Father taught me.

**29** The one who sent me is with me. The Father did not leave me alone, because I always do the things that please him."

**30** While he spoke these words, many people believed in him.

**31** Then Jesus said to the Jews who believed in him, "If you continue in my word, then you are truly my disciples.

**32** You will know the truth, and the truth will make you free."

**33** They answered him, "We are the descendants of Abraham. We were never slaves to any man. Why do you say, 'You will become free'?"

**34** Jesus answered them, "I tell you the truth. Each man who sins is a slave of sin.

**35** A slave does not stay in the house always. But the son stays always.

**36** Thus if the Son makes you free, you will be truly free.

**37** I know that you are descendants of Abraham. But you try to kill me, because my word has no place in you.

**38** I speak the things that I saw with my Father. And you do the things that you saw with your father."

**39** They answered him, "Abraham is our father." Jesus said to them, "If you were the children of Abraham, you would do the works of Abraham.

**40** But now you try to kill me. I am a man who told you the truth that I heard from God. Abraham did not do this.

**41** You do the works of your father." Then they said to him, "We are not children of sexual sin. We have 1 Father, God."

**42** Jesus said to them, "If God were your Father, you would love me. I came from God. I did not come from myself. God sent me.

**43** Why do you not understand what I say? Because you cannot hear my word.

**44** You are from your father, the devil. You want to do what your father wants. He was a killer from the start. He does not stay in the truth, because there is no truth in him. When he tells a lie, he speaks from his own nature. He is a liar and the father of lies.

**45** But I tell the truth, and thus you do not believe me.

**46** Which of you can show that I sin? If I tell the truth, why do you not believe me?

**47** The man who is from God hears the words of God. You do not hear them, because you are not from God."

**48** Then the Jews answered him, "We are correct when we say that you are a Samaritan and have a demon."

**49** Jesus answered, "I do not have a demon. I honor my Father, but you dishonor me.

**50** I do not try to get glory for myself. There is one who tries to get glory for me, and he judges.

**51** I tell you the truth. If a man obeys my word, he will never see death."

**52** Then the Jews said to him, "Now we know that you have a demon. Abraham died, and the prophets died. But you say, 'If a man obeys my word, he will never taste death.'

**53** Are you greater than our father Abraham? He died, and the prophets died. Who do you think you are?"

**54** Jesus answered, "If I give glory to myself, my glory is nothing. My Father gives me glory. You say that he is your God.

**55** But you do not know him. I know him. If I said that I do not know him, I would be a liar like you. But I know him, and I obey his word.

**56** Your father Abraham was glad that he was to see my day. He saw it and he was glad."

**57** Then the Jews said to him, "You are not 50 years old yet. Did you see Abraham?"

**58** Jesus said to them, "I tell you the truth. Before Abraham was born, I am."

**59** Then they took up stones to throw at him. But Jesus hid himself and went out of the temple. He went through the middle of them and went away.

**1** As Jesus went along, he saw a man who was blind from birth.

**2** His disciples asked him, "Teacher, who sinned, this man or his parents? Why was he born blind?"

**3** Jesus answered, "This man did not sin, and his parents did not sin. He was born blind so that the people can see the works of God in him.

**4** I must do the works of the one who sent me while it is day. The night comes, and then no man can work.

**5** While I am in the world, I am the light of the world."

**6** After he said this, he spat on the ground. He made mud with the spit and put the mud on the eyes of the blind man.

**7** He said to the man, "Go and wash in the pool of Siloam." (Siloam means "Sent.") The man went and washed, and he came back with sight.

**8** His neighbors and the people who saw him before as a beggar said, "Is this not the man who sat and begged?"

**9** Some people said, "This is the man." Other people said, "He looks like him." But the man said, "I am the man."

**10** Then they said to him, "How did your eyes open?"

**11** He answered, "A man with the name Jesus made mud and put the mud on my eyes. He said to me, 'Go to the pool of Siloam and wash.' I went and washed, and then I could see."

**12** Then they said to him, "Where is he?" The man said, "I do not know."

**13** They brought the man who was blind before to the Pharisees.

**14** Jesus made the mud and opened the eyes of the man on a Sabbath day.

**15** Then the Pharisees also asked him how he got his sight. He said to them, "He put mud on my eyes. I washed, and now I see."

**16** Some of the Pharisees said, "This man is not from God, because he does not obey the Sabbath law." Other people said, "How can a man who is a sinner do such miracles?" Thus they divided among themselves.

**17** They spoke to the blind man again. They said, "What do you say about him? He opened your eyes." The man said, "He is a prophet."

**18** But the Jews did not believe that the man was blind and then got his sight. Thus they called the parents of the man.

**19** They asked the parents, "Is this your son? Do you say that he was born blind? How does he see now?"

**20** His parents answered them, "We know that this is our son. We know that he was born blind.

**21** But we do not know how he sees now. We do not know who opened his eyes. He is an adult. Ask him. He will speak for himself."

**22** His parents said these words because they were afraid of the Jews. The Jews agreed before that they would put a man out of the synagogue if he said that Jesus was the Christ.

**23** Thus his parents said, "He is an adult. Ask him."

**24** Then the Pharisees called the man who was blind a second time. They said to him, "Give glory to God. We know that this man is a sinner."

**25** He answered, "I do not know if he is a sinner. I know 1 thing. I was blind, and now I see."

**26** Then they said to him again, "What did he do to you? How did he open your eyes?"

**27** He answered them, "I told you already, and you did not listen. Why do you want to hear it again? Do you also want to become his disciples?"

**28** Then they insulted him. They said, "You are his disciple. But we are disciples of Moses.

**29** We know that God spoke to Moses. But we do not know where this man comes from."

**30** The man answered them, "This is very strange. You do not know where he comes from, but he opened my eyes.

**31** We know that God does not listen to sinners. But if a man worships God and does his will, God listens to him.

**32** Since the start of the world, no man ever heard that a person opened the eyes of a man who was born blind.

**33** If this man were not from God, he could do nothing."

**34** They answered him, "You were born fully in sins. Do you teach us?" Then they threw him out.

**35** Jesus heard that they threw him out. When Jesus found him, he said to the man, "Do you believe in the Son of God?"

**36** The man answered, "Who is he, Lord? Tell me, so that I can believe in him."

**37** Jesus said to him, "You saw him. He is the one who speaks with you now."

**38** The man said, "Lord, I believe." And he worshipped Jesus.

**39** Jesus said, "I came into this world for judgment. I came so that the people who do not see will see. I came so that the people who see will become blind."

**40** Some of the Pharisees who were with him heard these words. They said to him, "Are we also blind?"

**41** Jesus said to them, "If you were blind, you would have no sin. But now you say, 'We see.' Thus your sin stays."

**23** Jesus walked in the temple in the porch of Solomon.

**24** Then the Jews came around him. They said to him, "How long will you keep us in doubt? If you are the Christ, tell us clearly."

**31** The Jews were with Mary in the house to comfort her. They saw her get up quickly and go out. They followed her. They said, "She goes to the tomb to cry there."

**33** Jesus saw her cry. He saw that the Jews who came with her also cried. He felt strong emotion in his spirit, and he was troubled.

**34** He said, "Where did you put him?" They said to him, "Lord, come and see."

**35** Jesus cried.

**36** Then the Jews said, "Look how much he loved Lazarus!"

**37** Some of them said, "This man opened the eyes of the blind man. Could he not also stop the death of this man?"

**38** Jesus again felt strong emotion in himself. He came to the tomb. The tomb was a cave, and a stone was on its opening.

**39** Jesus said, "Take away the stone." Martha, the sister of the dead man, said to him, "Lord, by now the body smells bad. He is dead for 4 days."

**40** Jesus said to her, "I told you that if you believe, you will see the glory of God."

**41** Then they took away the stone from the tomb. Jesus looked up and said, "Father, I thank you because you heard me.

**42** I know that you always hear me. But I say this because of the people who stand here. I want them to believe that you sent me."

**43** After he said this, he called out with a loud voice, "Lazarus, come out!"

**45** Many of the Jews who came to Mary saw the things that Jesus did. They believed in him.

**46** But some of them went to the Pharisees. They told the Pharisees the things that Jesus did.

**47** Then the chief priests and the Pharisees called a meeting of the council. They said, "What will we do? This man does many miracles.

**48** If we let him continue, all the people will believe in him. Then the Romans will come and take away our place and our nation."

**55** The Passover of the Jews was near. Many people went from the country up to Jerusalem before the Passover. They went to make themselves clean.

**56** They looked for Jesus. They stood in the temple and said to each other, "What do you think? Will he not come to the feast?"

**57** The chief priests and the Pharisees gave an order. They said that if a man knew where Jesus was, he must tell them. Then they could seize him.

## Chapter 12

**1** 6 days before the Passover, Jesus came to Bethany. Lazarus lived there. Jesus brought Lazarus back to life from the dead.

**2** They made a dinner for Jesus there. Martha served the food. Lazarus was one of the people who sat at the table with him.

**3** Then Mary took about half a liter of perfume. The perfume was from pure nard and cost much money. She put the perfume on the feet of Jesus and wiped his feet with her hair. The smell of the perfume filled the house.

**4** Then one of his disciples, Judas Iscariot, the son of Simon, said this. Judas was the man who was to betray Jesus.

**5** He said, "Why did she not sell this perfume for 300 denarii? Then we could give the money to the poor people."

**6** He did not say this because he cared about the poor people. He said it because he was a thief. He kept the money bag and took money from it.

**7** Then Jesus said, "Leave her alone. She kept this perfume for the day of my burial.

**8** You will always have poor people with you. But you will not always have me."

**9** A large crowd of the Jews knew that Jesus was there. They came not only because of Jesus. They also wanted to see Lazarus, whom Jesus brought back to life from the dead.

**10** But the chief priests made plans to also kill Lazarus.

**11** They did this because many of the Jews went away from them because of Lazarus and believed in Jesus.

**12** On the next day, a large crowd came to the feast. They heard that Jesus came to Jerusalem.

**13** They took branches of palm trees and went out to meet him. They shouted, "Hosanna! God, bless the King of Israel who comes in the name of the Lord!"

**14** Jesus found a young donkey and sat on it. The scripture says,

**15** "Do not be afraid, daughter of Zion. Look, your King comes. He sits on the young of a donkey."

**16** At first, his disciples did not understand these things. But after Jesus got his glory, they remembered that the scriptures said these things about him. They remembered that the people did these things to him.

**17** The crowd that was with him when he called Lazarus out of the tomb told other people about it. Jesus brought Lazarus back to life from the dead.

**18** For this reason the crowd also went to meet him. They heard that he did this miracle.

**19** Thus the Pharisees said among themselves, "You see that we can do nothing. Look, all the world follows him."

**20** There were some Greeks among the people who came to worship at the feast.

**21** They came to Philip, who was from Bethsaida in Galilee. They said to him, "Sir, we want to see Jesus."

**22** Philip came and told Andrew. Then Andrew and Philip told Jesus.

**23** Jesus answered them, "The time comes for the Son of Man to get his glory.

**24** I tell you the truth. A grain of wheat must fall into the ground and die. If it does not die, it stays only 1 grain. But if it dies, it makes much fruit.

**25** The man who loves his life will lose it. The man who hates his life in this world will keep it for eternal life.

**26** If a man serves me, he must follow me. My servant will be in the place where I am. If a man serves me, my Father will honor him.

**27** Now I am troubled in my soul. What will I say? 'Father, save me from this time'? No. I came for this time.

**28** Father, give glory to your name." Then a voice came from heaven. The voice said, "I gave it glory, and I will give it glory again."

**29** The crowd that stood there heard the voice. Some people said, "It was thunder." Other people said, "An angel spoke to him."

**30** Jesus answered, "This voice did not come for me. It came for you.

**31** Now is the judgment of this world. Now God will throw out the ruler of this world.

**32** When people lift me up from the earth, I will pull all people to me."

**33** He said this to show what type of death he was to die.

**34** The crowd answered him, "The law tells us that the Christ stays always. Then why do you say, 'The Son of Man must be lifted up'? Who is this Son of Man?"

**35** Then Jesus said to them, "The light will be with you a short time more. Walk while you have the light, so that darkness does not catch you. The man who walks in darkness does not know where he goes.

**36** While you have the light, believe in the light. Then you will become children of light." After Jesus said these things, he went away and hid himself from them.

**37** He did many miracles in front of them, but they did not believe in him.

**38** This occurred so that the word of the prophet Isaiah came true. Isaiah said, "Lord, who believed our message? To whom did the Lord show his power?"

**39** Thus they could not believe. Isaiah also said,

**40** "God made their eyes blind and made their hearts hard. Thus they do not see with their eyes or understand with their hearts. They do not turn to me so that I heal them."

**41** Isaiah said these things when he saw the glory of Jesus. He spoke about Jesus.

**42** But many of the leaders also believed in him. But they did not say it openly because of the Pharisees. They did not want the Pharisees to put them out of the synagogue.

**43** They loved the praise of men more than the praise of God.

**44** Jesus called out, "The man who believes in me does not believe only in me. He also believes in the one who sent me.

**45** The man who sees me sees the one who sent me.

**46** I came as a light into the world. Each man who believes in me will not stay in darkness.

**47** If a man hears my words and does not believe, I do not judge him. I did not come to judge the world. I came to save the world.

**48** A man who rejects me and does not receive my words has a judge. The word that I spoke will judge him on the last day.

**49** I did not speak from myself. The Father who sent me gave me an order. He told me what I must say and speak.

**50** I know that his order gives eternal life. Thus I speak the things that the Father told me to speak."

## Chapter 13

**1** It was before the Feast of the Passover. Jesus knew that his time came to go out of this world to the Father. He loved his own people who were in the world. He loved them to the end.

**2** Jesus and his disciples ate the evening meal. The devil already put into the heart of Judas Iscariot, the son of Simon, the plan to betray Jesus.

**3** Jesus knew that the Father gave all things into his hands. He knew that he came from God and that he went to God.

**4** He got up from the meal and put aside his outer clothes. He took a towel and put it around his waist.

**5** After that, he put water into a bowl. He started to wash the feet of the disciples. He dried their feet with the towel that was around his waist.

**6** Then he came to Simon Peter. Peter said to him, "Lord, will you wash my feet?"

**7** Jesus answered him, "You do not know now what I do. But you will know later."

**8** Peter said to him, "You will never wash my feet." Jesus answered him, "If I do not wash you, you have no part with me."

**9** Simon Peter said to him, "Lord, then do not wash only my feet. Also wash my hands and my head."

**10** Jesus said to him, "A man who washed his body needs to wash only his feet. His full body is clean. You are clean, but not all of you."

**11** He knew the man who was to betray him. Thus he said, "Not all of you are clean."

**12** After he washed their feet, he put on his clothes and sat down again. He said to them, "Do you know what I did to you?

**13** You call me Teacher and Lord. You are correct, because I am.

**14** I, your Lord and Teacher, washed your feet. Thus you must also wash the feet of each other.

**15** I gave you an example. Do the same as I did to you.

**16** I tell you the truth. A servant is not greater than his master. A man who goes with a message is not greater than the man who sent him.

**17** If you know these things, you will be happy if you do them.

**18** I do not speak about all of you. I know the men that I chose. But the scripture must come true: 'The man who eats bread with me turned against me.'

**19** I tell you now, before it occurs. Then when it occurs, you will believe that I am he.

**20** I tell you the truth. A man who receives the person that I send receives me. And a man who receives me receives the one who sent me."

**21** After Jesus said these things, he was troubled in his spirit. He spoke clearly. He said, "I tell you the truth. One of you will betray me."

**22** Then the disciples looked at each other. They did not know about whom he spoke.

**23** One of his disciples, whom Jesus loved, lay at the table next to Jesus.

**24** Simon Peter made a sign to this disciple. Peter wanted him to ask Jesus about whom he spoke.

**25** The disciple leaned back against Jesus and said to him, "Lord, who is it?"

**26** Jesus answered, "It is the man to whom I will give this piece of bread after I put it in the dish." Then he put the bread in the dish. He gave it to Judas Iscariot, the son of Simon.

**27** After Judas took the bread, Satan went into him. Then Jesus said to him, "Do quickly what you will do."

**28** No man at the table knew why Jesus said this to him.

**29** Judas kept the money bag. Thus some of them thought that Jesus told him to buy things for the feast. Or they thought that Jesus told him to give something to the poor people.

**30** Judas took the piece of bread and went out immediately. It was night.

**31** After Judas went out, Jesus said, "Now the Son of Man gets his glory. And God gets glory in him.

**32** If God gets glory in him, God will also give him glory in himself. God will give him glory immediately.

**33** Little children, I will be with you a short time more. You will look for me. I told the Jews, 'You cannot come to the place where I go.' Now I tell you the same.

**34** I give you a new order: love each other. Love each other as I loved you.

**35** If you love each other, all people will know that you are my disciples."

**36** Simon Peter said to him, "Lord, where do you go?" Jesus answered him, "You cannot follow me now to the place where I go. But you will follow me later."

**37** Peter said to him, "Lord, why can I not follow you now? I will give my life for you."

**38** Jesus answered him, "Will you give your life for me? I tell you the truth. Before the rooster calls, you will say 3 times that you do not know me."

## Chapter 14

**1** "Do not let your heart be troubled. Believe in God. Also believe in me.

**2** There are many rooms in the house of my Father. If this were not true, I would tell you. I go to make a place ready for you.

**3** If I go and make a place ready for you, I will come again. I will take you to be with me. Then you will also be in the place where I am.

**4** You know the place where I go, and you know the way."

**5** Thomas said to him, "Lord, we do not know where you go. How can we know the way?"

**6** Jesus said to him, "I am the way, the truth, and the life. No man comes to the Father if he does not come through me.

**7** If you knew me, you would also know my Father. From now on you know him and you saw him."

**8** Philip said to him, "Lord, show us the Father. That is enough for us."

**9** Jesus said to him, "Philip, I was with you for a long time. Do you not know me yet? The man who saw me saw the Father. Why do you say, 'Show us the Father'?

**10** Do you not believe that I am in the Father and the Father is in me? I do not speak the words that I tell you from myself. The Father lives in me, and he does his works.

**11** Believe me that I am in the Father and the Father is in me. If you do not believe my words, then believe because of the works.

**12** I tell you the truth. The man who believes in me will also do the works that I do. He will do greater works than these, because I go to my Father.

**13** I will do all the things that you ask in my name. Then the Father will get glory in the Son.

**14** If you ask for a thing in my name, I will do it.

**15** If you love me, obey my orders.

**16** I will ask the Father, and he will give you a different Helper. The Helper will be with you always.

**17** He is the Spirit of truth. The world cannot receive him, because it does not see him or know him. But you know him, because he lives with you and will be in you.

**18** I will not leave you as children with no parents. I will come to you.

**19** After a short time, the world will not see me again. But you will see me. Because I live, you will also live.

**20** On that day, you will know that I am in my Father. You will know that you are in me, and I am in you.

**21** The man who has my orders and obeys them is the man who loves me. My Father will love the man who loves me. I will also love him and show myself to him."

**22** Judas (not Judas Iscariot) said to him, "Lord, why will you show yourself to us and not to the world?"

**23** Jesus answered him, "If a man loves me, he will obey my word. My Father will love him. We will come to him and live with him.

**24** The man who does not love me does not obey my words. The word that you hear is not mine. It is from the Father who sent me.

**25** I told you these things while I am still with you.

**26** But the Father will send the Helper, the Holy Spirit, in my name. The Holy Spirit will teach you all things. He will help you remember all the things that I told you.

**27** I leave peace with you. I give my peace to you. I do not give it to you as the world gives. Do not let your heart be troubled, and do not be afraid.

**28** You heard me say to you, 'I go away, and I will come back to you.' If you loved me, you would be glad that I go to the Father. The Father is greater than I am.

**29** I told you now, before it occurs. Then when it occurs, you will believe.

**30** I will not speak much more with you, because the ruler of this world comes. He has no power over me.

**31** But I do as the Father told me, so that the world knows that I love the Father. Get up. We will go away from here."

## Chapter 15

**1** "I am the true grapevine, and my Father is the farmer.

**2** He removes each branch in me that does not make fruit. He cuts and cleans each branch that makes fruit, so that it makes more fruit.

**3** You are already clean because of the word that I told you.

**4** Stay in me, and I will stay in you. A branch cannot make fruit by itself. It must stay in the grapevine. You also cannot make fruit if you do not stay in me.

**5** I am the grapevine. You are the branches. If a man stays in me and I stay in him, he makes much fruit. You can do nothing without me.

**6** If a man does not stay in me, people throw him away like a branch, and he becomes dry. People collect such branches and throw them into the fire, and they burn.

**7** If you stay in me and my words stay in you, ask for what you want. You will get it.

**8** My Father gets glory when you make much fruit. Then you show that you are my disciples.

**9** I loved you as the Father loved me. Stay in my love.

**10** If you obey my orders, you will stay in my love. I obeyed the orders of my Father, and I stay in his love.

**11** I told you these things so that my joy stays in you. I want your joy to be full.

**12** This is my order: love each other as I loved you.

**13** No man has greater love than this: a man gives his life for his friends.

**14** You are my friends if you do the things that I tell you to do.

**15** I do not call you servants now. A servant does not know what his master does. But I call you friends, because I told you all the things that I heard from my Father.

**16** You did not choose me, but I chose you. I gave you the task to go and make fruit. I want your fruit to stay. Then the Father will give you all the things that you ask in my name.

**17** I give you these orders so that you love each other.

**18** If the world hates you, know that it hated me before it hated you.

**19** If you were from the world, the world would love its own people. But you are not from the world. I chose you out of the world. Thus the world hates you.

**20** Remember the word that I told you: 'A servant is not greater than his master.' If they caused me to suffer, they will also cause you to suffer. If they obeyed my word, they will also obey your word.

**21** But they will do all these things to you because of my name. They do not know the one who sent me.

**22** If I did not come and speak to them, they would have no sin. But now they have no excuse for their sin.

**23** The man who hates me also hates my Father.

**24** I did works among them that no other man did. If I did not do these works, they would have no sin. But now they saw these works, and they hated me and my Father.

**25** This occurred so that the word in their law came true: 'They hated me with no reason.'

**26** I will send you the Helper from the Father. He is the Spirit of truth, who comes from the Father. When he comes, he will speak about me.

**27** You will also speak about me, because you were with me from the start."

## Chapter 16

**1** "I told you these things so that you do not stop your faith.

**2** They will put you out of the synagogues. A time will come when a man who kills you will think that he serves God.

**3** They will do these things because they do not know the Father or me.

**4** I told you these things so that you remember them when the time comes. You will remember that I told you. I did not tell you these things at the start, because I was with you.

**5** But now I go to the one who sent me. None of you asks me, 'Where do you go?'

**6** But because I told you these things, your heart is full of sadness.

**7** But I tell you the truth. It is good for you that I go away. If I do not go away, the Helper will not come to you. But if I go, I will send him to you.

**8** When he comes, he will show the world that it is wrong about sin, about righteousness, and about judgment.

**9** He will show them about sin, because they do not believe in me.

**10** He will show them about righteousness, because I go to my Father, and you will not see me again.

**11** He will show them about judgment, because God judged the ruler of this world.

**12** I have many more things to tell you, but you cannot accept them now.

**13** But when the Spirit of truth comes, he will guide you into all the truth. He will not speak from himself. He will speak the things that he hears. He will tell you the things that will come.

**14** He will give me glory, because he will take things from me and tell them to you.

**15** All the things that the Father has are mine. Thus I said that the Spirit will take things from me and tell them to you.

**16** After a short time, you will not see me. Then after a short time again, you will see me, because I go to the Father."

**17** Then some of his disciples said to each other, "What does he mean? He tells us, 'After a short time, you will not see me. Then after a short time again, you will see me.' He also says, 'I go to the Father.'"

**18** They said, "What does he mean by 'a short time'? We do not know what he says."

**19** Jesus knew that they wanted to ask him. He said to them, "Do you ask each other about what I said? I said, 'After a short time, you will not see me. Then after a short time again, you will see me.'

**20** I tell you the truth. You will cry and be sad, but the world will be glad. You will be sad, but your sadness will change into joy.

**21** When a woman gives birth, she has pain, because her time came. But after she gives birth to the child, she does not remember the pain. She is glad that a person was born into the world.

**22** Thus you are sad now. But I will see you again, and your heart will be glad. No man will take your joy from you.

**23** On that day, you will ask me no questions. I tell you the truth. If you ask the Father for a thing in my name, he will give it to you.

**24** Until now, you asked for nothing in my name. Ask, and you will receive. Then your joy will be full.

**25** I told you these things in examples. But the time comes when I will not speak to you in examples. I will tell you clearly about the Father.

**26** On that day, you will ask in my name. I do not say that I will ask the Father for you.

**27** The Father himself loves you, because you loved me. You believed that I came from God.

**28** I came from the Father and came into the world. Now I leave the world and go to the Father."

**29** His disciples said to him, "Look, now you speak clearly. You do not use examples.

**30** Now we know that you know all things. You do not need a man to ask you questions. Thus we believe that you came from God."

**31** Jesus answered them, "Do you believe now?

**32** Look, the time comes, and it is now here, when you will go in all directions. Each of you will go to his own place, and you will leave me alone. But I am not alone, because the Father is with me.

**33** I told you these things so that you have peace in me. In the world you will have trouble. But be brave. I defeated the world."

## Chapter 17

**1** Jesus said these words. Then he looked up to heaven and said, "Father, the time came. Give glory to your Son, so that your Son can give glory to you.

**2** You gave him authority over all people. Thus he will give eternal life to all the people that you gave him.

**3** This is eternal life: to know you, the only true God, and to know Jesus Christ, whom you sent.

**4** I gave you glory on the earth. I completed the work that you gave me to do.

**5** Now, Father, give me glory with you. Give me the glory that I had with you before the world was.

**6** I showed your name to the people that you gave me out of the world. They were yours, and you gave them to me. They obeyed your word.

**7** Now they know that all the things that you gave me come from you.

**8** I gave them the words that you gave me. They received the words. They know truly that I came from you. They believed that you sent me.

**9** I pray for them. I do not pray for the world. I pray for the people that you gave me, because they are yours.

**10** All my things are yours, and your things are mine. I get glory through them.

**11** I will not be in the world again, but they are in the world. I come to you. Holy Father, keep them safe through your name, the name that you gave me. Then they will be one, as we are one.

**12** While I was with them in the world, I kept them safe in your name. I protected the people that you gave me. None of them was lost except the son of destruction. Thus the scripture came true.

**13** Now I come to you. I say these things in the world so that they have my joy fully in them.

**14** I gave them your word. The world hated them, because they are not from the world. I am also not from the world.

**15** I do not ask you to take them out of the world. I ask you to protect them from the evil one.

**16** They are not from the world, as I am not from the world.

**17** Make them holy through your truth. Your word is truth.

**18** You sent me into the world. In the same way I sent them into the world.

**19** For them I make myself holy, so that they also become holy through the truth.

**20** I do not pray only for these people. I also pray for the people who will believe in me through their word.

**21** I pray that all of them will be one. Father, you are in me, and I am in you. I pray that they will also be one in us. Then the world will believe that you sent me.

**22** I gave them the glory that you gave me. I did this so that they will be one, as we are one.

**23** I am in them, and you are in me. I want them to become fully one. Then the world will know that you sent me. It will know that you loved them as you loved me.

**24** Father, I want the people that you gave me to be with me in the place where I am. I want them to see my glory, the glory that you gave me. You loved me before you made the world.

**25** Righteous Father, the world does not know you. But I know you. These people know that you sent me.

**26** I showed them your name, and I will continue to show it. Then the love that you have for me will be in them, and I will be in them."

## Chapter 18

**1** After Jesus spoke these words, he went out with his disciples. They went across the Kidron Valley. There was a garden there. Jesus and his disciples went into the garden.

**2** Judas, the man who betrayed him, also knew the place. Jesus often met there with his disciples.

**3** Then Judas got a group of soldiers and officers from the chief priests and the Pharisees. He came there with them. They had lights, torches, and weapons.

**4** Jesus knew all the things that were to occur to him. Thus he went out and said to them, "Whom do you look for?"

**5** They answered him, "Jesus of Nazareth." Jesus said to them, "I am he." Judas, the man who betrayed him, also stood with them.

**6** When Jesus said to them, "I am he," they moved back and fell to the ground.

**7** Then he asked them again, "Whom do you look for?" They said, "Jesus of Nazareth."

**8** Jesus answered, "I told you that I am he. If you look for me, let these men go away."

**9** This occurred so that his words came true: "I lost none of the people that you gave me."

**10** Then Simon Peter had a sword. He took it out and hit the servant of the high priest. He cut off the right ear of the servant. The name of the servant was Malchus.

**11** Then Jesus said to Peter, "Put your sword back into its cover. The Father gave me this cup of suffering. Must I not drink it?"

**12** Then the soldiers, their commander, and the officers of the Jews seized Jesus and tied him.

**13** They took him first to Annas. Annas was the father-in-law of Caiaphas, who was the high priest that year.

**14** Caiaphas was the man who told the Jews before that it was better for 1 man to die for the people.

**15** Simon Peter and a different disciple followed Jesus. The high priest knew that disciple. That disciple went with Jesus into the courtyard of the high priest.

**16** But Peter stood outside at the door. Then the other disciple, whom the high priest knew, went out. He spoke to the woman who kept the door, and he brought Peter in.

**17** Then the servant woman who kept the door said to Peter, "Are you not also one of the disciples of this man?" Peter said, "I am not."

**18** The servants and officers stood there. They made a fire of coals, because it was cold. They stood and made themselves warm. Peter stood with them and made himself warm.

**19** Then the high priest asked Jesus about his disciples and about his teaching.

**20** Jesus answered him, "I spoke openly to the world. I always taught in the synagogue and in the temple, where all the Jews meet. I said nothing in secret.

**21** Why do you ask me? Ask the people who heard me. Ask them what I said to them. They know what I said."

**22** When Jesus said this, one of the officers who stood there hit Jesus with his hand. He said, "Do you answer the high priest in this way?"

**23** Jesus answered him, "If I said something wrong, tell me what was wrong. But if I said the truth, why do you hit me?"

**24** Then Annas sent Jesus, still tied, to Caiaphas the high priest.

**25** Simon Peter stood and made himself warm. The people said to him, "Are you not also one of his disciples?" Peter said that he was not. He said, "I am not."

**26** One of the servants of the high priest was a relative of the man whose ear Peter cut off. He said, "Did I not see you in the garden with him?"

**27** Then Peter again said that he was not. Immediately the rooster called.

**28** Then they took Jesus from Caiaphas to the palace of the governor. It was early in the morning. The Jews did not go into the palace, because they did not want to become unclean. They wanted to eat the Passover.

**29** Then Pilate went out to them. He said, "What do you say against this man?"

**30** They answered him, "If he were not a criminal, we would not give him to you."

**31** Then Pilate said to them, "You take him and judge him by your law." The Jews said to him, "The law does not permit us to kill any man."

**32** This occurred so that the word of Jesus came true. He spoke about the type of death that he was to die.

**33** Then Pilate went into the palace again and called Jesus. He said to him, "Are you the King of the Jews?"

**34** Jesus answered him, "Do you say this from yourself? Or did other people tell you about me?"

**35** Pilate answered, "Am I a Jew? Your own nation and the chief priests gave you to me. What did you do?"

**36** Jesus answered, "My kingdom is not from this world. If my kingdom were from this world, my servants would fight. They would fight so that the Jews do not seize me. But my kingdom is not from here."

**37** Then Pilate said to him, "Then are you a king?" Jesus answered, "You say that I am a king. I was born for this reason, and I came into the world for this reason: to speak about the truth. Each person who is from the truth hears my voice."

**38** Pilate said to him, "What is truth?" After Pilate said this, he went out again to the Jews. He said to them, "I find no crime in him.

**39** But you have a custom: I release 1 man to you at the Passover. Do you want me to release the King of the Jews to you?"

**40** Then all of them shouted again, "Not this man, but Barabbas!" Barabbas was a robber.

## Chapter 19

**1** Then Pilate took Jesus and caused soldiers to beat him with a whip.

**2** The soldiers made a crown of thorns and put it on his head. They put a purple robe on him.

**3** They said, "Hail, King of the Jews!" And they hit him with their hands.

**4** Then Pilate went out again. He said to the people, "Look, I bring him out to you. I want you to know that I find no crime in him."

**5** Then Jesus came out. He wore the crown of thorns and the purple robe. Pilate said to them, "Look at the man!"

**6** The chief priests and officers saw him. They shouted, "Crucify him! Crucify him!" Pilate said to them, "You take him and crucify him. I find no crime in him."

**7** The Jews answered him, "We have a law. By our law he must die, because he made himself the Son of God."

**8** When Pilate heard these words, he became more afraid.

**9** He went into the palace again. He said to Jesus, "Where do you come from?" But Jesus gave him no answer.

**10** Then Pilate said to him, "Do you not speak to me? Do you not know that I have authority to crucify you? I also have authority to release you."

**11** Jesus answered, "You would have no authority over me if God did not give it to you. Thus the man who gave me to you has the greater sin."

**12** From that time, Pilate tried to release him. But the Jews shouted, "If you release this man, you are not a friend of Caesar. Each man who makes himself a king speaks against Caesar."

**13** When Pilate heard these words, he brought Jesus out. He sat down on the judgment seat. The place had the name the Stone Pavement. In Hebrew, the name is Gabbatha.

**14** It was the day of preparation for the Passover. It was about the sixth hour. Pilate said to the Jews, "Look at your King!"

**15** But they shouted, "Take him away! Take him away! Crucify him!" Pilate said to them, "Must I crucify your King?" The chief priests answered, "We have no king but Caesar."

**16** Then Pilate gave Jesus to them to be crucified. They took Jesus and led him away.

**17** Jesus carried his cross and went out to a place with the name "The Place of the Skull." In Hebrew, the name is Golgotha.

**18** There they crucified him. They also crucified 2 other men with him, 1 on each side. Jesus was in the middle.

**19** Pilate wrote a sign and put it on the cross. The sign said, "JESUS OF NAZARETH, THE KING OF THE JEWS."

**20** Many of the Jews read this sign, because the place where they crucified Jesus was near the city. The sign was in Hebrew, Greek, and Latin.

**21** Then the chief priests of the Jews said to Pilate, "Do not write, 'The King of the Jews.' Write, 'This man said, I am King of the Jews.'"

**22** Pilate answered, "I wrote what I wrote."

**23** After the soldiers crucified Jesus, they took his clothes. They divided the clothes into 4 parts, 1 part for each soldier. They also took his coat. The coat had no seam. It was made in 1 piece from the top to the bottom.

**24** They said to each other, "We will not tear it. We will throw lots to decide who gets it." This occurred so that the scripture came true: "They divided my clothes among them. They threw lots for my coat." Thus the soldiers did these things.

**25** The mother of Jesus stood near the cross of Jesus. The sister of his mother, Mary the wife of Clopas, and Mary Magdalene also stood there.

**26** Jesus saw his mother. He also saw the disciple whom he loved, who stood near her. Jesus said to his mother, "Woman, look, this is your son."

**27** Then he said to the disciple, "Look, this is your mother." From that time, the disciple took her into his own house.

**28** After this, Jesus knew that all things were now complete. So that the scripture came true, he said, "I am thirsty."

**29** A container full of sour wine stood there. They put a sponge full of sour wine on a hyssop branch. They put the sponge to his mouth.

**30** Jesus took the sour wine. Then he said, "It is complete." He put down his head and gave up his spirit.

**31** It was the day of preparation. The Jews did not want the bodies to stay on the cross on the Sabbath, because that Sabbath was a special day. Thus they asked Pilate to break the legs of the men and take the bodies away.

**32** Then the soldiers came. They broke the legs of the first man and of the other man whom they crucified with Jesus.

**33** But when they came to Jesus, they saw that he was already dead. Thus they did not break his legs.

**34** But one of the soldiers put a spear into his side. Immediately blood and water came out.

**35** The man who saw this tells about it, and his words are true. He knows that he tells the truth. He tells it so that you also believe.

**36** These things occurred so that the scripture came true: "Not 1 of his bones will be broken."

**37** A different scripture also says, "They will look at the man whom they pierced."

**38** After these things, Joseph of Arimathea asked Pilate for permission to take away the body of Jesus. Joseph was a disciple of Jesus, but secretly, because he was afraid of the Jews. Pilate gave him permission. Thus Joseph came and took the body of Jesus.

**39** Nicodemus also came. He was the man who came to Jesus at night at the start. He brought a mixture of myrrh and aloes, about 34 kilograms.

**40** Then they took the body of Jesus. They put strips of linen cloth with the spices around it. This was the custom of the Jews for burial.

**41** There was a garden in the place where they crucified him. In the garden there was a new tomb. No person was in that tomb before.

**42** It was the day of preparation of the Jews, and the tomb was near. Thus they put Jesus there.

## Chapter 20

**1** On the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene came early to the tomb. It was still dark. She saw that the stone was not on the tomb.

**2** Then she ran and came to Simon Peter and to the other disciple, whom Jesus loved. She said to them, "They took the Lord out of the tomb. We do not know where they put him."

**3** Then Peter and the other disciple went out to the tomb.

**4** The 2 men ran together. The other disciple ran faster than Peter and came to the tomb first.

**5** He bent down and looked in. He saw the strips of linen cloth there, but he did not go in.

**6** Then Simon Peter came after him. Peter went into the tomb. He saw the strips of linen cloth there.

**7** He saw the cloth that was on the head of Jesus. That cloth was not with the strips of linen. It was folded in a different place.

**8** Then the other disciple, who came to the tomb first, also went in. He saw and he believed.

**9** They did not yet understand the scripture. The scripture said that Jesus must come back to life from the dead.

**10** Then the disciples went back to their homes.

**11** But Mary stood outside the tomb and cried. While she cried, she bent down and looked into the tomb.

**12** She saw 2 angels in white clothes. They sat in the place where the body of Jesus was before. 1 angel sat at the head, and 1 angel sat at the feet.

**13** They said to her, "Woman, why do you cry?" She said to them, "They took away my Lord. I do not know where they put him."

**14** After she said this, she turned around. She saw Jesus stand there, but she did not know that it was Jesus.

**15** Jesus said to her, "Woman, why do you cry? Whom do you look for?" She thought that he was the gardener. She said to him, "Sir, if you carried him away, tell me where you put him. Then I will take him away."

**16** Jesus said to her, "Mary." She turned and said to him, "Rabboni!" (This Hebrew word means "Teacher.")

**17** Jesus said to her, "Do not hold me, because I did not go up to my Father yet. Go to my brothers. Tell them, 'I go up to my Father and your Father, to my God and your God.'"

**18** Mary Magdalene went and told the disciples that she saw the Lord. She told them that he said these things to her.

**19** On the evening of that day, the first day of the week, the disciples were together. The doors were locked, because the disciples were afraid of the Jews. Jesus came and stood in the middle of them. He said to them, "Peace to you."

**20** After he said this, he showed them his hands and his side. The disciples were glad when they saw the Lord.

**21** Then Jesus said to them again, "Peace to you. The Father sent me. In the same way I send you."

**22** After he said this, he blew his breath on them. He said to them, "Receive the Holy Spirit.

**23** If you forgive the sins of a person, God forgives them. If you do not forgive the sins of a person, God does not forgive them."

**24** But Thomas, one of the 12 disciples, was not with them when Jesus came. Thomas had the name Didymus (Twin).

**25** The other disciples said to him, "We saw the Lord." But Thomas said to them, "I must see the marks of the nails in his hands. I must put my finger into the marks of the nails. I must put my hand into his side. If I do not do these things, I will not believe."

**26** After 8 days, his disciples were inside again. Thomas was with them. The doors were locked, but Jesus came and stood in the middle of them. He said, "Peace to you."

**27** Then he said to Thomas, "Put your finger here and see my hands. Put your hand into my side. Stop your doubt, and believe."

**28** Thomas answered him, "My Lord and my God!"

**29** Jesus said to him, "Thomas, you believed because you saw me. Happy are the people who did not see me and believed."

**30** Jesus did many other miracles in front of his disciples. They are not written in this book.

**31** But these miracles are written so that you believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God. If you believe, you will have life through his name.

## Chapter 21

**1** After these things, Jesus showed himself again to the disciples at the Sea of Tiberias. He showed himself in this way.

**2** Simon Peter, Thomas (Didymus), and Nathanael from Cana in Galilee were together. The sons of Zebedee and 2 other disciples were also with them.

**3** Simon Peter said to them, "I go to fish." They said to him, "We will also go with you." They went out and got into the boat immediately. That night they caught nothing.

**4** Early in the morning, Jesus stood on the shore. But the disciples did not know that it was Jesus.

**5** Then Jesus said to them, "Children, do you have any food?" They answered him, "No."

**6** He said to them, "Throw the net on the right side of the boat, and you will find fish." They threw the net. Then they could not pull it in, because there were very many fish.

**7** Then the disciple whom Jesus loved said to Peter, "It is the Lord!" When Simon Peter heard that it was the Lord, he put on his outer coat, because he did not wear it. Then he jumped into the sea.

**8** The other disciples came in the small boat. They pulled the net full of fish. They were not far from land, about 90 meters.

**9** When they came to land, they saw a fire of coals there. Fish was on the fire, and there was bread.

**10** Jesus said to them, "Bring some of the fish that you caught now."

**11** Simon Peter went and pulled the net to land. It was full of large fish, 153 fish. There were very many fish, but the net did not tear.

**12** Jesus said to them, "Come and eat." None of the disciples asked him, "Who are you?" They knew that it was the Lord.

**13** Jesus came and took the bread and gave it to them. He did the same with the fish.

**14** This was the third time that Jesus showed himself to his disciples after he came back to life from the dead.

**15** After they ate, Jesus said to Simon Peter, "Simon, son of Jonah, do you love me more than these?" Peter said to him, "Yes, Lord. You know that I love you." Jesus said to him, "Give food to my lambs."

**16** Jesus said to him again a second time, "Simon, son of Jonah, do you love me?" Peter said to him, "Yes, Lord. You know that I love you." Jesus said to him, "Be a shepherd to my sheep."

**17** Jesus said to him the third time, "Simon, son of Jonah, do you love me?" Peter was sad because Jesus asked him the third time, "Do you love me?" Peter said to him, "Lord, you know all things. You know that I love you." Jesus said to him, "Give food to my sheep.

**18** I tell you the truth. When you were young, you put on your belt and walked to the places that you wanted. But when you are old, you will put out your hands. A different person will put a belt on you. He will take you to places that you do not want."

**19** Jesus said this to show the type of death by which Peter was to give glory to God. After he said this, he said to Peter, "Follow me."

**20** Then Peter turned around and saw the disciple whom Jesus loved. That disciple followed them. He was the disciple who leaned back on Jesus at the evening meal. He asked, "Lord, who is the man who will betray you?"

**21** Peter saw him and said to Jesus, "Lord, what will occur to this man?"

**22** Jesus said to him, "If I want him to stay until I come, that is not your problem. You must follow me."

**23** Thus a story went out among the brothers that this disciple was not to die. But Jesus did not say to Peter that he was not to die. Jesus said, "If I want him to stay until I come, that is not your problem."

**24** This is the disciple who tells about these things and wrote these things. We know that his words are true.

**25** Jesus also did many other things. If people wrote about each of them, I think that the world could not hold all the books. Amen.
