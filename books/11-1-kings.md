# 1 Kings

## Chapter 1

**1** King David was very old. His servants put covers on him, but he did not become warm.

**2** Thus his servants said to him, "Let us find a young woman for our lord the king. She will stand near the king and take care of him. She will lie next to you, and then our lord the king will be warm."

**3** So they looked for a beautiful young woman in all the areas of Israel. They found Abishag, a woman from Shunem, and they brought her to the king.

**4** The young woman was very beautiful. She took care of the king and served him. But the king did not have sexual relations with her.

**5** Then Adonijah, the son of Haggith, made himself important. He said, "I will be king." He got chariots and horsemen for himself, and 50 men to run in front of him.

**6** His father never made him unhappy. His father never asked him, "Why did you do that?" Adonijah was also a very handsome man. His mother gave birth to him after Absalom.

**7** Adonijah spoke with Joab, the son of Zeruiah, and with Abiathar the priest. These 2 men followed Adonijah and helped him.

**8** But Zadok the priest, Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, Nathan the prophet, Shimei, Rei, and the strong soldiers of David did not join Adonijah.

**9** Adonijah killed sheep, oxen, and fat cattle as sacrifices at the stone of Zoheleth, near En-rogel. He invited all his brothers, the sons of the king. He also invited all the men of Judah who were servants of the king.

**10** But he did not invite Nathan the prophet, Benaiah, the strong soldiers, or his brother Solomon.

**11** Then Nathan spoke to Bathsheba, the mother of Solomon. He said, "Did you hear that Adonijah, the son of Haggith, is now king? And our lord David does not know about it.

**12** Now come, and let me give you advice. Then you can save your life and the life of your son Solomon.

**13** Go in to King David and say to him, 'My lord the king, you made a promise to me, your servant. You said, "Your son Solomon will be king after me. He will sit on my throne." Why then is Adonijah king?'

**14** While you still speak with the king, I will come in after you. I will tell the king that your words are true."

**15** So Bathsheba went in to the king in his room. The king was very old. Abishag from Shunem served the king.

**16** Bathsheba bowed down low in front of the king. The king said, "What do you want?"

**17** She said to him, "My lord, you made a promise to me, your servant, in the name of the LORD your God. You said, 'Your son Solomon will be king after me. He will sit on my throne.'

**18** But now Adonijah is king, and you, my lord the king, do not know about it.

**19** Adonijah killed many oxen, fat cattle, and sheep as sacrifices. He invited all the sons of the king, Abiathar the priest, and Joab the commander of the army. But he did not invite your servant Solomon.

**20** My lord the king, all the people of Israel look at you. They wait for you to tell them who will sit on your throne after you.

**21** If you do not tell them, then people will think that my son Solomon and I are criminals when you die."

**22** While she still spoke with the king, Nathan the prophet came in.

**23** The servants told the king, "Nathan the prophet is here." Nathan came in front of the king and bowed down with his face to the ground.

**24** Nathan said, "My lord the king, did you say, 'Adonijah will be king after me, and he will sit on my throne'?

**25** Today Adonijah went down and killed many oxen, fat cattle, and sheep as sacrifices. He invited all the sons of the king, the commanders of the army, and Abiathar the priest. Now they eat and drink with him. They say, 'Long live King Adonijah!'

**26** But Adonijah did not invite me, your servant. He did not invite Zadok the priest, Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, or your servant Solomon.

**27** Did my lord the king do this? Did you not tell your servants who will sit on your throne after you?"

**28** Then King David said, "Call Bathsheba to me." She came in and stood in front of the king.

**29** The king made a promise. He said, "The LORD saved me from all trouble. As surely as the LORD lives,

**30** I will do today what I promised you in the name of the LORD, the God of Israel. I said, 'Your son Solomon will be king after me. He will sit on my throne in my place.' I will surely do this today."

**31** Then Bathsheba bowed down with her face to the ground in front of the king. She said, "Let my lord King David live always!"

**32** King David said, "Call Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada to me." They came in front of the king.

**33** The king said to them, "Take the servants of your lord with you. Put my son Solomon on my own mule. Take him down to Gihon.

**34** There Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet must anoint him king over Israel. Then blow the trumpet and say, 'Long live King Solomon!'

**35** Then come up after him. He will come and sit on my throne. He will be king in my place. I chose him to be ruler over Israel and Judah."

**36** Benaiah the son of Jehoiada answered the king. He said, "Amen! Let the LORD, the God of my lord the king, make it so.

**37** The LORD was with my lord the king. Let the LORD also be with Solomon. Let the LORD make the throne of Solomon greater than the throne of my lord King David."

**38** So Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, the Cherethites, and the Pelethites went down. They put Solomon on the mule of King David and took him to Gihon.

**39** Zadok the priest took a horn of oil from the tabernacle and anointed Solomon. Then they blew the trumpet. All the people said, "Long live King Solomon!"

**40** All the people went up after him. They played flutes and they were very happy. The noise was so loud that the ground shook.

**41** Adonijah and all his guests heard the noise when they stopped their meal. Joab heard the sound of the trumpet. He said, "Why is there so much noise in the city?"

**42** While Joab still spoke, Jonathan the son of Abiathar the priest came. Adonijah said, "Come in. You are a good man. You bring good news."

**43** Jonathan answered Adonijah, "No! Our lord King David made Solomon king.

**44** The king sent Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, the Cherethites, and the Pelethites with Solomon. They put Solomon on the mule of the king.

**45** Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet anointed Solomon king in Gihon. They came up from there with much joy, and the city is full of noise. This is the noise that you hear.

**46** Also, Solomon now sits on the throne of the kingdom.

**47** The servants of the king also came to bless our lord King David. They said, 'Let God make the name of Solomon more famous than your name. Let God make his throne greater than your throne.' The king bowed down on his bed.

**48** The king also said, 'Praise the LORD, the God of Israel. Today the LORD put a man on my throne, and my eyes see it.'"

**49** Then all the guests of Adonijah became afraid. They got up and each man went away.

**50** Adonijah was afraid of Solomon. He got up and went to the altar. He held the horns of the altar.

**51** Someone told Solomon, "Adonijah is afraid of King Solomon. He holds the horns of the altar. He says, 'Let King Solomon promise me today that he will not kill me, his servant, with the sword.'"

**52** Solomon said, "If Adonijah shows that he is an honest man, then no hair of his head will fall to the ground. But if he does wrong, he will die."

**53** Then King Solomon sent men, and they brought Adonijah down from the altar. Adonijah came and bowed down in front of King Solomon. Solomon said to him, "Go to your house."

## Chapter 2

**1** The time came near for David to die. He gave instructions to his son Solomon. He said,

**2** "I will soon die, as all people die. Be strong and show that you are a man.

**3** Obey the instructions of the LORD your God. Live in the way that he shows you. Obey his laws, his commands, his rules, and his statements, as the law of Moses tells. Then you will be successful in all that you do and in all places that you go.

**4** Then the LORD will keep the promise that he made to me. He said, 'Your sons must be careful in the way that they live. They must obey me faithfully with all their heart and all their soul. If they do this, then a man from your family will always sit on the throne of Israel.'

**5** You also know what Joab, the son of Zeruiah, did to me. He killed the 2 commanders of the armies of Israel, Abner the son of Ner and Amasa the son of Jether. He killed them in a time of peace, as if it was a time of war. He put their blood on his belt and on his shoes.

**6** Use your wisdom and do what is correct. Do not let Joab become old and die in peace.

**7** But be kind to the sons of Barzillai from Gilead. Let them eat at your table. They helped me when I ran away from your brother Absalom.

**8** Also, Shimei the son of Gera is with you. He is a Benjamite from Bahurim. He spoke a terrible curse against me on the day that I went to Mahanaim. Later he came down to meet me at the Jordan River. I made a promise to him in the name of the LORD. I said, 'I will not kill you with the sword.'

**9** But do not think that he is innocent. You are a wise man. You will know what to do to him. Make sure that he has a violent death in his old age."

**10** Then David died and they buried him in the City of David.

**11** David was king over Israel for 40 years. He was king for 7 years in Hebron and for 33 years in Jerusalem.

**12** Then Solomon sat on the throne of his father David. His kingdom became very strong.

**13** Adonijah, the son of Haggith, came to Bathsheba, the mother of Solomon. She said, "Do you come in peace?" He said, "Yes, I come in peace."

**14** Then he said, "I have something to say to you." She said, "Speak."

**15** He said, "You know that the kingdom was mine. All the people of Israel expected me to be king. But the kingdom changed and went to my brother. The LORD gave it to him.

**16** Now I have 1 request for you. Do not refuse me." She said to him, "Speak."

**17** He said, "Please speak to King Solomon. He will not refuse you. Ask him to give me Abishag from Shunem as my wife."

**18** Bathsheba said, "Good. I will speak to the king for you."

**19** So Bathsheba went to King Solomon to speak to him for Adonijah. The king got up to meet her and bowed down to her. Then he sat down on his throne. He told his servants to bring a seat for his mother, and she sat at his right side.

**20** She said, "I have 1 small request for you. Do not refuse me." The king said to her, "Ask, my mother. I will not refuse you."

**21** She said, "Give Abishag from Shunem to your brother Adonijah as his wife."

**22** King Solomon answered his mother, "Why do you ask for Abishag from Shunem for Adonijah? Why not also ask for the kingdom for him? He is my older brother. Abiathar the priest and Joab the son of Zeruiah are on his side!"

**23** Then King Solomon made a promise in the name of the LORD. He said, "Let God punish me very much if Adonijah does not die for this request.

**24** The LORD made me strong and put me on the throne of my father David. The LORD gave me a dynasty, as he promised. As surely as the LORD lives, Adonijah will die today."

**25** Then King Solomon sent Benaiah the son of Jehoiada. Benaiah attacked Adonijah and killed him.

**26** The king said to Abiathar the priest, "Go to your fields in Anathoth. You must die for what you did. But I will not kill you now. You carried the ark of the Lord GOD in front of my father David. Also, you had all the same troubles as my father."

**27** So Solomon removed Abiathar from his work as priest of the LORD. This made the word of the LORD come true. The LORD spoke this word about the family of Eli in Shiloh.

**28** Joab heard the news. Joab supported Adonijah, but he did not support Absalom. Joab ran to the tabernacle of the LORD and held the horns of the altar.

**29** Someone told King Solomon, "Joab ran to the tabernacle of the LORD. He is next to the altar." Then Solomon sent Benaiah the son of Jehoiada. Solomon said, "Go, attack him and kill him."

**30** Benaiah went to the tabernacle of the LORD. He said to Joab, "The king tells you to come out." But Joab said, "No, I will die here." Benaiah went back to the king and told him what Joab said.

**31** The king said to Benaiah, "Do as Joab said. Attack him and kill him, and bury him. Joab killed innocent people. Then my family and I will not be guilty for that blood.

**32** The LORD will punish Joab for the people that he killed. Joab attacked 2 men who were better and more honest than he was. He killed them with the sword, and my father David did not know about it. The first man was Abner the son of Ner, commander of the army of Israel. The second man was Amasa the son of Jether, commander of the army of Judah.

**33** The guilt for their deaths will always be on Joab and on his descendants. But the LORD will always give peace to David, his descendants, his family, and his throne."

**34** So Benaiah the son of Jehoiada went up and attacked Joab and killed him. They buried Joab at his house in the wilderness.

**35** The king put Benaiah the son of Jehoiada in the place of Joab as commander of the army. The king put Zadok the priest in the place of Abiathar.

**36** Then the king sent for Shimei. The king said to him, "Build a house in Jerusalem and live there. Do not go out of the city to any other place.

**37** On the day that you go out and cross the Kidron Valley, you will surely die. Know this for certain. You will be responsible for your own death."

**38** Shimei said to the king, "Your words are good. I, your servant, will do as my lord the king says." So Shimei lived in Jerusalem for a long time.

**39** But after 3 years, 2 slaves of Shimei ran away to Achish, the son of Maacah, the king of Gath. Someone told Shimei, "Your slaves are in Gath."

**40** So Shimei put a saddle on his donkey and went to Achish in Gath to find his slaves. Shimei went and brought his slaves back from Gath.

**41** Someone told Solomon that Shimei went from Jerusalem to Gath and came back.

**42** The king sent for Shimei. He said to him, "I made you promise in the name of the LORD, and I told you clearly what will happen. I said, 'On the day that you go out to any other place, you will surely die. Know this for certain.' And you said to me, 'Your words are good. I will obey.'

**43** Why did you not keep your promise to the LORD? Why did you not obey the command that I gave you?"

**44** The king also said to Shimei, "You know in your heart all the bad things that you did to my father David. Now the LORD will punish you for those bad things.

**45** But the LORD will bless King Solomon. The LORD will make the throne of David strong forever."

**46** Then the king gave a command to Benaiah the son of Jehoiada. Benaiah went out and attacked Shimei and killed him. So the kingdom was strong in the control of Solomon.

## Chapter 3

**1** Solomon made an agreement with Pharaoh, the king of Egypt. Solomon married the daughter of Pharaoh. He brought her to the City of David. She stayed there until he finished his palace, the house of the LORD, and the wall around Jerusalem.

**2** But the people still made sacrifices at the high places (places of worship on hills). At that time, no one built a house for the name of the LORD.

**3** Solomon loved the LORD. He obeyed the laws of his father David. But he made sacrifices and burned incense at the high places.

**4** The king went to Gibeon to make sacrifices there, because Gibeon was the most important high place. Solomon gave 1,000 burnt offerings on that altar.

**5** At Gibeon, the LORD came to Solomon in a dream at night. God said, "Ask for what you want me to give you."

**6** Solomon said, "You showed great kindness to your servant David, my father. He obeyed you honestly. He was correct and good in his heart to you. You continued to show him great kindness. You gave him a son to sit on his throne today.

**7** O LORD my God, you made me, your servant, king in the place of my father David. But I am only a small child. I do not know how to lead the people.

**8** I, your servant, am among your people that you chose. They are a great people. There are too many of them to count.

**9** So give me, your servant, a heart that understands. Then I can judge your people and know the difference between good and bad. Without your help, no one can judge this great people of yours."

**10** The Lord was happy that Solomon asked for this.

**11** So God said to him, "You asked for this. You did not ask for a long life for yourself. You did not ask for riches. You did not ask for the death of your enemies. You asked for wisdom to know what is right.

**12** Thus I will do what you asked. I will give you a wise heart that understands. No person before you was like you, and no person after you will be like you.

**13** I will also give you what you did not ask for. I will give you riches and honor. No other king will be like you during all of your life.

**14** Live in my way and obey my laws and my commands, as your father David did. If you do this, I will give you a long life."

**15** Then Solomon woke up. He knew that it was a dream. He went to Jerusalem and stood in front of the ark of the covenant of the LORD. He gave burnt offerings and peace offerings. Then he made a feast for all his servants.

**16** Then 2 women who were prostitutes came to the king and stood in front of him.

**17** One woman said, "My lord, this woman and I live in the same house. I gave birth to a child while she was in the house with me.

**18** 3 days after I gave birth, this woman also gave birth. We were alone. There was no other person with us in the house. Only the 2 of us were there.

**19** During the night, the son of this woman died, because she lay on him.

**20** She got up at midnight. She took my son from my side while I, your servant, slept. She put my son next to her, and she put her dead son next to me.

**21** In the morning, I got up to feed my son, but he was dead. Then I looked at him carefully in the light. He was not the son that I gave birth to."

**22** The other woman said, "No! The son who lives is mine, and the dead son is yours." But the first woman said, "No! The dead son is yours, and the son who lives is mine." So they argued in front of the king.

**23** Then the king said, "This woman says, 'My son is alive and your son is dead.' The other woman says, 'No! Your son is dead and my son is alive.'"

**24** Then the king said, "Bring me a sword." So they brought a sword to the king.

**25** The king said, "Cut the child who lives into 2 parts. Give half to one woman and half to the other woman."

**26** The real mother of the child who lived loved her son very much. She said to the king, "My lord, give her the child who lives! Do not kill him!" But the other woman said, "The child will be neither mine nor yours. Cut him in 2!"

**27** Then the king said, "Give the child who lives to the first woman. Do not kill him. She is his mother."

**28** All the people of Israel heard about the decision of the king. They respected the king very much. They saw that he had wisdom from God to judge correctly.

## Chapter 4

**1** King Solomon was king over all Israel.

**2** These were his chief officials: Azariah the son of Zadok was the priest.

**3** Elihoreph and Ahijah, the sons of Shisha, were secretaries. Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud was the recorder.

**4** Benaiah the son of Jehoiada was the commander of the army. Zadok and Abiathar were priests.

**5** Azariah the son of Nathan was the chief of the district officials. Zabud the son of Nathan was a priest and the advisor of the king.

**6** Ahishar was the chief of the palace. Adoniram the son of Abda was the chief of the forced workers.

**7** Solomon had 12 district officials over all Israel. They supplied food for the king and his family. Each official supplied food for 1 month of the year.

**8** These are their names: Ben-hur was in the hill country of Ephraim.

**9** Ben-deker was in Makaz, Shaalbim, Beth-shemesh, and Elon-beth-hanan.

**10** Ben-hesed was in Arubboth. Socoh and all the land of Hepher were also his area.

**11** Ben-abinadab was in all the area of Dor. He married Taphath, the daughter of Solomon.

**12** Baana the son of Ahilud had Taanach, Megiddo, and all of Beth-shean. Beth-shean is near Zarethan, below Jezreel. His area went from Beth-shean to Abel-meholah, as far as the area on the other side of Jokmeam.

**13** Ben-geber was in Ramoth-gilead. He had the towns of Jair, the son of Manasseh, in Gilead. He also had the area of Argob in Bashan. There were 60 large cities with walls and bronze bars on their gates.

**14** Ahinadab the son of Iddo had Mahanaim.

**15** Ahimaaz was in Naphtali. He married Basemath, the daughter of Solomon.

**16** Baana the son of Hushai was in Asher and in Bealoth.

**17** Jehoshaphat the son of Paruah was in Issachar.

**18** Shimei the son of Ela was in Benjamin.

**19** Geber the son of Uri was in the land of Gilead. This was the land of Sihon, king of the Amorites, and of Og, king of Bashan. Geber was the only official in that land.

**20** There were as many people in Judah and Israel as the grains of sand on the seashore. They ate and drank, and they were happy.

**21** Solomon ruled over all the kingdoms from the Euphrates River to the land of the Philistines and to the border of Egypt. These kingdoms brought gifts and served Solomon during all of his life.

**22** Each day, Solomon used 30 measures of fine flour and 60 measures of meal.

**23** He also used 10 fat oxen, 20 oxen from the fields, and 100 sheep. He also used deer, gazelles, roebucks, and fat birds.

**24** Solomon ruled over all the area west of the Euphrates River, from Tiphsah to Gaza. He ruled over all the kings west of the river. He had peace on all sides.

**25** During all the life of Solomon, the people of Judah and Israel lived in safety. From Dan to Beersheba, each man sat under his own vine and his own fig tree.

**26** Solomon had 40,000 stalls of horses for his chariots, and 12,000 horsemen.

**27** The district officials supplied food for King Solomon and for all the people who ate at his table. Each official supplied food for his month. They made sure that the king had all that he needed.

**28** They also brought barley and straw for the horses and the fast horses. Each official brought these to the correct place, as instructed.

**29** God gave Solomon very much wisdom and knowledge. His knowledge was as large as the sand on the seashore.

**30** The wisdom of Solomon was greater than the wisdom of all the people of the east. It was greater than all the wisdom of Egypt.

**31** He was wiser than all other men. He was wiser than Ethan the Ezrahite, and Heman, Calcol, and Darda, the sons of Mahol. People in all the nations around Israel knew about him.

**32** He spoke 3,000 proverbs. He wrote 1,005 songs.

**33** He spoke about plants, from the cedar tree in Lebanon to the hyssop plant that grows out of a wall. He also spoke about animals, birds, small creatures that move on the ground, and fish.

**34** People from all nations came to hear the wisdom of Solomon. They came from all the kings of the earth who heard about his wisdom.

## Chapter 5

**1** Hiram, the king of Tyre, sent his servants to Solomon. Hiram heard that the people anointed Solomon king in the place of his father. Hiram was always a friend of David.

**2** Solomon sent this message to Hiram:

**3** "You know that my father David could not build a house for the name of the LORD his God. He had wars with enemies on all sides. He fought them until the LORD put them under his control.

**4** But now the LORD my God gave me peace on all sides. I have no enemy and no trouble.

**5** So I plan to build a house for the name of the LORD my God. The LORD told my father David, 'I will put your son on your throne in your place. Your son will build the house for my name.'

**6** So give a command to your men to cut cedar trees for me in Lebanon. My servants will work with your servants. I will pay your servants the wages that you ask. You know that none of my people can cut wood as well as the Sidonians."

**7** Hiram heard the words of Solomon and he was very happy. He said, "Praise the LORD today. The LORD gave David a wise son to rule over this great nation."

**8** Hiram sent this message to Solomon: "I received your message. I will do all that you want. I will give you cedar wood and pine wood.

**9** My servants will bring the wood down from Lebanon to the sea. I will tie the logs together and float them by sea to the place that you choose. There I will separate the logs, and you will take them. In return, give food to my palace. This is what I want."

**10** So Hiram gave Solomon all the cedar wood and pine wood that he wanted.

**11** Solomon gave Hiram 20,000 measures of wheat as food for his palace. He also gave him 20 measures of pure olive oil. Solomon gave these to Hiram each year.

**12** The LORD gave Solomon wisdom, as he promised. There was peace between Hiram and Solomon. The 2 kings made a treaty.

**13** King Solomon made men from all Israel do forced work. There were 30,000 men.

**14** He sent them to Lebanon in groups of 10,000 men each month. Each group worked 1 month in Lebanon and then stayed home for 2 months. Adoniram was the chief of the forced workers.

**15** Solomon also had 70,000 men who carried loads and 80,000 men who cut stone in the mountains.

**16** He also had 3,300 chief officials who controlled the work. They supervised the men who did the work.

**17** The king gave a command, and the workers cut large, expensive stones. They used these cut stones for the foundation of the house.

**18** The builders of Solomon, the builders of Hiram, and the men from Gebal cut the stones. They prepared the wood and the stones to build the house.

## Chapter 6

**1** Solomon started to build the house of the LORD in the 480th year after the people of Israel came out of Egypt. This was in the 4th year that Solomon was king over Israel. It was in the month Ziv, which is the 2nd month.

**2** The house that King Solomon built for the LORD was 60 cubits long, 20 cubits wide, and 30 cubits high.

**3** The porch in front of the main room of the house was 20 cubits long. This was the same as the width of the house. The porch went out 10 cubits from the front of the house.

**4** He made windows with frames in the house.

**5** He built rooms against the walls of the house, on all sides. The rooms were around the main room and the inner room. He made side rooms all around.

**6** The lowest floor of rooms was 5 cubits wide. The middle floor was 6 cubits wide. The 3rd floor was 7 cubits wide. He made the outer wall of the house thinner at each floor. Thus the beams did not go into the walls of the house.

**7** The workers prepared the stones at the quarry before they brought them to the house. Thus no person heard a hammer, an axe, or any iron tool at the house while they built it.

**8** The door to the lowest floor of rooms was on the south side of the house. Stairs went up to the middle floor, and from the middle floor to the 3rd floor.

**9** So Solomon built the house and finished it. He made the roof of the house with beams and boards of cedar.

**10** He built the side rooms against all the house. Each floor was 5 cubits high. Cedar beams attached the rooms to the house.

**11** Then the word of the LORD came to Solomon. The LORD said,

**12** "You build this house now. Obey my laws, do what I command, and keep all my commands. If you do this, then I will keep the promise that I made to your father David.

**13** I will live among the people of Israel. I will not leave my people Israel."

**14** So Solomon built the house and finished it.

**15** He covered the inside walls of the house with cedar boards, from the floor to the ceiling. He covered the floor of the house with pine boards.

**16** He built a room 20 cubits long at the back of the house. He covered its walls with cedar boards from the floor to the ceiling. He built this inner room as the Most Holy Place.

**17** The main room in front of the inner room was 40 cubits long.

**18** The cedar inside the house had carvings of gourds and open flowers. All of it was cedar. No person could see a stone.

**19** He prepared the inner room in the house to put the ark of the covenant of the LORD there.

**20** The inner room was 20 cubits long, 20 cubits wide, and 20 cubits high. He covered it with pure gold. He also covered the altar of cedar with gold.

**21** Solomon covered the inside of the house with pure gold. He put gold chains across the front of the inner room. He covered the inner room with gold.

**22** He covered the whole house with gold until he finished all the house. He also covered with gold the whole altar that was near the inner room.

**23** In the inner room he made 2 cherubim (angel figures) of olive wood. Each cherub was 10 cubits high.

**24** One wing of a cherub was 5 cubits long, and the other wing was 5 cubits long. From the end of one wing to the end of the other wing was 10 cubits.

**25** The 2nd cherub was also 10 cubits. The 2 cherubim had the same measurements and the same shape.

**26** One cherub was 10 cubits high. The other cherub was also 10 cubits high.

**27** He put the cherubim inside the inner room. Their wings were open. The wing of one cherub touched one wall. The wing of the other cherub touched the other wall. Their wings touched each other in the middle of the room.

**28** He covered the cherubim with gold.

**29** On all the walls of the house, in the inner room and the outer room, he carved figures of cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers.

**30** He covered the floor of the house with gold, in the inner room and the outer room.

**31** For the entrance of the inner room he made doors of olive wood. The top beam and the side posts had 5 sides.

**32** The 2 doors were of olive wood. He carved cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers on them. He covered the doors with gold. He put gold on the cherubim and the palm trees.

**33** For the entrance of the main room, he also made door posts of olive wood. These posts had 4 sides.

**34** He made 2 doors of pine wood. Each door had 2 parts that folded.

**35** He carved cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers on the doors. He covered the carvings with gold.

**36** He built the inner courtyard with 3 rows of cut stone and 1 row of cedar beams.

**37** The workers put the foundation of the house of the LORD in the 4th year of Solomon, in the month Ziv.

**38** In the 11th year, in the month Bul, which is the 8th month, they finished the house. They made all of it as the plans showed. Solomon used 7 years to build the house.

## Chapter 7

**1** Solomon used 13 years to build his own palace. Then he finished all of his palace.

**2** He also built the House of the Forest of Lebanon. It was 100 cubits long, 50 cubits wide, and 30 cubits high. It had 4 rows of cedar pillars, with cedar beams on the pillars.

**3** It had a roof of cedar above the beams. The beams were on 45 pillars, 15 in each row.

**4** There were windows in 3 rows. Each window was across from another window in 3 rows.

**5** All the doors and door posts had square frames. The windows were across from each other in 3 rows.

**6** He made the Hall of Pillars. It was 50 cubits long and 30 cubits wide. There was a porch in front of it, with pillars and a roof.

**7** He made the Hall of the Throne, where he judged the people. This was the Hall of Judgment. He covered it with cedar from the floor to the ceiling.

**8** His own palace, where he lived, was in another courtyard behind the hall. The workers made it in the same way. Solomon also made a palace like this hall for the daughter of Pharaoh, his wife.

**9** The workers made all these buildings of expensive stones. They cut the stones to the correct size with saws on the inside and the outside. They used these stones from the foundation to the top of the walls, and outside to the great courtyard.

**10** The workers made the foundation of large, expensive stones. Some stones were 10 cubits long and some were 8 cubits long.

**11** Above the foundation were expensive stones, cut to the correct size, and cedar beams.

**12** The great courtyard had 3 rows of cut stone and 1 row of cedar beams around it. The workers also made the inner courtyard of the house of the LORD and the porch of the house in this way.

**13** King Solomon sent men to bring Hiram from Tyre.

**14** Hiram was the son of a widow from the tribe of Naphtali. His father was a man from Tyre who made things from bronze. Hiram had much wisdom, knowledge, and skill to make all kinds of bronze work. He came to King Solomon and did all his work.

**15** He made 2 bronze pillars. Each pillar was 18 cubits high and 12 cubits around.

**16** He made 2 tops of bronze to put on the pillars. Each top was 5 cubits high.

**17** He made nets of chains to decorate the tops of the pillars. There were 7 for each top.

**18** He made 2 rows of pomegranates around each net to decorate the tops of the pillars. He did the same for the 2nd top.

**19** The tops of the pillars in the porch had the shape of lilies. They were 4 cubits high.

**20** On the tops of the 2 pillars, above the round part next to the net, there were 200 pomegranates in rows around each top.

**21** He put up the pillars at the porch of the main room. He put up the pillar on the south side and gave it the name Jachin. He put up the pillar on the north side and gave it the name Boaz.

**22** The tops of the pillars had the shape of lilies. So he finished the work on the pillars.

**23** He made a large round bowl of bronze, called the Sea. It was 10 cubits from one edge to the other edge. It was 5 cubits high and 30 cubits around.

**24** Below the edge, there were gourds all around it, 10 for each cubit. The gourds were in 2 rows. Hiram made the gourds and the Sea in 1 piece.

**25** The Sea was on the backs of 12 bronze oxen. 3 oxen looked north, 3 looked west, 3 looked south, and 3 looked east. The Sea was on top of them. The back parts of the oxen were toward the center.

**26** The Sea was as thick as the width of a hand. Its edge was like the edge of a cup, like the flower of a lily. The Sea held 2,000 baths of water.

**27** He also made 10 bronze stands. Each stand was 4 cubits long, 4 cubits wide, and 3 cubits high.

**28** This is how he made the stands. They had side panels, and the panels were between frames.

**29** On the panels between the frames there were lions, oxen, and cherubim. On the frames there was a stand above. Above and below the lions and oxen there were decorations.

**30** Each stand had 4 bronze wheels with bronze axles. At the 4 corners there were supports for a basin. Hiram made the supports of cast bronze, with decorations on each side.

**31** On the stand there was a round hole for the basin. The hole was 1 cubit deep and 1.5 cubits wide. There were carvings around the hole. The side panels were square, not round.

**32** The 4 wheels were under the side panels. Hiram attached the axles of the wheels to the stand. Each wheel was 1.5 cubits high.

**33** Hiram made the wheels like the wheels of a chariot. He made their axles, hubs, rims, and spokes all of cast bronze.

**34** Each stand had 4 supports at its 4 corners. The supports were part of the stand.

**35** At the top of the stand there was a round band, 0.5 cubit high. The supports and the side panels at the top of the stand were part of the stand.

**36** On the surfaces of the supports and side panels, he carved cherubim, lions, and palm trees. He put them where there was space, with decorations all around.

**37** He made the 10 stands in this way. All of them had the same mold, the same measurements, and the same shape.

**38** Then he made 10 bronze basins. Each basin held 40 baths of water. Each basin was 4 cubits wide. He put 1 basin on each of the 10 stands.

**39** He put 5 stands on the south side of the house and 5 stands on the north side. He put the Sea on the south side of the house, at the southeast corner.

**40** Hiram also made the basins, the shovels, and the bowls. So Hiram finished all the work that he did for King Solomon on the house of the LORD:

**41** He made the 2 pillars and the 2 round tops on the pillars. He made the 2 nets to cover the 2 round tops on the pillars.

**42** He made 400 pomegranates for the 2 nets. There were 2 rows of pomegranates for each net, to cover the 2 round tops on the pillars.

**43** He made the 10 stands and the 10 basins on the stands.

**44** He made the Sea and the 12 oxen under the Sea.

**45** He made the pots, the shovels, and the bowls. Hiram made all these items for King Solomon for the house of the LORD. He made them of polished bronze.

**46** The king cast them in clay molds in the plain of the Jordan River, between Succoth and Zarethan.

**47** Solomon did not weigh all these items, because there were very many. No one found out the weight of the bronze.

**48** Solomon also made all the items for the house of the LORD. He made the gold altar and the gold table for the bread of the Presence.

**49** He made the lampstands of pure gold, 5 on the south side and 5 on the north side, in front of the inner room. He made the gold flowers, lamps, and tongs.

**50** He made the cups, the lamp cutters, the bowls, the dishes, and the fire pans of pure gold. He made the gold hinges for the doors of the inner room, the Most Holy Place, and for the doors of the main room.

**51** So King Solomon finished all the work on the house of the LORD. Then Solomon brought in the things that his father David gave to the LORD. He put the silver, the gold, and the other items in the treasury of the house of the LORD.

## Chapter 8

**1** Then King Solomon called the elders of Israel to Jerusalem. He called all the leaders of the tribes and the chiefs of the families of Israel. They came to bring up the ark of the covenant of the LORD from the City of David, which is Zion.

**2** All the men of Israel came together to King Solomon at the feast in the month Ethanim. This is the 7th month.

**3** All the elders of Israel came, and the priests lifted up the ark.

**4** The priests and the Levites brought up the ark of the LORD and the tent where the people came together. They also brought up all the holy items that were in the tent.

**5** King Solomon and all the people of Israel who came to him were in front of the ark. They killed sheep and oxen as sacrifices. There were too many to count.

**6** The priests brought the ark of the covenant of the LORD to its place. They put it in the inner room of the house, the Most Holy Place, under the wings of the cherubim.

**7** The wings of the cherubim were open above the place of the ark. Thus the cherubim covered the ark and its poles.

**8** The poles were very long. People in the Holy Place in front of the inner room could see the ends of the poles. But people outside could not see them. The poles are there today.

**9** There was nothing in the ark except the 2 stone tablets. Moses put them there at Horeb. There the LORD made a covenant with the people of Israel after they came out of Egypt.

**10** The priests came out of the Holy Place. Then a cloud filled the house of the LORD.

**11** The priests could not stand and do their work because of the cloud. The glory of the LORD filled the house of the LORD.

**12** Then Solomon said, "The LORD said that he will live in thick darkness.

**13** I surely built a great house for you. It is a place for you to live in forever."

**14** Then the king turned around and blessed all the people of Israel, while they stood there.

**15** He said, "Praise the LORD, the God of Israel. He made a promise to my father David with his own mouth. Now he made it come true with his own power. He said,

**16** 'Since the day that I brought my people Israel out of Egypt, I did not choose a city from any tribe of Israel. I did not choose a city to build a house for my name. But I chose David to rule over my people Israel.'

**17** My father David wanted to build a house for the name of the LORD, the God of Israel.

**18** But the LORD said to my father David, 'You wanted to build a house for my name. It was good that you wanted to do this.

**19** But you will not build the house. Your own son will build the house for my name.'

**20** The LORD kept the promise that he made. I took the place of my father David. I sit on the throne of Israel, as the LORD promised. I built the house for the name of the LORD, the God of Israel.

**21** I made a place there for the ark. In the ark is the covenant of the LORD. He made this covenant with our ancestors when he brought them out of Egypt."

**22** Then Solomon stood in front of the altar of the LORD, in front of all the people of Israel. He lifted his hands toward heaven.

**23** He said, "LORD, God of Israel, there is no God like you in heaven above or on the earth below. You keep your covenant and show your love to your servants who obey you with all their heart.

**24** You kept your promise to your servant David, my father. You made the promise with your mouth. Today you made it come true with your power.

**25** Now, LORD, God of Israel, keep the other promise that you made to your servant David, my father. You said, 'A man from your family will always sit on the throne of Israel in front of me. But your sons must be careful to obey me as you did.'

**26** Now, God of Israel, please make your word come true. You spoke this word to your servant David, my father.

**27** But will God really live on the earth? Heaven, and the highest heaven, cannot hold you. This house that I built cannot hold you either!

**28** But listen to the prayer of your servant and to his request, O LORD my God. Hear the cry and the prayer that your servant prays to you today.

**29** Watch this house night and day. You said about this place, 'My name will be there.' Hear the prayer that your servant prays toward this place.

**30** Hear the requests of your servant and of your people Israel when they pray toward this place. Hear from heaven, the place where you live. When you hear, forgive.

**31** A man can do wrong to his neighbor and must make an oath. He can come and make the oath in front of your altar in this house.

**32** Then hear from heaven and act. Judge your servants. Punish the guilty person for what he did. Declare that the innocent person is not guilty, and reward him for his good actions.

**33** Your people Israel can lose a battle against an enemy because they sinned against you. Then they can turn back to you and praise your name. They can pray to you and ask for help in this house.

**34** Then hear from heaven and forgive the sin of your people Israel. Bring them back to the land that you gave to their ancestors.

**35** The sky can close and there can be no rain, because the people sinned against you. You can punish them in this way. Then they can pray toward this place, praise your name, and stop their sin.

**36** Then hear from heaven and forgive the sin of your servants, your people Israel. Teach them the good way to live. Send rain on your land, which you gave to your people as their possession.

**37** There can be famine or disease in the land. There can be damage to the crops from hot wind, mold, locusts, or grasshoppers. An enemy can attack the cities of the land. There can be any disaster or any sickness.

**38** Then any person or all your people Israel can pray and ask for help. Each person can know his own trouble and lift his hands toward this house.

**39** Then hear from heaven, the place where you live. Forgive and act. Give to each person what is correct for all that he does. You know his heart. Only you know the hearts of all people.

**40** Then they will respect you during all their life in the land that you gave to our ancestors.

**41** A foreigner who is not from your people Israel can come from a far country because of your name.

**42** He will hear about your great name and your great power. He can come and pray toward this house.

**43** Then hear from heaven, the place where you live. Do all that the foreigner asks of you. Then all the people of the earth will know your name. They will respect you, as your people Israel do. They will know that this house that I built has your name.

**44** Your people can go out to fight against their enemy, in the direction that you send them. They can pray to the LORD toward the city that you chose and toward the house that I built for your name.

**45** Then hear their prayer and their request from heaven. Give them success.

**46** Your people can sin against you, because all people sin. You can become angry with them and give them to an enemy. The enemy can take them as prisoners to the land of the enemy, far away or near.

**47** In that land, they can change their minds and turn back to you. They can ask you for help in the land of their enemies. They can say, 'We sinned. We did wrong. We did evil things.'

**48** They can turn back to you with all their heart and all their soul in the land of their enemies. They can pray to you toward their land that you gave to their ancestors. They can pray toward the city that you chose and the house that I built for your name.

**49** Then hear their prayer and their request from heaven, the place where you live. Give them justice.

**50** Forgive your people who sinned against you. Forgive all the wrong things that they did against you. Make their enemies show mercy to them.

**51** They are your people and your possession. You brought them out of Egypt, as from a hot iron furnace.

**52** Listen to the requests of your servant and of your people Israel. Hear them each time that they call to you.

**53** You chose them from all the nations of the earth to be your own people. You said this through your servant Moses when you brought our ancestors out of Egypt, O Lord GOD."

**54** Solomon finished all this prayer and request to the LORD. He was on his knees in front of the altar of the LORD, with his hands lifted toward heaven. Then he stood up.

**55** He stood and blessed all the people of Israel with a loud voice. He said,

**56** "Praise the LORD! He gave peace to his people Israel, as he promised. He kept every word of all the good promises that he made through his servant Moses.

**57** Let the LORD our God be with us, as he was with our ancestors. Let him never leave us.

**58** Let him turn our hearts to him. Then we will live in all his ways. We will obey his commands, his laws, and his rules that he gave to our ancestors.

**59** I prayed these words to the LORD. Let the LORD our God remember these words day and night. Let him help his servant and his people Israel each day, as they need it.

**60** Then all the people of the earth will know that the LORD is God. There is no other god.

**61** You must be fully faithful to the LORD our God. Live by his laws and obey his commands, as you do today."

**62** Then the king and all the people of Israel with him gave sacrifices to the LORD.

**63** Solomon gave 22,000 oxen and 120,000 sheep as peace offerings to the LORD. In this way, the king and all the people of Israel dedicated the house of the LORD.

**64** On the same day, the king made holy the middle of the courtyard in front of the house of the LORD. There he gave the burnt offerings, the grain offerings, and the fat of the peace offerings. He did this because the bronze altar in front of the LORD was too small. It could not hold all these offerings.

**65** At that time Solomon and all the people of Israel had a feast. A very large group of people came from Lebo-hamath to the Brook of Egypt. They had the feast in front of the LORD our God for 7 days and then 7 more days, a total of 14 days.

**66** On the next day, Solomon sent the people home. They blessed the king. They went to their homes with joy and happy hearts. They were happy because of all the good things that the LORD did for his servant David and for his people Israel.

## Chapter 9

**1** Solomon finished the house of the LORD and his palace. He finished all that he wanted to build.

**2** Then the LORD came to Solomon a 2nd time, as he came to him at Gibeon.

**3** The LORD said to him, "I heard your prayer and your request to me. I made holy this house that you built. I put my name there forever. My eyes and my heart will always be there.

**4** You must obey me, as your father David did, with an honest and good heart. Do all that I commanded you, and obey my laws and my rules.

**5** If you do this, then I will make your throne over Israel strong forever. I promised this to your father David. I said, 'A man from your family will always be on the throne of Israel.'

**6** But you or your sons can turn away from me. You can disobey my commands and my laws that I gave to you. You can go and serve other gods and worship them.

**7** If you do this, then I will remove Israel from the land that I gave them. I will reject this house that I made holy for my name. All the nations will make jokes about Israel and insult Israel.

**8** This house is now very great. But all people who go past it will be very surprised and afraid. They will make sounds to show their contempt. They will ask, 'Why did the LORD do this to this land and to this house?'

**9** Other people will answer, 'The people left the LORD their God. The LORD brought their ancestors out of Egypt. But the people followed other gods. They worshipped and served those gods. Thus the LORD brought all this disaster on them.'"

**10** Solomon used 20 years to build the 2 buildings, the house of the LORD and the palace of the king.

**11** Hiram, the king of Tyre, gave Solomon all the cedar wood, pine wood, and gold that he wanted. At the end of the 20 years, King Solomon gave Hiram 20 cities in the land of Galilee.

**12** Hiram came from Tyre to see the cities that Solomon gave him. But Hiram did not like them.

**13** Hiram said, "My brother, what are these cities that you gave me?" He gave them the name "the land of Cabul" ("worthless land"). They still have this name today.

**14** Hiram sent 120 talents of gold to the king.

**15** This is the reason for the forced work of King Solomon. He used the forced workers to build the house of the LORD, his palace, the Millo, and the wall of Jerusalem. He also used them to build Hazor, Megiddo, and Gezer.

**16** Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, attacked Gezer and captured it. He burned it with fire and killed the Canaanites who lived in the city. Then he gave the city as a gift to his daughter, the wife of Solomon.

**17** So Solomon built Gezer again. He also built Lower Beth-horon,

**18** Baalath, and Tamar in the wilderness of Judah.

**19** He also built all his cities for supplies, and the cities for his chariots and his horsemen. He built all that he wanted to build in Jerusalem, in Lebanon, and in all the land that he ruled.

**20** Some people were still alive from the Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites. These people were not from Israel.

**21** Their descendants stayed in the land. The people of Israel could not destroy them completely. Solomon made these descendants do forced work as slaves. They still do this work today.

**22** But Solomon did not make slaves of any people of Israel. They were his soldiers, his officials, his commanders, his captains, and the commanders of his chariots and horsemen.

**23** There were 550 chief officials over the work of Solomon. They supervised the people who did the work.

**24** The daughter of Pharaoh moved from the City of David to the palace that Solomon built for her. Then Solomon built the Millo.

**25** 3 times each year, Solomon gave burnt offerings and peace offerings on the altar that he built for the LORD. He also burned incense in front of the LORD. So he finished the work on the house.

**26** King Solomon also built ships at Ezion-geber. This town is near Eloth, on the shore of the Red Sea, in the land of Edom.

**27** Hiram sent his servants with the ships. They were sailors who knew the sea. They worked with the servants of Solomon.

**28** They went to Ophir and got 420 talents of gold. They brought the gold to King Solomon.

## Chapter 10

**1** The queen of Sheba heard about the fame of Solomon and his relation to the name of the LORD. So she came to test him with difficult questions.

**2** She came to Jerusalem with a very large group of servants. She had camels that carried spices, very much gold, and expensive stones. She came to Solomon and asked him about all that was in her mind.

**3** Solomon answered all her questions. No question was too difficult for the king to explain to her.

**4** The queen of Sheba saw all the wisdom of Solomon. She saw the palace that he built.

**5** She saw the food on his table and the seats of his officials. She saw his servants as they served and their clothes. She saw his cup carriers and the burnt offerings that he gave at the house of the LORD. She was very surprised.

**6** She said to the king, "The report that I heard in my own country about your actions and your wisdom is true.

**7** But I did not believe the report until I came and saw with my own eyes. Now I see that the report did not tell me even half. Your wisdom and your riches are much greater than the report that I heard.

**8** Your men are very fortunate! Your servants who always stand in front of you and hear your wisdom are very fortunate!

**9** Praise the LORD your God! He was happy with you and put you on the throne of Israel. The LORD always loves Israel. Thus he made you king to do what is fair and correct."

**10** Then she gave the king 120 talents of gold, very many spices, and expensive stones. No one ever again brought as many spices as the queen of Sheba gave to King Solomon.

**11** The ships of Hiram that brought gold from Ophir also brought very much almug wood and expensive stones from Ophir.

**12** The king used the almug wood to make supports for the house of the LORD and for the palace of the king. He also made harps and lyres for the singers. No one ever again brought almug wood like this. No one saw wood like this again, up to this day.

**13** King Solomon gave the queen of Sheba all that she wanted and all that she asked for. He also gave her many other gifts from his riches. Then she went back to her own country with her servants.

**14** Each year Solomon received 666 talents of gold.

**15** He also received money from the traders and from the business of the merchants. He received money from all the kings of Arabia and from the governors of the land.

**16** King Solomon made 200 large shields of hammered gold. He used 600 shekels of gold for each shield.

**17** He also made 300 small shields of hammered gold. He used 3 minas of gold for each shield. The king put them in the House of the Forest of Lebanon.

**18** The king also made a large throne of ivory and covered it with the best gold.

**19** The throne had 6 steps. The back of the throne had a round top. There were arm rests on each side of the seat. A lion stood next to each arm rest.

**20** 12 lions stood on the 6 steps, 1 at each end of each step. No other kingdom had a throne like this one.

**21** All the cups of King Solomon were of gold. All the dishes in the House of the Forest of Lebanon were of pure gold. No item was of silver, because silver had little value in the time of Solomon.

**22** The king had ships of Tarshish at sea, with the ships of Hiram. Each 3 years the ships of Tarshish came back. They brought gold, silver, ivory, monkeys, and peacocks.

**23** King Solomon was richer and wiser than all the other kings of the earth.

**24** People from all the earth wanted to see Solomon. They wanted to hear the wisdom that God put in his heart.

**25** Each year each visitor brought a gift. They brought items of silver and gold, clothes, weapons, spices, horses, and mules.

**26** Solomon collected chariots and horsemen. He had 1,400 chariots and 12,000 horsemen. He kept them in the cities for chariots and with him in Jerusalem.

**27** In Jerusalem the king made silver as common as stones. He made cedar wood as common as the sycamore trees in the low hills.

**28** Solomon got his horses from Egypt and from Kue. The merchants of the king bought them from Kue for a price.

**29** A chariot from Egypt cost 600 shekels of silver. A horse cost 150 shekels. The merchants also sold horses and chariots to all the kings of the Hittites and the kings of Syria.

## Chapter 11

**1** But King Solomon loved many foreign women. He loved the daughter of Pharaoh and also women from Moab, Ammon, Edom, Sidon, and the Hittites.

**2** These women were from the nations about which the LORD gave a command to the people of Israel. He said, "Do not marry people from these nations. They will surely turn your hearts to their gods." But Solomon loved these women very much.

**3** He had 700 wives who were princesses and 300 secondary wives (concubines). His wives turned his heart away from the LORD.

**4** When Solomon was old, his wives turned his heart to other gods. His heart was not fully faithful to the LORD his God. His heart was not like the heart of his father David.

**5** Solomon worshipped Ashtoreth, the goddess of the Sidonians. He worshipped Milcom, the terrible idol of the Ammonites.

**6** So Solomon did evil in the sight of the LORD. He did not fully follow the LORD, as his father David did.

**7** Solomon built a high place on the hill east of Jerusalem for Chemosh, the terrible idol of Moab. He also built a high place for Molech, the terrible idol of the Ammonites.

**8** He did the same for all his foreign wives. They burned incense and made sacrifices to their gods.

**9** The LORD was angry with Solomon, because his heart turned away from the LORD, the God of Israel. The LORD came to him 2 times.

**10** The LORD told him not to follow other gods. But Solomon did not obey the command of the LORD.

**11** So the LORD said to Solomon, "You did this. You did not keep my covenant and my laws that I commanded you. Thus I will surely take the kingdom away from you. I will give it to one of your servants.

**12** But I will not do this during your life, because of your father David. I will take the kingdom away from your son.

**13** But I will not take away all the kingdom. I will give 1 tribe to your son because of my servant David and because of Jerusalem, the city that I chose."

**14** Then the LORD caused an enemy to attack Solomon. This enemy was Hadad from Edom. He was from the family of the king of Edom.

**15** Before this, David fought against Edom. Joab, the commander of the army, went to bury the dead men. Joab killed every male in Edom.

**16** Joab and all the army of Israel stayed there for 6 months. They killed every male in Edom.

**17** But Hadad ran away to Egypt with some servants of his father from Edom. Hadad was a small boy at that time.

**18** They left Midian and went to Paran. They took men from Paran with them and went to Egypt, to Pharaoh, the king of Egypt. Pharaoh gave Hadad a house, food, and land.

**19** Pharaoh liked Hadad very much. So Pharaoh gave Hadad the sister of his wife, Queen Tahpenes, as his wife.

**20** The sister of Tahpenes gave birth to a son for Hadad. The name of the son was Genubath. Tahpenes raised him in the palace of Pharaoh. Genubath lived in the palace with the sons of Pharaoh.

**21** In Egypt, Hadad heard that David died. He also heard that Joab, the commander of the army, was dead. Then Hadad said to Pharaoh, "Let me go back to my own country."

**22** Pharaoh said to him, "Do you need something here? Why do you want to go back to your own country?" Hadad answered, "I need nothing. But please let me go."

**23** God also caused another enemy to attack Solomon. This enemy was Rezon the son of Eliada. Rezon ran away from his master Hadadezer, king of Zobah.

**24** David killed the army of Zobah. After this, Rezon collected men and became the leader of a band of fighters. They went to Damascus and lived there. Rezon became king in Damascus.

**25** Rezon was an enemy of Israel during all the life of Solomon. He caused trouble, as Hadad also did. Rezon hated Israel and he ruled over Syria.

**26** Jeroboam the son of Nebat also rebelled against the king. Jeroboam was an official of Solomon. He was an Ephraimite from Zeredah. His mother was a widow, and her name was Zeruah.

**27** This is the story of how Jeroboam rebelled against the king. Solomon built the Millo and repaired the holes in the wall of the City of David, his father.

**28** Jeroboam was a strong and capable man. Solomon saw that the young man worked hard. So Solomon made him the chief of all the forced workers from the tribes of Joseph.

They did not live in my ways. They did not do what is right in my sight. They did not obey my laws and my rules, as David, the father of Solomon, did.

**9** He said to them, "These people said to me, 'Make the load that your father put on us lighter.' What answer do you tell me to give to them?"

**13** The king gave the people a hard answer. He rejected the advice of the old men.

**16** All the people of Israel saw that the king did not listen to them. So they answered the king, "We have no share in David! We have no part in the son of Jesse! People of Israel, go home! David, take care of your own family!" So the people of Israel went to their homes.

**17** But Rehoboam still ruled over the people of Israel who lived in the cities of Judah.

**18** Then King Rehoboam sent Adoram, the chief of the forced workers. But all the people of Israel threw stones at Adoram until he died. King Rehoboam quickly got into his chariot and ran away to Jerusalem.

**24** 'The LORD says this: Do not go up and fight against your brothers, the people of Israel. Each man must go home. I caused this to occur.'" So the people obeyed the word of the LORD. They went home, as the LORD told them.

On you, Josiah will kill the priests of the high places who now burn incense on you. Josiah will burn human bones on you.'"

**16** The man of God said, "I cannot go back with you or go into your home. I will not eat food or drink water with you in this place.

**26** The old prophet who brought the man back heard about this. He said, "That is the man of God who disobeyed the word of the LORD. Thus the LORD gave him to the lion. The lion attacked him and killed him, as the LORD told him."

**27** Then the old prophet said to his sons, "Put a saddle on the donkey for me." So they did.

**28** He went and found the body on the road. The donkey and the lion stood next to the body. The lion did not eat the body. The lion did not attack the donkey.

**1** At that time Abijah, the son of Jeroboam, became sick.

## Chapter 17

**1** Elijah from Tishbe in Gilead said to Ahab, "I serve the LORD, the God of Israel. As surely as the LORD lives, there will be no dew or rain during the next years. There will be rain only when I say so."

**2** Then the word of the LORD came to Elijah. The LORD said,

**3** "Go away from here. Go east and hide near the Brook Cherith, east of the Jordan River.

**4** You will drink water from the brook. I commanded the ravens to bring you food there."

**5** So Elijah did as the LORD told him. He went and lived near the Brook Cherith, east of the Jordan River.

**6** The ravens brought him bread and meat in the morning and in the evening. He drank water from the brook.

**7** After some time the brook became dry, because there was no rain in the land.

**8** Then the word of the LORD came to Elijah. The LORD said,

**9** "Go to Zarephath, a town near Sidon, and live there. I commanded a widow there to give you food."

**10** So Elijah went to Zarephath. At the gate of the town, he saw a widow who collected sticks. He called to her, "Please bring me a little water in a cup. I want to drink."

**11** She went to get the water. Then he called to her, "Please also bring me a piece of bread."

**12** She said, "As surely as the LORD your God lives, I have no bread. I have only a handful of flour in a jar and a little oil in a jar. Now I collect some sticks. Then I will go home and make the last meal for my son and me. We will eat it, and then we will die."

**13** Elijah said to her, "Do not be afraid. Go and do what you said. But first make a small loaf of bread for me and bring it to me. Then make food for yourself and your son.

**14** The LORD, the God of Israel, says this: 'The jar of flour will not become empty. The jar of oil will not become empty. This will continue until the day that the LORD sends rain on the land.'"

**15** So she went and did as Elijah told her. She, Elijah, and her family ate for many days.

**16** The jar of flour did not become empty. The jar of oil did not become empty. This was what the LORD said through Elijah.

**17** After this, the son of the woman became sick. She was the owner of the house. His sickness became worse and worse until he had no breath in him.

**18** She said to Elijah, "Man of God, what do you have against me? Did you come to remind God of my sin and to kill my son?"

**19** Elijah said to her, "Give me your son." He took the boy from her arms. He carried him up to the upper room where Elijah stayed. He put the boy on his own bed.

**20** Then he called to the LORD. He said, "O LORD my God, I stay with this widow. Did you also bring trouble on her? Did you kill her son?"

**21** Then Elijah lay on the boy 3 times. He called to the LORD, "O LORD my God, please let the life of this boy come back into him."

**22** The LORD heard the voice of Elijah. The life of the boy came back into him, and he lived.

**23** Elijah took the boy down from the upper room into the house. He gave the boy to his mother. Elijah said, "Look, your son is alive."

**24** The woman said to Elijah, "Now I know that you are a man of God. I know that the word of the LORD from your mouth is true."

## Chapter 18

**1** After a long time, in the 3rd year, the word of the LORD came to Elijah. The LORD said, "Go and show yourself to Ahab. I will send rain on the land."

**2** So Elijah went to show himself to Ahab. At that time, the famine in Samaria was very bad.

**3** Ahab called Obadiah, the chief of his palace. Obadiah respected the LORD very much.

**4** Before this, Jezebel killed the prophets of the LORD. Then Obadiah took 100 prophets and hid them in 2 caves, 50 in each cave. He gave them bread and water.

**5** Ahab said to Obadiah, "Go through the land to all the springs and all the valleys. Maybe we can find grass to keep the horses and mules alive. Then we will not lose all our animals."

**6** So they divided the land between them to look through all of it. Ahab went in one direction alone. Obadiah went in a different direction alone.

**7** While Obadiah went on the road, Elijah met him. Obadiah knew who Elijah was. He bowed down with his face to the ground and said, "Is it you, my lord Elijah?"

**8** Elijah answered him, "Yes, it is I. Go and tell your master, 'Elijah is here.'"

**9** Obadiah said, "What did I do wrong? Why do you give me, your servant, to Ahab to kill me?

**10** As surely as the LORD your God lives, my master sent men to look for you in all nations and kingdoms. When a nation said, 'Elijah is not here,' Ahab made that nation promise that they did not find you.

**11** And now you tell me, 'Go and tell your master that Elijah is here.'

**12** When I leave you, the Spirit of the LORD will take you to a place that I do not know. Then I will go and tell Ahab, and he will not find you. Then he will kill me. But I, your servant, respected the LORD since I was young.

**13** My lord, did no one tell you what I did when Jezebel killed the prophets of the LORD? I hid 100 prophets of the LORD in 2 caves, 50 in each cave. I gave them bread and water.

**14** And now you tell me, 'Go and tell your master that Elijah is here.' He will kill me!"

**15** Elijah said, "I serve the LORD of armies. As surely as the LORD of armies lives, I will show myself to Ahab today."

**16** So Obadiah went to meet Ahab and told him. Then Ahab went to meet Elijah.

**17** Ahab saw Elijah and said to him, "Is it you, the man who causes trouble for Israel?"

**18** Elijah answered, "I do not cause trouble for Israel. You and the family of your father cause the trouble. You disobeyed the commands of the LORD and followed the Baals.

**19** Now send a message to all the people of Israel. Tell them to meet me at Mount Carmel. Also bring the 450 prophets of Baal and the 400 prophets of Asherah, who eat at the table of Jezebel."

**20** So Ahab sent a message to all the people of Israel. He brought the prophets together at Mount Carmel.

**21** Elijah went in front of all the people and said, "How long will you be unable to decide between 2 choices? If the LORD is God, follow him. But if Baal is God, follow Baal." But the people said nothing.

**22** Then Elijah said to the people, "I am the only prophet of the LORD who is still alive. But Baal has 450 prophets.

**23** Bring us 2 bulls. Let the prophets of Baal choose 1 bull for themselves. Let them cut it into pieces and put it on the wood. But they must not light a fire. I will prepare the other bull and put it on the wood. I also will not light a fire.

**24** Then you will call to your god, and I will call to the LORD. The God who answers with fire is the true God." Then all the people said, "That is a good plan."

**25** Elijah said to the prophets of Baal, "Choose 1 bull for yourselves and prepare it first, because there are many of you. Call to your god. But do not light a fire."

**26** So they took the bull that they received, and they prepared it. They called to Baal from the morning until noon. They shouted, "Baal, answer us!" But there was no voice. No one answered. They danced around the altar that they made.

**27** At noon, Elijah laughed at them. He said, "Shout louder! Surely he is a god! Maybe he thinks deeply, or he is busy, or he is on a trip. Maybe he sleeps and you must wake him up!"

**28** So they shouted louder. They cut themselves with swords and spears until blood flowed. This was their custom.

**29** Noon went past, and they continued to shout like prophets until the time of the evening sacrifice. But there was no voice. No one answered. No one paid attention.

**30** Then Elijah said to all the people, "Come near to me." All the people came near to him. Elijah repaired the altar of the LORD, which someone broke down.

**31** Elijah took 12 stones, 1 stone for each tribe of the sons of Jacob. The LORD spoke to Jacob and said, "Your name will be Israel."

**32** With the stones, Elijah built an altar in the name of the LORD. Around the altar he dug a ditch. The ditch was large enough to hold 2 measures of seed.

**33** He put the wood on the altar. He cut the bull into pieces and put the pieces on the wood. Then he said, "Fill 4 large jars with water. Pour the water on the sacrifice and on the wood."

**34** Then he said, "Do it again." So they did it again. Then he said, "Do it a 3rd time." So they did it a 3rd time.

**35** The water flowed around the altar. Elijah also filled the ditch with water.

**36** At the time of the evening sacrifice, Elijah the prophet came near the altar. He said, "LORD, God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, show today that you are God in Israel. Show that I am your servant. Show that I did all these things because you commanded me.

**37** Answer me, O LORD, answer me. Then these people will know that you, LORD, are God. They will know that you turn their hearts back to you."

**38** Then the fire of the LORD fell. It burned the sacrifice, the wood, the stones, and the dirt. It also dried up the water in the ditch.

**39** All the people saw this. They bowed down with their faces to the ground. They said, "The LORD is God! The LORD is God!"

**40** Elijah said to them, "Catch the prophets of Baal. Do not let any of them escape." The people caught them. Elijah took them down to the Brook Kishon and killed them there.

**41** Then Elijah said to Ahab, "Go up, eat and drink. I hear the sound of a heavy rain."

**42** So Ahab went up to eat and drink. But Elijah went up to the top of Mount Carmel. He bowed down to the ground and put his face between his knees.

**43** He said to his servant, "Go up and look toward the sea." The servant went up and looked. He said, "There is nothing." Elijah said, "Go again." He told him to do this 7 times.

**44** The 7th time, the servant said, "A small cloud comes up from the sea. It is as small as the hand of a man." Elijah said, "Go and tell Ahab, 'Prepare your chariot and go down now. If you do not, the rain will stop you.'"

**45** Soon the sky became black with clouds and wind. There was a heavy rain. Ahab rode in his chariot and went to Jezreel.

**46** The power of the LORD was on Elijah. He tied his clothes around his waist. He ran in front of Ahab to the entrance of Jezreel.

## Chapter 19

**1** Ahab told Jezebel all that Elijah did. He told her that Elijah killed all the prophets of Baal with the sword.

**2** So Jezebel sent a messenger to Elijah. The messenger said, "Jezebel says, 'Let the gods punish me very much if I do not kill you by this time tomorrow. I will kill you as you killed the prophets.'"

**3** Elijah was afraid. He got up and ran away to save his life. He went to Beersheba in Judah. He left his servant there.

**4** Then Elijah went 1 day into the wilderness. He sat down under a broom tree. He asked God to let him die. He said, "I cannot continue, LORD. Take my life. I am not better than my ancestors."

**5** He lay down under the broom tree and slept. Then an angel touched him and said, "Get up and eat."

**6** Elijah looked around. Near his head there was bread that someone baked on hot stones, and a jar of water. He ate and drank, and then he lay down again.

**7** The angel of the LORD came again a 2nd time. He touched Elijah and said, "Get up and eat. If you do not eat, the trip will be too long for you."

**8** So Elijah got up and ate and drank. The food gave him strength. He walked for 40 days and 40 nights to Horeb, the mountain of God.

**9** There he went into a cave and stayed the night. Then the word of the LORD came to him. The LORD said, "Elijah, what do you do here?"

**10** Elijah said, "I worked very hard for the LORD, the God of armies. The people of Israel broke your covenant. They destroyed your altars. They killed your prophets with the sword. I am the only prophet who is still alive. Now they try to kill me too."

**11** The LORD said, "Go out and stand on the mountain in front of the LORD." Then the LORD went past. A very strong wind broke the mountains and the rocks into pieces in front of the LORD. But the LORD was not in the wind. After the wind there was an earthquake. But the LORD was not in the earthquake.

**12** After the earthquake there was a fire. But the LORD was not in the fire. After the fire there was a quiet, soft voice.

**13** Elijah heard the voice. He covered his face with his coat. He went out and stood at the entrance of the cave. Then a voice said to him, "Elijah, what do you do here?"

**14** Elijah said, "I worked very hard for the LORD, the God of armies. The people of Israel broke your covenant. They destroyed your altars. They killed your prophets with the sword. I am the only prophet who is still alive. Now they try to kill me too."

**15** The LORD said to him, "Go back the way that you came, to the wilderness of Damascus. When you arrive, anoint Hazael as king over Syria.

**16** Also anoint Jehu the son of Nimshi as king over Israel. Anoint Elisha the son of Shaphat from Abel-meholah as prophet in your place.

**17** Jehu will kill the people who escape from the sword of Hazael. Elisha will kill the people who escape from the sword of Jehu.

**18** But I will keep 7,000 people in Israel alive. These are all the people who did not bow down to Baal and did not kiss his idol."

**19** So Elijah left there and found Elisha the son of Shaphat. Elisha plowed a field with 12 pairs of oxen. Elisha drove the 12th pair. Elijah went to him and put his coat on Elisha.

**20** Elisha left the oxen and ran after Elijah. He said, "Please let me kiss my father and mother goodbye. Then I will follow you." Elijah said to him, "Go back. I did nothing to stop you."

**21** So Elisha went back. He took his pair of oxen and killed them. He burned the wood of the plow to cook the meat. He gave the meat to the people, and they ate. Then Elisha got up and followed Elijah. He became the servant of Elijah.

## Chapter 20

**1** Ben-hadad, the king of Syria, collected all his army. 32 kings were with him, with horses and chariots. He went up and surrounded Samaria and attacked it.

**2** He sent messengers into the city to Ahab, the king of Israel. They said to him, "Ben-hadad says this:

**3** 'Your silver and your gold are mine. Your most beautiful wives and children are also mine.'"

**4** The king of Israel answered, "My lord the king, I agree with what you say. I am yours, and all that I have is yours."

**5** The messengers came back again. They said, "Ben-hadad says this: 'I sent a message to you. I said, "Give me your silver, your gold, your wives, and your children."

**6** But tomorrow at this time I will send my officials to you. They will search your palace and the houses of your officials. They will take away all that you value.'"

**7** Then the king of Israel called all the elders of the land. He said, "Look, this man wants to cause trouble. He sent a message to me for my wives, my children, my silver, and my gold. I did not refuse him."

**8** All the elders and all the people said to him, "Do not listen to him. Do not agree."

**9** So Ahab said to the messengers of Ben-hadad, "Tell my lord the king this: 'I will do all that you asked at first. But I cannot do this new thing.'" The messengers went away and took this answer back to Ben-hadad.

**10** Then Ben-hadad sent another message to Ahab. He said, "Let the gods punish me very much if I do not destroy Samaria. There will not be sufficient dust in Samaria to give a handful to each of my soldiers."

**11** The king of Israel answered, "Tell him this: 'A soldier who puts on his armor must not boast.' Only a soldier who takes off his armor after the battle can boast."

**12** Ben-hadad and the kings drank in their tents when Ben-hadad heard this message. He said to his men, "Prepare to attack!" So they prepared to attack the city.

**13** Then a prophet came to Ahab, the king of Israel. He said, "The LORD says this: 'Do you see this large army? Today I will give it to you. Then you will know that I am the LORD.'"

**14** Ahab asked, "Who will do this?" The prophet said, "The LORD says this: 'The young soldiers of the district governors will do it.'" Then Ahab asked, "Who will start the battle?" The prophet answered, "You."

**15** So Ahab counted the young soldiers of the district governors. There were 232 men. After this, he counted all the people of Israel. There were 7,000 men.

**16** They went out at noon. At that time, Ben-hadad and the 32 kings who helped him were in their tents. They drank wine and became drunk.

**17** The young soldiers of the district governors went out first. Ben-hadad sent men to look, and they told him, "Men come out of Samaria."

**18** Ben-hadad said, "If they come out for peace, catch them alive. If they come out for war, catch them alive."

**19** The young soldiers of the district governors went out of the city, and the army followed them.

**20** Each soldier of Israel killed an enemy soldier. The Syrians ran away, and the army of Israel chased them. But Ben-hadad, the king of Syria, escaped on a horse with some horsemen.

**21** The king of Israel went out and attacked the horses and the chariots. He killed very many Syrians.

**22** Then the prophet came to the king of Israel. He said to him, "Go and make your army strong. Think carefully about what you must do. In the spring, the king of Syria will attack you again."

**23** The officials of the king of Syria said to him, "The gods of Israel are gods of the hills. Thus the army of Israel was stronger than we were. But we must fight against them on the flat land. Then surely we will be stronger than they are.

**24** Do this: Remove the 32 kings from their positions. Put commanders in their places.

**25** Then collect an army like the army that you lost. Get horse for horse and chariot for chariot. We will fight against Israel on the flat land. Surely we will be stronger than they are." Ben-hadad agreed with their advice and did this.

**26** In the spring, Ben-hadad collected the Syrians. He went up to Aphek to fight against Israel.

**27** The people of Israel also collected their army and got supplies. They went out to fight against the Syrians. The army of Israel camped in front of the Syrians. The army of Israel was like 2 small groups of goats. But the Syrians filled all the land.

**28** A man of God came to the king of Israel. He said, "The LORD says this: 'The Syrians said this. "The LORD is a god of the hills, but he is not a god of the valleys." So I will give all this large army to you. Then you will know that I am the LORD.'"

**29** The 2 armies camped across from each other for 7 days. On the 7th day the battle started. In 1 day the army of Israel killed 100,000 soldiers of the Syrians.

**30** The other Syrians ran away into the city of Aphek. There a wall fell on 27,000 of those men. Ben-hadad also ran into the city and hid in an inner room.

**31** The officials of Ben-hadad said to him, "We heard that the kings of Israel are merciful. Let us put sackcloth around our waists and ropes on our heads. Then let us go out to the king of Israel. Maybe he will let you live."

**32** So they put sackcloth around their waists and ropes on their heads. They went to the king of Israel and said, "Your servant Ben-hadad says, 'Please let me live.'" Ahab said, "Is he still alive? He is my brother."

**33** The men looked for a good sign from Ahab. They quickly agreed with his words and said, "Yes, Ben-hadad is your brother!" Ahab said, "Go and bring him." Then Ben-hadad came out to Ahab. Ahab made him come up into his chariot.

**34** Ben-hadad said to him, "I will give back the cities that my father took from your father. You can make markets for yourself in Damascus, as my father did in Samaria." Ahab said, "With this treaty I will let you go." So Ahab made a treaty with him and let him go.

**35** A man from the group of prophets spoke to another prophet because the LORD told him. He said, "Please hit me." But the other man refused to hit him.

**36** So the prophet said to him, "You did not obey the voice of the LORD. Thus when you leave me, a lion will kill you." When the man left him, a lion found him and killed him.

**37** Then the prophet found another man. He said, "Please hit me." So the man hit him and wounded him.

**38** Then the prophet went and waited for the king next to the road. He put a cloth over his eyes so that no one knew who he was.

**39** The king went past, and the prophet shouted to him, "I, your servant, went into the battle. A soldier came to me with a prisoner. He said, 'Guard this man. If he escapes, then you will die in his place. Or you must pay a talent of silver.'

**40** But I, your servant, was busy with other things, and the prisoner escaped." The king of Israel said to him, "You decided your own punishment. You must accept it."

**41** Then the prophet quickly took the cloth away from his eyes. The king of Israel saw that he was one of the prophets.

**42** The prophet said to the king, "The LORD says this: 'I decided that Ben-hadad must die. But you let him go. Thus you will die in his place. Your people will die in the place of his people.'"

**43** The king of Israel went to his palace in Samaria. He was angry and sad.

## Chapter 21

**1** After these events, there was a vineyard that belonged to Naboth from Jezreel. The vineyard was in Jezreel, next to the palace of Ahab, the king of Samaria.

**2** Ahab said to Naboth, "Give me your vineyard. It is near my palace and I want to use it as a vegetable garden. In return I will give you a better vineyard. Or, if you prefer, I will pay you its value in money."

**3** But Naboth said to Ahab, "The LORD does not let me give you the land of my ancestors."

**4** So Ahab went home angry and sad because of what Naboth from Jezreel said to him. Naboth said, "I will not give you the land of my ancestors." Ahab lay on his bed and turned his face away. He did not eat.

**5** His wife Jezebel came to him. She asked him, "Why are you so sad? Why do you not eat?"

**6** He said to her, "I spoke to Naboth from Jezreel. I said to him, 'Sell me your vineyard. Or, if you prefer, I will give you another vineyard in its place.' But he said, 'I will not give you my vineyard.'"

**7** His wife Jezebel said to him, "Are you not the king of Israel? Get up and eat. Be happy. I will get the vineyard of Naboth from Jezreel for you."

**8** So Jezebel wrote letters with the name of Ahab. She put his seal on them. She sent the letters to the elders and the leaders who lived in the city of Naboth.

**9** She wrote in the letters, "Tell the people to fast. Give Naboth a seat of honor among the people.

**10** Put 2 worthless men across from him. Let them say that Naboth spoke against God and the king. Then take Naboth out of the city and throw stones at him until he dies."

**11** The elders and the leaders who lived in the city of Naboth did as Jezebel told them. They did as she wrote in the letters that she sent.

**12** They told the people to fast. They gave Naboth a seat of honor among the people.

**13** Then the 2 worthless men came and sat across from Naboth. They spoke against Naboth in front of the people. They said, "Naboth spoke against God and the king." So the people took Naboth out of the city. They threw stones at him until he died.

**14** Then the elders sent a message to Jezebel. They said, "Naboth is dead. The people killed him with stones."

**15** Jezebel heard that the people killed Naboth with stones. She said to Ahab, "Get up and take the vineyard of Naboth from Jezreel. Naboth refused to sell it to you. But now he is not alive. He is dead."

**16** Ahab heard that Naboth was dead. Then he got up and went down to the vineyard of Naboth from Jezreel to take it.

**17** Then the word of the LORD came to Elijah from Tishbe. The LORD said,

**18** "Go down to meet Ahab, the king of Israel, who lives in Samaria. He is now in the vineyard of Naboth. He went there to take it.

**19** Tell him, 'The LORD says this: You killed a man, and now you take his property!' Also tell him, 'The LORD says this: In the place where dogs licked the blood of Naboth, dogs will lick your blood too.'"

**20** Ahab said to Elijah, "So, my enemy, you found me!" Elijah answered, "Yes, I found you. You decided to do evil in the sight of the LORD.

**21** The LORD says this: 'I will bring disaster on you. I will completely destroy your descendants. I will kill every male in the family of Ahab in Israel, slave or free.

**22** I will make your family like the family of Jeroboam the son of Nebat and like the family of Baasha the son of Ahijah. I will do this because you made me angry and caused Israel to sin.'

**23** The LORD also said about Jezebel, 'Dogs will eat Jezebel next to the wall of Jezreel.'

**24** Dogs will eat the people of the family of Ahab who die in the city. Birds will eat the people who die in the fields."

**25** There was no other person like Ahab. He decided to do evil in the sight of the LORD. His wife Jezebel encouraged him to do evil.

**26** He did very terrible things. He worshipped idols, as the Amorites did. The LORD removed the Amorites in front of the people of Israel.

**27** Ahab heard these words. Then he tore his clothes and put on sackcloth. He did not eat. He slept in sackcloth and walked quietly and sadly.

**28** Then the word of the LORD came to Elijah from Tishbe. The LORD said,

**29** "Do you see that Ahab made himself humble in front of me? Because he made himself humble, I will not bring the disaster during his life. I will bring the disaster on his family during the life of his son."

## Chapter 22

**1** For 3 years there was no war between Syria and Israel.

**2** In the 3rd year, Jehoshaphat, the king of Judah, went down to visit the king of Israel.

**3** The king of Israel said to his officials, "You know that Ramoth-gilead belongs to us. But we do nothing to take it back from the king of Syria."

**4** He said to Jehoshaphat, "Will you go with me to fight against Ramoth-gilead?" Jehoshaphat said to the king of Israel, "I will help you. My soldiers are your soldiers. My horses are your horses."

**5** Jehoshaphat also said to the king of Israel, "But first ask the LORD what to do."

**6** So the king of Israel collected the prophets, about 400 men. He asked them, "Must I attack Ramoth-gilead or not?" They said, "Attack it. The Lord will give it to the king."

**7** But Jehoshaphat said, "Is there not another prophet of the LORD here? We can ask him."

**8** The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "There is 1 other man. We can ask the LORD through him. His name is Micaiah the son of Imlah. But I hate him. He never says good things about me. He always says bad things." Jehoshaphat said, "The king must not say that."

**9** So the king of Israel called an official. He said, "Bring Micaiah the son of Imlah quickly."

**10** The king of Israel and Jehoshaphat, the king of Judah, wore their royal clothes. They sat on their thrones at the floor for grain near the entrance of the gate of Samaria. All the prophets spoke as prophets in front of them.

**11** Zedekiah the son of Chenaanah made horns of iron for himself. He said, "The LORD says this: 'With these horns you will attack the Syrians until you destroy them.'"

**12** All the prophets said the same thing. They said, "Attack Ramoth-gilead and win. The LORD will give it to the king."

**13** The messenger who went to call Micaiah said to him, "All the prophets say that the king will win. Please agree with them and say something good."

**14** But Micaiah said, "As surely as the LORD lives, I will say only what the LORD tells me."

**15** Micaiah came to the king. The king asked him, "Micaiah, must we attack Ramoth-gilead or not?" Micaiah answered, "Attack and win. The LORD will give it to the king."

**16** But the king spoke to him again. He said, "How many times must I tell you to speak only the truth to me in the name of the LORD?"

**17** So Micaiah said, "I saw all the army of Israel on the hills. They were like sheep with no shepherd. The LORD said, 'These people have no leader. Let each man go home in peace.'"

**18** The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "I told you that he never says good things about me. He says only bad things."

**19** Micaiah said, "So hear the word of the LORD. I saw the LORD as he sat on his throne. All the army of heaven stood around him, on his right side and on his left side.

**20** The LORD said, 'Who will trick Ahab, so that he will attack Ramoth-gilead and die there?' One angel said one thing, and another angel said a different thing.

**21** Then a spirit came and stood in front of the LORD. The spirit said, 'I will trick Ahab.'

**22** The LORD asked him, 'How?' The spirit said, 'I will go out and make all his prophets tell lies.' The LORD said, 'You will trick him and you will succeed. Go and do it.'

**23** So now, the LORD made all these prophets of yours tell lies. The LORD decided to bring disaster on you."

**24** Then Zedekiah the son of Chenaanah went to Micaiah and hit him on the face. Zedekiah said, "How did the Spirit of the LORD leave me to speak to you?"

**25** Micaiah said, "You will find out on the day that you go into an inner room to hide."

**26** Then the king of Israel said, "Take Micaiah. Bring him back to Amon, the governor of the city, and to Joash, the son of the king.

**27** Tell them, 'The king says this: Put this man in prison. Give him only a little bread and water until I come back safely.'"

**28** Micaiah said, "If you come back safely, then the LORD did not speak through me." He also said, "Remember my words, all you people!"

**29** So the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat, the king of Judah, attacked Ramoth-gilead.

**30** The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "I will change my appearance and go into the battle. But you will wear your royal clothes." So the king of Israel changed his appearance and went into the battle.

**31** The king of Syria gave a command to his 32 chariot commanders. He said, "Do not fight with any other soldier, small or great. Fight only with the king of Israel."

**32** The chariot commanders saw Jehoshaphat and thought, "Surely that is the king of Israel." So they turned to attack him. But Jehoshaphat shouted.

**33** The chariot commanders saw that he was not the king of Israel. So they stopped and did not chase him.

**34** But a soldier shot an arrow without a specific target. The arrow hit the king of Israel between the plates of his armor. The king said to the driver of his chariot, "Turn around and take me out of the battle. I have a wound."

**35** The battle was very fierce that day. Soldiers held the king up in his chariot, with his face toward the Syrians. In the evening he died. The blood from his wound flowed into the bottom of the chariot.

**36** At sunset a shout went through the army. The shout said, "Each man must go back to his city and his country!"

**37** So the king died. They brought him to Samaria and buried him there.

**38** They washed the chariot at the pool of Samaria, where the prostitutes washed. Dogs licked up his blood. This was what the LORD said.

**39** The Book of the History of the Kings of Israel tells the other events of the life of Ahab. It tells about all that he did and the palace that he decorated with ivory. It also tells about all the cities that he built.

**40** Ahab died, and his son Ahaziah became king in his place.

**41** Jehoshaphat the son of Asa became king over Judah in the 4th year that Ahab was king of Israel.

**42** Jehoshaphat was 35 years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for 25 years. The name of his mother was Azubah, the daughter of Shilhi.

**43** He lived as his father Asa did. He did not stop. He did what was right in the sight of the LORD. But he did not remove the high places. The people still made sacrifices and burned incense at the high places.

**44** Jehoshaphat also made peace with the king of Israel.

**45** The Book of the History of the Kings of Judah tells the other events of the life of Jehoshaphat. It tells about the power that he showed and about his wars.

**46** He removed from the land the male prostitutes of the shrines who stayed after the time of his father Asa.

**47** At that time there was no king in Edom. A governor ruled Edom.

**48** Jehoshaphat made ships of Tarshish to go to Ophir for gold. But the ships did not go, because they broke at Ezion-geber.

**49** Then Ahaziah the son of Ahab said to Jehoshaphat, "Let my sailors go with your sailors in the ships." But Jehoshaphat refused.

**50** Jehoshaphat died, and they buried him with his ancestors in the City of David, his ancestor. His son Jehoram became king in his place.

**51** Ahaziah the son of Ahab became king over Israel in Samaria in the 17th year that Jehoshaphat was king of Judah. Ahaziah ruled over Israel for 2 years.

**52** He did evil in the sight of the LORD. He lived as his father and his mother did. He lived as Jeroboam the son of Nebat did, who caused Israel to sin.

**53** Ahaziah served and worshipped Baal. He made the LORD, the God of Israel, angry, as his father did.
