# Haggai

## Chapter 1

**1** This occurred in the second year of King Darius, on the first day of the sixth month. The LORD gave a message through the prophet Haggai. The message was for Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, the governor of Judah. It was also for Joshua the son of Josedech, the high priest. The message said:

**2** "The LORD of armies says this: 'These people say, "The time did not come yet to build the house of the LORD."'"

**3** Then the LORD gave a message through the prophet Haggai. The message said:

**4** "You live in houses with good wood panels on the walls. But my house is in ruins. Is it correct for you to do this?"

**5** Thus the LORD of armies says this: "Think carefully about what you do.

**6** You put much seed in the ground, but you get only a small harvest. You eat, but you do not have sufficient food. You drink, but you are still thirsty. You put on clothes, but no person is warm. A worker gets his pay, but the money goes away as if into a bag with holes."

**7** The LORD of armies says this: "Think carefully about what you do.

**8** Go up to the mountains. Bring wood and build my house. Then the house will make me glad, and people will give me honor," says the LORD.

**9** "You expected much, but you got only a little. When you brought the harvest home, I blew it away. Why did I do this?" says the LORD of armies. "I did this because my house is in ruins. But each one of you is busy with his own house.

**10** Thus the sky above you holds back the dew. The earth holds back its crops.

**11** I caused a time with no rain on the land and on the mountains. The dry time came on the grain, the new wine, and the olive oil. It came on all the crops of the ground. It came on people and on animals, and on all the work of your hands."

**12** Then Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel and Joshua the son of Josedech, the high priest, obeyed the voice of the LORD their God. All the people who remained obeyed too. They obeyed the words of the prophet Haggai, because the LORD their God sent him. The people had fear and respect for the LORD.

**13** Then Haggai, the messenger of the LORD, gave the message of the LORD to the people. He said, "The LORD says, 'I am with you.'"

**14** The LORD gave new energy to Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, the governor of Judah. He gave new energy to Joshua the son of Josedech, the high priest. He gave new energy to all the people who remained. They came and started to work on the house of the LORD of armies, their God.

**15** They started on the 24th day of the sixth month, in the second year of King Darius.

## Chapter 2

**1** On the 21st day of the seventh month, the LORD gave a message through the prophet Haggai. The message said:

**2** "Speak to Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, the governor of Judah. Speak to Joshua the son of Josedech, the high priest. Speak to the people who remain. Tell them this:

**3** 'Which of you saw this house in its first glory? How does the house look to you now? To you, it seems to be nothing.

**4** But now be strong, Zerubbabel,' says the LORD. 'Be strong, Joshua the son of Josedech, the high priest. Be strong, all you people of the land,' says the LORD. 'Do the work, because I am with you,' says the LORD of armies.

**5** 'I made a promise to you when you came out of Egypt. Because of that promise, my Spirit stays among you. Do not be afraid.'

**6** The LORD of armies says this: 'After a short time, I will shake the heavens and the earth one more time. I will shake the sea and the dry land.

**7** I will shake all the nations. Then the valuable things of all the nations will come here. I will fill this house with glory,' says the LORD of armies.

**8** 'The silver is mine and the gold is mine,' says the LORD of armies.

**9** 'The glory of this new house will be greater than the glory of the old house,' says the LORD of armies. 'In this place I will give peace,' says the LORD of armies."

**10** On the 24th day of the ninth month, in the second year of Darius, the LORD gave a message to the prophet Haggai. The message said:

**11** "The LORD of armies says this: 'Ask the priests a question about the law.

**12** A man carries holy meat in the fold of his clothes. Then his clothes touch bread, soup, wine, olive oil, or other food. Does that food become holy?'" The priests answered, "No."

**13** Then Haggai said, "A person touches a dead body, and thus he becomes unclean. Then he touches one of these foods. Does that food become unclean?" The priests answered, "Yes, it becomes unclean."

**14** Then Haggai said, "The LORD says, 'These people and this nation are the same in my view. All the work of their hands is unclean. All that they offer there is unclean.

**15** Now think carefully about the time before today. Think about the time before you put one stone on another stone in the temple of the LORD.

**16** In that time, a man came to a pile of grain and expected 20 measures. But he found only 10. A man came to the wine container to get 50 measures. But he found only 20.

**17** I attacked all the work of your hands with hot wind, plant disease, and hail. But you did not come back to me,' says the LORD.

**18** 'Think carefully about the time from today forward. Today is the 24th day of the ninth month. On this day you put down the base of the temple of the LORD. Think about it carefully.

**19** Is there seed in the storage building now? The vine, the fig tree, the pomegranate tree, and the olive tree did not produce fruit yet. But from today I will bless you.'"

**20** On the 24th day of the month, the LORD gave a second message to Haggai. The message said:

**21** "Speak to Zerubbabel, the governor of Judah. Tell him, 'I will shake the heavens and the earth.

**22** I will throw down the thrones of kingdoms. I will destroy the power of the kingdoms of the nations. I will throw down the chariots and the men in them. The horses and their riders will fall. Each man will kill another man with his sword.

**23** "At that time," says the LORD of armies, "I will take you, Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, my servant," says the LORD. "I will make you like my seal ring, because I chose you," says the LORD of armies.'"
