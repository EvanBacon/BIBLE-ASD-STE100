# Isaiah

## Chapter 1

**1** This is the vision that Isaiah the son of Amoz saw about Judah and Jerusalem. He saw it during the rule of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, the kings of Judah.

**2** Heavens, hear me! Earth, listen! The LORD speaks: "I fed my children and I made them grow. But they turned against me.

**3** The ox knows its owner. The donkey knows the feed box of its master. But Israel does not know me. My people do not understand."

**4** You sinful nation, you are in very bad trouble! You are a people with a heavy load of sin. You are the children of evil people. You do corrupt things. You left the LORD. You made the Holy One of Israel angry. You turned away from him.

**5** Why do you want more punishment? Why do you continue to turn against the LORD? Your whole head is sick. Your whole heart is weak.

**6** From the bottom of the foot to the top of the head, no part of the body is healthy. There are only cuts, bruises, and open sores. Nobody cleaned them. Nobody put bandages or oil on them.

**7** Your country is empty and ruined. Fire burned your cities. Foreign people eat the crops of your land while you watch. The land is ruined, as when foreign people destroy a land.

**8** The city of Zion is alone, as a small shelter in a vineyard. It is as a hut in a field of cucumbers. It is as a city that an enemy surrounds.

**9** The LORD of armies kept a small number of us alive. If he did not, we would be as Sodom. We would be as Gomorrah.

**10** Hear the word of the LORD, you rulers of Sodom! Listen to the law of our God, you people of Gomorrah!

**11** The LORD says, "Your many sacrifices are of no use to me. I have too many burnt offerings of rams and fat from fed animals. I do not like the blood of bulls, lambs, or goats.

**12** You come to my temple to meet me. But who told you to do this? Who told you to walk on the floors of my courts?

**13** Do not bring more offerings that have no value. Your incense is disgusting to me. I cannot accept your festivals of the new moon, your Sabbaths, and your meetings. I cannot accept your sin together with your holy meetings.

**14** I hate your festivals of the new moon and your other festivals. They are a heavy load for me. I am tired of them.

**15** When you lift your hands to pray, I will not look at you. When you make many prayers, I will not listen. Your hands are full of blood.

**16** Wash yourselves and make yourselves clean. Remove your evil actions from my sight. Stop doing evil.

**17** Learn to do good. Look for justice. Help the people who suffer. Defend the rights of children with no father. Speak for the widows in court.

**18** Come now, and we will talk about this together," says the LORD. "Your sins are as red as scarlet, but they will become as white as snow. They are as red as crimson, but they will become as white as wool.

**19** If you agree and obey, you will eat the good food of the land.

**20** But if you refuse and turn against me, the sword will kill you." The LORD himself speaks these words.

**21** The faithful city became as a prostitute! Before, the city was full of justice. Good people lived in it. But now murderers live in it.

**22** Your silver became waste metal. Your wine has water in it.

**23** Your leaders turn against God. They are friends of thieves. They all love bribes and they try to get payments. They do not defend the rights of children with no father. They do not hear the case of the widow.

**24** Thus the Lord, the LORD of armies, the Mighty One of Israel, says, "I will punish my enemies. I will take revenge on the people who are against me.

**25** I will turn my hand against you. I will make you clean, as fire removes waste from metal. I will remove all your impurities.

**26** I will give you good judges again, as at the start. I will give you good advisors, as before. After that, people will call you 'The city of righteousness, the faithful city.'"

**27** The LORD will save Zion with justice. He will save the people of Zion who turn back to him with righteousness.

**28** But the LORD will destroy the sinners and the people who turn against him together. The people who leave the LORD will die.

**29** You will be ashamed of the sacred oak trees that you loved. You will be ashamed of the gardens that you chose for your idols.

**30** You will be as an oak tree with dry leaves. You will be as a garden that has no water.

**31** The strong man will be as dry fiber. His work will be as a spark. They will both burn together. Nobody will stop the fire.

## Chapter 2

**1** This is the message that Isaiah the son of Amoz saw about Judah and Jerusalem.

**2** In the last days, the mountain of the temple of the LORD will become the highest of all mountains. It will be higher than the hills. People from all nations will come to it.

**3** Many people will come and say, "Come, we will go up to the mountain of the LORD, to the temple of the God of Jacob. He will teach us his ways. We will live as he tells us." The law will come from Zion. The word of the LORD will come from Jerusalem.

**4** The LORD will judge between the nations. He will settle the arguments of many peoples. They will hammer their swords into plows. They will make their spears into tools to cut plants. Nations will not fight against nations. They will not train for war again.

**5** People of Jacob, come! We will live in the light of the LORD.

**6** LORD, you left your people, the family of Jacob. They are full of customs from the east. They use magic as the Philistines do. They make agreements with foreign people.

**7** Their land is full of silver and gold. Their treasures have no end. Their land is full of horses. Their chariots have no end.

**8** Their land is full of idols. They worship the things that their own hands made. They worship the things that their own fingers made.

**9** So all people will become low. Each person will be humble. LORD, do not forgive them.

**10** Go into the rocks. Hide in the ground. Hide from the terror of the LORD and from his great glory.

**11** The LORD will make proud people humble. He will put arrogant men down. Only the LORD will be great on that day.

**12** The LORD of armies has a day. On that day he will punish all proud and arrogant people. He will make all high things low.

**13** He will punish all the tall cedar trees of Lebanon and all the oak trees of Bashan.

**14** He will punish all the high mountains and all the tall hills.

**15** He will punish every high tower and every strong wall.

**16** He will punish all the ships of Tarshish and all the beautiful boats.

**17** The LORD will put proud men down. He will make arrogant men low. Only the LORD will be great on that day.

**18** The LORD will remove all the idols.

**19** People will go into caves in the rocks and holes in the ground. They will hide from the terror of the LORD and from his great glory. They will hide when he comes to shake the earth.

**20** On that day people will throw away their idols of silver and gold. They made these idols for worship. But they will throw them to the moles and to the bats.

**21** People will go into the cracks of the rocks and into the high cliffs. They will hide from the terror of the LORD and from his great glory. They will hide when he comes to shake the earth.

**22** Stop trusting in humans. Humans are only breath. They have no value.

## Chapter 3

**1** Look! The Lord, the LORD of armies, will take away from Jerusalem and Judah all the things that they depend on. He will take away all their food and all their water.

**2** He will take away the soldiers and the men of war. He will take away the judges, the prophets, the people who tell the future, and the old leaders.

**3** He will take away the officers of the army and the important men. He will take away the advisors, the skilled workers, and the people who use magic.

**4** The LORD says, "I will make boys their leaders. Children will rule them."

**5** The people will be cruel to each other. Each man will be cruel to his neighbor. Young people will be rude to old people. Bad people will be rude to good people.

**6** A man will hold his brother in the house of his father and say, "You have a coat. Be our ruler! Take control of this ruined country!"

**7** But on that day the brother will refuse. He will say, "I cannot solve your problems. I have no food or clothing in my house. Do not make me ruler of the people."

**8** Jerusalem will fall. Judah will be ruined. The words and actions of the people are against the LORD. They insult his glory.

**9** The look on their faces shows that they are guilty. They show their sin as Sodom did. They do not hide it. They are in very bad trouble. They cause their own disaster.

**10** Tell the righteous people that good things will come to them. They will get the good results of their actions.

**11** But evil people are in very bad trouble. Bad things will come to them. They will get punishment for their actions.

**12** Young people are cruel to my people. Women rule over them. My people, your leaders cause you to go the wrong way. They make you lose the correct path.

**13** The LORD stands up to start his case in court. He stands to judge the people.

**14** The LORD starts to judge the old men and the leaders of his people. He says, "You destroyed my vineyard. The things that you stole from the poor are in your houses.

**15** Why do you crush my people? Why do you hurt the poor people so much?" says the Lord GOD of armies.

**16** The LORD says, "The women of Zion are proud. They walk with their heads high. They look at men with desire. They take small steps when they walk. Their ankle ornaments make noise.

**17** Thus the Lord will put sores on the heads of the women of Zion. The LORD will make them bald."

**18** On that day the Lord will take away their beautiful things. He will take away their ankle ornaments, their headbands, and their moon-shaped necklaces.

**19** He will take away their earrings, their bracelets, and their veils.

**20** He will take away their hats, their ankle chains, their belts, their bottles of perfume, and their charms.

**21** He will take away their rings and their nose rings.

**22** He will take away their fine clothes, their coats, their scarves, and their purses.

**23** He will take away their mirrors, their linen clothes, their head covers, and their long veils.

**24** They will smell bad, not good. They will wear a rope, not a belt. They will be bald, not have beautiful hair. They will wear rough cloth (sackcloth), not fine clothes. They will have marks of shame, not beauty.

**25** Your men will die by the sword. Your soldiers will die in the war.

**26** The people at the gates of Jerusalem will cry and mourn. The city will be empty. It will sit on the ground.

## Chapter 4

**1** On that day 7 women will take hold of 1 man. They will say, "We will get our own food and our own clothes. Only let us use your name. Take away our shame."

**2** On that day the branch of the LORD will be beautiful and glorious. The fruit of the land will be the pride of the people of Israel who stay alive.

**3** The people who stay in Zion and Jerusalem will be holy. God wrote their names in the list of the living in Jerusalem.

**4** The Lord will wash away the dirt of the women of Zion. He will clean the blood out of Jerusalem. He will do this with a spirit of judgment and a spirit of fire.

**5** Then the LORD will put a cloud of smoke over all of Mount Zion during the day. He will put a bright fire over it during the night. He will put it over all the places where the people meet. The glory of the LORD will cover and protect everything.

**6** It will be a shelter that gives shade from the heat in the day. It will be a safe place to hide from storms and rain.

## Chapter 5

**1** I will sing a song for the man that I love. The song is about his vineyard. My friend had a vineyard on a very fertile hill.

**2** He dug the ground and removed the stones. He planted the best vines in it. He built a tower in the middle of it. He also cut a winepress in it. He expected it to grow good grapes. But it grew bad grapes.

**3** "Now, people who live in Jerusalem and men of Judah, judge between me and my vineyard.

**4** What more could I do for my vineyard? I did everything for it. I expected it to grow good grapes. Why did it grow bad grapes?

**5** Now I will tell you what I will do to my vineyard. I will remove its hedge, and animals will eat it. I will break down its wall, and animals will walk on it.

**6** I will make it a waste area. Nobody will cut the vines or dig the ground. Thorns and weeds will grow there. I will tell the clouds not to rain on it."

**7** The vineyard of the LORD of armies is the nation of Israel. The people of Judah are the plants that he loved. He expected justice, but he saw murder. He expected righteousness, but he heard cries for help.

**8** You are in very bad trouble, you who buy house after house and field after field! You take all the land. You want to live alone in the land.

**9** The LORD of armies told me this: "Many houses will be empty. Large and beautiful houses will have no people in them.

**10** A vineyard of 10 acres will make only 6 gallons of wine. 10 bushels of seed will make only 1 bushel of grain."

**11** You are in very bad trouble, you who get up early in the morning to drink beer! You stay up late at night until wine makes you drunk.

**12** You have harps, lyres, tambourines, flutes, and wine at your feasts. But you do not think about the work of the LORD. You do not see what his hands do.

**13** Thus enemies will take my people away as prisoners, because they do not know me. Their important men will be hungry. All the people will be very thirsty.

**14** Thus the grave (Sheol) will open its mouth very wide. The leaders of Jerusalem and the noisy, happy crowds will go down into it.

**15** All people will become low. Each person will be humble. The proud people will look down in shame.

**16** But the LORD of armies will be great because of his justice. The holy God will show that he is holy by his righteousness.

**17** Then lambs will eat grass in the ruins as in their own fields. Strangers will eat in the ruined places of the rich.

**18** You are in very bad trouble, you who pull your sins behind you with ropes of lies! You pull sin as with the ropes of a cart.

**19** You say, "Let God hurry and do his work, so that we can see it! Let the plan of the Holy One of Israel come, so that we can know it!"

**20** You are in very bad trouble, you who say that evil is good and good is evil! You say that darkness is light and light is darkness. You say that bitter is sweet and sweet is bitter.

**21** You are in very bad trouble, you who think that you are wise! You think that you are clever.

**22** You are in very bad trouble, you who are strong when you drink wine! You are brave when you mix drinks.

**23** You take bribes and you let guilty people go free. You do not give justice to innocent people.

**24** Fire burns dry grass. Flames burn straw. In the same way, the roots of these people will rot. Their flowers will blow away as dust. They refused the law of the LORD of armies. They hated the word of the Holy One of Israel.

**25** Thus the LORD is angry with his people. He lifted his hand against them and he hit them. The mountains shook. Dead bodies lay in the streets as garbage. But after all this, his anger did not stop. His hand is still ready to punish.

**26** The LORD will lift up a flag as a signal to a far nation. He will whistle to call the people from the ends of the earth. Look! They will come very quickly.

**27** None of the soldiers will be tired or fall. None of them will sleep. Their belts will stay tight. The straps of their sandals will not break.

**28** Their arrows are sharp. All their bows are ready. The hooves of their horses are as hard as stone. The wheels of their chariots turn as fast as a storm.

**29** Their shout is as the roar of a lion. They roar as young lions. They growl and catch their prey. They carry it away, and nobody can save it.

**30** On that day they will roar over Israel as the sea roars. A person will look at the land and see only darkness and trouble. Clouds will make even the light dark.

## Chapter 6

**1** In the year that King Uzziah died, I saw the Lord. He sat on a high throne. The bottom of his robe filled the temple.

**2** Seraphim stood above him. Each seraph had 6 wings. With 2 wings each seraph covered his face. With 2 wings he covered his feet. With 2 wings he flew.

**3** The seraphim called to each other. They said, "Holy, holy, holy is the LORD of armies! The whole earth is full of his glory!"

**4** Their voices shook the posts of the doors. Smoke filled the temple.

**5** Then I said, "I am in very bad trouble! I am ruined! My lips are not clean. I live among people whose lips are not clean. But my eyes saw the King, the LORD of armies!"

**6** Then one of the seraphim flew to me. He held a hot coal in his hand. He took the coal from the altar with tongs.

**7** He touched my mouth with the coal. He said, "Look, this coal touched your lips. God removed your guilt. He forgave your sin."

**8** Then I heard the voice of the Lord. He said, "Whom will I send? Who will go for us?" I said, "I am here. Send me!"

**9** The Lord said, "Go and tell these people: 'You will hear and hear, but you will not understand. You will look and look, but you will not know what you see.'

**10** Make the minds of these people dull. Make their ears deaf. Close their eyes. If you do not, they will see with their eyes. They will hear with their ears. They will understand with their minds. Then they will turn back to me and I will heal them."

**11** Then I said, "Lord, how long must I do this?" He said, "Do it until the cities are ruined and have no people. Do it until the houses are empty and the land is completely destroyed.

**12** Do it until the LORD sends the people far away. The land will be empty.

**13** Even if 1 tenth of the people stay in the land, the LORD will destroy them again. But a terebinth tree or an oak tree leaves a stump when a person cuts it down. In the same way, a holy part of the people will stay as a stump."

## Chapter 7

**1** Ahaz was the son of Jotham and the grandson of Uzziah. Ahaz was the king of Judah. During his rule, Rezin, the king of Syria, attacked Jerusalem. Pekah the son of Remaliah, the king of Israel, attacked with him. But they could not defeat the city.

**2** A messenger told the family of David, "Syria made an agreement with Ephraim." Then Ahaz and his people were very afraid. They shook as the trees of a forest shake in the wind.

**3** Then the LORD said to Isaiah, "Go out with your son Shear-Jashub and meet Ahaz. Meet him at the end of the water channel of the upper pool. It is on the road to the field where people wash cloth.

**4** Tell Ahaz, 'Be careful and stay calm. Do not be afraid. Do not lose courage because of these 2 men. Rezin of Syria and the son of Remaliah are very angry. But they are only as the smoke of 2 burned sticks.

**5** Syria, Ephraim, and the son of Remaliah made an evil plan against you. They said,

**6** "We will attack Judah and make the people afraid. We will defeat Judah and take it for us. We will make the son of Tabeal the king of Judah."'

**7** But the Lord GOD says, 'Their plan will not succeed. It will not happen.

**8** Damascus is the capital of Syria. Rezin is only the king of Damascus. In 65 years Ephraim will be destroyed. It will not be a nation.

**9** Samaria is the capital of Ephraim. The son of Remaliah is only the king of Samaria. If you do not stay firm in your faith, you will not stay firm at all.'"

**10** The LORD spoke to Ahaz again. He said,

**11** "Ask the LORD your God for a sign. Ask for a sign from the deepest place or from the highest place."

**12** But Ahaz said, "I will not ask. I will not test the LORD."

**13** Then Isaiah said, "Listen, family of David! You make people tired. Is that not sufficient? Must you also make my God tired?

**14** Thus the Lord himself will give you a sign. Look! A virgin will become pregnant. She will give birth to a son. She will call him Immanuel.

**15** He will eat butter and honey when he becomes old enough to know right from wrong.

**16** Before the boy knows how to refuse evil and choose good, the land of the 2 kings that you fear will be empty.

**17** The LORD will bring a time of trouble on you, on your people, and on the family of your father. This time will be worse than any time since Ephraim separated from Judah. The LORD will bring the king of Assyria."

**18** On that day the LORD will whistle for the flies from the far rivers of Egypt. He will whistle for the bees from the land of Assyria.

**19** They will come and stay in the steep valleys and in the holes of the rocks. They will be on all the thorn bushes and all the fields.

**20** On that day the Lord will use the king of Assyria as a razor. The Lord will hire the king from the other side of the Euphrates River. The Lord will shave your head and the hair of your legs. He will also cut off your beard.

**21** On that day a man will keep only 1 young cow and 2 goats alive.

**22** But they will give a large quantity of milk. The man will eat butter. All the people who stay in the land will eat butter and honey.

**23** On that day, thorns and weeds will cover each place that had 1,000 vines. Each of these vineyards had a value of 1,000 silver coins.

**24** Men will go there with bows and arrows to hunt. Thorns and weeds will cover all the land.

**25** People will not go to the hills that farmers dug before. They will be afraid of the thorns and weeds. Cattle and sheep will eat grass there.

## Chapter 8

**1** The LORD said to me, "Take a large scroll. Write these words on it with an ordinary pen: 'Maher-Shalal-Hash-Baz.'"

**2** I got 2 reliable men as witnesses. They were Uriah the priest and Zechariah the son of Jeberechiah.

**3** Then I had sex with my wife, the prophetess. She became pregnant and gave birth to a son. The LORD said to me, "Call him Maher-Shalal-Hash-Baz.

**4** Before the boy can say 'My father' or 'My mother,' the king of Assyria will take away the riches of Damascus. He will also take away the goods of Samaria."

**5** The LORD spoke to me again. He said,

**6** "These people refuse the calm water of the Shiloah channel. They are happy with Rezin and the son of Remaliah.

**7** Thus the Lord will bring the king of Assyria and all his army against them. The king will be as the strong flood of the Euphrates River. He will fill all the channels and go over all the banks.

**8** He will move through Judah as a flood. The water will come up to the neck of the people. His army will spread out as wings and cover all your land, Immanuel!"

**9** Nations, make your war cries! But you will be broken. Listen, all you far countries! Prepare for war, but you will be broken! Prepare for war, but you will be broken!

**10** Make your plans, but they will fail. Give your orders, but they will not succeed. God is with us.

**11** The LORD held me with his strong hand. He told me not to live as these people live. He said,

**12** "Do not call everything a conspiracy, as these people do. Do not fear what they fear. Do not be afraid.

**13** The LORD of armies is holy. Honor him only. Fear him only. Respect him with fear.

**14** He will be a safe place. But for the 2 kingdoms of Israel, he will be a stone that causes people to fall. He will be a rock that makes them trip. For the people of Jerusalem, he will be a trap and a net.

**15** Many of them will fall over this stone. They will fall and break. They will be caught in the trap."

**16** Keep this message safe. Seal the teaching and give it to my disciples.

**17** I will wait for the LORD. He hides his face from the family of Jacob. But I will trust him.

**18** I and the children that the LORD gave me are signs in Israel. We are warnings from the LORD of armies. He lives on Mount Zion.

**19** People will tell you, "Ask the people who speak with the dead. Ask the people who whisper and mumble magic words." But people must ask their God. Why ask the dead to help the living?

**20** Go to the law and the testimony of God! If people do not speak by this word, they have no light.

**21** They will walk through the land in trouble and hunger. When they are hungry, they will become angry. They will curse their king and their God. They will look up,

**22** and then they will look at the earth. But they will see only trouble and darkness. They will see terrible gloom. Someone will push them into complete darkness.

## Chapter 9

**1** But the people in trouble will not always be in darkness. Before, God made the land of Zebulun and the land of Naphtali humble. But in the future, he will give honor to Galilee of the nations. Galilee is by the road to the sea, on the other side of the Jordan.

**2** The people who walked in darkness saw a great light. A light shone on the people who lived in the land of deep darkness.

**3** LORD, you made the nation large. You made the people very happy. They are happy in your presence, as people are happy at the harvest. They are happy as soldiers when they divide the goods that they took.

**4** You broke the heavy load on their shoulders. You broke the rod of the man who was cruel to them. You did this as you did on the day when you defeated Midian.

**5** Fire will burn every boot of the soldiers. It will burn every coat with blood on it.

**6** A child is born to us. A son is given to us. He will rule over us. His names will be Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God, Eternal Father, and Prince of Peace.

**7** His rule and his peace will grow. They will have no end. He will rule from the throne of David over his kingdom. He will make it strong with justice and righteousness from now until always. The strong love of the LORD of armies will do this.

**8** The Lord sent a message against Jacob. The message will come on Israel.

**9** All the people will know it. The people of Ephraim and Samaria will know it. They say with pride and arrogance,

**10** "The bricks fell down, but we will build with cut stones. Enemies cut down the fig trees, but we will put cedar trees in their place."

**11** But the LORD will make the enemies of Rezin strong against Israel. He will cause their enemies to attack.

**12** The Syrians will come from the east. The Philistines will come from the west. They will eat Israel with open mouths. But after all this, the anger of the LORD will not stop. His hand is still ready to punish.

**13** But the people do not turn back to the LORD, who punishes them. They do not look for the LORD of armies.

**14** Thus the LORD will cut off the head and the tail of Israel in 1 day. He will cut off the palm branch and the reed.

**15** The old and important leaders are the head. The prophets who teach lies are the tail.

**16** The leaders of these people cause them to go the wrong way. The people who follow them are destroyed.

**17** Thus the Lord is not happy with the young men. He will not show mercy to the children with no father and the widows. All the people are godless and evil. Each mouth speaks foolish things. But after all this, the anger of the LORD will not stop. His hand is still ready to punish.

**18** Evil burns as a fire. It burns the thorns and weeds. It burns the thick trees of the forest. Thick smoke goes up.

**19** The anger of the LORD of armies burns the land. The people are as fuel for the fire. No man shows mercy to his brother.

**20** People will take food on the right but they will still be hungry. They will eat on the left but they will not be satisfied. Each man will eat the flesh of his own arm.

**21** Manasseh will fight Ephraim. Ephraim will fight Manasseh. Together they will fight Judah. But after all this, the anger of the LORD will not stop. His hand is still ready to punish.

## Chapter 10

**1** You are in very bad trouble, you who make unfair laws! You write rules that cause suffering.

**2** You do not give justice to the poor. You take the rights of the poor people of God. You steal from widows. You rob children with no father.

**3** What will you do on the day of punishment? What will you do when disaster comes from far away? To whom will you run for help? Where will you put your riches?

**4** You will bow down among the prisoners. You will fall among the dead. But after all this, the anger of the LORD will not stop. His hand is still ready to punish.

**5** "Assyria, you are in very bad trouble! Assyria is the rod of my anger. The stick in the hand of Assyria is my anger.

**6** I send Assyria against a godless nation. I order Assyria to attack the people that make me angry. I tell Assyria to take their goods. Assyria will walk on them as on mud in the streets.

**7** But the king of Assyria does not think this. That is not his plan. He only wants to destroy many nations.

**8** He says, 'All my commanders are as kings.

**9** I defeated Calno, as I defeated Carchemish. I defeated Hamath, as I defeated Arpad. I defeated Samaria, as I defeated Damascus.

**10** I defeated the kingdoms of the idols. Their idols were more than the idols of Jerusalem and Samaria.

**11** I destroyed Samaria and its idols. I will do the same to Jerusalem and its idols.'"

**12** The Lord will finish all his work against Mount Zion and Jerusalem. Then he will say, "I will punish the king of Assyria for his proud heart. I will punish him for his arrogant look.

**13** He says, 'I did this with my own strength. I did it with my wisdom, because I am clever. I removed the borders of nations. I took their treasures. I defeated their kings as a strong bull.

**14** I took the riches of the nations, as a person takes eggs from a nest. I took all the earth, as a person takes eggs that a bird left. No bird moved its wings or opened its mouth to make a noise.'"

**15** Can an ax be prouder than the man who uses it? Can a saw be greater than the man who cuts with it? A rod cannot lift the man who holds it! A stick of wood cannot lift a person!

**16** Thus the Lord, the LORD of armies, will send a disease among the strong soldiers of Assyria. Their bodies will become thin. A fire will burn under their glory.

**17** The Light of Israel will become a fire. The Holy One of Israel will become a flame. In 1 day he will burn the thorns and weeds of Assyria.

**18** He will completely destroy the beautiful forests and the fertile fields of Assyria. Assyria will become weak as a sick man.

**19** Only a few trees of its forest will stay. A child will be able to count them.

**20** On that day the people of Israel who stay alive will not trust Assyria again. The people of Jacob who escape will not trust the nation that defeated them. They will truly trust the LORD, the Holy One of Israel.

**21** A small number of people will come back. A small number of the people of Jacob will come back to the Mighty God.

**22** Israel, your people are as many as the sand of the sea. But only a small number will come back. God decided to destroy the nation, and his decision is fair.

## Chapter 34

**1** Come near, nations, and hear! Listen, peoples! Let the earth and all the things in it listen! Let the world and all the things that come from it listen!

**2** The LORD is angry with all the nations. He is very angry with all their armies. He will completely destroy them. He will give them to be killed.

**3** Their dead bodies will lie on the ground. The bodies will smell bad. Their blood will flow down the mountains.

**4** All the stars in the sky will disappear. The sky will roll up as a scroll. All the stars will fall as leaves fall from a vine. They will fall as dry figs fall from a fig tree.

**5** The LORD says, "My sword is ready in the sky. Look! It will come down on Edom to judge it. It will come down on the people that I decided to destroy."

**6** The sword of the LORD is covered with blood and fat. It has the blood of lambs and goats. It has the fat from the kidneys of rams. The LORD will make a sacrifice in Bozrah. He will kill many people in the land of Edom.

**7** Wild oxen will die with them. Young bulls and strong bulls will die. Blood will cover the land. Fat will cover the ground.

**8** The LORD has a day of revenge. It is a year when he will pay back the enemies of Zion.

**9** The streams of Edom will become tar. Its dust will become burning sulfur. Its land will become burning tar.

**10** The fire will burn day and night. Nobody will stop it. Its smoke will go up always. The land will be empty for all generations. Nobody will travel through it again.

**11** Desert owls and hedgehogs will take control of it. Large owls and ravens will live there. God will measure Edom with a line of destruction. He will use a plumb line to make it empty.

**12** Its important people will have no kingdom to rule. All its leaders will disappear.

**13** Thorns will grow in its palaces. Weeds and thorn bushes will grow in its fortresses. It will be a home for jackals and a place for ostriches.

**14** Desert animals will meet with hyenas. Wild goats will call to each other. Night animals will rest there and find a place to stay.

**15** Owls will make their nests there. They will lay eggs and hatch their young. They will protect their young under their wings. Hawks will also come there, each with its mate.

**16** Look in the book of the LORD and read it. None of these animals will be missing. Each animal will have its mate. The LORD gave the order. His Spirit will bring the animals together.

**17** He divided the land and gave each animal its part. He measured the land for them with a line. They will own the land for all time. They will live there for all generations.

## Chapter 35

**1** The desert and the dry land will be happy. The wilderness will be full of joy and it will flower as a crocus.

**2** It will be full of flowers. It will shout and sing with joy. God will give it the beauty of Lebanon. He will give it the glory of Carmel and Sharon. The people will see the glory of the LORD and the greatness of our God.

**3** Make the weak hands strong. Make the weak knees firm.

**4** Tell the people who are afraid, "Be strong! Do not be afraid! Look, your God will come. He will take revenge on your enemies. He will pay them back. He will come and save you."

**5** Then the eyes of blind people will open. The ears of deaf people will hear.

**6** Then lame people will jump as a deer. People who cannot speak will shout with joy. Water will flow in the desert. Streams will flow in the dry land.

**7** The hot sand will become a pool. The dry ground will have springs of water. Grass, reeds, and rushes will grow in the places where jackals lived.

**8** A road will go through that land. People will call it "The Way of Holiness." Unclean people will not travel on it. It will be only for the people of God. Even foolish people will not go the wrong way on it.

**9** No lion will be there. No dangerous animal will come on it. The people whom the LORD saved will walk on it.

**10** The people whom the LORD rescued will come back. They will come to Zion with songs. Joy will be on their heads always. They will be happy and full of joy. Sadness and crying will go away.

## Chapter 36

**1** In the 14th year of King Hezekiah, Sennacherib, the king of Assyria, attacked all the strong cities of Judah. He captured them.

**2** The king of Assyria sent his field commander from Lachish to King Hezekiah in Jerusalem. The commander came with a large army. He stopped at the water channel of the upper pool. It is on the road to the field where people wash cloth.

**3** Eliakim the son of Hilkiah came out to meet him. Eliakim was the manager of the palace. Shebna the secretary and Joah the son of Asaph, the recorder, also came out.

**4** The field commander said to them, "Tell Hezekiah this. The great king, the king of Assyria, says: 'Why are you so confident?

**5** You say that you have plans and power for war. But your words have no value. Whom do you trust? Why do you turn against me?

**6** Look! You trust Egypt. But Egypt is as a broken reed. If a man uses it as a walking stick, it will cut his hand. Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, is the same for all the people who trust him.

**7** But maybe you will tell me, "We trust the LORD our God." But Hezekiah removed the high places and the altars of the LORD! He told Judah and Jerusalem, "You must worship at this altar."

**8** Now make an agreement with my master, the king of Assyria. I will give you 2,000 horses, if you can find riders for them!

**9** You cannot defeat even 1 officer, the least important of the servants of my master. Why do you trust Egypt for chariots and horsemen?

**10** Did I come to attack and destroy this land without the LORD? No! The LORD told me to attack this land and destroy it.'"

**11** Then Eliakim, Shebna, and Joah said to the field commander, "Please speak to your servants in the Aramaic language. We understand it. Do not speak to us in the Hebrew language. The people on the wall can hear you."

**12** But the field commander said, "My master did not send me to speak these words only to your master and to you. He sent me to speak to the men who sit on the wall. They also will eat their own excrement and drink their own urine, as you will."

**13** Then the field commander stood up and shouted in Hebrew. He said, "Hear the words of the great king, the king of Assyria!

**14** The king says: Do not let Hezekiah trick you. He cannot save you.

**15** Do not let Hezekiah make you trust the LORD. He says, 'The LORD will save us. The king of Assyria will not capture this city.'

**16** Do not listen to Hezekiah! The king of Assyria says: Make peace with me and come out to me. Then each of you will eat fruit from your own vine and your own fig tree. Each of you will drink water from your own well.

**17** Then I will come and take you to a land that is the same as your land. It is a land of grain and new wine. It is a land of bread and vineyards.

**18** Do not let Hezekiah trick you when he says, 'The LORD will save us.' The gods of the other nations did not save their lands from the king of Assyria.

**19** Where are the gods of Hamath and Arpad? Where are the gods of Sepharvaim? Did they save Samaria from me?

**20** None of the gods of these lands saved its land from me. So how can the LORD save Jerusalem from me?"

**21** But the people were silent. They did not say anything, because King Hezekiah told them, "Do not answer him."

**22** Then Eliakim the son of Hilkiah, the manager of the palace, went to Hezekiah. Shebna the secretary and Joah the son of Asaph, the recorder, went with him. They tore their clothes. They told Hezekiah what the field commander said.

## Chapter 37

**1** When King Hezekiah heard this, he tore his clothes. He put on rough cloth (sackcloth). He went into the temple of the LORD.

**2** He sent Eliakim, the manager of the palace, Shebna the secretary, and the leaders of the priests to the prophet Isaiah the son of Amoz. They all wore rough cloth.

**3** They said to Isaiah, "Hezekiah says: Today is a day of trouble, punishment, and shame. We are as a woman who is ready to give birth. But she has no strength to give birth to the baby.

**4** The king of Assyria sent his field commander to insult the living God. Maybe the LORD your God will hear his words. Maybe he will punish him for the words that the LORD your God heard. Thus pray for the people who stay alive."

**5** The officials of King Hezekiah came to Isaiah.

**6** Isaiah said to them, "Tell your master: The LORD says, 'Do not be afraid of the words that you heard. The servants of the king of Assyria insulted me with these words.

**7** Listen! I will put a spirit in the king of Assyria. He will hear a report and go back to his own country. I will make him die by the sword in his own country.'"

**8** The field commander heard that the king of Assyria left Lachish. So he went back and found the king. The king fought against Libnah.

**9** Then the king of Assyria got news that Tirhakah, the king of Cush, came to fight against him. When he heard this, he sent messengers to Hezekiah. He told them,

**10** "Tell Hezekiah, the king of Judah: Do not let the God that you trust trick you. Do not believe him when he says, 'The king of Assyria will not capture Jerusalem.'

**11** Look! You heard what the kings of Assyria did to all the countries. They destroyed them completely. Do you think that you will escape?

**12** My ancestors destroyed the nations of Gozan, Haran, and Rezeph. They destroyed the people of Eden who lived in Telassar. The gods of these nations did not save them.

**13** Where is the king of Hamath? Where is the king of Arpad? Where are the kings of the cities of Sepharvaim, Hena, and Ivvah?"

**14** Hezekiah took the letter from the messengers and read it. Then he went up to the temple of the LORD. He opened the letter in front of the LORD.

**15** Hezekiah prayed to the LORD. He said,

**16** "LORD of armies, God of Israel, you sit on your throne above the cherubim. Only you are God of all the kingdoms of the earth. You made the heavens and the earth.

**17** LORD, listen and hear. LORD, open your eyes and see. Hear all the words that Sennacherib sent to insult the living God.

**18** LORD, it is true that the kings of Assyria destroyed all these nations and their lands.

**19** They threw the gods of these nations into the fire. But those were not gods. They were only wood and stone that men made with their hands. Thus the Assyrians destroyed them.

**20** Now, LORD our God, save us from the king of Assyria. Then all the kingdoms of the earth will know that only you are the LORD."

**21** Then Isaiah the son of Amoz sent a message to Hezekiah. He said, "The LORD, the God of Israel, says: You prayed to me about Sennacherib, the king of Assyria.

**22** This is the message that the LORD speaks against him: 'The young woman, Zion, hates you and laughs at you. The city of Jerusalem shakes her head at you.

**23** Whom did you insult? Against whom did you speak bad words? Against whom did you shout with pride? You did this against the Holy One of Israel!

**24** You used your servants to insult the Lord. You said, "With my many chariots I went up to the tops of the mountains. I went up to the highest parts of Lebanon. I cut down its tallest cedar trees and its best pine trees. I went to its highest places and its best forests.

**25** I dug wells and I drank water in foreign lands. With my feet I dried up all the streams of Egypt."

**26** Did you not hear? Long ago I planned this. In the past I decided it. Now I made it happen. I let you make strong cities into piles of stones.

**27** Their people had no power. They were afraid and ashamed. They were as grass in the field and as young plants. They were as grass on a roof that dries up before it grows.

**28** But I know where you stay. I know when you go out and when you come in. I know how angry you are with me.

**29** You are angry with me and proud. I heard it. So I will put my hook in your nose. I will put my bridle in your mouth. I will make you go back on the road that you came on.'

**30** Hezekiah, this will be a sign for you: This year you will eat the grain that grows by itself. Next year you will eat the grain that grows from that grain. But in the third year you will plant seeds and collect the harvest. You will plant vineyards and eat their fruit.

**31** The people of Judah who stay alive will again put roots down into the ground. They will give fruit above the ground.

**32** A small number of people will go out from Jerusalem. A group of survivors will come from Mount Zion. The strong love of the LORD of armies will do this.

**33** Thus the LORD says this about the king of Assyria: 'He will not come into this city. He will not shoot an arrow here. He will not come near it with shields. He will not build a ramp against its walls.

**34** He will go back on the road that he came on. He will not come into this city,' says the LORD.

**35** 'I will defend this city and save it. I will do this for my own honor and for my servant David.'"

**36** Then the angel of the LORD went out. He killed 185,000 soldiers in the camp of the Assyrians. When the people got up early in the morning, they saw that all the soldiers were dead.

**37** So Sennacherib, the king of Assyria, went away. He went back to Nineveh and stayed there.

**38** One day he worshipped in the temple of his god Nisroch. His sons Adrammelech and Sharezer killed him with a sword. Then they escaped to the land of Ararat. His son Esarhaddon became king after him.

## Chapter 38

**1** At that time Hezekiah became very sick and almost died. The prophet Isaiah the son of Amoz came to him. He said, "The LORD says: Put everything in your house in order, because you will die. You will not get well."

**2** Hezekiah turned his face to the wall and prayed to the LORD.

**3** He said, "LORD, please remember that I was faithful to you. I served you with all my heart. I did what was good in your sight." Then Hezekiah cried very much.

**4** Then the LORD gave a message to Isaiah. He said,

**5** "Go and tell Hezekiah: The LORD, the God of your ancestor David, says: I heard your prayer. I saw your tears. Look! I will give you 15 more years of life.

**6** I will save you and this city from the king of Assyria. I will defend this city.

**7** The LORD will give you this sign. It will show that the LORD will do what he promised:

**8** Look! I will make the shadow on the stairway of Ahaz go back 10 steps." So the shadow of the sun went back the 10 steps that it went down.

**9** Hezekiah, the king of Judah, wrote this after he was sick and got well:

**10** I said, "In the middle of my life I must go to the gates of the place of the dead (Sheol). I must lose the rest of my years."

**11** I said, "I will not see the LORD again in the land of the living. I will not see people again among the people who live in the world.

**12** My life is pulled down and taken away from me, as a tent of a shepherd. My life is cut off, as a weaver cuts cloth from the loom. From day until night you bring me to an end.

**13** I waited calmly until morning. But he broke all my bones as a lion does. From day until night you bring me to an end.

**14** I cried as a swallow or a crane. I made sad sounds as a dove. My eyes became tired as I looked up to heaven. Lord, I am in trouble! Help me!

**15** But what can I say? He spoke to me, and he did it. I will walk humbly all my years because of the bitterness of my life.

**16** Lord, people live because of these things. My spirit also finds life in them. You made me healthy. You let me live.

**17** Look! My great bitterness was for my good. In your love you saved my life from the pit of destruction. You put all my sins behind your back.

**18** The dead in the grave cannot praise you. Death cannot sing praises to you. The people who go down into the pit cannot hope for your faithfulness.

**19** The living people, only the living people, praise you, as I do today. Fathers tell their children about your faithfulness.

**20** The LORD will save me. So we will play music on stringed instruments in the temple of the LORD. We will do this all the days of our lives."

**21** Isaiah said, "Make a paste of figs and put it on the sore. Then Hezekiah will get well."

**22** Hezekiah asked, "What will be the sign that I will go up to the temple of the LORD?"

**6** Look! A time will come when people will take everything in your palace to Babylon. They will take all the treasures that your ancestors saved until now. Nothing will stay," says the LORD.

**1** "Comfort my people. Comfort them," says your God.

**2** "Speak kindly to the people of Jerusalem. Tell them that their hard service is finished. Tell them that I forgave their sin. The LORD punished them 2 times for all their sins."

**3** A voice calls, "Prepare a road for the LORD in the wilderness! Make a straight road in the desert for our God!

**4** Fill every valley. Make every mountain and hill low. Make the rough ground level. Make the hilly places flat.

**5** Then the glory of the LORD will appear. All people will see it together. The LORD himself says this."

**6** A voice says, "Call out!" I said, "What must I call out?" "All people are as grass. All their beauty is as the flowers in the field.

**7** The grass dries up and the flowers die, when the breath of the LORD blows on them. Truly, the people are as grass.

**8** The grass dries up and the flowers die. But the word of our God will stay always."

**9** Zion, you bring good news. Go up on a high mountain. Jerusalem, you bring good news. Shout it loudly! Shout it, and do not be afraid! Tell the cities of Judah, "Here is your God!"

**10** Look! The Lord GOD comes with power. He rules with his strong arm. Look! He brings his reward with him. He brings the people that he won.

**11** He takes care of his flock as a shepherd. He collects the lambs in his arms. He carries them close to his heart. He gently leads the sheep that have young.

**12** Who measured the water of the sea in his hand? Who measured the sky with his fingers? Who put all the dust of the earth in a basket? Who weighed the mountains on a scale and the hills on a balance?

**13** Who can know the Spirit of the LORD? Who can be his advisor and teach him?

**14** God did not ask anyone for advice. Nobody taught him the correct path. Nobody gave him knowledge. Nobody showed him the way of understanding.

**15** Look! The nations are as a drop of water in a bucket. They are as dust on a scale. He lifts the islands as fine dust.

**16** All the trees of Lebanon are not sufficient for the fire on his altar. All the animals of Lebanon are not sufficient for a burnt offering.

**17** All the nations are as nothing in front of him. He thinks that they are less than nothing. They have no value to him.

**18** To whom can you compare God? What image can you compare with him?

**19** A worker makes an idol from metal. A goldsmith covers it with gold. He makes silver chains for it.

**20** A poor man cannot give that much. So he chooses wood that will not rot. He looks for a skilled worker to make an idol that will not fall over.

**21** Do you not know? Did you not hear? Did nobody tell you from the start? Do you not understand how God made the earth?

**22** God sits on his throne above the circle of the earth. The people on the earth are as grasshoppers to him. He spreads out the sky as a curtain. He spreads it out as a tent to live in.

**23** He makes rulers into nothing. He makes the judges of the world as nothing.

**24** They are as new plants. As soon as they start to grow, he blows on them. Then they dry up. The wind takes them away as straw.

**25** "To whom can you compare me? Who is equal to me?" says the Holy One.

**26** Look up at the sky. Who made all the stars? He leads them out as an army, and he counts them. He calls each star by its name. His power is great and his strength is strong. Thus not 1 star is missing.

**27** People of Jacob, why do you complain? People of Israel, why do you say, "The LORD does not see my trouble. My God does not give me justice"?

**28** Do you not know? Did you not hear? The LORD is the eternal God. He made all the earth. He does not become weak or tired. Nobody can know how great his understanding is.

**29** He gives strength to tired people. He gives more power to weak people.

**30** Even young people become tired and weak. Young men fall down.

**31** But the people who trust the LORD will get new strength. They will fly high as eagles. They will run and not become tired. They will walk and not become weak.

**5** The islands saw it and they were afraid. The ends of the earth shook with fear. They came near.

**6** Each person helped his neighbor. Each said to his brother, 'Be strong!'

**28** I look, but there is nobody. None of the idols can give advice. When I ask them a question, they cannot answer.

**29** Look! All of them are false. Their actions are nothing. Their metal idols are only wind and confusion."

**7** You will open the eyes of blind people. You will free the prisoners from jail. You will bring out the people who sit in darkness in prison.

**22** But these people are robbed and their goods are taken. They are all caught in holes. They are hidden in prisons. Enemies take them, and nobody saves them. Enemies rob them, and nobody says, "Give them back!"

**23** Which of you will listen to this? Who will pay attention in the future?

**24** Who let the enemies rob the people of Jacob? Who gave Israel to the thieves? The LORD did it, because we sinned against him. The people did not live as he told them. They did not obey his teaching.

**25** Thus the LORD poured his strong anger on them. He sent war against them. His anger burned all around them, but they did not understand. It burned them, but they did not learn.

## Chapter 43

**1** But now the LORD made you, people of Jacob. He formed you, people of Israel. He says, "Do not be afraid, because I saved you. I called you by your name. You are mine.

**2** When you go through deep water, I will be with you. When you go through rivers, they will not cover you. When you walk through fire, you will not be burned. The flames will not hurt you.

**3** I am the LORD your God, the Holy One of Israel, your Savior. I gave Egypt as the price to free you. I gave Cush and Seba in your place.

**4** You are valuable to me. I honor you and I love you. So I will give other people in your place. I will give other nations in exchange for your life.

**10** Your holy cities are now a desert. Zion is a desert. Jerusalem is empty and destroyed.

**11** Our holy and beautiful temple is burned. Our ancestors praised you there. All the things that we loved are destroyed.

**12** LORD, after all these things, will you not do something? Will you be silent and punish us very much?

## Chapter 65

**1** The LORD says, "I was ready to answer people who did not ask for me. I was ready for people who did not look for me to find me. I said, 'I am here, I am here,' to a nation that did not pray to me.

**2** All day I held out my hands to a people who rebel. They do things that are not good. They do what they want.

**3** These people always make me angry in front of me. They give sacrifices in gardens to their idols. They burn incense on altars of bricks.

**4** They sit among the graves. They spend the night in secret places. They eat pork. Their pots contain soup made of unclean meat.

**5** They say to other people, 'Stay away. Do not come near me. I am more holy than you.' These people are like smoke in my nose. They make me angry all day, like a fire that burns all day.

**6** Look, I wrote down my decision. I will not be silent. I will punish them completely.

**7** I will punish them for their sins and for the sins of their ancestors," says the LORD. "They burned incense on the mountains. They insulted me on the hills. Thus I will punish them for all their past acts."

**8** The LORD says this: "When a person finds juice in a bunch of grapes, other people say, 'Do not destroy it. There is something good in it.' Thus I will not destroy all my people. I will do this for my servants.

**9** I will bring descendants from Jacob. I will bring people from Judah who will own my mountains. My chosen people will own the land. My servants will live there.

**10** The plain of Sharon will be a field for sheep. The Valley of Achor will be a place for cattle to rest. These places will be for my people who look for me.

**11** But some of you left the LORD and forgot my holy mountain. You prepare food for the god Fortune. You pour out mixed wine for the god Fate.

**12** Thus I will decide that you will die in war. All of you will bow down to be killed. When I called, you did not answer. When I spoke, you did not listen. You did evil things in front of me. You chose to do things that I do not like."

**13** Thus the Lord GOD says this: "Look, my servants will eat, but you will be hungry. My servants will drink, but you will be thirsty. My servants will be happy, but you will be ashamed.

**14** My servants will sing with happy hearts. But you will cry with sad hearts. You will cry loudly because your spirit is broken.

**15** My chosen people will use your name as a curse. The Lord GOD will kill you. But he will give his servants another name.

**16** People in the land who ask for a blessing will ask the God of truth. People in the land who make a promise will promise by the God of truth. I will forget the past troubles. I will not see them again.

**17** Look, I will make new heavens and a new earth. People will not remember the past things. They will not think about them.

**18** Be happy and full of joy always because of the things that I make. Look, I will make Jerusalem a city of joy. I will make her people happy.

**19** I will be happy with Jerusalem. I will be glad about my people. People will not hear the sound of crying in the city again.

**20** In that city no baby will die after only a few days. All old people will live a long life. A person who dies at 100 years old will seem young. A person who does not live 100 years will seem cursed.

**21** My people will build houses and live in them. They will plant vineyards and eat the grapes.

**22** They will not build houses for other people to live in. They will not plant food for other people to eat. My people will live as long as trees. My chosen people will enjoy the things that they make for a long time.

**23** They will not work for nothing. Their children will not have a life of trouble. The LORD will bless them and their descendants.

**24** I will answer them before they call to me. I will hear them while they still speak to me.

**25** The wolf and the lamb will eat together. The lion will eat straw like the ox. The snake will eat only dust. Nothing will hurt or destroy on all my holy mountain," says the LORD.

## Chapter 66

**1** The LORD says this: "Heaven is my throne. The earth is the place where I put my feet. Thus where is the house that you will build for me? Where is the place where I will rest?

**2** My hand made all these things. Thus all these things came into existence," says the LORD. "But I look with favor on the person who is humble and sorry for his sins. I look with favor on the person who respects my word with fear.

**3** But some people kill an ox as a sacrifice, and they also kill a man. Some people sacrifice a lamb, and they also break the neck of a dog. Some people give a grain offering, and they also give the blood of a pig. Some people burn incense, and they also worship an idol. These people chose their own ways. They love the things that I hate.

**4** Thus I will choose to punish them. I will bring on them the things that they fear. When I called, no person answered. When I spoke, they did not listen. They did evil things in front of me. They chose to do things that I do not like."

**5** Hear the word of the LORD, you who respect his word with fear. "Your brothers hate you. They send you away because you are loyal to me. They say, 'Let the LORD show his glory. Then we will see your joy.' But they will be ashamed.

**6** Hear the loud noise from the city! Hear the sound from the temple! It is the sound of the LORD. He punishes his enemies for what they did.

**7** Zion gave birth before she felt the pain of birth. Before the pain came, she gave birth to a son.

**8** Who ever heard of a thing like this? Who ever saw things like these? Can a country be born in 1 day? Can a nation be born in 1 moment? But when Zion felt the pain of birth, she gave birth to her children at once.

**9** I start the birth of a child. Thus I will not stop the birth," says the LORD. "I cause the birth of a child. Thus I will not close the womb," says your God.

**10** "Be happy with Jerusalem, all you who love her. Be glad with her. Be very happy with her, all you who were sad for her.

**11** Then you will get comfort from her, as a baby gets milk from its mother. You will be satisfied. You will enjoy her great riches.

**12** The LORD says this: "Look, I will give Jerusalem peace that flows like a river. The riches of the nations will come to her like a stream that flows over its banks. You will be like a baby that its mother feeds. She will carry you in her arms. She will play with you on her knees.

**13** As a mother comforts her child, I will comfort you. You will get comfort in Jerusalem."

**14** When you see this, your heart will be happy. Your body will become strong like new grass. The LORD will show his power to help his servants. He will show his anger against his enemies.

**15** Look, the LORD will come with fire. His chariots will come like a storm. He will show his anger and punish his enemies with flames of fire.

**16** The LORD will judge all people with fire and with his sword. The LORD will kill many people.

**17** "Some people make themselves holy and clean to worship in the gardens. They follow a leader who stands in the center. They eat pork, rats and other unclean things. All these people will die together," says the LORD.

**18** "I know what they do and what they think. The time will come when I will gather all nations and all languages. They will come and they will see my glory.

**19** I will put a sign among them. I will send some of the survivors to the nations. I will send them to Tarshish, to Pul and to Lud, where people use bows and arrows. I will send them to Tubal, to Greece and to the islands far away. Those people did not hear about me or see my glory. My messengers will tell the nations about my glory.

**20** They will bring all your brothers from all the nations to my holy mountain, Jerusalem. They will bring them as an offering to the LORD. They will bring them on horses, in chariots and in wagons, and on mules and camels," says the LORD. "The people of Israel bring offerings to the temple of the LORD in clean containers. Thus your brothers will be like an offering in a clean container.

**21** I will also choose some of them to be priests and Levites," says the LORD.

**22** "The new heavens and the new earth that I will make will continue in front of me," says the LORD. "Thus your descendants and your name will continue always.

**23** On each new moon and on each Sabbath, all people will come and worship in front of me," says the LORD.

**24** "They will go out and see the dead bodies of the people who rebelled against me. The worms that eat those bodies will not die. The fire that burns them will not stop. All people will find those bodies horrible."
