# 1 Samuel

## Chapter 1

**1** There was a man from Ramathaim-zophim, in the hill country of Ephraim. His name was Elkanah. He was the son of Jeroham, the son of Elihu, the son of Tohu, the son of Zuph. He was an Ephrathite.

**2** Elkanah had 2 wives. The name of one wife was Hannah, and the name of the other wife was Peninnah. Peninnah had children, but Hannah had no children.

**3** Each year, Elkanah went up from his city to Shiloh. He went there to worship and to make sacrifices to the LORD of armies. The 2 sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas, were priests of the LORD at Shiloh.

**4** On the day when Elkanah made his sacrifice, he gave parts of the meat to his wife Peninnah. He also gave parts to all her sons and daughters.

**5** But Elkanah gave a double part to Hannah, because he loved Hannah. But the LORD did not let Hannah have children.

**6** Peninnah was the rival of Hannah. Peninnah caused Hannah much pain and anger, because the LORD did not let Hannah have children.

**7** This occurred year after year. Each time Hannah went up to the house of the LORD, Peninnah caused her pain. Thus Hannah cried and did not eat.

**8** Her husband Elkanah said to her, "Hannah, why do you cry? Why do you not eat? Why is your heart sad? Am I not better to you than 10 sons?"

**9** After they ate and drank in Shiloh, Hannah stood up. Eli the priest sat on a seat near the doorpost of the temple of the LORD.

**10** Hannah was very sad in her soul. She prayed to the LORD and cried very much.

**11** She made a promise (vow). She said, "LORD of armies, look at the trouble of your servant. Remember me and do not forget me. Give me a son. If you do this, I will give him to the LORD for all his life. No razor will touch his head."

**12** Hannah continued to pray before the LORD for a long time. Eli watched her mouth.

**13** Hannah spoke in her heart. Only her lips moved, and no one heard her voice. Thus Eli thought that she was drunk.

**14** Eli said to her, "How long will you be drunk? Stop your wine."

**15** Hannah said, "No, my lord. I am a woman with a very sad spirit. I did not drink wine or strong drink. I told all my trouble to the LORD."

**16** "Do not think that your servant is a bad woman. I spoke until now because of my great pain and sorrow."

**17** Then Eli said, "Go in peace. I pray that the God of Israel will give you what you asked from him."

**18** Hannah said, "I pray that you will be kind to your servant." Then the woman went away and ate. Her face was not sad any more.

**19** Early in the morning they got up and worshipped before the LORD. Then they went back to their house in Ramah. Elkanah had sexual relations with his wife Hannah, and the LORD remembered her.

**20** In time, Hannah became pregnant and gave birth to a son. She gave him the name Samuel. She said, "I asked the LORD for him."

**21** Elkanah and all his family went up to make the yearly sacrifice to the LORD and to keep his vow.

**22** But Hannah did not go up. She said to her husband, "I will not go up until the child stops his mother's milk. Then I will bring him to the LORD. He will stay there for all time."

**23** Her husband Elkanah said to her, "Do what you think is good. Stay until the child stops your milk. I pray that the LORD will make his word come true." So Hannah stayed at home. She gave her son milk until he stopped.

**24** After the child stopped her milk, Hannah took him with her. She also took 3 bulls, 1 ephah of flour, and a container of wine. She brought the boy to the house of the LORD in Shiloh. The child was young.

**25** They killed a bull, and they brought the child to Eli.

**26** Hannah said, "My lord, I tell you the truth. I am the woman who stood here near you and prayed to the LORD."

**27** "I prayed for this child. The LORD gave me what I asked from him."

**28** "Thus I give him to the LORD. For all his life, he will belong to the LORD." Then Elkanah worshipped the LORD there.

## Chapter 2

**1** Hannah prayed and said, "My heart is glad in the LORD. The LORD makes me strong. I laugh at my enemies, because I am glad that you saved me."

**2** "No one is holy like the LORD. There is no one but you. There is no rock like our God."

**3** "Do not speak with much pride. Do not let proud words come from your mouth. The LORD is a God who knows all things. He measures the actions of all people."

**4** "The LORD breaks the bows of the strong soldiers. But the weak people become strong."

**5** "The people who had much food now work for bread. But the hungry people are not hungry now. The woman who had no children now has 7 children. But the woman with many children becomes weak."

**6** "The LORD kills, and he gives life. He sends people down to the grave, and he brings them up."

**7** "The LORD makes people poor, and he makes people rich. He makes people low, and he lifts them up."

**8** "He lifts the poor person from the dust. He lifts the person in need from the ash pile. He puts them with princes and gives them a seat of honor. The foundations of the earth belong to the LORD. He put the world on them."

**9** "He will guard the feet of his faithful people. But the wicked people will become silent in the darkness. No person wins by his own strength."

**10** "The LORD will break his enemies into pieces. He will send thunder from heaven against them. The LORD will judge all the earth. He will give strength to his king. He will make his chosen king (anointed one) powerful."

**11** Then Elkanah went home to Ramah. But the child Samuel served the LORD under Eli the priest.

**12** The sons of Eli were wicked men. They did not know the LORD.

**13** This was the custom of the priests with the people. When a man made a sacrifice, the servant of the priest came while the meat boiled. The servant had a fork with 3 points in his hand.

**14** He pushed the fork into the pan, the pot, or the kettle. The priest took for himself all the meat that the fork brought up. The priests did this to all the Israelites who came to Shiloh.

**15** Also, before the people burned the fat, the servant of the priest came. He said to the man who made the sacrifice, "Give meat to the priest to roast. He will not take boiled meat from you. He wants raw meat."

**16** Sometimes the man said to the servant, "First, let the priests burn the fat. Then take as much as you want." But the servant said, "No. Give it to me now. If you do not, I will take it by force."

**17** The sin of these young men was very great before the LORD. They caused people to hate the offerings of the LORD.

**18** But Samuel served the LORD. He was a boy, and he wore a linen ephod.

**19** Each year his mother made a small robe for him. She brought it to him when she came up with her husband to make the yearly sacrifice.

**20** Eli blessed Elkanah and his wife. He said, "I pray that the LORD will give you children from this woman. These children will take the place of the child that she gave to the LORD." Then they went home.

**21** The LORD was kind to Hannah. She became pregnant and gave birth to 3 sons and 2 daughters. The boy Samuel grew up before the LORD.

**22** Eli was very old. He heard about all the bad things that his sons did to all Israel. He heard that they had sexual relations with the women who served at the door of the tabernacle.

**23** Eli said to his sons, "Why do you do these things? All the people tell me about your evil actions."

**24** "No, my sons! The report that I hear is not good. You cause the people of the LORD to sin."

**25** "If a man sins against another man, God can judge between them. But if a man sins against the LORD, who will speak for him?" But the sons did not obey their father, because the LORD wanted to kill them.

**26** The boy Samuel continued to grow. The LORD was happy with him, and people were happy with him.

**27** A man of God came to Eli and spoke to him. He said, "The LORD says this. 'I showed myself to the family of your father when they were in Egypt. They were slaves to Pharaoh.'"

**28** "'I chose your ancestor from all the tribes of Israel to be my priest. I chose him to offer sacrifices on my altar, to burn incense, and to wear an ephod before me. I gave your family all the offerings that the Israelites burn with fire.'"

**29** "'Why do you show no respect for my sacrifices and my offerings? I commanded these for my house. Why do you honor your sons more than me? You make yourselves fat with the best parts of all the offerings of my people Israel.'"

**30** "Thus the LORD, the God of Israel, says this: 'I said that your family will serve me for all time. But now I will not let this occur. I will honor the people who honor me. But I will not respect the people who hate me.'"

**31** "'Look, the time will come when I will remove the strength of your family. No man in your family will live to become old.'"

**32** "'You will see trouble in my house. God will give good things to Israel. But no man in your family will ever live to become old.'"

**33** "'I will not remove all your descendants from my altar. But the man who stays will cry until his eyes fail, and his heart will be sad. All your descendants will die when they are young men.'"

**34** "'This will be a sign to you. Your 2 sons, Hophni and Phinehas, will both die on the same day.'"

**35** "'I will choose a faithful priest for myself. He will do what is in my heart and in my mind. I will give him a family that will last. He will serve before my chosen king for all time.'"

**36** "'Each person who stays alive in your family will come and bow down to him. He will ask for a piece of silver and a piece of bread. He will say, "Please give me work as a priest, so that I can have bread to eat."'"

## Chapter 3

**1** The boy Samuel served the LORD under Eli. In those days, the word of the LORD came very rarely. There were not many visions.

**2** At that time, Eli lay down in his usual place. His eyes started to become weak, and he could not see well.

**3** The lamp of God did not go out yet. Samuel lay down to sleep in the temple of the LORD, where the ark of God was.

**4** Then the LORD called Samuel. Samuel said, "Here I am."

**5** Samuel ran to Eli and said, "Here I am. You called me." But Eli said, "I did not call you. Lie down again." So Samuel went and lay down.

**6** The LORD called again, "Samuel." Samuel got up and went to Eli. He said, "Here I am. You called me." Eli said, "I did not call you, my son. Lie down again."

**7** Samuel did not yet know the LORD. The word of the LORD did not yet come to him.

**8** The LORD called Samuel a third time. Samuel got up and went to Eli. He said, "Here I am. You called me." Then Eli understood that the LORD called the boy.

**9** So Eli said to Samuel, "Go and lie down. If he calls you, then say, 'Speak, LORD. Your servant listens.'" So Samuel went and lay down in his place.

**10** The LORD came and stood there. He called as at the other times, "Samuel! Samuel!" Then Samuel said, "Speak. Your servant listens."

**11** The LORD said to Samuel, "Look, I will do a thing in Israel. The ears of each person who hears about it will ring."

**12** "On that day I will do to Eli all the things that I said against his family. I will do all of it from the start to the end."

**13** "I told Eli that I will punish his family for all time. I will do this because of the sin that he knows about. His sons spoke evil against God, and Eli did not stop them."

**14** "Thus I made a solemn promise to the family of Eli. No sacrifice or offering will ever remove the sin of the family of Eli."

**15** Samuel lay down until the morning. Then he opened the doors of the house of the LORD. Samuel was afraid to tell Eli about the vision.

**16** But Eli called Samuel and said, "Samuel, my son." Samuel said, "Here I am."

**17** Eli said, "What did the LORD say to you? Please do not hide it from me. God will punish you very much if you hide any of his words from me."

**18** So Samuel told Eli all of it. He hid nothing from him. Eli said, "He is the LORD. Let him do what he thinks is good."

**19** Samuel grew up, and the LORD was with him. The LORD made all the words of Samuel come true.

**20** All Israel, from Dan to Beersheba, knew that the LORD made Samuel a true prophet.

**21** The LORD continued to show himself in Shiloh. He showed himself to Samuel in Shiloh through his word.

## Chapter 4

**1** The word of Samuel went out to all Israel. The Israelites went out to fight against the Philistines. The Israelites made their camp at Ebenezer, and the Philistines made their camp at Aphek.

**2** The Philistines put their army in lines against Israel. The battle started, and the Philistines defeated Israel. The Philistines killed about 4,000 Israelite soldiers on the battlefield.

**3** The soldiers came back to the camp. The elders of Israel said, "Why did the LORD let the Philistines defeat us today? We will bring the ark of the covenant of the LORD from Shiloh. If the ark comes with us, it will save us from our enemies."

**4** So the people sent men to Shiloh. The men brought back the ark of the covenant of the LORD of armies. The LORD sits on his throne between the cherubim on the ark. The 2 sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas, were there with the ark.

**5** The ark of the covenant of the LORD came into the camp. All the Israelites shouted very loudly, and the ground shook.

**6** The Philistines heard the noise of the shout. They said, "What is this loud shout in the camp of the Hebrews?" Then they learned that the ark of the LORD came into the camp.

**7** The Philistines were afraid. They said, "A god came into their camp. We are in trouble! A thing like this never occurred before."

**8** "We are in trouble! Who will save us from the power of these strong gods? These are the gods who killed the Egyptians with all kinds of plagues in the desert."

**9** "Philistines, be strong and be brave men! If you are not, you will become slaves to the Hebrews. The Hebrews were your slaves. Be brave men, and fight!"

**10** The Philistines fought, and they defeated Israel. Each Israelite ran away to his tent. Very many people died. 30,000 Israelite foot soldiers died.

**11** The Philistines took the ark of God. Eli's 2 sons, Hophni and Phinehas, died.

**12** A man from the tribe of Benjamin ran from the battle. He came to Shiloh on the same day. His clothes had tears in them, and he had dust on his head.

**13** When the man came, Eli sat on his seat near the road. Eli watched, because his heart was afraid for the ark of God. The man came into the city and told the news. Then all the people in the city cried out.

**14** Eli heard the noise of the cries. He said, "What is this loud noise?" The man came quickly and told Eli.

**15** Eli was 98 years old. His eyes were weak, and he could not see.

**16** The man said to Eli, "I came from the battle. I ran away from the battle today." Eli said, "What occurred, my son?"

**17** The messenger said, "Israel ran away from the Philistines. Very many of our people died. Your 2 sons, Hophni and Phinehas, are dead. And the Philistines took the ark of God."

**18** When the man spoke about the ark of God, Eli fell back from his seat near the gate. His neck broke, and he died, because he was old and heavy. Eli was judge of Israel for 40 years.

**19** Eli's daughter-in-law, the wife of Phinehas, was pregnant. It was almost the time for the birth. She heard that the Philistines took the ark of God. She also heard that her father-in-law and her husband were dead. Then she bent down, and the pains of birth started. She gave birth.

**20** She was near death. The women who were with her said, "Do not be afraid. You gave birth to a son." But she did not answer, and she did not pay attention.

**21** She gave the child the name Ichabod. She said, "The glory went away from Israel." She said this because the Philistines took the ark of God, and because her father-in-law and her husband were dead.

**22** She said, "The glory went away from Israel, because the Philistines took the ark of God."

## Chapter 5

**1** The Philistines took the ark of God. They brought it from Ebenezer to Ashdod.

**2** The Philistines carried the ark of God into the temple of Dagon. They put the ark next to the statue of Dagon.

**3** The people of Ashdod got up early on the next day. They saw that the statue of Dagon was on the ground on its face before the ark of the LORD. They lifted Dagon and put him in his place again.

**4** On the next morning they got up early again. Dagon was again on the ground on its face before the ark of the LORD. The head and both hands of Dagon broke off. They were on the doorstep. Only the body of Dagon stayed.

**5** Thus, until now, the priests of Dagon do not step on the doorstep of the temple of Dagon in Ashdod. All the people who go into the temple also do not step on it.

**6** The LORD punished the people of Ashdod very much. He destroyed them. He caused tumors on the people of Ashdod and of the area near it.

**7** The men of Ashdod saw what occurred. They said, "The ark of the God of Israel must not stay with us. His power is hard against us and against Dagon our god."

**8** So they sent messengers and brought together all the rulers of the Philistines. They said, "What must we do with the ark of the God of Israel?" The rulers said, "Move the ark of the God of Israel to Gath." So they moved the ark of the God of Israel to Gath.

**9** But after they moved the ark, the LORD was against that city too. He caused much panic there. He caused tumors on the men of the city, young and old.

**10** So they sent the ark of God to Ekron. When the ark of God came to Ekron, the people of Ekron cried out. They said, "They brought the ark of the God of Israel to us. They want to kill us and our people!"

**11** So they sent messengers and brought together all the rulers of the Philistines. The people said, "Send away the ark of the God of Israel. Send it back to its own place, so that it does not kill us and our people." There was fear of death in all the city. God punished the city very much.

**12** The men who did not die had tumors. The cry of the city went up to heaven.

## Chapter 6

**1** The ark of the LORD was in the land of the Philistines for 7 months.

**2** The Philistines called their priests and the men who tell the future. They said, "What must we do with the ark of the LORD? Tell us how to send it back to its place."

**3** The priests said, "If you send away the ark of the God of Israel, do not send it empty. You must send an offering for guilt with it. Then you will become healthy. And you will know why his power is against you."

**4** The people said, "What offering for guilt must we send to him?" The priests said, "Send 5 gold tumors and 5 gold rats. There are 5 rulers of the Philistines. The same plague came on you and on your rulers."

**5** "Make models of your tumors and models of the rats that destroy the land. Give honor to the God of Israel. Possibly he will stop his punishment of you, your gods, and your land."

**6** "Why do you make your hearts hard, as the Egyptians and Pharaoh did? God punished them very much. Then the Egyptians let the Israelites go, and the Israelites went away."

**7** "Now make a new cart. Get 2 cows that give milk and that never pulled a cart. Attach the cows to the cart. But take their calves away from them, and keep the calves at home."

**8** "Put the ark of the LORD on the cart. Put the gold objects in a box next to the ark. These objects are your offering for guilt. Then send the cart away, and let it go."

**9** "Watch the cart. If it goes up toward its own land, to Beth-shemesh, then the LORD caused this great disaster to us. If it does not, then we will know that his power did not hurt us. It was only chance."

**10** The men did this. They took 2 cows that gave milk and attached them to the cart. They kept their calves at home.

**11** They put the ark of the LORD on the cart. They also put on the cart the box with the gold rats and the models of their tumors.

**12** The cows went straight on the road toward Beth-shemesh. They stayed on the road and made loud sounds as they went. They did not turn to the right or to the left. The rulers of the Philistines followed them to the border of Beth-shemesh.

**13** The people of Beth-shemesh cut their wheat in the valley. They looked up and saw the ark. They were very glad when they saw it.

**14** The cart came into the field of Joshua from Beth-shemesh. It stopped there next to a large rock. The people cut the wood of the cart into pieces. They offered the cows as a burnt offering to the LORD.

**15** The Levites took down the ark of the LORD and the box with the gold objects. They put them on the large rock. On that day the men of Beth-shemesh offered burnt offerings and made sacrifices to the LORD.

**16** The 5 rulers of the Philistines saw this. Then they went back to Ekron on the same day.

**17** The Philistines sent these gold tumors as an offering for guilt to the LORD. There was 1 for Ashdod, 1 for Gaza, 1 for Ashkelon, 1 for Gath, and 1 for Ekron.

**18** The number of the gold rats was the same as the number of the cities of the 5 Philistine rulers. These included the cities with walls and the villages with no walls. The large rock where the people put the ark of the LORD is a witness. That rock is still in the field of Joshua from Beth-shemesh.

**19** But God killed some men of Beth-shemesh, because they looked into the ark of the LORD. He killed 50,070 men. The people cried very much because the LORD killed so many of them.

**20** The men of Beth-shemesh said, "No person can stand before the LORD, this holy God. Where must the ark go away from us?"

**21** They sent messengers to the people of Kiriath-jearim. The messengers said, "The Philistines sent back the ark of the LORD. Come down and take it up to your city."

## Chapter 7

**1** The men of Kiriath-jearim came and took the ark of the LORD. They brought it into the house of Abinadab on the hill. They made his son Eleazar holy to guard the ark of the LORD.

**2** The ark stayed in Kiriath-jearim for a long time. It stayed there for 20 years. All the people of Israel were sad and wanted the LORD.

**3** Samuel spoke to all the people of Israel. He said, "If you come back to the LORD with all your heart, then remove the foreign gods and the Ashtoreth statues. Give your hearts to the LORD and serve only him. Then he will save you from the Philistines."

**4** So the Israelites removed the statues of Baal and Ashtoreth. They served only the LORD.

**5** Samuel said, "Bring all Israel to Mizpah. I will pray to the LORD for you."

**6** The people came together at Mizpah. They got water and poured it out before the LORD. On that day they did not eat food (fasted). They said, "We sinned against the LORD." Samuel was judge of the Israelites at Mizpah.

**7** The Philistines heard that the Israelites came together at Mizpah. So the rulers of the Philistines went up to attack Israel. The Israelites heard about this, and they were afraid of the Philistines.

**8** The Israelites said to Samuel, "Do not stop. Pray to the LORD our God for us. Ask him to save us from the Philistines."

**9** Samuel took a young lamb that still drank milk. He offered all of it as a burnt offering to the LORD. Samuel prayed to the LORD for Israel, and the LORD answered him.

**10** Samuel offered the burnt offering. At that time the Philistines came near to attack Israel. But on that day the LORD sent very loud thunder against the Philistines. The thunder caused panic in the Philistine army. So Israel defeated the Philistines.

**11** The men of Israel went out of Mizpah and chased the Philistines. They killed Philistines all along the road, to a place below Beth-car.

**12** Then Samuel took a stone and put it between Mizpah and Shen. He gave it the name Ebenezer. He said, "The LORD helped us until now."

**13** Thus Israel defeated the Philistines. The Philistines did not come into the land of Israel again. The LORD was against the Philistines for all the life of Samuel.

**14** Israel got back the cities that the Philistines took, from Ekron to Gath. Israel also took back the land near these cities from the Philistines. There was also peace between Israel and the Amorites.

**15** Samuel was judge of Israel for all his life.

**16** Each year he went in a circle to Bethel, Gilgal, and Mizpah. He was judge of Israel in all those places.

**17** Then he always went back to Ramah, because his home was there. He was also judge of Israel there. He built an altar to the LORD in Ramah.

## Chapter 8

**1** When Samuel became old, he made his sons judges over Israel.

**2** The name of his first son was Joel. The name of his second son was Abijah. They were judges in Beersheba.

**3** But his sons did not live as Samuel lived. They wanted money. They took bribes and did not judge honestly.

**4** So all the elders of Israel came together. They came to Samuel at Ramah.

**5** They said to him, "You are old, and your sons do not live as you live. Now give us a king to judge us. All the other nations have a king."

**6** Samuel was not happy when they said, "Give us a king to judge us." So Samuel prayed to the LORD.

**7** The LORD said to Samuel, "Do all that the people tell you. They did not refuse you. They refused me as their king."

**8** "Since the day that I brought them out of Egypt, they always left me and served other gods. Now they do the same thing to you."

**9** "Do what they ask. But warn them seriously. Tell them how the king who rules them will act."

**10** Samuel told all the words of the LORD to the people who asked for a king.

**11** He said, "This is how the king who rules you will act. He will take your sons. He will make them work with his chariots and his horses. Some of them will run in front of his chariots."

**12** "He will make some of them commanders of 1,000 men and commanders of 50 men. He will make others plow his fields and cut his crops. He will make others make weapons and equipment for his chariots."

**13** "He will take your daughters to make perfumes, to cook, and to bake bread."

**14** "He will take the best of your fields, vineyards, and olive groves. He will give them to his officials."

**15** "He will take a tenth of your grain and of your grapes. He will give it to his officers and his officials."

**16** "He will take your male and female servants, your best young men, and your donkeys. He will make them do his work."

**17** "He will take a tenth of your sheep. And you will become his slaves."

**18** "On that day you will cry out because of the king that you chose. But the LORD will not answer you on that day."

**19** But the people did not listen to Samuel. They said, "No! We want a king over us."

**20** "Then we will be like all the other nations. Our king will judge us. He will lead us and fight our battles."

**21** Samuel heard all the words of the people. He told them to the LORD.

**22** The LORD said to Samuel, "Do what they ask, and give them a king." Then Samuel said to the men of Israel, "Each man must go back to his city."

## Chapter 9

**1** There was a man from the tribe of Benjamin. His name was Kish. He was the son of Abiel, the son of Zeror, the son of Becorath, the son of Aphiah. Kish was an important and rich man.

**2** Kish had a son with the name Saul. Saul was a handsome young man. No man in Israel was more handsome than Saul. He was taller than all the other people by the height of his head and shoulders.

**3** The donkeys of Kish, the father of Saul, became lost. Kish said to his son Saul, "Take one of the servants with you. Go and look for the donkeys."

**4** Saul went through the hill country of Ephraim and through the land of Shalishah. But they did not find the donkeys. They went through the land of Shaalim, but the donkeys were not there. Saul went through the land of Benjamin, but they did not find the donkeys.

**5** They came to the land of Zuph. Saul said to his servant, "Come, we must go back. If we do not, my father will stop his worry about the donkeys. He will start to worry about us."

**6** The servant said, "Look, a man of God lives in this city. People respect him. All that he says comes true. We will go there now. Possibly he can tell us where to go."

**7** Saul said to his servant, "If we go, what will we give the man? We have no bread in our bags. We have no gift for the man of God. What do we have?"

**8** The servant answered Saul again. He said, "Look, I have a quarter of a shekel of silver. I will give it to the man of God. Then he will tell us where to go."

**9** (In past times in Israel, when a person went to ask God a question, he said, "Come, we will go to the seer." Now people call a seer a prophet.)

**10** Saul said to his servant, "Good. Come, we will go." So they went to the city where the man of God was.

**11** They went up the hill to the city. They met some young women who came out to get water. Saul and his servant said to them, "Is the seer here?"

**12** The women said, "Yes, he is. He is in front of you. Go quickly. He came to the city today, because the people make a sacrifice today at the high place."

**13** "When you go into the city, you will find him immediately. He will go up to the high place to eat. The people will not eat until he comes, because he must bless the sacrifice. After that, the guests eat. Go up now. You will find him soon."

**14** So they went up to the city. As they went into the city, Samuel came out toward them. He went up to the high place.

**15** On the day before Saul came, the LORD spoke to Samuel. The LORD said,

**16** "At this time tomorrow, I will send a man to you from the land of Benjamin. Pour oil on him to make him the leader of my people Israel. He will save my people from the Philistines. I saw the trouble of my people, and I heard their cry."

**17** When Samuel saw Saul, the LORD said to Samuel, "Look, this is the man that I told you about. He will rule my people."

**18** Saul came near to Samuel at the gate. Saul said, "Please tell me where the house of the seer is."

**19** Samuel said to Saul, "I am the seer. Go up in front of me to the high place. Today you will eat with me. Tomorrow morning I will let you go. I will tell you all that is in your heart."

**20** "Do not worry about the donkeys that you lost 3 days ago. People found them. All Israel wants you and the family of your father."

**21** Saul said, "But I am from the tribe of Benjamin, the smallest tribe of Israel. My family is the least important of all the families in the tribe of Benjamin. Why do you say this to me?"

## Chapter 10

**1** Samuel took a small container of oil. He poured the oil on the head of Saul and kissed him. Samuel said, "The LORD chose you (anointed you) to be the leader of his people."

**2** "When you go away from me today, you will meet 2 men near the tomb of Rachel. The tomb is at Zelzah, at the border of Benjamin. The men will say to you, 'People found the donkeys that you looked for. Now your father does not worry about the donkeys. He worries about you. He says, "What must I do to find my son?"'"

**3** "Then you will continue to the large oak tree of Tabor. There you will meet 3 men who go up to worship God at Bethel. One man will carry 3 young goats. Another will carry 3 loaves of bread. Another will carry a container of wine."

**4** "They will greet you and give you 2 loaves of bread. Take the bread from them."

**5** "After that you will come to the hill of God. Some Philistine soldiers are there. When you come to the city, you will meet a group of prophets. They will come down from the high place. People with harps, tambourines, flutes, and lyres will go in front of them. The prophets will prophesy."

**6** "Then the Spirit of the LORD will come on you with power. You will prophesy with them. You will become a different man."

**7** "When these signs occur, do what you must do. God is with you."

**8** "Go down to Gilgal before me. I will come down to you there. I will offer burnt offerings and peace offerings. Wait 7 days until I come to you. Then I will tell you what to do."

**9** Saul turned to go away from Samuel. Then God changed the heart of Saul. All these signs occurred on that day.

**10** Saul and his servant came to the hill. A group of prophets met him. The Spirit of God came on Saul with power, and he prophesied with them.

**11** The people who knew Saul before saw him prophesy with the prophets. They said to each other, "What occurred to the son of Kish? Is Saul also one of the prophets?"

**12** A man from that place said, "And who is the father of these prophets?" Thus this became a proverb: "Is Saul also one of the prophets?"

**13** When Saul stopped his prophecy, he went to the high place.

**14** The uncle of Saul said to Saul and his servant, "Where did you go?" Saul said, "We looked for the donkeys. When we did not find them, we went to Samuel."

**15** The uncle of Saul said, "Please tell me what Samuel said to you."

**16** Saul said to his uncle, "He told us that people found the donkeys." But Saul did not tell his uncle what Samuel said about the kingdom.

**17** Samuel called the people together to the LORD at Mizpah.

**18** He said to the Israelites, "The LORD, the God of Israel, says this: 'I brought Israel out of Egypt. I saved you from the Egyptians. I saved you from all the kingdoms that caused you trouble.'"

**19** "But today you refused your God. He saves you from all your troubles and pains. But you said, 'No, give us a king.' So now stand before the LORD by your tribes and by your family groups."

**20** Samuel brought all the tribes of Israel near. The LORD chose the tribe of Benjamin.

**21** Then Samuel brought the tribe of Benjamin near, family by family. The LORD chose the family of Matri. Then the LORD chose Saul, the son of Kish. But when the people looked for Saul, they did not find him.

**22** So they asked the LORD again, "Did the man come here?" The LORD said, "Yes. He hid himself among the baggage."

**23** They ran and brought Saul from there. He stood among the people. He was taller than all the other people by the height of his head and shoulders.

**24** Samuel said to all the people, "Look at the man that the LORD chose. There is no person like him among all the people." Then all the people shouted, "We pray that the king will live long!"

**26** Saul also went home to Gibeah. A group of brave men went with him. God touched their hearts.

**27** But some wicked men said, "How can this man save us?" They did not respect Saul, and they did not bring him gifts. But Saul said nothing.

## Chapter 11

**1** Nahash the Ammonite came up and put his army around Jabesh-gilead. All the men of Jabesh said to Nahash, "Make a covenant with us, and we will serve you."

**2** Nahash the Ammonite said to them, "I will make a covenant with you on one condition. I will remove the right eye of each of you. Thus I will bring shame on all Israel."

**3** The elders of Jabesh said to him, "Give us 7 days. We will send messengers to all the land of Israel. If no person comes to save us, then we will come out to you."

**4** The messengers came to Gibeah, where Saul lived. They told the news to the people. All the people cried loudly.

**5** At that time Saul came from the field with his oxen. He said, "Why do the people cry?" They told him the news from the men of Jabesh.

**6** When Saul heard the news, the Spirit of God came on him with power. He became very angry.

**7** He took 2 oxen and cut them into pieces. He gave the pieces to messengers and sent them to all the land of Israel. The messengers said, "Each man must follow Saul and Samuel. If a man does not, then the same thing will occur to his oxen." The people feared the LORD. They came out together as one man.

**8** Saul counted the men at Bezek. There were 300,000 men from Israel and 30,000 men from Judah.

**9** They said to the messengers who came, "Tell the men of Jabesh-gilead this. 'Tomorrow, when the sun is hot, you will get help.'" The messengers came and told this to the men of Jabesh. The men of Jabesh were very glad.

**10** Then the men of Jabesh said to the Ammonites, "Tomorrow we will come out to you. Then you can do to us all that you think is good."

**11** On the next day Saul put his men into 3 groups. Before dawn they went into the camp of the Ammonites. They killed Ammonites until the middle of the day. The Ammonites who stayed alive ran in different directions. No 2 of them stayed together.

**12** Then the people said to Samuel, "Who said, 'Saul must not rule over us'? Bring those men to us. We will kill them."

**13** But Saul said, "No man will die today. Today the LORD saved Israel."

**14** Then Samuel said to the people, "Come, we will go to Gilgal. There we will make the kingdom new again."

**15** So all the people went to Gilgal. There they made Saul king before the LORD. They made peace offerings before the LORD. Saul and all the men of Israel were very glad.

## Chapter 12

**1** Samuel said to all Israel, "I did all that you asked me. I gave you a king."

**2** "Now you have a king to lead you. I am old, and my hair is gray. My sons are here with you. I led you from when I was a boy until today."

**3** "Here I am. Speak against me before the LORD and before his chosen king. Did I take the ox or the donkey of any person? Did I cheat any person? Did I hurt any person? Did I take a bribe from any person to ignore a wrong? If I did, I will pay it back to you."

**4** The people said, "You did not cheat us or hurt us. You did not take anything from any person."

**5** Samuel said to them, "Today the LORD is a witness, and his chosen king is a witness. They know that you did not find anything wrong in me." The people said, "He is a witness."

**6** Then Samuel said to the people, "The LORD chose Moses and Aaron. He brought your ancestors out of the land of Egypt."

**7** "Now stand here before the LORD. I will remind you of all the good things that the LORD did for you and for your ancestors."

**8** "After Jacob went into Egypt, your ancestors cried to the LORD for help. The LORD sent Moses and Aaron. They brought your ancestors out of Egypt and caused them to live in this place."

**9** "But your ancestors forgot the LORD their God. So he gave them to Sisera, the commander of the army of Hazor. He gave them to the Philistines and to the king of Moab. These enemies fought against them."

**10** "Then your ancestors cried to the LORD. They said, 'We sinned. We left the LORD and served the statues of Baal and Ashtoreth. But now save us from our enemies, and we will serve you.'"

**11** "Then the LORD sent Jerub-baal, Bedan, Jephthah, and Samuel. He saved you from the enemies around you. Then you lived in safety."

**12** "But you saw Nahash, the king of the Ammonites, come against you. Then you said to me, 'No, we want a king to rule over us.' But the LORD your God was your king."

**13** "Now here is the king that you chose. You asked for him. Look, the LORD gave you a king."

**14** "You must fear the LORD, serve him, and obey him. Do not go against the commands of the LORD. If you and your king follow the LORD your God, then all will be well."

**15** "But if you do not obey the LORD, and you go against his commands, then the LORD will be against you. He was against your ancestors in the same way."

**16** "Now stand here and see this great thing. The LORD will do it in front of you."

**17** "It is now the time of the wheat harvest. I will pray to the LORD, and he will send thunder and rain. Then you will know that you did a very evil thing. You did evil before the LORD when you asked for a king."

**18** Then Samuel prayed to the LORD. On that day the LORD sent thunder and rain. All the people were very afraid of the LORD and of Samuel.

**19** All the people said to Samuel, "Pray to the LORD your God for your servants, so that we do not die. We added this evil to all our sins. We asked for a king."

**20** Samuel said to the people, "Do not be afraid. You did all this evil. But do not turn away from the LORD. Serve the LORD with all your heart."

**21** "Do not turn away to follow false gods. They cannot help you or save you. They are of no use."

**22** "The LORD will not leave his people, because he wants to keep the honor of his great name. The LORD was happy to make you his people."

**23** "I will never sin against the LORD. I will not stop my prayers for you. I will teach you the good and correct way to live."

**24** "But you must fear the LORD. Serve him faithfully with all your heart. Think about the great things that he did for you."

**25** "But if you continue to do evil, then the LORD will destroy you and your king."

## Chapter 13

**1** Saul was king for 1 year. When he was king over Israel for 2 years,

**2** Saul chose 3,000 men of Israel. 2,000 men were with Saul in Michmash and in the hill country of Bethel. 1,000 men were with Jonathan in Gibeah of Benjamin. Saul sent the other men home to their tents.

**3** Jonathan attacked the Philistine soldiers at Geba. The Philistines heard about it. Saul blew the trumpet in all the land. He said, "The Hebrews must hear this!"

**4** All Israel heard the news: "Saul attacked the Philistine soldiers. Now the Philistines hate Israel." Then the people came together to follow Saul at Gilgal.

**5** The Philistines came together to fight against Israel. They had 30,000 chariots and 6,000 horsemen. They had as many soldiers as the sand on the seashore. They went up and made their camp at Michmash, to the east of Beth-aven.

**6** The men of Israel saw that they were in danger. The army was in much trouble. So the people hid in caves, in bushes, among rocks, in holes, and in wells.

**7** Some Hebrews went across the Jordan River to the land of Gad and Gilead. Saul stayed in Gilgal. All the people who followed him shook with fear.

**8** Saul waited 7 days, the time that Samuel told him. But Samuel did not come to Gilgal. The people started to leave Saul.

**9** So Saul said, "Bring me the burnt offering and the peace offerings." Then Saul offered the burnt offering.

**10** Saul stopped the burnt offering. Immediately Samuel came. Saul went out to meet him and to greet him.

**11** Samuel said, "What did you do?" Saul said, "I saw that the people left me. You did not come at the time that you said. And the Philistines came together at Michmash."

**12** "So I thought, 'Now the Philistines will come down against me at Gilgal. And I did not yet ask the LORD for help.' So I made myself do it. I offered the burnt offering."

**13** Samuel said to Saul, "You did a foolish thing. You did not obey the command of the LORD your God. If you obeyed, the LORD was ready to make your kingdom over Israel last for all time."

**14** "But now your kingdom will not continue. The LORD looked for a man who obeys him with all his heart. The LORD chose that man to be the leader of his people. He did this because you did not obey the command of the LORD."

**15** Then Samuel got up and went from Gilgal to Gibeah of Benjamin. Saul counted the men who were with him. There were about 600 men.

**16** Saul, his son Jonathan, and their men stayed in Gibeah of Benjamin. The Philistines made their camp at Michmash.

**17** Raiders came out of the Philistine camp in 3 groups. One group went toward Ophrah, in the land of Shual.

**18** Another group went toward Beth-horon. Another group went toward the border above the valley of Zeboim, toward the desert.

**19** There was no metal worker in all the land of Israel. The Philistines said, "If there are metal workers, then the Hebrews will make swords or spears."

**20** So all the Israelites went down to the Philistines to make their tools sharp. They took their plows, their hoes, their axes, and their sickles.

**21** The price was 2 thirds of a shekel for plows and hoes. The price was 1 third of a shekel for forks and axes, and to make the ox sticks sharp.

**22** So on the day of the battle, no soldier with Saul and Jonathan had a sword or a spear. Only Saul and his son Jonathan had them.

**23** The Philistine soldiers went out to the pass at Michmash.

## Chapter 14

**1** One day Jonathan, the son of Saul, spoke to the young man who carried his weapons. Jonathan said, "Come, we will go across to the Philistine camp on the other side." But Jonathan did not tell his father.

**2** Saul stayed at the edge of Gibeah, under a pomegranate tree in Migron. There were about 600 men with him.

**3** Ahijah was there, and he wore an ephod. Ahijah was the son of Ahitub, the brother of Ichabod. Ichabod was the son of Phinehas, the son of Eli, the priest of the LORD in Shiloh. The people did not know that Jonathan went away.

**4** Jonathan wanted to go through the pass to the Philistine camp. There was a steep rock on each side of the pass. The name of one rock was Bozez. The name of the other rock was Seneh.

**5** One rock was on the north side, near Michmash. The other rock was on the south side, near Geba.

**6** Jonathan said to the young man who carried his weapons, "Come, we will go across to the camp of these uncircumcised men. Possibly the LORD will help us. Nothing can stop the LORD. He can save with many men or with few men."

**7** The man who carried his weapons said to him, "Do all that you want. Go ahead. I am with you in all that you decide."

**8** Then Jonathan said, "We will go across to these men. We will let them see us."

**9** "If they say to us, 'Wait there until we come to you,' then we will stay in our place. We will not go up to them."

**10** "But if they say, 'Come up to us,' then we will go up. That will be a sign to us that the LORD gave them to us."

**11** So the 2 men let the Philistine soldiers see them. The Philistines said, "Look, the Hebrews come out of the holes where they hid."

**12** The Philistine soldiers called to Jonathan and the man who carried his weapons. They said, "Come up to us, and we will teach you a lesson." Jonathan said to the man who carried his weapons, "Climb up after me. The LORD gave them to Israel."

**13** Jonathan climbed up with his hands and feet. The man who carried his weapons climbed after him. Jonathan attacked the Philistines, and they fell. The man who carried his weapons came after Jonathan and killed them.

**14** In that first attack, Jonathan and the man who carried his weapons killed about 20 men. They did this in an area of about half an acre.

**15** Then much fear came on all the Philistines in the camp and in the field. The soldiers in the camp and the raiders also shook with fear. The ground shook too. God sent this great fear.

**16** The guards of Saul in Gibeah of Benjamin looked. They saw that the Philistine army ran in all directions.

**17** Saul said to the men who were with him, "Count the men. Find who is not here." They counted the men. Jonathan and the man who carried his weapons were not there.

**18** Saul said to Ahijah, "Bring the ark of God here." At that time the ark of God was with the Israelites.

**19** While Saul talked to the priest, the noise in the Philistine camp became louder and louder. So Saul said to the priest, "Stop. Do not continue."

**20** Then Saul and all his men came together and went to the battle. They found that the Philistines attacked each other with their swords. There was very much confusion.

**21** Some Hebrews were with the Philistines before. They went with the Philistines into their camp. But now these Hebrews went to the side of the Israelites who were with Saul and Jonathan.

**22** Some Israelites hid in the hill country of Ephraim. They heard that the Philistines ran away. So they also chased the Philistines in the battle.

**23** Thus the LORD saved Israel on that day. The battle went on past Beth-aven.

**24** The men of Israel were in much trouble on that day. Saul made the people make a solemn promise. He said, "God will curse any man who eats food before evening, before I take revenge on my enemies." So none of the people ate any food.

**25** All the army went into a forest. There was honey on the ground.

**26** The people went into the forest and saw the honey. But no man put any honey in his mouth, because they were afraid of the promise.

**27** But Jonathan did not hear when his father made the people promise. So he put the end of his stick into a honeycomb. He put the honey in his mouth, and he felt strong again.

**28** Then one of the soldiers said, "Your father made the army make a solemn promise. He said, 'God will curse any man who eats food today.' That is why the people are so weak."

**29** Jonathan said, "My father caused trouble for the land. Look how strong I feel, because I ate a little of this honey."

**30** "It was a mistake that the people did not eat today from the goods that they took from their enemies. With that food, we could kill many more Philistines."

**31** On that day the Israelites killed Philistines from Michmash to Aijalon. The people were very weak.

**32** The people ran to the goods that they took. They took sheep, cattle, and calves. They killed the animals on the ground. Then they ate the meat with the blood still in it.

**33** Some men told Saul, "Look, the people sin against the LORD. They eat meat with the blood in it." Saul said, "You were not faithful. Roll a large stone to me now."

**34** Then Saul said, "Go among the people. Tell them, 'Each man must bring his ox or his sheep to me. Kill them here, and eat. Do not sin against the LORD. Do not eat meat with the blood in it.'" So that night each man brought his ox and killed it there.

**35** Then Saul built an altar to the LORD. It was the first altar that he built to the LORD.

**36** Saul said, "We will go down tonight and chase the Philistines. We will take their goods until the morning. We will not let any of them stay alive." The people said, "Do all that you think is good." But the priest said, "First we must ask God here."

**37** So Saul asked God, "Must I go down and chase the Philistines? Will you give them to Israel?" But God did not answer Saul on that day.

**38** Saul said, "All the leaders of the army must come here. We must find what sin occurred today."

**39** "I make a promise by the LORD, who lives and who saves Israel. The man who sinned will die, even if he is my son Jonathan." But none of the people said anything.

**40** Then Saul said to all Israel, "Stand on one side. My son Jonathan and I will stand on the other side." The people said to Saul, "Do what you think is good."

**41** Then Saul said to the LORD, the God of Israel, "Show us the truth." The lot showed Jonathan and Saul. It did not show the people.

**42** Saul said, "Throw the lot between me and my son Jonathan." The lot showed Jonathan.

**43** Then Saul said to Jonathan, "Tell me what you did." Jonathan told him. He said, "I ate a little honey with the end of my stick. Here I am. I am ready to die."

**44** Saul said, "Jonathan, you must die. If you do not, then God will punish me very much."

**45** But the people said to Saul, "Jonathan must not die! He won this great victory for Israel. Never! We make a promise by the LORD, who lives. Not one hair of his head will fall to the ground. Today he did this with the help of God." So the people saved Jonathan, and he did not die.

**46** Then Saul stopped his chase of the Philistines. The Philistines went back to their own land.

**47** After Saul became king over Israel, he fought against all his enemies around him. He fought against Moab, the Ammonites, Edom, the kings of Zobah, and the Philistines. He defeated his enemies in all directions.

**48** Saul fought bravely and defeated the Amalekites. He saved Israel from the people who took their goods.

**49** The sons of Saul were Jonathan, Ishvi, and Malchi-shua. He had 2 daughters. The name of the older daughter was Merab. The name of the younger daughter was Michal.

**50** The name of the wife of Saul was Ahinoam, the daughter of Ahimaaz. The name of the commander of his army was Abner. Abner was the son of Ner, the uncle of Saul.

**51** Kish was the father of Saul. Ner, the father of Abner, was the son of Abiel.

**52** There was much war against the Philistines for all the life of Saul. When Saul saw a strong or brave man, he put that man into his army.

**1** Samuel said to Saul, "The LORD sent me to pour oil on you (anoint you). He made you king over his people Israel. Now listen to the message from the LORD."

**2** "The LORD of armies says this: 'I will punish the Amalekites for what they did to Israel. They attacked Israel on the road when Israel came up from Egypt.'"

**3** "'Now go and attack the Amalekites. Destroy all that they have. Do not let any of them live. Kill men and women, children and babies, cattle and sheep, camels and donkeys.'"

**5** Saul went to the city of Amalek. He hid his army in the valley.

**6** Saul said to the Kenites, "Go away from the Amalekites. If you do not, I will destroy you with them. You were kind to all the Israelites when they came up from Egypt." So the Kenites went away from the Amalekites.

**10** Then the LORD spoke to Samuel. He said,

**11** "I am sorry that I made Saul king. He turned away from me and did not obey my commands." Samuel was angry. He cried to the LORD all night.

**14** Samuel said, "Then why do I hear the noise of sheep and cattle?"

**15** Saul said, "The soldiers brought them from the Amalekites. They kept the best sheep and cattle to make sacrifices to the LORD your God. But we destroyed all the other animals."

**17** Samuel said, "Before, you thought that you were not important. But you became the leader of the tribes of Israel. The LORD chose you (anointed you) to be king over Israel."

**18** "The LORD sent you on a mission. He said, 'Go and destroy the Amalekites, those sinners. Fight against them until you kill all of them.'"

**19** "Why did you not obey the LORD? Why did you run to take the goods? You did evil before the LORD."

**20** Saul said to Samuel, "But I did obey the LORD. I went on the mission that the LORD gave me. I brought back Agag, the king of the Amalekites. And I destroyed the Amalekites."

**21** "But the soldiers took sheep and cattle from the goods. These were the best of the things that we must destroy. The soldiers took them to make sacrifices to the LORD your God at Gilgal."

**24** Then Saul said to Samuel, "I sinned. I did not obey the command of the LORD and your words. I was afraid of the people, so I did what they wanted."

**25** "Now please forgive my sin. Come back with me, so that I can worship the LORD."

**26** Samuel said to Saul, "I will not go back with you. You refused the word of the LORD. So the LORD refuses you as king over Israel."

**27** Samuel turned to go away. Saul held the edge of the robe of Samuel, and the robe tore.

**28** Samuel said to him, "Today the LORD tore the kingdom of Israel from you. He gave it to another man who is better than you."

**29** "God, the Glory of Israel, does not lie, and he does not change his mind. He is not a man. Thus he does not change his mind."

**30** Saul said, "I sinned. But please honor me now before the elders of my people and before Israel. Come back with me, so that I can worship the LORD your God."

**31** So Samuel went back with Saul, and Saul worshipped the LORD.

**32** Then Samuel said, "Bring Agag, the king of the Amalekites, to me." Agag came to him with confidence. Agag thought, "Now the danger of death is past."

**33** Samuel said, "Your sword caused women to lose their children. Now your mother will lose her child." Then Samuel cut Agag into pieces before the LORD at Gilgal.

**34** Then Samuel went to Ramah. Saul went up to his home in Gibeah.

**35** Samuel did not see Saul again until the day of his death. But Samuel was sad for Saul. The LORD was sorry that he made Saul king over Israel.

## Chapter 16

**1** The LORD said to Samuel, "How long will you be sad for Saul? I refused him as king over Israel. Fill your horn with oil and go. I will send you to Jesse in Bethlehem. I chose one of his sons to be king."

**2** Samuel said, "How can I go? If Saul hears about it, he will kill me." The LORD said, "Take a young cow with you. Say, 'I came to make a sacrifice to the LORD.'"

**3** "Invite Jesse to the sacrifice. Then I will show you what to do. Pour oil on (anoint) the man that I show you."

**4** Samuel did what the LORD said. He went to Bethlehem. The elders of the town came to meet him, and they shook with fear. They said, "Do you come in peace?"

**5** Samuel said, "Yes, I come in peace. I came to make a sacrifice to the LORD. Make yourselves holy, and come with me to the sacrifice." Then Samuel made Jesse and his sons holy. He invited them to the sacrifice.

**6** When they came, Samuel saw Eliab. Samuel thought, "This man in front of the LORD is surely the man that the LORD chose."

**7** But the LORD said to Samuel, "Do not look at his face or at how tall he is. I refused him. The LORD does not see as people see. People look at the outside of a person. But the LORD looks at the heart."

**8** Then Jesse called Abinadab and caused him to walk in front of Samuel. But Samuel said, "The LORD did not choose this man either."

**9** Then Jesse caused Shammah to walk in front of Samuel. But Samuel said, "The LORD did not choose this man either."

**10** Jesse caused 7 of his sons to walk in front of Samuel. But Samuel said to Jesse, "The LORD did not choose any of these men."

**11** Samuel said to Jesse, "Are these all your sons?" Jesse said, "There is still the youngest son. He takes care of the sheep." Samuel said to Jesse, "Send for him. We will not sit down to eat until he comes here."

**12** So Jesse sent for him and brought him in. He had a healthy red color, beautiful eyes, and a handsome face. The LORD said, "Get up and pour oil on him. He is the man."

**13** So Samuel took the horn of oil and poured it on him in front of his brothers. From that day on, the Spirit of the LORD came on David with power. Then Samuel got up and went to Ramah.

**14** The Spirit of the LORD went away from Saul. An evil spirit from the LORD caused him much trouble.

**15** The servants of Saul said to him, "Look, an evil spirit from God causes you trouble."

**16** "Our lord, command your servants to look for a man who can play the harp well. When the evil spirit from God comes on you, the man will play the harp. Then you will feel better."

**17** Saul said to his servants, "Find a man who plays well, and bring him to me."

**18** One of the young men said, "I saw a son of Jesse from Bethlehem. He plays the harp well. He is a brave man and a good soldier. He speaks wisely, and he is handsome. The LORD is with him."

**19** So Saul sent messengers to Jesse. He said, "Send me your son David, who is with the sheep."

**20** Jesse took a donkey with a load of bread, a container of wine, and a young goat. He sent them with his son David to Saul.

**21** David came to Saul and served him. Saul loved David very much. David became the man who carried his weapons.

**22** Saul sent a message to Jesse. He said, "Please let David stay and serve me. I am happy with him."

**23** When the evil spirit from God came on Saul, David took the harp and played it. Then Saul felt better, and the evil spirit went away from him.

## Chapter 17

**1** The Philistines brought their armies together for battle. They came together at Socoh in Judah. They made their camp between Socoh and Azekah, at Ephes-dammim.

**2** Saul and the men of Israel came together. They made their camp in the valley of Elah. They put their army in lines to fight against the Philistines.

**3** The Philistines stood on a hill on one side. The Israelites stood on a hill on the other side. There was a valley between them.

**4** A champion came out of the Philistine camp. His name was Goliath, and he was from Gath. He was 6 cubits and 1 span tall (about 3 meters).

**5** He had a bronze helmet on his head. He wore bronze armor that weighed 5,000 shekels (about 57 kilograms).

**6** He had bronze armor on his legs. He carried a bronze spear on his back.

**7** The shaft of his spear was as large as a weaver's beam. The iron point of his spear weighed 600 shekels (about 7 kilograms). A man who carried his shield walked in front of him.

**8** Goliath stood and shouted to the army of Israel. He said, "Why do you come out and make lines for battle? I am a Philistine, and you are servants of Saul. Choose a man and send him down to me."

**9** "If he can fight me and kill me, then we will be your servants. But if I defeat him and kill him, then you will be our servants and serve us."

**10** The Philistine said, "Today I insult the army of Israel. Give me a man, and we will fight each other."

**11** Saul and all the Israelites heard these words of the Philistine. They were very afraid.

**12** David was the son of Jesse, an Ephrathite from Bethlehem in Judah. Jesse had 8 sons. In the time of Saul, Jesse was very old.

**13** The 3 oldest sons of Jesse followed Saul to the battle. The name of the first son was Eliab. The second son was Abinadab, and the third son was Shammah.

**14** David was the youngest son. The 3 oldest sons followed Saul.

**15** But David went back and forth from Saul to Bethlehem. He took care of the sheep of his father.

**16** For 40 days, the Philistine came forward each morning and each evening. He stood there in front of the Israelites.

**17** Jesse said to his son David, "Take this ephah of roasted grain and these 10 loaves of bread for your brothers. Run to their camp quickly."

**18** "Also take these 10 pieces of cheese to the commander of their unit. See how your brothers are. Bring back something from them to show me that they are well."

**19** Saul, the brothers of David, and all the men of Israel were in the valley of Elah. They fought against the Philistines.

**20** David got up early in the morning. He gave the sheep to another man to guard. He took the food and went, as Jesse told him. He came to the camp when the army went out to the battle lines. The soldiers shouted the battle cry.

**21** The Israelites and the Philistines made their battle lines, army against army.

**22** David gave his baggage to the man who guarded the baggage. He ran to the battle lines and greeted his brothers.

**23** While he talked with them, the champion came out of the Philistine army. He was Goliath, the Philistine from Gath. Goliath said the same words again, and David heard them.

**24** All the men of Israel saw Goliath. They ran away from him, because they were very afraid.

**25** The men of Israel said, "Did you see this man who comes out? He comes out to insult Israel. The king will give much money to the man who kills him. The king will also give his daughter to that man. And the family of that man will not pay taxes in Israel."

**26** David spoke to the men who stood near him. He said, "What will the king do for the man who kills this Philistine and removes this shame from Israel? This Philistine does not have circumcision. He must not insult the army of the God who lives!"

**27** The people told David the same thing again. They said, "The king will do this for the man who kills him."

**28** Eliab, the oldest brother of David, heard David talk with the men. Eliab became angry with David. He said, "Why did you come down here? Who takes care of those few sheep in the desert? I know your pride and the evil in your heart. You came down only to watch the battle."

**29** David said, "What did I do now? I only asked a question."

**30** Then David turned away from Eliab to another man. He asked the same question. The people gave him the same answer as before.

**31** Some men heard what David said. They told Saul, and Saul sent for David.

**32** David said to Saul, "No person must be afraid because of this Philistine. I, your servant, will go and fight him."

**33** Saul said to David, "You cannot go and fight this Philistine. You are only a boy. He was a soldier since he was a boy."

**34** But David said to Saul, "I, your servant, took care of the sheep of my father. Sometimes a lion or a bear came and took a lamb from the flock."

**35** "Then I went after it and hit it. I took the lamb from its mouth. When the animal attacked me, I held it by its jaw. I hit it and killed it."

**36** "I, your servant, killed both lions and bears. This Philistine does not have circumcision. He will be like one of those animals, because he insulted the army of the God who lives."

**37** David also said, "The LORD saved me from the claws of the lion and the bear. He will save me from this Philistine." Saul said to David, "Go, and I pray that the LORD will be with you."

**38** Then Saul put his own armor on David. He put a bronze helmet on the head of David. He also put a coat of armor on him.

**39** David put the sword of Saul over the armor. He tried to walk, but he was not familiar with the armor. David said to Saul, "I cannot go with these things. I am not familiar with them." So David took them off.

**40** David took his stick in his hand. He chose 5 smooth stones from the stream. He put them in his shepherd's bag. He held his sling in his hand. Then he went near to the Philistine.

**41** The Philistine came closer to David. The man who carried his shield walked in front of him.

**42** The Philistine looked at David and saw that he was only a boy. David had a healthy red color and a handsome face. So the Philistine did not respect him.

**43** The Philistine said to David, "Do you think that I am a dog? Why do you come to me with sticks?" The Philistine cursed David by the names of his gods.

**44** The Philistine said to David, "Come to me. I will give your body to the birds and the wild animals."

**45** David said to the Philistine, "You come to me with a sword, a spear, and a javelin. But I come to you in the name of the LORD of armies. He is the God of the army of Israel. You insulted him."

**46** "Today the LORD will give you to me. I will kill you and cut off your head. Today I will give the bodies of the Philistine soldiers to the birds and the wild animals. Then all the world will know that there is a God in Israel."

**47** "All the people here will know that the LORD does not save with sword and spear. The battle belongs to the LORD. He will give all of you to us."

**48** The Philistine moved forward to attack David. David ran quickly toward the battle line to meet him.

**49** David put his hand into his bag and took out a stone. He threw the stone with his sling. The stone hit the Philistine in the forehead and went into it. The Philistine fell on his face to the ground.

**50** Thus David defeated the Philistine with a sling and a stone. He hit the Philistine and killed him. David did not have a sword in his hand.

**51** David ran and stood over the Philistine. He took the sword of the Philistine out of its cover. He killed him and cut off his head with the sword. The Philistines saw that their champion was dead. So they ran away.

**52** The men of Israel and Judah shouted and chased the Philistines. They chased them to the valley and to the gates of Ekron. The dead Philistines were on the road from Shaaraim to Gath and to Ekron.

**53** The Israelites stopped their chase of the Philistines. Then they came back and took the goods from the Philistine camp.

**54** David took the head of the Philistine and brought it to Jerusalem. He put the weapons of the Philistine in his own tent.

**55** Saul watched David go out against the Philistine. Saul said to Abner, the commander of the army, "Abner, whose son is this young man?" Abner said, "My king, I make a solemn promise. I do not know."

**56** The king said, "Find out whose son this young man is."

**57** David came back after he killed the Philistine. Abner took him and brought him to Saul. David held the head of the Philistine in his hand.

**58** Saul said to him, "Young man, whose son are you?" David said, "I am the son of your servant Jesse from Bethlehem."

## Chapter 18

**1** David stopped his talk with Saul. Then Jonathan felt a strong bond with David. Jonathan loved David as he loved himself.

**2** From that day Saul kept David with him. Saul did not let David go back home to the house of his father.

**3** Jonathan made an agreement (covenant) with David, because he loved David as he loved himself.

**4** Jonathan took off the robe that he wore and gave it to David. He also gave David his clothes, his sword, his bow, and his belt.

**5** David went wherever Saul sent him, and he was successful. So Saul made him a commander over the soldiers. This pleased all the people and also the officials of Saul.

**6** The army came home after David killed the Philistine. Then women came out from all the cities of Israel to meet King Saul. They sang and danced with joy. They played tambourines and other musical instruments.

**7** As the women played, they sang to each other. They sang, "Saul killed thousands of his enemies. But David killed tens of thousands."

**8** Saul became very angry. He did not like this song. He said, "They give David credit for tens of thousands. But they give me credit for only thousands. Now the only thing that he does not have is the kingdom!"

**9** From that day on, Saul watched David with suspicion.

**10** On the next day, an evil spirit from God came on Saul with power. Saul spoke wildly in his house. David played the harp, as he usually did. Saul held a spear in his hand.

**11** Saul threw the spear. He thought, "I will pin David to the wall." But David moved away from him 2 times.

**12** Saul was afraid of David, because the LORD was with David. The LORD left Saul.

**13** So Saul sent David away from him. He made David the commander of 1,000 soldiers. David led the soldiers out to battle and back again.

**14** David was successful in all that he did, because the LORD was with him.

**15** Saul saw that David was very successful. So Saul was afraid of him.

**16** But all the people of Israel and Judah loved David, because he led them in their battles.

**17** Saul said to David, "Here is my older daughter Merab. I will give her to you as your wife. But you must serve me as a brave soldier. You must fight the battles of the LORD." Saul thought, "I will not kill him. The Philistines will kill him."

**18** But David said to Saul, "Who am I? My family in Israel is not important. I must not become the son-in-law of the king."

**19** But when the time came to give Merab to David, Saul gave her to Adriel from Meholah as his wife.

**20** Michal, the other daughter of Saul, loved David. People told Saul about this, and he was happy.

**21** Saul thought, "I will give her to David. She will be a trap for him. Then the Philistines will kill him." So Saul said to David a second time, "Today you can become my son-in-law."

**22** Saul gave a command to his servants. He said, "Speak to David secretly. Say to him, 'Look, the king is happy with you. All his servants love you. Now become the son-in-law of the king.'"

**23** The servants of Saul told these words to David. But David said, "Do you think that it is easy to become the son-in-law of the king? I am poor, and I am not important."

**24** The servants of Saul told him what David said.

**25** Saul said, "Tell David this: 'The king does not want money for the bride. He wants only 100 foreskins of Philistines. Thus he will take revenge on his enemies.'" Saul thought, "The Philistines will kill David."

**26** The servants of Saul told these words to David. David was happy to become the son-in-law of the king. Before the time limit ended,

**27** David and his men went out and killed 200 Philistines. David brought their foreskins and gave all of them to the king. He did this to become the son-in-law of the king. So Saul gave his daughter Michal to David as his wife.

**28** Saul saw and knew that the LORD was with David. Saul also knew that his daughter Michal loved David.

**29** So Saul became even more afraid of David. Saul was the enemy of David for all the rest of his life.

**30** The commanders of the Philistines continued to come out to fight. Each time, David was more successful than all the other officers of Saul. Thus the name of David became very famous.

## Chapter 19

**1** Saul told his son Jonathan and all his officials to kill David.

**2** But Jonathan, the son of Saul, liked David very much. Jonathan told David, "My father Saul wants to kill you. Please be careful tomorrow morning. Go to a secret place and hide there."

**3** "I will go out and stand near my father in the field where you hide. I will speak to my father about you. I will tell you what I learn."

**4** Jonathan spoke good things about David to his father Saul. He said to Saul, "The king must not sin against his servant David. David did not sin against you. All that David did helped you very much."

**5** "He put his life in danger when he killed the Philistine. The LORD gave a great victory to all Israel. You saw it, and you were glad. Why do you want to sin against an innocent man? Why do you want to kill David for no reason?"

**6** Saul listened to Jonathan. Saul made a solemn promise. He said, "I promise by the LORD, who lives. I will not kill David."

**7** Then Jonathan called David and told him all these things. Jonathan brought David to Saul. Then David served Saul as he did before.

**8** War started again. David went out and fought the Philistines. He defeated them very badly, and they ran away from him.

**9** But an evil spirit from the LORD came on Saul. Saul sat in his house with his spear in his hand. David played the harp.

**10** Saul tried to pin David to the wall with the spear. But David moved away, and the spear went into the wall. David ran away and escaped that night.

**11** Saul sent messengers to the house of David to watch him. They were to kill him in the morning. But Michal, the wife of David, warned him. She said, "If you do not escape tonight, then tomorrow you will be dead."

**12** So Michal helped David go down through a window. He ran away and escaped.

**13** Then Michal took a household idol and put it in the bed. She put goat hair at the head of it. She covered it with a cloth.

**14** Saul sent messengers to take David. But Michal said, "He is sick."

**15** Then Saul sent the messengers back to see David. Saul said, "Bring him to me in his bed. Then I will kill him."

**16** The messengers went in. They found the household idol in the bed, with the goat hair at its head.

**17** Saul said to Michal, "Why did you trick me like this? Why did you let my enemy escape?" Michal said to Saul, "David said to me, 'Let me go. If you do not, I will kill you.'"

**18** David ran away and escaped. He went to Samuel at Ramah. He told Samuel all that Saul did to him. Then David and Samuel went and stayed at Naioth.

**19** Someone told Saul, "David is at Naioth in Ramah."

**20** So Saul sent messengers to take David. The messengers saw a group of prophets who prophesied. Samuel stood there as their leader. Then the Spirit of God came on the messengers of Saul, and they also prophesied.

**21** People told Saul about this. So he sent other messengers, and they also prophesied. Saul sent messengers a third time, and they also prophesied.

**22** Then Saul went to Ramah himself. He came to the large well at Secu. He asked, "Where are Samuel and David?" Someone said, "They are at Naioth in Ramah."

**23** So Saul went to Naioth in Ramah. The Spirit of God came on him too. He continued to prophesy as he walked until he came to Naioth in Ramah.

**24** He took off his clothes and prophesied before Samuel. He lay on the ground with no clothes for all that day and all that night. That is why people say, "Is Saul also one of the prophets?"

## Chapter 20

**1** David ran away from Naioth in Ramah. He came to Jonathan and said, "What did I do? What is my crime? How did I sin against your father? Why does he want to kill me?"

**2** Jonathan said to him, "No! You will not die. My father does nothing, large or small, if he does not tell me first. Why will my father hide this from me? It is not true."

**3** But David made a solemn promise. He said, "Your father knows that you like me. So he thinks, 'Jonathan must not know about this, or he will be sad.' But I make a promise by the LORD, who lives, and by your life. I am only 1 step away from death."

**4** Jonathan said to David, "I will do anything that you want."

**5** David said to Jonathan, "Look, tomorrow is the festival of the New Moon. I must eat with the king. But let me go and hide in the field until the evening of the third day."

**6** "If your father sees that I am not there, then tell him this: 'David asked me to let him go quickly to Bethlehem, his city. His family makes a yearly sacrifice there.'"

**7** "If your father says, 'Good,' then I, your servant, am safe. But if he becomes very angry, then you will know that he decided to hurt me."

**8** "So be kind to me, your servant. You made an agreement (covenant) with me before the LORD. But if I did wrong, then kill me yourself. Why take me to your father?"

**9** Jonathan said, "Never! If I know that my father decided to hurt you, I will tell you."

**10** David said to Jonathan, "Who will tell me if your father gives you an angry answer?"

**11** Jonathan said to David, "Come, we will go out into the field." So the 2 men went out into the field.

**12** Jonathan said to David, "I promise by the LORD, the God of Israel. At this time tomorrow or on the third day, I will learn what my father thinks. If he is kind toward you, I will send a message to tell you."

**13** "But if my father wants to hurt you, I will tell you. I will send you away, so that you can go safely. If I do not, then I pray that the LORD will punish me very much. I pray that the LORD will be with you as he was with my father."

**14** "While I live, show me the faithful love of the LORD, so that I do not die."

**15** "Do not ever stop your kindness to my family. Do not stop it even when the LORD destroys all the enemies of David from the earth."

**16** So Jonathan made an agreement with the family of David. He said, "I pray that the LORD will punish the enemies of David."

**17** Jonathan caused David to make his promise again, because Jonathan loved David. He loved David as he loved himself.

**18** Then Jonathan said to David, "Tomorrow is the festival of the New Moon. People will see that you are not there, because your seat will be empty."

**19** "On the day after tomorrow, go quickly to the place where you hid before, when this trouble started. Wait near the stone Ezel."

**20** "I will shoot 3 arrows to the side of the stone, as if I shoot at a target."

**21** "Then I will send a boy and tell him, 'Go and find the arrows.' If I say to the boy, 'Look, the arrows are on this side of you. Bring them here,' then come out. I promise by the LORD, who lives, that you will be safe. There will be no danger."

**22** "But if I say to the boy, 'Look, the arrows are farther on, past you,' then go away. The LORD sends you away."

**23** "And the LORD will always be a witness of the promises that you and I made to each other."

**24** So David hid in the field. The festival of the New Moon came, and the king sat down to eat.

**25** The king sat in his usual seat by the wall. Jonathan sat opposite him, and Abner sat next to Saul. But the seat of David was empty.

**26** Saul said nothing on that day. He thought, "Something occurred to David. He is not ceremonially clean. Surely he is not clean."

**27** On the next day, the second day of the month, the seat of David was empty again. Saul said to his son Jonathan, "Why did the son of Jesse not come to eat yesterday or today?"

**28** Jonathan said to Saul, "David asked me to let him go to Bethlehem."

**29** "He said, 'Please let me go. My family makes a sacrifice in the city, and my brother told me to come. If you are my friend, let me go and see my brothers.' That is why he did not come to the table of the king."

**30** Then Saul became very angry with Jonathan. He said to him, "You are the son of an evil woman who does not obey! I know that you chose the son of Jesse. This brings shame on you and on your mother."

**31** "While the son of Jesse lives on the earth, you and your kingdom will not be safe. Now send for him and bring him to me. He must die!"

**32** Jonathan said to his father Saul, "Why must David die? What did he do?"

**33** But Saul threw his spear at Jonathan to kill him. Then Jonathan knew that his father decided to kill David.

**34** Jonathan got up from the table very angry. He did not eat on that second day of the month. He was sad for David, because his father brought shame on David.

**35** In the morning Jonathan went out into the field to meet David, as they agreed. A small boy went with Jonathan.

**36** Jonathan said to the boy, "Run and find the arrows that I shoot." The boy ran, and Jonathan shot an arrow past him.

**37** The boy came to the place where the arrow of Jonathan fell. Jonathan shouted to the boy, "The arrow is farther on, past you!"

**38** Then Jonathan shouted to the boy, "Go quickly! Do not stop!" The boy picked up the arrows and came back to his master.

**39** The boy did not know anything about this. Only Jonathan and David knew.

**40** Then Jonathan gave his weapons to the boy. He said to him, "Go and carry them to the city."

**41** The boy went away. Then David came out from his place on the south side of the stone. He bowed down 3 times with his face to the ground. David and Jonathan kissed each other and cried together. But David cried more.

**42** Jonathan said to David, "Go in peace. We both made a promise in the name of the LORD. We said, 'The LORD is a witness between you and me. He is a witness between my descendants and your descendants for all time.'" Then David went away, and Jonathan went back to the city.

## Chapter 21

**1** David went to Nob, to Ahimelech the priest. Ahimelech shook with fear when he met David. He said, "Why are you alone? Why is no person with you?"

**2** David said to Ahimelech the priest, "The king gave me a mission. He said to me, 'No person must know about the mission that I give you.' I told my men to meet me at a certain place."

**3** "Now, what food do you have? Give me 5 loaves of bread, or anything that you have."

**4** The priest said to David, "I do not have any common bread. But I have holy bread. Your men can eat it if they did not have sexual relations with women recently."

**5** David said to the priest, "We did not touch women for 3 days. That is our custom when I go on a mission. The bodies of the men are holy even on common missions. Thus today they are surely holy."

**6** So the priest gave David the holy bread. There was no other bread there. There was only the Bread of the Presence. The priests removed this bread from before the LORD and put hot bread in its place on the same day.

**7** One of the officials of Saul was there on that day. He had to stay before the LORD. His name was Doeg the Edomite. He was the chief of the shepherds of Saul.

**8** David said to Ahimelech, "Do you have a spear or a sword here? I did not bring my sword or my other weapons. The mission of the king was urgent."

**9** The priest said, "The sword of Goliath the Philistine is here. You killed him in the valley of Elah. The sword is in a cloth behind the ephod. If you want it, take it. There is no other sword here." David said, "There is no sword as good as that sword. Give it to me."

**10** On that day David ran away from Saul. He went to Achish, the king of Gath.

**11** The officials of Achish said to him, "Is this not David, the king of the land? The people danced and sang about him. They sang, 'Saul killed thousands of his enemies. But David killed tens of thousands.'"

**12** David thought about these words. He was very afraid of Achish, the king of Gath.

**13** So David acted as if he was mad in front of them. He made marks on the doors of the gate. He let saliva go down his beard.

**14** Achish said to his officials, "Look, the man is mad! Why did you bring him to me?"

**15** "I have enough mad men! Why did you bring this man to act mad in front of me? This man must not come into my house!"

## Chapter 22

**1** David went away from Gath and escaped to the cave of Adullam. His brothers and all the family of his father heard about it. They went down to him there.

**2** All the people who were in trouble or in debt or who were unhappy came to David. He became their leader. There were about 400 men with him.

**3** From there David went to Mizpah in Moab. He said to the king of Moab, "Please let my father and mother come and stay with you. Let them stay until I know what God will do for me."

**4** So David left his parents with the king of Moab. They stayed with him all the time that David was in the stronghold.

**5** But the prophet Gad said to David, "Do not stay in the stronghold. Go to the land of Judah." So David went away and went to the forest of Hereth.

**6** Saul heard that people found David and his men. At that time Saul sat under a tree on the hill at Gibeah. He held his spear in his hand. All his officials stood around him.

**7** Saul said to his officials who stood around him, "Listen, men of Benjamin! Will the son of Jesse give fields and vineyards to all of you? Will he make all of you commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds?"

**8** "All of you plan against me! No person told me when my son made an agreement with the son of Jesse. None of you is sorry for me. None of you told me that my son caused my servant to rebel against me. Now David hides and waits to attack me."

**9** Doeg the Edomite stood with the officials of Saul. He said, "I saw the son of Jesse come to Nob. He came to Ahimelech, the son of Ahitub."

**10** "Ahimelech asked the LORD for him. He gave David food and the sword of Goliath the Philistine."

**11** Then the king sent for Ahimelech the priest, the son of Ahitub. He also sent for all the family of Ahimelech, the priests in Nob. All of them came to the king.

**12** Saul said, "Listen now, son of Ahitub." Ahimelech said, "Yes, my lord."

**13** Saul said to him, "Why did you and the son of Jesse plan against me? You gave him bread and a sword. You asked God for him. Now he rebels against me. He hides and waits to attack me."

**14** Ahimelech said to the king, "None of your officials is as faithful as David. He is the son-in-law of the king. He is the captain of your guards. All the people in your house respect him."

**15** "Was that the first time that I asked God for him? No! The king must not blame me, his servant, or my family. I, your servant, knew nothing about this matter."

**16** But the king said, "Ahimelech, you will surely die. You and all your family will die."

**17** Then the king said to the guards who stood around him, "Turn and kill the priests of the LORD. They also help David. They knew that he ran away, but they did not tell me." But the officials of the king did not want to attack the priests of the LORD.

**18** Then the king said to Doeg, "You turn and attack the priests." So Doeg the Edomite turned and attacked the priests. On that day he killed 85 men who wore the linen ephod.

**19** He also attacked Nob, the city of the priests, with the sword. He killed men and women, children and babies, cattle, donkeys, and sheep.

**20** But one son of Ahimelech, the son of Ahitub, escaped. His name was Abiathar. He ran away and went to David.

**21** Abiathar told David that Saul killed the priests of the LORD.

**22** David said to Abiathar, "On that day I saw that Doeg the Edomite was there. I knew this: he will surely tell Saul. I am responsible for the death of all your family."

**23** "Stay with me. Do not be afraid. The man who wants to kill me also wants to kill you. You will be safe with me."

## Chapter 23

**1** Some people told David, "Look, the Philistines attack Keilah. They steal the grain from the floors where workers beat grain."

**2** So David asked the LORD, "Must I go and attack these Philistines?" The LORD said to David, "Go and attack the Philistines. Save Keilah."

**3** But the men of David said to him, "Look, we are afraid here in Judah. We will be much more afraid if we go to Keilah against the Philistine army."

**4** So David asked the LORD again. The LORD answered him. He said, "Go down to Keilah. I will give the Philistines to you."

**5** So David and his men went to Keilah and fought the Philistines. They took the animals of the Philistines and killed many Philistines. Thus David saved the people of Keilah.

**6** Abiathar, the son of Ahimelech, ran away to David at Keilah. Abiathar brought an ephod with him.

**7** Someone told Saul that David came to Keilah. Saul said, "God gave David to me. David went into a town with gates and bars. Now he cannot get out."

**8** So Saul called all his army to battle. He wanted to go down to Keilah and put his army around David and his men.

**9** David learned that Saul made a plan to hurt him. So he said to Abiathar the priest, "Bring the ephod here."

**10** Then David said, "LORD, God of Israel, I, your servant, heard that Saul wants to come to Keilah. He wants to destroy the city because of me."

**11** "Will the men of Keilah give me to Saul? Will Saul come down, as I heard? LORD, God of Israel, please tell your servant." The LORD said, "Saul will come down."

**12** Then David said, "Will the men of Keilah give me and my men to Saul?" The LORD said, "They will give you to him."

**13** So David and his men went away from Keilah. There were about 600 men. They went from place to place. Someone told Saul that David escaped from Keilah. So Saul did not go there.

**14** David stayed in strongholds in the desert. He stayed in the hill country of the desert of Ziph. Each day Saul looked for David. But God did not give David to Saul.

**15** David was at Horesh in the desert of Ziph. He learned that Saul came out to kill him.

**16** Jonathan, the son of Saul, went to David at Horesh. Jonathan helped David to trust God and to be strong.

**17** Jonathan said to David, "Do not be afraid. My father Saul will not find you. You will be king over Israel, and I will be second after you. My father Saul also knows this."

**18** The 2 men made an agreement (covenant) before the LORD. Then Jonathan went home, but David stayed at Horesh.

**19** Some people from Ziph went up to Saul at Gibeah. They said, "David hides with us in the strongholds at Horesh. He is on the hill of Hachilah, south of Jeshimon."

**20** "Now, king, come down when you want. We will give David to you."

**21** Saul said, "I pray that the LORD will bless you, because you feel pity for me."

**22** "Go and learn more. Find out where he stays. Find out who saw him there. People tell me that he is very clever."

**23** "Find out all the places where he hides. Then come back to me with correct information. Then I will go with you. If he is in that area, I will look for him among all the families of Judah."

**24** So the men of Ziph went back to Ziph before Saul. David and his men were in the desert of Maon, in the Arabah, south of Jeshimon.

**25** Saul and his men went to look for David. But people told David about this. So David went down to the rock and stayed in the desert of Maon. Saul heard about this, and he chased David into the desert of Maon.

**26** Saul went on one side of the mountain. David and his men went on the other side. David hurried to escape from Saul. Saul and his men started to go around David and his men to catch them.

**27** But a messenger came to Saul. The messenger said, "Come quickly! The Philistines attack the land."

**28** So Saul stopped his chase of David and went to fight the Philistines. Thus people call that place Sela-hammahlekoth, which means "the Rock of Escape."

**29** David went up from there and stayed in the strongholds at En-gedi.

## Chapter 24

**1** Saul came back after he chased the Philistines. Then people told him, "David is in the desert of En-gedi."

**2** So Saul took 3,000 of the best men from all Israel. He went to look for David and his men near the Rocks of the Wild Goats.

**3** Saul came to the sheep pens near the road. A cave was there. Saul went into the cave to go to the toilet. David and his men were far back in the cave.

**4** The men of David said to him, "Today is the day that the LORD told you about. He said, 'I will give your enemy to you. Do to him what you think is good.'" Then David went quietly and cut off a corner of the robe of Saul.

**5** After that, David felt guilty, because he cut off the corner of the robe of Saul.

**6** He said to his men, "The LORD does not permit me to do this thing to my master. He is the king that the LORD chose (anointed). I must not attack him, because the LORD chose him."

**7** With these words David stopped his men. He did not let them attack Saul. Then Saul got up, went out of the cave, and continued on his way.

**8** After that, David also went out of the cave. He shouted after Saul, "My lord the king!" Saul looked behind him. David bowed down with his face to the ground.

**9** David said to Saul, "Why do you listen to people who say, 'David wants to hurt you'?"

**10** "Today you can see that the LORD gave you to me in the cave. Some men told me to kill you. But I did not hurt you. I said, 'I will not attack my lord. He is the king that the LORD chose.'"

**11** "My father, look at the corner of your robe in my hand. I cut off the corner of your robe, but I did not kill you. Thus you can know that I do not plan evil or rebellion. I did not sin against you. But you hunt me to kill me."

**12** "The LORD will judge between you and me. The LORD will punish you for the wrong that you do to me. But I will not attack you."

**13** "An old proverb says, 'Evil acts come from evil people.' But I will not attack you."

**14** "Who does the king of Israel chase? You chase a person who is like a dead dog or a flea."

**15** "The LORD will be the judge. He will judge between you and me. He will see and defend me. He will save me from you."

**16** David finished these words to Saul. Then Saul said, "Is that your voice, my son David?" Then Saul cried loudly.

**17** He said to David, "You are more righteous than I am. You were good to me, but I did evil to you."

**18** "Today you showed how good you are to me. The LORD gave me to you, but you did not kill me."

**19** "When a man finds his enemy, he does not let him go away safely. I pray that the LORD will reward you for what you did for me today."

**20** "Now I know that you will surely be king. The kingdom of Israel will become strong under your rule."

**21** "Now make a solemn promise to me by the LORD. Promise that you will not kill my descendants. Promise that you will not remove my name from the family of my father."

**22** So David made this promise to Saul. Then Saul went home. But David and his men went up to the stronghold.

## Chapter 25

**1** Samuel died. All the Israelites came together and cried for him. They buried him at his home in Ramah. Then David went down to the desert of Paran.

**2** A man lived in Maon. He had land at Carmel. He was very rich. He had 3,000 sheep and 1,000 goats. At that time, he cut the wool from his sheep in Carmel.

**3** The name of the man was Nabal. The name of his wife was Abigail. She was a wise and beautiful woman. But Nabal was rude and evil in his actions. He was from the family of Caleb.

**4** David was in the desert. He heard that Nabal cut the wool from his sheep.

**5** So David sent 10 young men. David said to them, "Go up to Carmel. Go to Nabal and greet him for me."

**6** "Say to him, 'Long life to you! Peace to you and to your family! Peace to all that you have!'"

**7** "'I hear that you cut the wool from your sheep now. Your shepherds were with us, and we did not hurt them. They did not lose anything all the time that they were in Carmel.'"

**8** "'Ask your young men, and they will tell you. So be kind to my young men. We come on a day of celebration. Please give anything that you can to your servants and to your son David.'"

**9** The young men of David came to Nabal. They told him all these words from David. Then they waited.

**10** Nabal answered the servants of David. He said, "Who is David? Who is the son of Jesse? Now there are many servants who run away from their masters."

**11** "Must I take my bread, my water, and the meat that I killed for my workers? Must I give them to men from an unknown place?"

**12** So the young men of David turned and went back. They told David all these words.

**13** David said to his men, "Each man must put on his sword." So each man put on his sword, and David also put on his sword. About 400 men went up with David. 200 men stayed with the baggage.

**14** But one of the young servants told Abigail, the wife of Nabal. He said, "David sent messengers from the desert to greet our master. But Nabal shouted insults at them."

**15** "But the men of David were very good to us. They did not hurt us. We did not lose anything all the time that we were with them in the fields."

**16** "They were like a wall around us, day and night. They guarded us all the time that we took care of the sheep near them."

**17** "Now think about what you must do. Disaster will come to our master and all his family. He is such a wicked man that no person can speak to him."

**18** Abigail moved quickly. She took 200 loaves of bread and 2 containers of wine. She took 5 sheep ready to cook and 5 measures of roasted grain. She took 100 cakes of raisins and 200 cakes of figs. She put all these things on donkeys.

**19** She said to her servants, "Go ahead of me. I will come after you." But she did not tell her husband Nabal.

**20** She rode on her donkey and came down a hidden path on the hill. At that time David and his men came down toward her. She met them.

**21** David said before this, "I guarded all that this man had in the desert. He did not lose anything. But I did this for no reason! He gives me evil for good."

**22** "I pray that God will punish me very much if I let 1 male of his family live until the morning."

**23** Abigail saw David. She quickly got down from her donkey. She bowed down in front of David with her face to the ground.

**24** She fell at his feet and said, "My lord, let the blame be on me only. Please let me, your servant, speak to you. Hear the words of your servant."

**25** "My lord, please do not pay attention to this wicked man Nabal. His name means 'fool,' and he is a fool. I, your servant, did not see the young men that my lord sent."

**26** "Now, my lord, the LORD stopped you. You did not kill people, and you did not take revenge with your own hand. I make a promise by the LORD, who lives, and by your life. Let your enemies and all who want to hurt you become like Nabal."

**27** "Now I, your servant, brought this gift to my lord. Please give it to the young men who follow you."

**28** "Please forgive the wrong of your servant. The LORD will surely give my lord a family of kings that will last. My lord fights the battles of the LORD. You did no evil in all your life."

**29** "Possibly a man will chase you and try to kill you. But the LORD your God will keep your life safe, as a person keeps something valuable in a bag. He will throw away the lives of your enemies, as a man throws a stone from a sling."

**30** "The LORD will do for my lord all the good things that he promised you. He will make you the leader of Israel."

**31** "Then you will not feel guilty or sad in your heart. You will not feel guilty because you killed people for no reason, or because you took revenge. When the LORD gives you success, then remember me, your servant."

**32** David said to Abigail, "Praise the LORD, the God of Israel. He sent you today to meet me."

**33** "God will bless you for your good advice. God will bless you, because today you stopped me. I did not kill people. I did not take revenge with my own hand."

**34** "The LORD, the God of Israel, stopped me. I did not hurt you. I promise by the LORD, who lives. You came quickly to meet me. If you did not come, no male of the family of Nabal will be alive in the morning."

**35** Then David took from her the gifts that she brought to him. He said to her, "Go home in peace. I listened to you, and I will do what you asked."

**36** Abigail went back to Nabal. He had a party in his house, like the party of a king. Nabal was very happy and very drunk. So she told him nothing until the morning.

**37** In the morning Nabal was not drunk. Then his wife told him these things. His heart failed, and he became like a stone.

**38** About 10 days later, the LORD struck Nabal, and he died.

**39** David heard that Nabal was dead. He said, "Praise the LORD! Nabal insulted me, but the LORD defended me. The LORD stopped me, his servant, from doing evil. The LORD punished Nabal for his evil." Then David sent a message to Abigail. He asked her to become his wife.

**40** The servants of David went to Abigail at Carmel. They said to her, "David sent us to you. He wants to take you as his wife."

**41** She got up and bowed down with her face to the ground. She said, "I, your servant, am ready to serve you. I am ready to wash the feet of the servants of my lord."

**42** Abigail got up quickly and rode on a donkey. 5 of her female servants went with her. She followed the messengers of David and became his wife.

**43** David also married Ahinoam from Jezreel. Both women became his wives.

**44** But Saul gave his daughter Michal, the wife of David, to Palti, the son of Laish. Palti was from Gallim.

## Chapter 26

**1** The people of Ziph came to Saul at Gibeah. They said, "David hides on the hill of Hachilah, opposite Jeshimon."

**2** So Saul went down to the desert of Ziph. He took 3,000 of the best men of Israel with him. He went to look for David in the desert of Ziph.

**3** Saul made his camp on the hill of Hachilah, near the road opposite Jeshimon. David stayed in the desert. He saw that Saul followed him into the desert.

**4** So David sent out spies. He learned that Saul surely came.

**5** Then David went to the place where Saul made his camp. He saw where Saul and Abner slept. Abner, the son of Ner, was the commander of the army of Saul. Saul slept in the middle of the camp, and the army slept around him.

**6** Then David spoke to Ahimelech the Hittite and to Abishai, the son of Zeruiah. Abishai was the brother of Joab. David said, "Who will go down with me into the camp of Saul?" Abishai said, "I will go with you."

**7** So David and Abishai went to the army at night. Saul lay asleep in the middle of the camp. His spear was in the ground near his head. Abner and the soldiers lay around him.

**8** Abishai said to David, "Today God gave your enemy to you. Now let me pin him to the ground with 1 hit of the spear. I will not need to hit him 2 times."

**9** But David said to Abishai, "Do not kill him! The LORD chose (anointed) him as king. Any person who attacks him will be guilty."

**10** David also said, "I promise by the LORD, who lives. The LORD himself will kill Saul. Or Saul will die at his natural time. Or he will go into battle and die."

**11** "The LORD does not permit me to attack the king that he chose. Now take the spear and the water jar that are near his head. Then we will go."

**12** So David took the spear and the water jar from near the head of Saul. Then they went away. No person saw them or knew about it. No person woke up. All of them slept, because the LORD caused them to sleep deeply.

**13** Then David went across to the other side. He stood on the top of a hill far away. There was a large distance between them.

**14** David shouted to the army and to Abner, the son of Ner. He said, "Abner, will you not answer me?" Abner said, "Who are you? Why do you shout to the king?"

**15** David said to Abner, "You are a brave man. There is no man like you in Israel. Why did you not guard your lord the king? A soldier came into the camp to kill the king, your lord."

**16** "What you did is not good. I promise by the LORD, who lives, that you and your men must die. You did not guard your master, the king that the LORD chose. Now look! Where is the spear of the king? Where is the water jar that was near his head?"

**17** Saul knew the voice of David. He said, "Is that your voice, my son David?" David said, "Yes, it is my voice, my lord the king."

**18** David also said, "Why does my lord chase his servant? What did I do? What evil did I do?"

**19** "Now, my lord the king, please listen to the words of your servant. If the LORD caused you to attack me, let him accept an offering. But if men caused you to do this, then I pray that the LORD will curse them. Today they forced me to leave the land of the LORD. They said, 'Go and serve other gods.'"

**20** "Do not let me die far from the presence of the LORD. The king of Israel came out to look for a flea. He is like a man who hunts a bird in the mountains."

**21** Then Saul said, "I sinned. Come back, my son David. I will not try to hurt you again, because today you thought my life was valuable. Look, I was a fool. I made a very bad mistake."

**22** David said, "Here is the spear of the king. Let one of your young men come across and get it."

**23** "The LORD rewards each person for his righteous acts and his faithfulness. Today the LORD gave you to me. But I did not attack the king that the LORD chose."

**24** "Today I thought that your life was valuable. In the same way, I pray that the LORD will think that my life is valuable. I pray that he will save me from all trouble."

**25** Then Saul said to David, "I pray that God will bless you, my son David. You will do great things, and you will surely be successful." Then David went on his way, and Saul went back home.

## Chapter 27

**1** But David thought, "One day Saul will kill me. The best thing for me is to escape quickly to the land of the Philistines. Then Saul will stop his search for me in Israel. Thus I will escape from him."

**2** So David and his 600 men went to Achish, the son of Maoch, the king of Gath.

**3** David and his men lived with Achish in Gath. Each man had his family with him. David had his 2 wives with him. They were Ahinoam from Jezreel and Abigail from Carmel, the widow of Nabal.

**4** Someone told Saul that David ran away to Gath. So Saul stopped his search for David.

**5** Then David said to Achish, "If you are my friend, give me a place in one of the towns in the country. Let me live there. I, your servant, must not live with you in the royal city."

**6** So on that day Achish gave him the town of Ziklag. Thus Ziklag still belongs to the kings of Judah today.

**7** David lived in the land of the Philistines for 1 year and 4 months.

**8** During that time David and his men went out and attacked the Geshurites, the Girzites, and the Amalekites. These people lived for a long time in the land from Shur to Egypt.

**9** When David attacked an area, he did not let any man or woman live. He took the sheep, cattle, donkeys, camels, and clothes. Then he came back to Achish.

**10** Achish said, "Where did you attack today?" David said, "I attacked the south of Judah." Or he said, "The south of the Jerahmeelites," or "The south of the Kenites."

**11** David did not let any man or woman live to come to Gath. He thought, "They can tell about us. They can say, 'David did this.'" David did this all the time that he lived in the land of the Philistines.

**12** Achish trusted David. Achish thought, "David caused his own people, the Israelites, to hate him very much. Thus he will be my servant for all time."

## Chapter 28

**1** In those days the Philistines brought their armies together to fight against Israel. Achish said to David, "You must know that you and your men will go out with me to the battle."

**2** David said to Achish, "Good. Then you will see what I, your servant, can do." Achish said to David, "Good. I will make you my bodyguard for all time."

**3** Samuel was dead. All Israel cried for him and buried him in Ramah, his own city. Saul removed from the land the people who spoke with the spirits of the dead.

**4** The Philistines came together and made their camp at Shunem. Saul brought all Israel together, and they made their camp at Gilboa.

**5** Saul saw the Philistine army. He was afraid, and his heart shook very much.

**6** Saul asked the LORD what to do. But the LORD did not answer him. He did not answer by dreams, by the Urim, or by prophets.

**7** Then Saul said to his servants, "Find me a woman who speaks with the spirits of the dead. I will go to her and ask her." His servants said to him, "There is a woman at Endor who speaks with the spirits of the dead."

**8** So Saul changed his appearance and put on other clothes. Saul and 2 men went to the woman at night. Saul said, "Please talk to a spirit for me. Bring up the man that I tell you."

**9** But the woman said to him, "You know what Saul did. He removed from the land the people who speak with the spirits of the dead. Why do you set a trap for me? Do you want me to die?"

**10** Saul made a solemn promise to her by the LORD. He said, "I promise by the LORD, who lives. No one will punish you for this."

**11** Then the woman said, "Whom must I bring up for you?" Saul said, "Bring up Samuel."

**12** The woman saw Samuel, and she screamed loudly. She said to Saul, "Why did you trick me? You are Saul!"

**13** The king said to her, "Do not be afraid. What do you see?" The woman said to Saul, "I see a spirit come up out of the ground."

**14** Saul said to her, "What does he look like?" She said, "An old man comes up. He wears a robe." Then Saul knew that it was Samuel. Saul bowed down with his face to the ground.

**15** Samuel said to Saul, "Why did you disturb me? Why did you bring me up?" Saul said, "I am in much trouble. The Philistines fight against me. God went away from me. He does not answer me now, by prophets or by dreams. So I called you to tell me what to do."

**16** Samuel said, "The LORD went away from you and became your enemy. So why do you ask me?"

**17** "The LORD did what he told me to say. The LORD tore the kingdom from you. He gave it to another man, to David."

**18** "You did not obey the LORD. You did not do what he said in his fierce anger against the Amalekites. Thus the LORD does this to you today."

**19** "The LORD will give you and Israel to the Philistines. Tomorrow you and your sons will be with me among the dead. The LORD will give the army of Israel to the Philistines."

**20** Immediately Saul fell flat on the ground. He was very afraid because of the words of Samuel. He had no strength, because he did not eat any food all that day and all that night.

**21** The woman came to Saul. She saw that he was very afraid. She said to him, "Look, I, your servant, obeyed you. I put my life in danger. I did what you told me to do."

**22** "Now please listen to me, your servant. Let me give you a little food. Eat it, so that you have strength for your journey."

**23** But Saul refused. He said, "I will not eat." But his servants and the woman urged him to eat. So he listened to them. He got up from the ground and sat on the bed.

**24** The woman had a fat calf in the house. She quickly killed it. She took flour and mixed it. Then she baked bread with no yeast.

**25** She put the food in front of Saul and his servants, and they ate. Then they got up and went away that night.

## Chapter 29

**1** The Philistines brought all their armies together at Aphek. The Israelites made their camp near the spring in Jezreel.

**2** The rulers of the Philistines marched with their units of hundreds and of thousands. David and his men marched at the back with Achish.

**3** The commanders of the Philistines said, "Why are these Hebrews here?" Achish said to the commanders of the Philistines, "This is David. He was an official of Saul, the king of Israel. He was with me for more than a year. Since the day that he came to me, I found nothing wrong with him."

**4** But the commanders of the Philistines were angry with Achish. They said to him, "Send this man back to the place that you gave him. He must not go down with us into the battle. In the battle he can become our enemy. He can make his master pleased with him again. He will do it with the heads of our men!"

**5** "This is David. The people sang about him as they danced. They sang, 'Saul killed thousands of his enemies. But David killed tens of thousands.'"

**6** So Achish called David. He said to him, "I promise by the LORD, who lives. You are honest. I am happy when you go into battle with me. I found nothing wrong with you since the day that you came to me. But the rulers do not trust you."

**7** "So go back now, and go in peace. Do not do anything that displeases the rulers of the Philistines."

**8** David said to Achish, "But what did I do? What wrong did you find in me, your servant, since the day that I came to you? Why can I not go and fight against the enemies of my lord the king?"

**9** Achish said to David, "I know that you are as good to me as an angel of God. But the commanders of the Philistines said, 'He must not go up with us into the battle.'"

**10** "So get up early in the morning, with the servants of your master who came with you. Go away when it is light."

**11** So David and his men got up early in the morning. They went back to the land of the Philistines. The Philistines went up to Jezreel.

## Chapter 30

**1** On the third day, David and his men came to Ziklag. The Amalekites attacked the Negev and Ziklag before they came. The Amalekites defeated Ziklag and burned it.

**2** They took as prisoners the women and all the other people in the town, young and old. They did not kill any person. They took the people with them and went on their way.

**3** David and his men came to the town. They saw that the enemy burned it. The enemy took their wives, sons, and daughters as prisoners.

**4** David and his men cried loudly until they had no more strength to cry.

**5** The enemy also took the 2 wives of David as prisoners. They were Ahinoam from Jezreel and Abigail, the widow of Nabal from Carmel.

**6** David was in much trouble. His men talked about how they will kill him with stones. Each man was very angry because of his sons and daughters. But David found strength in the LORD his God.

**7** David said to Abiathar the priest, the son of Ahimelech, "Bring me the ephod." Abiathar brought the ephod to David.

**8** David asked the LORD, "Must I chase these raiders? Will I catch them?" The LORD said, "Chase them. You will surely catch them, and you will surely save the prisoners."

**9** So David and his 600 men went. They came to the Besor Brook. Some of the men stayed there.

**10** 200 men were too tired to go across the Besor Brook. So they stayed behind. But David and 400 men continued the chase.

**11** David's men found an Egyptian in a field. They brought him to David. They gave him bread to eat and water to drink.

**12** They gave him a piece of a cake of figs and 2 cakes of raisins. He ate, and his strength came back. He did not eat any food or drink any water for 3 days and 3 nights.

**13** David said to him, "Who is your master? Where are you from?" He said, "I am a young Egyptian. I am the slave of an Amalekite. My master left me 3 days ago, because I became sick."

**14** "We attacked the Negev of the Cherethites, the land of Judah, and the Negev of Caleb. We also burned Ziklag."

**15** David said to him, "Can you take me down to these raiders?" The man said, "Make a solemn promise to me by God. Promise that you will not kill me. Promise that you will not give me back to my master. Then I will take you down to them."

**16** The Egyptian took David down to them. The Amalekites were everywhere on the ground. They ate, drank, and danced. They celebrated because of all the goods that they took from the land of the Philistines and from the land of Judah.

**17** David attacked them from dawn until the evening of the next day. None of them escaped, but 400 young men rode away on camels.

**18** David got back all that the Amalekites took. He also saved his 2 wives.

**19** They did not lose anything, small or large. They got back their sons, their daughters, and their goods. They got back all that the Amalekites took. David brought back all of it.

**20** David also took all the sheep and cattle of the Amalekites. His men drove these animals in front of the other animals. They said, "These are the goods of David."

**21** Then David came to the 200 men who were too tired to follow him. They stayed at the Besor Brook. They came out to meet David and the men with him. David came near to them and greeted them.

**22** But some evil and wicked men among the men of David said, "These men did not go with us. So we will not give them any of the goods that we got back. Each man can only take his wife and children and go away."

**23** But David said, "My brothers, you must not do this with the things that the LORD gave us. He kept us safe. He gave us the raiders who attacked us."

**24** "No person will agree with you about this matter. The man who stays with the baggage will get the same part as the man who goes into the battle. All will get equal parts."

**25** From that day on, David made this a rule and a law for Israel. It is still the law today.

**26** David came to Ziklag. He sent some of the goods to the elders of Judah, who were his friends. He said, "Here is a gift for you from the goods of the enemies of the LORD."

**27** He sent gifts to the elders in Bethel, in Ramoth of the Negev, and in Jattir.

**28** He sent gifts to the elders in Aroer, in Siphmoth, and in Eshtemoa.

**29** He sent gifts to the elders in Racal, in the towns of the Jerahmeelites, and in the towns of the Kenites.

**30** He sent gifts to the elders in Hormah, in Bor-ashan, and in Athach.

**31** He sent gifts to the elders in Hebron and in all the places where David and his men went.

## Chapter 31

**1** The Philistines fought against Israel. The men of Israel ran away from the Philistines. Many Israelites died on Mount Gilboa.

**2** The Philistines chased Saul and his sons. The Philistines killed the sons of Saul, Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchi-shua.

**3** The battle against Saul became very hard. The archers found him and wounded him badly.

**4** Saul said to the man who carried his weapons, "Take out your sword and kill me. If you do not, these uncircumcised men will come and kill me. They will make fun of me." But the man who carried his weapons refused, because he was very afraid. So Saul took his own sword and fell on it.

**5** The man who carried his weapons saw that Saul was dead. Then he also fell on his sword and died with Saul.

**6** Thus Saul, his 3 sons, the man who carried his weapons, and all his men died together on that day.

**7** Some Israelites lived on the other side of the valley and on the other side of the Jordan River. They saw that the army of Israel ran away. They saw that Saul and his sons were dead. So they left their cities and ran away. Then the Philistines came and lived in those cities.

**8** On the next day the Philistines came to take the things from the dead men. They found Saul and his 3 sons dead on Mount Gilboa.

**9** They cut off the head of Saul and took his armor. They sent messengers to all the land of the Philistines. The messengers told the news in the temples of their idols and to the people.

**10** They put the armor of Saul in the temple of Ashtoreth. They attached his body to the wall of Beth-shan.

**11** The people of Jabesh-gilead heard what the Philistines did to Saul.

**12** All the brave men of Jabesh got up and walked all night. They took the body of Saul and the bodies of his sons from the wall of Beth-shan. They came to Jabesh and burned the bodies there.

**13** Then they took the bones and buried them under the tamarisk tree at Jabesh. They did not eat food (fasted) for 7 days.
