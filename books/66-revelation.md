# Revelation

## Chapter 1

**1** This is the revelation of Jesus Christ. God gave the revelation to him to show his servants the things that must happen soon. Christ sent his angel to his servant John to make these things known.

**2** John told about the word of God and about the testimony of Jesus Christ. He told about all the things that he saw.

**3** God blesses the person who reads the words of this prophecy aloud. God blesses the people who hear the words and obey them. The time is near.

**4** John writes to the 7 churches in the province of Asia. I pray that grace and peace come to you from God. He is, he was, and he will come. Grace and peace also come from the 7 Spirits in front of his throne.

**5** Grace and peace also come from Jesus Christ. He is the loyal witness. He is the first to come back to life from death. He is the ruler of the kings of the earth. He loves us. He made us free from our sins with his blood.

**6** He made us kings and priests for God, his Father. Glory and power belong to Jesus Christ forever and ever. Amen.

**7** Look! He comes with the clouds. All people will see him. The people who put a spear into him will also see him. All the tribes of the earth will cry because of him. Yes, this will happen. Amen.

**8** The Lord God says, "I am the Alpha and the Omega. I am the start and the end. I am, I was, and I will come. I am the Almighty."

**9** I, John, am your brother. I share with you in the trouble, in the kingdom, and in the patience that we have in Jesus. I was on the island of Patmos. I was there because I told people the word of God and the testimony of Jesus.

**10** On the Lord's day, the Spirit controlled me. I heard a loud voice behind me. The voice was like the sound of a trumpet.

**11** The voice said, "I am the Alpha and the Omega, the first and the last. Write in a book the things that you see. Send the book to the 7 churches in Asia. Send it to Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum, Thyatira, Sardis, Philadelphia, and Laodicea."

**12** I turned to see who spoke to me. When I turned, I saw 7 gold lampstands.

**13** In the middle of the lampstands, I saw a person who was like a son of man. He wore a long robe that went down to his feet. He had a gold belt around his chest.

**14** The hair on his head was white, as white as wool or snow. His eyes were like flames of fire.

**15** His feet were like bright bronze that burns in a furnace. His voice was like the sound of a large quantity of water that flows fast.

**16** He held 7 stars in his right hand. A sharp sword with 2 edges came out of his mouth. His face was as bright as the sun at noon.

**17** When I saw him, I fell down at his feet like a dead man. He put his right hand on me and said, "Do not be afraid. I am the first and the last.

**18** I am the one who lives. I was dead, but look, I am alive forever and ever. I have the keys of death and of the place of the dead.

**19** Thus, write the things that you saw. Write the things that are now and the things that will happen after this.

**20** I will tell you the secret meaning of the 7 stars that you saw in my right hand. I will also tell you the secret of the 7 gold lampstands. The 7 stars are the angels of the 7 churches. The 7 lampstands are the 7 churches."

## Chapter 2

**1** "Write this to the angel of the church in Ephesus: The one who holds the 7 stars in his right hand says these things. He walks among the 7 gold lampstands. He says:

**2** I know the things that you do. I know your hard work and your patience. I know that you do not accept bad people. You tested the people who say that they are apostles, but they are not. You found that they are liars.

**3** You have patience. You had much trouble because of my name, but you did not become tired.

**4** But I have this against you: you do not love me as you loved me at the start.

**5** Thus, remember the high position that you fell from. Repent and do the things that you did at the start. If you do not repent, I will come to you. I will remove your lampstand from its place.

**6** But you have this good thing: you hate the things that the Nicolaitans do. I also hate those things.

**7** If you have ears, listen to what the Spirit says to the churches. I will let the person who wins the victory eat from the tree of life. The tree is in the paradise of God."

**8** "Write this to the angel of the church in Smyrna: The first and the last says these things. He was dead and came back to life. He says:

**9** I know your troubles and that you are poor. But really, you are rich. Some people say that they are Jews, but they are not. They speak bad words against you. They are a synagogue of Satan.

**10** Do not be afraid of the things that you will suffer. Look, the devil will put some of you in prison to test you. You will have trouble for 10 days. Stay loyal to me, even if you must die. Then I will give you life as your crown.

**11** If you have ears, listen to what the Spirit says to the churches. The second death will not hurt the person who wins the victory."

**12** "Write this to the angel of the church in Pergamum: The one who has the sharp sword with 2 edges says these things:

**13** I know where you live. Satan has his throne there. But you stay loyal to my name. You did not refuse your faith in me. You stayed loyal at the time when people killed Antipas in your city. Antipas was my loyal witness. Satan lives in your city.

**14** But I have a few things against you. Some of you follow the teaching of Balaam. Balaam taught Balak to cause the people of Israel to sin. He caused them to eat food that people offered to idols. He caused them to do sexual sins.

**15** Also, some of you follow the teaching of the Nicolaitans.

**16** Thus, repent! If you do not repent, I will come to you soon. I will fight against those people with the sword of my mouth.

**17** If you have ears, listen to what the Spirit says to the churches. I will give some of the hidden manna to the person who wins the victory. I will also give that person a white stone. A new name is on the stone. Only the person who gets the stone knows the name."

**18** "Write this to the angel of the church in Thyatira: The Son of God says these things. His eyes are like flames of fire. His feet are like bright bronze. He says:

**19** I know the things that you do. I know your love, your faith, your service, and your patience. You do more now than you did at the start.

**20** But I have this against you: you let that woman Jezebel teach. She says that she is a prophet. But she teaches my servants wrong things. She causes them to do sexual sins and to eat food that people offered to idols.

**21** I gave her time to repent, but she does not want to stop her sexual sins.

**22** Look, I will put her on a bed of pain. The people who do adultery with her will also have much trouble. This will happen if they do not repent of the things that she does.

**23** I will kill her children. Then all the churches will know that I examine the thoughts and the hearts of people. I will pay each of you for the things that you did.

**24** Now I speak to the other people in Thyatira. You do not follow the teaching of Jezebel. You did not learn what some people call the deep secrets of Satan. I will not put any other load on you.

**25** But hold tightly to the truth that you have until I come.

**26** I will give authority over the nations to the person who wins the victory. That person must do the things that I want until the end.

**27** That person will rule the nations with an iron rod. He will break them into pieces as a person breaks clay pots. My Father gave me this authority.

**28** I will also give that person the morning star.

**29** If you have ears, listen to what the Spirit says to the churches."

## Chapter 3

**1** "Write this to the angel of the church in Sardis: The one who has the 7 Spirits of God and the 7 stars says these things: I know the things that you do. People say that you are alive, but you are dead.

**2** Wake up! Make strong the things that remain, because they will soon die. In the view of my God, your work is not complete.

**3** Thus, remember what you got and heard. Obey it and repent. If you do not wake up, I will come like a thief. You will not know the hour when I come to you.

**4** But you have a few people in Sardis who did not make their clothes dirty. They will walk with me in white clothes, because they deserve it.

**5** The person who wins the victory will wear white clothes. I will not remove the name of that person from the book of life. I will say his name in front of my Father and his angels.

**6** If you have ears, listen to what the Spirit says to the churches."

**7** "Write this to the angel of the church in Philadelphia: The one who is holy and true says these things. He has the key of David. When he opens a door, no person can close it. When he closes a door, no person can open it. He says:

**8** I know the things that you do. Look, I put an open door in front of you. No person can close it. You have only a little strength. But you obeyed my word, and you did not refuse my name.

**9** Look, some people are from the synagogue of Satan. They say that they are Jews, but they are not. They are liars. I will cause them to come and bow down at your feet. They will know that I love you.

**10** You obeyed my command to have patience. Thus, I will keep you safe from the time of testing. That time will come to the whole world. It will test the people who live on the earth.

**11** I come soon. Hold tightly to what you have. Then no person can take your crown.

**12** I will make the person who wins the victory a pillar in the temple of my God. He will never go out of the temple. I will write on him the name of my God. I will write on him the name of the city of my God, the new Jerusalem. The city comes down out of heaven from my God. I will also write on him my new name.

**13** If you have ears, listen to what the Spirit says to the churches."

**14** "Write this to the angel of the church in Laodicea: The Amen says these things. He is the loyal and true witness. He is the ruler of all that God made. He says:

**15** I know the things that you do. You are not cold or hot. I want you to be cold or hot!

**16** But you are only warm, not hot or cold. Thus, I will spit you out of my mouth.

**17** You say, 'I am rich. I have much money. I need nothing.' But you do not know that you are in a very bad condition. You are poor, blind, and naked. People must feel sorry for you.

**18** I tell you to buy gold from me. Fire made this gold pure. Then you will be rich. Buy white clothes from me to wear. Then people will not see the shame of your naked body. Buy medicine to put on your eyes. Then you will see.

**19** I correct and punish all the people whom I love. Thus, be serious about this and repent.

**20** Look, I stand at the door and knock. If a person hears my voice and opens the door, I will come in. I will eat with that person, and that person will eat with me.

**21** I will let the person who wins the victory sit with me on my throne. In the same manner, I won the victory and sat down with my Father on his throne.

**22** If you have ears, listen to what the Spirit says to the churches."

## Chapter 4

**1** After these things, I looked and saw an open door in heaven. I heard the same voice that I heard before. The voice was like a trumpet. The voice said, "Come up here. I will show you the things that must happen after this."

**2** Immediately the Spirit controlled me. I saw a throne in heaven, and a person sat on the throne.

**3** The person on the throne looked like jasper and carnelian stones. A rainbow was all around the throne. The rainbow looked like an emerald.

**4** Around the throne were 24 other thrones. 24 elders sat on these thrones. They wore white clothes and had gold crowns on their heads.

**5** Lightning flashes, sounds, and thunder came out of the throne. 7 lamps of fire burned in front of the throne. These lamps are the 7 Spirits of God.

**6** In front of the throne was a sea of glass, clear as crystal. In the center, around the throne, were 4 living creatures. They had many eyes on their front and on their back.

**7** The first living creature was like a lion. The second was like a young bull. The third had a face like a man. The fourth was like an eagle that flies.

**8** Each of the 4 living creatures had 6 wings. They had eyes all around and inside. Day and night they do not stop. They say, "Holy, holy, holy is the Lord God Almighty. He was, he is, and he will come."

**9** The living creatures give glory, honor, and thanks to the person who sits on the throne. He lives forever and ever.

**10** When the creatures do this, the 24 elders bow down in front of him. They worship the one who lives forever and ever. They put their crowns in front of the throne and say,

**11** "Our Lord and God, you deserve to get glory, honor, and power. You made all things. All things exist because you wanted them. You made them because you wanted them."

## Chapter 5

**1** Then I saw a scroll in the right hand of the person who sat on the throne. The scroll had writing on the inside and on the outside. 7 seals closed the scroll.

**2** I saw a strong angel. He spoke with a loud voice. He said, "Who deserves to break the seals and open the scroll?"

**3** But no person in heaven, on the earth, or under the earth could open the scroll or look inside it.

**4** I cried very much, because no person deserved to open the scroll or look inside it.

**5** Then one of the elders said to me, "Do not cry. Look! The Lion from the tribe of Judah won the victory. He is the Root of David. He can open the scroll and its 7 seals."

**6** Then I saw a Lamb. He stood in the center, near the throne and the 4 living creatures and among the elders. The Lamb looked as if someone killed him. He had 7 horns and 7 eyes. The eyes are the 7 Spirits of God. God sends them out into all the earth.

**7** The Lamb came and took the scroll from the right hand of the person who sat on the throne.

**8** When the Lamb took the scroll, the 4 living creatures and the 24 elders bowed down in front of him. Each elder had a harp. Each elder also had gold bowls full of incense. The incense is the prayers of God's holy people.

**9** They sang a new song: "You deserve to take the scroll and to open its seals. People killed you. With your blood you bought us for God. We are from every tribe, language, people, and nation.

**10** You made us kings and priests for our God. We will rule on the earth."

**11** Then I looked, and I heard the voices of many angels. They stood around the throne, the living creatures, and the elders. There were thousands and thousands of angels. There were millions of angels.

**12** They said with a loud voice, "People killed the Lamb. He deserves to get power, wealth, wisdom, strength, honor, glory, and praise!"

**13** Then I heard all creatures in heaven, on the earth, under the earth, and in the sea. All the creatures in those places said, "Praise, honor, glory, and power belong to the one who sits on the throne and to the Lamb, forever and ever!"

**14** The 4 living creatures said, "Amen." Then the 24 elders bowed down. They worshiped the one who lives forever and ever.

## Chapter 6

**1** I watched when the Lamb opened the first of the 7 seals. Then I heard one of the 4 living creatures speak with a voice like thunder. It said, "Come!"

**2** I looked and saw a white horse. The rider of the horse had a bow. God gave him a crown. He went out to win battles. He wanted to win the victory.

**3** The Lamb opened the second seal. Then I heard the second living creature say, "Come!"

**4** Then another horse came out. It was red. God gave its rider power to take peace from the earth. The rider caused people to kill each other. God gave him a large sword.

**5** The Lamb opened the third seal. Then I heard the third living creature say, "Come!" I looked and saw a black horse. Its rider held a pair of scales in his hand.

**6** Then I heard a sound like a voice among the 4 living creatures. The voice said, "A quart of wheat costs the pay for 1 day of work. 3 quarts of barley cost the pay for 1 day of work. But do not damage the olive oil and the wine!"

**7** The Lamb opened the fourth seal. Then I heard the voice of the fourth living creature say, "Come!"

**8** I looked and saw a pale horse. The name of its rider was Death. Hades followed close behind him. God gave them power over a fourth of the earth. They killed people with the sword, with hunger, with disease, and with the wild animals of the earth.

**9** The Lamb opened the fifth seal. Then I saw under the altar the souls of people. People killed them because they told the word of God. They stayed loyal to their testimony.

**10** These souls shouted with a loud voice. They said, "Holy and true Lord, how long will you wait? When will you judge the people who live on the earth? When will you punish them because they killed us?"

**11** Then God gave each of the souls a white robe. God told them to rest a short time more. Some of their fellow servants and brothers must die as they died. They must wait until that number is complete.

**12** I watched when the Lamb opened the sixth seal. There was a large earthquake. The sun became black like a cloth of black hair. The whole moon became red like blood.

**13** The stars in the sky fell to the earth. They fell as figs that are not ripe fall from a fig tree in a strong wind.

**14** The sky went away like a scroll that a person rolls up. Every mountain and island moved from its place.

**15** Then the kings of the earth, the rulers, and the army commanders hid in caves and among the rocks of the mountains. The rich people, the powerful people, all slaves, and all free people also hid there.

**16** They said to the mountains and the rocks, "Fall on us! Hide us from the face of the one who sits on the throne. Hide us from the anger of the Lamb!

**17** The great day of their anger has come. No person can stand against it."

## Chapter 7

**1** After this, I saw 4 angels. They stood at the 4 corners of the earth. They held back the 4 winds of the earth. They stopped the wind. Thus no wind blew on the land, on the sea, or on any tree.

**2** Then I saw another angel. He came up from the east. He had the seal of the living God. God gave the 4 angels power to damage the land and the sea. The other angel shouted with a loud voice to them.

**3** He said, "Do not damage the land, the sea, or the trees yet. First we must put a seal on the foreheads of the servants of our God."

**4** Then I heard the number of the people who got the seal. There were 144,000 people. They were from all the tribes of Israel.

**5** 12,000 were from the tribe of Judah. 12,000 were from the tribe of Reuben. 12,000 were from the tribe of Gad.

**6** 12,000 were from the tribe of Asher. 12,000 were from the tribe of Naphtali. 12,000 were from the tribe of Manasseh.

**7** 12,000 were from the tribe of Simeon. 12,000 were from the tribe of Levi. 12,000 were from the tribe of Issachar.

**8** 12,000 were from the tribe of Zebulun. 12,000 were from the tribe of Joseph. 12,000 were from the tribe of Benjamin.

**9** After this, I looked and saw a very large crowd. No person could count the people in it. They were from every nation, tribe, people, and language. They stood in front of the throne and in front of the Lamb. They wore white robes. They held palm branches in their hands.

**10** They shouted with a loud voice, "Our God, who sits on the throne, and the Lamb have the power to save!"

**11** All the angels stood around the throne, the elders, and the 4 living creatures. The angels bowed down with their faces to the ground in front of the throne. They worshiped God.

**12** They said, "Amen! Praise, glory, wisdom, thanks, honor, power, and strength belong to our God forever and ever. Amen!"

**13** Then one of the elders asked me, "Who are these people in white robes? Where did they come from?"

**14** I said to him, "Sir, you know." He said to me, "These are the people who came out of the time of great trouble. They washed their robes in the blood of the Lamb. The blood made the robes white.

**15** Thus they are in front of the throne of God. They serve God day and night in his temple. The one who sits on the throne will protect them with his presence.

**16** They will never be hungry again. They will never be thirsty again. The sun and the hot wind will not burn them.

**17** The Lamb in the center of the throne will be their shepherd. He will lead them to springs of the water of life. God will remove all tears from their eyes."

## Chapter 8

**1** The Lamb opened the seventh seal. Then heaven was silent for about half an hour.

**2** Then I saw the 7 angels who stand in front of God. God gave them 7 trumpets.

**3** Another angel came and stood at the altar. He had a gold container for incense. Someone gave him much incense to offer with the prayers of all God's holy people. He offered it on the gold altar in front of the throne.

**4** The smoke of the incense went up with the prayers of God's holy people. The smoke went up from the hand of the angel to God.

**5** Then the angel took the container for incense. He filled it with fire from the altar and threw it on the earth. Then there was thunder, loud sounds, lightning, and an earthquake.

**6** Then the 7 angels who had the 7 trumpets got ready to blow them.

**7** The first angel blew his trumpet. Then hail and fire, mixed with blood, came down on the earth. A third of the trees burned. All the green grass burned.

**8** The second angel blew his trumpet. Then something like a large mountain that burned with fire fell into the sea. A third of the sea became blood.

**9** A third of the living creatures in the sea died. The fire destroyed a third of the ships.

**10** The third angel blew his trumpet. Then a large star fell from the sky. It burned like a torch. It fell on a third of the rivers and on the springs of water.

**11** The name of the star is Wormwood. A third of the water became bitter like wormwood. Many people died because they drank the bitter water.

**12** The fourth angel blew his trumpet. Then God struck a third of the sun, a third of the moon, and a third of the stars. Thus a third of them became dark. For a third of the day there was no light. The night was the same.

**13** As I watched, I heard an angel that flew high in the sky. He said with a loud voice, "Terrible! Terrible! Terrible things will happen to the people who live on the earth! These things will happen when the other 3 angels blow their trumpets."

## Chapter 9

**1** The fifth angel blew his trumpet. Then I saw a star that fell from the sky to the earth. God gave the star the key to the shaft of the bottomless pit.

**2** The star opened the shaft of the bottomless pit. Smoke came up out of the shaft, like smoke from a large furnace. The smoke from the shaft made the sun and the air dark.

**3** Locusts came out of the smoke and went down onto the earth. God gave them power like the power of scorpions.

**4** God told the locusts not to damage the grass, the plants, or the trees. They could hurt only the people who did not have the seal of God on their foreheads.

**5** God did not let the locusts kill these people. But the locusts could cause them pain for 5 months. The pain was like the pain from the sting of a scorpion.

**6** At that time, people will try to find death, but they will not find it. They will want to die, but death will run away from them.

**7** The locusts looked like horses that are ready for battle. On their heads they had something like crowns of gold. Their faces were like the faces of people.

**8** They had hair like the hair of women. Their teeth were like the teeth of lions.

**9** They had armor on their chests like iron armor. The sound of their wings was like the sound of many horses and chariots that run into battle.

**10** They had tails with stings like scorpions. They had power in their tails to hurt people for 5 months.

**11** The locusts had a king over them. He is the angel of the bottomless pit. His name in Hebrew is Abaddon. In Greek, his name is Apollyon. These names mean "Destroyer."

**12** The first terrible event is past. After it, 2 more terrible events will come.

**13** The sixth angel blew his trumpet. Then I heard a voice from the 4 horns of the gold altar in front of God.

**14** The voice spoke to the sixth angel who had the trumpet. It said, "Release the 4 angels who are tied at the large river Euphrates."

**15** The sixth angel released the 4 angels. God kept them ready for this hour, day, month, and year. They were ready to kill a third of the people.

**16** I heard the number of soldiers on horses in their army. There were 200 million.

**17** In my vision, I saw the horses and their riders. The riders had armor on their chests. The armor was red like fire, dark blue, and yellow like sulfur. The heads of the horses were like the heads of lions. Fire, smoke, and sulfur came out of their mouths.

**18** The fire, the smoke, and the sulfur came out of the mouths of the horses. These 3 terrible things killed a third of the people.

**19** The power of the horses was in their mouths and in their tails. Their tails were like snakes, with heads. They hurt people with these heads.

**20** These terrible things did not kill all the other people. But those people did not repent of the things that they did. They did not stop their worship of demons. They did not stop their worship of idols of gold, silver, bronze, stone, and wood. These idols cannot see, hear, or walk.

**21** The people did not repent of their murders or their magic. They did not repent of their sexual sins or their thefts.

## Chapter 10

**1** Then I saw another strong angel. He came down from heaven. A cloud was around him. A rainbow was over his head. His face was like the sun. His legs were like columns of fire.

**2** He held a small scroll in his hand. The scroll was open. He put his right foot on the sea and his left foot on the land.

**3** He shouted with a loud voice, like the roar of a lion. When he shouted, the 7 thunders spoke.

**4** When the 7 thunders spoke, I started to write. But I heard a voice from heaven. The voice said, "Keep secret the things that the 7 thunders said. Do not write them."

**5** Then the angel that I saw on the sea and on the land lifted his hand to heaven.

**6** He made a promise in the name of God, who lives forever and ever. God made heaven, the earth, the sea, and all things in them. The angel said, "There will be no more delay.

**7** The seventh angel will soon blow his trumpet. At that time, God will complete his secret plan. He told this plan to his servants the prophets."

**8** Then the voice from heaven spoke to me again. It said, "Go and take the open scroll. The angel who stands on the sea and on the land holds it in his hand."

**9** I went to the angel and told him to give me the small scroll. He said to me, "Take it and eat it. It will be as sweet as honey in your mouth. But it will make your stomach bitter."

**10** I took the small scroll from the hand of the angel and ate it. In my mouth it was as sweet as honey. But after I ate it, my stomach became bitter.

**11** Then someone said to me, "You must speak a prophecy again about many peoples, nations, languages, and kings."

## Chapter 11

**1** Someone gave me a measuring stick like a rod. The angel said to me, "Go and measure the temple of God and the altar. Count the people who worship there.

**2** But do not measure the court outside the temple. God gave that court to the Gentiles. They will walk on the holy city and damage it for 42 months.

**3** I will give power to my 2 witnesses. They will speak prophecy for 1,260 days. They will wear sackcloth, rough clothes that show sorrow."

**4** These 2 witnesses are the 2 olive trees and the 2 lampstands. They stand in front of the God of the earth.

**5** If any person tries to hurt the witnesses, fire comes out of their mouths and destroys their enemies. Any person who tries to hurt them must die in this manner.

**6** The witnesses have power to close the sky. Then no rain falls during the time of their prophecy. They have power to change water into blood. They can strike the earth with every type of disaster. They can do this as often as they want.

**7** When the 2 witnesses complete their testimony, the beast will attack them. The beast comes up from the bottomless pit. It will win the fight against them and kill them.

**8** Their dead bodies will lie in the street of the great city. People call that city Sodom and Egypt, as a symbol. That city is also where people nailed our Lord to a cross.

**9** For 3 and a half days, people from every people, tribe, language, and nation will look at their dead bodies. They will not let anyone bury the bodies.

**10** The people who live on the earth will be happy because the 2 witnesses are dead. They will have a celebration. They will send gifts to each other. They do this because these 2 prophets caused much pain to the people who live on the earth.

**11** But after the 3 and a half days, the breath of life from God went into the 2 witnesses. They stood up on their feet. The people who saw them were very afraid.

**12** Then the 2 witnesses heard a loud voice from heaven. It said to them, "Come up here!" They went up to heaven in a cloud. Their enemies watched them.

**13** At that same hour there was a strong earthquake. A tenth of the city fell down. The earthquake killed 7,000 people. The other people were afraid. They gave glory to the God of heaven.

**14** The second terrible event is past. The third terrible event will come soon.

**15** The seventh angel blew his trumpet. Then there were loud voices in heaven. They said, "The kingdom of the world now belongs to our Lord and to his Christ. He will rule forever and ever."

**16** The 24 elders sat on their thrones in front of God. They bowed down with their faces to the ground and worshiped God.

**17** They said, "Lord God Almighty, we thank you. You are, and you were, and you will come. You took your great power and started to rule.

**18** The nations were angry, but now the time of your anger is here. It is the time to judge the dead. It is the time to reward your servants the prophets and your holy people. You will reward all people who respect your name, the small and the great. It is the time to destroy the people who destroy the earth."

**19** Then God opened his temple in heaven. I saw the ark of his covenant in his temple. There was lightning, loud sounds, thunder, an earthquake, and a large storm of hail.

## Chapter 12

**1** Then a great sign appeared in heaven. I saw a woman. The sun was around her like clothes. The moon was under her feet. A crown of 12 stars was on her head.

**2** She was pregnant. She cried out with pain because she was about to give birth.

**3** Then another sign appeared in heaven. I saw a large red dragon. It had 7 heads and 10 horns. It had 7 crowns on its heads.

**4** The tail of the dragon pulled a third of the stars out of the sky. It threw them down to the earth. The dragon stood in front of the woman who was about to give birth. It wanted to eat her child immediately after the birth.

**4** They will see his face. His name will be on their foreheads.

**5** There will be no night there. The people will not need the light of a lamp or the light of the sun. The Lord God will give them light. They will rule for all time.

**6** The angel said to me, "These words are true, and you can trust them. The Lord God of the holy prophets sent his angel to show his servants the things that must occur soon."

**7** "Look! I will come soon! God blesses the person who obeys the words of the prophecy in this book."

**8** I, John, am the person who heard and saw these things. After I heard and saw them, I fell down to worship at the feet of the angel. He was the angel who showed me these things.

**9** But the angel said to me, "Do not do this! I am a servant, the same as you. I am a servant with your brothers the prophets. I am a servant with all the people who obey the words of this book. Worship God!"

**10** Then the angel said to me, "Do not keep the words of the prophecy of this book secret. The time is near.

**11** Let the person who does wrong continue to do wrong. Let the person who is not clean stay unclean. Let the person who does right continue to do right. Let the person who is holy stay holy."

**12** "Look! I will come soon! I will bring my reward with me. I will give each person a reward for the things that he or she did.

**13** I am the Alpha and the Omega, the first and the last, the start and the end.

**14** God blesses the people who obey his commands. They will have the right to eat from the tree of life. They will go through the gates into the city.

**15** Outside the city are the dogs, the people who use magic, and the people who commit sexual sins. Also outside are the murderers, the people who worship idols, and all the people who love lies and tell lies.

**16** I, Jesus, sent my angel to tell you these things for the churches. I am the root and the descendant of David. I am the bright morning star."

**17** The Spirit and the bride say, "Come!" Let each person who hears say, "Come!" Let each person who is thirsty come. Let each person who wants the water of life take it free of cost.

**18** I warn each person who hears the words of the prophecy of this book. If a person adds to these words, then God will add to him the plagues in this book.

**19** If a person removes words from the book of this prophecy, then God will remove his part in the book of life. God will also remove his part in the holy city. These things are in this book.

**20** Jesus, who tells these things, says, "Yes, I will come soon." Amen. Come, Lord Jesus!

**21** Let the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with all of you. Amen.
