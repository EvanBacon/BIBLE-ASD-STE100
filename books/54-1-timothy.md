# 1 Timothy

## Chapter 1

**1** I, Paul, write this letter. I am an apostle of Jesus Christ by the command of God our Savior and of the Lord Jesus Christ. Jesus Christ is our hope.

**2** I write to Timothy. You are my true son in the faith. I ask that God our Father and Jesus Christ our Lord give you grace, mercy, and peace.

**3** When I went into Macedonia, I asked you to stay in Ephesus. Stay there now. Tell some people to stop their false lessons.

**4** Tell them not to give attention to stories and to long lists of ancestors. These things cause arguments. They do not help God's work, which comes through faith.

**5** The purpose of this command is love. This love comes from a pure heart, a good conscience, and a true faith.

**6** Some people went away from these things. Now they talk about things that have no value.

**7** They want to be teachers of the law. But they do not understand what they say. They do not understand the things that they say with confidence.

**8** We know that the law is good if a person uses it correctly.

**9** We know that God did not give the law for good people. He gave it for people who do not obey the law and who rebel. He gave it for people who do not respect God and for sinners. He gave it for people who are not holy and who insult holy things. He gave it for people who kill their fathers or mothers, and for all murderers.

**10** He gave the law for people who commit sexual sin and for men who have sex with men. He gave it for people who steal people and sell them as slaves. He gave it for liars and for people who lie under oath. He gave it for all other actions that are against the true lessons.

**11** This message agrees with the gospel about the glory of the blessed God. God gave this gospel to me as a trust.

**12** I thank Christ Jesus our Lord, who gives me strength. He thought that I was faithful. Thus he put me into his service.

**13** Before, I spoke against God. I attacked his people and I was violent. But God gave me mercy, because I did not believe and I did not know what I did.

**14** The grace of our Lord came to me in very large quantity. With it came the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus.

**15** This statement is true, and all people must accept it. Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners. I am the worst sinner.

**16** But for this reason God gave me mercy. I was the worst sinner. Thus Jesus Christ used me to show all his patience. I am an example for the people who will believe in him and get life that does not end.

**17** Give honor and glory to the King who lives always. He cannot die, and no one can see him. He is the only God. Give him honor and glory always. Amen.

**18** Timothy, my son, I give you this command. It agrees with the prophecies that people spoke about you before. Use those prophecies to fight the good fight.

**19** Keep your faith and a good conscience. Some people did not keep a good conscience. Thus they destroyed their faith, as a ship breaks on the rocks.

**20** Hymenaeus and Alexander are 2 of these people. I gave them to Satan, so that they will learn not to speak against God.

## Chapter 2

**1** Thus, first of all, I tell you to pray for all people. Make requests to God for them, and thank God for them.

**2** Pray for kings and for all people who have authority. Then we can live quiet and peaceful lives. We can respect God and live in an honest way.

**3** This is good, and it pleases God our Savior.

**4** God wants to save all people. He wants them to know the truth.

**5** There is one God. There is also one person who brings God and men together. He is the man Christ Jesus.

**6** Christ Jesus gave his life as the price to make all people free. God showed this at the correct time.

**7** For this purpose, God chose me to be a preacher and an apostle. I tell the truth in Christ, and I do not lie. God chose me to teach the Gentiles about faith and truth.

**8** Thus I want the men in all places to pray. They must lift up holy hands. They must not be angry or argue.

**9** Also, I want women to wear modest clothes. They must be modest and have self-control. They must not make themselves beautiful with special hair styles, gold, pearls, or expensive clothes.

**10** Women who say that they worship God must make themselves beautiful with good works.

**11** A woman must learn quietly and obey completely.

**12** I do not let a woman teach a man or have authority over a man. She must be quiet.

**13** God made Adam first. Then he made Eve.

**14** The serpent did not trick Adam. The serpent tricked the woman, and she sinned.

**15** But God will save women through the birth of children. They must continue in faith, love, and holiness, with self-control.

## Chapter 3

**1** This statement is true: If a man wants to be a church leader (bishop), he wants to do good work.

**2** Thus a bishop must have a good life that no one can find a fault in. He must have only 1 wife. He must have self-control and good judgment. He must behave well. He must welcome guests into his home. He must be able to teach.

**3** He must not drink too much wine. He must not be violent. He must be gentle. He must not argue. He must not love money.

**4** He must manage his own family well. His children must obey him and respect him.

**5** If a man does not know how to manage his own family, he cannot take care of the church of God.

**6** A bishop must not be a new believer. If he is a new believer, he can become proud. Then he will fall under the same judgment as the devil.

**7** Also, the people who are not believers must think well of him. If they do not, people will say bad things about him. Then he can fall into the trap of the devil.

**8** In the same way, the church helpers (deacons) must be serious and honest. They must not drink too much wine. They must not try to get money in a dishonest way.

**9** They must keep the secret truth of the faith with a clean conscience.

**10** First, the church must examine them. If no one finds a fault in them, then they can serve as deacons.

**11** In the same way, their wives must be serious. They must not say bad things about other people. They must have self-control and be faithful in all things.

**12** A deacon must have only 1 wife. He must manage his children and his family well.

**13** The deacons who serve well get a good position for themselves. They also get great confidence in the faith that is in Christ Jesus.

**14** I write these things to you. I hope to come to you soon.

**15** But possibly I will be late. Then you will know how people must behave in the family of God. This family is the church of the God who lives. The church supports the truth and makes it strong.

**16** All people agree that the secret truth about God is great: Christ appeared in a human body. The Spirit showed that he was right. The angels saw him. People told the Gentiles about him. People in the world believed in him. God took him up into glory.

## Chapter 4

**1** The Spirit says clearly that, in the last times, some people will leave the faith. They will obey spirits that trick them. They will obey the teachings of demons.

**2** These teachings come from liars who say false things. Their consciences are dead, as if a hot iron burned them.

**3** These liars tell people not to marry. They tell people not to eat some foods. But God made these foods. He wants the people who believe and know the truth to eat them and thank him.

**4** All things that God made are good. Do not refuse anything if you thank God for it.

**5** The word of God and prayer make it holy.

**6** Tell these things to the brothers. Then you will be a good servant of Christ Jesus. The words of the faith and the good lessons that you followed will make you strong.

**7** Do not listen to foolish stories that are not holy. Train yourself to obey God.

**8** Training of the body gives a small advantage. But a life that obeys God gives an advantage in all things. It gives a promise for the life now and for the life that will come.

**9** This statement is true, and all people must accept it.

**10** For this reason, we work hard and struggle. We trust in the God who lives. He is the Savior of all people, and especially of the people who believe.

**11** Command and teach these things.

**12** Do not let anyone think that you are not important because you are young. Be an example to the believers in how you speak and how you live. Be an example in love, faith, and purity.

**13** Until I come, read the Scriptures to the people. Encourage them and teach them.

**14** Do not forget the gift that is in you. God gave it to you through prophecy, when the elders put their hands on you.

**15** Think about these things. Give all your attention to them. Then all people will see your progress.

**16** Be careful about how you live and what you teach. Continue to do these things. Then you will save yourself and the people who hear you.

## Chapter 5

**1** Do not speak angrily to an older man. Speak to him with respect, as to a father. Speak to younger men as to brothers.

**2** Speak to older women as to mothers. Speak to younger women as to sisters, with all purity.

**3** Give respect and help to widows who have no family to help them.

**4** If a widow has children or grandchildren, they must first learn to obey God in their own family. They must give help to their parents and grandparents. This pleases God.

**5** A true widow is alone. She trusts in God. She continues to pray to God night and day.

**6** But a widow who lives only for pleasure is spiritually dead, although she is alive.

**7** Give these instructions to the people. Then no one can find a fault in them.

**8** A person must take care of his relatives, and especially of his own family. If he does not, he refuses the faith. He is worse than a person who does not believe.

**9** Do not put a widow on the list of widows if she is less than 60 years old. She must be faithful to her husband.

**10** People must know about her good works. Did she take care of her children? Did she welcome strangers into her home? Did she wash the feet of God's people? Did she help people who had troubles? Did she do all types of good work?

**11** But do not put younger widows on the list. Their physical desires can take them away from Christ. Then they will want to marry.

**12** Thus they will be guilty, because they did not keep their first promise.

**13** Also, they learn to be lazy. They go from house to house. They are not only lazy. They also talk too much and put themselves into the business of other people. They say things that they must not say.

**14** Thus I want the younger widows to marry and have children. They must manage their homes. They must not give the enemy a reason to speak badly about us.

**15** Some widows already left the correct way to follow Satan.

**16** If a believer has widows in his or her family, the believer must help them. Do not give the church this expense. Then the church can help the widows who are truly alone.

**17** The elders who lead the church well must get double honor. This is especially true of the elders who work hard to preach and teach.

**18** The Scripture says, "Do not put a cover on the mouth of an ox when it walks on the grain." It also says, "A worker must get his pay."

**19** If a person says that an elder did wrong, do not accept it. Accept it only if 2 or 3 witnesses say it.

**20** If elders continue to sin, tell them that they are wrong in front of all the church. Then the other people will also be afraid to sin.

**21** I give you a serious command before God, the Lord Jesus Christ, and the chosen angels. Obey these instructions without a wrong opinion. Do not do anything to give an advantage to one person.

**22** Do not be quick to put your hands on a person and give him a position in the church. Do not share in the sins of other people. Keep yourself pure.

**23** Do not drink only water. Drink a little wine for your stomach, because you are often sick.

**24** The sins of some people are easy to see. These sins go before them to the judgment. But the sins of other people become known later.

**25** In the same way, the good works of some people are easy to see. The other good works cannot stay hidden.

## Chapter 6

**1** All slaves must think that their masters are worthy of all honor. Then no one will speak against the name of God and our message.

**2** Some slaves have masters who are believers. These slaves must not think less of their masters because the masters are brothers. They must serve them better, because the masters are believers and loved. The masters get the advantage of their good work. Teach these things and tell the people to do them.

**3** A person can teach a different message. He can refuse the true words of our Lord Jesus Christ. He can refuse the lessons that agree with a life that obeys God.

**4** Such a person is proud and knows nothing. He likes arguments about questions and words. These arguments cause jealousy, fights, insults, and evil suspicions.

**5** These arguments also cause bad quarrels among men with bad minds. Those men do not know the truth. They think that religion is a way to get money. Stay away from such people.

**6** But a life that obeys God, with a satisfied mind, is a large advantage.

**7** We brought nothing into the world. We surely cannot take anything out of the world.

**8** If we have food and clothes, we must be happy with these things.

**9** But people who want to become rich fall into temptation and into a trap. They get many foolish and harmful desires. These desires pull people down into ruin and destruction.

**10** The love of money is the cause of all types of evil. Some people wanted money very much. Thus they went away from the faith. They caused much pain for themselves.

**11** But you are a man of God. Run away from these things. Try to be good, to obey God, and to have faith and love. Try to be patient and gentle.

**12** Fight the good fight of the faith. Hold on to the life that does not end. God called you to this life. You said that you believe, in front of many witnesses.

**13** I give you a command before God, who gives life to all things. I also give it before Christ Jesus. Jesus spoke the truth well before Pontius Pilate.

**14** Obey this command completely. Do not let anyone find a fault in you. Obey it until our Lord Jesus Christ appears.

**15** God will make Christ appear at the correct time. God is the blessed and only ruler. He is the King of kings and the Lord of lords.

**16** Only God cannot die. He lives in a light that no one can come near. No one saw him, and no one can see him. Give him honor and power always. Amen.

**17** Give a command to the people who are rich in this world. Tell them not to be proud. Tell them not to trust in money, because money does not last. Tell them to trust in God. God gives us all things richly for our pleasure.

**18** Tell them to do good. Tell them to be rich in good works. They must give freely and share with other people.

**19** Thus they will keep a treasure for themselves in the future. It will be a strong base. Then they will hold on to the true life.

**20** Timothy, keep safe the things that God gave you as a trust. Stay away from foolish talk that is not holy. Stay away from the false ideas that some people call knowledge.

**21** Some people said that they had this knowledge. Thus they went away from the faith. I ask that grace be with you. Amen.
