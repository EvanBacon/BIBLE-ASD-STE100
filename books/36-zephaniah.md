# Zephaniah

## Chapter 1

**1** The LORD gave this message to Zephaniah. Zephaniah was the son of Cushi. Cushi was the son of Gedaliah. Gedaliah was the son of Amariah, and Amariah was the son of Hezekiah. The LORD spoke to Zephaniah when Josiah, the son of Amon, was king of Judah.

**2** The LORD says, "I will fully destroy all things on the surface of the earth.

**3** I will destroy people and animals. I will destroy the birds in the sky and the fish in the sea. I will destroy the idols that cause people to sin, and I will destroy evil people. I will remove all people from the earth," says the LORD.

**4** "I will punish Judah and all the people who live in Jerusalem. I will remove from this place all the things that people use to worship Baal. I will remove the names of the idol priests and the other priests.

**5** I will destroy the people who worship the stars from the roofs of their houses. I will destroy the people who worship the LORD and also make promises in the name of Molech.

**6** I will destroy the people who turn away from the LORD. I will destroy the people who do not look for the LORD or ask him for help."

**7** Be silent in front of the Lord GOD! The day of the LORD is near. The LORD prepared a sacrifice. He told his guests to make themselves holy.

**8** "On the day of the LORD's sacrifice, I will punish the officials and the sons of the king. I will punish all the people who wear the clothes of foreign nations.

**9** On that day, I will punish all the people who worship as foreigners do. They fill the houses of their masters with violence and lies."

**10** The LORD says, "On that day, people will shout from the Fish Gate. People will cry loudly in the new part of the city. People will hear a loud noise of destruction from the hills.

**11** Cry loudly, you people who live in the market area! All the merchants will die. All the people who trade with silver will die.

**12** At that time, I will use lamps to look carefully in all of Jerusalem. I will punish the people who are lazy and satisfied. They think, 'The LORD will not do good. He will not do bad.'

**13** Thus enemies will steal their possessions. Enemies will destroy their houses. The people will build houses, but they will not live in them. They will plant vineyards, but they will not drink the wine."

**14** The great day of the LORD is near. It is near and it comes very quickly. Listen! On the day of the LORD, the strong soldiers will cry loudly in pain.

**15** That day will be a day of anger. It will be a day of trouble and pain. It will be a day of ruin and destruction. It will be a day of darkness and gloom. It will be a day of clouds and thick darkness.

**16** It will be a day when soldiers blow the trumpets and shout for war. They will attack the strong cities and the high towers.

**17** "I will bring trouble on the people. They will walk like blind men, because they sinned against the LORD. Their blood will flow out like dust on the ground. Their dead bodies will lie like waste on the ground.

**18** Their silver and their gold will not save them on the day of the LORD's anger. The fire of his jealous anger will destroy all the earth. He will suddenly destroy all the people who live on the earth."

## Chapter 2

**1** Come together, you shameful nation. Come together.

**2** Do this before the LORD's decision becomes true. Do this before the day goes away like dust in the wind. Do this before the strong anger of the LORD comes on you. Do this before the day of the LORD's anger comes.

**3** All you humble people of the earth, look for the LORD. You obey his laws. Look for what is right. Be humble. Maybe the LORD will protect you on the day of his anger.

**4** No people will live in Gaza. Ashkelon will become a ruin. Enemies will force the people of Ashdod out at noon. Enemies will destroy Ekron.

**5** The people who live by the sea, the nation of the Cherethites, will have much trouble! The LORD speaks against you, Canaan, the land of the Philistines. "I will destroy you, and no people will live in you."

**6** The land by the sea will become fields for shepherds. It will have places for sheep.

**7** The land by the sea will belong to the people of Judah who stay alive. They will feed their sheep there. In the evening, they will lie down in the houses of Ashkelon. The LORD their God will help them. He will bring back their prisoners.

**8** "I heard the insults of Moab and the insults of the people of Ammon. They insulted my people. They said proud things about their land and tried to take it.

**9** Thus, as I live," says the LORD of armies, the God of Israel, "Moab will become like Sodom. The people of Ammon will become like Gomorrah. Their land will be full of weeds and salt pits. It will always be a ruin. My people who stay alive will take their possessions. My people will take their land."

**10** Moab and Ammon will get this punishment because of their pride. They insulted the people of the LORD of armies and said proud things against them.

**11** The LORD will cause these people to be very afraid. He will destroy all the gods of the earth. Then all the nations will worship the LORD, each nation in its own land.

**12** "You people of Ethiopia, my sword will also kill you."

**13** The LORD will use his power against the north and destroy Assyria. He will make Nineveh a ruin. Nineveh will become dry like a desert.

**14** Flocks of sheep and all types of animals will lie down in the city. Owls will sleep on the tops of the broken columns. Their calls will come through the windows. There will be ruins at the doors. The LORD will remove the cedar wood from the buildings.

**15** Nineveh was a happy city that felt safe. The people of Nineveh said to themselves, "We are the most important city. There is no other city like us." But now Nineveh is a ruin, a place where wild animals lie down! All the people who go past the city laugh at it. They wave their fists at it.

## Chapter 3

**1** Jerusalem will have much trouble! The city does not obey God, and it is not clean. The people in the city are cruel to other people.

**2** The people do not obey the voice of the LORD. They do not accept correction. They do not trust in the LORD. They do not come near to their God.

**3** The officials in the city are like lions that roar. Her judges are like wolves in the evening. In the morning, they leave nothing.

**4** Her prophets are proud, and people cannot trust them. Her priests make the holy place unclean. They break the law.

**5** But the LORD is in the city, and he is fair. He does no wrong. Every morning he gives fair decisions. He never fails. But the evil people feel no shame.

**6** "I destroyed nations. I destroyed their towers. I made their streets empty, and no person goes on them. I destroyed their cities. No person lives there now.

**7** I said to Jerusalem, 'Surely you will respect me now. You will accept correction.' Then I would not destroy her houses or punish her. But the people were eager to do more evil actions."

**8** The LORD says, "Thus wait for me. Wait until the day that I stand up to accuse the nations. I decided to gather the nations and the kingdoms. I will show them all my strong anger. The fire of my jealous anger will destroy all the earth.

**9** Then I will make the speech of the peoples pure. Then all of them will call on the name of the LORD. They will all serve him together.

**10** My people are in many places, even on the other side of the rivers of Ethiopia. They will pray to me and bring offerings to me.

**11** On that day, you will not feel shame for the wrong things that you did against me. I will remove the proud people from the city. You will not be proud again on my holy mountain.

**12** But I will keep in the city a group of humble and poor people. They will trust in the name of the LORD.

**13** The people of Israel who stay alive will not do wrong things. They will not tell lies. They will not try to trick other people. They will eat and rest. No person will make them afraid."

**14** Sing, people of Zion! Shout, people of Israel! Be happy and have joy with all your heart, people of Jerusalem!

**15** The LORD stopped your punishment. He sent your enemies away. The LORD, the king of Israel, is with you. You will not be afraid of harm again.

**16** On that day, people will say to Jerusalem, "Do not be afraid, Zion. Do not let your hands become weak.

**17** The LORD your God is with you. He is strong, and he will save you. He will be very happy about you. He will be quiet in his love for you. He will sing with joy about you."

**18** "I will gather the people who are sad because they cannot go to the holy festivals. They belong to you. Their shame was a heavy load for them.

**19** At that time, I will punish all the people who caused you trouble. I will save the people who cannot walk well. I will gather the people whom enemies forced away. I will give them praise and honor in every land where people put shame on them.

**20** At that time, I will gather you and bring you home. I will make you famous and give you praise among all the peoples of the earth. You will see me bring back your possessions," says the LORD.
