# Ecclesiastes

## Chapter 1

**1** These are the words of the Teacher. He was the son of David and the king in Jerusalem.

**2** The Teacher says, "Nothing has meaning. Nothing has meaning. All things have no meaning."

**3** A man works hard on the earth all his life. What does he get for all his work?

**4** One generation goes, and another generation comes. But the earth stays forever.

**5** The sun comes up and the sun goes down. Then it moves quickly back to the place where it comes up.

**6** The wind blows to the south and turns to the north. The wind turns around and around. It always comes back on its circuit.

**7** All the rivers flow into the sea, but the sea is never full. The rivers go back to their start, and they flow again.

**8** All things make people tired. Nobody can fully tell about them. The eye always wants to see more. The ear always wants to hear more.

**9** The things that occurred before will occur again. The things that people did before, they will do again. There is nothing new on the earth.

**10** Can a person show something and say, "Look, this is new"? No. That thing was there a long time before us.

**11** People do not remember the people of the past. Also, the people who come later will not remember the people who come before them.

**12** I, the Teacher, was king over Israel in Jerusalem.

**13** I used my wisdom to study all the things that people do on the earth. God gives people a heavy load of work to do.

**14** I saw all the things that people do on the earth. All of it has no meaning. It is like trying to catch the wind.

**15** People cannot make straight a thing that is bent. People cannot count a thing that is not there.

**16** I said to myself, "I am great. I have more wisdom than all the kings in Jerusalem before me. I know much about wisdom and knowledge."

**17** I tried to understand wisdom. I also tried to understand madness and foolishness. But I learned that this is also like trying to catch the wind.

**18** With much wisdom comes much sorrow. A person who gets more knowledge gets more pain.

## Chapter 2

**1** I said to myself, "I will test pleasure. I will enjoy good things." But this also had no meaning.

**2** I said, "Laughter is foolish. Pleasure does nothing good."

**3** I decided to make myself happy with wine. But I let wisdom guide my mind. I tried foolishness too. I wanted to learn what is good for people to do during their short life on the earth.

**4** I did great things. I built houses for myself. I planted vineyards.

**5** I made gardens and parks. I planted all types of fruit trees in them.

**6** I made pools of water. I used the water for the many trees that grew in the forest.

**7** I bought male and female slaves. Some slaves were born in my house. I also had more cattle and sheep than all the people in Jerusalem before me.

**8** I also collected silver and gold and the treasures of kings and countries. I got men and women to sing for me. I also got musical instruments of all types.

**9** So I became great. I became greater than all the people in Jerusalem before me. And my wisdom stayed with me.

**10** I got all the things that I wanted. I did not refuse myself any pleasure. My work made me happy. That joy was my reward for all my work.

**11** Then I looked at all the things that I did. I looked at all the hard work. But all of it had no meaning. It was like trying to catch the wind. Nothing on the earth gave a true profit.

**12** Then I started to think about wisdom, madness and foolishness. What can the next king do? He can only do what the king before him did.

**13** Then I saw that wisdom is better than foolishness. Wisdom is better as light is better than darkness.

**14** A wise man can see where he goes. But a fool walks in darkness. Yet I saw that the same end comes to both of them.

**15** I said to myself, "The end of the fool will also be my end. So why did I become very wise?" I said to myself, "This also has no meaning."

**16** People will not remember the wise man forever, and they will not remember the fool. In the future, people will forget both of them. The wise man dies as the fool dies.

**17** So I hated life. All the work on the earth gave me grief. All of it had no meaning. It was like trying to catch the wind.

**18** I hated all the work that I did on the earth. I must leave the results of that work to the man who comes after me.

**19** Nobody knows if that man will be wise or foolish. But he will control all the things that I made with my work and my wisdom. This also has no meaning.

**20** So I started to lose hope about all my hard work on the earth.

**21** A man can work with wisdom, knowledge and skill. Then he must leave all the results to a man who did not work for them. This also has no meaning, and it is very bad.

**22** A man works hard and worries on the earth. What does he get from all that work?

**23** All his days, his work gives him pain and grief. Even at night, his mind does not rest. This also has no meaning.

**24** Nothing is better for a man than to eat and drink and enjoy his work. I saw that this also comes from the hand of God.

**25** Without God, nobody can eat or have joy.

**26** God gives wisdom, knowledge and joy to the man who pleases Him. But God gives the sinner the work to collect and store things. Then God gives those things to the man who pleases Him. This also has no meaning. It is like trying to catch the wind.

## Chapter 3

**1** There is a time for every thing. There is a time for every activity on the earth.

**2** There is a time to be born and a time to die. There is a time to plant and a time to pull up plants.

**3** There is a time to kill and a time to heal. There is a time to tear down and a time to build.

**4** There is a time to cry and a time to laugh. There is a time to be sad and a time to dance.

**5** There is a time to throw away stones and a time to collect stones. There is a time to hold people close and a time to stop.

**6** There is a time to look for things and a time to stop looking. There is a time to keep things and a time to throw things away.

**7** There is a time to tear and a time to sew. There is a time to be quiet and a time to speak.

**8** There is a time to love and a time to hate. There is a time for war and a time for peace.

**9** What does a worker get from all his hard work?

**10** I saw the heavy load that God gives people to carry.

**11** God made every thing beautiful at the correct time. He also put a feeling of eternity into the minds of people. But people cannot understand all of the work of God from the start to the end.

**12** I know that nothing is better for people than to be happy and do good during their life.

**13** Every person must eat and drink and enjoy the good results of his work. This is a gift from God.

**14** I know that all that God does will continue forever. Nobody can add to it or remove from it. God does this so that people will fear Him.

**15** The things that occur now occurred before. The things that will occur occurred before. God makes the past occur again.

**16** I also saw this on the earth. In the place of justice, there was wickedness. In the place of righteousness, there was wickedness.

**17** I said to myself, "God will judge the righteous and the wicked. There is a time for every activity and every action."

**18** I said to myself, "God tests people. God shows people that they are like animals."

**19** The same end comes to people and to animals. Both die. Both have the same breath. People are not better than animals. All things have no meaning.

**20** All go to the same place. All come from dust, and all go back to dust.

**21** Nobody knows if the spirit of a person goes up. Nobody knows if the spirit of an animal goes down into the earth.

**22** So I saw that nothing is better for a man than to enjoy his work. That is his reward. Nobody can show him what will occur after he dies.

## Chapter 4

**1** Again I looked at all the cruel things that people do on the earth. I saw the tears of the oppressed people. Nobody gave them comfort. Their oppressors had power. And nobody gave the oppressed people comfort.

**2** So I said that the dead people are more fortunate than the living people.

**3** But the person who is not yet born is better than both. That person did not see the evil things that people do on the earth.

**4** I saw that people work hard and with skill because they are jealous of their neighbors. This also has no meaning. It is like trying to catch the wind.

**5** The fool folds his hands and does no work. He destroys himself.

**6** One handful with peace is better than 2 handfuls with hard work. Hard work for more is like trying to catch the wind.

**7** Again I saw something on the earth that has no meaning.

**8** There is a man who is alone. He has no son and no brother. But he never stops his work. He is never satisfied with his riches. He does not ask, "For whom do I work? Why do I refuse myself pleasure?" This also has no meaning. It is a sad situation.

**9** 2 people are better than 1. Together they get a good reward for their work.

**10** If one person falls, the other person can help him up. But it is bad for a person who is alone when he falls. Nobody will help him up.

**11** Also, if 2 people lie down together, they keep warm. But a person who is alone cannot get warm.

**12** An enemy can defeat one person, but 2 people can defend themselves. A rope of 3 strands does not break quickly.

**13** A poor, wise young man is better than an old, foolish king. The old king does not listen to advice any more.

**14** The young man can come out of prison and become king. He can be born poor in that kingdom.

**15** I saw all the people on the earth follow the young man. He took the place of the old king.

**16** There was no end to all the people that he led. But the people who come later will not be happy with him. This also has no meaning. It is like trying to catch the wind.

## Chapter 5

**1** Be careful when you go to the house of God. Go to listen. Do not give foolish sacrifices as fools do. Fools do not know that they do wrong.

**2** Do not speak too quickly. Do not make quick promises to God. God is in heaven and you are on the earth. So say only a few words.

**3** Too much worry causes bad dreams. Too many words cause foolish talk.

**4** When you make a promise (a vow) to God, do it quickly. God is not pleased with fools. Do what you promise.

**5** It is better not to make a vow than to make a vow and not do it.

**6** Do not let your words cause you to sin. Do not tell the priest, "My vow was a mistake." Why make God angry about your words? He can destroy the work of your hands.

**7** Too many dreams and too many words have no meaning. Fear God.

**8** In a country, you can see that people oppress the poor. You can see that they refuse justice to the poor. Do not be surprised. An official watches the official below him. Higher officials watch both of them.

**9** All people get the profit of the land. The king also gets food from the fields.

**10** A man who loves money will never have sufficient money. A man who loves riches will never be satisfied with his income. This also has no meaning.

**11** When a man gets more things, more people come to use them. The owner only gets to look at them.

**12** A worker sleeps well, if he eats little or much. But the riches of a rich man do not let him sleep.

**13** I saw a very bad thing on the earth. A man kept his riches, but the riches caused him damage.

**14** He lost his riches in a bad business deal. Then he had a son, but he had nothing to give to his son.

**15** A man comes from his mother with no clothes. He will go away with no clothes. He will take nothing from his work in his hand.

**16** This also is a very bad thing. A man goes away the same as he comes. What does he get from his work? He works for the wind.

**17** All his life, he eats in darkness. He has much worry, sickness and anger.

**18** I saw that it is good and correct for a man to eat and drink. He must enjoy his work on the earth during the short life that God gives him. That is his reward.

**19** God gives some people riches and property. God also lets them enjoy these things. They accept their reward and are happy in their work. This is a gift from God.

**20** These people do not think much about the short time of their life. God keeps them busy with joy.

## Chapter 6

**1** I saw another bad thing on the earth. It is heavy on people.

**2** God gives a man riches, property and honor. The man has all that he wants. But God does not let him enjoy these things. A stranger enjoys them. This has no meaning. It is a very bad thing.

**3** A man can have 100 children and live many years. But if he does not enjoy good things and does not get a correct burial, then a baby who is born dead is better than that man.

**4** That baby comes with no meaning and goes into darkness. Darkness covers its name.

**5** That baby never sees the sun and never knows anything. But it has more rest than that man.

**6** That man can live 2,000 years and not enjoy good things. All people go to the same place.

**7** A man works only to get food for his mouth. But he is never satisfied.

**8** What advantage does a wise man have over a fool? What advantage does a poor man get if he knows how to act in front of other people?

**9** It is better to enjoy what you have than to want more. Wanting more has no meaning. It is like trying to catch the wind.

**10** All things that occur already have a name. People know what man is. A man cannot argue with God, who is stronger than he is.

**11** More words give less meaning. Words do not help a man.

**12** Nobody knows what is good for a man during his short life. His life goes quickly like a shadow. Nobody can tell a man what will occur on the earth after he dies.

## Chapter 7

**1** A good name is better than expensive perfume. The day of death is better than the day of birth.

**2** It is better to go to a house of sorrow than to a house of celebration. Death is the end of all people. The living must think about this.

**3** Sorrow is better than laughter. A sad face can make the heart better.

**4** A wise man thinks about death. A fool thinks only about pleasure.

**5** It is better to hear the correction of a wise man than to hear the songs of fools.

**6** The laughter of a fool is like the noise of thorns that burn under a pot. This also has no meaning.

**7** Oppression makes a wise man foolish. A bribe destroys the mind.

**8** The end of a thing is better than its start. Patience is better than pride.

**9** Do not become angry quickly. Anger stays in the mind of fools.

**10** Do not say, "Why were the old days better than these days?" That is not a wise question.

**11** Wisdom is good, as an inheritance is good. Wisdom helps the living.

**12** Wisdom protects, as money protects. But knowledge has this advantage: wisdom keeps alive the person who has it.

**13** Think about the work of God. Nobody can make straight a thing that God makes bent.

**14** When times are good, be happy. When times are bad, think about this. God makes both good times and bad times. So a man cannot know his future.

**15** During my short life, I saw all these things. A righteous man can die in his righteousness. A wicked man can live long in his wickedness.

**16** Do not be too righteous or too wise. Why destroy yourself?

**17** Do not be too wicked, and do not be a fool. Why die before your time?

**18** It is good to hold on to one warning and not let go of the other. A man who fears God will avoid both extremes.

**19** Wisdom makes one wise man stronger than 10 rulers in a city.

**20** There is no righteous man on the earth who always does good and never sins.

**21** Do not listen to all the things that people say. If you listen, you can hear your servant curse you.

**22** You know that you also cursed other people many times.

**23** I tested all these things with wisdom. I said, "I will be wise." But wisdom was far from me.

**24** Wisdom is far away and very deep. Nobody can find it.

**25** I decided to know, to study and to look for wisdom and the reason for things. I wanted to know that wickedness is foolish and that foolishness is madness.

**26** I found something more bitter than death. It is a woman who sets traps for men. Her love is like a net, and her hands are like chains. A man who pleases God will escape from her. But she will catch the sinner.

**27** The Teacher says, "Look, this is what I found. I put one thing with another thing to find the answer.

**28** I continue to look, but I do not find the answer. I found 1 good man in 1,000. But I did not find 1 good woman among them.

**29** I found only this: God made people honest. But people looked for many evil plans."

## Chapter 8

**1** Who is like the wise man? Who knows the meaning of things? Wisdom makes a man's face shine. It makes his hard face gentle.

**2** I tell you to obey the command of the king. You made a promise to God about this.

**3** Do not leave the king quickly. Do not take part in an evil plan. The king does what he wants.

**4** The word of the king has power. Nobody can ask him, "Why do you do this?"

**5** A person who obeys the command will have no trouble. A wise man knows the correct time and the correct way to act.

**6** There is a correct time and way for every action. But a man has many troubles.

**7** Nobody knows the future. Nobody can tell a man what will occur.

**8** Nobody has the power to stop the wind. Nobody has power over the day of his death. Nobody is free from that war. Wickedness will not save the wicked.

**9** I saw all this. I thought about all the things that people do on the earth. Sometimes one person has power over another person and causes him damage.

**10** Then I saw the burial of wicked people. They often went into and out of the holy place. People praised them in the city where they did evil things. This also has no meaning.

**11** People do not punish crime quickly. So people decide to do evil things.

**12** A sinner can do evil 100 times and still live a long life. But I know that it will be better for people who fear God and respect Him.

**13** But it will not be good for the wicked. Their lives will not continue long. Their lives are like a shadow, because they do not fear God.

**14** There is another thing on the earth that has no meaning. Some righteous people get the punishment that the wicked deserve. Some wicked people get the reward that the righteous deserve. I said that this also has no meaning.

**15** So I recommend joy. Nothing is better for a man on the earth than to eat, drink and be happy. Then joy will stay with him in his work during the life that God gives him.

**16** I tried to know wisdom. I tried to see all the work that people do on the earth. Some people do not sleep in the day or in the night.

**17** Then I saw all the work of God. A man cannot understand the things that occur on the earth. He can work hard to look for the answer, but he will not find it. A wise man can say that he knows. But he cannot understand it.

## Chapter 9

**1** I thought about all this, and I saw that God controls the righteous and the wise and their actions. Nobody knows if love or hate will come to him.

**2** The same end comes to all people. It comes to the righteous and the wicked. It comes to the good, the clean and the unclean. It comes to people who give sacrifices and people who do not give sacrifices. It comes to the good man and the sinner. It comes to people who make oaths and people who fear to make oaths.

**3** This is the evil in all things that occur on the earth. The same end comes to all people. Also, the hearts of people are full of evil. They are foolish during their life, and after that, they die.

**4** But a person who is alive has hope. A living dog is better than a dead lion.

**5** The living know that they will die. But the dead know nothing. They get no more reward. People forget them.

**6** Their love, their hate and their jealousy stopped long ago. They will never again take part in the things that occur on the earth.

**7** Go, eat your bread with joy, and drink your wine with a happy heart. God already accepts what you do.

**8** Always wear white clothes. Always put oil on your head.

**9** Enjoy life with the wife whom you love. Enjoy all the days of the short life that God gives you on the earth. That is your reward in life and in your hard work on the earth.

**10** Do all your work with all your strength. You go to the grave (Sheol). In the grave, there is no work, no planning, no knowledge and no wisdom.

**11** I saw something else on the earth. The fastest runner does not always win the race. The strongest soldier does not always win the battle. The wise do not always have food. The intelligent do not always have riches. The skilled do not always get favor. Time and chance come to all of them.

**12** Nobody knows when his time will come. People are like fish that a net catches. People are like birds that a trap catches. A bad time comes to people suddenly.

**13** I also saw this example of wisdom on the earth. I thought that it was very important.

**14** There was a small city with few men in it. A powerful king came against the city. He put his army around it and built large siege walls against it.

**15** A poor, wise man lived in that city. He saved the city with his wisdom. But nobody remembered that poor man.

**16** So I said, "Wisdom is better than strength." But people hated the wisdom of the poor man. They did not listen to his words.

**17** It is better to listen to the quiet words of a wise man than to the shouts of a ruler of fools.

**18** Wisdom is better than weapons of war. But one sinner can destroy many good things.

## Chapter 10

**1** Dead flies make perfume smell bad. Also, a small quantity of foolishness can destroy much wisdom and honor.

**2** The mind of a wise man goes to the correct side. But the mind of a fool goes to the wrong side.

**3** When a fool walks on the road, he does not use good sense. He shows everyone that he is a fool.

**4** If a ruler becomes angry with you, do not leave your work. If you stay calm, you can stop large mistakes.

**5** I saw an evil thing on the earth. It is a type of mistake that rulers make.

**6** Rulers give high positions to fools. Rich people get low positions.

**7** I saw slaves on horses. I saw princes walk on the ground like slaves.

**8** A man who digs a pit can fall into it. A snake can bite a man who breaks through a wall.

**9** A man who cuts stones can get hurt by them. A man who cuts wood can get injured.

**10** If an ax is not sharp and a man does not sharpen it, he must use more strength. But wisdom helps a man to have success.

**11** If a snake bites before the charmer controls it, the charmer gets no profit.

**12** The words of a wise man bring him favor. But the words of a fool destroy him.

**13** A fool starts to speak with foolishness. He ends his talk with dangerous madness.

**14** A fool talks too much. Nobody knows what will occur. Nobody can tell a man what will occur after him.

**15** The work of a fool makes him tired. He does not even know the road to the city.

**16** It is bad for a country when its king is a young boy. It is bad when its leaders feast in the morning.

**17** It is good for a country when its king is from a noble family. It is good when its leaders eat at the correct time. They eat for strength, not to become drunk.

**18** If a man is lazy, the roof becomes weak. If he does not use his hands, water comes into the house.

**19** People make feasts to laugh. Wine makes people happy. Money gets all things.

**20** Do not curse the king, even in your thoughts. Do not curse a rich man, even in your bedroom. A bird can carry your words. A bird can tell what you said.

## Chapter 11

**1** Send your bread on the waters. After many days, you will find it again.

**2** Divide your possessions among 7 or 8 people. You do not know what disaster will come on the earth.

**3** When clouds are full of rain, they pour the rain on the earth. A tree can fall to the south or to the north. The tree will stay where it falls.

**4** A farmer who waits for the correct wind will not plant. A farmer who watches the clouds will not harvest.

**5** You do not know the path of the wind. You do not know how a baby grows inside its mother. Also, you do not know the work of God, who makes all things.

**6** Plant your seed in the morning. Do not stop your work in the evening. You do not know which work will have success. Both can be good.

**7** Light is sweet. It is good for the eyes to see the sun.

**8** A man can live many years. He must enjoy all of them. But he must also remember that the dark days will be many. All that comes has no meaning.

**9** Young man, be happy while you are young. Let your heart give you joy in the days of your youth. Do what you want and what you see. But know that God will judge you for all these things.

**10** So remove worry from your mind. Keep away pain from your body. Youth and the strength of youth go quickly.

## Chapter 12

**1** Remember your Creator while you are young. Remember Him before the bad days come. Remember Him before the years come when you will say, "I have no pleasure in them."

**2** Remember Him before the sun, the light, the moon and the stars become dark for you. Remember Him before the clouds come back after the rain.

**3** At that time, your arms will shake and your legs will become weak. Your teeth will be few and stop chewing. Your eyes will become dim.

**4** Your ears will be closed to the noise of the street. The sound of the mill will be quiet. You will wake up when a bird sings. But you will hardly hear songs.

**5** You will be afraid of high places and of danger on the road. Your hair will become white like the almond tree in bloom. You will drag yourself along like a grasshopper. Your desire will stop. Then you go to your eternal home, and people cry in the streets.

**6** Remember your Creator before the silver cord breaks and the gold bowl breaks. Remember Him before the jar breaks at the spring and the wheel breaks at the well.

**7** Then the body will go back to the dust of the earth. The spirit will go back to God, who gave it.

**8** The Teacher says, "Nothing has meaning. All things have no meaning."

**9** The Teacher was wise. He also taught knowledge to the people. He thought about many proverbs. He studied them and put them in order.

**10** The Teacher tried to find the correct words. He wrote words that were honest and true.

**11** The words of wise people are like sticks that guide cattle. Their collected sayings are like nails that are hammered in firmly. One Shepherd gives these words.

**12** My son, be careful of anything more than these words. People will never stop the writing of books. Too much study makes the body tired.

**13** This is the end of the matter. You heard all of it. Fear God and obey His commands. This is the duty of all people.

**14** God will judge every action and every secret thing. He will judge the good and the evil.
