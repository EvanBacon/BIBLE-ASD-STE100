# 1 Peter

## Chapter 1

**1** I, Peter, am an apostle of Jesus Christ. I write to the people of God who live as foreigners in many countries. They live in Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia.

**2** God the Father knew you and chose you before the start. The Spirit makes you holy. God chose you to obey Jesus Christ and to be made clean by his blood. I pray that God will give you much grace and peace.

**3** Praise the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ! Because of his great mercy, he gave us new life. He raised Jesus Christ from death. Thus we have a living hope.

**4** God will give us an inheritance that nothing can destroy, make dirty, or cause to fade. God keeps this inheritance for you in heaven.

**5** Through your faith, the power of God protects you. Then you will receive salvation. God is ready to show this salvation at the end of time.

**6** Thus you are very glad. But now, for a short time, you must suffer many types of trouble. These troubles make you sad.

**7** These troubles test your faith. A fire tests gold, but gold does not last. Your faith is of much more value than gold. Your faith will bring praise, glory, and honor when Jesus Christ comes again.

**8** You did not see Jesus, but you love him. You do not see him now, but you believe in him. Thus you have a great and glorious joy that words cannot express.

**9** You receive the result of your faith. That result is the salvation of your souls.

**10** The prophets spoke about the grace that God will give to you. They studied this salvation very carefully.

**11** The Spirit of Christ was in the prophets. The Spirit told them about the sufferings of Christ and the glory that will come after them. The prophets tried to know the person and the time that the Spirit spoke about.

**12** God showed the prophets that their work was not for themselves. Their work was for you. Now other people told you these things. They told you the gospel through the Holy Spirit, who came from heaven. Even the angels want to understand these things.

**13** Thus prepare your minds for action. Control yourselves. Put all your hope in the grace that God will give you when Jesus Christ comes again.

**14** Obey God like children obey their father. In the past you did not know the truth, and you followed your wrong desires. Do not live like that now.

**15** God, who called you, is holy. Thus you also must be holy in all that you do.

**16** The Scriptures say, "Be holy, because I am holy."

**17** You call God your Father. He judges each person by what the person does. He does not show favor. Thus respect God during the time that you live as foreigners on earth.

**18** You know that God paid the price to make you free. He freed you from the empty life that you learned from your ancestors. He did not pay with silver or gold, which do not last.

**19** He paid with the valuable blood of Christ. Christ was like a lamb with no fault and no defect.

**20** God chose Christ before he made the world. But God showed Christ to the world in these last times, for you.

**21** Through Christ you believe in God. God raised Christ from death and gave him glory. Thus your faith and hope are in God.

**22** You obeyed the truth, and thus you made your souls pure. Now you have true love for your brothers. Thus love each other strongly from a pure heart.

**23** God gave you new life. This new life did not come from a seed that dies. It came from a seed that does not die. It came through the word of God, which lives and continues for all time.

**24** The Scriptures say, "All people are like grass. All their glory is like the flower of the grass. The grass dries up, and the flower falls.

**25** But the word of the Lord continues for all time." This word is the gospel that people told you.

## Chapter 2

**1** Thus remove all evil from your lives. Stop all lies, false actions, jealousy, and all evil talk about other people.

**2** Newborn babies want milk. In the same way, you must want the pure milk of the word of God. Then you will grow and receive salvation.

**3** You tasted the goodness of the Lord.

**4** Come to the Lord. He is the living stone. People rejected him, but God chose him. God thinks that he is of great value.

**5** You also are living stones. God uses you to build a spiritual house. You are holy priests. You offer spiritual sacrifices that God accepts through Jesus Christ.

**6** The Scriptures say, "Look, I put a stone in Zion. It is the main cornerstone. I chose it, and it is of great value. The person who believes in him will never be ashamed."

**7** Thus for you who believe, this stone is of great value. But for the people who do not believe, the Scriptures say, "The builders rejected the stone. But the stone became the main cornerstone."

**8** The Scriptures also say, "The stone causes people to trip. It is a rock that causes people to fall." They trip because they do not obey the word of God. This is what God planned for them.

**9** But God chose you as his people. You are royal priests. You are a holy nation. You belong to God. God called you out of darkness into his wonderful light. Thus you must tell other people about the great things that he did.

**10** In the past, you were not a people. But now you are the people of God. In the past, you did not receive mercy. But now you received mercy.

**11** Dear friends, you are foreigners and travelers in this world. I ask you to keep away from the wrong desires of the body. These desires fight against your soul.

**12** Live a good life among the people who do not know God. They say that you do wrong. But they will see your good works. Then they will give glory to God on the day when he comes.

**13** Obey every human authority because of the Lord. Obey the king, because he has the highest authority.

**14** Obey the governors. The king sends them to punish people who do wrong and to praise people who do good.

**15** God wants you to do good. Then you will stop the foolish talk of people who do not know the truth.

**16** Live as free people. But do not use your freedom as an excuse to do evil. Live as servants of God.

**17** Respect all people. Love your fellow believers. Fear God. Respect the king.

**18** Slaves, obey your masters with all respect. Obey the masters who are good and kind. Also obey the masters who are cruel.

**19** Sometimes a person suffers when he did nothing wrong. If he continues firm in pain because he knows God, God is pleased.

**20** If people beat you because you do wrong, you get no praise when you accept it with patience. But if you do good and you suffer for it, you must accept it with patience. Then God is pleased.

**21** God called you to do this. Christ also suffered for you. He gave you an example. You must follow his steps.

**22** Christ did not sin. He never told a lie.

**23** People insulted Christ, but he did not insult them. He suffered, but he did not threaten anyone. He trusted God, who judges fairly.

**24** Christ himself took our sins in his body on the cross. He did this so that we will die to sin and live for what is right. His wounds healed you.

**25** You were like sheep that went the wrong way. But now you came back to the Shepherd and Guardian of your souls.

## Chapter 3

**1** In the same way, wives, obey your husbands. Some husbands do not obey the word of God. Then your behavior can bring them to God without words.

**2** They will see that your life is pure and that you respect God.

**3** Your beauty must not come from things outside your body. It must not come from the style of your hair, gold jewelry, or fine clothes.

**4** Your beauty must come from inside your heart. A gentle and quiet spirit is a beauty that does not fade. God thinks that this beauty is of great value.

**5** Holy women in the past made themselves beautiful in this way. They trusted God, and they obeyed their husbands.

**6** For example, Sarah obeyed Abraham and called him her master. You are her daughters if you do what is right. Do not let anything make you afraid.

**7** In the same way, husbands, live with your wives with understanding. Respect your wife, because she is weaker than you. Also, God gives the gift of life to her and to you together. Do this so that nothing will stop your prayers.

**8** Finally, all of you must agree with each other. Care for each other. Love each other as brothers. Be kind and humble.

**9** If people do evil to you, do not do evil to them. If people insult you, do not insult them. But bless them. God called you to do this, so that you will receive a blessing.

**10** The Scriptures say, "If a person wants to love life and see good days, he must stop his tongue from evil. He must not tell lies.

**11** He must turn away from evil and do good. He must look for peace and try hard to get it.

**12** The Lord watches over righteous people. He listens to their prayers. But the Lord is against the people who do evil."

**13** If you want to do good, nobody can really harm you.

**14** But if you suffer because you do what is right, God will bless you. Do not be afraid of what people fear. Do not be troubled.

**15** In your hearts, respect Christ as Lord. Always be ready to give an answer to each person who asks you about your hope. But be gentle and respectful.

**16** Keep your conscience clear. Then people who say evil things about your good life in Christ will be ashamed.

**17** If God wants you to suffer, it is better to suffer for good actions than for evil actions.

**18** Christ also suffered and died for sins 1 time for all time. He was righteous, but he died for people who are not righteous. He did this to bring you to God. People killed his body, but the Spirit gave him life.

**19** Through the Spirit, Christ also went and spoke to the spirits in prison.

**20** These spirits did not obey God in the past, in the time of Noah. God waited with patience while Noah built the large boat (ark). God saved only a few people through the water. He saved only 8 people.

**21** That water is an example of baptism, which now saves you. Baptism does not remove dirt from the body. Baptism is a promise to God from a clean conscience. Baptism saves you through the resurrection of Jesus Christ.

**22** Jesus went into heaven. He is at the right hand of God. All angels, authorities, and powers obey him.

## Chapter 4

**1** Christ suffered in his body. Thus you also must prepare yourselves to think as Christ thought. A person who suffers in the body stops sin.

**2** Thus he does not live the rest of his life on earth for wrong human desires. He lives to do what God wants.

**3** In the past, you spent enough time to do what the pagans like to do. You lived in sexual sin and in wrong desires. You got drunk. You went to wild parties and drinking parties. You worshipped idols, which God hates.

**4** Now the pagans are surprised that you do not join them in their wild life. Thus they say evil things about you.

**5** But they must give an account to God. He is ready to judge the living and the dead.

**6** Thus people also told the gospel to people who are now dead. People judged them in their bodies, as people judge all humans. But now they can live in the spirit, as God lives.

**7** The end of all things is near. Thus keep your minds clear and control yourselves. Then you can pray.

**8** Above all things, love each other deeply. Love covers many sins.

**9** Welcome each other into your homes and do not complain.

**10** God gave each of you a gift. Use your gift to serve each other. Use the many types of grace from God well, as good managers.

**11** If a person speaks, he must speak as if he speaks the words of God. If a person serves, he must serve with the strength that God gives. Then people will give glory to God in all things through Jesus Christ. Glory and power belong to Jesus Christ for all time. Amen.

**12** Dear friends, do not be surprised at the painful troubles that test you. Do not think that something strange happens to you.

**13** But be glad, because you share in the sufferings of Christ. Then you will be very glad when Christ shows his glory.

**14** If people insult you because of the name of Christ, God blesses you. The Spirit of glory, the Spirit of God, stays on you. Those people insult the Spirit, but you give glory to him.

**15** None of you must suffer because you killed a person, stole, or did evil. None of you must suffer because you interfered in the business of other people.

**16** But if you suffer because you are a Christian, do not be ashamed. Give glory to God because you have that name.

**17** It is time for God to start his judgment with his own people. If he starts with us, the end will be terrible for the people who do not obey the gospel of God.

**18** The Scriptures say, "It is hard for righteous people to be saved. Thus the end will be much worse for people who do not respect God and for sinners."

**19** Thus the people who suffer because God wants it must trust their souls to God. God made them, and he is faithful. They must continue to do good.

## Chapter 5

**1** I speak now to the elders among you. I am also an elder. I saw the sufferings of Christ. I will also share in the glory that Christ will show. I ask you elders,

**2** take care of the people of God that God put in your care. Watch over them like a shepherd watches over sheep. Do this because you want to do it, as God wants. Do not do it only because you must. Do not do it to get money. Do it because you are eager to serve.

**3** Do not act like a master over the people in your care. Be an example to them.

**4** The Chief Shepherd will come again. Then you will receive a crown of glory that will never fade.

**5** In the same way, you young men, obey the elders. All of you must be humble with each other. The Scriptures say, "God opposes proud people, but he gives grace to humble people."

**6** Thus make yourselves humble under the powerful hand of God. Then God will lift you up at the correct time.

**7** Give all your worries to God, because he cares for you.

**8** Control yourselves and be alert. Your enemy the devil goes around like a lion that roars. He looks for a person to destroy.

**9** Fight against the devil and stay firm in your faith. You know that your fellow believers in all the world suffer the same troubles.

**10** God gives all grace. He called you through Christ to share his eternal glory. You will suffer for a short time. After that, God himself will make you complete. He will make you strong, firm, and stable.

**11** All power belongs to God for all time. Amen.

**12** Silvanus helped me write this short letter to you. I think that he is a faithful brother. I write to encourage you. I tell you that this is the true grace of God. Stand firm in this grace.

**13** The church in Babylon sends greetings to you. God chose that church with you. My son Mark also sends greetings to you.

**14** Greet each other with a kiss of love. I pray for peace to all of you who are in Christ. Amen.
