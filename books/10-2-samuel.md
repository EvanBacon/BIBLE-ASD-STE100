# 2 Samuel

## Chapter 1

**1** Saul died. David came back after he killed the Amalekites. David stayed in Ziklag for 2 days.

**2** On the third day, a man came from the camp of Saul. His clothes were torn, and he had dirt on his head. He came to David and bowed down to the ground to show respect.

**3** David asked him, "Where do you come from?" The man said, "I escaped from the camp of Israel."

**4** David said to him, "Tell me what occurred." The man said, "The people ran away from the battle. Many of the people fell and died. Saul and his son Jonathan are dead too."

**5** David said to the young man, "How do you know that Saul and his son Jonathan are dead?"

**6** The young man said, "By chance I was on Mount Gilboa. Saul leaned on his spear. The chariots and the horsemen came near to him quickly.

**7** Saul looked behind him and saw me. He called to me, and I said, 'I am here.'

**8** He asked me, 'Who are you?' I said to him, 'I am an Amalekite.'

**9** He said to me, 'Stand over me and kill me. I have much pain, but I am still alive.'

**10** So I stood over him and killed him. I was sure that he could not live after he fell. I took the crown from his head and the band from his arm. I brought them here to you, my lord."

**11** Then David took hold of his clothes and tore them. All the men with him did the same.

**12** They mourned and cried. They did not eat until the evening. They mourned for Saul and for his son Jonathan. They mourned for the people of the LORD and for the nation of Israel, because the enemy killed them with the sword.

**13** David said to the young man, "Where do you come from?" The man said, "I am the son of a foreigner, an Amalekite."

**14** David said to him, "Why were you not afraid to lift your hand and kill the king that the LORD chose (anointed)?"

**15** David called one of his young men and said, "Go and kill him." The young man hit the Amalekite, and the Amalekite died.

**16** David said to the dead man, "You are responsible for your death. Your own words were evidence against you. You said, 'I killed the anointed king of the LORD.'"

**17** David sang this sad song about Saul and his son Jonathan.

**18** He told the people to teach the men of Judah this song, "The Bow." It is written in the Book of Jashar.

**19** "Israel, your glory lies dead on your high hills. The strong men fell!

**20** Do not tell the news in Gath. Do not tell the news in the streets of Ashkelon. If you tell it, the daughters of the Philistines will be happy. The daughters of the uncircumcised men will celebrate.

**21** Mountains of Gilboa, let no dew and no rain fall on you. Let no fields on you give crops for offerings. There the shield of the strong man lies dirty and thrown away. The shield of Saul has no oil on it now.

**22** The bow of Jonathan did not go back without blood from the enemy. The sword of Saul did not come back empty. They killed the strong men of the enemy.

**23** Saul and Jonathan were loved and kind in their lives. They did not separate in their death. They were faster than eagles. They were stronger than lions.

**24** Daughters of Israel, cry for Saul! He gave you fine red clothes. He put gold decorations on your clothes.

**25** The strong men fell in the battle! Jonathan lies dead on your high hills.

**26** I am very sad for you, my brother Jonathan. You were very good to me. Your love for me was wonderful. It was better than the love of women.

**27** The strong men fell! Their weapons of war are gone!"

## Chapter 2

**1** After this, David asked the LORD, "Must I go up to one of the cities of Judah?" The LORD said to him, "Go up." David asked, "Where must I go?" The LORD said, "Go to Hebron."

**2** So David went up to Hebron. His 2 wives went with him. They were Ahinoam from Jezreel and Abigail, the widow of Nabal from Carmel.

**3** David also brought the men who were with him. Each man brought his family. They lived in the towns near Hebron.

**4** The men of Judah came to Hebron. There they anointed David as king over the people of Judah. They told David, "The men of Jabesh Gilead buried Saul."

**5** David sent messengers to the men of Jabesh Gilead. The messengers said to them, "May the LORD bless you. You showed kindness to your lord Saul when you buried him.

**6** Now may the LORD show kindness and truth to you. I also will be good to you because you did this.

**7** Now be strong and brave. Your master Saul is dead. The people of Judah anointed me as their king."

**8** But Abner, the son of Ner, was the commander of the army of Saul. He took Ish-bosheth, the son of Saul, and brought him to Mahanaim.

**9** Abner made Ish-bosheth king over Gilead, the Ashurites, Jezreel, Ephraim, and Benjamin. Thus Ish-bosheth was king over all Israel.

**10** Ish-bosheth, the son of Saul, was 40 years old when he became king over Israel. He was king for 2 years. But the people of Judah followed David.

**11** David was king over the people of Judah in Hebron for 7 years and 6 months.

**12** Abner, the son of Ner, went from Mahanaim to Gibeon. The officers of Ish-bosheth, the son of Saul, went with him.

**13** Joab, the son of Zeruiah, and the officers of David also went out. The 2 groups met at the pool of Gibeon. One group sat on one side of the pool. The other group sat on the other side.

**14** Abner said to Joab, "Let some young men stand up and fight in front of us." Joab said, "Let them stand up."

**15** So 12 men from Benjamin stood up for Ish-bosheth, the son of Saul. 12 of the officers of David also stood up. They went across to fight.

**16** Each man took hold of the head of his enemy. Each man pushed his sword into the side of his enemy. Thus all of them fell down together. So the people called that place Helkath Hazzurim. It is in Gibeon.

**17** The battle that day was very bad. The men of David defeated Abner and the men of Israel.

**18** The 3 sons of Zeruiah were there: Joab, Abishai, and Asahel. Asahel could run as fast as a wild deer.

**19** Asahel ran after Abner. He did not turn to the right or to the left. He followed only Abner.

**20** Abner looked behind him and said, "Are you Asahel?" Asahel said, "Yes, I am."

**21** Abner said to him, "Turn to your right or to your left. Catch one of the young men and take his weapons." But Asahel did not stop. He continued to follow Abner.

**22** Again Abner said to Asahel, "Stop and do not follow me. I do not want to kill you. If I kill you, I cannot look at your brother Joab."

**23** But Asahel did not stop. So Abner pushed the back end of his spear into the stomach of Asahel. The spear came out of his back. Asahel fell down and died there. All the men who came to that place stopped and stood still.

**24** But Joab and Abishai continued to follow Abner. The sun went down when they came to the hill of Ammah. This hill is near Giah, on the road to the desert of Gibeon.

**25** The men of Benjamin came together behind Abner. They became one group and stood on the top of a hill.

**26** Abner called to Joab, "Must the sword kill forever? You know that the end will be bitter. When will you tell your men to stop the attack on their brothers?"

**27** Joab said, "As surely as God lives, if you did not speak, my men would follow their brothers until the morning."

**28** So Joab blew a trumpet. All his men stopped. They did not follow the men of Israel again, and they did not fight again.

**29** Abner and his men went through the Jordan Valley all that night. They went across the Jordan River and through all of Bithron. Then they came to Mahanaim.

**30** Joab stopped and came back from his attack on Abner. He brought all his men together. 19 of the men of David were missing. Asahel was also missing.

**31** But the men of David killed 360 men from Benjamin who were with Abner.

**32** The men of David took Asahel and buried him in the tomb of his father in Bethlehem. Joab and his men walked all night. They came to Hebron at sunrise.

## Chapter 3

**1** The war between the family of Saul and the family of David continued for a long time. David became stronger and stronger. The family of Saul became weaker and weaker.

**2** Sons were born to David in Hebron. His first son was Amnon. Amnon's mother was Ahinoam from Jezreel.

**3** His second son was Chileab. Chileab's mother was Abigail, the widow of Nabal from Carmel. The third son was Absalom. Absalom's mother was Maacah, the daughter of Talmai, the king of Geshur.

**4** The fourth son was Adonijah, the son of Haggith. The fifth son was Shephatiah, the son of Abital.

**5** The sixth son was Ithream. His mother was Eglah, the wife of David. These sons were born to David in Hebron.

**6** During the war between the family of Saul and the family of David, Abner made his own power large in the family of Saul.

**7** Saul had a secondary wife (concubine). Her name was Rizpah, the daughter of Aiah. Ish-bosheth said to Abner, "Why did you have sex with the concubine of my father?"

**8** Abner became very angry because of the words of Ish-bosheth. He said, "Do you think that I am a dog of Judah? Today I am loyal to the family of your father Saul, to his brothers, and to his friends. I did not give you to David. But today you accuse me of a wrong act with this woman!

**9** The LORD made a promise to David. Now I will help to make that promise come true. If I do not do this, may God punish me very severely.

**10** I will take the kingdom from the family of Saul. I will make David king over Israel and Judah, from Dan to Beersheba."

**11** Ish-bosheth could not say a word to Abner, because he was afraid of him.

**12** Abner sent messengers to David for him. Abner said, "Who controls the land? Make an agreement with me. Then I will help you. I will bring all Israel to you."

**13** David said, "Good. I will make an agreement with you. But I tell you to do 1 thing. When you come to see me, bring Michal, the daughter of Saul. If you do not bring her, do not come to see me."

**14** David sent messengers to Ish-bosheth, the son of Saul. David said, "Give me my wife Michal. I paid 100 foreskins of the Philistines to marry her."

**15** So Ish-bosheth sent men. They took Michal from her husband Paltiel, the son of Laish.

**16** Her husband went with her and cried behind her all the way to Bahurim. Then Abner said to him, "Go back home." So he went back.

**17** Abner spoke with the leaders (elders) of Israel. He said, "In the past you wanted David as your king.

**18** Now do it! The LORD spoke about David. The LORD said, 'I will use my servant David to save my people Israel from the Philistines and from all their enemies.'"

**19** Abner also spoke to the people of Benjamin. Then Abner went to Hebron to speak to David. He told David all that Israel and all the people of Benjamin wanted to do.

**20** Abner came to David at Hebron with 20 men. David made a feast for Abner and his men.

**21** Abner said to David, "I will go now and bring all Israel to you, my lord the king. They will make an agreement with you. Then you will rule over all that you want." So David sent Abner away, and Abner went in peace.

**22** Then the men of David and Joab came back from an attack. They brought many valuable things with them. Abner was not with David in Hebron, because David sent him away in peace.

**23** Joab and all his army arrived. The people told Joab, "Abner, the son of Ner, came to the king. The king sent him away, and Abner went in peace."

**24** Then Joab went to the king and said, "What did you do? Abner came to you. Why did you let him go?

**25** You know Abner, the son of Ner! He came to trick you. He came to learn about all your movements and all that you do."

**26** Joab left David and sent messengers after Abner. They brought Abner back from the well of Sirah. But David did not know about it.

**27** Abner came back to Hebron. Joab took him to the side of the gate to speak with him quietly. There Joab pushed a knife into the stomach of Abner, and Abner died. Joab did this because Abner killed his brother Asahel.

**28** Later, David heard about it. He said, "I and my kingdom are not guilty of the blood of Abner, the son of Ner. The LORD knows this forever.

**29** May the guilt fall on Joab and on all his family. May the family of Joab always have a man with a sore, or a man with a skin disease. May it always have a man who needs a stick to walk, or a man who dies by the sword, or a man who has no food."

**30** Joab and his brother Abishai killed Abner because Abner killed their brother Asahel in the battle at Gibeon.

**31** David said to Joab and to all the people with him, "Tear your clothes. Put on rough cloth (sackcloth). Mourn for Abner." King David walked behind the body of Abner.

**32** They buried Abner in Hebron. The king cried loudly at the grave of Abner, and all the people cried.

**33** The king sang this sad song about Abner: "Must Abner die like a fool dies?

**34** No one tied your hands. No one put chains on your feet. You fell as a man falls in front of evil men." And all the people cried again for Abner.

**35** All the people came to David to make him eat food during the day. But David made a promise. He said, "May God punish me very severely if I eat bread or other food before the sun goes down."

**36** All the people saw this, and they were pleased. All that the king did pleased the people.

**37** That day all the people and all Israel knew that the king did not cause the death of Abner, the son of Ner.

**38** The king said to his officers, "You know that a great leader died today in Israel.

**39** Today I am weak, although I am the anointed king. These sons of Zeruiah are too strong for me. May the LORD punish the evil man as his evil deserves."

## Chapter 4

**1** Ish-bosheth, the son of Saul, heard that Abner died in Hebron. Then he lost his courage, and all the people of Israel became afraid.

**2** The son of Saul had 2 men who were captains of raid groups. Their names were Baanah and Rechab. They were the sons of Rimmon from Beeroth, of the tribe of Benjamin. The people thought that Beeroth was a part of Benjamin.

**3** The people of Beeroth ran away to Gittaim. They still live there as foreigners today.

**4** Jonathan, the son of Saul, had a son who could not walk well. The news about Saul and Jonathan came from Jezreel when the boy was 5 years old. His nurse picked him up and ran away. She ran quickly, and the boy fell. After that, he could not walk well. His name was Mephibosheth.

**5** Rechab and Baanah, the sons of Rimmon from Beeroth, went to the house of Ish-bosheth. They arrived in the hottest part of the day. Ish-bosheth was on his bed at noon.

**6** They went into the house as if they wanted to get wheat. They pushed a knife into the stomach of Ish-bosheth. Then Rechab and his brother Baanah escaped.

**7** When they went into the house, Ish-bosheth was on his bed in his bedroom. They hit him and killed him. They cut off his head and took it with them. They traveled all night on the road through the Jordan Valley.

**8** They brought the head of Ish-bosheth to David at Hebron. They said to the king, "Here is the head of Ish-bosheth, the son of Saul. He was your enemy, and he tried to kill you. Today the LORD punished Saul and his family for you, my lord the king."

**9** David spoke to Rechab and his brother Baanah, the sons of Rimmon from Beeroth. He said, "The LORD saved me from all trouble. As surely as the LORD lives, I tell you this:

**10** A man told me, 'Saul is dead.' He thought that his news was good. But I took hold of him and killed him in Ziklag. That was the reward that I gave him for his news.

**11** You are evil men. You killed a good man in his own house on his bed! Thus I will punish you more severely. I will make you pay for his death. I will remove you from the earth!"

**12** David gave an order to his young men, and they killed Rechab and Baanah. They cut off their hands and their feet. They hung the bodies near the pool in Hebron. But they took the head of Ish-bosheth and buried it in the tomb of Abner in Hebron.

## Chapter 5

**1** Then all the tribes of Israel came to David at Hebron. They said, "We are your own family.

**2** In the past, Saul was our king. But you led the army of Israel in battle and brought it back. The LORD said to you, 'You will take care of my people Israel like a shepherd. You will be the leader of Israel.'"

**3** So all the leaders (elders) of Israel came to King David at Hebron. The king made an agreement with them in Hebron in front of the LORD. They anointed David as king over Israel.

**4** David was 30 years old when he became king. He ruled for 40 years.

**5** In Hebron he ruled over Judah for 7 years and 6 months. In Jerusalem he ruled over all Israel and Judah for 33 years.

**6** The king and his men went to Jerusalem to attack the Jebusites who lived there. The Jebusites said to David, "You will not come in here. Even the blind men and the lame men can stop you." They thought, "David cannot come in here."

**7** But David captured the fortress of Zion. It is now the City of David.

**8** On that day David said, "To attack the Jebusites, go up through the water tunnel. Attack those 'lame and blind' enemies that I hate." Thus the people say, "The blind and the lame will not come into the house."

**9** David lived in the fortress and called it the City of David. David built walls around the city, from the Millo inward.

**10** David became more and more powerful, because the LORD God of armies was with him.

**11** Hiram, the king of Tyre, sent messengers to David. He also sent cedar trees, carpenters, and stone workers. They built a palace for David.

**12** Then David knew that the LORD made him king over Israel. He knew that the LORD made his kingdom great for the people of Israel.

**13** After David came from Hebron, he took more concubines and wives in Jerusalem. More sons and daughters were born to him.

**14** These are the names of the sons born to him in Jerusalem: Shammua, Shobab, Nathan, Solomon,

**15** Ibhar, Elishua, Nepheg, Japhia,

**16** Elishama, Eliada, and Eliphelet.

**17** The Philistines heard that the people anointed David as king over Israel. So all the Philistines went to find David. David heard about it and went down to the fortress.

**18** The Philistines came and spread out in the Valley of Rephaim.

**19** David asked the LORD, "Must I attack the Philistines? Will you give them to me?" The LORD said to David, "Attack them. I will surely give the Philistines to you."

**20** So David went to Baal Perazim, and he defeated the Philistines there. He said, "The LORD broke through my enemies in front of me as a flood of water breaks through." Thus he called that place Baal Perazim.

**21** The Philistines left their idols there. David and his men burned the idols.

**22** The Philistines came again. They spread out in the Valley of Rephaim.

**23** David asked the LORD again. The LORD said, "Do not attack them from the front. Go around behind them. Attack them in front of the balsam trees.

**24** You will hear a sound like soldiers that march in the tops of the balsam trees. Then attack quickly. At that time the LORD will go in front of you to defeat the army of the Philistines."

**25** David did as the LORD told him. He defeated the Philistines from Geba to Gezer.

## Chapter 6

**1** Again David brought together all the best men of Israel. There were 30,000 men.

**2** David and all his men went to Baalah in Judah. They went to bring the ark of God from there to Jerusalem. The ark (the sacred box) has the name of the LORD of armies on it. The LORD sits on his throne between the cherubim on the ark.

**3** They put the ark of God on a new cart. They took it from the house of Abinadab on the hill. Uzzah and Ahio, the sons of Abinadab, drove the new cart.

**4** They brought the ark of God from the house of Abinadab on the hill. Ahio walked in front of the ark.

**5** David and all the people of Israel celebrated in front of the LORD. They played all types of instruments made of pine wood. They played lyres, harps, tambourines, rattles, and cymbals.

**6** They came to the threshing floor of Nacon. There the oxen stumbled. So Uzzah put out his hand and took hold of the ark of God.

**7** The LORD became angry with Uzzah. God killed him there because of his mistake. Uzzah died there near the ark of God.

**8** David was angry because the LORD killed Uzzah suddenly. David called that place Perez Uzzah. It has that name today.

**9** That day David was afraid of the LORD. He said, "How can the ark of the LORD come to me?"

**10** So David did not take the ark of the LORD into the City of David. He took it to the house of Obed-Edom from Gath.

**11** The ark of the LORD stayed in the house of Obed-Edom from Gath for 3 months. The LORD blessed Obed-Edom and all his family.

**12** People told King David, "The LORD blessed the family of Obed-Edom and all that he has because of the ark of God." So David went and brought the ark of God from the house of Obed-Edom to the City of David. He was very happy.

**13** The men who carried the ark of the LORD walked 6 steps. Then David sacrificed a bull and a fat calf.

**14** David danced in front of the LORD with all his strength. He wore a linen apron (ephod).

**15** David and all the people of Israel brought up the ark of the LORD. They shouted and blew trumpets.

**16** The ark of the LORD came into the City of David. Michal, the daughter of Saul, looked through a window. She saw King David as he jumped and danced in front of the LORD. Then she hated him in her heart.

**17** They brought in the ark of the LORD. They put it in its place in the tent that David put up for it. David gave burnt offerings and peace offerings in front of the LORD.

**18** David finished the burnt offerings and the peace offerings. Then he blessed the people in the name of the LORD of armies.

**19** He gave food to all the people of Israel, to the men and to the women. He gave each person a loaf of bread, a good piece of meat, and a container of wine. Then all the people went back to their homes.

**20** David went home to bless his family. Michal, the daughter of Saul, came out to meet him. She said, "Today the king of Israel did not act with honor! Today he took off his clothes in front of the slave girls of his officers. He acted like a foolish man with no shame!"

**21** David said to Michal, "I did it in front of the LORD. The LORD chose me instead of your father and all his family. He made me ruler over the people of the LORD, over Israel. Thus I will celebrate in front of the LORD.

**22** I will make myself even less important than this. I will be humble in my own eyes. But the slave girls that you spoke about will give me honor."

**23** Michal, the daughter of Saul, had no children all her life.

## Chapter 7

**1** King David lived in his palace. The LORD gave him peace from all the enemies around him.

**2** Then the king said to Nathan the prophet, "Look! I live in a palace of cedar wood. But the ark of God stays in a tent."

**3** Nathan said to the king, "Go and do all that you want to do. The LORD is with you."

**4** But that night the LORD spoke to Nathan. He said,

**5** "Go and tell my servant David, 'The LORD says this: You will not build a house for me to live in.

**6** I did not live in a house from the day that I brought the people of Israel out of Egypt until today. I moved from place to place in a tent (tabernacle).

**7** I moved with all the people of Israel to many places. I told leaders of the tribes to take care of my people Israel like shepherds. I did not ask one of them, "Why did you not build a house of cedar for me?"'

**8** Now tell my servant David, 'The LORD of armies says this: I took you from the field where you took care of the sheep. I made you the leader of my people Israel.

**9** I was with you in all the places where you went. I destroyed all your enemies in front of you. I will make your name great, as the names of the great men on the earth.

**10** I will make a place for my people Israel. I will put them there like a tree in the ground. They will live in their own place, and they will not move again. Evil people will not hurt them again as before.

**11** In the past I put judges over my people Israel. But I will give you peace from all your enemies. The LORD tells you that he will make a family of kings from you.

**12** Your life will end, and you will die and be buried with your ancestors. Then I will make one of your own sons king after you. I will make his kingdom strong.

**13** He will build a house for my name. I will make the throne of his kingdom strong forever.

**14** I will be his father, and he will be my son. If he does wrong, I will punish him as a father punishes his son.

**15** But I will not stop my love for him. I took my love from Saul, and I removed Saul from your path. But I will not do that to your son.

**16** Your family and your kingdom will continue forever in front of me. Your throne will continue forever.'"

**17** Nathan told David all these words and all that God showed him.

**18** Then King David went in and sat in front of the LORD. He said, "Lord GOD, who am I? My family is not important. But you brought me to this place.

**19** Lord GOD, you think that this is a small thing. So you also spoke about the future of my family for a long time to come. Lord GOD, this is not usual for a man!

**20** What more can I, David, say to you? Lord GOD, you know me, your servant.

**21** You did all these great things because of your word and because you wanted to do them. You told me, your servant, about them.

**22** Lord GOD, you are great! No one is like you. There is no God but you. We heard all this with our own ears.

**23** No other nation on earth is like your people Israel. God went to free them, and he made them his own people. He made his name famous. He did great and wonderful things for them. He made other nations and their gods go out in front of your people. You freed your people from Egypt for yourself.

**24** You made your people Israel your own people forever. And you, LORD, became their God.

**25** Now, LORD God, keep your promise about me, your servant, and about my family forever. Do as you said.

**26** Then your name will be great forever. People will say, 'The LORD of armies is God over Israel!' Then the family of your servant David will continue strong in front of you.

**27** LORD of armies, God of Israel, you showed this to me, your servant. You said, 'I will build a family of kings from you.' Thus I, your servant, have the courage to pray this prayer to you.

**28** Lord GOD, you are God! Your words are true. You promised these good things to me, your servant.

**29** Now please bless the family of your servant. Let it continue forever in front of you. Lord GOD, you spoke these words. With your blessing, the family of your servant will have blessing forever."

## Chapter 8

**1** After this, David defeated the Philistines and made them weak. He took control of Metheg Ammah from the Philistines.

**2** David also defeated the people of Moab. He made the men lie down on the ground in rows. He measured them with a rope. He killed the men in 2 lengths of rope. He let the men in 1 length of rope live. So the people of Moab became servants of David and paid money (tribute) to him.

**3** David also defeated Hadadezer, the son of Rehob, the king of Zobah. Hadadezer went to take back control of the land near the Euphrates River.

**4** David captured 1,000 chariots, 700 horsemen, and 20,000 foot soldiers from him. David cut the leg tendons of all the chariot horses. But he kept enough horses for 100 chariots.

**5** The Syrians from Damascus came to help Hadadezer, the king of Zobah. But David killed 22,000 of the Syrians.

**6** Then David put groups of soldiers in Syria near Damascus. The Syrians became servants of David and paid money (tribute) to him. The LORD gave David victory in all the places where he went.

**7** David took the gold shields of the officers of Hadadezer. He brought them to Jerusalem.

**8** King David also took very much bronze from Betah and Berothai. These were cities of Hadadezer.

**9** Toi, the king of Hamath, heard that David defeated all the army of Hadadezer.

**10** So Toi sent his son Joram to King David. Joram greeted David and congratulated him because David fought against Hadadezer and defeated him. Hadadezer was often at war with Toi. Joram brought with him objects of silver, gold, and bronze.

**11** King David gave these objects to the LORD. He also gave to the LORD the silver and gold from all the nations that he defeated.

**12** He took these from Syria, Moab, the Ammonites, the Philistines, and Amalek. He also took valuable things from Hadadezer, the son of Rehob, the king of Zobah.

**13** David became famous when he came back. He killed 18,000 Syrians in the Valley of Salt.

**14** He put groups of soldiers in all of Edom. All the people of Edom became servants of David. The LORD gave David victory in all the places where he went.

**15** David ruled over all Israel. He did what was right and fair for all his people.

**16** Joab, the son of Zeruiah, was the commander of the army. Jehoshaphat, the son of Ahilud, wrote the records of the kingdom.

**17** Zadok, the son of Ahitub, and Ahimelech, the son of Abiathar, were the priests. Seraiah was the secretary.

**18** Benaiah, the son of Jehoiada, was the commander of the Kerethites and the Pelethites. The sons of David were chief officials.

## Chapter 9

## Chapter 10

**1** After this, the king of the Ammonites died. His son Hanun became king after him.

**2** David said, "I will be kind to Hanun, the son of Nahash, because his father was kind to me." So David sent some of his officers to comfort Hanun about the death of his father. The officers of David came into the land of the Ammonites.

**3** But the leaders of the Ammonites said to their lord Hanun, "Do you think that David honors your father because he sent men to comfort you? No! David sent his officers to look at the city. They will spy on it and then destroy it."

**4** So Hanun took the officers of David. He shaved off half of the beard of each man. He cut off their clothes in the middle, at the hips. Then he sent them away.

**5** People told David about this. He sent messengers to meet the men, because the men were very ashamed. The king said, "Stay at Jericho until your beards grow again. Then come back."

**6** The Ammonites knew that David hated them now. So the Ammonites hired 20,000 foot soldiers from the Syrians of Beth Rehob and Zobah. They also hired 1,000 men from the king of Maacah and 12,000 men from Tob.

**7** David heard about it. He sent Joab with all the army of strong soldiers.

**8** The Ammonites came out and got ready for battle at the entrance of the city gate. The Syrians of Zobah and Rehob, and the men of Tob and Maacah, stayed by themselves in the open field.

**9** Joab saw that the enemy was in front of him and behind him. So he chose some of the best soldiers of Israel. He put them in position against the Syrians.

**10** He gave the other men to his brother Abishai. Abishai put them in position against the Ammonites.

**11** Joab said, "If the Syrians are too strong for me, then you must help me. If the Ammonites are too strong for you, then I will come and help you.

**12** Be strong! Let us fight bravely for our people and for the cities of our God. The LORD will do what he thinks is good."

**13** Joab and his men went near to attack the Syrians. The Syrians ran away from him.

**14** The Ammonites saw that the Syrians ran away. So they also ran away from Abishai and went into the city. Then Joab stopped the attack on the Ammonites and went back to Jerusalem.

**15** The Syrians saw that Israel defeated them. So they came together again.

**16** Hadadezer sent messengers and brought the Syrians from the other side of the Euphrates River. They came to Helam. Shobach, the commander of the army of Hadadezer, led them.

**17** People told David about this. He brought all Israel together, went across the Jordan River, and came to Helam. The Syrians got ready for battle against David and fought with him.

**18** But the Syrians ran away from Israel. David killed the men of 700 Syrian chariots and 40,000 horsemen. He also attacked Shobach, the commander of their army, and Shobach died there.

**19** All the kings who served Hadadezer saw that Israel defeated them. So they made peace with Israel and became servants of Israel. After that, the Syrians were afraid to help the Ammonites again.

**3** David sent someone to find out about the woman. The man said, "She is Bathsheba, the daughter of Eliam. She is the wife of Uriah the Hittite."

## Chapter 12

**1** The LORD sent Nathan to David. Nathan came to David and said, "There were 2 men in a city. One man was rich, and the other man was poor.

**2** The rich man had very many sheep and cattle.

**3** But the poor man had nothing, only 1 small female lamb. He bought it and took care of it. It grew up with him and his children. It ate from his food and drank from his cup. It slept in his arms. It was like a daughter to him.

**4** A traveler came to the rich man. The rich man did not want to take one of his own sheep or cattle to make a meal for the traveler. So he took the lamb of the poor man. He cooked it for his guest."

**5** David became very angry with the rich man. He said to Nathan, "As surely as the LORD lives, the man who did this must die!

**6** He must pay back 4 lambs for that lamb, because he did this and had no pity."

**7** Nathan said to David, "You are that man! The LORD God of Israel says this: 'I anointed you king over Israel. I saved you from Saul.

**8** I gave you the house of your master and the wives of your master. I gave you the people of Israel and Judah. If that was not enough, I would give you much more.

**9** Why did you show no respect for the command of the LORD? Why did you do this evil thing in front of him? You killed Uriah the Hittite with the sword. You took his wife as your wife. You used the sword of the Ammonites to kill him.

**10** Thus the sword will never go away from your family. You showed no respect for me. You took the wife of Uriah the Hittite as your wife.'

**11** The LORD says this: 'I will cause trouble against you from your own family. In front of your eyes, I will take your wives and give them to another man. He will have sex with your wives in the daylight.

**12** You did your sin secretly. But I will do this in front of all Israel, in the daylight.'"

**13** David said to Nathan, "I sinned against the LORD." Nathan said to David, "The LORD forgives your sin. You will not die.

**14** But you did this, and thus you caused the enemies of the LORD to insult him. So the son who was born to you will surely die."

**15** Then Nathan went home. The LORD caused the child that the wife of Uriah had by David to become very sick.

**16** David prayed to God for the child. He did not eat. He went into his room and lay on the ground all night.

**17** The leaders of his household came to him. They tried to lift him up from the ground. But he did not agree, and he did not eat food with them.

**18** On the seventh day, the child died. The officers of David were afraid to tell him that the child was dead. They said, "When the child was still alive, we spoke to David. But he did not listen to us. If we tell him that the child is dead, he will possibly hurt himself."

**19** But David saw that his officers whispered to each other. So he knew that the child was dead. David asked his officers, "Is the child dead?" They said, "Yes, he is dead."

**20** Then David got up from the ground. He washed, put on oil, and changed his clothes. He went into the house of the LORD and worshipped. Then he went to his own house. He asked for food, and they gave him food. He ate.

**21** His officers said to him, "Why do you do this? When the child was alive, you did not eat and you cried. But now the child is dead, and you get up and eat food!"

**22** David said, "When the child was still alive, I did not eat and I cried. I thought, 'Possibly the LORD will be kind to me and let the child live.'

**23** But now the child is dead. Why must I continue to fast? I cannot bring him back. I will go to him, but he will not come back to me."

**24** Then David comforted his wife Bathsheba. He went to her and had sex with her. She had a son, and David gave him the name Solomon. The LORD loved the child.

**25** The LORD sent a message through Nathan the prophet. Nathan gave the child the name Jedidiah, because the LORD loved him.

**26** Joab fought against Rabbah of the Ammonites. He captured the royal fortress.

**27** Joab sent messengers to David. He said, "I fought against Rabbah. I captured the water supply of the city.

**28** Now bring together the rest of the army. Attack the city and capture it. If you do not, I will capture the city. Then people will call the city by my name."

**29** So David brought together all the army and went to Rabbah. He fought against the city and captured it.

**30** David took the crown from the head of their king. The crown was made of gold and had valuable stones on it. It weighed about 34 kilograms. The people put the crown on the head of David. David also took very many valuable things from the city.

**31** David brought out the people of the city. He made them work with saws, iron picks, and iron axes. He made them work at the brick ovens. David did the same to all the cities of the Ammonites. Then David and all the army went back to Jerusalem.

## Chapter 13

**1** After this, Amnon, the son of David, loved Tamar. Tamar was the beautiful sister of Absalom, another son of David.

**2** Amnon wanted Tamar so much that he became sick. She was a virgin. Amnon thought that he could not do anything to her.

**3** Amnon had a friend with the name Jonadab. Jonadab was the son of Shimeah, the brother of David. Jonadab was a very clever man.

**4** Jonadab said to Amnon, "You are the son of the king. Why do you become thinner each day? Tell me." Amnon said to him, "I love Tamar, the sister of my brother Absalom."

**5** Jonadab said to him, "Lie down on your bed and act as if you are sick. Your father will come to see you. Then say to him, 'Please let my sister Tamar come and give me food. Let her make the food in front of me. Then I can see it and eat it from her hand.'"

**6** So Amnon lay down and acted as if he was sick. The king came to see him. Amnon said to the king, "Please let my sister Tamar come. Let her make 2 cakes in front of me. Then I can eat from her hand."

**7** David sent a message to Tamar at the palace. He said, "Go to the house of your brother Amnon and make food for him."

**8** So Tamar went to the house of her brother Amnon. He was in bed. She took flour and mixed it. She made cakes in front of him and cooked them.

**9** She took the pan and put the cakes in front of him. But he did not eat. Amnon said, "Send all the people out of here." So all the people went out.

**10** Then Amnon said to Tamar, "Bring the food into the bedroom. Then I can eat from your hand." Tamar took the cakes that she made. She brought them into the bedroom to her brother Amnon.

**11** She brought the cakes to him to eat. But he took hold of her and said, "My sister, come and have sex with me."

**12** She said to him, "No, my brother! Do not force me! People must not do this in Israel. Do not do this foolish act!

**13** Where can I go with my shame? And you will be like one of the foolish men in Israel. Please speak to the king. He will let you marry me."

**14** But Amnon did not listen to her. He was stronger than she was. He forced her and raped her.

**15** Then Amnon hated her very much. His hate for her was greater than his love for her before. Amnon said to her, "Get up and go away!"

**16** She said to him, "No! If you send me away, that is a worse wrong than the wrong that you did to me." But he did not listen to her.

**17** He called his young servant and said, "Put this woman out of my room. Lock the door behind her."

**18** Tamar wore a long robe with sleeves. The virgin daughters of the king wore robes of this type. The servant put her out and locked the door behind her.

**19** Tamar put ashes on her head. She tore the long robe that she wore. She put her hand on her head and went away. She cried loudly as she went.

**20** Her brother Absalom said to her, "Did your brother Amnon do this to you? Now be quiet, my sister. He is your brother. Do not let this trouble you." So Tamar lived in the house of her brother Absalom. She was alone and very sad.

**21** King David heard about all these things. He became very angry.

**22** Absalom did not speak to his brother Amnon, good or bad. Absalom hated Amnon because Amnon raped his sister Tamar.

**23** After 2 full years, Absalom had men who cut the wool from his sheep in Baal Hazor, near Ephraim. Absalom invited all the sons of the king.

**24** Absalom went to the king and said, "Your servant has men who cut the wool from the sheep. Please let the king and his officers come with me."

**25** The king said to Absalom, "No, my son. We will not all go. We will be too much trouble for you." Absalom asked him again and again. But the king did not go. He blessed Absalom.

**26** Then Absalom said, "If you do not go, please let my brother Amnon go with us." The king said to him, "Why must he go with you?"

**27** But Absalom asked again and again. So the king let Amnon and all the sons of the king go with Absalom.

**28** Absalom gave an order to his servants. He said, "Watch Amnon. When he is happy from the wine, I will say to you, 'Attack Amnon!' Then kill him. Do not be afraid. I gave you the order. Be strong and brave."

**29** So the servants of Absalom did to Amnon as Absalom told them. Then all the other sons of the king got up. Each man got on his mule and ran away.

**31** The king got up and tore his clothes. He lay on the ground. All his officers stood near him with their clothes torn.

**32** But Jonadab, the son of Shimeah, the brother of David, spoke. He said, "My lord, do not think that they killed all the young men, the sons of the king. Only Amnon is dead. Absalom made this plan on the day that Amnon raped his sister Tamar.

**33** My lord the king, do not believe the news that all the sons of the king are dead. Only Amnon is dead."

**34** But Absalom ran away. The young man who was the guard looked up. He saw many people who came on the road from the west, along the side of the hill.

**35** Jonadab said to the king, "Look, the sons of the king come! It is as I, your servant, said."

**36** When he finished speaking, the sons of the king arrived. They cried loudly. The king and all his officers also cried very much.

**37** But Absalom ran away. He went to Talmai, the son of Ammihud, the king of Geshur. David mourned for his son every day.

**38** Absalom ran away to Geshur. He stayed there for 3 years.

**39** Then King David wanted very much to go to Absalom. He stopped his sadness about the death of Amnon.

## Chapter 18

**2** David sent out the army in 3 groups. Joab led 1 group. Abishai, the son of Zeruiah and the brother of Joab, led 1 group. Ittai from Gath led 1 group. The king said to the army, "I will surely go out with you too."

**11** Joab said to the man, "You saw him! Why did you not kill him there? I would give you 10 pieces of silver and a belt."

**13** If I killed him, the king would know. Nothing is hidden from the king. And you would not help me."

**16** Then Joab blew the trumpet. The army stopped the attack on Israel, because Joab stopped them.

## Chapter 19

**1** People told Joab, "The king cries and mourns for Absalom."

## Chapter 21

**1** During the rule of David, there was a famine for 3 years, one year after another. David asked the LORD about it. The LORD said, "The famine is because of Saul and his family of murderers. Saul killed the Gibeonites."

**2** The Gibeonites were not Israelites. They were the last of the Amorites. The Israelites made a promise to protect them. But Saul tried to kill them because he was too eager to help Israel and Judah. So King David called the Gibeonites and spoke to them.

**3** David said to the Gibeonites, "What can I do for you? How can I make payment (atonement) for the wrong? I want you to bless the people of the LORD."

**4** The Gibeonites said to him, "We do not want silver or gold from Saul or his family. And we do not want to kill a man in Israel." David said, "I will do for you what you ask."

**5** They said to the king, "Saul tried to destroy us. He made plans to kill all of us, so that we cannot live in any part of Israel.

**6** Give us 7 of his male descendants. We will hang them in front of the LORD at Gibeah of Saul, the king that the LORD chose." The king said, "I will give them to you."

**7** But the king protected Mephibosheth, the son of Jonathan, the son of Saul. David did this because of the promise in the name of the LORD between David and Jonathan, the son of Saul.

**8** The king took Armoni and Mephibosheth, the 2 sons of Rizpah, the daughter of Aiah. Rizpah had them by Saul. The king also took the 5 sons of Michal, the daughter of Saul. Michal brought them up for Adriel, the son of Barzillai from Meholah.

**9** David gave them to the Gibeonites. The Gibeonites hanged them on the hill in front of the LORD. All 7 of them died together. They died at the start of the barley harvest.

**10** Rizpah, the daughter of Aiah, took a rough cloth (sackcloth) and put it on the rock for herself. She stayed there from the start of the harvest until the rain fell on the bodies. She did not let the birds land on them during the day. She did not let the wild animals come near at night.

**11** People told David what Rizpah, the daughter of Aiah and the concubine of Saul, did.

**12** Then David went to the men of Jabesh Gilead. He took the bones of Saul and his son Jonathan from them. The men of Jabesh Gilead took the bones secretly from the public square of Beth Shan. The Philistines hung the bodies there after they killed Saul on Gilboa.

**13** David brought the bones of Saul and his son Jonathan from there. The people also collected the bones of the 7 men that the Gibeonites hanged.

**14** They buried the bones of Saul and his son Jonathan in the tomb of Kish, the father of Saul. The tomb is in Zela, in the land of Benjamin. They did all that the king told them. After that, God answered their prayers for the land.

**15** There was war again between the Philistines and Israel. David and his officers went down and fought against the Philistines. David became very tired.

**16** Ishbi-Benob was one of the descendants of the giants. His bronze spear weighed 300 shekels, about 3.5 kilograms. He also had a new sword. He thought that he could kill David.

**17** But Abishai, the son of Zeruiah, helped David. He attacked the Philistine and killed him. Then the men of David made a promise. They said to him, "You will not go out to battle with us again. If you die, the light of Israel will go out."

**18** After this, there was another battle with the Philistines at Gob. There Sibbecai the Hushathite killed Saph. Saph was one of the descendants of the giants.

**19** There was another battle with the Philistines at Gob. There Elhanan, the son of Jaare-Oregim from Bethlehem, killed the brother of Goliath from Gath. The shaft of the spear of Goliath was as thick as the beam of a weaver.

**20** There was another battle at Gath. A very tall man was there. He had 6 fingers on each hand and 6 toes on each foot. He had 24 fingers and toes in total. He was also a descendant of the giants.

**21** He insulted Israel. So Jonathan, the son of Shimea, the brother of David, killed him.

**22** These 4 men were descendants of the giants in Gath. David and his officers killed them.

## Chapter 22

**1** The LORD saved David from all his enemies and from Saul. On that day David sang the words of this song to the LORD.

**2** He said, "The LORD is my rock and my fortress. He is the one who saves me.

**3** My God is my rock. I trust him for protection. He is my shield and my strong savior. He is my high tower and my safe place. He is my savior. You save me from violent people.

**4** I call to the LORD, who deserves praise. Then he saves me from my enemies.

**5** Death was all around me like waves. Evil people came against me like a flood and made me afraid.

**6** The ropes of the grave (Sheol) were all around me. Death put traps in front of me.

**7** In my trouble I called to the LORD. I called to my God. He heard my voice from his temple. My cry came to his ears.

**8** Then the earth shook and moved. The foundations of the sky shook, because God was angry.

**9** Smoke went up from his nose. Fire that destroys came out of his mouth. Hot coals burned from it.

**10** He opened the sky and came down. Dark clouds were under his feet.

**11** He rode on a cherub and flew. People saw him on the wings of the wind.

**12** He put darkness around him like a tent. He put dark rain clouds and thick clouds of the sky around him.

**13** From the bright light in front of him, coals of fire burned.

**14** The LORD made thunder from the sky. The Most High God made his voice loud.

**15** He shot arrows and sent the enemies in all directions. He sent lightning and made them run away in fear.

**16** The LORD spoke angrily, and a strong wind blew from his nose. Then the bottom of the sea became visible. The foundations of the world became visible.

**17** He reached down from above and took hold of me. He pulled me out of the deep water.

**18** He saved me from my strong enemy. He saved me from the people who hated me. They were too strong for me.

**19** They attacked me on the day of my trouble. But the LORD supported me.

**20** He brought me out to a safe, open place. He saved me because he was pleased with me.

**21** The LORD gave me a reward because I did what was right. He gave me a reward because I did no wrong.

**22** I obeyed the laws of the LORD. I did not do evil and turn away from my God.

**23** I kept all his laws in front of me. I did not disobey his rules.

**24** I was honest in front of him. I kept myself away from sin.

**25** Thus the LORD gave me a reward because I did what was right. He saw that I was clean.

**26** You are loyal to the people who are loyal. You are honest with the people who are honest.

**27** You are pure with the people who are pure. But you are clever against the people who are evil.

**28** You save humble people. But you watch the proud people, and you make them low.

**29** LORD, you are my lamp. The LORD gives light to my darkness.

**30** With your help I can attack an army. With the help of my God I can jump over a wall.

**31** The way of God is perfect. The word of the LORD is true. He is a shield for all the people who trust him.

**32** Only the LORD is God. Only our God is a rock.

**33** God is my strong fortress. He makes my path safe.

**34** He makes my feet as fast as the feet of a deer. He lets me stand on high places.

**35** He teaches my hands to fight. My arms can bend a bow of bronze.

**36** You gave me the shield of your salvation. Your help made me great.

**37** You made a wide path for my feet. My feet did not slip.

**38** I chased my enemies and destroyed them. I did not come back until I killed them.

**39** I destroyed them and wounded them. They could not stand up. They fell under my feet.

**40** You gave me strength for the battle. You made my enemies bow down in front of me.

**41** You made my enemies turn and run away. I destroyed the people who hated me.

**42** They looked for help, but no one saved them. They called to the LORD, but he did not answer them.

**43** I crushed them like the dust of the ground. I walked on them like the mud in the streets. I spread them out.

**44** You saved me when my own people attacked me. You kept me as the leader of the nations. People that I did not know serve me.

**45** Foreigners bow down to me. When they hear about me, they obey me.

**46** Foreigners lose their courage. They come out of their fortresses and shake with fear.

**47** The LORD lives! Praise my rock! Praise God, the rock who saves me!

**48** God punishes my enemies for me. He makes the nations obey me.

**49** He saves me from my enemies. You lift me up above the people who attack me. You save me from violent men.

**50** Thus I will thank you, LORD, among the nations. I will sing praises to your name.

**51** God gives great victories to his king. He shows his love to his anointed king, to David and his descendants forever."

## Chapter 23

**1** These are the last words of David. David was the son of Jesse. God made him great. The God of Jacob anointed him. He was the pleasant writer of the songs of Israel. David said this:

**2** "The Spirit of the LORD spoke through me. His word was on my tongue.

**3** The God of Israel spoke. The Rock of Israel said to me, 'A ruler of people must be fair. He must rule with respect for God.

**4** He will be like the light of the morning when the sun comes up on a day with no clouds. He will be like the light after rain that makes the grass grow from the ground.'

**5** My family is truly like this with God. He made a covenant (an agreement) with me that will continue forever. All the parts of the agreement are clear and sure. God will make my salvation complete. He will give me all that I want.

**6** But all evil men are like thorns that people throw away. No one can pick up thorns with his hands.

**7** A man who touches them must use an iron tool or the shaft of a spear. People burn the thorns completely in the place where they are."

**8** These are the names of the strong soldiers of David: Josheb-Basshebeth the Tahkemonite was the chief of the 3 best soldiers. He was also called Adino the Eznite. He used his spear to kill 800 men in 1 battle.

**10** But Eleazar stood and attacked the Philistines until his hand was tired. His hand was stiff on his sword. The LORD gave a great victory that day. The army came back to Eleazar only to take the valuable things from the dead men.

**13** 3 of the 30 chief soldiers went down to David at the cave of Adullam during the harvest. A group of Philistines had their camp in the Valley of Rephaim.

**18** Abishai, the brother of Joab and the son of Zeruiah, was the chief of the 30. He used his spear to kill 300 men. He became as famous as the 3 best soldiers.

**19** He had more honor than the 30, and he became their commander. But he was not one of the 3 best soldiers.

**20** Benaiah, the son of Jehoiada, was a brave soldier from Kabzeel. He did many brave things. He killed 2 of the best soldiers of Moab. One day when there was snow, he went down into a hole and killed a lion.

## Chapter 24

**1** Again the LORD was angry with Israel. He caused David to act against the people. The LORD said to David, "Go and count the people of Israel and Judah."

**2** So the king said to Joab, the commander of the army who was with him, "Go through all the tribes of Israel, from Dan to Beersheba. Count the people. I want to know how many people there are."

**3** Joab said to the king, "May the LORD your God make the people 100 times more than they are now. And may my lord the king see it. But why does my lord the king want to do this?"

**4** But the king gave his order, and Joab and the commanders of the army had to obey. So Joab and the commanders left the king. They went out to count the people of Israel.

**5** They went across the Jordan River. They made their camp near Aroer, south of the town in the valley. Then they went through Gad and to Jazer.

**6** Then they went to Gilead and to the land of Tahtim Hodshi. They went to Dan Jaan and around to Sidon.

**7** They went to the fortress of Tyre and to all the cities of the Hivites and the Canaanites. Then they went to the south of Judah, to Beersheba.

**8** They went through all the land. After 9 months and 20 days, they came back to Jerusalem.

**9** Joab gave the king the number of the people. In Israel there were 800,000 strong men who could fight with the sword. In Judah there were 500,000 men.

**10** After David counted the people, he felt guilty. David said to the LORD, "I did a very bad sin. LORD, please forgive me, your servant. I did a very foolish thing."

**11** David got up in the morning. Before that, the LORD spoke to the prophet Gad, the seer of David. The LORD said,

**12** "Go and tell David, 'The LORD says this: I give you 3 choices. Choose 1 of them, and I will do it to you.'"

**13** So Gad went to David and told him. Gad said, "Choose 1 of these 3 things. Will 7 years of famine come to your land? Or will you run away from your enemies for 3 months while they chase you? Or will a terrible disease come to your land for 3 days? Think about it now. Tell me what answer I must give to the LORD who sent me."

**14** David said to Gad, "I am in great trouble. Let us fall into the hands of the LORD, because his mercy is great. But do not let me fall into the hands of men."

**15** So the LORD sent a terrible disease on Israel. It started in the morning and continued until the time that the LORD chose. 70,000 people died, from Dan to Beersheba.

**16** The angel lifted his hand to destroy Jerusalem. But the LORD felt sorry about the disaster. He said to the angel who killed the people, "Enough! Stop now!" At that time the angel of the LORD was at the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite.

**17** David saw the angel who killed the people. David said to the LORD, "I am the one who sinned. I did the wrong thing. These people are like sheep. They did nothing wrong. Please punish me and my family."

**18** That day Gad came to David and said to him, "Go up and build an altar to the LORD on the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite."

**19** So David went up as Gad told him. He did what the LORD told him.

**20** Araunah looked and saw the king and his officers. They came toward him. So Araunah went out and bowed down to the ground in front of the king.

**21** Araunah said, "Why did my lord the king come to me, his servant?" David said, "I want to buy your threshing floor. I will build an altar to the LORD there. Then the terrible disease will stop."

**22** Araunah said to David, "My lord the king, take what you want and sacrifice it. Here are oxen for the burnt offering. Here are the threshing tools and the yokes of the oxen for wood."

**23** "King, I, Araunah, give all this to you." Araunah also said to the king, "May the LORD your God accept your offering."

**24** But the king said to Araunah, "No. I will pay you for it. I will not give burnt offerings to the LORD my God that cost me nothing." So David bought the threshing floor and the oxen for 50 shekels of silver.

**25** David built an altar to the LORD there. He gave burnt offerings and peace offerings. Then the LORD answered the prayers for the land. The terrible disease in Israel stopped.
