# Philippians

## Chapter 1

**1** Paul and Timothy, servants of Jesus Christ, write this letter. We write to all the saints in Christ Jesus in Philippi. We also write to the leaders and the helpers (deacons) of the church.

**2** We ask that God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you grace and peace.

**3** Each time that I remember you, I thank my God.

**4** In all my prayers for all of you, I always pray with joy.

**5** I thank God because you helped me with the gospel from the first day until now.

**6** I am sure of this: God started a good work in you. He will continue this work until the day when Jesus Christ comes. Then the work will be complete.

**7** It is correct for me to think this about all of you, because you are in my heart. I am in chains now. Before, I defended the gospel and showed that it is true. At all these times, you shared God's grace with me.

**8** God knows that I want very much to see all of you. I love you with the love of Jesus Christ.

**9** This is my prayer: I pray that your love will grow more and more. I pray that it will grow in knowledge and in all understanding.

**10** Then you will know which things are the best. You will be pure and without fault until the day of Christ.

**11** Jesus Christ will fill you with the good results of righteousness. Then people will give glory and praise to God.

**12** Brothers, I want you to know this: the things that happened to me helped the gospel to spread.

**13** All the palace guard and all the other people know that I am in chains because of Christ.

**14** Because I am in chains, most of the brothers in the Lord have more confidence. They speak the word of God with more courage and without fear.

**15** It is true that some people tell about Christ because they are jealous and want to fight. But other people tell about Christ because they have good will.

**16** The first group tells about Christ to compete with me. Their reasons are not honest. They think that they will give me more trouble while I am in chains.

**17** But the other group tells about Christ because of love. They know that God put me here to defend the gospel.

**18** What is the result? People tell about Christ in all ways, with false reasons or with true reasons. Because of this, I am happy. And I will continue to be happy.

**19** You pray for me, and the Spirit of Jesus Christ helps me. Thus, I know that these things will make me free.

**20** I hope very much that I will have no shame. I hope that I will have courage, now and always. Then, by my life or by my death, people will see that Christ is great.

**21** For me, life is Christ, and death is a gain.

**22** If I continue to live in my body, I can do useful work. But I do not know which thing to choose.

**23** I find it difficult to choose between the 2 things. I want to leave this life and be with Christ. That is much better.

**24** But it is more necessary for you that I stay in my body.

**25** I am sure of this. Thus, I know that I will stay and continue with all of you. I will help you to grow and to have joy in your faith.

**26** When I come to you again, you will have more reasons to be proud in Christ Jesus because of me.

**27** Only live in a way that is correct for the gospel of Christ. Maybe I will come and see you, or maybe I will be away. But I want to hear this about you: you stand strong in one spirit. You work together with one mind for the faith of the gospel.

**28** Do not let your enemies make you afraid in any way. This will show your enemies that God will destroy them. But it will show you that God will save you.

**29** God gave you the chance to believe in Christ. God also gave you the chance to suffer for Christ.

**30** You have the same fight that you saw me have. And now you hear that I still have this fight.

## Chapter 2

**1** Christ gives you encouragement. His love gives you comfort. You share in the Spirit. You have kindness and mercy.

**2** Thus, make my joy complete: think in the same way. Have the same love. Agree with each other and have one purpose.

**3** Do nothing because you want to compete or because you are proud. Be humble. Think that other people are more important than you.

**4** Each person must not think only about his own interests. Each person must also think about the interests of other people.

**5** Have the same attitude that Christ Jesus had.

**6** Christ was God by nature. But he did not think that he must hold on to his equality with God.

**7** Instead, he gave up all things. He took the nature of a servant. He became a human.

**8** He was a man, and he made himself humble. He obeyed God until his death. He died on a cross.

**9** Thus, God raised Christ to the highest place. God gave him the name that is above every name.

**10** God did this so that every person will go down on his knees at the name of Jesus. All in heaven, on earth and under the earth will do this.

**11** Every person will say that Jesus Christ is Lord. This will give glory to God the Father.

**12** My dear friends, you always obeyed me when I was with you. Now I am away from you, so you must obey more. With fear and respect, continue to work out your salvation.

**13** God works in you. He gives you the will and the power to do the things that make him happy.

**14** Do all things without complaints and arguments.

**15** Then you will be pure and without fault. You will be children of God without fault among bad and dishonest people. Among them, you shine like lights in the world.

**16** Hold on to the word of life. Then, on the day of Christ, I will be proud of you. I will know that my work for you was not useless.

**17** Your faith is a sacrifice and a service to God. Maybe my blood will be poured out like an offering on your sacrifice. If this occurs, I will be glad. I will share my joy with all of you.

**18** In the same way, you must also be glad and share your joy with me.

**19** I hope in the Lord Jesus to send Timothy to you soon. Then I will know how you are, and this news will give me comfort.

**20** I have no other person like Timothy. He truly cares about you.

**21** All the other people think only about their own interests. They do not think about the interests of Jesus Christ.

**22** But you know that Timothy showed his value. Like a son with his father, he served with me in the work of the gospel.

**23** Thus, I hope to send Timothy to you immediately after I know what will occur to me.

**24** And I trust in the Lord that I myself will also come soon.

**25** But I think that it is necessary to send Epaphroditus back to you. He is my brother. He works with me and fights with me like a soldier. You sent him to help me with my needs.

**26** He wants very much to see all of you. He was very sad because you heard that he was sick.

**27** It is true that he was sick and almost died. But God had mercy on him. God also had mercy on me. Thus, I did not have sorrow added to my sorrow.

**28** Thus, I want very much to send him to you. When you see him again, you will be glad. And I will have less sorrow.

**29** Receive him in the Lord with much joy. Honor people like him.

**30** He almost died for the work of Christ. He put his life in danger to help me. He did the work that you could not do for me.

## Chapter 3

**1** Last, my brothers, be glad in the Lord. I am happy to write the same things to you again. These things will keep you safe.

**2** Be careful of the bad people. They are like dogs. They do evil work. They want to cut your body.

**3** We are the true circumcision. We worship God by the Spirit of God. We are proud of Christ Jesus. We do not trust in human actions.

**4** But I can also have trust in human actions. Some people think that they have reasons to trust in human actions. I have more reasons than they have.

**5** People circumcised me on the eighth day after my birth. I am from the people of Israel and from the tribe of Benjamin. I am a Hebrew, and my parents are Hebrews. About the law, I was a Pharisee.

**6** I had much zeal, so I attacked the church. About the righteousness in the law, I had no fault.

**7** But these things were a gain to me before. Now I think that they are a loss because of Christ.

**8** It is true that I think all things are a loss. To know Christ Jesus my Lord is much better than all things. Because of Christ, I lost all things. I think that these things are garbage. I want only to get Christ.

**9** I want to be in Christ. My righteousness does not come from the law. My righteousness comes through faith in Christ. This righteousness comes from God, and it is through faith.

**10** I want to know Christ and the power of his resurrection. I want to share in his sufferings. I want to become like him in his death.

**11** Then, in some way, I will rise from the dead.

**12** I do not say that I have all these things now. I do not say that I am perfect now. But I continue to try to get the prize. Christ Jesus took me for this purpose.

**13** Brothers, I do not think that I have this prize now. But I do one thing. I forget the things behind me. I reach for the things in front of me.

**14** I run to the finish to get the prize. God calls me to heaven in Christ Jesus to get this prize.

**15** All of us who are mature must think in this way. If you think differently about something, God will show you the truth about that thing.

**16** But we must continue to live by the truth that we have now.

**17** Brothers, all of you must copy me. Watch the people who live as we live. We are an example for you.

**18** Many people live as enemies of the cross of Christ. I told you about these people many times. Now I cry while I tell you again.

**19** In the end, God will destroy these people. Their god is their stomach. They are proud of their shameful actions. They think only about things on earth.

**20** But we are citizens of heaven. We wait for our Savior, the Lord Jesus Christ, to come from heaven.

**21** Christ will change our weak human bodies. He will make our bodies like his glorious body. He will do this by the power that lets him control all things.

## Chapter 4

**1** Thus, my dear brothers, I love you and I want to see you. You are my joy and my crown. Stand strong in the Lord, my dear friends.

**2** I ask Euodia and Syntyche to agree with each other in the Lord.

**3** And I also ask you, my true partner: help these women. They worked hard with me for the gospel. Clement and my other helpers also worked with me. Their names are in the book of life.

**4** Always be glad in the Lord. I tell you again: be glad.

**5** Let all people know that you are gentle. The Lord is near.

**6** Do not worry about anything. In all things, pray and ask God for what you need. Thank him and tell him your requests.

**7** Then the peace of God will keep your hearts and minds safe in Christ Jesus. This peace is greater than all understanding.

**8** Last, brothers, think about the things that are true, honorable, correct, pure, lovely and good. Think about the things that are excellent and that deserve praise.

**9** Do the things that you learned, received, heard and saw in me. Then the God of peace will be with you.

**10** I am very glad in the Lord, because now again you show that you care about me. You cared about me before, but you did not have a chance to show it.

**11** I do not say this because I need something. I learned to be content in all conditions.

**12** I know how to live with little. I know how to live with much. I learned how to live in all conditions. I can be full or hungry. I can have much or have less than I need.

**13** I can do all things through Christ, who gives me strength.

**14** But you did a good thing when you shared my troubles.

**15** You Philippians know this: at the start of the gospel work, I left Macedonia. At that time, only your church shared with me. Only you gave to me and received from me.

**16** When I was in Thessalonica, you sent help for my needs more than one time.

**17** I do not want a gift for myself. I want a good result that will be added to your account.

**18** I have all that I need, and more. I have much, because Epaphroditus brought me your gifts. Your gifts are like a sweet smell to God. They are a sacrifice that God accepts and that makes him happy.

**19** My God will give you all that you need. He will do this from his great riches in glory in Christ Jesus.

**20** Give glory to our God and Father for all time. Amen.

**21** Greet every saint in Christ Jesus. The brothers who are with me send you greetings.

**22** All the saints send you greetings. Most of all, the saints in the house of Caesar send you greetings.

**23** I ask that the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ will be with all of you. Amen.
