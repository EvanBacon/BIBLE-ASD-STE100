# Habakkuk

## Chapter 1

**1** This is the message that Habakkuk the prophet saw.

**2** LORD, how long must I call to you for help? You do not hear me. I shout to you about violence, but you do not save us.

**3** Why do you show me bad actions? Why do you cause me to see trouble? People destroy things and do violent actions in front of me. People fight and argue.

**4** Thus people do not obey the law. Judges never give fair decisions. Evil people are all around the good people. Thus the judges give wrong decisions.

**5** "Look at the nations and watch carefully. You will be very surprised. I will do a thing in your time that you will not believe. You will not believe it when people tell you about it.

**6** I will cause the Chaldeans to become strong. They are a cruel nation that moves quickly. They will go across all the earth. They will take homes that are not theirs.

**7** People are very afraid of the Chaldeans. The Chaldeans make their own laws. They give honor to themselves.

**8** Their horses are faster than leopards. Their horses are more dangerous than wolves in the evening. Their horsemen come from far away. They attack quickly, as an eagle flies down quickly to eat its food.

**9** All of the Chaldeans come to do violence. Their army moves forward like the east wind. They take very many prisoners, as many as the grains of sand.

**10** The Chaldeans laugh at kings. They laugh at rulers. They laugh at all the strong cities. They put earth up against the walls, and they capture the cities.

**11** Then they move on quickly like the wind. They are guilty, because their strength is their god."

**12** LORD, you lived always, from the start of time. You are my God and my Holy One. We will not die. LORD, you chose the Chaldeans to punish us. Mighty God, you made them strong to correct us.

**13** Your eyes are too pure to look at evil. You cannot look at bad actions. Thus why do you look at people who are not loyal? Why are you silent when evil people destroy people who are better than them?

**14** You make people like the fish in the sea. You make them like small animals that have no ruler.

**15** The enemy catches all of these people with hooks. He catches them in his net. He gathers them in his large net. Thus he is very happy.

**16** Thus he offers sacrifices to his net. He burns incense to his large net. His net gives him a rich life and much good food.

**17** Will he always empty his net? Will he always kill the nations without pity?

## Chapter 2

**1** I will stand at my place of watch. I will stay on the tower. I will watch to see what the LORD will say to me. I will see what answer I must give about my complaint.

**2** The LORD answered me. He said, "Write the vision. Write it clearly on tablets. Then a messenger can read it easily and run with the message.

**3** The vision will come true at the correct time. At the end, the vision will become true. It will not be false. If it is slow, wait for it. It will certainly come, and it will not be late.

**4** Look! The proud man does not have a good spirit. But the good person will live by his faith.

**5** Wine makes the proud man do wrong things. He is proud and he does not stay at home. He always wants more, as the grave always wants more people. He is like death and is never satisfied. He takes all the nations for himself. He gathers all the peoples for himself.

**6** All these nations will laugh at him. They will speak a song about him and say, 'He will have much trouble! He takes things that are not his. How long will he do this? He makes himself rich with things that he took from other people.'

**7** Your enemies will suddenly attack you. They will wake up and cause you to be afraid. They will take your possessions.

**8** You stole from many nations. Thus all the people who stay alive will steal from you. You killed many people. You did violent actions to the land, to the cities and to all the people who live in them.

**9** The man who gets money for his family by evil actions will have much trouble! He builds his house in a high place. He thinks that he will be safe from danger there.

**10** You made plans to kill many people. These plans bring shame on your family. You sinned against yourself.

**11** The stones in the wall will shout against you. The wood in the roof will say the same thing.

**12** The man who builds a town with the blood of people will have much trouble! The man who makes a city strong by evil actions will have much trouble!

**13** The LORD of armies decided that the work of the nations will burn in the fire. The peoples will become tired for nothing.

**14** The waters fill the sea. In the same way, the knowledge of the glory of the LORD will fill the earth.

**15** The man who gives wine to his neighbor will have much trouble! He makes his neighbor drunk. Then he looks at the naked bodies of the people.

**16** You will be full of shame and not of honor. Now you must drink too, and show your naked body. The LORD will give you the cup of punishment from his right hand. Shame will cover your honor.

**17** You did violent actions to the forests of Lebanon. Now violence will come on you. You killed the animals, and now you will be afraid. You killed people. You did violent actions to the land, to the cities and to all the people who live in them.

**18** An idol does not help a person. A man makes an idol from wood or from metal. The idol teaches lies. The man who makes the idol trusts in his own work. But idols cannot speak.

**19** The man who says to a piece of wood, 'Wake up!' will have much trouble! He says to a stone that cannot speak, 'Get up!' Can the idol teach? Look! Gold and silver cover it. But it has no breath in it.

**20** But the LORD is in his holy temple. All the earth must be silent in front of him."

## Chapter 3

**1** This is a prayer of Habakkuk the prophet. It is a song.

**2** LORD, I heard about you, and I am afraid of the things that you did. LORD, do your work again in our time. Make your work known in our time. When you are angry, remember to show mercy.

**3** God came from Teman. The Holy One came from Mount Paran. Selah. His glory covered the skies. The earth was full of his praise.

**4** His brightness was like the sun. Light came from his hand. His power was there, in a secret place.

**5** Disease went in front of him. Death followed behind him.

**6** He stood and shook the earth. He looked at the nations, and they shook with fear. The very old mountains broke into pieces. The old hills fell down. His ways are always the same.

**7** I saw the people of Cushan in trouble. The people in the tents of Midian shook with fear.

**8** LORD, were you angry with the rivers? Were you angry with the sea? You rode on your horses and in your chariots to save your people.

**9** You took out your bow. You used your arrows as you promised. Selah. You split the earth with rivers.

**10** The mountains saw you, and they shook. Water flowed past in a flood. The deep water made a loud noise. The waves went up high.

**11** The sun and the moon stopped in the sky. They stopped when they saw the light of your arrows and your spear that shines.

**12** You went across the earth in anger. You walked on the nations in anger.

**13** You went out to save your people. You went to save your chosen king. You killed the leader of the evil nation. You destroyed his house fully, from the base to the top. Selah.

**14** The enemy soldiers came like a storm to scatter us. They were happy, as if they were about to destroy poor people in secret. But you killed their leader with his own spear.

**15** You walked on the sea with your horses. You moved through the large waves.

**16** I heard this, and my body shook. My lips shook at the sound. My bones became weak. My legs shook below me. But I will wait quietly for the day of trouble. That day will come to the nation that attacks us.

**17** Maybe the fig trees will have no flowers, and the vines will have no grapes. Maybe the olive trees will have no olives, and the fields will make no food. Maybe the sheep will die in the field, and there will be no cattle in the barns.

**18** But I will be happy in the LORD. I will have joy in the God who saves me.

**19** The LORD God is my strength. He makes my feet strong like the feet of a deer. He lets me walk on the high places. For the music director. Play this song on stringed instruments.
