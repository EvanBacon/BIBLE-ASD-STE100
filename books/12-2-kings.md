# 2 Kings

## Chapter 1

**1** After Ahab died, Moab started a rebellion against Israel.

**2** Ahaziah fell through the lattice of his upper room in Samaria, and he became sick. He sent messengers and told them, "Go and ask Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron, if I will become well again from this sickness."

**3** But the angel of the LORD spoke to Elijah the Tishbite. The angel said, "Go up and meet the messengers of the king of Samaria. Ask them, 'Is there no God in Israel? Is that why you go to ask Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron?'

**4** Thus the LORD says, 'You will not come down from the bed that you are on. You will surely die.'" Then Elijah went away.

**5** The messengers went back to the king. He asked them, "Why did you come back?"

**6** They told him, "A man came up to meet us. He said, 'Go back to the king who sent you. Tell him that the LORD says this: Is there no God in Israel? Is that why you send men to ask Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron? Thus you will not come down from the bed that you are on. You will surely die.'"

**7** The king asked them, "What type of man came up to meet you and told you these words?"

**8** They answered, "He was a man with much hair. He wore a leather belt around his waist." The king said, "That man is Elijah the Tishbite."

**9** Then the king sent a captain with his 50 men to Elijah. The captain went up to Elijah, who sat on the top of a hill. The captain said, "Man of God, the king says, 'Come down.'"

**10** Elijah answered the captain, "If I am a man of God, then let fire come down from heaven. Let the fire burn you and your 50 men." Then fire came down from heaven and burned the captain and his 50 men.

**11** The king sent another captain with his 50 men to Elijah. This captain said, "Man of God, the king says, 'Come down quickly.'"

**12** Elijah answered, "If I am a man of God, let fire come down from heaven. Let the fire burn you and your 50 men." Then the fire of God came down from heaven and burned the captain and his 50 men.

**13** The king sent a third captain with his 50 men. The third captain went up and fell on his knees in front of Elijah. He begged Elijah, "Man of God, please let my life and the lives of these 50 servants be valuable to you.

**14** Fire came down from heaven and burned the first 2 captains and their men. But please let my life now be valuable to you."

**15** The angel of the LORD said to Elijah, "Go down with him. Do not be afraid of him." So Elijah got up and went down with the captain to the king.

**16** Elijah told the king, "The LORD says this: 'You sent messengers to ask Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron. Is there no God in Israel to ask? Thus you will not come down from the bed that you are on. You will surely die.'"

**17** So Ahaziah died, as the LORD said through Elijah. Ahaziah had no son. Thus Jehoram became king after him. This occurred in the second year of Jehoram son of Jehoshaphat, the king of Judah.

**18** The book of the history of the kings of Israel tells the other things that Ahaziah did.

## Chapter 2

**1** The LORD was about to take Elijah up into heaven in a whirlwind. At that time, Elijah and Elisha went from Gilgal.

**2** Elijah said to Elisha, "Please stay here, because the LORD sent me to Bethel." But Elisha said, "As surely as the LORD lives, and as surely as you live, I will not leave you." So they went down to Bethel.

**3** The sons of the prophets at Bethel came out to Elisha. They asked him, "Do you know that the LORD will take your master away from you today?" Elisha said, "Yes, I know. Be quiet."

**4** Elijah said to him, "Elisha, please stay here, because the LORD sent me to Jericho." Elisha said, "As surely as the LORD lives, and as surely as you live, I will not leave you." So they came to Jericho.

**5** The sons of the prophets at Jericho came to Elisha. They asked him, "Do you know that the LORD will take your master away from you today?" Elisha answered, "Yes, I know. Be quiet."

**6** Elijah said to him, "Please stay here, because the LORD sent me to the Jordan River." Elisha said, "As surely as the LORD lives, and as surely as you live, I will not leave you." So the 2 men went on together.

**7** 50 men of the sons of the prophets went and stood at a distance to watch. Elijah and Elisha stood by the Jordan.

**8** Elijah took his cloak and rolled it up. He hit the water with it. The water divided to the left and to the right. Then the 2 men went across on dry ground.

**9** After they went across, Elijah said to Elisha, "Tell me what I can do for you before God takes me away from you." Elisha said, "Please let me get a double part of your spirit."

**10** Elijah said, "You ask for a difficult thing. If you see me when God takes me from you, then you will get it. If you do not see me, you will not get it."

**11** They continued to walk and talk. Then a chariot of fire and horses of fire suddenly came between the 2 men. Elijah went up into heaven in a whirlwind.

**12** Elisha saw it and cried out, "My father, my father! The chariots and horsemen of Israel!" Elisha did not see Elijah again. Then Elisha took his own clothes and tore them into 2 pieces.

**13** Elisha picked up the cloak that fell from Elijah. He went back and stood on the bank of the Jordan.

**14** He hit the water with the cloak of Elijah. He said, "Where is the LORD, the God of Elijah?" When he hit the water, the water divided to the left and to the right. Then Elisha went across.

**15** The sons of the prophets from Jericho watched him. They said, "The spirit of Elijah is on Elisha." They came to meet him and bowed down to the ground in front of him.

**16** They said to him, "Look, we, your servants, have 50 strong men. Please let them go and look for your master. Maybe the Spirit of the LORD took him up and put him on a mountain or in a valley." Elisha said, "Do not send them."

**17** But they continued to ask him until he felt ashamed. Then he said, "Send them." So they sent 50 men. The men looked for Elijah for 3 days, but they did not find him.

**18** The men came back to Elisha, who stayed at Jericho. He said to them, "I told you not to go."

**19** The men of the city said to Elisha, "Please look. The position of this city is good, as you can see, my lord. But the water is bad, and the land produces no crops."

**20** Elisha said, "Bring me a new bowl and put salt in it." So they brought it to him.

**21** Elisha went out to the spring of water and threw the salt into it. He said, "The LORD says this: 'I make this water good. It will not cause death or bad land again.'"

**22** So the water became good, and it is good to this day. It occurred as Elisha said.

**23** Elisha went up from there to Bethel. As he went up on the road, some young boys came out of the city. They laughed at him and said, "Go up, you bald man! Go up, you bald man!"

**24** Elisha turned around and looked at them. He cursed them in the name of the LORD. Then 2 female bears came out of the forest and attacked 42 of the boys.

**25** Elisha went from there to Mount Carmel. Then he went back to Samaria.

## Chapter 3

**1** Jehoram son of Ahab became king of Israel in Samaria in the 18th year of Jehoshaphat, the king of Judah. Jehoram ruled for 12 years.

**2** Jehoram did evil in the sight of the LORD. But he was not as bad as his father and mother. He removed the sacred stone of Baal that his father made.

**3** But Jehoram continued in the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin. Jehoram did not stop these sins.

**4** Mesha the king of Moab kept sheep. He gave the king of Israel 100,000 lambs and the wool of 100,000 rams each year.

**5** But after Ahab died, the king of Moab started a rebellion against the king of Israel.

**6** At that time King Jehoram went out of Samaria. He gathered all the men of Israel for war.

**7** He sent a message to Jehoshaphat the king of Judah. He said, "The king of Moab started a rebellion against me. Will you go with me to fight against Moab?" Jehoshaphat said, "I will go. I am as you are. My people are as your people. My horses are as your horses."

**8** Jehoshaphat asked, "Which road will we use?" Jehoram answered, "The road through the desert of Edom."

**9** So the king of Israel, the king of Judah, and the king of Edom went. They went around for 7 days. Then there was no water for the army or for the animals with them.

**10** The king of Israel said, "Oh no! The LORD called us 3 kings together to give us to Moab!"

**11** But Jehoshaphat asked, "Is there no prophet of the LORD here? We can ask the LORD through him." One of the officers of the king of Israel answered, "Elisha son of Shaphat is here. He was the servant of Elijah."

**12** Jehoshaphat said, "The word of the LORD is with Elisha." So the king of Israel, Jehoshaphat, and the king of Edom went down to Elisha.

**13** Elisha said to the king of Israel, "I have nothing to do with you. Go to the prophets of your father and of your mother." The king of Israel said, "No. The LORD called us 3 kings together to give us to Moab."

**14** Elisha said, "As surely as the LORD of armies lives, whom I serve, I respect Jehoshaphat the king of Judah. If he is not here, I will not look at you or see you.

**15** But now bring me a man who plays the harp." While the man played, the power of the LORD came on Elisha.

**16** Elisha said, "The LORD says this: 'Dig many ditches in this valley.'

**17** The LORD says, 'You will not see wind or rain. But this valley will become full of water. You, your cattle, and your other animals will drink.'

**18** This is an easy thing for the LORD. He will also give Moab to you.

**19** You will attack every city with walls and every important city. You will cut down every good tree. You will block all the springs of water. You will put stones on every good field and make it bad."

**20** In the morning, at the time of the grain offering, water came from the direction of Edom. The land became full of water.

**21** All the Moabites heard that the kings came to fight against them. So they gathered all the men who could fight, young and old. They stood at the border.

**22** They got up early in the morning. The sun shone on the water. To the Moabites, the water across from them looked as red as blood.

**23** They said, "That is blood! The kings fought and killed each other. Now, Moab, let us take their possessions!"

**24** But when the Moabites came to the camp of Israel, the Israelites got up and attacked them. The Moabites ran away from them. The Israelites went into the land of Moab and continued to kill the Moabites.

**25** They destroyed the cities. Each man threw a stone on every good field until the fields were full of stones. They blocked all the springs of water. They cut down all the good trees. Only the city of Kir-haraseth stayed with its stone walls. But the men with slings surrounded it and attacked it.

**26** The king of Moab saw that the battle was too strong for him. So he took 700 men with swords. They tried to break through to the king of Edom, but they could not.

**27** Then the king of Moab took his oldest son, who was to become king after him. He sacrificed his son as a burnt offering on the city wall. Then there was great anger against Israel. So the Israelites went away from the king of Moab and went back to their land.

## Chapter 4

**1** The wife of one of the sons of the prophets cried out to Elisha. She said, "My husband, your servant, is dead. You know that he respected the LORD. But a man to whom he owed money came. That man wants to take my 2 sons as his slaves."

**2** Elisha said to her, "What can I do for you? Tell me, what do you have in your house?" She said, "I, your servant, have nothing in the house but a small jar of oil."

**3** Elisha said, "Go out and borrow empty jars from all your neighbors. Get many jars.

**4** Then go into your house. Close the door behind you and your sons. Pour the oil into all those jars. Put each full jar to the side."

**5** So she went away from him. She closed the door behind her and her sons. Her sons brought the jars to her, and she poured the oil.

**6** When the jars were full, she said to her son, "Bring me another jar." He said to her, "There are no more jars." Then the oil stopped.

**7** She went and told the man of God. He said, "Go, sell the oil, and pay your debt. You and your sons can live on the money that remains."

**8** One day Elisha went to Shunem. An important woman lived there. She asked him strongly to eat a meal. After that, each time he went by, he stopped there to eat.

**9** The woman said to her husband, "I know that this man who often comes by is a holy man of God.

**10** Please let us make a small room on the roof. Let us put a bed, a table, a chair, and a lamp in it for him. Then he can stay there when he comes to us."

**11** One day Elisha came there. He went into the room and lay down.

**12** He said to Gehazi, his servant, "Call this Shunammite woman." Gehazi called her, and she stood in front of Elisha.

**13** Elisha said to Gehazi, "Tell her this: 'You did much work to help us. What can we do for you? Can we speak for you to the king or to the commander of the army?'" She answered, "I live safely among my own people."

**14** Elisha asked, "Then what can we do for her?" Gehazi answered, "She has no son, and her husband is old."

**15** Elisha said, "Call her." Gehazi called her, and she stood in the doorway.

**16** Elisha said, "At this time next year, you will hold a son in your arms." She said, "No, my lord, man of God. Do not lie to me, your servant."

**17** But the woman became pregnant. At that time the next year, she gave birth to a son, as Elisha told her.

**18** The child grew. One day he went out to his father, who was with the men who cut the grain.

**19** The boy said to his father, "My head! My head!" His father said to a servant, "Carry him to his mother."

**20** The servant took him and brought him to his mother. The boy sat on her lap until noon. Then he died.

**21** She went up and put him on the bed of the man of God. She closed the door and went out.

**22** She called her husband and said, "Please send me one of the servants and one of the donkeys. I will go quickly to the man of God and come back."

**23** Her husband asked, "Why do you go to him today? It is not the new moon or the Sabbath." She said, "All is well."

**24** She put a saddle on a donkey. She said to her servant, "Go fast. Do not go slowly for me unless I tell you."

**25** So she went to the man of God at Mount Carmel. The man of God saw her at a distance. He said to Gehazi, his servant, "Look, there is the Shunammite woman.

**26** Please run now to meet her. Ask her, 'Are you well? Is your husband well? Is your child well?'" She answered, "All is well."

**27** She came to the man of God on the hill and held his feet. Gehazi came near to push her away. But the man of God said, "Let her be. She is very sad. The LORD did not show me the cause. He did not tell me."

**28** She said, "My lord, did I ask you for a son? Did I not tell you, 'Do not give me false hope'?"

**29** Elisha said to Gehazi, "Prepare to go. Take my staff in your hand and go. If you meet a man, do not stop to speak to him. If a man speaks to you, do not answer. Put my staff on the face of the child."

**30** But the mother of the child said, "As surely as the LORD lives, and as surely as you live, I will not leave you." So Elisha got up and followed her.

**31** Gehazi went ahead of them. He put the staff on the face of the child. But there was no sound and no response. So Gehazi went back to meet Elisha. He told him, "The child did not wake up."

**32** Elisha came into the house. The child lay dead on his bed.

**33** Elisha went in and closed the door behind the 2 of them. Then he prayed to the LORD.

**34** He went up on the bed and lay on the child. He put his mouth on the mouth of the child and his eyes on the eyes of the child. He put his hands on the hands of the child. He stretched out on the child, and the body of the child became warm.

**35** Elisha got up and walked back and forth in the house. Then he went up and stretched out on the child again. The child sneezed 7 times and opened his eyes.

**36** Elisha called Gehazi and said, "Call the Shunammite woman." So Gehazi called her. When she came in, Elisha said, "Pick up your son."

**37** She came in and fell at his feet. She bowed down to the ground. Then she picked up her son and went out.

**38** Elisha went back to Gilgal. At that time there was a famine in the land. The sons of the prophets sat in front of him. He said to his servant, "Put the large pot on the fire. Cook a stew for the sons of the prophets."

**39** One of them went out into the field to gather plants. He found a wild vine. He picked wild gourds from it until his cloak was full. He came back and cut them into the pot of stew. They did not know what the gourds were.

**40** They poured out the stew for the men to eat. But when the men ate the stew, they cried out, "Man of God, there is death in the pot!" They could not eat it.

**41** Elisha said, "Bring some flour." He threw the flour into the pot. He said, "Pour out the stew for the people to eat." Then the stew in the pot was not dangerous.

**42** A man came from Baal-shalishah. He brought the man of God 20 loaves of barley bread from the first crop. He also brought some fresh grain in a bag. Elisha said, "Give it to the people to eat."

**43** His servant said, "How can I put this in front of 100 men?" Elisha said again, "Give it to the people to eat. The LORD says this: 'They will eat, and some food will remain.'"

**44** So the servant put it in front of them. They ate, and some food remained, as the LORD said.

## Chapter 5

**1** Naaman was the commander of the army of the king of Syria. His master thought that he was a great and important man. Through Naaman, the LORD gave victory to Syria. Naaman was a strong soldier, but he had leprosy, a skin disease.

**2** Groups of Syrian soldiers went out and attacked Israel. They took a young girl from the land of Israel as a prisoner. She became the servant of the wife of Naaman.

**3** The girl said to her mistress, "I wish that my master was with the prophet who is in Samaria! That prophet can cure him of his leprosy."

**4** Naaman went in and told his master, "The girl from the land of Israel said these things."

**5** The king of Syria said, "Go now. I will send a letter to the king of Israel." So Naaman went. He took with him 10 talents of silver, 6,000 pieces of gold, and 10 sets of clothes.

**6** He brought the letter to the king of Israel. The letter said, "With this letter I send my servant Naaman to you. Cure him of his leprosy."

**7** The king of Israel read the letter. Then he tore his clothes and said, "Am I God? Can I kill and make alive? Why does this man send a man to me to cure him of leprosy? Look, the king of Syria tries to start a fight with me."

**8** Elisha the man of God heard that the king of Israel tore his clothes. So he sent this message to the king: "Why did you tear your clothes? Let the man come to me. Then he will know that there is a prophet in Israel."

**9** So Naaman came with his horses and his chariot. He stopped at the door of the house of Elisha.

**10** Elisha sent a messenger to him. The messenger said, "Go and wash in the Jordan River 7 times. Then your skin will become healthy again, and you will be clean."

**11** But Naaman became angry and went away. He said, "I thought that he will surely come out to me. I thought that he will stand and call on the name of the LORD his God. I thought that he will move his hand over the area and cure the leprosy.

**12** The Abana and the Pharpar, the rivers of Damascus, are better than all the water in Israel. Can I not wash in them and be clean?" So he turned and went away very angry.

**13** His servants came near and spoke to him. They said, "My father, if the prophet told you to do a difficult thing, you were ready to do it. He only told you, 'Wash and be clean.' Thus you must surely obey him."

**14** So Naaman went down and went under the water of the Jordan 7 times, as the man of God told him. His skin became healthy again, as the skin of a little child. He was clean.

**15** Naaman and all his men went back to the man of God. Naaman stood in front of him and said, "Now I know that there is no God in all the earth, but only in Israel. Please accept a gift from me, your servant."

**16** But Elisha said, "As surely as the LORD lives, whom I serve, I will not accept a gift." Naaman asked him strongly to take it, but Elisha did not accept.

**17** Naaman said, "If you do not accept, then please give me, your servant, as much earth as 2 mules can carry. From now on I will not give burnt offerings or sacrifices to other gods. I will give them only to the LORD.

**18** But I ask the LORD to forgive me for one thing. My master goes into the temple of Rimmon to worship there. He leans on my arm, and I must bow down in the temple of Rimmon. When I bow down in the temple of Rimmon, I ask the LORD to forgive me for this thing."

**19** Elisha said to him, "Go in peace." So Naaman went a short distance away from him.

**20** Gehazi was the servant of Elisha the man of God. He said to himself, "My master let this Syrian Naaman go. He did not accept the gifts that Naaman brought. As surely as the LORD lives, I will run after him and get something from him."

**21** So Gehazi followed Naaman. Naaman saw him run after him. So he got down from the chariot to meet him. He asked, "Is all well?"

**22** Gehazi said, "All is well. My master sent me. He said, 'Now 2 young men of the sons of the prophets came to me from the hill country of Ephraim. Please give them a talent of silver and 2 sets of clothes.'"

**23** Naaman said, "Please take 2 talents." He asked Gehazi strongly to accept. He tied 2 talents of silver in 2 bags with 2 sets of clothes. He gave them to 2 of his servants. The servants carried the bags in front of Gehazi.

**24** When Gehazi came to the hill, he took the bags from the servants. He put them in the house. Then he sent the men away, and they left.

**25** Gehazi went in and stood in front of his master. Elisha asked him, "Gehazi, where did you go?" Gehazi said, "I, your servant, did not go to a place."

**26** Elisha said to him, "My spirit was with you when the man got down from his chariot to meet you. This is not the time to accept money and clothes. It is not the time to get olive groves, vineyards, sheep, cattle, and servants.

**27** Thus the leprosy of Naaman will be on you and on your descendants for all time." Gehazi went out from Elisha with leprosy. His skin was as white as snow.

## Chapter 6

**1** The sons of the prophets said to Elisha, "Look, the place where we live with you is too small for us.

**2** Please let us go to the Jordan. Each of us will get a log there. Then we will make a place to live there." Elisha said, "Go."

**3** One of them said, "Please come with your servants." Elisha answered, "I will come."

**4** So he went with them. They came to the Jordan and started to cut down trees.

**5** While one man cut down a tree, the iron head of his axe fell into the water. He cried out, "Oh no, my master! I borrowed that axe!"

**6** The man of God asked, "Where did it fall?" The man showed him the place. Elisha cut a stick and threw it into the water there. Then the iron came up and floated.

**7** Elisha said, "Take it out." So the man put out his hand and took it.

**8** The king of Syria was at war with Israel. He spoke with his officers. He said, "I will put my camp at this place."

**9** But the man of God sent a message to the king of Israel. He said, "Be careful. Do not go by that place, because the Syrians go down there."

**10** So the king of Israel sent men to the place that the man of God told him about. The man of God warned the king many times, and the king was careful there.

**11** This made the king of Syria very angry. He called his officers and said to them, "Tell me! Which of us helps the king of Israel?"

**12** One of his officers said, "None of us, my lord the king. But Elisha, the prophet in Israel, tells the king of Israel the words that you speak in your bedroom."

**13** The king said, "Go and find where Elisha is. Then I can send men to catch him." Men told the king, "Elisha is in Dothan."

**14** So the king sent horses, chariots, and a large army there. They came at night and surrounded the city.

**15** The servant of the man of God got up early in the morning and went out. He saw an army with horses and chariots around the city. The servant said, "Oh no, my master! What will we do?"

**16** Elisha answered, "Do not be afraid. The army with us is larger than the army with them."

**17** Then Elisha prayed, "LORD, please open his eyes so that he can see." The LORD opened the eyes of the young man, and he saw. The hills around Elisha were full of horses and chariots of fire.

**18** The Syrians came down toward Elisha. Then Elisha prayed to the LORD, "Please make these people blind." So the LORD made them blind, as Elisha asked.

**19** Elisha said to them, "This is not the road, and this is not the city. Follow me. I will bring you to the man that you look for." But he took them to Samaria.

**20** When they came into Samaria, Elisha said, "LORD, open the eyes of these men so that they can see." The LORD opened their eyes, and they saw. They were in the center of Samaria.

**21** When the king of Israel saw them, he asked Elisha, "My father, must I kill them? Must I kill them?"

**22** Elisha answered, "Do not kill them. You do not kill men that you capture with your sword and bow. Give them food and water. Let them eat and drink. Then let them go back to their master."

**23** So the king prepared a large meal for them. After they ate and drank, he sent them away. They went back to their master. After that, the groups of Syrian soldiers did not attack the land of Israel again.

**24** Some time later, Ben-hadad the king of Syria gathered all his army. He went up and surrounded Samaria to attack it.

**25** Thus there was a very bad famine in Samaria. The Syrians surrounded the city for a long time. At last, the head of a donkey cost 80 pieces of silver. A quarter of a cab of dove dung cost 5 pieces of silver.

**26** The king of Israel walked on the wall of the city. A woman cried out to him, "Help me, my lord the king!"

**27** The king said, "If the LORD does not help you, how can I help you? I have no grain from the grain floor. I have no wine from the winepress."

**28** Then the king asked her, "What is your problem?" She answered, "This woman said to me, 'Give me your son. We will eat him today. Tomorrow we will eat my son.'

**29** So we cooked my son and ate him. The next day I said to her, 'Give me your son. We will eat him.' But she hid her son."

**30** The king heard the words of the woman. He tore his clothes. While he walked on the wall, the people looked at him. They saw that he wore sackcloth under his clothes, against his skin.

**31** The king said, "Let God punish me very much if the head of Elisha son of Shaphat stays on his body today!"

**32** Elisha sat in his house, and the elders sat with him. The king sent a messenger ahead of him. But before the messenger came, Elisha said to the elders, "This son of a murderer sent a man to cut off my head. Look, when the messenger comes, close the door. Hold the door shut against him. The sound of the feet of his master comes after him."

**33** While Elisha still spoke with them, the messenger came down to him. The king said, "The LORD caused this trouble. Why must I wait for the LORD more?"

## Chapter 7

**1** Elisha said, "Hear the word of the LORD. The LORD says this: 'Tomorrow at about this time, a measure of fine flour will cost 1 shekel. 2 measures of barley will cost 1 shekel. People will sell them at the gate of Samaria.'"

**2** The king leaned on the arm of an officer. That officer answered the man of God, "Even if the LORD makes windows in heaven, this cannot occur." Elisha said, "You will see it with your eyes. But you will not eat any of it."

**3** There were 4 men with leprosy at the entrance of the city gate. They said to each other, "Why do we sit here until we die?

**4** If we go into the city, we will die there, because there is famine in the city. If we stay here, we will also die. So let us go to the camp of the Syrians. If they let us live, we will live. If they kill us, we will only die."

**5** At sunset they got up and went to the camp of the Syrians. They came to the edge of the camp. But there were no men there.

**6** The Lord caused the Syrian army to hear the sound of chariots and horses, as the sound of a large army. The Syrians said to each other, "The king of Israel paid the kings of the Hittites and the kings of Egypt to attack us!"

**7** So the Syrians got up and ran away at sunset. They left their tents, their horses, and their donkeys. They left the camp as it was and ran to save their lives.

**8** The men with leprosy came to the edge of the camp. They went into a tent. They ate and drank. They took silver, gold, and clothes from it. Then they went and hid these things. They came back and went into another tent. They took things from it too and hid them.

**8** They followed the customs of the nations that the LORD sent away from the Israelites. They also followed the customs that the kings of Israel started.

**9** The Israelites secretly did things that were wrong against the LORD their God. They built high places of worship in all their towns, from watchtowers to cities with walls.

**10** They put up sacred stones and Asherah poles on every high hill and under every green tree.

**11** They burned incense at all the high places, as the nations did. The LORD sent those nations away from the Israelites. The Israelites did evil things and made the LORD angry.

**12** They worshipped idols. But the LORD told them, "Do not do this."

**15** They rejected the laws of the LORD. They rejected the covenant that he made with their ancestors. They rejected his warnings. They worshipped idols that have no value, and they became people with no value. They followed the nations around them. But the LORD told them not to do as these nations did.

**16** They did not obey all the commands of the LORD their God. They made 2 metal idols in the shape of calves. They made an Asherah pole. They worshipped all the stars of the sky. They served Baal.

**17** They sacrificed their sons and daughters in the fire. They used magic and tried to know the future. They gave themselves completely to evil in the sight of the LORD. Thus they made the LORD angry.

**19** But Judah also did not obey the commands of the LORD their God. The people of Judah followed the customs that Israel started.

**20** So the LORD rejected all the descendants of Israel. He punished them. He gave them to enemies who took their possessions. At last he sent them away from his presence.

Thus the god sent lions among them. The lions kill the people, because the people do not know the laws of the god of the land."

**32** These people worshipped the LORD. But they also chose all types of people from among themselves as priests of the high places. These priests made sacrifices for them in the temples on the high places.

**33** They worshipped the LORD, but they also served their own gods. They followed the customs of the nations from which the Assyrians took them.

**35** The LORD made a covenant with the Israelites. He commanded them, "Do not worship other gods. Do not bow down to them, serve them, or make sacrifices to them.

**36** Worship only the LORD, who brought you out of the land of Egypt with great power and a strong arm. Bow down to him. Make sacrifices to him.

**37** Always obey the laws, the rules, and the commands that he wrote for you. Do not worship other gods.

**38** Do not forget the covenant that I made with you. Do not worship other gods.

**39** Worship the LORD your God. He will save you from all your enemies."

**40** But the people did not listen. They continued to follow their old customs.

**41** So these nations worshipped the LORD, but they also served their idols. Their children and their grandchildren continue to do as their ancestors did, to this day.

**21** This is the word that the LORD says against Sennacherib: 'The young woman, the city of Zion, hates you and laughs at you. The city of Jerusalem shakes her head at you as you run away.

**22** Whom did you insult? Against whom did you speak bad words? Against whom did you shout and look up proudly? You did these things against the Holy One of Israel!

**23** You used your messengers to insult the Lord. You said, "With my many chariots I went up to the tops of the mountains, to the highest parts of Lebanon. I cut down its tall cedar trees and its best pine trees. I went into its farthest places and its best forests.

**24** I dug wells in foreign lands and drank their water. I walked through the rivers of the cities that I attacked, and the rivers became dry."

**25** Did you not hear? Long ago I decided this. In ancient times I planned it. Now I make it occur. I let you change cities with walls into piles of broken stones.

**26** Thus the people of those cities had no power. They were afraid and ashamed. They were as grass and green plants in the field. They were as grass on the roof that dries up before it grows.

**27** But I know where you stay, and when you go out and come in. I know how angry you are against me.

**28** You are angry against me, and I heard your proud words. Thus I will put my hook in your nose and my bit in your mouth. I will make you go back on the road by which you came.'

**29** Hezekiah, this will be a sign for you. This year you will eat the grain that grows by itself. Next year you will eat what grows from that grain. But in the 3rd year, plant seeds and cut the crops. Plant vineyards and eat their fruit.

**30** The people of Judah who escape and remain will grow strong again. They will be as a plant that puts its roots down and produces fruit above.

**31** Some people will remain in Jerusalem. Some people will escape from Mount Zion. The strong love of the LORD of armies will do this.

**32** Thus the LORD says this about the king of Assyria: 'He will not come into this city. He will not shoot an arrow here. He will not come near it with a shield. He will not build a ramp of earth against its walls.

**33** He will go back on the road by which he came. He will not come into this city,' says the LORD.

**34** 'I will protect this city and save it. I will do this for myself and for my servant David.'"

**35** That night the angel of the LORD went out and killed 185,000 men in the camp of the Assyrians. When the people got up early in the morning, they saw all the dead bodies.

**36** So Sennacherib the king of Assyria left. He went back home and stayed in Nineveh.

**37** One day he worshipped in the temple of his god Nisroch. His sons Adrammelech and Sharezer killed him with the sword. Then they escaped to the land of Armenia. His son Esarhaddon became king after him.

## Chapter 20

**1** At that time Hezekiah became sick and was about to die. The prophet Isaiah son of Amoz came to him. Isaiah said, "The LORD says this: 'Put your household in order, because you will die. You will not become well.'"

**2** Then Hezekiah turned his face to the wall and prayed to the LORD. He said,

**3** "LORD, please remember how I served you faithfully with all my heart. I did what is good in your sight." Then Hezekiah cried loudly.

**4** Isaiah left. But before he went out of the middle courtyard, the LORD spoke to him. The LORD said,

**5** "Go back and tell Hezekiah, the ruler of my people, this: 'The LORD, the God of your ancestor David, says this: I heard your prayer. I saw your tears. I will make you well. On the 3rd day you will go up to the temple of the LORD.

**6** I will add 15 years to your life. I will save you and this city from the king of Assyria. I will protect this city for myself and for my servant David.'"

**7** Isaiah said, "Get a mass of pressed figs." They got it and put it on the boil. Then Hezekiah became well.

**8** Hezekiah asked Isaiah, "What will be the sign that the LORD will make me well? What will show that I will go up to the temple of the LORD on the 3rd day?"

**9** Isaiah said, "This is the sign from the LORD. It will show that the LORD will do what he said. Do you want the shadow to go forward 10 steps or back 10 steps?"

**10** Hezekiah answered, "It is easy for the shadow to go forward 10 steps. No, let the shadow go back 10 steps."

**11** Isaiah the prophet called to the LORD. Then the LORD made the shadow go back 10 steps on the stairway of Ahaz.

**12** At that time Berodach-baladan son of Baladan, the king of Babylon, sent letters and a gift to Hezekiah. He heard that Hezekiah was sick.

**13** Hezekiah welcomed the messengers. He showed them all his storehouse of valuable things. He showed them the silver, the gold, the spices, and the expensive oil. He showed them his armory and all that was in his treasuries. Hezekiah showed them all the things in his palace and in all his kingdom.

**14** Then Isaiah the prophet came to King Hezekiah. He asked him, "What did these men say? Where did they come from?" Hezekiah said, "They came from a far country, from Babylon."

**15** Isaiah asked, "What did they see in your palace?" Hezekiah answered, "They saw all the things in my palace. I showed them all my treasures."

**16** Then Isaiah said to Hezekiah, "Hear the word of the LORD.

**17** 'A time will come when people will take all the things in your palace to Babylon. They will also take all the things that your ancestors stored until this day. Nothing will remain,' says the LORD.

**18** 'They will take away some of your own descendants. These men will become palace officials in the palace of the king of Babylon.'"

**19** Hezekiah said to Isaiah, "The word of the LORD that you spoke is good." He thought, "Peace and safety will continue during my life. So that is good."

**20** The book of the history of the kings of Judah tells the other things that Hezekiah did, and all his power. The book tells how he made the pool and the tunnel that brought water into the city.

**21** Hezekiah died. Then his son Manasseh became king after him.

## Chapter 21

**1** Manasseh was 12 years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for 55 years. His mother was Hephzibah.

**2** He did evil in the sight of the LORD. He did the terrible things of the nations that the LORD sent away from the Israelites.

**3** He built again the high places of worship that his father Hezekiah destroyed. He built altars for Baal and made an Asherah pole, as Ahab the king of Israel did. He bowed down to all the stars of the sky and served them.

**4** He built altars in the temple of the LORD. About this temple the LORD said, "I will put my name in Jerusalem."

**5** In the 2 courtyards of the temple of the LORD, he built altars for all the stars of the sky.

**6** He sacrificed his son in the fire. He tried to know the future and used magic. He asked mediums and spiritists for advice. He did much evil in the sight of the LORD and made him angry.

**7** Manasseh made a carved Asherah pole and put it in the temple. About this temple the LORD said to David and to his son Solomon, "I chose Jerusalem from all the tribes of Israel. I will put my name in this temple and in Jerusalem for all time.

**8** I will not make the Israelites leave the land that I gave to their ancestors again. But they must be careful to do all that I commanded them. They must obey all the law that my servant Moses gave them."

**9** But the people did not listen. Manasseh caused them to do more evil than the nations did. The LORD destroyed those nations in front of the Israelites.

**10** The LORD spoke through his servants the prophets. He said,

**11** "Manasseh the king of Judah did these terrible things. He did more evil than the Amorites who lived here before him. He also caused Judah to sin with his idols.

**12** Thus the LORD, the God of Israel, says this: 'I will bring such disaster on Jerusalem and Judah. The ears of all who hear about it will ring.

**13** I will measure Jerusalem with the line that I used for Samaria. I will use the plumb line that I used for the family of Ahab. I will clean Jerusalem as a man cleans a dish. He cleans it and turns it upside down.

**14** I will leave the people of mine who remain. I will give them to their enemies. All their enemies will take them and their possessions.

**15** I will do this because they did evil in my sight. They made me angry from the day that their ancestors came out of Egypt until this day.'"

**16** Manasseh also killed very many innocent people. Their blood filled Jerusalem from one end to the other. He did this in addition to the sin that he caused Judah to do. He caused them to do evil in the sight of the LORD.

**17** The book of the history of the kings of Judah tells the other things that Manasseh did, and the sin that he did.

**18** Manasseh died, and the people buried him in the garden of his palace, the garden of Uzza. Then his son Amon became king after him.

**19** Amon was 22 years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for 2 years. His mother was Meshullemeth, the daughter of Haruz from Jotbah.

**20** Amon did evil in the sight of the LORD, as his father Manasseh did.

**21** He lived as his father lived. He served the idols that his father served, and he bowed down to them.

**22** He left the LORD, the God of his ancestors. He did not follow the way of the LORD.

**23** The officers of Amon made a secret plan against him. They killed the king in his palace.

**24** Then the people of the land killed all the men who made the plan against King Amon. The people of the land made his son Josiah king after him.

**25** The book of the history of the kings of Judah tells the other things that Amon did.

**26** The people buried Amon in his tomb in the garden of Uzza. Then his son Josiah became king after him.

## Chapter 22

**1** Josiah was 8 years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for 31 years. His mother was Jedidah, the daughter of Adaiah from Bozkath.

**2** He did what was right in the sight of the LORD. He lived as his ancestor David lived. He did not turn away from the right way.

**3** In the 18th year of King Josiah, the king sent Shaphan the secretary to the temple of the LORD. Shaphan was the son of Azaliah, son of Meshullam. The king said,

**4** "Go to Hilkiah the high priest. Tell him to count the money that people brought to the temple of the LORD. The gatekeepers collected this money from the people.

**5** Let the priests give the money to the men who supervise the work on the temple of the LORD. Let these men pay the workers who repair the temple of the LORD.

**6** Let them pay the carpenters, the builders, and the stone workers. Let them also buy wood and cut stone to repair the temple."

**7** But they did not ask the supervisors to show how they used the money, because the supervisors were honest.

**8** Hilkiah the high priest said to Shaphan the secretary, "I found the book of the law in the temple of the LORD." Hilkiah gave the book to Shaphan, and Shaphan read it.

**9** Then Shaphan the secretary went to the king and gave him this report: "Your officers took the money that was in the temple. They gave it to the men who supervise the work on the temple of the LORD."

**10** Shaphan the secretary also told the king, "Hilkiah the priest gave me a book." Then Shaphan read it to the king.

**11** The king heard the words of the book of the law. Then he tore his clothes.

**12** The king gave orders to Hilkiah the priest, Ahikam son of Shaphan, and Achbor son of Micaiah. He also gave orders to Shaphan the secretary and Asaiah, the servant of the king. He said,

**13** "Go and ask the LORD for me, for the people, and for all Judah. Ask about the words of this book that we found. The LORD is very angry with us. Our ancestors did not obey the words of this book. They did not do all that this book tells us to do."

**14** So Hilkiah the priest, Ahikam, Achbor, Shaphan, and Asaiah went to Huldah the prophetess. She was the wife of Shallum son of Tikvah, son of Harhas. Shallum was in charge of the clothes. Huldah lived in the Second District of Jerusalem. The men spoke with her.

**15** She said to them, "The LORD, the God of Israel, says this: 'Tell the man who sent you to me,

**16** The LORD says this: I will bring disaster on this place and on its people. I will do all the things that the book says. The king of Judah read that book.

**17** The people left me. They burned incense to other gods. They made me angry with all the idols that they made. Thus my anger will burn against this place, and it will not stop.'

**18** The king of Judah sent you to ask the LORD. Tell him this: 'The LORD, the God of Israel, says this about the words that you heard:

**19** I said that this place and its people will become empty and cursed. When you heard this, your heart was sad, and you made yourself humble in front of the LORD. You tore your clothes and cried in front of me. Thus I heard you, says the LORD.

**20** So I will let you die and go to your ancestors. You will go to your grave in peace. Your eyes will not see all the disaster that I will bring on this place.'" Then the men took her answer back to the king.

## Chapter 23

**1** The king sent messengers, and they gathered all the elders of Judah and Jerusalem to him.

**2** The king went up to the temple of the LORD. All the men of Judah and all the people of Jerusalem went with him. The priests, the prophets, and all the people, unimportant and important, went with him. He read to them all the words of the book of the covenant. The priest found this book in the temple of the LORD.

**3** The king stood by the pillar. He made a covenant in front of the LORD. He promised to follow the LORD and to obey his commands, his rules, and his laws with all his heart and soul. He promised to do the words of the covenant that this book contains. All the people agreed to the covenant.

**4** The king gave orders to Hilkiah the high priest, the priests of the second rank, and the gatekeepers. He told them to remove all the objects for Baal, for Asherah, and for all the stars of the sky. They removed these objects from the temple of the LORD. He burned these objects outside Jerusalem in the fields of the Kidron Valley. He took their ashes to Bethel.

**5** The kings of Judah chose pagan priests to burn incense at the high places in the towns of Judah and around Jerusalem. Josiah removed these priests. He also removed the priests who burned incense to Baal, to the sun, and to the moon. They also burned incense to the planets and to all the stars of the sky.

**6** He took the Asherah pole from the temple of the LORD to the Kidron Valley outside Jerusalem. He burned it there and crushed it into powder. He threw the powder on the graves of the common people.

**7** He destroyed the rooms of the male temple prostitutes. These rooms were at the temple of the LORD. In these rooms women made woven cloth for Asherah.

**8** Josiah brought all the priests from the towns of Judah. He made the high places unclean where the priests burned incense, from Geba to Beersheba. He destroyed the high places at the gates. One was at the entrance of the gate of Joshua, the governor of the city. It was on the left side of the city gate.

**9** The priests of the high places did not serve at the altar of the LORD in Jerusalem. But they ate bread without yeast with the other priests.

**10** Josiah made Topheth in the Valley of the Son of Hinnom unclean. He did this so that no person could sacrifice his son or daughter in the fire to Molech.

**11** He removed the horses that the kings of Judah gave to the sun. These horses were at the entrance of the temple of the LORD, near the room of Nathan-melech the official. This room was in the courtyard. Josiah burned the chariots of the sun.

**12** The kings of Judah made altars on the roof of the upper room of Ahaz. Manasseh made altars in the 2 courtyards of the temple of the LORD. The king broke all these altars. He removed them and threw their dust into the Kidron Valley.

**13** Solomon the king of Israel built high places east of Jerusalem, on the south side of the Hill of Corruption. He built them for Ashtoreth, the hateful goddess of the Sidonians. He built them for Chemosh, the hateful god of the Moabites, and for Milcom, the hateful god of the Ammonites. King Josiah made these high places unclean.

**14** He broke the sacred stones into pieces and cut down the Asherah poles. He filled their places with human bones.

**15** Jeroboam son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin, made the altar and the high place at Bethel. Josiah broke down that altar and that high place. He burned the high place and crushed it into powder. He also burned the Asherah pole.

**16** Then Josiah turned around and saw the tombs on the hill. He sent men to take the bones out of the tombs. He burned the bones on the altar to make it unclean. This occurred as the LORD said. The man of God announced these things long before.

**17** Josiah asked, "What is that monument that I see?" The men of the city told him, "That is the tomb of the man of God who came from Judah. He announced these things that you did against the altar of Bethel."

**18** Josiah said, "Let it be. Do not let a person move his bones." So they did not move his bones or the bones of the prophet who came from Samaria.

**19** The kings of Israel built temples on the high places in the towns of Samaria. These temples made the LORD angry. Josiah removed all of them. He did to them all the things that he did at Bethel.

**20** He killed all the priests of the high places on the altars there. He burned human bones on the altars. Then he went back to Jerusalem.

**21** The king gave an order to all the people. He said, "Celebrate the Passover to the LORD your God, as this book of the covenant tells you."

**22** The people did not celebrate a Passover as this one since the time of the judges who led Israel. They did not celebrate one as this during all the time of the kings of Israel and the kings of Judah.

**23** In the 18th year of King Josiah, the people celebrated this Passover to the LORD in Jerusalem.

**24** Josiah also removed the mediums, the spiritists, the household gods, the idols, and all the other terrible things in Judah and Jerusalem. He did this to obey the words of the law. These words were in the book that Hilkiah the priest found in the temple of the LORD.

**25** There was no king before Josiah who turned to the LORD as he did. He turned to the LORD with all his heart, all his soul, and all his strength. He obeyed all the law of Moses. After him, there was no king as Josiah.

**26** But the LORD did not stop his great anger against Judah. He was angry because of all the things that Manasseh did to make him angry.

**27** The LORD said, "I will remove Judah from my presence, as I removed Israel. I will reject this city Jerusalem, which I chose. I will reject the temple about which I said, 'My name will be there.'"

**28** The book of the history of the kings of Judah tells the other things that Josiah did.

**29** During the rule of Josiah, Pharaoh Neco the king of Egypt went to the Euphrates River to meet the king of Assyria. King Josiah went to fight against Neco. When Neco saw Josiah at Megiddo, Neco killed him.

**30** The officers of Josiah carried his body in a chariot from Megiddo to Jerusalem. They buried him in his own tomb. Then the people of the land took Jehoahaz son of Josiah. They anointed him and made him king after his father.

**31** Jehoahaz was 23 years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for 3 months. His mother was Hamutal, the daughter of Jeremiah from Libnah.

**32** He did evil in the sight of the LORD, as his ancestors did.

**33** Pharaoh Neco put him in chains at Riblah in the land of Hamath. Thus Jehoahaz could not rule in Jerusalem. Neco made Judah pay a tax of 100 talents of silver and 1 talent of gold.

**34** Pharaoh Neco made Eliakim son of Josiah king after his father Josiah. Neco changed the name of Eliakim to Jehoiakim. Neco took Jehoahaz to Egypt, and Jehoahaz died there.

**35** Jehoiakim paid the silver and the gold to Pharaoh. But he taxed the land to get the money that Pharaoh demanded. He made each person pay silver and gold as he decided. He gave it to Pharaoh Neco.

**36** Jehoiakim was 25 years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for 11 years. His mother was Zebidah, the daughter of Pedaiah from Rumah.

**37** He did evil in the sight of the LORD, as his ancestors did.

## Chapter 24

**1** During the rule of Jehoiakim, Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon attacked Judah. Jehoiakim became his servant for 3 years. Then Jehoiakim changed his mind and started a rebellion against him.

**2** The LORD sent groups of soldiers against Jehoiakim. These groups came from the Chaldeans, the Syrians, the Moabites, and the Ammonites. The LORD sent them to destroy Judah. This occurred as the LORD said through his servants the prophets.

**3** These things occurred to Judah because the LORD commanded them. He wanted to remove the people of Judah from his presence because of the sins of Manasseh. He punished them for all that Manasseh did.

**4** The LORD also punished them for the innocent blood that Manasseh shed. Manasseh filled Jerusalem with innocent blood. The LORD did not want to forgive this.

**5** The book of the history of the kings of Judah tells the other things that Jehoiakim did.

**6** Jehoiakim died. Then his son Jehoiachin became king after him.

**7** The king of Egypt did not go out from his land again. The king of Babylon took all the land of the king of Egypt, from the Brook of Egypt to the Euphrates River.

**8** Jehoiachin was 18 years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for 3 months. His mother was Nehushta, the daughter of Elnathan from Jerusalem.

**9** He did evil in the sight of the LORD, as his father did.

**10** At that time the officers of Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon attacked Jerusalem. They surrounded the city.

**11** Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon came to the city while his officers surrounded it.

**12** Jehoiachin the king of Judah went out to the king of Babylon. His mother, his servants, his commanders, and his officials went with him. The king of Babylon captured Jehoiachin. This occurred in the 8th year of the rule of the king of Babylon.

**13** Nebuchadnezzar took all the treasures from the temple of the LORD and from the palace. He cut into pieces all the gold objects that Solomon the king of Israel made for the temple of the LORD. This occurred as the LORD said.

**14** He took all the people of Jerusalem as prisoners. He took all the commanders and all the strong soldiers, 10,000 prisoners in total. He also took all the skilled workers and metal workers. Only the poorest people of the land remained.

**15** He took Jehoiachin as a prisoner to Babylon. He also took the mother of the king, the wives of the king, his officials, and the important men of the land. He took them all from Jerusalem to Babylon.

**16** The king of Babylon also took to Babylon all 7,000 strong soldiers and 1,000 skilled workers and metal workers. All of them were strong and could fight in war.

**17** The king of Babylon made Mattaniah, the uncle of Jehoiachin, king in his place. He changed the name of Mattaniah to Zedekiah.

**18** Zedekiah was 21 years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for 11 years. His mother was Hamutal, the daughter of Jeremiah from Libnah.

**19** He did evil in the sight of the LORD, as Jehoiakim did.

**20** These things occurred in Jerusalem and Judah because the LORD was angry with the people. At last he sent them away from his presence. Then Zedekiah started a rebellion against the king of Babylon.

## Chapter 25

**1** In the 9th year of the rule of Zedekiah, on the 10th day of the 10th month, Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon attacked Jerusalem. He came with all his army and made a camp near the city. They built siege walls all around it.

**2** The army surrounded the city until the 11th year of King Zedekiah.

**3** By the 9th day of the 4th month, the famine in the city was very bad. The people of the land had no food.

**4** Then the enemy broke through the city wall. All the soldiers ran away at night. They went through the gate between the 2 walls near the garden of the king. The Chaldeans were all around the city. The king went toward the Jordan Valley.

**5** But the army of the Chaldeans followed the king. They caught him in the plains of Jericho. All his army went away from him in different directions.

**6** The Chaldeans captured the king. They brought him to the king of Babylon at Riblah. There the king of Babylon decided his punishment.

**7** They killed the sons of Zedekiah while Zedekiah watched. Then they made Zedekiah blind. They tied him with bronze chains and took him to Babylon.

**8** On the 7th day of the 5th month, Nebuzaradan came to Jerusalem. That was the 19th year of Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon. Nebuzaradan was the commander of the guard and an officer of the king of Babylon.

**9** He burned the temple of the LORD, the palace of the king, and all the houses of Jerusalem. He burned every important building.

**10** All the army of the Chaldeans with the commander of the guard broke down the walls around Jerusalem.

**11** Nebuzaradan the commander of the guard took the people who remained in the city as prisoners. He also took the people who went over to the side of the king of Babylon. He took the rest of the people too.

**12** But the commander of the guard let some of the poorest people of the land stay. He made them work in the vineyards and the fields.

**13** The Chaldeans broke into pieces the bronze pillars of the temple of the LORD. They also broke the stands and the large bronze water tank, called the Sea, in the temple of the LORD. They took the bronze to Babylon.

**14** They also took the pots, the shovels, the wick cutters, the spoons, and all the bronze objects that the priests used in the temple.

**15** The commander of the guard took the fire pans and the bowls. He took all the objects made of pure gold or pure silver.

**16** Solomon made the 2 pillars, the Sea, and the stands for the temple of the LORD. The bronze of all these objects was too heavy to weigh.

**17** Each pillar was 18 cubits high. The bronze top on each pillar was 3 cubits high. A network of bronze chains and bronze pomegranates went around each top. The second pillar was the same as the first, with its network.

**18** The commander of the guard took Seraiah the chief priest, Zephaniah the second priest, and the 3 gatekeepers as prisoners.

**19** From the city he took the officer in charge of the soldiers and 5 advisers of the king. He also took the secretary of the army commander, who gathered the people of the land for the army. He took 60 men of the people of the land who were in the city.

**20** Nebuzaradan the commander of the guard took all these men. He brought them to the king of Babylon at Riblah.

**21** The king of Babylon killed them at Riblah in the land of Hamath. Thus the Babylonians took the people of Judah away from their land as prisoners.

**22** Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon let some people stay in the land of Judah. He made Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, the governor over them.

**23** All the army commanders and their men heard that the king of Babylon made Gedaliah governor. So they came to Gedaliah at Mizpah. These men were Ishmael son of Nethaniah, Johanan son of Kareah, Seraiah son of Tanhumeth the Netophathite, and Jaazaniah son of the Maacathite. Their men came with them.

**24** Gedaliah made a promise to them and their men. He told them, "Do not be afraid of the officials of the Chaldeans. Live in the land and serve the king of Babylon. Then things will go well for you."

**25** But in the 7th month, Ishmael son of Nethaniah, son of Elishama, came with 10 men. Ishmael was from the royal family. They attacked and killed Gedaliah. They also killed the Jews and the Chaldeans who were with him at Mizpah.

**26** Then all the people, unimportant and important, and the army commanders got up and went to Egypt. They were afraid of the Chaldeans.

**27** In the 37th year after the Babylonians took Jehoiachin the king of Judah as a prisoner, Evil-merodach became king of Babylon. On the 27th day of the 12th month of that year, he let Jehoiachin the king of Judah out of prison.

**28** He spoke kindly to Jehoiachin. He gave him a seat of more honor than the seats of the other kings who were with him in Babylon.

**29** So Jehoiachin took off his prison clothes. For the rest of his life, he ate regularly at the table of the king.

**30** Each day the king gave Jehoiachin the things that he needed, for the rest of his life.
