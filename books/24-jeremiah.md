# Jeremiah

## Chapter 1

**1** These are the words of Jeremiah, the son of Hilkiah. Hilkiah was 1 of the priests in the town of Anathoth, in the land of Benjamin.

**2** The LORD spoke his word to Jeremiah when Josiah, the son of Amon, was king of Judah. This occurred in the 13th year that Josiah was king.

**3** The LORD also spoke to Jeremiah when Jehoiakim, the son of Josiah, was king of Judah. He continued to speak to Jeremiah until the end of the 11th year of Zedekiah, the son of Josiah, king of Judah. In the 5th month of that year, the enemy took the people of Jerusalem away as prisoners.

**4** The LORD spoke his word to me. He said,

**5** "I knew you before I made you in the body of your mother. Before your birth, I chose you for a special task. I made you a prophet to the nations."

**6** Then I said, "Ah, Lord GOD! Look, I do not know how to speak. I am only a boy."

**7** But the LORD said to me, "Do not say, 'I am only a boy.' You must go to all the people to whom I send you. You must speak all the words that I tell you.

**8** Do not be afraid of the people. I am with you, and I will protect you," says the LORD.

**9** Then the LORD put out his hand and touched my mouth. The LORD said to me, "Look, I put my words in your mouth.

**10** Today I give you authority over nations and kingdoms. You will pull up and pull down. You will destroy and throw down. You will build and plant."

**11** The LORD spoke his word to me. He said, "Jeremiah, what do you see?" I said, "I see a stick from an almond tree."

**12** Then the LORD said to me, "You saw correctly. I watch my word to make sure that it occurs."

**13** The LORD spoke his word to me a 2nd time. He said, "What do you see?" I said, "I see a pot of water that boils. The pot tips from the north toward the south."

**14** Then the LORD said to me, "Disaster will come from the north. It will come on all the people who live in this land.

**15** Look, I will call all the families of the kingdoms of the north," says the LORD. "They will come. Each king will put his throne at the entrance to the gates of Jerusalem. They will attack all its walls. They will attack all the cities of Judah.

**16** I will announce my judgments against my people because of all their evil. They left me. They burned incense to other gods. They worshipped the idols that they made with their own hands.

**17** Thus get ready! Stand up and tell them all the things that I command you. Do not be afraid of them. If you are afraid, I will make you afraid in front of them.

**18** Look, today I make you strong like a city with strong walls. You will be like an iron pillar and like bronze walls. You will stand against all the land. You will stand against the kings of Judah, its officials, its priests and the people of the land.

**19** They will fight against you. But they will not defeat you, because I am with you and I will protect you," says the LORD.

## Chapter 2

**1** The LORD spoke his word to me. He said,

**2** "Go and shout this message to the people of Jerusalem. Say that the LORD says this: 'I remember how loyal you were when you were young. You loved me like a young bride. You followed me through the desert, through a land where people did not plant crops.

**3** Israel was holy to the LORD. Israel was like the first part of his harvest. All the people who attacked Israel became guilty. Disaster came on them,'" says the LORD.

**4** Hear the word of the LORD, family of Jacob, all the families of the people of Israel.

**5** The LORD says this: "What wrong thing did your ancestors find in me? Why did they go far away from me? They followed idols that have no value. Thus they also became people with no value.

**6** They did not ask, 'Where is the LORD? He brought us out of the land of Egypt. He led us through the desert. He led us through a land of dry ground and holes. He led us through a dry and dark land. No person goes through that land, and no person lives there.'

**7** I brought you into a land with many good crops. I wanted you to eat its fruit and its good things. But you came into my land and made it unclean. You made my land a place that I hate.

**8** The priests did not ask, 'Where is the LORD?' The teachers of the law did not know me. The leaders rebelled against me. The prophets spoke in the name of Baal. They followed idols that have no value.

**9** Thus I will accuse you again," says the LORD. "I will accuse your grandchildren.

**10** Go across the sea to the coasts of Cyprus and look. Send people to Kedar and look carefully. See if a thing like this ever occurred.

**11** Did a nation ever change its gods? And those gods are not real gods! But my people changed their glorious God for idols that have no value.

**12** Heavens, look at this with horror. Shake with great fear," says the LORD.

**13** "My people did 2 evil things. They left me, the spring of water that gives life. And they dug their own water tanks. But those water tanks have cracks. They cannot hold water.

**14** Is Israel a servant? Was he born a slave? Why did enemies take everything from him?

**15** The enemies roared at him like young lions. They made his land empty. They burned his cities. No person lives in them now.

**16** The men of Memphis and Tahpanhes in Egypt also broke your head.

**17** You caused this to occur to yourself. You left the LORD your God when he led you on the road.

**18** Now why do you go to Egypt to drink water from the Nile River? Why do you go to Assyria to drink water from the Euphrates River?

**19** Your evil will punish you. Your rebellion will show that you are wrong. Understand that it is very bad and painful to leave the LORD your God. It is bad that you do not respect me," says the Lord GOD of armies.

**20** "A long time ago you broke your yoke. You broke the ropes that I put on you. You said, 'I will not serve you.' On every high hill and under every large green tree, you worshipped other gods. You were like a woman who sells her body.

**21** I planted you like a very good vine from the best seed. Thus why did you become a bad, wild vine?

**22** Possibly you will wash yourself with strong soap. Possibly you use much soap. But I still see the stain of your sin," says the Lord GOD.

**23** "How can you say, 'I am not unclean. I did not follow the Baals'? Look at what you did in the valley. Know what you did. You are like a young female camel that runs here and there.

**24** You are like a wild donkey in the desert. When she wants a mate, she smells the wind. No person can stop her. The males do not need to look for her. At the correct time they will find her.

**25** Do not run until your shoes wear out and your throat is dry. But you said, 'It is no use! I love foreign gods, and I will follow them.'

**26** A thief feels shame when people catch him. Thus the people of Israel feel shame. Their kings, their officials, their priests and their prophets feel shame.

**27** They say to a piece of wood, 'You are my father.' They say to a stone, 'You gave birth to me.' They turned their backs to me, not their faces. But when they have trouble, they say, 'Come and save us!'

**28** Where are the gods that you made for yourselves? Let them come and save you when you have trouble! Judah, you have as many gods as you have cities.

**29** Why do you accuse me? All of you rebelled against me," says the LORD.

**30** "I punished your children, but it had no result. They did not learn from the punishment. You killed your prophets with your swords like a hungry lion.

**31** You people of this generation, think about the word of the LORD. Was I like a desert to Israel? Was I like a dark land? Why do my people say, 'We are free to go where we want. We will not come back to you'?

**32** A young woman does not forget her jewelry. A bride does not forget her wedding clothes. But my people forgot me for a very long time.

**33** You know very well how to find lovers. Even the most wicked women can learn from you.

**34** The blood of poor and innocent people is on your clothes. You did not catch them when they broke into your house. But you killed them.

**35** But you say, 'I am innocent. Surely the LORD is not angry with me now.' But look, I will judge you because you say, 'I did not sin.'

**36** Why do you change your plans so frequently? Egypt will disappoint you, as Assyria disappointed you.

**37** You will also leave Egypt with your hands on your head in shame. The LORD refused the nations that you trust. They will not help you."

## Chapter 3

**1** "If a man divorces his wife, and she leaves him and marries another man, will the 1st husband take her back again? If he does, the land will become very unclean. But you were unfaithful to me with many lovers. Do you now want to come back to me?" says the LORD.

**2** "Look at the bare hills. Is there a place where you did not have sex with your lovers? You sat by the road and waited for them, like an Arab in the desert. You made the land unclean with your evil acts and your unfaithfulness.

**3** Thus the rain stopped. The spring rain did not come. But you have no shame, like a woman who sells her body. You refuse to feel shame.

**4** Now you call to me, 'My father, you were my friend when I was young.

**5** Will you always be angry? Will your anger continue until the end?' This is what you say. But you continue to do as much evil as you can."

**6** When Josiah was king, the LORD said to me, "Did you see what unfaithful Israel did? She went up on every high hill and under every large green tree. There she worshipped other gods like a woman who sells her body.

**7** I thought, 'After she does all these things, she will come back to me.' But she did not come back. Her disloyal sister Judah saw this.

**8** Israel was unfaithful to me. Thus I sent her away and I gave her a document of divorce. But her disloyal sister Judah was not afraid. Judah also went and worshipped other gods like a woman who sells her body.

**9** Israel did not think that her unfaithfulness was serious. She made the land unclean. She worshipped idols of stone and wood.

**10** After all this, her disloyal sister Judah did not come back to me with all her heart. She only pretended to come back," says the LORD.

**11** The LORD said to me, "Unfaithful Israel is less guilty than disloyal Judah.

**12** Go and announce these words toward the north. Say, 'Come back, unfaithful Israel,' says the LORD. 'I will not be angry with you, because I am merciful,' says the LORD. 'I will not be angry always.

**13** Only admit your sin. Admit that you rebelled against the LORD your God. You gave your love to foreign gods under every large green tree. You did not obey me,'" says the LORD.

**14** "Come back, unfaithful children," says the LORD. "I am your master. I will take 1 person from each city and 2 persons from each family. I will bring you to Zion.

**15** I will give you leaders who obey me. They will lead you with knowledge and wisdom.

**16** In those days your people will become very many in the land," says the LORD. "Then people will not talk about the Ark of the Covenant of the LORD again. They will not think about it or remember it. They will not miss it, and they will not make another.

**17** At that time people will call Jerusalem 'The Throne of the LORD.' All the nations will come together in Jerusalem to honor the name of the LORD. They will not follow the wishes of their stubborn and evil hearts again.

**18** In those days the people of Judah will join the people of Israel. They will come together from the land of the north. They will come to the land that I gave to your ancestors."

**19** "I thought, 'I want to treat you as my children. I want to give you a pleasant land. I want to give you the most beautiful land of all the nations.' I thought that you will call me 'My father.' I thought that you will not turn away from me.

**20** But you were not loyal to me, people of Israel. You were like a wife who is not loyal to her husband," says the LORD.

**21** People hear a sound on the bare hills. The people of Israel cry and pray. They chose the wrong way. They forgot the LORD their God.

**22** "Come back, unfaithful children. I will cure you of your unfaithfulness." "Yes, we will come to you, because you are the LORD our God.

**23** The idols on the hills and the noisy worship on the mountains cannot help us. Only the LORD our God can save Israel.

**24** Since we were young, the shameful idols took all the things that our ancestors worked for. They took their flocks and their cattle. They took their sons and their daughters.

**25** Let us lie down in our shame. Let our disgrace cover us. We and our ancestors sinned against the LORD our God. From our youth until today, we did not obey the LORD our God."

## Chapter 4

**1** "Israel, if you come back," says the LORD, "come back to me. Remove your idols that I hate from in front of me. Do not go away from me again.

**2** Make your promises in my name. Say, 'As surely as the LORD lives.' Do this with truth, with justice and with honesty. Then the nations will ask the LORD to bless them. They will praise him."

**3** The LORD says this to the people of Judah and Jerusalem: "Plow your fields that were not used. Do not plant seeds among thorns.

**4** Circumcise yourselves to the LORD. Make your hearts ready to obey him, people of Judah and Jerusalem. If you do not, my anger will burn like fire. No person will be able to stop it. This will occur because of the evil things that you do."

**5** Announce this in Judah and in Jerusalem. Say, "Blow the trumpet in the land!" Shout loudly and say, "Come together! Let us go into the cities that have strong walls!"

**6** Raise a flag to show the way to Zion. Run to a safe place! Do not stop! The LORD says, "I will bring disaster from the north. I will cause great destruction."

**7** A lion came out from the bushes where it hides. The destroyer of nations started his journey. He left his home to destroy your land. Your cities will be ruins. No person will live in them.

**8** Thus put on rough cloth (sackcloth). Cry and shout with sorrow. The strong anger of the LORD did not turn away from us.

**9** "On that day," says the LORD, "the king and the officials will lose their courage. The priests will feel shock. The prophets will be very surprised."

**10** Then I said, "Ah, Lord GOD! Surely you tricked these people and the people of Jerusalem. You said, 'You will have peace.' But now the sword is at our throats."

**11** At that time the LORD will speak to these people and to Jerusalem. He will say, "A hot wind blows from the bare hills in the desert toward my people. It is not a soft wind. Farmers do not use it to clean the grain.

**12** The wind that comes from me is too strong for that. Now I will announce my judgment against them."

**13** Look! The enemy comes like clouds. His chariots come like a storm. His horses are faster than eagles. We will have much trouble! Our enemies destroy us!

**14** Jerusalem, clean the evil from your heart. Then God will save you. How long will you continue to think evil thoughts?

**15** A messenger speaks from the city of Dan. He announces disaster from the hills of Ephraim.

**16** "Tell this to the nations. Announce it to Jerusalem: 'Enemy soldiers come from a far country. They shout their war cry against the cities of Judah.

**17** They surround Jerusalem like men who guard a field. They do this because Jerusalem rebelled against me,'" says the LORD.

**18** "Your actions and your evil acts caused this to occur to you. This is your punishment. It is very painful. It goes deep into your heart."

**19** I feel great pain! I feel great pain! My heart hurts. My heart beats loudly. I cannot be silent. I hear the sound of the trumpet. I hear the alarm of war.

**20** People shout about 1 disaster after another. Enemies destroy all the land. Suddenly they destroy my tents. In a moment they destroy my curtains.

**21** How long must I see the war flag? How long must I hear the sound of the trumpet?

**22** "My people are foolish. They do not know me. They are stupid children. They do not understand. They are clever when they do evil. But they do not know how to do good."

**23** I looked at the earth. It had no shape and it was empty. I looked at the sky, and it had no light.

**24** I looked at the mountains, and they shook. All the hills moved from side to side.

**25** I looked, and there were no people. All the birds of the sky flew away.

**26** I looked, and the good land was a desert. All its cities were in ruins. The LORD and his strong anger caused this.

**27** The LORD says this: "All the land will become a ruin. But I will not destroy it completely.

**28** Thus the earth will be sad. The sky will become dark. I spoke and I decided. I will not change my mind. I will not turn back."

**29** All the people in each town run away when they hear the horsemen and the soldiers with bows. Some go into the bushes. Some climb up on the rocks. All the towns are empty. No person lives in them.

**30** Your enemies destroy you, Jerusalem! What will you do? You put on red clothes. You put on gold jewelry. You paint your eyes. But you make yourself beautiful for no reason. Your lovers hate you. They want to kill you.

**31** I hear a cry like the cry of a woman who gives birth. It is a cry of pain like the cry of a woman who gives birth to her first child. It is the cry of the people of Zion. They breathe with difficulty and they hold out their hands. They say, "Help! I am weak because murderers attack me."

## Chapter 5

**1** "Go up and down the streets of Jerusalem. Look around and think. Look in the public squares. Try to find 1 person who is honest and who wants the truth. If you find 1, I will forgive this city.

**2** The people say, 'As surely as the LORD lives.' But they do not tell the truth when they make promises."

**3** LORD, you look for the truth. You punished the people, but they did not feel sorry. You crushed them, but they refused to learn. They made their faces harder than rock. They refused to come back to you.

**4** I thought, "These are only poor people. They are foolish. They do not know the way of the LORD. They do not know the law of their God.

**5** I will go to the leaders and speak to them. Surely they know the way of the LORD. They know the law of their God." But all of them broke their yoke. They broke the ropes that God put on them.

**6** Thus a lion from the forest will attack them. A wolf from the desert will destroy them. A leopard will wait near their cities. It will tear to pieces each person who comes out. This will occur because their rebellion is very bad. They turned away from God many times.

**7** "How can I forgive you? Your children left me. They make promises by gods that are not real. I gave them all the things that they needed. But they were not loyal to me. They went in crowds to the houses of women who sell their bodies.

**8** They are like strong, well-fed horses. Each man wants the wife of another man.

**9** Must I not punish them for these things?" says the LORD. "Must I not punish a nation like this?

**10** Go through the vineyards of Judah and destroy them. But do not destroy them completely. Cut off their branches, because these people do not belong to the LORD.

**11** The people of Israel and the people of Judah were not loyal to me at all," says the LORD.

**12** They told lies about the LORD. They said, "He will do nothing! No disaster will come on us. We will not see war or famine.

**13** The prophets are only wind. The word of the LORD is not in them. Let the things that they say occur to them!"

**14** Thus the LORD God of armies says this: "The people said these things. Thus I will make my words in your mouth like fire. These people will be like wood. The fire will burn them.

**15** People of Israel, look," says the LORD. "I will bring a nation from far away against you. It is a strong nation. It is a very old nation. You do not know their language. You will not understand what they say.

**16** Their arrows bring death. All of them are strong soldiers.

**17** They will eat your harvest and your food. They will kill your sons and your daughters. They will eat your sheep and your cattle. They will destroy your grape vines and your fig trees. With their swords they will destroy the cities with strong walls that you trust.

**18** But even in those days," says the LORD, "I will not destroy you completely.

**19** Your people will ask, 'Why did the LORD our God do all these things to us?' Then you will say to them, 'You left the LORD and you served foreign gods in your own land. Thus you will serve foreigners in a land that is not yours.'

**20** Announce this to the family of Jacob. Tell it in Judah. Say,

**21** 'Hear this, you foolish people who do not understand. You have eyes, but you do not see. You have ears, but you do not hear.

**22** Are you not afraid of me?' says the LORD. 'Why do you not shake with fear in front of me? I made the sand the border of the sea. It is a law that will continue always. The sea cannot go past the sand. The waves move and make a loud noise, but they cannot go past it.

**23** But these people have stubborn hearts. They rebel against me. They turned away from me and left me.

**24** They do not think, "Let us respect the LORD our God. He gives us rain in autumn and in spring at the correct time. He makes sure that we have the weeks of harvest."

**25** Your sins stopped these good things. Your sins kept them from you.

**26** There are wicked men among my people. They wait in secret like men who catch birds. They make traps to catch men.

**27** A basket is full of birds. Thus their houses are full of the things that they stole with lies. That is how they became powerful and rich.

**28** They are fat and their skin shines. Their evil acts have no limit. They do not judge fairly. They do not defend the rights of children who have no father. They do not defend the rights of poor people. But they have success.

**29** Must I not punish them for these things?' says the LORD. 'Must I not punish a nation like this?'

**30** A strange and horrible thing occurs in the land.

**31** The prophets tell lies. The priests rule by their own authority. And my people like it this way. But what will you do at the end?"

## Chapter 6

**1** People of Benjamin, run away from Jerusalem to a safe place! Blow the trumpet in the town of Tekoa. Light a fire as a signal in Beth Haccerem. Disaster comes from the north. Great destruction comes.

**2** Jerusalem, you are like a beautiful and delicate woman. But I will destroy you.

**3** Enemy rulers will come against you with their armies, like shepherds with their flocks. They will put up their tents all around you. Each will take the part of the land that he wants.

**4** They say, "Get ready to fight against Jerusalem! Come, let us attack at noon!" But they also say, "We have a problem! The day ends. The evening shadows become long.

**5** Thus come, let us attack in the night. Let us destroy her strong buildings."

**6** The LORD of armies says this: "Cut down the trees. Build a ramp of earth against the walls of Jerusalem. I must punish this city. It is full of cruel acts.

**7** A well keeps its water fresh. Thus Jerusalem keeps her evil fresh. People hear the sounds of violence and destruction in the city. I always see her sickness and her wounds.

**8** Jerusalem, learn from this instruction. If you do not, I will turn away from you. I will make your land empty. No person will live in it."

**9** The LORD of armies says this: "The enemy will take all the survivors of Israel. They will be like a man who picks all the grapes from a vine. Go over the branches again, as a man who picks grapes does."

**10** To whom can I speak? Who will listen when I warn them? Look, their ears do not open. They cannot hear. Look, they laugh at the word of the LORD. They do not want it.

**11** But I am full of the anger of the LORD. I am weary because I hold it in. The LORD said, "Pour out my anger on the children in the street. Pour it out on the groups of young men. Enemies will take husbands and wives. They will take old people and very old people.

**12** Other people will get their houses, their fields and their wives. I will use my power against the people who live in this land," says the LORD.

**13** "All of them want more money, from the least important to the most important. From the prophets to the priests, all of them tell lies.

**14** They try to heal the injuries of my people, but they do not make them better. They say, 'All is well, all is well!' But all is not well.

**15** Do they feel shame because of the things that I hate? No, they feel no shame at all. They do not even know how to blush. Thus they will fall with the other people who fall. When I punish them, they will fall down," says the LORD.

**16** The LORD says this: "Stand at the crossroads and look. Ask where the old roads are. Ask where the good road is, and walk on it. Then you will find rest for your souls." But they said, "We will not walk on it."

**17** "I put guards over you. I said, 'Listen to the sound of the trumpet.'" But they said, "We will not listen."

**18** "Thus hear, nations! Know, people of the world, what will occur to them.

**19** Hear, earth! Look, I will bring disaster on these people. This is the result of their evil plans. They did not listen to my words. They refused my law.

**20** Why do you bring me incense from Sheba? Why do you bring me good spices from a far country? I will not accept your burnt offerings. Your sacrifices do not please me."

**21** Thus the LORD says this: "Look, I will put obstacles in front of these people. Fathers and sons will fall over them together. Neighbors and friends will die."

**22** The LORD says this: "Look, an army comes from the land of the north. A great nation will come from the far parts of the earth.

**23** The soldiers hold bows and spears. They are cruel and they have no mercy. Their noise is like the noise of the sea. They ride on horses. They are ready for war against you, Jerusalem."

**24** We heard the news about them. Our hands are weak with fear. Pain holds us like the pain of a woman who gives birth.

**25** Do not go out into the fields. Do not walk on the roads. The enemy has swords. There is terror on all sides.

**26** My people, put on rough cloth (sackcloth) and roll in ashes. Cry as a person cries for an only son. Cry with much sorrow. The destroyer will suddenly attack us.

**27** The LORD said to me, "I made you a person who tests my people as a worker tests metal. You will watch them and test their ways.

**28** All of them are very stubborn rebels. They go around and tell lies about other people. They are like bronze and iron. All of them do evil.

**29** The fire is very hot. The lead melts in the fire. But the worker tries to make the metal pure for no reason. The evil parts do not come out.

**30** People will call them 'impure silver', because the LORD refused them."

## Chapter 7

**1** This is the message that came to Jeremiah from the LORD:

**2** "Stand at the gate of the temple of the LORD. Announce this message there. Say, 'Hear the word of the LORD, all you people of Judah. You come through these gates to worship the LORD.

**3** The LORD of armies, the God of Israel, says this: Change your ways and your actions. Then I will let you live in this place.

**4** Do not trust the people who tell lies. They say, "This is the temple of the LORD. This is the temple of the LORD. This is the temple of the LORD. Thus we are safe."

**5** You must change your ways and your actions completely. You must be fair to each other.

**6** Do not be cruel to foreigners, to children who have no father, or to widows. Do not kill innocent people in this place. Do not follow other gods, because that will hurt you.

**7** If you do these things, I will let you live in this place. I will let you live in the land that I gave to your ancestors always.

**8** But look, you trust in lies that have no value.

**9** You steal and murder. You are not loyal to your wives. You tell lies when you make promises. You burn incense to Baal. You follow other gods that you did not know before.

**10** Then you come and stand in front of me in this temple that has my name. You say, "We are safe." Then you continue to do all these evil things that I hate.

**11** This temple has my name. Do you think that it is a cave where robbers hide? Look, I see what you do,' says the LORD.

**12** 'Go now to my place in Shiloh. At first my name lived there. See what I did to it because of the evil of my people Israel.

**13** You did all these things,' says the LORD. 'I spoke to you again and again, but you did not listen. I called you, but you did not answer.

**14** Thus I will do to this temple what I did to Shiloh. This temple has my name, and you trust in it. I gave this place to you and to your ancestors. But I will destroy it.

**15** I will send you away from me. I sent away all your relatives, the people of Ephraim. Thus I will also send you away.'

**16** Jeremiah, do not pray for these people. Do not cry or pray for them. Do not ask me to help them, because I will not listen to you.

**17** Do you not see what they do in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem?

**18** The children gather wood. The fathers light the fire. The women make dough to make cakes for the Queen of Heaven, a false goddess. They pour out drink offerings to other gods to make me angry.

**19** But do they hurt me?" says the LORD. "No, they hurt themselves. They cause their own shame."

**20** Thus the Lord GOD says this: "Look, I will pour out my strong anger on this place. I will pour it out on people and on animals. I will pour it out on the trees in the field and on the crops. My anger will burn like a fire, and no person will stop it."

**21** The LORD of armies, the God of Israel, says this: "Add your burnt offerings to your other sacrifices. Eat the meat yourselves.

**22** When I brought your ancestors out of Egypt, I did not speak to them only about burnt offerings and sacrifices. I did not command them only about these things.

**23** But I gave them this command: 'Obey me. Then I will be your God and you will be my people. Do all the things that I command you. Then things will go well for you.'

**24** But they did not listen. They did not pay attention. They followed the stubborn wishes of their evil hearts. They went backward and not forward.

**25** From the day that your ancestors left Egypt until today, I sent my servants the prophets to you. I sent them to you again and again, day after day.

**26** But the people did not listen to me. They did not pay attention. They were stubborn. They did more evil than their ancestors.

**27** You will tell them all these things, but they will not listen to you. You will call to them, but they will not answer you.

**28** Thus say to them, 'This nation did not obey the LORD its God. It did not learn from punishment. The people do not tell the truth. Truth disappeared from their mouths.'

**29** People of Jerusalem, cut off your hair and throw it away. Sing a sad song on the bare hills. The LORD refused and left this generation that made him angry.

**30** The people of Judah did evil in front of me," says the LORD. "They put their idols, which I hate, in the temple that has my name. They made it unclean.

**31** They built altars at Topheth in the Valley of Ben Hinnom. There they burned their sons and their daughters as sacrifices. I did not command this. I never thought of a thing like this.

**32** Thus look, a time will come," says the LORD, "when people will not call it Topheth or the Valley of Ben Hinnom. They will call it the Valley of Killing. They will bury dead people in Topheth until there is no more space.

**33** The dead bodies of these people will be food for the birds and the wild animals. No person will chase them away.

**34** I will stop the sounds of joy and happiness in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem. I will stop the voices of the bride and the bridegroom. The land will be empty."

## Chapter 8

**1** "At that time," says the LORD, "the enemy will take the bones of the kings and officials of Judah out of their graves. They will take the bones of the priests, the prophets and the people of Jerusalem.

**2** They will spread the bones on the ground under the sun, the moon and all the stars. The people loved and served these things in the sky. They followed them, asked them for help and worshipped them. No person will gather the bones or bury them. They will be like garbage on the ground.

**3** I will send the survivors of this evil nation to many places. All these survivors will prefer to die than to live," says the LORD of armies.

**4** "Say to them, 'The LORD says this: When people fall down, do they not stand up again? When a person goes the wrong way, does he not turn back?

**5** Then why do these people of Jerusalem always turn away from me? They hold on to lies. They refuse to come back.

**6** I listened carefully, but they do not say what is right. No person is sorry for his evil. No person says, "What did I do?" Each person goes his own way, like a horse that runs into battle.

**7** Even the stork in the sky knows its seasons. The dove, the swift and the thrush know when to move to another place. But my people do not know the laws of the LORD.

**8** How can you say, "We are wise, because we have the law of the LORD"? Look, the scribes wrote lies with their pens.

**9** The wise men will feel shame. They will be afraid, and the enemy will catch them. They refused the word of the LORD. Thus what wisdom do they have?

**10** Thus I will give their wives to other men. I will give their fields to new owners. All of them want more money, from the least important to the most important. From the prophets to the priests, all of them tell lies.

**11** They try to heal the injuries of my people, but they do not make them better. They say, "All is well, all is well!" But all is not well.

**12** Do they feel shame for the evil acts that I hate? No, they feel no shame at all. They do not even know how to feel shame. Thus they will fall with the other people who fall. When I punish them, they will fall down,' says the LORD.

**13** 'I will take away their harvest,' says the LORD. 'There will be no grapes on the vine. There will be no figs on the fig tree. The leaves will dry up. I will take away the things that I gave them.'"

**14** The people say, "Why do we sit here? Come together! Let us go into the cities with strong walls and die there. The LORD our God decided that we must die. He gave us poisoned water to drink, because we sinned against the LORD.

**15** We hoped for peace, but nothing good came. We hoped for a time when we become well again, but only terror came."

**16** People hear the noise of the horses of the enemy from the city of Dan. All the land shakes at the sound of their strong horses. They come and destroy the land and all things in it. They destroy the city and all the people who live there.

**17** "Look, I will send poisonous snakes among you. You cannot control them with magic. They will bite you," says the LORD.

**18** My sorrow has no cure. My heart is sick.

**19** Listen to the cry of my people from a land far away. They ask, "Is the LORD not in Zion? Is her King not there?" The LORD answers, "Why did they make me angry with their idols? Why did they worship foreign gods that have no value?"

**20** The people say, "The harvest is past. The summer is over. But no person saved us."

**21** My people are hurt, thus I am hurt. I am very sad. Fear holds me.

**22** Is there no medicine in Gilead? Is there no doctor there? Then why are my people not healed?

## Chapter 9

**1** I wish that my head was a spring of water. I wish that my eyes were a fountain of tears. Then I could cry day and night for my people whom the enemy killed.

**2** I wish that I had a place to stay in the desert, like a house for travelers. Then I could leave my people and go away from them. All of them are not loyal to God. They are a group of disloyal people.

**3** "They use their tongues like bows to shoot lies. They do not use truth to become powerful in the land. They do one evil thing after another. They do not know me," says the LORD.

**4** "Be careful of your neighbors. Do not trust your brothers. Each brother tricks his brother. Each neighbor tells lies about other people.

**5** Each person lies to his neighbor. No person tells the truth. They taught their tongues to tell lies. They make themselves tired with their sins.

**6** You live among liars. Because of their lies, they refuse to know me," says the LORD.

**7** Thus the LORD of armies says this: "Look, I will test them as a worker makes metal pure in the fire. What else can I do because of the sin of my people?

**8** Their tongues are like arrows that kill. They tell lies. A person speaks kind words to his neighbor. But in his heart he plans a trap for him.

**9** Must I not punish them for these things?" says the LORD. "Must I not punish a nation like this?"

**10** I will cry and be sad for the mountains. I will sing a sad song for the fields in the desert. Fire burned them. No person goes through them. People do not hear the sound of cattle. The birds and the animals ran away. They are gone.

**11** "I will make Jerusalem a pile of ruins. Jackals will live there. I will make the cities of Judah empty. No person will live in them."

**12** Who is wise enough to understand these things? To whom did the LORD speak, so that he can explain them? Why is the land destroyed? Why is it burned like a desert that no person goes through?

**13** The LORD says, "The people left my law that I gave them. They did not obey me or follow my law.

**14** They followed the stubborn wishes of their own hearts. They followed the Baals, as their ancestors taught them."

**15** Thus the LORD of armies, the God of Israel, says this: "Look, I will make these people eat bitter food. I will give them poisoned water to drink.

**16** I will send them among nations that they and their ancestors did not know. I will send enemies with swords after them until I destroy them."

**17** The LORD of armies says this: "Think about this! Call the women who sing sad songs at funerals. Send for the most skilled of them.

**18** Tell them to come quickly and cry for us. Then our eyes will have many tears. Water will flow from our eyes."

**19** People hear the sound of loud cries from Zion: "The enemy destroyed us! We feel much shame! We must leave our land. The enemy destroyed our houses."

**20** Women, hear the word of the LORD. Listen to the words of his mouth. Teach your daughters how to cry loudly. Teach each other a sad song.

**21** Death came in through our windows. It came into our strong buildings. It kills the children in the streets. It kills the young men in the public squares.

**22** Say this: "The LORD says, 'Dead bodies will lie like garbage on the open fields. They will be like grain that the farmer cuts and leaves behind. No person will gather them.'"

**23** The LORD says this: "Wise people must not be proud of their wisdom. Strong people must not be proud of their strength. Rich people must not be proud of their riches.

**24** But if a person wants to be proud, he must be proud of this: he understands and knows me. He knows that I am the LORD. I show love, justice and goodness on the earth. These things make me happy," says the LORD.

**25** "Look, a time will come," says the LORD, "when I will punish all people who received circumcision only in their bodies.

**26** I will punish Egypt, Judah, Edom, Ammon and Moab. I will punish the people who live in the desert and cut the hair on the sides of their heads. All these nations did not truly receive circumcision. And all the people of Israel did not receive circumcision in their hearts."

## Chapter 10

**1** People of Israel, hear the word that the LORD speaks to you.

**2** The LORD says this: "Do not learn the ways of the other nations. Do not be afraid of signs in the sky, although the nations are afraid of them.

**3** The customs of those people have no value. They cut down a tree in the forest. A worker shapes the wood with his tool.

**4** They decorate it with silver and gold. They use nails and hammers to fix the idol in its place, so that it does not fall.

**5** Their idols are like scarecrows in a field of melons. The idols cannot speak. People must carry them, because they cannot walk. Do not be afraid of them. They cannot hurt you, and they cannot help you."

**6** LORD, no person is like you. You are great. Your name is great and powerful.

**7** King of the nations, all people must respect you with fear. This is what you deserve. Among all the wise men of the nations and in all their kingdoms, no person is like you.

**8** The people of the nations are stupid and foolish. They learn from idols of wood that have no value.

**9** They bring silver from Tarshish and gold from Uphaz. Skilled workers make the idols. They dress them in blue and purple clothes. Skilled men make all of them.

**10** But the LORD is the true God. He is the God who lives, and the King who lives always. When he is angry, the earth shakes. The nations cannot stand against his anger.

**11** Tell them this: "These gods did not make the heavens and the earth. They will disappear from the earth and from under the heavens."

**12** The LORD made the earth with his power. He made the world with his wisdom. He spread out the sky with his knowledge.

**13** When he speaks, the waters in the sky make a loud noise. He causes clouds to come up from the ends of the earth. He makes lightning for the rain. He brings out the wind from his storehouses.

**14** All people are stupid and know nothing. Each metal worker feels shame because of the idols that he makes. His idols are false. They have no breath in them.

**15** They have no value. People laugh at them. When the LORD judges them, they will die.

**16** But the God of Jacob is not like these idols. He made all things. Israel is the tribe that belongs to him. His name is the LORD of armies.

**17** People of Jerusalem, the enemy surrounds you. Pick up your bags and get ready to leave the land.

**18** The LORD says this: "Look, now I will throw the people out of this land. I will cause them to suffer, so that they will learn."

**19** I have much trouble because of my injury! No one can heal my wound. But I said, "This is my sickness. I must accept it."

**20** The enemy destroyed my tent. They broke all its ropes. My children left me. They are gone. No person is here to put up my tent again or to hang its curtains.

**21** The leaders are stupid. They did not ask the LORD for help. Thus they do not have success. All their people go away in different directions.

**22** Listen! The news comes. A great noise comes from the land of the north. The enemy army will make the cities of Judah empty. Jackals will live there.

**23** LORD, I know that a person does not control his own life. A person cannot guide his own steps.

**24** LORD, correct me, but be fair. Do not correct me in your anger. If you do, you will destroy me.

**25** Pour out your anger on the nations that do not know you. Pour it out on the people who do not pray to you. They destroyed the people of Jacob. They destroyed them completely. They made their land empty.

## Chapter 11

**1** This is the message that came to Jeremiah from the LORD:

**2** "Listen to the words of this agreement (covenant). Tell them to the people of Judah and to the people who live in Jerusalem.

**3** Tell them that the LORD, the God of Israel, says this: 'I will curse the person who does not obey the words of this covenant.

**4** I gave these words to your ancestors when I brought them out of Egypt. Egypt was like a hot furnace for iron. I said, "Obey me and do all the things that I command you. Then you will be my people, and I will be your God."

**5** Then I will do what I promised to your ancestors. I promised to give them a land with much milk and honey. You live in that land today.'" I answered, "Yes, LORD. Let it be so."

**6** Then the LORD said to me, "Announce all these words in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem. Say, 'Listen to the words of this covenant and obey them.

**7** I brought your ancestors out of Egypt. From that time until today, I warned them again and again. I said, "Obey me."

**8** But they did not obey. They did not pay attention. Each of them followed the stubborn wishes of his evil heart. Thus I caused all the curses of this covenant to come on them. I commanded them to obey the covenant, but they did not obey it.'"

**9** Then the LORD said to me, "The people of Judah and the people of Jerusalem made a secret plan against me.

**10** They went back to the sins of their ancestors. Their ancestors refused to obey my words. They followed other gods and served them. The people of Israel and the people of Judah broke the covenant that I made with their ancestors."

**11** Thus the LORD says this: "Look, I will bring a disaster on them. They will not escape from it. They will call to me for help, but I will not listen to them.

**12** Then the people of the cities of Judah and Jerusalem will go to their gods. They burn incense to those gods, and they will call to them for help. But those gods will not save them when they have trouble.

**13** Judah, you have as many gods as you have cities. People of Jerusalem, you built as many altars to Baal as there are streets in Jerusalem. You burn incense to that shameful god.

**14** Jeremiah, do not pray for these people. Do not cry or pray for them. When they call to me because of their trouble, I will not listen.

**15** My people, whom I love, have no right to be in my temple. They do many evil things. Can sacrifices of meat take away your punishment? When you do evil, you are happy.

**16** The LORD called you 'A green olive tree, beautiful with good fruit.' But with a loud noise like a storm, he will set the tree on fire. The fire will break its branches.

**17** The LORD of armies planted you. But he announced that disaster will come on you. The people of Israel and the people of Judah did evil. They made me angry when they burned incense to Baal."

**18** The LORD told me about the plan of my enemies. Thus I knew about it. He showed me what they did.

**19** I was like a gentle lamb that people lead to its death. I did not know that they made plans against me. They said, "Let us destroy the tree and its fruit. Let us kill him. Then people will not remember his name again."

**20** But LORD of armies, you judge fairly. You test the thoughts and the hearts of people. Let me see you punish them. I gave my case to you.

**21** The men of Anathoth want to kill you, Jeremiah. They say, "Do not speak as a prophet in the name of the LORD. If you do, we will kill you." Thus the LORD says this about them.

**22** The LORD of armies says this: "Look, I will punish them. Their young men will die in war. Their sons and their daughters will die from hunger.

**23** No person from Anathoth will survive. I will bring disaster on the men of Anathoth in the year that I punish them."

## Chapter 12

**1** LORD, you are always fair when I complain to you. But I want to talk to you about justice. Why do wicked people have success? Why do all disloyal people live easy lives?

**2** You plant them, and they put down roots. They grow and they have fruit. They speak about you, but they do not love you in their hearts.

**3** But LORD, you know me. You see me and you test my heart. You know that I am loyal to you. Pull out the wicked people like sheep that people will kill. Make them ready for the day when people kill them.

**4** How long must the land be dry? How long must the grass in each field die? The animals and the birds die because the people in the land are wicked. The people say, "God will not see what occurs to us."

**5** The LORD answered, "You ran with men on foot and they made you tired. Thus how can you run against horses? You fall down in a land of peace. Thus what will you do in the thick bushes by the Jordan River?

**6** Even your brothers and your own family were not loyal to you. They also shout loudly against you. Do not trust them, though they speak kind words to you.

**7** I left my temple. I left the people that belong to me. I gave the people that I love to their enemies.

**8** The people that belong to me became like a lion in the forest. They roar at me. Thus I hate them.

**9** The people that belong to me are like a bird with many colors. Other birds attack it from all sides. Go and gather all the wild animals. Bring them to eat.

**10** Many rulers destroyed my vineyard. They walked on my field. They made my beautiful field an empty desert.

**11** They made it empty. It is dry and sad in front of me. All the land is empty, but no person cares.

**12** Destroyers come over all the bare hills in the desert. The sword of the LORD will kill people from 1 end of the land to the other end. No person will be safe.

**13** The people planted wheat, but they will cut only thorns. They worked very hard, but they will get nothing. They will feel shame because of their harvest. This is because of the strong anger of the LORD."

**14** The LORD says this: "Wicked nations live around my people Israel. They attack the land that I gave to Israel. Look, I will pull them out of their land. I will pull the people of Judah out from among them.

**15** But after I pull them out, I will have mercy on them again. I will bring each of them back to his own land and his own property.

**16** These nations taught my people to make promises in the name of Baal. But if they learn the ways of my people well, then I will let them live among my people. They must make promises in my name. They must say, 'As surely as the LORD lives.'

**17** But if a nation does not obey, I will pull it out completely and destroy it," says the LORD.

## Chapter 13

**1** The LORD said to me, "Go and get a linen belt. Put the belt around your waist. Do not put the belt in water."

**2** Thus I got a belt as the LORD told me. I put the belt around my waist.

**3** Then the word of the LORD came to me a second time. The LORD said,

**4** "Take the belt that you got, which is around your waist. Go to the Euphrates River. Hide the belt there in a hole in the rock."

**5** Thus I went and hid the belt near the Euphrates, as the LORD told me.

**6** After many days, the LORD said to me, "Go to the Euphrates. Take from there the belt that I told you to hide there."

**7** Then I went to the Euphrates and I dug. I took the belt from the place where I hid it. The belt was rotten. It was not good for anything.

**8** Then the word of the LORD came to me. The LORD said,

**9** "The LORD says this: In the same way, I will damage the pride of Judah and the great pride of Jerusalem.

**10** These evil people refuse to hear my words. They follow the stubborn thoughts of their hearts. They follow other gods, and they serve and worship those gods. Thus these people will be like this belt, which is not good for anything.

**11** A belt holds tightly to the waist of a man. In the same way, I caused all the people of Israel and Judah to hold tightly to me," says the LORD. "I wanted them to be my people. I wanted them to bring me fame, praise and honor. But they did not listen.

**12** "Thus speak this word to them: 'The LORD, the God of Israel, says this: Every wine jar will be full of wine.' The people will say to you, 'We certainly know that every wine jar will be full of wine.'

**13** Then say to them, 'The LORD says this: I will fill all the people of this land with drunkenness. I will fill the kings who sit on the throne of David, the priests, the prophets and all the people of Jerusalem.

**14** I will hit them against each other, the fathers and the sons together,' says the LORD. 'I will not show pity. I will not spare them. I will not show mercy. I will destroy them.'"

**15** Listen and pay attention. Do not be proud, because the LORD spoke.

**16** Give honor to the LORD your God before he causes darkness. Do this before your feet fall on the dark mountains. You look for light, but he will change the light into deep darkness.

**17** But if you do not listen, I will cry in secret because of your pride. My eyes will cry much, and tears will flow from them. I will cry because the enemy will take the flock of the LORD away as prisoners.

**18** Say to the king and to the queen mother, "Make yourselves humble. Sit down. Your beautiful crowns will fall from your heads."

**19** The enemy will close the cities of the Negev. No person will open them. The enemy will take all of Judah away as prisoners. The enemy will take all the people away.

**20** Jerusalem, look up and see the enemies who come from the north. Where is the flock that God gave to you, your beautiful flock?

**21** What will you say when the LORD puts over you the people that you taught to be your friends? Pain will hold you like the pain of a woman who gives birth to a child.

**22** You will ask in your heart, "Why did these things happen to me?" These things happened because of your many sins. Thus the enemy pulled up your skirts and hurt you.

**23** An Ethiopian cannot change the color of his skin. A leopard cannot change its spots. In the same way, you cannot do good, because you usually do evil.

**24** "I will scatter the people as the desert wind blows away dry straw.

**25** This is your punishment. This is the part that I give to you," says the LORD. "This is because you forgot me and you trusted in lies.

**26** I will pull your skirts up over your face, and all people will see your shame.

**27** I saw your adultery and your desire for other gods. I saw your shameful worship of idols on the hills and in the fields. Jerusalem, you will have much trouble! You will not become clean. How long will this continue?"

## Chapter 14

**1** This is the word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah about the time with no rain.

**2** "Judah is sad, and her cities become weak. The people sit on the ground in sadness. The people of Jerusalem cry out for help.

**3** The rich people send their servants for water. The servants go to the wells, but they find no water. They go back with empty jars. They feel shame and do not know what to do. They cover their heads.

**4** The ground is dry and cracked, because there is no rain in the land. Thus the farmers feel shame, and they cover their heads.

**5** The female deer gives birth in the field. Then she leaves her young, because there is no grass.

**6** The wild donkeys stand on the bare hills. They breathe in the air like jackals. Their eyes become weak, because there is no food."

**7** LORD, our sins show that we are guilty. But help us for the honor of your name. We turned away from you many times. We sinned against you.

**8** You are the hope of Israel. You save Israel in the time of trouble. Why are you like a stranger in the land? Why are you like a traveler who stays only 1 night?

**9** Why are you like a man who does not know what to do? Why are you like a strong soldier who cannot save? LORD, you are among us. We are your people, and we have your name. Do not leave us!

**10** The LORD says this about these people: "They like to go away from me. They do not stop their feet. Thus the LORD does not accept them. Now the LORD will remember their evil and he will punish their sins."

**11** Then the LORD said to me, "Do not pray for good things for these people.

**12** When they do not eat food (fast), I will not hear their cry. When they give burnt offerings and grain offerings, I will not accept the offerings. I will destroy the people with war, famine and disease."

**13** Then I said, "Ah, Lord GOD! The prophets tell the people, 'You will not see war. You will not have famine. The LORD will give you true peace in this place.'"

**14** Then the LORD said to me, "The prophets tell lies in my name. I did not send them. I did not command them. I did not speak to them. They tell you false visions and false magic. They tell you things that have no value, which come from their own minds.

**15** Thus the LORD says this about the prophets who speak in my name: I did not send them. But they say, 'No war and no famine will come into this land.' War and famine will kill those prophets.

**16** The people who hear these prophets will die in the streets of Jerusalem because of famine and war. Their bodies will lie in the streets. No person will bury them, their wives, their sons or their daughters. I will cause the evil of the people to come back on them.

**17** "Speak this word to the people: 'Let tears come from my eyes night and day, and let them not stop. My people are like a young woman with a very bad wound. She has a terrible injury.

**18** If I go into the fields, I see people that the sword killed. If I go into the city, I see people that are sick because of famine. The prophet and the priest go to a land that they do not know.'"

**19** LORD, did you fully reject Judah? Do you hate Zion? Why did you hurt us so badly that we cannot become well? We hoped for peace, but nothing good came. We hoped for a time when we become well again, but only terror came.

**20** LORD, we know that we are evil. We know the sins of our ancestors. We sinned against you.

**21** For the honor of your name, do not reject us. Do not dishonor your glorious throne. Remember your covenant with us, and do not break it.

**22** The false gods of the nations cannot cause rain. The sky cannot give rain by itself. LORD our God, only you can do these things. Thus we hope in you, because you made all these things.

## Chapter 15

**1** Then the LORD said to me, "Moses and Samuel could stand in front of me and pray for these people. But even then I will not change my mind about the people. Send the people away from me! Let them go!

**2** The people will ask you, 'Where will we go?' Then tell them, 'The LORD says this: The people that I chose for death will die. The people that I chose for the sword will die by the sword. The people that I chose for famine will die by famine. The people that I chose to be prisoners will become prisoners.'

**3** "I will send 4 kinds of destroyers against them," says the LORD. "I will send the sword to kill them. I will send the dogs to pull their bodies away. I will send the birds of the air and the wild animals to eat and destroy their bodies.

**4** I will cause all the kingdoms of the earth to be afraid of these people. This is because of what Manasseh did in Jerusalem. Manasseh was the son of Hezekiah, king of Judah.

**5** "Jerusalem, no person will show pity to you. No person will cry for you. No person will stop to ask, 'How are you?'

**6** You left me," says the LORD. "You turned away from me. Thus I will lift my hand against you and I will destroy you. I am weary of mercy to you.

**7** I will separate the people as a farmer separates grain from straw at the gates of the land. I will take their children away. I will destroy my people, because they do not stop their evil ways.

**8** Their widows will become more than the sand of the sea. At noon I will bring a destroyer against the mothers of young men. I will suddenly bring fear and terror on them.

**9** A mother of 7 sons will become weak and she will die. Her life will stop while she is still young. She will feel shame and fear. I will cause the enemy to kill the people who stay alive," says the LORD.

**10** My mother, I have much trouble because you gave birth to me! All the people in the land argue and fight with me. I did not lend money to any person, and no person lent money to me. But all the people curse me.

**11** The LORD said, "I will certainly help you for a good purpose. In the time of trouble and pain, I will cause your enemies to ask you for help.

**12** A person cannot break iron. A person cannot break the iron and bronze from the north.

**13** I will give your wealth and your treasures to your enemies. They will take them without cost. This is because of all your sins in all your land.

**14** I will cause you to serve your enemies in a land that you do not know. My anger is like a fire that will burn against you."

**15** LORD, you know about me. Remember me and help me. Punish the people who attack me. You are patient, but do not let them kill me. Know that people insult me because I serve you.

**16** Your words came to me, and I took them into me. Your words caused much joy in my heart. LORD God of armies, I carry your name.

**17** I did not sit with the people who laugh and have fun. I sat alone because your hand was on me. You filled me with anger against their sin.

**18** Why does my pain continue always? Why does my wound not become well? Will you be to me like a stream that becomes dry? Will you be like water that stops?

**19** Thus the LORD says this: "If you come back to me, I will let you serve me again. Then you will stand in front of me. If you speak good words and not bad words, you will speak for me. Let the people come back to you. But do not go back to them.

**20** I will make you strong like a bronze wall against these people. They will fight against you, but they will not defeat you. I am with you to save you and rescue you," says the LORD.

**21** "I will rescue you from the power of evil people. I will save you from the power of cruel people."

## Chapter 16

**1** Then the word of the LORD came to me. The LORD said,

**2** "Do not marry a wife. Do not have sons or daughters in this place."

**3** The LORD says this about the sons and daughters whom their mothers bear in this place. He also speaks about their mothers and fathers in this land:

**4** "They will die from terrible diseases. No person will cry for them or bury them. Their bodies will lie on the ground like animal waste. War and famine will kill them. Their dead bodies will become food for the birds and the wild animals."

**5** The LORD says this: "Do not go into a house where people mourn for the dead. Do not go to cry or show sorrow for them. I took away my peace, my love and my mercy from these people," says the LORD.

**6** "Important people and common people will die in this land. No person will bury them or mourn for them. No person will cut himself or shave his head for them.

**7** No person will give food to the people who mourn, to comfort them for their dead. No person will give them a cup of wine to comfort them for a dead father or mother.

**8** Do not go into a house where people have a feast. Do not sit with them to eat and drink."

**9** The LORD of armies, the God of Israel, says this: "Look! In your time, I will stop the sounds of joy and happiness in this place. I will stop the voices of the bridegroom and the bride. You will see this.

**10** "You will tell these people all these words. Then they will ask you, 'Why did the LORD say that this terrible disaster will come to us? What did we do wrong? What sin did we do against the LORD our God?'

**11** Then say to them, 'Your ancestors left me,' says the LORD. 'They followed other gods. They served and worshipped those gods. They left me and did not obey my law.

**12** You did worse things than your ancestors. Each of you follows the stubborn thoughts of your evil heart. You do not listen to me.

**13** Thus I will throw you out of this land. I will send you into a land that you and your ancestors do not know. There you will serve other gods day and night. I will not show you kindness.'

**14** "But the days will come," says the LORD, "when people will not say, 'The LORD lives.' The LORD brought the people of Israel out of the land of Egypt.

**15** Then they will say, 'The LORD lives, who brought the people of Israel out of the land of the north. He brought them from all the lands where he sent them.' I will bring them back to the land that I gave to their ancestors.

**16** "Look! I will send for many fishermen," says the LORD, "and they will catch the people. After that, I will send for many hunters. They will hunt the people on every mountain and hill and in the holes of the rocks.

**17** I see everything that they do. They cannot hide their actions from me. They cannot hide their sins from my eyes.

**18** First I will punish them 2 times for their sins. This is because they made my land unclean with their dead idols, which I hate. They filled my land with their evil idols."

**19** LORD, you are my strength and my strong place. You are my safe place in the time of trouble. The nations will come to you from the ends of the earth. They will say, "Our ancestors had only false gods. Those gods had no value and they gave no help.

**20** Can a person make his own gods? No, those things are not gods!"

**21** "Thus I will teach them," says the LORD. "This time I will cause them to know my power and my strength. Then they will know that my name is the LORD."

**1** "Judah wrote its sin with an iron pen. It cut its sin with a diamond point on their hearts. It cut its sin on the corners of their altars.

**2** Their children remember their altars and their Asherah poles. These are near the green trees on the high hills.

**3** My mountain in the land, I will give your wealth and your treasures to your enemies. I will give your high places to them, because you sinned in all your land.

**6** He will be like a small bush in the desert. He will not see good things come. He will live in the dry places of the desert, in a salt land where no people live.

**7** "Blessed is the man who trusts in the LORD. The LORD is his hope.

**8** He will be like a tree that grows near water. The tree sends its roots into the stream. It is not afraid when the heat comes. Its leaves stay green. It does not worry in a year with no rain. It always gives fruit."

**9** The human heart is more false than all things. It is very sick. No person can know it fully.

**10** "I, the LORD, look into the heart. I examine the mind. I give each person what his actions deserve. I give him the results of his actions."

**11** A partridge sits on eggs that it did not lay. In the same way, a man gets rich in a wrong way. In the middle of his life, he will lose his wealth. At the end of his life, he will be a fool.

**12** Our temple is a glorious throne. It is high from the start.

**13** LORD, you are the hope of Israel. All the people who leave you will feel shame. The people who turn away from you will be like names in the dust. This is because they left the LORD, the spring of water that gives life.

**14** LORD, heal me, and then I will be well. Save me, and then I will be safe. You are the one that I praise.

**15** The people say to me, "Where is the word of the LORD? Let it come now!"

**16** I did not run away from my work as your shepherd. I did not want the day of disaster. You know this. You know what I said.

**17** Do not be a terror to me. You are my safe place in the time of disaster.

**18** Let the people who attack me feel shame, but do not let me feel shame. Let them be afraid, but do not let me be afraid. Bring the day of disaster on them. Destroy them 2 times.

**19** The LORD said to me, "Go and stand at the People's Gate, where the kings of Judah go in and out. Then stand at all the gates of Jerusalem.

**20** Say to the people, 'Listen to the word of the LORD, you kings of Judah and all the people of Judah. Listen, all the people of Jerusalem who come in through these gates.

**21** The LORD says this: Be careful! Do not carry a load on the Sabbath day. Do not bring a load in through the gates of Jerusalem.

**22** Do not carry a load out of your houses on the Sabbath day. Do not do any work on the Sabbath. Keep the Sabbath day holy, as I commanded your ancestors.'"

**23** But the ancestors did not obey or listen. They were stubborn. They did not listen to me or accept my instruction.

**24** "But you must obey me fully," says the LORD. "Do not bring a load in through the gates of this city on the Sabbath day. Keep the Sabbath day holy and do no work on that day.

**25** If you do this, then kings and princes who sit on the throne of David will come in through the gates of this city. They will come in chariots and on horses. The men of Judah and the people of Jerusalem will come with them. People will live in this city always.

**26** People will come from the cities of Judah and from the area around Jerusalem. They will come from the land of Benjamin, the western hills, the mountains and the Negev. They will bring burnt offerings, sacrifices, grain offerings and incense. They will bring offerings of thanks to the house of the LORD.

**27** But you must obey me and keep the Sabbath day holy. Do not carry a load in through the gates of Jerusalem on the Sabbath. If you do not obey, I will start a fire in the gates of Jerusalem. The fire will burn the palaces of Jerusalem, and no person will stop it."

## Chapter 18

**1** This is the word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD:

**2** "Go down to the house of the potter. There I will give you my message."

**3** Thus I went down to the house of the potter. I saw him at work on his wheel.

**4** He made a pot from clay, but the pot became bad in his hands. Thus the potter made the clay into a different pot, as he thought best.

**5** Then the word of the LORD came to me. The LORD said,

**6** "People of Israel, I can do with you as this potter does with the clay," says the LORD. "The clay is in the hand of the potter. In the same way, you are in my hand, people of Israel.

**7** Sometimes I say that I will pull up, break down and destroy a nation or a kingdom.

**8** But if that nation stops its evil, I will change my mind. I will not bring the disaster that I planned for it.

**9** At other times I say that I will build up and plant a nation or a kingdom.

**10** But if that nation does evil and does not obey me, I will change my mind. I will not do the good things that I planned for it.

**11** "Now speak to the men of Judah and the people of Jerusalem. Tell them, 'The LORD says this: Look! I prepare a disaster against you. I make a plan against you. Each of you must stop your evil ways. Change your ways and your actions, and do good.'

**12** But the people will say, 'It is no use! We will follow our own plans. Each of us will do what his stubborn, evil heart wants.'"

**13** Thus the LORD says this: "Ask among the nations. Did any person hear of things like this? The people of Israel did a very terrible thing.

**14** The snow on the mountains of Lebanon does not go away from the rocks. The cold streams that flow from far places do not become dry.

**15** But my people forgot me. They burn incense to false gods. These false gods caused my people to fall in their ways, on the old roads. They caused my people to walk on bad paths, not on good roads.

**16** Thus their land will become empty. People will always whistle at it in shock. All the people who go past it will feel shock and shake their heads.

**17** I will scatter my people in front of their enemies, as the east wind blows dust. In the time of their disaster, I will turn my back to them. I will not show them my face."

**18** Then the people said, "Come, let us make plans against Jeremiah. We will always have priests to teach the law. We will always have wise men to give advice. We will always have prophets to speak the word. Come, let us attack him with words. Let us not listen to anything that he says."

**19** LORD, listen to me! Hear what my enemies say against me.

**20** Must people do evil to a person who did good to them? But they dug a hole to kill me. Remember that I stood in front of you to speak for them. I asked you to stop your anger against them.

**21** Thus let their children die in famine. Let the sword kill them. Let their wives lose their children and become widows. Let disease kill their men. Let the sword kill their young men in battle.

**22** Suddenly send an army against them. Let people hear the cries from their houses. This is because they dug a hole to catch me. They hid traps for my feet.

**23** But LORD, you know all their plans to kill me. Do not forgive their evil. Do not remove their sin from your sight. Let them fall in front of you. Punish them in the time of your anger.

## Chapter 19

**1** The LORD says this: "Go and buy a clay jar from a potter. Take with you some of the leaders of the people and some of the leaders of the priests.

**2** Go out to the Valley of Ben Hinnom, near the entrance of the Potsherd Gate. There, speak the words that I tell you.

**3** Say, 'Kings of Judah and people of Jerusalem, listen to the word of the LORD. The LORD of armies, the God of Israel, says this: Look! I will bring a disaster on this place. The ears of all the people who hear about it will ring.

**6** Thus the days will come,' says the LORD, 'when people will not call this place Topheth or the Valley of Ben Hinnom. They will call it the Valley of Killing.

**7** In this place I will destroy the plans of Judah and Jerusalem. I will cause their enemies to kill them with the sword. Their enemies want to kill them. I will give their dead bodies as food to the birds and the wild animals.

**8** I will make this city empty, and people will whistle at it in shock. All the people who go past it will feel shock. They will whistle because of all its wounds.

**9** Their enemies will surround the city and try to kill them. The people in the city will have no food. Thus I will cause them to eat the bodies of their sons and their daughters. Each of them will eat the body of his friend.'

**10** "Then break the jar in front of the men who go with you.

**11** Say to them, 'The LORD of armies says this: I will break this people and this city as a person breaks a clay jar. No person can repair it. People will bury their dead in Topheth until there is no more space.

**12** I will do this to this place and to its people,' says the LORD. 'I will make this city like Topheth.

**13** The houses of Jerusalem and the houses of the kings of Judah will become unclean like Topheth. This is because the people burned incense on the roofs of these houses to the stars of the sky. They poured out drink offerings to other gods.'"

**14** Then Jeremiah came back from Topheth, where the LORD sent him to prophesy. Jeremiah stood in the courtyard of the house of the LORD. He said to all the people,

**15** "The LORD of armies, the God of Israel, says this: 'Look! I will bring on this city and its villages all the disaster that I said. This is because the people are stubborn and do not listen to my words.'"

## Chapter 20

**1** Pashhur the son of Immer was a priest. He was the chief officer in the house of the LORD. Pashhur heard Jeremiah prophesy these things.

**2** Then Pashhur hit Jeremiah the prophet. Pashhur put Jeremiah in wooden stocks at the Upper Gate of Benjamin, near the house of the LORD.

**3** On the next day, Pashhur released Jeremiah from the stocks. Then Jeremiah said to him, "The LORD does not call you Pashhur. The LORD calls you Magor-Missabib, which means 'terror on every side.'

**4** The LORD says this: 'I will cause you to be a terror to yourself and to all your friends. Your enemies will kill your friends with the sword, and you will see it. I will give all of Judah to the king of Babylon. He will take the people to Babylon as prisoners, or he will kill them with the sword.

**5** I will give all the wealth of this city to their enemies. I will give them all its products and valuable things. I will give them all the treasures of the kings of Judah. The enemies will take these things and carry them to Babylon.

**6** Pashhur, you and all the people in your house will go to Babylon as prisoners. You will die there and people will bury you there. Your friends will also die there. You told lies to them as a prophet.'"

**7** LORD, you persuaded me, and I let you persuade me. You are stronger than I am, and you won. All day people laugh at me. All the people mock me.

**8** Each time that I speak, I shout. I shout, "Violence and destruction!" Thus the word of the LORD causes people to insult me and laugh at me all day.

**9** Sometimes I say, "I will not speak about the LORD. I will not speak in his name again." But then his word is in my heart like a fire. It burns in my bones. I become tired when I try to keep it in. I cannot keep it in.

**10** I hear many people whisper, "Terror on every side! Tell people about him! We will tell people about him!" All my friends wait for me to fall. They say, "Possibly he will make a mistake. Then we will defeat him and get our revenge on him."

**11** But the LORD is with me like a strong soldier. Thus the people who attack me will fall. They will not win. They will be very ashamed because they will fail. People will always remember their shame.

**12** LORD of armies, you test the people who do right. You see the heart and the mind. Let me see you punish my enemies, because I gave my problem to you.

**13** Sing to the LORD! Praise the LORD! He saves the poor person from the power of evil people.

**14** Let there be a curse on the day when my mother gave birth to me! Do not let anyone bless the day when my mother gave birth to me!

**15** Let there be a curse on the man who brought the news to my father. He said, "Your wife gave birth to a son!" He caused my father to be very glad.

**16** Let that man be like the cities that the LORD destroyed without mercy. Let him hear a cry of pain in the morning and a shout of war at noon.

**17** This is because he did not kill me before my mother gave birth to me. Then my mother will be my grave. I will always stay in her body.

**18** Why did I come out of my mother's body? Why did I come to see trouble and sorrow? Why must my life end in shame?

I will also give his officials and the people who stay alive after the disease, war and famine. I will give them to their enemies who want to kill them. Nebuchadnezzar will kill them with the sword. **13** You live above the valley, on the rock of the plain. Look! I am against you,' says the LORD. 'You say, "No person will attack us. No person will come into our homes."

**4** If you obey these commands, then kings who sit on the throne of David will come in through the gates of this palace. They will come in chariots and on horses, with their officials and their people.

**7** I will send destroyers against you. Each destroyer will have his weapons. They will cut down your best cedar beams and throw them into the fire.

**8** "People from many nations will go past this city. They will ask each other, 'Why did the LORD do this to this great city?'

**9** Then the people will answer, 'This is because the people of the city did not obey the covenant of the LORD their God. They worshipped and served other gods.'"

**10** Do not cry for the dead king. Do not mourn for him. But cry much for the king who goes away. He will not come back. He will not see his native country again.

**11** The LORD says this about Shallum son of Josiah. Shallum became king of Judah after his father Josiah. Shallum went away from this place. "He will not come back here again.

**14** You say, 'I will build a big palace for myself with large upper rooms.' You cut windows in it. You cover the walls with cedar wood and paint them red.

**15** A big cedar palace does not make you a good king. Your father had food and drink. He did what was fair and right, and things went well for him.

**16** He helped the poor and needy people, and things went well for him. That is what it means to know me," says the LORD.

**17** "But you think only about money that you can get dishonestly. You kill people who did no wrong. You are cruel and violent to people."

**18** Thus the LORD says this about Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah: "People will not mourn for him. They will not say, 'Ah, my brother!' or, 'Ah, my sister!' They will not mourn for him. They will not say, 'Ah, master!' or, 'Ah, his glory!'

**19** People will bury him like a donkey. They will pull his body away and throw it outside the gates of Jerusalem."

**20** "People of Jerusalem, go up to Lebanon and cry out. Shout in Bashan. Cry out from Abarim, because the enemy destroyed all your allies.

**21** I spoke to you when things were good for you. But you said, 'I will not listen!' You did this from the time that you were young. You did not obey me.

**22** The wind will blow away all your leaders. Your allies will become prisoners. Then you will feel shame and fear because of all your evil.

**23** You live in Lebanon. You make your home in the cedar trees. But you will cry out when pain comes on you. The pain will be like the pain of a woman who gives birth to a child."

**24** "As surely as I live," says the LORD, "I will pull you away, Coniah son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah. Possibly you are like a ring with my seal on my right hand. But I will pull you off.

**25** I will give you to the people who want to kill you. I will give you to the people that you are afraid of. I will give you to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and to the Chaldeans.

**26** I will throw you and your mother into another country. Neither of you started your life there. You will both die there.

**27** You will want to come back to this land, but you will not come back."

**28** Is this man Coniah like a broken pot that people do not want? Is he like a jar that no person likes? Why will the enemy throw out Coniah and his children? Why will the enemy throw them into a land that they do not know?

**29** Land, land, land, listen to the word of the LORD!

**30** The LORD says this: "Write in the records that this man has no children. He will not be successful in his life. None of his descendants will be successful. None of them will sit on the throne of David or rule in Judah again."

**19** Look! The anger of the LORD will come out like a storm. It will be like a strong wind. The storm will fall on the heads of evil people.

**21** I did not send these prophets, but they ran with their message. I did not speak to them, but they prophesied.

**35** Each of you must say this to his friend and to his relative: 'What did the LORD answer?' or, 'What did the LORD say?'

**36** You must not say 'the burden of the LORD' again. The words of each person become his own burden. You change the words of the God who lives, the LORD of armies, our God.

**37** Say this to the prophet: 'What did the LORD answer you?' or, 'What did the LORD say?'

**38** But you possibly say, 'This is the burden of the LORD.' Then the LORD says this: 'You use the words "the burden of the LORD." I told you not to use the words "the burden of the LORD."

**39** Thus I will forget you fully. I will throw you away from me. I will also throw away the city that I gave to you and your ancestors.

**40** I will bring shame on you that will continue always. People will never forget your shame.'"

## Chapter 24

**1** Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon took Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, away from Jerusalem as a prisoner. He also took the officials of Judah, the skilled workers and the metal workers to Babylon. After this, the LORD showed me 2 baskets of figs in front of the temple of the LORD.

**2** One basket had very good figs, like figs that become ripe early. The other basket had very bad figs. The figs were so bad that people could not eat them.

**3** Then the LORD asked me, "What do you see, Jeremiah?" I said, "Figs. The good figs are very good. The bad figs are very bad. They are so bad that people cannot eat them."

**4** Then the word of the LORD came to me. The LORD said,

**5** "The LORD, the God of Israel, says this: I sent some people from Judah away from this place. They went to the land of the Chaldeans as prisoners. I think of them as good, like these good figs.

**6** I will watch over them for their good. I will bring them back to this land. I will build them up and not break them down. I will plant them and not pull them up.

**7** I will give them a heart to know that I am the LORD. They will be my people, and I will be their God. They will come back to me with all their heart.

**8** "But the bad figs are so bad that people cannot eat them," says the LORD. "In the same way, I will think of Zedekiah king of Judah, his officials and the other people of Jerusalem as bad. This includes the people who stay in this land and the people who live in Egypt.

**9** I will cause all the kingdoms of the earth to hate them. I will send them to many places. In all those places, people will insult them and mock them. People will use their names as a curse.

**10** I will send war, famine and disease against them. I will do this until they are gone from the land that I gave to them and their ancestors."

## Chapter 25

**2** Jeremiah the prophet spoke this word to all the people of Judah and to all the people of Jerusalem. He said,

**3** "For 23 years the LORD gave his word to me. This started in the 13th year of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah, and it continues to this day. I spoke to you again and again, but you did not listen.

**4** The LORD sent all his servants the prophets to you again and again. But you did not listen or pay attention.

**5** The prophets said, 'Each of you must turn from your evil ways and evil actions. Then you can live in the land that the LORD gave to you and your ancestors always.

**6** Do not follow other gods. Do not serve or worship them. Do not make me angry with the idols that you make. Then I will not harm you.'

**7** "But you did not listen to me," says the LORD. "You made me angry with the idols that you made. Thus you caused harm to yourselves."

**8** Thus the LORD of armies says this: "You did not listen to my words.

**9** Thus I will send for all the people of the north," says the LORD. "I will send for my servant Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon. I will bring them against this land, its people and all the nations around it. I will fully destroy these nations. I will make them a thing of horror and mockery. They will be ruins always.

**10** I will stop the sounds of joy and happiness among them. I will stop the voices of the bridegroom and the bride. I will stop the sound of the millstones. I will stop the light of the lamps.

**11** All this land will become an empty ruin. These nations will serve the king of Babylon for 70 years.

**12** "When the 70 years are complete, I will punish the king of Babylon and his nation for their sins," says the LORD. "I will punish the land of the Chaldeans and make it an empty ruin always.

**13** I will bring on that land all the things that I said against it. I will bring all the things that this book contains. Jeremiah prophesied these things against all the nations.

**14** Many nations and great kings will make the Babylonians their slaves. I will punish the Babylonians as their actions deserve."

**15** The LORD, the God of Israel, said this to me: "Take from my hand this cup of wine, which is my anger. Cause all the nations to which I send you to drink from it.

**16** They will drink it. Then they will fall about and act like crazy people because of the war that I will send among them."

**17** Thus I took the cup from the hand of the LORD. I caused all the nations to which the LORD sent me to drink from it.

**18** I gave it to Jerusalem and the cities of Judah, and to their kings and officials. I did this to make them a ruin and a thing of horror. People mock them and use their name as a curse. They are like this today.

**19** I gave it to Pharaoh king of Egypt, his servants, his officials and all his people.

**20** I gave it to all the foreign people in Egypt and all the kings of the land of Uz. I gave it to all the kings of the Philistines in Ashkelon, Gaza, Ekron and the rest of the people of Ashdod.

**21** I gave it to Edom, Moab and the people of Ammon.

**22** I gave it to all the kings of Tyre and Sidon, and to the kings of the coastlands across the sea.

**23** I gave it to Dedan, Tema, Buz and all the people who cut the corners of their hair.

**24** I gave it to all the kings of Arabia and all the kings of the foreign people who live in the desert.

**25** I gave it to all the kings of Zimri, Elam and Media.

**26** I gave it to all the kings of the north, near and far, one after the other. I gave it to all the kingdoms on the earth. After all of them, the king of Sheshach (Babylon) will drink it.

**27** "Then say to the nations, 'The LORD of armies, the God of Israel, says this: Drink and become drunk, and vomit. Fall down and do not get up, because of the war that I will send among you.'

**28** But they will possibly refuse to take the cup from your hand and drink. Then say to them, 'The LORD of armies says this: You must drink it!

**29** Look! I start to bring disaster on the city that has my name. You will certainly not be free from punishment. You will not be free, because I will send war against all the people of the earth,' says the LORD of armies.

**30** "Thus prophesy all these words against the nations. Say to them, 'The LORD will roar from heaven. He will shout from his holy place. He will roar loudly against his land. He will shout like the people who crush grapes. He will shout against all the people of the earth.

**31** The noise will go to the ends of the earth. This is because the LORD makes accusations against the nations. He will judge all people. He will give the evil people to the sword,' says the LORD."

**32** The LORD of armies says this: "Look! Disaster goes from nation to nation. A great storm comes from the ends of the earth."

**33** At that time, the people that the LORD kills will lie from one end of the earth to the other end. No person will mourn for them, gather them or bury them. They will lie on the ground like animal waste.

**34** Cry and shout, you shepherds! Roll in the dust, you leaders of the flock! The time comes for your death. The enemy will scatter you. You will fall and break like a valuable jar.

**35** The shepherds will have no place to run. The leaders of the flock will not escape.

**36** Hear the cry of the shepherds! Hear the loud cry of the leaders of the flock! The LORD destroys their fields.

**37** The peaceful fields become empty because of the strong anger of the LORD.

**38** The LORD leaves his home like a lion. The land of the people becomes empty because of the sword of the cruel enemy. It becomes empty because of the strong anger of the LORD.

**4** Say to them, 'The LORD says this: You must listen to me and obey my law that I gave to you.

**5** You must listen to the words of my servants the prophets. I sent them to you again and again, but you did not listen.

**14** As for me, I am in your power. Do with me what you think is good and right.

**21** King Jehoiakim, all his soldiers and all his officials heard the words of Uriah. Then the king tried to kill him. But Uriah heard about this. He was afraid and ran away to Egypt.

**22** Then King Jehoiakim sent Elnathan son of Achbor and some other men to Egypt.

**8** "But a nation or a kingdom will possibly not serve Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon. Possibly that nation will not put its neck under his yoke. Then I will punish that nation with war, famine and disease," says the LORD. "I will do this until I use Nebuchadnezzar to destroy that nation.

**13** Why must you and your people die by war, famine and disease? The LORD said that this will happen to any nation that does not serve the king of Babylon.

**14** Do not listen to the words of the prophets who say to you, 'You will not serve the king of Babylon.' They tell you lies.

**15** 'I did not send them,' says the LORD. 'They tell lies in my name. Thus I will send you out, and you will die. You and the prophets who prophesy to you will die.'"

**17** Do not listen to them. Serve the king of Babylon, and you will live. Why must this city become a ruin?

**18** If they are prophets, and if they have the word of the LORD, let them pray to the LORD of armies. Let them pray that the objects that stay in the house of the LORD will not go to Babylon. Let them pray for the objects in the palace of the king of Judah and in Jerusalem.'

**21** The LORD of armies, the God of Israel, says this about the objects that stay in the house of the LORD. He also speaks about the objects in the palace of the king of Judah and in Jerusalem:

**22** 'The enemy will take them to Babylon. They will stay there until the day that I come for them,' says the LORD. 'Then I will bring them back to this place.'"

**32** It will not be like the covenant that I made with their ancestors. I made that covenant when I took them by the hand and led them out of Egypt. They broke my covenant, but I was a husband to them," says the LORD.

**33** "But this is the covenant that I will make with the people of Israel after that time," says the LORD. "I will put my law in their minds. I will write it on their hearts. I will be their God, and they will be my people.

**34** People will not need to teach their neighbors or their relatives to know the LORD. All of them will know me, from the least important to the most important," says the LORD. "I will forgive their sins. I will not remember their sins again."

**35** The LORD gives the sun to give light in the day. He makes the moon and the stars to give light at night. He moves the sea and causes its waves to roar. His name is the LORD of armies. He says this:

**36** "These laws of nature will not stop," says the LORD. "In the same way, the descendants of Israel will always be a nation in front of me."

**37** The LORD says this: "Possibly people can measure the sky above and examine the foundations of the earth below. Only then will I reject all the descendants of Israel because of all that they did," says the LORD.

**38** "The days will come," says the LORD, "when people will build the city again for the LORD. They will build from the Tower of Hananel to the Corner Gate.

**39** The line that people use to measure will go straight to the hill of Gareb. Then it will turn to Goah.

**40** All the valley where people throw the dead bodies and the ashes will be holy to the LORD. All the fields to the Kidron Valley, and east to the corner of the Horse Gate, will be holy to the LORD. People will never again pull down or destroy the city."

## Chapter 32

**1** This word came to Jeremiah from the LORD in the 10th year of Zedekiah king of Judah. That was the 18th year of Nebuchadnezzar.

**2** At that time, the army of the king of Babylon surrounded Jerusalem. The prophet Jeremiah was a prisoner in the courtyard of the guard, in the palace of the king of Judah.

**3** Zedekiah king of Judah put Jeremiah there. Zedekiah said, "Why do you prophesy these things? You say, 'The LORD says this: Look! I will give this city to the king of Babylon, and he will capture it.

**4** Zedekiah king of Judah will not escape from the Chaldeans. He will certainly go into the power of the king of Babylon. Zedekiah will speak with him face to face. He will see the king of Babylon with his own eyes.

**5** The king of Babylon will take Zedekiah to Babylon. Zedekiah will stay there until I come for him,' says the LORD. 'If you fight against the Chaldeans, you will not win.'"

**6** Jeremiah said, "The word of the LORD came to me. The LORD said,

**7** 'Hanamel, the son of your uncle Shallum, will come to you. He will say, "Buy my field at Anathoth. You are my nearest relative, thus you have the right to buy it."'

**8** "Then my cousin Hanamel came to me in the courtyard of the guard, as the LORD said. He said to me, 'Please buy my field at Anathoth, in the land of Benjamin. You have the right to own it and to buy it. Buy it for yourself.' Then I knew that this was the word of the LORD.

**9** "Thus I bought the field at Anathoth from my cousin Hanamel. I weighed out the money for him, 17 shekels of silver.

**10** I signed the document of sale and sealed it. I called witnesses. I weighed the money on scales.

**11** Then I took the documents of sale. I sealed one copy, and it had the conditions of the sale. The other copy was open.

**12** I gave the documents to Baruch son of Neriah, son of Mahseiah. I did this in front of my cousin Hanamel and the witnesses who signed the document. I also did this in front of all the Jews who sat in the courtyard of the guard.

**13** "In front of them, I gave Baruch this command:

**14** 'The LORD of armies, the God of Israel, says this: Take these documents, the sealed copy and the open copy. Put them in a clay jar. Then they will stay safe for a long time.

**15** The LORD of armies, the God of Israel, says this: People will again buy houses, fields and vineyards in this land.'

**16** "I gave the document of sale to Baruch son of Neriah. Then I prayed to the LORD. I said,

**17** 'Ah, Lord GOD! You made the heavens and the earth with your great power and your strong arm. Nothing is too difficult for you.

**18** You show love to thousands of people. But you also punish the children for the sins of their fathers. You are the great and strong God. Your name is the LORD of armies.

**19** Your plans are great and your actions are strong. You see all that people do. You give each person what his actions deserve.

**20** You did miraculous signs and wonders in Egypt. You continue to do them in Israel and among all people to this day. Thus your name is famous today.

**21** You brought your people Israel out of Egypt with miraculous signs and wonders. You used your strong hand and your powerful arm. You caused great terror.

**22** You gave them this land. You promised to give it to their ancestors. It is a land with much milk and honey.

**23** They came into the land and took it. But they did not obey you or follow your law. They did not do anything that you commanded. Thus you brought all this disaster on them.

**24** Look! The enemy builds ramps against the city to capture it. Because of war, famine and disease, the Chaldeans who attack it will capture it. What you said happens now. You see it.

**25** But Lord GOD, you said to me, "Buy the field with money and call witnesses." You said this, but the Chaldeans will capture the city!'"

**26** Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah. The LORD said,

**27** "I am the LORD, the God of all people. Nothing is too difficult for me.

**28** Thus the LORD says this: I will give this city to the Chaldeans and to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon. He will capture it.

**29** The Chaldeans who attack this city will come in and set it on fire. They will burn the houses where the people made me angry. On the roofs of these houses, the people burned incense to Baal and poured out drink offerings to other gods.

**30** The people of Israel and Judah did only evil in front of me since they were young. The people of Israel made me angry with the idols that they made," says the LORD.

**31** "This city made me angry and furious from the day that they built it to this day. Thus I will remove it from my sight.

**32** This is because of all the evil that the people of Israel and Judah did to make me angry. Their kings, officials, priests and prophets did evil. The people of Judah and Jerusalem did evil.

**33** They turned their backs to me and not their faces. I taught them again and again, but they did not listen or learn.

**34** They put their idols, which I hate, in the temple that has my name. Thus they made it unclean.

**35** They built high places for Baal in the Valley of Ben Hinnom. There they sacrificed their sons and daughters in the fire to Molech. I did not command them to do this. I did not think of it. I did not want them to do this evil thing that I hate and cause Judah to sin.

**36** "You say about this city, 'War, famine and disease will give it to the king of Babylon.' But now the LORD, the God of Israel, says this:

**37** Look! I will gather the people from all the countries where I sent them in my anger, fury and great wrath. I will bring them back to this place. I will let them live in safety.

**38** They will be my people, and I will be their God.

**39** I will give them 1 heart and 1 purpose. Then they will always respect me. This will be good for them and for their children after them.

**40** I will make a covenant with them that will continue always. I will not stop doing good to them. I will cause them to respect me in their hearts. Then they will not turn away from me.

**41** I will be glad to do good to them. I will truly plant them in this land with all my heart and all my soul."

**42** The LORD says this: "I brought all this great disaster on these people. In the same way, I will give them all the good things that I promise.

**43** You say about this land, 'It is an empty ruin, with no people or animals. The Chaldeans have it.' But people will buy fields in this land again.

**44** People will buy fields with money. They will sign and seal documents and call witnesses. They will do this in the land of Benjamin, in the area around Jerusalem and in the cities of Judah. They will do this in the cities of the hill country, of the western hills and of the Negev. I will make the people free again," says the LORD.

## Chapter 33

**1** Jeremiah was still a prisoner in the courtyard of the guard. Then the word of the LORD came to him a second time. The LORD said,

**2** "The LORD made the earth. The LORD formed it and made it firm. His name is the LORD. He says this:

**3** Call to me, and I will answer you. I will tell you great and secret things that you do not know.

**4** The LORD, the God of Israel, speaks about the houses of this city and the palaces of the kings of Judah. The people pulled down these houses to make defenses against the ramps and the sword of the enemy.

**5** The people fight against the Chaldeans. But the houses will be full of the dead bodies of the people. I will kill them in my anger and fury. I turned my face away from this city because of all its evil.

**6** "But look! I will bring health and a cure to this city. I will heal the people. I will let them have much peace and safety.

**7** I will make Judah and Israel free again. I will build them up again, as before.

**8** I will make them clean from all the sins that they did against me. I will forgive all the sins that they did when they rebelled against me.

**9** This city will cause me to have a name of joy, praise and honor. All the nations of the earth will hear about all the good things that I do for the city. The nations will be afraid and they will shake. This is because of all the good things and the peace that I give to the city."

**10** The LORD says this: "You say about this place, 'It is empty. There are no people or animals.' The cities of Judah and the streets of Jerusalem are empty. No people or animals live there. But people will hear these sounds again:

**11** They will hear the sounds of joy and happiness. They will hear the voices of the bridegroom and the bride. They will hear the voices of the people who bring offerings of thanks to the house of the LORD. They will say, 'Give thanks to the LORD of armies, because the LORD is good. His love continues always.' I will make the land free again, as before," says the LORD.

**12** The LORD of armies says this: "This place is empty, with no people or animals. But again in this place and in all its cities, there will be fields for shepherds. They will let their flocks rest there.

**13** Shepherds will count their sheep again in the cities of the hill country, the western hills and the Negev. They will count their sheep in the land of Benjamin, in the area around Jerusalem and in the cities of Judah," says the LORD.

**14** "The days will come," says the LORD, "when I will do the good thing that I promised to the people of Israel and Judah.

**15** In those days and at that time, I will cause a good Branch to grow from the family of David. He will do what is fair and right in the land.

**16** In those days God will save Judah. Jerusalem will live in safety. People will call Jerusalem by this name: 'The LORD Our Righteousness.'"

**17** The LORD says this: "David will always have a descendant to sit on the throne of Israel.

**18** The Levite priests will always have a man to serve in front of me. He will give burnt offerings, burn grain offerings and do sacrifices always."

**19** The word of the LORD came to Jeremiah. The LORD said,

**20** "The LORD says this: I made a covenant with the day and the night. Day and night come at their correct times. Possibly you can break this covenant.

**21** Only then can someone break my covenant with my servant David. Only then will he not have a son to rule on his throne. Only then can someone break my covenant with the Levite priests who serve me.

**22** No person can count the stars of the sky. No person can measure the sand of the sea. In the same way, I will make the descendants of my servant David and the Levites who serve me very many."

**23** The word of the LORD came to Jeremiah. The LORD said,

**24** "Did you hear what these people say? They say, 'The LORD chose 2 families, Israel and Judah, but now he rejects them.' Thus they insult my people. They do not think that my people are a nation.

**25** The LORD says this: I made a covenant with the day and the night. I made the fixed laws of the heavens and the earth.

**26** Thus I will not reject the descendants of Jacob and my servant David. I will choose rulers from the descendants of David to rule over the descendants of Abraham, Isaac and Jacob. I will make them free again, and I will show mercy to them."

## Chapter 34

**1** Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, all his army and all the kingdoms and people that he ruled fought against Jerusalem and all its cities. At that time, this word came to Jeremiah from the LORD:

**2** "The LORD, the God of Israel, says this: Go and speak to Zedekiah king of Judah. Tell him, 'The LORD says this: Look! I will give this city to the king of Babylon. He will burn it with fire.

**3** You will not escape from him. He will certainly capture you. You will go into his power. You will see the king of Babylon with your own eyes. He will speak with you face to face. Then you will go to Babylon.

**4** But Zedekiah king of Judah, listen to the word of the LORD. The LORD says this about you: You will not die by the sword.

**5** You will die in peace. People burned spices to honor your ancestors, the kings before you. In the same way, people will burn spices to honor you. They will mourn for you and say, "Ah, master!" I promise this,' says the LORD."

**6** Then the prophet Jeremiah spoke all these words to Zedekiah king of Judah in Jerusalem.

**7** At that time, the army of the king of Babylon attacked Jerusalem. It also attacked the other cities of Judah, Lachish and Azekah. Only these strong cities of Judah stayed.

**8** The word came to Jeremiah from the LORD after King Zedekiah made a covenant with all the people in Jerusalem. The covenant said that the people must make their slaves free.

**9** Each person must let his Hebrew male and female slaves go free. No person must keep a fellow Jew as a slave.

**10** All the officials and all the people who made the covenant agreed to let their male and female slaves go free. They agreed not to keep them as slaves again. They obeyed and let the slaves go.

**11** But after that, they changed their minds. They took back the male and female slaves that they made free. They forced them to be slaves again.

**12** Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah. The LORD said,

**13** "The LORD, the God of Israel, says this: I made a covenant with your ancestors when I brought them out of Egypt. They were slaves there. I said,

**14** 'At the end of each 7 years, each of you must let your Hebrew slaves go free. A Hebrew person who sells himself to you will serve you for 6 years. Then you must let him go free.' But your ancestors did not listen to me or pay attention.

**15** Recently you changed and did what is right in my sight. Each of you made his fellow Hebrews free. You made a covenant in front of me in the temple that has my name.

**16** But then you changed your minds and dishonored my name. Each of you took back the male and female slaves that you made free. You forced them to be your slaves again.

**17** "Thus the LORD says this: You did not obey me. You did not make your fellow Hebrews free. Thus I will make you 'free,'" says the LORD. "I will make you free to die by war, disease and famine. I will cause all the kingdoms of the earth to hate you.

**18** Some men broke my covenant. They did not obey the words of the covenant that they made in front of me. They cut a calf in 2 pieces and walked between the pieces. I will make them like that calf.

**19** The officials of Judah and Jerusalem, the palace officials, the priests and all the people of the land walked between the pieces of the calf.

**20** I will give them to their enemies who want to kill them. Their dead bodies will become food for the birds and the wild animals.

**21** I will give Zedekiah king of Judah and his officials to their enemies who want to kill them. I will give them to the army of the king of Babylon. That army went away from you for a short time.

**22** Look! I will give the command," says the LORD. "I will bring the army back to this city. They will attack it, capture it and burn it. I will make the cities of Judah empty ruins where no people live."

## Chapter 35

**1** This is the word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD when Jehoiakim son of Josiah was king of Judah:

**2** "Go to the family of the Rechabites and speak to them. Bring them into one of the side rooms of the house of the LORD. Give them wine to drink."

**3** Thus I went to get Jaazaniah son of Jeremiah, son of Habazziniah. I also got his brothers, all his sons and all the family of the Rechabites.

**4** I brought them into the house of the LORD. I brought them into the room of the sons of Hanan son of Igdaliah, a man of God. This room was near the room of the officials. It was above the room of Maaseiah son of Shallum, the doorkeeper.

**5** I put jars full of wine and some cups in front of the men of the Rechabite family. I said to them, "Drink some wine."

**6** But they said, "We do not drink wine. This is because our ancestor Jonadab son of Rechab gave us a command. He said, 'You and your descendants must never drink wine.

**7** Do not build houses. Do not plant seeds or vineyards. Do not own these things. Always live in tents. Then you will live a long time in the land where you are foreigners.'

**8** We obeyed all the commands of our ancestor Jonadab son of Rechab. We, our wives, our sons and our daughters never drink wine.

**9** We do not build houses to live in. We do not have vineyards, fields or seeds.

**10** We live in tents. We obey all that our ancestor Jonadab commanded us.

**11** But Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon attacked this land. Then we said, 'Come, we must go to Jerusalem. We must escape from the armies of the Chaldeans and the Arameans.' Thus we live in Jerusalem now."

**12** Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah. The LORD said,

**13** "The LORD of armies, the God of Israel, says this: Go and tell the people of Judah and Jerusalem this message. 'You must learn a lesson and obey my words,' says the LORD.

**14** 'Jonadab son of Rechab commanded his descendants not to drink wine. They obey this command. To this day they do not drink wine, because they obey the command of their ancestor. But I spoke to you again and again, and you did not obey me.

**15** I sent all my servants the prophets to you again and again. They said, "Each of you must stop your evil ways and change your actions. Do not follow other gods to serve them. Then you will live in the land that I gave to you and your ancestors." But you did not pay attention or listen to me.

**16** The descendants of Jonadab son of Rechab obey the command of their ancestor. But these people do not obey me.'

**17** "Thus the LORD God of armies, the God of Israel, says this: 'Look! I will bring on Judah and on all the people of Jerusalem all the disaster that I said. I spoke to them, but they did not listen. I called to them, but they did not answer.'"

**18** Then Jeremiah spoke to the family of the Rechabites. He said, "The LORD of armies, the God of Israel, says this. 'You obey the command of your ancestor Jonadab. You keep all his rules. You do all that he commanded.'

**19** Thus the LORD of armies, the God of Israel, says this: 'Jonadab son of Rechab will always have a descendant to serve me.'"

## Chapter 36

**1** In the 4th year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, this word came to Jeremiah from the LORD:

**2** "Take a scroll and write on it all the words that I spoke to you about Israel, Judah and all the nations. Write all that I said from the time of Josiah to today.

**3** Possibly the people of Judah will hear about all the disaster that I plan to bring on them. Then each of them will possibly turn from his evil way. Then I will forgive their evil and their sin."

**4** Thus Jeremiah called Baruch son of Neriah. Jeremiah told Baruch all the words that the LORD spoke to him. Baruch wrote them on a scroll.

**5** Then Jeremiah commanded Baruch, "I am not free. I cannot go to the house of the LORD.

**6** Thus you must go to the house of the LORD on a day when the people do not eat food (fast). Read to the people the words of the LORD from the scroll that you wrote. I told you these words. Read them to all the people of Judah who come from their cities.

**7** Possibly they will ask the LORD for mercy. Possibly each of them will turn from his evil way. The LORD said that his anger and fury against these people are very great."

**8** Baruch son of Neriah did all that the prophet Jeremiah commanded. He read the words of the LORD from the scroll in the house of the LORD.

**9** In the 9th month of the 5th year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, the leaders called for a fast. All the people of Jerusalem and all the people from the cities of Judah came to Jerusalem to fast in front of the LORD.

**10** Then Baruch read the words of Jeremiah from the scroll to all the people in the house of the LORD. He read in the room of Gemariah son of Shaphan the secretary. That room was in the upper courtyard, at the entrance of the New Gate of the house of the LORD.

**11** Micaiah son of Gemariah, son of Shaphan, heard all the words of the LORD from the scroll.

**12** Then Micaiah went down to the room of the secretary in the palace of the king. All the officials sat there. Elishama the secretary, Delaiah son of Shemaiah and Elnathan son of Achbor were there. Gemariah son of Shaphan, Zedekiah son of Hananiah and all the other officials were there.

**13** Micaiah told them all the words that he heard when Baruch read the scroll to the people.

**14** Then all the officials sent Jehudi son of Nethaniah, son of Shelemiah, son of Cushi, to Baruch. Jehudi said, "Take the scroll that you read to the people, and come." Thus Baruch son of Neriah took the scroll and went to them.

**15** The officials said to him, "Please sit down and read the scroll to us." Thus Baruch read it to them.

**16** The officials heard all the words. Then they looked at each other with fear. They said to Baruch, "We must certainly tell the king all these words."

**17** Then they asked Baruch, "Tell us, how did you write all these words? Did Jeremiah tell them to you?"

**18** Baruch answered, "Yes. Jeremiah spoke all these words to me. I wrote them with ink on the scroll."

**19** Then the officials said to Baruch, "You and Jeremiah must go and hide. Do not let any person know where you are."

**20** The officials put the scroll in the room of Elishama the secretary. Then they went to the king in the courtyard. They told the king all the words.

**21** The king sent Jehudi to get the scroll. Jehudi took it from the room of Elishama the secretary. Jehudi read it to the king and to all the officials who stood near the king.

**22** It was the 9th month. The king sat in his winter house. A fire burned in a fire pot in front of him.

**23** Each time that Jehudi read 3 or 4 columns, the king cut them off with a knife. The king threw them into the fire. He continued until the fire burned all the scroll.

**24** The king and all his servants heard all these words. But they were not afraid. They did not tear their clothes in sorrow.

**25** Elnathan, Delaiah and Gemariah asked the king not to burn the scroll. But he did not listen to them.

**26** Then the king gave a command to Jerahmeel the king's son, Seraiah son of Azriel and Shelemiah son of Abdeel. He told them to arrest Baruch the secretary and Jeremiah the prophet. But the LORD hid them.

**27** The king burned the scroll that had the words that Jeremiah told Baruch to write. After this, the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah. The LORD said,

**28** "Take another scroll. Write on it all the words that were on the first scroll. Jehoiakim king of Judah burned the first scroll.

**29** Say to Jehoiakim king of Judah, 'The LORD says this: You burned the scroll. You said, "Why did Jeremiah write that the king of Babylon will certainly come and destroy this land? Why did he write that the king of Babylon will kill the people and the animals in it?"

**30** Thus the LORD says this about Jehoiakim king of Judah: None of his descendants will sit on the throne of David. His dead body will lie outside in the heat of the day and the cold of the night.

**31** I will punish him, his children and his officials for their evil. I will bring on them all the disaster that I said. I will bring it on the people of Jerusalem and Judah, because they did not listen.'"

**32** Then Jeremiah took another scroll and gave it to Baruch son of Neriah, the secretary. Jeremiah spoke, and Baruch wrote on the scroll all the words of the scroll that Jehoiakim king of Judah burned. They also added many similar words.

## Chapter 37

**1** Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon made Zedekiah son of Josiah king of Judah. Zedekiah ruled in the place of Coniah son of Jehoiakim.

**2** But Zedekiah, his officials and the people of the land did not listen to the words of the LORD. The LORD spoke these words through the prophet Jeremiah.

**3** King Zedekiah sent Jehucal son of Shelemiah and the priest Zephaniah son of Maaseiah to the prophet Jeremiah. They said, "Please pray to the LORD our God for us."

**4** At that time Jeremiah was free to go among the people. The officials did not put him in prison yet.

**5** The army of Pharaoh came out of Egypt. The Chaldeans who surrounded Jerusalem heard the news about it. Thus the Chaldeans went away from Jerusalem.

**6** Then the word of the LORD came to the prophet Jeremiah. The LORD said,

**7** "The LORD, the God of Israel, says this: The king of Judah sent you to ask me for advice. Say this to him: 'The army of Pharaoh came out to help you. But it will go back to its own land, Egypt.

**8** Then the Chaldeans will come back. They will attack this city, capture it and burn it.'

**9** The LORD says this: Do not trick yourselves. Do not say, 'The Chaldeans will certainly go away from us.' They will not go away.

**10** Possibly you can defeat all the army of the Chaldeans that attacks you. Possibly only wounded men stay in their tents. But those men will get up and burn this city."

**11** The army of the Chaldeans went away from Jerusalem because of the army of Pharaoh.

**12** Then Jeremiah started to go out of Jerusalem to the land of Benjamin. He wanted to get his share of property among the people there.

**13** Jeremiah came to the Benjamin Gate. The captain of the guard there was Irijah son of Shelemiah, son of Hananiah. Irijah arrested the prophet Jeremiah. He said, "You go over to the Chaldeans!"

**14** Jeremiah said, "That is not true! I do not go over to the Chaldeans." But Irijah did not listen to him. Irijah arrested Jeremiah and took him to the officials.

**15** The officials were angry with Jeremiah. They hit him and put him in prison in the house of Jonathan the secretary. They used that house as a prison.

**16** Jeremiah went into a cell under the ground. He stayed there for many days.

**17** Then King Zedekiah sent for Jeremiah and brought him to the palace. The king asked him in secret, "Is there a word from the LORD?" Jeremiah answered, "Yes, there is. You will go into the power of the king of Babylon."

**18** Then Jeremiah said to King Zedekiah, "What crime did I do against you, your officials or these people? Why did you put me in prison?

**19** Where are your prophets now? They prophesied to you, 'The king of Babylon will not attack you or this land.'

**20** But now, my master the king, please listen to me. Please accept my request. Do not send me back to the house of Jonathan the secretary. If you send me there, I will die."

**21** Then King Zedekiah gave a command. The guards put Jeremiah in the courtyard of the guard. Each day they gave him a piece of bread from the street of the bakers. They did this until there was no more bread in the city. Thus Jeremiah stayed in the courtyard of the guard.

## Chapter 38

**1** Shephatiah son of Mattan, Gedaliah son of Pashhur, Jehucal son of Shelemiah and Pashhur son of Malkijah heard what Jeremiah said to all the people. Jeremiah said,

**2** "The LORD says this: 'The people who stay in this city will die by war, famine and disease. But the people who go out to the Chaldeans will live. They will stay alive.'

**3** The LORD says this: 'The army of the king of Babylon will certainly capture this city.'"

**4** Then the officials said to the king, "This man must die. He causes the soldiers who stay in this city to become weak. He causes all the people to become weak with his words. This man does not want good things for these people. He wants disaster for them."

**5** King Zedekiah said, "He is in your power. The king can do nothing against you."

**6** Thus they took Jeremiah and put him into the well of Malkijah, the king's son. The well was in the courtyard of the guard. They let Jeremiah down with ropes. There was no water in the well, only mud. Jeremiah went down into the mud.

**7** Ebed-Melech from Ethiopia was an official in the palace of the king. He heard that they put Jeremiah into the well. At that time the king sat at the Benjamin Gate.

**8** Ebed-Melech went out of the palace and spoke to the king. He said,

**9** "My master the king, these men did an evil thing to the prophet Jeremiah. They put him into the well. He will die of hunger there, because there is no more bread in the city."

**10** Then the king commanded Ebed-Melech from Ethiopia, "Take 30 men from here with you. Lift the prophet Jeremiah out of the well before he dies."

**11** Thus Ebed-Melech took the men with him. He went into a room below the treasury in the palace. He took some old clothes and old pieces of cloth from there. He let them down with ropes into the well to Jeremiah.

**12** Ebed-Melech from Ethiopia said to Jeremiah, "Put these old clothes and pieces of cloth under your arms. Put them between your arms and the ropes." Jeremiah did this.

**13** Then they pulled Jeremiah up with the ropes and lifted him out of the well. Jeremiah stayed in the courtyard of the guard.

**14** Then King Zedekiah sent for the prophet Jeremiah. The king brought Jeremiah to the 3rd entrance of the house of the LORD. The king said to Jeremiah, "I will ask you a question. Do not hide anything from me."

**15** Jeremiah said to Zedekiah, "If I tell you the answer, you will certainly kill me. If I give you advice, you will not listen to me."

**4** I sent all my servants the prophets to you again and again. They said, 'Do not do this evil thing that I hate!'

**6** Thus I showed my fury and anger. My anger burned in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem. Today they are empty ruins.

**9** Did you forget the evil that your ancestors did? Did you forget the evil that the kings of Judah and their wives did? Did you forget the evil that you and your wives did in the land of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem?

**10** To this day, you do not make yourselves humble. You do not respect me. You do not obey my law and my rules that I gave to you and your ancestors.

**11** "Thus the LORD of armies, the God of Israel, says this: Look! I decided to bring disaster on you. I will destroy all of Judah.

**12** The rest of the people of Judah decided to go to Egypt and live there. I will destroy them all in Egypt. They will die by war and famine. All of them will die, from the least important to the most important. People will curse them and insult them. People will feel shock at them.

**16** "We will not listen to the message that you speak to us in the name of the LORD.

**17** We will certainly do all that we promised. We will burn incense to the Queen of Heaven. We will pour out drink offerings to her. We, our ancestors, our kings and our officials did this in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem. At that time we had much food. Things went well for us, and we had no trouble.

**22** The LORD could not accept your evil actions and the evil things that he hates, which you did. Thus your land is an empty ruin today. People curse it and they feel shock at it. No people live in it.

**24** Then Jeremiah spoke to all the people and to all the women. He said, "All you people of Judah in Egypt, listen to the word of the LORD.

**25** The LORD of armies, the God of Israel, says this: 'You and your wives do what you say. You said, "We will certainly do what we promised. We will burn incense to the Queen of Heaven. We will pour out drink offerings to her." Thus go and do what you promised! Keep your promises!

**27** Look! I watch over them to bring disaster and not good. All the people of Judah in Egypt will die by war and famine until all of them are dead.

**29** This will be the sign to you," says the LORD, "that I will punish you in this place. Then you will know that my words against you will certainly come true.

**30** The LORD says this: Look! I will give Pharaoh Hophra king of Egypt to his enemies who want to kill him. I did the same to Zedekiah king of Judah. I gave Zedekiah to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, his enemy, who wanted to kill him."'"

**6** On the 9th day of the 4th month, the famine in the city was very bad. The people of the land had no food.

**7** Then the enemy broke through the city wall. All the soldiers ran away. They went out of the city at night through the gate between the 2 walls, near the garden of the king. The Chaldeans were all around the city. The soldiers went toward the Jordan Valley.

**8** But the army of the Chaldeans chased the king. They caught Zedekiah in the plains near Jericho. All his army left him and went in different directions.

**9** The Chaldeans captured the king. They took him to the king of Babylon at Riblah in the land of Hamath. There the king of Babylon decided his punishment.

**10** At Riblah the king of Babylon killed the sons of Zedekiah while Zedekiah watched. He also killed all the officials of Judah.

**11** Then he removed the eyes of Zedekiah. The king of Babylon put him in bronze chains and took him to Babylon. He kept Zedekiah in prison until the day of his death.

**12** On the 10th day of the 5th month, Nebuzaradan came to Jerusalem. This was in the 19th year of the rule of Nebuchadnezzar, the king of Babylon. Nebuzaradan was the commander of the guard and served the king of Babylon.

**13** He burned the house of the LORD and the palace of the king. He burned all the houses in Jerusalem. He burned all the important buildings.

**14** All the army of the Chaldeans with the commander of the guard broke down all the walls around Jerusalem.

**15** Nebuzaradan, the commander of the guard, took some of the poorest people as prisoners. He also took the other people who stayed in the city. He took the people who went over to the king of Babylon. He took the other workers who stayed.

**16** But Nebuzaradan, the commander of the guard, left some of the poorest people of the land. He left them to work in the vineyards and the fields.

**17** The Chaldeans broke the bronze pillars in the house of the LORD. They broke the bronze stands and the large bronze bowl, called "the Sea." They took all the bronze to Babylon.

**18** They also took the pots, the shovels, the wick cutters, the bowls and the dishes. They took all the bronze tools that the priests used in the temple.

**19** The commander of the guard took the basins, the fire pans, the bowls, the pots, the lampstands, the dishes, and the cups. He took all the things of gold or silver.

**20** King Solomon made the 2 pillars, the Sea, the 12 bronze bulls under the Sea, and the stands for the house of the LORD. The bronze of all these things was too heavy to weigh.

**21** Each pillar was 18 cubits high. A line of 12 cubits went around each pillar. The metal was 4 fingers thick. The pillar was hollow.

**22** A bronze top was on each pillar. Each top was 5 cubits high. A net and pomegranates of bronze went all around the top. The second pillar, with its pomegranates, was the same.

**23** There were 96 pomegranates on the sides. In total there were 100 pomegranates on the net around the top.

**24** The commander of the guard took Seraiah the chief priest and Zephaniah the second priest as prisoners. He also took the 3 doorkeepers.

**25** From the city he took the officer who commanded the soldiers. He took 7 advisers of the king who were still in the city. He took the scribe of the army commander, who listed the people of the land for the army. He also took 60 men of the land who were in the city.

**26** Nebuzaradan, the commander of the guard, took all these people to the king of Babylon at Riblah.

**27** The king of Babylon killed them at Riblah in the land of Hamath. Thus the Babylonians took the people of Judah away from their land as prisoners.

**28** This is the number of people that Nebuchadnezzar took away as prisoners. In the 7th year, he took 3,023 Jews.

**29** In the 18th year of Nebuchadnezzar, he took 832 people from Jerusalem.

**30** In the 23rd year of Nebuchadnezzar, Nebuzaradan, the commander of the guard, took 745 Jews. In total, the Babylonians took 4,600 people.

**31** In the 37th year after the Babylonians took Jehoiachin, the king of Judah, as a prisoner, Evil-Merodach became king of Babylon. On the 25th day of the 12th month of that first year, he was kind to Jehoiachin. He released him from prison.

**32** He spoke kindly to Jehoiachin. He gave him a seat of more honor than the seats of the other kings with him in Babylon.

**33** Jehoiachin removed his prison clothes. For all the other days of his life, he ate at the table of the king.

**34** Each day the king of Babylon gave Jehoiachin an amount of food. The king did this until the day of the death of Jehoiachin, for all the days of his life.
