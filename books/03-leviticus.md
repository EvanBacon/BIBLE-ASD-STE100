# Leviticus

## Chapter 1

**1** The LORD called to Moses and spoke to him from the Tent of Meeting. He said,

**2** "Speak to the people of Israel. Tell them, 'When a person among you brings an offering to the LORD, bring an animal from the cattle or from the sheep and goats.

**3** If the offering is a burnt offering from the cattle, the person must offer a male with no defects. He must offer it at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. Then the LORD will accept him.

**4** He must put his hand on the head of the burnt offering. Then the LORD will accept the offering for him, to remove his sin (make atonement).

**5** He must kill the young bull in front of the LORD. The priests, the sons of Aaron, must bring the blood. They must sprinkle the blood on all sides of the altar at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

**6** He must remove the skin from the burnt offering and cut it into pieces.

**7** The sons of Aaron the priest must put fire on the altar. They must put wood on the fire.

**8** The priests, the sons of Aaron, must put the pieces, the head, and the fat on the wood. This wood is on the fire on the altar.

**9** The person must wash the inner organs and the legs with water. The priest must burn all of it on the altar. It is a burnt offering, an offering by fire. Its smell pleases the LORD.

**10** If the burnt offering is from the flock, from the sheep or the goats, the person must bring a male with no defects.

**11** He must kill it on the north side of the altar in front of the LORD. The priests, the sons of Aaron, must sprinkle its blood on all sides of the altar.

**12** He must cut it into pieces. The priest must put the pieces, the head, and the fat on the wood. This wood is on the fire on the altar.

**13** The person must wash the inner organs and the legs with water. The priest must bring all of it and burn it on the altar. It is a burnt offering, an offering by fire. Its smell pleases the LORD.

**14** If the burnt offering to the LORD is a bird, the person must bring a dove or a young pigeon.

**15** The priest must bring the bird to the altar. He must twist off its head and burn the head on the altar. He must drain its blood on the side of the altar.

**16** He must remove the crop and its contents. He must throw them on the east side of the altar, at the place for the ashes.

**17** He must tear the bird open by its wings, but he must not divide it into 2 parts. The priest must burn it on the altar, on the wood on the fire. It is a burnt offering, an offering by fire. Its smell pleases the LORD.'"

## Chapter 2

**1** "'When a person brings a grain offering to the LORD, the offering must be fine flour. He must pour oil on it and put frankincense on it.

**2** He must bring it to the priests, the sons of Aaron. The priest must take a handful of the flour and oil, with all the frankincense. The priest must burn this part on the altar as a memorial portion. It is an offering by fire, and its smell pleases the LORD.

**3** The remaining part of the grain offering is for Aaron and his sons. It is a most holy part of the offerings by fire to the LORD.

**4** If you bring a grain offering that you bake in an oven, it must be fine flour without yeast. Bring thick loaves mixed with oil, or thin wafers with oil on them.

**5** If your grain offering is cooked on a flat pan, it must be fine flour without yeast, mixed with oil.

**6** Break it into pieces and pour oil on it. It is a grain offering.

**7** If your grain offering is cooked in a pot, make it from fine flour and oil.

**8** Bring the grain offering that you make from these things to the LORD. Give it to the priest, and he must bring it to the altar.

**9** The priest must take the memorial portion from the grain offering. He must burn it on the altar. It is an offering by fire, and its smell pleases the LORD.

**10** The remaining part of the grain offering is for Aaron and his sons. It is a most holy part of the offerings by fire to the LORD.

**11** Do not make with yeast any grain offering that you bring to the LORD. Do not burn yeast or honey in any offering by fire to the LORD.

**12** You can offer yeast and honey to the LORD as an offering of the first crops. But do not burn them on the altar as an offering with a pleasing smell.

**13** Put salt on each grain offering. Salt is a sign of the covenant with your God. Always put salt in your grain offerings. Offer salt with all your offerings.

**14** If you offer a grain offering of your first crops to the LORD, offer new grain. Roast the heads of grain in the fire and crush them.

**15** Put oil and frankincense on it. It is a grain offering.

**16** The priest must burn the memorial portion. He must burn some of the crushed grain and some of the oil, with all the frankincense. It is an offering by fire to the LORD.'"

## Chapter 3

**1** "'A person can offer a peace offering. If he offers an animal from the cattle, it can be a male or a female. He must offer an animal with no defects in front of the LORD.

**2** He must put his hand on the head of the animal. He must kill it at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. The priests, the sons of Aaron, must sprinkle the blood on all sides of the altar.

**3** From the peace offering, he must offer an offering by fire to the LORD. He must offer the fat that covers the inner organs and all the fat on them.

**4** He must also remove the 2 kidneys with their fat, near the lower back. He must remove the best part of the liver with the kidneys.

**5** The sons of Aaron must burn these parts on the altar, on top of the burnt offering. The burnt offering is on the wood on the fire. It is an offering by fire, and its smell pleases the LORD.

**6** If the peace offering to the LORD is from the flock, it can be a male or a female. He must offer an animal with no defects.

**7** If he offers a lamb, he must offer it in front of the LORD.

**8** He must put his hand on the head of the animal. He must kill it in front of the Tent of Meeting. The sons of Aaron must sprinkle its blood on all sides of the altar.

**9** From the peace offering, he must offer an offering by fire to the LORD. He must offer the fat and all of the fat tail. He must cut the tail off near the backbone. He must offer the fat that covers the inner organs and all the fat on them.

**10** He must also remove the 2 kidneys with their fat, near the lower back. He must remove the best part of the liver with the kidneys.

**11** The priest must burn these parts on the altar. It is food, an offering by fire to the LORD.

**12** If his offering is a goat, he must offer it in front of the LORD.

**13** He must put his hand on the head of the goat. He must kill it in front of the Tent of Meeting. The sons of Aaron must sprinkle its blood on all sides of the altar.

**14** From the goat, he must offer an offering by fire to the LORD. He must offer the fat that covers the inner organs and all the fat on them.

**15** He must also remove the 2 kidneys with their fat, near the lower back. He must remove the best part of the liver with the kidneys.

**16** The priest must burn these parts on the altar. It is food, an offering by fire, and its smell pleases the LORD. All the fat is for the LORD.

**17** This is a permanent law for all your descendants, in all the places where you live. Do not eat any fat or any blood.'"

## Chapter 4

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**2** "Speak to the people of Israel. Tell them, 'A person can sin by accident. He can do a thing that one of the commands of the LORD does not permit.

**3** If the anointed priest sins, he causes guilt on the people. Then he must bring a young bull with no defects to the LORD. It is a sin offering for the sin that he did.

**4** He must bring the bull to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting in front of the LORD. He must put his hand on the head of the bull and kill the bull in front of the LORD.

**5** The anointed priest must take some of the blood of the bull. He must bring the blood into the Tent of Meeting.

**6** The priest must put his finger in the blood. He must sprinkle some of the blood 7 times in front of the LORD, in front of the curtain of the holy place.

**7** The priest must put some of the blood on the horns of the altar of sweet incense in front of the LORD. This altar is in the Tent of Meeting. He must pour out all the remaining blood of the bull at the bottom of the altar of burnt offering. This altar is at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

**8** He must remove all the fat from the bull of the sin offering. He must remove the fat that covers the inner organs and all the fat on them.

**9** He must also remove the 2 kidneys with their fat, near the lower back. He must remove the best part of the liver with the kidneys.

**10** He must remove these parts in the same way as from the bull of the peace offering. The priest must burn them on the altar of burnt offering.

**11** But he must take the skin of the bull, all its meat, its head, its legs, its inner organs, and its dung.

**12** He must carry all of the bull outside the camp to a clean place. This is the place where the priests pour the ashes. He must burn the bull on a wood fire there.

**13** All the community of Israel can sin by accident. They can do a thing that one of the commands of the LORD does not permit. They are guilty, but the community does not know about the sin.

**14** When they learn about the sin, the community must offer a young bull as a sin offering. They must bring it in front of the Tent of Meeting.

**15** The elders of the community must put their hands on the head of the bull in front of the LORD. Then a person must kill the bull in front of the LORD.

**16** The anointed priest must bring some of the blood of the bull into the Tent of Meeting.

**17** The priest must put his finger in the blood. He must sprinkle the blood 7 times in front of the LORD, in front of the curtain.

**18** He must put some of the blood on the horns of the altar in front of the LORD in the Tent of Meeting. He must pour out all the remaining blood at the bottom of the altar of burnt offering. This altar is at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

**19** He must remove all the fat from the bull and burn it on the altar.

**20** He must do the same things with this bull as with the bull for the sin offering of the priest. The priest must make atonement for the people, and God will forgive them.

**21** He must carry the bull outside the camp. He must burn it in the same way as the first bull. It is the sin offering for the community.

**22** A leader can sin by accident. He can do a thing that one of the commands of the LORD his God does not permit. Then he is guilty.

**23** When a person tells him about his sin, he must bring a male goat with no defects as his offering.

**24** He must put his hand on the head of the goat. He must kill it in front of the LORD, in the place where they kill the burnt offering. It is a sin offering.

**25** The priest must put his finger in the blood of the sin offering. He must put some blood on the horns of the altar of burnt offering. He must pour out the remaining blood at the bottom of the altar of burnt offering.

**26** He must burn all the fat on the altar, the same as the fat of the peace offering. The priest must make atonement for the sin of the leader, and God will forgive him.

**27** A usual person of the community can sin by accident. He can do a thing that one of the commands of the LORD does not permit. Then he is guilty.

**28** When a person tells him about his sin, he must bring a female goat with no defects. It is his offering for the sin that he did.

**29** He must put his hand on the head of the sin offering. He must kill it in the place of the burnt offering.

**30** The priest must put his finger in some of the blood. He must put the blood on the horns of the altar of burnt offering. He must pour out all the remaining blood at the bottom of the altar.

**31** He must remove all the fat, as the priests remove the fat from the peace offering. The priest must burn it on the altar, and its smell will please the LORD. The priest must make atonement for him, and God will forgive him.

**32** If he brings a lamb as his sin offering, he must bring a female with no defects.

**33** He must put his hand on the head of the sin offering. He must kill it as a sin offering in the place where they kill the burnt offering.

**34** The priest must put his finger in some of the blood of the sin offering. He must put the blood on the horns of the altar of burnt offering. He must pour out all the remaining blood at the bottom of the altar.

**35** He must remove all the fat, as the priests remove the fat of the lamb from the peace offering. The priest must burn it on the altar with the offerings by fire to the LORD. The priest must make atonement for the sin of the person, and God will forgive him.'"

## Chapter 5

**1** "'A person can hear a public call to give evidence about a thing that he saw or knows. If he does not speak, he sins and he is responsible for his guilt.

**2** A person can touch an unclean thing without knowledge. It can be the dead body of an unclean wild animal, an unclean farm animal, or an unclean small animal. Then that person is unclean and guilty.

**3** A person can touch an unclean thing from a human without knowledge. It can be any type of uncleanness. When he knows about it, he is guilty.

**4** A person can make a careless promise to do bad or good. People can make many types of careless promises. When he knows about his promise, he is guilty.

**5** When a person is guilty of 1 of these things, he must say that he did the sin.

**6** He must bring a guilt offering to the LORD for his sin. He must bring a female lamb or goat from the flock as a sin offering. The priest must make atonement for his sin.

**7** If he does not have sufficient money for a lamb, he must bring 2 doves or 2 young pigeons to the LORD for his sin. He must bring 1 bird for a sin offering and 1 bird for a burnt offering.

**8** He must bring them to the priest. The priest must offer the bird for the sin offering first. He must twist its head from its neck, but he must not remove the head.

**9** He must sprinkle some of the blood of the sin offering on the side of the altar. He must drain the remaining blood at the bottom of the altar. It is a sin offering.

**10** He must offer the second bird as a burnt offering, as the rules tell. The priest must make atonement for the sin of the person, and God will forgive him.

**11** If he does not have sufficient money for 2 doves or 2 young pigeons, he must bring fine flour as his sin offering. He must bring 1/10 of an ephah. He must not put oil or frankincense on it, because it is a sin offering.

**12** He must bring it to the priest. The priest must take a handful of it as a memorial portion. He must burn it on the altar with the offerings by fire to the LORD. It is a sin offering.

**13** The priest must make atonement for the person for his sin, and God will forgive him. The remaining flour is for the priest, the same as a grain offering.'"

**14** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**15** "A person can be unfaithful and sin by accident about the holy things of the LORD. Then he must bring a ram with no defects from the flock to the LORD. You must calculate its value in silver shekels, by the shekel of the holy place. It is a guilt offering.

**16** He must pay for the damage that he caused to the holy thing. He must add 1/5 of its value and give it to the priest. The priest must make atonement for him with the ram of the guilt offering, and God will forgive him.

**17** A person can sin and do a thing that the commands of the LORD do not permit. If he does not know about it, he is still guilty. He is responsible for his guilt.

**18** He must bring to the priest a ram with no defects from the flock, at your calculated value. It is a guilt offering. The priest must make atonement for the mistake that the person did without knowledge. Then God will forgive him.

**19** It is a guilt offering. He is guilty in front of the LORD."

## Chapter 6

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**2** "A person can sin and be unfaithful to the LORD. He can lie to his neighbor about a thing that the neighbor gave him to keep. He can lie about a thing that the neighbor left with him. He can steal from his neighbor or cheat him.

**3** He can find a lost thing and lie about it. He can make a false promise. A person can sin in any of these ways.

**4** If he sins in this way and is guilty, he must give back the thing that he stole. He must give back the thing that he got by cheating. He must give back the thing that the neighbor gave him to keep, or the lost thing that he found.

**5** He must give back anything about which he made a false promise. He must pay the full value and add 1/5 to it. He must give it to the owner on the day that he brings his guilt offering.

**6** He must bring his guilt offering to the priest for the LORD. It must be a ram with no defects from the flock, at your calculated value.

**7** The priest must make atonement for him in front of the LORD. Then God will forgive him for any of the things that he did wrong."

**8** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**9** "Command Aaron and his sons. Tell them, 'This is the law of the burnt offering. The burnt offering must stay on the altar all night until the morning. The fire on the altar must continue to burn.

**10** The priest must put on his linen clothes and his linen underwear. He must remove the ashes of the burnt offering that the fire burned on the altar. He must put the ashes next to the altar.

**11** Then he must remove his clothes and put on other clothes. He must carry the ashes outside the camp to a clean place.

**12** The fire on the altar must continue to burn. Do not let it stop. Each morning the priest must put wood on the fire. He must put the burnt offering on the wood. He must burn the fat of the peace offerings on it.

**13** The fire must always burn on the altar. It must never stop.

**14** This is the law of the grain offering. The sons of Aaron must offer it in front of the LORD, in front of the altar.

**15** The priest must take a handful of the flour and oil of the grain offering, with all the frankincense on it. He must burn it on the altar as the memorial portion. Its smell pleases the LORD.

**16** Aaron and his sons must eat the remaining part. They must eat it without yeast in a holy place. They must eat it in the courtyard of the Tent of Meeting.

**17** Do not bake it with yeast. I give it to the priests as their part of my offerings by fire. It is most holy, the same as the sin offering and the guilt offering.

**18** All the males among the descendants of Aaron can eat it. This is a permanent law for all your descendants about the offerings by fire to the LORD. Each thing that touches these offerings will become holy.'"

**19** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**20** "This is the offering that Aaron and his sons must offer to the LORD on the day of the anointing of a priest. They must offer 1/10 of an ephah of fine flour as a regular grain offering. They must offer half of it in the morning and half of it in the evening.

**21** Make it with oil on a flat pan. Mix it well and bring it in. Offer the grain offering in baked pieces. Its smell pleases the LORD.

**22** The son of Aaron who becomes the anointed priest after him must offer it. This is a permanent law. The priest must burn all of it for the LORD.

**23** The priest must burn all of each grain offering for a priest. No person must eat it."

**24** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**25** "Speak to Aaron and his sons. Tell them, 'This is the law of the sin offering. Kill the sin offering in front of the LORD, in the place where you kill the burnt offering. It is most holy.

**26** The priest who offers it for sin must eat it. He must eat it in a holy place, in the courtyard of the Tent of Meeting.

**27** Each thing that touches its meat will become holy. If some of its blood falls on clothing, wash the clothing in a holy place.

**28** If you cook the meat in a clay pot, break the pot. If you cook the meat in a bronze pot, clean the pot fully and wash it with water.

**29** All the males among the priests can eat it. It is most holy.

**30** But if a priest brings any of the blood of a sin offering into the Tent of Meeting to make atonement in the holy place, no person must eat that offering. Burn it in the fire.'"

## Chapter 7

**1** "'This is the law of the guilt offering. It is most holy.

**2** Kill the guilt offering in the place where you kill the burnt offering. The priest must sprinkle its blood on all sides of the altar.

**3** He must offer all its fat. He must offer the fat tail and the fat that covers the inner organs.

**4** He must also remove the 2 kidneys with their fat, near the lower back. He must remove the best part of the liver with the kidneys.

**5** The priest must burn these parts on the altar as an offering by fire to the LORD. It is a guilt offering.

**6** All the males among the priests can eat it. They must eat it in a holy place. It is most holy.

**7** The same law is for the sin offering and the guilt offering. The offering is for the priest who makes atonement with it.

**8** When a priest offers a burnt offering for a person, the skin of that animal is for the priest.

**9** Each grain offering that a person bakes in an oven or cooks in a pot or on a flat pan is for the priest who offers it.

**10** Each grain offering, mixed with oil or dry, is for all the sons of Aaron. Each son must get an equal part.

**11** This is the law of the peace offering that a person offers to the LORD.

**12** If he offers it to say thanks, he must offer bread with the sacrifice. He must offer thick loaves without yeast, mixed with oil. He must offer thin wafers without yeast, with oil on them. He must offer loaves of fine flour, mixed well with oil.

**13** With his peace offering of thanks, he must also offer loaves of bread made with yeast.

**14** He must offer 1 loaf of each type as a gift to the LORD. This bread is for the priest who sprinkles the blood of the peace offering.

**15** The person must eat the meat of his peace offering of thanks on the day that he offers it. He must not keep any of it until the morning.

**16** But his sacrifice can be for a promise (vow), or it can be a free offering. Then he must eat it on the day that he offers it. He can eat the remaining part on the next day.

**17** But on the third day, he must burn the remaining meat of the sacrifice.

**18** If a person eats any of the meat of his peace offering on the third day, the LORD will not accept the offering. The offering will not help the person who offered it. The meat will be disgusting. The person who eats it will be responsible for his guilt.

**19** Do not eat meat that touches any unclean thing. Burn it. Any person who is clean can eat the other meat.

**20** If a person is unclean and eats the meat of a peace offering for the LORD, remove that person from his people.

**21** A person can touch an unclean thing. It can be an unclean thing from a human, an unclean animal, or any disgusting unclean thing. If that person eats the meat of a peace offering for the LORD, remove that person from his people.'"

**22** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**23** "Speak to the people of Israel. Tell them, 'Do not eat any fat from cattle, sheep, or goats.

**24** You can use the fat of an animal that dies naturally or that wild animals kill. You can use it for other work. But you must not eat it.

**25** People offer some animals as an offering by fire to the LORD. If a person eats the fat of such an animal, remove that person from his people.

**26** In all the places where you live, do not eat any blood. Do not eat the blood of birds or of animals.

**27** If a person eats any blood, remove that person from his people.'"

**28** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**29** "Speak to the people of Israel. Tell them, 'A person who offers a peace offering to the LORD must bring a part of it as a gift to the LORD.

**30** He must bring the offering by fire to the LORD with his own hands. He must bring the fat with the breast. The priest must wave the breast in front of the LORD as a wave offering.

**31** The priest must burn the fat on the altar. But the breast is for Aaron and his sons.

**32** Give the right thigh from your peace offerings to the priest as a gift.

**33** The son of Aaron who offers the blood and the fat of the peace offering must get the right thigh as his part.

**34** I take the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the gift from the people of Israel. I take these parts from their peace offerings. I give them to Aaron the priest and to his sons. This is a permanent law for the people of Israel.'"

**35** This is the part of the offerings by fire to the LORD for Aaron and his sons. They got this part on the day when Moses brought them to serve the LORD as priests.

**36** On the day that the LORD anointed them, he commanded the people of Israel to give them this part. It is a permanent law for all their descendants.

**37** This is the law for the burnt offering, the grain offering, the sin offering, and the guilt offering. It is the law for the ordination offering and the peace offering.

**38** The LORD gave this law to Moses on Mount Sinai. He gave it on the day that he commanded the people of Israel to bring their offerings to the LORD in the desert of Sinai.

## Chapter 8

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**2** "Bring Aaron and his sons. Bring the clothes, the anointing oil, the bull for the sin offering, the 2 rams, and the basket of bread without yeast.

**3** Gather all the community at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting."

**4** Moses did as the LORD commanded him. The community gathered at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

**5** Moses said to the community, "This is what the LORD commands us to do."

**6** Moses brought Aaron and his sons and washed them with water.

**7** He put the coat on Aaron and tied the sash around him. He put the robe on him and put the ephod on him. He tied the woven belt of the ephod around him to attach the ephod.

**8** He put the breastplate on Aaron. He put the Urim and the Thummim in the breastplate.

**9** He put the turban on the head of Aaron. On the front of the turban, he put the gold plate, the holy crown. Moses did as the LORD commanded him.

**10** Moses took the anointing oil. He poured it on the tabernacle and on all the things in it, and he made them holy.

**11** He sprinkled some of the oil on the altar 7 times. He poured oil on the altar and all its equipment, and on the basin and its base, to make them holy.

**12** He poured some of the anointing oil on the head of Aaron. He anointed Aaron to make him holy.

**13** Moses brought the sons of Aaron. He put coats on them and tied sashes around them. He put caps on their heads. Moses did as the LORD commanded him.

**14** Moses brought the bull for the sin offering. Aaron and his sons put their hands on the head of the bull.

**15** Moses killed the bull. He took the blood and put it on all the horns of the altar with his finger. Thus, he made the altar pure. He poured out the remaining blood at the bottom of the altar. He made the altar holy, to make atonement for it.

**16** Moses took all the fat on the inner organs, the best part of the liver, and the 2 kidneys with their fat. He burned them on the altar.

**17** But he burned the bull, its skin, its meat, and its dung outside the camp. Moses did as the LORD commanded him.

**18** Then Moses brought the ram for the burnt offering. Aaron and his sons put their hands on the head of the ram.

**19** Moses killed the ram. He sprinkled the blood on all sides of the altar.

**20** He cut the ram into pieces. Moses burned the head, the pieces, and the fat.

**21** He washed the inner organs and the legs with water. Moses burned all of the ram on the altar. It was a burnt offering, an offering by fire to the LORD, and its smell pleased the LORD. Moses did as the LORD commanded him.

**22** Then Moses brought the other ram, the ram for the ordination. Aaron and his sons put their hands on the head of the ram.

**23** Moses killed the ram. He took some of its blood and put it on the bottom of the right ear of Aaron. He put blood on the thumb of his right hand and on the big toe of his right foot.

**24** Moses brought the sons of Aaron. He put some of the blood on the bottom of their right ears. He put blood on the thumbs of their right hands and on the big toes of their right feet. Moses sprinkled the remaining blood on all sides of the altar.

**25** He took the fat, the fat tail, and all the fat on the inner organs. He took the best part of the liver, the 2 kidneys with their fat, and the right thigh.

**26** From the basket of bread without yeast in front of the LORD, he took 1 thick loaf, 1 loaf with oil, and 1 thin wafer. He put them on the fat and on the right thigh.

**27** He put all these things in the hands of Aaron and his sons. They waved them in front of the LORD as a wave offering.

**28** Then Moses took these things from their hands. He burned them on the altar on top of the burnt offering. It was an ordination offering, and its smell pleased the LORD. It was an offering by fire to the LORD.

**29** Moses took the breast and waved it in front of the LORD as a wave offering. It was the part of the ram of ordination for Moses, as the LORD commanded him.

**30** Moses took some of the anointing oil and some of the blood on the altar. He sprinkled it on Aaron and his clothes, and on his sons and their clothes. Thus, he made Aaron, his sons, and their clothes holy.

**31** Moses said to Aaron and his sons, "Boil the meat at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. Eat it there with the bread in the basket of the ordination offerings. I commanded, 'Aaron and his sons must eat it.'

**32** Burn the remaining meat and bread.

**33** Do not go out of the entrance of the Tent of Meeting for 7 days, until the days of your ordination are complete. Your ordination will continue for 7 days.

**34** The LORD commanded us to do what we did today, to make atonement for you.

**35** Stay at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting day and night for 7 days. Obey the commands of the LORD. If you do not obey, you will die. The LORD commanded me to tell you this."

**36** Aaron and his sons did all the things that the LORD commanded through Moses.

## Chapter 9

**1** On the eighth day, Moses called Aaron, his sons, and the elders of Israel.

**2** He said to Aaron, "Take a young calf for a sin offering and a ram for a burnt offering. Both animals must have no defects. Offer them in front of the LORD.

**3** Then tell the people of Israel, 'Take a male goat for a sin offering. Take a calf and a lamb for a burnt offering. Both animals must be 1 year old with no defects.

**4** Take a bull and a ram for peace offerings, to sacrifice in front of the LORD. Take a grain offering mixed with oil. Today the LORD will appear to you.'"

**5** The people brought the things that Moses commanded to the front of the Tent of Meeting. All the community came near and stood in front of the LORD.

**6** Moses said, "The LORD commands you to do this. Then the glory of the LORD will appear to you."

**7** Moses said to Aaron, "Go to the altar. Offer your sin offering and your burnt offering. Make atonement for yourself and for the people. Offer the offering of the people and make atonement for them, as the LORD commanded."

**8** Thus, Aaron went to the altar. He killed the calf as the sin offering for himself.

**4** These are the festivals of the LORD. They are holy meetings. You must announce them at their correct times.

**5** The Passover of the LORD starts in the evening on the 14th day of the 1st month.

**6** On the 15th day of the same month, the Festival of Bread Without Yeast to the LORD starts. For 7 days you must eat bread without yeast.

**7** On the 1st day, you must have a holy meeting. Do not do your usual work on that day.

**8** For 7 days you must give an offering by fire to the LORD. On the 7th day, you must have a holy meeting. Do not do your usual work on that day.'"

**9** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**10** "Speak to the people of Israel. Tell them, 'You will go into the land that I give you. When you cut the grain there, bring the first bundle of grain to the priest.

**11** The priest must lift up the bundle in front of the LORD. Then the LORD will accept it for you. The priest must do this on the day after the Sabbath.

**12** On the day that you lift up the bundle, offer a male lamb that is 1 year old. It must have no defect. It is a burnt offering to the LORD.

**13** With it, give a grain offering of 2/10 of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil. It is an offering by fire to the LORD, and its smell pleases him. Also give a drink offering of 1/4 of a hin of wine.

**14** Do not eat bread, roasted grain or new grain until that day. First you must bring the offering to your God. This is a permanent rule for all your generations in all your homes.

**15** Count 7 full weeks from the day after the Sabbath. Start on the day that you bring the bundle of grain as a wave offering.

**16** Count 50 days to the day after the 7th Sabbath. Then give a new grain offering to the LORD.

**17** From your homes, bring 2 loaves of bread as a wave offering. Make each loaf from 2/10 of an ephah of fine flour. Bake them with yeast. They are the first part of the harvest for the LORD.

**18** With the bread, offer 7 lambs that are 1 year old with no defect. Also offer 1 young bull and 2 rams. They are a burnt offering to the LORD. Give them with their grain offering and their drink offerings. They are an offering by fire, and their smell pleases the LORD.

**19** Then sacrifice 1 male goat for a sin offering. Also sacrifice 2 lambs that are 1 year old for a peace offering.

**20** The priest must lift up the 2 lambs and the bread of the first part of the harvest. They are a wave offering in front of the LORD. They are holy to the LORD and they are for the priest.

**21** On that same day, announce a holy meeting. Do not do your usual work on that day. This is a permanent rule in all your homes for all your generations.

**22** When you cut the grain in your land, do not cut the grain at the edges of your field. Do not pick up the grain that falls. Leave it for the poor people and for the foreigners. I am the LORD your God.'"

**23** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**24** "Speak to the people of Israel. Tell them, 'The 1st day of the 7th month is a day of rest for you. You must blow trumpets to remember it. It is a holy meeting.

**25** Do not do your usual work on that day. Give an offering by fire to the LORD.'"

**26** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**27** "The 10th day of this 7th month is the Day of Atonement. On that day the priest removes the guilt of your sins (atonement). It is a holy meeting for you. You must not eat food on that day, and you must offer an offering by fire to the LORD.

**28** Do not do any work on that day. It is the Day of Atonement. On that day the priest makes atonement for you in front of the LORD your God.

**29** A person who eats food on that day must be removed from his people.

**30** If a person does any work on that day, I will destroy that person from among his people.

**31** Do not do any work. This is a permanent rule for all your generations in all your homes.

**32** It is a Sabbath of full rest for you. You must not eat food. Keep your Sabbath from the evening of the 9th day of the month until the next evening."

**33** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**34** "Speak to the people of Israel. Tell them, 'On the 15th day of this 7th month, the Festival of Shelters starts. It continues for 7 days for the LORD.

**35** On the 1st day there is a holy meeting. Do not do your usual work on that day.

**36** For 7 days you must give an offering by fire to the LORD. On the 8th day you must have a holy meeting. Give an offering by fire to the LORD. It is a special meeting. Do not do your usual work on that day.

**37** These are the festivals of the LORD. You must announce them as holy meetings. At these festivals you must give offerings by fire to the LORD. Give burnt offerings, grain offerings, sacrifices and drink offerings. Give each offering on its correct day.

**38** These offerings are in addition to the Sabbaths of the LORD. They are also in addition to your gifts, your vows and your freewill offerings to the LORD.

**39** On the 15th day of the 7th month, you will collect the crops of the land. Then keep the festival of the LORD for 7 days. The 1st day is a day of rest. The 8th day is also a day of rest.

**40** On the 1st day, take good fruit from the trees. Also take palm branches, branches from leafy trees and willows from the streams. Be happy in front of the LORD your God for 7 days.

**41** Keep this festival to the LORD for 7 days each year. This is a permanent rule for all your generations. Keep it in the 7th month.

**42** Live in shelters for 7 days. All the people who are born in Israel must live in shelters.

**43** Then your descendants will know that I made the people of Israel live in shelters. I did this when I brought them out of the land of Egypt. I am the LORD your God.'"

**44** Thus Moses told the people of Israel about the festivals of the LORD.

## Chapter 24

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**2** "Tell the people of Israel to bring you pure olive oil from crushed olives. The oil is for the light, so that the lamps always burn.

**3** In the Tent of Meeting, outside the curtain in front of the box of the Testimony, Aaron must keep the lamps burning. They must burn in front of the LORD from evening until morning. This is a permanent rule for all your generations.

**4** Aaron must always keep the lamps on the pure gold lampstand in front of the LORD.

**5** Take fine flour and bake 12 loaves of bread. Use 2/10 of an ephah of flour for each loaf.

**6** Put them in 2 rows, with 6 loaves in each row. Put them on the pure gold table in front of the LORD.

**7** Put pure incense on each row. The incense is a reminder for the bread. It is an offering by fire to the LORD.

**8** On each Sabbath, Aaron must put the bread in rows in front of the LORD. The people of Israel must always give this bread. It is a permanent agreement (covenant).

**9** The bread is for Aaron and his sons. They must eat it in a holy place, because it is a most holy part of the offerings by fire to the LORD. This is a permanent rule."

**10** The son of an Israelite woman and an Egyptian man went out among the people of Israel. In the camp, he started to fight with an Israelite man.

**11** The son of the Israelite woman spoke against the name of the LORD and he cursed. Thus the people brought him to Moses. His mother's name was Shelomith. She was the daughter of Dibri, from the tribe of Dan.

**12** The people kept him under guard until the LORD told them what to do.

**13** Then the LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**14** "Take the man who cursed outside the camp. All the people who heard him must put their hands on his head. Then all the people must throw stones at him until he dies.

**15** Tell the people of Israel, 'A person who curses his God will be guilty.

**16** A person who speaks against the name of the LORD must die. All the people must throw stones at him until he dies. The foreigner and the native Israelite must die if they speak against the name of the LORD.

**17** A person who kills a man must die.

**18** A person who kills an animal of another person must pay for it. He must give an animal for an animal.

**19** If a person injures another person, you must do the same thing to him.

**20** Give a broken bone for a broken bone, an eye for an eye and a tooth for a tooth. The person must get the same injury that he caused.

**21** A person who kills an animal must pay for it. But a person who kills a man must die.

**22** You must have the same law for the foreigner and for the native Israelite. I am the LORD your God.'"

**23** Then Moses spoke to the people of Israel. They took the man who cursed outside the camp. They threw stones at him until he died. The people of Israel did as the LORD commanded Moses.

## Chapter 25

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai. He said,

**2** "Speak to the people of Israel. Tell them, 'You will go into the land that I give you. Then the land must have a Sabbath rest to the LORD.

**3** For 6 years you can plant your fields. For 6 years you can cut back your vines and collect their fruit.

**4** But the 7th year is a Sabbath of full rest for the land. It is a Sabbath for the LORD. Do not plant your fields and do not cut back your vines.

**5** Do not cut the crops that grow without your help. Do not collect the grapes from vines that you did not cut back. It is a year of rest for the land.

**6** The crops that grow during the Sabbath year will be food for all of you. They will be food for you, your male and female slaves, your hired worker and the foreigner who lives with you.

**7** The crops will also be food for your livestock and for the wild animals in your land.

**8** Count 7 Sabbaths of years, 7 times 7 years. The 7 Sabbaths of years are a total of 49 years.

**9** Then on the 10th day of the 7th month, blow the trumpet loudly. On the Day of Atonement, blow the trumpet in all your land.

**10** Make the 50th year holy. Announce freedom in all the land for all the people who live there. It is a Jubilee year for you. Each man must go back to his own land and to his own family.

**11** The 50th year is a Jubilee for you. Do not plant. Do not cut the crops that grow without your help. Do not collect grapes from vines that you did not cut back.

**12** It is the Jubilee, and it is holy for you. Eat only the crops that grow in the fields.

**13** In this year of Jubilee, each man must go back to his own land.

**14** When you sell land to your neighbor or buy land from your neighbor, do not cheat one another.

**15** When you buy from your neighbor, calculate the price from the number of years after the Jubilee. He must sell to you as a function of the number of harvests that remain.

**16** If there are many years, increase the price. If there are few years, decrease the price. He sells you only the number of harvests.

**17** Do not cheat one another. Respect your God. I am the LORD your God.

**18** Obey my rules and my laws. Then you will live safely in the land.

**19** The land will give its fruit. You will eat as much as you want, and you will live safely in the land.

**20** You can ask, "What will we eat in the 7th year? We will not plant and we will not collect our crops."

**21** I will send my blessing to you in the 6th year. The land will give sufficient crops for 3 years.

**22** When you plant in the 8th year, you will still eat the old crops. You will eat the old crops until the harvest of the 9th year.

**23** Do not sell the land permanently, because the land is mine. You are only foreigners and guests with me.

**24** In all the land that you own, you must let the seller buy back the land.

**25** Your fellow Israelite can become poor and sell some of his land. Then his nearest relative must come and buy back what his relative sold.

**26** A man can have no relative to buy back the land. But later he can become rich and have sufficient money to buy it back himself.

**27** Then he must count the years after the sale. He must pay the remaining value to the man who bought the land. Then he can go back to his land.

**28** If he does not have sufficient money to buy it back, the buyer keeps the land until the year of Jubilee. In the Jubilee the land goes back to the seller. Then the seller can go back to his land.

**29** A man can sell a house in a city with walls. He can buy it back for 1 full year after the sale.

**30** If he does not buy it back in 1 full year, the house becomes the permanent property of the buyer and his descendants. The house does not go back to the seller in the Jubilee.

**31** But houses in villages without walls are the same as fields. The seller can buy them back. They go back to the seller in the Jubilee.

**32** But the Levites can always buy back their houses in the cities that they own.

**33** A Levite can sell a house in a Levite city and not buy it back. That house will go back to him in the Jubilee. The houses in the cities of the Levites are their property among the people of Israel.

**34** The fields around the Levite cities must not be sold. They are the permanent property of the Levites.

**35** Your fellow Israelite can become poor and unable to support himself. Then you must help him. Help him as you help a foreigner or a guest, so that he can continue to live near you.

**36** Do not take interest or profit from him. Respect your God. Thus your fellow Israelite can continue to live near you.

**37** Do not lend him money for interest. Do not sell him food for profit.

**38** I am the LORD your God. I brought you out of the land of Egypt to give you the land of Canaan and to be your God.

**39** Your fellow Israelite can become poor and sell himself to you. Do not make him work as a slave.

**40** Treat him as a hired worker or as a guest. He must work for you until the year of Jubilee.

**41** Then he and his children can leave you. He will go back to his family and to the land of his ancestors.

**42** The people of Israel are my servants. I brought them out of the land of Egypt. Thus they must not be sold as slaves.

**43** Do not be cruel to him. Respect your God.

**44** You can buy male and female slaves from the nations around you.

**45** You can also buy slaves from the foreigners who live among you. You can buy them from their families who are born in your land. They can become your property.

**46** You can give them to your children after you as permanent property. They can be your slaves permanently. But do not be cruel to your fellow Israelites.

**47** A foreigner or a guest among you can become rich. Your fellow Israelite can become poor and sell himself to that foreigner or to a member of the family of that foreigner.

**48** After he sells himself, one of his relatives can buy him back.

**49** His uncle, his cousin or any near relative from his family can buy him back. Or, if he gets sufficient money, he can buy himself back.

**50** He and the buyer must count the years from the sale to the year of Jubilee. The price for his freedom is a function of the number of years. Calculate his value as the pay of a hired worker for those years.

**51** If many years remain, he must pay back a larger part of his sale price.

**52** If only a few years remain until the Jubilee, he must count them. He must pay the price for his freedom from the number of years.

**53** He must be with the buyer as a worker that is hired each year. Make sure that the buyer is not cruel to him.

**54** If no one buys him back in these ways, he and his children will go free in the year of Jubilee.

**55** The people of Israel are my servants. I brought them out of the land of Egypt. I am the LORD your God.'"

## Chapter 26

**1** "Do not make idols for yourselves. Do not put up carved images or holy stone pillars. Do not put carved stones in your land to bow down to them. I am the LORD your God.

**2** Keep my Sabbaths and respect my holy place. I am the LORD.

**3** You must obey my rules and keep my commands.

**4** If you do, I will give you rain at the correct time. The land will give its crops, and the trees will give their fruit.

**5** You will have so much grain that you will continue to separate it until the grape harvest. The grape harvest will continue until the time to plant. You will eat all the food that you want, and you will live safely in your land.

**6** I will give peace in the land. You will lie down and no one will make you afraid. I will remove dangerous animals from the land. No army will attack your land.

**7** You will chase your enemies, and you will kill them with the sword.

**8** 5 of you will chase 100 enemies. 100 of you will chase 10,000 enemies. You will kill your enemies with the sword.

**9** I will be kind to you. I will give you many children and make you a large nation. I will keep my agreement (covenant) with you.

**10** You will still eat old crops when the new crops come. You will have to remove the old crops to make space for the new crops.

**11** I will put my tabernacle among you. I will not hate you.

**12** I will walk among you. I will be your God, and you will be my people.

**13** I am the LORD your God. I brought you out of the land of Egypt, so that you were not slaves of the Egyptians. I broke the heavy load on you and made you free.

**14** But you can refuse to listen to me and to obey all these commands.

**15** You can refuse my rules and hate my laws. You can disobey all my commands and break my covenant.

**16** If you do, I will do these things to you. I will send terror, disease and fever. These will make you blind and they will make you weak. You will plant your seed for no result, because your enemies will eat the crops.

**17** I will turn against you, and your enemies will defeat you. The people who hate you will rule you. You will run away when no one chases you.

**18** If you still do not listen to me after all this, I will punish you 7 times more for your sins.

**19** I will destroy your proud strength. I will make the sky above you as hard as iron. I will make the ground below you as hard as bronze.

**20** Your work will be for no result. Your land will not give its crops, and the trees will not give their fruit.

**21** If you continue to act against me and refuse to listen to me, I will punish you 7 times more for your sins.

**22** I will send wild animals among you. They will kill your children and destroy your cattle. You will become few, and your roads will be empty.

**23** If these things do not correct you and you continue to act against me,

**24** then I will act against you. I will punish you 7 times more for your sins.

**25** I will bring war against you to punish you because you broke my covenant. You will go into your cities for safety, but I will send disease among you. Then your enemies will defeat you.

**26** I will stop your supply of food. 10 women will bake all your bread in 1 oven. They will measure your bread by weight. You will eat, but you will still be hungry.

**27** If you still do not listen to me after all this, and you continue to act against me,

**28** then I will act against you in anger. I myself will punish you 7 times more for your sins.

**29** You will be so hungry that you will eat the bodies of your sons and your daughters.

**30** I will destroy your high places of worship. I will cut down your incense altars. I will throw your dead bodies on the remains of your idols. I will hate you.

**31** I will make your cities into ruins. I will destroy your holy places. I will not accept the pleasant smell of your offerings.

**32** I will destroy the land. Your enemies who come to live there will be shocked at it.

**33** I will send you away among the nations. I will pull out my sword and chase you. Your land will be empty and your cities will be ruins.

**34** Then the land will have its Sabbath years. The land will rest while it is empty and you are in the land of your enemies. Then the land will rest and have its Sabbaths.

**35** While the land is empty, it will rest. It did not rest during your Sabbaths when you lived on it.

**36** I will make the survivors very afraid in the lands of their enemies. The sound of a leaf in the wind will make them run away. They will run as if from a sword. They will fall when no one chases them.

**37** They will fall over one another, as if they run from a sword. But no one will chase them. You will not have the strength to fight your enemies.

**38** You will die among the nations. The land of your enemies will destroy you.

**39** The survivors will become weak in the lands of their enemies because of their sins. They will also become weak because of the sins of their ancestors.

**40** But the survivors can confess their sins and the sins of their ancestors. They can confess that they were not loyal to me and that they acted against me.

**41** They can know that I acted against them and brought them into the land of their enemies. Their stubborn hearts can become humble, and they can accept the punishment for their sins.

**42** Then I will remember my covenant with Jacob, my covenant with Isaac and my covenant with Abraham. I will also remember the land.

**43** The people will leave the land empty, and the land will have its Sabbaths while it is empty. The people will accept the punishment for their sins. They will get this punishment because they refused my laws and hated my rules.

**44** But when they are in the land of their enemies, I will not refuse them. I will not hate them so much that I destroy them all. I will not break my covenant with them. I am the LORD their God.

**45** For them I will remember the covenant with their ancestors. I brought those ancestors out of the land of Egypt while the nations watched. I did this to be their God. I am the LORD."

**46** These are the rules, the decisions and the laws that the LORD gave on Mount Sinai. He gave them through Moses to the people of Israel.

## Chapter 27

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**2** "Speak to the people of Israel. Tell them, 'A man can make a special promise (vow) to give a person to the LORD. He can pay money for the value of that person.

**3** The value of a male from 20 to 60 years old is 50 shekels of silver. Use the standard shekel of the holy place.

**4** The value of a female of that age is 30 shekels.

**5** The value of a male from 5 to 20 years old is 20 shekels. The value of a female of that age is 10 shekels.

**6** The value of a male from 1 month to 5 years old is 5 shekels of silver. The value of a female of that age is 3 shekels of silver.

**7** The value of a male who is 60 years old or more is 15 shekels. The value of a female of that age is 10 shekels.

**8** But the man who makes the vow can be too poor to pay the value. Then he must bring the person to the priest. The priest will set a value that the man can pay.

**9** A man can promise to give an animal that the LORD accepts as an offering. All such animals that a man gives to the LORD become holy.

**10** He must not change the animal for a different animal. He must not change a good animal for a bad animal, or a bad animal for a good animal. If he changes one animal for a different animal, both animals become holy.

**11** A man can promise to give an unclean animal that the LORD does not accept as an offering. Then he must bring the animal to the priest.

**12** The priest will set its value as good or bad. The value that the priest sets is the value.

**13** If the man wants to buy back the animal, he must pay its value and add 1/5.

**14** A man can give his house as a holy gift to the LORD. Then the priest will set its value as good or bad. The value that the priest sets is the value.

**15** If the man who gave the house wants to buy it back, he must pay its value and add 1/5. Then the house will be his again.

**16** A man can give to the LORD a part of the land of his family. Then calculate its value from the quantity of seed that is necessary to plant it. The value is 50 shekels of silver for each homer of barley seed.

**17** If he gives his field during the year of Jubilee, it keeps the full value.

**18** But if he gives his field after the Jubilee, the priest will calculate its value from the years that remain until the next Jubilee. The priest will decrease the full value.

**19** If the man who gave the field wants to buy it back, he must pay its value and add 1/5. Then the field will be his again.

**20** But he can fail to buy back the field, or he can sell the field to a different man. Then he cannot buy it back again.

**21** When the field becomes free in the Jubilee, it will be holy to the LORD. It will be a field that is given permanently to the LORD. It will become the property of the priests.

**22** A man can give to the LORD a field that he bought, which is not part of the land of his family.

**23** Then the priest will calculate its value until the year of Jubilee. The man must pay that value on the same day. The money is holy to the LORD.

**24** In the year of Jubilee, the field will go back to the man who sold it. It will go back to the person who owned the land.

**25** Calculate all values with the standard shekel of the holy place. 1 shekel is 20 gerahs.

**26** But no man can give the firstborn of an animal to the LORD, because the firstborn is already the property of the LORD. A firstborn bull or sheep is the LORD's.

**27** If the firstborn is from an unclean animal, the man can buy it back. He must pay its value and add 1/5. If he does not buy it back, the priest must sell it for its value.

**28** But a man can give a thing permanently to the LORD. It can be a person, an animal or a field from his family land. No one can sell it or buy it back. All things that are given permanently are most holy to the LORD.

**29** A person who is given permanently for destruction must not be bought back. That person must die.

**30** 1/10 (the tithe) of all the crops of the land is the LORD's. This includes the grain of the fields and the fruit of the trees. It is holy to the LORD.

**31** If a man wants to buy back a part of his tithe, he must pay its value and add 1/5.

**32** Every 10th animal from the herds and flocks is holy to the LORD. The shepherd counts the animals as they go under his stick.

**33** The owner must not choose between good and bad animals. He must not change one animal for a different animal. If he changes one, both animals become holy. He cannot buy them back.'"

**34** These are the commands that the LORD gave to Moses on Mount Sinai for the people of Israel.
