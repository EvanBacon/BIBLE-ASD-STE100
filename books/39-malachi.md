# Malachi

## Chapter 1

**1** This is a message from the LORD to Israel. The LORD gave it through Malachi.

**2** "I loved you," says the LORD. "But you ask, 'How did you love us?' Esau was the brother of Jacob," says the LORD. "But I loved Jacob,

**3** and I rejected Esau. I made the mountains of Esau empty. I gave his land to the wild dogs of the desert."

**4** The people of Edom say, "Our enemies destroyed us. But we will build our ruined cities again." The LORD of armies says this: "They will build, but I will destroy. People will call Edom 'the evil land.' They will call its people 'the people with whom the LORD is always angry.'

**5** You will see this with your eyes. You will say, 'The LORD is great, even outside the borders of Israel.'

**6** A son gives honor to his father. A servant gives honor to his master. I am your father, so where is my honor? I am your master, so where is your respect for me?" The LORD of armies says this to you, priests. "You show no respect for my name. But you ask, 'How did we show no respect for your name?'

**7** You put unclean food on my altar. But you ask, 'How did we make you unclean?' You did it when you said, 'The table of the LORD is not important.'

**8** You bring blind animals as a sacrifice. That is wrong! You bring animals that cannot walk well or that are sick. That is wrong! Give them to your governor. Will he be pleased with you? Will he accept you?" says the LORD of armies.

**9** "Now ask God to be kind to us. But you did these bad things. Will God accept you?" says the LORD of armies.

**10** "I want one of you to close the doors of the temple. Then you will not light useless fires on my altar. I am not pleased with you," says the LORD of armies. "I will not accept an offering from your hands.

**11** My name will be great among the nations, from the east to the west. In every place people will burn incense to me. They will bring clean offerings to me. My name will be great among the nations," says the LORD of armies.

**12** "But you show no respect for my name. You say, 'The table of the Lord is unclean. Its food is not important.'

**13** You say, 'This work is too difficult!' You show no respect for it," says the LORD of armies. "You bring animals that you stole. You bring animals that cannot walk well or that are sick. You bring these as offerings! Must I accept them from your hands?" says the LORD.

**14** "A cheat has a good male animal in his flock. He promises to give it to the Lord. But then he sacrifices a bad animal to the Lord. I will curse that cheat! I am a great King," says the LORD of armies. "The nations have fear of my name."

## Chapter 2

**1** "Now, priests, this command is for you.

**2** You must listen to me. You must decide in your heart to give honor to my name," says the LORD of armies. "If you do not, I will send a curse on you. I will change your blessings into curses. I already cursed them, because you did not decide in your heart to give me honor.

**3** I will punish your children. I will put on your faces the waste from the animals of your festivals. People will throw you out with the waste.

**4** Then you will know that I sent this command to you. I want my agreement (covenant) with Levi to continue," says the LORD of armies.

**5** "My covenant with Levi gave him life and peace. I gave these things to him so that he would respect me. He respected me and had fear of my name.

**6** He taught true instructions. He did not say wrong things. He lived with me in peace and honesty. He turned many people away from sin.

**7** A priest must keep knowledge and teach it. People must get instructions from him, because he is the messenger of the LORD of armies.

**8** But you turned away from the correct way. Your teaching caused many people to sin. You broke the covenant with Levi," says the LORD of armies.

**9** "Thus I made all the people think that you are low and have no value. You did not obey my ways. When you taught the law, you treated people unfairly."

**10** We all have one father. One God made us all. Thus why do we cheat each other? When we do this, we show no respect for the covenant of our ancestors.

**11** The people of Judah cheated. The people of Israel and Jerusalem did a terrible thing. The men of Judah showed no respect for the holy temple that the LORD loves. They married women who worship foreign gods.

**12** May the LORD remove from the people of Jacob every man who does this. It does not matter who he is. It does not matter if he brings offerings to the LORD of armies.

**13** You also do another thing. You cover the altar of the LORD with tears. You cry aloud, because the LORD does not look at your offerings now. He does not accept them from your hands with pleasure.

**14** You ask, "Why does he not accept them?" He does not accept them because the LORD saw what you did to your wife. You married her when you were young. You promised to be faithful to her. She is your partner. But you were not faithful to her.

**15** God made you and your wife one. Your body and spirit belong to him. Why did he make you one? He wants children who obey God. Thus be careful. Do not be unfaithful to the wife that you married when you were young.

**16** "I hate divorce," says the LORD, the God of Israel. "I hate it when a man is cruel to his wife," says the LORD of armies. "Thus be careful, and do not be unfaithful."

**17** You make the LORD tired with your words. But you ask, "How do we make him tired?" You do it when you say, "All people who do evil are good to the LORD. He is pleased with them." You also do it when you ask, "Where is the God who is fair?"

## Chapter 3

**1** "Look, I will send my messenger. He will prepare the way in front of me. Then the Lord whom you look for will come quickly to his temple. The messenger of the covenant whom you want will come," says the LORD of armies.

**2** But who can live through the day when he comes? Who can stand when he appears? He will be like a fire that makes metal pure. He will be like strong soap that makes clothes clean.

**3** He will sit like a worker who makes silver pure. He will make the sons of Levi pure, as a worker makes gold and silver pure. Then they will bring correct offerings to the LORD.

**4** Then the LORD will accept with pleasure the offerings of Judah and Jerusalem. It will be as it was in the past, in the years before.

**5** "I will come near to you to judge you. I will quickly speak against people who use magic. I will speak against people who are not faithful in marriage. I will speak against people who make false promises. I will speak against people who cheat workers of their pay. I will speak against people who are cruel to widows, to children with no father, and to foreigners. These people do not respect me," says the LORD of armies.

**6** "I am the LORD, and I do not change. Thus you, the descendants of Jacob, are not destroyed.

**7** Since the time of your ancestors, you turned away from my laws. You did not obey them. Come back to me, and I will come back to you," says the LORD of armies. "But you ask, 'How must we come back?'

**8** Can a man steal from God? But you steal from me! You ask, 'How do we steal from you?' You steal the tenth part (tithe) and the offerings that you must give.

**9** A curse is on you, because all of you, the whole nation, steal from me.

**10** Bring the full tithe into the storage room so that there is food in my house. Test me in this," says the LORD of armies. "You will see that I will open the windows of heaven for you. I will pour out a very large blessing on you. You will not have sufficient space for all of it.

**11** I will stop the insects that eat your crops. They will not destroy the crops of your ground. Your grapes will not fall from the vine before the correct time," says the LORD of armies.

**12** "All the nations will say that you are blessed. Your land will be a land that gives pleasure," says the LORD of armies.

**13** "You spoke strong words against me," says the LORD. "But you ask, 'What did we say against you?'

**14** You said, 'It has no value to serve God. We obeyed his commands. We showed the LORD of armies that we were sorry for our sins. But we got nothing for it.

**15** Thus now we say that proud people are happy. People who do evil are successful. They test God, and nothing bad occurs to them.'"

**16** Then the people who respected the LORD spoke with each other. The LORD listened and heard them. In front of the LORD, a book of memories was written about the people who respected the LORD and thought about his name.

**17** "They will be mine," says the LORD of armies. "On the day when I act, they will be my special possession. I will be kind to them, as a man is kind to his son who serves him.

**18** Then you will again see the difference between good people and evil people. You will see the difference between people who serve God and people who do not serve him."

## Chapter 4

**1** "Look, the day will come that will burn like an oven. All the proud people and all the people who do evil will be like dry straw. That day will burn them up," says the LORD of armies. "Nothing will remain of them, not a root and not a branch.

**2** But for you who respect my name, the sun of goodness will rise. Its rays will heal you. You will go out and jump with joy like young cows that someone lets out of the barn.

**3** Then you will walk on the evil people. They will be like ashes under your feet on the day when I do these things," says the LORD of armies.

**4** "Remember the law of my servant Moses. I gave him the laws and rules on Mount Horeb for all the people of Israel.

**5** Look, I will send the prophet Elijah to you. He will come before the great and terrible day of the LORD.

**6** He will bring fathers and their children together again in love. If he does not, I will come and destroy the land with a curse."
