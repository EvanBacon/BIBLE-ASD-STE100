# Ruth

## Chapter 1

**1** At the time when the judges ruled, there was a famine in the land. A man from Beth-lehem in Judah went to live for a time in the country of Moab. His wife and his 2 sons went with him.

**2** The name of the man was Elimelech. The name of his wife was Naomi. The names of his 2 sons were Mahlon and Chilion. They were Ephrathites from Beth-lehem in Judah. They went into the country of Moab and lived there.

**3** Then Elimelech, the husband of Naomi, died. Naomi stayed with her 2 sons.

**4** The 2 sons married women from Moab. The name of one woman was Orpah. The name of the other woman was Ruth. They lived there for approximately 10 years.

**5** Then Mahlon and Chilion also died. Naomi had no husband and no sons.

**6** In the country of Moab, Naomi heard that the LORD helped his people and gave them food. So she and her daughters-in-law prepared to go back from the country of Moab.

**7** She left the place where she lived, and her 2 daughters-in-law went with her. They started on the road back to the land of Judah.

**8** Naomi said to her 2 daughters-in-law, "Go back, each of you, to the house of your mother. You were kind to your dead husbands and to me. May the LORD be kind to you also.

**9** May the LORD let each of you find rest in the house of a new husband." Then she kissed them, and they cried loudly.

**10** They said to her, "No, we will go back with you to your people."

**11** But Naomi said, "Go back, my daughters. Why do you want to go with me? I cannot have more sons who can become your husbands.

**12** Go back, my daughters, go home. I am too old to have a husband. Possibly I could have a husband tonight and give birth to sons.

**13** But would you wait for them until they become adults? Would you not marry for all that time? No, my daughters. My life is more bitter than your lives, because the LORD is against me."

**14** Again they cried loudly. Then Orpah kissed her mother-in-law and left. But Ruth held on to Naomi.

**15** Naomi said, "Look, your sister-in-law goes back to her people and to her gods. Go back with her."

**16** But Ruth said, "Do not tell me to leave you or to stop following you. Where you go, I will go. Where you stay, I will stay. Your people will be my people, and your God will be my God.

**17** Where you die, I will die. There they will bury me. Only death will separate you and me. If I break this promise, may the LORD punish me very strongly."

**18** Naomi saw that Ruth was firmly decided to go with her. So Naomi stopped arguing with her.

**19** So the 2 women went on until they came to Beth-lehem. When they arrived in Beth-lehem, all the people of the city were excited because of them. The women of the city said, "Is this Naomi?"

**20** Naomi said to them, "Do not call me Naomi (Pleasant). Call me Mara (Bitter), because the Almighty made my life very bitter.

**21** I went away full, but the LORD brought me back empty. Why do you call me Naomi? The LORD is against me. The Almighty caused me to have much trouble."

**22** So Naomi came back from the country of Moab with Ruth the Moabite, her daughter-in-law. They came to Beth-lehem at the start of the barley harvest.

## Chapter 2

**1** Naomi had a relative of her husband. He was a rich and important man from the clan of Elimelech. His name was Boaz.

**2** Ruth the Moabite said to Naomi, "Please let me go to the fields. I will pick up the grain that the harvest workers leave. I will follow the man who is kind to me." Naomi said to her, "Go, my daughter."

**3** So Ruth went and picked up grain in a field behind the harvest workers. By chance, she came to a part of the field that was the property of Boaz. Boaz was from the clan of Elimelech.

**4** Then Boaz came from Beth-lehem. He said to the harvest workers, "The LORD be with you." They answered him, "The LORD bless you."

**5** Boaz said to the servant who controlled the harvest workers, "Who is this young woman? To whom is she related?"

**6** The servant who controlled the harvest workers answered, "She is the young Moabite woman who came back with Naomi from the country of Moab.

**7** She said, 'Please let me pick up the grain among the bundles behind the harvest workers.' She came and worked from morning until now. She only rested a short time in the shelter."

**8** Then Boaz said to Ruth, "Listen to me, my daughter. Do not go to pick up grain in a different field. Do not go away from here. Stay here near my female workers.

**9** Watch the field where the men cut the grain, and follow the women. I told the young men not to touch you. When you are thirsty, go to the water jars. Drink the water that the young men put in the jars."

**10** Then Ruth bowed down with her face to the ground. She said to Boaz, "I am a foreigner. Why are you so kind to me? Why do you notice me?"

**11** Boaz answered her, "People told me all that you did for your mother-in-law after your husband died. You left your father and mother and the land of your birth. You came to live with a people that you did not know before.

**12** May the LORD pay you back for what you did. You came to the LORD, the God of Israel, for protection, as a bird hides below the wings of its mother. May he give you a full reward."

**13** Ruth said, "My lord, I hope that you continue to be kind to me. You comforted me and spoke kindly to me, your servant. But I am not equal to one of your servants."

**14** At mealtime Boaz said to her, "Come here. Eat some bread and put it in the vinegar." So she sat next to the harvest workers. He gave her some roasted grain. She ate until she was full, and she had some food left.

**15** Then she got up to pick up grain again. Boaz gave a command to his young men. He said, "Let her pick up grain even among the bundles. Do not stop her.

**16** Also, pull out some grain from the bundles for her and leave it on the ground. Let her pick it up. Do not speak angrily to her."

**17** So Ruth picked up grain in the field until evening. Then she beat the grain that she gathered to separate it. There was approximately 1 ephah (approximately 22 liters) of barley.

**18** She took the barley and went into the city. Her mother-in-law saw how much grain she gathered. Ruth also gave Naomi the food that she kept after she ate her meal.

**19** Her mother-in-law said to her, "Where did you pick up grain today? Where did you work? May God bless the man who noticed you." Ruth told her mother-in-law about the man in whose field she worked. She said, "The name of the man in whose field I worked today is Boaz."

**20** Naomi said to her daughter-in-law, "May the LORD bless him! The LORD continues to show kindness to the living and to the dead." Naomi also said to her, "This man is our close relative. He is one of the relatives who must help our family."

**21** Then Ruth the Moabite said, "Boaz also said to me, 'Stay near my young workers until they complete all my harvest.'"

**22** Naomi said to Ruth, her daughter-in-law, "My daughter, it is good for you to go out with his female workers. In a different field, people could hurt you."

**23** So Ruth stayed near the female workers of Boaz. She picked up grain until the end of the barley harvest and the wheat harvest. She lived with her mother-in-law.

## Chapter 3

**1** One day Naomi, her mother-in-law, said to Ruth, "My daughter, I must find a home for you, where you will be safe and happy.

**2** Boaz, with whose female workers you were, is our relative. Tonight he will separate the barley from the chaff on the threshing floor.

**3** So wash yourself and put perfume on. Put on your best clothes and go down to the threshing floor. But do not let Boaz know that you are there until he finishes his meal.

**4** When he lies down, look at the place where he lies. Then go in, uncover his feet, and lie down there. He will tell you what to do."

**5** Ruth said to her, "I will do all that you tell me."

**6** So Ruth went down to the threshing floor. She did all that her mother-in-law told her to do.

**7** Boaz ate and drank, and he was happy. He went to lie down at the end of the pile of grain. Then Ruth came quietly. She uncovered his feet and lay down.

**8** In the middle of the night, something made Boaz afraid. He turned over and saw a woman who lay at his feet.

**9** He said, "Who are you?" She answered, "I am Ruth, your servant. Put the edge of your garment over me, your servant, because you are a close relative who must help my family."

**10** Boaz said, "May the LORD bless you, my daughter. Now you show more kindness than before. You did not go after young men, poor or rich.

**11** Now, my daughter, do not be afraid. I will do for you all that you ask. All the people of my town know that you are a good woman.

**12** It is true that I am a close relative who must help your family. But there is a relative who is closer than I.

**13** Stay here tonight. In the morning, if he will help you as a close relative, good. Let him do it. But if he does not want to do it, then I will do it. I promise this as surely as the LORD lives. Lie down here until the morning."

**14** So Ruth lay at his feet until the morning. She got up before people could see each other. Boaz said, "No one must know that a woman came to the threshing floor."

**15** Boaz also said, "Bring the shawl that you wear and hold it out." She held it out. He put 6 measures of barley in it and put it on her shoulder. Then Ruth went into the city.

**16** Ruth came to her mother-in-law. Naomi said, "How did it go, my daughter?" Ruth told her all that Boaz did for her.

**17** She said, "He gave me these 6 measures of barley. He said to me, 'Do not go back to your mother-in-law with nothing.'"

**18** Then Naomi said, "Wait here, my daughter, until you know what will occur. The man will not rest until he settles this matter today."

## Chapter 4

**1** Boaz went up to the town gate and sat down there. Then the close relative that Boaz spoke about came past. Boaz said to him, "Come here, my friend, and sit down." So the man came and sat down.

**2** Boaz took 10 men from the elders of the town. He said, "Sit down here." So they sat down.

**3** Then Boaz said to the close relative, "Naomi came back from the country of Moab. She sells the piece of land that was the property of our relative Elimelech.

**4** I decided to tell you about it. Buy it in front of the people who sit here and in front of the elders of my people. If you want to buy it back for the family, do it. If you do not want to buy it, tell me. Then I will know. You have the first right to buy it, and I am next after you." The man said, "I will buy it."

**5** Then Boaz said, "When you buy the field from Naomi, you also get Ruth the Moabite, the widow of the dead man. You must marry her. Then the name of the dead man will stay with his property."

**6** The close relative said, "Then I cannot buy it back for myself. I could cause damage to my own property. You buy it. Take my right, because I cannot buy it back."

**7** In past times in Israel there was a custom for the purchase and exchange of property. One man removed his sandal and gave it to the other man. This custom showed that the agreement was legal in Israel.

**8** So the close relative said to Boaz, "Buy it for yourself." Then he removed his sandal.

**9** Boaz said to the elders and to all the people, "Today you see that I buy from Naomi all the property of Elimelech, Chilion, and Mahlon.

**10** I also get Ruth the Moabite, the widow of Mahlon, as my wife. I do this to keep the name of the dead man with his property. Then his name will not disappear from his family and from his town. Today you see that this is true."

**11** All the people at the gate and the elders said, "We see that this is true. May the LORD make the woman who comes into your house like Rachel and Leah. These 2 women gave birth to the family of Israel. May you become rich in Ephratah and famous in Beth-lehem.

**12** May the LORD give you children from this young woman. May your family become like the family of Pharez, the son of Tamar and Judah."

**13** So Boaz took Ruth, and she became his wife. He had sexual relations with her. The LORD let her become pregnant, and she gave birth to a son.

**14** The women said to Naomi, "Praise the LORD! Today he gave you a close relative to help your family. May the name of this boy become famous in Israel!

**15** He will give you new life. He will take care of you when you are old. Your daughter-in-law, who loves you, gave birth to him. She is better to you than 7 sons."

**16** Naomi took the child and held him in her arms. She took care of him.

**17** The women who lived near Naomi gave the child a name. They said, "Naomi has a son!" They called him Obed. Obed became the father of Jesse, and Jesse became the father of David.

**18** These are the descendants of Pharez. Pharez became the father of Hezron.

**19** Hezron became the father of Ram. Ram became the father of Amminadab.

**20** Amminadab became the father of Nahshon. Nahshon became the father of Salmon.

**21** Salmon became the father of Boaz. Boaz became the father of Obed.

**22** Obed became the father of Jesse. Jesse became the father of David.
