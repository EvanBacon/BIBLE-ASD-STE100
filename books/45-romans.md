# Romans

## Chapter 1

**1** I am Paul, a servant of Jesus Christ. God called me to be an apostle. God chose me to tell the gospel of God.

**2** God promised this gospel at an earlier time. He gave the promise through his prophets in the holy scriptures.

**3** The gospel is about his Son, Jesus Christ our Lord. As a human, he came from the family of David.

**4** By the Spirit of holiness, God showed with power that Jesus is the Son of God. God did this when Jesus came back to life from the dead.

**5** Through Jesus, we got grace and the work of an apostle. Our work is to cause people from all nations to obey through faith. We do this for the name of Jesus.

**6** You are also among these nations. Jesus Christ called you to be his people.

**7** I write to all the people in Rome whom God loves and calls to be holy people. I ask God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ to give you grace and peace.

**8** First, I thank my God through Jesus Christ for all of you. People in all the world speak about your faith.

**9** I serve God with all my spirit when I tell the gospel of his Son. God knows that I always speak about you in my prayers.

**10** In my prayers, I ask God for a way to come to you at last. I ask that he will permit this.

**11** I want very much to see you. I want to give you a spiritual gift. This gift will make you strong.

**12** Thus, you and I can help each other. Your faith will help me, and my faith will help you.

**13** Brothers, I want you to know this. Many times I made a plan to come to you, but something always stopped me. I want to see good results among you. I saw such results among the other Gentiles.

**14** I have a duty to Greeks and to foreign people. I have a duty to wise people and to foolish people.

**15** Thus, I am ready to tell the gospel to you in Rome also.

**16** I do not feel shame about the gospel. The gospel is the power of God that saves each person who believes. It is first for the Jew, and also for the Greek.

**17** The gospel shows how God makes a person righteous. This starts with faith and ends with faith. The scripture says, "The righteous person will live by faith."

**18** God shows his anger from heaven against all the sin and evil of people. These people use their evil to hide the truth.

**19** People can know some things about God. These things are clear to them, because God showed these things to them.

**20** People cannot see the qualities of God. But since God made the world, people can see these qualities clearly in the things that he made. They see his eternal power and that he is God. Thus, people have no excuse.

**21** They knew God, but they did not honor him as God. They did not thank him. Their thoughts became useless, and their foolish minds became dark.

**22** They said that they were wise, but they became fools.

**23** God lives forever and is glorious. But they changed the glory of God for statues. These statues looked like humans who die, and like birds, animals and snakes.

**24** Thus, God let them follow the bad desires of their hearts. They did impure things, and they brought shame to their bodies with each other.

**25** They changed the truth of God for a lie. They worshipped and served the things that God made, not God himself. God is the Creator, and people must praise him forever. Amen.

**26** Thus, God let them follow their shameful desires. Their women changed the natural use of their bodies for a use that is not natural.

**27** Also, the men stopped the natural relations with women. They had a strong desire for each other. Men did shameful things with other men. They got the correct punishment in their bodies for their wrong actions.

**28** They did not think it important to know God. Thus, God let them have a bad mind. They did things that people must not do.

**29** They are full of all kinds of sin, sexual sin, evil, greed and hate. They are full of jealousy, murder, fights, lies and bad thoughts. They tell secrets about other people.

**30** They say bad things about other people. They hate God. They are rude, proud and boastful. They find new ways to do evil. They do not obey their parents.

**31** They have no sense. They do not keep their promises. They have no love and no mercy.

**32** They know the law of God. The law says that people who do these things must die. But they do these things, and they also approve of other people who do them.

## Chapter 2

**1** Thus, you have no excuse when you judge other people. When you judge another person, you also judge yourself. You do the same things that you judge.

**2** We know that God judges correctly the people who do such things.

**3** You judge the people who do such things, but you do the same things. Do you think that you will escape the judgment of God?

**4** God is very kind and patient with you. Do you think that his kindness is not important? Do you not know that God is kind to you because he wants you to repent?

**5** But your heart is hard, and you do not repent. Thus, you cause more anger of God against yourself. God will show his anger on the day when he judges correctly.

**6** God will give to each person the result of what that person did.

**7** Some people continue to do good things and look for glory, honor and life that does not end. God will give them eternal life.

**8** Other people are selfish. They do not obey the truth, but they obey evil. God will show his anger to them.

**9** Each person who does evil will have trouble and pain. This is first for the Jew, and also for the Gentile.

**10** But each person who does good will get glory, honor and peace. This is first for the Jew, and also for the Gentile.

**11** God treats all people equally.

**12** Some people sin, but they do not have the law of Moses. They will die without the law. Other people sin, and they have the law. The law will judge them.

**13** God does not make people righteous because they hear the law. God makes people righteous when they obey the law.

**14** The Gentiles do not have the law. But sometimes they do naturally what the law says. Thus, they show that they know the law, although they do not have it.

**15** They show that the law is in their hearts. Their conscience also agrees. Their thoughts sometimes say that they are guilty and sometimes say that they are not guilty.

**16** This will occur on the day when God judges the secret things of people through Jesus Christ. My gospel tells this.

**17** You say that you are a Jew. You trust in the law, and you are proud of your relation with God.

**18** You know what God wants. The law teaches you, and you know what is best.

**19** You are sure that you can lead blind people. You think that you are a light for people in the dark.

**20** You think that you can teach foolish people and children. You think that the law gives you all knowledge and truth.

**21** You teach other people, but do you not teach yourself? You say that people must not steal, but do you steal?

**22** You say that people must not commit adultery, but do you commit adultery? You hate idols, but do you steal from temples?

**23** You are proud of the law, but you disobey the law. Thus, you bring shame to God.

**24** The scripture says, "Because of you, the Gentiles say bad things about the name of God."

**25** Circumcision is of value if you obey the law. But if you disobey the law, your circumcision has no value.

**26** A man who did not receive circumcision can obey the law. Then God will think of him as a man who received circumcision.

**27** Some men did not receive circumcision, but they obey the law. These men will judge you. You have the written law and circumcision, but you disobey the law.

**28** A person is not a true Jew only because of the outside of his body. True circumcision is not only on the body.

**29** A true Jew is a Jew on the inside. True circumcision is a change in the heart. The Spirit makes this change, not the written law. Such a person gets praise from God, not from people.

## Chapter 3

**1** Then what is the advantage of a Jew? What is the value of circumcision?

**2** The advantage is large in all ways. First, God gave his words to the Jews.

**3** Some Jews did not believe. Can their unbelief stop the faithfulness of God?

**4** No! God is always true, although each human is a liar. The scripture says to God, "Your words show that you are right. When people judge you, you will win."

**5** Our evil shows clearly that God is righteous. Then what can we say? Is God wrong when he punishes us? I speak as humans speak.

**6** No! If God is wrong, then he cannot judge the world.

**7** My lie can show more clearly that God is true. Then God gets more glory. Why then does God judge me as a sinner?

**8** Some people tell lies about us. They say that we teach, "Let us do evil, so that good things can come." God will judge these people correctly.

**9** What then? Are we Jews better than other people? No! We showed that Jews and Gentiles are all under the power of sin.

**10** The scripture says, "No person is righteous, not even one.

**11** No person understands. No person looks for God.

**12** All people went away from God. All people became useless. No person does good, not even one.

**13** Their mouths are like an open grave. They use their tongues to tell lies. Their lips have poison like the poison of snakes.

**14** Their mouths are full of curses and bitter words.

**15** They go quickly to kill people.

**16** They cause damage and pain wherever they go.

**17** They do not know the way of peace.

**18** They do not respect God."

**19** We know that the law speaks to the people who are under the law. Thus, no person can make an excuse. All the world is guilty before God.

**20** No person becomes righteous before God because he obeys the law. The law only shows us what sin is.

**21** But now God shows how he makes people righteous without the law. The law and the prophets tell about this.

**22** God makes people righteous through faith in Jesus Christ. He does this for all people who believe. There is no difference between people.

**23** All people sinned, and all people fail to have the glory of God.

**24** But God makes them righteous as a free gift through his grace. He does this because Christ Jesus paid to make them free.

**25** God gave Christ as a sacrifice that takes away sin. People get this through faith in his blood. Thus, God showed that he is righteous. In the past, God was patient and did not punish sins.

**26** Now God shows that he is righteous. He is just, and he makes righteous the person who has faith in Jesus.

**27** Then can we be proud of ourselves? No! What law stops us? Is it the law of works? No! The law of faith stops us.

**28** Thus, we say that God makes a person righteous through faith. The person does not need to obey the law for this.

**29** Is God only the God of the Jews? Is he not also the God of the Gentiles? Yes, he is also the God of the Gentiles.

**30** There is only one God. He makes righteous the circumcised people through faith. He also makes righteous the uncircumcised people through faith.

**31** Do we then stop the law because of faith? No! We make the law strong.

## Chapter 4

**1** Then what can we say about Abraham, our human ancestor? What did he find?

**2** If Abraham became righteous because of his works, then he can be proud. But he cannot be proud before God.

**3** The scripture says, "Abraham believed God. Thus, God thought of him as righteous."

**4** When a person works, his pay is not a gift. His employer must pay him.

**5** But think about a person who does not work for it. He only believes God, who makes evil people righteous. God thinks of the faith of that person as righteousness.

**6** David also speaks about the happiness of a person whom God thinks of as righteous without works.

**7** David says, "The people whose wrong actions God forgives are happy. The people whose sins God covers are happy.

**8** The man whose sin the Lord does not count is happy."

**9** Is this happiness only for the circumcised people? Or is it also for the uncircumcised people? We say that God thought of the faith of Abraham as righteousness.

**10** When did God do this? Was it after Abraham received circumcision, or before? It was not after, but before.

**11** Abraham got circumcision as a sign. It showed that God thought of him as righteous through faith before his circumcision. Thus, Abraham is the father of all people who believe but did not receive circumcision. God also thinks of them as righteous.

**12** Abraham is also the father of circumcised people. But these people must also follow the faith of Abraham. Abraham had this faith before his circumcision.

**13** God promised Abraham and his descendants, "The world will be yours." God did not give this promise through the law. He gave it through the righteousness that comes from faith.

**14** If the people who obey the law get the promise, then faith is useless. Then the promise has no value.

**15** The law causes the anger of God. But where there is no law, there is no disobedience.

**16** Thus, the promise comes through faith, and it is a gift of grace. Thus, the promise is sure for all the descendants of Abraham. It is not only for the people who have the law. It is also for the people who have the faith of Abraham. Abraham is the father of all of us.

**17** The scripture says, "I made you the father of many nations." Abraham is our father before God, whom he believed. God gives life to the dead. God speaks about things that do not exist as if they exist.

**18** Abraham had no reason to hope, but he continued to hope and believe. Thus, he became the father of many nations. God told him, "Your descendants will be very many."

**19** Abraham was almost 100 years old. He knew that his body was almost dead. He knew that Sarah could not have children. But his faith did not become weak.

**20** Abraham did not doubt the promise of God. His faith became strong, and he gave glory to God.

**21** He was sure that God can do what he promised.

**22** Thus, God thought of Abraham as righteous.

**23** The words "God thought of him as righteous" are not only for Abraham.

**24** These words are also for us. God will think of us as righteous if we believe in God. God raised Jesus our Lord from the dead.

**25** God gave Jesus to die because of our sins. God raised Jesus to life to make us righteous.

## Chapter 5

**1** God made us righteous through faith. Thus, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ.

**2** Through Christ, we can come by faith into this grace. We stand in this grace now. We are happy because we hope for the glory of God.

**3** Also, we are happy when we have troubles. We know that troubles teach us patience.

**4** Patience makes our character strong, and a strong character gives us hope.

**5** This hope will not fail us. God gave us the Holy Spirit, and the Holy Spirit filled our hearts with the love of God.

**6** We were weak and could not help ourselves. At the correct time, Christ died for evil people.

**7** It is rare for a person to die for a righteous person. Possibly a person will dare to die for a good person.

**8** But God shows his love for us in this way. Christ died for us while we were still sinners.

**9** Now the blood of Christ made us righteous. Thus, it is much more sure that Christ will save us from the anger of God.

**10** We were enemies of God. But the death of his Son made us friends with God. Now we are his friends. Thus, it is much more sure that the life of his Son will save us.

**11** Also, we are happy in God through our Lord Jesus Christ. Through Christ, we are now friends with God.

**12** Sin came into the world through one man, Adam. Death came through sin. Thus, death came to all people, because all people sinned.

**13** Before God gave the law, sin was in the world. But when there is no law, God does not count sin.

**14** But death had power over all people from the time of Adam to the time of Moses. Death had power also over people who did not disobey a command like Adam. Adam was a picture of the one who was to come.

**15** But the gift of God is not like the sin of Adam. Many people died because of the sin of one man. But the grace of God is much greater. The gift of grace came through one man, Jesus Christ. This gift came to many people.

**16** The gift is not like the result of the sin of one man. After one sin, the judgment caused punishment. But after many sins, the free gift made people righteous.

**17** Death had power because of the sin of one man. But Jesus Christ is much greater. Some people get the large gift of grace and of righteousness. They will rule in life through the one man, Jesus Christ.

**18** One sin caused punishment for all people. In the same way, one righteous act makes all people righteous and gives them life.

**19** One man disobeyed God, and many people became sinners. In the same way, one man obeyed God, and many people will become righteous.

**20** The law came so that sin became larger. But where sin became larger, grace became much larger.

**21** Sin had power, and it caused death. In the same way, grace has power through righteousness. Grace gives eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.

## Chapter 6

**1** Then what can we say? Must we continue to sin, so that grace becomes larger?

**2** No! We died to sin. Then how can we continue to live in sin?

**3** You know that all of us who received baptism into Christ Jesus received baptism into his death.

**4** When we received baptism, we shared in the death and burial of Christ. The glory of the Father raised Christ from the dead. In the same way, we also must live a new life.

**5** We share in the death of Christ. Thus, we will also share in his resurrection.

**6** We know that our old self died on the cross with Christ. Thus, the power of sin in our body is gone. We are no longer slaves to sin.

**7** A person who died is free from sin.

**8** We died with Christ. Thus, we believe that we will also live with him.

**9** We know that God raised Christ from the dead. Christ will not die again. Death has no power over him now.

**10** Christ died one time to stop the power of sin. Now he lives for God.

**11** In the same way, you must think of yourselves as dead to sin. But you are alive for God through Christ Jesus our Lord.

**12** Do not let sin rule in your body, which will die. Do not obey the bad desires of your body.

**13** Do not give the parts of your body to sin as tools for evil. Give yourselves to God, as people who came back to life from death. Give the parts of your body to God as tools for righteousness.

**14** Sin will not rule you. You are not under the law, but under grace.

**15** What then? Must we sin because we are not under the law, but under grace? No!

**16** You know this. When you give yourselves to obey a person, you become the slaves of that person. You can be slaves of sin, which causes death. Or you can be slaves of obedience, which causes righteousness.

**17** In the past, you were slaves of sin. But now you obey with all your heart the true message that people gave you. I thank God for this.

**18** You are free from sin. You became slaves of righteousness.

**19** I use an example from human life, because your human nature is weak. In the past, you gave the parts of your body as slaves to impure things and to evil. Now give them as slaves to righteousness. This will make you holy.

**20** When you were slaves of sin, righteousness did not control you.

**21** What result did you get from those things then? Now you feel shame about those things. The result of those things is death.

**22** But now you are free from sin, and you are slaves of God. The result is that you become holy. At the end, you will get eternal life.

**23** The payment for sin is death. But the gift of God is eternal life through Christ Jesus our Lord.

## Chapter 7

**1** Brothers, I speak to people who know the law. You know that the law has power over a person only while that person lives.

**2** For example, the law joins a married woman to her husband while he lives. But if her husband dies, the law of marriage does not hold her.

**3** If she marries another man while her husband lives, people will call her an adulteress. But if her husband dies, that law does not hold her. Then she is not an adulteress if she marries another man.

**4** My brothers, in the same way, you died to the law through the body of Christ. Now you can belong to another person. You belong to Christ, whom God raised from the dead. Thus, we can make good results for God.

**5** When our human nature controlled us, the law caused sinful desires to start in our bodies. These desires caused results that end in death.

**6** But now we are free from the law. We died to the law that held us. Thus, we serve God in the new way of the Spirit. We do not serve in the old way of the written law.

**7** What can we say then? Is the law sin? No! But without the law, I did not know what sin is. The law said, "Do not want things that belong to other people." Without this law, I did not know that such a desire is wrong.

**8** But sin used this commandment as an opportunity. Sin caused all types of wrong desires in me. Without the law, sin had no power.

**9** At one time I lived without the law. But when the commandment came, sin became alive, and I died.

**10** The commandment had the purpose to give life. But I found that the commandment caused my death.

**11** Sin used the commandment as an opportunity. Sin deceived me, and through the commandment sin killed me.

**12** Thus, the law is holy. The commandment is holy, fair and good.

**13** Then did the good thing cause my death? No! Sin caused my death. Sin used the good thing to cause my death. Thus, people can see that sin is truly sin. Through the commandment, sin shows how very bad it is.

**14** We know that the law is spiritual. But I am human, and sin controls me like a slave.

**15** I do not understand the things that I do. I do not do the things that I want to do. I do the things that I hate.

**16** I do the things that I do not want to do. Thus, I agree that the law is good.

**17** Thus, I am not the person who does these things. The sin that lives in me does them.

**18** I know that nothing good lives in me, that is, in my human nature. I want to do good, but I cannot do it.

**19** I do not do the good things that I want to do. I do the evil things that I do not want to do.

**20** I do the things that I do not want to do. Thus, I am not the person who does them. The sin that lives in me does them.

**21** Thus, I find this rule in me. When I want to do good, evil is there with me.

**22** In my inner self, I am happy with the law of God.

**23** But I see a different law in the parts of my body. This law fights against the law of my mind. It makes me a prisoner of the law of sin in my body.

**24** I am a very unhappy man! Who will make me free from this body, which causes death?

**25** I thank God! He makes me free through Jesus Christ our Lord. Thus, with my mind, I serve the law of God. But with my human nature, I serve the law of sin.

## Chapter 8

**1** Thus, God does not now find guilty the people who are in Christ Jesus. These people do not live by their human nature, but by the Spirit.

**2** The law of the Spirit gives life in Christ Jesus. This law made me free from the law of sin and death.

**3** The law could not save us, because our human nature made the law weak. But God did what the law could not do. He sent his own Son with a human body like the body of a sinful person. God sent him as an offering for sin. Thus, God gave the punishment for sin in the human body of Jesus.

**4** God did this so that we can obey what the law tells us to do. We do not live by our human nature, but by the Spirit.

**5** Some people live by their human nature. They think about the things that their human nature wants. Other people live by the Spirit. They think about the things that the Spirit wants.

**6** If a person lets human nature control the mind, the result is death. If a person lets the Spirit control the mind, the result is life and peace.

**7** The mind that human nature controls is an enemy of God. It does not obey the law of God. It cannot obey the law of God.

**8** Thus, people who live by their human nature cannot please God.

**9** But your human nature does not control you. The Spirit controls you, if the Spirit of God lives in you. If a person does not have the Spirit of Christ, that person does not belong to Christ.

**10** If Christ is in you, your body will die because of sin. But the Spirit gives you life because God made you righteous.

**11** God raised Jesus from the dead. If the Spirit of God lives in you, then God will also give life to your bodies, which will die. He will do this through his Spirit, who lives in you.

**12** Thus, brothers, we have a duty. But this duty is not to our human nature. We must not live by our human nature.

**13** If you live by your human nature, you will die. But through the Spirit, you can stop the evil things that your body does. If you do this, you will live.

**14** All people whom the Spirit of God leads are the sons of God.

**15** God did not give you a spirit that makes you slaves again. That spirit causes fear. God gave you the Spirit who makes you his children. Through the Spirit, we call out to God, "Abba, Father!"

**16** The Spirit himself speaks together with our spirit. He tells us that we are the children of God.

**17** If we are his children, then we will get what God promised to his children. We will get these things from God together with Christ. But we must suffer with Christ. Then we will also have glory with him.

**18** I think that our pain at this time is small. It is small when we compare it with the glory that God will show in us.

**19** All the things that God made wait with a strong desire. They wait for God to show who his sons are.

**20** God made the things that he made weak and useless. They did not choose this. God did this, but he gave hope with it.

**21** The hope is that God will make all things free from decay. All things will be free like the children of God. They will have the glory of the children of God.

**22** We know that all the things that God made feel pain until now. They feel pain like a woman who gives birth to a child.

**23** We also feel this pain. We have the first part of the gifts of the Spirit. But we feel pain in ourselves. We wait for God to make us his children completely. We wait for God to make our bodies free.

**24** God saved us, and we have this hope. But if we see the thing that we hope for, then it is not hope. A person does not hope for a thing that he sees now.

**25** But we hope for a thing that we do not see. Thus, we wait for it with patience.

**26** In the same way, the Spirit helps us when we are weak. We do not know how to pray correctly. But the Spirit himself prays to God for us. He prays with deep sounds that words cannot tell.

**27** God knows what is in the hearts of all people. He knows what the Spirit thinks. The Spirit prays for the holy people in the way that God wants.

**28** We know that God causes all things to work together for good. He does this for the people who love him. He called these people for his purpose.

**29** God knew these people before. He chose them before to become like his Son. Thus, his Son is the first among many brothers.

**30** God chose these people before. Then he called them. He made righteous the people whom he called. He gave glory to the people whom he made righteous.

**31** What can we say about these things? If God is on our side, then nobody can win against us.

**32** God did not keep his own Son from death. He gave him to die for all of us. Thus, God will also give us all things freely with his Son.

**33** Nobody can accuse the people whom God chose. God is the one who makes them righteous.

**34** Nobody can say that they are guilty. Christ Jesus died for us. More than that, God raised him to life. He is at the right side of God. He also prays to God for us.

**35** Nothing can take us away from the love of Christ. Trouble, pain, attacks, hunger, no clothes, danger or the sword cannot do it.

**36** The scripture says, "For you, people kill us all day. They think that we are sheep for the butcher."

**37** But in all these things, we win completely. We win through Christ, who loved us.

**38** I am sure of this. Death, life, angels, rulers, things now, things in the future and powers cannot do it.

**39** Things above, things below, and all other things that God made cannot do it. Nothing can take us away from the love of God. This love is in Christ Jesus our Lord.

## Chapter 9

**1** I am in Christ, and I tell the truth. I do not lie. My conscience agrees with me through the Holy Spirit.

**2** I have much sadness. The pain in my heart does not stop.

**3** I almost want God to curse me and take me away from Christ. I want this for my brothers, my own people by birth.

**4** They are the people of Israel. God made them his children. God gave them his glory and his covenants. God gave them the law, the worship of God and the promises.

**5** The great fathers of our nation belong to them. As a human, Christ came from their family. Christ is God over all things. People must praise him forever. Amen.

**6** I do not say that the word of God failed. Not all the people who come from Israel are the true people of Israel.

**7** Not all the people who come from Abraham are his true children. God said to Abraham, "Your family will come through Isaac."

**8** Thus, the children by human birth are not the children of God. The children of the promise are the true family of Abraham.

**9** God gave this promise: "At this time next year I will come back. Then Sarah will have a son."

**10** There is also another example. Rebecca had 2 sons from 1 man, our father Isaac.

**11** The sons were not yet born. They did not yet do good or bad things. But God showed that his purpose comes from his choice. His choice does not come from the things that people do. It comes from God, who calls people.

**12** God said to Rebecca, "The older son will serve the younger son."

**13** The scripture says, "I loved Jacob, but I hated Esau."

**14** What can we say then? Is God not fair? No!

**15** God said to Moses, "I will show mercy to the person whom I choose. I will show kindness to the person whom I choose."

**16** Thus, the choice of God does not come from what people want or do. It comes from God, who shows mercy.

**17** The scripture says to Pharaoh, "I made you king for this purpose. I will show my power through you. Then people in all the earth will know my name."

**18** Thus, God shows mercy to the person whom he chooses. And he makes stubborn the person whom he chooses.

**19** You will say to me, "Then why does God still say that people are wrong? Nobody can stop what God wants."

**20** But you are only a man. You cannot argue with God. A clay pot cannot say to the person who made it, "Why did you make me like this?"

**21** The potter has the right to use the clay as he wants. From the same clay, he can make one pot for special use. He can make another pot for ordinary use.

**22** God wanted to show his anger and his power. But he was very patient with the people who received his anger. These people were ready for destruction.

**23** God did this to show his great glory to the people who receive his mercy. He made these people ready for glory before.

**24** We are these people. God called us from the Jews and also from the Gentiles.

**25** God says in the book of Hosea, "I will call them 'my people,' but they were not my people. I will call her 'loved,' but I did not love her."

**26** "In the place where I said to them, 'You are not my people,' there people will call them 'the children of the God who lives.'"

**27** Isaiah calls out about Israel, "The people of Israel can be as many as the sand of the sea. But God will save only a small group of them.

**28** The Lord will complete his judgment on the earth. He will do it quickly and fairly."

**29** Isaiah also said before, "The Lord of armies left some of our people alive. Because of this, we did not become like Sodom. We did not become like Gomorrah."

**30** What can we say then? The Gentiles did not try to become righteous. But they became righteous through faith.

**31** But the people of Israel tried to become righteous through the law. They did not get the result.

**32** Why not? Because they did not try through faith. They tried through the things that they did. They fell over the stone that causes people to fall.

**33** The scripture says, "Look, I put a stone in Zion that causes people to fall. It is a rock that causes them to fall down. But the person who believes in him will not feel shame."

## Chapter 10

**1** Brothers, I want with all my heart that God will save the people of Israel. I pray to God for them.

**2** I tell you the truth about them. They want very much to serve God. But they do not have the correct knowledge.

**3** They do not know how God makes people righteous. They try to make themselves righteous in their own way. Thus, they did not accept the way of God to make people righteous.

**4** Christ is the end of the law. Through Christ, God makes righteous all the people who believe.

**5** Moses writes about the righteousness that comes from the law. He says, "The person who obeys these laws will live by them."

**6** But the righteousness that comes from faith says this. "Do not say in your heart, 'Who will go up into heaven?'" That is, who will go up to bring Christ down?

**7** "Or do not say, 'Who will go down into the deep place?'" That is, who will go down to bring Christ up from the dead?

**8** But what does it say? "The word is near you. It is in your mouth and in your heart." This is the word of faith that we teach.

**9** Say with your mouth, "Jesus is Lord." Believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead. If you do these things, God will save you.

**10** A person believes with the heart, and God makes that person righteous. A person says with the mouth what he believes, and God saves that person.

**11** The scripture says, "Each person who believes in him will not feel shame."

**12** There is no difference between the Jew and the Greek. The same Lord is Lord of all people. He gives much to all people who call to him.

**13** The scripture says, "God will save each person who calls to the name of the Lord."

**14** But how can people call to the Lord if they do not believe in him? How can they believe in him if they did not hear about him? How can they hear if nobody tells them?

**15** How can people tell them if nobody sends them? The scripture says, "The people who bring good news are very beautiful!"

**16** But not all the people obeyed the gospel. Isaiah says, "Lord, who believed our message?"

**17** Thus, faith comes when people hear the message. People hear the message when someone tells the word of Christ.

**18** But I ask, "Did they not hear?" Yes, they heard. The scripture says, "Their voice went out into all the earth. Their words went to the ends of the world."

**19** I ask again, "Did the people of Israel not understand?" First, Moses says, "I will make you jealous of people who are not a nation. I will make you angry through a foolish nation."

**20** Then Isaiah speaks boldly. He says, "The people who did not look for me found me. I showed myself to the people who did not ask for me."

**21** But about Israel, God says, "All day I held out my hands to a people who do not obey me. They speak against me."

## Chapter 11

**1** Thus, I ask, "Did God reject his people?" No! I am also a man of Israel. I come from the family of Abraham and from the tribe of Benjamin.

**2** God knew his people before, and he did not reject them. You know what the scripture says about Elijah. Elijah spoke to God against Israel. He said,

**3** "Lord, they killed your prophets. They destroyed your altars. I am the only prophet who is alive, and they try to kill me."

**4** What did God say to him? God said, "I kept 7000 men for myself. These men did not bow down to Baal."

**5** In the same way, at this time also, there is a small group of people. God chose them because of his grace.

**6** If God chose them by grace, then he did not choose them because of the things that they did. If he did, then grace is not grace.

**7** What does this mean? The people of Israel did not get the thing that they looked for. But the people whom God chose got it. God made the other people stubborn.

**8** The scripture says, "God made their minds slow. He gave them eyes that do not see and ears that do not hear." This is true until now.

**9** David says, "Let their feasts become a trap for them. Let their feasts cause them to fall. Let their feasts be their punishment.

**10** Let their eyes become dark, so that they cannot see. Let their backs always bend down under a heavy load."

**11** Thus, I ask, "Did the people of Israel fall so that they will not get up again?" No! But because they did wrong, God saved the Gentiles. Thus, Israel will become jealous.

**12** The wrong things that Israel did caused much good for the world. Their loss caused much good for the Gentiles. Thus, when all of Israel comes back to God, the good result will be much greater.

**13** Now I speak to you Gentiles. I am an apostle to the Gentiles, and I honor this work.

**14** I hope to make my own people jealous. Then I can help to save some of them.

**15** When God rejected them, God made the world his friend. When God accepts them again, the result will be life from the dead.

**16** If the first part of the bread dough is holy, then all the dough is holy. If the root of a tree is holy, then the branches are holy.

**17** Some of the branches broke off the olive tree. You Gentiles are like branches of a wild olive tree. God put you into the good olive tree in the place of those branches. Now you get life and food from the root of the good olive tree.

**18** Do not think that you are better than the branches that broke off. If you are proud, remember this: you do not hold up the root. The root holds you up.

**19** You will say, "God broke off branches so that he can put me into the tree."

**20** That is true. God broke off those branches because they did not believe. You stay in the tree only because you believe. Do not be proud, but be afraid.

**21** God did not keep the natural branches. Thus, he will possibly not keep you.

**22** Thus, see that God is kind and that he is strict. He is strict with the people who fell. But he is kind to you if you continue in his kindness. If you do not continue, God will also cut you off.

**23** If the people of Israel stop their unbelief, God will put them back into the tree. God has the power to put them in again.

**24** By nature, you came from a wild olive tree. God cut you from that tree. Against nature, God put you into a good olive tree. Thus, God can much more easily put the natural branches back into their own olive tree.

**25** Brothers, I want you to know this secret. Then you will not think that you are wise. Part of Israel is stubborn. This will continue until the full number of Gentiles comes to God.

**26** Thus, God will save all Israel. The scripture says, "The person who saves will come from Zion. He will take away all evil from the people of Jacob.

**27** This will be my covenant with them, when I take away their sins."

**28** The people of Israel are enemies of the gospel, and this helps you. But God chose them, and he loves them because of their fathers.

**29** God does not take back his gifts or his call.

**30** In the past, you did not obey God. But now God shows mercy to you, because the people of Israel did not obey.

**31** In the same way, the people of Israel do not obey God now. God showed mercy to you. Thus, God will also show mercy to them.

**32** God let all people be prisoners of their disobedience. Thus, he can show mercy to all people.

**33** The riches, the wisdom and the knowledge of God are very great! Nobody can understand his decisions. Nobody can know his ways.

**34** The scripture says, "Nobody knows the mind of the Lord. Nobody can give him advice."

**35** "Nobody gave a thing to God first, so that God must pay that person back."

**36** All things come from God. All things exist through him and for him. Glory to God forever! Amen.

## Chapter 12

**1** Thus, brothers, God shows you much mercy. Because of this, I ask you strongly to give your bodies to God as a live sacrifice. Your bodies must be holy and must please God. This is the correct way to worship him.

**2** Do not copy the ways of this world. Let God change you. Let him make your mind new. Then you will know what God wants. You will know what is good, what pleases him, and what is perfect.

**3** God gave grace to me. Thus, I tell each person among you this. Do not think that you are more important than you are. Think about yourself correctly. Use the quantity of faith that God gave to you.

**4** A body has many parts. All the parts do not have the same function.

**5** In the same way, we are many people, but we are one body in Christ. Each person is a part of the other people.

**6** God gave us different gifts through his grace. If your gift is to speak messages from God, then speak them by your faith.

**7** If your gift is to serve, then serve. If your gift is to teach, then teach.

**8** If your gift is to encourage people, then encourage them. If you give to other people, give freely. If you lead, lead with care. If you show mercy, show mercy happily.

**9** Your love must be true. Hate what is evil. Hold strongly to what is good.

**10** Love each other as brothers love each other. Honor other people more than you honor yourself.

**11** Do not be lazy. Work hard and serve the Lord with a strong spirit.

**12** Be happy because of your hope. Be patient when you have trouble. Always continue to pray.

**13** Give to the holy people who need help. Always welcome visitors into your home.

**14** Ask God to bless the people who attack you. Bless them, and do not curse them.

**15** Be happy with people who are happy. Cry with people who cry.

**16** Agree with each other. Do not be proud. Be friends with people who are not important. Do not think that you are wise.

**17** If a person does evil to you, do not do evil to him. Try to do what all people think is good.

**18** If possible, live in peace with all people. Do all that you can to do this.

**19** Dear friends, do not try to get revenge. Let God punish people in his anger. The scripture says, "The Lord says, 'I will punish. I will pay back.'"

**20** Thus, if your enemy is hungry, give him food. If he is thirsty, give him a drink. If you do this, he will feel ashamed, as if you put hot coals on his head.

**21** Do not let evil defeat you. Defeat evil with good.

## Chapter 13

**1** Each person must obey the rulers of the government. All authority comes from God. God put the rulers in their positions.

**2** Thus, a person who opposes the rulers opposes what God put there. The people who do this will receive punishment.

**3** Rulers do not cause fear to people who do good. They cause fear to people who do evil. Do you want to have no fear of the rulers? Then do what is good. The rulers will praise you.

**4** The ruler is a servant of God, and he helps you. But if you do evil, be afraid. The ruler has the power to punish. He is a servant of God and he punishes the people who do evil.

**5** Thus, you must obey the rulers. Obey them because of the punishment, and also because of your conscience.

**6** This is also the reason that you pay taxes. The rulers are servants of God, and they always do this work.

**7** Give to each person what you must give. Pay taxes to the people who get taxes. Pay money to the people who get money. Respect the people whom you must respect. Honor the people whom you must honor.

**8** Do not owe anything to anybody. But you must always love each other. A person who loves other people obeys all of the law.

**9** The commandments say, "Do not commit adultery. Do not kill. Do not steal. Do not tell lies about other people. Do not want things that belong to other people." These commandments and all other commandments are in this one rule: "Love your neighbor as you love yourself."

**10** Love does not hurt other people. Thus, love does all that the law tells us to do.

**11** Do this, because you know what time it is. Now is the time to wake up from your sleep. The day when God saves us is nearer now than when we first believed.

**12** The night is almost gone, and the day is near. Thus, we must stop the evil things that people do in the dark. We must put on light like armor.

**13** We must live correctly, as people who live in the day. Do not go to wild parties, and do not get drunk. Do not commit sexual sins, and do not do impure things. Do not fight, and do not be jealous.

**14** Put on the Lord Jesus Christ like clothes. Do not think about how to satisfy the bad desires of your human nature.

## Chapter 14

**1** Accept the person whose faith is weak. But do not argue with him about his opinions.

**2** One person believes that he can eat all foods. Another person has weak faith, and he eats only vegetables.

**3** The person who eats all foods must not think that the other person is bad. The person who does not eat all foods must not judge the person who eats them. God accepted that person.

**4** You cannot judge the servant of another person. His own master decides if he is good or bad. The Lord will make him strong, because the Lord has the power to do this.

**5** One person thinks that one day is more important than another day. Another person thinks that all days are the same. Each person must be sure about his own opinion.

**6** The person who thinks that one day is special does this for the Lord. The person who eats all foods eats them for the Lord, because he thanks God. The person who does not eat some foods does this for the Lord, and he also thanks God.

**7** None of us lives only for himself. None of us dies only for himself.

**8** If we live, we live for the Lord. If we die, we die for the Lord. Thus, if we live or die, we belong to the Lord.

**9** Christ died and came back to life for this purpose. He is the Lord of the dead people and of the people who live.

**10** Thus, why do you judge your brother? Why do you think that your brother is not important? All of us will stand in front of God, and he will judge us.

**11** The scripture says, "The Lord says, 'As I live, all people will bow down to me. All people will say that I am God.'"

**12** Thus, each of us will tell God about what he did.

**13** Thus, we must stop the judgment of each other. Decide this: you will not do a thing that causes your brother to fall into sin.

**14** I know through the Lord Jesus, and I am sure, that no food is unclean in itself. But if a person thinks that a food is unclean, then it is unclean for that person.

**15** If the food that you eat hurts your brother, you do not live in love. Do not destroy your brother with the food that you eat. Christ died for him.

**16** Do not let people say bad things about the good things that you do.

**17** The kingdom of God is not about food and drink. It is about righteousness, peace and joy in the Holy Spirit.

**18** The person who serves Christ in this way pleases God. People also think that he is good.

**19** Thus, we must try to do the things that cause peace. We must do the things that make each other strong.

**20** Do not destroy the work of God because of food. All foods are clean. But a person does wrong if his food causes another person to sin.

**21** It is good not to eat meat or drink wine if these things cause your brother to sin. Do not do anything that causes your brother to sin or to become weak.

**22** Keep your beliefs about these things between you and God. A person is happy if his conscience does not find him guilty for the things that he accepts.

**23** But if a person has doubts when he eats, God finds him guilty. He does not eat with faith. All things that people do without faith are sin.

## Chapter 15

**1** We who are strong in faith must help the weak people with their problems. We must not do only the things that please ourselves.

**2** Each of us must please his neighbor. We must do what is good for him and make him strong.

**3** Christ also did not do the things that pleased himself. The scripture says, "The bad words that people said against you fell on me."

**4** All the things that people wrote in the scriptures in the past teach us. The scriptures give us patience and encouragement. Thus, we have hope.

**5** God gives patience and encouragement. I pray that God will help you to agree with each other, as Christ Jesus wants.

**6** Then you will all praise God together with one voice. He is the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**7** Thus, accept each other as Christ accepted you. This gives glory to God.

**8** I tell you that Christ became a servant of the Jews. He did this to show that God tells the truth. He made the promises of God to the fathers of the Jews come true.

**9** He also did this so that the Gentiles will praise God for his mercy. The scripture says, "Thus, I will praise you among the Gentiles. I will sing to your name."

**10** Also the scripture says, "Gentiles, be happy together with his people."

**11** Also it says, "All you Gentiles, praise the Lord. All people, praise him."

**12** Isaiah also says, "A person will come from the family of Jesse. He will rule over the Gentiles. The Gentiles will put their hope in him."

**13** God gives hope. I pray that God will fill you with joy and peace because you believe in him. Then the power of the Holy Spirit will give you much hope.

**14** My brothers, I am sure that you are full of goodness. You have all knowledge. You can teach each other.

**15** But in some parts of this letter, I wrote to you boldly. I want to help you remember some things. I do this because of the grace that God gave to me.

**16** God gave me this work. I serve Christ Jesus among the Gentiles, as a priest of the gospel of God. Thus, the Gentiles become an offering that pleases God. The Holy Spirit makes them holy.

**17** Thus, I am proud of my work for God in Christ Jesus.

**18** I will speak only about what Christ did through me. Through my words and actions, Christ caused the Gentiles to obey God.

**19** He did this through the power of signs and miracles. He did it through the power of the Spirit of God. Thus, I told the gospel of Christ completely, from Jerusalem to all areas as far as Illyricum.

**20** I always wanted to tell the gospel in places where people did not know about Christ. I did not want to build on the work that another person started.

**21** The scripture says, "The people who did not hear about him will see. The people who did not hear will understand."

**22** This is the reason that many things stopped me from a visit to you.

**23** But now I have no more work in these areas. For many years, I wanted to come to you.

**24** I plan to go to Spain. I hope to see you on my trip there. I want to stay with you for some time. Then I hope that you will help me on my trip to Spain.

**25** But now I go to Jerusalem to help the holy people there.

**26** The people in Macedonia and Achaia were glad to give money. They gave it for the poor people among the holy people in Jerusalem.

**27** They were glad to do this. But they also have a duty to the Jews. The Gentiles got spiritual blessings from the Jews. Thus, the Gentiles must help the Jews with material things.

**28** I will complete this work. I will make sure that the holy people in Jerusalem get all this money. Then I will visit you on my trip to Spain.

**29** I know that when I come to you, Christ will give us much blessing.

**30** Brothers, I ask you strongly through our Lord Jesus Christ and through the love of the Spirit. Help me in my work. Pray to God for me.

**31** Pray that God will keep me safe from the people in Judea who do not believe. Pray that the holy people in Jerusalem will accept the help that I bring.

**32** Then, if God wants, I will come to you with joy. Then you and I will rest together.

**33** God gives peace. I pray that God will be with all of you. Amen.

## Chapter 16

**1** I tell you good things about our sister Phoebe. She is a servant of the church in Cenchreae.

**2** Accept her in the Lord, as holy people must do. Help her with all the things that she needs. She helped many people, and she also helped me.

**3** Say hello to Priscilla and Aquila. They work with me for Christ Jesus.

**4** They put their own lives in danger to save my life. I thank them. All the churches of the Gentiles also thank them.

**5** Also say hello to the church that meets in their house. Say hello to my dear friend Epenetus. He was the first person in Achaia who became a follower of Christ.

**6** Say hello to Mary. She worked very hard for you.

**7** Say hello to Andronicus and Junia. They are my relatives, and they were in prison with me. The apostles think that they are very important. They became followers of Christ before I did.

**8** Say hello to Ampliatus, my dear friend in the Lord.

**9** Say hello to Urbanus, who works with us for Christ. Say hello to my dear friend Stachys.

**10** Say hello to Apelles. He showed that he is true to Christ. Say hello to the people in the family of Aristobulus.

**11** Say hello to Herodion, my relative. Say hello to the people in the family of Narcissus who belong to the Lord.

**12** Say hello to Tryphena and Tryphosa. These women work for the Lord. Say hello to my dear friend Persis. She worked very hard for the Lord.

**13** Say hello to Rufus, whom the Lord chose. Say hello to his mother, who is also like a mother to me.

**14** Say hello to Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermas, Patrobas, Hermes and the brothers who are with them.

**15** Say hello to Philologus, Julia, Nereus and his sister, and Olympas. Say hello to all the holy people who are with them.

**16** Say hello to each other with a holy kiss. All the churches of Christ say hello to you.

**17** Brothers, I ask you strongly to be careful of some people. These people cause divisions and problems. They go against the truth that you learned. Keep away from them.

**18** Such people do not serve our Lord Christ. They serve their own desires. They use good and pleasant words to deceive people who do not know better.

**19** All people know that you obey God. Thus, I am happy about you. But I want you to be wise about what is good. I want you to have no part in what is evil.

**20** God gives peace, and he will soon put Satan under your feet. I pray that the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ will be with you. Amen.

**21** Timothy works with me, and he says hello to you. Lucius, Jason and Sosipater, my relatives, also say hello.

**22** I am Tertius. I wrote this letter for Paul. I say hello to you in the Lord.

**23** Gaius says hello to you. I stay in his house, and the whole church meets there. Erastus, the treasurer of the city, says hello to you. Our brother Quartus also says hello.

**24** I pray that the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ will be with all of you. Amen.

**25** God has the power to make you strong in your faith. He does this through the gospel that I tell, the message about Jesus Christ. This message shows the secret that God kept hidden for a very long time.

**26** But now God shows this secret. The eternal God gave a command, and people told this secret through the scriptures of the prophets. Now all nations know it. Thus, they can believe and obey God.

**27** God alone is wise. Glory to him forever through Jesus Christ! Amen.
