# 2 Corinthians

## Chapter 1

**1** This letter is from Paul and from our brother Timothy. God chose Paul to be an apostle of Jesus Christ. We write to the church of God in Corinth and to all the saints in all of Achaia.

**2** We pray that God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ will give you grace and peace.

**3** Praise God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ! He is the Father who gives mercy. He is the God who gives all comfort.

**4** God gives us comfort in all our troubles. Thus, we can give comfort to other people in their troubles. We give them the same comfort that God gives us.

**5** We suffer much for Christ. In the same way, we also get much comfort through Christ.

**6** If we have troubles, our troubles give you comfort and salvation. If we get comfort, our comfort gives you comfort. Then you can patiently endure the same sufferings that we endure.

**7** Our hope for you is firm. We know that you share in our sufferings. Thus, you will also share in our comfort.

**8** Brothers, we want you to know about the troubles that we had in the province of Asia. The troubles were too heavy for our strength. We thought that we would die.

**9** In our hearts, we felt that we had a sentence of death. This occurred so that we did not trust ourselves. We trusted God, who raises the dead.

**10** God saved us from very great danger of death, and he will save us again. We trust that he will continue to save us.

**11** You also help us when you pray for us. Many people will pray for us. Then many people will thank God for the good things that he gives us.

**12** We are proud of this: Our conscience tells us that we lived honestly and sincerely in the world. We did not use human wisdom. We used the grace of God. We lived like this especially with you.

**13** We write to you only things that you can read and understand. I hope that you will understand completely.

**14** You understand us now only partly. But I hope that on the day of the Lord Jesus, you will be proud of us. And we will be proud of you.

**15** I was sure of this. Thus, I made a plan to visit you first. Then you could get a benefit 2 times.

**16** I planned to visit you on my way to Macedonia. Then I planned to come back to you from Macedonia. After that, you could help me to go to Judea.

**17** When I made this plan, did I make it carelessly? Do I make my plans as worldly people do? Do I say "yes" and "no" at the same time?

**18** God is faithful. Thus, our message to you is not "yes" and "no" at the same time.

**19** Silvanus, Timothy, and I preached to you about Jesus Christ, the Son of God. Jesus Christ is not "yes" and "no." In him, the answer is always "yes."

**20** All the promises of God are "yes" in Christ. Thus, through Christ we say "Amen" to give glory to God.

**21** God makes us and you strong in Christ. God anointed us.

**22** God put his seal on us to show that we belong to him. He put his Spirit in our hearts as a guarantee of the things that he will give us.

**23** I call on God as my witness. I tell you the truth: I did not come back to Corinth because I did not want to hurt you.

**24** We do not try to control your faith. We work with you for your joy, because you stand firmly by faith.

## Chapter 2

**1** I decided that I will not visit you again to cause you sorrow.

**2** If I cause you sorrow, no person can make me happy. Only the people whom I made sad can make me happy.

**3** I wrote that letter to you for this reason. I did not want to come and get sorrow from the people who must make me happy. I am sure that my joy is also the joy of all of you.

**4** I wrote to you with much trouble and pain in my heart. I wrote with many tears. I did not want to make you sad. I wanted you to know how much I love you.

**5** A person caused sorrow. He did not cause sorrow only to me. Partly, he caused sorrow to all of you. I do not want to say too much.

**6** Most of you punished that man. That punishment is sufficient for him.

**7** Thus, now you must forgive him and give him comfort. If you do not, he will possibly have too much sorrow.

**8** So I ask you to show him again that you love him.

**9** I also wrote to you to test you. I wanted to know if you obey in all things.

**10** If you forgive a person, I also forgive that person. If I forgave something, I forgave it for you in the presence of Christ.

**11** We do this so that Satan cannot trick us. We know his plans.

**12** I went to Troas to preach the gospel of Christ. There, the Lord gave me an opportunity for work.

**13** But my spirit had no rest, because I did not find my brother Titus there. Thus, I said goodbye to the people there and went to Macedonia.

**14** But we thank God! In Christ, God always leads us as in a victory parade. Through us, God spreads the knowledge of Christ to all places, like a sweet smell.

**15** For God, we are the sweet smell of Christ among the people whom God saves. We are also that smell among the people who die in their sins.

**16** To the people who die, we are a smell of death that causes death. To the people whom God saves, we are a smell of life that gives life. Which person is sufficient for this work?

**17** We are not like many people who sell the word of God for money. We speak sincerely, as people whom God sent. We speak in Christ, and God sees us.

## Chapter 3

**1** Do we start to praise ourselves again? Some people need letters of recommendation to you or from you. Do we need such letters?

**2** You are our letter. God wrote this letter in our hearts. All people know this letter and read it.

**3** You show clearly that you are a letter from Christ. We delivered this letter. People did not write it with ink. The Spirit of the living God wrote it. The Spirit did not write it on pieces of stone. The Spirit wrote it on human hearts.

**4** We are sure of this before God because of Christ.

**5** We cannot do anything good by our own ability. Our ability comes from God.

**6** God made us able to serve the new covenant (agreement). This covenant is not a written law. It is from the Spirit. The written law causes death, but the Spirit gives life.

**7** God wrote the old law with letters on stone. That law caused death, but it came with glory. The face of Moses shone with glory. Thus, the people of Israel could not look at his face. But that glory went away.

**8** Thus, the work of the Spirit has a much greater glory.

**9** The old law caused people to receive punishment, and it had glory. Thus, the work that makes people righteous has much more glory.

**10** The old law had glory. But now its glory is like nothing, because the new glory is much greater.

**11** The old law that went away had glory. Thus, the new covenant that stays has much more glory.

**12** We have this hope. Thus, we speak very clearly.

**13** We are not like Moses. He put a cover over his face. Thus, the people of Israel did not see the end of the glory that went away.

**14** But the minds of the people became closed. Even now, when they read the old covenant, the same cover stays on their minds. Only Christ removes that cover.

**15** Even now, when the people read the law of Moses, a cover is on their hearts.

**16** But when a person turns to the Lord, the Lord removes the cover.

**17** The Lord is the Spirit. Where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is freedom.

**18** None of us has a cover on our faces. We see the glory of the Lord, as in a mirror. The Spirit of the Lord changes us to be like the Lord. Our glory becomes greater and greater.

## Chapter 4

**1** God gave us this work because of his mercy. Thus, we do not lose courage.

**2** We stopped all shameful and secret things. We do not trick people. We do not change the word of God. We tell the truth clearly. Thus, before God, we show all people that we are honest.

**3** If our gospel is hidden, it is hidden only from the people who are lost.

**4** The god of this world closed the minds of unbelievers. Thus, they do not see the light of the gospel. The gospel tells about the glory of Christ, who is the image of God.

**5** We do not preach about ourselves. We preach that Christ Jesus is the Lord. We are your servants for Jesus.

**6** God said, "Let light shine out of darkness." That same God put his light in our hearts. That light gives us the knowledge of the glory of God. We see that glory in the face of Jesus Christ.

**7** We have this treasure, but we are like pots of clay. This shows that the great power comes from God, not from us.

**8** We have troubles on all sides, but these troubles do not defeat us. We are confused, but we do not lose hope.

**9** People attack us, but God does not leave us. People knock us down, but they do not destroy us.

**10** In our bodies we always share the death of Jesus. Thus, the life of Jesus also shows in our bodies.

**11** While we live, we are always in danger of death for Jesus. Thus, the life of Jesus also shows in our bodies that will die.

**12** So, death works in us, but life works in you.

**13** The scripture says, "I believed, thus I spoke." We have the same spirit of faith. We also believe, thus we speak.

**14** We know that God raised the Lord Jesus from the dead. God will also raise us with Jesus. And God will bring us with you into his presence.

**15** All these things are for you. Grace comes to more and more people. Thus, more people give thanks, and God gets more glory.

**16** Thus, we do not lose courage. Our bodies become weaker. But each day God makes our spirits new.

**17** Our troubles are small and they last only for a short time. These troubles get for us an eternal glory. That glory is much greater than the troubles.

**18** We do not look at the things that we can see. We look at the things that we cannot see. The things that we can see last only for a short time. But the things that we cannot see are eternal.

## Chapter 5

**1** Our body on earth is like a tent. We know that this tent will possibly be destroyed. But God will give us a building in heaven. Human hands did not make that house. It is eternal.

**2** In this tent we make sad sounds. We have a strong wish to put on our home from heaven, as we put on clothes.

**3** When we put on that home, we will not be naked.

**4** While we live in this tent, we make sad sounds, because we have a heavy load. We do not want to remove this body. We want to put on the new body. Then life will take the place of the body that dies.

**5** God made us for this purpose. He gave us his Spirit as a guarantee of the things that he will give us.

**6** Thus, we are always confident. We know that while we live in this body, we are away from the Lord.

**7** We live by faith, not by sight.

**8** We are confident. We prefer to leave this body and to be at home with the Lord.

**9** Thus, our goal is to please the Lord. We want to please him while we are in this body or away from it.

**10** We must all stand before Christ, and he will judge us. Each person will get what he must get for the things that he did in his body. He will get it for the good things or the bad things.

**11** We know what it means to fear the Lord. Thus, we try to persuade people. God knows us completely. I hope that you also know us in your conscience.

**12** We do not praise ourselves again to you. We give you a reason to be proud of us. Then you can answer the people who are proud of things that they see. Those people are not proud of what is in the heart.

**13** If we seem crazy, it is for God. If we have a clear mind, it is for you.

**14** The love of Christ controls us. We are sure of this: 1 person died for all people. Thus, all people died.

**15** Christ died for all people. Thus, the people who live must not live for themselves. They must live for Christ, who died for them and rose again.

**16** Thus, from now on, we do not think about any person from a human point of view. Before, we thought about Christ from a human point of view. But now we do not think that way.

**17** If a person is in Christ, he is a new creation. The old things went away. All things are new.

**18** All this comes from God. Through Christ, God made peace between himself and us. And God gave us the work of making peace between people and God.

**19** In Christ, God made peace between himself and the world. He did not count the sins of people against them. And he gave us the message of peace with God.

**20** Thus, we speak for Christ. God speaks to you through us. We ask you, for Christ, "Make peace with God."

**21** Christ had no sin. But God made Christ to be sin for us. Thus, in Christ we become righteous before God.

## Chapter 6

**1** We work with God. Thus, we ask you not to receive the grace of God without a result.

**2** God says, "At the correct time I heard you. On the day of salvation I helped you." Now is the correct time. Now is the day of salvation!

**3** We do not cause problems for any person. Thus, no person can find a fault in our work.

**4** In all things we show that we are servants of God. We are very patient in troubles, in difficulties, and in pain.

**5** People beat us and put us in prison. Crowds attacked us. We worked hard, and we did not sleep. We did not eat.

**6** We show that we are servants of God by pure lives and by knowledge. We show it by patience and kindness, by the Holy Spirit, and by true love.

**7** We speak the truth with the power of God. We use righteousness as weapons in our right hand and in our left hand.

**8** Some people honor us, and some people do not honor us. Some people say bad things about us, and some say good things. People think that we are liars, but we speak the truth.

**9** People do not know us, but many people know us well. We seem near death, but we live. People punish us, but they do not kill us.

**10** We have sorrow, but we always have joy. We are poor, but we make many people rich. We have nothing, but we have all things.

**11** People of Corinth, we spoke openly to you. Our hearts are open to you.

**12** We do not hold back our love from you. But you hold back your love from us.

**13** I speak to you as to my children. Open your hearts to us, as we opened our hearts to you.

**14** Do not join with unbelievers. Righteousness and evil cannot be partners. Light and darkness cannot be together.

**15** Christ and Belial (Satan) cannot agree. A believer and an unbeliever cannot share their lives.

**16** The temple of God and idols cannot agree. We are the temple of the living God. God said, "I will live with them and walk among them. I will be their God, and they will be my people."

**17** Thus, the Lord says, "Come out from among them, and be separate. Do not touch any unclean thing. Then I will accept you.

**18** I will be your Father, and you will be my sons and daughters," says the Lord Almighty.

## Chapter 7

**1** Dear friends, God gave us these promises. Thus, we must make ourselves clean from all things that make the body and spirit unclean. We must respect God and become completely holy.

**2** Open your hearts to us. We did not do wrong to any person. We did not cause any person to become bad. We did not cheat any person.

**3** I do not say this to accuse you. I told you before that you are in our hearts. We will die with you or live with you.

**4** I speak very boldly to you. I am very proud of you. You give me much comfort. In all our troubles, I have very much joy.

**5** When we came into Macedonia, our bodies had no rest. We had troubles on all sides. Outside there were fights, and inside there were fears.

**6** But God gives comfort to sad people. God gave us comfort when Titus came.

**7** Titus gave us comfort, and also you gave comfort to Titus. Titus told us that you want to see me. He told us about your sorrow and your strong love for me. Thus, my joy became greater.

**8** My letter made you sad, but I am not sorry that I wrote it. For a short time I was sorry, because I saw that the letter made you sad. But you were sad only for a short time.

**9** Now I am happy. I am not happy because you became sad. I am happy because your sorrow caused you to repent. God used your sorrow. Thus, we did not hurt you.

**10** Sorrow that comes from God causes a person to repent. Then God saves that person, and the person has no regret. But the sorrow of the world causes death.

**11** Look at what this sorrow from God did for you! You became very serious. You wanted to show that you were not guilty. You became angry and afraid. You wanted to see me. You were eager to make the problem correct. In all things you showed that you are not guilty in this problem.

**12** I wrote that letter to you. But I did not write it because of the man who did the wrong. And I did not write it because of the man who suffered the wrong. I wrote it so that you can see before God how much you care about us.

**13** Because of all this, we have comfort. Also, the joy of Titus made us very happy. All of you gave new strength to his spirit.

**14** I told Titus that I was proud of you. And you did not make me ashamed. All the things that we told you were true. In the same way, the good things that we told Titus about you were true.

**15** Titus loves you more and more. He remembers that all of you obeyed. You received him with fear and respect.

**16** I am happy because I can trust you completely.

## Chapter 8

**1** Brothers, now we want you to know about the grace that God gave to the churches in Macedonia.

**2** Those churches had many troubles that tested them. They were very poor. But they had much joy, and they gave very generously.

**3** I am a witness that they gave as much as they could. They gave even more than they could. They gave because they wanted to give.

**4** They asked us many times to let them share in this help for the saints.

**5** They did more than we hoped. First, they gave themselves to the Lord. Then they gave themselves to us, as God wanted.

**6** Thus, we asked Titus to help you complete this generous gift. He started this work with you before.

**7** You are rich in all things: in faith, in speech, in knowledge, in eagerness, and in your love for us. Thus, make sure that you are also rich in this act of giving.

**8** I do not give you a command. I tell you about the eagerness of other people. Thus, I test if your love is true.

**9** You know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ. He was rich, but he became poor for you. Thus, through his poverty you became rich.

**10** I give you my advice about this. It is good for you to complete this work. Last year you were the first people to start to give. You were also the first people who wanted to give.

**11** Now complete the work. You were eager to start it. In the same way, complete it with the things that you have.

**12** If a person is eager to give, God accepts the gift. God accepts the gift that the person can give. He does not ask for things that the person does not have.

**13** I do not want to make life easy for other people and difficult for you.

**14** I want things to be equal. Now you have much, and you can help them in their need. Then, at a different time, they will have much. Then they can help you in your need. Thus, things will be equal.

**15** The scripture says, "The person who collected much did not have too much. The person who collected little had sufficient."

**16** We thank God! He put in the heart of Titus the same care for you that I have.

**17** Titus agreed to our request. And he was very eager to visit you. He decided by himself to go to you.

**18** We send with Titus a brother whom all the churches praise for his work for the gospel.

**19** Also, the churches chose this brother to travel with us. He helps us take this gift to Jerusalem. We do this work to give glory to the Lord and to show that we are eager to help.

**20** We want to be careful about this large gift. We do not want any person to criticize us for how we use this gift.

**21** We try to do what is honest. We want the Lord to see it, and also people.

**22** Also, we send with them another of our brothers. We tested him many times, and he showed that he is eager in many things. Now he is even more eager, because he trusts you very much.

**23** Titus is my partner, and he works with me to help you. The other brothers are messengers from the churches. They give glory to Christ.

**24** Thus, show these men your love. Show the churches that our pride in you is correct.

## Chapter 9

**1** I do not need to write to you about the help for the saints.

**2** I know that you are eager to help. I told the people of Macedonia that I am proud of you. I said, "The people in Achaia were ready to give 1 year ago." Your eagerness caused many of them to act.

**3** But I send the brothers to you. I do not want our pride in you to be wrong in this matter. I want you to be ready, as I said.

**4** Possibly some people from Macedonia will come with me. If they find that you are not ready, we will be ashamed. You will also be ashamed. We were too sure of you.

**5** Thus, I thought that it was necessary to ask the brothers to go to you before me. They will prepare the gift that you promised. Then the gift will be ready. It will be a generous gift, not a gift that you give unwillingly.

**6** Remember this: A person who plants few seeds will get a small harvest. A person who plants many seeds will get a large harvest.

**7** Each person must give what he decided in his heart. He must not give unwillingly or because people force him. God loves a person who gives happily.

**8** God can give you much grace. Then you will always have sufficient of all things. And you will have much to give for each good work.

**9** The scripture says, "He gives freely to the poor. His righteousness continues forever."

**10** God gives seed to the farmer and bread for food. God will also give you seed and make it increase. He will make the harvest of your righteousness larger.

**11** God will make you rich in all things. Then you can always give generously. When we take your gifts to the people, they will thank God.

**12** This work of help gives the saints what they need. It also causes many people to thank God.

**13** This work proves who you are. The saints will praise God because you obey the gospel of Christ. They will praise God because you share generously with them and with all people.

**14** They will pray for you, and they will have a strong wish to see you. They will do this because God gave you very much grace.

**15** We thank God for his gift. The gift is too great for words!

## Chapter 10

**1** I, Paul, ask you, by the gentleness and kindness of Christ. Some people say that I am humble when I am with you, but bold when I am away.

**2** I ask you not to make me bold when I come. I think that I will be bold against some people. Those people think that we live as worldly people.

**3** We live in the world. But we do not fight as worldly people fight.

**4** The weapons that we use are not human weapons. God makes our weapons powerful to destroy strong forts.

**5** We destroy false arguments and all proud things that are against the knowledge of God. We take each thought prisoner and make it obey Christ.

**6** We are ready to punish all disobedience after you obey completely.

**7** You look only at the things in front of you. A person can be sure that he belongs to Christ. Then that person must think again. We belong to Christ as much as he does.

**8** The Lord gave us authority to make you stronger, not to destroy you. If I am proud of that authority, I am not ashamed.

**9** I do not want you to think that I try to frighten you with my letters.

**10** Some people say, "His letters are serious and strong. But when he is with us, he is weak. And his speech is bad."

**11** Such people must know this: When we are away, we write letters. When we are with you, we will do the things that we write.

**12** We do not compare ourselves with people who praise themselves. Those people measure themselves by themselves. They compare themselves with themselves. They are not wise.

**13** But we will not be proud of things outside the limits of our work. We will be proud only of the work that God gave us. That work includes our work with you.

**14** We do not go past our limits. We would go past our limits if we did not come to you. But we came to you with the gospel of Christ.

**15** We are not proud of the work that other people did. But we hope that your faith will become stronger. Then our work among you will become much larger.

**16** Then we can preach the gospel in places past your city. We do not want to be proud of work that another person did in his area.

**17** The scripture says, "A person who is proud must be proud of the Lord."

**18** The Lord does not accept a person who praises himself. He accepts the person whom the Lord praises.

## Chapter 11

**1** I want you to be patient with me while I speak a little foolishly. Please be patient with me.

**2** I am jealous for you, with a jealousy from God. I promised you to 1 husband, Christ. I want to give you to Christ as a pure virgin.

**3** But I am afraid. The snake tricked Eve with his cleverness. In the same way, a person can possibly cause your minds to leave your sincere and pure devotion to Christ.

**4** A person comes and preaches a different Jesus. That Jesus is not the Jesus whom we preached. Or you receive a different spirit or a different gospel. You accept these things very easily.

**5** I do not think that I am less important than those "great apostles."

**6** Possibly I am not a trained speaker, but I have knowledge. We showed you this clearly in all things.

**7** I preached the gospel of God to you without payment. I made myself humble to make you great. Was that a sin?

**8** Other churches paid me so that I could serve you. It was as if I robbed them.

**9** When I was with you and I needed something, I did not ask any person for money. The brothers who came from Macedonia gave me what I needed. I did not let myself be a load for you. And I will continue to do this.

**10** The truth of Christ is in me. Thus, no person in Achaia will stop me from my pride about this.

**11** Why do I do this? Is it because I do not love you? God knows that I love you!

**12** I will continue to do what I do now. Some people want an opportunity to say that they work as we work. I will not give them that opportunity.

**13** Such people are false apostles. They are dishonest workers. They change themselves to look like apostles of Christ.

**14** This does not surprise me. Satan himself changes his appearance to look like an angel of light.

**15** Thus, it is not surprising if the servants of Satan also look like servants of righteousness. In the end, they will get the punishment that their actions deserve.

**16** I say again: No person must think that I am a fool. But if you think so, then accept me as a fool. Then I can be proud a little.

**17** What I say now does not come from the Lord. I speak as a fool who is proud.

**18** Many people are proud of worldly things. Thus, I will also be proud.

**19** You think that you are wise. Thus, you are happy to be patient with fools!

**20** You are patient when a person makes you his slaves. You are patient when a person takes all that you have, or uses you for his gain. You are patient when a person is proud, or hits you in the face.

**21** I am ashamed to say that we were too weak to do such things! But if another person is bold, I am also bold. I speak as a fool.

**22** Are they Hebrews? I am also a Hebrew. Are they Israelites? I am also an Israelite. Are they descendants of Abraham? I am also a descendant of Abraham.

**23** Are they servants of Christ? I speak as a crazy person, but I am a better servant. I worked much harder. I was in prison more frequently. People beat me more times than I can count. Many times I was near death.

**24** 5 times the Jews hit me with a whip 39 times.

**25** 3 times people beat me with sticks. 1 time people threw stones at me. 3 times my ship was destroyed. 1 time I was in the sea for a night and a day.

**26** I traveled many times. I was in danger from rivers and from robbers. I was in danger from my own people and from people who are not Jews. I was in danger in cities, in the wilderness, and at sea. I was in danger from false brothers.

**27** I worked very hard, and I had much pain. Many times I did not sleep. I was hungry and thirsty. Many times I did not have food. I was cold, and I did not have sufficient clothes.

**28** Also, each day I have the care of all the churches.

**29** When a person is weak, I feel weak too. When a person causes another person to sin, I become very angry.

**30** If I must be proud, I will be proud of the things that show my weakness.

**31** God, the Father of the Lord Jesus, knows that I do not lie. People must praise him forever.

**32** In Damascus, the governor under King Aretas put guards around the city to catch me.

**33** But my friends put me in a basket. They let me down through a window in the wall. Thus, I escaped from the governor.

## Chapter 12

**1** I must be proud, but it does not help. Now I will speak about visions and revelations from the Lord.

**2** I know a man in Christ. 14 years ago, God took that man up to the third heaven. I do not know if the man was in his body or out of his body. God knows.

**3** I know that this man was taken up. I do not know if he was in his body or out of his body. God knows.

**4** God took this man up into paradise. There, the man heard words that a person cannot say. No person has permission to say those words.

**5** I will be proud of that man. But I will not be proud of myself, except of my weaknesses.

**6** If I want to be proud, I will not be a fool, because I will tell the truth. But I will not be proud. I do not want people to think more of me than what they see and hear.

**7** God gave me very great revelations. Thus, God gave me a pain in my body, so that I would not become too proud. The pain was like a sharp thorn. It was a messenger from Satan to hurt me.

**8** I asked the Lord 3 times to remove this pain from me.

**9** But the Lord said to me, "My grace is sufficient for you. My power is strongest when you are weak." Thus, I am very happy to be proud of my weaknesses. Then the power of Christ will stay on me.

**10** Thus, I am happy in my weaknesses for Christ. I am happy when people insult me, when I have difficulties, when people attack me, and when I have troubles. When I am weak, then I am strong.

**11** I spoke like a fool, but you forced me to do it. You must praise me. I am nothing, but I am not less than those "great apostles."

**12** I showed you the signs of an apostle with much patience. I did signs, wonders, and miracles among you.

**13** You got all the things that other churches got. But I did not ask you for money. Please forgive me this wrong!

**14** Now I am ready to visit you for the third time. And I will not ask you for money. I do not want your things. I want you. Children must not save money for their parents. Parents must save money for their children.

**15** I will be very happy to spend all that I have for you. I will also give myself for you. If I love you more, will you love me less?

**16** You agree that I was not a load for you. But some people say that I was clever and tricked you.

**17** Did I cheat you through the men whom I sent to you?

**18** I asked Titus to visit you, and I sent our brother with him. Did Titus cheat you? No! Titus and I have the same spirit. We do the same things.

**19** Possibly you think that we try to defend ourselves to you. No, we speak before God in Christ. Dear friends, all that we do is to make you stronger.

**20** I am afraid that when I come, you will not be as I want. And possibly I will not be as you want. I am afraid that there will be arguments, jealousy, and anger. I am afraid of fights, evil talk, gossip, pride, and disorder.

**21** I am afraid that when I come again, my God will make me humble among you. I am afraid that I will be sad for many people who sinned before. They did not repent of their impurity, sexual sins, and shameful actions.

## Chapter 13

**1** This will be the third time that I visit you. The scripture says, "2 or 3 witnesses must confirm each accusation."

**2** On my second visit, I warned the people who sinned. Now I am away, but I warn them again, and all other people too. When I come again, I will not be gentle.

**3** You want proof that Christ speaks through me. Christ is not weak when he deals with you. He is powerful among you.

**4** Christ was weak when people crucified him. But he lives by the power of God. We are also weak in Christ. But by the power of God we will live with Christ to serve you.

**5** Examine yourselves to see if you live in the faith. Test yourselves. You know that Jesus Christ is in you, unless you fail the test.

**6** I hope that you will know that we did not fail the test.

**7** We pray to God that you do nothing wrong. We do not pray this to show that we passed the test. We pray that you will do what is correct, even if people think that we failed.

**8** We cannot do anything against the truth. We can only do things for the truth.

**9** We are happy when we are weak and you are strong. We pray that you will become complete.

**10** Thus, I write these things while I am away. Then, when I am with you, I will not need to be severe. The Lord gave me authority to make you stronger, not to destroy you.

**11** Finally, brothers, goodbye. Try to become complete. Encourage each other. Agree with each other. Live in peace. Then the God of love and peace will be with you.

**12** Greet each other with a holy kiss.

**13** All the saints here send you their greetings.

**14** I pray that the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, the love of God, and the friendship of the Holy Spirit will be with all of you. Amen.
