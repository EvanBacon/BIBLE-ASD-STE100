# Mark

## Chapter 1

**1** This is the start of the good news (gospel) about Jesus Christ, the Son of God.

**2** The prophet Isaiah wrote, "I will send my messenger before you. He will prepare the road for you.

**3** A man shouts in the desert, 'Prepare the road for the Lord. Make his paths straight.'"

**4** John came and baptized people in the desert. He told the people, "Change your thoughts and your ways (repent), and be baptized. Then God will forgive your sins."

**5** All the people of Judea and all the people of Jerusalem went out to John. They told him their sins. Then John baptized them in the Jordan River.

**6** John wore clothes made from camel hair. He wore a leather belt around his waist. He ate locusts and wild honey.

**7** John told the people, "A man more powerful than I will come after me. I am not good enough to bend down and untie his sandals.

**8** I baptize you with water. But he will baptize you with the Holy Spirit."

**9** At that time, Jesus came from Nazareth in Galilee. John baptized him in the Jordan River.

**10** Immediately Jesus came up out of the water. He saw the heavens open. He saw the Spirit come down on him like a dove.

**11** A voice came from heaven. The voice said, "You are my Son, and I love you. I am very pleased with you."

**12** Immediately the Spirit sent Jesus into the desert.

**13** Jesus was in the desert for 40 days. Satan tempted him there. Jesus was with the wild animals, and angels helped him.

**14** After the authorities put John in prison, Jesus went to Galilee. He told the people the good news of God.

**15** He said, "The correct time is here. The kingdom of God is near. Repent and believe the good news!"

**16** Jesus walked near the Sea of Galilee. He saw Simon and Andrew, the brother of Simon. They threw a net into the sea, because they were fishermen.

**17** Jesus said to them, "Come and follow me. I will teach you to catch people, not fish."

**18** Immediately they left their nets and followed him.

**19** Jesus went a short distance farther. He saw James and John, the sons of Zebedee. They were in their boat and repaired their nets.

**20** Immediately Jesus called them. They left their father Zebedee in the boat with the paid workers. Then they followed Jesus.

**21** Jesus and his disciples went into the town of Capernaum. On the Sabbath day, Jesus went into the synagogue (the Jewish meeting house) and taught.

**22** The people were very surprised at his teaching. He taught them as a man with authority. He did not teach as the teachers of the law did.

**23** In the synagogue, there was a man with an evil spirit. The man shouted,

**24** "Jesus of Nazareth, what do you want with us? Did you come to destroy us? I know who you are. You are the Holy One of God!"

**25** Jesus spoke strongly to the evil spirit. He said, "Be quiet! Come out of the man!"

**26** The evil spirit shook the man violently and shouted with a loud voice. Then it came out of him.

**27** All the people were very surprised. They asked each other, "What is this? This is a new teaching with authority! He gives orders even to evil spirits, and the spirits obey him!"

**28** Immediately the news about Jesus went quickly to all areas of Galilee.

**29** Immediately they left the synagogue. They went with James and John into the house of Simon and Andrew.

**30** The mother of the wife of Simon was in bed. She had a fever. Immediately they told Jesus about her.

**31** Jesus went to her. He held her hand and helped her to get up. The fever went away, and she served them food.

**32** In the evening, after the sun went down, the people brought all the sick people to Jesus. They also brought the people who had demons in them.

**33** All the people of the town came together at the door.

**34** Jesus healed many people of many different diseases. He also forced many demons to go out of people. He did not let the demons speak, because they knew who he was.

**35** Early in the morning, while it was still dark, Jesus got up. He left the house and went to a place where there were no people. There he prayed.

**36** Simon and his friends went to look for Jesus.

**37** They found him and said, "All the people look for you."

**38** Jesus said to them, "We must go to the other towns near here. I must tell the good news there also. That is why I came."

**39** Thus Jesus went through all of Galilee. He told the good news in the synagogues, and he forced demons out of people.

**40** A man with a skin disease (leprosy) came to Jesus. He went down on his knees and asked for help. He said, "If you are willing, you can make me clean."

**41** Jesus felt pity for him. Jesus put out his hand and touched the man. He said, "I am willing. Be clean!"

**42** Immediately the leprosy went away, and the man was clean.

**43** Jesus sent the man away immediately with a strong warning.

**44** He said, "Do not tell anyone about this. But go and show yourself to the priest. Give the offering that Moses ordered for people who become clean. This will show the people that you are healed."

**45** But the man went out and told many people. He told the news everywhere. Thus Jesus could not go into a town openly. He stayed outside in places where no people lived. But people came to him from all areas.

## Chapter 2

**1** Some days later, Jesus went back to Capernaum. The people heard that he was at home.

**2** Many people came together there. There was no space for more people, not even near the door. Jesus told them the word of God.

**3** Some people came to Jesus with a paralyzed man. 4 men carried him.

**4** They could not get near Jesus because of the crowd. Thus they made a hole in the roof above Jesus. Then they lowered the mat with the paralyzed man on it.

**5** Jesus saw their faith. He said to the paralyzed man, "Son, God forgives your sins."

**6** Some teachers of the law sat there. They thought,

**7** "Why does this man speak like this? He speaks against God (blasphemy)! Only God can forgive sins!"

**8** Immediately Jesus knew in his spirit what they thought. He said to them, "Why do you think these things?

**9** Which is easier to say to the paralyzed man? 'God forgives your sins'? Or, 'Get up, take your mat, and walk'?

**10** But I will show you that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins." Then Jesus spoke to the paralyzed man.

**11** He said, "I tell you, get up. Take your mat and go home."

**12** Immediately the man got up. He took his mat and went out in front of all the people. All the people were very surprised. They praised God and said, "We never saw a thing like this!"

**13** Jesus went out again to the shore of the sea. All the crowd came to him, and he taught them.

**14** As Jesus walked, he saw Levi, the son of Alphaeus. Levi sat at the tax office. Jesus said to him, "Follow me." Levi got up and followed him.

**15** Later, Jesus ate at the house of Levi. Many tax collectors and sinners ate with Jesus and his disciples. Many of these people followed Jesus.

**16** Some teachers of the law who were Pharisees saw him. They saw that he ate with tax collectors and sinners. They asked his disciples, "Why does he eat and drink with tax collectors and sinners?"

**17** Jesus heard this. He said to them, "Healthy people do not need a doctor. Sick people need a doctor. I did not come to call good people. I came to call sinners to repent."

**18** The disciples of John and the Pharisees often did not eat food for a time (fasted). Some people came to Jesus. They asked, "The disciples of John and the disciples of the Pharisees fast. Why do your disciples not fast?"

**19** Jesus said to them, "The guests at a wedding do not fast while the bridegroom is with them. While the bridegroom is with them, they cannot fast.

**20** But a time will come when people will take the bridegroom away from them. Then they will fast."

**21** "People do not sew a piece of new cloth onto an old coat. If they do, the new piece will pull away from the old coat. Then the hole will become worse.

**22** People do not put new wine into old wineskins. If they do, the new wine will break the wineskins. Then the wine will flow out, and the wineskins will be destroyed. People must put new wine into new wineskins."

**23** On a Sabbath day, Jesus went through some grain fields. As his disciples walked, they started to pick some heads of grain.

**24** The Pharisees said to Jesus, "Look! Why do your disciples do a thing that the law does not let people do on the Sabbath?"

**25** Jesus said to them, "Did you never read what David did? David and his men were hungry and needed food.

**26** At the time of Abiathar the high priest, David went into the house of God. He ate the holy bread. The law lets only the priests eat that bread. But David ate it, and he also gave some to his men."

**27** Then Jesus said to them, "God made the Sabbath to help people. God did not make people for the Sabbath.

**28** Thus the Son of Man is Lord even of the Sabbath."

## Chapter 3

**1** Jesus went into the synagogue again. A man with a crippled hand was there.

**2** Some people watched Jesus carefully. They wanted to see if he would heal the man on the Sabbath. They wanted a reason to accuse Jesus.

**3** Jesus said to the man with the crippled hand, "Come and stand here."

**4** Then Jesus asked the people, "Which does the law let us do on the Sabbath? Do good or do bad? Save a life or kill?" But they did not speak.

**5** Jesus looked around at them angrily. He was very sad because they were stubborn. He said to the man, "Hold out your hand." The man held out his hand, and his hand became healthy again.

**6** The Pharisees went out. Immediately they started to make a plan with the supporters of Herod. They wanted to kill Jesus.

**7** Jesus went away with his disciples to the sea. A large crowd from Galilee followed him. People also came from Judea,

**8** from Jerusalem, from Idumea, and from the other side of the Jordan River. People also came from the area near Tyre and Sidon. A large crowd heard about the great things that Jesus did, and they came to him.

**9** The crowd was very large. Thus Jesus told his disciples to have a small boat ready for him. Then the crowd could not push against him.

**10** Jesus healed many people. Thus all the sick people pushed forward to touch him.

**11** When evil spirits saw Jesus, they fell down in front of him. They shouted, "You are the Son of God!"

**12** But Jesus gave the evil spirits strict orders. He told them not to tell people who he was.

**13** Jesus went up on a mountain. He called the men that he wanted, and they came to him.

**14** He chose 12 men to be with him. He wanted to send them out to tell the good news.

**15** He also gave them authority to heal sick people and to force demons out of people.

**16** These are the 12 men: Simon, to whom Jesus gave the name Peter,

**17** James the son of Zebedee, and John, the brother of James. Jesus gave James and John the name Boanerges. This name means "Sons of Thunder."

**18** The others were Andrew, Philip, Bartholomew, Matthew, Thomas, James the son of Alphaeus, Thaddaeus, Simon the Zealot,

**19** and Judas Iscariot. Later, Judas gave Jesus to his enemies. Then Jesus went into a house.

**20** Again a crowd came together. Thus Jesus and his disciples could not even eat.

**21** The family of Jesus heard about this. They went to take control of him. They said, "He is crazy."

**22** The teachers of the law came down from Jerusalem. They said, "Beelzebul (Satan) is in him. The ruler of the demons gives him power to force out demons."

**23** Jesus called them to him. He spoke to them in stories (parables). He said, "Satan cannot force out Satan.

**24** If a kingdom fights against itself, that kingdom cannot continue.

**25** If a family fights against itself, that family cannot continue.

**26** If Satan fights against himself and divides, he cannot continue. His end will come.

**27** A person cannot go into the house of a strong man and steal his property. First he must tie up the strong man. Then he can steal from the house.

**28** I tell you the truth. God can forgive people all their sins. God can also forgive all the bad things that they say against him.

**29** But if a person says bad things against the Holy Spirit, God will never forgive him. That person is guilty of a sin that will continue forever."

**30** Jesus said this because the teachers of the law said, "He has an evil spirit."

**31** Then the mother and the brothers of Jesus came. They stood outside. They sent a person in to call him.

**32** A crowd sat around Jesus. People said to him, "Your mother and your brothers are outside. They look for you."

**33** Jesus asked them, "Who is my mother? Who are my brothers?"

**34** He looked at the people who sat around him. He said, "These people are my mother and my brothers!

**35** The people who do what God wants are my brother, my sister, and my mother."

## Chapter 4

**1** Again Jesus started to teach near the sea. A very large crowd came together around him. Thus he went into a boat and sat in it on the water. All the crowd stayed on the shore.

**2** Jesus taught them many things with stories (parables). In his teaching he said,

**3** "Listen! A farmer went out to plant seed.

**4** As he spread the seed, some seed fell on the path. The birds came and ate it.

**5** Some seed fell on rocky ground where there was not much soil. The plants grew quickly, because the soil was not deep.

**6** But when the sun came up, the sun burned the plants. The plants had no roots, so they died.

**7** Some seed fell among thorny plants. The thorny plants grew and choked the good plants. Thus the good plants made no grain.

**8** Other seed fell on good soil. The plants grew and made grain. Some plants made 30 times more seed. Some made 60 times more. Some made 100 times more."

**9** Then Jesus said, "If you have ears to hear, then listen!"

**10** Later, Jesus was alone with the 12 disciples and the other people near him. They asked him about the stories.

**11** He said to them, "God lets you know the secret of the kingdom of God. But for the people outside, I tell all things in stories.

**12** Thus they will look but not see. They will hear but not understand. If they understood, they possibly would turn back to God, and God would forgive them."

**13** Then Jesus said to them, "Do you not understand this story? Then how will you understand any of the stories?

**14** The farmer plants the word of God.

**15** Some people are like the path where the seed fell. They hear the word. But immediately Satan comes and takes away the word that was planted in them.

**16** Other people are like the rocky ground. They hear the word, and immediately they accept it with joy.

**17** But the word has no roots in them. Thus they continue for only a short time. Trouble and persecution come because of the word. Then immediately they stop their belief.

**18** Other people are like the ground with thorny plants. They hear the word.

**19** But they worry about this life. They want to be rich, and they want other things. These things choke the word. Thus the word makes no fruit in them.

**20** Other people are like the good soil. They hear the word and accept it. They make fruit. Some make 30 times more. Some make 60 times more. Some make 100 times more."

**21** Jesus said to them, "People do not put a lamp under a basket or under a bed. They put it on a lampstand.

**22** All the things that are hidden will become known. All the things that are secret will come into the light.

**23** If you have ears to hear, then listen!"

**24** He also said to them, "Think carefully about what you hear. God will measure to you in the same way that you measure to other people. God will give you more.

**25** God will give more to the person who has something. But the person who has nothing will lose even the little that he has."

**26** Jesus said, "The kingdom of God is like a man who puts seed on the ground.

**27** Night and day he sleeps and gets up. The seed grows, but he does not know how.

**28** The soil makes the plant grow without help. First the stem grows. Then the head grows. Then the full grain grows in the head.

**29** When the grain is ready, the man cuts it immediately with his blade, because it is time for the harvest."

**30** Jesus also said, "What can I compare the kingdom of God to? What story can I use to explain it?

**31** The kingdom of God is like a mustard seed. A mustard seed is the smallest of all the seeds that people plant.

**32** But after a person plants it, it grows. It becomes larger than all the other garden plants. It has large branches. The birds can make their nests in its shade."

**33** Jesus used many stories like these to tell the people the word. He taught them as much as they could understand.

**34** He did not speak to them without a story. But when he was alone with his disciples, he explained all things to them.

**35** On that day, in the evening, Jesus said to his disciples, "We will go across to the other side of the lake."

**36** They left the crowd. They took Jesus with them in the boat where he sat. Other boats were also with him.

**37** A very strong wind storm started. The waves came into the boat. The boat almost filled with water.

**38** Jesus was in the back of the boat. He slept on a cushion. The disciples woke him up. They said, "Teacher, we will die! Do you not care?"

**39** Jesus got up. He spoke strongly to the wind. He said to the water, "Be quiet! Be calm!" The wind stopped, and the water became very calm.

**40** He said to them, "Why are you so afraid? Do you still have no faith?"

**41** The disciples were very afraid. They asked each other, "Who is this man? Even the wind and the water obey him!"

## Chapter 5

**1** Jesus and his disciples went across the sea to the area of the Gerasenes.

**2** Jesus got out of the boat. Immediately a man with an evil spirit came out of the tombs to meet him.

**3** This man lived among the tombs. No person could tie him up, not even with chains.

**4** People often tied his hands and feet with chains. But he always broke the chains. No person was strong enough to control him.

**5** Night and day he was among the tombs and in the hills. He always shouted and cut himself with stones.

**6** The man saw Jesus from a distance. He ran to Jesus and went down on his knees in front of him.

**7** He shouted with a loud voice, "Jesus, Son of the Most High God, what do you want with me? I ask you to make a promise to God. Do not cause me pain!"

**8** He said this because Jesus said to him, "Evil spirit, come out of the man!"

**9** Jesus asked him, "What is your name?" The man said, "My name is Legion, because there are many of us."

**10** He asked Jesus many times not to send the spirits out of that area.

**11** A large herd of pigs ate on the hillside near there.

**12** All the demons asked Jesus, "Send us into the pigs. Let us go into them."

**13** Jesus let them go. The evil spirits came out of the man and went into the pigs. The herd ran down the steep bank into the sea. There were about 2,000 pigs, and they drowned in the water.

**14** The men who took care of the pigs ran away. They told the news in the town and in the farm areas. The people went out to see what occurred.

**15** They came to Jesus. They saw the man who had the many demons. He sat there with clothes on, and his mind was normal. The people were afraid.

**16** The people who saw it told the others what occurred to the man with the demons. They also told about the pigs.

**17** Then the people started to ask Jesus to leave their area.

**18** Jesus went into the boat. The man who had the demons asked Jesus, "Let me go with you."

**19** But Jesus did not let him go. Jesus said to him, "Go home to your family. Tell them how much the Lord did for you. Tell them that the Lord showed you mercy."

**20** The man went away. He started to tell the people in Decapolis how much Jesus did for him. All the people were surprised.

**21** Jesus went in the boat across to the other side of the sea again. A large crowd came together around him on the shore.

**22** One of the leaders of the synagogue came there. His name was Jairus. He saw Jesus and fell down at his feet.

**23** He asked Jesus again and again, "My little daughter is almost dead. Please come and put your hands on her. Then she will be healed and live."

**24** Jesus went with him. A large crowd followed Jesus and pushed against him.

**25** A woman in the crowd had a bleeding disease for 12 years.

**26** She suffered much under the care of many doctors. She spent all her money. But she did not become better. She became worse.

**27** She heard about Jesus. She came behind him in the crowd and touched his clothes.

**28** She thought, "If I only touch his clothes, I will be healed."

**29** Immediately her bleeding stopped. She felt in her body that her disease was healed.

**30** Immediately Jesus knew that power went out from him. He turned around in the crowd and asked, "Who touched my clothes?"

**31** His disciples said to him, "You see that the crowd pushes against you. Why do you ask, 'Who touched me?'"

**32** But Jesus looked around to see the woman who did it.

**33** The woman knew what occurred to her. She was afraid and she shook. She came and fell down in front of him. She told him all the truth.

**34** Jesus said to her, "Daughter, your faith healed you. Go in peace. Your disease is gone."

**35** While Jesus spoke, some men came from the house of Jairus, the synagogue leader. They said, "Your daughter is dead. Do not cause more trouble for the Teacher."

**36** Jesus heard what they said. He said to the synagogue leader, "Do not be afraid. Only believe."

**37** Jesus let only Peter, James, and John, the brother of James, go with him.

**38** They came to the house of the synagogue leader. Jesus saw much noise and confusion. The people cried loudly.

**39** He went in and said to them, "Why do you make this noise and cry? The child is not dead. She is asleep."

**40** But they laughed at him. Jesus sent all the people out. He took the father and the mother of the child and his 3 disciples. He went into the room where the child was.

**41** Jesus held the hand of the child. He said to her, "Talitha koum." These words mean, "Little girl, I tell you, get up!"

**42** Immediately the girl got up and walked. She was 12 years old. The people were very, very surprised.

**43** Jesus gave them strict orders not to tell anyone about this. He told them to give her some food.

## Chapter 6

**1** Jesus left that place and went to his hometown. His disciples followed him.

**2** On the Sabbath, he started to teach in the synagogue. Many people heard him and were very surprised. They asked, "Where did this man get these things? Who gave him this wisdom? How does he do these miracles?

**3** He is only the carpenter. He is the son of Mary. He is the brother of James, Joses, Judas, and Simon. His sisters are here with us." Thus the people did not accept him.

**4** Jesus said to them, "People honor a prophet everywhere. But in his hometown, among his family, and in his own house, people do not honor him."

**5** Jesus could not do any miracles there. He only put his hands on a few sick people and healed them.

**6** He was surprised that the people did not believe. Then Jesus went to the villages in that area and taught.

**7** Jesus called the 12 disciples to him. He started to send them out in groups of 2. He gave them authority over evil spirits.

**8** He told them, "Take nothing for the journey but a walking stick. Do not take bread, a bag, or money in your belt.

**9** Wear sandals, but do not take 2 coats."

**10** He said to them, "When you go into a house, stay there until you leave that town.

**11** If the people in a place do not accept you or listen to you, then leave that place. Shake the dust off your feet as a warning to them. I tell you the truth. On the day of judgment, the punishment for Sodom and Gomorrah will be less than the punishment for that town."

**12** The disciples went out. They told the people to repent.

**13** They forced many demons out of people. They put olive oil on many sick people and healed them.

**14** King Herod heard about Jesus, because many people knew his name. Some people said, "John the Baptist came back to life from the dead. That is why he has power to do miracles."

**15** Other people said, "He is Elijah." Other people said, "He is a prophet, like one of the prophets from long ago."

**16** But Herod heard about this and said, "He is John. I cut off his head, but he came back to life from the dead!"

**17** At an earlier time, Herod sent his soldiers to arrest John. They tied John and put him in prison. Herod did this because of Herodias. She was the wife of Philip, the brother of Herod. But Herod married her.

**18** John told Herod, "The law of God does not let you have the wife of your brother."

**19** Thus Herodias hated John and wanted to kill him. But she could not,

**20** because Herod was afraid of John. Herod knew that John was a good and holy man. Thus Herod kept John safe. When Herod heard John, he was very troubled. But he liked to listen to John.

**21** Then Herodias got her chance. On his birthday, Herod gave a dinner for his officials, his army commanders, and the important men of Galilee.

**22** The daughter of Herodias came in and danced. Herod and his guests liked her dance. The king said to the girl, "Ask me for anything that you want. I will give it to you."

**23** He made a solemn promise to her: "I will give you anything that you ask for, up to half of my kingdom."

**24** The girl went out and asked her mother, "What must I ask for?" Her mother said, "Ask for the head of John the Baptist."

**25** Immediately the girl went quickly back to the king. She said, "I want you to give me the head of John the Baptist on a plate now."

**26** The king became very sad. But he made a promise in front of his guests. Thus he did not want to refuse her.

**27** Immediately the king sent a soldier. He ordered the soldier to bring the head of John. The soldier went to the prison and cut off the head of John.

**28** He brought the head on a plate and gave it to the girl. The girl gave it to her mother.

**29** The disciples of John heard about this. They came and took his body. They put it in a tomb.

**30** The apostles came back to Jesus. They told him all the things that they did and taught.

**31** Many people came and went, and the apostles did not even have time to eat. Thus Jesus said to them, "Come with me to a quiet place where there are no people. Rest for a short time."

**32** They went away alone in the boat to a place where there were no people.

**33** But many people saw them go and knew who they were. People from all the towns ran there on foot. They arrived before Jesus and his apostles.

**34** Jesus got out of the boat and saw a large crowd. He felt pity for them, because they were like sheep without a shepherd. Thus he started to teach them many things.

**35** It became late in the day. His disciples came to him and said, "No people live in this place, and it is already very late.

**36** Send the people away. Then they can go to the farms and villages near here and buy food for themselves."

**37** But Jesus said to them, "You give them something to eat." They said to him, "Do you want us to go and spend 200 days' pay on bread? Then we can give them food."

**38** Jesus asked them, "How many loaves of bread do you have? Go and look." They looked. Then they said, "5 loaves and 2 fish."

**39** Jesus told the disciples to make all the people sit down in groups on the green grass.

**40** The people sat down in groups of 100 and groups of 50.

**41** Jesus took the 5 loaves and the 2 fish. He looked up to heaven and thanked God. He broke the loaves and gave them to his disciples to give to the people. He also divided the 2 fish among all of them.

**42** All the people ate until they were full.

**43** The disciples filled 12 baskets with the pieces of bread and fish that remained.

**44** There were about 5,000 men who ate the bread.

**45** Immediately Jesus told his disciples to go into the boat. He told them to go before him to Bethsaida on the other side of the sea. Meanwhile, he sent the crowd away.

**46** After he sent the people away, he went up on a mountain to pray.

**47** In the evening, the boat was in the middle of the sea. Jesus was alone on the land.

**48** He saw that the disciples worked hard to row the boat, because the wind was against them. Between 3 o'clock and 6 o'clock in the morning, Jesus walked on the water to them. He wanted to go past them.

**49** But they saw him walk on the water. They thought that he was a ghost. They shouted,

**50** because they all saw him and they were very afraid. Immediately Jesus spoke to them. He said, "Be brave! It is I. Do not be afraid."

**51** Then he went into the boat with them, and the wind stopped. The disciples were very, very surprised.

**52** They did not understand the miracle of the loaves. Their minds were closed.

**53** They went across the sea and came to land at Gennesaret. They tied the boat at the shore.

**54** They got out of the boat. Immediately the people knew Jesus.

**55** They ran through all that area. They carried sick people on mats to all the places where they heard that Jesus was.

**56** Jesus went into villages, towns, and farms. In all these places, the people put the sick people in the market places. They asked Jesus to let the sick people touch only the edge of his coat. All the people who touched him were healed.

## Chapter 7

**1** The Pharisees and some teachers of the law came from Jerusalem. They came together around Jesus.

**2** They saw that some of his disciples ate food with unclean hands. That means that the disciples did not wash their hands in the special way.

**3** The Pharisees and all the Jews do not eat until they wash their hands in the special way. They obey the traditions of their old leaders.

**4** When they come from the market, they do not eat until they wash. They obey many other traditions too. For example, they wash cups, pots, metal containers, and beds in a special way.

**5** Thus the Pharisees and the teachers of the law asked Jesus, "Why do your disciples not obey the traditions of our old leaders? They eat food with unclean hands."

**6** Jesus said to them, "You are hypocrites! Isaiah told the truth about you. He wrote, 'These people say good words about me. But their hearts are far from me.

**7** Their worship of me is useless. They teach rules that people made, as if these rules come from God.'

**8** You do not obey the commands of God. You obey the traditions of people."

**9** Then Jesus said to them, "You are very skilled at the rejection of the commands of God. You do this so that you can keep your own traditions!

**10** Moses said, 'Honor your father and your mother.' He also said, 'If a person curses his father or his mother, you must kill that person.'

**11** But you teach this: A person can say to his father or his mother, 'I could help you with this thing. But it is Corban. That means it is a gift to God.'

**12** Then you do not let that person do anything for his father or his mother.

**13** Thus you cancel the word of God with the traditions that you teach. And you do many other things like this."

**14** Jesus called the crowd to him again. He said, "All of you, listen to me and understand.

**15** Things that go into a person from outside cannot make him unclean. The things that come out of a person make him unclean.

**16** If you have ears to hear, then listen!"

**17** Then Jesus left the crowd and went into the house. His disciples asked him about this story.

**18** He said to them, "Do you also not understand? Food goes into a person from outside. You must understand that this food cannot make him unclean.

**19** The food does not go into his heart. It goes into his stomach, and then it goes out of his body." With these words, Jesus said that all foods are clean.

**20** He also said, "The things that come out of a person make him unclean.

**21** From inside, from the heart of a person, come evil thoughts. Sexual sins, theft, murder, and adultery come from there.

**22** Greed, evil acts, lies, and shameful acts also come from there. Jealousy, insults, pride, and foolishness also come from there.

**23** All these evil things come from inside a person. They make a person unclean."

**24** Jesus left that place and went to the area of Tyre and Sidon. He went into a house. He did not want any person to know that he was there. But he could not stay hidden.

**25** A woman heard about him. Her little daughter had an evil spirit in her. Immediately the woman came and fell down at the feet of Jesus.

**26** The woman was a Greek. She was born in Syrian Phoenicia. She asked Jesus to force the demon out of her daughter.

**27** Jesus said to her, "First let the children eat all that they want. It is not correct to take the bread of the children and throw it to the dogs."

**28** She said to him, "Yes, Lord. But even the dogs under the table eat the small pieces of food that the children drop."

**29** Then Jesus said to her, "Because you said this, you can go. The demon left your daughter."

**30** The woman went home. She found her daughter in bed, and the demon was gone.

**31** Then Jesus left the area of Tyre. He went through Sidon to the Sea of Galilee. He went through the area of Decapolis.

**32** Some people brought a man to Jesus. The man was deaf and could not speak well. The people asked Jesus to put his hand on the man.

**33** Jesus took the man away from the crowd. He put his fingers into the ears of the man. Then Jesus spat and touched the tongue of the man.

**34** Jesus looked up to heaven and made a deep sound of sadness. He said to the man, "Ephphatha!" This word means, "Open!"

**35** Immediately the ears of the man opened. His tongue became free, and he spoke clearly.

**36** Jesus ordered the people not to tell anyone. But the more he ordered them, the more they told the news.

**37** The people were very, very surprised. They said, "He does all things well. He makes deaf people hear, and he makes people who cannot speak speak."

## Chapter 8

**1** At that time, another large crowd came together. They had nothing to eat. Jesus called his disciples to him and said,

**2** "I feel pity for these people. They stayed with me for 3 days, and they have nothing to eat.

**3** If I send them home hungry, they will become weak on the road. Some of them came from far away."

**4** His disciples said to him, "No people live here. Where can we get enough bread to feed all these people?"

**5** Jesus asked them, "How many loaves of bread do you have?" They said, "7."

**6** Jesus told the crowd to sit down on the ground. He took the 7 loaves and thanked God. He broke the loaves and gave them to his disciples. The disciples gave the bread to the crowd.

**7** They also had a few small fish. Jesus thanked God for the fish. He told the disciples to give the fish to the people too.

**8** The people ate until they were full. Then the disciples filled 7 baskets with the pieces that remained.

**9** There were about 4,000 people. Then Jesus sent them away.

**10** Immediately Jesus went into the boat with his disciples. He went to the area of Dalmanutha.

**11** The Pharisees came out and started to argue with Jesus. They wanted to test him. Thus they asked him for a miraculous sign from heaven.

**12** Jesus made a deep sound of sadness in his spirit. He said, "Why do the people of this time ask for a sign? I tell you the truth. God will not give a sign to the people of this time."

**13** Then he left them. He went into the boat again and went across to the other side.

**14** The disciples forgot to bring bread. They had only 1 loaf of bread with them in the boat.

**15** Jesus warned them, "Be careful! Be on guard against the yeast of the Pharisees and the yeast of Herod."

**16** The disciples talked among themselves. They said, "He said this because we have no bread."

**17** Jesus knew what they said. He said to them, "Why do you talk about the bread that you do not have? Do you still not see or understand? Are your minds closed?

**18** You have eyes, but you do not see. You have ears, but you do not hear. Do you not remember?

**19** I broke 5 loaves for 5,000 people. How many baskets of pieces did you fill?" They said to him, "12."

**20** "And I broke 7 loaves for 4,000 people. How many baskets of pieces did you fill?" They said, "7."

**21** Jesus said to them, "Do you still not understand?"

**22** Jesus and his disciples came to Bethsaida. Some people brought a blind man to Jesus. They asked Jesus to touch him.

**23** Jesus held the hand of the blind man and took him out of the village. Jesus spat on the eyes of the man and put his hands on him. Then Jesus asked, "Can you see anything?"

**24** The man looked up and said, "I see people. But they look like trees that walk."

**25** Then Jesus put his hands on the eyes of the man again. The man looked carefully. His eyes became healthy, and he saw all things clearly.

**26** Jesus sent him home. He said, "Do not go into the village. Do not tell anyone in the village about this."

**27** Jesus and his disciples went to the villages near Caesarea Philippi. On the road, Jesus asked his disciples, "Who do people say that I am?"

**28** They said, "Some people say that you are John the Baptist. Other people say Elijah. Other people say one of the prophets."

**29** Jesus asked them, "But who do you say that I am?" Peter said to him, "You are the Christ."

**30** Jesus gave them a strong warning. He told them not to tell anyone about him.

**31** Then Jesus started to teach them about the Son of Man. He said, "The Son of Man must suffer many things. The elders, the chief priests, and the teachers of the law will reject him. They will kill him. After 3 days, he will come back to life."

**32** Jesus told them this clearly. Then Peter took Jesus to one side and started to speak strongly against him.

**33** But Jesus turned and looked at his disciples. He spoke strongly to Peter. He said, "Go away from me, Satan! You do not think about the things of God. You think about the things of people."

**34** Then Jesus called the crowd and his disciples to him. He said to them, "If a person wants to follow me, he must forget about himself. He must take up his cross and follow me.

**35** If a person wants to save his life, he will lose it. But if a person loses his life for me and for the good news, he will save it.

**36** A person can get all the world but lose his soul. Then what good is the world to him?

**37** A person cannot give anything to buy back his soul.

**38** The people of this time are not loyal to God, and they are sinful. If a person is ashamed of me and my words among these people, then the Son of Man will be ashamed of that person. That will occur when the Son of Man comes in the glory of his Father with the holy angels."

## Chapter 9

**1** Jesus said to them, "I tell you the truth. Some people who stand here will not die before they see the kingdom of God come with power."

**2** After 6 days, Jesus took Peter, James, and John with him. He took them up on a high mountain. They were alone there. In front of them, the appearance of Jesus changed.

**3** His clothes became very bright and white. No person on earth could wash clothes so white.

**4** Then Elijah and Moses appeared to them. They spoke with Jesus.

**5** Peter said to Jesus, "Teacher, it is good for us to be here. We will make 3 shelters. One will be for you, one for Moses, and one for Elijah."

**6** Peter did not know what to say, because the disciples were very afraid.

**7** Then a cloud came and covered them. A voice came from the cloud. The voice said, "This is my Son, and I love him. Listen to him!"

**8** Suddenly the disciples looked around. They saw no other person with them. Only Jesus was there.

**9** They came down from the mountain. Jesus ordered them, "Do not tell anyone what you saw. Wait until the Son of Man comes back to life from the dead."

**10** The disciples kept this news to themselves. But they asked each other, "What does 'come back to life from the dead' mean?"

**11** They asked Jesus, "Why do the teachers of the law say that Elijah must come first?"

**12** Jesus said to them, "It is true that Elijah comes first. He makes all things correct again. But why do the scriptures say that the Son of Man must suffer much? Why do they say that people will reject him?

**13** I tell you that Elijah already came. The people did to him all the bad things that they wanted. The scriptures tell about this."

**14** Jesus, Peter, James, and John came back to the other disciples. They saw a large crowd around them. The teachers of the law argued with the disciples.

**15** Immediately all the crowd saw Jesus and became very surprised. They ran to him and greeted him.

**16** Jesus asked them, "What do you argue about with my disciples?"

**17** A man in the crowd said, "Teacher, I brought my son to you. He has a spirit in him that does not let him speak.

**18** When the spirit takes control of him, it throws him to the ground. Foam comes from his mouth, and he grinds his teeth. His body becomes stiff. I asked your disciples to force out the spirit, but they could not."

**19** Jesus said to them, "The people of this time do not believe! How long must I stay with you? How long must I be patient with you? Bring the boy to me."

**20** They brought the boy to Jesus. The spirit saw Jesus. Immediately it shook the boy violently. The boy fell on the ground. He rolled on the ground, and foam came from his mouth.

**21** Jesus asked the father, "How long did your son have this problem?" The father said, "Since he was a child.

**22** The spirit often throws him into fire or into water to kill him. If you can do anything, please feel pity for us and help us."

**23** Jesus said to him, "You said, 'If you can.' All things are possible for the person who believes."

**24** Immediately the father of the boy shouted, "I believe! Help me to believe more!"

**25** Jesus saw that a crowd ran together to them. He spoke strongly to the evil spirit. He said, "Deaf spirit that does not let him speak, I order you to come out of him. Never go into him again!"

**26** The spirit shouted and shook the boy violently. Then it came out. The boy looked like a dead body. Many people said, "He is dead."

**27** But Jesus held the hand of the boy and helped him up. The boy stood up.

**28** Jesus went into a house. His disciples asked him when they were alone, "Why could we not force the spirit out?"

**29** Jesus said to them, "Only prayer and fasting can force out this type of spirit."

**30** Jesus and his disciples left that place. They went through Galilee. Jesus did not want any person to know where they were.

**31** He taught his disciples. He said to them, "People will give the Son of Man to his enemies. They will kill him. After 3 days, he will come back to life."

**32** But the disciples did not understand what he said. They were afraid to ask him.

**33** Jesus and his disciples came to Capernaum. When they were in the house, he asked them, "What did you argue about on the road?"

**34** But they did not speak. On the road, they argued about which of them was the most important.

**35** Jesus sat down. He called the 12 disciples to him. He said, "If a person wants to be first, he must be last of all and the servant of all."

**36** Jesus took a little child and put him in front of them. He held the child in his arms and said to them,

**37** "If a person accepts a child like this in my name, he accepts me. If a person accepts me, he also accepts God, who sent me."

**38** John said to him, "Teacher, we saw a man force demons out of people in your name. We told him to stop, because he was not one of us."

**39** Jesus said, "Do not stop him. A person who does a miracle in my name cannot soon say bad things about me.

**40** The person who is not against us is with us.

**41** I tell you the truth. A person possibly will give you a cup of water in my name, because you belong to Christ. God will surely reward that person.

**42** Some little ones believe in me. If a person causes one of them to sin, there will be a bad result for that person. It is better for him if people tie a large stone around his neck and throw him into the sea.

**43** If your hand causes you to sin, cut it off. It is better to go into life with 1 hand than to go into hell with 2 hands. The fire in hell never stops.

**44** In hell, the worms do not die, and the fire never stops.

**45** If your foot causes you to sin, cut it off. It is better to go into life with 1 foot than to go into hell with 2 feet. The fire in hell never stops.

**46** In hell, the worms do not die, and the fire never stops.

**47** If your eye causes you to sin, take it out. It is better to go into the kingdom of God with 1 eye than to go into hell with 2 eyes.

**48** In hell, the worms do not die, and the fire never stops.

**49** God will make each person pure with fire, as salt makes each sacrifice pure.

**50** Salt is good. But if salt loses its taste, you cannot make it salty again. Have salt in yourselves, and live in peace with each other."

## Chapter 10

**1** Jesus left that place. He went into the area of Judea and across the Jordan River. Again crowds came to him. Again he taught them, as he usually did.

**2** Some Pharisees came to Jesus to test him. They asked him, "Does the law let a man divorce his wife?"

**3** Jesus asked them, "What did Moses order you to do?"

**4** They said, "Moses let a man write a divorce paper and send his wife away."

**5** Jesus said to them, "Moses wrote this rule for you because you are stubborn.

**6** But at the start of creation, God made people male and female.

**7** Thus a man will leave his father and his mother and stay with his wife.

**8** The 2 people will become 1 body. Thus they are not 2 people any more. They are 1 body.

**9** God joined the man and the woman together. Thus people must not separate them."

**10** In the house, the disciples asked Jesus about this again.

**11** He said to them, "A man possibly will divorce his wife and marry another woman. Then he does adultery against his wife.

**12** And a woman possibly will divorce her husband and marry another man. Then she does adultery."

**13** People brought little children to Jesus, so that he would touch them. But the disciples spoke strongly against these people.

**14** Jesus saw this and became angry. He said to the disciples, "Let the little children come to me. Do not stop them. The kingdom of God belongs to people like these children.

**15** I tell you the truth. You must accept the kingdom of God as a little child accepts it. If you do not, you will never go into it."

**16** Then Jesus held the children in his arms. He put his hands on them and asked God to bless them.

**17** Jesus started to go on his journey. A man ran to him and went down on his knees in front of him. The man asked, "Good Teacher, what must I do to get eternal life?"

**18** Jesus said to him, "Why do you call me good? Only God is good.

**19** You know the commands: 'Do not do adultery. Do not kill. Do not steal. Do not lie about other people. Do not cheat. Honor your father and mother.'"

**20** The man said to him, "Teacher, I obeyed all these commands since I was a boy."

**21** Jesus looked at him and loved him. Jesus said to him, "You need 1 more thing. Go and sell all the things that you have. Give the money to the poor people. Then you will have riches in heaven. Then come and follow me."

**22** The man heard this and became sad. He went away very unhappy, because he was very rich.

**23** Jesus looked around and said to his disciples, "It is very hard for rich people to go into the kingdom of God!"

**24** The disciples were surprised at his words. But Jesus said again, "Children, it is very hard to go into the kingdom of God!

**25** It is easier for a camel to go through the hole in a needle than for a rich man to go into the kingdom of God."

**26** The disciples were very, very surprised. They said to each other, "Then who can God save?"

**27** Jesus looked at them and said, "People cannot do this, but God can. God can do all things."

**28** Peter started to say to Jesus, "Look, we left all things and followed you."

**29** Jesus said, "I tell you the truth. Some people left their house, brothers, sisters, mother, father, children, or fields for me and for the good news.

**30** Each of these people will get 100 times more in this life. They will get houses, brothers, sisters, mothers, children, and fields. They will also get persecution. And in the future world, they will get eternal life.

**31** But many people who are first now will be last. And many people who are last now will be first."

**32** Jesus and his disciples were on the road up to Jerusalem. Jesus walked in front of them. The disciples were surprised, and the people who followed were afraid. Again Jesus took the 12 disciples to one side. He told them what would occur to him.

**33** He said, "Listen, we go up to Jerusalem. There, people will give the Son of Man to the chief priests and the teachers of the law. They will condemn him to death. They will give him to the Gentiles (the people who are not Jews).

**34** The Gentiles will laugh at him and spit on him. They will hit him with whips and kill him. After 3 days, he will come back to life."

**35** Then James and John, the sons of Zebedee, came to Jesus. They said, "Teacher, we want you to do for us what we ask."

**36** He asked them, "What do you want me to do for you?"

**37** They said to him, "When you are king in your glory, let us sit with you. Let one of us sit at your right side and one at your left side."

**38** Jesus said to them, "You do not know what you ask. Can you drink the cup of suffering that I drink? Can you be baptized with the baptism of suffering that I will have?"

**39** They said to him, "We can." Jesus said to them, "You will drink the cup that I drink. You will be baptized with the baptism that I will have.

**40** But I cannot choose who will sit at my right side or my left side. Those places are for the people for whom God prepared them."

**41** The other 10 disciples heard this. They became angry with James and John.

**42** Jesus called them together. He said, "You know that the rulers of the Gentiles show their power over their people. Their important officials use authority over the people.

**43** But you must not be like that. If a person among you wants to be great, he must be your servant.

**44** If a person among you wants to be first, he must be the servant of all.

**45** The Son of Man did not come so that people will serve him. He came to serve people. He came to give his life as a payment to free many people."

**46** Jesus and his disciples came to Jericho. Jesus left Jericho with his disciples and a large crowd. A blind man sat at the side of the road. His name was Bartimaeus, the son of Timaeus. He asked people for money.

**47** He heard that Jesus of Nazareth was there. Then he started to shout, "Jesus, Son of David, show mercy to me!"

**48** Many people spoke strongly to him. They told him to be quiet. But he shouted louder, "Son of David, show mercy to me!"

**49** Jesus stopped and said, "Call him." They called the blind man. They said to him, "Be brave! Get up! He calls you."

**50** The man threw off his coat, jumped up, and came to Jesus.

**51** Jesus asked him, "What do you want me to do for you?" The blind man said, "Teacher, I want to see again."

**52** Jesus said to him, "Go. Your faith healed you." Immediately the man could see. Then he followed Jesus on the road.

## Chapter 11

**1** Jesus and his disciples came near Jerusalem. They came to the towns of Bethphage and Bethany at the Mount of Olives. Jesus sent 2 of his disciples ahead.

**2** He said to them, "Go into the village in front of you. When you go into it, you will find a young donkey with a rope on it. No person ever sat on this donkey. Untie it and bring it here.

**3** A person possibly will ask you, 'Why do you do this?' Then tell him, 'The Lord needs the donkey. He will send it back here soon.'"

**4** The 2 disciples went. They found a young donkey on a rope next to a door, out in the street. They untied it.

**5** Some people who stood there asked them, "Why do you untie that donkey?"

**6** The disciples told them what Jesus said. Then the people let them go.

**7** The disciples brought the young donkey to Jesus. They put their coats on it, and Jesus sat on it.

**8** Many people put their coats on the road. Other people cut branches from the fields and put them on the road.

**9** The people in front of Jesus and the people behind him shouted, "Hosanna (Praise God)! God blesses the man who comes in the name of the Lord!

**10** God blesses the kingdom of our father David that comes! Hosanna in the highest heaven!"

**11** Jesus went into Jerusalem and went into the temple. He looked around at all things there. It was already late. Thus he went out to Bethany with the 12 disciples.

**12** The next day, they left Bethany. Jesus was hungry.

**13** He saw a fig tree with leaves at a distance. He went to the tree to see if it had any fruit. But he found only leaves on it, because it was not the season for figs.

**14** Jesus said to the tree, "No person will ever eat fruit from you again." His disciples heard him say this.

**15** They came to Jerusalem. Jesus went into the temple. He started to force out the people who sold and bought things there. He turned over the tables of the men who exchanged money. He turned over the seats of the men who sold doves.

**16** He did not let any person carry goods through the temple courtyard.

**17** Jesus taught the people. He said, "The scriptures say, 'My house will be called a house of prayer for all nations.' But you changed it into a hiding place for thieves."

**18** The chief priests and the teachers of the law heard this. They looked for a way to kill Jesus. They were afraid of him, because all the crowd was very surprised at his teaching.

**19** In the evening, Jesus and his disciples left the city.

**20** In the morning, they went past the fig tree. They saw that the tree was dead, down to its roots.

**21** Peter remembered the tree. He said to Jesus, "Teacher, look! The fig tree that you cursed is dead!"

**22** Jesus said to them, "Have faith in God.

**23** I tell you the truth. A person possibly will say to this mountain, 'Go and fall into the sea.' If he does not doubt in his heart, and he believes that it will occur, then God will do it for him.

**24** Thus I tell you, when you pray and ask for things, believe that you received them. Then you will have them.

**25** When you stand and pray, forgive other people if you have anything against them. Then your Father in heaven will also forgive your sins.

**26** But if you do not forgive, your Father in heaven will not forgive your sins."

**27** Jesus and his disciples went back to Jerusalem. Jesus walked in the temple. The chief priests, the teachers of the law, and the elders came to him.

**28** They asked him, "What authority do you have to do these things? Who gave you this authority?"

**29** Jesus said to them, "I will ask you 1 question. Answer me. Then I will tell you what authority I have to do these things.

**30** Did the baptism of John come from heaven or from people? Answer me!"

**31** They argued among themselves. They said, "If we say, 'From heaven,' then he will say, 'Then why did you not believe John?'

**32** But if we say, 'From people,' then there will be a problem." They were afraid of the people, because all the people believed that John was truly a prophet.

**33** Thus they said to Jesus, "We do not know." Jesus said to them, "Then I will not tell you what authority I have to do these things."

## Chapter 12

**1** Jesus started to speak to them with stories (parables). He said, "A man planted a vineyard. He put a wall around it. He dug a hole for a winepress and built a tower. Then he rented the vineyard to some farmers and went to another country.

**2** At harvest time, he sent a servant to the farmers. He wanted the servant to get his part of the grapes from the farmers.

**3** But the farmers took the servant and hit him. They sent him away with nothing.

**4** Then the man sent another servant to them. The farmers threw stones at this servant and hit him on the head. They treated him shamefully and sent him away.

**5** The man sent another servant, and the farmers killed him. He sent many other servants. The farmers hit some of them and killed some of them.

**6** The man had 1 more person to send. That person was his son, and he loved his son. Last of all, he sent his son to them. He said, 'They will respect my son.'

**7** But the farmers said to each other, 'This is the son who will get the property. Come, we will kill him. Then the property will be ours.'

**8** Thus they took the son and killed him. They threw his body out of the vineyard.

**9** What will the owner of the vineyard do? He will come and kill those farmers. Then he will give the vineyard to other farmers.

**10** Did you not read this in the scriptures? 'The builders rejected a stone. That stone became the most important stone in the building.

**11** The Lord did this. It is wonderful to us.'"

**12** The leaders knew that Jesus told this story against them. Thus they wanted to arrest him. But they were afraid of the crowd. Thus they left him and went away.

**13** Later, the leaders sent some Pharisees and some supporters of Herod to Jesus. They wanted to trap him with his own words.

**14** They came and said to him, "Teacher, we know that you are honest. You are not afraid of what people think about you. You do not show favor to important people. You teach the truth about the way of God. Tell us, is it correct to pay taxes to Caesar or not?

**15** Must we pay or not?" But Jesus knew that they were hypocrites. He said to them, "Why do you try to trap me? Bring me a coin. Let me look at it."

**16** They brought him a coin. He asked them, "Whose picture and name are on this coin?" They said, "Caesar's."

**17** Jesus said to them, "Give to Caesar the things that belong to Caesar. Give to God the things that belong to God." They were very surprised at him.

**18** Then some Sadducees came to Jesus. The Sadducees say that dead people do not come back to life. They asked him a question.

**19** They said, "Teacher, Moses wrote a law for us. If a man dies and has a wife but no children, then his brother must marry the widow. Then they must have children for the dead brother.

**20** There were 7 brothers. The first brother married a woman. Then he died without children.

**21** The second brother married the widow, and he also died without children. The same thing occurred to the third brother.

**22** All 7 brothers married her and died without children. Last of all, the woman also died.

**23** All 7 brothers married her. Thus when people come back to life from the dead, whose wife will she be?"

**24** Jesus said to them, "You are wrong because you do not know the scriptures. You also do not know the power of God.

**25** When people come back to life from the dead, they do not marry. They are like the angels in heaven.

**26** Now I will tell you about dead people who come back to life. Did you not read in the book of Moses about the burning bush? God spoke to Moses there. God said, 'I am the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.'

**27** God is not the God of dead people. He is the God of living people. You are very wrong!"

**28** One of the teachers of the law came and heard them argue. He saw that Jesus gave them a good answer. Thus he asked Jesus, "Which command is the most important of all?"

**29** Jesus said, "The most important command is this: 'Listen, Israel! The Lord our God is the only Lord.

**30** Love the Lord your God with all your heart, all your soul, all your mind, and all your strength.' This is the first command.

**31** The second command is this: 'Love other people as you love yourself.' No other command is more important than these 2 commands."

**32** The teacher of the law said to Jesus, "That is a good answer, Teacher. You told the truth. There is only 1 God, and there is no other God.

**33** We must love God with all our heart, all our understanding, and all our strength. We must love other people as we love ourselves. These commands are more important than all the burnt offerings and sacrifices."

**34** Jesus saw that the man gave a wise answer. Jesus said to him, "You are not far from the kingdom of God." After that, no person was brave enough to ask Jesus more questions.

**35** While Jesus taught in the temple, he asked, "Why do the teachers of the law say that the Christ is the son of David?

**36** The Holy Spirit told David to say this: 'The Lord said to my Lord, "Sit at my right side until I put your enemies under your feet."'

**37** David himself calls the Christ 'Lord.' Thus how can the Christ be the son of David?" The large crowd liked to listen to Jesus.

**38** In his teaching, Jesus said, "Be careful about the teachers of the law. They like to walk around in long robes. They like people to greet them with respect in the market places.

**39** They like to have the most important seats in the synagogues. They like the places of honor at dinners.

**40** They take the houses of widows by tricks. They make long prayers to look good. God will punish these men more than other people."

**41** Jesus sat across from the temple offering box. He watched the people put money into the box. Many rich people put in much money.

**42** Then a poor widow came. She put in 2 very small copper coins. These coins had a value of almost nothing.

**43** Jesus called his disciples to him. He said to them, "I tell you the truth. This poor widow put more into the offering box than all the other people.

**44** They gave only a part of the money that they did not need. But she is very poor, and she gave all the money that she had. She gave all the money that she had to live on."

## Chapter 13

**1** Jesus went out of the temple. One of his disciples said to him, "Teacher, look at these large stones and these beautiful buildings!"

**2** Jesus said to him, "Do you see these large buildings? Enemies will destroy all of them. No stone will stay on top of another stone."

**3** Later, Jesus sat on the Mount of Olives, across from the temple. Peter, James, John, and Andrew came to him when he was alone. They asked him,

**4** "Tell us, when will these things occur? What sign will show that these things are about to occur?"

**5** Jesus said to them, "Be careful. Do not let any person deceive you.

**6** Many people will come in my name. They will say, 'I am the Christ.' They will deceive many people.

**7** You will hear about wars and news of wars. Do not be afraid. These things must occur, but the end will come later.

**8** Nations will fight against nations. Kingdoms will fight against kingdoms. There will be earthquakes in many places. There will be times when people have no food. These things are only the start of the pain, like the start of the pain of a birth.

**9** Be careful. People will take you to the courts. They will hit you in the synagogues. Because of me, you will stand in front of governors and kings. You will tell them about me.

**10** But first, people must tell the good news to all nations.

**11** People will arrest you and take you to court. Do not worry before that time about what you will say. At that time, say the words that God gives you. You will not speak. The Holy Spirit will speak.

**12** A brother will give his brother to people who will kill him. A father will do the same to his child. Children will turn against their parents and cause their deaths.

**13** All people will hate you because of me. But the person who continues strong to the end will be saved.

**14** You will see 'the horrible thing that causes destruction.' It will stand in a place where it must not be. (The reader must understand this.) At that time, the people in Judea must run away to the mountains.

**15** A person who is on the roof of his house must not go down. He must not go into his house to take anything out.

**16** A person who is in the field must not go back to get his coat.

**17** At that time, there will be much trouble for women who are pregnant and for women who feed babies.

**18** Pray that these things will not occur in the winter.

**19** In those days there will be very bad trouble. Since God made the world, there was never trouble as bad as that. And there will never be trouble as bad as that again.

**20** If the Lord did not make that time shorter, no person would live. But the Lord made that time shorter to help the people that he chose.

**21** At that time, a person possibly will say to you, 'Look, here is the Christ!' or 'Look, there he is!' Do not believe him.

**22** False Christs and false prophets will come. They will do signs and miracles. They will try to deceive even the people that God chose, if possible.

**23** Thus be careful! I told you all these things before they occur.

**24** In those days, after that trouble, 'the sun will become dark. The moon will not give its light.

**25** The stars will fall from the sky. The powers in the sky will shake.'

**26** Then people will see the Son of Man come in the clouds with great power and glory.

**27** He will send his angels. They will bring together the people that God chose from all parts of the world. They will bring them from the ends of the earth to the ends of the sky.

**28** Learn a lesson from the fig tree. When its branches become soft and its leaves start to grow, you know that summer is near.

**29** In the same way, when you see these things occur, you will know that the Son of Man is near. He is almost at the door.

**30** I tell you the truth. All these things will occur before the people of this time die.

**31** The sky and the earth will stop. But my words will never stop.

**32** No person knows the day or the time when these things will occur. The angels in heaven do not know. The Son does not know. Only the Father knows.

**33** Be careful! Stay awake and pray! You do not know when that time will come.

**34** It is like a man who goes on a long journey. He leaves his house and gives his servants authority. He gives each servant a task. He tells the guard at the door to stay awake.

**35** Thus stay awake! You do not know when the owner of the house will come back. He possibly will come in the evening, at midnight, before dawn, or in the morning.

**36** If he comes suddenly, he must not find you asleep.

**37** I tell you this, and I tell all people: Stay awake!"

## Chapter 14

**1** The Passover and the festival of bread without yeast were after 2 days. The chief priests and the teachers of the law looked for a way to arrest Jesus by a trick. They wanted to kill him.

**2** But they said, "We must not do it during the festival. If we do, the people possibly will start a riot."

**3** Jesus was in Bethany at the house of Simon. Simon had a skin disease (leprosy) at one time. While Jesus sat at the table, a woman came to him. She had an alabaster jar of very expensive perfume made from nard. She broke the jar and poured the perfume on his head.

**4** Some people there became angry. They said to each other, "Why did she waste this perfume?

**5** We could sell it for more than 300 days' pay. Then we could give the money to the poor people." They spoke angrily to the woman.

**6** But Jesus said, "Leave her alone. Why do you cause problems for her? She did a good thing for me.

**7** You will always have poor people with you. You can help them at any time that you want. But you will not always have me.

**8** She did what she could. She put perfume on my body to prepare it for burial before the time.

**9** I tell you the truth. People will tell the good news in all the world. In each place, they will tell what this woman did. Thus they will remember her."

**10** Then Judas Iscariot, one of the 12 disciples, went to the chief priests. He went to give Jesus to them.

**11** They heard what Judas said and were very happy. They promised to give him money. Thus Judas looked for a good time to give Jesus to them.

**12** On the first day of the festival of bread without yeast, the people killed the Passover lambs. The disciples of Jesus asked him, "Where do you want us to go and prepare the Passover meal for you?"

**13** Jesus sent 2 of his disciples. He said to them, "Go into the city. A man who carries a jar of water will meet you. Follow him.

**14** He will go into a house. Say to the owner of that house, 'The Teacher asks, "Where is my guest room? I will eat the Passover meal there with my disciples."'

**15** He will show you a large room upstairs. The room has furniture and is ready. Prepare the meal for us there."

**16** The disciples went into the city. They found all things as Jesus told them. They prepared the Passover meal.

**17** In the evening, Jesus came with the 12 disciples.

**18** While they sat at the table and ate, Jesus said, "I tell you the truth. One of you will give me to my enemies. He eats with me now."

**19** The disciples became sad. Each of them said to him, "Surely I am not the man?"

**20** Jesus said to them, "It is one of the 12. He puts his bread into the dish with me.

**21** The Son of Man will die as the scriptures tell about him. But there will be much trouble for the man who gives the Son of Man to his enemies. It was better for that man if he was never born."

**22** While they ate, Jesus took bread and thanked God for it. He broke the bread and gave it to them. He said, "Take it. This is my body."

**23** Then Jesus took a cup and thanked God. He gave the cup to them, and they all drank from it.

**24** He said to them, "This is my blood of the new agreement (covenant). I pour out my blood for many people.

**25** I tell you the truth. I will not drink wine again until the day when I drink new wine in the kingdom of God."

**26** They sang a song of praise (hymn). Then they went out to the Mount of Olives.

**27** Jesus said to them, "All of you will leave me. The scriptures say, 'I will kill the shepherd, and the sheep will go in all directions.'

**28** But after I come back to life, I will go to Galilee before you."

**29** Peter said to him, "All the other disciples possibly will leave you. But I will not leave you."

**30** Jesus said to him, "I tell you the truth. Tonight, before the rooster calls 2 times, you will say 3 times that you do not know me."

**31** But Peter spoke very strongly. He said, "Possibly I must die with you. But I will never say that I do not know you!" All the other disciples said the same thing.

**32** Jesus and his disciples went to a place with the name Gethsemane. Jesus said to his disciples, "Sit here while I pray."

**33** He took Peter, James, and John with him. He started to become very troubled and sad.

**34** He said to them, "My soul is very sad. My sadness is so strong that I feel near to death. Stay here and stay awake."

**35** Jesus went a short distance farther. He fell to the ground and prayed. He asked God to take this time of suffering away from him, if possible.

**36** He said, "Abba, Father, you can do all things. Take this cup of suffering away from me. But do not do what I want. Do what you want."

**37** Then Jesus came back and found the disciples asleep. He said to Peter, "Simon, are you asleep? Could you not stay awake for 1 hour?

**38** Stay awake and pray, so that you do not do wrong when temptation comes. The spirit wants to do the correct thing, but the body is weak."

**39** Again Jesus went away and prayed. He said the same words.

**40** Then he came back again and found them asleep. Their eyes were very tired. They did not know what to say to him.

**41** Jesus came back the third time. He said to them, "Do you continue to sleep and rest? That is enough! The time came. Look, people will give the Son of Man to sinners.

**42** Get up! We must go. Look, the man who gives me to my enemies is here."

**43** While Jesus spoke, Judas came. Judas was one of the 12 disciples. A crowd with swords and clubs came with him. The chief priests, the teachers of the law, and the elders sent them.

**44** Judas gave them a sign before. He said, "The man that I kiss is Jesus. Arrest him and take him away under guard."

**45** Judas came and went directly to Jesus. He said, "Teacher!" Then he kissed Jesus.

**46** The men put their hands on Jesus and arrested him.

**47** One of the people near Jesus took out his sword. He hit the servant of the high priest and cut off his ear.

**48** Jesus said to them, "Do you think that I am a criminal? You came with swords and clubs to arrest me.

**49** Each day I was with you in the temple and taught. You did not arrest me then. But these things occur so that the scriptures will come true."

**50** Then all the disciples left Jesus and ran away.

**51** A young man followed Jesus. He wore only a linen cloth around his body. The men took hold of him.

**52** But he left the linen cloth and ran away from them with no clothes on.

**53** The men took Jesus to the high priest. All the chief priests, the elders, and the teachers of the law met together there.

**54** Peter followed Jesus at a distance. He went into the courtyard of the high priest. He sat with the guards and made himself warm at the fire.

**55** The chief priests and all the council looked for evidence against Jesus so that they could kill him. But they found none.

**56** Many people told lies about him. But their stories were not the same.

**57** Then some men stood up and told lies against him. They said,

**58** "We heard him say, 'I will destroy this temple that people made. In 3 days I will build another temple that people did not make.'"

**59** But even then, their stories were not the same.

**60** The high priest stood up in front of them. He asked Jesus, "Will you not answer? What is this evidence that these men give against you?"

**61** But Jesus did not speak. He did not answer. Again the high priest asked him, "Are you the Christ, the Son of the Blessed God?"

**62** Jesus said, "I am. You will see the Son of Man. He will sit at the right side of God, the Powerful One. And he will come on the clouds of heaven."

**63** The high priest tore his clothes. He said, "We do not need more witnesses!

**64** You heard him speak against God (blasphemy). What do you think?" All of them said that he was guilty and must die.

**65** Some people started to spit on Jesus. They put a cloth over his eyes and hit him with their fists. They said to him, "Prophesy!" Then the guards took him and hit him.

**66** Peter was below in the courtyard. One of the servant girls of the high priest came there.

**67** She saw Peter as he made himself warm. She looked at him carefully. She said, "You also were with Jesus of Nazareth."

**68** But Peter said that this was not true. He said, "I do not know or understand what you say." Then he went out to the entrance. A rooster called.

**69** The servant girl saw him again. She said to the people who stood near, "This man is one of them."

**70** Again Peter said that this was not true. After a short time, the people who stood near said to Peter, "Surely you are one of them. You are from Galilee. Your accent shows it."

**71** Then Peter started to curse and to make an oath. He said, "I do not know this man about whom you speak!"

**72** Immediately the rooster called a second time. Then Peter remembered the words of Jesus: "Before the rooster calls 2 times, you will say 3 times that you do not know me." Peter thought about this and cried.

## Chapter 15

**1** Early in the morning, the chief priests met with the elders, the teachers of the law, and all the council. They tied Jesus and took him away. They gave him to Pilate.

**2** Pilate asked Jesus, "Are you the King of the Jews?" Jesus answered him, "You say so."

**3** The chief priests said that Jesus did many wrong things. But Jesus did not answer.

**4** Pilate asked Jesus again, "Do you not answer? Look at how many things these men say against you."

**5** But Jesus still did not answer. Thus Pilate was very surprised.

**6** At each Passover feast, Pilate released 1 prisoner to the people. The people chose the prisoner.

**7** A man with the name Barabbas was in prison. He was with other men who fought against the government. Barabbas killed a man during that fight.

**8** The crowd shouted loudly. They asked Pilate to do for them the thing that he always did.

**9** Pilate answered them, "Do you want me to release the King of the Jews to you?"

**10** Pilate knew that the chief priests gave Jesus to him because they were jealous.

**11** But the chief priests caused the people to ask for Barabbas. They wanted Pilate to release Barabbas to them.

**12** Pilate spoke to the people again. He said, "Then what do you want me to do with the man that you call the King of the Jews?"

**13** The people shouted again, "Crucify him!"

**14** Pilate said to them, "Why? What wrong thing did he do?" But they shouted more loudly, "Crucify him!"

**15** Pilate wanted to satisfy the people. Thus he released Barabbas to them. He told the soldiers to hit Jesus with whips. Then he gave Jesus to them to crucify.

**16** The soldiers took Jesus into the courtyard of the governor's palace (the Praetorium). They called together all the soldiers of the unit.

**17** They put a purple robe on Jesus. They made a crown from thorns and put it on his head.

**18** Then they started to call to him, "Hail, King of the Jews!"

**19** They hit him on the head with a stick. They spat on him. They went down on their knees and pretended to worship him.

**20** After they mocked Jesus, they took the purple robe off him. They put his own clothes on him. Then they took him out to crucify him.

**21** A man from Cyrene, with the name Simon, came in from the country. He was the father of Alexander and Rufus. The soldiers made Simon carry the cross of Jesus.

**22** They took Jesus to the place with the name Golgotha. This name means "The Place of the Skull."

**23** They gave Jesus wine with myrrh in it. But he did not drink it.

**24** Then they crucified him. They divided his clothes among them. They threw lots to decide which part each man got.

**25** It was 9 o'clock in the morning when they crucified him.

**26** They wrote the charge against him on a sign above him. The sign said, "THE KING OF THE JEWS."

**27** They crucified 2 thieves with Jesus. One thief was on his right side, and the other thief was on his left side.

**28** Thus the words in the scripture came true: "People put him with the criminals."

**29** The people who went past insulted Jesus. They moved their heads and said, "You said that you will destroy the temple and build it again in 3 days.

**30** Save yourself now, and come down from the cross!"

**31** The chief priests and the teachers of the law also mocked him. They said to each other, "He saved other people, but he cannot save himself.

**32** Let the Christ, the King of Israel, come down from the cross now. Then we will see it and believe." The 2 men on the crosses with Jesus also insulted him.

**33** At noon, darkness came over all the land. The darkness stayed until 3 o'clock in the afternoon.

**34** At 3 o'clock, Jesus shouted with a loud voice, "Eloi, Eloi, lama sabachthani?" These words mean, "My God, my God, why did you leave me?"

**35** Some of the people near the cross heard this. They said, "Listen, he calls Elijah."

**36** One man ran and filled a sponge with sour wine. He put the sponge on a stick and gave it to Jesus to drink. He said, "Wait. We will see if Elijah comes to take him down."

**37** Then Jesus shouted with a loud voice and died.

**38** The curtain in the temple tore into 2 parts, from the top to the bottom.

**39** The centurion (the army officer) stood in front of Jesus. He saw how Jesus shouted and died. He said, "Truly, this man was the Son of God!"

**40** Some women watched from a distance. Mary Magdalene was with them. Mary, the mother of James the younger and of Joses, was there. Salome was also there.

**41** These women followed Jesus and helped him when he was in Galilee. Many other women who came with him to Jerusalem were also there.

**42** Evening came. It was the Day of Preparation, which is the day before the Sabbath.

**43** Joseph from Arimathea came. He was an important member of the council. He also waited for the kingdom of God. He went to Pilate bravely and asked for the body of Jesus.

**44** Pilate was surprised that Jesus was already dead. He called the centurion. He asked the centurion if Jesus was dead for a long time.

**45** The centurion told Pilate that Jesus was dead. Then Pilate gave the body to Joseph.

**46** Joseph bought fine linen cloth. He took the body down and put the linen around it. He put the body in a tomb that workers cut into a rock. Then he rolled a stone in front of the door of the tomb.

**47** Mary Magdalene and Mary the mother of Joses saw the place where Joseph put the body.

## Chapter 16

**1** After the Sabbath ended, Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James, and Salome bought spices. They wanted to go and put the spices on the body of Jesus.

**2** Very early on the first day of the week, they went to the tomb. The sun came up.

**3** They said to each other, "Who will roll the stone away from the door of the tomb for us?"

**4** Then they looked up and saw that someone rolled the stone away. The stone was very large.

**5** They went into the tomb. They saw a young man in a long white robe. He sat on the right side. The women were very afraid.

**6** The young man said to them, "Do not be afraid. You look for Jesus from Nazareth, whom the people crucified. He came back to life. He is not here. Look at the place where they put him.

**7** Go and tell his disciples and Peter this message: 'Jesus goes in front of you into Galilee. You will see him there, as he told you.'"

**8** The women went out quickly and ran from the tomb. They shook with fear and surprise. They did not say anything to anyone, because they were afraid.

**9** Jesus came back to life early on the first day of the week. He showed himself first to Mary Magdalene. Before, Jesus forced 7 demons out of her.

**10** Mary went and told the followers of Jesus. They were sad and cried.

**11** Mary told them that Jesus was alive and that she saw him. But they did not believe her.

**12** After that, Jesus showed himself in a different form to 2 of his followers. These 2 men walked into the country.

**13** They went back and told the other followers. But the other followers did not believe them either.

**14** Later, Jesus showed himself to the 11 disciples while they ate. He criticized them because they did not believe and were stubborn. They did not believe the people who saw him after he came back to life.

**15** Jesus said to them, "Go into all the world. Tell the gospel to all people.

**16** God will save each person who believes and is baptized. But God will condemn each person who does not believe.

**17** The people who believe will do these signs. They will use my name to force demons out of people. They will speak in new languages.

**18** They will pick up snakes. If they drink poison, the poison will not hurt them. They will put their hands on sick people, and the sick people will become well."

**19** After the Lord Jesus spoke to them, God took him up into heaven. Jesus sat at the right side of God.

**20** The disciples went out and told the message everywhere. The Lord worked with them. He showed that their message was true with the signs that followed it. Amen.
