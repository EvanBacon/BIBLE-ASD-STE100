# Obadiah

## Chapter 1

**1** This is the vision that Obadiah saw. The Lord GOD speaks about Edom. We heard a message from the LORD. The LORD sent a messenger to the nations. The messenger said, "Get up! We will go and fight against Edom."

**2** The LORD says to Edom, "I will make you small among the nations. People will hate you very much."

**3** "Your pride tricked you. You live in the holes of the rock, and your home is high. You say to yourself, 'No one can bring me down to the ground.'

**4** You can go up high like an eagle. You can put your nest among the stars. But I will bring you down from there," says the LORD.

**5** "If thieves came to you at night, they would take only what they wanted. If people came to collect your grapes, they would leave some grapes. But your enemies will destroy you completely!

**6** The enemies will search all the property of Esau. They will find all his hidden valuable things.

**7** All your allies will push you out to the border. The men who were your friends will trick you and defeat you. The men who eat your bread will set a trap for you. Edom will not understand it.

**8** On that day I will destroy the wise men of Edom," says the LORD. "I will remove understanding from the mountain of Esau.

**9** Teman, your strong soldiers will be very afraid. Thus the enemies will kill all the people of the mountain of Esau.

**10** You did violent things to your brother Jacob. Thus shame will cover you, and I will destroy you for all time.

**11** On that day you stood and watched. Strangers took away the army of Jacob as prisoners. Foreigners went into the gates of Jerusalem. The foreigners threw lots for the city. You were like one of these foreigners.

**12** You must not look with pleasure at the bad day of your brother. You must not be happy when the people of Judah are destroyed. You must not speak proudly on their day of trouble.

**13** You must not go into the gate of my people on the day of their disaster. You must not look with pleasure at their trouble on that day. You must not take their property on the day of their disaster.

**14** You must not stand at the crossroads to kill the people who escape. You must not give the survivors to their enemies on the day of trouble.

**15** The day of the LORD is near for all the nations. The LORD will do to you the same things that you did. The bad things that you did will come back on you.

**16** You drank on my holy mountain. In the same way, all the nations will drink my punishment continually. They will drink and swallow it. Then they will be as if they did not exist.

**17** But some people will escape and live on Mount Zion. Mount Zion will be holy. The people of Jacob will take back their land.

**18** The people of Jacob will be like a fire. The people of Joseph will be like a flame. The people of Esau will be like dry grass. Jacob and Joseph will burn Esau and destroy him. No one from the people of Esau will stay alive. The LORD said this.

**19** The people from the south will take the mountain of Esau. The people from the low hills will take the land of the Philistines. They will take the fields of Ephraim and the fields of Samaria. Benjamin will take Gilead.

**20** The army of Israelite prisoners will take the land of the Canaanites as far as Zarephath. The prisoners from Jerusalem who live in Sepharad will take the cities of the south.

**21** Rescuers will go up on Mount Zion to judge the mountain of Esau. The kingdom will belong to the LORD."
