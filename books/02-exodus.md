# Exodus

## Chapter 1

**1** These are the names of the sons of Israel who came into Egypt with Jacob. Each man came with his family.

**2** The sons were Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah,

**3** Issachar, Zebulun, and Benjamin,

**4** Dan, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher.

**5** The descendants of Jacob were 70 persons in total. Joseph was already in Egypt.

**6** Then Joseph died. All his brothers and all the people of that generation also died.

**7** But the Israelites had many children. Their number became very large, and they became very strong. They filled the land.

**8** Then a new king started to rule over Egypt. This king did not know about Joseph.

**9** The king said to his people, "Look, the Israelites are more numerous and stronger than we are.

**10** We must be careful with them. If we do nothing, their number will increase more. If a war starts, they will join our enemies. They will fight against us and go out of the land."

**11** Thus the Egyptians put slave masters over the Israelites to make their work very hard. The Israelites built the storage cities Pithom and Raamses for Pharaoh.

**12** But when the Egyptians made the work harder, the number of the Israelites increased more. The Egyptians became afraid of the Israelites.

**13** The Egyptians made the Israelites work very hard as slaves.

**14** They made the lives of the Israelites bitter with hard work. The Israelites made mortar and bricks and did all types of work in the fields. The Egyptians were cruel to them in all this work.

**15** The king of Egypt spoke to the Hebrew midwives. The name of one midwife was Shiphrah, and the name of the other was Puah.

**16** The king said, "When you help the Hebrew women give birth, look at the baby. If the baby is a son, kill him. If the baby is a daughter, let her live."

**17** But the midwives had respect for God. They did not do what the king of Egypt told them. They let the boys live.

**18** The king of Egypt called the midwives. He said to them, "Why did you do this? Why did you let the boys live?"

**19** The midwives said to Pharaoh, "The Hebrew women are not like the Egyptian women. The Hebrew women are strong. They give birth before the midwife comes to them."

**20** Thus God was good to the midwives. The number of the people increased, and they became very strong.

**21** Because the midwives had respect for God, God gave them families of their own.

**22** Then Pharaoh gave an order to all his people. He said, "Throw every newborn Hebrew son into the river Nile. But let every daughter live."

## Chapter 2

**1** A man from the tribe of Levi married a woman from the tribe of Levi.

**2** The woman became pregnant and gave birth to a son. She saw that he was a beautiful child. She hid him for 3 months.

**3** After that time, she could not hide him longer. She got a basket made of reeds. She covered the basket with tar and pitch to keep the water out. She put the child in the basket. Then she put the basket in the reeds at the edge of the river.

**4** The sister of the child stood at a distance. She wanted to know what would happen to him.

**5** The daughter of Pharaoh came down to the river to wash. Her female servants walked along the edge of the river. She saw the basket among the reeds. She sent her servant to get the basket.

**6** She opened the basket and saw the child. The baby cried. She felt pity for him. She said, "This is one of the Hebrew children."

**7** Then the sister of the baby said to the daughter of Pharaoh, "Do you want me to find a Hebrew woman to feed the baby for you?"

**8** The daughter of Pharaoh said to her, "Go." The girl went and called the mother of the child.

**9** The daughter of Pharaoh said to the woman, "Take this child and feed him for me. I will pay you." The woman took the child and fed him.

**10** The child grew. Then the woman brought him to the daughter of Pharaoh, and he became her son. She gave him the name Moses. She said, "I pulled him out of the water."

**11** When Moses was a man, he went out to his own people, the Hebrews. He saw their hard work. He saw an Egyptian who hit a Hebrew, one of his own people.

**12** Moses looked around in all directions. He saw that no person was there. Then he killed the Egyptian and hid the body in the sand.

**13** On the next day, Moses went out again. He saw 2 Hebrew men who fought. He said to the man who was wrong, "Why do you hit your fellow Hebrew?"

**14** The man said, "Who made you a ruler and a judge over us? Do you plan to kill me as you killed the Egyptian?" Then Moses was afraid. He said, "Surely other people know what I did."

**15** Pharaoh heard about this and tried to kill Moses. But Moses ran away from Pharaoh. He went to live in the land of Midian. He sat down near a well.

**16** The priest of Midian had 7 daughters. They came to the well and got water. They filled the troughs to give water to the flock of their father.

**17** Some shepherds came and pushed the daughters away. But Moses stood up and helped the daughters. He gave water to their flock.

**18** The daughters went back to Reuel, their father. He said, "Why did you come back so early today?"

**19** They said, "An Egyptian saved us from the shepherds. He also got water for us and gave water to the flock."

**20** Reuel said to his daughters, "Where is the man? Why did you leave him there? Invite him to eat with us."

**21** Moses agreed to live with Reuel. Reuel gave his daughter Zipporah to Moses as his wife.

**22** Zipporah gave birth to a son. Moses gave him the name Gershom. Moses said, "I am a foreigner in a foreign land."

**23** After a long time, the king of Egypt died. The Israelites were sad because of their hard work as slaves. They cried for help. Their cry went up to God.

**24** God heard their sad cries. God remembered his agreement (covenant) with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

**25** God saw the Israelites, and God knew about their trouble.

## Chapter 3

**1** Moses kept the flock of his father-in-law, Jethro, the priest of Midian. Moses took the flock to the far side of the desert. He came to Horeb, the mountain of God.

**2** There the angel of the LORD appeared to Moses in a flame of fire from the middle of a bush. Moses looked. The bush burned, but the fire did not destroy the bush.

**3** Moses said, "I will go near and look at this strange thing. I want to know why the bush does not burn up."

**4** The LORD saw that Moses came near to look. God called to him from the middle of the bush. God said, "Moses! Moses!" Moses said, "I am here."

**5** God said, "Do not come nearer. Take off your sandals. The place where you stand is holy ground."

**6** God also said, "I am the God of your father. I am the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob." Moses hid his face, because he was afraid to look at God.

**7** The LORD said, "I saw the trouble of my people in Egypt. I heard their cries because of their slave masters. I know about their pain.

**8** I came down to save them from the Egyptians. I will bring them out of Egypt to a good, large land. That land has much milk and honey. It is the land of the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites.

**9** The cry of the Israelites came to me. I also saw how the Egyptians are cruel to them.

**10** Thus go now. I will send you to Pharaoh. You will bring my people, the Israelites, out of Egypt."

**11** Moses said to God, "I am not an important person. How can I go to Pharaoh? How can I bring the Israelites out of Egypt?"

**12** God said, "I will be with you. This will be the sign that I sent you: when you bring the people out of Egypt, you will all worship God on this mountain."

**13** Moses said to God, "I will go to the Israelites. I will tell them, 'The God of your ancestors sent me to you.' Then they will ask me, 'What is his name?' What must I tell them?"

**14** God said to Moses, "I AM WHO I AM." He said, "Tell the Israelites this: 'I AM sent me to you.'"

**15** God also said to Moses, "Tell the Israelites this: 'The LORD, the God of your ancestors, sent me to you. He is the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.' This is my name for all time. All generations will remember me by this name.

**16** Go and gather the leaders of Israel. Tell them, 'The LORD, the God of your ancestors, appeared to me. He is the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. He said, "I watched you carefully. I saw what the Egyptians do to you.

**17** I promise that I will bring you out of your trouble in Egypt. I will bring you to the land of the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites. That land has much milk and honey."'

**18** The leaders will listen to you. Then you and the leaders of Israel must go to the king of Egypt. Tell him, 'The LORD, the God of the Hebrews, met with us. Please let us go on a journey of 3 days into the desert. There we will offer sacrifices to the LORD our God.'

**19** I know that the king of Egypt will not let you go. He will not let you go, even if a strong hand pushes him.

**20** Thus I will use my power and attack Egypt. I will do many miracles among the Egyptians. After that, the king will let you go.

**21** I will cause the Egyptians to be kind to my people. When you go, you will not go with nothing.

**22** Each woman will ask her neighbor and any woman who lives in her house for silver and gold jewelry and clothing. You will put these things on your sons and daughters. In this way you will take the riches of the Egyptians."

## Chapter 4

**1** Moses said, "But the Israelites will not believe me. They will not listen to me. They will say, 'The LORD did not appear to you.'"

**2** The LORD said to Moses, "What is that in your hand?" Moses said, "A staff."

**3** The LORD said, "Throw it on the ground." Moses threw it on the ground, and the staff became a snake. Moses ran away from the snake.

**4** The LORD said to Moses, "Put out your hand and take the snake by the tail." Moses put out his hand and took the snake. The snake became a staff in his hand again.

**5** The LORD said, "Do this, and the people will believe that the LORD appeared to you. I am the LORD, the God of their ancestors, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob."

**6** The LORD also said to Moses, "Put your hand inside your coat." Moses put his hand inside his coat. When he took it out, his hand had a skin disease. The hand was white like snow.

**7** God said, "Put your hand inside your coat again." Moses put his hand inside his coat again. When he took it out, the hand was healthy again, as the rest of his body.

**8** The LORD said, "The people possibly will not believe you or the first sign. Then they will believe the second sign.

**9** If they do not believe these 2 signs and do not listen to you, take some water from the river Nile. Pour the water on the dry ground. The water from the river will become blood on the dry ground."

**10** Moses said to the LORD, "Please, Lord, I am not a good speaker. I was not a good speaker in the past. I am not a good speaker now that you spoke to me. My speech is slow, and my tongue is slow."

**11** The LORD said to him, "Who made the mouth of man? Who makes a person unable to speak or unable to hear? Who gives sight or makes a person blind? I, the LORD, do these things.

**12** Thus go now. I will help you speak. I will teach you what to say."

**13** But Moses said, "Please, Lord, send a different person."

**14** Then the LORD became angry with Moses. He said, "Your brother Aaron the Levite can speak well. I know this. Aaron comes now to meet you. When he sees you, he will be glad.

**15** Speak to Aaron and tell him what to say. I will help you and Aaron speak. I will teach you both what to do.

**16** Aaron will speak to the people for you. He will be your mouth, and you will be like God to him.

**17** Take this staff in your hand. You will do the signs with the staff."

**18** Moses went back to Jethro, his father-in-law. Moses said to him, "Please let me go back to my people in Egypt. I want to see if they are still alive." Jethro said to Moses, "Go in peace."

**19** The LORD said to Moses in Midian, "Go back to Egypt. All the men who wanted to kill you are dead."

**20** Moses put his wife and his sons on a donkey and went back to Egypt. Moses held the staff of God in his hand.

**21** The LORD said to Moses, "When you go back to Egypt, do all the miracles in front of Pharaoh. I gave you the power to do them. But I will make Pharaoh stubborn. He will not let the people go.

**22** Then tell Pharaoh, 'The LORD says this: Israel is my son, my firstborn son.

**23** I told you to let my son go so that he can worship me. But you refused to let him go. Thus I will kill your son, your firstborn son.'"

**24** On the journey, Moses stopped at a place for the night. There the LORD met Moses and tried to kill him.

**25** Then Zipporah took a sharp stone and cut off the foreskin of her son. She touched the feet of Moses with the foreskin. She said, "Surely you are a husband of blood to me."

**26** Then the LORD let Moses live. Zipporah said, "You are a husband of blood," because of the circumcision.

**27** The LORD said to Aaron, "Go into the desert to meet Moses." Aaron went and met Moses at the mountain of God. Aaron kissed Moses.

**28** Moses told Aaron all the words that the LORD sent him to speak. Moses also told Aaron about all the signs that the LORD told him to do.

**29** Moses and Aaron went and gathered all the leaders of the Israelites.

**30** Aaron told them all the words that the LORD spoke to Moses. Aaron also did the signs in front of the people.

**31** The people believed. They heard that the LORD came to help the Israelites. They heard that he saw their trouble. Then they bowed their heads and worshipped.

## Chapter 5

**1** After that, Moses and Aaron went to Pharaoh. They said, "The LORD, the God of Israel, says this: 'Let my people go, so that they can hold a festival for me in the desert.'"

**2** Pharaoh said, "Who is the LORD? Why must I obey him and let Israel go? I do not know the LORD. I will not let Israel go."

**3** Moses and Aaron said, "The God of the Hebrews met with us. Please let us go on a journey of 3 days into the desert. There we will offer sacrifices to the LORD our God. If we do not do this, he will possibly attack us with disease or with the sword."

**4** But the king of Egypt said to them, "Moses and Aaron, why do you stop the people from their work? Go back to your work!"

**5** Pharaoh also said, "Look, the people of the land are now many. And you make them stop their work."

**6** On that same day, Pharaoh gave orders to the slave masters and the foremen of the people. He said,

**7** "Do not give the people straw to make bricks as before. Let them go and gather straw for themselves.

**8** But they must make the same number of bricks as before. Do not decrease the number. They are lazy. That is why they cry, 'Let us go and offer sacrifices to our God.'

**9** Give the men more work. Keep them busy, so that they do not listen to lies."

**10** The slave masters and the foremen of the people went out. They said to the people, "Pharaoh says this: 'I will not give you straw.

**11** Go and get straw where you can find it. But your work will not decrease.'"

**12** Thus the people went to all parts of Egypt to gather stubble for straw.

**13** The slave masters pushed them to work fast. They said, "Each day, finish all your work, as when you had straw."

**14** Pharaoh's slave masters put Israelite foremen over the people. The slave masters hit these foremen. They asked, "Why did you not make the usual number of bricks yesterday and today, as before?"

**15** Then the Israelite foremen went to Pharaoh and cried out to him. They said, "Why do you do this to your servants?

**16** Your officials give us no straw. But they tell us, 'Make bricks!' And they hit your servants. But the fault is with your own people."

**17** Pharaoh said, "You are lazy! You are lazy! That is why you say, 'Let us go and offer sacrifices to the LORD.'

**18** Go now and work. We will give you no straw. But you must make the full number of bricks."

**19** The Israelite foremen saw that they were in trouble. Pharaoh told them, "You must not decrease the number of bricks each day."

**20** The foremen came out from Pharaoh. They met Moses and Aaron, who waited for them.

**21** The foremen said to Moses and Aaron, "The LORD will look at what you did and judge you. You caused Pharaoh and his officials to hate us. You gave them a reason to kill us."

**22** Then Moses went back to the LORD. Moses said, "Lord, why did you cause trouble for this people? Why did you send me?

**23** I went to Pharaoh to speak in your name. Since that time, he did more evil to this people. And you did not save your people at all."

## Chapter 6

**1** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Now you will see what I will do to Pharaoh. My strong power will cause him to let the people go. My strong power will cause him to push them out of his land."

**2** God also spoke to Moses. He said, "I am the LORD.

**3** I appeared to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob as God Almighty. But I did not let them know me by my name, the LORD.

**4** I also made my agreement (covenant) with them. I promised to give them the land of Canaan. They lived in that land as foreigners.

**5** Also, I heard the sad cries of the Israelites. The Egyptians keep them as slaves. I remembered my covenant.

**6** Thus tell the Israelites this: 'I am the LORD. I will bring you out from under the hard work of the Egyptians. I will make you free from slavery. I will use my great power to save you. I will punish the Egyptians severely.

**7** I will take you as my people, and I will be your God. Then you will know that I am the LORD your God. I will bring you out from under the hard work of the Egyptians.

**8** I will bring you to the land that I promised to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. I made a solemn promise to give it to them. I will give that land to you as your property. I am the LORD.'"

**9** Moses told this to the Israelites. But they did not listen to Moses. Their spirits were weak because of their cruel slavery.

**10** Then the LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**11** "Go and tell Pharaoh, king of Egypt, to let the Israelites go out of his land."

**12** But Moses said to the LORD, "The Israelites did not listen to me. Thus Pharaoh will not listen to me. I am not a good speaker."

**13** But the LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron. He gave them orders for the Israelites and for Pharaoh, king of Egypt. He told them to bring the Israelites out of the land of Egypt.

**14** These are the heads of the families of Israel. The sons of Reuben, the firstborn son of Israel, were Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi. These were the families of Reuben.

**15** The sons of Simeon were Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul. The mother of Shaul was a Canaanite woman. These were the families of Simeon.

**16** These are the names of the sons of Levi, in the order of their birth: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. Levi lived 137 years.

**17** The sons of Gershon were Libni and Shimei, with their families.

**18** The sons of Kohath were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. Kohath lived 133 years.

**19** The sons of Merari were Mahli and Mushi. These were the families of Levi, in the order of their birth.

**20** Amram married Jochebed, the sister of his father. She gave birth to Aaron and Moses. Amram lived 137 years.

**21** The sons of Izhar were Korah, Nepheg, and Zichri.

**22** The sons of Uzziel were Mishael, Elzaphan, and Zithri.

**23** Aaron married Elisheba. She was the daughter of Amminadab and the sister of Nahshon. She gave birth to Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.

**24** The sons of Korah were Assir, Elkanah, and Abiasaph. These were the families of the Korahites.

**25** Eleazar, the son of Aaron, married one of the daughters of Putiel. She gave birth to Phinehas. These were the heads of the families of the Levites.

**26** These are the same Aaron and Moses to whom the LORD spoke. The LORD said, "Bring the Israelites out of the land of Egypt, group by group, as an army."

**27** Moses and Aaron spoke to Pharaoh, king of Egypt. They told him to let the Israelites go out of Egypt.

**28** On a day in Egypt, the LORD spoke to Moses.

**29** The LORD said to Moses, "I am the LORD. Tell Pharaoh, king of Egypt, all the things that I tell you."

**30** But Moses said to the LORD, "I am not a good speaker. Pharaoh will not listen to me."

## Chapter 7

**1** The LORD said to Moses, "Look, I make you like God to Pharaoh. Your brother Aaron will be your prophet.

**2** Speak all the things that I tell you. Then your brother Aaron must tell Pharaoh to let the Israelites go out of his land.

**3** But I will make Pharaoh stubborn. I will do many signs and miracles in the land of Egypt.

**4** Pharaoh will not listen to you. Then I will use my power against Egypt. I will punish Egypt severely. I will bring my people, the Israelites, out of Egypt group by group.

**5** I will use my power against Egypt and bring the Israelites out. Then the Egyptians will know that I am the LORD."

**6** Moses and Aaron did all the things that the LORD told them.

**7** Moses was 80 years old, and Aaron was 83 years old, when they spoke to Pharaoh.

**8** The LORD said to Moses and Aaron,

**9** "Pharaoh will tell you, 'Show me a miracle.' Then tell Aaron, 'Take your staff and throw it down in front of Pharaoh.' The staff will become a snake."

**10** Moses and Aaron went to Pharaoh. They did what the LORD told them. Aaron threw down his staff in front of Pharaoh and his officials. The staff became a snake.

**11** Then Pharaoh called his wise men and sorcerers. The magicians of Egypt used their secret arts and did the same thing.

**12** Each magician threw down his staff, and each staff became a snake. But the staff of Aaron swallowed their staffs.

**13** But Pharaoh became stubborn. He did not listen to Moses and Aaron, as the LORD said.

**14** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Pharaoh is stubborn. He refuses to let the people go.

**15** Go to Pharaoh in the morning when he goes out to the water. Stand on the bank of the river Nile to meet him. Hold in your hand the staff that became a snake.

**16** Tell him, 'The LORD, the God of the Hebrews, sent me to you. He told you, "Let my people go, so that they can worship me in the desert." But until now you did not listen.

**17** The LORD says this: "By this you will know that I am the LORD." Look, I will hit the water of the river Nile with the staff in my hand. The water will become blood.

**18** The fish in the river will die. The river will smell bad. The Egyptians will not be able to drink water from the river.'"

**19** The LORD said to Moses, "Tell Aaron, 'Take your staff. Hold your hand out over the waters of Egypt.' Hold it over their streams, canals, ponds, and all their pools. The water will become blood. There will be blood in all the land of Egypt. There will be blood even in containers of wood and stone."

**20** Moses and Aaron did what the LORD told them. Aaron lifted up the staff and hit the water of the river Nile. Pharaoh and his officials saw this. All the water in the river became blood.

**21** The fish in the river died. The river smelled bad. The Egyptians could not drink water from the river. There was blood in all the land of Egypt.

**22** But the magicians of Egypt did the same thing with their secret arts. Thus Pharaoh stayed stubborn. He did not listen to Moses and Aaron, as the LORD said.

**23** Pharaoh turned and went into his house. He did not think about this miracle.

**24** All the Egyptians dug along the river to find water to drink. They could not drink the water of the river.

**25** 7 days went by after the LORD hit the river.

## Chapter 8

**1** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Go to Pharaoh and tell him, 'The LORD says this: Let my people go, so that they can worship me.

**2** If you refuse to let them go, I will send frogs on all your country.

**3** The river Nile will be full of frogs. The frogs will come into your palace, into your bedroom, and onto your bed. They will come into the houses of your officials and onto your people. They will come into your ovens and into your bowls for bread.

**4** The frogs will come up on you, on your people, and on all your officials.'"

**5** The LORD said to Moses, "Tell Aaron, 'Hold your staff out over the streams, the canals, and the ponds. Cause frogs to come up on the land of Egypt.'"

**6** Aaron held his hand out over the waters of Egypt. The frogs came up and covered the land of Egypt.

**7** But the magicians did the same thing with their secret arts. They also caused frogs to come up on the land of Egypt.

**8** Then Pharaoh called Moses and Aaron. He said, "Pray to the LORD. Ask him to take the frogs away from me and from my people. Then I will let the people go to offer sacrifices to the LORD."

**9** Moses said to Pharaoh, "You can choose the time. Tell me when to pray for you, your officials, and your people. Then the frogs will go away from you and your houses. Frogs will stay only in the river Nile."

**10** Pharaoh said, "Tomorrow." Moses said, "It will be as you say. Then you will know that there is no god like the LORD our God.

**11** The frogs will go away from you, your houses, your officials, and your people. Frogs will stay only in the river Nile."

**12** Moses and Aaron went out from Pharaoh. Then Moses prayed to the LORD about the frogs that the LORD sent on Pharaoh.

**13** The LORD did what Moses asked. The frogs died in the houses, the yards, and the fields.

**14** The people put the dead frogs in piles. The land smelled bad.

**15** But Pharaoh saw that the trouble stopped. Then he became stubborn again. He did not listen to Moses and Aaron, as the LORD said.

**16** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Tell Aaron, 'Hold out your staff and hit the dust on the ground.' The dust will become gnats in all the land of Egypt."

**17** Moses and Aaron did this. Aaron held out his hand with the staff and hit the dust on the ground. Gnats came on the people and on the animals. All the dust in the land of Egypt became gnats.

**18** The magicians tried to make gnats with their secret arts, but they could not. The gnats stayed on the people and on the animals.

**19** The magicians said to Pharaoh, "God did this with his power." But Pharaoh stayed stubborn. He did not listen to Moses and Aaron, as the LORD said.

**20** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Get up early in the morning. Stand in front of Pharaoh when he goes out to the water. Tell him, 'The LORD says this: Let my people go, so that they can worship me.

**21** If you do not let my people go, I will send swarms of flies on you, your officials, and your people. The flies will go into your houses. The houses of the Egyptians will be full of flies. The ground also will be full of flies.

**22** But on that day I will keep the land of Goshen separate, where my people live. No flies will be there. Then you will know that I, the LORD, am in this land.

**23** I will make a difference between my people and your people. This sign will occur tomorrow.'"

**24** The LORD did this. Large swarms of flies came into the palace of Pharaoh and into the houses of his officials. The flies went into all the land of Egypt. The flies destroyed the land.

**25** Then Pharaoh called Moses and Aaron. He said, "Go and offer sacrifices to your God here in this land."

**26** But Moses said, "That is not correct. The Egyptians hate the sacrifices that we give to the LORD our God. If we offer those sacrifices in front of the Egyptians, they will throw stones at us and kill us.

**27** We must go on a journey of 3 days into the desert. There we will offer sacrifices to the LORD our God, as he tells us."

**28** Pharaoh said, "I will let you go to offer sacrifices to the LORD your God in the desert. But do not go very far. Pray for me."

**29** Moses said, "I will go out from you and pray to the LORD. Tomorrow the flies will go away from Pharaoh, his officials, and his people. But Pharaoh must not lie to us again. He must let the people go to offer sacrifices to the LORD."

**30** Moses went out from Pharaoh and prayed to the LORD.

**31** The LORD did what Moses asked. He took the flies away from Pharaoh, his officials, and his people. Not 1 fly stayed.

**32** But Pharaoh became stubborn again. He did not let the people go.

## Chapter 9

**1** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Go to Pharaoh and tell him, 'The LORD, the God of the Hebrews, says this: Let my people go, so that they can worship me.

**2** Possibly you will refuse to let them go and keep them.

**3** Then the LORD will use his power against your animals in the fields. He will send a very bad disease on your horses, donkeys, camels, cattle, sheep, and goats.

**4** But the LORD will make a difference between the animals of Israel and the animals of Egypt. No animal that belongs to the Israelites will die.'"

**5** The LORD set a time. He said, "Tomorrow the LORD will do this in the land."

**6** On the next day, the LORD did this. All the animals of the Egyptians died. But not 1 animal of the Israelites died.

**7** Pharaoh sent men to look. Not 1 animal of the Israelites was dead. But Pharaoh stayed stubborn. He did not let the people go.

**8** Then the LORD said to Moses and Aaron, "Take handfuls of ashes from a furnace. Moses must throw the ashes into the air in front of Pharaoh.

**9** The ashes will become fine dust over all the land of Egypt. The dust will cause painful sores on the people and on the animals in all the land of Egypt."

**10** Moses and Aaron took ashes from a furnace and stood in front of Pharaoh. Moses threw the ashes into the air. Painful sores came on the people and on the animals.

**11** The magicians could not stand in front of Moses because of the sores. The sores were on the magicians and on all the Egyptians.

**12** But the LORD made Pharaoh stubborn. Pharaoh did not listen to Moses and Aaron, as the LORD told Moses.

**13** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Get up early in the morning and stand in front of Pharaoh. Tell him, 'The LORD, the God of the Hebrews, says this: Let my people go, so that they can worship me.

**14** This time I will send all my plagues against you, your officials, and your people. Then you will know that there is no god like me in all the earth.

**15** I could use my power now and attack you and your people with disease. Then you would be gone from the earth.

**16** But I let you live for this reason: to show you my power. I want all people on the earth to know my name.

**17** You still act proudly against my people. You do not let them go.

**18** Thus tomorrow at this time I will send very heavy hail. There was never hail like this in Egypt from the start of the nation until now.

**19** Thus send an order now. Bring your animals and all that you have in the fields into a shelter. The hail will fall on every person and animal that stays in the field. They will die.'"

**20** Some officials of Pharaoh had respect for the word of the LORD. They quickly brought their slaves and their animals into shelters.

**21** But other officials did not pay attention to the word of the LORD. They left their slaves and their animals in the fields.

**22** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Hold your hand up toward the sky. Then hail will fall on all the land of Egypt. It will fall on the people, on the animals, and on all the plants in the fields of Egypt."

**23** Moses held his staff up toward the sky. The LORD sent thunder and hail. Lightning hit the ground. The LORD caused hail to fall on the land of Egypt.

**24** The hail was very heavy, and lightning flashed among the hail. There was never a storm like this in all of Egypt since it became a nation.

**25** In all the land of Egypt, the hail hit all the people and animals in the fields. The hail destroyed all the plants in the fields. It broke all the trees.

**26** Only in the land of Goshen, where the Israelites lived, was there no hail.

**27** Then Pharaoh sent for Moses and Aaron. He said to them, "This time I sinned. The LORD is right, and I and my people are wrong.

**28** Pray to the LORD. We had enough of this thunder and hail. I will let you go. You do not have to stay here longer."

**29** Moses said to Pharaoh, "When I go out of the city, I will lift up my hands in prayer to the LORD. The thunder will stop, and there will be no more hail. Then you will know that the earth belongs to the LORD.

**30** But I know that you and your officials still do not have respect for the LORD God."

**31** The hail destroyed the flax and the barley. The barley had heads of grain, and the flax had flowers.

**32** But the hail did not destroy the wheat and the spelt, because they grow later.

**33** Moses left Pharaoh and went out of the city. He lifted up his hands to the LORD. The thunder and the hail stopped. The rain stopped falling on the land.

**34** Pharaoh saw that the rain, the hail, and the thunder stopped. Then he sinned again. Pharaoh and his officials became stubborn again.

**35** Pharaoh stayed stubborn. He did not let the Israelites go, as the LORD said through Moses.

## Chapter 10

**1** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Go to Pharaoh. I made Pharaoh and his officials stubborn. I did this so that I can show these miraculous signs among them.

**2** I also did this so that you can tell your children and your grandchildren about my actions in Egypt. Tell them about the signs that I did among the Egyptians. Then you will all know that I am the LORD."

**3** Moses and Aaron went to Pharaoh. They said to him, "The LORD, the God of the Hebrews, says this: 'How long will you refuse to be humble in front of me? Let my people go, so that they can worship me.

**4** If you refuse to let my people go, tomorrow I will bring locusts into your country.

**5** The locusts will cover the ground. No person will be able to see the ground. They will eat the few plants that the hail did not destroy. They will eat all the trees that grow in your fields.

**6** They will fill your houses, the houses of your officials, and the houses of all the Egyptians. Your fathers and your grandfathers never saw so many locusts. They did not see this from the time that they lived on the earth until today.'" Then Moses turned and went out from Pharaoh.

**7** The officials of Pharaoh said to him, "How long will this man be a danger to us? Let the men go, so that they can worship the LORD their God. Do you not know that Egypt is destroyed?"

**8** Then the officials brought Moses and Aaron back to Pharaoh. Pharaoh said to them, "Go and worship the LORD your God. But tell me, who will go?"

**9** Moses said, "We will go with our young people and our old people. We will go with our sons and our daughters, and with our flocks and our herds. We must hold a festival for the LORD."

**10** Pharaoh said to them, "I will never let you go with your children. The LORD will need to be with you for that! Look, you plan to do evil.

**11** No! Only the men can go and worship the LORD. That is what you asked for." Then Pharaoh's officials pushed Moses and Aaron out from Pharaoh.

**12** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Hold your hand out over the land of Egypt. Then locusts will come on the land of Egypt. They will eat all the plants in the land that the hail did not destroy."

**13** Moses held his staff out over the land of Egypt. Then the LORD caused an east wind to blow over the land all that day and all that night. In the morning, the east wind brought the locusts.

**14** The locusts came on all the land of Egypt. They landed on all parts of Egypt. There were very many locusts. There were never so many locusts before, and there will never be so many again.

**15** The locusts covered the ground until the land was dark. They ate all the plants in the land and all the fruit on the trees that the hail did not destroy. No green thing stayed on the trees or on the plants in all the land of Egypt.

**16** Then Pharaoh quickly called Moses and Aaron. He said, "I sinned against the LORD your God and against you.

**17** Now please forgive my sin only this 1 time. Pray to the LORD your God. Ask him to take away this deadly plague from me."

**18** Moses went out from Pharaoh and prayed to the LORD.

**19** The LORD changed the wind to a very strong west wind. The wind took away the locusts and blew them into the Red Sea. Not 1 locust stayed in all of Egypt.

**20** But the LORD made Pharaoh stubborn. Pharaoh did not let the Israelites go.

**21** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Hold your hand up toward the sky. Then darkness will come over the land of Egypt. The darkness will be so thick that people can feel it."

**22** Moses held his hand up toward the sky. Then there was total darkness in all the land of Egypt for 3 days.

**23** The people could not see each other. No person went out of his house for 3 days. But all the Israelites had light in the places where they lived.

**24** Then Pharaoh called Moses and said, "Go and worship the LORD. Your children can also go with you. But your flocks and your herds must stay here."

**25** But Moses said, "You must also let us take animals for sacrifices and burnt offerings. We must offer them to the LORD our God.

**26** Our animals must also go with us. Not 1 animal can stay here. We must use some of them to worship the LORD our God. We will not know which animals to use until we arrive there."

**27** But the LORD made Pharaoh stubborn. Pharaoh did not let them go.

**28** Pharaoh said to Moses, "Go away from me! Be careful! Do not come to see me again. On the day that you see my face, you will die."

**29** Moses said, "You are correct. I will not see your face again."

**1** The LORD said to Moses, "I will send 1 more plague on Pharaoh and on Egypt. After that, Pharaoh will let you go from here. When he lets you go, he will push all of you out.

**2** Speak to the people. Tell each man and each woman to ask their neighbors for silver and gold jewelry."

**3** The LORD caused the Egyptians to be kind to the people. Also, the officials of Pharaoh and the people of Egypt thought that Moses was a very important man.

**4** Moses said to Pharaoh, "The LORD says this: 'At about midnight I will go out through Egypt.

## Chapter 12

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron in the land of Egypt. He said,

**2** "This month will be the first month of the year for you.

**3** Tell all the community of Israel this: On the 10th day of this month, each man must take a lamb for his family. He must take 1 lamb for each household.

**4** If a household is too small for a whole lamb, the family must share a lamb with their nearest neighbor. Divide the lamb by the number of people. Calculate how much each person will eat.

**5** The animal must be a male, 1 year old, with no defect. You can take a sheep or a goat.

**6** Keep the animal until the 14th day of this month. Then all the community of Israel must kill their animals at twilight.

**7** Take some of the blood. Put the blood on the 2 side posts and the top of the door frame of each house where people eat the lamb.

**8** On that night, eat the meat. Cook the meat over a fire. Eat it with bread made without yeast and with bitter herbs.

**9** Do not eat the meat raw or boiled in water. Cook the whole animal over a fire, with the head, the legs, and the inner parts.

**10** Do not keep any of the meat until morning. Burn in the fire any meat that stays until morning.

**11** This is how you must eat the meal. Wear your belt, put on your sandals, and hold your staff in your hand. Eat the meal quickly. This is the Passover of the LORD.

**12** On that night I will go through the land of Egypt. I will kill every firstborn son and every firstborn animal in Egypt. I will punish all the gods of Egypt. I am the LORD.

**13** The blood on your houses will be a sign for you. When I see the blood, I will pass over you. When I attack Egypt, the plague will not touch you or kill you.

**14** You must always remember this day. Hold this day as a festival to the LORD in all future generations. This is a permanent law.

**15** For 7 days you must eat bread made without yeast. On the first day, remove all yeast from your houses. If a person eats bread made with yeast during these 7 days, remove that person from Israel.

**16** On the first day and on the seventh day, hold a holy meeting. Do no work on these days. You can only prepare food for each person to eat.

**17** Hold the Festival of Bread Without Yeast. On this day I brought your groups out of the land of Egypt. Thus hold this day in all future generations. This is a permanent law.

**18** In the first month, eat bread made without yeast. Start on the evening of the 14th day. Stop on the evening of the 21st day.

**19** For 7 days, there must be no yeast in your houses. If a person eats food made with yeast, remove that person from the community of Israel. This law is for foreigners and for people born in the land.

**20** Do not eat food made with yeast. In all the places where you live, eat bread made without yeast."

**21** Then Moses called all the leaders of Israel. He said to them, "Go and choose a lamb for each of your families. Kill the Passover lamb.

**22** Take a bunch of hyssop and put it into the blood in the bowl. Put some of the blood on the top and on the 2 side posts of the door frame. Do not go out of your house until morning.

**23** The LORD will go through the land to kill the Egyptians. He will see the blood on the top and on the 2 side posts of the door frame. Then the LORD will pass over that door. He will not let the destroyer come into your houses to kill you.

**24** Obey these instructions. This is a permanent law for you and your descendants.

**25** When you go into the land that the LORD will give you, as he promised, continue this ceremony.

**26** Your children will ask you, 'What is the meaning of this ceremony?'

**27** Then tell them, 'This is the Passover sacrifice to the LORD. He passed over the houses of the Israelites in Egypt. He killed the Egyptians, but he saved our families.'" Then the people bowed down and worshipped.

**28** The Israelites went and did all that the LORD told Moses and Aaron.

**29** At midnight, the LORD killed all the firstborn sons in the land of Egypt. He killed the firstborn son of Pharaoh, who sat on his throne. He killed the firstborn son of the prisoner in the prison. He also killed all the firstborn animals.

**30** Pharaoh, all his officials, and all the Egyptians got up in the night. There was loud crying in Egypt. In each house, a person was dead.

**31** During the night, Pharaoh called Moses and Aaron. He said, "Get up! Go away from my people, you and the Israelites! Go and worship the LORD, as you asked.

**32** Take your flocks and your herds, as you asked, and go. Also ask God to bless me."

**33** The Egyptians told the people to go out of the land quickly. The Egyptians said, "If you do not go, we will all die!"

**34** Thus the people took their dough before they added yeast to it. They put their bowls for dough in their clothes and carried them on their shoulders.

**35** The Israelites did what Moses told them. They asked the Egyptians for silver and gold jewelry and for clothing.

**36** The LORD caused the Egyptians to be kind to the people. Thus the Egyptians gave them what they asked for. In this way the Israelites took the riches of the Egyptians.

**37** The Israelites went from Rameses to Succoth. There were about 600,000 men on foot, and also women and children.

**38** Many other people went with them. They also took very many animals, flocks and herds.

**39** The people baked flat bread with the dough that they brought out of Egypt. The dough did not have yeast. The Egyptians pushed them out of Egypt, and they could not wait. They did not prepare food for the journey.

**40** The Israelites lived in Egypt for 430 years.

**41** At the end of the 430 years, on that same day, all the groups of the LORD's people went out of the land of Egypt.

**42** On that night the LORD kept watch to bring them out of the land of Egypt. Thus on this night all the Israelites must keep watch to honor the LORD. They must do this in all future generations.

**43** The LORD said to Moses and Aaron, "These are the rules for the Passover. No foreigner can eat the Passover meal.

**44** But a slave that you bought with money can eat it after you circumcise him.

**45** A foreigner who lives with you for a short time or a hired worker must not eat it.

**46** Eat the meal in 1 house. Do not take any of the meat out of the house. Do not break any of the bones of the animal.

**47** All the community of Israel must hold the Passover.

**48** A foreigner who lives among you can want to hold the Passover of the LORD. Then you must circumcise all the males in his household. After that, he can come and hold the Passover. He will be the same as a person born in the land. But no uncircumcised male can eat the Passover meal.

**49** The same law is for people born in the land and for foreigners who live among you."

**50** All the Israelites did all that the LORD told Moses and Aaron.

**51** On that same day, the LORD brought the Israelites out of the land of Egypt, group by group.

## Chapter 13

**3** Moses said to the people, "Remember this day. On this day you came out of Egypt, where you were slaves. The LORD used his great power to bring you out of this place. Do not eat bread made with yeast.

**4** Today, in the month of Abib, you go out of Egypt.

**5** The LORD will bring you into the land of the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Amorites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites. He promised your ancestors that he will give you this land. That land has much milk and honey. There you must hold this ceremony in this month each year.

**6** For 7 days eat bread made without yeast. On the seventh day, hold a festival to the LORD.

**7** Eat bread made without yeast for 7 days. No bread made with yeast must be among you. No yeast must be in all your land.

**8** On that day, tell your son, 'I do this because of what the LORD did for me when I came out of Egypt.'

**9** This festival will be like a sign on your hand and like a reminder on your forehead. It will help you to speak the law of the LORD. The LORD used his great power to bring you out of Egypt.

**10** Thus obey this law at the correct time each year.

**11** The LORD will bring you into the land of the Canaanites and give it to you. He promised this to you and to your ancestors.

**12** Then you must give to the LORD every firstborn son. Also, every firstborn male of your animals belongs to the LORD.

**13** Buy back every firstborn donkey with a lamb. If you do not buy it back, break its neck. Also buy back every firstborn son among your children.

**14** In the future, your son will ask you, 'What does this mean?' Then tell him, 'The LORD used his great power to bring us out of Egypt, where we were slaves.

**16** This will be like a sign on your hand and like a mark on your forehead. It shows that the LORD used his great power to bring us out of Egypt."

**19** Moses took the bones of Joseph with him. Joseph made the Israelites give a solemn promise. Joseph told them, "God will surely come to help you. Then you must carry my bones with you out of this place."

**20** The Israelites went from Succoth and made camp at Etham, at the edge of the desert.

**21** The LORD went in front of them. In the day, he was in a column of cloud to show them the way. In the night, he was in a column of fire to give them light. Thus they could travel in the day and in the night.

**22** The column of cloud in the day and the column of fire in the night always stayed in front of the people.

## Chapter 14

**1** Then the LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**2** "Tell the Israelites to turn back and make camp near Pi Hahiroth. This place is between Migdol and the sea, opposite Baal Zephon. Make camp near the sea, opposite Baal Zephon.

**3** Pharaoh will think, 'The Israelites are lost in the land. The desert keeps them in.'

**4** I will make Pharaoh stubborn, and he will chase the Israelites. But I will defeat Pharaoh and all his army. Then people will honor me. The Egyptians will know that I am the LORD." The Israelites did as the LORD told them.

**5** A person told the king of Egypt that the people ran away. Then Pharaoh and his officials changed their minds about the people. They said, "Why did we do this? We let the Israelites go, and now they will not work for us."

**6** Thus Pharaoh made his chariot ready and took his army with him.

**7** He took 600 of his best chariots and all the other chariots of Egypt. An officer was on each chariot.

**8** The LORD made Pharaoh, king of Egypt, stubborn. Pharaoh chased the Israelites. The Israelites went out of Egypt boldly.

**9** The Egyptians chased the Israelites with all the horses and chariots of Pharaoh, his horsemen, and his army. The Egyptians came near the Israelites where they camped by the sea, near Pi Hahiroth, opposite Baal Zephon.

**10** Pharaoh came near. The Israelites looked up and saw the Egyptians behind them. The Israelites were very afraid. They cried out to the LORD.

**11** They said to Moses, "Were there no graves in Egypt? Why did you bring us out here to die in the desert? Why did you bring us out of Egypt?

**12** In Egypt we told you, 'Do not disturb us. Let us work for the Egyptians.' It is better for us to work for the Egyptians than to die in the desert!"

**13** Moses said to the people, "Do not be afraid. Stand firm. Then you will see how the LORD will save you today. You see the Egyptians today, but you will never see them again.

**15** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Why do you cry out to me? Tell the Israelites to go forward.

**16** Lift up your staff and hold your hand out over the sea. Divide the water. Then the Israelites will go through the middle of the sea on dry ground.

**17** I will make the Egyptians stubborn, and they will follow the Israelites into the sea. Then I will defeat Pharaoh, all his army, his chariots, and his horsemen. People will honor me because of this.

**18** When I defeat Pharaoh, his chariots, and his horsemen, the Egyptians will know that I am the LORD."

**21** Moses held his hand out over the sea. All that night the LORD pushed the sea back with a strong east wind. He made the sea into dry land. The water divided.

**22** The Israelites went through the middle of the sea on dry ground. The water was like a wall on their right side and on their left side.

**23** The Egyptians chased them. All the horses, chariots, and horsemen of Pharaoh followed them into the middle of the sea.

**24** Just before sunrise, the LORD looked down on the Egyptian army from the column of fire and cloud. He caused confusion in the Egyptian army.

**25** He caused the wheels of their chariots to come off. Thus the Egyptians could drive only with difficulty. The Egyptians said, "Let us run away from the Israelites! The LORD fights for them against Egypt."

**26** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Hold your hand out over the sea. Then the water will come back over the Egyptians, their chariots, and their horsemen."

**27** Moses held his hand out over the sea. At sunrise, the sea went back to its usual place. The Egyptians tried to run away from the water. But the LORD threw the Egyptians into the middle of the sea.

**28** The water came back and covered the chariots and the horsemen. It covered all the army of Pharaoh that followed the Israelites into the sea. Not 1 of the Egyptians lived.

**29** But the Israelites walked through the middle of the sea on dry ground. The water was like a wall on their right side and on their left side.

**30** On that day the LORD saved Israel from the Egyptians. The Israelites saw the dead Egyptians on the shore of the sea.

**31** The Israelites saw the great power that the LORD used against the Egyptians. Thus the people had respect for the LORD. They believed in the LORD and in his servant Moses.

**10** But you blew with your wind, and the sea covered them. They sank like lead in the deep water.

**27** Then they came to Elim. There were 12 springs of water and 70 palm trees at Elim. They made camp there near the water.

## Chapter 16

**1** All the community of Israel went from Elim. They came to the desert of Sin, between Elim and Sinai. They arrived there on the 15th day of the second month after they left Egypt.

**2** In the desert, all the community of Israel complained against Moses and Aaron.

**3** The Israelites said to them, "We wanted the LORD to kill us in Egypt! There we sat near pots of meat. There we ate all the bread that we wanted. But you brought us into this desert to kill all of us with hunger."

**4** Then the LORD said to Moses, "I will cause bread to fall from the sky for you like rain. Each day the people must go out and gather enough for that day. In this way I will test them. I will see if they obey my instructions.

**5** On the sixth day, they must prepare what they bring in. On that day they will have 2 times as much as on the other days."

**6** Moses and Aaron said to all the Israelites, "This evening you will know that the LORD brought you out of Egypt.

**7** In the morning you will see the glory of the LORD. He heard your complaints against him. We are not important. Why do you complain against us?"

**8** Moses also said, "The LORD will give you meat to eat in the evening. He will give you all the bread that you want in the morning. He heard your complaints against him. We are not important. You do not complain against us. You complain against the LORD."

**9** Moses said to Aaron, "Tell all the community of Israel, 'Come near to the LORD. He heard your complaints.'"

**10** Aaron spoke to all the community of Israel. While he spoke, they looked toward the desert. There they saw the glory of the LORD in the cloud.

**11** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**12** "I heard the complaints of the Israelites. Tell them, 'At twilight you will eat meat. In the morning you will have all the bread that you want. Then you will know that I am the LORD your God.'"

**13** That evening, quails came and covered the camp. In the morning, there was dew around the camp.

**14** When the dew went away, there were thin flakes on the ground of the desert. The flakes were small like frost on the ground.

**15** The Israelites saw the flakes. They did not know what the flakes were. Thus they said to each other, "What is it?" Moses said to them, "This is the bread that the LORD gives you to eat.

**16** The LORD gave this command: 'Each person must gather as much as he needs. Take 1 omer for each person in your tent.'"

**17** The Israelites did this. Some people gathered more, and some people gathered less.

**18** Then they measured it with an omer. The people who gathered much did not have too much. The people who gathered little did not have too little. Each person gathered as much as he needed.

**19** Moses said to them, "No person must keep any of it until morning."

**20** But some people did not listen to Moses. They kept some of it until morning. Then worms grew in it, and it smelled bad. Moses was angry with these people.

**21** Each morning, each person gathered as much as he needed. When the sun became hot, the flakes on the ground melted.

**22** On the sixth day, they gathered 2 times as much bread, 2 omers for each person. All the leaders of the community came and told Moses about this.

**23** Moses said to them, "The LORD told me this: 'Tomorrow is a day of rest, a holy Sabbath to the LORD. Bake what you want to bake today. Boil what you want to boil. Keep the rest until morning.'"

**24** Thus they kept it until morning, as Moses told them. It did not smell bad, and there were no worms in it.

**25** Moses said, "Eat it today, because today is a Sabbath to the LORD. Today you will not find any in the field.

**26** Gather it for 6 days. But on the seventh day, the Sabbath, there will be none."

**27** On the seventh day, some of the people went out to gather it, but they found none.

**28** Then the LORD said to Moses, "How long will you refuse to obey my commands and my instructions?

**29** Look, the LORD gave you the Sabbath. Thus on the sixth day he gives you bread for 2 days. On the seventh day, each person must stay where he is. No person must go out."

**30** Thus the people rested on the seventh day.

**31** The Israelites gave the bread the name manna. It was white, like coriander seed. It tasted like thin cakes made with honey.

**32** Moses said, "The LORD gave this command: 'Keep 1 omer of manna for your future generations. Then they will see the bread that I gave you to eat in the desert. I gave it to you when I brought you out of Egypt.'"

**33** Moses said to Aaron, "Take a jar and put 1 omer of manna in it. Put the jar in front of the LORD. Keep it for your future generations."

**34** Aaron put the manna in front of the tablets of the law, to keep it. He did as the LORD told Moses.

**35** The Israelites ate manna for 40 years, until they came to a land where people lived. They ate manna until they came to the border of the land of Canaan.

**36** An omer is 1/10 of an ephah.

## Chapter 17

**1** All the community of Israel went from the desert of Sin. They traveled from place to place, as the LORD told them. They made camp at Rephidim. But there was no water for the people to drink.

**2** Thus the people argued with Moses. They said, "Give us water to drink." Moses said to them, "Why do you argue with me? Why do you test the LORD?"

**3** The people were very thirsty there. They complained against Moses. They said, "Why did you bring us out of Egypt? Do you want to kill us, our children, and our animals with thirst?"

**4** Then Moses cried out to the LORD. He said, "What must I do with these people? They are almost ready to kill me with stones."

**5** The LORD said to Moses, "Go in front of the people. Take some of the leaders of Israel with you. Take in your hand the staff that you used to hit the river Nile. Then go.

**6** I will stand in front of you there on the rock at Horeb. Hit the rock, and water will come out of it. Then the people can drink." Moses did this, and the leaders of Israel saw it.

**7** Moses gave the place the names Massah and Meribah. These names mean "test" and "argument." The Israelites argued there. They also tested the LORD. They said, "Is the LORD with us or not?"

**8** Then the Amalekites came and attacked Israel at Rephidim.

**9** Moses said to Joshua, "Choose some men for us. Go out and fight against the Amalekites. Tomorrow I will stand on the top of the hill with the staff of God in my hand."

**10** Joshua did what Moses told him. He fought against the Amalekites. Moses, Aaron, and Hur went up to the top of the hill.

**11** When Moses held up his hands, Israel won. When Moses put down his hands, the Amalekites won.

**12** The arms of Moses became tired. Thus Aaron and Hur took a stone and put it under Moses. Moses sat on the stone. Aaron and Hur held up his hands, 1 man on each side. Thus his hands stayed up until the sun went down.

**13** Joshua defeated the Amalekite army with the sword.

**14** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Write this in a book so that people will remember it. Tell Joshua about it. I will completely remove all memory of the Amalekites from the earth."

**15** Moses built an altar. He gave the altar the name "The LORD Is My Flag."

**16** Moses said, "The Amalekites lifted their hand against the throne of the LORD. Thus the LORD will fight against the Amalekites in all generations."

## Chapter 18

**1** Jethro, the priest of Midian, was the father-in-law of Moses. Jethro heard about all that God did for Moses and for his people Israel. He heard that the LORD brought Israel out of Egypt.

**2** Before this time, Moses sent his wife Zipporah back to Jethro. Then Jethro took Zipporah,

**3** and her 2 sons. The name of 1 son was Gershom. Moses said, "I was a foreigner in a foreign land."

**4** The name of the other son was Eliezer. Moses said, "The God of my father helped me. He saved me from the sword of Pharaoh."

**5** Jethro, the father-in-law of Moses, came to Moses in the desert. He brought the sons and the wife of Moses with him. Moses was in his camp near the mountain of God.

**6** Jethro sent a message to Moses. He said, "I, your father-in-law Jethro, come to you. Your wife and her 2 sons come with me."

**7** Moses went out to meet his father-in-law. Moses bowed down and kissed him. They asked about the health of each other. Then they went into the tent.

**8** Moses told his father-in-law all that the LORD did to Pharaoh and to the Egyptians to help Israel. He told him about all the troubles that they had on the journey. He told him how the LORD saved them.

**9** Jethro was glad about all the good things that the LORD did for Israel. The LORD saved them from the Egyptians.

**10** Jethro said, "Praise the LORD! He saved you from the Egyptians and from Pharaoh. He saved the people from the power of the Egyptians.

**11** Now I know that the LORD is greater than all gods. The Egyptians were proud and cruel to Israel, but the LORD defeated them."

**12** Then Jethro, the father-in-law of Moses, brought a burnt offering and other sacrifices to God. Aaron and all the leaders of Israel came to eat a meal with Jethro in front of God.

**13** On the next day, Moses sat as judge for the people. The people stood around Moses from morning until evening.

**14** The father-in-law of Moses saw all that Moses did for the people. He said, "What do you do for the people? Why do you sit as judge alone? Why do all the people stand around you from morning until evening?"

**15** Moses said to his father-in-law, "The people come to me to learn the will of God.

**16** When they have a problem, they come to me. I make a decision between the 2 persons. I teach them the laws and the instructions of God."

**17** The father-in-law of Moses said to him, "What you do is not good.

**18** You and these people will become very tired. This work is too much for you. You cannot do it alone.

**19** Now listen to me. I will give you advice, and God will be with you. You must speak for the people to God. Bring their problems to God.

**20** Teach them the laws and the instructions. Show them how to live and what work they must do.

**21** Also choose capable men from all the people. Choose men who respect God, who are honest, and who hate bribes. Make these men leaders over the people. Some will be leaders of 1,000 people, some of 100, some of 50, and some of 10.

**22** Let these men judge the people at all times. They must bring the difficult problems to you. But they can decide the small problems themselves. This will make your work easier, because they will share it with you.

**23** If you do this, and God tells you to do it, then you will not become too tired. Also, all these people will go home satisfied."

**24** Moses listened to his father-in-law and did all that he said.

**25** Moses chose capable men from all Israel. He made them leaders over the people. Some were leaders of 1,000 people, some of 100, some of 50, and some of 10.

**26** These men judged the people at all times. They brought the difficult problems to Moses. But they decided all the small problems themselves.

**27** Then Moses let his father-in-law go. Jethro went back to his own country.

## Chapter 19

**1** The Israelites came to the desert of Sinai in the third month after they left Egypt. They came there on the first day of that month.

**2** They went from Rephidim and came into the desert of Sinai. They made camp in the desert. Israel made camp there in front of the mountain.

**3** Moses went up the mountain to God. The LORD called to him from the mountain. The LORD said, "Speak to the family of Jacob. Tell the Israelites this:

**4** 'You saw what I did to the Egyptians. You saw how I carried you, as an eagle carries its young on its wings. I brought you to me.

**5** Now if you fully obey me and keep my agreement (covenant), you will be my special people. All the earth belongs to me, but you will be my own people.

**6** You will be a kingdom of priests for me and a holy nation.' Tell these words to the Israelites."

**7** Moses went back and called the leaders of the people. He told them all the words that the LORD told him.

**8** All the people answered together, "We will do all that the LORD said." Moses took the answer of the people back to the LORD.

**9** The LORD said to Moses, "I will come to you in a thick cloud. Then the people will hear me when I speak with you. Then they will always believe you." Moses told the LORD what the people said.

**10** The LORD said to Moses, "Go to the people. Today and tomorrow, prepare them to be holy. They must wash their clothes.

**11** They must be ready on the third day. On the third day, the LORD will come down on Mount Sinai. All the people will see this.

**12** Put limits around the mountain for the people. Tell them, 'Be careful. Do not go up the mountain. Do not touch the edge of the mountain. Any person who touches the mountain must be killed.

**13** No hand must touch that person. Kill that person with stones or with arrows. Kill any person or animal that touches the mountain. When the trumpet sounds a long blast, the people can come up to the mountain.'"

**14** Moses went down the mountain to the people. He prepared the people to be holy. They washed their clothes.

**15** Moses said to the people, "Be ready on the third day. Do not have sexual relations with your wives."

**16** On the morning of the third day, there was thunder and lightning. A thick cloud was on the mountain. A trumpet made a very loud sound. All the people in the camp shook with fear.

**17** Moses led the people out of the camp to meet God. They stood at the bottom of the mountain.

**18** Smoke covered all of Mount Sinai, because the LORD came down on it in fire. The smoke went up like the smoke from a furnace. The whole mountain shook violently.

**19** The sound of the trumpet became louder and louder. Moses spoke, and God answered him with thunder.

**20** The LORD came down on the top of Mount Sinai. The LORD called Moses to the top of the mountain. Moses went up.

**21** The LORD said to Moses, "Go down and warn the people. They must not push through the limits to look at the LORD. If they do, many of them will die.

**22** The priests who come near the LORD must also prepare themselves to be holy. If they do not, the LORD will attack them."

**23** Moses said to the LORD, "The people cannot come up Mount Sinai. You warned us. You said, 'Put limits around the mountain and make it holy.'"

**24** The LORD said to him, "Go down. Then come back up with Aaron. But the priests and the people must not push through to come up to the LORD. If they do, he will attack them."

**25** Thus Moses went down to the people and told them.

## Chapter 20

**1** God spoke all these words. He said,

**2** "I am the LORD your God. I brought you out of the land of Egypt, where you were slaves.

**3** Do not have any other gods except me.

**4** Do not make an idol for yourself. Do not make a statue of any thing in the sky, on the earth, or in the water under the earth.

**5** Do not bow down to idols or worship them. I, the LORD your God, am a jealous God. I punish the children for the sin of their fathers who hate me. I punish the children to the third and fourth generation.

**6** But I show my faithful love to 1,000 generations of the people who love me and obey my commands.

**7** Do not use the name of the LORD your God for a wrong purpose. The LORD will punish any person who uses his name for a wrong purpose.

**8** Remember the Sabbath day and keep it holy.

**9** Work for 6 days and do all your work.

**10** But the seventh day is a Sabbath to the LORD your God. On that day do not do any work. This rule is for you, your son, your daughter, your male servant, and your female servant. It is also for your animals and for the foreigner who lives in your towns.

**11** In 6 days the LORD made the sky, the earth, the sea, and all things in them. Then he rested on the seventh day. Thus the LORD blessed the Sabbath day and made it holy.

**12** Honor your father and your mother. Then you will live a long time in the land that the LORD your God gives you.

**13** Do not murder.

**14** Do not commit adultery.

**15** Do not steal.

**16** Do not tell lies about your neighbor in court.

**17** Do not want to take the house of your neighbor. Do not want to take the wife of your neighbor, his male servant, his female servant, his ox, or his donkey. Do not want to take any thing that belongs to your neighbor."

**18** All the people heard the thunder and the sound of the trumpet. They saw the lightning and the smoke on the mountain. They shook with fear and stood far away.

**19** They said to Moses, "You speak to us, and we will listen. But do not let God speak to us. If he does, we will die."

**20** Moses said to the people, "Do not be afraid. God came to test you. He wants you to have respect for him, so that you do not sin."

**21** The people stood far away. But Moses went near the thick darkness where God was.

**22** The LORD said to Moses, "Tell the Israelites this: 'You saw that I spoke to you from heaven.

**23** Do not make gods of silver or gold for yourselves. Do not worship them with me.

**24** Make an altar of earth for me. On that altar, offer your burnt offerings and your peace offerings, your sheep and your cattle. In each place where I cause people to honor my name, I will come to you and bless you.

**25** If you make an altar of stones for me, do not use cut stones. If you use a tool on the stones, you will make the altar unclean.

**26** Do not make steps up to my altar. If you use steps, people will see your naked body under your clothes.'"

## Chapter 21

**1** These are the laws that you must give to the Israelites.

**2** If you buy a Hebrew slave, he must work for you for 6 years. In the seventh year, he can go free. He does not have to pay you.

**3** If he came alone, he must go free alone. If he had a wife when he came, his wife can go free with him.

**4** If his master gave him a wife, and she gave birth to sons or daughters, the wife and her children belong to the master. Only the man can go free.

**5** But the slave can say clearly, 'I love my master, my wife, and my children. I do not want to go free.'

**6** Then his master must bring him to God. The master must bring him to the door or to the door post. The master must make a hole in the ear of the slave with an awl. Then the slave will serve his master for all his life.

**7** If a man sells his daughter as a slave, she will not go free as the male slaves do.

**8** Her master can choose her to be his wife. But if he is not pleased with her, he must let her family buy her back. He cannot sell her to foreigners, because he did not keep his promise to her.

**9** If the master chooses her to be the wife of his son, he must treat her as a daughter.

**10** If the master marries another woman, he must continue to give the first wife food, clothing, and marriage rights.

**11** If he does not give her these 3 things, she can go free. She does not have to pay money.

**12** If a person hits a man and kills him, you must kill that person.

**13** But possibly the person did not plan to kill. Possibly God let it happen. Then I will choose a place where that person can go to be safe.

**14** But a person can kill his neighbor with a plan and on purpose. Then take that person away, even from my altar, and kill him.

**15** If a person hits his father or his mother, you must kill that person.

**16** If a person kidnaps another person, you must kill the kidnapper. This is true if he sold the person or if he still has the person.

**17** If a person curses his father or his mother, you must kill that person.

**18** Men can fight, and 1 man can hit the other with a stone or with his fist. The injured man possibly does not die but must stay in bed.

**19** Then later the injured man gets up and walks outside with a stick. Then the man who hit him will not be punished. But he must pay the injured man for the time that he lost. He must also make sure that the injured man becomes fully healthy.

**20** If a man hits his male or female slave with a stick, and the slave dies, the man must be punished.

**21** But if the slave lives for 1 or 2 days, do not punish the man. The slave is his property.

**22** Men can fight and hit a pregnant woman, and she gives birth early. If there is no serious injury, the man who hit her must pay money. He must pay what the husband of the woman asks, and what the judges approve.

**23** But if there is a serious injury, the punishment must be a life for a life.

**24** It must be an eye for an eye, a tooth for a tooth, a hand for a hand, and a foot for a foot.

**25** It must be a burn for a burn, a wound for a wound, and a bruise for a bruise.

**26** If a man hits his male or female slave in the eye and destroys the eye, he must let the slave go free. This is the payment for the eye.

**27** If he knocks out a tooth of his male or female slave, he must let the slave go free. This is the payment for the tooth.

**28** If an ox kills a man or a woman with its horns, kill the ox with stones. Do not eat its meat. But do not punish the owner of the ox.

**29** But possibly the ox attacked people with its horns in the past. People warned the owner, but he did not keep the ox in a pen. Then if the ox kills a man or a woman, kill the ox with stones. Also kill the owner.

**30** But the judges can tell the owner to pay money for his life. Then he must pay all that they tell him.

**31** The same law applies if the ox kills a son or a daughter.

**32** If the ox kills a male or female slave, the owner must pay 30 shekels of silver to the master of the slave. Also kill the ox with stones.

**33** A man can open a pit or dig a pit and not cover it. Then an ox or a donkey can fall into the pit.

**34** The owner of the pit must pay for the loss. He must pay money to the owner of the animal. The dead animal will belong to the owner of the pit.

**35** If the ox of a man hurts the ox of another man, and the hurt ox dies, the 2 men must sell the live ox. They must divide the money. They must also divide the dead ox.

**36** But possibly people knew that the ox attacked with its horns in the past, and the owner did not keep the ox in a pen. Then the owner must pay an ox for the dead ox. The dead ox will belong to him.

## Chapter 22

**1** If a man steals an ox or a sheep and kills it or sells it, he must pay back 5 oxen for the ox. He must pay back 4 sheep for the sheep.

**2** If a person finds a thief who breaks into a house at night, and hits him and kills him, the person is not guilty of murder.

**3** But if this occurs after sunrise, the person is guilty of murder. A thief must pay back all that he stole. If he has nothing, sell him as a slave to pay for what he stole.

**4** Possibly a person finds the stolen animal alive in the possession of the thief. The animal can be an ox, a donkey, or a sheep. Then the thief must pay back 2 times its value.

**5** A man can let his animals eat in a field or a vineyard, and the animals go into the field of another man and eat there. Then the man must pay with the best crops from his own field and vineyard.

**6** A fire can start and spread into bushes with thorns. Then the fire can burn the stacks of grain, the grain in the field, or the whole field. The person who started the fire must pay for the loss.

**7** A man can give money or goods to his neighbor to keep safe. Then a thief can steal them from the house of the neighbor. If people find the thief, the thief must pay back 2 times the value.

**8** If people do not find the thief, the owner of the house must go to the judges. The judges will decide if he stole the goods of his neighbor.

**9** People can argue about property, for example an ox, a donkey, a sheep, clothing, or any lost thing. Each person can say, 'This is mine.' Then the 2 persons must bring their case to the judges. The person whom the judges find guilty must pay back 2 times the value to the other person.

**10** A man can give a donkey, an ox, a sheep, or another animal to his neighbor to keep. Then the animal can die, get hurt, or be stolen, and no person sees it.

**11** Then the neighbor must make a solemn promise in the name of the LORD. He must promise that he did not take the property of the other man. The owner must accept this promise. The neighbor does not have to pay for the animal.

**12** But if a thief stole the animal from the neighbor, the neighbor must pay the owner.

**13** If a wild animal killed the animal, the neighbor must bring the remains as proof. He does not have to pay for the dead animal.

**14** A man can borrow an animal from his neighbor. Then the animal can get hurt or die when the owner is not there. The man who borrowed the animal must pay for it.

**15** But if the owner was there, the man does not have to pay. If the man rented the animal, the rent pays for the loss.

**16** A man can persuade a virgin who is not engaged to have sexual relations with him. Then he must pay the bride price for her and marry her.

**17** If her father refuses to give her to the man, the man must still pay money. He must pay the usual bride price for a virgin.

**18** Do not let a witch live.

**19** If a person has sexual relations with an animal, you must kill that person.

**20** If a person offers sacrifices to any god other than the LORD, you must destroy that person.

**21** Do not be cruel to a foreigner or oppress him. Remember that you were foreigners in the land of Egypt.

**22** Do not be cruel to any widow or to any child without a father.

**23** If you are cruel to them, and they cry out to me, I will surely hear their cry.

**24** I will become very angry. I will kill you with the sword. Then your wives will become widows, and your children will have no fathers.

**25** If you lend money to a poor person among my people, do not act like a moneylender. Do not make him pay interest.

**26** If you take the coat of your neighbor as a guarantee for a loan, give the coat back to him before sunset.

**27** That coat is the only cover that he has for his body. He needs it to sleep. If he cries out to me, I will hear him, because I am kind.

**28** Do not speak against God. Do not curse a ruler of your people.

**29** Do not wait to give me the first part of your crops and of your wine and oil. Give me your firstborn sons.

**30** Do the same with your cattle and your sheep. Let the firstborn animal stay with its mother for 7 days. On the eighth day, give it to me.

**31** You must be my holy people. Do not eat the meat of an animal that wild animals killed in the field. Throw that meat to the dogs.

## Chapter 23

**1** Do not spread false reports. Do not help an evil person by a false statement in court.

**2** Do not follow a crowd to do evil. When you speak in court, do not agree with the crowd to change the correct decision.

**3** Do not give special help to a poor man in his case in court.

**4** If you find the ox or the donkey of your enemy and it is lost, take it back to him.

**5** You can see the donkey of a person who hates you, and the donkey fell under its load. Do not leave it there. Help the owner with the donkey.

**6** Do not refuse justice to the poor people among you in their cases in court.

**7** Do not make false accusations. Do not kill innocent or honest people. I will not say that a guilty person is innocent.

**8** Do not accept a bribe. A bribe makes people blind to the truth. It changes the words of honest people.

**9** Do not oppress a foreigner. You know how a foreigner feels, because you were foreigners in the land of Egypt.

**10** For 6 years plant seeds in your land and gather the crops.

**11** But in the seventh year, let the land rest. Do not plant or gather crops. Then the poor people among you can get food from the land. The wild animals can eat what the poor people leave. Do the same with your vineyards and your olive trees.

**12** Do your work for 6 days. On the seventh day, rest. Then your ox and your donkey can rest. The son of your female slave and the foreigner can also rest.

**13** Be careful to do all that I told you. Do not pray to other gods. Do not even speak their names.

**14** Each year, hold 3 festivals for me.

**15** Hold the Festival of Bread Without Yeast. For 7 days eat bread made without yeast, as I told you. Do this at the correct time in the month of Abib. In that month you came out of Egypt. No person must come to me with nothing to give.

**16** Hold the Festival of Harvest. At this festival, give me the first part of the crops that you planted in your fields. Also hold the Festival of Gathering at the end of the year. At that time you gather your crops from the fields.

**17** 3 times each year, all your men must come to worship the Lord GOD.

**18** Do not offer the blood of a sacrifice to me with bread made with yeast. Do not keep the fat of my festival sacrifice until morning.

**19** Bring the best of the first crops of your land to the house of the LORD your God. Do not cook a young goat in the milk of its mother.

**20** Look, I will send an angel in front of you. He will keep you safe on the journey. He will bring you to the place that I prepared.

**21** Pay attention to him and obey him. Do not disobey him. He will not forgive your sins, because my name is in him.

**22** If you carefully obey him and do all that I say, I will be an enemy to your enemies. I will fight against all who fight against you.

**23** My angel will go in front of you. He will bring you into the land of the Amorites, the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Canaanites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites. I will destroy these nations.

**24** Do not bow down to their gods or worship them. Do not do what those nations do. You must destroy their gods. Break their sacred stone pillars into pieces.

**25** Worship the LORD your God. Then he will bless your food and your water. I will take disease away from among you.

**26** In your land, no woman will lose her baby before birth. No woman will be unable to have children. I will give you a full, long life.

**27** I will cause terror of me to go in front of you. I will cause confusion among all the nations that you meet. I will cause all your enemies to turn and run away from you.

**28** I will send hornets in front of you. The hornets will push the Hivites, the Canaanites, and the Hittites out of your way.

**29** But I will not push them out in 1 year. If I did, the land would become empty. Then the wild animals would become too many for you.

**30** I will push them out a little at a time. I will do this until your number increases and you can take the land.

**31** I will set the borders of your land. Your land will go from the Red Sea to the Mediterranean Sea, and from the desert to the river Euphrates. I will give you power over the people who live in the land. You will push them out.

**32** Do not make an agreement with these people or with their gods.

**33** Do not let them live in your land. If you do, they will cause you to sin against me. If you worship their gods, that will be a trap for you.

## Chapter 24

**1** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Come up to the LORD. Bring Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, and 70 of the leaders of Israel. All of you must worship at a distance.

**2** Only Moses must come near to the LORD. The others must not come near. The people must not come up with him."

**3** Moses went and told the people all the words and all the laws of the LORD. All the people answered together, "We will do all the things that the LORD said."

**4** Then Moses wrote all the words of the LORD. Early in the morning, he got up and built an altar at the bottom of the mountain. He also put up 12 stone pillars, 1 for each of the 12 tribes of Israel.

**5** Moses sent young Israelite men to offer burnt offerings. They also killed bulls as peace offerings to the LORD.

**6** Moses put half of the blood in bowls. He threw the other half of the blood on the altar.

**7** Then Moses took the Book of the Covenant and read it to the people. They said, "We will do all that the LORD said. We will obey."

**8** Then Moses took the blood and threw it on the people. He said, "This is the blood of the covenant. The LORD made this covenant with you when he gave you all these words."

**9** Moses, Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, and 70 of the leaders of Israel went up the mountain.

**10** They saw the God of Israel. Under his feet was a surface like sapphire stone. It was clear and blue like the sky.

**11** God did not hurt these leaders of the Israelites. They saw God, and they ate and drank.

**12** The LORD said to Moses, "Come up the mountain to me and stay there. I will give you the tablets of stone with the law and the commands. I wrote them to teach the people."

**13** Thus Moses and his assistant Joshua got up. Moses went up the mountain of God.

**14** Moses said to the leaders, "Wait here for us until we come back to you. Aaron and Hur are with you. If a person has a problem, he can go to them."

**15** Moses went up the mountain, and a cloud covered the mountain.

**16** The glory of the LORD stayed on Mount Sinai. The cloud covered the mountain for 6 days. On the seventh day, the LORD called to Moses from the middle of the cloud.

**17** The Israelites saw the glory of the LORD on the top of the mountain. It looked like a fire that burned everything.

**18** Moses went into the middle of the cloud and went up the mountain. Moses stayed on the mountain for 40 days and 40 nights.

## Chapter 25

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**2** "Tell the Israelites to bring me an offering. Accept the offering from each person who wants to give.

**3** These are the offerings that you must accept from them: gold, silver, and bronze,

## Chapter 29

**1** Do these things to make Aaron and his sons holy, so that they can serve me as priests. Take 1 young bull and 2 rams with no defects.

**2** Use fine wheat flour without yeast to make bread, thick loaves mixed with olive oil, and thin wafers with olive oil on them.

**3** Put the bread, the loaves, and the wafers in 1 basket. Bring them in the basket with the bull and the 2 rams.

**4** Bring Aaron and his sons to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. Wash them with water.

**5** Take the clothes and put them on Aaron. Put on him the tunic, the robe of the ephod, the ephod, and the breastpiece. Tie the woven waistband of the ephod around him.

**6** Put the turban on his head. Attach the holy crown to the turban.

**7** Then take the anointing oil. Pour the oil on his head and anoint him.

**8** Bring his sons and put tunics on them.

**9** Tie sashes around Aaron and his sons. Put caps on the sons. The work of priest will belong to them as a permanent law. In this way, ordain Aaron and his sons.

**10** Bring the bull to the front of the Tent of Meeting. Aaron and his sons must put their hands on the head of the bull.

**11** Kill the bull in front of the LORD at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

**12** Take some of the blood of the bull. Put the blood on the horns of the altar with your finger. Pour the rest of the blood at the bottom of the altar.

**13** Take all the fat that covers the inner organs, the long lobe of the liver, the 2 kidneys, and the fat on the kidneys. Burn them on the altar.

**14** But burn the meat, the skin, and the dung of the bull outside the camp. This is a sin offering.

**15** Take 1 of the rams. Aaron and his sons must put their hands on the head of the ram.

**16** Kill the ram. Take its blood and throw it on all sides of the altar.

**17** Cut the ram into pieces. Wash the inner organs and the legs. Put them with the other pieces and the head.

**18** Burn the whole ram on the altar. It is a burnt offering to the LORD. It is a sweet smell, an offering by fire to the LORD.

**19** Take the other ram. Aaron and his sons must put their hands on the head of the ram.

**20** Kill the ram and take some of its blood. Put the blood on the bottom of the right ears of Aaron and his sons. Also put the blood on the thumbs of their right hands and on the big toes of their right feet. Throw the rest of the blood on all sides of the altar.

**21** Take some of the blood on the altar and some of the anointing oil. Sprinkle them on Aaron and his clothes, and on his sons and their clothes. Then Aaron, his sons, and their clothes will be holy.

**22** Take the fat from the ram, the fat tail, the fat that covers the inner organs, the long lobe of the liver, the 2 kidneys with their fat, and the right thigh. This is the ram for the ordination.

**23** Also take 1 loaf of bread, 1 thick loaf made with oil, and 1 wafer. Take them from the basket of bread made without yeast that is in front of the LORD.

**24** Put all these things in the hands of Aaron and his sons. They must lift them up in front of the LORD as a wave offering.

**25** Then take these things from their hands. Burn them on the altar with the burnt offering. They are a sweet smell to the LORD, an offering by fire to the LORD.

**26** Take the breast of the ram for the ordination of Aaron. Lift it up in front of the LORD as a wave offering. This part will be your share.

**27** Set apart the breast and the thigh of the ram for the ordination. These are the parts that are waved and lifted up. They belong to Aaron and his sons.

**28** The Israelites must always give these parts to Aaron and his sons. This is a permanent law. When the Israelites give their peace offerings, they must give these parts as a contribution to the LORD.

**29** The holy clothes of Aaron will belong to his descendants after him. They will wear these clothes when people anoint and ordain them.

**30** The son who becomes priest after Aaron must wear these clothes for 7 days. He must wear them when he comes into the Tent of Meeting to serve in the Holy Place.

**31** Take the ram for the ordination. Boil its meat in a holy place.

**32** Aaron and his sons must eat the meat of the ram and the bread in the basket. They must eat it at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

**33** They must eat these things that made atonement for them when they were ordained and made holy. No other person can eat them, because these things are holy.

**34** If some of the meat or the bread stays until morning, burn it. Do not eat it, because it is holy.

**35** Do all these things to Aaron and his sons, as I told you. Use 7 days to ordain them.

**36** Each day, offer a bull as a sin offering to make atonement. Make the altar clean by this atonement. Anoint the altar to make it holy.

**37** For 7 days, make atonement for the altar and make it holy. Then the altar will be most holy. Any thing that touches the altar will become holy.

**38** Each day, always offer these things on the altar: 2 lambs that are 1 year old.

**39** Offer 1 lamb in the morning. Offer the other lamb at twilight.

**40** With the first lamb, offer 1/10 of an ephah of fine flour mixed with 1/4 of a hin of olive oil. Also offer 1/4 of a hin of wine as a drink offering.

**41** Offer the other lamb at twilight. Offer it with the same grain offering and drink offering as in the morning. This is a sweet smell, an offering by fire to the LORD.

**42** You and your descendants must always offer this burnt offering. Offer it in front of the LORD at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. I will meet you there and speak to you.

**43** I will meet with the Israelites there. My glory will make that place holy.

**44** I will make the Tent of Meeting and the altar holy. I will also make Aaron and his sons holy, so that they can serve me as priests.

**45** I will live among the Israelites, and I will be their God.

**46** They will know that I am the LORD their God. I brought them out of the land of Egypt so that I can live among them. I am the LORD their God.

**37** Do not make incense with the same mixture for yourselves. This incense is holy, and it belongs to the LORD.

## Chapter 32

**1** The people saw that Moses stayed on the mountain for a long time. They gathered around Aaron and said to him, "Come, make gods for us to lead us. Moses brought us out of Egypt, but we do not know what happened to him."

**2** Aaron said to them, "Take off the gold earrings from the ears of your wives, your sons, and your daughters. Bring them to me."

**3** Thus all the people took off their gold earrings and brought them to Aaron.

**4** Aaron took the gold from them. He melted it and made a statue of a calf. He used a tool to give it shape. Then the people said, "Israel, these are your gods who brought you out of Egypt!"

**5** Aaron saw this and built an altar in front of the calf. Then Aaron said, "Tomorrow we will hold a festival to the LORD."

**6** On the next day, the people got up early. They offered burnt offerings and brought peace offerings. Then the people sat down to eat and drink. After that, they got up to celebrate wildly.

**7** The LORD said to Moses, "Go down, because your people, whom you brought out of Egypt, became corrupt.

**25** Do not give the blood of my sacrifice with bread that has yeast. Do not keep any of the Passover sacrifice until the morning.

**26** Bring the best of the first crops of your land to the house of the LORD your God. Do not cook a young goat in the milk of its mother."

**16** They made 2 gold settings and 2 gold rings. They put the 2 rings on the 2 top corners of the breastplate.

**17** They put the 2 gold chains through the 2 rings on the corners of the breastplate.

**18** They tied the other 2 ends of the chains to the 2 settings. They put the settings on the shoulder pieces of the ephod, on the front.

**19** They made 2 gold rings and put them on the 2 bottom corners of the breastplate. They put them on the inside edge, near the ephod.

**20** They made 2 more gold rings. They put them on the bottom of the 2 shoulder pieces, on the front of the ephod. They put the rings near the seam, above the belt of the ephod.

**21** They tied the rings of the breastplate to the rings of the ephod with a blue cord. Thus, the breastplate stayed above the belt and did not move from the ephod. They did as the LORD commanded Moses.

**22** He made the robe of the ephod. He wove the robe with only blue cloth.

**23** There was a hole for the head in the center of the robe. The hole had a woven edge, the same as a collar of armor. Thus, the hole did not tear.

**24** On the bottom edge of the robe, they made pomegranates (fruits) of blue, purple, and red yarn and fine linen.

**25** They made bells of pure gold. They put the bells between the pomegranates around the bottom edge of the robe.

**26** There was a bell and a pomegranate, then a bell and a pomegranate, around the bottom edge of the robe. Aaron wore the robe when he did his work. They did as the LORD commanded Moses.

**27** They wove coats of fine linen for Aaron and his sons.

**28** They made the turban of fine linen and the decorated caps of fine linen. They made the underwear of fine linen.

**29** They made the sash of fine linen and blue, purple, and red yarn, with sewn designs. They did as the LORD commanded Moses.

**30** They made the plate of the holy crown of pure gold. They engraved words on the plate, as a person engraves a seal: "HOLY TO THE LORD."

**31** They tied a blue cord to the plate to attach it to the front of the turban. They did as the LORD commanded Moses.

**32** Thus, the people of Israel completed all the work on the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting. The people of Israel did all the things that the LORD commanded Moses.

**33** They brought the tabernacle to Moses. They brought the tent and all its equipment, its hooks, its frames, its bars, its posts, and its bases.

**34** They brought the covering of red ram skins, the covering of fine leather, and the curtain that hangs in front of the ark.

**35** They brought the ark of the testimony, its poles, and the mercy seat.

**36** They brought the table, all its equipment, and the holy bread.

**37** They brought the pure gold lampstand, its lamps in their positions, all its equipment, and the oil for the light.

**38** They brought the gold altar, the anointing oil, the sweet incense, and the curtain for the entrance of the tent.

**39** They brought the bronze altar, its bronze grate, its poles, and all its equipment. They brought the basin and its base.

**40** They brought the curtains of the courtyard, its posts, and its bases. They brought the curtain for the gate of the courtyard, its ropes, and its tent pegs. They brought all the equipment for the work of the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting.

**41** They brought the woven clothes for the work in the holy place. They brought the holy clothes for Aaron the priest and the clothes for his sons. His sons wore these clothes when they did the work of priests.

**42** The people of Israel did all the work as the LORD commanded Moses.

**43** Moses examined all the work. The people did the work as the LORD commanded. Then Moses blessed them.

## Chapter 40

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**2** "On the first day of the first month, set up the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting.

**3** Put the ark of the testimony in the tabernacle. Hang the curtain in front of the ark.

**4** Bring in the table and put its items on it. Bring in the lampstand and light its lamps.

**5** Put the gold altar for incense in front of the ark of the testimony. Hang the curtain at the entrance of the tabernacle.

**6** Put the altar of burnt offering in front of the entrance of the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting.

**7** Put the basin between the Tent of Meeting and the altar. Put water in the basin.

**8** Set up the courtyard around the tabernacle. Hang the curtain at the gate of the courtyard.

**9** Take the anointing oil. Pour it on the tabernacle and on all the things in it. Make the tabernacle and all its equipment holy. Then it will be holy.

**10** Pour the anointing oil on the altar of burnt offering and on all its equipment. Make the altar holy. Then the altar will be most holy.

**11** Pour the anointing oil on the basin and its base. Make them holy.

**12** Bring Aaron and his sons to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. Wash them with water.

**13** Put the holy clothes on Aaron. Pour the anointing oil on him and make him holy. Then he will serve me as priest.

**14** Bring his sons and put coats on them.

**15** Pour the anointing oil on them, as you did on their father. Then they will serve me as priests. Because of this anointing, they and their descendants will be priests for all time."

**16** Moses did all the things that the LORD commanded him.

**17** On the first day of the first month of the second year, Moses set up the tabernacle.

**18** Moses set up the tabernacle. He put its bases in position and set up its frames. He put in its bars and set up its posts.

**19** He spread the tent over the tabernacle. He put the covering on top of the tent, as the LORD commanded Moses.

**20** He took the testimony (the stone tablets) and put it into the ark. He put the poles on the ark. He put the mercy seat on top of the ark.

**21** He brought the ark into the tabernacle. He hung the curtain to cover the ark of the testimony, as the LORD commanded Moses.

**22** He put the table in the Tent of Meeting, on the north side of the tabernacle, outside the curtain.

**23** He put the bread on the table in front of the LORD, as the LORD commanded Moses.

**24** He put the lampstand in the Tent of Meeting, opposite the table, on the south side of the tabernacle.

**25** He lit the lamps in front of the LORD, as the LORD commanded Moses.

**26** He put the gold altar in the Tent of Meeting, in front of the curtain.

**27** He burned sweet incense on the gold altar, as the LORD commanded Moses.

**28** He hung the curtain at the entrance of the tabernacle.

**29** He put the altar of burnt offering near the entrance of the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting. He offered the burnt offering and the grain offering on it, as the LORD commanded Moses.

**30** He put the basin between the Tent of Meeting and the altar. He put water in the basin for washing.

**31** Moses, Aaron, and the sons of Aaron washed their hands and their feet with the water.

**32** They washed when they went into the Tent of Meeting and when they went near the altar. They did as the LORD commanded Moses.

**33** Moses set up the courtyard around the tabernacle and the altar. He hung the curtain at the gate of the courtyard. Thus, Moses completed the work.

**34** Then the cloud covered the Tent of Meeting. The glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle.

**35** Moses could not go into the Tent of Meeting. The cloud stayed on the tent, and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle.

**36** When the cloud went up from the tabernacle, the people of Israel started to travel. They did this on all their journeys.

**37** But if the cloud did not go up, they did not travel. They waited until the day that the cloud went up.

**38** The cloud of the LORD was on the tabernacle during the day. Fire was in the cloud during the night. All the people of Israel saw it during all their journeys.
