# Colossians

## Chapter 1

**1** I, Paul, am an apostle of Jesus Christ because God wants this. Timothy, our brother, is with me.

**2** We write to the saints and the faithful brothers in Christ in Colossae. We ask that God our Father give you grace and peace.

**3** We always pray for you. We thank God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**4** We heard about your faith in Christ Jesus. We also heard about your love for all the saints.

**5** You have faith and love because of the hope that God keeps for you in heaven. You heard about this hope before in the true word of the gospel.

**6** The gospel came to you. In all the world, the gospel gives good results and grows. The gospel also did this among you. It started on the day when you heard the gospel and truly understood the grace of God.

**7** You learned the gospel from Epaphras. We love him, and he serves with us. He is a faithful servant of Christ for us.

**8** Epaphras told us about your love in the Spirit.

**9** Thus, since the day we heard about you, we do not stop our prayers for you. We ask God to fill you with the knowledge of his will. We ask him to give you all wisdom and spiritual understanding.

**10** Then you will live in a way that is correct for the Lord. You will make him happy in all things. You will do all types of good work. You will know God better and better.

**11** We pray that God will make you strong with all strength from his glorious power. Then you will be patient in all things, and you will have joy.

**12** We pray that you will thank the Father. He made you able to share the inheritance of the saints in the kingdom of light.

**13** God made us free from the power of darkness. He brought us into the kingdom of his Son, whom he loves.

**14** Through the Son, God bought us back to be free. God forgave our sins.

**15** The Son is the image of God, whom no person can see. The Son is first and highest over all things that God made.

**16** God made all things through the Son. He made all things in heaven and on earth, the things that people can see and the things that they cannot see. He made all thrones, powers, rulers and authorities. God made all things through the Son and for the Son.

**17** The Son was before all things. The Son keeps all things together.

**18** The Son is the head of the body, which is the church. He is the start. He is the first to rise from the dead. Thus, he is the most important in all things.

**19** God wanted all his fullness to live in the Son.

**20** Through the Son, God brought all things back to himself. He brought back the things on earth and the things in heaven. God made peace through the blood of the Son on the cross.

**21** Before, you were far from God. You were his enemies in your minds because of your evil actions.

**22** But now God brought you back to himself through the death of Christ in his human body. God did this to put you in front of himself as holy people. You will have no fault, and no person will accuse you.

**23** But you must continue in the faith. Stand strong on its foundation. Do not move away from the hope of the gospel. You heard this gospel. People told it to all people under heaven. I, Paul, became a servant of this gospel.

**24** Now I am glad when I suffer for you. In my body, I complete the sufferings of Christ that are not complete. I do this for his body, which is the church.

**25** I became a servant of the church because God gave me this work. God gave me the work for you. My work is to tell the word of God completely.

**26** This word is the secret that God kept hidden through all ages and generations. But now God shows this secret to his saints.

**27** God wanted to show to his saints the rich glory of this secret among the Gentiles. This is the secret: Christ is in you. Christ is your hope of glory.

**28** We tell all people about Christ. We warn every person, and we teach every person with all wisdom. We want to put every person in front of God as mature in Christ.

**29** For this purpose, I work and fight hard. I use the strength of Christ, which works powerfully in me.

## Chapter 2

**1** I want you to know how hard I work for you. I also work hard for the people in Laodicea. I work for all the people who did not meet me in person.

**2** I want them to have encouragement in their hearts. I want love to join them together. I want them to have the riches of complete and certain understanding. Then they will know the secret of God, which is Christ.

**3** All the treasures of wisdom and knowledge are hidden in Christ.

**4** I tell you this so that no person will trick you with words that sound good.

**5** My body is not with you, but I am with you in my spirit. I am happy to see that you live in good order. I am happy that your faith in Christ is strong.

**6** You received Christ Jesus as Lord. Thus, continue to live in him.

**7** Let your roots grow deep into Christ, and let your lives be built on him. Become strong in the faith, as people taught you. Always give thanks.

**8** Be careful. Do not let any person take control of you through empty and false ideas. These ideas come from human traditions and from the spiritual powers of the world. They do not come from Christ.

**9** All the fullness of God lives in Christ in a human body.

**10** And you are complete in Christ. He is the head over all rulers and authorities.

**11** In Christ, you also received circumcision. Human hands did not do this circumcision. Christ did this circumcision. He removed your sinful nature.

**12** When you received baptism, God buried you with Christ. In baptism, God also raised you with Christ through your faith. You believed in the power of God, who raised Christ from the dead.

**13** You were dead because of your sins. You were not circumcised in your sinful nature. But God made you alive with Christ. God forgave all your sins.

**14** There was a written record of our debts against us. The rules in this record were against us. God removed this record. He nailed it to the cross.

**15** God took the power from the rulers and authorities. He showed everyone that they had no power. He won against them through the cross.

**16** Thus, do not let any person judge you about food or drink. Do not let any person judge you about a holy day, a new moon or a Sabbath day.

**17** These things are only a shadow of the things that will come. The true thing is Christ.

**18** Some people like to show false humility. They worship angels. They speak about visions that they say they saw. Their human minds make them proud for no reason. Do not let these people take your prize from you.

**19** These people do not hold on to Christ, who is the head. The whole body gets food from the head through its joints and ligaments. The body joins together and grows as God makes it grow.

**20** You died with Christ, and you are free from the spiritual powers of the world. Thus, why do you live as if you are in the world? Why do you obey its rules?

**21** These rules say, "Do not hold this. Do not taste that. Do not touch that."

**22** All these things will be destroyed when people use them. These rules are only human commandments and teachings.

**23** It is true that these rules seem wise. They cause people to worship in their own way, to show false humility and to treat the body badly. But these rules do not help people to stop the desires of the sinful nature.

## Chapter 3

**1** God raised you with Christ. Thus, try to get the things in heaven. In heaven, Christ sits at the right side of God.

**2** Think about the things in heaven. Do not think about the things on earth.

**3** You died, and your life is hidden with Christ in God.

**4** Christ is your life. When Christ comes again, you will also come with him in glory.

**5** Thus, kill the earthly desires in you. Stop sexual sin, impure actions, bad passions, evil desires and greed. Greed is the same as the worship of idols.

**6** Because of these sins, God is angry with the people who do not obey him.

**7** You also did these sins before, when you lived in them.

**8** But now you must also stop all these things: anger, rage, wishes to hurt other people, insults and dirty words.

**9** Do not lie to each other. You stopped your old self and its actions.

**10** You started your new self. God makes this new self new again and again in knowledge. He makes it like himself, because he made it.

**11** In this new life, there is no difference between Greek and Jew. There is no difference between circumcised and uncircumcised people. There is no difference between foreigner, Scythian, slave or free person. Christ is all, and he is in all.

**12** God chose you. You are holy, and he loves you. Thus, have pity and kindness. Be humble, gentle and patient.

**13** Accept each other. If a person has a complaint against another person, forgive him. Forgive as the Lord forgave you.

**14** Most of all, have love. Love joins all these things together completely.

**15** Let the peace of Christ control your hearts. God called you to this peace as members of one body. And be thankful.

**16** Let the word of Christ live richly in you. Teach and warn each other with all wisdom. Sing psalms, hymns and spiritual songs. Thank God in your hearts when you sing.

**17** In all the things that you say or do, do them in the name of the Lord Jesus. Thank God the Father through him.

**18** Wives, obey your husbands, as is correct in the Lord.

**19** Husbands, love your wives. Do not be harsh with them.

**20** Children, obey your parents in all things, because this makes the Lord happy.

**21** Fathers, do not make your children angry. If you do, they will lose courage.

**22** Slaves, obey your masters on earth in all things. Do not obey them only when they watch you, to make them happy. Obey them with an honest heart, because you respect the Lord.

**23** In all the things that you do, work with all your heart. Work for the Lord, not for people.

**24** You know that the Lord will give you an inheritance as a reward. You serve the Lord Christ.

**25** A person who does wrong will get the result of his wrong. God treats all people the same.

## Chapter 4

**1** Masters, give your slaves what is correct and fair. You know that you also have a Master in heaven.

**2** Continue to pray. Be alert when you pray, and thank God.

**3** Also pray for us. Pray that God will give us a chance to speak the secret of Christ. Because of this secret, I am in chains.

**4** Pray that I will speak the secret clearly, as I must.

**5** Be wise in how you act with people who are not believers. Use every chance well.

**6** Always speak with grace and with good words. Then you will know how to answer each person.

**7** Tychicus will tell you all the news about me. He is a brother that we love. He is a faithful servant, and he serves with me in the Lord.

**8** I send Tychicus to you for this purpose. I want him to know about you. I want him to give encouragement to your hearts.

**9** Onesimus comes with Tychicus. Onesimus is a faithful brother that we love. He is one of your people. They will tell you all the things that occur here.

**10** Aristarchus, who is in prison with me, sends you greetings. Mark, the cousin of Barnabas, also sends greetings. You received instructions about Mark. If he comes to you, receive him.

**11** Jesus, who has the name Justus, also sends greetings. Of the Jews, only these men work with me for the kingdom of God. They give me comfort.

**12** Epaphras, who is one of your people, sends you greetings. He is a servant of Christ. He always prays hard for you. He prays that you will stand mature and completely sure in all the will of God.

**13** I tell you the truth: Epaphras works hard for you. He also works hard for the people in Laodicea and in Hierapolis.

**14** Luke, the doctor that we love, sends you greetings. Demas also sends you greetings.

**15** Greet the brothers in Laodicea. Also greet Nympha and the church that meets in her house.

**16** After you read this letter, send it to the church of the Laodiceans so that they read it. And you must also read the letter from Laodicea.

**17** Say to Archippus, "Make sure that you complete the work that you received in the Lord."

**18** I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand. Remember that I am in chains. I ask that God give you grace. Amen.
