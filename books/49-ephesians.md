# Ephesians

## Chapter 1

**1** I, Paul, am an apostle of Jesus Christ because God wants this. I write this letter to the saints in Ephesus. I write to the people who are faithful in Christ Jesus.

**2** I ask that God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you grace and peace.

**3** Praise the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ. God gave us all spiritual blessings in the heavenly places in Christ.

**4** Before God made the world, he chose us in Christ. He chose us so that we will be holy and without fault in front of him. He did this because of his love.

**5** Before that time, God decided to make us his children through Jesus Christ. God did this because it was his good will and his pleasure.

**6** God did this so that people will praise his great grace. He gave this grace to us freely in the Son that he loves.

**7** In Christ, God bought us back to be free through the blood of Christ. God forgave our sins because his grace is very rich.

**8** God gave us much grace. He also gave us all wisdom and understanding.

**9** God told us the secret of his will. He did this because of his good pleasure. He decided on this plan in himself.

**10** God has a plan for the correct time. At that time, he will bring together all things under Christ as head. He will bring together the things in heaven and the things on earth in Christ.

**11** In Christ, we also got an inheritance. God chose us for this before that time. God does all things as his will decides.

**12** God did this so that we, who first put our hope in Christ, will cause people to praise his glory.

**13** You also trusted in Christ after you heard the word of truth. This word is the gospel that saves you. After you believed, God put his seal on you. This seal is the Holy Spirit that he promised.

**14** The Holy Spirit is the first payment of our inheritance. He is the proof that God will make his people completely free. Then people will praise the glory of God.

**15** Thus, I heard about your faith in the Lord Jesus. I also heard about your love for all the saints.

**16** Thus, I do not stop when I thank God for you. I speak about you in my prayers.

**17** I pray to the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory. I ask him to give you a spirit of wisdom and revelation. Then you will know God better.

**18** I pray that God will give light to the eyes of your heart. Then you will know the hope that he calls you to. You will know the rich glory of his inheritance in the saints.

**19** Also, you will know that God's power for us who believe is very great. This power is the same as the work of his strong power.

**20** God used this power in Christ when he raised Christ from the dead. God put Christ at his right side in the heavenly places.

**21** Christ is far above all rulers, authorities, powers and lords. He is above every name that people use, in this world and also in the world that will come.

**22** God put all things under the feet of Christ. God made Christ the head over all things for the church.

**23** The church is the body of Christ. The church is full of Christ, who fills all things in every way.

## Chapter 2

**1** You were dead because of your wrong actions and your sins. But God made you alive.

**2** Before, you lived in these sins. You followed the ways of this world. You obeyed the ruler of the power of the air. This ruler is the spirit that now works in the people who do not obey God.

**3** At one time, we all also lived among these people. We did the things that our sinful nature wanted. We obeyed the desires of our body and our mind. Like the other people, our nature caused God to be angry with us.

**4** But God has much mercy. He loved us with a great love.

**5** We were dead because of our sins. But God made us alive with Christ. God saved you because of his grace.

**6** God raised us up with Christ. God put us with Christ in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus.

**7** God did this so that, in the time that will come, he can show the very great riches of his grace. He shows his grace by his kindness to us in Christ Jesus.

**8** God saved you by grace, through faith. You did not save yourselves. Your salvation is a gift from God.

**9** Your salvation does not come from your good actions. Thus, no person can say that he is proud of his work.

**10** God made us. He made us in Christ Jesus so that we will do good works. Before that time, God prepared these good works for us to do.

**11** Remember that before, you were Gentiles by birth. The Jews call themselves "the circumcision." They called you "the uncircumcision." But their circumcision is only a thing that hands do to the body.

**12** At that time, you were without Christ. You were not citizens of Israel. You were strangers to the agreements (covenants) of the promise. You had no hope, and you did not know God in the world.

**13** Before, you were far from God. But now in Christ Jesus, the blood of Christ brings you near.

**14** Christ is our peace. He made the Jews and the Gentiles into one group. He broke down the wall of hate that kept them apart.

**15** Through his body, Christ stopped the hate. He stopped the law of commandments and rules. He did this to make one new people from the 2 groups. Thus, he made peace.

**16** Through the cross, Christ brought both groups back to God in one body. On the cross, Christ killed the hate.

**17** Christ came and told the good news of peace to you who were far away. He also told it to the people who were near.

**18** Through Christ, we both can come to the Father by one Spirit.

**19** Thus, you are not strangers and foreigners now. You are citizens together with the saints. You are members of the family of God.

**20** You are a building on the foundation of the apostles and prophets. Jesus Christ himself is the most important stone at the corner.

**21** In Christ, all the building joins together correctly. The building grows into a holy temple in the Lord.

**22** In Christ, God also builds you together with the others. You become a place where God lives through the Spirit.

## Chapter 3

**1** Because of this, I pray. I, Paul, am a prisoner of Jesus Christ for you Gentiles.

**2** You heard about the work that God gave me. God gave me his grace so that I can help you.

**3** God showed me the secret plan by revelation. I wrote a short letter to you about this before.

**4** When you read that letter, you can understand my knowledge of the secret plan of Christ.

**5** In past times, God did not show this secret to the people. But now the Spirit shows it to God's holy apostles and prophets.

**6** This is the secret: through the gospel, the Gentiles will get an inheritance together with the Jews. They are members of the same body. They share the promise of God in Christ Jesus.

**7** I became a servant of this gospel. God gave me this work as a gift of his grace. He did this through the work of his power.

**8** I am less important than all the saints. But God gave me this grace. He gave it so that I will tell the Gentiles about the riches of Christ. No person can know all these riches.

**9** God sent me to show all people how this secret plan works. From the start of the world, God kept this plan hidden in himself. God made all things through Jesus Christ.

**10** Now God shows his wisdom through the church to the rulers and authorities in the heavenly places. His wisdom has many parts.

**11** God did this because of his plan for all time. He did this plan through Christ Jesus our Lord.

**12** In Christ, we can come near to God with courage and confidence because of our faith in Christ.

**13** Thus, I ask you: do not lose courage because of my troubles for you. My troubles bring honor to you.

**14** Because of this, I go down on my knees in front of the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**15** Every family in heaven and on earth gets its name from the Father.

**16** I pray that God will give you strength in your heart through his Spirit. His glory is very rich.

**17** I pray that Christ will live in your hearts through faith. I pray that your roots and your foundation will be in love.

**18** Then, with all the saints, you will understand how wide, long, high and deep the love of Christ is.

**19** I pray that you will know the love of Christ. This love is greater than all knowledge. Then God will fill you completely with all his fullness.

**20** God can do much more than all the things that we ask or think. He does this through his power that works in us.

**21** Give glory to God in the church and in Christ Jesus. Give him glory through all ages, for all time. Amen.

## Chapter 4

**1** Thus, I, a prisoner for the Lord, ask you to live as people that God called. Live in a way that is correct for that call.

**2** Always be humble and gentle. Be patient. Accept each other in love.

**3** Try hard to keep the unity that the Spirit gives. Peace keeps you together.

**4** There is one body and one Spirit. God called you to one hope.

**5** There is one Lord, one faith and one baptism.

**6** There is one God and Father of all people. He is over all, through all and in all.

**7** But Christ gave grace to each of us. He decided how much to give to each person.

**8** Thus, the scripture says, "When he went up to the highest place, he took many prisoners. He gave gifts to people."

**9** What does "he went up" mean? It means that he also went down first to the lower parts of the earth.

**10** The person who went down is the same person who went up far above all the heavens. He did this to fill all things.

**11** Christ gave some people to be apostles. He gave some to be prophets. He gave some to be evangelists. He gave some to be pastors and teachers.

**12** He did this to prepare the saints for the work of service. They will make the body of Christ strong.

**13** This work will continue until we all agree in the faith. We will all know the Son of God. We will become mature, and we will be as complete as Christ.

**14** Then we will not be like children. False teachings will not push us here and there like waves in the wind. Bad people will not trick us with their clever lies.

**15** But we will speak the truth in love. In all things, we will grow to be more like Christ. Christ is the head.

**16** Christ joins the whole body together. Each joint helps to keep the body together. When each part does its work, the body grows. The body makes itself strong in love.

**17** Thus, I tell you this in the name of the Lord: Do not live like the Gentiles live. Their thoughts have no value.

**18** Their minds are in darkness. They are far from the life of God because they do not know God. Their hearts are hard.

**19** They do not feel shame now. They give themselves to sexual sin. They want to do all types of bad things, and they always want more.

**20** But you did not learn this about Christ.

**21** You heard about Christ, and people taught you the truth that is in Jesus.

**22** They taught you to stop your old way of life. Your old self is bad because of its desires that trick you.

**23** Let God make the attitude of your minds new.

**24** Become the new person that God made. This new person is like God. He is good and truly holy.

**25** Thus, stop your lies. Each person must speak the truth to his neighbor, because we are all members of one body.

**26** When you are angry, do not sin. Stop your anger before the sun goes down.

**27** Do not give the devil a chance to work.

**28** The person who stole must stop stealing. He must work hard with his hands and do good things. Then he will have something to give to people who need help.

**29** Do not speak bad words. Only say good things that help other people grow. Then your words will help the people who hear them.

**30** Do not make the Holy Spirit of God sad. God put the seal of the Spirit on you for the day when he will make you free.

**31** Stop all bitterness, rage and anger. Stop all shouts and bad words against other people. Stop all wishes to hurt other people.

**32** Be kind to each other and have pity on each other. Forgive each other, as God forgave you through Christ.

## Chapter 5

**1** Thus, copy God, because you are his children and he loves you.

**2** Live in love, as Christ loved us. Christ gave himself for us as an offering and a sacrifice to God. This sacrifice was like a sweet smell to God.

**3** Sexual sin, all bad actions and greed must not be among you. Not one person must even speak about these things. These things are not correct for saints.

**4** Do not say dirty or foolish things. Do not make bad jokes. These things are not correct. Instead, thank God.

**5** You know this: a person who does sexual sin will get no inheritance in the kingdom of Christ and of God. A person who does bad things or who is greedy will get no inheritance. A greedy person worships idols.

**6** Do not let any person trick you with empty words. Because of these sins, God is angry with the people who do not obey him.

**7** Thus, do not join these people.

**8** Before, you were in darkness. But now you are light in the Lord. Live as children of light.

**9** The light gives all goodness, righteousness and truth.

**10** Learn the things that make the Lord happy.

**11** Do not join in the bad works of darkness, because they give nothing good. Instead, show that they are bad.

**12** These people do things in secret. It is shameful even to speak about those things.

**13** But the light shows all things clearly. And the light makes all things visible.

**14** Thus, the scripture says, "Wake up, you who sleep. Rise from the dead. Christ will give you light."

**15** Thus, be careful how you live. Do not live as foolish people. Live as wise people.

**16** Use every chance well, because these days are bad.

**17** Thus, do not be foolish. Understand what the Lord wants.

**18** Do not become drunk with wine, because wine causes a bad way of life. Instead, let the Spirit fill you.

**19** Speak to each other with psalms, hymns and spiritual songs. Sing and make music in your heart to the Lord.

**20** Always thank God the Father for all things in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**21** Obey each other because you respect Christ.

**22** Wives, obey your husbands as you obey the Lord.

**23** The husband is the head of the wife, as Christ is the head of the church. Christ is the Savior of the church, which is his body.

**24** The church obeys Christ. In the same way, wives must obey their husbands in all things.

**25** Husbands, love your wives as Christ loved the church. Christ gave himself for the church.

**26** He did this to make the church holy. He made the church clean with the washing of water through the word.

**27** He did this to put the church in front of himself as a glorious church. The church will have no spot and no wrinkle or other fault. The church will be holy and without fault.

**28** In the same way, husbands must love their wives as they love their own bodies. A man who loves his wife loves himself.

**29** No person hates his own body. Each person feeds his body and cares for it. Christ does the same for the church.

**30** We are members of the body of Christ.

**31** "Because of this, a man will leave his father and mother and join his wife. The 2 people will become one body."

**32** This secret is very important. But I speak about Christ and the church.

**33** But each of you must love his wife as he loves himself. And the wife must respect her husband.

## Chapter 6

**1** Children, obey your parents in the Lord, because this is correct.

**2** "Honor your father and mother." This is the first commandment that has a promise.

**3** The promise is this: "Then things will go well for you, and you will live a long time on the earth."

**4** Fathers, do not make your children angry. Teach and correct them in the way of the Lord.

**5** Slaves, obey your masters on earth. Respect them and fear them. Obey them with an honest heart, as you obey Christ.

**6** Do not obey them only when they watch you, to make them happy. Obey them as slaves of Christ. Do the will of God with all your heart.

**7** Serve your masters with a good attitude. Serve them as you serve the Lord, not as you serve people.

**8** You know that the Lord will reward each person for the good things that he does. He will reward a slave and a free person.

**9** Masters, act in the same way to your slaves. Do not threaten them. You know that the Master of the slaves and of you is in heaven. He treats all people the same.

**10** Last, my brothers, be strong in the Lord and in his great power.

**11** Put on all the armor of God. Then you can stand against the tricks of the devil.

**12** We do not fight against human people. We fight against the rulers, the authorities and the powers of the darkness in this world. We fight against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly places.

**13** Thus, take all the armor of God. Then you can fight against the enemy on the bad day. After you do all things, you will continue to stand.

**14** Thus, stand strong. Put on truth like a belt around your waist. Put on righteousness like armor on your chest.

**15** Put on the readiness of the gospel of peace like shoes on your feet.

**16** Also, take faith as a shield. With this shield, you can stop all the burning arrows of the evil one.

**17** Take salvation as a helmet. Take the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God.

**18** Always pray in the Spirit with all types of prayers and requests. Watch carefully and do not stop. Pray for all the saints.

**19** Pray for me also. Pray that God will give me the correct words when I speak. Then I can tell the secret of the gospel with courage.

**20** I am a messenger for the gospel, and now I am in chains. Pray that I will speak the gospel with courage, as I must.

**21** Tychicus is a brother that we love. He is a faithful servant in the Lord. He will tell you all things about me. Then you will know about my life and what I do.

**22** I send Tychicus to you for this purpose. I want you to know about us. I want Tychicus to make your hearts strong.

**23** I ask that God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give peace to the brothers. I ask them to give you love with faith.

**24** I ask God to give grace to all the people who love our Lord Jesus Christ with a love that does not stop. Amen.
