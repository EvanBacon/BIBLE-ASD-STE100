# Micah

## Chapter 1

**1** The LORD gave this message to Micah from Moresheth. Micah received the message when Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah were the kings of Judah. Micah saw these things about Samaria and Jerusalem.

**2** Listen, all you nations! Listen, earth and all the things on it! The Lord GOD will speak against you. The Lord will speak from his holy temple.

**3** Look, the LORD comes out from his place. He will come down and walk on the high places of the earth.

**4** The mountains will melt under him. The valleys will break open. They will be like wax near a fire. They will be like water that flows down a steep slope.

**5** All this will occur because of the sins of Jacob and the sins of the people of Israel. What is the sin of Jacob? It is Samaria. What is the center of false worship in Judah? It is Jerusalem.

**6** "Thus I will make Samaria a pile of stones in a field. Samaria will be a place to plant vines. I will push its stones down into the valley. I will make its foundations bare.

**7** People will break all the idols of Samaria into pieces. Fire will burn all the gifts from her temple prostitutes. I will destroy all her idols. She got these things with the pay of a prostitute. Her enemies will use them again as the pay of prostitutes."

**8** Because of this, I will cry loudly. I will walk without shoes and without clothes. I will cry like a jackal. I will make a sad sound like an owl.

**9** The wound of Samaria cannot be cured. The disaster comes to Judah. The disaster comes to the gate of my people, to Jerusalem.

**10** Do not tell the news in Gath. Do not cry at all. In Beth-le-aphrah, roll in the dust.

**11** People of Shaphir, go away as prisoners, naked and ashamed. The people of Zaanan will not come out. Beth-ezel will cry, and it will not give you protection.

**12** The people of Maroth wait anxiously for good things. But the LORD sent disaster down to the gate of Jerusalem.

**13** People of Lachish, attach fast horses to your chariots. Lachish started the sin of the people of Zion. The sins of Israel were found in Lachish.

**14** Thus you will give farewell gifts to Moresheth-gath. The towns of Achzib will not help the kings of Israel. Achzib will disappoint them.

**15** People of Mareshah, I will bring an enemy who will take your land. The great men of Israel will hide in the cave of Adullam.

**16** Shave your heads and cut your hair to show your sadness. Mourn for the children that you love. Make yourselves bald like a vulture. Your enemies will take your children away as prisoners.

## Chapter 2

**1** Disaster will come to the people who plan evil things! They lie in their beds and plan bad actions. In the morning they do these bad actions, because they have the power.

**2** They want fields, and they take the fields by force. They want houses, and they take the houses. They cheat a man and take his house. They take the property of his family.

**3** Thus the LORD says, "I plan a disaster against these people. You will not escape from this disaster. You will not walk proudly, because it will be a bad time.

**4** On that day people will make a sad song about you. They will cry and say, 'We are completely destroyed! The LORD took away the land of my people. He took the land from me! He gave our fields to our enemies.'"

**5** Thus none of you will get a part of the land when the people of the LORD divide the land.

**6** The false prophets say, "Do not prophesy. Do not prophesy about these things. Disgrace will not come on us."

**7** People of Jacob, must people say this? "The Spirit of the LORD is not patient. The LORD does not do these things." My words do good to the person who lives correctly.

**8** But recently my people became like an enemy. You take the coats from people who pass by. These people feel safe, like men who come back from war.

**9** You push the women of my people out of their good houses. You take my blessing away from their children for all time.

**10** Get up and go away! This land is not a place of rest for you. Sin made this land unclean. Thus the land will destroy you completely.

**11** A man can tell lies and say, "I will prophesy to you about wine and strong drink." That man is the right prophet for these people!

**12** "Jacob, I will certainly bring all of you together. I will certainly collect the remnant of Israel. I will put them together like sheep in a pen. They will be like a flock in its field. The place will be full of people and noise.

**13** The one who breaks open the way will go in front of them. They will break through the gate and go out. Their king will go in front of them. The LORD will lead them."

## Chapter 3

**1** I said, "Listen, leaders of Jacob and rulers of Israel! You must know what is just.

**2** But you hate good and love evil. You pull the skin off my people. You pull the flesh off their bones.

**3** You eat the flesh of my people. You pull off their skin and break their bones. You cut them into pieces like meat for a cooking pot."

**4** Then these leaders will call to the LORD for help. But the LORD will not answer them. At that time he will hide his face from them, because they did evil things.

**5** The LORD says this about the prophets who lead my people the wrong way: "When people give these prophets food, the prophets say, 'You will have peace.' When people do not give them food, the prophets start a war against them.

**6** Thus night will come on you, and you will not see visions. Darkness will come on you, and you will not tell the future. The sun will go down on the prophets. The day will become dark for them.

**7** The seers will be ashamed. The people who tell the future will be disgraced. They will all cover their faces, because God will not answer them."

**8** But the Spirit of the LORD fills me with power. He gives me justice and strength. Thus I can tell Jacob about his crimes. I can tell Israel about his sin.

**9** Listen to this, leaders of Jacob and rulers of Israel! You hate justice. You make right things wrong.

**10** You build Zion with murder. You build Jerusalem with evil actions.

**11** The leaders of the city judge for bribes. The priests teach for pay. The prophets tell the future for money. But they lean on the LORD. They say, "The LORD is with us. No disaster will come to us."

**12** Because of you, enemies will plow Zion like a field. Jerusalem will become a pile of stones. Trees and bushes will cover the hill of the temple.

## Chapter 4

**1** In the last days, the mountain of the temple of the LORD will be the highest mountain. It will be higher than the hills. People from many nations will go to it.

**2** Many nations will come and say, "Come, we will go up to the mountain of the LORD. We will go to the temple of the God of Jacob. He will teach us his ways. We will obey him." The law will come from Zion. The word of the LORD will come from Jerusalem.

**3** The LORD will judge between many nations. He will settle the arguments of strong nations far away. They will make their swords into plows. They will make their spears into tools to cut vines. Nations will not fight against other nations. They will not train for war again.

**4** Each man will sit under his own grapevine and his own fig tree. No person will make them afraid. The LORD of armies said this.

**5** All the nations follow their own gods. But we will follow the LORD our God for all time.

**6** "On that day," says the LORD, "I will bring together the people who are lame. I will collect the people who were sent away. I will collect the people whom I punished.

**7** I will make the lame people a remnant. I will make the people who were far away a strong nation. The LORD will rule them on Mount Zion from that time and for all time.

**8** Jerusalem, you are like a watchtower for a flock. You are the fortress of the people of Zion. Your old power will come back to you. The kingdom will come back to the people of Jerusalem."

**9** Now why do you cry loudly? Do you have no king? Did your adviser die? You feel pain like a woman who gives birth.

**10** People of Zion, have pain and push like a woman who gives birth. Now you must go out of the city and live in the fields. You will go to Babylon. There the LORD will save you. He will rescue you from your enemies.

**11** Now many nations are together against you. They say, "Let Zion become unclean. We want to look at Zion and be glad."

**12** But they do not know the thoughts of the LORD. They do not understand his plan. He will collect them like bundles of grain on the threshing floor.

**13** "People of Zion, get up and crush them! I will make you strong like an ox with horns of iron. I will make your hooves of bronze. You will break many nations into pieces. You will give their stolen wealth to the LORD. You will give their property to the Lord of all the earth."

## Chapter 5

**1** City of soldiers, now collect your troops. The enemy attacks us. The enemy will hit the ruler of Israel on the cheek with a stick.

**2** "But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah, are small among the clans of Judah. But a ruler of Israel will come out from you for me. His family starts from very old times, from long ago."

**3** Thus the LORD will leave Israel to its enemies until the woman in labor gives birth. Then the rest of his brothers will come back to the people of Israel.

**4** The ruler will stand and take care of his people like a shepherd. He will do this in the strength of the LORD, in the majesty of the name of the LORD his God. His people will live safely. His greatness will go to the ends of the earth.

**5** He will be our peace. The Assyrians will come into our land and walk through our fortresses. Then we will send 7 shepherds and 8 leaders against them.

**6** These leaders will rule the land of Assyria with the sword. They will rule the land of Nimrod at its gates. The ruler will save us from the Assyrians when they come into our land and cross our borders.

**7** The remnant of Jacob will live among many nations. They will be like dew from the LORD. They will be like rain on the grass. The rain does not wait for people.

**8** The remnant of Jacob will live among the nations. They will be like a lion among the animals of the forest. They will be like a young lion among flocks of sheep. When the lion goes through, it pushes the sheep down and tears them. No one can save the sheep.

**9** You will defeat your enemies. All your enemies will be destroyed.

**10** "On that day," says the LORD, "I will take your horses away from you. I will destroy your chariots.

**11** I will destroy the cities of your land. I will pull down all your fortresses.

**12** I will stop your witchcraft. You will not have fortune-tellers again.

**13** I will destroy your idols and your sacred stones. You will not worship the things that you make with your hands again.

**14** I will pull up your Asherah poles from among you. I will destroy your cities.

**15** I will punish the nations that do not obey me. I will punish them with anger and fury. The nations never heard of a punishment like this."

## Chapter 6

**1** Listen to what the LORD says: "Get up and state your case in front of the mountains. Let the hills hear your voice."

**2** Mountains, listen to the accusation of the LORD. Strong foundations of the earth, listen. The LORD has a case against his people. He will argue against Israel.

**3** "My people, what did I do to you? How did I make you tired? Tell me!

**4** I brought you out of the land of Egypt. I freed you from slavery. I sent Moses, Aaron, and Miriam to lead you.

**5** My people, remember what Balak king of Moab planned. Remember how Balaam the son of Beor answered him. Remember your trip from Shittim to Gilgal. Then you will know that the LORD does right things."

**6** What must I bring when I come to the LORD? What must I bring when I bow down to God most high? Must I come with burnt offerings? Must I bring calves that are 1 year old?

**7** Will the LORD be pleased with 1,000s of rams? Will he be pleased with 10,000s of rivers of olive oil? Must I give my firstborn child for my sins? Must I give my child for the sin of my soul?

**8** Man, the LORD showed you what is good. The LORD wants you to do what is right. He wants you to love mercy. He wants you to live humbly with your God.

**9** The LORD calls to the city. A wise person respects your name. Listen to the punishment and to the one who sends it.

**10** Wicked people still keep treasures that they got by evil means. They still use false measures. I hate these false measures.

**11** Must I say that a man is innocent if he uses false scales? Must I say that he is innocent if he has a bag of false weights?

**12** The rich people of the city are full of violence. The people who live there tell lies. Their words are false.

**13** Thus I will hit you and make you sick. I will destroy you because of your sins.

**14** You will eat, but you will not be satisfied. Your stomach will stay empty. You will try to save your property, but you will not save it. I will give to the sword the things that you save.

**15** You will plant seeds, but you will not harvest the crops. You will crush olives, but you will not put the oil on your body. You will crush grapes, but you will not drink the wine.

**16** You obey the laws of Omri. You do all the things that the family of Ahab did. You follow their advice. Thus I will destroy you. People will laugh at the people of your city. You will feel the shame of my people."

## Chapter 7

**1** I am very sad! I am like a person who comes after the summer harvest. The workers collected all the grapes. There is no fruit to eat. I want to eat the first figs, but there are none.

**2** All the good people are gone from the land. No honest person remains. All the people wait in hiding to kill. Each man hunts his brother with a net.

**3** The people are skilled at doing evil things with both hands. The ruler and the judge ask for bribes. The important man tells what he wants. Together they plan evil.

**4** The best of them is like a thorny bush. The most honest of them is worse than a thorn hedge. The day of your punishment comes. Your watchmen told you about this day. Now the people will be confused.

**5** Do not trust a neighbor. Do not trust a friend. Be careful what you say, even to your wife.

**6** A son does not respect his father. A daughter goes against her mother. A daughter-in-law goes against her mother-in-law. The enemies of a man are the people of his own family.

**7** But I will look to the LORD. I will wait for God, who saves me. My God will hear me.

**8** My enemy, do not be happy about my trouble. When I fall, I will get up again. When I sit in darkness, the LORD will be my light.

**9** I sinned against the LORD. Thus I will accept his anger until he takes my side and gives me justice. He will bring me out into the light. I will see his righteousness.

**10** Then my enemy will see this, and shame will cover her. She said to me, "Where is the LORD your God?" I will see her fall. People will walk on her like mud in the streets.

**11** A day will come when people build your walls again. On that day your borders will become larger.

**12** On that day people will come to you from Assyria and from the cities of Egypt. They will come from Egypt to the Euphrates River, from sea to sea, and from mountain to mountain.

**13** But the earth will become empty because of the people who live on it. This will occur because of the evil things that they do.

**14** LORD, take care of your people with your staff, like a shepherd. Your people are like a flock. They live alone in a forest, in the middle of fertile land. Let them eat in Bashan and Gilead, as in the old days.

**15** "I will show them wonderful things, as when you came out of the land of Egypt."

**16** The nations will see this and be ashamed of all their power. They will put their hands over their mouths. Their ears will become deaf.

**17** They will lick the dust like a snake. They will come out of their holes like worms. They will come to the LORD our God in fear. They will be afraid of you.

**18** No god is like you. You forgive sin. You forgive the crimes of the remnant of your people. You do not stay angry for all time, because you like to show mercy.

**19** You will have pity on us again. You will defeat our sins. You will throw all our sins into the deep sea.

**20** You will be faithful to Jacob. You will show your love to Abraham. You promised these things to our ancestors long ago.
