# Nahum

## Chapter 1

**1** This is the message about Nineveh. This is the book of the vision of Nahum, the man from Elkosh.

**2** God is a jealous God, and the LORD punishes his enemies. The LORD punishes, and he is very angry. The LORD will punish the people who are against him. He keeps his anger for his enemies.

**3** The LORD is slow to become angry, and he has great power. The LORD will not let guilty people go free. The LORD moves in the strong wind and in the storm. The clouds are like the dust below his feet.

**4** He gives a command to the sea, and the sea becomes dry. He causes all the rivers to become dry. The land of Bashan and the land of Carmel become weak. The flowers of Lebanon become dry and die.

**5** The mountains shake in front of him, and the hills melt. The earth shakes when he comes. The world and all the people who live in it shake.

**6** Who can stand in front of his anger? Who can stay alive when his anger is strong? His anger flows out like fire. He breaks the rocks into pieces.

**7** The LORD is good. He is a strong place of safety in the time of trouble. He knows the people who trust him.

**8** But he will send a large flood and he will fully destroy Nineveh. He will send his enemies into darkness.

**9** What do you plan against the LORD? He will fully destroy his enemies. Trouble will not come a second time.

**10** The enemies are like thorn bushes that grow together. They are like drunk men. Fire will burn them as it burns dry straw.

**11** A man came out from you, Nineveh. He plans evil against the LORD. He gives bad advice.

**12** The LORD says this: "The Assyrians are strong, and there are many of them. But I will cut them down, and they will go away. Judah, I caused you trouble before. But I will not cause you trouble again.

**13** Now I will break the heavy load that the Assyrians put on you. I will break the chains that hold you."

**14** The LORD gave a command about you, king of Assyria: "You will have no sons to keep your name alive. I will remove the carved idols and the metal idols from the temple of your gods. I will make your grave, because you are very bad."

**15** Look! A man comes on the mountains. He brings good news, and he tells about peace. People of Judah, keep your holy festivals. Do the things that you promised to do. The evil enemy will not attack you again. The LORD will fully destroy him.

## Chapter 2

**1** Nineveh, an enemy comes to attack you and break you into pieces. Guard your strong walls. Watch the road. Make yourself ready for war. Make your army very strong.

**2** The enemies took everything from the people of Jacob. They destroyed their vines. But the LORD will make the people of Jacob great again, as the people of Israel were great before.

**3** The shields of the enemy soldiers are red. The brave soldiers wear red clothes. The metal on the chariots shines like fire on the day that the soldiers prepare for war. The soldiers shake their spears of fir wood.

**4** The chariots move very quickly in the streets. They hit one another in the wide roads. They look like burning torches. They move as fast as lightning.

**5** The king of Nineveh calls his best officers. But they fall as they walk. They run quickly to the wall of the city. The enemy puts a shield in position to protect the soldiers who attack.

**6** The enemy opens the gates of the rivers, and the palace falls down.

**7** The enemy takes away the queen of the city as a prisoner. Her women servants make sad sounds like doves. They hit their chests because they are sad.

**8** Nineveh was full of people as a pool is full of water. But now the people run away. Some people shout, "Stop! Stop!" But no person turns back.

**9** Take the silver! Take the gold! The city has a very large quantity of valuable things. There is no end to the treasure.

**10** The enemy takes everything from Nineveh, and the city is empty and destroyed. The people are very afraid, and their knees shake. Their bodies have much pain. All their faces become pale.

**11** Where is the home of the lions now? Where is the place where the young lions ate? The male lion, the female lion and the young lions walked there. No person made them afraid.

**12** The lion killed enough food for his young lions. He killed animals for his female lions. He filled his caves with the animals that he killed.

**13** The LORD of armies says, "I am against you, Nineveh. I will burn your chariots, and they will become smoke. The sword will kill your young soldiers. I will stop you from taking things from the earth. People will not hear the voices of your messengers again."

## Chapter 3

**1** Nineveh will have much trouble! The people of that city killed many people. The city is full of lies and of things that people stole. The people in the city always take things from other people.

**2** Listen to the sound of the whips! Listen to the noise of the wheels! Hear the horses that run and the chariots that jump!

**3** The horsemen attack with shining swords and bright spears. They kill very many people. There are very many dead bodies. People fall over the dead bodies.

**4** All this happens because Nineveh is like a beautiful prostitute. She uses magic to get power. She makes nations into slaves by her prostitution. She controls families with her magic.

**5** The LORD of armies says, "I am against you, Nineveh. I will lift your skirt up over your face. I will show your naked body to the nations. The kingdoms will see your shame.

**6** I will throw dirt on you. I will cause people to think that you are worthless. I will make you an example for all people to see.

**7** All the people who see you will run away from you. They will say, 'Nineveh is destroyed. Who will be sad for her?' Nineveh, I cannot find a person who will comfort you."

**8** Nineveh, are you better than the city of Thebes? Thebes was near the Nile River, and water was all around it. The river was its wall of defense. The water protected it.

**9** Ethiopia and Egypt gave Thebes very much strength. Put and Libya also helped Thebes.

**10** But the enemy took the people of Thebes away as prisoners. The enemy killed her young children at the corners of all the streets. The enemy threw lots for her important men. They put chains on all her leaders.

**11** Nineveh, you will also become like a drunk person. You will try to hide. You will look for a place of safety from your enemy.

**12** All your strong towers are like fig trees with the first ripe figs. When a person shakes the trees, the figs fall into the mouth of the person who eats them.

**13** Look at your soldiers. They are weak like women. The gates of your land are open to your enemies. Fire burns the bars of your gates.

**14** Get water to prepare for the attack. Make your strong towers stronger. Go and get clay. Push the clay with your feet to make mud. Make bricks in the oven.

**15** But the fire will burn you there. The sword will kill you. The sword will destroy you as locusts eat plants. Make your people as many as the locusts. Make your people as many as the grasshoppers.

**16** You made your merchants more than the stars in the sky. But they are like locusts that eat everything and then fly away.

**17** Your officials are like locusts. Your officers are like large grasshoppers. Grasshoppers stay in the walls on a cold day. But when the sun comes up, they fly away. No person knows where they go.

**18** King of Assyria, your leaders are asleep. Your important men lie down and rest. Your people are in many different places on the mountains. No person brings them together.

**19** No person can make your injury better. Your wound is very bad. All the people who hear the news about you will clap their hands with joy. All people felt the pain of your cruel actions all the time.
