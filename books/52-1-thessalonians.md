# 1 Thessalonians

## Chapter 1

**1** Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy write this letter to the church of the Thessalonians. This church is in God the Father and in the Lord Jesus Christ. We ask that God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you grace and peace.

**2** We always thank God for all of you. We speak about you in our prayers.

**3** Before God our Father, we always remember your work that comes from faith. We remember your hard work that comes from love. We also remember your patience that comes from hope in our Lord Jesus Christ.

**4** Brothers, God loves you. We know that God chose you.

**5** Our gospel did not come to you only in words. It also came with power, with the Holy Spirit, and with a strong certainty. You know how we lived among you for your good.

**6** You did as we did, and as the Lord did. You received the word with much trouble. But you also received it with the joy that the Holy Spirit gives.

**7** Thus you became an example to all the believers in Macedonia and in Achaia.

**8** The word of the Lord went out from you. It went out into Macedonia and Achaia. Also, the news of your faith in God went out to all places. Thus we do not need to say anything.

**9** The people in those places tell how you received us. They tell how you turned to God from idols. Now you serve the living and true God.

**10** They tell how you wait for the Son of God to come from heaven. God made him alive again after his death. He is Jesus, who saves us from the anger of God that will come.

## Chapter 2

**1** Brothers, you know that our visit to you was not without a result.

**2** Before we came to you, we suffered in Philippi. The people there treated us badly. You know this. But our God gave us courage. Thus we told you the gospel of God, although many people opposed us.

**3** Our message did not come from a lie. It did not come from an unclean purpose. We did not try to trick you.

**4** God examined us and found us correct. He gave the gospel to us as a trust. Thus we speak. We do not speak to please men. We speak to please God, who examines our hearts.

**5** You know that we never used false praise. We never hid a wish to get money. God sees this, and he is our witness.

**6** We did not try to get honor from men, from you, or from other people. As apostles of Christ, we had the right to ask for your support.

**7** But we were gentle among you. We were like a mother who takes care of her own children.

**8** We loved you very much. Thus we were glad to give you the gospel of God. We were also glad to give you our own lives. We did this because you were very dear to us.

**9** Brothers, you remember our hard work. We worked night and day so that we did not cause expense to any of you. While we worked, we told you the gospel of God.

**10** You are witnesses, and God is also a witness. We lived among you believers in a holy, correct, and good way. No one could find a fault in us.

**11** You know how we spoke to each of you. We encouraged you, comforted you, and told you what to do. We did as a father does with his children.

**12** We told you to live in a way that is worthy of God. God calls you into his kingdom and his glory.

**13** For this reason, we also thank God all the time. You heard the word of God from us. You did not receive it as a word from men. You received it as the word of God, and it is truly his word. This word works in you who believe.

**14** Brothers, your experience is the same as the experience of the churches of God in Judea. Those churches are in Christ Jesus. Your own people made you suffer. In the same way, the Jews made those churches suffer.

**15** Those Jews killed the Lord Jesus and their own prophets. They also made us go away. They do not please God. They are enemies of all men.

**16** They try to stop us when we speak to the Gentiles, so that the Gentiles cannot be saved. Thus they always make their sins bigger. But at last the anger of God came on them.

**17** Brothers, we were away from you for a short time. Our bodies were away, but our hearts were with you. We wanted very much to see you again. Thus we tried very hard to come to you.

**18** We wanted to come to you. I, Paul, tried to come to you again and again. But Satan stopped us.

**19** What is our hope, our joy, and our crown of honor? You are! You will be our joy before our Lord Jesus Christ when he comes.

**20** Truly, you are our glory and our joy.

## Chapter 3

**1** Thus we could not wait longer. We decided to stay alone in Athens.

**2** We sent Timothy to you. He is our brother and a servant of God. He works with us in the gospel of Christ. We sent him to make your faith strong and to encourage you.

**3** We did not want these troubles to make any of you weak. You know that God chose us for these troubles.

**4** When we were with you, we told you before that we would suffer troubles. And the troubles came, as you know.

**5** For this reason, I could not wait longer. I sent Timothy to learn about your faith. I was afraid that the tempter tempted you. I was afraid that our work among you was without a result.

**6** But now Timothy came back to us from you. He brought us good news about your faith and your love. He told us that you always remember us with kindness. He told us that you want to see us. We also want to see you.

**7** Thus, brothers, your faith comforted us in all our pain and trouble.

**8** Now we truly live, because you stand strong in the Lord.

**9** We cannot thank God enough for you. Before our God, we have much joy because of you.

**10** Night and day we pray very much that we can see you again. We want to give you the things that your faith does not have yet.

**11** We ask that God our Father and our Lord Jesus Christ open the way for us to come to you.

**12** We ask that the Lord make your love grow more and more. We ask that you love each other and all people. We love you in the same way.

**13** We ask that the Lord make your hearts strong. Then you will be holy and without fault before God our Father. This will be true when our Lord Jesus Christ comes with all his holy people.

## Chapter 4

**1** Finally, brothers, we ask you and encourage you in the Lord Jesus. You learned from us how to live and how to please God. You do these things now. Do them more and more.

**2** You know the instructions that we gave you by the authority of the Lord Jesus.

**3** God wants you to be holy. Do not commit sexual sin.

**4** Each of you must learn to control his own body in a holy and honorable way.

**5** Do not control your body with strong sexual desire, as the Gentiles do. The Gentiles do not know God.

**6** In this matter, no man must do wrong to his brother or cheat him. The Lord will punish all people who do these things. We told you this before, and we gave you a strong warning.

**7** God did not call us to be unclean. He called us to be holy.

**8** Thus a person who refuses this instruction does not refuse a man. He refuses God. God gives his Holy Spirit to you.

**9** I do not need to write to you about love for the brothers. God taught you to love each other.

**10** Truly, you love all the brothers in all Macedonia. But, brothers, we ask you to love them more and more.

**11** Try hard to live a quiet life. Do your own work. Work with your own hands, as we told you.

**12** Then the people who are not believers will respect how you live. Also, you will not need help from other people.

**13** Brothers, we want you to know about the believers who died. We do not want you to be sad as the other people are sad. Those people have no hope.

**14** We believe that Jesus died and became alive again. Thus we also believe that God will bring back with Jesus the believers who died in Jesus.

**15** We tell you this from the word of the Lord. Some of us will be alive when the Lord comes. We will not go before the believers who died.

**16** The Lord himself will come down from heaven with a loud command. The chief angel will call out. The trumpet of God will sound. Then the dead people who are in Christ will rise first.

**17** After that, God will lift up us who are still alive. We will go up together with them in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air. Then we will always be with the Lord.

**18** Thus comfort each other with these words.

## Chapter 5

**1** Brothers, I do not need to write to you about the times and the dates.

**2** You know very well that the day of the Lord will come like a thief in the night.

**3** People will say, "We have peace and safety." Then sudden destruction will come on them. It will come as pain comes on a woman who will soon give birth. Those people will not escape.

**4** But, brothers, you do not live in darkness. Thus that day will not surprise you like a thief.

**5** All of you are children of light and children of the day. We do not belong to the night or to the darkness.

**6** Thus we must not sleep as other people do. We must stay alert and have self-control.

**7** People who sleep, sleep at night. People who become drunk, become drunk at night.

**8** But we belong to the day. Thus we must have self-control. We must put on faith and love as armor for the chest. We must put on the hope of salvation as a helmet.

**9** God did not choose us to receive his anger. He chose us to receive salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ.

**10** Jesus died for us. Thus we will live together with him, if we are alive or if we are dead.

**11** Thus encourage each other and make each other strong. You do this now.

**12** Brothers, we ask you to respect the people who work hard among you. They lead you in the Lord, and they give you instructions.

**13** Respect them very much and love them because of their work. Live in peace with each other.

**14** Brothers, we encourage you to do these things. Warn the people who are lazy. Encourage the people who are afraid. Help the weak people. Be patient with all people.

**15** Make sure that no one does wrong to a person who did wrong to him. Always try to do good to each other and to all people.

**16** Always be glad.

**17** Pray all the time.

**18** Thank God in all conditions. God wants you to do this in Christ Jesus.

**19** Do not stop the work of the Spirit.

**20** Do not think that prophecies have no value.

**21** Examine all things. Keep the things that are good.

**22** Stay away from all types of evil.

**23** We ask that the God of peace make you completely holy. We ask that God keep your whole spirit, soul, and body without fault until our Lord Jesus Christ comes.

**24** God, who calls you, is faithful. He will do this.

**25** Brothers, pray for us.

**26** Greet all the brothers with a holy kiss.

**27** By the authority of the Lord, I tell you to read this letter to all the holy brothers.

**28** We ask that the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you. Amen.
