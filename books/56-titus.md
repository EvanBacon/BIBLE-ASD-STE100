# Titus

## Chapter 1

**1** I am Paul, a servant of God and an apostle of Jesus Christ. God sent me to help the faith of the people that he chose. God sent me to help them know the truth that leads to a holy life.

**2** This faith and truth give hope of life that does not end. God, who cannot lie, promised this life before time started.

**3** At the correct time, God showed his word through preaching. God our Savior gave me this work, and he ordered me to do it.

**4** I write to Titus. You are my true son in the faith that we share. I pray that God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ our Savior will give you grace, mercy, and peace.

**5** I left you in Crete for this reason: you must put in order the things that are not complete. Also, you must choose elders in each city, as I told you.

**6** An elder must have no fault. He must have only 1 wife. His children must believe. People must not say that his children are wild or disobedient.

**7** A bishop is a manager for God. Thus he must have no fault. He must not be proud or quickly angry. He must not drink too much wine. He must not be violent. He must not try to get money in dishonest ways.

**8** He must be glad to receive guests in his home. He must love what is good. He must have self-control. He must be fair and holy. He must control his desires.

**9** He must hold firmly to the true message, as people taught it to him. Then he will be able to encourage people with correct teaching. He will also be able to show the errors of the people who oppose it.

**10** Many people do not obey. They talk foolishly and deceive other people. This is true most of all for the people of the circumcision group.

**11** You must stop them from talking. They teach things that they must not teach, because they want dishonest money. Thus they destroy whole families.

**12** One of the people of Crete, their own prophet, said, "The people of Crete always lie. They are like bad animals. They are lazy and they eat too much."

**13** This statement is true. Thus correct them strongly. Then they will have a correct faith.

**14** They must not pay attention to Jewish stories that are not true. They must not obey the commands of people who turn away from the truth.

**15** To clean people, all things are clean. But to people who are not clean and who do not believe, nothing is clean. Their minds and their consciences are not clean.

**16** They say that they know God, but their works show that they do not know him. They are hateful and disobedient. They are not fit to do any good work.

## Chapter 2

**1** But you must teach the things that agree with correct teaching.

**2** Teach the old men to have self-control. They must be serious and sensible. They must have a correct faith, love, and patience.

**3** Also teach the old women to live in a holy way. They must not say false things about other people. They must not drink too much wine. They must teach what is good.

**4** Then they can train the young women to love their husbands and their children.

**5** The young women must be sensible and pure. They must do good work in their homes. They must be kind and obey their husbands. Then nobody will speak against the word of God.

**6** Also encourage the young men to have self-control.

**7** In all things, show yourself as an example of good works. When you teach, be honest and serious.

**8** Speak correct words that nobody can criticize. Then the people who oppose you will be ashamed, because they will have nothing bad to say about us.

**9** Tell slaves to obey their masters. They must please their masters in all things. They must not argue with them.

**10** They must not steal from their masters. They must show that they are always faithful. Then in all things they will make the teaching of God our Savior attractive.

**11** The grace of God came, and it gives salvation to all people.

**12** This grace teaches us to refuse evil and bad worldly desires. It teaches us to live with self-control, to do what is right, and to live a holy life now in this world.

**13** We wait for the hope that gives joy. We wait for the glory of our great God and Savior, Jesus Christ, to appear.

**14** Jesus Christ gave himself for us. He did this to free us from all evil. He did this to make us clean. We are his own people, and we want to do good works.

**15** Teach these things. Encourage and correct people with all authority. Do not let anybody think that you are not important.

## Chapter 3

**1** Tell the people that they must obey rulers and those who have authority. They must be ready to do each good work.

**2** They must not speak bad things about anybody. They must not fight. They must be kind and gentle to all people.

**3** In the past, we also were foolish and disobedient. People deceived us. Many bad desires and pleasures controlled us. We lived with evil and envy. People hated us, and we hated each other.

**4** But then God our Savior showed his kindness and his love for people.

**5** He saved us. He did not save us because of the good things that we did. He saved us because of his mercy. He washed us and gave us a new birth and a new life through the Holy Spirit.

**6** God gave the Holy Spirit to us in a large quantity through Jesus Christ our Savior.

**7** Thus, because of his grace, God accepts us as right with him. We will receive life that does not end, as we hope.

**8** This statement is true. I want you to teach these things with confidence. Then the people who believe in God will be careful to do good works. These things are good and useful for people.

**9** But do not take part in foolish arguments and arguments about lists of ancestors. Do not argue or fight about the law. These arguments are useless and they do not help.

**10** Warn a person who causes division 1 time, then warn him a 2nd time. After that, have nothing to do with him.

**11** You know that such a person turned away from the truth. He sins, and his actions show that he is guilty.

**12** I will send Artemas or Tychicus to you. When I send him, try hard to come to me at Nicopolis. I decided to stay there during the winter.

**13** Do all that you can to help Zenas the lawyer and Apollos on their journey. Make sure that they have all that they need.

**14** Our people also must learn to do good works for necessary needs. Then their lives will produce good results.

**15** All the people who are with me say hello to you. Say hello to the people who love us in the faith. I pray that grace will be with you all. Amen.
