# Joshua

## Chapter 1

**1** Moses, the servant of the LORD, died. After his death, the LORD spoke to Joshua the son of Nun. Joshua was the helper of Moses. The LORD said,

**2** "My servant Moses is dead. Thus, get up now and go across the Jordan River. You and all these people must go into the land. I give this land to the people of Israel.

**3** I give you each place where you put your foot. I made this promise to Moses.

**4** Your borders will go from the desert and Lebanon to the big river, the Euphrates River. All the land of the Hittites will be yours. Your border will go west to the Great Sea.

**5** No person will be able to stop you during all your life. I was with Moses, and I will be with you in the same way. I will not fail you. I will not leave you.

**6** Be strong and brave. You will divide the land among these people as their property. I made a promise to their fathers to give them this land.

**7** Be strong and very brave. Obey all the law that my servant Moses gave you. Do not turn away from the law to the right or to the left. Then you will have success in all the places that you go.

**8** Always speak the words of this book of the law. Think about the law during the day and during the night. Then you will obey all the things that it tells you to do. Then you will have success, and your work will go well.

**9** I gave you a command. Be strong and brave. Do not be afraid. Do not lose your courage. The LORD your God is with you in all the places that you go."

**10** Then Joshua gave a command to the officers of the people. He said,

**11** "Go through the camp and tell the people this: 'Prepare food for yourselves. In 3 days you will go across the Jordan River. You will go in and take the land. The LORD your God gives you this land as your property.'"

**12** Joshua also spoke to the tribe of Reuben, the tribe of Gad, and half the tribe of Manasseh. He said,

**13** "Remember the command that Moses, the servant of the LORD, gave you. He said, 'The LORD your God gives you rest. He gives you this land.'

**14** Your wives, your small children, and your animals will stay in the land on the east side of the Jordan River. Moses gave you that land. But all your strong soldiers must go across in front of your brothers. Take your weapons and help them.

**15** Help them until the LORD gives your brothers rest, as he gave rest to you. Help them until they also take the land that the LORD your God gives them. Then you can go back to your own land and live in it. Moses, the servant of the LORD, gave you that land on the east side of the Jordan River."

**16** The men answered Joshua, "We will do all the things that you tell us to do. We will go to each place where you send us.

**17** We obeyed Moses in all things. We will obey you in the same way. We pray that the LORD your God will be with you, as he was with Moses.

**18** If a person does not obey your command and does not obey your words, we will kill that person. Only be strong and brave."

## Chapter 2

**1** Joshua the son of Nun secretly sent 2 men from Shittim to spy. He told them, "Go and look at the land, and look at Jericho." The 2 men went. They came to the house of a prostitute with the name Rahab, and they stayed there that night.

**2** A person told the king of Jericho, "Look, some men from the people of Israel came here tonight. They came to search the country."

**3** The king of Jericho sent a message to Rahab. He said, "Bring out the men who came to you into your house. They came to search all the country."

**4** But the woman took the 2 men and hid them. She said, "Yes, the men came to me. But I did not know where they came from.

**5** When it was dark, at the time to close the city gate, the men went out. I do not know where they went. Go after them quickly. You can catch them."

**6** But Rahab took the men up to the roof of the house. She hid them below the stems of flax plants. She put these stems in lines on the roof.

**7** The king's men went after the 2 men on the road to the places where people cross the Jordan River. The people of Jericho closed the gate after the king's men went out.

**8** The 2 men did not go to sleep yet. Rahab went up to the roof to them.

**9** She said to the men, "I know that the LORD gives you this land. We are very afraid of you. All the people who live in this land have no courage because of you.

**10** We heard how the LORD made the water of the Red Sea dry for you when you came out of Egypt. We also heard what you did to Sihon and Og. They were the 2 kings of the Amorites on the east side of the Jordan River. You destroyed them completely.

**11** When we heard these things, we became very afraid. Nobody has courage now because of you. The LORD your God is God in heaven above and on the earth below.

**12** Now please make a promise to me by the LORD. I was kind to you. Thus, promise that you will also be kind to my family. Give me a sign that I can trust.

**13** Promise that you will keep my father, my mother, my brothers, my sisters, and all their families alive. Save us from death."

**14** The men answered her, "We will give our lives for your lives. But you must not tell anybody about our work. When the LORD gives us the land, we will be kind and honest with you."

**15** Then Rahab used a rope to let the men down through the window. Her house was part of the city wall, and she lived on the wall.

**16** She told them, "Go to the hills. Then the men who search for you will not find you. Hide there for 3 days until those men come back. After that, you can go on your way."

**17** The men said to her, "We will keep the promise that you caused us to make. But you must do these things.

**18** When we come into the land, tie this red rope in the window. You used this window to let us down. Bring your father, your mother, your brothers, and all your family into your house.

**19** If a person goes out of the doors of your house into the street, that person will be responsible for his own death. We will not be guilty. But if a person in the house with you gets hurt, we will be responsible for that person's death.

**20** But if you tell anybody about our work, then we will not have to keep the promise that you caused us to make."

**21** Rahab said, "I agree with your words." Then she sent them away, and they went. She tied the red rope in the window.

**22** The 2 men went into the hills and stayed there for 3 days. They stayed until the men who searched for them went back. The king's men searched along all the road, but they did not find the 2 men.

**23** Then the 2 men came down from the hills and went back across the river. They came to Joshua the son of Nun. They told him all the things that occurred.

**24** They said to Joshua, "Truly, the LORD gave all the land to us. All the people who live in the country have no courage because of us."

## Chapter 3

**1** Joshua got up early in the morning. He and all the people of Israel left Shittim and came to the Jordan River. They stayed there before they went across.

**2** After 3 days, the officers went through the camp.

**3** They gave a command to the people. They said, "You will see the ark of the covenant (the holy box of the agreement) of the LORD your God. The priests, who are Levites, will carry it. When you see the ark, leave your place and follow it.

**4** But keep a distance of approximately 2,000 cubits (900 meters) between you and the ark. Do not go near it. Then you will know which way to go, because you did not go on this road before."

**5** Joshua said to the people, "Make yourselves holy. Tomorrow the LORD will do wonderful things among you."

**6** Joshua said to the priests, "Lift the ark of the covenant and go across in front of the people." Thus, the priests lifted the ark of the covenant and went in front of the people.

**7** The LORD said to Joshua, "Today I will start to make you great in the eyes of all Israel. Then the people will know that I am with you, as I was with Moses.

**8** Give a command to the priests who carry the ark of the covenant. Tell them, 'When you come to the edge of the water of the Jordan, stop and stand in the river.'"

**9** Joshua said to the people of Israel, "Come here. Listen to the words of the LORD your God."

**10** Joshua said, "This is how you will know that the God who lives is among you. He will surely push out the people in front of you. He will push out the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Hivites, the Perizzites, the Girgashites, the Amorites, and the Jebusites.

**11** Look, the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth will go in front of you into the Jordan River.

**12** Now choose 12 men from the tribes of Israel, 1 man from each tribe.

**13** The priests carry the ark of the LORD, the Lord of all the earth. When their feet go into the water of the Jordan, the water will stop. The water that comes down from upstream will stop and stand up as a wall."

**14** The people left their tents to go across the Jordan River. The priests carried the ark of the covenant in front of the people.

**15** It was the time of harvest, and the Jordan River was full above all its banks. The priests who carried the ark came to the Jordan. Their feet touched the edge of the water.

**16** Then the water that came down from upstream stopped. The water stood up as a wall a long distance away, at the city of Adam near Zarethan. The water that flowed down to the Sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea, stopped completely. The people went across near Jericho.

**17** The priests who carried the ark of the covenant of the LORD stood firmly on dry ground in the middle of the Jordan. All the people of Israel went across on dry ground. The priests stood there until all the people were across the Jordan.

## Chapter 4

**1** All the people went across the Jordan River. Then the LORD spoke to Joshua. He said,

**2** "Choose 12 men from the people, 1 man from each tribe.

**3** Give them this command: 'Take 12 stones from the middle of the Jordan, from the place where the priests stood. Carry the stones with you. Put them down in the place where you will stay tonight.'"

**4** Then Joshua called the 12 men that he chose from the people of Israel, 1 man from each tribe.

**5** Joshua said to them, "Go in front of the ark of the LORD your God into the middle of the Jordan. Each man must lift a stone onto his shoulder. There must be 1 stone for each tribe of the people of Israel.

**6** These stones will be a sign among you. In the future, your children will ask, 'What do these stones mean?'

**7** Then tell them, 'The water of the Jordan stopped in front of the ark of the covenant of the LORD. When the ark went across the Jordan, the water stopped.' These stones will help the people of Israel to remember this event for all time."

**8** The people of Israel did as Joshua told them. They took 12 stones from the middle of the Jordan, as the LORD told Joshua. There was 1 stone for each tribe of the people of Israel. They carried the stones to the place where they stayed. They put the stones down there.

**9** Joshua also put 12 stones in the middle of the Jordan, at the place where the priests with the ark of the covenant stood. These stones are there today.

**10** The priests who carried the ark stood in the middle of the Jordan. They stood there until the people did all the things that the LORD told Joshua. Moses also gave Joshua these commands. The people went across quickly.

**11** When all the people were across, the ark of the LORD and the priests went across in front of the people.

**12** The men of Reuben, the men of Gad, and half the tribe of Manasseh went across in front of the people of Israel. They carried their weapons, as Moses told them.

**13** Approximately 40,000 soldiers, ready for war, went across in front of the LORD to the plains of Jericho.

**14** On that day, the LORD made Joshua great in the eyes of all Israel. The people respected Joshua all his life, as they respected Moses.

**15** The LORD said to Joshua,

**16** "Tell the priests who carry the ark of the testimony to come up out of the Jordan."

**17** Thus, Joshua gave a command to the priests. He said, "Come up out of the Jordan."

**18** The priests who carried the ark of the covenant of the LORD came up out of the middle of the Jordan. When their feet touched the dry land, the water of the Jordan went back to its place. The water flowed above all its banks again, as before.

**19** The people came up out of the Jordan on the 10th day of the 1st month. They made their camp at Gilgal, on the east border of Jericho.

**20** Joshua put up the 12 stones at Gilgal. The people took these stones from the Jordan.

**21** He said to the people of Israel, "In the future, your children will ask their fathers, 'What do these stones mean?'

**22** Then tell your children, 'Israel went across this Jordan River on dry land.'

**23** The LORD your God made the water of the Jordan dry in front of you until you were across. He did the same thing at the Red Sea. He made the Red Sea dry in front of us until we were across.

**24** He did this so that all the people of the earth will know that the hand of the LORD is strong. He did this so that you will respect the LORD your God for all time."

## Chapter 5

**1** All the kings of the Amorites on the west side of the Jordan heard about these things. All the kings of the Canaanites near the sea also heard. They heard that the LORD made the water of the Jordan dry until the people of Israel were across. Then the kings became very afraid. They had no courage because of the people of Israel.

**2** At that time the LORD said to Joshua, "Make knives from sharp stones. Circumcise the men of Israel again."

**3** Thus, Joshua made knives from sharp stones. He circumcised the men of Israel at Gibeath Haaraloth (the hill of foreskins).

**4** This is why Joshua circumcised them. All the males who came out of Egypt, all the soldiers, died in the desert on the journey after they left Egypt.

**5** All the men who came out of Egypt received circumcision. But nobody circumcised the boys born in the desert on the journey from Egypt.

**6** The people of Israel walked in the desert for 40 years. During that time, all the soldiers who came out of Egypt died, because they did not obey the LORD. So the LORD made this promise to them: 'You will not see the land.' He promised to give this land to our fathers. It is a land with much milk and honey.

**7** Joshua circumcised the sons of those soldiers. God made these sons the new people of Israel. Nobody circumcised them on the journey. Thus, they did not have circumcision.

**8** After Joshua circumcised all the men, they stayed in their places in the camp. They stayed there until their bodies healed.

**9** Then the LORD said to Joshua, "Today I removed from you the shame of Egypt." Thus, the name of that place is Gilgal today.

**10** The people of Israel made their camp at Gilgal on the plains of Jericho. They kept the Passover on the evening of the 14th day of the month.

**11** On the day after the Passover, they ate some of the food that grew in the land. On that same day they ate bread without yeast and cooked grain.

**12** On the day after they ate the food of the land, the manna stopped. The people of Israel did not have manna again. That year they ate the food that grew in the land of Canaan.

**13** Joshua was near Jericho. He looked up and saw a man in front of him. The man held a sword in his hand. Joshua went to him and said, "Are you on our side or on the side of our enemies?"

**14** The man said, "Neither. I am the commander of the army of the LORD, and I come now." Then Joshua bowed down with his face to the ground and worshiped. He said, "My lord, what do you tell me, your servant?"

**15** The commander of the army of the LORD said to Joshua, "Remove your shoes from your feet. The place where you stand is holy." Joshua did this.

## Chapter 6

**1** The people of Jericho closed the gates of the city tightly because of the people of Israel. Nobody went out and nobody came in.

**2** The LORD said to Joshua, "Look, I give Jericho, its king, and its strong soldiers to you.

**3** All your soldiers must march around the city 1 time each day. Do this for 6 days.

**4** 7 priests must carry 7 trumpets made from the horns of male sheep. They must go in front of the ark. On the 7th day, march around the city 7 times. The priests must blow the trumpets.

**5** The priests will make a long sound with the horns. When you hear the sound of the trumpet, all the people must shout loudly. Then the wall of the city will fall down. All the people will go up into the city. Each man will go straight in."

**6** Joshua the son of Nun called the priests. He said to them, "Lift the ark of the covenant. 7 priests must carry 7 trumpets of horns in front of the ark of the LORD."

**7** He said to the people, "Go forward and march around the city. The soldiers with weapons must go in front of the ark of the LORD."

**8** After Joshua spoke to the people, the 7 priests with the 7 trumpets went forward in front of the LORD. They blew the trumpets. The ark of the covenant of the LORD followed them.

**9** The soldiers with weapons went in front of the priests who blew the trumpets. Other soldiers came behind the ark. The priests continued to blow the trumpets.

**10** Joshua gave a command to the people. He said, "Do not shout. Do not make a noise with your voice. Do not say a word until the day that I tell you to shout. Then shout."

**11** Thus, the people carried the ark of the LORD around the city 1 time. Then they went back into the camp and stayed there that night.

**12** Joshua got up early in the morning. The priests lifted the ark of the LORD.

**13** The 7 priests with the 7 trumpets went in front of the ark of the LORD. They continued to walk and blow the trumpets. The soldiers with weapons went in front of them. Other soldiers came behind the ark of the LORD. The priests continued to blow the trumpets.

**14** On the 2nd day, they marched around the city 1 time. Then they went back to the camp. They did this for 6 days.

**15** On the 7th day, they got up early at dawn. They marched around the city in the same way, but 7 times. Only on that day did they march around the city 7 times.

**16** The 7th time, the priests blew the trumpets. Joshua said to the people, "Shout! The LORD gives you the city.

**17** The city and all the things in it belong to the LORD for destruction. Only Rahab the prostitute will live. She and all the people with her in her house will live, because she hid the men that we sent.

**18** Do not take any of the things that are for destruction. If you take them, you will also be for destruction. You will cause the camp of Israel to be for destruction, and you will bring trouble on it.

**19** All the silver and gold and all the containers of bronze and iron are holy to the LORD. They must go into the treasury of the LORD."

**20** The priests blew the trumpets, and the people shouted. When the people heard the sound of the trumpet, they shouted loudly. Then the wall fell down. The people went up into the city. Each man went straight in, and they took the city.

**21** They destroyed all the people and animals in the city with the sword. They killed men and women, young and old people. They killed the cattle, the sheep, and the donkeys.

**22** Joshua spoke to the 2 men who spied on the country. He said, "Go into the house of the prostitute. Bring out the woman and all her family, as you promised her."

**23** Thus, the young men who were spies went in. They brought out Rahab, her father, her mother, her brothers, and all the people who were with her. They brought out all her family. They put them in a place outside the camp of Israel.

**24** Then the people burned the city and all the things in it. But they put the silver, the gold, and the containers of bronze and iron into the treasury of the house of the LORD.

**25** But Joshua kept Rahab the prostitute alive. He also kept her family and all her people alive. She lives in Israel today. Joshua saved her because she hid the men that he sent to spy on Jericho.

**26** At that time Joshua made a serious promise in front of the people. He said, "The LORD will curse the man who builds this city Jericho again. When he builds the base of the city, his oldest son will die. When he puts up its gates, his youngest son will die."

**27** Thus, the LORD was with Joshua. People in all the country heard about him.

## Chapter 7

**1** But the people of Israel did not obey the command about the things for destruction. Achan took some of these things. Achan was the son of Carmi, the son of Zabdi, the son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah. Thus, the LORD became very angry with the people of Israel.

**2** Joshua sent men from Jericho to Ai. Ai is near Beth Aven, on the east side of Bethel. He told them, "Go up and look at the country." The men went up and looked at Ai.

**3** They came back to Joshua and said to him, "Do not send all the people. Send approximately 2,000 or 3,000 men to attack Ai. Do not cause all the people to go there, because the people of Ai are few."

**4** Thus, approximately 3,000 men went up there. But they ran away from the men of Ai.

**5** The men of Ai killed approximately 36 of them. The men of Ai chased them from the city gate to Shebarim. They killed them on the hillside. Then the people of Israel lost their courage completely.

**6** Joshua tore his clothes. He bowed down with his face to the ground in front of the ark of the LORD until evening. The leaders of Israel did the same thing. They put dust on their heads.

**7** Joshua said, "Oh, Lord GOD, why did you bring these people across the Jordan River? Did you bring us here to give us to the Amorites to destroy us? We wish that we stayed on the other side of the Jordan!

**8** Oh, Lord, what can I say now? Israel ran away from its enemies!

**9** The Canaanites and all the people of the land will hear about this. They will come around us and kill us all. Then nobody on the earth will remember our name. What will you do for your great name?"

**10** The LORD said to Joshua, "Get up! Why do you lie with your face on the ground?

**11** Israel sinned. The people did not obey my covenant that I gave them. They took some of the things that were for destruction. They stole and they lied. They put those things among their own possessions.

**12** That is why the people of Israel cannot stand against their enemies. They run away from their enemies, because they are now for destruction. I will not be with you again until you destroy the things for destruction that are among you.

**13** Get up! Make the people holy. Tell them, 'Make yourselves holy for tomorrow. The LORD, the God of Israel, says this: "Israel, there are things for destruction among you. You cannot stand against your enemies until you remove these things."'

**14** In the morning, come forward tribe by tribe. The LORD will choose 1 tribe. That tribe must come forward family by family. The LORD will choose 1 family. That family must come forward household by household. The LORD will choose 1 household. That household must come forward man by man.

**15** The LORD will choose the man who has the things for destruction. You must burn that man and all his possessions with fire. He did not obey the covenant of the LORD. He did a shameful thing in Israel."

**16** Joshua got up early in the morning. He caused Israel to come forward tribe by tribe. The LORD chose the tribe of Judah.

**17** Joshua caused the families of Judah to come forward. The LORD chose the family of Zerah. Joshua caused the family of Zerah to come forward man by man. The LORD chose Zabdi.

**18** Joshua caused the household of Zabdi to come forward man by man. The LORD chose Achan, the son of Carmi, the son of Zabdi, the son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah.

**19** Joshua said to Achan, "My son, give glory to the LORD, the God of Israel. Tell him your sin. Tell me now what you did. Do not hide it from me."

**20** Achan answered Joshua, "It is true. I sinned against the LORD, the God of Israel. This is what I did.

**21** Among the things that we took from the city, I saw a beautiful coat from Babylonia. I also saw 200 shekels (2.3 kilograms) of silver and a bar of gold with a weight of 50 shekels (570 grams). I wanted them, and I took them. They are in the ground below my tent. The silver is below the other things."

**22** Thus, Joshua sent men, and they ran to the tent. The things were in the ground below the tent, and the silver was below them.

**23** The men took the things from the tent. They brought them to Joshua and to all the people of Israel. They put them on the ground in front of the LORD.

**24** Then Joshua and all Israel took Achan the son of Zerah. They also took the silver, the coat, and the bar of gold. They took his sons and his daughters, his cattle, his donkeys, and his sheep. They took his tent and all his possessions. They brought them to the Valley of Achor.

**25** Joshua said, "Why did you bring this trouble on us? Today the LORD will bring trouble on you." Then all Israel threw stones at Achan and killed him. They threw stones at his family and killed them. Then they burned them with fire.

**26** They put a large pile of stones over Achan. This pile is there today. Then the LORD stopped his strong anger. Thus, the name of that place is the Valley of Achor (Valley of Trouble) today.

## Chapter 8

**1** The LORD said to Joshua, "Do not be afraid. Do not lose your courage. Take all your soldiers with you and go up to Ai. Look, I give you the king of Ai, his people, his city, and his land.

**2** Do to Ai and its king the same things that you did to Jericho and its king. But this time you can keep the possessions and the animals for yourselves. Put some soldiers in a hidden position behind the city."

**3** Thus, Joshua and all his soldiers got ready to go up to Ai. Joshua chose 30,000 strong and brave soldiers. He sent them out during the night.

**4** He gave them this command: "Hide behind the city and wait. Do not go very far from the city. All of you must be ready.

**5** I and all the people with me will go near the city. The men of the city will come out to attack us, as they did before. Then we will run away from them.

**6** They will chase us until we pull them away from the city. They will say, 'The Israelites run away from us as they did before.' Thus, we will run away from them.

**7** Then come out of your hidden position and take the city. The LORD your God will give the city to you.

**8** When you take the city, set it on fire. Do as the LORD tells you. Look, I give you this command."

**9** Thus, Joshua sent them out. They went to their hidden position and waited between Bethel and Ai, on the west side of Ai. But Joshua stayed with the people that night.

**10** Joshua got up early in the morning and prepared his soldiers. He and the leaders of Israel went in front of the soldiers to Ai.

**11** All the soldiers with Joshua went up and came near the city. They made their camp on the north side of Ai. There was a valley between them and Ai.

**12** Joshua took approximately 5,000 men. He put them in a hidden position between Bethel and Ai, on the west side of the city.

**13** Thus, the soldiers were in their positions. The main army was on the north side of the city. The hidden soldiers were on the west side of the city. That night Joshua went into the middle of the valley.

**14** The king of Ai saw the Israelites. Then he and all the men of the city got up early and quickly went out to fight against Israel. They went to a place in front of the plain. But the king did not know that soldiers waited in a hidden position behind the city.

**15** Joshua and all Israel acted as if the men of Ai defeated them. They ran away toward the desert.

**16** All the men in Ai got a command to chase the Israelites. They chased Joshua and went far from the city.

**17** All the men in Ai and Bethel went out after Israel. Nobody stayed in the cities. They left the city open and chased Israel.

**18** Then the LORD said to Joshua, "Hold out the spear in your hand toward Ai. I will give the city to you." Thus, Joshua held out the spear in his hand toward the city.

**19** When Joshua held out his hand, the hidden soldiers quickly got up from their position. They ran into the city and took it. They quickly set the city on fire.

**20** The men of Ai looked back and saw the smoke of their city. The smoke went up into the sky. The men of Ai could not escape in any direction. Then the Israelites who ran toward the desert turned back against the men who chased them.

**21** Joshua and all Israel saw that the hidden soldiers took the city. They saw the smoke that went up from the city. Then they turned back and killed the men of Ai.

**22** The other Israelites came out of the city to fight against the men of Ai. Thus, the men of Ai were in the middle, with Israelites on the 2 sides. The Israelites killed all of them. Nobody stayed alive or escaped.

**23** But the Israelites caught the king of Ai alive. They brought him to Joshua.

**24** The Israelites killed all the men of Ai in the fields and in the desert, where they chased them. All of them died by the sword. Then all the Israelites went back to Ai and killed the people in the city with the sword.

**25** On that day 12,000 men and women died. These were all the people of Ai.

**26** Joshua did not lower the hand that held out the spear until the Israelites destroyed all the people of Ai.

**27** But the Israelites kept the animals and the possessions of that city for themselves. The LORD gave this command to Joshua.

**28** Joshua burned Ai. He made it a pile of ruins for all time. It is empty today.

**29** Joshua hanged the king of Ai on a tree until evening. At sunset, Joshua told the people to take the body down from the tree. They threw the body down at the city gate. They put a large pile of stones on it. That pile is there today.

**30** Then Joshua built an altar to the LORD, the God of Israel, on Mount Ebal.

**31** Moses, the servant of the LORD, gave this command to the people of Israel. The command is in the book of the law of Moses. Joshua made the altar of whole stones. Nobody cut these stones with iron tools. On the altar the people offered burnt offerings and peace offerings to the LORD.

**32** There, Joshua wrote a copy of the law of Moses on the stones. He wrote it in front of the people of Israel.

**33** All Israel stood on the 2 sides of the ark, in front of the priests. The priests were Levites, and they carried the ark of the covenant of the LORD. The leaders, officers, and judges were there. The foreigners and the people of Israel were there. Half of the people stood in front of Mount Gerizim. Half of the people stood in front of Mount Ebal. Moses, the servant of the LORD, told them to do this when they blessed the people of Israel.

**34** After that, Joshua read all the words of the law. He read the blessings and the curses. He read all the things in the book of the law.

**35** Joshua read each word of all the commands of Moses. He read them in front of all the people of Israel. The women, the small children, and the foreigners who lived among them were also there.

## Chapter 9

**1** Many kings lived on the west side of the Jordan River. They lived in the hills, in the low hills, and along all the coast of the Great Sea to Lebanon. They were the kings of the Hittites, the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites. These kings heard about the things that Israel did.

**2** Thus, they came together as 1 army to fight against Joshua and Israel.

**3** The people who lived in Gibeon heard what Joshua did to Jericho and Ai.

**5** They wore old shoes that had repairs on them. They wore old clothes. All the bread that they took for food was dry and in pieces.

**10** We also heard about all the things that he did to the 2 kings of the Amorites on the east side of the Jordan. He defeated Sihon, the king of Heshbon, and Og, the king of Bashan, who lived in Ashtaroth.

**15** Joshua made peace with them. He made a treaty with them to let them live. The leaders of the people of Israel made a promise to them.

**16** 3 days after the Israelites made the treaty, they heard that these people were their neighbors. They lived near Israel.

**17** Thus, the people of Israel went out. On the 3rd day they came to the cities of these people. These cities were Gibeon, Kephirah, Beeroth, and Kiriath Jearim.

**18** But the people of Israel did not attack them. The leaders of the people made a promise to them by the LORD, the God of Israel. All the people of Israel complained against the leaders.

**3** Thus, Adoni-Zedek, the king of Jerusalem, sent a message to 4 kings. He sent it to Hoham king of Hebron, Piram king of Jarmuth, Japhia king of Lachish, and Debir king of Eglon. He said,

**10** 1 man of you can chase 1,000 men. The LORD your God fights for you, as he promised you.

**11** Thus, be very careful to love the LORD your God.

**12** But you possibly will turn away from the LORD. You possibly will join with the nations that remain among you. You possibly will marry their people and mix with them.

**13** If you do this, then know this: the LORD your God will not push out these nations in front of you again. These nations will be a trap for you. They will hurt you as a whip on your back and as thorns in your eyes. This will continue until you die and leave this good land. The LORD your God gave you this land.

**14** Now I will die soon, as all people on the earth die. You know with all your heart and with all your soul that the LORD your God kept all his good promises to you. All of them came true. Not 1 of them failed.

**15** The LORD your God kept all his good promises to you. In the same way, he will also bring all the bad things on you that he warned you about. He will do this until he removes you from this good land. The LORD your God gave you this land.

**16** If you do not obey the covenant of the LORD your God, then he will be angry. If you serve other gods and bow down to them, then the LORD will be very angry with you. Then you will die quickly and leave the good land that he gave you."

## Chapter 24

**1** Joshua called all the tribes of Israel together at Shechem. He called the elders, the leaders, the judges, and the officers of Israel. They came and stood in front of God.

**2** Joshua said to all the people, "The LORD, the God of Israel, says this. 'A long time ago, your ancestors lived on the other side of the Euphrates River. Terah, the father of Abraham and Nahor, lived there. They served other gods.

**3** But I took your father Abraham from the other side of the river. I led him through all the land of Canaan. I gave him many descendants. I gave him a son, Isaac.

**4** I gave Isaac 2 sons, Jacob and Esau. I gave Esau the hills of Seir as his property. But Jacob and his sons went down into Egypt.

**5** Then I sent Moses and Aaron. I sent disasters on Egypt by the things that I did there. After that, I brought you out.

**6** I brought your fathers out of Egypt, and you came to the sea. The Egyptians chased your fathers with chariots and soldiers on horses to the Red Sea.

**7** Your fathers called to the LORD for help. Then he put darkness between you and the Egyptians. He brought the sea over the Egyptians and covered them. You saw with your own eyes what I did in Egypt. Then you lived in the desert for a long time.

**8** I brought you into the land of the Amorites, who lived on the east side of the Jordan. They fought against you, but I gave them to you. I destroyed them in front of you, and you took their land.

**9** Then Balak the son of Zippor, the king of Moab, got ready to fight against Israel. He sent a message to Balaam the son of Beor. He asked Balaam to curse you.

**10** But I did not listen to Balaam. Thus, Balaam blessed you again and again. I saved you from Balak.

**11** Then you went across the Jordan and came to Jericho. The men of Jericho fought against you. The Amorites, the Perizzites, the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Girgashites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites also fought against you. But I gave them to you.

**12** I sent the hornet (an insect that stings) in front of you. The hornet pushed out the 2 kings of the Amorites in front of you. You did not do this with your sword or your bow.

**13** I gave you a land that you did not work for. I gave you cities that you did not build, and you live in them. You eat fruit from vineyards and olive trees that you did not plant.'

**14** Thus, now respect the LORD. Serve him honestly and faithfully. Throw away the gods that your ancestors served on the other side of the Euphrates River and in Egypt. Serve the LORD.

**15** Possibly you do not want to serve the LORD. Then choose today whom you will serve. You can serve the gods of your ancestors on the other side of the river. Or you can serve the gods of the Amorites, in whose land you live. But my family and I will serve the LORD."

**16** The people answered, "We will never leave the LORD to serve other gods!

**17** The LORD our God brought us and our fathers out of the land of Egypt, where we were slaves. He did those great miracles in front of us. He kept us safe on all our journey and among all the nations that we went through.

**18** The LORD pushed out all the nations in front of us. He pushed out the Amorites who lived in the land. Thus, we will also serve the LORD, because he is our God."

**19** Joshua said to the people, "You cannot serve the LORD. He is a holy God. He is a jealous God. He will not forgive your rebellion or your sins.

**20** If you leave the LORD and serve foreign gods, then he will turn against you. He was good to you before. But then he will bring disaster on you and destroy you."

**21** The people said to Joshua, "No! We will serve the LORD."

**22** Joshua said to the people, "You are witnesses against yourselves. You chose to serve the LORD." The people said, "Yes, we are witnesses."

**23** Joshua said, "Then throw away the foreign gods that are among you. Give your hearts completely to the LORD, the God of Israel."

**24** The people said to Joshua, "We will serve the LORD our God. We will obey him."

**25** Thus, on that day at Shechem, Joshua made a covenant with the people. He gave them laws and rules.

**26** Joshua wrote these words in the book of the law of God. Then he took a large stone. He put the stone up there below the oak tree near the holy place of the LORD.

**27** Joshua said to all the people, "Look, this stone will be a witness against us. It heard all the words that the LORD spoke to us. Thus, it will be a witness against you if you are not faithful to your God."

**28** Then Joshua sent the people away. Each person went to his own property.

**29** After these things, Joshua the son of Nun, the servant of the LORD, died. He was 110 years old.

**30** The people buried him on his own land at Timnath Serah, in the hills of Ephraim. This place is north of Mount Gaash.

**31** Israel served the LORD during all the life of Joshua. Israel also served the LORD during the lives of the elders who lived after Joshua. These elders knew all the things that the LORD did for Israel.

**32** The people of Israel brought the bones of Joseph up from Egypt. They buried the bones at Shechem, in the field that Jacob bought. Jacob bought the field from the sons of Hamor, the father of Shechem. He paid 100 pieces of silver for it. This field became the property of the descendants of Joseph.

**33** Eleazar the son of Aaron also died. The people buried him at Gibeah in the hills of Ephraim. The people gave this place to his son Phinehas.
