# Deuteronomy

## Chapter 1

**1** These are the words that Moses spoke to all Israel on the east side of the Jordan. Moses spoke them in the wilderness, in the plain opposite Suph. This plain is between Paran, Tophel, Laban, Hazeroth and Dizahab.

**2** The journey from Horeb to Kadesh-barnea by the Mount Seir road is 11 days.

**3** In the 40th year, on the first day of the 11th month, Moses spoke to the people of Israel. He told them all that the LORD commanded him to tell them.

**4** This occurred after Moses killed Sihon and Og. Sihon was the king of the Amorites, and he lived in Heshbon. Og was the king of Bashan, and he lived at Astaroth in Edrei.

**5** On the east side of the Jordan, in the land of Moab, Moses started to explain this law. He said,

**6** "The LORD our God spoke to us at Horeb. He said, 'You stayed at this mountain for a sufficient time.

**7** Turn and start your journey. Go to the hill country of the Amorites and to all the areas near it. Go to the plain, the hills, the lowlands, the south and the seacoast. Go to the land of the Canaanites and to Lebanon, as far as the great river, the Euphrates River.

**8** Look, I put the land in front of you. Go in and take the land. The LORD made a promise to give this land to your fathers, Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, and to their descendants after them.'

**9** "At that time I said to you, 'I cannot carry the responsibility for you alone.

**10** The LORD your God made you many. Now you are as many as the stars of the sky.

**11** I pray that the LORD, the God of your fathers, will make you 1,000 times more than you are now. I pray that he will bless you, as he promised.

**12** But I alone cannot carry your problems, your loads and your arguments.

**13** Choose wise men from your tribes. Choose men who understand and who have a good reputation. I will make them your leaders.'

**14** You answered me, 'Your plan is good. We must do it.'

**15** So I took the chief men of your tribes, wise men with a good reputation. I made them leaders over you. Some were commanders of 1,000 men, some of 100, some of 50 and some of 10. Others were officers for your tribes.

**16** At that time I gave a command to your judges. I said, 'Hear the disputes between your brothers. Judge correctly between each man and his brother, or the foreigner who lives with him.

**17** Do not show favor to one person more than to another when you judge. Hear the small person and the great person equally. Do not be afraid of any man, because the judgment is God's. Bring to me each case that is too difficult for you, and I will hear it.'

**18** At that time I told you all the things that you must do.

**19** "Then we went away from Horeb. We went through all that large and terrible wilderness that you saw on the road to the hill country of the Amorites. We did as the LORD our God commanded us. Then we came to Kadesh-barnea.

**20** I said to you, 'You came to the hill country of the Amorites. The LORD our God gives this land to us.

**21** Look, the LORD your God put the land in front of you. Go up and take it, as the LORD, the God of your fathers, told you. Do not be afraid, and do not lose courage.'

**22** "Then all of you came to me. You said, 'We will send men in front of us to examine the land. They will tell us which road to use and which cities we will come to.'

**23** I thought that this plan was good. So I chose 12 men from you, 1 man from each tribe.

**24** The men went up into the hill country. They came to the Valley of Eshcol and examined it.

**25** They took some of the fruit of the land and brought it down to us. They gave us a report. They said, 'The land that the LORD our God gives us is good.'

**26** "But you did not want to go up. You disobeyed the command of the LORD your God.

**27** You complained in your tents. You said, 'The LORD hates us. Thus he brought us out of Egypt to give us to the Amorites. He wants to destroy us.

**28** Where can we go? Our brothers caused us to lose courage. They said, "The people are larger and taller than we are. The cities are large, and their walls go up to the sky. Also, we saw the sons of the Anakim there."'

**29** "Then I said to you, 'Do not be afraid of them.

**30** The LORD your God goes in front of you. He will fight for you. He will do as he did for you in Egypt, where you saw it.

**31** He also helped you in the wilderness. There you saw that the LORD your God carried you, as a man carries his son. He carried you all the way until you came to this place.'

**32** But you did not trust the LORD your God.

**33** He went in front of you on the road to find a place for your camp. At night he went in fire, and in the day he went in a cloud. He showed you the road to use.

**34** "The LORD heard your words and he was angry. He made a solemn promise. He said,

**35** 'Not one man of this evil generation will see the good land that I promised to give to your fathers.

**36** Only Caleb the son of Jephunneh will see it. I will give him and his children the land that he walked on. I will do this because he obeyed the LORD completely.'

**37** Because of you, the LORD was also angry with me. He said, 'You also will not go into that land.

**38** Joshua the son of Nun, your helper, will go into it. Give him courage, because he will lead Israel to take the land.

**39** Your small children will go into the land. You said that your enemies will take them. Now your children do not know good from bad. But I will give the land to them, and they will take it.

**40** But you, turn around. Go into the wilderness on the road to the Red Sea.'

**41** "Then you answered me, 'We did sin against the LORD. We will go up and fight, as the LORD our God commanded us.' Each man put on his weapons. You thought that it was easy to go up into the hill country.

**42** But the LORD said to me, 'Tell them, "Do not go up, and do not fight. I am not with you. If you go, your enemies will defeat you."'

**43** So I told you, but you did not listen. You disobeyed the command of the LORD. You were proud and went up into the hill country.

**44** The Amorites who lived in that hill country came out against you. They chased you as bees chase a person. They defeated you in Seir, all the way to Hormah.

**45** You came back and cried in front of the LORD. But the LORD did not listen to you.

**46** So you stayed in Kadesh for many days.

## Chapter 2

**1** "Then we turned and went into the wilderness on the road to the Red Sea, as the LORD told me. For many days we went around Mount Seir.

**2** Then the LORD said to me,

**3** 'You went around this mountain for a sufficient time. Turn to the north.

**4** Give this command to the people: "You will go through the area of your brothers, the descendants of Esau, who live in Seir. They will be afraid of you. But be very careful.

**5** Do not start a fight with them. I will not give you any of their land, not even the width of a foot. I gave Mount Seir to Esau as his property.

**6** Pay them money for the food that you eat. Also pay them money for the water that you drink."'

**7** The LORD your God blessed you in all the work that you did. He knew about your journey through this large wilderness. During these 40 years the LORD your God was with you. You had all that you needed.

**8** "So we went past our brothers, the descendants of Esau, who live in Seir. We went away from the Arabah road, from Elath and Ezion-geber. Then we turned and went on the road to the wilderness of Moab.

**9** The LORD said to me, 'Do not attack the Moabites. Do not start a war with them. I will not give you any of their land as your property. I gave Ar to the descendants of Lot as their property.'

**10** The Emim lived there in the past. They were a large and strong people, and they were tall like the Anakim.

**11** People also thought that the Emim were giants, as the Anakim are. But the Moabites called them Emim.

**12** In the past the Horites also lived in Seir. But the descendants of Esau removed them. The descendants of Esau destroyed them and lived in their place. Israel did the same to the land that the LORD gave them as their property.

**13** The LORD said, 'Now go across the Zered Brook.' So we went across the Zered Brook.

**14** We went from Kadesh-barnea to the Zered Brook in 38 years. During that time, all the soldiers of that generation died in the camp. This occurred as the LORD promised them.

**15** The power of the LORD was against them. He removed them from the camp until all of them died.

**16** "All the soldiers among the people died.

**17** Then the LORD said to me,

**18** 'Today you will go through Ar, the area of Moab.

**19** You will come near the Ammonites. Do not attack them or start a fight with them. I will not give you any of the land of the Ammonites as your property. I gave that land to the descendants of Lot as their property.'

**20** People also thought that this land was a land of giants. Giants lived there in the past. The Ammonites called them Zamzummim.

**21** They were a large and strong people, and they were tall like the Anakim. But the LORD destroyed them in front of the Ammonites. The Ammonites removed them and lived in their place.

**22** The LORD did the same for the descendants of Esau, who live in Seir. He destroyed the Horites in front of them. The descendants of Esau removed the Horites and live in their place to this day.

**23** The Avvim lived in villages as far as Gaza. But the Caphtorim came from Caphtor and destroyed them. Then the Caphtorim lived in their place.

**24** "The LORD said, 'Go now. Start your journey and go across the Arnon River. Look, I give to you Sihon the Amorite, the king of Heshbon, and his land. Start to take his land, and fight a war with him.

**25** Today I will start to make all the nations on the earth afraid of you. They will hear about you. Then they will shake and feel great fear because of you.'

**26** "So I sent messengers from the wilderness of Kedemoth to Sihon the king of Heshbon. I gave them words of peace. I said,

**27** 'Let me go through your land. I will stay on the main road. I will not turn to the right or to the left.

**28** Sell me food for money, so that I can eat. Sell me water for money, so that I can drink. Only let me walk through your land.

**29** The descendants of Esau, who live in Seir, let me do this. The Moabites, who live in Ar, also let me do this. Let me go until I go across the Jordan into the land that the LORD our God gives us.'

**30** But Sihon the king of Heshbon did not let us go through his land. The LORD your God made his spirit hard and his heart stubborn. The LORD did this to give Sihon to you, as you see today.

**31** "The LORD said to me, 'Look, I started to give Sihon and his land to you. Start to take his land as your property.'

**32** Then Sihon and all his army came out to fight against us at Jahaz.

**33** The LORD our God gave Sihon to us. We killed him, his sons and all his army.

**34** At that time we took all his cities. We completely destroyed the men, the women and the children of each city. We did not let anyone stay alive.

**35** We took only the animals and the valuable things from the cities that we captured.

**36** No city was too strong for us. We took all the cities from Aroer, on the edge of the Arnon Valley, and from the city in the valley, as far as Gilead. The LORD our God gave all of them to us.

**37** But you did not go near the land of the Ammonites. You did not go near the area along the Jabbok River or the cities in the hill country. You did not go near any place that the LORD our God told us not to go.

## Chapter 3

**1** "Then we turned and went up on the road to Bashan. Og the king of Bashan and all his army came out to fight against us at Edrei.

**2** The LORD said to me, 'Do not be afraid of Og. I will give him, all his army and his land to you. Do to him as you did to Sihon the king of the Amorites, who lived at Heshbon.'

**3** So the LORD our God also gave to us Og the king of Bashan and all his army. We killed them until no one stayed alive.

**4** At that time we took all his cities. We took all 60 cities from them. We took all the area of Argob, the kingdom of Og in Bashan.

**5** All these cities had high walls, gates and bars. There were also very many towns with no walls.

**6** We completely destroyed them, as we did to Sihon the king of Heshbon. We destroyed the men, the women and the children of each city.

**7** But we took all the animals and the valuable things from the cities for ourselves.

**8** "At that time we took the land on the east side of the Jordan from the 2 kings of the Amorites. We took the land from the Arnon Valley to Mount Hermon.

**9** The Sidonians call Hermon 'Sirion,' and the Amorites call it 'Senir.'

**10** We took all the cities of the plateau, all Gilead and all Bashan as far as Salecah and Edrei. These were cities of the kingdom of Og in Bashan.

**11** Og the king of Bashan was the last of the giants. His bed was made of iron. It is in Rabbah of the Ammonites. The bed is 9 cubits long and 4 cubits wide, by the usual cubit.

**12** "At that time we took this land as our property. I gave to the Reubenites and the Gadites the land from Aroer, near the Arnon Valley. I also gave them half of the hill country of Gilead with its cities.

**13** I gave the remaining part of Gilead and all Bashan, the kingdom of Og, to the half tribe of Manasseh. I gave them all the area of Argob. People call all of Bashan the land of the giants.

**14** Jair, a descendant of Manasseh, took all the area of Argob as far as the border of the Geshurites and the Maacathites. He gave the villages of Bashan his name, Havvoth-jair. They have this name to this day.

**15** I gave Gilead to Machir.

**16** I gave to the Reubenites and the Gadites the land from Gilead to the Arnon Valley. The middle of the valley was the border. Their land went to the Jabbok River, which is the border of the Ammonites.

**17** Their land also included the Arabah, with the Jordan as its border. It went from Chinnereth to the Sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea, below the slopes of Pisgah on the east.

**18** "At that time I gave you this command: 'The LORD your God gave you this land as your property. All your strong men must take their weapons and go across in front of your brothers, the people of Israel.

**19** But your wives, your children and your animals will stay in the cities that I gave you. I know that you have many animals.

**20** Stay until the LORD gives rest to your brothers, as he gave rest to you. Stay until they take the land that the LORD your God gives them on the other side of the Jordan. Then each man of you can go back to the property that I gave you.'

**21** "At that time I gave this command to Joshua: 'You saw all that the LORD your God did to these 2 kings. The LORD will do the same to all the kingdoms where you will go.

**22** Do not be afraid of them. The LORD your God will fight for you.'

**23** "At that time I asked the LORD for help. I said,

**24** 'O Lord GOD, you started to show your servant your greatness and your strong hand. No god in heaven or on earth can do the great things that you do.

**25** Please let me go across and see the good land on the other side of the Jordan. Let me see that good hill country and Lebanon.'

**26** "But the LORD was angry with me because of you. He did not listen to me. The LORD said to me, 'That is sufficient. Do not speak to me about this again.

**27** Go up to the top of Pisgah. Look to the west, to the north, to the south and to the east. Look at the land with your eyes. You will not go across this Jordan.

**28** But give orders to Joshua. Give him courage and make him strong. He will go across in front of this people. He will lead them to take the land that you will see.'

**29** So we stayed in the valley opposite Beth-peor.

## Chapter 4

**1** "Now, Israel, listen to the laws and the rules that I teach you. Obey them so that you can live. Then you can go in and take the land that the LORD, the God of your fathers, gives you.

**2** Do not add to the commands that I give you. Do not remove anything from them. Keep the commands of the LORD your God that I give you.

**3** You saw what the LORD did at Baal-peor. The LORD your God destroyed from among you all the men who worshipped Baal of Peor.

**4** But all of you who stayed loyal to the LORD your God are alive today.

**5** Look, I taught you laws and rules, as the LORD my God commanded me. Obey them in the land that you go into to take as your property.

**6** Keep them and obey them. This will show your wisdom and your understanding to the nations. The nations will hear about all these laws. They will say, 'This great nation is a wise people who understand.'

**7** No other great nation has gods that are as near to them as the LORD our God is to us. He is near each time that we call to him.

**8** No other great nation has laws and rules that are as fair as all this law that I give you today.

**9** "But be careful, and watch yourself carefully. Do not forget the things that you saw. Do not let them go away from your heart during all your life. Teach them to your children and to your grandchildren.

**10** Remember the day that you stood in front of the LORD your God at Horeb. The LORD said to me, 'Gather the people to me. I will cause them to hear my words. Then they will learn to fear me all their lives on the earth. And they will teach my words to their children.'

**11** You came near and stood at the bottom of the mountain. The mountain burned with fire up to the sky. There was darkness, clouds and thick darkness.

**12** Then the LORD spoke to you from the fire. You heard the sound of words, but you saw no shape. You heard only a voice.

**13** He told you about his agreement (covenant), the 10 commandments. He commanded you to obey them. He wrote them on 2 tablets of stone.

**14** At that time the LORD commanded me to teach you laws and rules. You must obey them in the land that you go into to take as your property.

**15** "Thus be very careful. On the day that the LORD spoke to you at Horeb from the fire, you saw no shape.

**16** So do not do evil things. Do not make an idol for yourselves in the shape of any thing. Do not make the shape of a man or a woman.

**17** Do not make the shape of any animal on the earth or of any bird that flies in the air.

**18** Do not make the shape of any animal that moves on the ground or of any fish in the water below the earth.

**19** Be careful when you look up to the sky. You will see the sun, the moon, the stars and all the lights in the sky. Do not let them tempt you to bow down to them and worship them. The LORD your God gave them to all the nations on the earth.

**20** But the LORD took you and brought you out of Egypt, which was like a hot furnace for iron. He did this to make you his own people, as you are today.

**21** "Also, the LORD was angry with me because of you. He made a solemn promise that I will not go across the Jordan. I will not go into the good land that the LORD your God gives you as your property.

**22** I will die in this land. I will not go across the Jordan. But you will go across and take that good land.

**23** Be careful. Do not forget the covenant that the LORD your God made with you. Do not make an idol in the shape of any thing. The LORD your God told you not to do this.

**24** The LORD your God is a fire that burns everything. He is a jealous God.

**25** "You will have children and grandchildren. You will live in the land for a long time. If you then do evil things and make an idol in the shape of any thing, you do what is evil to the LORD your God. Thus you make him angry.

**26** If you do this, I tell heaven and earth to be witnesses against you today. You will quickly die and go out of the land that you go across the Jordan to take. You will not live there for long. You will be completely destroyed.

**27** The LORD will send you to many different nations. Only a few of you will stay alive among the nations where the LORD sends you.

**28** There you will serve gods that men made from wood and stone. These gods cannot see, hear, eat or smell.

**29** But from there you will look for the LORD your God. You will find him if you look for him with all your heart and with all your soul.

**30** You will have trouble, and all these things will occur to you. Then in the future, you will come back to the LORD your God and obey him.

**31** The LORD your God is a merciful God. He will not leave you or destroy you. He will not forget the covenant that he promised to your fathers.

**32** "Ask about the past, before you were born. Ask from the day that God made man on the earth. Ask from one end of the sky to the other end. Did a thing as great as this ever occur? Did anyone ever hear about a thing like this?

**33** Did any other people ever hear the voice of God speak from a fire, as you did, and stay alive?

**34** Did any god ever try to take a nation for himself from another nation? The LORD your God did this for you in Egypt, and you saw it. He used tests, signs, wonders and war. He used a strong hand, a powerful arm and great terrors.

**35** The LORD showed you these things so that you will know that the LORD is God. There is no other god.

**36** He let you hear his voice from heaven to teach you. On earth he showed you his great fire. You heard his words from the fire.

**37** He loved your fathers. Thus he chose their descendants after them. He was with you and brought you out of Egypt with his great power.

**38** He removed nations that were larger and stronger than you. He brought you into their land and gave it to you as your property, as it is today.

**39** So know this today, and think about it carefully: the LORD is God in heaven above and on the earth below. There is no other god.

**40** Obey his laws and his commands that I give you today. Then all will be well for you and for your children after you. You will live for a long time in the land that the LORD your God gives you for all time."

**41** Then Moses chose 3 cities on the east side of the Jordan.

**42** A person who killed another person by accident can run to one of these cities. If that person did not hate the dead person before, then he can go to one of these cities and stay alive.

**43** These are the cities: Bezer in the wilderness on the plateau, for the Reubenites; Ramoth in Gilead, for the Gadites; and Golan in Bashan, for the tribe of Manasseh.

**44** This is the law that Moses gave to the people of Israel.

**45** These are the laws, the rules and the decisions that Moses told the people of Israel after they came out of Egypt.

**46** He told them on the east side of the Jordan, in the valley opposite Beth-peor. This was in the land of Sihon, the king of the Amorites, who lived at Heshbon. Moses and the people of Israel defeated Sihon after they came out of Egypt.

**47** They took the land of Sihon and the land of Og the king of Bashan as their property. These 2 kings of the Amorites lived on the east side of the Jordan.

**48** The land went from Aroer, on the edge of the Arnon Valley, to Mount Sirion, which is Hermon.

**49** It included all the Arabah on the east side of the Jordan, as far as the Sea of the Arabah, below the slopes of Pisgah.

## Chapter 5

**1** Moses called all Israel together. He said to them, "Israel, hear the laws and the rules that I tell you today. Learn them and be careful to obey them.

**2** The LORD our God made a covenant with us at Horeb.

**3** The LORD did not make this covenant only with our fathers. He made it with us, all of us who are alive here today.

**4** The LORD spoke with you face to face from the fire on the mountain.

**5** At that time I stood between the LORD and you to tell you the word of the LORD. You were afraid of the fire, and you did not go up the mountain. The LORD said,

**6** 'I am the LORD your God. I brought you out of the land of Egypt, where you were slaves.

**7** Do not have other gods in addition to me.

**8** Do not make an idol for yourself. Do not make the shape of any thing in the sky above, on the earth below or in the water below the earth.

**9** Do not bow down to idols or worship them. I, the LORD your God, am a jealous God. I punish the children for the sins of the fathers who hate me. I punish them to the third and fourth generation.

**10** But I show love to thousands of generations of people who love me and obey my commands.

**11** Do not use the name of the LORD your God for an evil purpose. The LORD will punish each person who uses his name for an evil purpose.

**12** Keep the Sabbath day holy, as the LORD your God commanded you.

**13** Work for 6 days and do all your work.

**14** But the 7th day is a Sabbath to the LORD your God. On that day do not do any work. This rule is for you, your son, your daughter and your servants. It is for your ox, your donkey and all your animals. It is also for the foreigner who lives in your towns. Your servants must rest, as you do.

**15** Remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt. The LORD your God brought you out from there with a strong hand and a powerful arm. Thus the LORD your God commanded you to keep the Sabbath day.

**16** Honor your father and your mother, as the LORD your God commanded you. Then you will live a long time, and all will be well for you in the land that the LORD your God gives you.

**17** Do not murder.

**18** Do not commit adultery.

**19** Do not steal.

**20** Do not tell lies about your neighbor in court.

**21** Do not want to have your neighbor's wife. Do not want to have your neighbor's house, his field, his male servant, his female servant, his ox, his donkey or any thing that is your neighbor's.'

**22** "The LORD spoke these words with a loud voice to all of you at the mountain. He spoke from the fire, the cloud and the thick darkness. He did not add any other words. He wrote them on 2 tablets of stone and gave them to me.

**23** You heard the voice from the darkness, while the mountain burned with fire. Then all the leaders of your tribes and your elders came to me.

**24** You said, 'The LORD our God showed us his glory and his greatness. We heard his voice from the fire. Today we saw that a man can stay alive after God speaks to him.

**25** But why must we die now? This great fire will burn us up. If we hear the voice of the LORD our God again, we will die.

**26** No other human ever heard the voice of the living God speak from the fire, as we did, and stayed alive.

**27** You go near and hear all that the LORD our God says. Then tell us all that the LORD our God tells you. We will listen and obey.'

**28** "The LORD heard your words when you spoke to me. The LORD said to me, 'I heard the words that these people said to you. All that they said is good.

**29** I wish that they always had a heart that fears me and obeys all my commands. Then all would be well for them and for their children for all time.

**30** Go and tell them to go back to their tents.

**31** But you, stay here with me. I will tell you all the commands, the laws and the rules that you must teach them. They must obey them in the land that I give them as their property.'

**32** So be careful to do what the LORD your God commanded you. Do not turn to the right or to the left.

**33** Live in the way that the LORD your God commanded you. Then you will live, and all will be well for you. You will live for a long time in the land that you will take.

## Chapter 6

**1** "These are the commands, the laws and the rules that the LORD your God told me to teach you. Obey them in the land that you go into to take as your property.

**2** Fear the LORD your God, and keep all his laws and commands that I give you. You, your children and your grandchildren must keep them all your lives. Then you will live a long time.

**3** Israel, listen and be careful to obey. Then all will be well for you, and you will become very many. The LORD, the God of your fathers, promised you this. You will live in a land that has much milk and honey.

**4** "Israel, listen! The LORD is our God. The LORD is one.

**5** Love the LORD your God with all your heart, with all your soul and with all your strength.

**6** Keep in your heart these commands that I give you today.

**7** Teach them carefully to your children. Speak about them when you sit in your house and when you walk on the road. Speak about them when you lie down and when you get up.

**8** Tie them on your hand as a sign. Put them on your forehead as a mark.

**9** Write them on the doorposts of your house and on your gates.

**10** "The LORD your God will bring you into the land that he promised to your fathers, Abraham, Isaac and Jacob. He will give you large and good cities that you did not build.

**11** He will give you houses full of good things that you did not put there. He will give you wells that you did not dig, and vineyards and olive trees that you did not plant. You will eat and you will be satisfied.

**12** Then be careful. Do not forget the LORD, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, where you were slaves.

**13** Fear the LORD your God and serve him. Make your promises in his name.

**14** Do not worship other gods, the gods of the nations around you.

**15** The LORD your God, who is among you, is a jealous God. If you worship other gods, the LORD your God will become angry with you. He will destroy you from the earth.

**16** Do not test the LORD your God, as you tested him at Massah.

**17** Carefully obey the commands, the rules and the laws that the LORD your God gave you.

**18** Do what is right and good in the sight of the LORD. Then all will be well for you. You will go in and take the good land that the LORD promised to your fathers.

**19** He will push out all your enemies in front of you, as the LORD said.

**20** "In the future your son will ask you, 'What is the purpose of the rules, the laws and the decisions that the LORD our God gave you?'

**21** Then tell your son, 'We were slaves of Pharaoh in Egypt. But the LORD brought us out of Egypt with a strong hand.

**22** We saw the LORD do great and terrible signs and wonders against Egypt, Pharaoh and all his family.

**23** He brought us out from there. He did this to bring us into the land that he promised to our fathers and to give it to us.

## Chapter 15

**1** "At the end of each 7 years, cancel all debts.

**2** Do it in this way: Each person who lent money to his neighbor must cancel the debt. He must not demand payment from his neighbor or his brother, because the LORD announced the time to cancel debts.

**3** You can demand payment from a foreigner. But you must cancel any debt that your brother has to you.

**4** But there will be no poor people among you, because the LORD will greatly bless you. He will bless you in the land that the LORD your God gives you as your property.

**5** He will do this only if you carefully obey the LORD your God. Be careful to obey all these commands that I give you today.

**6** The LORD your God will bless you, as he promised you. You will lend money to many nations, but you will not borrow. You will rule many nations, but they will not rule you.

**7** "A poor man can live in one of your towns in the land that the LORD your God gives you. He can be one of your brothers. Do not be hard or selfish to your poor brother.

**8** Be generous to him. Lend him all that he needs.

**9** Be careful. Do not have an evil thought in your heart. Do not think, 'The 7th year, the year to cancel debts, is near.' Do not be unkind to your poor brother and give him nothing. If you do, he will cry to the LORD against you, and you will be guilty of sin.

**10** Give to him generously, and do not be sad when you give. Then the LORD your God will bless you in all your work and in all that you do.

**11** There will always be poor people in the land. Thus I command you to be generous to your brothers, the poor and the needy people in your land.

**12** "One of your people, a Hebrew man or a Hebrew woman, can sell himself or herself to you. That person will serve you for 6 years. In the 7th year, let that person go free.

**13** When you let the servant go free, do not send him away with nothing.

**14** Give him generously from your sheep, your grain and your wine. Give to him as the LORD your God blessed you.

**15** Remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt. The LORD your God made you free. Thus I give you this command today.

**16** But your servant can say to you, 'I do not want to go away from you.' He can say this because he loves you and your family, and all is well for him with you.

**17** If so, take a sharp tool and push it through his ear into the door. Then he will be your servant for all time. Do the same for your female servant.

**18** Do not be sad when you let your servant go free. During the 6 years, he served you for half the cost of a hired worker. Then the LORD your God will bless you in all that you do.

**19** "Make holy to the LORD your God all the firstborn males of your cattle and your sheep. Do not make the firstborn of your cattle work. Do not cut the wool of the firstborn of your sheep.

**20** Each year, you and your family must eat these animals in front of the LORD your God, in the place that the LORD will choose.

**21** But an animal can have a defect. It can be lame or blind, or it can have another bad defect. If so, do not sacrifice it to the LORD your God.

**22** Eat it in your towns. Clean and unclean persons can eat it. They can eat it as they eat gazelle or deer.

**23** But do not eat its blood. Pour the blood on the ground like water.

## Chapter 16

**1** "Keep the month of Abib. Keep the Passover to the LORD your God. In the month of Abib the LORD your God brought you out of Egypt at night.

**2** Sacrifice the Passover animal to the LORD your God from your sheep or your cattle. Do this in the place that the LORD will choose for his name.

**3** Do not eat bread with yeast with it. For 7 days eat bread without yeast, the bread of trouble. You went out of the land of Egypt very quickly. Remember all your life the day that you went out of the land of Egypt.

**4** For 7 days, there must be no yeast anywhere in your land. Do not keep until the morning any meat from the sacrifice that you make on the evening of the first day.

**5** Do not sacrifice the Passover in any town that the LORD your God gives you.

**6** Sacrifice it only in the place that the LORD your God will choose for his name. Sacrifice the Passover in the evening, when the sun goes down. This is the time of day when you went out of Egypt.

**7** Cook it and eat it in the place that the LORD your God will choose. In the morning, go back to your tents.

**8** For 6 days eat bread without yeast. On the 7th day, gather for a special meeting to the LORD your God. Do not do any work on that day.

**9** "Count 7 weeks. Start to count from the day when you start to cut the grain.

**10** Then keep the Festival of Weeks to the LORD your God. Bring a freewill offering to him. Give as much as the LORD your God blessed you.

**11** Be happy in front of the LORD your God in the place that he will choose for his name. You, your sons, your daughters and your servants must be happy. The Levites in your towns, the foreigners, the orphans and the widows among you must also be happy.

**12** Remember that you were slaves in Egypt. Be careful to obey these laws.

**13** "Keep the Festival of Shelters for 7 days. Do this after you gather the crops from your threshing floor and your winepress.

**14** Be happy at your festival. You, your sons, your daughters and your servants must be happy. The Levites, the foreigners, the orphans and the widows in your towns must also be happy.

**15** For 7 days, keep the festival to the LORD your God in the place that the LORD will choose. The LORD your God will bless all your crops and all your work. Thus you must be very happy.

**16** "3 times each year all your men must come to the LORD your God in the place that he will choose. They must come at the Festival of Bread Without Yeast, at the Festival of Weeks and at the Festival of Shelters. They must not come to the LORD with no gift.

**17** Each man must give as much as he can. He must give by the blessing that the LORD your God gave him.

**18** "Appoint judges and officers for each tribe in all the towns that the LORD your God gives you. They must judge the people fairly.

**19** Do not change justice. Do not show favor to one person more than to another. Do not take money to change your decision. Such money makes wise people blind. It changes the words of honest people.

**20** Always do what is fair. Then you will live and take the land that the LORD your God gives you.

**21** "Do not put a wooden Asherah pole next to the altar that you build for the LORD your God.

**22** Do not put up a stone pillar. The LORD your God hates these things.

## Chapter 17

**1** "Do not sacrifice to the LORD your God an ox or a sheep that has a defect or any bad problem. The LORD your God hates such sacrifices.

**2** A man or a woman in one of the towns that the LORD your God gives you can do evil in the sight of the LORD your God. That person can break his covenant.

**3** That person can worship and serve other gods. He can worship the sun, the moon or the stars, which I did not command.

**4** Someone can tell you about it. When you hear about it, examine the report carefully. The report can be true and certain. Such a hated thing can have occurred in Israel.

**5** If so, take the man or the woman who did this evil thing to the gate of your town. Throw stones at that person until he dies.

**6** You can kill a person only on the evidence of 2 or 3 witnesses. Do not kill a person on the evidence of only 1 witness.

**7** The witnesses must be the first to throw stones at the person to kill him. Then all the people must throw stones. Thus remove the evil from among you.

**8** "A case can be too difficult for you to judge. It can be about a murder, a dispute about rights or an attack. These cases can be disputes in your towns. Then go to the place that the LORD your God will choose.

**9** Go to the Levitical priests and to the judge who serves at that time. Ask them about the case. They will tell you the decision.

**10** Do what they tell you at the place that the LORD will choose. Be careful to do all that they tell you.

**11** Do as they teach you and as they tell you. Do not turn away from their decision to the right or to the left.

**12** A man can be proud and not obey the priest who serves the LORD your God there, or the judge. That man must die. Thus remove the evil from Israel.

**13** Then all the people will hear and be afraid. They will not be proud again.

**14** "You will come into the land that the LORD your God gives you. You will take it and live in it. Then you will say, 'I will put a king over me, as all the nations around me do.'

**15** Then be sure to make the king a man whom the LORD your God chooses. Choose a king from among your brothers. Do not make a foreigner your king, who is not your brother.

**16** But the king must not get many horses for himself. He must not send the people back to Egypt to get more horses. The LORD told you, 'Do not go back on that road again.'

**17** The king must not get many wives for himself. If he does, they will turn his heart away from the LORD. He must not get very much silver and gold for himself.

**18** When the king sits on the throne of his kingdom, he must write a copy of this law in a book. He must copy it from the book that the Levitical priests keep.

**19** He must keep this book with him and read it all his life. Then he will learn to fear the LORD his God. He will be careful to obey all the words of this law and these rules.

**20** Then he will not think that he is better than his brothers. He will not turn away from the commands to the right or to the left. Then he and his children will rule his kingdom in Israel for a long time.

## Chapter 18

**1** "The Levitical priests, all the tribe of Levi, will not have their own part of the land in Israel. They will eat the offerings that the people burn for the LORD. These offerings are their part.

**2** They will not have their own part of the land among their brothers. The LORD is their part, as he promised them.

**3** The people who offer an ox or a sheep as a sacrifice must give a part to the priest. They must give him the shoulder, the 2 cheeks and the stomach.

**4** Also give him the first part of your grain, your new wine and your olive oil. Give him the first wool that you cut from your sheep.

**5** The LORD your God chose the priest and his sons from all your tribes. He chose them to stand and serve in the name of the LORD for all time.

**6** "A Levite can move from any of your towns in Israel where he lives. He can come to the place that the LORD will choose, because he wants to serve there.

**7** Then he can serve in the name of the LORD his God, as all his brother Levites do. They stand there in front of the LORD.

**8** He will get an equal part of the food to eat. He can also keep the money from the sale of his family property.

**9** "You will come into the land that the LORD your God gives you. Do not learn to do the hated things that those nations do.

**10** No one among you must burn his son or his daughter as a sacrifice. No one must try to know the future by magic or signs. No one must use sorcery.

**11** No one must put spells on people. No one must ask spirits of the dead for help or try to speak with the dead.

**12** The LORD hates all people who do these things. Because of these hated things, the LORD your God pushes out those nations in front of you.

**13** Be completely loyal to the LORD your God.

**14** The nations whose land you will take listen to people who use sorcery and magic. But the LORD your God does not let you do these things.

**15** "The LORD your God will send you a prophet like me from among your own people. Listen to him.

**16** At Horeb, on the day that you gathered there, you asked the LORD your God for this. You said, 'Do not let us hear the voice of the LORD our God again. Do not let us see this great fire again. If we do, we will die.'

**17** Then the LORD said to me, 'What they said is good.

**18** I will send them a prophet like you from among their brothers. I will put my words in his mouth. He will tell them all that I command him.

**19** He will speak my words in my name. I will punish each person who does not listen to these words.

**20** But a prophet can be proud and speak a word in my name that I did not command him to speak. Or he can speak in the name of other gods. That prophet must die.'

**21** You can think, 'How can we know that the LORD did not speak a message?'

**22** A prophet can speak in the name of the LORD, but the thing does not occur. Then the LORD did not speak that message. That prophet was proud and spoke his own words. Do not be afraid of him.

## Chapter 19

**1** "The LORD your God will destroy the nations whose land he gives you. You will take their land and live in their cities and in their houses.

**2** Then choose 3 cities in the land that the LORD your God gives you as your property.

**3** Make roads to these cities. Divide into 3 parts the land that the LORD your God gives you as your property. Then any person who kills another person can run to one of these cities.

**4** "This is the rule for a person who kills another person and runs to one of these cities to stay alive: He killed his neighbor by accident. He did not hate his neighbor before.

**5** For example, a man goes into the forest with his neighbor to cut wood. He swings his axe to cut down a tree. The head of the axe comes off the handle and hits his neighbor. The neighbor dies. Then the man can run to one of these cities and stay alive.

**6** If the city is too far, the relative who must punish the killer can chase the killer while he is angry. He can catch the killer and kill him. But the killer must not die, because he did not hate his neighbor before.

**7** Thus I command you to choose 3 cities.

**8** "The LORD your God can make your land larger, as he promised your fathers. He can give you all the land that he promised to give to your fathers.

**9** He will do this if you carefully obey all these commands that I give you today. Love the LORD your God and always live in his ways. Then add 3 more cities to these 3 cities.

**10** Then no innocent person will die in the land that the LORD your God gives you as your property. And you will not be guilty of the death of an innocent person.

**11** "But a man can hate his neighbor and hide and wait for him. He can attack his neighbor and kill him. Then he can run to one of these cities.

**12** If so, the elders of his town must send men to bring him back from there. They must give him to the relative who must punish the killer. Then the killer will die.

**13** Do not feel pity for him. Remove from Israel the guilt of the death of an innocent person. Then all will be well for you.

**14** "Do not move the boundary stone of your neighbor. The people of the past put it there to mark his property. Do not move it in the land that the LORD your God gives you as your property.

**15** "One witness is not sufficient to find a man guilty of any crime or sin. Two or 3 witnesses must give evidence to prove the case.

**16** A false witness can accuse a man of a crime.

**17** Then the 2 men in the dispute must stand in front of the LORD. They must stand in front of the priests and the judges who serve at that time.

**18** The judges must examine the case carefully. The witness can be a false witness who told lies against his brother.

**19** If so, do to the false witness what he wanted to do to his brother. Thus remove the evil from among you.

**20** The other people will hear and be afraid. They will not do such an evil thing among you again.

**21** Do not feel pity. Use this rule: a life for a life, an eye for an eye, a tooth for a tooth, a hand for a hand and a foot for a foot.

## Chapter 20

**1** "You can go out to fight a war against your enemies. You can see horses, chariots and an army larger than yours. Do not be afraid of them. The LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, is with you.

**2** When you come near the battle, the priest must come forward and speak to the army.

**3** He must say to them, 'Israel, listen. Today you go to fight against your enemies. Do not lose courage. Do not be afraid, and do not shake with fear because of them.

**4** The LORD your God goes with you. He will fight for you against your enemies. He will save you.'

**5** "Then the officers must speak to the army. They must say, 'Did any man build a new house but not yet dedicate it? He must go back to his house. If he does not, he can die in the battle, and another man will dedicate the house.

**6** Did any man plant a vineyard but not yet eat its grapes? He must go back to his house. If he does not, he can die in the battle, and another man will eat the grapes.

**7** Is any man engaged to a woman but not yet married to her? He must go back to his house. If he does not, he can die in the battle, and another man will marry her.'

**8** The officers must also say, 'Is any man afraid or without courage? He must go back to his house. If he does not, he will cause his brothers to lose courage too.'

**9** When the officers stop speaking to the army, they must appoint commanders to lead the army.

**10** "When you come near a city to attack it, first offer peace to its people.

**11** The people of the city can accept peace and open their gates to you. If so, all the people in the city will work for you and serve you.

**12** But the city can refuse peace and fight against you. If so, put your army around the city to attack it.

**13** The LORD your God will give the city to you. Then kill all the men in it with the sword.

**14** But you can take for yourselves the women, the children, the animals and all the valuable things in the city. You can use the valuable things of your enemies that the LORD your God gives you.

**15** Do this to all the cities that are far away from you. These cities are not the cities of the nations near you.

**16** But do not let anyone stay alive in the cities of the nations that the LORD your God gives you as your property.

**17** Destroy them completely: the Hittites, the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Perizzites, the Hivites and the Jebusites. The LORD your God commanded you to do this.

**18** If you do not destroy them, they will teach you to do all the hated things that they do for their gods. Then you will sin against the LORD your God.

**19** "You can attack a city for a long time to capture it. Do not cut down its fruit trees with an axe. You can eat their fruit. Do not cut them down. The trees are not your enemies. Do not attack them as you attack the city.

**20** But you can cut down trees that you know do not give fruit. Use them to build attack structures against the city that fights you. Use them until the city is defeated.

## Chapter 21

**1** "Someone can find a dead person in a field in the land that the LORD your God gives you as your property. No one knows who killed that person.

**2** Then your elders and your judges must go out. They must measure the distance from the body to the cities around it.

**3** The elders of the city that is nearest to the body must take a young cow. The cow must not have worked or pulled a yoke.

**5** Then the priests, the sons of Levi, must come forward. The LORD your God chose them to serve him and to bless the people in the name of the LORD. They decide each dispute and each case of an attack.

**6** All the elders of the city nearest to the body must wash their hands over the young cow whose neck they broke in the valley.

**7** They must say, 'We did not kill this person. We did not see who did it.

**9** Thus remove from among you the guilt of the death of an innocent person. You will do what is right in the sight of the LORD.

**10** "You can go out to fight a war against your enemies. The LORD your God can give them to you, and you can take prisoners.

**11** You can see a beautiful woman among the prisoners. You can want her as your wife.

**12** Then bring her to your house. She must shave her head and cut her nails.

**21** Then all the men of his town must throw stones at him until he dies. Thus remove the evil from among you. All Israel will hear about it and be afraid.

**22** "A man can do a sin that deserves death. You can kill him and hang his body on a tree.

**23** Do not let his body stay on the tree during the night. Bury him on the same day. God curses each person who hangs on a tree. Do not make unclean the land that the LORD your God gives you as your property.

**1** "You can see the ox or the sheep of your brother go away from its place. Do not ignore it. Take it back to your brother.

**2** Your brother can live far from you, or you can not know who owns it. Then take it to your house. Keep it until your brother looks for it. Then give it back to him.

**4** You can see the donkey or the ox of your brother fall down on the road. Do not ignore it. Help him to lift it up.

**7** Let the mother go. You can take the young birds for yourself. Then all will be well for you, and you will live a long time.

**8** "When you build a new house, put a low wall around the edge of the roof. Then if a person falls from the roof, you will not be guilty of his death.

**9** "Do not plant 2 types of seed in your vineyard. If you do, you must give all the crops to the holy place, both the seed and the grapes.

**10** Do not plow with an ox and a donkey together.

**11** Do not wear clothes made of wool and linen together.

**12** Put tassels on the 4 corners of the coat that you wear.

**13** "A man can marry a woman and sleep with her. Then he can start to hate her.

**18** Then the elders of that town must take the man and punish him.

**22** "A man can have sex with the wife of another man. If someone finds them, both of them must die. The man and the woman must die. Thus remove the evil from Israel.

**27** The man met her in the field. The engaged young woman shouted for help, but no one was there to save her.

**28** "A man can meet a young woman who is a virgin and who is not engaged. He can take her and have sex with her. Then someone can find them.

## Chapter 24

**1** "A man can marry a woman, and then he is not pleased with her because he finds something shameful in her. Then he must write a document of divorce. He must give it to her and send her away from his house.

**3** The second husband can also hate her. He can write a document of divorce, give it to her and send her away from his house. Or the second husband can die.

**4** Then her first husband, who sent her away, must not marry her again. She is now unclean to him. The LORD hates this. Do not bring sin on the land that the LORD your God gives you as your property.

**5** "When a man marries a new wife, he must not go to war. Do not give him any other duty. He must be free to stay at home for 1 year and make his wife happy.

**6** "Do not take a millstone, either the lower or the upper stone, as a guarantee for a debt. If you do, you take the thing that a man needs to live.

**7** "A man can kidnap one of his brothers, a person of Israel. He can treat him as a slave or sell him. Then that kidnapper must die. Thus remove the evil from among you.

**8** "When there is a skin disease (leprosy), be careful to obey all that the Levitical priests teach you. Be careful to do as I commanded them.

**9** Remember what the LORD your God did to Miriam on the road after you came out of Egypt.

**20** I will bring them into the land that I promised to their fathers. It is a land that has much milk and honey. They will eat until they are satisfied and fat. Then they will turn to other gods and worship them. They will reject me and break my covenant.

**21** Then many disasters and troubles will come to them. This song will be a witness against them, because their descendants will not forget it. I know what they plan to do now, before I bring them into the land that I promised."

**22** So on that day Moses wrote this song and taught it to the people of Israel.

**23** Then the LORD gave Joshua the son of Nun his orders. He said, "Be strong and brave. You will bring the people of Israel into the land that I promised them. I will be with you."

**24** Moses wrote all the words of this law in a book until he completed it.

**25** Then Moses gave a command to the Levites who carried the ark of the covenant of the LORD. He said,

**26** "Take this book of the law and put it next to the ark of the covenant of the LORD your God. It will stay there as a witness against you.

**27** I know that you are stubborn and that you disobey. You disobeyed the LORD while I was alive with you. You will disobey him more after my death.

**28** Gather to me all the elders of your tribes and your officers. I will speak these words to them. I will tell heaven and earth to be witnesses against them.

**29** I know that after my death you will become completely evil. You will turn away from the way that I commanded you. In the future, disaster will come to you, because you will do evil in the sight of the LORD. You will make him angry with the idols that you make."

**30** Then Moses spoke all the words of this song to all the assembly of Israel, from the start to the end.

## Chapter 32

**1** "Heavens, listen, and I will speak. Earth, hear the words of my mouth.

**2** Let my teaching fall like rain. Let my words come down like dew, like light rain on new grass, like showers on plants.

**3** I will announce the name of the LORD. Praise the greatness of our God!

**4** He is the Rock. His work is perfect, and all his ways are fair. He is a faithful God who does no wrong. He is honest and right.

**5** But his people did evil things against him. They are not his children, because of their sin. They are an evil and dishonest generation.

**6** Is this how you repay the LORD, you foolish people with no wisdom? He is your father, who made you his own. He made you and gave you strength.

**7** Remember the past. Think about the years of many generations. Ask your father, and he will tell you. Ask your elders, and they will explain to you.

**8** The Most High gave the nations their land. He divided all humans into nations. He set the borders of the peoples by the number of the sons of Israel.

**9** The people of the LORD are his own part. Jacob is his own property.

**10** The LORD found Jacob in a desert land, in an empty wilderness where the wind blew. He protected him and took care of him. He guarded him as a man guards his own eye.

**11** An eagle shakes her nest and flies over her young birds. She spreads her wings to catch them and carries them on her wings.

**12** In the same way, the LORD alone led Jacob. No foreign god was with him.

**13** The LORD let Jacob live on the high land and eat the crops of the fields. He gave him honey from the rocks and olive oil from the hard rock.

**14** He gave him butter from the cattle and milk from the sheep. He gave him the best lambs, rams and goats from Bashan, and the best wheat. You drank the red wine of the grape.

**15** But Jeshurun (Israel) became fat and kicked against God. You became fat, heavy and full. Then Israel left God, who made him. He rejected the Rock who saved him.

**16** The people made God jealous with foreign gods. They made him angry with hated idols.

**17** They made sacrifices to demons, which are not God. They worshipped gods that they did not know. These were new gods that their fathers did not fear.

**18** You forgot the Rock who gave you life. You forgot God who made you.

**19** The LORD saw this and rejected them. His sons and his daughters made him angry.

**20** He said, 'I will hide my face from them. I will see what their end will be. They are an evil generation, children who are not faithful.

**21** They made me jealous with things that are not gods. They made me angry with their worthless idols. So I will make them jealous with people who are not a nation. I will make them angry with a foolish nation.

**22** My anger burns like a fire. It burns down to the deepest place of the dead. It burns the earth and its crops. It burns the bottom of the mountains.

**23** I will send many disasters on them. I will shoot all my arrows at them.

**24** They will become weak from hunger. Fever and terrible disease will destroy them. I will send wild animals to bite them. I will send poisonous snakes that move in the dust.

**25** Outside, the sword will kill them. Inside, terror will kill them. It will kill young men and young women. It will kill babies and old men.

**26** I said that I will send them far away. I will cause all people to forget them.

**27** But I did not want their enemies to say proud things. I did not want their enemies to misunderstand and say, "We won with our own power. The LORD did not do all this."'

**28** Israel is a nation without good sense. They do not understand.

**29** I wish that they were wise and understood this. I wish that they thought about their future!

**30** How can 1 enemy chase 1,000 of them? How can 2 enemies cause 10,000 to run away? Only if their Rock sold them. Only if the LORD gave them to their enemies.

**31** The rock of our enemies is not like our Rock. Even our enemies know this.

**32** The people of our enemies are like the grapevines of Sodom and the fields of Gomorrah. Their grapes are poisonous, and their bunches of grapes are bitter.

**33** Their wine is like the poison of snakes, the deadly poison of cobras.

**34** 'I keep this in mind. I keep it locked in my storehouse.

**35** I will punish them and pay them back. At the correct time, their feet will slip. The day of their disaster is near. Their destruction will come quickly.'

**36** The LORD will judge his people and show mercy to his servants. He will see that their strength is gone. He will see that no one is left, slave or free.

**37** Then he will say, 'Where are their gods now? Where is the rock that they trusted?

**38** Their gods ate the fat of their sacrifices and drank the wine of their drink offerings. Let those gods get up and help you! Let them protect you!

**39** Now see that I alone am God. There is no other god but me. I kill, and I give life. I wound, and I heal. No one can save anyone from my power.

**40** I lift up my hand to heaven and make a solemn promise. As surely as I live for all time,

**41** I will sharpen my shining sword. I will take hold of it to judge. I will punish my enemies. I will pay back the people who hate me.

**42** My arrows will be covered with their blood. My sword will kill many people. I will kill the wounded and the prisoners. I will kill the leaders of the enemy.'

**43** Nations, be happy with his people. He will punish the people who kill his servants. He will punish his enemies. He will make his land and his people clean from sin."

**44** Moses came with Joshua the son of Nun. They spoke all the words of this song to the people.

**45** Moses completed all these words to all Israel.

**46** Then he said to them, "Think carefully about all the words that I tell you today as a warning. Command your children to carefully obey all the words of this law.

**47** These words are not useless for you. They are your life. If you obey them, you will live for a long time in the land that you go across the Jordan to take."

**48** On that same day the LORD said to Moses,

**49** "Go up into the Abarim Mountains, to Mount Nebo in the land of Moab, opposite Jericho. Look at the land of Canaan, which I give to the people of Israel as their property.

**50** You will die on the mountain that you go up. You will join your people who died before you. This is as your brother Aaron died on Mount Hor and joined his people.

**51** Both of you were not faithful to me among the people of Israel. This occurred at the waters of Meribah-kadesh in the wilderness of Zin. You did not show the people of Israel that I am holy.

**52** You will see the land from far away. But you will not go into the land that I give to the people of Israel."

## Chapter 33

**1** This is the blessing that Moses, the man of God, gave to the people of Israel before his death.

**2** He said, "The LORD came from Sinai. He came to his people like the sunrise from Seir. He shone from Mount Paran. He came with 10,000 holy angels. A fiery law was in his right hand for them.

**3** The LORD loves his people. All his holy people are in his hand. They sit at his feet, and each one gets his words.

**4** Moses gave us a law. This law is the property of the people of Jacob.

**5** The LORD was king over Jeshurun (Israel). He was king when the leaders of the people and the tribes of Israel gathered together.

**6** "Let Reuben live and not die. But let his men be few."

**7** This is what Moses said about Judah: "LORD, hear the cry of Judah. Bring him back to his people. Fight for him with your power. Help him against his enemies."

**8** About Levi he said, "LORD, your Thummim and your Urim belong to your loyal servant. You tested him at Massah. You argued with him at the waters of Meribah.

**9** He said about his father and his mother, 'I do not have regard for them.' He did not accept his brothers or know his own children. The Levites obeyed your word and kept your covenant.

**10** They will teach your rules to Jacob and your law to Israel. They will put incense in front of you and burnt offerings on your altar.

**11** LORD, bless his work and accept all that he does. Crush his enemies and the people who hate him, so that they do not get up again."

**12** About Benjamin he said, "The LORD loves Benjamin. Let him live in safety near the LORD. The LORD protects him all day long. Benjamin lives between his shoulders."

**13** About Joseph he said, "Let the LORD bless his land with the best gifts from heaven. Let him bless it with dew and with the deep water below.

**14** Let him bless it with the best crops that the sun makes grow. Let him bless it with the best crops of each month.

**15** Let him bless it with the best things of the old mountains and the hills that last for all time.

**16** Let him bless it with the best gifts of the earth and all that it contains. Let him bless it with the kindness of the LORD, who lived in the burning bush. Let these blessings come on the head of Joseph. He was the prince among his brothers.

**17** He is strong like a firstborn bull. His horns are like the horns of a wild ox. With them he will push back the nations, all the way to the ends of the earth. These horns are the 10,000s of Ephraim and the 1,000s of Manasseh."

**18** About Zebulun he said, "Zebulun, be happy when you go out. Issachar, be happy in your tents.

**19** They will call the peoples to the mountain. There they will offer correct sacrifices. They will get wealth from the seas and from treasures hidden in the sand."

**20** About Gad he said, "Praise the LORD, who makes the land of Gad larger. Gad lives there like a lion. He tears off the arm and the head of his enemies.

**21** He chose the best land for himself. The part of a leader was kept for him. He came with the leaders of the people. He did what the LORD said was right. He obeyed the rules of the LORD with Israel."

**22** About Dan he said, "Dan is a young lion. He jumps out from Bashan."

**23** About Naphtali he said, "Naphtali has many good things from the LORD. He is full of the blessing of the LORD. He will take the land to the west and the south."

**24** About Asher he said, "Asher has the most blessings of all the sons. Let his brothers like him. Let him have very much olive oil.

**25** Your town gates will have bars of iron and bronze. Your strength will continue for all your life.

**26** "No one is like the God of Jeshurun (Israel). He rides across the sky to help you. He rides on the clouds in his majesty.

**27** The eternal God is your place of safety. His arms always hold you up. He will push your enemies away from you. He will say, 'Destroy them!'

**28** So Israel will live in safety. The descendants of Jacob will live alone in a land of grain and new wine. Dew will fall from the sky on that land.

**29** Israel, you are blessed! No other people is like you. The LORD saved you. He is your shield that protects you and your sword that wins for you. Your enemies will bow down to you. You will walk on their high places."

## Chapter 34

**1** Then Moses went up from the plains of Moab to Mount Nebo, to the top of Pisgah, opposite Jericho. There the LORD showed him all the land. He showed him Gilead as far as Dan,

**2** all Naphtali, the land of Ephraim and Manasseh, and all the land of Judah as far as the Mediterranean Sea.

**3** He showed him the Negev and the plain of the Valley of Jericho, the city of palm trees, as far as Zoar.

**4** Then the LORD said to Moses, "This is the land that I promised to Abraham, Isaac and Jacob. I said, 'I will give it to your descendants.' I let you see it with your eyes, but you will not go across into it."

**5** So Moses, the servant of the LORD, died there in the land of Moab, as the LORD said.

**6** The LORD buried him in a valley in the land of Moab, opposite Beth-peor. But to this day no one knows where his grave is.

**7** Moses was 120 years old when he died. His eyes were still good, and he was still strong.

**8** The people of Israel cried for Moses in the plains of Moab for 30 days. Then the time of mourning for Moses ended.

**9** Joshua the son of Nun was full of the spirit of wisdom, because Moses put his hands on him. So the people of Israel obeyed Joshua. They did as the LORD commanded Moses.

**10** Since that time, no prophet in Israel was like Moses. The LORD knew Moses face to face.

**11** The LORD sent Moses to do all the signs and the wonders in the land of Egypt. Moses did them against Pharaoh, all his officials and all his land.

**12** No other prophet showed such great power. No other prophet did such terrible things in front of all Israel as Moses did.
