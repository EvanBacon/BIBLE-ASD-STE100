# James

## Chapter 1

**1** I, James, am a servant of God and of the Lord Jesus Christ. I write to the 12 tribes that live in many countries. Greetings.

**2** My brothers, you will have many types of trouble. When these troubles come, be very happy.

**3** You know that trouble tests your faith. And this test makes you patient.

**4** Let your patience continue until it does its full work. Then you will be mature and complete. You will not need anything.

**5** If one of you needs wisdom, he must ask God for it. God gives generously to all people, and he does not criticize them. God will give wisdom to that person.

**6** But he must ask with faith and not doubt. A person who doubts is like a wave of the sea. The wind blows the wave from one side to the other.

**7** That person must not think that he will get anything from the Lord.

**8** He cannot decide what he wants. He is not stable in anything that he does.

**9** A believer who is poor must be glad, because God makes him high.

**10** A believer who is rich must be glad, because God makes him low. The rich person will disappear like a wild flower.

**11** The sun comes up with burning heat and dries the grass. The flower falls, and its beauty is destroyed. In the same way, the rich man will disappear while he does his business.

**12** The person who continues firm in trouble is blessed. After he passes the test, he will receive life as his crown. The Lord promised this life to the people who love him.

**13** When a person has temptation, he must not say, "God tempts me." Evil cannot tempt God, and God does not tempt anyone.

**14** But each person has temptation when his own wrong desires pull him away and trap him.

**15** Then the wrong desire grows and causes sin. When sin is fully grown, it causes death.

**16** My dear brothers, do not let anyone deceive you.

**17** Every good and perfect gift comes from heaven. It comes from the Father, who made the lights in the sky. The Father does not change like a moving shadow.

**18** God decided to give us new life through the message of truth. He did this so that we will be the first and best of all that he made.

**19** My dear brothers, know this: Each person must be quick to listen. He must be slow to speak and slow to become angry.

**20** The anger of a person does not produce the righteous life that God wants.

**21** Thus remove all dirty and evil things from your life. Humbly accept the word of God that God put in your hearts. This word can save your souls.

**22** Do what the word of God says. Do not only listen to it. If you only listen, you deceive yourselves.

**23** A person who listens to the word but does not do what it says is like a man who looks at his face in a mirror.

**24** He looks at himself and goes away. Then he immediately forgets how he looks.

**25** But another person looks carefully into the perfect law that makes people free. He continues to do this. He does not forget what he hears, but he does it. God will bless that person in what he does.

**26** A person can think that he is religious. But if he does not control his tongue, he deceives himself. His religion has no value.

**27** God the Father accepts this religion as pure and good: Help orphans and widows when they suffer. Do not let the world make you unclean.

## Chapter 2

**1** My brothers, you believe in our glorious Lord Jesus Christ. Thus do not treat some people better than other people.

**2** Sometimes a man comes into your meeting with a gold ring and fine clothes. A poor man in dirty clothes also comes in.

**3** You give special attention to the man in fine clothes. You say to him, "Sit here in this good seat." But you say to the poor man, "Stand there," or "Sit on the floor near my feet."

**4** Then you show favor to some people more than others. You judge people with evil thoughts.

**5** Listen, my dear brothers. God chose the people who are poor in the eyes of the world to be rich in faith. They will receive the kingdom that God promised to the people who love him.

**6** But you insult the poor. The rich people are the people who treat you badly. They take you to court.

**7** They insult the good name of Jesus. You belong to him.

**8** The Scriptures give the royal law: "Love your neighbor as you love yourself." If you obey this law, you do well.

**9** But if you show favor to some people more than others, you sin. The law shows that you are guilty.

**10** A person can obey all the law but fail to obey 1 command. Then he is guilty of breaking all the law.

**11** God said, "Do not commit adultery." He also said, "Do not kill." If you do not commit adultery but you kill, you break the law.

**12** God will judge you by the law that makes people free. Thus speak and act like people who know this.

**13** God will judge without mercy the person who showed no mercy. Mercy wins over judgment.

**14** My brothers, a person can say that he has faith. But if he does not do good works, his faith does not help him. That faith cannot save him.

**15** A brother or sister can need clothes and daily food.

**16** One of you can say to them, "Go in peace. Keep warm, and eat well." But if you do not give them what their bodies need, your words do not help them.

**17** Faith is like this too. Faith alone, without works, is dead.

**18** But a person can say, "You have faith, and I have works." I tell him, "Show me your faith without works. I will show you my faith through my works."

**19** You believe that there is 1 God. That is good. But the demons also believe this, and they shake with fear.

**20** Foolish person, do you want proof that faith without works has no value?

**21** Abraham our ancestor offered his son Isaac on the altar. God accepted Abraham as righteous because of what he did.

**22** You see that the faith of Abraham and his works acted together. His works made his faith complete.

**23** Thus the Scriptures became true: "Abraham believed God, and God accepted Abraham as righteous because of his faith." People called Abraham the friend of God.

**24** Thus you see that God accepts a person as righteous because of what he does. God does not accept him because of faith alone.

**25** The prostitute Rahab is another example. She received the messengers and sent them out by a different road. God accepted her as righteous because of what she did.

**26** A body without the spirit is dead. In the same way, faith without works is dead.

## Chapter 3

**1** My brothers, not many of you must become teachers. You know that God will judge us teachers more strictly.

**2** All of us make many mistakes. A person who never makes a mistake in what he says is perfect. He can also control all his body.

**3** We put a bit in the mouth of a horse to make the horse obey us. Thus we can control all its body.

**4** Also, think about ships. They are very large, and strong winds push them. But a very small rudder turns them. The pilot steers the ship where he wants.

**5** In the same way, the tongue is a small part of the body. But it boasts about great things. A very small fire can burn a very large forest.

**6** The tongue is like a fire. It is a world of evil among the parts of our body. It makes all the body unclean. It sets fire to all of our life. Hell sets fire to the tongue.

**7** People can control all types of animals, birds, snakes, and sea animals. People controlled them in the past.

**8** But nobody can control the tongue. The tongue is evil and does not stop. It is full of poison that kills.

**9** With the tongue we praise our Lord and Father. With the same tongue we curse people. But God made people like himself.

**10** Praise and curses come from the same mouth. My brothers, this must not happen.

**11** A spring of water cannot give fresh water and bitter water from the same opening.

**12** My brothers, a fig tree cannot grow olives. A grapevine cannot grow figs. In the same way, a salt spring cannot give fresh water.

**13** Who among you is wise and understands? He must show it through a good life. He must do good works with humility that comes from wisdom.

**14** But you can have bitter jealousy and selfish ambition in your hearts. Then do not boast, and do not lie against the truth.

**15** That type of wisdom does not come from heaven. It comes from the world. It is not spiritual. It comes from the devil.

**16** Where there is jealousy and selfish ambition, there is disorder and every type of evil.

**17** But the wisdom from heaven is first pure. It also likes peace. It is gentle and willing to listen. It is full of mercy and good actions. It does not show favor, and it is honest.

**18** People who make peace plant seeds in peace. They will get a harvest of righteousness.

## Chapter 4

**1** What causes fights and arguments among you? They come from your wrong desires that fight inside you.

**2** You want things, but you do not get them. Thus you kill. You want things very much, but you cannot get them. Thus you argue and fight. You do not have what you want because you do not ask God.

**3** When you ask, you do not receive. You ask for the wrong reasons. You want to use the things for your own pleasure.

**4** You are unfaithful to God! You know that friendship with the world makes you an enemy of God. Thus a person who wants to be a friend of the world becomes an enemy of God.

**5** The Scriptures have a purpose when they say this: "God wants the spirit that he put in us to belong only to him."

**6** But God gives us more grace. Thus the Scriptures say, "God opposes proud people, but he gives grace to humble people."

**7** Thus obey God. Fight against the devil, and the devil will run away from you.

**8** Come near to God, and God will come near to you. You sinners, make your hands clean. You people who cannot decide, make your hearts pure.

**9** Be sad, weep, and cry. Change your laughter into sadness. Change your joy into sorrow.

**10** Make yourselves humble in front of the Lord. Then the Lord will lift you up.

**11** Brothers, do not speak against each other. A person who speaks against his brother or judges his brother speaks against the law. He judges the law. If you judge the law, you do not obey the law. You make yourself a judge of it.

**12** Only 1 God gives the law and judges. He can save people, and he can destroy them. Thus you have no right to judge your neighbor.

**13** Listen, you people who say, "Today or tomorrow we will go to a city. We will stay there for 1 year. We will do business there and make money."

**14** You do not know what will happen tomorrow. Your life is like a mist. It is there for a short time, and then it disappears.

**15** You must say, "If the Lord wants, we will live and do this or that."

**16** But now you boast about your plans. All such boasting is evil.

**17** If a person knows the good thing that he must do, but he does not do it, he sins.

## Chapter 5

**1** Listen, you rich people. Weep and cry loudly because of the troubles that will come to you.

**2** Your riches are rotten. Insects ate your clothes.

**3** Rust destroyed your gold and silver. The rust will be a witness against you. It will eat your flesh like fire. You kept a large amount of treasure in these last days.

**4** You did not pay the workers who harvested your fields. Their pay cries out against you. The Lord of armies heard the cries of the harvesters.

**5** You lived on earth in luxury and pleasure. You made yourselves fat like animals that are ready to be killed.

**6** You judged innocent people guilty and killed them. They did not fight against you.

**7** Thus, brothers, be patient until the Lord comes. Look at the farmer. He waits with patience for the valuable crop of the land. He waits for the early rain and the late rain.

**8** You also must be patient. Make your hearts strong, because the Lord will come soon.

**9** Brothers, do not complain about each other. If you do, God will judge you. The Judge stands at the door.

**10** Brothers, think about the prophets who spoke in the name of the Lord. They are an example of patience when people suffer.

**11** We say that the people who continue firm are blessed. You heard about the patience of Job. You saw what the Lord did for Job at the end. The Lord is very kind and full of mercy.

**12** My brothers, this is most important: Do not make a solemn promise. Do not make it by heaven, by the earth, or by any other thing. When you say "yes," mean yes. When you say "no," mean no. Then God will not judge you guilty.

**13** If one of you is in trouble, he must pray. If one of you is happy, he must sing songs of praise.

**14** If one of you is sick, he must call the elders of the church. They must pray for him. They must put oil on him in the name of the Lord.

**15** The prayer that comes from faith will heal the sick person. The Lord will make him well. If he sinned, God will forgive him.

**16** Thus tell your sins to each other. Pray for each other, so that God will heal you. The prayer of a righteous person has great power and causes great results.

**17** Elijah was a human like us. He prayed seriously that it will not rain. Then it did not rain on the land for 3 years and 6 months.

**18** Then Elijah prayed again. The sky gave rain, and the land grew its crops.

**19** My brothers, one of you can go away from the truth. Another person can bring him back.

**20** Know this: The person who brings a sinner back from his wrong way will save the sinner's soul from death. He will cause God to forgive many sins.
