# 2 Thessalonians

## Chapter 1

**1** Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy write this letter to the church of the Thessalonians. This church is in God our Father and in the Lord Jesus Christ.

**2** We ask that God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you grace and peace.

**3** Brothers, we must always thank God for you, and this is correct. Your faith grows very much. The love of each of you for the others also grows.

**4** Thus we speak with pride about you to the churches of God. People attack you and give you troubles. But you continue to be patient, and you keep your faith.

**5** This shows that the judgment of God is correct. God will make you worthy of his kingdom. You suffer for his kingdom.

**6** God is just. He will give trouble to the people who give trouble to you.

**7** He will give rest to you who suffer, and to us also. He will do this when the Lord Jesus comes from heaven. Jesus will come with his powerful angels.

**8** He will come in a fire with flames. He will punish the people who do not know God. He will punish the people who do not obey the gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**9** God will punish those people with destruction that will not end. They will be away from the Lord and from the glory of his power.

**10** This will occur on the day when the Lord comes. On that day, his holy people will give him glory. All who believe will look at him with wonder. You will be with them, because you believed our message.

**11** For this reason, we always pray for you. We ask that our God make you worthy of the life that he calls you to. We ask that he use his power to complete all your good wishes. We ask that he complete each work that comes from your faith.

**12** Then you will give honor to the name of our Lord Jesus Christ. And he will give honor to you. This comes from the grace of our God and the Lord Jesus Christ.

## Chapter 2

**1** Brothers, we have a request about the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ. It is also about the time when God will bring us together to him.

**2** Do not let anything quickly confuse you or make you afraid. Someone can say that the day of the Lord came already. He can say that a spirit told him this. He can say that it came by a message or by a letter from us. Do not believe it.

**3** Do not let anyone trick you in any way. That day will not come until the great rebellion against God occurs first. Also, the man of sin must first appear. God will destroy that man.

**4** That man will oppose all things that people call god or worship. He will put himself above all of them. He will sit in the temple of God and say that he is God.

**5** I told you these things when I was still with you. Do you not remember?

**6** Now you know what stops that man. That man will appear only at the correct time.

**7** The secret power of sin works in the world now. But someone stops that power now. He will continue to stop it until God takes him away.

**8** Then the evil man will appear. The Lord Jesus will kill him with the breath of his mouth. The Lord will destroy him with the bright light of his coming.

**9** The evil man will come with the power of Satan. He will do all types of powerful acts, signs, and false wonders.

**10** He will use all types of evil to trick the people who will die. Those people will die because they refused to love the truth. If they loved the truth, God would save them.

**11** For this reason, God will send them a strong power that will trick them. Then they will believe a lie.

**12** God will judge all the people who did not believe the truth. They liked evil things.

**13** But, brothers, the Lord loves you. We must always thank God for you. From the start, God chose you to be saved. The Spirit makes you holy, and you believe the truth.

**14** God called you to this through the gospel that we told you. He called you so that you will share in the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**15** Thus, brothers, stand strong. Keep the teachings that we gave you, by our words or by our letter.

**16** Our Lord Jesus Christ himself and God our Father love us. By his grace, God gave us comfort that will not end and a good hope.

**17** We ask that they comfort your hearts. We ask that they make you strong in all good words and in all good work.

## Chapter 3

**1** Finally, brothers, pray for us. Pray that the word of the Lord moves quickly to other people. Pray that people give honor to the word, as you do.

**2** Pray that God saves us from bad and evil men. Not all people have faith.

**3** But the Lord is faithful. He will make you strong. He will keep you safe from the evil one.

**4** The Lord makes us confident about you. We are sure that you do the things that we tell you. We are sure that you will continue to do them.

**5** We ask that the Lord guide your hearts to the love of God. We ask that he guide you to the patience of Christ.

**6** Brothers, we give you a command in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ. Keep away from each brother who is lazy. Keep away from each brother who does not obey the teaching that you got from us.

**7** You know that you must do as we do. We were not lazy when we were with you.

**8** We did not eat food from any person without payment. We worked very hard night and day. We did this so that we did not cause expense to any of you.

**9** We had the right to get help from you. But we did not ask for it. We wanted to give you an example. We wanted you to do as we did.

**10** When we were with you, we gave you this command: "If a person does not want to work, he must not eat."

**11** We hear that some people among you are lazy. They do no work. But they put themselves into the business of other people.

**12** We command these people in the Lord Jesus Christ. We encourage them to work quietly and to earn their own food.

**13** But you, brothers, do not stop when you do good.

**14** Some people possibly will not obey the instructions in this letter. Identify those people. Do not associate with them. Then they will feel shame.

**15** But do not think that such a person is an enemy. Warn him as you warn a brother.

**16** We ask that the Lord of peace himself give you peace at all times and in all ways. We ask that the Lord be with all of you.

**17** I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand. This is the sign in all my letters. This is how I write.

**18** We ask that the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with all of you. Amen.
