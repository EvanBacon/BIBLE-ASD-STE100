# Hebrews

## Chapter 1

**1** In the past, God spoke to our ancestors many times and in many different ways. He spoke to them through the prophets.

**2** But now, in these last days, God spoke to us through his Son. God made the Son the heir of all things. God also made the worlds through the Son.

**3** The Son shows the bright glory of God. He is the exact image of the nature of God. He holds all things together by his powerful word. He made people clean from their sins. Then he sat down at the right hand of the Majesty in heaven.

**4** Thus the Son became much better than the angels. The name that he received from his Father is much better than the name of the angels.

**5** God never said to an angel, "You are my Son. Today I became your Father." God also never said about an angel, "I will be his Father, and he will be my Son."

**6** Also, when God brings his firstborn Son into the world, he says, "All the angels of God must worship him."

**7** About the angels God says, "God makes his angels winds. He makes his servants flames of fire."

**8** But about the Son God says, "Your throne, O God, will continue for all time. You will rule your kingdom with justice.

**9** You loved what is right, and you hated what is wrong. Thus God, your God, put the oil of joy on you. He put you above your companions."

**10** God also says, "Lord, at the start, you made the base of the earth. Your hands made the heavens.

**11** The heavens and the earth will come to an end, but you will continue. They will all become old like a garment.

**12** You will fold them up like a coat. You will change them like clothes. But you stay the same. Your years will never end."

**13** God never said to an angel, "Sit at my right hand until I put your enemies under your feet."

**14** All the angels are spirits that serve God. God sends them to help the people who will receive salvation.

## Chapter 2

**1** Thus we must pay more attention to the truths that we heard. If we do not, we will drift away from them.

**2** God gave the law through angels, and that message was firm. Each person who did not obey that message received the correct punishment.

**3** Thus we will not escape if we ignore such a great salvation. The Lord was the first to speak about this salvation. Then the people who heard him confirmed it to us.

**4** God also confirmed the message. He did this with signs, wonders, and many types of miracles. He also gave gifts of the Holy Spirit, as he wanted.

**5** God did not put the world that will come under the control of angels. That world is the world that we speak about.

**6** But a person wrote this in a part of the Scriptures: "What is man, that you think about him? What is the son of man, that you care for him?

**7** You made man a little lower than the angels for a short time. You gave him glory and honor as his crown. You put him over the things that your hands made.

**8** You put all things under his feet." God put all things under man. Thus God did not leave anything that is not under man. But now we do not yet see all things under man.

**9** But we see Jesus. God made him a little lower than the angels for a short time. Jesus suffered death, and now God gave him glory and honor as his crown. By the grace of God, Jesus died for every person.

**10** God made all things, and all things exist for him. God wants to bring many sons to glory. Thus it was correct for God to make Jesus perfect through pain. Jesus is the leader who brings them to salvation.

**11** Jesus makes people holy. Jesus and the people that he makes holy have the same Father. Thus Jesus does not feel shame to call them his brothers.

**12** He says, "I will tell my brothers about your name. I will sing praise to you in the assembly."

**13** He also says, "I will trust God." He also says, "I am here with the children that God gave me."

**14** The children are people with flesh and blood. Thus Jesus also became a human with flesh and blood. Jesus died so that he could destroy the devil. The devil had the power of death.

**15** Jesus also died to set people free. Those people were slaves all their lives because of their fear of death.

**16** It is clear that Jesus does not help angels. He helps the descendants of Abraham.

**17** Thus Jesus had to become like his brothers in every way. Then he could be a high priest who is kind and faithful in his service to God. Then he could make a sacrifice that removes the sins of the people.

**18** Jesus himself suffered when Satan tempted him. Thus he can help the people who have temptation now.

## Chapter 3

**1** Thus, holy brothers, God also calls you to heaven. Think carefully about Jesus. He is the Apostle and the High Priest of the faith that we declare.

**2** Jesus was faithful to God, who chose him. Moses also was faithful in all the house of God.

**3** But Jesus deserves more glory than Moses. The builder of a house gets more honor than the house.

**4** A person builds each house. But God built all things.

**5** Moses was a faithful servant in all the house of God. He told people about the things that God will say in the future.

**6** But Christ is faithful as a son over the house of God. We are the house of God if we keep our courage and our joyful hope firm until the end.

**7** Thus the Holy Spirit says, "Today, if you hear the voice of God,

**8** do not make your hearts hard. Your ancestors made their hearts hard when they rebelled against me. They tested me in the desert.

**9** There your ancestors tested me and tried me. They saw my works for 40 years.

**10** Thus I was angry with that generation. I said, 'Their hearts always go away from me. They do not know my ways.'

**11** Thus I was angry, and I made a solemn promise: 'They will not enter my rest.'"

**12** Brothers, be careful. Make sure that none of you has an evil heart without faith. Such a heart turns away from the God who lives.

**13** Encourage each other every day, while it is still "today." Then sin will not trick you and make your hearts hard.

**14** We share in Christ if we keep our first confidence firm until the end.

**15** The Scriptures say, "Today, if you hear the voice of God, do not make your hearts hard, as your ancestors did when they rebelled."

**16** Who heard God and then rebelled? All the people that Moses led out of Egypt rebelled.

**17** With whom was God angry for 40 years? He was angry with the people who sinned. Their dead bodies fell in the desert.

**18** To whom did God make a solemn promise that they will not enter his rest? He made this promise about the people who did not obey him.

**19** Thus we see that those people did not enter because they did not believe.

## Chapter 4

**1** God still gives us the promise that we can enter his rest. Thus we must be careful. Make sure that none of you fails to enter.

**2** We heard the good news, as those people heard it. But the message did not help them, because they did not believe it.

**3** We who believe enter that rest. God said, "I was angry, and I made a solemn promise. They will not enter my rest." But God finished his works when he made the world.

**4** In one part of the Scriptures, God speaks about the 7th day: "On the 7th day God rested from all his works."

**5** In another part, God says again, "They will not enter my rest."

**6** Some people will still enter that rest. But the people who first heard the good news did not enter, because they did not obey.

**7** Thus God again sets a day, "Today." He spoke about this day through David a long time after that. The Scriptures say, "Today, if you hear the voice of God, do not make your hearts hard."

**8** If Joshua gave the people rest, then God will not speak later about another day.

**9** Thus a Sabbath rest still waits for the people of God.

**10** The person who enters the rest of God also rests from his own works, as God rested from his works.

**11** Thus we must work hard to enter that rest. Then nobody will fall because of the same disobedience.

**12** The word of God is alive and powerful. It is sharper than any sword with 2 edges. It cuts between the soul and the spirit, and between the joints and the marrow. It judges the thoughts and the purposes of the heart.

**13** Nothing in all creation can hide from God. All things are open and clear to his eyes. We must give an account to him.

**14** We have a great high priest who went through the heavens. He is Jesus, the Son of God. Thus we must hold firmly to the faith that we declare.

**15** Our high priest understands our weaknesses. Satan tempted him in every way, as Satan tempts us. But Jesus did not sin.

**16** Thus we can come with courage to the throne of grace. There we will receive mercy, and we will find grace to help us when we need it.

## Chapter 5

**1** God chooses each high priest from among men. The high priest serves God for the people. He offers gifts and sacrifices for sins.

**2** The high priest is gentle with people who do not know the truth and with people who go away from the truth. He is gentle because he also is weak.

**3** Because he is weak, he must offer sacrifices for his own sins and for the sins of the people.

**4** No person takes this honor for himself. God must call him, as God called Aaron.

**5** Thus Christ also did not give glory to himself to become a high priest. But God said to him, "You are my Son. Today I became your Father."

**6** In another part of the Scriptures God also says, "You are a priest for all time, in the line of Melchizedek."

**7** When Jesus lived on earth, he prayed and asked God for help. He cried loudly and with tears to God, who could save him from death. God heard Jesus because Jesus respected and obeyed God.

**8** Jesus was the Son of God. But he learned obedience from the things that he suffered.

**9** Thus God made Jesus perfect. Jesus became the source of eternal salvation for all the people who obey him.

**10** God named Jesus as a high priest in the line of Melchizedek.

**11** We have many things to say about this subject. But it is hard to explain, because you are slow to understand.

**12** By now you must be teachers. But you need a person to teach you again the first basic truths of the word of God. You need milk, not solid food.

**13** A person who lives on milk is still a baby. He does not know about the word of what is right.

**14** But solid food is for adults. They practice and train their minds. Thus they know the difference between good and evil.

## Chapter 6

**1** Thus we must stop the study of the first lessons about Christ. We must go on to become mature. We must not again put down the base. That base is repentance from works that cause death, and faith in God.

**2** That base also includes the lessons about baptisms, and about the act when people put hands on people. It includes the resurrection of the dead, and the judgment that continues for all time.

**3** We will go on to become mature, if God lets us.

**4** Some people received the light of the truth. They tasted the gift from heaven. They shared in the Holy Spirit.

**5** They tasted the goodness of the word of God and the powers of the age that will come.

**6** If those people then turn away from God, nobody can bring them back to repentance. Their actions put the Son of God on the cross again. They bring public shame on him.

**7** Sometimes the rain falls often on the ground. Then the ground gives a good crop to the people who work on it. God blesses that ground.

**8** But if the ground grows thorns and weeds, it has no value. God will soon curse it. At the end, people will burn it.

**9** Dear friends, we speak like this. But we are sure that better things are true about you. These things come with salvation.

**10** God is fair. He will not forget your work. He will not forget the love that you showed for him. You helped his people, and you continue to help them.

**11** We want each of you to continue to work hard until the end. Then you will be sure of the things that you hope for.

**12** Do not become lazy. Copy the people who receive the promises of God through their faith and patience.

**13** God made a promise to Abraham. There was nobody greater than God. Thus God made a solemn promise on his own name.

**14** God said, "I will surely bless you. I will surely give you many descendants."

**15** Abraham waited with patience. Then he received what God promised.

**16** People make a solemn promise on the name of a person who is greater than they are. The solemn promise confirms what they say. It stops all argument.

**17** God wanted to show clearly that his purpose will not change. He wanted to show this to the people who will receive his promise. Thus God confirmed his promise with a solemn promise.

**18** God gave 2 things that cannot change. God cannot lie about these 2 things. Thus we, who ran to God for safety, have strong encouragement. We can hold firmly to the hope that God gives us.

**19** This hope holds our soul safely, as an anchor holds a ship. Our hope is sure and firm. It goes into the most holy place behind the curtain in heaven.

**20** Jesus went in there before us, for us. He became a high priest for all time, in the line of Melchizedek.

## Chapter 7

**1** Melchizedek was the king of Salem and a priest of the Most High God. Abraham defeated the kings and came back from the battle. Then Melchizedek met Abraham and blessed him.

**2** Abraham gave Melchizedek a tenth of all that he took in the battle. The name Melchizedek means "King of what is right." He was also the King of Salem. Salem means "peace." Thus he is also "King of peace."

**3** The Scriptures tell nothing about his father, his mother, or his ancestors. They tell nothing about the start or the end of his life. He is like the Son of God. He continues as a priest for all time.

**4** Think about how great Melchizedek was. Even Abraham, the ancestor of our nation, gave him a tenth of the best things that he took in the battle.

**5** The law tells the priests from the descendants of Levi to take a tenth from the people. The people are their brothers. These brothers are also descendants of Abraham.

**6** But Melchizedek was not a descendant of Levi. But he took a tenth from Abraham. He blessed Abraham, the man who had the promises of God.

**7** Nobody can disagree that the greater person blesses the lesser person.

**8** The priests who take a tenth are men who die. But Melchizedek took a tenth, and the Scriptures say that he lives.

**9** We can also say this: Levi, who takes a tenth, paid a tenth through Abraham.

**10** Levi was not yet born. He was still in the body of his ancestor Abraham when Melchizedek met Abraham.

**11** The people received the law under the priesthood of Levi. If that priesthood made people perfect, there was no need for another priest. But another priest came, in the line of Melchizedek. He was not in the line of Aaron.

**12** When the priesthood changes, the law must also change.

**13** The Scriptures say these things about our Lord. He came from a different tribe. Nobody from that tribe ever served at the altar.

**14** It is clear that our Lord came from the tribe of Judah. Moses said nothing about priests from that tribe.

**15** This becomes even more clear because another priest like Melchizedek came.

**16** He did not become a priest through a law about human ancestors. He became a priest through the power of a life that does not end.

**17** The Scriptures say about him, "You are a priest for all time, in the line of Melchizedek."

**18** Thus God stopped the old command, because it was weak and did not help.

**19** The law did not make anything perfect. But now God gives us a better hope. Through this hope we come near to God.

**20** Also, God made a solemn promise when he made Jesus a priest.

**21** God did not make a solemn promise when the other priests became priests. But he made a solemn promise when Jesus became a priest. God said to him, "The Lord made a solemn promise, and he will not change his mind: 'You are a priest for all time.'"

**22** Thus Jesus is the guarantee of a better covenant.

**23** There were many of the other priests, because death stopped each priest. No priest could continue.

**24** But Jesus lives for all time. Thus his priesthood does not change.

**25** Thus Jesus can save completely the people who come to God through him. He always lives to ask God to help them.

**26** Jesus is the high priest that we need. He is holy, and he does no wrong. He is pure, and he is separate from sinners. God put him higher than the heavens.

**27** The other high priests must offer sacrifices every day. First they offer them for their own sins, and then for the sins of the people. But Jesus does not need to do this. He offered himself 1 time for all people.

**28** The law makes men high priests, and these men are weak. But after the law, God made a solemn promise. Through this promise, God made his Son a high priest. The Son is perfect for all time.

## Chapter 8

**1** This is the main point of what we say: We have such a high priest. He sat down at the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in heaven.

**2** He serves in the holy place, in the true tent of worship (tabernacle). The Lord made that tabernacle. A human did not make it.

**3** God chooses each high priest to offer gifts and sacrifices. Thus our high priest also must have something to offer.

**4** If Jesus was on earth, he will not be a priest. There are already priests on earth who offer the gifts that the law tells them to offer.

**5** These priests serve in a copy and a shadow of the things in heaven. When Moses was ready to build the tabernacle, God warned him. God said, "Make all things the same as the plan that I showed you on the mountain."

**6** But now Jesus has a better service than the other priests. Also, he brings a better covenant between God and people. God put that covenant on better promises.

**7** If the first covenant had no fault, God did not need to look for a second covenant.

**8** But God found fault with the people. He said, "The days will come," says the Lord. "Then I will make a new covenant with the people of Israel and the people of Judah.

**9** This covenant will not be like the covenant that I made with their ancestors. I took them by the hand to lead them out of the land of Egypt. But they did not stay faithful to my covenant. Thus I turned away from them," says the Lord.

**10** "After those days, I will make this covenant with the people of Israel," says the Lord. "I will put my laws into their minds. I will write my laws on their hearts. I will be their God, and they will be my people.

**11** A person will not need to teach his neighbor or his brother. He will not need to say, 'Know the Lord.' All people will know me, from the least to the greatest.

**12** I will forgive the wrong that they do. I will not remember their sins any more."

**13** God calls this covenant "new." Thus he makes the first covenant old. And a thing that becomes old will soon disappear.

## Chapter 9

**1** The first covenant had rules for worship. It also had a holy place on earth.

**2** The people made a tabernacle. The first room was the holy place. In it were the lampstand, the table, and the holy bread.

**3** Behind the second curtain was the room that people call the Most Holy Place.

**4** In it was the gold altar for incense. In it also was the box of the covenant (ark), which had gold all over it. In the ark were a gold jar with manna, the stick of Aaron that grew buds, and the stone tablets of the covenant.

**5** Above the ark were the cherubim of glory. Their wings covered the lid of the ark, where the priest put the blood for forgiveness. We cannot now speak about these things in detail.

**6** The priests prepared all these things. Then the priests always went into the first room to do their service.

**7** But only the high priest went into the second room. He went in only 1 time each year. He always took blood with him. He offered the blood for his own sins and for the sins that the people did without knowledge.

**8** Through this, the Holy Spirit shows that the way into the Most Holy Place was not yet open. It was not open while the first tabernacle was still there.

**9** This is an example for the present time. The priests offered gifts and sacrifices. But these offerings could not make the conscience of the worshipper perfect.

**10** These things are only about food, drinks, and different types of washes. They are rules for the body. They continued only until the time when God made things new.

**11** But Christ came as the high priest of the good things that are now here. He went through a greater and more perfect tabernacle. Human hands did not make it. It is not a part of this creation.

**12** Christ did not enter with the blood of goats and calves. He entered the Most Holy Place 1 time for all time with his own blood. Thus he paid the price to make us free for all time.

**13** The priests put the blood of goats and bulls on people who were not clean. They also put the ashes of a young cow on those people. These things made their bodies clean.

**14** Thus the blood of Christ will do much more. Through the eternal Spirit, Christ offered himself to God with no fault. His blood will make our conscience clean from works that cause death. Then we can serve the God who lives.

**15** Thus Christ brings a new covenant between God and people. He died to free people from the sins that they did under the first covenant. Now the people that God calls can receive the eternal inheritance that he promised.

**16** When a person writes a will, people must prove that the person who wrote it is dead.

**17** A will is effective only when the person who wrote it is dead. It has no power while that person is alive.

**18** Thus Moses also started the first covenant with blood.

**19** First Moses told all the people every command of the law. Then he took the blood of calves and goats, with water, red wool, and branches of hyssop. He put the blood on the book and on all the people.

**20** He said, "This is the blood of the covenant. God commands you to obey this covenant."

**21** Moses also put blood on the tabernacle and on all the objects that the priests used in their service.

**22** The law says that almost everything becomes clean with blood. Without blood, God does not forgive sins.

**23** Thus the priests had to make the copies of the things in heaven clean with these sacrifices. But the things in heaven needed better sacrifices than these.

**24** Christ did not enter a holy place that human hands made. That place was only a copy of the true place. Christ entered heaven itself. Now he stands in front of God for us.

**25** The high priest enters the Most Holy Place every year with blood that is not his own. But Christ did not enter heaven to offer himself many times.

**26** If he did that, he must suffer many times since the start of the world. But now, at the end of the ages, he came 1 time for all time. He came to remove sin through the sacrifice of himself.

**27** All people must die 1 time. After that, God judges them.

**28** Thus Christ died as a sacrifice 1 time to take away the sins of many people. He will come a second time. That time he will not come to remove sin. He will bring salvation to the people who wait for him.

## Chapter 10

**1** The law is only a shadow of the good things that will come. It is not the true form of those things. The priests offer the same sacrifices every year. But these sacrifices can never make perfect the people who come to worship.

**2** If the sacrifices made the worshippers clean, the priests can stop the sacrifices. Then the worshippers are clean 1 time for all time. They do not feel guilty for their sins any more.

**3** But the sacrifices remind the people of their sins every year.

**4** The blood of bulls and goats cannot take away sins.

**5** Thus when Christ came into the world, he said to God, "You did not want sacrifices and offerings. But you prepared a body for me.

**6** You were not happy with burnt offerings and sacrifices for sin.

**7** Then I said, 'I am here, O God. I came to do what you want. The book of the law tells about me.'"

**8** First Christ said, "You did not want sacrifices, offerings, burnt offerings, and sacrifices for sin. You were not happy with them." The law tells the priests to offer these things.

**9** Then he said, "I am here. I came to do what you want." Thus God removes the first system to start the second system.

**10** God wanted Jesus Christ to offer his body as a sacrifice 1 time for all time. Through this sacrifice, God makes us holy.

**11** Every priest stands every day and does his service. He offers the same sacrifices many times. But these sacrifices can never take away sins.

**12** But Christ offered 1 sacrifice for sins for all time. Then he sat down at the right hand of God.

**13** Now Christ waits until God puts his enemies under his feet.

**14** With 1 offering, Christ made perfect for all time the people that God makes holy.

**15** The Holy Spirit also tells us this. First he says,

**16** "This is the covenant that I will make with them after those days," says the Lord. "I will put my laws on their hearts. I will write my laws in their minds."

**17** Then he says, "I will not remember their sins and the wrong that they do any more."

**18** When God forgives these sins, there is no more need for an offering for sin.

**19** Thus, brothers, we can enter the Most Holy Place with courage, because of the blood of Jesus.

**20** Jesus opened a new way for us, a way that gives life. This way goes through the curtain, and the curtain is his body.

**21** We also have a great high priest over the house of God.

**22** Thus we must come near to God with an honest heart and with complete faith. God made our hearts clean from a guilty conscience. He washed our bodies with pure water.

**23** We must hold firmly to the hope that we declare. We must not doubt, because God is faithful to his promises.

**24** We must think about how to encourage each other to love and to do good works.

**25** Some people stopped the habit to come together. But we must continue to come together. We must encourage each other. Do this more and more, because you see that the day of the Lord comes near.

**26** We received the knowledge of the truth. If we then continue to sin on purpose, no sacrifice for sins remains.

**27** Only a terrible wait for judgment remains. A fierce fire will destroy the enemies of God.

**28** If a person did not obey the law of Moses, he died without mercy. He died if 2 or 3 witnesses spoke against him.

**29** Thus a person who rejects the Son of God will get a much worse punishment. That person thinks that the blood of the covenant is not holy. But that blood made him holy. He also insults the Spirit of grace.

**30** We know God, who said, "I will punish people. I will pay them back." He also said, "The Lord will judge his people."

**31** It is terrible to fall into the hands of the God who lives.

**32** Remember the past days. At that time you received the light of the truth. Then you suffered much, but you continued to stand firm.

**33** Sometimes people insulted you and hurt you in public. Sometimes you helped other people who suffered the same things.

**34** You were kind to the prisoners. When people took away your property, you accepted this with joy. You knew that you had better possessions in heaven. Those possessions will continue for all time.

**35** Thus do not lose your courage. Your courage will bring you a great reward.

**36** You must be patient. Then, after you do what God wants, you will receive what he promised.

**37** The Scriptures say, "In a very short time, the person who will come will come. He will not wait.

**38** The person that I accept as righteous will live by faith. But if he turns back, I will not be happy with him."

**39** But we are not people who turn back and go to destruction. We are people who have faith, and God saves us.

## Chapter 11

**1** Faith is to be sure of the things that we hope for. Faith is to know that the things that we cannot see are true.

**2** Because of their faith, God approved of the people who lived long ago.

**3** Through faith we understand that God made the worlds with his word. Thus God did not make the things that we see from things that we can see.

**4** Through faith Abel offered God a better sacrifice than Cain did. Because of his faith, God accepted Abel as a righteous man. God showed that he accepted the gifts of Abel. Abel is dead, but through his faith he still speaks.

**5** Through faith God took Enoch away, and Enoch did not die. People did not find him, because God took him away. Before God took him, God said that Enoch pleased him.

**6** Without faith, nobody can please God. Each person who comes to God must believe that God exists. He must believe that God rewards the people who look for him seriously.

**7** Through faith Noah obeyed God. God warned him about events that nobody saw yet. Noah respected God, and he built a large boat (ark) to save his family. Through his faith, Noah showed that the world was guilty. He received the righteousness that comes through faith.

**8** Through faith Abraham obeyed when God called him. God told him to go to a land that he will receive later as his inheritance. Abraham went, but he did not know where he went.

**9** Through faith Abraham lived as a foreigner in the land that God promised him. He lived in tents. Isaac and Jacob also lived in tents. God gave them the same promise.

**10** Abraham waited for the city that has firm foundations. God planned that city and built it.

**11** Through faith Sarah also received the power to have a child. She was too old to have a child. But she believed that God was faithful to his promise.

**12** Thus Abraham was too old to have children, but he became the ancestor of very many descendants. They were as many as the stars in the sky. Nobody can count them, as nobody can count the sand on the seashore.

**13** All these people continued to have faith until they died. They did not receive the things that God promised. But they saw them far in the future, and they were glad. They said that they were foreigners and travelers on the earth.

**14** People who say such things show clearly that they look for a country of their own.

**15** If they thought about the country that they left, they could go back to it.

**16** But they wanted a better country, a country in heaven. Thus God feels no shame when people call him their God. He prepared a city for them.

**17** Through faith Abraham offered Isaac as a sacrifice when God tested him. God gave Abraham the promises. But Abraham was ready to offer his only son.

**18** God said to Abraham, "Your descendants will come through Isaac."

**19** Abraham thought that God could even bring Isaac back from death. In a figure, Abraham did receive Isaac back from death.

**20** Through faith Isaac blessed Jacob and Esau. He spoke about their future.

**21** Through faith Jacob blessed each of the sons of Joseph when Jacob was near death. He worshipped God while he leaned on the top of his staff.

**22** Through faith Joseph spoke about the time when the people of Israel will leave Egypt. He was near death. He told them what to do with his bones.

**23** Through faith the parents of Moses hid him for 3 months after his birth. They saw that he was a beautiful child. They were not afraid of the command of the king.

**24** Through faith Moses became a man. Then he refused the name "son of the daughter of Pharaoh."

**25** He chose to suffer with the people of God. He did not want to enjoy sin for a short time.

**26** He thought that to suffer for the Christ was of more value than all the treasures of Egypt. He looked forward to his reward.

**27** Through faith Moses left Egypt. He was not afraid of the anger of the king. He continued to be firm, because he saw God, who is invisible.

**28** Through faith Moses kept the Passover. He told the people to put blood on their doors. Then the angel who killed the firstborn sons did not touch the firstborn sons of Israel.

**29** Through faith the people of Israel went through the Red Sea on dry ground. When the Egyptians tried to do this, the water drowned them.

**30** Through faith the people of Israel walked around the walls of Jericho for 7 days. Then the walls fell down.

**31** Through faith the prostitute Rahab did not die with the people who did not obey God. She received the spies of Israel in peace.

**32** What more can I say? I do not have time to tell about Gideon, Barak, Samson, and Jephthah. I do not have time to tell about David, Samuel, and the prophets.

**33** Through faith these people defeated kingdoms. They did what is right. They received what God promised. They closed the mouths of lions.

**34** They stopped the power of fire. They escaped death by the sword. They were weak, but they became strong. They were strong in war. They made foreign armies run away.

**35** Women received their dead family members back to life. People tortured other believers. These believers refused to accept freedom, because they wanted a better resurrection.

**36** People laughed at some of them and beat them with whips. People put others in chains and in prison.

**37** People killed some of them with stones. People cut some of them into 2 parts with a saw. People killed some of them with the sword. Some of them went around in the skins of sheep and goats. They were poor, they suffered, and people treated them badly.

**38** The world was not good enough for these people. They went through deserts and mountains. They lived in caves and in holes in the ground.

**39** God approved of all these people because of their faith. But none of them received what God promised.

**40** God planned something better for us. He wanted them to become perfect only together with us.

## Chapter 12

**1** Thus a large crowd of witnesses is around us. We must put away every thing that slows us down. We must put away the sin that holds us so easily. We must run with patience the race that God put in front of us.

**2** We must look at Jesus. He started our faith, and he makes our faith perfect. He suffered death on the cross because of the joy that was in front of him. He ignored the shame of the cross. Now he sits at the right hand of the throne of God.

**3** Think about Jesus. Sinners fought against him, and he did not stop. Then you will not become tired and lose your courage.

**4** You fight against sin. But you did not yet fight so hard that you lost your blood.

**5** Did you forget the encouragement that God speaks to you as his children? He says, "My son, do not think that the discipline of the Lord is not important. Do not lose courage when he corrects you.

**6** The Lord disciplines each person that he loves. He punishes each person that he accepts as his son."

**7** Accept your troubles as discipline. God treats you as his sons. Every father disciplines his son.

**8** God disciplines all his children. If you do not get discipline, then you are not true sons. You are illegitimate children.

**9** Our human fathers disciplined us, and we respected them. Thus we must obey the Father of our spirits even more. Then we will live.

**10** Our human fathers disciplined us for a short time. They did what they thought was best. But God disciplines us for our good. He does this so that we can share in his holiness.

**11** When we get discipline, it does not feel good. It causes pain. But later, the people who learn from it receive peace and a righteous life.

**12** Thus make your weak hands and weak knees strong again.

**13** Make straight paths for your feet. Then the weak leg will not get worse. It will get better.

**14** Try to live in peace with all people. Try to be holy. Without holiness, nobody will see the Lord.

**15** Be careful. Make sure that nobody fails to receive the grace of God. Make sure that nobody becomes bitter like a poisonous plant that grows. A bitter person causes trouble and makes many people unclean.

**16** Make sure that nobody commits sexual sin. Make sure that nobody is like Esau, who did not respect God. Esau sold his rights as the oldest son for 1 meal.

**17** You know that later Esau wanted to get the blessing from his father. But his father refused. Esau cried and looked hard for a way to change his father's decision. But he could not change it.

**18** You did not come to a mountain that people can touch. You did not come to a mountain that burned with fire. You did not come to darkness, gloom, and a storm.

**19** You did not come to the sound of a trumpet and to a voice that spoke words. The people who heard that voice asked God to speak to them no more.

**20** They could not accept the command of God. God said, "If even an animal touches the mountain, you must kill it with stones."

**21** The sight was so terrible that Moses said, "I am very afraid, and I shake."

**22** But you came to Mount Zion. You came to the city of the God who lives, the Jerusalem in heaven. You came to thousands of angels in a joyful assembly.

**23** You came to the church of the firstborn. God wrote their names in heaven. You came to God, who is the judge of all people. You came to the spirits of righteous people that God made perfect.

**24** You came to Jesus, who brings the new covenant between God and people. You came to the blood that he sprinkled. His blood speaks better things than the blood of Abel.

**25** Be careful. Do not refuse to listen to God, who speaks to you. The people of Israel refused to listen to God when he warned them on earth. They did not escape. Thus we will surely not escape if we turn away from God, who warns us from heaven.

**26** At that time the voice of God shook the earth. But now he promises, "1 more time I will shake the earth and also the heavens."

**27** The words "1 more time" show that God will remove the things that he shook. These are the things that he made. Then the things that nobody can shake will remain.

**28** Thus we will receive a kingdom that nobody can shake. We must be thankful. We must worship God in a way that pleases him, with respect and fear.

**29** Our God is like a fire that destroys everything.

## Chapter 13

**1** Continue to love each other as brothers.

**2** Do not forget to welcome strangers into your homes. Some people did this, and they welcomed angels, but they did not know it.

**3** Remember the people who are in prison. Think of them as if you are in prison with them. Remember the people who suffer. Think of them as if you suffer with them.

**4** All people must respect marriage. Husbands and wives must stay faithful to each other. God will judge people who commit sexual sin and adultery.

**5** Do not love money. Be happy with what you have. God said, "I will never leave you. I will never abandon you."

**6** Thus we can say with confidence, "The Lord is my helper. I will not be afraid. What can people do to me?"

**7** Remember your leaders, who told you the word of God. Think about the good results of their lives. Copy their faith.

**8** Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today, and for all time.

**9** Do not let different and strange teachings lead you away. It is good that grace makes our hearts strong. Rules about food do not make our hearts strong. Those rules did not help the people who obeyed them.

**10** We have an altar. The priests who serve in the tabernacle have no right to eat from it.

**11** The high priest takes the blood of animals into the Most Holy Place as an offering for sin. But people burn the bodies of those animals outside the camp.

**12** Thus Jesus also suffered and died outside the gate of the city. He did this to make the people holy with his own blood.

**13** Thus we must go to Jesus outside the camp. We must accept the same shame that he accepted.

**14** Here on earth we do not have a city that will continue for all time. We look for the city that will come.

**15** Thus, through Jesus, we must always offer praise to God as our sacrifice. Our lips must declare his name and thank him.

**16** Do not forget to do good and to share with other people. These sacrifices please God.

**17** Obey your leaders and accept their authority. They watch over your souls, and they must give an account to God. Obey them so that they can do their work with joy and not with sadness. Sadness will not help you.

**18** Pray for us. We are sure that we have a clear conscience. We want to live honestly in all things.

**19** I ask you especially to pray. Then God will send me back to you soon.

**20** God gives peace. He brought our Lord Jesus back from death. Jesus is the great shepherd of the sheep. His blood confirms the eternal covenant.

**21** I pray that God will give you all the good things that you need to do what he wants. I pray that he will do in us what pleases him, through Jesus Christ. Glory to Jesus Christ for all time! Amen.

**22** Brothers, I ask you to listen with patience to my words of encouragement. My letter to you is short.

**23** I want you to know that our brother Timothy is free from prison. If he comes soon, I will bring him with me when I visit you.

**24** Say hello to all your leaders and to all the people of God. The believers from Italy say hello to you.

**25** I pray that the grace of God will be with all of you. Amen.
