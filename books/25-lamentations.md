# Lamentations

## Chapter 1

**1** The city of Jerusalem was full of people, but now she is alone! She is like a widow! She was great among the nations. She was a princess among the provinces. Now she is a slave who must pay tax!

**2** She cries bitterly at night. Tears are on her face. All her lovers left her, and none of them comforts her. All her friends turned against her. They became her enemies.

**3** Judah went away into exile after much pain and hard work as slaves. She lives among the other nations. She finds no rest. All the people who chase her caught her in her troubles.

**4** The roads to Zion are sad, because no people come to the holy festivals. All her gates are empty. Her priests cry with pain. Her young women are sad. Zion has bitter sorrow.

**5** Her enemies became her masters. Her enemies have success. The LORD caused her pain because of her many sins. Her children went away as prisoners in front of the enemy.

**6** All the beauty of Zion is gone. Her officials are like deer that find no grass. They have no strength, and they run away in front of the hunter.

**7** In the days of her pain and her trouble, Jerusalem remembers all her good things from past times. Her people fell into the hands of the enemy, and no person helped her. Her enemies looked at her. They laughed at her ruin.

**8** Jerusalem did very bad sins. Thus she became unclean. All the people who honored her now hate her, because they saw her shame. She cries with pain and turns away.

**9** Her dirt is on her clothes. She did not think about her future. Thus her fall was terrible. No person comforts her. "LORD, look at my pain, because the enemy won!"

**10** The enemy put his hand on all her valuable things. She saw foreign nations go into her temple. LORD, you commanded that these nations must not go into your assembly.

**11** All her people cry with pain while they look for bread. They give their valuable things for food to stay alive. "LORD, look at me and think about me. People hate me now."

**12** "You people who go past, does this not have importance to you? Look and see. Is there any pain like my pain? The LORD caused this pain on the day of his strong anger.

**13** He sent fire from above into my bones. He put a net in front of my feet to catch me. He turned me back. He made me empty and weak all day.

**14** He tied my sins together like a yoke. He put them on my neck. He took away my strength. The Lord gave me to enemies whom I cannot fight.

**15** The Lord rejected all my strong soldiers. He called an army against me to crush my young men. The Lord crushed the people of Judah as a person crushes grapes in a winepress.

**16** Because of these things, I cry. Tears flow from my eyes. No person is near to comfort me. No person is near to give me strength again. My children are alone, because the enemy won."

**17** Zion holds out her hands, but no person comforts her. The LORD commanded the neighbors of Jacob to be his enemies. Jerusalem became an unclean thing among them.

**18** "The LORD is right, because I disobeyed his command. Listen, all you people. Look at my pain. My young women and my young men went away as prisoners.

**19** I called to my lovers, but they betrayed me. My priests and my leaders died in the city. They looked for food to stay alive.

**20** LORD, look at me, because I am in distress! I have pain inside me. My heart is troubled, because I disobeyed you very much. Outside, the sword kills. Inside the house, there is death.

**21** People heard my cries, but no person comforts me. All my enemies heard about my trouble. They are glad that you did this to me. Bring the day that you promised. Then my enemies will be like me.

**22** Look at all their evil. Do to them what you did to me because of all my sins. I cry with pain many times, and my heart is weak."

## Chapter 2

**1** The Lord was angry, and he covered Zion with a dark cloud! He threw the glory of Israel down from the sky to the earth. On the day of his anger, he did not remember his temple, which is his footstool.

**2** The Lord destroyed all the homes of Jacob, and he had no pity. In his anger he broke down the forts of Judah. He threw them to the ground. He brought shame on the kingdom and its officials.

**3** In his strong anger he took away all the strength of Israel. He took back his right hand when the enemy came. He burned against Jacob like a fire that destroys all things around it.

**4** He pulled his bow like an enemy. He held up his right hand like an enemy. He killed all the people whom Zion loved. He poured out his anger like fire on the tents of Zion.

**5** The Lord was like an enemy. He destroyed Israel. He destroyed all her palaces and her forts. He caused much sorrow and many tears among the people of Judah.

**6** He broke down his temple as a person breaks down a garden hut. He destroyed the place where his people met. The LORD caused the people of Zion to forget the holy festivals and the Sabbaths. In his strong anger he rejected the king and the priest.

**7** The Lord rejected his altar. He hated his holy place. He gave the walls of her palaces to the enemy. The enemy shouted in the house of the LORD as people shout on a festival day.

**8** The LORD decided to destroy the wall of Zion. He measured the wall with a line. He did not stop his destruction. He caused the walls and the defenses to become sad. They fell together.

**9** Her gates went down into the ground. He destroyed and broke the bars of her gates. Her king and her officials are in exile among the nations. No person teaches the law now. Her prophets get no vision from the LORD.

**10** The leaders of Zion sit on the ground in silence. They put dust on their heads. They wear sackcloth. The young women of Jerusalem bow their heads to the ground.

**11** My eyes are weak from tears. I have pain inside me. My heart is full of sorrow because the enemy destroyed my people. Children and babies become weak in the streets of the city.

**12** The children say to their mothers, "Where is the bread and the wine?" They become weak like wounded men in the streets of the city. Their lives go away in the arms of their mothers.

**13** Jerusalem, what can I say to you? What can I compare you to? How can I comfort you, Zion? Your ruin is as large as the sea. Who can heal you?

**14** Your prophets saw false and foolish visions for you. They did not show you your sins. If they did, you could avoid exile. They gave you false messages that caused you to go away.

**15** All the people who go past clap their hands at you. They make sounds of contempt and shake their heads at Jerusalem. They say, "Is this the city that people called perfect in beauty, the joy of all the earth?"

**16** All your enemies open their mouths against you. They make sounds of contempt and grind their teeth. They say, "We destroyed her! This is the day that we waited for. Now we see it!"

**17** The LORD did what he planned. He did what he said a long time ago. He destroyed you and did not have pity. He caused your enemies to be glad about you. He made your enemies strong.

**18** The hearts of the people cry to the Lord. Wall of Zion, let your tears flow like a river, day and night. Do not rest. Do not let your eyes stop their tears.

**19** Get up and cry out in the night. At the start of each part of the night, tell the Lord all that is in your heart. Lift up your hands to him. Pray for the lives of your children. They become weak from hunger at the corner of each street.

**20** "LORD, look and think! You never did this to any other people. Must the women eat their own children, whom they hold and love? Must the enemy kill the priest and the prophet in the temple of the Lord?

**21** The young and the old lie on the ground in the streets. My young women and my young men died by the sword. You killed them on the day of your anger. You killed them and did not have pity.

**22** You called terrors against me from all sides, as people call others to a festival. On the day of the anger of the LORD, no person escaped. My enemy killed the children that I held and raised."

## Chapter 3

**1** I am a man who saw much pain. God punished me with the rod of his anger.

**2** He made me walk in darkness, not in light.

**3** He hits me again and again with his hand all day.

**4** He made my flesh and my skin old. He broke my bones.

**5** He built walls against me. He put bitterness and trouble around me.

**6** He made me live in darkness, like people who died a long time ago.

**7** He put a wall around me, and I cannot go out. He put heavy chains on me.

**8** I call out and shout for help, but he does not listen to my prayer.

**9** He blocked my roads with walls of stone. He made my paths crooked.

**10** He waited for me like a bear. He hid like a lion in a secret place.

**11** He pulled me off the road and tore me to pieces. He left me helpless.

**12** He pulled his bow. He made me the target for his arrows.

**13** He shot his arrows deep into my body.

**14** All my people laugh at me. They sing songs about me all day.

**15** He filled me with bitterness. He made me drink bitter things.

**16** He broke my teeth with stones. He pushed me down into the dust.

**17** I have no peace. I forgot what good times are.

**18** I said, "My strength is gone. I have no more hope in the LORD."

**19** Remember my pain and my trouble, my bitterness and my pain.

**20** I remember these things all the time. My spirit is low inside me.

**21** But I think about this, and thus I have hope:

**22** Because of the faithful love of the LORD, we are not destroyed. His mercy does not stop.

**23** His mercy is new each morning. LORD, you are very faithful.

**24** I say, "The LORD is all that I have. Thus I will put my hope in him."

**25** The LORD is good to people who wait for him. He is good to the person who looks for him.

**26** It is good to wait quietly for the LORD to save us.

**27** It is good for a man to carry a yoke while he is young.

**28** Let him sit alone in silence when the LORD puts the yoke on him.

**29** Let him bow down with his face in the dust. Possibly there is still hope.

**30** Let him turn his cheek to the person who hits him. Let him accept insults.

**31** The Lord will not reject people for all time.

**32** The Lord causes sorrow, but he will also show mercy. His faithful love is very great.

**33** He does not want to cause pain or sorrow to people.

**34** People crush all the prisoners of the earth under their feet.

**35** People refuse justice to a man in front of the Most High.

**36** People do wrong to a man in court. The Lord does not approve of these things.

**37** Who can speak and cause something to happen, if the Lord does not command it?

**38** Both bad things and good things come from the command of the Most High.

**39** Why does a man who is alive complain when God punishes him for his sins?

**40** We must examine our ways and test them. We must come back to the LORD.

**41** We must lift up our hearts and our hands to God in heaven.

**42** "We sinned and disobeyed you. You did not forgive us.

**43** You covered yourself with anger, and you chased us. You killed us and did not have pity.

**44** You covered yourself with a cloud. Thus our prayers cannot go through to you.

**45** You made us like dirt and garbage among the nations.

**46** All our enemies open their mouths to insult us.

**47** Fear and traps came on us. Ruin and destruction came on us."

**48** Rivers of tears flow from my eyes, because the enemy destroyed my people.

**49** My tears flow and do not stop. They flow all the time.

**50** My tears will flow until the LORD looks down from heaven and sees.

**51** My eyes cause me pain because of the sorrow of all the women of my city.

**52** My enemies hunted me like a bird. They had no reason to hate me.

**53** They threw me alive into a pit. They threw stones at me.

**54** Water flowed over my head. I said, "I will die."

**55** LORD, I called to you from the bottom of the pit.

**56** You heard my voice. Do not close your ear to my cry for help.

**57** You came near on the day that I called to you. You said, "Do not be afraid."

**58** Lord, you defended me. You saved my life.

**59** LORD, you saw the wrong that people did to me. Judge my case.

**60** You saw all their hate. You saw all their plans against me.

**61** LORD, you heard their insults and all their plans against me.

**62** My enemies talk against me and plan against me all day.

**63** Look at them! When they sit down and when they stand up, they sing songs about me to laugh at me.

**64** LORD, punish them for what they did.

**65** Make their hearts hard. Put your curse on them.

**66** In your anger, chase them and destroy them from under the sky of the LORD.

## Chapter 4

**1** The gold lost its shine! The pure gold changed! The holy stones lie at the corner of each street.

**2** The valuable people of Zion were worth their weight in pure gold. Now people think that they are only clay jars that a potter made!

**3** Even jackals give milk to their young. But my people became cruel, like ostriches in the desert.

**4** The tongues of babies stick to the top of their mouths because they are thirsty. The children ask for bread, but no person gives them bread.

**5** The people who ate good food now die in the streets. The people who wore expensive clothes now lie on piles of garbage.

**6** The punishment of my people is greater than the punishment of Sodom. God destroyed Sodom suddenly, and no human hands helped her.

**7** Her princes were cleaner than snow and whiter than milk. Their bodies were redder than rubies. Their appearance was like sapphire.

**8** Now their faces are blacker than coal. People do not know them in the streets. Their skin is tight on their bones. Their skin became dry like wood.

**9** The people who died by the sword were better than the people who died from hunger. The hungry people became weak and died because there was no food from the fields.

**10** Women who were kind cooked their own children with their own hands. The children became food for them when the enemy destroyed my people.

**11** The LORD showed all his anger. He poured out his strong anger. He started a fire in Zion that burned her foundations.

**12** The kings of the earth and all the people of the world did not believe this. They did not believe that an enemy could go through the gates of Jerusalem.

**13** But this happened because of the sins of her prophets and the evil of her priests. They killed good people in the city.

**14** Now these leaders wander in the streets like blind men. Blood makes them unclean. Thus no person can touch their clothes.

**15** People shouted at them, "Go away! You are unclean! Go away! Do not touch us!" Thus they ran away and wandered. The people of the other nations said, "They cannot stay here."

**16** The LORD in his anger sent them in different directions. He will not look after them again. The people did not respect the priests. They did not honor the leaders.

**17** Our eyes became tired while we looked for help. But no help came. We watched for a nation that could not save us.

**18** The enemy watched our steps. Thus we could not walk in our streets. Our end was near. Our days were finished. Our end came.

**19** The people who chased us were faster than eagles in the sky. They chased us on the mountains. They waited to attack us in the desert.

**20** The king whom the LORD chose was as important to us as our breath. But the enemy caught him in their traps. We said, "He will protect us among the nations."

**21** People of Edom, who live in the land of Uz, be happy and glad for now. But the cup of punishment will also come to you. You will become drunk and naked.

**22** People of Zion, your punishment is complete. The LORD will not send you into exile again. But people of Edom, he will punish your sins. He will show your evil.

## Chapter 5

**1** LORD, remember what happened to us. Look and see our shame.

**2** Strangers took our land. Foreigners took our houses.

**3** We are orphans with no fathers. Our mothers are like widows.

**4** We must pay money for our water. We must buy our wood.

**5** The enemy is close behind us. We are tired, and we have no rest.

**6** We made agreements with Egypt and Assyria to get enough food.

**7** Our fathers sinned, and they are dead. Now we get the punishment for their sins.

**8** Slaves rule over us. No person saves us from their power.

**9** We risk our lives to get food, because of the killers in the desert.

**10** Our skin is hot like an oven because of the terrible hunger.

**11** The enemy raped the women in Zion and the young women in the towns of Judah.

**12** The enemy hanged our princes by their hands. They did not respect our old leaders.

**13** The young men must grind grain at the mill. The boys fall down under heavy loads of wood.

**14** The old men do not sit at the city gate now. The young men stopped their music.

**15** The joy in our hearts stopped. Our dances changed into sorrow.

**16** The crown fell from our head. We have great trouble, because we sinned!

**17** Because of this, our hearts are weak. Because of these things, our eyes are dark.

**18** Mount Zion is empty, and foxes walk on it.

**19** But you, LORD, rule for all time. Your throne continues for all generations.

**20** Why do you always forget us? Why do you leave us for such a long time?

**21** LORD, bring us back to you, and we will come back. Make our lives new, as in past times.

**22** Or did you completely reject us? Are you very angry with us?
