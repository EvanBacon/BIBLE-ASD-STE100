# Numbers

## Chapter 1

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses in the Tent of Meeting in the desert of Sinai. This was on the 1st day of the 2nd month, in the 2nd year after the people of Israel came out of Egypt. The LORD said,

**2** "Count all the people of Israel. Count them by their clans and by their families. Write the name of each male.

**3** You and Aaron must count all the men in Israel who are 20 years old or more and who can fight in the army. Count them by their divisions.

**4** 1 man from each tribe must help you. Each man must be the leader of his family.

**5** These are the names of the men who will help you. From the tribe of Reuben, Elizur the son of Shedeur.

**6** From Simeon, Shelumiel the son of Zurishaddai.

**7** From Judah, Nahshon the son of Amminadab.

**8** From Issachar, Nethanel the son of Zuar.

**9** From Zebulun, Eliab the son of Helon.

**10** From the sons of Joseph: from Ephraim, Elishama the son of Ammihud, and from Manasseh, Gamaliel the son of Pedahzur.

**11** From Benjamin, Abidan the son of Gideoni.

**12** From Dan, Ahiezer the son of Ammishaddai.

**13** From Asher, Pagiel the son of Ocran.

**14** From Gad, Eliasaph the son of Deuel.

**15** From Naphtali, Ahira the son of Enan."

**16** These were the men that the people chose. They were the leaders of the tribes of their ancestors. They were the heads of the clans of Israel.

**17** Moses and Aaron took these men whose names the LORD gave.

**18** On the 1st day of the 2nd month, they called all the people together. The people recorded their ancestry by clans and by families. They wrote the name of each male who was 20 years old or more.

**19** Moses counted them in the desert of Sinai, as the LORD commanded him.

**20** They counted the descendants of Reuben, the firstborn son of Israel. They recorded them by clans and by families. They wrote the name of each male who was 20 years old or more and who could fight in the army.

**21** The total from the tribe of Reuben was 46,500.

**22** They counted the descendants of Simeon. They recorded them by clans and by families. They wrote the name of each male who was 20 years old or more and who could fight in the army.

**23** The total from the tribe of Simeon was 59,300.

**24** They counted the descendants of Gad. They recorded them by clans and by families. They wrote the name of each male who was 20 years old or more and who could fight in the army.

**25** The total from the tribe of Gad was 45,650.

**26** They counted the descendants of Judah. They recorded them by clans and by families. They wrote the name of each male who was 20 years old or more and who could fight in the army.

**27** The total from the tribe of Judah was 74,600.

**28** They counted the descendants of Issachar. They recorded them by clans and by families. They wrote the name of each male who was 20 years old or more and who could fight in the army.

**29** The total from the tribe of Issachar was 54,400.

**30** They counted the descendants of Zebulun. They recorded them by clans and by families. They wrote the name of each male who was 20 years old or more and who could fight in the army.

**31** The total from the tribe of Zebulun was 57,400.

**32** They counted the descendants of Joseph. From Ephraim, they recorded them by clans and by families. They wrote the name of each male who was 20 years old or more and who could fight in the army.

**33** The total from the tribe of Ephraim was 40,500.

**34** They counted the descendants of Manasseh. They recorded them by clans and by families. They wrote the name of each male who was 20 years old or more and who could fight in the army.

**35** The total from the tribe of Manasseh was 32,200.

**36** They counted the descendants of Benjamin. They recorded them by clans and by families. They wrote the name of each male who was 20 years old or more and who could fight in the army.

**37** The total from the tribe of Benjamin was 35,400.

**38** They counted the descendants of Dan. They recorded them by clans and by families. They wrote the name of each male who was 20 years old or more and who could fight in the army.

**39** The total from the tribe of Dan was 62,700.

**40** They counted the descendants of Asher. They recorded them by clans and by families. They wrote the name of each male who was 20 years old or more and who could fight in the army.

**41** The total from the tribe of Asher was 41,500.

**42** They counted the descendants of Naphtali. They recorded them by clans and by families. They wrote the name of each male who was 20 years old or more and who could fight in the army.

**43** The total from the tribe of Naphtali was 53,400.

**44** Moses, Aaron and the 12 leaders of Israel counted these men. Each leader represented his family.

**45** They counted all the men of Israel who were 20 years old or more and who could fight in the army. They counted them by families.

**46** The total was 603,550.

**47** But they did not count the Levites with the other tribes.

**48** The LORD told Moses,

**49** "Do not count the tribe of Levi. Do not include them with the other people of Israel.

**50** Make the Levites responsible for the tabernacle of the Testimony, for all its equipment and for all the things that belong to it. They must carry the tabernacle and all its equipment. They must do the work of the tabernacle. They must put their tents around it.

**51** When the tabernacle moves, the Levites must take it down. When the people stop to camp, the Levites must put it up. Any other person who comes near it must be killed.

**52** The people of Israel must put up their tents in their divisions. Each man must camp in his own camp, near the flag of his group.

**53** But the Levites must put up their tents around the tabernacle of the Testimony. Thus the anger of the LORD will not come on the people of Israel. The Levites must guard the tabernacle of the Testimony."

**54** The people of Israel did all that the LORD commanded Moses.

## Chapter 2

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron. He said,

**2** "Each man of Israel must camp near his flag, with the signs of his family. They must camp around the Tent of Meeting, at a distance from it.

**3** The divisions of the camp of Judah must camp on the east side, toward the sunrise. They must camp near their flag. Nahshon the son of Amminadab is the leader of the people of Judah.

**4** His division has 74,600 men.

**5** The tribe of Issachar must camp next to Judah. Nethanel the son of Zuar is the leader of the people of Issachar.

**6** His division has 54,400 men.

**7** Then the tribe of Zebulun must camp there. Eliab the son of Helon is the leader of the people of Zebulun.

**8** His division has 57,400 men.

**9** The total of the men in the camp of Judah is 186,400. They will move out first.

**10** The divisions of the camp of Reuben must camp on the south side near their flag. Elizur the son of Shedeur is the leader of the people of Reuben.

**11** His division has 46,500 men.

**12** The tribe of Simeon must camp next to Reuben. Shelumiel the son of Zurishaddai is the leader of the people of Simeon.

**13** His division has 59,300 men.

**14** Then the tribe of Gad must camp there. Eliasaph the son of Reuel is the leader of the people of Gad.

**15** His division has 45,650 men.

**16** The total of the men in the camp of Reuben is 151,450. They will move out second.

**17** Then the Tent of Meeting and the camp of the Levites will move out in the middle of the camps. The tribes must move out in the same order as they camp. Each man must stay in his position near his flag.

**18** The divisions of the camp of Ephraim must camp on the west side near their flag. Elishama the son of Ammihud is the leader of the people of Ephraim.

**19** His division has 40,500 men.

**20** The tribe of Manasseh must camp next to Ephraim. Gamaliel the son of Pedahzur is the leader of the people of Manasseh.

**21** His division has 32,200 men.

**22** Then the tribe of Benjamin must camp there. Abidan the son of Gideoni is the leader of the people of Benjamin.

**23** His division has 35,400 men.

**24** The total of the men in the camp of Ephraim is 108,100. They will move out third.

**25** The divisions of the camp of Dan must camp on the north side near their flag. Ahiezer the son of Ammishaddai is the leader of the people of Dan.

**26** His division has 62,700 men.

**27** The tribe of Asher must camp next to Dan. Pagiel the son of Ocran is the leader of the people of Asher.

**28** His division has 41,500 men.

**29** Then the tribe of Naphtali must camp there. Ahira the son of Enan is the leader of the people of Naphtali.

**30** His division has 53,400 men.

**31** The total of the men in the camp of Dan is 157,600. They will move out last, with their flags."

**32** These are the men of Israel that Moses and Aaron counted by families. The total of all the men in the camps was 603,550.

**33** But they did not count the Levites with the other people of Israel, as the LORD commanded Moses.

**34** The people of Israel did all that the LORD commanded Moses. They camped near their flags, and they moved out in that order. Each man stayed with his clan and his family.

## Chapter 3

**1** These are the descendants of Aaron and Moses at the time when the LORD spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai.

**2** The names of the sons of Aaron were Nadab, the firstborn, Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.

**3** These were the sons of Aaron, who were priests. Moses poured oil on them (anointed them) and made them holy to serve as priests.

**4** But Nadab and Abihu died in front of the LORD in the desert of Sinai. They died because they offered fire that the LORD did not permit. They had no sons. Thus Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests while their father Aaron was alive.

**5** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**6** "Bring the tribe of Levi to Aaron the priest. They must help him.

**7** They must do work for Aaron and for all the people at the Tent of Meeting. They must do the work of the tabernacle.

**8** They must take care of all the equipment of the Tent of Meeting. They must do the work of the tabernacle for the people of Israel.

**9** Give the Levites to Aaron and his sons. From all the people of Israel, the Levites are given completely to Aaron.

**10** Make Aaron and his sons responsible for the work of the priests. Any other person who comes near the holy place must be killed."

**11** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**12** "I take the Levites from among the people of Israel. I take them in place of all the firstborn sons of Israel. Thus the Levites are mine.

**13** All the firstborn are mine. I killed all the firstborn in the land of Egypt. On that day I made holy for myself all the firstborn in Israel, both men and animals. They are mine. I am the LORD."

**14** The LORD spoke to Moses in the desert of Sinai. He said,

**15** "Count the Levites by their families and by their clans. Count each male who is 1 month old or more."

**16** Thus Moses counted them, as the LORD commanded him.

**17** These were the sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath and Merari.

**18** The sons of Gershon were Libni and Shimei. They were the heads of clans.

**19** The sons of Kohath were Amram, Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel. They were the heads of clans.

**20** The sons of Merari were Mahli and Mushi. They were the heads of clans. These were the clans of the Levites by their families.

**21** The clans of the Libnites and the Shimeites came from Gershon. These were the clans of the Gershonites.

**22** The total of all the males who were 1 month old or more was 7,500.

**23** The clans of the Gershonites camped behind the tabernacle, on the west side.

**24** The leader of the families of the Gershonites was Eliasaph the son of Lael.

**25** At the Tent of Meeting, the Gershonites were responsible for the tabernacle, the tent, its coverings and the curtain at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

**26** They were also responsible for the curtains of the courtyard and the curtain at the entrance of the courtyard. The courtyard is around the tabernacle and the altar. They were also responsible for the ropes and for all the work on these things.

**27** The clans of the Amramites, the Izharites, the Hebronites and the Uzzielites came from Kohath. These were the clans of the Kohathites.

**28** The total of all the males who were 1 month old or more was 8,600. They were responsible for the holy place.

**29** The clans of the Kohathites camped on the south side of the tabernacle.

**30** The leader of the families of the Kohathites was Elizaphan the son of Uzziel.

**31** They were responsible for the ark, the table, the lampstand, the altars and the equipment that the priests used in the holy place. They were also responsible for the curtain and for all the work on these things.

**32** Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, was the chief leader of the Levites. He supervised the men who were responsible for the holy place.

**33** The clans of the Mahlites and the Mushites came from Merari. These were the clans of Merari.

**34** The total of all the males who were 1 month old or more was 6,200.

**35** The leader of the families of Merari was Zuriel the son of Abihail. They camped on the north side of the tabernacle.

**36** The Merarites were responsible for the frames of the tabernacle, its bars, its posts and its bases. They were also responsible for all its equipment and all the work on these things.

**37** They were also responsible for the posts around the courtyard, with their bases, pegs and ropes.

**38** Moses, Aaron and the sons of Aaron camped on the east side of the tabernacle, in front of the Tent of Meeting. They were responsible for the holy place for the people of Israel. Any other person who came near the holy place had to be killed.

**39** Moses and Aaron counted the Levites by their clans, as the LORD commanded. The total of all the males who were 1 month old or more was 22,000.

**40** The LORD told Moses, "Count all the firstborn males of Israel who are 1 month old or more. Write their names.

**41** Take the Levites for me in place of all the firstborn of the people of Israel. I am the LORD. Also take the livestock of the Levites in place of all the firstborn of the livestock of the people of Israel."

**42** Thus Moses counted all the firstborn of the people of Israel, as the LORD commanded him.

**43** The total of all the firstborn males who were 1 month old or more was 22,273.

**44** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**45** "Take the Levites in place of all the firstborn of the people of Israel. Take the livestock of the Levites in place of the livestock of the people. The Levites will be mine. I am the LORD.

**46** There are 273 more firstborn sons of Israel than Levites. You must pay a price to buy back these 273 sons.

**47** Take 5 shekels for each of them. Use the standard shekel of the holy place. 1 shekel is 20 gerahs.

**48** Give this money to Aaron and his sons. It is the price for the firstborn sons that are more than the Levites."

**49** Thus Moses took the money for the firstborn sons that were more than the Levites.

**50** He took the money from the firstborn of the people of Israel. The total was 1,365 shekels, with the standard shekel of the holy place.

**51** Moses gave this money to Aaron and his sons, as the LORD commanded him.

## Chapter 4

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron. He said,

**2** "Count the Kohathites, who are a part of the Levites. Count them by their clans and by their families.

**3** Count all the men from 30 to 50 years old who can do the work in the Tent of Meeting.

**4** This is the work of the Kohathites in the Tent of Meeting. They are responsible for the most holy things.

**5** When the camp moves out, Aaron and his sons must go into the tent. They must take down the curtain that is in front of the ark. They must put it over the ark of the Testimony.

**6** Then they must put a covering of fine leather on the ark. They must put a blue cloth over it. Then they must put the poles in their positions.

**7** They must put a blue cloth on the table for the holy bread. They must put the plates, the dishes, the bowls and the jars for drink offerings on the cloth. The bread that is always there must also stay on it.

**8** They must put a red cloth over these things, and a covering of fine leather over the red cloth. Then they must put the poles of the table in their positions.

**9** They must take a blue cloth and cover the lampstand. They must also cover its lamps, its tools, its trays and all the jars for the oil.

**10** They must put the lampstand and all its equipment in a covering of fine leather. Then they must put it on a frame for carrying.

**11** They must put a blue cloth on the gold altar. They must cover it with fine leather. Then they must put its poles in their positions.

**12** They must take all the equipment that the priests use in the holy place. They must put it in a blue cloth and cover it with fine leather. Then they must put it on a frame for carrying.

**13** They must remove the ashes from the bronze altar. Then they must put a purple cloth on the altar.

**14** They must put on it all the equipment of the altar. This includes the fire pans, the meat forks, the shovels and the bowls. They must put a covering of fine leather on it. Then they must put its poles in their positions.

**15** Aaron and his sons must cover the holy place and all its holy equipment. When they finish, the camp can move out. Then the Kohathites must come and carry these things. But the Kohathites must not touch the holy things, or they will die. These are the things in the Tent of Meeting that the Kohathites must carry.

**16** Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, is responsible for the oil for the light and for the sweet incense. He is also responsible for the daily grain offering and for the oil that the priests use to anoint. He must supervise all the tabernacle, all the holy things and all its equipment."

**17** The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron. He said,

**18** "Do not let the clans of the Kohathites be destroyed from among the Levites.

**19** Do this for them, so that they live and do not die when they come near the most holy things. Aaron and his sons must go in. They must give each Kohathite his work and the things that he must carry.

**20** But the Kohathites must not go in and look at the holy things, not even for a moment. If they do, they will die."

**21** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**22** "Count the Gershonites also, by their families and by their clans.

**23** Count all the men from 30 to 50 years old who can do the work in the Tent of Meeting.

**24** This is the work of the clans of the Gershonites. They must do work and carry loads.

**25** They must carry the curtains of the tabernacle and the Tent of Meeting. They must carry its covering and the outer covering of fine leather. They must carry the curtain at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

**26** They must carry the curtains of the courtyard around the tabernacle and the altar. They must carry the curtain at the entrance of the courtyard, the ropes and all the equipment for this work. They must do all the work with these things.

**27** Aaron and his sons must supervise all the work of the Gershonites. They must supervise all that the Gershonites carry and do. Tell the Gershonites all the things that they must carry.

**28** This is the work of the clans of the Gershonites at the Tent of Meeting. Ithamar, the son of Aaron the priest, must supervise their work.

**29** Count the Merarites by their clans and by their families.

**30** Count all the men from 30 to 50 years old who can do the work in the Tent of Meeting.

**31** These are the things that they must carry for their work at the Tent of Meeting. They must carry the frames of the tabernacle, its bars, its posts and its bases.

**32** They must also carry the posts around the courtyard, with their bases, pegs and ropes. They must carry all the equipment for this work. Give each man a list of the things that he must carry.

**33** This is the work of the clans of the Merarites at the Tent of Meeting. Ithamar, the son of Aaron the priest, must supervise their work."

**34** Moses, Aaron and the leaders of the people counted the Kohathites by their clans and by their families.

**35** They counted all the men from 30 to 50 years old who could do the work in the Tent of Meeting.

**36** The total by clans was 2,750.

**37** This was the total of the clans of the Kohathites who could do the work at the Tent of Meeting. Moses and Aaron counted them, as the LORD commanded through Moses.

**38** They counted the Gershonites by their clans and by their families.

**39** They counted all the men from 30 to 50 years old who could do the work in the Tent of Meeting.

**40** The total by clans and by families was 2,630.

**41** This was the total of the clans of the Gershonites who could do the work at the Tent of Meeting. Moses and Aaron counted them, as the LORD commanded.

**42** They counted the clans of the Merarites by their clans and by their families.

**43** They counted all the men from 30 to 50 years old who could do the work in the Tent of Meeting.

**44** The total by clans was 3,200.

**45** This was the total of the clans of the Merarites. Moses and Aaron counted them, as the LORD commanded through Moses.

**46** Moses, Aaron and the leaders of Israel counted all the Levites by their clans and by their families.

**47** They counted all the men from 30 to 50 years old who could do the work and carry the loads at the Tent of Meeting.

**48** The total was 8,580.

**49** Moses counted them as the LORD commanded. He gave each man his work and the things that he had to carry. He did as the LORD commanded Moses.

## Chapter 5

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**2** "Tell the people of Israel to send out of the camp all persons with a skin disease (leprosy). Also send out all persons who have a fluid that comes from their body. Send out all persons who are unclean because they touched a dead body.

**3** Send out the men and the women. Send them outside the camp. Thus they will not make the camp unclean, because I live among the people."

**4** The people of Israel did this. They sent these persons outside the camp. The people of Israel did as the LORD told Moses.

**5** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**6** "Tell the people of Israel, 'A man or a woman can do a wrong thing to another person. That person is not loyal to the LORD and is guilty.

**7** He must confess the sin that he did. He must pay the full value for the wrong and add 1/5. He must give it to the person that he did wrong to.

**8** But that person can have no near relative to receive the payment. Then the payment is for the LORD and it goes to the priest. The guilty person must also give a ram to the priest. With that ram, the priest makes atonement for him.

**9** All the holy gifts that the people of Israel bring to the priest are for that priest.

**10** Each man keeps his holy gifts. But the things that he gives to the priest are for the priest.'"

**11** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**12** "Speak to the people of Israel. Tell them, 'A man's wife can be unfaithful to him.

**13** She can have sex with a different man, and her husband does not know about it. She becomes unclean, but no one sees it and there is no witness.

**14** Her husband can become jealous and think that his wife is unclean. Or her husband can become jealous when his wife is not unclean.

**15** Then the man must bring his wife to the priest. He must bring an offering for her of 1/10 of an ephah of barley flour. He must not pour oil on it or put incense on it. It is a grain offering for jealousy. It is a grain offering to make people remember a sin.

**16** The priest must bring the woman near. He must put her in front of the LORD.

**17** The priest must put holy water in a clay jar. He must take some dust from the floor of the tabernacle and put it into the water.

**18** The priest must put the woman in front of the LORD and let her hair go loose. He must put the grain offering for jealousy in her hands. The priest must hold the bitter water that brings a curse.

**19** Then the priest must make the woman make a solemn promise (oath). He must say to the woman, "No other man had sex with you. You were not unfaithful to your husband and you did not become unclean. If this is true, then this bitter water that brings a curse will not hurt you.

**20** But you were possibly unfaithful to your husband and became unclean. A man who is not your husband possibly had sex with you."

**21** Then the priest must make the woman take an oath with a curse. The priest must say to the woman, "If you are guilty, the LORD will make your people curse you. He will make your thigh waste away and your belly swell.

**22** This water that brings a curse will go into your body. It will make your belly swell and your thigh waste away." Then the woman must say, "Amen. Amen."

**23** The priest must write these curses on a scroll. He must wash the words off into the bitter water.

**24** He must make the woman drink the bitter water that brings a curse. If she is guilty, the water will go into her and cause bitter pain.

**25** The priest must take the grain offering for jealousy from the hands of the woman. He must lift it up in front of the LORD and bring it to the altar.

**26** The priest must take a handful of the grain offering as a reminder. He must burn it on the altar. Then he must make the woman drink the water.

**27** If she is unclean and unfaithful to her husband, the water that brings a curse will go into her. It will cause bitter pain. Her belly will swell and her thigh will waste away. Her people will use her name as a curse.

**28** But if the woman is not unclean, she is clean. Then she will not be hurt, and she will be able to have children.

**29** This is the law about jealousy. It is for a wife who is unfaithful to her husband and becomes unclean.

**30** It is also for a husband who becomes jealous and thinks that his wife is unclean. He must put the woman in front of the LORD. The priest must do all this law to her.

**31** The husband will not be guilty of sin. But if the woman is guilty, she will get the punishment for her sin.'"

## Chapter 6

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**2** "Speak to the people of Israel. Tell them, 'A man or a woman can make a special promise to become a Nazirite. A Nazirite is a person who promises to separate himself to the LORD.

**3** He must not drink wine or other alcohol. He must not drink vinegar that is made from wine or from other alcohol. He must not drink grape juice. He must not eat fresh grapes or dried grapes.

**4** While he is a Nazirite, he must not eat anything from the grapevine. He must not eat the seeds or the skins.

**5** While he is a Nazirite, he must not cut the hair on his head. He is holy until the end of the time that he promised to the LORD. He must let the hair on his head grow long.

**6** While he is separated to the LORD, he must not go near a dead body.

**7** He must not make himself unclean, even if his father, mother, brother or sister dies. His long hair shows that he is separated to his God.

**8** While he is a Nazirite, he is holy to the LORD.

**9** A person can die suddenly near the Nazirite. That makes his hair unclean. Thus he must shave his head on the 7th day, when he becomes clean.

**10** On the 8th day, he must bring 2 doves or 2 young pigeons to the priest at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

**11** The priest must offer 1 bird as a sin offering and 1 bird as a burnt offering. Thus the priest makes atonement for him, because he became unclean near the dead body. On that same day, the Nazirite must make his head holy again.

**12** He must give himself to the LORD again for the full time of his promise. He must bring a male lamb that is 1 year old as a guilt offering. The days before do not count, because he became unclean during his time as a Nazirite.

**13** This is the law for the Nazirite when his time as a Nazirite is complete. He must go to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

**14** He must bring his offerings to the LORD. He must bring 1 male lamb that is 1 year old with no defect for a burnt offering. He must bring 1 female lamb that is 1 year old with no defect for a sin offering. He must bring 1 ram with no defect for a peace offering.

**15** He must also bring a basket of bread without yeast. The basket must contain loaves of fine flour mixed with oil and thin bread with oil on it. He must also bring their grain offerings and drink offerings.

**16** The priest must bring these things to the LORD. He must offer the sin offering and the burnt offering.

**17** He must offer the ram as a peace offering to the LORD, with the basket of bread without yeast. The priest must also offer the grain offering and the drink offering.

**18** At the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, the Nazirite must shave off his long hair. He must put the hair in the fire under the peace offering.

**19** After the Nazirite shaves his hair, the priest must take the boiled shoulder of the ram. He must also take 1 loaf and 1 thin piece of bread without yeast from the basket. He must put them in the hands of the Nazirite.

**20** Then the priest must lift them up as a wave offering in front of the LORD. They are holy and they are for the priest. The breast and the thigh of the ram are also for the priest. After this, the Nazirite can drink wine.

**21** This is the law for the Nazirite who makes a promise. He must give these offerings to the LORD as a Nazirite. He can also give more if he can. He must do all that he promised, as the law of the Nazirite tells him.'"

**22** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**23** "Tell Aaron and his sons, 'Bless the people of Israel with these words:

**24** "May the LORD bless you and keep you safe.

**25** May the LORD look at you with kindness and be good to you.

**26** May the LORD look at you with favor and give you peace."'

**27** Thus the priests will put my name on the people of Israel, and I will bless them."

**4** Eleazar the priest must put some of the blood on his finger. He must sprinkle the blood 7 times toward the front of the tabernacle.

**5** Then a man must burn the heifer while Eleazar watches. He must burn its skin, its meat, its blood, and its dung.

**6** The priest must take cedar wood, hyssop, and red cloth. He must throw these things into the fire that burns the heifer.

**7** Then the priest must wash his clothes and his body in water. After this, he can come into the camp. But he will be unclean until the evening.

**8** The man who burns the heifer must also wash his clothes and his body in water. He will be unclean until the evening.

**9** A man who is clean must collect the ashes of the heifer. He must put them in a clean place outside the camp. The community of Israel must keep the ashes. They will use the ashes to make the water for purification. This water removes sin.

**10** The man who collects the ashes must wash his clothes. He will be unclean until the evening. This rule continues for all time. It is for the people of Israel and for the foreigners who live among them.

**11** A person who touches a dead human body will be unclean for 7 days.

**12** He must make himself clean with the water on the 3rd day and on the 7th day. Then he will be clean. If he does not make himself clean on the 3rd day and on the 7th day, he will not be clean.

**13** A person who touches a dead body and does not make himself clean makes the tabernacle of the LORD unclean. You must remove that person from Israel. Nobody sprinkled the water for purification on him, thus he is unclean. He continues to be unclean.

**14** This is the law when a person dies in a tent. Each person who goes into the tent will be unclean for 7 days. Each person who is in the tent will also be unclean for 7 days.

**15** Each open container that has no cover on it is unclean.

**16** A person can be in the open fields and touch a person whom a sword killed. Or he can touch a dead body, a human bone, or a grave. Then he will be unclean for 7 days.

**17** For the unclean person, take some ashes from the burnt heifer of the sin offering. Put them in a container and add fresh water.

**18** A clean person must take hyssop and put it in the water. He must sprinkle the water on the tent, on all the containers, and on the people who were in the tent. He must also sprinkle it on the person who touched a bone, a person who was killed, a dead body, or a grave.

**19** The clean person must sprinkle the water on the unclean person on the 3rd day and on the 7th day. On the 7th day, the unclean person must make himself clean. He must wash his clothes and his body in water. In the evening he will be clean.

**20** But an unclean person who does not make himself clean must be removed from the community. He made the sanctuary of the LORD unclean. Nobody sprinkled the water for purification on him. He is unclean.

**21** This rule continues for all time. The person who sprinkles the water for purification must wash his clothes. The person who touches the water for purification will be unclean until the evening.

**22** All that an unclean person touches will become unclean. Each person who touches that thing will be unclean until the evening."

**3** The people argued with Moses. They said, "We wish that we died when our brothers died in front of the LORD!

**4** Why did you bring the assembly of the LORD into this desert? Do you want us and our animals to die here?

**6** Moses and Aaron went away from the assembly to the entrance of the tabernacle. They bowed down with their faces to the ground. Then the glory of the LORD appeared to them.

**7** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**8** "Take the rod. You and your brother Aaron must bring the community together. Speak to the rock while the people watch. Then the rock will give its water. Thus you will bring water out of the rock for the community and their animals."

**9** So Moses took the rod from in front of the LORD, as the LORD commanded him.

**10** Moses and Aaron brought the community together in front of the rock. Moses said to them, "Listen, you people who do not obey! Must we bring water out of this rock for you?"

**11** Then Moses lifted his hand. He hit the rock 2 times with his rod. Much water came out. The community and their animals drank.

**12** But the LORD said to Moses and Aaron, "You did not trust me. You did not show my holiness to the people of Israel. Thus you will not bring this community into the land that I give them."

**13** These were the waters of Meribah. At this place the people of Israel argued with the LORD, and he showed his holiness among them.

**14** Moses sent messengers from Kadesh to the king of Edom. They said, "Your brother Israel says this: 'You know all the troubles that we had.

**16** We called to the LORD, and he heard us. He sent an angel and brought us out of Egypt. Now we are at Kadesh. Kadesh is a city at the edge of your land.

**17** Please let us go through your country. We will not go through your fields or your vineyards. We will not drink water from your wells. We will stay on the King's Road. We will not turn to the right or to the left until we go through your land.'"

**18** But the king of Edom said, "You cannot go through my land. If you try, I will attack you with my army."

**19** The people of Israel said, "We will stay on the main road. If we or our animals drink your water, we will pay for it. We only want to walk through. We will do nothing more."

**20** But the king of Edom said, "You cannot go through." Then Edom came out against Israel with a large and strong army.

**22** All the community of Israel went from Kadesh and came to Mount Hor.

**23** At Mount Hor, near the border of Edom, the LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron. He said,

**25** Take Aaron and his son Eleazar up on Mount Hor.

**27** Moses did as the LORD commanded. They went up on Mount Hor while all the community watched.

**28** Moses removed the clothes of Aaron and put them on his son Eleazar. Aaron died there on the top of the mountain. Then Moses and Eleazar came down from the mountain.

**29** Moab, you will have much trouble! People of Chemosh, you are destroyed! Your god Chemosh let his sons run away. He let his daughters become prisoners of Sihon, the king of the Amorites.

## Chapter 22

**1** The people of Israel continued their trip and made their camp in the plains of Moab. This place is east of the Jordan River, across from Jericho.

**2** Balak, the son of Zippor, saw all that Israel did to the Amorites.

**4** The Moabites said to the elders of Midian, "This large group of people will destroy all the land around us. They will be the same as a bull that eats all the grass in a field." At that time Balak, the son of Zippor, was the king of Moab.

**5** Balak sent messengers to Balaam, the son of Beor. Balaam lived at Pethor, near the Euphrates River, in the land of his people. Balak said, "A nation came out of Egypt. They cover the land, and they live near me.

**6** Please come now and curse these people for me. They are too strong for me. Then maybe I can defeat them and force them out of the land. I know that the person whom you bless gets good things. I know that the person whom you curse gets bad things."

**8** Balaam said to them, "Stay here tonight. I will tell you the answer that the LORD gives me." So the leaders of Moab stayed with Balaam.

**9** God came to Balaam and said, "Who are these men with you?"

**10** Balaam said to God, "Balak, the son of Zippor, the king of Moab, sent them to me. He said,

**15** Then Balak sent more leaders. These leaders were more in number and more important than the first leaders.

**16** They came to Balaam and said to him, "Balak, the son of Zippor, says this: 'Please do not let anything stop you. Come to me.

**17** I will give you very great honor. I will do all that you tell me. Please come and curse these people for me.'"

**18** But Balaam said to the servants of Balak, "Balak can give me his palace full of silver and gold. But I cannot disobey the command of the LORD my God. I cannot do anything, small or large, that the LORD does not tell me.

**19** Please stay here tonight too. Then I will know what more the LORD will tell me."

**24** Then the angel of the LORD stood in a narrow path between 2 vineyards. There was a wall on each side of the path.

**25** The donkey saw the angel of the LORD. She moved against the wall and pushed the foot of Balaam against the wall. So Balaam hit her again.

**26** Then the angel of the LORD went farther ahead. He stood in a narrow place. There was no space to turn to the right or to the left.

**29** Balaam said to the donkey, "You made me look foolish! I wish that I had a sword in my hand. Then I would kill you now."

**30** The donkey said to Balaam, "I am your donkey. You rode on me all your life until today. Did I ever do this to you before?" Balaam said, "No."

**32** The angel of the LORD said to him, "Why did you hit your donkey these 3 times? I came to stop you, because your trip is wrong in my sight.

**37** Balak said to Balaam, "I sent a strong request for you to come. Why did you not come to me? I can give you great honor!"

**1** These are the trips of the people of Israel after they came out of Egypt. They went in their army groups under the leadership of Moses and Aaron.

**2** The LORD commanded Moses to write down the places where they went. These are the stages of their trip, from each place to the next place.

**3** The people of Israel went from Rameses on the 15th day of the 1st month. This was the day after the Passover. They went out boldly while all the Egyptians watched.

**4** At that time the Egyptians buried their firstborn sons, whom the LORD killed. The LORD also punished their gods.

**5** The people of Israel went from Rameses and made their camp at Succoth.

**6** They went from Succoth and made their camp at Etham, at the edge of the desert.

**7** They went from Etham and turned back to Pi Hahiroth, east of Baal Zephon. They made their camp near Migdol.

**8** They went from Pi Hahiroth and went through the middle of the sea into the desert. They traveled for 3 days in the desert of Etham and made their camp at Marah.

**9** They went from Marah and came to Elim. At Elim there were 12 springs of water and 70 palm trees. They made their camp there.

**10** They went from Elim and made their camp next to the Red Sea.

**11** They went from the Red Sea and made their camp in the desert of Sin.

**12** They went from the desert of Sin and made their camp at Dophkah.

**13** They went from Dophkah and made their camp at Alush.

**14** They went from Alush and made their camp at Rephidim. At Rephidim there was no water for the people to drink.

**15** They went from Rephidim and made their camp in the desert of Sinai.

**16** They went from the desert of Sinai and made their camp at Kibroth Hattaavah.

**17** They went from Kibroth Hattaavah and made their camp at Hazeroth.

**18** They went from Hazeroth and made their camp at Rithmah.

**19** They went from Rithmah and made their camp at Rimmon Perez.

**20** They went from Rimmon Perez and made their camp at Libnah.

**21** They went from Libnah and made their camp at Rissah.

**22** They went from Rissah and made their camp at Kehelathah.

**23** They went from Kehelathah and made their camp at Mount Shepher.

**24** They went from Mount Shepher and made their camp at Haradah.

**25** They went from Haradah and made their camp at Makheloth.

**26** They went from Makheloth and made their camp at Tahath.

**27** They went from Tahath and made their camp at Terah.

**28** They went from Terah and made their camp at Mithkah.

**29** They went from Mithkah and made their camp at Hashmonah.

**30** They went from Hashmonah and made their camp at Moseroth.

**31** They went from Moseroth and made their camp at Bene Jaakan.

**32** They went from Bene Jaakan and made their camp at Hor Haggidgad.

**33** They went from Hor Haggidgad and made their camp at Jotbathah.

**34** They went from Jotbathah and made their camp at Abronah.

**35** They went from Abronah and made their camp at Ezion Geber.

**36** They went from Ezion Geber and made their camp in the desert of Zin, at Kadesh.

**37** They went from Kadesh and made their camp at Mount Hor, at the edge of the land of Edom.

**38** The LORD commanded Aaron the priest to go up on Mount Hor. Aaron went up, and he died there. He died on the 1st day of the 5th month, in the 40th year after the people of Israel came out of Egypt.

**39** Aaron was 123 years old when he died on Mount Hor.

**40** The Canaanite king of Arad lived in the Negev, in the south of the land of Canaan. He heard that the people of Israel came.

**41** The people went from Mount Hor and made their camp at Zalmonah.

**42** They went from Zalmonah and made their camp at Punon.

**43** They went from Punon and made their camp at Oboth.

**44** They went from Oboth and made their camp at Iye Abarim, at the border of Moab.

**45** They went from Iyim and made their camp at Dibon Gad.

**46** They went from Dibon Gad and made their camp at Almon Diblathaim.

**47** They went from Almon Diblathaim and made their camp in the Abarim mountains, near Nebo.

**48** They went from the Abarim mountains and made their camp in the plains of Moab. This place was next to the Jordan River, across from Jericho.

**49** In the plains of Moab, their camp went along the Jordan River from Beth Jeshimoth to Abel Shittim.

**50** In the plains of Moab, next to the Jordan River, across from Jericho, the LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**51** "Speak to the people of Israel. Tell them, 'Soon you will go across the Jordan River into the land of Canaan.

**52** Then you must force out all the people who live in the land. Destroy all their carved stones and all their metal idols. Destroy all their high places of worship.

**53** Take the land and live in it. I give you the land as your property.

**54** Use lots to divide the land among your families. Give a large part of land to a large group. Give a small part of land to a small group. Each family will get the land where its lot falls. Divide the land among the tribes of your ancestors.

**55** But if you do not force out the people of the land, they will cause you much trouble. They will be the same as sharp sticks in your eyes and thorns in your sides. They will be your enemies in the land where you live.

**56** Then I will do to you what I planned to do to them.'"

## Chapter 34

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**2** "Give this command to the people of Israel. Tell them, 'Soon you will go into the land of Canaan. This is the land that you will get as your property. These are the borders of the land of Canaan:

**3** Your south side will go from the desert of Zin along the border of Edom. On the east, your south border will start at the end of the Dead Sea.

**4** The border will turn south of the Scorpion Pass and go on to Zin. It will go south of Kadesh Barnea. Then it will go to Hazar Addar and on to Azmon.

**5** From Azmon the border will turn and go to the stream of Egypt. It will end at the Mediterranean Sea.

**6** Your west border will be the coast of the Mediterranean Sea. This will be your west border.

**7** This will be your north border: Make a line from the Mediterranean Sea to Mount Hor.

**8** From Mount Hor make a line to Lebo Hamath. Then the border will go to Zedad.

**9** The border will go on to Ziphron and end at Hazar Enan. This will be your north border.

**10** For your east border, make a line from Hazar Enan to Shepham.

**11** The border will go down from Shepham to Riblah, on the east side of Ain. Then the border will go along the hills to the east of the Sea of Galilee (Chinnereth).

**12** Then the border will go down along the Jordan River and end at the Dead Sea. These will be the borders around your land.'"

**13** Moses gave this command to the people of Israel: "This is the land that you will get by lots. The LORD commanded that we give this land to the 9 tribes and to half of the tribe of Manasseh.

**14** The families of the tribe of Reuben, the families of the tribe of Gad, and half of the tribe of Manasseh already received their land.

**15** These 2 tribes and the half tribe received their land on the east side of the Jordan River, across from Jericho."

**16** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**17** "These are the names of the men who will divide the land for you: Eleazar the priest and Joshua, the son of Nun.

**18** Also choose 1 leader from each tribe to help divide the land.

**19** These are the names of the men: From the tribe of Judah, Caleb, the son of Jephunneh.

**20** From the tribe of Simeon, Shemuel, the son of Ammihud.

**21** From the tribe of Benjamin, Elidad, the son of Kislon.

**22** From the tribe of Dan, the leader Bukki, the son of Jogli.

**23** From the descendants of Joseph, from the tribe of Manasseh, the leader Hanniel, the son of Ephod.

**24** From the tribe of Ephraim, the leader Kemuel, the son of Shiphtan.

**25** From the tribe of Zebulun, the leader Elizaphan, the son of Parnach.

**26** From the tribe of Issachar, the leader Paltiel, the son of Azzan.

**27** From the tribe of Asher, the leader Ahihud, the son of Shelomi.

**28** From the tribe of Naphtali, the leader Pedahel, the son of Ammihud."

**29** The LORD commanded these men to divide the land of Canaan among the people of Israel.

## Chapter 35

**1** In the plains of Moab, next to the Jordan River, across from Jericho, the LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**2** "Command the people of Israel to give some cities to the Levites from the land that they get. The Levites will live in these cities. Also give the Levites the pastures around the cities.

**3** The Levites will live in the cities. The pastures will be for their cattle, their flocks, and all their other animals.

**4** The pastures around the cities that you give to the Levites will go out 1,000 cubits from the city wall on all sides.

**5** Measure 2,000 cubits outside the city on the east side, on the south side, on the west side, and on the north side. The city will be in the middle. This area will be the pastures for their cities.

**6** Give 6 of the cities to the Levites as cities of safety (refuge). A person who kills another person can run to a city of refuge. Also give the Levites 42 other cities.

**7** Thus you will give the Levites a total of 48 cities with their pastures.

**8** The cities that you give to the Levites must come from the land of the people of Israel. A tribe that has much land must give many cities. A tribe that has little land must give few cities. Each tribe must give cities to the Levites by the size of its land."

**9** The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

**10** "Speak to the people of Israel. Tell them, 'Soon you will go across the Jordan River into the land of Canaan.

**11** Then choose some cities to be your cities of refuge. A person who kills another person by accident can run to these cities.

**12** These cities will be a safe place from the relative who wants revenge. The person who killed must not die before the community judges him.

**13** You will choose 6 cities of refuge.

**14** Choose 3 cities on the east side of the Jordan River. Choose 3 cities in the land of Canaan. These will be the cities of refuge.

**15** These 6 cities will be a safe place for the people of Israel and for the foreigners who live among them. Each person who kills another person by accident can run there.

**16** But a person can hit another person with an iron object and kill him. Then the person who killed is a murderer. You must kill the murderer.

**17** A person can hit another person with a stone that can kill, and the other person dies. Then the person who killed is a murderer. You must kill the murderer.

**18** A person can hit another person with a wooden object that can kill, and the other person dies. Then the person who killed is a murderer. You must kill the murderer.

**19** The relative who must get revenge for the dead person will kill the murderer. When he finds the murderer, he will kill him.

**20** A person can hate another person and push him. Or he can wait to attack him and throw something at him. If the other person dies, the attacker is a murderer.

**21** Or a person can hit another person with his hand because of hate, and that person dies. You must kill the attacker, because he is a murderer. The relative who must get revenge will kill the murderer when he finds him.

**22** But a person can push another person suddenly without hate. Or he can throw something at him without the intention to hurt him.

**23** Or he can drop a stone that can kill on a person without seeing him. The person dies, but the attacker was not his enemy. He did not want to hurt him.

**24** Then the community must judge between the person who killed and the relative who wants revenge. They must use these rules.

**25** The community must protect the person who killed from the relative who wants revenge. The community must send him back to the city of refuge where he ran. He must stay there until the high priest dies. The high priest is the priest who received the holy oil.

**26** But the person who killed can go outside the border of his city of refuge.

**27** Then the relative who wants revenge can find him outside the city of refuge and kill him. The relative will not be guilty of murder.

**28** The person who killed must stay in his city of refuge until the high priest dies. After the high priest dies, the person who killed can go back to his own land.

**29** These rules are laws for you in all your generations and in all the places where you live.

**30** If a person kills another person, you must kill the murderer only when there are witnesses. You must not kill a person on the word of only 1 witness.

**31** Do not accept money for the life of a murderer who must die. You must kill him.

**32** Do not accept money from a person who ran to a city of refuge. He must not go back to live on his land before the high priest dies.

**33** Do not make the land where you live unclean. Murder makes the land unclean. Only the blood of the murderer can make the land clean after a murder.

**34** Do not make the land where you live unclean. I live in this land. I, the LORD, live among the people of Israel.'"

## Chapter 36

**1** The family leaders of the clan of Gilead came to Moses. Gilead was the son of Machir, and Machir was the son of Manasseh. These family leaders were from the descendants of Joseph. They spoke to Moses and to the leaders, the family leaders of Israel.

**2** They said, "The LORD commanded you, our master, to give the land to the people of Israel by lots. The LORD also commanded you to give the land of our relative Zelophehad to his daughters.

**3** But the daughters can marry men from other tribes of Israel. Then their land will go away from the land of our ancestors. It will go to the tribe of their husbands. Thus our tribe will lose a part of our land.

**4** In the Year of Jubilee for the people of Israel, their land will become a part of the tribe of their husbands. Thus the tribe of our ancestors will lose their land."

**5** Then Moses gave a command to the people of Israel from the LORD. He said, "The men of the tribe of Joseph are correct.

**6** This is what the LORD commands about the daughters of Zelophehad: They can marry any man that they choose. But they must marry men from the clan of the tribe of their father.

**7** Then the land of the people of Israel will not go from one tribe to a different tribe. Each Israelite must keep the land of the tribe of his ancestors.

**8** Each daughter who gets land in any tribe of Israel must marry a man from a clan in the tribe of her father. Then each Israelite will keep the land of his ancestors.

**9** The land must not go from one tribe to a different tribe. Each tribe of Israel must keep its own land."

**10** The daughters of Zelophehad did as the LORD commanded Moses.

**11** Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Noah were the daughters of Zelophehad. They married the sons of their uncles on the side of their father.

**12** They married men from the clans of the descendants of Manasseh, the son of Joseph. Thus their land stayed in the tribe and clan of their father.

**13** These are the commands and the laws that the LORD gave to the people of Israel through Moses. He gave them in the plains of Moab, next to the Jordan River, across from Jericho.
