# Hosea

## Chapter 1

**1** This is the word of the LORD that came to Hosea, the son of Beeri. It came during the rule of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, the kings of Judah. It also came during the rule of Jeroboam, the son of Joash, the king of Israel.

**2** This is the start of the word of the LORD through Hosea. The LORD said to Hosea, "Go and marry a woman who is not faithful. Have children with her. The children will also be the children of an unfaithful woman. I tell you to do this because the people of the land are not faithful to me. They go away from the LORD."

**3** So Hosea went and married Gomer, the daughter of Diblaim. She became pregnant and gave birth to a son for Hosea.

**4** The LORD said to Hosea, "Give the son the name Jezreel. Soon I will punish the family of Jehu for the blood that Jehu spilled at Jezreel. I will stop the kingdom of Israel.

**5** At that time I will break the bow of Israel in the valley of Jezreel. Thus I will break the military power of Israel."

**6** Gomer became pregnant again and gave birth to a daughter. God said to Hosea, "Give her the name Lo-ruhamah, which means 'No mercy.' I will not show mercy to the people of Israel again. I will take them away completely.

**7** But I will show mercy to the people of Judah. I, the LORD their God, will save them. I will not save them with bows, swords, or battles. I will not save them with horses or men on horses."

**8** Gomer stopped giving milk to Lo-ruhamah. Then Gomer became pregnant and gave birth to a son.

**9** God said, "Give the son the name Lo-ammi, which means 'Not my people.' I say this because you are not my people. And I will not be your God.

**10** But a day will come when the people of Israel will be as many as the grains of sand by the sea. Nobody can measure or count the sand. In the place where people said to them, 'You are not my people,' people will say to them, 'You are the sons of the living God.'

**11** The people of Judah and the people of Israel will come together again. They will choose 1 leader for themselves. They will come up out of the land. The day of Jezreel will be a great day."

## Chapter 2

**1** "Speak to your brothers and call them 'Ammi,' which means 'My people.' Speak to your sisters and call them 'Ruhamah,' which means 'Mercy.'

**2** Accuse your mother. Accuse her, because she is not my wife and I am not her husband. Tell her to stop her unfaithful acts. Tell her to stop her adultery.

**3** If she does not stop, I will remove her clothes. I will make her naked, as on the day of her birth. I will make her like a desert. I will make her like a dry land. I will kill her with thirst.

**4** I will not show mercy to her children, because they are the children of an unfaithful woman.

**5** Their mother was not faithful. The woman who gave birth to them did shameful things. She said, 'I will go after my lovers. They give me my bread and my water. They give me my wool, my linen, my oil, and my drinks.'

**6** Thus I will block her road with thorns. I will build a wall in front of her, and she will not find her paths.

**7** She will run after her lovers, but she will not catch them. She will look for them, but she will not find them. Then she will say, 'I will go back to my first husband. My life was better then than it is now.'

**8** She did not know that I gave her the grain, the new wine, and the oil. I gave her much silver and gold. But the people used that silver and gold for Baal.

**9** Thus I will take back my grain at the time of the harvest. I will take back my new wine in its season. I will take back my wool and my linen. I gave her these to cover her naked body.

**10** Now I will show her shame in front of her lovers. Nobody will save her from my power.

**11** I will stop all her joy. I will stop her festivals, her new moon celebrations, her Sabbaths, and all her special feasts.

**12** I will destroy her vines and her fig trees. She said, 'These are my payment. My lovers gave them to me.' I will change her vines and fig trees into a forest. The wild animals will eat them.

**13** I will punish her for the days when she worshipped the Baals. She burned incense to the Baals. She put on her earrings and her jewelry and went after her lovers. But she forgot me," the LORD says.

**14** "Thus I will attract her back to me. I will bring her into the desert. There I will speak kindly to her.

**15** There I will give her vineyards back to her. I will make the Valley of Achor, which means 'Trouble,' a door of hope. She will sing there as in the days when she was young. She will sing as on the day when she came out of the land of Egypt.

**16** At that time," the LORD says, "you will call me 'Ishi,' which means 'My husband.' You will not call me 'Baali,' which means 'My master,' again.

**17** I will remove the names of the Baals from her mouth. People will not remember their names again.

**18** At that time I will make an agreement (covenant) for my people with the wild animals, the birds of the sky, and the animals that move on the ground. I will remove the bow, the sword, and war from the land. I will let my people sleep safely.

**19** I will make you my wife for all time. I will make you my wife with righteousness and justice. I will make you my wife with love and mercy.

**20** I will make you my wife with faithfulness. Then you will know the LORD.

**21** At that time I will answer," the LORD says. "I will answer the sky. The sky will answer the earth with rain.

**22** The earth will answer with grain, new wine, and oil. And these will answer Jezreel.

**23** I will plant her in the land for myself. I will show mercy to Lo-ruhamah, who did not get mercy. I will say to Lo-ammi, who was not my people, 'You are my people.' And they will say, 'You are my God.'"

## Chapter 3

**1** The LORD said to me, "Go again and love a woman. Another man loves her, and she does adultery. Love her as the LORD loves the people of Israel. The LORD loves them, but they turn to other gods. They love cakes of raisins that they give to idols."

**2** So I bought her for 15 pieces of silver and about 9 baskets (1.5 homers) of barley.

**3** I said to her, "You must stay with me for many days. You must not be unfaithful. You must not have sex with another man. I also will stay with you."

**4** In the same way, the people of Israel will live for many days with no king and no prince. They will have no sacrifice and no sacred stone. They will have no ephod and no household idols.

**5** After that, the people of Israel will come back. They will look for the LORD their God and for David their king. In the last days they will come to the LORD with fear. They will come to him for his goodness.

## Chapter 4

**1** People of Israel, hear the word of the LORD. The LORD accuses the people who live in the land. There is no truth, no faithful love, and no knowledge of God in the land.

**2** The people curse, tell lies, kill, steal, and do adultery. They break all the laws. They do one murder after another murder.

**3** Thus the land becomes dry. All the people who live in the land become weak. The wild animals, the birds of the sky, and even the fish in the sea die.

**4** But no person must accuse another person. No person must blame another person. My accusation is against you, the priests.

**5** You will fall during the day. The prophets will also fall with you during the night. I will destroy your mother, Israel.

**6** My people die because they do not know me. You priests refused to know me. Thus I refuse to let you be my priests. You forgot the law of your God. Thus I will also forget your children.

**7** The more the priests increased, the more they sinned against me. I will change their honor into shame.

**8** The priests get food from the sin offerings of my people. Thus they want the people to sin more.

**9** The people and the priests will get the same punishment. I will punish them for their ways. I will pay them back for what they did.

**10** They will eat, but they will not have enough. They will have sex with prostitutes, but they will not have more children. They stopped obeying the LORD.

**11** Prostitution, wine, and new wine take away the good judgment of my people.

**12** My people ask a piece of wood for advice. A stick tells them what to do. A spirit of unfaithfulness causes them to go the wrong way. They are not faithful to their God.

**13** They offer sacrifices on the tops of the mountains. They burn incense on the hills under oak trees, poplar trees, and other big trees, because the shade is good. Thus your daughters become prostitutes. Your daughters-in-law do adultery.

**14** I will not punish your daughters when they become prostitutes. I will not punish your daughters-in-law when they do adultery. I will not punish them, because the men also go with prostitutes. The men offer sacrifices with temple prostitutes. People who do not understand will be destroyed.

**15** Israel, you are not faithful, but Judah must not become guilty. Do not go to Gilgal. Do not go up to Beth-aven. Do not make a promise with the words, "As the LORD lives."

**16** Israel is stubborn, like a stubborn young cow. Thus the LORD cannot feed Israel like a lamb in a large field.

**17** Ephraim joined with idols. Let Ephraim go its own way.

**18** When their drinks are finished, they go to prostitutes. Their rulers love shameful things.

**19** A wind will take them away. They will be ashamed of their sacrifices.

## Chapter 5

**1** "Priests, hear this. People of Israel, listen. Family of the king, listen. The judgment is against you. You were like a trap at Mizpah. You were like a net on Mount Tabor.

**2** The rebels go deep into murder. But I will punish all of them.

**3** I know Ephraim. Israel cannot hide from me. Ephraim, you are now unfaithful. Israel is not clean.

**4** Their actions do not let them come back to their God. A spirit of unfaithfulness is in them. They do not know the LORD.

**5** The pride of Israel is a witness against Israel. Israel and Ephraim will fall because of their sin. Judah will also fall with them.

**6** They will go with their flocks and their herds to look for the LORD. But they will not find him. He went away from them.

**7** They were not faithful to the LORD. They had children who are not his. Now in 1 month they will be destroyed together with their fields.

**8** Blow the horn in Gibeah. Blow the trumpet in Ramah. Shout a warning at Beth-aven. Benjamin, the enemy is behind you.

**9** Ephraim will become empty on the day of punishment. I tell the tribes of Israel what will surely occur.

**10** The leaders of Judah are like men who move the stones that show the borders of land. I will pour my anger on them like water.

**11** Ephraim suffers. Ephraim is crushed by judgment, because Ephraim chose to follow idols.

**12** I will be like a moth to Ephraim. I will be like rot to the people of Judah.

**13** Ephraim saw its sickness. Judah saw its wound. Then Ephraim went to Assyria and sent a message to the great king. But the king could not cure you. He could not heal your wound.

**14** I will be like a lion to Ephraim. I will be like a young lion to the people of Judah. I myself will tear them into pieces and go away. I will carry them away, and nobody will save them.

**15** Then I will go back to my place. I will wait until they admit their guilt. I will wait until they look for me. In their trouble they will look for me quickly."

## Chapter 6

**1** "Come, we will go back to the LORD. He tore us into pieces, but he will heal us. He hurt us, but he will put bandages on our wounds.

**2** After 2 days he will give us new life. On the third day he will lift us up. Then we will live in front of him.

**3** We will know the LORD. We will try hard to know him. He will surely come, as the sun comes in the morning. He will come to us like the rain. He will come like the spring rain that waters the land."

**4** "Ephraim, what will I do with you? Judah, what will I do with you? Your love is like a morning cloud. Your love goes away quickly, like the dew in the early morning.

**5** Thus I cut you into pieces with my prophets. I killed you with the words from my mouth. My judgments go out like the light.

**6** I want faithful love, not sacrifice. I want people to know God more than I want burnt offerings.

**7** But the people broke my covenant, as Adam did. There they were not faithful to me.

**8** Gilead is a city of people who do evil. Blood stains its streets.

**9** Groups of robbers wait to attack a man. In the same way, groups of priests kill people on the road to Shechem. They do shameful crimes.

**10** I saw a terrible thing in the nation of Israel. Ephraim is unfaithful there. Israel is not clean.

**11** Judah, a time of harvest is also ready for you. That time will come when I bring my people back from captivity."

## Chapter 7

**1** "When I want to heal Israel, the sin of Ephraim becomes clear. The crimes of Samaria also become clear. The people lie to each other. Thieves go into houses. Groups of robbers attack in the streets.

**2** The people do not think that I remember all their evil. Their sins are all around them. Their sins are always in front of me.

**3** The people make the king happy with their evil. They make the leaders happy with their lies.

**4** All of them do adultery. Their desire is hot like an oven. The baker does not make the fire hotter while the dough rises.

**5** On the festival day of our king, the leaders became sick with wine. The king joined with people who laugh at God.

**6** Their hearts are hot like an oven while they wait to attack. Their anger sleeps all night. In the morning their anger burns like a fire.

**7** All of them are hot like an oven. They kill their rulers. All their kings fall. Not one of them calls to me.

**8** Ephraim mixes with the other nations. Ephraim is like bread that is cooked on only 1 side.

**9** Foreign people take away his strength, but he does not know it. His hair becomes gray, but he does not know it.

**10** The pride of Israel is a witness against Israel. But the people do not come back to the LORD their God. They do not look for him, after all these troubles.

**11** Ephraim is like a dove that is easy to trick and has no sense. The people call to Egypt for help. Then they go to Assyria.

**12** When they go, I will throw my net over them. I will pull them down like birds from the sky. I will punish them, as I told their assembly.

**13** They will have much trouble, because they went away from me. They will be destroyed, because they rebelled against me. I wanted to save them, but they told lies about me.

**14** They do not cry to me from their hearts. They cry loudly on their beds. They gather together for grain and new wine. But they turn away from me.

**15** I trained them and made their arms strong. But they plan evil against me.

**16** They turn, but not to the Most High God. They are like a bow that does not shoot straight. Their leaders will die by the sword because of their angry words. Thus the people of Egypt will laugh at them."

## Chapter 8

**1** "Put the trumpet to your mouth. An enemy comes like an eagle against the house of the LORD. The people broke my covenant. They rebelled against my law.

**2** Israel cries to me, 'Our God, we know you.'

**3** But Israel refused what is good. Thus an enemy will chase Israel.

**4** They made kings, but I did not choose those kings. They chose leaders, but I did not approve the leaders. They used their silver and gold to make idols for themselves. Thus they will be destroyed.

**5** Samaria, I refuse your calf idol. My anger burns against the people. How long will they continue to be guilty?

**6** That calf comes from Israel. A worker made it, so it is not God. The calf of Samaria will be broken into pieces.

**7** They plant the wind, and they will harvest a storm. The grain has no head, and it will make no flour. If it makes flour, foreign people will eat it.

**8** Israel is swallowed up. Now Israel is among the nations like a pot that nobody wants.

**9** They went up to Assyria, like a wild donkey that goes alone. Ephraim paid lovers to help him.

**10** They paid the nations for help, but now I will gather them for punishment. Soon they will become weak under the heavy load of the great king.

**11** Ephraim built many altars to take away sin. But those altars became places to sin.

**12** I wrote many laws for Ephraim. But the people thought that my laws were strange.

**13** They offer sacrifices of meat to me and they eat the meat. But the LORD does not accept them. Now he will remember their sin. He will punish their sins. They will go back to Egypt.

**14** Israel forgot his Maker and built palaces. Judah built many cities with strong walls. But I will send a fire on their cities. The fire will destroy their strong buildings."

## Chapter 9

**1** Israel, do not be happy. Do not celebrate like the other nations. You were not faithful to your God. You loved the pay of a prostitute on every threshing floor.

**2** The threshing floor and the winepress will not feed the people. The new wine will not be sufficient for them.

**3** They will not stay in the land of the LORD. Ephraim will go back to Egypt. They will eat food that is not clean in Assyria.

**4** They will not pour out wine offerings to the LORD. Their sacrifices will not make him happy. Their sacrifices will be like the bread that people eat at a funeral. All people who eat that bread will become not clean. Their bread will be only for themselves. Their bread will not come into the house of the LORD.

**5** What will you do on the day of the special festival? What will you do on the day of the feast of the LORD?

**6** The people run away from destruction. But Egypt will gather them, and Memphis will bury them. Weeds will grow over their treasures of silver. Thorns will grow in their tents.

**7** The days of punishment come. The days of payment come. Israel will know it. Because of your many sins and your great hatred, you say, "The prophet is a fool. The man of the Spirit is crazy."

**8** The prophet is a guard over Ephraim for my God. But people put traps for him on all his paths. The people hate him in the house of his God.

**9** The people did very bad crimes, as in the days of Gibeah. God will remember their sin. He will punish their sins.

**10** "When I found Israel, it was like a discovery of grapes in the desert. When I saw your ancestors, it was like the first fruit on a fig tree. But they went to Baal-peor and gave themselves to that shameful idol. They became disgusting, like the idol that they loved.

**11** The glory of Ephraim will fly away like a bird. There will be no births, no pregnancies, and no conceptions.

**12** If they raise children, I will take those children from them. No person will remain. They will have much trouble when I go away from them.

**13** I saw Ephraim like the city of Tyre, planted in a pleasant place. But Ephraim will bring out his children to the killer."

**14** LORD, give them what they deserve. What will you give them? Give them wombs that lose babies. Give them breasts that are dry.

**15** "All their evil started at Gilgal. I started to hate them there. Because of their evil actions, I will push them out of my house. I will not love them again. All their leaders are rebels.

**16** Ephraim is like a plant with dry roots. It will make no fruit. If they have children, I will kill their loved children."

**17** My God will refuse them, because they did not obey him. They will wander among the nations.

## Chapter 10

**1** Israel is like a vine that grows much. It makes fruit for itself. When its fruit increased, it built more altars. When its land became better, it made better sacred stones.

**2** Their hearts are not loyal. Now they must suffer for their guilt. The LORD will break their altars. He will destroy their sacred stones.

**3** Then they will say, "We have no king because we did not fear the LORD. But what can a king do for us?"

**4** They speak empty words. They make false promises when they make agreements. Thus lawsuits grow like poisonous weeds in a plowed field.

**5** The people who live in Samaria will be afraid for the calf idol of Beth-aven. Its people will cry for it. Its priests, who celebrated it, will also cry. They will cry because its glory goes away from it.

**6** The enemy will carry the calf to Assyria as a gift for the great king. Ephraim will be ashamed. Israel will be ashamed of its idol.

**7** The king of Samaria will be destroyed. He will be like a stick that floats away on the water.

**8** The high places of Aven will be destroyed. Those places are the sin of Israel. Thorns and weeds will grow on their altars. Then the people will say to the mountains, "Cover us." They will say to the hills, "Fall on us."

**9** "Israel, you sinned from the days of Gibeah. You did not change since that time. Will war not come to the evil people in Gibeah?

**10** I will punish them when I want. The nations will gather against them. They will punish them for their 2 sins.

**11** Ephraim was like a trained young cow that loves to crush the grain. But I will put a yoke on her good neck. I will make Ephraim pull a load. Judah will plow. Jacob will break up the ground.

**12** Plant righteousness for yourselves. Then you will harvest faithful love. Plow your unused ground. It is time to look for the LORD. Look for him until he comes and makes righteousness fall on you like rain.

**13** But you planted evil, and you harvested sin. You ate the fruit of lies. You trusted in your own way. You trusted in your many soldiers.

**14** Thus the noise of war will start among your people. All your strong cities will be destroyed. Shalman destroyed Beth-arbel in the battle. There the enemy killed the mothers together with their children.

**15** The same thing will occur to you, Bethel, because your evil is very large. At dawn the king of Israel will be destroyed completely."

## Chapter 11

**1** "When Israel was a child, I loved him. I called my son out of Egypt.

**2** But the more I called them, the more they went away from me. They offered sacrifices to the Baals. They burned incense to idols.

**3** I taught Ephraim to walk. I held them in my arms. But they did not know that I healed them.

**4** I led them with kindness and with love. I was like a person who lifts a yoke from the neck of an animal. I bent down and fed them.

**5** They will not go back to the land of Egypt. But Assyria will rule them, because they refused to come back to me.

**6** War will go through their cities. The enemy will destroy the bars of their gates. The enemy will destroy them because of their bad plans.

**7** My people are determined to go away from me. They call to the Most High God, but he will not lift them up.

**8** Ephraim, how can I give you up? Israel, how can I give you to your enemies? How can I make you like the city of Admah? How can I destroy you like Zeboim? My heart changes inside me. All my pity becomes strong.

**9** I will not punish you with my strong anger. I will not destroy Ephraim again. I am God, and not a man. I am the Holy One among you. I will not come against you in anger.

**10** They will follow the LORD. He will roar like a lion. When he roars, his children will come from the west with fear.

**11** They will come quickly from Egypt like birds. They will come from the land of Assyria like doves. I will put them in their houses again," the LORD says.

**12** "Ephraim surrounds me with lies. The people of Israel surround me with tricks. And Judah still goes away from God. Judah goes away from the Holy One, who is faithful."

## Chapter 12

**1** Ephraim tries to feed on the wind. All day it chases the hot east wind. It increases its lies and its violence. It makes an agreement with Assyria. It sends olive oil to Egypt.

**2** The LORD also accuses Judah. He will punish Jacob for his ways. He will pay him back for what he did.

**3** Before birth, Jacob held the heel of his brother. As a man, Jacob fought with God.

**4** He fought with the angel and won. He cried and asked the angel for a blessing. He found God at Bethel. God spoke with us there.

**5** That God is the LORD God of armies. "The LORD" is the name that people use to remember him.

**6** Thus you must come back to your God. Be faithful in love and justice. Always trust your God.

**7** Ephraim is like a merchant who uses dishonest scales. He loves to cheat.

**8** Ephraim said, "I am rich. I got much wealth for myself. In all my work, nobody will find a sin or a crime in me."

**9** "I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt. I will make you live in tents again, as in the days of the special festival.

**10** I spoke to the prophets. I gave them many visions. I gave messages through the prophets with stories."

**11** Gilead is evil. Its people are worth nothing. The people offer bulls as sacrifices in Gilgal. Their altars will become piles of stones in the plowed fields.

**12** Jacob ran away to the country of Aram. Israel worked to get a wife. To pay for a wife, he kept sheep.

**13** The LORD used a prophet to bring Israel out of Egypt. Through a prophet the LORD kept Israel safe.

**14** But Ephraim made the LORD very angry. Thus his Lord will make Ephraim guilty for the blood that Ephraim spilled. His Lord will pay him back for his shameful actions.

## Chapter 13

**1** When Ephraim spoke, people were afraid. Ephraim was important in Israel. But Ephraim became guilty when it worshipped Baal. Then Ephraim died.

**2** Now the people sin more and more. They use their silver to make metal idols. They make idols with their own skill. Workers make all of these idols. The people say about the idols, "Offer human sacrifices and kiss the calf idols."

**3** Thus the people will be like a morning cloud. They will be like the early dew that goes away quickly. They will be like the chaff that the wind blows from a threshing floor. They will be like smoke that goes out of a window.

**4** "But I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt. You must know no god but me. There is no Savior but me.

**5** I took care of you in the desert, in a very dry land.

**6** When I fed them, they became full. When they were full, they became proud. Thus they forgot me.

**7** So I will be like a lion to them. I will wait by the road like a leopard.

**8** I will attack them like a bear that lost her cubs. I will tear open their chests. I will eat them like a lion. Like a wild animal, I will tear them into pieces.

**9** Israel, you are destroyed because you are against me. I am your help.

**10** Where is your king now? He cannot save you in all your cities. Where are your rulers? You said, 'Give us a king and leaders.'

**11** In my anger I gave you a king. In my anger I took him away.

**12** I keep a record of the sin of Ephraim. I keep the record of his sins in a safe place.

**13** Pain will come to him, as pain comes to a woman who gives birth. But he is a foolish son. When the time of birth comes, he does not come out of the womb.

**14** I will save them from the power of the grave. I will save them from death. Death, where are your diseases? Grave, where is your destruction? I will not show pity.

**15** Ephraim grows among his brothers. But the LORD will send a hot east wind. The wind will come from the desert. His springs and his wells will become dry. The enemy will take all his valuable treasures.

**16** The people of Samaria will become guilty, because they rebelled against their God. They will die by the sword. The enemy will throw their small children to the ground. The enemy will cut open their pregnant women."

## Chapter 14

**1** Israel, come back to the LORD your God. Your sins caused you to fall.

**2** Take words with you and come back to the LORD. Say to him, "Take away all our sin. Accept us with kindness. Then we will give you praise from our lips as an offering.

**3** Assyria will not save us. We will not ride on war horses. We will not say again to the idols that we made, 'You are our gods.' You show mercy to children who have no father."

**4** "I will heal them of their unfaithfulness. I will love them freely. My anger went away from them.

**5** I will be like the dew to Israel. Israel will grow like a lily. Israel will push down roots like the cedar trees of Lebanon.

**6** His branches will grow. He will be beautiful like an olive tree. He will smell good like the cedar trees of Lebanon.

**7** People will live again in his shade. They will grow like grain. They will grow like a vine. People will remember Israel like the wine of Lebanon.

**8** Ephraim, I will have nothing to do with idols again. I answer you and I take care of you. I am like a green pine tree. Your fruit comes from me."

**9** A wise person will understand these things. A person with good sense will know them. The ways of the LORD are right. Righteous people follow those ways. But rebels fall on those ways.
