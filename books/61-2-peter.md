# 2 Peter

## Chapter 1

**1** I, Simon Peter, am a servant and an apostle of Jesus Christ. I write to the people who got the same valuable faith that we have. You got this faith through the righteousness of our God and Savior, Jesus Christ.

**2** I pray that you get more and more grace and peace. These come through the knowledge of God and of Jesus our Lord.

**3** The power of God gave us all the things that we need for life and for a godly life. He gave these things through the knowledge of him. He called us to his own glory and goodness.

**4** Through these things, he gave us very large and valuable promises. Through these promises you can share in the nature of God. Thus you escape the corruption in the world that bad desires cause.

**5** For this reason, try very hard. Add goodness to your faith. Add knowledge to goodness.

**6** Add self-control to knowledge. Add patience to self-control. Add a godly life to patience.

**7** Add kindness to the brothers to a godly life. Add love to kindness.

**8** If you have these qualities and they increase, then you will not be useless. You will not be without results in your knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**9** But a person who does not have these qualities is blind. He cannot see far. He forgets that God made him clean from his old sins.

**10** Thus, brothers, try harder to make sure that God called you and chose you. If you do these things, you will never fall.

**11** In this way, God will give you a full entrance into the kingdom of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. That kingdom will continue for all time.

**12** Thus I will always remind you about these things. I will do this, although you know them. I will do this, although you are strong in the truth that you now have.

**13** I think that it is correct to remind you about these things. I will make you remember them while I live in this body.

**14** I know that I will soon leave this body. Our Lord Jesus Christ showed this to me.

**15** I will try hard to help you. Thus, after my death, you will always remember these things.

**16** We told you about the power of our Lord Jesus Christ and about the time when he will come. We did not use clever stories that men made. We saw his majesty with our own eyes.

**17** God the Father gave him honor and glory. A voice came to him from the great glory of God. The voice said, "This is my Son. I love him. I am very pleased with him."

**18** We heard this voice that came from heaven. We were with him on the holy mountain.

**19** Also, we have the words of the prophets, and we are more sure of them now. You do well when you pay attention to these words. They are like a light that shines in a dark place. Obey them until the day starts and the morning star rises in your hearts.

**20** First, you must know this: No prophecy in the scripture comes from the prophet's own explanation.

**21** A prophecy never came from the will of a man. Holy men spoke from God, and the Holy Spirit moved them.

## Chapter 2

**1** But there were also false prophets among the people of Israel. In the same way, there will be false teachers among you. They will secretly bring in false teachings that cause destruction. They will even refuse the Lord who bought them. Thus they will cause quick destruction for themselves.

**2** Many people will follow their immoral ways. Because of these false teachers, people will speak bad things about the way of truth.

**3** These teachers want more money. Thus they will use false words to get money from you. Their judgment from long ago is not slow. Their destruction is ready.

**4** God did not save the angels who sinned. He threw them down into hell. He put them in chains of darkness. He keeps them there until the judgment.

**5** God did not save the old world. He sent the flood on the world of the people who did not obey him. But he kept Noah safe with 7 other persons. Noah told people about righteousness.

**6** God burned the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah to ashes. He condemned them and destroyed them. Thus he made them an example for the people who will not obey God in the future.

**7** But God saved Lot, a righteous man. The immoral life of the evil people around him caused him much pain.

**8** That righteous man lived among them. Each day he saw and heard their evil actions. These actions caused pain to his righteous soul.

**9** Thus the Lord knows how to save godly people from tests. He also knows how to keep evil people for punishment on the day of judgment.

**10** This is mostly true for the people who follow the bad desires of the body. These people hate authority. They are proud and they do what they want. They are not afraid to speak bad things about glorious beings.

**11** Angels are stronger and more powerful than these people. But angels do not speak bad accusations against those beings in front of the Lord.

**12** But these people are like wild animals that have no reason. Such animals live only so that people can catch them and kill them. These people speak bad things about things that they do not understand. They will die in their own corruption.

**13** They will get the punishment for their wrong actions. They think that it is a pleasure to live in wild pleasures in the daytime. They are like dirty marks and spots. They enjoy their lies while they eat with you.

**14** Their eyes are full of adultery. They never stop their sin. They catch weak people. They trained their hearts to want more things. God will curse them.

**15** They left the correct way and went the wrong way. They followed the way of Balaam, the son of Bosor. Balaam loved the money that he got for wrong actions.

**16** But a donkey told Balaam that his actions were wrong. The donkey could not speak, but it spoke with the voice of a man. Thus it stopped the foolish actions of the prophet.

**17** These people are like wells without water. They are like clouds that a storm moves. God keeps the deepest darkness for them for all time.

**18** They speak proud words that have no value. They use the bad desires of the body to catch people. These people only just escaped from those who live in error.

**19** The false teachers promise freedom to these people. But the false teachers are slaves of corruption. When a thing controls a person, that person is a slave to that thing.

**20** People can escape the dirty things of the world through the knowledge of the Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. But if those things catch them again and control them, then their end is worse than their start.

**21** It is better for them if they never knew the way of righteousness. It is worse when they know it and then leave the holy commandment that they got.

**22** The true proverb tells what happens to them: "A dog goes back to its own vomit." Also: "A pig that a man washed goes back to roll in the mud."

## Chapter 3

**1** Dear friends, this is the second letter that I write to you. In both letters, I remind you of these things. I want to wake up your pure minds.

**2** Remember the words that the holy prophets spoke before. Remember the commandment of the Lord and Savior. We, the apostles, gave this commandment to you.

**3** First, you must know this: In the last days, people will come who laugh at the truth. They will follow their own bad desires.

**4** They will say, "He promised that he will come. Where is he? Our fathers died. But all things continue the same as at the start of the creation."

**5** But these people choose to forget this: Long ago, God spoke, and the heavens came into existence. The earth came from water and through water.

**6** Then God used water to destroy the world of that time. The water flooded it.

**7** God's word now keeps the heavens and the earth for fire. He keeps them until the day of judgment. On that day he will destroy the people who are not godly.

**8** But, dear friends, do not forget this one thing: For the Lord, 1 day is like 1,000 years. And 1,000 years are like 1 day.

**9** The Lord is not slow to keep his promise. Some people think that he is slow. But he is patient with you. He does not want anyone to die. He wants all people to repent.

**10** But the day of the Lord will come like a thief at night. On that day the heavens will go away with a loud noise. Very hot fire will melt the elements. Fire will burn up the earth and all the works on it.

**11** God will destroy all these things in this way. Thus you must be holy and godly in all your life.

**12** Wait for the day of God and make it come more quickly. On that day, fire will destroy the heavens. Very hot fire will melt the elements.

**13** But God gave us a promise. Thus we wait for new heavens and a new earth. Righteousness will live there.

**14** Thus, dear friends, you wait for these things. Try very hard to be at peace with God. Be clean and without fault when he finds you.

**15** Remember that the patience of our Lord gives people the chance to receive salvation. Our dear brother Paul also wrote to you about this. God gave Paul wisdom.

**16** Paul writes about these things in all his letters. Some things in his letters are hard to understand. People who do not have knowledge and are not stable change the meaning of these things. They also change the meaning of the other scriptures. Thus they cause their own destruction.

**17** Thus, dear friends, you know these things before they happen. Be careful. Do not let the error of evil people lead you away. Do not fall from your strong position.

**18** But grow in grace and in the knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. Give glory to him now and for all time. Amen.
