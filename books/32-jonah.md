# Jonah

## Chapter 1

**1** The LORD spoke a message to Jonah, the son of Amittai. The LORD said,

**2** "Get up and go to Nineveh, the large city. Speak against the city. I see the evil things that its people do."

**3** But Jonah got up and ran away from the LORD to Tarshish. He went down to Joppa. There he found a ship that went to Tarshish. He paid the price of the trip and went onto the ship. He wanted to go with the sailors to Tarshish, away from the LORD.

**4** But the LORD sent a strong wind onto the sea. A very bad storm started on the sea. The storm almost broke the ship.

**5** The sailors were afraid. Each sailor called to his own god for help. The sailors threw the cargo into the sea to make the ship lighter. But Jonah went down into the lowest part of the ship. He lay down there and slept deeply.

**6** The captain of the ship came to Jonah. The captain said, "Why do you sleep? Get up! Call to your God. Maybe your God will think about us. Then we will not die."

**7** The sailors said to each other, "Come, we will throw lots. Then we will know who caused this trouble." The sailors threw lots, and the lot showed Jonah.

**8** Then the sailors said to Jonah, "Please tell us who caused this trouble. What is your work? Where do you come from? What is your country? What is your nation?"

**9** Jonah said to them, "I am a Hebrew. I respect and worship the LORD, the God of heaven. He made the sea and the dry land."

**10** Then the men were very afraid. They said to Jonah, "What did you do?" The men knew that Jonah ran away from the LORD, because Jonah told them.

**11** The sea became more and more violent. The sailors said to Jonah, "What must we do to you? We want the sea to become calm."

**12** Jonah said to them, "Lift me up and throw me into the sea. Then the sea will become calm for you. I know that this large storm came on you because of me."

**13** But the men rowed hard to bring the ship back to the land. They could not do it, because the sea became more and more violent against them.

**14** Then the men called to the LORD. They said, "Please, LORD, do not let us die for the life of this man. Do not blame us for the death of an innocent man. You, LORD, did what you wanted to do."

**15** Then the men lifted Jonah and threw him into the sea. The sea stopped its violent movement.

**16** The men feared the LORD very much. They gave a sacrifice to the LORD and made promises (vows) to him.

**17** The LORD prepared a large fish to swallow Jonah. Jonah was in the stomach of the fish for 3 days and 3 nights.

## Chapter 2

**1** Then Jonah prayed to the LORD his God from the stomach of the fish.

**2** Jonah said, "I was in trouble, and I called to the LORD. The LORD answered me. I called for help from the place of the dead. You heard my voice.

**3** You threw me into the deep water, into the middle of the seas. The water flowed all around me. All your large waves went over me.

**4** Then I said, 'You sent me away from your sight. But I will look again at your holy temple.'

**5** The water closed around me and almost killed me. The deep sea was all around me. Seaweed was around my head.

**6** I went down to the bottom of the mountains. The earth closed its gates on me for all time. But you, LORD my God, brought my life up from the pit.

**7** When my life became weak, I remembered the LORD. My prayer came to you, into your holy temple.

**8** People who worship false gods turn away from the love that God can give them.

**9** But I will sing thanks to you and give a sacrifice to you. I will do what I promised. The LORD is the one who saves."

**10** Then the LORD spoke to the fish. The fish vomited Jonah out onto the dry land.

## Chapter 3

**1** The LORD spoke a message to Jonah a second time. The LORD said,

**2** "Get up and go to Nineveh, the large city. Tell the people the message that I give you."

**3** Jonah got up and went to Nineveh, as the LORD told him. Nineveh was a very large city. A person needed 3 days to walk across it.

**4** Jonah went into the city and walked for 1 day. He shouted, "After 40 more days, God will destroy Nineveh!"

**5** The people of Nineveh believed God. They told all the people to fast. All the people put on sackcloth, from the most important to the least important.

**6** The king of Nineveh heard the message. He got up from his throne and took off his royal clothes. He put on sackcloth and sat in ashes.

**7** The king and his officials sent an order through Nineveh. The order said, "People and animals must not eat anything. The cattle and the sheep must not eat anything. They must not eat food or drink water.

**8** People and animals must put on sackcloth. All people must pray strongly to God. Each person must stop his evil actions and his violence.

**9** Maybe God will change his mind. Maybe he will stop his strong anger. Then we will not die."

**10** God saw what the people did. He saw that they stopped their evil actions. Thus God changed his mind. He did not cause the disaster that he said he would cause.

## Chapter 4

**1** But Jonah did not like this at all. He became very angry.

**2** Jonah prayed to the LORD. He said, "LORD, I said this when I was still in my country! Thus I ran away to Tarshish at the start. I knew that you are a kind and merciful God. You do not become angry quickly. You have much love. You change your mind and do not send disaster.

**3** Now, LORD, please take my life. It is better for me to die than to live."

**4** The LORD said, "Is it right for you to be angry?"

**5** Jonah went out of the city and sat down at the east side of the city. There he made a shelter. He sat under the shelter in the shade. He waited to see what would happen to the city.

**6** The LORD God prepared a vine. The LORD God made the vine grow up over Jonah. The vine gave shade over his head and made him feel better. Jonah was very happy about the vine.

**7** But at sunrise on the next day, God prepared a worm. The worm attacked the vine, and the vine became dry.

**8** When the sun came up, God prepared a hot east wind. The sun shone on the head of Jonah, and he became weak. Jonah wanted to die. He said, "It is better for me to die than to live."

**9** God said to Jonah, "Is it right for you to be angry about the vine?" Jonah said, "Yes, it is right for me to be angry, even to death."

**10** The LORD said, "You feel sorry for the vine. You did not work for the vine, and you did not make it grow. The vine grew in 1 night and died in 1 night.

**11** Nineveh is a large city. More than 120,000 people live in it. These people do not know the difference between their right hand and their left hand. Also, many animals live in Nineveh. Thus I must feel sorry for Nineveh too."
