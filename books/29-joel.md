# Joel

## Chapter 1

**1** This is the word of the LORD that came to Joel, the son of Pethuel.

**2** Old men, hear this. All people who live in the land, listen. Did a thing like this occur during your life? Did it occur during the life of your ancestors?

**3** Tell your children about it. Your children must tell their children. And their children must tell the next generation.

**4** The cutting locusts ate some of the crops. The swarming locusts ate what remained. The young locusts ate what remained after that. The other locusts ate what remained after that.

**5** Wake up, you drunk people, and cry. All you people who drink wine, cry loudly. The new wine is taken away from your mouths.

**6** An army of locusts came against my land. It is strong, and nobody can count it. Its teeth are like the teeth of a lion. It has the jaws of a strong lion.

**7** It destroyed my vines. It broke my fig trees. It removed all the bark and threw it away. The branches became white.

**8** Cry like a young woman who wears sackcloth because her husband died.

**9** The grain offering and the drink offering stop at the house of the LORD. The priests, who serve the LORD, are sad.

**10** The fields are destroyed. The ground is sad, because the grain is destroyed. The new wine is dry. The olive oil is gone.

**11** Farmers, be ashamed. People who work in the vineyards, cry loudly. Cry for the wheat and for the barley, because the harvest of the field is destroyed.

**12** The vine is dry. The fig tree becomes weak. The pomegranate tree, the palm tree, the apple tree, and all the trees of the field are dry. The joy of the people is gone.

**13** Priests, put on sackcloth and cry. You who serve at the altar, cry loudly. You who serve my God, come and stay all night in sackcloth. The grain offering and the drink offering stop at the house of your God.

**14** Announce a holy fast. Call a special assembly. Gather the elders and all the people who live in the land. Bring them into the house of the LORD your God. Cry to the LORD.

**15** That day will be terrible. The day of the LORD is near. It will come as destruction from the Almighty.

**16** Our food is taken away in front of our eyes. Joy and happiness are taken away from the house of our God.

**17** The seeds become dry under the ground. The storehouses are empty. The barns fall down, because the grain is dry.

**18** The animals make sad noises. The herds of cattle wander, because they have no grass. Even the flocks of sheep suffer.

**19** LORD, I cry to you. Fire destroyed the grass in the desert. Flames burned all the trees of the field.

**20** Even the wild animals cry to you. The streams of water are dry. Fire destroyed the grass in the desert.

## Chapter 2

**1** Blow the trumpet in Zion. Make an alarm on my holy mountain. All the people who live in the land must shake with fear. The day of the LORD comes. It is near.

**2** It is a day of darkness and gloom. It is a day of clouds and thick darkness. A large and strong army spreads over the mountains, like the light at dawn. There was never an army like it before. There will never be an army like it again, for many generations.

**3** In front of the army, a fire destroys everything. Behind the army, a flame burns. In front of the army, the land is like the garden of Eden. Behind the army, the land is an empty desert. Nothing escapes from the army.

**4** They look like horses. They run like war horses.

**5** They jump on the tops of the mountains with a noise like chariots. They make a noise like a fire that burns dry grass. They are like a strong army that is ready for battle.

**6** When the people see the army, they are very afraid. All their faces become pale.

**7** They run like soldiers. They climb the walls like warriors. Each of them goes straight ahead. They do not move out of their lines.

**8** They do not push each other. Each of them goes on his own path. They go through the defenses, and nobody can stop them.

**9** They attack the city. They run on the walls. They climb into the houses. They go in through the windows like thieves.

**10** The earth shakes in front of them. The sky shakes. The sun and the moon become dark. The stars stop their light.

**11** The LORD shouts in front of his army. His army is very large. The army that obeys his command is strong. The day of the LORD is great and very terrible. Nobody can survive it.

**12** "But even now," the LORD says, "come back to me with all your heart. Come back with fasting, with crying, and with sadness."

**13** Tear your hearts, not your clothes. Come back to the LORD your God. He is kind and merciful. He is slow to become angry, and his love is very large. He changes his mind and does not send disaster.

**14** Maybe he will change his mind and give a blessing to you. Then you can give a grain offering and a drink offering to the LORD your God.

**15** Blow the trumpet in Zion. Announce a holy fast. Call a special assembly.

**16** Gather the people. Make the assembly holy. Bring the elders together. Gather the children, and also the babies who drink milk from their mothers. The husband must come out of his room. The bride must come out of her room.

**17** The priests, who serve the LORD, must cry between the porch of the temple and the altar. They must say, "LORD, save your people. Do not let other people laugh at the nation that belongs to you. Do not let the nations rule them. Do not let the nations say, 'Where is their God?'"

**18** Then the LORD became protective of his land. He showed pity to his people.

**19** The LORD answered his people. He said, "I will send you grain, new wine, and olive oil. You will have sufficient food. I will not let the nations laugh at you again.

**20** I will send the army from the north far away from you. I will push it into a dry and empty land. Its front lines will go into the eastern sea. Its back lines will go into the western sea. Its dead bodies will smell very bad. I will do this because the army did terrible things."

**21** Land, do not be afraid. Be glad and celebrate, because the LORD did great things.

**22** Wild animals, do not be afraid. The grass in the desert becomes green. The trees make their fruit. The fig tree and the vine give many crops.

**23** People of Zion, be glad. Celebrate because of the LORD your God. He gives you the autumn rain in the correct quantity. He sends you the rain. He sends the autumn rain and the spring rain, as before.

**24** The threshing floors will be full of grain. The containers will be full of new wine and olive oil.

**25** "I will give back to you the crops that the locusts ate during those years. I sent the swarming locusts, the young locusts, the other locusts, and the cutting locusts. They were my large army that I sent against you.

**26** You will have much food to eat, and you will be satisfied. You will praise the name of the LORD your God. He did wonderful things for you. My people will never be ashamed again.

**27** Then you will know that I am among the people of Israel. You will know that I am the LORD your God, and that there is no other God. My people will never be ashamed again.

**28** After that, I will pour out my Spirit on all people. Your sons and your daughters will speak as prophets. Your old men will dream dreams. Your young men will see visions.

**29** At that time I will also pour out my Spirit on the male servants and the female servants.

**30** I will show wonders in the sky and on the earth. There will be blood, fire, and columns of smoke.

**31** The sun will become dark. The moon will become red like blood. These things will occur before the great and terrible day of the LORD comes.

**32** Then the LORD will save each person who calls to him. There will be people who escape on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem, as the LORD said. The LORD will call the people who remain."

## Chapter 3

**1** "At that time I will bring back the captives of Judah and Jerusalem.

**2** Then I will gather all the nations. I will bring them down into the Valley of Jehoshaphat. There I will judge them for what they did to my people Israel. They scattered my people among the nations. They divided my land.

**3** They threw lots for my people. They gave a boy to pay for a prostitute. They sold a girl to get wine to drink.

**4** Tyre, Sidon, and all the regions of Philistia, what do you have against me? Do you try to pay me back for something? If you try to pay me back, I will quickly pay you back for what you did.

**5** You took my silver and my gold. You carried my valuable treasures into your temples.

**6** You sold the people of Judah and Jerusalem to the Greeks. You sent them far away from their land.

**7** I will bring my people out of the places where you sold them. I will pay you back for what you did.

**8** I will sell your sons and your daughters to the people of Judah. The people of Judah will sell them to the Sabeans, a nation far away. The LORD said this."

**9** Announce this among the nations: "Prepare for war. Wake up the soldiers. All the warriors must come near and attack.

**10** Change your plows into swords. Change your pruning knives into spears. The weak person must say, 'I am strong.'

**11** All you nations around Israel, come quickly. Gather together there." LORD, send your warriors down.

**12** "The nations must wake up. They must come to the Valley of Jehoshaphat. There I will sit to judge all the nations around Israel.

**13** Use the sickle, because the harvest is ready. Come and crush the grapes, because the winepress is full. The containers are too full, because the evil of the nations is very large."

**14** Very large crowds of people are in the valley of decision. The day of the LORD is near in the valley of decision.

**15** The sun and the moon become dark. The stars stop their light.

**16** The LORD roars from Zion. He shouts from Jerusalem. The sky and the earth shake. But the LORD is a safe place for his people. He is a strong place of protection for the people of Israel.

**17** "Then you will know that I am the LORD your God. I live in Zion, my holy mountain. Jerusalem will be holy. Foreign armies will not go through Jerusalem again.

**18** At that time the mountains will be full of new wine. Milk will flow from the hills. Water will flow in all the streams of Judah. A spring will come out of the house of the LORD. The spring will give water to the Valley of Shittim.

**19** Egypt will become empty. Edom will become an empty desert. The people of Egypt and Edom attacked the people of Judah. They killed innocent people in the land of Judah.

**20** But people will live in Judah for all time. People will live in Jerusalem for all generations.

**21** I will forgive the guilt of their blood that I did not forgive before. The LORD lives in Zion."
