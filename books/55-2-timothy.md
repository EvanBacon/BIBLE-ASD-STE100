# 2 Timothy

## Chapter 1

**1** I am Paul. I am an apostle of Jesus Christ because God wants this. God sent me to tell about the promise of life that is in Christ Jesus.

**2** I write to Timothy, my dear son. I pray that God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord will give you grace, mercy, and peace.

**3** I serve God with a clean conscience, as my ancestors did. I thank God because I always remember you in my prayers, at night and in the day.

**4** I remember your tears. I want very much to see you, because then I will have much joy.

**5** I remember your true faith. This faith lived first in your grandmother Lois and in your mother Eunice. I am sure that the same faith lives in you also.

**6** Thus I tell you to make the gift of God strong again. God gave you this gift when I put my hands on you.

**7** God did not give us a spirit of fear. God gave us a spirit of power, of love, and of self-control.

**8** Thus do not feel shame to tell about our Lord. Do not feel shame because of me, his prisoner. With the power of God, accept your part of the pain for the gospel.

**9** God saved us and called us to a holy life. He did not do this because of our works. He did this because of his own purpose and grace. God gave us this grace in Christ Jesus before time started.

**10** But now God shows this grace clearly, because our Savior Jesus Christ came. Christ stopped the power of death. Through the gospel, he shows the way to life that does not end.

**11** God chose me to preach this gospel. He made me an apostle and a teacher of the Gentiles.

**12** For this reason I also suffer these things. But I do not feel shame. I know the person that I believe in. I am sure that he can keep safe what I gave to him until that day.

**13** Keep the pattern of the correct words that you heard from me. Keep them with the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus.

**14** God gave you a good thing to keep. Keep it safe with the help of the Holy Spirit, who lives in us.

**15** You know that all the people in Asia turned away from me. Phygelus and Hermogenes are 2 of those people.

**16** I pray that the Lord will give mercy to the family of Onesiphorus. Onesiphorus often gave me new strength. He did not feel shame because of my chain.

**17** When he was in Rome, he looked for me very carefully, and he found me.

**18** I pray that the Lord will give him mercy on that day. You know very well how much he helped me in Ephesus.

## Chapter 2

**1** Thus, my son, be strong in the grace that is in Christ Jesus.

**2** You heard many things from me in front of many witnesses. Give these things to faithful men. These men must be able to teach other people also.

**3** Accept your part of the hard life, as a good soldier of Jesus Christ does.

**4** A soldier does not let the matters of normal life control him. He wants to please the officer who chose him to be a soldier.

**5** Also, an athlete does not get the prize if he does not obey the rules.

**6** The farmer who works hard must be the first to get a part of the crops.

**7** Think about what I tell you. The Lord will help you to understand all things.

**8** Remember Jesus Christ. He came from the family of David. God made him alive again after his death. This is the gospel that I preach.

**9** Because of this gospel, I suffer. People put me in chains as a criminal. But nobody can put the word of God in chains.

**10** Thus I accept all things for the people that God chose. I want them also to get the salvation that is in Christ Jesus, with glory that does not end.

**11** This statement is true: "If we died with him, we will also live with him.

**12** If we continue to suffer, we will also rule with him. If we say that we do not know him, he also will say that he does not know us.

**13** If we are not faithful, he continues to be faithful. He cannot go against his own nature."

**14** Tell the people these things again. In front of the Lord, warn them not to argue about words. Such arguments do not help. They destroy the people who hear them.

**15** Work hard to show yourself as a worker that God accepts. Be a worker who has no reason to feel shame. Teach the word of truth correctly.

**16** Do not take part in foolish talk that is not holy. Such talk causes people to go more and more away from God.

**17** Their words will spread like a disease that eats the body. Hymenaeus and Philetus are 2 of these teachers.

**18** These men went away from the truth. They say that the resurrection from the dead already occurred. Thus they destroy the faith of some people.

**19** But the strong foundation of God does not move. These words are on it like a seal: "The Lord knows the people who are his." Also: "Each person who speaks the name of the Lord must stop doing evil."

**20** In a large house, there are containers of gold and of silver. There are also containers of wood and of clay. Some containers are for special use, and some are for common use.

**21** If a person makes himself clean from these bad things, he will be a container for special use. He will be holy and useful to the master. He will be ready for each good work.

**22** Run away from the bad desires of young people. Follow what is right. Follow faith, love, and peace with the people who call on the Lord with a clean heart.

**23** Do not take part in foolish and stupid arguments. You know that these arguments cause fights.

**24** The servant of the Lord must not fight. He must be kind to all people. He must be able to teach, and he must be patient.

**25** He must teach the people who oppose him with a gentle attitude. Possibly God will let them change their minds. Then they will know the truth.

**26** Then they will become sensible again and escape from the trap of the devil. The devil caught them to do what he wants.

## Chapter 3

**1** Know this: in the last days there will be times of much trouble.

**2** People will love only themselves. They will love money. They will be boastful and proud. They will speak against God. They will not obey their parents. They will not be thankful or holy.

**3** They will have no natural love. They will not make peace. They will say false things about other people. They will have no self-control. They will be cruel. They will hate good things.

**4** They will betray their friends. They will act without thought and they will be very proud. They will love pleasure more than they love God.

**5** They will seem to have a holy life, but they will refuse its power. Stay away from such people.

**6** Some of these men go into houses secretly. They get control of weak women. These women have many sins and many different bad desires control them.

**7** These women always learn, but they never come to know the truth.

**8** Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses. Thus these men also oppose the truth. Their minds are bad. They failed the test of faith.

**9** But they will not go much farther. All people will see their foolishness clearly, as the people saw the foolishness of Jannes and Jambres.

**10** But you know my lessons, my way of life, and my purpose. You know my faith, my patience, my love, and my endurance.

**11** You know about my persecutions and my pain. You know what occurred to me in Antioch, in Iconium, and in Lystra. I endured much persecution, but the Lord saved me from all of it.

**12** All people who want to live a holy life in Christ Jesus will suffer persecution.

**13** But bad people and false teachers will become worse and worse. They will deceive other people, and other people will deceive them.

**14** But you must continue in the things that you learned. You are sure that these things are true. You know the people who taught you.

**15** Since you were a child, you knew the holy scriptures. The scriptures can make you wise for salvation through faith in Christ Jesus.

**16** God gave all scripture through his breath. Scripture is useful to teach and to show errors. It is useful to correct people and to train them to do what is right.

**17** Thus the man of God will be complete. He will have all that is necessary for each good work.

## Chapter 4

**1** God and the Lord Jesus Christ see me. Christ will judge the people who live and the dead people when he comes and starts to rule as king. Thus I give you this order:

**2** Preach the word. Be ready at all times. Correct people, warn them, and encourage them. Do this with much patience and careful instruction.

**3** A time will come when people will not accept correct lessons. They will want to hear new things. Thus they will get many teachers who say what the people want.

**4** They will stop their ears to the truth. They will turn to stories that are not true.

**5** But you must think clearly in all things. Accept pain and trouble. Do the work of an evangelist. Do all the tasks of your ministry.

**6** My life is now like an offering that a priest pours out. The time for me to die is near.

**7** I fought the good fight. I completed the race. I kept the faith.

**8** Now a crown of righteousness waits for me. The Lord, the judge who is fair, will give it to me on that day. He will not give it only to me. He will also give it to all people who want him to come.

**9** Try hard to come to me soon.

**10** Demas left me because he loves this world. He went to Thessalonica. Crescens went to Galatia, and Titus went to Dalmatia.

**11** Only Luke is with me. Get Mark and bring him with you, because he is useful to me in the work.

**12** I sent Tychicus to Ephesus.

**13** I left my coat with Carpus at Troas. When you come, bring the coat with you. Also bring the books, and most of all the parchments.

**14** Alexander, the man who works with copper, did many bad things to me. The Lord will pay him back for what he did.

**15** You also must be careful of Alexander, because he strongly opposed our message.

**16** At my first defense in court, nobody stood with me. All people left me. I pray that God will not hold this against them.

**17** But the Lord stood with me and gave me strength. Through me, the Lord made the full message known, and all the Gentiles heard it. The Lord saved me from the mouth of the lion.

**18** The Lord will save me from all evil attacks. He will bring me safely into his kingdom in heaven. Glory to him for all time. Amen.

**19** Say hello to Prisca and Aquila, and to the family of Onesiphorus.

**20** Erastus stayed in Corinth. Trophimus was sick, and I left him in Miletus.

**21** Try hard to come before winter. Eubulus, Pudens, Linus, Claudia, and all the brothers say hello to you.

**22** I pray that the Lord Jesus Christ will be with your spirit. I pray that grace will be with you all. Amen.
