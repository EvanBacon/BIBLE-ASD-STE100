# 3 John

## Chapter 1

**1** The elder writes to Gaius, my dear friend. I love you in the truth.

**2** Dear friend, I pray that all things go well for you. I pray that your body is healthy, as your soul is healthy.

**3** Some brothers came to me. They told me about your loyalty to the truth. They told me that you live in the truth. This news gave me much joy.

**4** Nothing gives me more joy than this news: my children live in the truth.

**5** Dear friend, you are loyal in all the work that you do for the brothers. You do this work also for strangers.

**6** These brothers told the church about your love. Help them on their journey in a manner that pleases God. If you do this, you do well.

**7** They went out to work for the name of Christ. They took no help from the Gentiles.

**8** Thus we must welcome and help men such as these. Then we work together with them for the truth.

**9** I wrote a letter to the church. But Diotrephes wants to be the most important person among them. He does not accept us.

**10** Thus, if I come, I will speak about the things that he does. He speaks bad and false words against us. That is not enough for him. He does not welcome the brothers. He also stops the people who want to welcome them. He puts those people out of the church.

**11** Dear friend, do not copy what is bad. Copy what is good. The person who does good is from God. The person who does bad did not see God.

**12** All people speak well of Demetrius. The truth also speaks well of him. We also speak well of him. You know that our words are true.

**13** I have many things to write to you. But I do not want to write them with pen and ink.

**14** I hope to see you soon. Then we will speak face to face. Peace to you. Our friends send you their greetings. Greet each of our friends by name.
