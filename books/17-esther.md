# Esther

## Chapter 1

**1** This event occurred in the time of King Ahasuerus. Ahasuerus ruled over 127 provinces, from India to Ethiopia.

**2** At that time, King Ahasuerus sat on the throne of his kingdom in the fortress city of Susa.

**3** In the 3rd year of his rule, the king gave a feast for all his officials and his servants. The commanders of the army of Persia and Media were there. The nobles and the officials of the provinces were also there.

**4** For 180 days, the king showed the riches of his kingdom. He also showed the honor and the splendor of his great power.

**5** When these days were at an end, the king gave a feast for all the people in the fortress city of Susa. The feast was for important people and for common people. The feast continued for 7 days in the garden court of the king's palace.

**6** White, green, and blue cloth hung there. Cords of fine linen and purple attached the cloth to silver rings and marble pillars. There were couches of gold and silver on a floor of red, blue, white, and black stone.

**7** The servants gave drinks in gold cups. Each cup was different from the other cups. There was much royal wine, as the king was very rich.

**8** The law for the drinking was this: "No person must force a guest to drink." The king told all the officials of his palace to let each man drink as he wanted.

**9** Also, Queen Vashti gave a feast for the women in the royal palace of King Ahasuerus.

**10** On the 7th day, the king was happy because of the wine. He gave an order to the 7 officials who served him. Their names were Mehuman, Biztha, Harbona, Bigtha, Abagtha, Zethar, and Carcas.

**11** He told them to bring Queen Vashti to him with the royal crown on her head. He wanted to show her beauty to the people and the officials, because she was very beautiful.

**12** But Queen Vashti refused to come when the officials gave her the order of the king. Thus the king became very angry. His anger burned in him.

**13** The king usually asked for advice from men who knew the law and justice. Thus he spoke to the wise men who understood the times.

**14** The men nearest to him were Carshena, Shethar, Admatha, Tarshish, Meres, Marsena, and Memucan. They were the 7 officials of Persia and Media. They could come to the king at any time, and they had the highest positions in the kingdom.

**15** The king asked, "What must we do to Queen Vashti by law? She did not obey the order of King Ahasuerus that the officials gave her."

**16** Memucan answered in front of the king and the officials, "Queen Vashti did wrong not only to the king. She also did wrong to all the officials and to all the people in all the provinces of King Ahasuerus.

**17** All the women will hear what the queen did. Then they will not respect their husbands. They will say, 'King Ahasuerus told Queen Vashti to come to him, but she did not come.'

**18** Today the noble women of Persia and Media will hear about the act of the queen. They will speak in the same way to all the officials of the king. Thus there will be much disrespect and anger.

**19** If the king agrees, let him send a royal order. Write the order in the laws of Persia and Media, so that no person can change it. The order must say that Vashti will never come to King Ahasuerus again. Then let the king give her royal position to another woman who is better than she is.

**20** The king will make his order known in all his large kingdom. Then all the wives will respect their husbands, from the most important to the least important."

**21** The king and the officials liked this advice. Thus the king did as Memucan said.

**22** He sent letters to all the provinces of the king. He wrote to each province in its own writing and to each people in its own language. The letters said that each man must be the ruler in his own house. Each man must speak the language of his own people.

## Chapter 2

**1** After these events, the anger of King Ahasuerus became less. Then he remembered Vashti. He remembered what she did and the order against her.

**2** Then the servants of the king who helped him said, "Let your officials look for beautiful young virgins for the king.

**3** Let the king choose officials in all the provinces of his kingdom. Let these officials bring all the beautiful young virgins to the house of the women in the fortress city of Susa. Put the young women in the care of Hegai, the official of the king who controls the women. Give the young women the oils and creams for their beauty treatment.

**4** Then let the young woman who pleases the king become queen in the place of Vashti." The king liked this advice, and he did it.

**5** In the fortress city of Susa there was a Jew named Mordecai. He was the son of Jair, the son of Shimei, the son of Kish. Mordecai was from the tribe of Benjamin.

**6** Nebuchadnezzar, the king of Babylon, took Mordecai away from Jerusalem. Mordecai was with the prisoners that Nebuchadnezzar took away with Jeconiah, the king of Judah.

**7** Mordecai raised Hadassah, who was also named Esther. She was the daughter of his uncle. She did not have a father or a mother. The young woman had a good shape and was beautiful. When her father and mother died, Mordecai took her as his own daughter.

**8** The people heard the order and the law of the king. Then the officials brought many young women to the fortress city of Susa, into the care of Hegai. The officials also took Esther to the palace of the king, into the care of Hegai, who controlled the women.

**9** Hegai liked the young woman, and she got his favor. He quickly gave her the oils and creams for her beauty treatment and her special food. He gave her 7 female servants that he chose from the palace of the king. He moved her and her female servants to the best place in the house of the women.

**10** Esther did not tell anyone who her people and her family were, because Mordecai told her not to tell.

**11** Each day Mordecai walked in front of the court of the house of the women. He wanted to know how Esther was and what happened to her.

**12** Before each young woman went to King Ahasuerus, she completed 12 months of beauty treatments. This was the rule for the women. She received 6 months of treatment with oil of myrrh. Then she received 6 months of treatment with perfumes and creams.

**13** Then each young woman went to the king. She could take with her anything that she wanted from the house of the women to the palace of the king.

**14** She went to the king in the evening. In the morning she went back to the second house of the women. There she was in the care of Shaashgaz, the official of the king who controlled the concubines (wives of lower rank). She did not go to the king again unless the king was pleased with her and called her by name.

**15** Esther was the daughter of Abihail, the uncle of Mordecai. Mordecai took her as his daughter. When it was the time for Esther to go to the king, she asked only for the things that Hegai told her to take. Hegai was the official of the king who controlled the women. All the people who saw Esther liked her.

**16** The officials took Esther to King Ahasuerus in his royal palace. This occurred in the 10th month, the month of Tebeth, in the 7th year of his rule.

**17** The king loved Esther more than all the other women. She got his favor and kindness more than all the other virgins. Thus he put the royal crown on her head and made her queen in the place of Vashti.

**18** Then the king gave a large feast for all his officials and his servants. It was the feast of Esther. He also gave a holiday to the provinces. He gave gifts from his great riches.

**19** The officials brought the virgins together a 2nd time. At that time Mordecai sat at the gate of the king.

**20** Esther did not yet tell anyone about her family or her people, because Mordecai told her not to tell. Esther obeyed Mordecai, as she did when he raised her.

**21** At that time, Mordecai sat at the gate of the king. 2 officials of the king, Bigthan and Teresh, became angry. These men guarded the door. They made a plan to kill King Ahasuerus.

**22** Mordecai learned about the plan and told Queen Esther. Esther told the king about the plan, and she said that the information came from Mordecai.

**23** The officials examined the report and found that it was true. Thus the officials hanged the 2 men on a post. The scribes wrote this event in the book of the records of the kings, in front of the king.

## Chapter 3

**1** After these events, King Ahasuerus gave a higher position to Haman. Haman was the son of Hammedatha the Agagite. The king put Haman in a position above all the other officials.

**2** All the servants of the king at the gate of the king bowed down to Haman and showed respect to him. The king gave an order for them to do this. But Mordecai did not bow down, and he did not show respect to Haman.

**3** Then the servants of the king at the gate said to Mordecai, "Why do you not obey the order of the king?"

**4** Each day they spoke to him, but he did not listen to them. Mordecai told them that he was a Jew. Thus they told Haman about Mordecai. They wanted to see if Haman would accept the reason of Mordecai.

**5** Haman saw that Mordecai did not bow down or show respect to him. Then Haman became very angry.

**6** People told Haman who the people of Mordecai were. Thus Haman did not want to kill only Mordecai. Haman made a plan to destroy all the Jews in the kingdom of Ahasuerus. These Jews were the people of Mordecai.

**7** In the 1st month, the month of Nisan, in the 12th year of King Ahasuerus, Haman's men threw Pur (lots). They threw the lots in front of Haman to choose a day and a month. The lot chose the 12th month, the month of Adar.

**8** Then Haman said to King Ahasuerus, "There is a group of people in all the provinces of your kingdom. They live in different places among the other peoples. Their laws are different from the laws of all other peoples. They do not obey the laws of the king. Thus it is not good for the king to let them live.

**9** If the king agrees, write an order to destroy these people. I will give 10,000 talents of silver to the officials who do this work. They will put the silver in the treasury of the king."

**10** The king removed his signet ring from his hand. He gave the ring to Haman, the son of Hammedatha the Agagite, the enemy of the Jews.

**11** The king said to Haman, "Keep the silver. Do with the people as you think is good."

**12** On the 13th day of the 1st month, Haman called the scribes of the king. They wrote all the orders of Haman. They wrote to the high officials of the king, to the governors of each province, and to the officials of each people. They wrote to each province in its own writing and to each people in its own language. They wrote in the name of King Ahasuerus and put the seal of the ring of the king on the letters.

**13** Messengers took the letters to all the provinces of the king. The letters gave an order to destroy, to kill, and to remove all the Jews. This order was for young and old people, small children and women. The order was for 1 day, the 13th day of the 12th month, the month of Adar. The letters also said, "Take all the property of the Jews."

**14** A copy of the order went to each province as a law. The officials told all the peoples about the order, so that they could be ready for that day.

**15** The order of the king made the messengers go quickly. The officials also gave the order in the fortress city of Susa. The king and Haman sat down to drink. But the city of Susa was confused and afraid.

## Chapter 4

**1** Mordecai learned all that occurred. Then he tore his clothes and put on sackcloth (rough cloth) and ashes. He went out into the middle of the city and cried with a loud and bitter cry.

**2** He went only as far as the gate of the king, because no person in sackcloth could go through the gate of the king.

**3** In each province where the order of the king came, the Jews mourned very much. They fasted, cried, and wailed. Many Jews lay in sackcloth and ashes.

**4** The female servants and the officials of Esther came and told her about Mordecai. The queen became very sad. She sent clothes to Mordecai to wear in the place of his sackcloth. But he did not accept the clothes.

**5** Then Esther called Hatach. He was one of the officials that the king chose to serve her. She sent Hatach to Mordecai to find out what was wrong and why.

**6** Hatach went out to Mordecai in the city square in front of the gate of the king.

**7** Mordecai told Hatach all that occurred to him. He also told Hatach about the quantity of money that Haman promised to put in the treasury of the king to destroy the Jews.

**8** Mordecai also gave Hatach a copy of the order to destroy the Jews. The officials gave this order in Susa. Mordecai told Hatach to show the order to Esther and to explain it to her. Mordecai told Hatach to tell Esther to go to the king. She must ask the king for mercy and speak to him for her people.

**9** Hatach went back and told Esther the words of Mordecai.

**10** Then Esther spoke to Hatach and told him to give this message to Mordecai:

**11** "All the servants of the king and all the people of the provinces of the king know this law: If a man or a woman goes to the king in the inner court without an order, that person must die. The only exception is if the king holds out his gold scepter (royal rod) to that person. Then that person will live. But the king did not call me to come to him for 30 days."

**12** The servants told Mordecai the words of Esther.

**13** Then Mordecai told them to give this answer to Esther: "Do not think that you will be safe in the palace of the king, and that only the other Jews will die.

**14** If you do not speak now, help and rescue for the Jews will come from another place. But you and the family of your father will die. Possibly you became queen for a time such as this."

**15** Then Esther told them to give this answer to Mordecai:

**16** "Go and bring together all the Jews in Susa. Fast for me. Do not eat or drink for 3 days, at night or in the day. My female servants and I will also fast in the same way. Then I will go to the king, although that is against the law. If I die, then I die."

**17** Mordecai went away and did all that Esther told him to do.

## Chapter 5

**1** On the 3rd day, Esther put on her royal clothes. She stood in the inner court of the palace of the king, opposite the hall of the king. The king sat on his royal throne in the royal hall, opposite the entrance.

**2** The king saw Queen Esther as she stood in the court, and he was pleased with her. The king held out to Esther the gold scepter in his hand. Esther came near and touched the top of the scepter.

**3** Then the king said to her, "What do you want, Queen Esther? What is your request? I will give it to you, up to half of my kingdom."

**4** Esther answered, "If the king agrees, let the king and Haman come today to the feast that I made ready for the king."

**5** Then the king said, "Bring Haman quickly, so that we can do as Esther asks." Thus the king and Haman went to the feast that Esther made ready.

**6** While they drank wine at the feast, the king said to Esther, "What do you want? I will give it to you. What is your request? Even if it is half of my kingdom, I will do it."

**7** Esther answered, "This is what I want and what I ask:

**8** If the king is pleased with me, and if the king agrees to give me what I want, let the king and Haman come to another feast tomorrow. I will make it ready for them. Then tomorrow I will answer the question of the king."

**9** Haman went out that day happy and glad. But then he saw Mordecai at the gate of the king. Mordecai did not stand up and did not move in front of Haman. Thus Haman became very angry with Mordecai.

**10** But Haman controlled his anger and went home. He called his friends and his wife Zeresh to come to him.

**11** Haman told them about his great riches and his many sons. He told them about all the ways that the king gave him honor. He told them how the king put him above the other officials and servants of the king.

**12** Haman also said, "Queen Esther invited only me to come with the king to the feast that she made ready. Tomorrow she also invited me to come with the king.

**13** But all this does not satisfy me while I see Mordecai the Jew sit at the gate of the king."

**14** Then his wife Zeresh and all his friends said to him, "Make a gallows (a post to hang a person) 50 cubits high. In the morning, tell the king to hang Mordecai on it. Then go with the king to the feast and be happy." Haman liked this advice. Thus he told his servants to make the gallows.

## Chapter 6

**1** That night the king could not sleep. He told a servant to bring the book of the records of the kings. The servants read the book to the king.

**2** The book had a record about Mordecai. The record said that Mordecai told about Bigthana and Teresh. These 2 officials of the king guarded the door. They made a plan to kill King Ahasuerus.

**3** The king asked, "What honor and reward did Mordecai get for this?" The servants of the king who helped him said, "Mordecai got nothing."

**4** The king said, "Who is in the court?" At that time Haman came into the outer court of the palace of the king. Haman wanted to tell the king to hang Mordecai on the gallows that Haman made ready for him.

**5** The servants of the king said to him, "Haman stands in the court." The king said, "Let him come in."

**6** Haman came in, and the king asked him, "What must I do for the man that the king wants to honor?" Haman thought, "The king wants to honor no man more than me."

**7** Thus Haman answered the king, "For the man that the king wants to honor,

**8** let servants bring a royal robe that the king wore. Let them bring a horse that the king rode, with a royal crown on its head.

**9** Give the robe and the horse to one of the most important officials of the king. Let the official put the robe on the man that the king wants to honor. Let the official put the man on the horse and lead him through the city square. Let the official shout in front of him, 'This is what the king does for the man that he wants to honor!'"

**10** Then the king said to Haman, "Go quickly. Take the robe and the horse, as you said. Do all these things for Mordecai the Jew, who sits at the gate of the king. Do not omit any of the things that you said."

**11** Thus Haman took the robe and the horse. He put the robe on Mordecai. He led Mordecai on the horse through the city square. He shouted in front of Mordecai, "This is what the king does for the man that he wants to honor!"

**12** Then Mordecai went back to the gate of the king. But Haman went home quickly. He was very sad, and he covered his head.

**13** Haman told his wife Zeresh and all his friends about all that occurred to him. His wise men and his wife Zeresh said to him, "You started to fall in front of Mordecai. Mordecai is a Jew. Thus you will not win against him. You will surely fall in front of him."

**14** While they spoke to Haman, the officials of the king came. They quickly took Haman to the feast that Esther made ready.

## Chapter 7

**1** Thus the king and Haman went to eat and drink with Queen Esther.

**2** On this 2nd day, while they drank wine, the king asked Esther again, "Queen Esther, what do you want? I will give it to you. What is your request? Even if it is half of my kingdom, I will do it."

**3** Then Queen Esther answered, "King, if you are pleased with me, and if you agree, give me my life. This is what I want. Also save my people. This is my request.

**4** People sold me and my people to be destroyed, killed, and removed. If they sold us only as male and female slaves, I would not speak. That problem would not be sufficient to disturb the king."

**5** Then King Ahasuerus asked Queen Esther, "Who is the man that dared to do this? Where is he?"

**6** Esther said, "The enemy is this evil man Haman!" Then Haman became very afraid in front of the king and the queen.

**7** The king became very angry. He stood up from the feast and went out into the palace garden. Haman knew that the king decided to punish him. Thus Haman stayed and asked Queen Esther to save his life.

**8** Then the king came back from the palace garden to the place of the feast. Haman lay on the couch where Esther was. The king said, "Will he also attack the queen while I am in the house?" When the king said these words, the servants covered the face of Haman.

**9** Harbonah, one of the officials, said to the king, "Look, there is a gallows 50 cubits high at the house of Haman. Haman made it for Mordecai, who gave the report that saved the king." The king said, "Hang Haman on it!"

**10** Thus the servants hanged Haman on the gallows that he made ready for Mordecai. Then the anger of the king became calm.

## Chapter 8

**1** On that day, King Ahasuerus gave Queen Esther the house of Haman, the enemy of the Jews. Mordecai came to the king, because Esther told the king that Mordecai was her relative.

**2** The king removed his signet ring, which he took back from Haman. He gave the ring to Mordecai. Esther put Mordecai in control of the house of Haman.

**3** Then Esther spoke to the king again. She fell down at his feet and cried. She asked him to stop the evil plan of Haman the Agagite against the Jews.

**4** The king held out the gold scepter to Esther. Thus Esther stood up in front of the king.

**5** She said, "If the king agrees, and if he is pleased with me, let him write an order. Let the order cancel the letters of Haman, the son of Hammedatha the Agagite. Haman wrote these letters to destroy the Jews in all the provinces of the king. Please do this if the king thinks that it is correct, and if I please him.

**6** I cannot watch while this evil thing occurs to my people. I cannot watch while my family is destroyed."

**7** Then King Ahasuerus said to Queen Esther and to Mordecai the Jew, "I gave the house of Haman to Esther. My servants hanged Haman on the gallows, because he tried to attack the Jews.

**8** Now write another order for the Jews in the name of the king, as you think is best. Put the seal of the ring of the king on it. No person can cancel an order that is written in the name of the king and has the seal of the ring of the king."

**9** Then Mordecai called the scribes of the king. This was on the 23rd day of the 3rd month, the month of Sivan. They wrote all the orders of Mordecai to the Jews. They also wrote to the high officials, the governors, and the officials of the 127 provinces from India to Ethiopia. They wrote to each province in its own writing and to each people in its own language. They also wrote to the Jews in their own writing and language.

**10** Mordecai wrote in the name of King Ahasuerus and put the seal of the ring of the king on the letters. He sent the letters with messengers on fast horses that came from the royal herds.

**11** In these letters, the king gave the Jews in each city the right to come together and protect their lives. The Jews could destroy, kill, and remove any armed force of any people or province that attacked them. This included the children and women of the attackers. The Jews could also take the property of their enemies.

**12** The order was for 1 day in all the provinces of King Ahasuerus. The day was the 13th day of the 12th month, the month of Adar.

**13** A copy of the order went to each province as a law. The officials told all the peoples about the order. Thus the Jews could be ready on that day to fight back against their enemies.

**14** The messengers rode out quickly on the fast royal horses, because the order of the king made them hurry. The officials also gave the order in the fortress city of Susa.

**15** Mordecai went out from the king in royal clothes of blue and white. He wore a large gold crown and a robe of fine linen and purple. The city of Susa shouted and was happy.

**16** The Jews had light, happiness, joy, and honor.

**17** In each province and each city where the order of the king came, the Jews were happy and glad. They had a feast and a holiday. Many people of other nations became Jews, because they were afraid of the Jews.

## Chapter 9

**1** The order of the king was to occur on the 13th day of the 12th month, the month of Adar. On that day the enemies of the Jews hoped to defeat the Jews. But the opposite occurred. The Jews defeated the people who hated them.

**2** The Jews came together in their cities in all the provinces of King Ahasuerus. They attacked the people who wanted to hurt them. No person could fight against the Jews, because all the peoples were afraid of them.

**3** All the officials of the provinces, the high officials, the governors, and the officials of the king helped the Jews. They did this because they were afraid of Mordecai.

**4** Mordecai was very important in the palace of the king. People in all the provinces heard about him. Mordecai became more and more powerful.

**5** The Jews attacked all their enemies with the sword. They killed and destroyed them. They did what they wanted to the people who hated them.

**6** In the fortress city of Susa, the Jews killed and destroyed 500 men.

**7** They also killed Parshandatha, Dalphon, Aspatha,

**8** Poratha, Adalia, Aridatha,

**9** Parmashta, Arisai, Aridai, and Vajezatha.

**10** These men were the 10 sons of Haman, the son of Hammedatha, the enemy of the Jews. The Jews killed them. But the Jews did not take their property.

**11** On that day, the officials told the king the number of men that the Jews killed in the fortress city of Susa.

**12** The king said to Queen Esther, "The Jews killed and destroyed 500 men and the 10 sons of Haman in the fortress city of Susa. They possibly did much more in the other provinces of the king. Now what do you want? I will give it to you. What else do you ask? I will do it."

**13** Esther said, "If the king agrees, let the Jews in Susa do the same thing tomorrow as they did today. Also, hang the bodies of the 10 sons of Haman on the gallows."

**14** Thus the king gave the order to do this. The officials gave the order in Susa. The Jews hanged the bodies of the 10 sons of Haman.

**15** The Jews in Susa came together again on the 14th day of the month of Adar. They killed 300 men in Susa. But they did not take any property.

**16** The other Jews in the provinces of the king also came together to protect their lives. They got peace from their enemies. They killed 75,000 of the people who hated them. But they did not take any property.

**17** This occurred on the 13th day of the month of Adar. On the 14th day they rested. They made that day a day of feasting and happiness.

**18** But the Jews in Susa came together on the 13th day and on the 14th day. On the 15th day they rested. They made that day a day of feasting and happiness.

**19** Thus the Jews who live in the villages without walls make the 14th day of the month of Adar a day of happiness and feasting. It is a holiday. On that day they send gifts of food to each other.

**20** Mordecai wrote a record of these events. He sent letters to all the Jews in all the provinces of King Ahasuerus, near and far.

**21** He told them to celebrate the 14th day and the 15th day of the month of Adar each year.

**22** These were the days when the Jews got peace from their enemies. In that month their sadness changed to joy, and their mourning changed to a holiday. Mordecai told them to make these days, days of feasting and joy. On these days they must send gifts of food to each other and gifts to poor people.

**23** Thus the Jews agreed to continue the celebration that they started. They agreed to do what Mordecai wrote to them.

**24** Haman, the son of Hammedatha the Agagite, was the enemy of all the Jews. He made a plan to destroy the Jews. He threw Pur (the lot) to kill and destroy them.

**25** But Esther went to the king. Then the king gave an order in letters. The order made the evil plan of Haman against the Jews come back on Haman. The king told his servants to hang Haman and his sons on the gallows.

**26** Thus the Jews called these days Purim, from the word Pur. The Jews read all the words of the letter of Mordecai. They knew what they saw and what occurred to them.

**27** Thus the Jews made a rule for themselves, for their descendants, and for all people who join them. They must always celebrate these 2 days each year. They must do this as the letter said, at the correct time.

**28** All generations, all families, all provinces, and all cities must remember and celebrate these days. The Jews must never stop the celebration of Purim. Their descendants must never forget these days.

**29** Then Queen Esther, the daughter of Abihail, and Mordecai the Jew wrote with full authority. They wrote this 2nd letter about Purim to make it a law.

**30** Mordecai sent letters to all the Jews in the 127 provinces of the kingdom of Ahasuerus. The letters had words of peace and truth.

**31** The letters made the days of Purim a law at their correct times. Mordecai the Jew and Queen Esther gave this order. The Jews also made rules about the fasts and the times of mourning, for themselves and for their descendants.

**32** The order of Esther made the rules of Purim a law. A scribe wrote these rules in a book.

## Chapter 10

**1** King Ahasuerus made the people of the land and of the islands of the sea pay a tax.

**2** All the powerful acts of King Ahasuerus are written in the book of the records of the kings of Media and Persia. The full report of the greatness of Mordecai is also in that book. The king made Mordecai a very important man.

**3** Mordecai the Jew was 2nd in rank after King Ahasuerus. He was important among the Jews, and many of his people respected him. He worked for the good of his people. He spoke for the peace of all his descendants.
