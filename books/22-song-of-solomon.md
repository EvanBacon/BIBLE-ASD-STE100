# Song of Solomon

## Chapter 1

**1** This is the best song. Solomon wrote it.

**2** The woman says, "I want him to kiss me with his mouth. Your love is better than wine.

**3** Your perfume smells good. Your name is like perfume that a person pours out. So the young women love you.

**4** Take me with you. We will run together. The king brought me into his rooms. We will be glad and have joy in you. We will praise your love more than wine. It is correct that the women love you."

**5** "Women of Jerusalem, my skin is dark, but I am beautiful. I am dark like the tents of Kedar. I am beautiful like the curtains of Solomon.

**6** Do not look at me because my skin is dark. The sun made it dark. My brothers were angry with me. They made me work in the vineyards. But I did not take care of my own vineyard, my own body.

**7** You are the man whom I love. Tell me where you feed your sheep. Tell me where your sheep rest at noon. I do not want to look for you like a stranger among the sheep of your friends."

**8** The friends say, "You are the most beautiful of women. If you do not know, follow the tracks of the sheep. Feed your young goats near the tents of the shepherds."

**9** The man says, "My love, you are like a beautiful horse among the chariots of Pharaoh.

**10** Your cheeks are beautiful with jewels. Your neck is beautiful with gold chains.

**11** We will make gold chains for you, with silver decorations."

**12** The woman says, "While the king sat at his table, my perfume gave its sweet smell.

**13** My love is like a small bag of myrrh to me. He will rest all night between my breasts.

**14** My love is like a group of henna flowers to me. They are the flowers in the vineyards of En-gedi."

**15** The man says, "My love, you are beautiful. You are very beautiful. Your eyes are gentle like doves."

**16** The woman says, "My love, you are handsome. You are very pleasant. The green grass is our bed.

**17** The cedar trees are the beams of our house. The fir trees are our roof."

## Chapter 2

**1** The woman says, "I am only a flower of Sharon. I am only a lily of the valleys."

**2** The man says, "My love among the other young women is like a lily among thorns."

**3** The woman says, "My love among the other young men is like an apple tree among the trees of the forest. I sat in his shade with great pleasure. His fruit was sweet to my taste.

**4** He brought me to the house of the feast. His love was like a flag over me.

**5** Give me raisins to make me strong. Give me apples to refresh me. My love for him makes me weak.

**6** His left hand is under my head. His right hand holds me.

**7** Women of Jerusalem, make a promise to me by the deer and the wild animals of the field. Do not wake up love until the correct time."

**8** "I hear the voice of my love! Look, he comes! He jumps over the mountains. He runs over the hills.

**9** My love is like a deer or a young stag. Look, he stands behind our wall. He looks through the windows. He looks through the openings in the wall.

**10** My love spoke to me. He said, 'Get up, my love, my beautiful one. Come away with me.

**11** Look, the winter is past. The rain stopped and went away.

**12** Flowers grow in the land. The time for birds to sing comes. We hear the sound of the dove in our land.

**13** The fig tree makes its first figs. The vines have flowers and give a sweet smell. Get up, my love, my beautiful one. Come away with me.'"

**14** The man says, "My love, you are like a dove that hides in the openings in the rock. You hide in the secret places on the mountain. Let me see your face. Let me hear your voice. Your voice is sweet, and your face is beautiful."

**15** "Catch the foxes for us. Catch the little foxes that destroy the vineyards. Our vineyards have flowers now."

**16** The woman says, "My love is mine, and I am his. He feeds his sheep among the lilies.

**17** My love, before the morning comes and the shadows go away, come back. Be like a deer or a young stag on the rough mountains."

## Chapter 3

**1** The woman says, "At night on my bed, I looked for the man whom I love. I looked for him, but I did not find him.

**2** I said, 'I will get up now and go around the city. I will look in the streets and in the squares for the man whom I love.' I looked for him, but I did not find him.

**3** The guards who go around the city found me. I asked them, 'Did you see the man whom I love?'

**4** I went only a short distance past them. Then I found the man whom I love. I held him, and I did not let him go. I brought him into the house of my mother. I brought him into the room where she gave birth to me.

**5** Women of Jerusalem, make a promise to me by the deer and the wild animals of the field. Do not wake up love until the correct time."

**6** Who is this that comes from the desert like a column of smoke? The perfume of myrrh and incense comes from it. It has all the spices of the merchants.

**7** Look, it is the carriage of Solomon. 60 strong soldiers are around it. They are the best soldiers of Israel.

**8** All of them hold swords. All of them are experts in war. Every man has his sword at his side, ready for the dangers of the night.

**9** King Solomon made a carriage for himself. He made it from the wood of Lebanon.

**10** He made its posts of silver. He made its back of gold. He made its seat of purple cloth. The women of Jerusalem decorated the inside with love.

**11** Women of Zion, go out and look at King Solomon. He wears the crown that his mother put on him on the day of his wedding. That was the day when his heart was very happy.

## Chapter 4

**1** The man says, "My love, you are beautiful. You are very beautiful. Behind your veil, your eyes are gentle like doves. Your hair is like a group of black goats that come down from Mount Gilead.

**2** Your teeth are white like a group of sheep with new-cut wool. The sheep come up from the washing. Each tooth has its pair, and none is missing.

**3** Your lips are like a red thread. Your mouth is beautiful. Behind your veil, your cheeks are red like a piece of a pomegranate.

**4** Your neck is tall like the tower of David. David built that tower for weapons. 1,000 shields hang on the tower. All of them are the shields of strong soldiers.

**5** Your 2 breasts are like 2 young deer. They are twins that feed among the lilies.

**6** Before the morning comes and the shadows go away, I will go to the mountain of myrrh. I will go to the hill of incense.

**7** My love, you are completely beautiful. You have no fault.

**8** My bride, come with me from Lebanon. Come with me from Lebanon. Come down from the top of Amana, from the top of Senir and Hermon. Come from the homes of the lions and the mountains of the leopards.

**9** My sister, my bride, you took my heart. You took my heart with one look of your eyes. You took it with one jewel of your necklace.

**10** My sister, my bride, your love is very beautiful. Your love is much better than wine. The smell of your perfume is better than all spices.

**11** My bride, your lips are sweet like honey. Honey and milk are under your tongue. The smell of your clothes is like the smell of Lebanon.

**12** My sister, my bride, you are like a garden that is locked. You are like a spring that is closed. You are like a fountain that is sealed.

**13** You are like a garden of pomegranate trees with the best fruit. You have henna and nard plants.

**14** You have nard and saffron, calamus and cinnamon. You have all types of incense trees. You have myrrh and aloes and all the best spices.

**15** You are like a fountain in a garden. You are like a well of fresh water. You are like streams that flow down from Lebanon."

**16** The woman says, "North wind, wake up! South wind, come! Blow on my garden, and spread its sweet smells. Let my love come into his garden. Let him eat its best fruits."

## Chapter 5

**1** The man says, "My sister, my bride, I came into my garden. I gathered my myrrh and my spice. I ate my honey and the honeycomb. I drank my wine and my milk." The friends say, "Eat, friends! Drink! Drink much of love!"

**2** The woman says, "I slept, but my heart was awake. I heard my love knock on the door. He said, 'Open the door to me, my sister, my love, my dove, my perfect one. My head is wet with dew. My hair is wet with the drops of the night.'

**3** I said, 'I took off my clothes. Must I put them on again? I washed my feet. Must I make them dirty again?'

**4** My love put his hand through the opening in the door. My heart beat fast for him.

**5** I got up to open the door to my love. My hands were wet with myrrh. My fingers were wet with myrrh on the handles of the lock.

**6** I opened the door to my love. But my love turned and went away. My heart failed when he spoke. I looked for him, but I did not find him. I called him, but he did not answer.

**7** The guards who go around the city found me. They hit me and hurt me. The guards of the walls took my veil from me.

**8** Women of Jerusalem, make a promise to me. If you find my love, tell him that my love for him makes me weak."

**9** The friends say, "You are the most beautiful of women. Why is your love better than other men? Why do you ask us to make this promise?"

**10** The woman says, "My love is handsome and strong. He is the best among 10,000 men.

**11** His head is like the best gold. His hair is wavy and black like a raven.

**12** His eyes are like doves beside streams of water. They are white like milk, and they are set like jewels.

**13** His cheeks are like beds of spices with a sweet smell. His lips are like lilies that drip with myrrh.

**14** His arms are like gold rods with jewels. His body is like bright ivory with sapphires on it.

**15** His legs are like pillars of marble on bases of fine gold. He is tall like Lebanon. He is excellent like the cedar trees.

**16** His mouth is very sweet. He is completely beautiful. Women of Jerusalem, this is my love. This is my friend."

## Chapter 6

**1** The friends say, "You are the most beautiful of women. Where did your love go? Which way did he turn? We will look for him with you."

**2** The woman says, "My love went down to his garden, to the beds of spices. He went to feed his sheep in the gardens and to collect lilies.

**3** I am his, and my love is mine. He feeds his sheep among the lilies."

**4** The man says, "My love, you are beautiful like the city of Tirzah. You are lovely like Jerusalem. You are as impressive as an army with flags.

**5** Turn your eyes away from me. They make me helpless. Your hair is like a group of black goats that come down from Gilead.

**6** Your teeth are white like a group of sheep that come up from the washing. Each tooth has its pair, and none is missing.

**7** Behind your veil, your cheeks are red like a piece of a pomegranate.

**8** There are 60 queens and 80 secondary wives (concubines). There are more young women than anyone can count.

**9** But my dove, my perfect one, is the only one for me. She is the special daughter of her mother. She is the favorite of the mother who gave birth to her. The young women saw her and called her blessed. The queens and the concubines praised her."

**10** They said, "Who is this woman? She looks out like the morning. She is beautiful like the moon and bright like the sun. She is as impressive as an army with flags."

**11** The woman says, "I went down to the garden of nut trees. I wanted to see the new plants in the valley. I wanted to see if the vines had buds and if the pomegranate trees had flowers.

**12** Before I knew it, my desire put me among the chariots of my people's prince."

**13** The friends say, "Come back, come back, Shulamite woman! Come back, come back, so that we can look at you!" The woman says, "Why do you want to look at the Shulamite woman, as at a dance between 2 groups?"

## Chapter 7

**1** The man says, "Daughter of a prince, your feet are beautiful in sandals. The curves of your legs are like jewels. A skilled worker made them.

**2** Your navel is like a round bowl that is always full of wine. Your waist is like a pile of wheat with lilies around it.

**3** Your 2 breasts are like 2 young deer that are twins.

**4** Your neck is like a tower of ivory. Your eyes are like the pools in Heshbon, near the gate of Bath-rabbim. Your nose is like the tower of Lebanon that looks toward Damascus.

**5** Your head is beautiful like Mount Carmel. Your hair shines like purple cloth. Your hair holds the king like a prisoner.

**6** My love, you are very beautiful and very pleasant. You give me much delight.

**7** You are tall like a palm tree. Your breasts are like clusters of fruit.

**8** I said, 'I will climb the palm tree. I will hold its branches.' Let your breasts be like clusters of grapes. Let your breath smell like apples.

**9** Let your kisses be like the best wine." The woman says, "Let the wine go down smoothly to my love. Let it flow gently over the lips of people who sleep.

**10** I am my love's, and he wants me.

**11** Come, my love, we will go out to the fields. We will stay the night in the villages.

**12** We will get up early and go to the vineyards. We will see if the vines have buds and the flowers open. We will see if the pomegranate trees have flowers. There I will give you my love.

**13** The mandrake plants give a sweet smell. At our door there are all types of good fruits, new and old. I kept them for you, my love.

## Chapter 8

**1** I want you to be like my brother, who drank milk from my mother. Then I can kiss you if I find you outside. Nobody will think badly of me.

**2** I will lead you and bring you into the house of my mother. She taught me. I will give you spiced wine to drink. I will give you the juice of my pomegranates.

**3** His left hand is under my head. His right hand holds me.

**4** Women of Jerusalem, make a promise to me. Do not wake up love until the correct time."

**5** The friends say, "Who is this woman? She comes up from the desert and leans on her love." The woman says, "I woke you under the apple tree. There your mother gave birth to you. There she had the pain of birth.

**6** Put me like a seal on your heart. Put me like a seal on your arm. Love is as strong as death. Jealousy is as strong as the grave. Love burns like a very hot fire.

**7** Much water cannot stop love. Rivers cannot drown love. A man can give all his riches for love. But people will laugh at him."

**8** The brothers say, "We have a young sister. Her breasts are not grown. What will we do for our sister on the day when a man asks to marry her?

**9** If she is like a wall, we will build silver towers on her. If she is like a door, we will protect her with boards of cedar."

**10** The woman says, "I am like a wall, and my breasts are like towers. So in his eyes I am a woman who brings peace.

**11** Solomon had a vineyard at Baal-hamon. He gave the vineyard to farmers to take care of it. Each farmer had to pay 1,000 pieces of silver for its fruit.

**12** But my vineyard belongs to me. Solomon, you can have the 1,000 pieces of silver. The farmers who take care of the fruit can have 200 pieces."

**13** The man says, "You live in the gardens. My friends listen to your voice. Let me hear your voice."

**14** The woman says, "Come quickly, my love. Be like a deer or a young stag on the mountains of spices."
