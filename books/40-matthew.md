# Matthew

## Chapter 1

**1** This is the record of the family line of Jesus Christ. Jesus was a descendant of David, and David was a descendant of Abraham.

**2** Abraham became the father of Isaac. Isaac became the father of Jacob. Jacob became the father of Judah and his brothers.

**3** Judah became the father of Perez and Zerah, and Tamar was their mother. Perez became the father of Hezron. Hezron became the father of Ram.

**4** Ram became the father of Amminadab. Amminadab became the father of Nahshon. Nahshon became the father of Salmon.

**5** Salmon became the father of Boaz, and Rahab was the mother of Boaz. Boaz became the father of Obed, and Ruth was the mother of Obed. Obed became the father of Jesse.

**6** Jesse became the father of King David. David became the father of Solomon. The mother of Solomon was the woman who was the wife of Uriah before.

**7** Solomon became the father of Rehoboam. Rehoboam became the father of Abijah. Abijah became the father of Asa.

**8** Asa became the father of Jehoshaphat. Jehoshaphat became the father of Joram. Joram became the father of Uzziah.

**9** Uzziah became the father of Jotham. Jotham became the father of Ahaz. Ahaz became the father of Hezekiah.

**10** Hezekiah became the father of Manasseh. Manasseh became the father of Amon. Amon became the father of Josiah.

**11** Josiah became the father of Jeconiah and his brothers. This occurred at the time when the enemy took the people away to Babylon.

**12** After the enemy took the people to Babylon, Jeconiah became the father of Shealtiel. Shealtiel became the father of Zerubbabel.

**13** Zerubbabel became the father of Abiud. Abiud became the father of Eliakim. Eliakim became the father of Azor.

**14** Azor became the father of Zadok. Zadok became the father of Achim. Achim became the father of Eliud.

**15** Eliud became the father of Eleazar. Eleazar became the father of Matthan. Matthan became the father of Jacob.

**16** Jacob became the father of Joseph. Joseph was the husband of Mary. Mary was the mother of Jesus, and people call Jesus the Christ.

**17** Thus there were 14 generations from Abraham to David. There were 14 generations from David to the time when the enemy took the people to Babylon. There were 14 generations from that time to the Christ.

**18** This is how the birth of Jesus Christ occurred. Mary, his mother, was promised in marriage to Joseph. But before they lived together, Mary found that she was pregnant by the Holy Spirit.

**19** Joseph, her husband, was a good man. He did not want to put shame on Mary in public. Thus he decided to end the marriage agreement secretly.

**20** But while Joseph thought about these things, an angel of the Lord came to him in a dream. The angel said, "Joseph, descendant of David, do not be afraid to take Mary as your wife. The child in her is from the Holy Spirit.

**21** She will give birth to a son. You must give him the name Jesus, because he will save his people from their sins."

**22** All this occurred to make true the words that the Lord spoke through the prophet. The prophet said,

**23** "A virgin will become pregnant and give birth to a son. People will call him Immanuel." The name Immanuel means "God with us."

**24** Joseph woke up from his sleep. He did what the angel of the Lord told him to do. He took Mary as his wife.

**25** But Joseph did not have sexual relations with her until she gave birth to her first son. Joseph gave the son the name Jesus.

## Chapter 2

**1** Jesus was born in Bethlehem in Judea at the time when Herod was king. After Jesus was born, wise men from the east came to Jerusalem.

**2** The wise men asked, "Where is the child who is born King of the Jews? We saw his star in the east. We came to worship him."

**3** King Herod heard about this, and he became very troubled. All the people in Jerusalem also became troubled.

**4** Herod brought together all the chief priests and the teachers of the law. He asked them, "Where will the Christ be born?"

**5** They told him, "He will be born in Bethlehem in Judea. The prophet wrote this:

**6** 'You, Bethlehem, in the land of Judah, are not the least important among the rulers of Judah. A ruler will come from you. He will lead my people Israel like a shepherd.'"

**7** Then Herod secretly called the wise men. He asked them for the exact time when the star appeared.

**8** He sent them to Bethlehem. He said, "Go and search carefully for the young child. When you find him, come back and tell me. Then I also can go and worship him."

**9** The wise men listened to the king, and then they went away. The star that they saw in the east went in front of them. The star stopped above the place where the young child was.

**10** When the wise men saw the star, they were very, very happy.

**11** They went into the house and saw the young child with Mary, his mother. They bowed down and worshipped him. Then they opened their boxes of valuable things. They gave him gifts of gold, frankincense, and myrrh.

**12** God told the wise men in a dream not to go back to Herod. Thus they went back to their country by a different road.

**13** After the wise men went away, an angel of the Lord came to Joseph in a dream. The angel said, "Get up! Take the young child and his mother and go quickly to Egypt. Stay there until I tell you. Herod will look for the child to kill him."

**14** Joseph got up. During the night he took the young child and his mother and went to Egypt.

**15** He stayed there until Herod died. This made true the words that the Lord spoke through the prophet: "I called my son out of Egypt."

**16** Herod saw that the wise men tricked him, and he became very angry. He sent soldiers to Bethlehem and to all the area near it. The soldiers killed all the boys who were 2 years old and younger. Herod used the time that he learned from the wise men to calculate this age.

**17** This made true the words that the prophet Jeremiah spoke:

**18** "People heard a voice in Ramah. They heard crying and much sorrow. Rachel cried for her children. She did not let anyone comfort her, because her children were dead."

**19** After Herod died, an angel of the Lord came in a dream to Joseph in Egypt.

**20** The angel said, "Get up! Take the young child and his mother and go to the land of Israel. The people who tried to kill the child are dead."

**21** Joseph got up. He took the young child and his mother and went to the land of Israel.

**22** But Joseph heard that Archelaus was the king of Judea after his father, Herod. Thus Joseph was afraid to go there. God warned him in a dream, and Joseph went to the area of Galilee.

**23** He went and lived in a town with the name Nazareth. This made true the words that the prophets spoke: "People will call him a Nazarene."

## Chapter 3

**1** At that time John the Baptist came. He preached in the desert of Judea.

**2** He said, "Change your hearts and stop your sins (repent), because the kingdom of heaven is near."

**3** The prophet Isaiah spoke about John. Isaiah said, "A voice calls out in the desert: 'Make the road ready for the Lord. Make his paths straight.'"

**4** John wore clothes that people made from camel hair. He had a leather belt around his waist. He ate locusts and wild honey.

**5** People from Jerusalem, from all of Judea, and from all the area near the Jordan River went out to John.

**6** They told about their sins, and John baptized them in the Jordan River.

**7** John saw many Pharisees and Sadducees come to the place where he baptized. He said to them, "You are like a family of poisonous snakes! Who told you to run away from the anger of God that will come?

**8** Show by what you do that you repented.

**9** Do not think that you can say, 'Abraham is our father.' I tell you, God can make children for Abraham from these stones.

**10** The axe is now ready at the roots of the trees. God will cut down every tree that does not make good fruit. He will throw it into the fire.

**11** I baptize you with water to show that you repented. But a man will come after me who is more powerful than I. I am not good enough to carry his shoes. He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and with fire.

**12** He holds his tool in his hand to separate the grain. He will clean the floor where he separates the grain. He will put his wheat into the storehouse. But he will burn the unwanted part of the grain in a fire that nobody can stop."

**13** Then Jesus came from Galilee to the Jordan River. He came to John so that John could baptize him.

**14** But John tried to stop him. John said, "I must get baptism from you. Why do you come to me?"

**15** Jesus answered him, "Let it be this way now. It is correct for us to do all that God wants." Then John agreed.

**16** John baptized Jesus. Immediately Jesus came up out of the water. Then heaven opened, and Jesus saw the Spirit of God. The Spirit came down like a dove and came on him.

**17** Then a voice from heaven said, "This is my Son. I love him. I am very pleased with him."

## Chapter 4

**1** Then the Spirit led Jesus into the desert. There the devil tried to make Jesus sin.

**2** Jesus did not eat for 40 days and 40 nights. After that he was hungry.

**3** The devil, who tries to make people sin, came to him. The devil said, "If you are the Son of God, tell these stones to become bread."

**4** But Jesus answered, "It is written: 'A person must not live only on bread. A person must live on every word that God speaks.'"

**5** Then the devil took Jesus to the holy city, Jerusalem. He put Jesus on the highest point of the temple.

**6** The devil said to him, "If you are the Son of God, jump down. It is written: 'God will give orders to his angels about you. They will hold you up in their hands. Then you will not hit your foot against a stone.'"

**7** Jesus said to him, "It is also written: 'Do not test the Lord your God.'"

**8** Again the devil took Jesus to a very high mountain. He showed Jesus all the kingdoms of the world and all their greatness.

**9** The devil said to him, "I will give you all these things if you bow down and worship me."

**10** Then Jesus said to him, "Go away, Satan! It is written: 'Worship the Lord your God. Serve only him.'"

**11** Then the devil went away from Jesus. Angels came and helped Jesus.

**12** Jesus heard that the authorities put John in prison. Then Jesus went back to Galilee.

**13** He went away from Nazareth and lived in Capernaum. Capernaum is near the Sea of Galilee, in the area of Zebulun and Naphtali.

**14** This made true the words that the prophet Isaiah spoke:

**15** "The land of Zebulun and the land of Naphtali are on the road to the sea, on the other side of the Jordan River. This is Galilee, where the Gentiles live.

**16** The people who lived in darkness saw a great light. The people who lived in the land of the shadow of death saw a light come to them."

**17** From that time Jesus started to preach. He said, "Repent, because the kingdom of heaven is near."

**18** Jesus walked by the Sea of Galilee. He saw 2 brothers, Simon, who had the name Peter, and his brother Andrew. They threw a net into the sea, because they were fishermen.

**19** Jesus said to them, "Follow me. I will teach you to bring people to God, as you catch fish."

**20** Immediately they left their nets and followed him.

**21** Jesus went on from there and saw 2 other brothers. They were James the son of Zebedee and his brother John. They were in a boat with their father, Zebedee, and they repaired their nets. Jesus called them.

**22** Immediately they left the boat and their father, and they followed Jesus.

**23** Jesus went to all the areas of Galilee. He taught in the synagogues and preached the good news (gospel) of the kingdom. He healed all types of sickness and disease among the people.

**24** People in all of Syria heard about Jesus. They brought to him all the people who were sick with different diseases and pains. They brought people who had demons in them, people who had seizures, and people who could not move their bodies. Jesus healed them.

**25** Large crowds of people followed him. They came from Galilee, the Ten Cities, Jerusalem, Judea, and the other side of the Jordan River.

## Chapter 5

**1** Jesus saw the crowds, and he went up on a mountain. He sat down, and his disciples came to him.

**2** He started to teach them. He said,

**3** "God blesses the people who know that they need him. The kingdom of heaven is theirs.

**4** God blesses the people who are sad. God will comfort them.

**5** God blesses the people who are humble. They will receive the earth.

**6** God blesses the people who want very much to do what is right. God will satisfy them.

**7** God blesses the people who show mercy. God will show mercy to them.

**8** God blesses the people who have pure hearts. They will see God.

**9** God blesses the people who make peace. God will call them his children.

**10** God blesses the people who suffer because they do what is right. The kingdom of heaven is theirs.

**11** God blesses you when people insult you and hurt you because you follow me. God blesses you when people tell lies and say all types of bad things about you.

**12** Be happy and very glad, because your reward in heaven is large. People also hurt the prophets who lived before you.

**13** You are the salt of the earth. But if the salt loses its taste, you cannot make it salty again. Then the salt is not good for anything. People throw it out and walk on it.

**14** You are the light of the world. People cannot hide a city that is on a hill.

**15** People do not light a lamp and put it under a bowl. They put the lamp on a lampstand. Then the lamp gives light to all the people in the house.

**16** Thus let your light shine in front of people. Then they will see the good things that you do. They will praise your Father in heaven.

**17** Do not think that I came to destroy the law or the words of the prophets. I did not come to destroy them. I came to make them complete.

**18** I tell you the truth, until heaven and earth go away, not one letter of the law will go away. Not even the smallest mark will go away until all things occur.

**19** Thus a person may disobey one of the smallest commands and teach other people to do the same. God will call that person the least in the kingdom of heaven. But a person may obey the commands and teach them. God will call that person great in the kingdom of heaven.

**20** I tell you, you must be more righteous than the teachers of the law and the Pharisees. If not, you will not go into the kingdom of heaven.

**21** You heard that God told the people long ago, 'Do not murder. A person who murders will go to the court for judgment.'

**22** But I tell you, every person who is angry with his brother will go to the court for judgment. A person who insults his brother will go to the high council. A person who says, 'You fool!' will be in danger of the fire of hell.

**23** Thus you may bring your gift to the altar and remember that your brother has a complaint against you.

**24** Then leave your gift there in front of the altar. First go and make peace with your brother. Then come back and give your gift.

**25** If a person takes you to court, make an agreement with him quickly while you are on the road with him. If not, he will give you to the judge. The judge will give you to the officer, and the officer will put you in prison.

**26** I tell you the truth, you will not come out of there until you pay the last coin.

**27** You heard that God said, 'Do not commit adultery.'

**28** But I tell you, a man may look at a woman and want her sexually. In his heart, that man already committed adultery with her.

**29** If your right eye causes you to sin, take it out and throw it away. It is better to lose one part of your body than to have all of your body go into hell.

**30** If your right hand causes you to sin, cut it off and throw it away. It is better to lose one part of your body than to have all of your body go into hell.

**31** God also said, 'A man who divorces his wife must give her a written document of divorce.'

**32** But I tell you, if a man divorces his wife for a reason other than sexual sin, he causes her to commit adultery. Also, a man who marries a divorced woman commits adultery.

**33** Also, you heard that God told the people long ago, 'Do not break your promises. Do the things that you promised to the Lord.'

**34** But I tell you, do not make a promise with an oath at all. Do not make an oath by heaven, because heaven is the throne of God.

**35** Do not make an oath by the earth, because the earth is where God puts his feet. Do not make an oath by Jerusalem, because Jerusalem is the city of the great King.

**36** Do not make an oath by your head, because you cannot make one hair white or black.

**37** Only say 'Yes' when you mean yes, and 'No' when you mean no. Words that are more than this come from the evil one.

**38** You heard that God said, 'An eye for an eye, and a tooth for a tooth.'

**39** But I tell you, do not fight against an evil person. If a person hits you on your right cheek, let him also hit the other cheek.

**40** If a person takes you to court to get your shirt, let him have your coat also.

**41** If a soldier forces you to go 1 mile with him, go 2 miles with him.

**42** Give to the person who asks you for something. Do not turn away from the person who wants to borrow from you.

**43** You heard that God said, 'Love your neighbor and hate your enemy.'

**44** But I tell you, love your enemies. Ask God to bless the people who curse you. Do good things to the people who hate you. Pray for the people who treat you badly and hurt you.

**45** Then you will be the children of your Father in heaven. He causes his sun to rise on bad people and on good people. He sends rain on the people who do right and on the people who do wrong.

**46** If you love only the people who love you, what reward will you get? Even the tax collectors do that.

**47** If you greet only your friends, you do not do more than other people. Even the tax collectors do that.

**48** Thus be perfect, as your Father in heaven is perfect."

## Chapter 6

**1** "Be careful. Do not do your good things in front of people so that they see you. If you do, you will get no reward from your Father in heaven.

**2** When you give money to poor people, do not make a loud announcement about it. The hypocrites do that in the synagogues and in the streets, so that people will praise them. I tell you the truth, they already have all of their reward.

**3** When you give to poor people, do not let your left hand know what your right hand does.

**4** Then your gift will be secret. Your Father sees what you do in secret, and he will reward you.

**5** When you pray, do not be like the hypocrites. They like to stand and pray in the synagogues and on the street corners, so that people will see them. I tell you the truth, they already have all of their reward.

**6** But when you pray, go into your room and close the door. Pray to your Father, whom you cannot see. Your Father sees what you do in secret, and he will reward you.

**7** When you pray, do not say the same words again and again with no meaning. The Gentiles do that. They think that God will hear them because they say many words.

**8** Do not be like them. Your Father knows what you need before you ask him.

**9** Thus you must pray like this: 'Our Father in heaven, let all people honor your name as holy.

**10** Let your kingdom come. Let people do what you want on earth, as the angels do in heaven.

**11** Give us today the food that we need for today.

**12** Forgive us for our sins, as we forgive the people who sin against us.

**13** Do not lead us into temptation, but keep us safe from the evil one. The kingdom, the power, and the glory are yours forever. Amen.'

**14** If you forgive other people when they sin against you, your Father in heaven will also forgive you.

**15** But if you do not forgive other people when they sin, your Father will not forgive your sins.

**16** When you fast, do not look sad like the hypocrites. They make their faces look bad to show people that they fast. I tell you the truth, they already have all of their reward.

**17** But when you fast, put oil on your head and wash your face.

**18** Then other people will not know that you fast. Only your Father, whom you cannot see, will know. Your Father sees what you do in secret, and he will reward you.

**19** Do not keep valuable things for yourselves on earth. On earth, insects and rust destroy things, and thieves break in and steal things.

**20** Keep your valuable things in heaven. In heaven, insects and rust do not destroy things, and thieves do not break in and steal things.

**21** Your heart will be at the same place as your valuable things.

**22** The eye is the lamp of the body. If your eye is good, all of your body will be full of light.

**23** But if your eye is bad, all of your body will be full of darkness. If the light in you is really darkness, then the darkness is very great!

**24** No person can serve 2 masters. He will hate one master and love the other. Or he will be loyal to one master and not respect the other. You cannot serve God and money at the same time.

**25** Thus I tell you, do not be anxious about your life. Do not be anxious about what you will eat or drink. Do not be anxious about the clothes for your body. Life is more important than food, and the body is more important than clothes.

**26** Look at the birds in the sky. They do not plant seeds, they do not cut the grain, and they do not put food into buildings for storage. But your Father in heaven gives them food. You are much more valuable than the birds.

**27** No person can add 1 hour to his life because he is anxious.

**28** Why are you anxious about clothes? Look at how the flowers in the field grow. They do not work and they do not make cloth.

**29** But I tell you, even King Solomon in all his greatness did not have clothes as beautiful as one of these flowers.

**30** God gives such beautiful clothes to the grass in the field. The grass lives today, and tomorrow people throw it into the fire. Thus God will give clothes to you much more. Your faith is very small!

**31** Thus do not be anxious. Do not say, 'What will we eat?' or 'What will we drink?' or 'What will we wear?'

**32** The Gentiles try to get all these things. But your Father in heaven knows that you need them.

**33** First, look for the kingdom of God and the things that God thinks are right. Then God will also give you all these things.

**34** Thus do not be anxious about tomorrow. Tomorrow will have its own problems. Each day has sufficient trouble of its own."

## Chapter 7

**1** "Do not judge other people, so that God does not judge you.

**2** God will judge you in the same way that you judge other people. God will use the same measure on you that you use on other people.

**3** Why do you look at the small piece of dust in the eye of your brother? You do not see the large piece of wood in your own eye.

**4** How can you say to your brother, 'Let me take the dust out of your eye'? There is a large piece of wood in your own eye!

**5** You hypocrite! First take the large piece of wood out of your own eye. Then you will see clearly to take the dust out of the eye of your brother.

**6** Do not give holy things to dogs. Do not throw your pearls in front of pigs. If you do, the pigs will walk on the pearls. Then the dogs will turn and attack you.

**7** Ask, and God will give to you. Look, and you will find. Knock, and God will open the door for you.

**8** Every person who asks will receive. The person who looks will find. God will open the door to the person who knocks.

**9** If your son asks you for bread, you will not give him a stone.

**10** If he asks for a fish, you will not give him a snake.

**11** You are evil, but you know how to give good gifts to your children. Thus your Father in heaven will give good things to the people who ask him. He will give much more than you do!

**12** Thus do to other people what you want them to do to you. This is the meaning of the law and the words of the prophets.

**13** Go in through the narrow gate. The gate to destruction is wide, and the road to it is easy. Many people go in through that gate.

**14** But the gate to life is narrow, and the road to it is difficult. Only a small number of people find it.

**15** Be careful of false prophets. They come to you and look like sheep, but inside they are like wild wolves.

**16** You will know them by what they do, as you know a tree by its fruit. People do not get grapes from thorn bushes. They do not get figs from weeds.

**17** Every good tree makes good fruit, but a bad tree makes bad fruit.

**18** A good tree cannot make bad fruit, and a bad tree cannot make good fruit.

**19** People cut down every tree that does not make good fruit and throw it into the fire.

**20** Thus you will know false prophets by their fruit.

**21** Not every person who says to me, 'Lord, Lord,' will go into the kingdom of heaven. Only the person who does what my Father in heaven wants will go in.

**22** On the day of judgment, many people will say to me, 'Lord, Lord, we spoke as prophets in your name. In your name we threw out demons and did many miracles.'

**23** Then I will tell them clearly, 'I never knew you. Go away from me, you people who do evil!'

**24** Thus a person who hears my words and obeys them is like a wise man. The wise man built his house on rock.

**25** The rain fell, the floods came, and the winds blew and hit that house. But the house did not fall, because its foundation was on rock.

**26** But a person who hears my words and does not obey them is like a foolish man. The foolish man built his house on sand.

**27** The rain fell, the floods came, and the winds blew and hit that house. The house fell, and its destruction was complete."

**28** Jesus stopped speaking these words. The crowds were very surprised at his teaching.

**29** He taught like a person who had authority. He did not teach like their teachers of the law.

## Chapter 8

**1** Jesus came down from the mountain, and large crowds followed him.

**2** A man with leprosy came and bowed down in front of Jesus. The man said, "Lord, if you are willing, you can make me clean."

**3** Jesus put out his hand and touched the man. Jesus said, "I am willing. Be clean!" Immediately the leprosy went away from the man.

**4** Then Jesus said to him, "Do not tell anyone about this. But go and show yourself to the priest. Give the gift that Moses commanded. This will show the people that you are healed."

**5** Jesus went into Capernaum. A Roman army officer came to him and asked for help.

**6** The officer said, "Lord, my servant is in bed at my house. He cannot move his body, and he has very bad pain."

**7** Jesus said to him, "I will come and heal him."

**8** The officer answered, "Lord, I am not good enough for you to come into my house. Only say the word, and my servant will be healed.

**9** I also am a man under the authority of other officers. I have soldiers under my authority. I tell one soldier, 'Go,' and he goes. I tell another soldier, 'Come,' and he comes. I tell my servant, 'Do this,' and he does it."

**10** Jesus heard this and was surprised. He said to the people who followed him, "I tell you the truth, I did not find a person with such great faith in Israel.

**11** I tell you, many people will come from the east and from the west. They will sit down to eat with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob in the kingdom of heaven.

**12** But God will throw out the people who should have the kingdom. He will throw them into the darkness outside. There people will cry and grind their teeth in pain."

**13** Then Jesus said to the officer, "Go. What you believed will occur for you." The servant was healed at that same time.

**14** Jesus went into the house of Peter. He saw the mother of Peter's wife in bed with a fever.

**15** Jesus touched her hand, and the fever went away. She got up and started to serve him.

**16** In the evening, people brought to Jesus many people who had demons in them. Jesus threw out the evil spirits with a word. He healed all the sick people.

**17** This made true the words that the prophet Isaiah spoke: "He took our weaknesses and carried our diseases."

**18** Jesus saw the crowd around him. He told his disciples to go to the other side of the lake.

**19** A teacher of the law came to him and said, "Teacher, I will follow you to all places that you go."

**20** Jesus said to him, "Foxes have holes and birds have nests. But the Son of Man has no place to rest his head."

**21** Another disciple said to him, "Lord, let me first go and bury my father."

**22** But Jesus said to him, "Follow me. Let the people who are spiritually dead bury their own dead."

**23** Jesus got into a boat, and his disciples followed him.

**24** Suddenly a very bad storm started on the lake. The waves came over the boat. But Jesus was asleep.

**25** The disciples went to him and woke him. They said, "Lord, save us! We will die!"

**26** Jesus said to them, "Why are you afraid? Your faith is very small!" Then he got up and gave an order to the winds and the water. Then the lake became very calm.

**27** The men were surprised. They said, "What type of man is this? Even the winds and the water obey him!"

**28** Jesus came to the other side of the lake, to the land of the Gadarenes. 2 men with demons in them came out of the burial caves and met him. They were very dangerous. Thus nobody could go along that road.

**29** The men shouted, "What do you want with us, Son of God? Did you come here to hurt us before the correct time?"

**30** At a distance from them, a large group of pigs ate food.

**31** The demons asked Jesus, "If you throw us out, send us into the group of pigs."

**32** Jesus said to them, "Go!" The demons came out of the men and went into the pigs. Then all the pigs ran down the steep hill into the lake. The pigs died in the water.

**33** The men who kept the pigs ran away. They went into the town and told everything. They told what occurred to the men with the demons.

**34** Then all the people of the town came out to meet Jesus. When they saw him, they asked him to go away from their area.

## Chapter 9

**1** Jesus got into a boat and went across the lake. He came to his own town.

**2** Some people brought to him a man who could not move his body. The man was on a bed. Jesus saw their faith. He said to the man, "Son, be brave. I forgive your sins."

**3** Some of the teachers of the law said to themselves, "This man speaks against God!"

**4** Jesus knew their thoughts. He said, "Why do you think evil things in your hearts?

**5** Which is easier to say: 'I forgive your sins,' or 'Get up and walk'?

**6** But I will show you that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins." Then Jesus said to the man, "Get up. Take your bed and go home."

**7** The man got up and went home.

**8** The crowds saw this and were afraid. They praised God, because he gave such authority to people.

**9** Jesus went on from there. He saw a man with the name Matthew, who sat at the tax office. Jesus said to him, "Follow me." Matthew got up and followed him.

**10** Later, Jesus ate a meal in the house of Matthew. Many tax collectors and sinners came and ate with Jesus and his disciples.

**11** The Pharisees saw this. They said to his disciples, "Why does your teacher eat with tax collectors and sinners?"

**12** Jesus heard this. He said, "Healthy people do not need a doctor. Only sick people need a doctor.

**13** Go and learn the meaning of these words: 'I want mercy and not sacrifice.' I did not come to call good people. I came to call sinners to repent."

**14** Then the disciples of John came to Jesus. They asked, "We and the Pharisees fast frequently. Why do your disciples not fast?"

**15** Jesus said to them, "The friends of the man who marries cannot be sad while he is with them. But the time will come when people take the man away from them. Then they will fast.

**16** People do not repair an old coat with a piece of new cloth. The new piece will become smaller and pull away from the coat. Then the hole will become bigger.

**17** Also, people do not put new wine into old leather bags. If they do, the bags will break. The wine will flow out, and the bags will be destroyed. People put new wine into new bags. Then both the wine and the bags stay in good condition."

**18** While Jesus spoke these things, a leader of the synagogue came. He bowed down in front of Jesus and said, "My daughter died a short time ago. But come and put your hand on her, and she will live."

**19** Jesus got up and went with him. The disciples of Jesus also went.

**20** A woman had a problem with blood that flowed from her body for 12 years. She came behind Jesus and touched the edge of his coat.

**21** She said to herself, "If I only touch his coat, I will be healed."

**22** Jesus turned and saw her. He said, "Daughter, be brave. Your faith made you well." The woman was healed at that time.

**23** Jesus came into the house of the leader. He saw the people who played music for the funeral. He also saw a crowd that made much noise.

**24** He said, "Go away. The girl is not dead. She is asleep." But the people laughed at him.

**25** After the crowd went outside, Jesus went in. He held the hand of the girl, and she got up.

**26** The news about this went to all the area.

**27** Jesus went away from there, and 2 blind men followed him. They shouted, "Son of David, show mercy to us!"

**28** Jesus went into the house, and the blind men came to him. Jesus said to them, "Do you believe that I can do this?" They said, "Yes, Lord."

**29** Then Jesus touched their eyes. He said, "Let this occur for you because you have faith."

**30** Then the men could see. Jesus gave them a strong warning. He said, "Make sure that nobody knows about this."

**31** But the men went out and told people about Jesus in all that area.

**32** As the 2 men went out, people brought to Jesus a man with a demon in him. The man could not speak.

**33** Jesus threw out the demon, and then the man spoke. The crowds were surprised. They said, "We never saw a thing like this in Israel!"

**34** But the Pharisees said, "He throws out demons with the power of the ruler of the demons."

**35** Jesus went to all the towns and villages. He taught in their synagogues and preached the good news of the kingdom. He healed every disease and every sickness.

**36** Jesus saw the crowds, and he felt pity for them. They were tired and had no help. They were like sheep that have no shepherd.

**37** Then Jesus said to his disciples, "The harvest is large, but there are only a small number of workers.

**38** Thus pray to the Lord of the harvest. Ask him to send out workers into his harvest."

## Chapter 10

**1** Jesus called his 12 disciples to him. He gave them authority to throw out evil spirits. He also gave them authority to heal all types of disease and sickness.

**2** These are the names of the 12 apostles: first, Simon, who has the name Peter, and his brother Andrew; James the son of Zebedee, and his brother John;

**3** Philip and Bartholomew; Thomas and Matthew the tax collector; James the son of Alphaeus, and Thaddaeus;

**4** Simon the Zealot, and Judas Iscariot. Judas later helped the enemies of Jesus to arrest him.

**5** Jesus sent out these 12 men with these instructions: "Do not go to the Gentiles. Do not go into a town of the Samaritans.

**6** Go to the people of Israel. They are like lost sheep.

**7** As you go, preach this message: 'The kingdom of heaven is near.'

**8** Heal sick people. Bring dead people back to life. Make people with leprosy clean. Throw out demons. You received without payment, thus give without payment.

**9** Do not take gold, silver, or copper money in your belts.

**10** Do not take a bag for the journey, or extra clothes, or shoes, or a walking stick. A worker must get the food that he needs.

**11** When you go into a town or a village, find a good person there who will accept you. Stay at his house until you go away from that place.

**12** When you go into a house, greet the people there.

**13** If the people in the house accept you, let your peace stay with them. If they do not accept you, take your peace back.

**14** Some people will not accept you or listen to your words. When you go away from that house or town, remove the dust from your feet.

**15** I tell you the truth, on the day of judgment, God will punish that town more than the land of Sodom and Gomorrah.

**16** I send you out like sheep among wolves. Thus be clever like snakes, and do no harm, like doves.

**17** Be careful of people. They will take you to the courts. They will hit you with whips in their synagogues.

**18** They will take you to governors and kings because you follow me. You will tell those rulers and the Gentiles about me.

**19** When they arrest you, do not be anxious about what you will say or how you will say it. At that time God will give you the words to say.

**20** You will not be the person who speaks. The Spirit of your Father will speak through you.

**21** A brother will give his brother to the authorities to kill him. A father will do the same to his child. Children will fight against their parents and cause their deaths.

**22** All people will hate you because you follow me. But the person who stays faithful until the end will be saved.

**23** When people hurt you in one town, go quickly to another town. I tell you the truth, the Son of Man will come before you go to all the towns of Israel.

**24** A student is not more important than his teacher. A servant is not more important than his master.

**25** It is sufficient for a student to become like his teacher, and for a servant to become like his master. People called the head of the house Beelzebul. Thus they will say much worse things about the people of his house.

**26** Thus do not be afraid of them. All things that are hidden will become known. All secrets will become known.

**27** I tell you things in the dark, but you must tell them in the light. I speak quietly into your ear, but you must shout my words from the roofs of the houses.

**28** Do not be afraid of people who can kill the body. They cannot kill the soul. Be afraid of God, who can destroy both soul and body in hell.

**29** People sell 2 sparrows for 1 small coin. But not one sparrow falls to the ground without the permission of your Father.

**30** God even knows the number of the hairs on your head.

**31** Thus do not be afraid. You are more valuable than many sparrows.

**32** If a person tells other people that he follows me, I will tell my Father in heaven that this person belongs to me.

**33** But if a person tells other people that he does not know me, I will tell my Father in heaven that I do not know that person.

**34** Do not think that I came to bring peace to the earth. I did not come to bring peace. I came to bring a sword.

**35** I came to cause a man to fight against his father. A daughter will fight against her mother. A daughter-in-law will fight against her mother-in-law.

**36** The enemies of a person will be the people in his own family.

**37** A person who loves his father or mother more than me is not good enough for me. A person who loves his son or daughter more than me is not good enough for me.

**38** A person who does not take his cross and follow me is not good enough for me.

**39** A person who tries to keep his life will lose it. But a person who gives up his life for me will find it.

**40** A person who accepts you accepts me. A person who accepts me accepts God, who sent me.

**41** A person who accepts a prophet because he is a prophet will get the reward of a prophet. A person who accepts a good man because he is good will get the reward of a good man.

**42** A person may give a cup of cold water to one of these humble people because that person is my disciple. I tell you the truth, that person will surely get his reward."

## Chapter 11

**1** Jesus stopped giving instructions to his 12 disciples. Then he went away from there to teach and preach in the towns of Galilee.

**2** John the Baptist was in prison. He heard about the things that the Christ did. Thus John sent 2 of his disciples to Jesus.

**3** The disciples asked Jesus, "Are you the man who God said will come? Or must we wait for a different man?"

**4** Jesus answered them, "Go back and tell John what you hear and see.

**5** Blind people see. People who cannot walk now walk. People with leprosy become clean. Deaf people hear. Dead people come back to life. Poor people hear the good news.

**6** God blesses the person who does not stop his belief in me."

**7** The disciples of John went away. Then Jesus started to speak to the crowds about John. He said, "What did you go out into the desert to see? Did you go to see a tall grass that the wind moves?

**8** What did you go out to see? Did you go to see a man in expensive clothes? No, people who wear expensive clothes live in the houses of kings.

**9** What did you go out to see? Did you go to see a prophet? Yes, and I tell you, John is more than a prophet.

**10** The scriptures say this about John: 'Look, I will send my messenger in front of you. He will make the road ready for you.'

**11** I tell you the truth, no person born of a woman is greater than John the Baptist. But the least important person in the kingdom of heaven is greater than John.

**12** From the time of John the Baptist until now, the kingdom of heaven has strong attacks. Violent people try to take control of it by force.

**13** All the prophets and the law spoke about the future until the time of John.

**14** If you will accept it, John is the Elijah who the prophets said will come.

**15** You have ears. Thus listen and understand!

**16** What can I compare the people of this time to? They are like children who sit in the market place. The children shout to other children,

**17** 'We played music for you, but you did not dance. We sang sad songs, but you did not cry.'

**18** John came and did not eat or drink like other people. Then people said, 'He has a demon in him.'

**19** The Son of Man came, and he ate and drank. Then people said, 'Look at him! He eats too much and drinks too much wine. He is a friend of tax collectors and sinners.' But the results of wisdom show that it is correct."

**20** Then Jesus started to speak against the towns where he did most of his miracles. He spoke against them because the people did not repent.

**21** "It will be very bad for you, Chorazin! It will be very bad for you, Bethsaida! I did miracles in you. If I did these miracles in Tyre and Sidon, those people would repent long ago. They would wear rough clothes and put ashes on their heads to show their sorrow.

**22** But I tell you, on the day of judgment, God will punish you more than Tyre and Sidon.

**23** And you, Capernaum, do you think that God will lift you up to heaven? No, God will send you down to the place of the dead. I did miracles in you. If I did these miracles in Sodom, Sodom would still be there today.

**24** But I tell you, on the day of judgment, God will punish you more than the land of Sodom."

**25** At that time Jesus said, "I thank you, Father, Lord of heaven and earth. You hid these things from wise and clever people. You showed them to people who are like little children.

**26** Yes, Father, you did this because it pleased you.

**27** My Father gave all things to me. Only the Father knows the Son. Only the Son knows the Father. Also, the people to whom the Son chooses to show the Father know him.

**28** Come to me, all you people who are tired and have heavy loads. I will give you rest.

**29** Take my yoke on you and learn from me, because I am gentle and humble in heart. Then you will find rest for your souls.

**30** My yoke is easy, and the load that I give you is light."

## Chapter 12

**1** At that time Jesus walked through the fields of grain on the Sabbath day. His disciples were hungry. They started to pull off the tops of the grain and eat them.

**2** The Pharisees saw this. They said to Jesus, "Look! Your disciples do a thing that the law does not let people do on the Sabbath."

**3** Jesus said to them, "Did you not read what David did? David and the men with him were hungry.

**4** David went into the house of God. He and his men ate the holy bread. The law did not let them eat that bread. The law let only the priests eat it.

**5** Did you not also read in the law about the priests? On the Sabbath, the priests in the temple do work and thus break the law of the Sabbath. But they are not guilty.

**6** I tell you, there is a person here who is greater than the temple.

**7** The scriptures say, 'I want mercy and not sacrifice.' If you knew the meaning of these words, you would not say that innocent people are guilty.

**8** The Son of Man is Lord of the Sabbath."

**9** Jesus went away from there and went into their synagogue.

**10** A man with a paralyzed hand was there. The Pharisees wanted a reason to accuse Jesus. Thus they asked him, "Does the law let people heal on the Sabbath?"

**11** Jesus said to them, "If you have 1 sheep and it falls into a hole on the Sabbath, you will hold it and lift it out.

**12** A person is much more valuable than a sheep. Thus the law lets people do good things on the Sabbath."

**13** Then Jesus said to the man, "Put out your hand." The man put out his hand. The hand became healthy again, the same as the other hand.

**14** But the Pharisees went out and made plans against Jesus. They planned how to kill him.

**15** Jesus knew about their plans, and he went away from there. Many people followed him, and he healed all the sick people.

**16** He told them not to tell other people about him.

**17** This made true the words that the prophet Isaiah spoke:

**18** "Look at my servant. I chose him. I love him, and I am very pleased with him. I will put my Spirit on him. He will tell the Gentiles about justice.

**19** He will not fight or shout. People will not hear his voice in the streets.

**20** He will not break a weak plant. He will not put out a small flame. He will continue until he makes justice win.

**21** The Gentiles will put their hope in him."

**22** Then people brought to Jesus a man with a demon in him. The man was blind and could not speak. Jesus healed him. Then the man could speak and see.

**23** All the people were very surprised. They said, "Is this man possibly the Son of David?"

**24** The Pharisees heard this. They said, "This man throws out demons only with the power of Beelzebul, the ruler of the demons."

**25** Jesus knew their thoughts. He said to them, "If a kingdom fights against itself, the kingdom will be destroyed. If a city or a family fights against itself, the city or family will not continue.

**26** If Satan throws out Satan, then Satan fights against himself. Then his kingdom will not continue.

**27** If I use the power of Beelzebul to throw out demons, what power do your followers use? Thus your followers will show that you are wrong.

**28** But I use the Spirit of God to throw out demons. Thus the kingdom of God came to you.

**29** A person cannot go into the house of a strong man and steal his things without first tying up the strong man. After that, he can steal the things from the house.

**30** A person who is not with me is against me. A person who does not help me to bring people together causes them to go away.

**31** Thus I tell you, God will forgive people for all sins and for all bad words against him. But God will not forgive people who speak against the Holy Spirit.

**32** God will forgive a person who speaks against the Son of Man. But God will not forgive a person who speaks against the Holy Spirit. He will not forgive him now or in the future.

**33** If a tree is good, its fruit will be good. If a tree is bad, its fruit will be bad. You know a tree by its fruit.

**34** You are like a family of poisonous snakes! You are evil. Thus you cannot say good things. The mouth speaks what fills the heart.

**35** A good person has good things in his heart, and he says good things. An evil person has evil things in his heart, and he says evil things.

**36** I tell you, on the day of judgment, people must explain every careless word that they spoke.

**37** God will use your words to judge you. Your words will show that you are innocent or that you are guilty."

**38** Then some of the teachers of the law and Pharisees spoke to Jesus. They said, "Teacher, we want to see a miracle from you as a sign."

**39** Jesus answered them, "The people of this time are evil and not faithful to God. They ask for a miraculous sign. But God will give them no sign other than the sign of the prophet Jonah.

**40** Jonah was in the stomach of a big fish for 3 days and 3 nights. The Son of Man will be in the earth for 3 days and 3 nights in the same way.

**41** On the day of judgment, the people of Nineveh will stand up with the people of this time. The people of Nineveh will show that you are guilty. They repented when Jonah preached to them. And now a person greater than Jonah is here.

**42** On the day of judgment, the Queen of the South will stand up with the people of this time. She will show that you are guilty. She came from a land very far away to hear the wisdom of Solomon. And now a person greater than Solomon is here.

**43** When an evil spirit comes out of a person, the spirit goes through dry places. It looks for a place to rest, but it does not find one.

**44** Then the spirit says, 'I will go back to the house that I came from.' The spirit goes back and finds the house empty, clean, and in good order.

**45** Then the evil spirit goes and gets 7 other spirits that are more evil than itself. They go in and live there. Then the condition of that person is worse than before. The same thing will occur to the evil people of this time."

**46** While Jesus spoke to the people, his mother and brothers stood outside. They wanted to speak to him.

**47** A person said to Jesus, "Your mother and your brothers stand outside. They want to speak to you."

**48** Jesus answered the person, "Who is my mother? Who are my brothers?"

**49** Jesus pointed to his disciples and said, "Look, these people are my mother and my brothers!

**50** A person who does what my Father in heaven wants is my brother, my sister, and my mother."

## Chapter 13

**1** On that same day, Jesus went out of the house and sat by the lake.

**2** Large crowds came to him. Thus he got into a boat and sat in it. All the people stood on the shore.

**3** Jesus taught them many things with stories (parables). He said, "A farmer went out to plant his seed.

**4** While he threw the seed, some seed fell along the path. Birds came and ate it.

**5** Some seed fell on ground with many rocks. There was not much soil. The plants grew quickly, because the soil was not deep.

**6** But when the sun came up, the sun burned the plants. They became dry and died, because they had no roots.

**7** Some seed fell among thorn bushes. The thorn bushes grew and stopped the growth of the plants.

**8** But some seed fell on good soil. It made grain. Some plants made 100 times more grain than the farmer planted. Some made 60 times more, and some made 30 times more.

**9** You have ears. Thus listen and understand!"

**10** The disciples came to Jesus and asked, "Why do you speak to the people with parables?"

**11** Jesus answered, "God lets you know the secrets of the kingdom of heaven. But he does not let them know.

**12** A person who has some understanding will get more. He will have much. But a person who does not have understanding will lose even the little that he has.

**13** Thus I speak to the people with parables. They look, but they do not see. They listen, but they do not hear or understand.

**14** The words of the prophet Isaiah are true about them. Isaiah said, 'You will listen and listen, but you will not understand. You will look and look, but you will not see.

**15** The hearts of these people became hard. Their ears do not hear well. They closed their eyes. If not, they could see with their eyes and hear with their ears. They could understand with their hearts and turn back to me. Then I would heal them.'

**16** But God blesses your eyes, because they see. He blesses your ears, because they hear.

**17** I tell you the truth, many prophets and good people wanted to see the things that you see. But they did not see them. They wanted to hear the things that you hear. But they did not hear them.

**18** Thus listen to the meaning of the parable of the farmer.

**19** Some people hear the message about the kingdom but do not understand it. Then the evil one comes and takes away what was planted in their hearts. This is the seed that fell along the path.

**20** The seed that fell on the ground with rocks is a person who hears the message. Immediately he accepts it with joy.

**21** But he does not have deep roots, and he stays faithful only for a short time. Trouble or suffering comes because of the message. Then he quickly stops his belief.

**22** The seed that fell among the thorn bushes is a person who hears the message. But he is anxious about this life, and he wants to be rich. These things stop the message from growing. Thus the message does not make fruit in his life.

**23** The seed that fell on good soil is a person who hears the message and understands it. He makes fruit. Some people make 100 times more, some 60 times more, and some 30 times more."

**24** Jesus told them another parable. He said, "The kingdom of heaven is like a man who planted good seed in his field.

**25** But while people slept, his enemy came. The enemy planted weeds among the wheat and went away.

**26** The wheat grew and made grain. Then the weeds also became visible.

**27** The servants of the owner came to him and said, 'Sir, you planted good seed in your field. Where did the weeds come from?'

**28** The owner said to them, 'An enemy did this.' The servants asked him, 'Do you want us to go and pull out the weeds?'

**29** The owner said, 'No. If you pull out the weeds, you will possibly pull out the wheat also.

**30** Let both grow together until the harvest. At the time of the harvest, I will tell the workers this: First, collect the weeds and tie them together. Then burn them. Then collect the wheat and put it into my storehouse.'"

**31** Jesus told them another parable. He said, "The kingdom of heaven is like a mustard seed. A man planted the seed in his field.

**32** The mustard seed is the smallest of all seeds. But when it grows, it is the largest of the garden plants. It becomes a tree. Birds come and make nests in its branches."

**33** Jesus told them another parable. He said, "The kingdom of heaven is like yeast. A woman mixed the yeast into a large quantity of flour. Then the yeast went through all of the dough."

**34** Jesus used parables to tell the crowd all these things. He did not speak to them without a parable.

**35** This made true the words that the prophet spoke: "I will speak with parables. I will tell things that were secret since God made the world."

**36** Then Jesus sent the crowd away and went into the house. His disciples came to him. They said, "Explain to us the parable of the weeds in the field."

**37** Jesus answered, "The man who planted the good seed is the Son of Man.

**38** The field is the world. The good seed is the people who belong to the kingdom. The weeds are the people who belong to the evil one.

**39** The enemy who planted the weeds is the devil. The harvest is the end of the world. The workers who cut the harvest are the angels.

**40** People collect the weeds and burn them in the fire. The same thing will occur at the end of the world.

**41** The Son of Man will send out his angels. They will remove from his kingdom all the people who cause sin and all the people who do evil.

**42** The angels will throw them into the fire. There people will cry and grind their teeth in pain.

**43** Then the good people will shine like the sun in the kingdom of their Father. You have ears. Thus listen and understand!

**44** The kingdom of heaven is like a valuable thing that was hidden in a field. A man found it and hid it again. He was very happy. He went and sold all that he had, and he bought that field.

**45** Also, the kingdom of heaven is like a merchant who looked for good pearls.

**46** He found 1 pearl that was very valuable. He went and sold all that he had, and he bought that pearl.

**47** Also, the kingdom of heaven is like a net. Fishermen threw the net into the lake, and it caught all types of fish.

**48** When the net was full, the fishermen pulled it onto the shore. They sat down and put the good fish into baskets. But they threw the bad fish away.

**49** The same thing will occur at the end of the world. The angels will come and separate the evil people from the good people.

**50** The angels will throw the evil people into the fire. There people will cry and grind their teeth in pain."

**51** Jesus asked them, "Do you understand all these things?" They said, "Yes, Lord."

**52** Then Jesus said to them, "Thus every teacher of the law who becomes a disciple of the kingdom of heaven is like the owner of a house. He brings out new things and old things from his storeroom."

**53** Jesus stopped telling these parables. Then he went away from there.

**54** He went to his own town. He taught the people in their synagogue, and they were very surprised. They said, "Where did this man get this wisdom and the power to do these miracles?

**55** He is the son of the carpenter. His mother is Mary. His brothers are James, Joseph, Simon, and Judas.

**56** All his sisters are here with us. Where did this man get all these things?"

**57** Thus the people did not accept him. But Jesus said to them, "People honor a prophet in all places but his own town and his own house."

**58** Jesus did not do many miracles there, because the people did not believe.

## Chapter 14

**1** At that time Herod, the ruler of Galilee, heard the news about Jesus.

**2** He said to his servants, "This man is John the Baptist. John came back to life from the dead. That is why he has the power to do these miracles."

**3** Before this time, Herod arrested John. He tied him and put him in prison. Herod did this because of Herodias. Herodias was the wife of Philip, the brother of Herod.

**4** John told Herod many times, "The law does not let you have her as your wife."

**5** Herod wanted to kill John. But he was afraid of the people, because they thought that John was a prophet.

**6** On the birthday of Herod, the daughter of Herodias danced in front of the guests. Her dance pleased Herod very much.

**7** Thus he made an oath to give her all that she asked.

**8** Her mother told her what to say. Thus the girl said, "Give me the head of John the Baptist here on a large plate."

**9** The king was sad. But he made an oath in front of his guests. Thus he ordered his men to give her the head.

**10** Herod sent a man to the prison to cut off the head of John.

**11** The man brought the head on a large plate and gave it to the girl. The girl took it to her mother.

**12** The disciples of John came and took his body. They buried it. Then they went and told Jesus.

**13** Jesus heard about what occurred to John. He went away from there in a boat to a quiet place to be alone. But the crowds heard about this. They followed him on foot from the towns.

**14** Jesus got out of the boat and saw a large crowd. He felt pity for the people and healed their sick people.

**15** In the evening, his disciples came to him. They said, "This place is far from towns, and it is late. Send the crowds away. Then they can go into the villages and buy food for themselves."

**16** But Jesus said to them, "They do not need to go away. You give them food to eat."

**17** The disciples said to him, "We have only 5 loaves of bread and 2 fish here."

**18** Jesus said, "Bring them here to me."

**19** He told the crowd to sit down on the grass. He took the 5 loaves and the 2 fish. He looked up to heaven and thanked God. He broke the loaves and gave them to the disciples. The disciples gave the bread to the people.

**20** All the people ate as much as they wanted. Then the disciples collected 12 baskets full of the pieces that were left.

**21** About 5,000 men ate. Women and children also ate.

**22** Immediately Jesus told his disciples to get into the boat. He told them to go in front of him to the other side of the lake. Then he sent the crowds away.

**23** After Jesus sent the crowds away, he went up on a mountain alone to pray. In the evening he was there alone.

**24** At that time the boat was far from the land. The waves hit the boat, because the wind blew against it.

**25** Between 3 and 6 o'clock in the morning, Jesus came to the disciples. He walked on the water.

**26** The disciples saw him walk on the water, and they were very afraid. They said, "It is a ghost!" They shouted because they were afraid.

**27** But immediately Jesus spoke to them. He said, "Be brave! It is I. Do not be afraid."

**28** Peter answered him, "Lord, if it is you, tell me to come to you on the water."

**29** Jesus said, "Come." Peter got out of the boat and walked on the water to Jesus.

**30** But Peter saw the strong wind, and he became afraid. He started to go down into the water. He shouted, "Lord, save me!"

**31** Immediately Jesus put out his hand and held Peter. Jesus said to him, "Your faith is very small! Why did you doubt?"

**32** Jesus and Peter got into the boat, and the wind stopped.

**33** Then the disciples in the boat worshipped Jesus. They said, "Truly you are the Son of God!"

**34** They went across the lake and came to land at Gennesaret.

**35** The men of that place knew Jesus. They sent the news to all the area near there. People brought all the sick people to Jesus.

**36** The sick people asked Jesus to let them touch only the edge of his coat. All the people who touched it were completely healed.

## Chapter 15

**1** Then some Pharisees and teachers of the law came to Jesus from Jerusalem. They asked him,

**2** "Why do your disciples disobey the traditions of the leaders of long ago? They do not wash their hands in the correct way before they eat."

**3** Jesus answered them, "Why do you disobey the command of God because of your traditions?

**4** God said, 'Honor your father and your mother.' He also said, 'A person who says bad things about his father or mother must die.'

**5** But you teach a different thing. You say that a person can tell his father or mother, 'I can give you help. But I will give this help to God as a gift.'

**6** Then that person does not need to honor his father or mother. Thus you cancel the word of God because of your traditions.

**7** You hypocrites! Isaiah spoke correctly about you. He said,

**8** 'These people honor me with their words. But their hearts are far from me.

**9** Their worship of me has no value. They teach rules that people made, as if the rules come from God.'"

**10** Jesus called the crowd to him. He said to them, "Listen and understand.

**11** The things that go into the mouth of a person do not make him unclean. The things that come out of his mouth make him unclean."

**12** Then the disciples came to Jesus and said, "Do you know that the Pharisees became angry when they heard these words?"

**13** Jesus answered, "My Father in heaven will pull out every plant that he did not plant.

**14** Do not pay attention to the Pharisees. They are blind guides who lead blind people. If a blind person leads a blind person, both will fall into a hole."

**15** Peter said to Jesus, "Explain this parable to us."

**16** Jesus said, "Do you also still not understand?

**17** You must understand this. All food that goes into the mouth goes into the stomach. Then it goes out of the body.

**18** But the things that come out of the mouth come from the heart. Those things make a person unclean.

**19** These evil things come from the heart: evil thoughts, murder, adultery, sexual sins, theft, lies about other people, and bad words against other people.

**20** These things make a person unclean. But if a person eats with unwashed hands, that does not make him unclean."

**21** Jesus went away from there to the area of Tyre and Sidon.

**22** A Canaanite woman from that area came to him. She shouted, "Lord, Son of David, show mercy to me! A demon is in my daughter and causes her very bad suffering."

**23** But Jesus did not say a word to her. His disciples came to him and said, "Send her away. She continues to shout behind us."

**24** Jesus answered, "God sent me only to the people of Israel. They are like lost sheep."

**25** Then the woman came and bowed down in front of him. She said, "Lord, help me!"

**26** Jesus answered, "It is not correct to take the bread of the children and throw it to the dogs."

**27** The woman said, "Yes, Lord. But even the dogs eat the small pieces of food that fall from the table of their masters."

**28** Then Jesus answered her, "Woman, your faith is great! What you want will occur." Her daughter was healed at that same time.

**29** Jesus went away from there and went along the Sea of Galilee. He went up on a mountain and sat down there.

**30** Large crowds came to him. They brought people who could not walk, blind people, people who could not speak, people with damaged bodies, and many other sick people. They put them at the feet of Jesus, and he healed them.

**31** The crowd was very surprised. They saw that people who could not speak now spoke. People with damaged bodies became healthy. People who could not walk now walked. Blind people now saw. Thus the people praised the God of Israel.

**32** Jesus called his disciples to him. He said, "I feel pity for these people. They stayed with me for 3 days, and they have no food. I do not want to send them away hungry. They can possibly become weak on the road."

**33** His disciples said to him, "We are in a place far from towns. Where can we get sufficient bread to feed such a large crowd?"

**34** Jesus asked them, "How many loaves of bread do you have?" They said, "7, and a small number of little fish."

**35** Jesus told the crowd to sit down on the ground.

**36** He took the 7 loaves and the fish, and he thanked God. He broke them and gave them to his disciples. The disciples gave the food to the people.

**37** All the people ate as much as they wanted. Then the disciples collected 7 baskets full of the pieces that were left.

**38** 4,000 men ate. Women and children also ate.

**39** Jesus sent the crowd away. Then he got into the boat and went to the area of Magadan.

## Chapter 16

**1** The Pharisees and Sadducees came to Jesus to test him. They asked him to show them a miraculous sign from heaven.

**2** Jesus answered them, "In the evening you say, 'The weather will be good, because the sky is red.'

**3** In the morning you say, 'Today there will be a storm, because the sky is red and dark.' You hypocrites! You know how to understand the signs in the sky. But you cannot understand the signs of what occurs at this time.

**4** The people of this time are evil and not faithful to God. They ask for a miraculous sign. But God will give them no sign other than the sign of the prophet Jonah." Then Jesus went away from them.

**5** The disciples went across the lake. But they forgot to take bread.

**6** Jesus said to them, "Be careful! Be careful of the yeast of the Pharisees and Sadducees."

**7** The disciples spoke about this among themselves. They said, "He says this because we did not bring bread."

**8** Jesus knew what they said. He said, "Your faith is very small! Why do you speak among yourselves about the bread that you do not have?

**9** You still do not understand. Do you not remember the 5 loaves for the 5,000 people? Do you not remember how many baskets you collected?

**10** Do you not remember the 7 loaves for the 4,000 people? Do you not remember how many baskets you collected?

**11** Why do you not understand that I did not speak about bread? I told you to be careful of the yeast of the Pharisees and Sadducees."

**12** Then the disciples understood. Jesus did not tell them to be careful of the yeast in bread. He told them to be careful of the teaching of the Pharisees and Sadducees.

**13** Jesus came to the area of Caesarea Philippi. He asked his disciples, "Who do people say that the Son of Man is?"

**14** They said, "Some people say that you are John the Baptist. Other people say that you are Elijah. Other people say that you are Jeremiah or one of the prophets."

**15** Jesus said to them, "But who do you say that I am?"

**16** Simon Peter answered, "You are the Christ, the Son of the living God."

**17** Jesus answered him, "God blesses you, Simon son of Jonah. No person showed this to you. My Father in heaven showed it to you.

**18** I tell you, you are Peter, which means 'rock.' On this rock I will build my church. The power of death will not win against my church.

**19** I will give you the keys of the kingdom of heaven. The things that you forbid on earth will be forbidden in heaven. The things that you permit on earth will be permitted in heaven."

**20** Then Jesus gave a strong order to his disciples. He told them not to tell anyone that he was the Christ.

**21** From that time Jesus started to tell his disciples what must occur. He must go to Jerusalem. The leaders, the chief priests, and the teachers of the law will make him suffer much. They will kill him. On the third day he will come back to life.

**22** Peter took Jesus to one side and started to speak strongly against him. Peter said, "God forbid it, Lord! This must never occur to you!"

**23** Jesus turned and said to Peter, "Go away from me, Satan! You try to stop me. You do not think about the things of God. You think about the things of people."

**24** Then Jesus said to his disciples, "If a person wants to follow me, he must stop doing what he wants. He must take up his cross and follow me.

**25** A person who wants to save his life will lose it. But a person who gives up his life for me will find it.

**26** A person may get all the world but lose his soul. Then he gets nothing of value. A person can give nothing to buy back his soul.

**27** The Son of Man will come with his angels in the glory of his Father. Then he will reward each person for what that person did.

**28** I tell you the truth, some people who stand here will not die before they see the Son of Man come in his kingdom."

## Chapter 17

**1** After 6 days, Jesus took Peter, James, and John, the brother of James. He took them up on a high mountain where they were alone.

**2** There, in front of them, the appearance of Jesus changed. His face shone like the sun. His clothes became as white as light.

**3** Then Moses and Elijah appeared to them. Moses and Elijah spoke with Jesus.

**4** Peter said to Jesus, "Lord, it is good for us to be here. If you want, I will make 3 shelters here. I will make 1 for you, 1 for Moses, and 1 for Elijah."

**5** While Peter spoke, a bright cloud covered them. A voice from the cloud said, "This is my Son. I love him. I am very pleased with him. Listen to him!"

**6** The disciples heard this. They fell down with their faces to the ground, and they were very afraid.

**7** Jesus came and touched them. He said, "Get up. Do not be afraid."

**8** The disciples looked up. They saw nobody but Jesus.

**9** They came down from the mountain. Jesus ordered them, "Do not tell anyone what you saw. Wait until the Son of Man comes back to life from the dead."

**10** The disciples asked him, "Why do the teachers of the law say that Elijah must come first?"

**11** Jesus answered, "It is true that Elijah will come first. He will make all things correct again.

**12** But I tell you, Elijah already came. The people did not know him. They did to him all the bad things that they wanted. In the same way, they will make the Son of Man suffer."

**13** Then the disciples understood that Jesus spoke to them about John the Baptist.

**14** They came to the crowd. A man came to Jesus and went down on his knees in front of him.

**15** The man said, "Lord, show mercy to my son. He has seizures and suffers very much. He frequently falls into the fire or into the water.

**16** I brought him to your disciples, but they could not heal him."

**17** Jesus answered, "You people of this time have no faith and are very bad! How long must I stay with you? How long must I be patient with you? Bring the boy here to me."

**18** Jesus gave a strong order to the demon, and the demon came out of the boy. The boy was healed at that time.

**19** Then the disciples came to Jesus when they were alone with him. They asked, "Why could we not throw out the demon?"

**20** Jesus said to them, "Because your faith is small. I tell you the truth, your faith can be as small as a mustard seed. Then you can tell this mountain, 'Move from here to there,' and it will move. You will be able to do all things.

**21** But this type of demon comes out only with prayer and fasting."

**22** The disciples came together in Galilee. Jesus said to them, "A person will give the Son of Man to the authority of people.

**23** They will kill him. On the third day he will come back to life." The disciples became very sad.

**24** Jesus and his disciples came to Capernaum. The men who collected the temple tax came to Peter. They said, "Does your teacher pay the temple tax?"

**25** Peter said, "Yes, he does." Peter went into the house. Before Peter spoke, Jesus asked him, "What do you think, Simon? From whom do the kings of the earth collect taxes? Do they collect taxes from their own children or from other people?"

**26** Peter said, "From other people." Jesus said to him, "Then the children do not need to pay.

**27** But we do not want to make these men angry. Thus go to the lake and throw out a fishing line. Take the first fish that you catch. Open its mouth, and you will find a coin. Take that coin and give it to them for me and for you."

## Chapter 18

**1** At that time the disciples came to Jesus. They asked, "Who is the greatest in the kingdom of heaven?"

**2** Jesus called a little child to him. He put the child among them.

**3** He said, "I tell you the truth, you must change and become like little children. If not, you will never go into the kingdom of heaven.

**4** Thus the person who makes himself humble like this child is the greatest in the kingdom of heaven.

**5** A person who accepts a child like this because he follows me accepts me.

**6** But a person may cause one of these little ones who believe in me to sin. It is better for that person to have a large stone tied around his neck and to drown in the deep sea.

**7** It will be very bad for the world because of the things that cause people to sin. Such things must come. But it will be very bad for the person who causes them!

**8** If your hand or your foot causes you to sin, cut it off and throw it away. It is better for you to go into life with only 1 hand or 1 foot. That is better than to have 2 hands and 2 feet and go into the fire that will never stop.

**9** If your eye causes you to sin, take it out and throw it away. It is better for you to go into life with only 1 eye. That is better than to have 2 eyes and go into the fire of hell.

**10** Be careful that you do not think that one of these little ones is not important. I tell you, their angels in heaven always see the face of my Father in heaven.

**11** The Son of Man came to save the people who are lost.

**12** What do you think? A man has 100 sheep, and 1 sheep goes away and is lost. He will leave the 99 sheep on the hills. He will go and look for the lost sheep.

**13** I tell you the truth, if he finds that sheep, he will be very happy. He will be happier about that 1 sheep than about the 99 sheep that did not go away.

**14** In the same way, your Father in heaven does not want one of these little ones to be lost.

**15** If your brother sins against you, go and speak to him alone. Tell him his fault. If he listens to you, you got your brother back.

**16** But if he does not listen to you, take 1 or 2 other people with you. Then 2 or 3 witnesses can confirm all that you say.

**17** If he does not listen to them, tell the church. If he does not listen to the church, treat him like a Gentile or a tax collector.

**18** I tell you the truth, the things that you forbid on earth will be forbidden in heaven. The things that you permit on earth will be permitted in heaven.

**19** Also, I tell you, 2 of you on earth may agree about a thing that you ask for. Then my Father in heaven will do it for you.

**20** Where 2 or 3 people come together because they follow me, I am there with them."

**21** Then Peter came to Jesus and asked, "Lord, my brother may sin against me many times. How many times must I forgive him? Must I forgive him 7 times?"

**22** Jesus said to him, "I do not tell you 7 times. I tell you 77 times.

**23** Thus the kingdom of heaven is like a king who wanted to examine the accounts of his servants.

**24** The king started to examine the accounts. His men brought to him a servant who owed him 10,000 talents of silver.

**25** The servant did not have the money to pay. Thus his master ordered his men to sell the servant, his wife, his children, and all that he had. The money from the sale would pay the debt.

**26** The servant went down on his knees in front of the king. He said, 'Master, be patient with me, and I will pay you all the money.'

**27** The master felt pity for the servant. He canceled the debt and let him go.

**28** Then that servant went out and found another servant. The other servant owed him 100 silver coins. The first servant held him by the throat. He said, 'Pay me the money that you owe!'

**29** The other servant went down on his knees in front of him. He said, 'Be patient with me, and I will pay you all the money.'

**30** But the first servant refused. He put the other servant in prison until he paid the debt.

**31** The other servants saw what occurred, and they were very sad. They went and told their master all that occurred.

**32** Then the master called the first servant. He said, 'You evil servant! You asked me for help, and I canceled all of your debt.

**33** You should also show mercy to the other servant, as I showed mercy to you.'

**34** The master was very angry. He gave the servant to the guards to punish him until he paid all that he owed.

**35** My Father in heaven will do the same thing to each of you if you do not forgive your brother from your heart."

## Chapter 19

**1** Jesus stopped speaking these words. Then he went away from Galilee. He went to the area of Judea on the other side of the Jordan River.

**2** Large crowds followed him, and he healed the sick people there.

**3** Some Pharisees came to Jesus to test him. They asked, "Does the law let a man divorce his wife for any reason?"

**4** Jesus answered, "Did you not read the scriptures? At the start, God made people male and female.

**5** God said, 'Thus a man will leave his father and mother and stay with his wife. The 2 people will become 1 body.'

**6** Thus they are not 2 people but 1 body. God joined them together. Thus no person must separate them."

**7** The Pharisees asked him, "Then why did Moses command a man to give his wife a written document of divorce and send her away?"

**8** Jesus said to them, "Moses let you divorce your wives because your hearts were hard. But at the start it was not like this.

**9** I tell you, a man may divorce his wife for a reason other than sexual sin and marry another woman. That man commits adultery. Also, a man who marries a divorced woman commits adultery."

**10** The disciples said to him, "If this is the condition between a man and his wife, it is better not to marry."

**11** Jesus said to them, "Not all people can accept this teaching. Only the people to whom God gives this ability can accept it.

**12** Some men cannot marry because they were born that way. Some men cannot marry because other people made them that way. Some men choose not to marry because of the kingdom of heaven. The person who can accept this teaching must accept it."

**13** Then people brought little children to Jesus. They wanted him to put his hands on the children and pray for them. But the disciples spoke strongly against the people.

**14** Jesus said, "Let the little children come to me. Do not stop them. The kingdom of heaven belongs to people like these children."

**15** Jesus put his hands on the children. Then he went away from there.

**16** A man came to Jesus and asked, "Good teacher, what good thing must I do to get eternal life?"

**17** Jesus said to him, "Why do you call me good? Only one is good, and that is God. If you want to go into life, obey the commands."

**18** The man asked, "Which commands?" Jesus said, "Do not murder. Do not commit adultery. Do not steal. Do not tell lies about other people.

**19** Honor your father and your mother. Love your neighbor as you love yourself."

**20** The young man said to him, "I obeyed all these commands since I was young. What more must I do?"

**21** Jesus said to him, "If you want to be perfect, go and sell all that you have. Give the money to poor people. Then you will have riches in heaven. Then come and follow me."

**22** The young man heard this, and he went away sad, because he was very rich.

**23** Then Jesus said to his disciples, "I tell you the truth, it is very difficult for a rich person to go into the kingdom of heaven.

**24** Again I tell you, it is easier for a camel to go through the hole in a needle than for a rich person to go into the kingdom of God."

**25** The disciples heard this and were very surprised. They asked, "Then who can be saved?"

**26** Jesus looked at them and said, "People cannot do this. But God can do all things."

**27** Then Peter said to him, "Look, we left all that we had and followed you. Thus what will we get?"

**28** Jesus said to them, "I tell you the truth, in the new world, the Son of Man will sit on his glorious throne. Then you who followed me will also sit on 12 thrones. You will judge the 12 tribes of Israel.

**29** Some people left houses, brothers, sisters, father, mother, wife, children, or fields because they follow me. Those people will get 100 times more. They will also receive eternal life.

**30** But many people who are first now will be last. Many people who are last now will be first."

## Chapter 20

**1** "The kingdom of heaven is like the owner of a farm. Early in the morning he went out to hire workers for his vineyard.

**2** He agreed to pay the workers 1 silver coin for each day. Then he sent them into his vineyard.

**3** At about 9 o'clock in the morning, he went out again. He saw other men in the market place who stood and did no work.

**4** He said to them, 'You also go and work in my vineyard. I will pay you a correct amount.' Thus they went.

**5** He went out again at about 12 o'clock and at about 3 o'clock, and he did the same thing.

**6** At about 5 o'clock he went out again. He found other men who stood and did no work. He said to them, 'Why do you stand here all day and do no work?'

**7** They said to him, 'Because nobody hired us.' He said to them, 'You also go and work in my vineyard.'

**8** In the evening, the owner of the vineyard spoke to his manager. He said, 'Call the workers and pay them. Start with the last workers and end with the first workers.'

**9** The workers who started at about 5 o'clock came. Each man got 1 silver coin.

**10** Then the first workers came. They thought that they would get more. But each of them also got 1 silver coin.

**11** They got their money, and they complained against the owner.

**12** They said, 'These last workers worked for only 1 hour. But you paid them the same as us. We worked hard all day in the hot sun.'

**13** The owner answered one of them, 'Friend, I do nothing wrong to you. You agreed to work for 1 silver coin.

**14** Take your money and go. I want to give the last worker the same amount that I gave you.

**15** I can do what I want with my own money. Are you jealous because I am generous?'

**16** Thus the last people will be first, and the first people will be last. God calls many people, but he chooses only a small number."

**17** Jesus went up to Jerusalem. On the road, he took the 12 disciples to one side. He said to them,

**18** "Listen, we go up to Jerusalem. A person will give the Son of Man to the chief priests and the teachers of the law. They will say that he must die.

**19** They will give him to the Gentiles. The Gentiles will laugh at him, hit him with whips, and nail him to a cross. On the third day he will come back to life."

**20** Then the mother of the sons of Zebedee came to Jesus with her sons. She bowed down in front of him and asked him for a thing.

**21** Jesus said to her, "What do you want?" She said to him, "Promise me that my 2 sons will sit with you in your kingdom. Let one sit at your right side and the other at your left side."

**22** Jesus answered, "You do not know what you ask. Can you drink from the cup of suffering that I will drink?" They said to him, "We can."

**23** Jesus said to them, "You will drink from my cup. But I cannot choose who will sit at my right side or my left side. My Father made those places ready for the people that he chose."

**24** The other 10 disciples heard this. They became angry with the 2 brothers.

**25** Jesus called the disciples to him and said, "You know that the rulers of the Gentiles control their people. Their important leaders use their authority over the people.

**26** But you must not be like that. If a person among you wants to be great, he must serve you.

**27** If a person among you wants to be first, he must be your servant.

**28** Do as the Son of Man did. He did not come to get help from other people. He came to serve. He came to give his life to pay the price to make many people free."

**29** Jesus and his disciples went out of Jericho, and a large crowd followed him.

**30** 2 blind men sat by the road. They heard that Jesus went past. They shouted, "Lord, Son of David, show mercy to us!"

**31** The crowd spoke strongly against the blind men and told them to be quiet. But the men shouted louder, "Lord, Son of David, show mercy to us!"

**32** Jesus stopped and called the men. He asked, "What do you want me to do for you?"

**33** They said to him, "Lord, we want to see."

**34** Jesus felt pity for them, and he touched their eyes. Immediately they could see. Then they followed Jesus.

## Chapter 21

**1** Jesus and his disciples came near to Jerusalem. They came to Bethphage, at the Mount of Olives. Then Jesus sent 2 disciples in front of him.

**2** He said to them, "Go into the village in front of you. Immediately you will find a female donkey there with a young donkey. Untie them and bring them to me.

**3** If a person says something to you, tell him, 'The Lord needs them.' Then he will immediately send them."

**4** This occurred to make true the words that the prophet spoke:

**5** "Tell the people of Zion, 'Look, your King comes to you. He is humble. He sits on a donkey, on a young donkey.'"

**6** The disciples went and did what Jesus told them.

**7** They brought the female donkey and the young donkey. They put their coats on the donkeys, and Jesus sat on them.

**8** A very large crowd put their coats on the road. Other people cut branches from the trees and put them on the road.

**9** Some people of the crowd went in front of Jesus, and some people followed him. They all shouted, "Hosanna to the Son of David! God blesses the man who comes in the name of the Lord! Hosanna in the highest heaven!"

**10** Jesus went into Jerusalem, and all the people in the city became very excited. They asked, "Who is this man?"

**11** The crowd said, "This is Jesus, the prophet from Nazareth in Galilee."

**12** Jesus went into the temple area. He threw out all the people who sold and bought things there. He turned over the tables of the men who exchanged money. He also turned over the chairs of the men who sold doves.

**13** He said to them, "It is written, 'People will call my house a house of prayer.' But you made it a place where thieves hide."

**14** Blind people and people who could not walk came to Jesus in the temple. He healed them.

**15** The chief priests and the teachers of the law saw the miracles that Jesus did. They also heard the children shout in the temple, "Hosanna to the Son of David!" Thus the chief priests and the teachers became angry.

**16** They said to Jesus, "Do you hear what these children say?" Jesus said to them, "Yes. Did you never read this in the scriptures? 'You taught children and babies to give you praise.'"

**17** Then Jesus went away from them. He went out of the city to Bethany and stayed there for the night.

**18** Early in the morning, Jesus went back to the city. He was hungry.

**19** He saw a fig tree by the road and went to it. But he found only leaves on it. He said to the tree, "You will never have fruit again!" Immediately the fig tree became dry and died.

**20** The disciples saw this and were surprised. They asked, "How did the fig tree die so quickly?"

**21** Jesus answered, "I tell you the truth, you must have faith and not doubt. Then you can do what I did to the fig tree. You can also tell this mountain, 'Go and fall into the sea,' and it will occur.

**22** If you believe, you will receive all the things that you ask for in prayer."

**23** Jesus went into the temple area and taught there. The chief priests and the leaders of the people came to him. They asked, "What authority do you have to do these things? Who gave you this authority?"

**24** Jesus answered them, "I also will ask you a question. If you answer me, I will tell you what authority I have to do these things.

**25** Where did the authority of John to baptize come from? Did it come from heaven or from people?" They spoke about this among themselves. They said, "If we say, 'From heaven,' he will ask us, 'Then why did you not believe John?'

**26** But if we say, 'From people,' we are afraid of the crowd. All the people think that John was a prophet."

**27** Thus they answered Jesus, "We do not know." Then Jesus said to them, "Then I will not tell you what authority I have to do these things.

**28** What do you think about this story? A man had 2 sons. He went to the first son and said, 'Son, go and work in the vineyard today.'

**29** The son answered, 'I will not go.' But later he changed his mind and went.

**30** Then the father went to the second son and said the same thing. The son answered, 'I will go, sir.' But he did not go.

**31** Which of the 2 sons did what his father wanted?" They said, "The first son." Jesus said to them, "I tell you the truth, the tax collectors and the prostitutes will go into the kingdom of God before you.

**32** John came to you and showed you the right way to live. But you did not believe him. The tax collectors and the prostitutes believed him. You saw this, but even then you did not repent and believe him.

**33** Listen to another parable. A man planted a vineyard. He put a wall around it. He dug a hole for a winepress and built a tower. Then he let some farmers rent the vineyard, and he went to another country.

**34** The time came to collect the grapes. The owner sent his servants to the farmers to get his part of the grapes.

**35** But the farmers took his servants. They hit one servant, killed another servant, and threw stones at another servant.

**36** Again the owner sent other servants, more than the first time. The farmers did the same things to them.

**37** Last, the owner sent his son to them. He said, 'They will respect my son.'

**38** But the farmers saw the son. They said to each other, 'This man will get the vineyard when the owner dies. Come, we will kill him and take the vineyard.'

**39** Thus they took the son and threw him out of the vineyard. Then they killed him.

**40** When the owner of the vineyard comes, what will he do to those farmers?"

**41** The people said to Jesus, "He will kill those evil men in a terrible way. Then he will rent the vineyard to other farmers. They will give him his part of the grapes at the correct time."

**42** Jesus said to them, "Did you never read this in the scriptures? 'The builders refused the stone. But the stone became the most important stone of the building. The Lord did this, and it is wonderful for us to see.'

**43** Thus I tell you, God will take the kingdom of God away from you. He will give it to people who will make the fruit of the kingdom.

**44** A person who falls on this stone will break into pieces. If the stone falls on a person, it will crush him."

**45** The chief priests and the Pharisees heard the parables of Jesus. They knew that he spoke about them.

**46** They wanted to arrest him. But they were afraid of the crowds, because the people thought that Jesus was a prophet.

## Chapter 22

**3** The king sent his servants to call the guests to the wedding. But the guests did not want to come.

**4** Then he sent other servants. He said to them, 'Tell the guests that I made the meal ready. I killed my bulls and my best animals. All things are ready. Come to the wedding!'

**5** But the guests did not pay attention. One man went to his farm. Another man went to his business.

**6** The other guests took the servants of the king. They hurt them and killed them.

**7** The king was very angry. He sent his army and killed those murderers. The army burned their city.

**8** Then the king said to his servants, 'The wedding meal is ready. But the people that I invited were not good enough to come.

**9** Thus go to the main roads. Invite to the wedding all the people that you find.'

**10** The servants went out to the roads. They brought together all the people that they found, both bad and good. Then the wedding hall was full of guests.

**11** The king came in to see the guests. He saw a man there who did not wear wedding clothes.

**12** The king said to him, 'Friend, how did you come in here without wedding clothes?' The man did not answer.

**13** Then the king said to the servants, 'Tie his hands and feet. Throw him out into the darkness. There people will cry and grind their teeth in pain.'

**14** God invites many people, but he chooses only a small number."

**15** Then the Pharisees went away. They made a plan to cause Jesus to say a wrong thing.

**16** They sent some of their disciples to Jesus with some supporters of Herod. They said, "Teacher, we know that you are honest. You teach the truth about the way of God. You are not afraid of people, because you treat all people the same.

**17** Thus tell us what you think. Does the law let us pay taxes to Caesar or not?"

**18** But Jesus knew their evil plan. He said, "You hypocrites! Why do you try to trick me?

**19** Show me the coin that you use to pay the tax." They brought him a silver coin.

**20** Jesus asked them, "Whose picture and name are on this coin?"

**21** They said to him, "The picture and name of Caesar." Then Jesus said to them, "Thus give to Caesar the things that belong to Caesar. Give to God the things that belong to God."

**22** They heard this and were surprised. They went away from him.

**23** On that same day, some Sadducees came to Jesus. The Sadducees say that dead people do not come back to life. They asked him a question.

**24** They said, "Teacher, Moses said this: 'A man may die and have no children. Then his brother must marry the wife and have children for the dead man.'

**25** There were 7 brothers among us. The first brother married and then died. He had no children. Thus he left his wife to his brother.

**26** The same thing occurred to the second brother and the third brother. It occurred to all 7 brothers.

**27** Last, the woman also died.

**28** Thus when people come back to life from the dead, whose wife will she be? All 7 brothers married her."

**29** Jesus answered them, "You are wrong, because you do not know the scriptures or the power of God.

**30** When people come back to life from the dead, they will not marry. They will be like the angels in heaven.

**31** Did you not read what God told you about the dead people who come back to life? God said,

**32** 'I am the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.' God is not the God of dead people. He is the God of living people."

**33** The crowd heard this, and they were very surprised at his teaching.

**34** The Pharisees heard that Jesus stopped the Sadducees from arguing with him. Thus the Pharisees came together.

**35** One of them was an expert in the law. He tested Jesus with a question.

**36** He asked, "Teacher, which command in the law is the most important?"

**37** Jesus said to him, "'Love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your mind.'

**38** This is the first and most important command.

**39** The second command is almost the same: 'Love your neighbor as you love yourself.'

**40** All the law and the teachings of the prophets come from these 2 commands."

**41** While the Pharisees were together, Jesus asked them a question.

**42** He said, "What do you think about the Christ? Whose son is he?" They said to him, "He is the son of David."

**43** Jesus said to them, "Then why does David call him 'Lord'? The Spirit caused David to say this:

**44** 'The Lord said to my Lord, "Sit at my right side until I put your enemies under your feet."'

**45** David calls him 'Lord.' Thus how can the Christ be the son of David?"

**46** Nobody could answer Jesus with a word. From that day, nobody was brave enough to ask him more questions.

## Chapter 23

**2** He said, "The teachers of the law and the Pharisees have the authority to explain the law of Moses.

**3** Thus obey all that they tell you. Do what they tell you. But do not do what they do, because they do not do what they teach.

**4** They make heavy loads that are difficult to carry, and they put them on the shoulders of other people. But they will not use even 1 finger to move these loads.

**5** They do all their actions so that people will see them. They make their prayer boxes (phylacteries) wide. They make the edges of their clothes long.

**6** They like to sit in the most important places at meals. They like the most important seats in the synagogues.

**7** They like people to greet them with respect in the market places. They like people to call them 'Rabbi.'

**8** But do not let people call you 'Rabbi.' You have only 1 teacher, and you are all brothers.

**9** Do not call any person on earth 'father.' You have only 1 Father, and he is in heaven.

**10** Do not let people call you 'master.' You have only 1 master, the Christ.

**11** The greatest person among you must be your servant.

**12** God will make low the person who makes himself important. God will make important the person who makes himself humble.

**13** It will be very bad for you, teachers of the law and Pharisees! You are hypocrites! You close the door of the kingdom of heaven in front of people. You do not go in, and you stop the people who try to go in.

**14** It will be very bad for you, teachers of the law and Pharisees! You are hypocrites! You take the houses of widows. Then you say long prayers so that people will think that you are good. Thus God will punish you more.

**15** It will be very bad for you, teachers of the law and Pharisees! You are hypocrites! You travel across sea and land to make 1 person follow your religion. Then you make that person 2 times more ready for hell than you are.

**16** It will be very bad for you, blind guides! You say, 'If a person makes an oath by the temple, the oath is nothing. But if a person makes an oath by the gold of the temple, he must do what he promised.'

**17** You are foolish and blind! Which is more important, the gold or the temple? The temple makes the gold holy.

**18** You also say, 'If a person makes an oath by the altar, the oath is nothing. But if a person makes an oath by the gift on the altar, he must do what he promised.'

**19** You are blind! Which is more important, the gift or the altar? The altar makes the gift holy.

**20** Thus a person who makes an oath by the altar makes an oath by the altar and by all the things on it.

**21** A person who makes an oath by the temple makes an oath by the temple and by God, who lives in it.

**22** A person who makes an oath by heaven makes an oath by the throne of God and by God, who sits on it.

**23** It will be very bad for you, teachers of the law and Pharisees! You are hypocrites! You give God 1 tenth of your mint, dill, and cumin. But you do not obey the more important parts of the law. You do not do justice, show mercy, or stay faithful. You must do these things, and you must not forget the other things.

**24** You blind guides! You remove a small insect from your drink, but you swallow a camel.

**25** It will be very bad for you, teachers of the law and Pharisees! You are hypocrites! You clean the outside of the cup and the plate. But inside, they are full of things that you got by greed and by force.

**26** You blind Pharisee! First clean the inside of the cup and the plate. Then the outside will also be clean.

**27** It will be very bad for you, teachers of the law and Pharisees! You are hypocrites! You are like graves that people paint white. The outside of the graves looks beautiful. But inside they are full of the bones of dead people and of all things that are unclean.

**28** You are the same. On the outside, you look good to people. But inside, you are full of hypocrisy and sin.

**29** It will be very bad for you, teachers of the law and Pharisees! You are hypocrites! You build tombs for the prophets. You make beautiful the graves of good people.

**30** You say, 'If we lived at the time of our ancestors, we would not help them to kill the prophets.'

**31** Thus you show that you are the descendants of the people who killed the prophets.

**32** Thus go on and complete the sins that your ancestors started!

**33** You are snakes! You are like a family of poisonous snakes! You will not escape the punishment of hell.

**34** Thus I will send to you prophets, wise men, and teachers. You will kill some of them and nail some of them to crosses. You will hit some of them with whips in your synagogues. You will chase them from town to town.

**35** Thus you will be guilty for the deaths of all the good people that people killed on earth. You will be guilty from the death of Abel, who was good, to the death of Zechariah son of Berekiah. You killed Zechariah between the temple and the altar.

**36** I tell you the truth, God will punish the people of this time for all these things.

**37** Jerusalem, Jerusalem! You kill the prophets. You throw stones at the people whom God sends to you. Many times I wanted to bring your children together, as a chicken brings her baby chickens under her wings. But you did not let me.

**38** Look, your house will be left empty.

**39** I tell you, you will not see me again until you say, 'God blesses the man who comes in the name of the Lord.'"

## Chapter 24

**1** Jesus went out of the temple area and went away. His disciples came to him to show him the buildings of the temple.

**2** Jesus said to them, "You see all these buildings. I tell you the truth, not one stone will stay on top of another stone. People will throw down every stone."

**3** Later Jesus sat on the Mount of Olives. The disciples came to him when he was alone. They said, "Tell us, when will these things occur? What will be the sign that you will come again and that the world will end?"

**4** Jesus answered them, "Be careful that no person deceives you.

**5** Many people will come and use my name. They will say, 'I am the Christ.' They will deceive many people.

**6** You will hear about wars and news of wars. Do not be afraid. These things must occur, but the end will not come at that time.

**7** Nations will fight against nations, and kingdoms will fight against kingdoms. There will be no food, there will be diseases, and there will be earthquakes in many places.

**8** All these things are only the start of the pains, like the first pains of a woman who gives birth.

**9** Then people will arrest you, hurt you, and kill you. All nations will hate you because you follow me.

**10** At that time many people will stop their belief. They will turn against each other and hate each other.

**11** Many false prophets will come and deceive many people.

**12** There will be more and more evil. Thus the love of many people will become cold.

**13** But the person who stays faithful until the end will be saved.

**14** People will preach this good news of the kingdom in all the world. All nations will hear it. Then the end will come.

**15** The prophet Daniel spoke about the terrible thing that destroys. You will see it stand in the holy place. The person who reads this must understand it.

**16** At that time the people in Judea must run away to the mountains.

**17** A person who is on the roof of his house must not go down to take things from his house.

**18** A person who is in the field must not go back to get his coat.

**19** It will be very bad at that time for women who are pregnant and for women who feed babies!

**20** Pray that you do not have to run away in the winter or on the Sabbath.

**21** At that time there will be very great trouble. Such trouble did not occur since the start of the world until now. Such trouble will never occur again.

**22** If God did not make that time shorter, no person would live. But God will make that time shorter for the people that he chose.

**23** At that time a person may say to you, 'Look, the Christ is here!' or 'He is there!' Do not believe him.

**24** False Christs and false prophets will come. They will do great signs and miracles. If possible, they will deceive even the people that God chose.

**25** Look, I tell you these things before they occur.

**26** Thus if people say to you, 'Look, the Christ is in the desert,' do not go out there. If they say, 'Look, he is in the inner rooms,' do not believe them.

**27** Lightning comes from the east and shines to the west. The coming of the Son of Man will be the same.

**28** Vultures come together at the place where there is a dead body.

**29** Immediately after the trouble of that time, the sun will become dark. The moon will not give its light. The stars will fall from the sky. God will shake the powers in the sky.

**30** Then the sign of the Son of Man will appear in the sky. Then all the people of the earth will cry with sorrow. They will see the Son of Man come on the clouds of the sky with power and great glory.

**31** He will send his angels with a loud sound of a trumpet. They will bring together the people that he chose from all places on the earth. They will bring them from one end of the sky to the other end.

**32** Learn a lesson from the fig tree. When its branches become soft and the leaves grow, you know that summer is near.

**33** In the same way, when you see all these things, you know that the time is near. It is very near.

**34** I tell you the truth, the people of this time will not all die before all these things occur.

**35** Heaven and earth will go away, but my words will never go away.

**36** No person knows the day or the hour when these things will occur. Even the angels in heaven do not know. Only my Father knows.

**37** The coming of the Son of Man will be like the time of Noah.

**38** Before the flood, people ate and drank. They married and gave their daughters in marriage. They did these things until the day when Noah went into the ship (ark).

**39** They did not know what would occur until the flood came and killed them all. The coming of the Son of Man will be the same.

**40** At that time 2 men will be in the field. God will take one man, and he will leave the other man.

**41** 2 women will grind grain together. God will take one woman, and he will leave the other woman.

**42** Thus be ready and watch, because you do not know the day when your Lord will come.

**43** You know this: if the owner of a house knew the time of night when a thief would come, he would stay awake. He would not let the thief break into his house.

**44** Thus you also must be ready. The Son of Man will come at a time when you do not think that he will come.

**45** Who is the faithful and wise servant? His master gives him the authority over the other servants. He gives them their food at the correct time.

**46** That servant will be very happy when his master comes back and finds him at his work.

**47** I tell you the truth, the master will give him the authority over all that the master has.

**48** But an evil servant may say to himself, 'My master will not come back soon.'

**49** Then he will start to hit the other servants. He will eat and drink with people who are drunk.

**50** Then the master of that servant will come on a day when the servant does not expect him. He will come at a time that the servant does not know.

**51** The master will punish him very much. He will send him to the place where the hypocrites go. There people will cry and grind their teeth in pain."

**17** In the same way, the servant who received 2 bags got 2 more bags.

**68** They said, "Christ, prophesy to us! Tell us who hit you!"

**69** Peter sat outside in the courtyard. A servant girl came to him. She said, "You also were with Jesus of Galilee."

**70** But Peter said in front of all of them, "I do not know what you talk about."

**71** Peter went out to the gate. Another servant girl saw him. She said to the people there, "This man was also with Jesus of Nazareth."

**72** Again Peter said that this was not true. He made a solemn promise (oath) and said, "I do not know the man."

**73** After a short time, the people near him came to Peter. They said, "Surely you are also one of them. Your accent shows it."

**74** Then Peter started to curse and to make an oath. He said, "I do not know the man!" Immediately a rooster called.

**75** Then Peter remembered the words of Jesus: "Before the rooster calls, you will say 3 times that you do not know me." Peter went outside and cried bitterly.

## Chapter 27

**1** In the early morning, all the chief priests and the elders of the people made a plan to kill Jesus.

**2** They tied him and took him away. They gave him to Pontius Pilate, the governor.

**3** Judas, the man who gave Jesus to his enemies, saw that they condemned Jesus. Then Judas felt very sorry. He took the 30 silver coins back to the chief priests and the elders.

**4** He said, "I sinned. I gave an innocent man to death." They said, "That is not our problem. That is your problem."

**5** Judas threw the silver coins into the temple and went away. Then he went and hanged himself.

**6** The chief priests took the silver coins. They said, "The law does not let us put this money into the temple treasury. This money paid for the death of a man."

**7** They agreed on a plan. With the money, they bought the field of the potter. They used the field as a place to bury foreigners.

**8** Thus people call that field "The Field of Blood" to this day.

**9** Thus the words of the prophet Jeremiah came true. He said, "They took the 30 silver coins. That was the price that the people of Israel put on him.

**10** They used the coins to buy the field of the potter, as the Lord told me."

**11** Jesus stood in front of the governor. The governor asked him, "Are you the King of the Jews?" Jesus said to him, "You say it."

**12** The chief priests and the elders accused him. But he did not answer.

**13** Then Pilate said to him, "Do you not hear all the things that they say against you?"

**14** But Jesus did not answer even 1 word. The governor was very surprised.

**15** At each Passover festival, the governor usually released 1 prisoner to the people. The people chose the prisoner.

**16** At that time there was a well-known prisoner. His name was Barabbas.

**17** The crowd came together. Pilate asked them, "Which man do you want me to release to you? Barabbas, or Jesus who is called Christ?"

**18** Pilate knew that the leaders gave Jesus to him because they were jealous.

**19** Pilate sat on the seat of the judge. While he sat there, his wife sent a message to him. She said, "Do not do anything to that innocent man. Today I suffered much in a dream because of him."

**20** But the chief priests and the elders persuaded the crowd. They told the crowd to ask for Barabbas and to cause the death of Jesus.

**21** The governor asked them, "Which of the 2 men do you want me to release to you?" They said, "Barabbas."

**22** Pilate said to them, "Then what must I do with Jesus who is called Christ?" They all said, "Crucify him!"

**23** The governor asked, "Why? What wrong thing did he do?" But they shouted louder, "Crucify him!"

**24** Pilate saw that he could not stop the crowd. He saw that a riot started. Thus he took water and washed his hands in front of the crowd. He said, "I am not guilty of the death of this man. That is your responsibility."

**25** All the people said, "We and our children will be responsible for his death."

**26** Then Pilate released Barabbas to them. He ordered the soldiers to hit Jesus with whips. Then he gave Jesus to them to crucify.

**27** Then the soldiers of the governor took Jesus into the palace of the governor. They brought all the soldiers together around him.

**28** They took off his clothes and put a red robe on him.

**29** They made a crown from thorns and put it on his head. They put a stick in his right hand. Then they went down on their knees in front of him. They laughed at him and said, "Greetings, King of the Jews!"

**30** They spat on him. They took the stick and hit him on the head with it.

**31** After they laughed at him, they took the robe off him. They put his own clothes on him. Then they took him away to crucify him.

**32** As they went out, they found a man from Cyrene. His name was Simon. The soldiers made Simon carry the cross of Jesus.

**33** They came to a place with the name Golgotha. This name means "The Place of the Skull."

**34** They gave Jesus wine with a bitter liquid in it. He tasted it, but he did not drink it.

**35** They crucified him. Then they divided his clothes among them. They used lots (a type of dice) to make the decision. Thus the words of the prophet came true: "They divided my clothes among them. They used lots for my robe."

**36** Then they sat down and watched him there.

**37** Above his head they put a sign with the charge against him. The sign said, "THIS IS JESUS, THE KING OF THE JEWS."

**38** The soldiers also crucified 2 thieves with Jesus. One thief was on his right side. The other thief was on his left side.

**39** The people who went past him insulted him. They moved their heads to show that they hated him.

**40** They said, "You said that you can destroy the temple and build it again in 3 days. Save yourself! If you are the Son of God, come down from the cross!"

**41** The chief priests, the teachers of the law, and the elders also laughed at him. They said,

**42** "He saved other people, but he cannot save himself! He is the King of Israel! Let him come down from the cross now. Then we will believe him.

**43** He trusts in God. If God wants him, let God save him now. He said, 'I am the Son of God.'"

**44** The thieves on the crosses near him also insulted him in the same way.

**45** From 12 noon until 3 o'clock, there was darkness over all the land.

**46** At about 3 o'clock, Jesus shouted with a loud voice, "Eli, Eli, lama sabachthani?" This means, "My God, my God, why did you leave me?"

**47** Some of the people there heard this. They said, "This man calls for Elijah."

**48** Immediately one of them ran and got a sponge. He filled the sponge with sour wine and put it on a stick. He gave it to Jesus to drink.

**49** The other people said, "Wait. We will see if Elijah comes to save him."

**50** Jesus shouted again with a loud voice. Then he died.

**51** At that time, the curtain of the temple tore into 2 parts, from the top to the bottom. The earth shook, and the rocks broke.

**52** The tombs opened. Many holy people of God who were dead came back to life.

**53** They came out of the tombs after Jesus came back to life. They went into the holy city, Jerusalem. Many people saw them.

**54** The army officer (centurion) and the soldiers with him guarded Jesus. They saw the earthquake and all the things that occurred. They were very afraid. They said, "Truly this man was the Son of God!"

**55** Many women were there. They watched from a distance. These women followed Jesus from Galilee to help him.

**56** Mary Magdalene was one of these women. Mary, the mother of James and Joses, was also there. The mother of the sons of Zebedee was also there.

**57** In the evening, a rich man from Arimathea came. His name was Joseph. He was also a disciple of Jesus.

**58** Joseph went to Pilate and asked for the body of Jesus. Pilate ordered the soldiers to give the body to Joseph.

**59** Joseph took the body and put a clean linen cloth around it.

**60** He put the body in his own new tomb. Workers cut this tomb into the rock. Joseph rolled a large stone in front of the door of the tomb. Then he went away.

**61** Mary Magdalene and the other Mary were there. They sat across from the tomb.

**62** The next day was after the day of preparation. On that day, the chief priests and the Pharisees went together to Pilate.

**63** They said, "Sir, we remember what that liar said while he was alive. He said, 'After 3 days I will come back to life.'

**64** Thus order your soldiers to guard the tomb until the third day. If you do not, his disciples possibly will come at night and steal the body. Then they will tell the people, 'He came back to life from the dead.' This last lie will be worse than the first lie."

**65** Pilate said to them, "Take some guards. Go and make the tomb as safe as you can."

**66** Thus they went and made the tomb safe. They put a seal on the stone, and they put guards there.

## Chapter 28

**1** After the Sabbath, the first day of the week started. At dawn, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary went to look at the tomb.

**2** Suddenly there was a strong earthquake. An angel of the Lord came down from heaven. He went to the tomb and rolled the stone away from the door. Then he sat on the stone.

**3** His face was bright like lightning. His clothes were white like snow.

**4** The guards were so afraid of him that they shook. They became like dead men.

**5** The angel said to the women, "Do not be afraid. I know that you look for Jesus, who was crucified.

**6** He is not here. He came back to life, as he said. Come and see the place where the Lord was.

**7** Then go quickly and tell his disciples, 'He came back to life from the dead. He will go to Galilee before you. You will see him there.' Remember what I told you."

**8** The women went away from the tomb quickly. They were afraid, but they also had much joy. They ran to tell his disciples.

**9** Suddenly Jesus met them. He said, "Greetings." The women came to him, held his feet, and worshiped him.

**10** Then Jesus said to them, "Do not be afraid. Go and tell my brothers to go to Galilee. They will see me there."

**11** While the women went, some of the guards went into the city. They told the chief priests all the things that occurred.

**12** The chief priests met with the elders and made a plan. They gave a large quantity of money to the soldiers.

**13** They said to the soldiers, "Tell the people, 'His disciples came at night and stole his body while we slept.'

**14** If the governor hears about this, we will persuade him. We will keep you safe from trouble."

**15** Thus the soldiers took the money and did what the leaders told them. The Jews tell this story frequently to this day.

**16** Then the 11 disciples went to Galilee. They went to the mountain where Jesus told them to go.

**17** They saw Jesus and worshiped him. But some of them were not sure.

**18** Jesus came to them and said, "God gave me all authority in heaven and on earth.

**19** Thus go and make disciples of all nations. Baptize them in the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit.

**20** Teach them to obey all the things that I told you to do. Remember, I am always with you, until the end of the world."
