# Ezekiel

## Chapter 1

**1** In the 30th year, on the 5th day of the 4th month, I was with the exiles near the Chebar River. The heavens opened, and I saw visions from God.

**2** It was the 5th day of the month, in the 5th year of the exile of King Jehoiachin.

**3** The LORD spoke his word to Ezekiel the priest, the son of Buzi. This occurred in the land of the Chaldeans, near the Chebar River. There the power of the LORD came on Ezekiel.

**4** I looked and saw a storm wind that came from the north. I saw a large cloud with fire that flashed in it. A bright light was around the cloud. In the center of the fire there was something that shone like bright metal.

**5** In the center of the fire there were shapes like 4 creatures that were alive. The creatures had the shape of a man.

**6** Each creature had 4 faces and 4 wings.

**7** Their legs were straight. Their feet were like the feet of a calf. They shone like polished bronze.

**8** Under their wings on their 4 sides, the creatures had the hands of a man. All 4 creatures had faces and wings.

**9** The wings of each creature touched the wings of the other creatures. The creatures did not turn when they moved. Each creature went straight forward.

**10** Each of the 4 creatures had the face of a man at the front. Each had the face of a lion on the right side. Each had the face of an ox on the left side. Each also had the face of an eagle.

**11** These were their faces. Their wings spread out above them. Each creature had 2 wings that touched the wings of the creatures next to it. Its 2 other wings covered its body.

**12** Each creature went straight forward. They went to the place where the spirit went. They did not turn when they moved.

**13** The creatures looked like coals of fire that burned, or like torches. Fire moved back and forth among the creatures. The fire was bright, and flashes of light came out of the fire.

**14** The creatures moved quickly forward and back like flashes of light.

**15** While I looked at the creatures, I saw a wheel on the ground next to each creature. There were 4 wheels, one for each creature.

**16** The wheels shone like beryl, a yellow stone. All 4 wheels looked the same. Each wheel looked like a wheel inside another wheel.

**17** When the wheels moved, they went in any of the 4 directions. They did not turn when they moved.

**18** The rims of the wheels were very high and terrible. The rims of all 4 wheels were full of eyes all around.

**19** When the creatures moved, the wheels moved next to them. When the creatures went up from the ground, the wheels also went up.

**20** The creatures went to the place where the spirit went. The wheels went up with the creatures, because the spirit of the creatures was in the wheels.

**21** When the creatures moved, the wheels moved. When the creatures stopped, the wheels stopped. When the creatures went up from the ground, the wheels went up with them. The spirit of the creatures was in the wheels.

**22** Above the heads of the creatures there was a shape like a dome. The dome shone like ice and caused fear. The dome spread out above their heads.

**23** Under the dome, the wings of each creature spread straight out toward the other creatures. Each creature also had 2 wings that covered its body.

**24** When the creatures moved, I heard the noise of their wings. The noise was like the noise of a large flood. It was like the voice of the Almighty. It was like the noise of an army. When the creatures stopped, they lowered their wings.

**25** When the creatures stopped and lowered their wings, a voice came from above the dome over their heads.

**26** Above the dome over their heads there was a shape like a throne. The throne looked like sapphire. On the throne there was a shape like a man.

**27** From his waist up, he looked like bright metal with fire all around inside it. From his waist down, he looked like fire. A bright light was all around him.

**28** The light around him looked like a rainbow in the clouds on a day of rain. This was the appearance of the glory of the LORD. When I saw it, I fell down with my face to the ground. Then I heard a voice that spoke.

## Chapter 2

**1** He said to me, "Son of man, stand up. I will speak to you."

**2** While he spoke to me, the Spirit came into me. The Spirit put me on my feet. Then I heard the voice that spoke to me.

**3** He said to me, "Son of man, I send you to the people of Israel. They are a nation that rebels against me. They and their ancestors sinned against me until this day.

**4** The people are stubborn, and their hearts are hard. I send you to them. You will say to them, 'The Lord GOD says this.'

**5** Possibly they will listen, and possibly they will not listen, because they are a people who rebel. But they will know that a prophet was among them.

**6** Son of man, do not be afraid of them or their words. Thorns and sharp plants are around you, and you live among scorpions. But do not be afraid of their words. Do not be afraid of their angry faces. They are a people who rebel.

**7** You must speak my words to them. Possibly they will listen, and possibly they will not listen. They are a people who rebel very much.

**8** But you, son of man, listen to what I tell you. Do not rebel like those people who rebel. Open your mouth and eat what I give you."

**9** Then I looked and saw a hand that reached out to me. In the hand there was a scroll.

**10** He opened the scroll in front of me. There were words on the front and on the back. The words were about sad songs, sorrow, and trouble.

## Chapter 3

**1** He said to me, "Son of man, eat what you find. Eat this scroll. Then go and speak to the people of Israel."

**2** Thus I opened my mouth, and he gave me the scroll to eat.

**3** He said to me, "Son of man, eat this scroll that I give you. Fill your stomach with it." Thus I ate the scroll. It was as sweet as honey in my mouth.

**4** He said to me, "Son of man, go to the people of Israel. Speak my words to them.

**5** I do not send you to a people with a strange language that is difficult. I send you to the people of Israel.

**6** I do not send you to many nations with strange and difficult languages. You cannot understand their words. If I sent you to those nations, they would listen to you.

**7** But the people of Israel will not listen to you, because they do not want to listen to me. All the people of Israel are stubborn, and their hearts are hard.

**8** But I made you as stubborn as they are. I made your face as hard as their faces.

**9** I made your forehead as hard as a diamond, harder than flint. Do not be afraid of them. Do not be afraid of their angry faces. They are a people who rebel."

**10** He also said to me, "Son of man, listen carefully to all the words that I tell you. Keep them in your heart.

**11** Go to your people who are in exile. Speak to them. Tell them, 'The Lord GOD says this.' Possibly they will listen, and possibly they will not listen."

**12** Then the Spirit lifted me up. Behind me I heard the sound of a loud noise. The voice said, "Praise the glory of the LORD in his place!"

**13** I heard the noise of the wings of the creatures as they touched each other. I heard the noise of the wheels next to them. It was the sound of a loud noise.

**14** The Spirit lifted me up and took me away. I went with bitterness and anger in my spirit. The power of the LORD was strong on me.

**15** I came to the exiles at Tel Abib, who lived near the Chebar River. I sat among them for 7 days. I was very shocked.

**16** At the end of the 7 days, the LORD spoke his word to me. He said,

**17** "Son of man, I made you a guard for the people of Israel. When you hear a word from me, you must warn them for me.

**18** I can say to an evil man, 'You will surely die.' If you do not warn him, he will die because of his sin. If you do not tell him to stop his evil ways to save his life, he will die. But I will make you responsible for his death.

**19** But if you warn the evil man, and he does not stop his evil ways, he will die because of his sin. But you will save your own life.

**20** A good man can stop his good life and do evil. Then I will put an obstacle in front of him, and he will die. If you did not warn him, he will die because of his sin. People will not remember the good things that he did. But I will make you responsible for his death.

**21** But if you warn the good man not to sin, and he does not sin, he will surely live because he obeyed you. And you will save your own life."

**22** The power of the LORD came on me there. He said to me, "Get up and go out to the plain. I will speak to you there."

**23** Thus I got up and went out to the plain. The glory of the LORD was there. It was like the glory that I saw near the Chebar River. I fell down with my face to the ground.

**24** Then the Spirit came into me and put me on my feet. He spoke to me and said, "Go and lock yourself inside your house.

**25** Son of man, the people will tie you with ropes. Thus you cannot go out among them.

**26** I will make your tongue stick to the top of your mouth. You will not be able to speak. You will not be able to correct them, because they are a people who rebel.

**27** But when I speak to you, I will open your mouth. Then you will say to them, 'The Lord GOD says this.' Some people will listen. Some people will refuse to listen. They are a people who rebel."

## Chapter 4

**1** "Son of man, take a clay brick. Put it in front of you. Draw a picture of the city of Jerusalem on the brick.

**2** Show an army that attacks the city. Build a siege wall against it. Build a ramp of dirt against it. Put army camps around it. Put heavy logs around it to break the walls.

**3** Then take an iron plate. Put it as a wall of iron between you and the city. Turn your face toward the city. The city will be under attack, and you will attack it. This will be a sign to the people of Israel.

**4** Then lie down on your left side. Put the sin of the people of Israel on yourself. You will carry their sin for the number of days that you lie on your side.

**5** I give you 1 day for each year of their sin. Thus you will carry the sin of the people of Israel for 390 days.

**6** When you finish these days, lie down again on your right side. Then you will carry the sin of the people of Judah for 40 days. I give you 1 day for each year.

**7** Turn your face toward the attack on Jerusalem. Show your bare arm. Speak as a prophet against the city.

**8** I will tie you with ropes. Thus you cannot turn from one side to the other side until you finish the days of your attack.

**9** Take wheat, barley, beans, lentils, millet, and spelt. Put them in one jar and make bread from them. You will eat this bread for the 390 days that you lie on your side.

**10** You must weigh the food that you eat. Eat 20 shekels of food each day. Eat it at set times.

**11** You must also measure the water that you drink. Drink 1/6 of a hin of water each day. Drink it at set times.

**12** Eat the food as you eat barley cakes. Bake it over a fire of dried human dung while the people watch."

**13** The LORD said, "Thus the people of Israel will eat unclean food among the nations. I will send them to these nations."

**14** Then I said, "Oh, Lord GOD! I never made myself unclean. From the time that I was young until now, I never ate an animal that died naturally. I never ate an animal that wild animals killed. No unclean meat ever went into my mouth."

**15** Then he said to me, "Look, I will let you use cow dung instead of human dung. You can bake your bread over the cow dung."

**16** He also said to me, "Son of man, I will stop the supply of bread in Jerusalem. The people will weigh their bread and eat it with worry. They will measure their water and drink it with fear.

**17** They will not have bread or water. They will look at each other with shock. They will become weak and die because of their sin."

## Chapter 5

**1** "Son of man, take a sharp sword. Use it as a razor. Shave your head and your beard. Then take scales and divide the hair into 3 parts.

**2** When the days of the attack on the city end, burn 1 third of the hair in the center of the city. Take another third of the hair and cut it with the sword around the city. Throw the last third into the wind. I will chase them with a sword.

**3** Take a small number of hairs and tie them in the edge of your clothes.

**4** Then take some of these hairs again and throw them into the fire. Burn them. A fire will come from there against all the people of Israel.

**5** The Lord GOD says this: "This is Jerusalem. I put her in the center of the nations, with countries around her.

**6** But she rebelled against my laws and my rules. She did more evil than the nations and the countries around her. Her people rejected my laws. They did not obey my rules.

**7** Thus the Lord GOD says this: You caused more trouble than the nations around you. You did not obey my rules. You did not keep my laws. You did not even obey the laws of the nations around you.

**8** Thus the Lord GOD says this: I am against you, Jerusalem. I will punish you while the nations watch.

**9** Because of all your detestable idols, I will do to you what I never did before. I will never do it again.

**10** Thus in Jerusalem fathers will eat their sons, and sons will eat their fathers. I will punish you. I will throw all your people who stay alive to the winds.

**11** As surely as I live, says the Lord GOD, I will cut you down. You made my holy temple unclean with all your detestable idols and evil things. I will not spare you. I will have no pity.

**12** One third of your people will die from disease and famine in the city. One third will die by the sword around the city. I will throw one third to the winds and chase them with a sword.

**13** Then my anger will be complete. I will pour out my anger on them, and then I will be satisfied. When I complete my anger against them, they will know that I, the LORD, spoke because of my strong love.

**14** I will make you a ruin. The nations around you will insult you. All the people who go past will see this.

**15** You will become a thing that the nations around you insult and laugh at. You will be an example that teaches and frightens them. This will occur when I punish you with anger and strong correction. I, the LORD, said it.

**16** I will send against you the terrible arrows of famine that destroy. I will send them to destroy you. I will make the famine worse. I will stop your supply of bread.

**17** I will send famine and wild animals against you. They will kill your children. Disease and violent death will go through your land. I will bring the sword against you. I, the LORD, said it."

## Chapter 6

**1** The LORD spoke his word to me. He said,

**2** "Son of man, turn your face toward the mountains of Israel. Speak as a prophet against them.

**3** Say, 'Mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord GOD. The Lord GOD says this to the mountains, the hills, the ravines, and the valleys: I will bring a sword against you. I will destroy your high places of worship.

**4** Your altars will become ruins. I will break your incense altars. I will throw down your dead people in front of your idols.

**5** I will put the dead bodies of the people of Israel in front of their idols. I will throw your bones around your altars.

**6** In all the places where you live, your towns will become ruins. Your high places will be empty. Your altars will be ruins. Your idols will be broken and destroyed. Your incense altars will be cut down. All that you made will be destroyed.

**7** Your people will die among you. Then you will know that I am the LORD.

**8** But I will let some of you stay alive. Some of you will escape the sword. I will send you to live among other nations and countries.

**9** The people who escape will remember me in the nations where they are prisoners. They will remember how I felt pain because of their unfaithful hearts. They left me and chased their idols. Then they will hate themselves for all the evil and detestable things that they did.

**10** They will know that I am the LORD. They will know that my threat to bring this disaster on them was not empty.

**11** The Lord GOD says this: Clap your hands and stamp your foot. Say, "Oh, how terrible are all the evil and detestable acts of the people of Israel!" They will die by the sword, by famine, and by disease.

**12** People who are far away will die from disease. People who are near will die by the sword. People who stay in the city under attack will die from famine. Thus I will complete my anger against them.

**13** Their dead people will lie among their idols around their altars. They will lie on each high hill, on all the tops of the mountains, and under each green tree and each thick oak. In these places they burned incense to all their idols. Then you will know that I am the LORD.

**14** I will put out my hand against them. I will make the land empty, from the desert to Diblah. I will make all the places where they live empty. Then they will know that I am the LORD.'"

## Chapter 7

**1** The LORD spoke his word to me again. He said,

**2** "Son of man, the Lord GOD says this to the land of Israel: The end comes! The end comes on all 4 corners of the land.

**3** Now the end comes on you. I will send my anger against you. I will judge you for your actions. I will punish you for all your detestable acts.

**4** I will not spare you. I will have no pity. I will punish you for your actions and for your detestable acts among you. Then you will know that I am the LORD.

**5** The Lord GOD says this: Disaster comes! One disaster after another disaster comes!

**6** The end comes! The end comes! It is ready to attack you. It comes!

**7** People of the land, your destruction comes. The time comes. The day of trouble is near. There will be no shouts of joy on the mountains.

**8** Soon I will pour out my anger on you. I will complete my anger against you. I will judge you for your actions. I will punish you for all your detestable acts.

**9** I will not spare you. I will have no pity. I will punish you for your actions and for your detestable acts among you. Then you will know that I, the LORD, am the one who punishes you.

**10** Look, the day comes! Your destruction comes! Violence grows like a plant. Pride grows like a flower.

**11** Violence grows into a rod to punish evil. None of the people will stay alive. None of their crowds and none of their wealth will stay. Nothing of value will stay among them.

**12** The time comes. The day is near. The buyer must not be glad. The seller must not be sad. My anger is on all the crowds of people.

**13** The seller will not get back what he sold, while both of them are alive. My vision is for all the crowd, and it will not change. No person will save his life because of his sin.

**14** The people blow the trumpet and make all things ready. But no person goes to the battle, because my anger is on all the crowd.

**15** Outside the city, there is the sword. Inside the city, there are disease and famine. People in the field will die by the sword. Famine and disease will kill the people in the city.

**16** Some people will escape to the mountains. They will be like doves of the valleys. Each person will cry in sorrow for his sin.

**17** All hands will become weak. All knees will become as weak as water.

**18** They will wear sackcloth, and terror will cover them. Shame will be on all their faces. All their heads will be shaved.

**19** They will throw their silver into the streets. Their gold will be like an unclean thing. Their silver and gold cannot save them on the day of the anger of the LORD. Their money cannot satisfy their hunger or fill their stomachs. Their money caused them to sin.

**20** They were proud of their beautiful jewelry. They used it to make their detestable idols. Thus I will make their jewelry an unclean thing to them.

**21** I will give their wealth to foreigners. Evil people of the earth will take it and make it unclean.

**22** I will turn my face away from my people. Robbers will go into my holy place and make it unclean.

**23** Make chains, because the land is full of murder and the city is full of violence.

**24** I will bring the most evil of the nations. They will take the houses of my people. I will stop the pride of the strong. Their holy places will become unclean.

**25** Terror comes! The people will look for peace, but they will find no peace.

**26** Disaster after disaster will come. One bad report after another bad report will come. The people will look for a vision from the prophet. But the priests will not teach the law, and the leaders will not give advice.

**27** The king will cry in sorrow. The prince will be full of despair. The hands of the people of the land will shake with fear. I will do to them what they did. I will judge them as they judged other people. Then they will know that I am the LORD."

## Chapter 8

**1** In the 6th year, on the 5th day of the 6th month, I sat in my house. The leaders of Judah sat in front of me. There the power of the Lord GOD came on me.

**2** I looked and saw a shape like a man. From his waist down, he looked like fire. From his waist up, he was bright like polished metal.

**3** He put out a shape like a hand and took me by the hair of my head. The Spirit lifted me up between the earth and the sky. In visions from God he took me to Jerusalem. He took me to the entrance of the north gate of the inner court. There was an idol in that place. The idol caused God to become angry and jealous.

**4** The glory of the God of Israel was there. It was like the vision that I saw in the plain.

**5** Then he said to me, "Son of man, look toward the north." Thus I looked toward the north. North of the altar gate, at the entrance, I saw the idol that caused jealousy.

**6** He said to me, "Son of man, do you see what they do? The people of Israel do very detestable things here. These things push me away from my holy place. But turn again, and you will see things that are more detestable."

**7** Then he took me to the entrance of the court. I looked and saw a hole in the wall.

**8** He said to me, "Son of man, dig through the wall." Thus I dug through the wall and found a door.

**9** He said to me, "Go in and see the evil and detestable things that they do here."

**10** Thus I went in and looked. On all the walls I saw pictures of all types of animals that crawl and unclean animals. I also saw all the idols of the people of Israel.

**11** In front of these pictures stood 70 leaders of the people of Israel. Jaazaniah, the son of Shaphan, stood among them. Each man had a pan for incense in his hand. A thick cloud of the smoke of incense went up.

**12** He said to me, "Son of man, do you see what the leaders of Israel do in the dark? Each man is in the room of his own idol. They say, 'The LORD does not see us. The LORD left the land.'"

**13** He also said to me, "Turn again, and you will see them do things that are more detestable."

**14** Then he took me to the entrance of the north gate of the house of the LORD. I saw women who sat there. They cried for the god Tammuz.

**15** He said to me, "Son of man, do you see this? Turn again, and you will see things that are more detestable than these."

**16** Then he took me into the inner court of the house of the LORD. At the entrance of the temple, between the porch and the altar, there were about 25 men. Their backs were toward the temple of the LORD, and their faces were toward the east. They bowed down to the sun in the east.

**17** He said to me, "Son of man, do you see this? Are these detestable things that the people of Judah do here a small matter? They fill the land with violence. They make me angry again and again. Look, they put a branch to their nose to insult me.

**18** Thus I will act in my anger. I will not spare them. I will have no pity. They will shout in my ears with a loud voice, but I will not listen to them."

## Chapter 9

**1** Then I heard him shout with a loud voice. He said, "Bring near the men who will punish the city. Each man must have a weapon in his hand to destroy."

**2** I saw 6 men come from the upper gate, which faces north. Each man had a weapon in his hand to kill. With them was a man who wore linen clothes. He had a writer's case at his side. They went in and stood next to the bronze altar.

**3** Then the glory of the God of Israel went up from above the cherub, an angel with wings. The glory moved to the entrance of the temple. The LORD called to the man who wore linen clothes and had the writer's case at his side.

**4** The LORD said to him, "Go through all the city of Jerusalem. Put a mark on the foreheads of the men who are sad and cry about all the detestable things that people do in the city."

**5** I heard him say to the other men, "Follow him through the city and kill. Do not spare any person. Have no pity.

**6** Kill old men, young men, young women, little children, and women. But do not touch any person who has the mark. Start at my holy temple." Thus they started with the leaders who were in front of the temple.

**7** He said to them, "Make the temple unclean. Fill the courts with dead bodies. Go!" Thus they went out and killed the people in the city.

**8** While they killed the people, I was alone. I fell down with my face to the ground. I cried out, "Oh, Lord GOD! Will you destroy all the people of Israel who stay alive, when you pour out your anger on Jerusalem?"

**9** He said to me, "The sin of the people of Israel and Judah is very great. The land is full of murder. The city is full of injustice. They say, 'The LORD left the land. The LORD does not see.'

**10** Thus I will not spare them. I will have no pity. I will punish them for what they did."

**11** Then the man who wore linen clothes and had the writer's case at his side came back. He said, "I did all that you commanded me."

## Chapter 10

**1** Then I looked at the dome above the heads of the cherubim. Above them I saw a shape like a throne. It looked like a sapphire stone.

**2** The LORD said to the man who wore linen clothes, "Go in between the wheels under the cherubim. Fill your hands with coals of fire from between the cherubim. Throw the coals over the city." While I watched, the man went in.

**3** When the man went in, the cherubim stood on the south side of the temple. A cloud filled the inner court.

**4** Then the glory of the LORD went up from above the cherub. It moved to the entrance of the temple. The cloud filled the temple. The court was full of the bright light of the glory of the LORD.

**5** People could hear the sound of the wings of the cherubim in the outer court. It was like the voice of God Almighty when he speaks.

**6** The LORD commanded the man who wore linen clothes, "Take fire from between the wheels, from between the cherubim." Then the man went in and stood next to a wheel.

**7** One cherub put out his hand to the fire between the cherubim. He took some of the fire and put it into the hands of the man who wore linen clothes. The man took the fire and went out.

**8** The cherubim had the shape of the hands of a man under their wings.

**9** I looked and saw 4 wheels next to the cherubim, one wheel next to each cherub. The wheels shone like a beryl stone.

**10** All 4 wheels looked the same. Each wheel looked like a wheel inside another wheel.

**11** When the wheels moved, they went in any of the 4 directions. They did not turn when they moved. The wheels went in the direction that the head of the cherub faced. They did not turn when they moved.

**12** The whole bodies of the cherubim were full of eyes. Their backs, their hands, their wings, and their 4 wheels were full of eyes all around.

**13** I heard a voice that called the wheels "the whirlwind."

**14** Each cherub had 4 faces. The first face was the face of a cherub. The second face was the face of a man. The third face was the face of a lion. The fourth face was the face of an eagle.

**15** Then the cherubim went up. These were the creatures that I saw near the Chebar River.

**16** When the cherubim moved, the wheels moved next to them. When the cherubim spread their wings to go up from the ground, the wheels stayed next to them.

**17** When the cherubim stopped, the wheels stopped. When the cherubim went up, the wheels went up with them. The spirit of the creatures was in the wheels.

**18** Then the glory of the LORD went away from the entrance of the temple. It stopped above the cherubim.

**19** While I watched, the cherubim spread their wings and went up from the ground. The wheels went with them. They stopped at the entrance of the east gate of the house of the LORD. The glory of the God of Israel was above them.

**20** These were the creatures that I saw under the God of Israel near the Chebar River. I knew that they were cherubim.

**21** Each cherub had 4 faces and 4 wings. Under their wings there was a shape like the hands of a man.

**22** Their faces looked the same as the faces that I saw near the Chebar River. Each cherub went straight forward.

## Chapter 11

**1** Then the Spirit lifted me up and took me to the east gate of the house of the LORD. At the entrance of the gate there were 25 men. Among them I saw Jaazaniah, the son of Azzur, and Pelatiah, the son of Benaiah. These men were leaders of the people.

**2** The LORD said to me, "Son of man, these are the men who plan evil and give bad advice in this city.

**3** They say, 'Soon we will build houses again. This city is a pot, and we are the meat in it. The pot will keep us safe.'

**4** Thus speak as a prophet against them. Speak as a prophet, son of man."

**5** Then the Spirit of the LORD came on me. He told me to say, "The LORD says this: People of Israel, I know what you say. I know all your thoughts.

**6** You killed many people in this city. You filled its streets with dead bodies.

**7** Thus the Lord GOD says this: The people that you killed in the city are the meat. The city is the pot. But I will take you out of the city.

**8** You are afraid of the sword. Thus I will bring the sword against you, says the Lord GOD.

**9** I will take you out of the city. I will give you to foreigners. I will punish you.

**10** You will die by the sword. I will punish you at the border of Israel. Then you will know that I am the LORD.

**11** This city will not be a pot to protect you. You will not be the meat in it. I will punish you at the border of Israel.

**12** Then you will know that I am the LORD. You did not obey my rules or keep my laws. You followed the customs of the nations around you."

**13** While I spoke as a prophet, Pelatiah the son of Benaiah died. I fell down with my face to the ground. I cried with a loud voice, "Oh, Lord GOD! Will you destroy all the people of Israel who stay alive?"

**14** The LORD spoke his word to me again. He said,

**15** "Son of man, the people in Jerusalem speak about your brothers, your relatives, and all the people of Israel in exile. They say, 'Those people are far away from the LORD. The LORD gave this land to us.'

**7** Each guard room was 1 rod long and 1 rod wide. The walls between the guard rooms were 5 cubits thick. The threshold of the gate next to the porch, on the inner side, was 1 rod deep.

**8** He measured the porch of the gate on the inner side. It was 1 rod deep.

**9** He measured the porch of the gate. It was 8 cubits deep. Its posts were 2 cubits thick. The porch of the gate faced the temple.

**10** There were 3 guard rooms on each side of the east gate. All 3 had the same measurements. The posts on each side also had the same measurements.

**11** He measured the width of the entrance of the gate. It was 10 cubits. The length of the gate was 13 cubits.

**12** In front of each guard room there was a low wall 1 cubit high. The guard rooms were 6 cubits on each side.

**13** He measured the gate from the back wall of one guard room to the back wall of the opposite guard room. The distance was 25 cubits from one door to the opposite door.

**12** All types of fruit trees will grow on each bank of the river. Their leaves will not become dry, and their fruit will not stop. Each month they will give new fruit, because the water comes from the sanctuary. Their fruit will be food, and their leaves will be medicine."

**13** The Lord GOD says this: "These are the borders of the land. You will divide the land among the 12 tribes of Israel. Joseph will get 2 parts.

**14** Divide it equally among the tribes. I lifted my hand and promised to give the land to your ancestors. Thus this land will be your property.

**15** "This is the border of the land on the north side: It goes from the Mediterranean Sea along the road to Hethlon, and to the entrance of Zedad.

**16** It continues to Hamath, Berothah and Sibraim, which are between the border of Damascus and the border of Hamath. It continues to Hazer Hatticon, on the border of Hauran.

**17** Thus the border goes from the sea to Hazar Enan, on the border of Damascus. The border of Hamath is to the north. This is the north side.

**18** "On the east side, the border goes between Hauran and Damascus. It goes along the Jordan River between Gilead and the land of Israel. It goes to the Dead Sea and to Tamar. This is the east side.

**19** "On the south side, the border goes from Tamar to the water of Meribah Kadesh. Then it goes along the Brook of Egypt to the Mediterranean Sea. This is the south side.

**20** "On the west side, the Mediterranean Sea is the border. It goes to a point opposite the entrance of Hamath. This is the west side.

**21** "Divide this land among you by the tribes of Israel.

**22** Divide it by lot as property for yourselves and for the foreigners who live among you and have children among you. You must think of them as people who were born in Israel. They will get property with you among the tribes of Israel.

**23** In each tribe where a foreigner lives, give him his property, says the Lord GOD."

## Chapter 48

**1** These are the names of the tribes. The tribe of Dan will get 1 part of the land. The part of Dan is at the north end. Its border goes along the road to Hethlon, to Lebo Hamath, and to Hazar Enan. The border of Damascus is to the north, near Hamath. The part of Dan goes from the east side to the west side.

**2** The tribe of Asher will get 1 part. It is next to the border of Dan, from the east side to the west side.

**3** The tribe of Naphtali will get 1 part. It is next to the border of Asher, from the east side to the west side.

**4** The tribe of Manasseh will get 1 part. It is next to the border of Naphtali, from the east side to the west side.

**5** The tribe of Ephraim will get 1 part. It is next to the border of Manasseh, from the east side to the west side.

**6** The tribe of Reuben will get 1 part. It is next to the border of Ephraim, from the east side to the west side.

**7** The tribe of Judah will get 1 part. It is next to the border of Reuben, from the east side to the west side.

**8** Next to the border of Judah, from the east side to the west side, is the special part. You will give this special part to the LORD. It will be 25,000 reeds wide. Its length from east to west will be the same as the length of the other parts. The holy place (sanctuary) will be in the middle of the special part.

**9** The special part that you give to the LORD will be 25,000 reeds long and 10,000 reeds wide.

**10** This holy part will be for the priests. On the north side it will be 25,000 reeds long. On the west side it will be 10,000 reeds wide. On the east side it will be 10,000 reeds wide. On the south side it will be 25,000 reeds long. The sanctuary of the LORD will be in the middle of the holy part.

**11** This land will be for the priests from the family of Zadok. I made these priests holy. They obeyed my orders. When the people of Israel went away from me, these priests did not go away. But the Levites went away from me.

**12** The priests will have this special part of the land as a very holy part. It will be next to the land of the Levites.

**13** Next to the land of the priests, the Levites will have a part. It will be 25,000 reeds long and 10,000 reeds wide. All the length will be 25,000 reeds, and the width will be 10,000 reeds.

**14** The Levites must not sell this land. They must not exchange it. They must not give this best part of the land to other people. The land is holy to the LORD.

**15** 5,000 reeds of width will stay, next to the 25,000 reeds. This land will be for common use by the city. The people will build houses and open land there. The city will be in the middle of this land.

**16** These will be the measurements of the city. The north side will be 4,500 reeds. The south side will be 4,500 reeds. The east side will be 4,500 reeds. The west side will be 4,500 reeds.

**17** The open land around the city will be 250 reeds to the north, 250 reeds to the south, 250 reeds to the east, and 250 reeds to the west.

**18** Some land will stay along the length, next to the holy part. It will be 10,000 reeds to the east and 10,000 reeds to the west. The crops from this land will be food for the workers of the city.

**19** The workers of the city will come from all the tribes of Israel.

**20** All the special part will be 25,000 reeds by 25,000 reeds. You will give the holy part as a square, together with the land of the city.

**21** The land on the 2 sides of the holy part and of the city land will be for the prince. This land goes from the 25,000 reeds of the holy part to the east border. It also goes from the 25,000 reeds to the west border. These parts are next to the parts of the tribes, and they will be for the prince. The holy part and the sanctuary of the temple will be in the middle.

**22** The land of the Levites and the land of the city are in the middle of the land of the prince. The land of the prince will be between the border of Judah and the border of Benjamin.

**23** These are the parts for the other tribes. The tribe of Benjamin will get 1 part, from the east side to the west side.

**24** The tribe of Simeon will get 1 part. It is next to the border of Benjamin, from the east side to the west side.

**25** The tribe of Issachar will get 1 part. It is next to the border of Simeon, from the east side to the west side.

**26** The tribe of Zebulun will get 1 part. It is next to the border of Issachar, from the east side to the west side.

**27** The tribe of Gad will get 1 part. It is next to the border of Zebulun, from the east side to the west side.

**28** The south border of Gad will go from Tamar to the waters of Meribah Kadesh. Then it will go along the stream of Egypt to the Great Sea.

**29** This is the land that you will divide by lot among the tribes of Israel. These are their parts. The Lord GOD says this.

**30** These are the exits of the city. The north side will be 4,500 reeds long.

**31** The gates of the city will have the names of the tribes of Israel. There will be 3 gates on the north side. There will be 1 gate for Reuben, 1 gate for Judah, and 1 gate for Levi.

**32** The east side will be 4,500 reeds long, with 3 gates. There will be 1 gate for Joseph, 1 gate for Benjamin, and 1 gate for Dan.

**33** The south side will be 4,500 reeds long, with 3 gates. There will be 1 gate for Simeon, 1 gate for Issachar, and 1 gate for Zebulun.

**34** The west side will be 4,500 reeds long, with 3 gates. There will be 1 gate for Gad, 1 gate for Asher, and 1 gate for Naphtali.

**35** The distance around the city will be 18,000 reeds. From that day, the name of the city will be "The LORD Is There."
