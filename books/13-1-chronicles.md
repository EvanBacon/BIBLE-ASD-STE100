# 1 Chronicles

## Chapter 1

**1** Adam, Sheth, Enosh,

**2** Kenan, Mahalaleel, Jered,

**3** Henoch, Methuselah, Lamech,

**4** Noah, Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

**5** The sons of Japheth were Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras.

**6** The sons of Gomer were Ashchenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah.

**7** The sons of Javan were Elishah, Tarshish, Kittim, and Dodanim.

**8** The sons of Ham were Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan.

**9** The sons of Cush were Seba, Havilah, Sabta, Raamah, and Sabtecha. The sons of Raamah were Sheba and Dedan.

**10** Cush became the father of Nimrod. Nimrod became a strong man on the earth.

**11** Mizraim became the father of Ludim, Anamim, Lehabim, Naphtuhim,

**12** Pathrusim, Casluhim, and Caphthorim. The Philistines came from Casluhim.

**13** Canaan became the father of Zidon, his first son, and of Heth.

**14** He was also the father of the Jebusites, the Amorites, the Girgashites,

**15** the Hivites, the Arkites, the Sinites,

**16** the Arvadites, the Zemarites, and the Hamathites.

**17** The sons of Shem were Elam, Asshur, Arphaxad, Lud, Aram, Uz, Hul, Gether, and Meshech.

**18** Arphaxad became the father of Shelah. Shelah became the father of Eber.

**19** Eber had 2 sons. The name of one son was Peleg, because in his time the people of the earth became divided. The name of his brother was Joktan.

**20** Joktan became the father of Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, Jerah,

**21** Hadoram, Uzal, Diklah,

**22** Ebal, Abimael, Sheba,

**23** Ophir, Havilah, and Jobab. All these men were the sons of Joktan.

**24** Shem, Arphaxad, Shelah,

**25** Eber, Peleg, Reu,

**26** Serug, Nahor, Terah,

**27** and Abram. Abram is Abraham.

**28** The sons of Abraham were Isaac and Ishmael.

**29** These are their descendants. The first son of Ishmael was Nebaioth. Then came Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam,

**30** Mishma, Dumah, Massa, Hadad, Tema,

**31** Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah. These were the sons of Ishmael.

**32** Keturah was a secondary wife (concubine) of Abraham. Her sons were Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah. The sons of Jokshan were Sheba and Dedan.

**33** The sons of Midian were Ephah, Epher, Henoch, Abida, and Eldaah. All these were the descendants of Keturah.

**34** Abraham became the father of Isaac. The sons of Isaac were Esau and Israel.

**35** The sons of Esau were Eliphaz, Reuel, Jeush, Jaalam, and Korah.

**36** The sons of Eliphaz were Teman, Omar, Zephi, Gatam, Kenaz, Timna, and Amalek.

**37** The sons of Reuel were Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah.

**38** The sons of Seir were Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan.

**39** The sons of Lotan were Hori and Homam. Timna was the sister of Lotan.

**40** The sons of Shobal were Alian, Manahath, Ebal, Shephi, and Onam. The sons of Zibeon were Aiah and Anah.

**41** The son of Anah was Dishon. The sons of Dishon were Amram, Eshban, Ithran, and Cheran.

**42** The sons of Ezer were Bilhan, Zavan, and Jakan. The sons of Dishan were Uz and Aran.

**43** These are the kings who ruled in the land of Edom before a king ruled over the people of Israel. Bela the son of Beor was king. The name of his city was Dinhabah.

**44** When Bela died, Jobab the son of Zerah from Bozrah became king after him.

**45** When Jobab died, Husham from the land of the Temanites became king after him.

**46** When Husham died, Hadad the son of Bedad became king after him. Hadad defeated Midian in the country of Moab. The name of his city was Avith.

**47** When Hadad died, Samlah from Masrekah became king after him.

**48** When Samlah died, Shaul from Rehoboth by the river became king after him.

**49** When Shaul died, Baal-hanan the son of Achbor became king after him.

**50** When Baal-hanan died, Hadad became king after him. The name of his city was Pai. The name of his wife was Mehetabel. She was the daughter of Matred, the daughter of Mezahab.

**51** Then Hadad died. The chiefs of Edom were chief Timnah, chief Aliah, chief Jetheth,

**52** chief Aholibamah, chief Elah, chief Pinon,

**53** chief Kenaz, chief Teman, chief Mibzar,

**54** chief Magdiel, and chief Iram. These were the chiefs of Edom.

## Chapter 2

**1** These are the sons of Israel: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Zebulun,

**2** Dan, Joseph, Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher.

**3** The sons of Judah were Er, Onan, and Shelah. The mother of these 3 sons was the daughter of Shua, a Canaanite woman. Er, the first son of Judah, was evil in the sight of the LORD. Thus the LORD killed him.

**4** Tamar, the daughter-in-law of Judah, gave birth to Pharez and Zerah for him. Judah had 5 sons in total.

**5** The sons of Pharez were Hezron and Hamul.

**6** The sons of Zerah were Zimri, Ethan, Heman, Calcol, and Dara. They were 5 in total.

**7** The son of Carmi was Achar. Achar brought trouble on Israel because he took things that God told the people to destroy.

**8** The son of Ethan was Azariah.

**9** The sons of Hezron were Jerahmeel, Ram, and Chelubai.

**10** Ram became the father of Amminadab. Amminadab became the father of Nahshon, the leader of the people of Judah.

**11** Nahshon became the father of Salma. Salma became the father of Boaz.

**12** Boaz became the father of Obed. Obed became the father of Jesse.

**13** Jesse became the father of Eliab, his first son. His second son was Abinadab, and his third son was Shimma.

**14** His fourth son was Nethaneel, and his fifth son was Raddai.

**15** His sixth son was Ozem, and his seventh son was David.

**16** Their sisters were Zeruiah and Abigail. The 3 sons of Zeruiah were Abishai, Joab, and Asahel.

**17** Abigail gave birth to Amasa. The father of Amasa was Jether the Ishmeelite.

**18** Caleb the son of Hezron had children by Azubah his wife and by Jerioth. The sons of Azubah were Jesher, Shobab, and Ardon.

**19** When Azubah died, Caleb married Ephrath. She gave birth to Hur for him.

**20** Hur became the father of Uri. Uri became the father of Bezaleel.

**21** Later, Hezron married the daughter of Machir, the father of Gilead. Hezron was 60 years old when he married her. She gave birth to Segub for him.

**22** Segub became the father of Jair. Jair had 23 cities in the land of Gilead.

**23** But Geshur and Aram took the towns of Jair from them. They also took Kenath and its towns, a total of 60 cities. All these cities belonged to the descendants of Machir, the father of Gilead.

**24** Hezron died in Caleb-ephratah. After his death, Abiah the wife of Hezron gave birth to Ashur for him. Ashur was the father of Tekoa.

**25** The sons of Jerahmeel, the first son of Hezron, were Ram, his first son, Bunah, Oren, Ozem, and Ahijah.

**26** Jerahmeel also had another wife. Her name was Atarah. She was the mother of Onam.

**27** The sons of Ram, the first son of Jerahmeel, were Maaz, Jamin, and Eker.

**28** The sons of Onam were Shammai and Jada. The sons of Shammai were Nadab and Abishur.

**29** The name of the wife of Abishur was Abihail. She gave birth to Ahban and Molid for him.

**30** The sons of Nadab were Seled and Appaim. Seled died and had no children.

**31** The son of Appaim was Ishi. The son of Ishi was Sheshan. The son of Sheshan was Ahlai.

**32** The sons of Jada, the brother of Shammai, were Jether and Jonathan. Jether died and had no children.

**33** The sons of Jonathan were Peleth and Zaza. These were the descendants of Jerahmeel.

**34** Sheshan had no sons. He had only daughters. Sheshan had an Egyptian servant. The name of the servant was Jarha.

**35** Sheshan gave his daughter to Jarha his servant as a wife. She gave birth to Attai for him.

**36** Attai became the father of Nathan. Nathan became the father of Zabad.

**37** Zabad became the father of Ephlal. Ephlal became the father of Obed.

**38** Obed became the father of Jehu. Jehu became the father of Azariah.

**39** Azariah became the father of Helez. Helez became the father of Eleasah.

**40** Eleasah became the father of Sisamai. Sisamai became the father of Shallum.

**41** Shallum became the father of Jekamiah. Jekamiah became the father of Elishama.

**42** The sons of Caleb, the brother of Jerahmeel, were Mesha, his first son, who was the father of Ziph, and the sons of Mareshah, the father of Hebron.

**43** The sons of Hebron were Korah, Tappuah, Rekem, and Shema.

**44** Shema became the father of Raham, the father of Jorkoam. Rekem became the father of Shammai.

**45** The son of Shammai was Maon. Maon was the father of Beth-zur.

**46** Ephah, a concubine of Caleb, gave birth to Haran, Moza, and Gazez. Haran became the father of Gazez.

**47** The sons of Jahdai were Regem, Jotham, Geshan, Pelet, Ephah, and Shaaph.

**48** Maachah, a concubine of Caleb, gave birth to Sheber and Tirhanah.

**49** She also gave birth to Shaaph the father of Madmannah, and Sheva the father of Machbenah and Gibea. The daughter of Caleb was Achsah.

**50** These were the descendants of Caleb. The sons of Hur, the first son of Ephratah, were Shobal the father of Kirjath-jearim,

**51** Salma the father of Beth-lehem, and Hareph the father of Beth-gader.

**52** Shobal the father of Kirjath-jearim had sons. They were Haroeh and half of the Manahethites.

**53** The families of Kirjath-jearim were the Ithrites, the Puhites, the Shumathites, and the Mishraites. The Zareathites and the Eshtaulites came from these families.

**54** The descendants of Salma were Beth-lehem, the Netophathites, Ataroth of the family of Joab, half of the Manahethites, and the Zorites.

**55** The families of the scribes who lived at Jabez were the Tirathites, the Shimeathites, and the Suchathites. These were the Kenites who came from Hemath, the father of the family of Rechab.

## Chapter 3

**1** These were the sons of David who were born to him in Hebron. The first son was Amnon. His mother was Ahinoam from Jezreel. The second son was Daniel. His mother was Abigail from Carmel.

**2** The third son was Absalom. His mother was Maachah, the daughter of Talmai king of Geshur. The fourth son was Adonijah. His mother was Haggith.

**3** The fifth son was Shephatiah. His mother was Abital. The sixth son was Ithream. His mother was Eglah, the wife of David.

**4** These 6 sons were born to David in Hebron. David ruled there for 7 years and 6 months. He ruled in Jerusalem for 33 years.

**5** These sons were born to David in Jerusalem: Shimea, Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon. These 4 sons were the sons of Bath-shua the daughter of Ammiel.

**6** There were also Ibhar, Elishama, Eliphelet,

**7** Nogah, Nepheg, Japhia,

**8** Elishama, Eliada, and Eliphelet. These were 9 sons.

**9** All these were the sons of David. David also had sons from his concubines. Tamar was the sister of these sons.

**10** The son of Solomon was Rehoboam. The son of Rehoboam was Abia. The son of Abia was Asa. The son of Asa was Jehoshaphat.

**11** The son of Jehoshaphat was Joram. The son of Joram was Ahaziah. The son of Ahaziah was Joash.

**12** The son of Joash was Amaziah. The son of Amaziah was Azariah. The son of Azariah was Jotham.

**13** The son of Jotham was Ahaz. The son of Ahaz was Hezekiah. The son of Hezekiah was Manasseh.

**14** The son of Manasseh was Amon. The son of Amon was Josiah.

**15** The sons of Josiah were Johanan, the first son, Jehoiakim, the second son, Zedekiah, the third son, and Shallum, the fourth son.

**16** The sons of Jehoiakim were Jeconiah and Zedekiah.

**17** The sons of Jeconiah, the prisoner, were Salathiel,

**18** Malchiram, Pedaiah, Shenazar, Jecamiah, Hoshama, and Nedabiah.

**19** The sons of Pedaiah were Zerubbabel and Shimei. The sons of Zerubbabel were Meshullam and Hananiah. Shelomith was their sister.

**20** There were also 5 other sons: Hashubah, Ohel, Berechiah, Hasadiah, and Jushab-hesed.

**21** The sons of Hananiah were Pelatiah and Jesaiah. Also listed are the sons of Rephaiah, the sons of Arnan, the sons of Obadiah, and the sons of Shechaniah.

**22** The son of Shechaniah was Shemaiah. The sons of Shemaiah were Hattush, Igeal, Bariah, Neariah, and Shaphat. They were 6 in total.

**23** The 3 sons of Neariah were Elioenai, Hezekiah, and Azrikam.

**24** The 7 sons of Elioenai were Hodaiah, Eliashib, Pelaiah, Akkub, Johanan, Dalaiah, and Anani.

## Chapter 4

**1** The descendants of Judah were Pharez, Hezron, Carmi, Hur, and Shobal.

**2** Reaiah the son of Shobal became the father of Jahath. Jahath became the father of Ahumai and Lahad. These were the families of the Zorathites.

**3** These were the sons of the father of Etam: Jezreel, Ishma, and Idbash. The name of their sister was Hazelelponi.

**4** Penuel was the father of Gedor. Ezer was the father of Hushah. These were the descendants of Hur, the first son of Ephratah and the father of Beth-lehem.

**5** Ashur the father of Tekoa had 2 wives, Helah and Naarah.

**6** Naarah gave birth to Ahuzam, Hepher, Temeni, and Haahashtari for him. These were the sons of Naarah.

**7** The sons of Helah were Zereth, Jezoar, and Ethnan.

**8** Coz became the father of Anub and Zobebah. He was also the ancestor of the families of Aharhel the son of Harum.

**9** Jabez had more honor than his brothers. His mother gave him the name Jabez. She said, "I gave birth to him with much pain."

**10** Jabez prayed to the God of Israel. He said, "Please bless me and make my land larger. Let your hand be with me. Keep me from harm, so that I do not have pain." God gave Jabez what he asked for.

**11** Chelub the brother of Shuah became the father of Mehir. Mehir was the father of Eshton.

**12** Eshton became the father of Beth-rapha, Paseah, and Tehinnah. Tehinnah was the father of Ir-nahash. These were the men of Rechah.

**13** The sons of Kenaz were Othniel and Seraiah. The son of Othniel was Hathath.

**14** Meonothai became the father of Ophrah. Seraiah became the father of Joab. Joab was the father of the people in the Valley of Charashim. They had this name because they were skilled workers.

**15** The sons of Caleb the son of Jephunneh were Iru, Elah, and Naam. The son of Elah was Kenaz.

**16** The sons of Jehaleleel were Ziph, Ziphah, Tiria, and Asareel.

**17** The sons of Ezra were Jether, Mered, Epher, and Jalon. A wife of Mered gave birth to Miriam, Shammai, and Ishbah. Ishbah was the father of Eshtemoa.

**18** His wife from Judah gave birth to Jered the father of Gedor, Heber the father of Socho, and Jekuthiel the father of Zanoah. These were the sons of Bithiah, the daughter of Pharaoh. Mered married Bithiah.

**19** His wife Hodiah was the sister of Naham. Her sons were the father of Keilah the Garmite and Eshtemoa the Maachathite.

**20** The sons of Shimon were Amnon, Rinnah, Ben-hanan, and Tilon. The sons of Ishi were Zoheth and Ben-zoheth.

**21** The sons of Shelah the son of Judah were Er the father of Lecah, and Laadah the father of Mareshah. Shelah was also the ancestor of the families of linen workers at Beth-ashbea.

**22** He was also the ancestor of Jokim, the men of Chozeba, Joash, and Saraph. Joash and Saraph ruled in Moab and returned to Lehem. These records are very old.

**23** These men made clay pots. They lived at Netaim and Gederah. They lived there and did work for the king.

**24** The sons of Simeon were Nemuel, Jamin, Jarib, Zerah, and Shaul.

**25** The son of Shaul was Shallum. The son of Shallum was Mibsam. The son of Mibsam was Mishma.

**26** The descendants of Mishma were Hamuel his son, Zacchur his son, and Shimei his son.

**27** Shimei had 16 sons and 6 daughters. But his brothers did not have many children. Their families did not become as large as the families of Judah.

**28** They lived at Beer-sheba, Moladah, Hazar-shual,

**29** Bilhah, Ezem, Tolad,

**30** Bethuel, Hormah, Ziklag,

**31** Beth-marcaboth, Hazar-susim, Beth-birei, and Shaaraim. These were their cities until David became king.

**32** Their villages were Etam, Ain, Rimmon, Tochen, and Ashan. These were 5 cities.

**33** They also had all the villages around these cities, as far as Baal. These were the places where they lived. They kept a record of their families.

**34** Meshobab, Jamlech, Joshah the son of Amaziah,

**35** Joel, Jehu the son of Josibiah, the son of Seraiah, the son of Asiel,

**36** Elioenai, Jaakobah, Jeshohaiah, Asaiah, Adiel, Jesimiel, Benaiah,

**37** and Ziza the son of Shiphi, the son of Allon, the son of Jedaiah, the son of Shimri, the son of Shemaiah.

**38** These men, whose names are written here, were leaders of their families. Their families became very large.

**39** They went to the entrance of Gedor, to the east side of the valley. They looked for grass for their flocks.

**40** They found rich, good grass. The land was wide, quiet, and peaceful. Some descendants of Ham lived there before them.

**41** These men, whose names are written here, came in the time of Hezekiah king of Judah. They attacked the tents of the people there and the Meunites who lived there. They destroyed these people completely, and the people are not there today. Then these men lived in their place, because there was grass there for their flocks.

**42** 500 men of the descendants of Simeon went to Mount Seir. Their leaders were Pelatiah, Neariah, Rephaiah, and Uzziel, the sons of Ishi.

**43** They killed the remaining Amalekites who escaped. They live there today.

## Chapter 5

**1** These are the sons of Reuben, the first son of Israel. Reuben was the first son. But he slept with a wife of his father and made his father's bed unclean. Thus the rights of the first son went to the sons of Joseph the son of Israel. Because of this, the family records do not list Reuben as the first son.

**2** Judah became stronger than his brothers, and a ruler came from Judah. But the rights of the first son went to Joseph.

**3** The sons of Reuben, the first son of Israel, were Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi.

**4** The descendants of Joel were Shemaiah his son, Gog his son, Shimei his son,

**5** Micah his son, Reaia his son, Baal his son,

**6** and Beerah his son. Tilgath-pilneser king of Assyria took Beerah away as a prisoner. Beerah was a leader of the Reubenites.

**7** The family records list his relatives by their families. The chief was Jeiel, then Zechariah,

**8** and Bela the son of Azaz, the son of Shema, the son of Joel. Bela lived in Aroer, as far as Nebo and Baal-meon.

**9** To the east, he lived as far as the edge of the desert near the Euphrates River. He lived there because their cattle became very many in the land of Gilead.

**10** In the time of Saul, they fought a war against the Hagarites and defeated them. Then they lived in the tents of the Hagarites in all the land east of Gilead.

**11** The descendants of Gad lived near them in the land of Bashan, as far as Salchah.

**12** Joel was the chief, and Shapham was the second. Jaanai and Shaphat were also leaders in Bashan.

**13** Their relatives by their families were Michael, Meshullam, Sheba, Jorai, Jachan, Zia, and Heber. They were 7 in total.

**14** These were the sons of Abihail the son of Huri, the son of Jaroah, the son of Gilead, the son of Michael, the son of Jeshishai, the son of Jahdo, the son of Buz.

**15** Ahi the son of Abdiel, the son of Guni, was chief of their family.

**16** They lived in Gilead, in Bashan and its towns, and in all the fields of Sharon, as far as their borders.

**17** All these names went into the family records in the time of Jotham king of Judah and in the time of Jeroboam king of Israel.

**18** The tribe of Reuben, the tribe of Gad, and half the tribe of Manasseh had 44,760 brave soldiers. These men could carry shields and swords and use bows. They were skilled in war.

**19** They fought a war against the Hagarites, Jetur, Nephish, and Nodab.

**20** God helped them in the fight. He gave the Hagarites and all their allies into their hands. God did this because they called to him in the battle. He answered them because they trusted him.

**21** They took the animals of the Hagarites: 50,000 camels, 250,000 sheep, and 2,000 donkeys. They also took 100,000 people.

**22** Many enemy soldiers died, because the war came from God. Then they lived in that land until the time of the exile.

**23** The people of the half tribe of Manasseh lived in the land. They became many. They lived from Bashan to Baal-hermon, Senir, and Mount Hermon.

**24** These were the leaders of their families: Epher, Ishi, Eliel, Azriel, Jeremiah, Hodaviah, and Jahdiel. They were strong, brave, and famous men. They were leaders of their families.

**25** But these people did not obey the God of their fathers. They were not faithful to him. They worshipped the gods of the people of the land. God destroyed those people before them.

**26** Thus the God of Israel caused Pul king of Assyria to attack. Pul is Tilgath-pilneser king of Assyria. He took away the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half tribe of Manasseh. He took them to Halah, Habor, Hara, and the river of Gozan. They are there today.

## Chapter 6

**1** The sons of Levi were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.

**2** The sons of Kohath were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel.

**3** The children of Amram were Aaron, Moses, and Miriam. The sons of Aaron were Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.

**4** Eleazar became the father of Phinehas. Phinehas became the father of Abishua.

**5** Abishua became the father of Bukki. Bukki became the father of Uzzi.

**6** Uzzi became the father of Zerahiah. Zerahiah became the father of Meraioth.

**7** Meraioth became the father of Amariah. Amariah became the father of Ahitub.

**8** Ahitub became the father of Zadok. Zadok became the father of Ahimaaz.

**9** Ahimaaz became the father of Azariah. Azariah became the father of Johanan.

**10** Johanan became the father of Azariah. This Azariah served as priest in the temple that Solomon built in Jerusalem.

**11** Azariah became the father of Amariah. Amariah became the father of Ahitub.

**12** Ahitub became the father of Zadok. Zadok became the father of Shallum.

**13** Shallum became the father of Hilkiah. Hilkiah became the father of Azariah.

**14** Azariah became the father of Seraiah. Seraiah became the father of Jehozadak.

**15** Jehozadak went away as a prisoner when the LORD sent Judah and Jerusalem into exile. The LORD used Nebuchadnezzar to do this.

**16** The sons of Levi were Gershom, Kohath, and Merari.

**17** These are the names of the sons of Gershom: Libni and Shimei.

**18** The sons of Kohath were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel.

**19** The sons of Merari were Mahli and Mushi. These are the families of the Levites, listed by their fathers.

**20** The descendants of Gershom were Libni his son, Jahath his son, Zimmah his son,

**21** Joah his son, Iddo his son, Zerah his son, and Jeaterai his son.

**22** The descendants of Kohath were Amminadab his son, Korah his son, Assir his son,

**23** Elkanah his son, Ebiasaph his son, Assir his son,

**24** Tahath his son, Uriel his son, Uzziah his son, and Shaul his son.

**25** The sons of Elkanah were Amasai and Ahimoth.

**26** The descendants of Elkanah were Zophai his son, Nahath his son,

**27** Eliab his son, Jeroham his son, and Elkanah his son.

**28** The sons of Samuel were Vashni, the first son, and Abiah.

**29** The descendants of Merari were Mahli, Libni his son, Shimei his son, Uzza his son,

**30** Shimea his son, Haggiah his son, and Asaiah his son.

**31** David put these men in charge of the music in the house of the LORD. He did this after the ark (the holy box of the agreement) came to rest there.

**32** They served with music in front of the tabernacle (the holy tent) of meeting. They did this until Solomon built the house of the LORD in Jerusalem. They did their work in the order that David gave them.

**33** These are the men who served, with their sons. From the Kohathites there was Heman the singer. He was the son of Joel, the son of Shemuel,

**34** the son of Elkanah, the son of Jeroham, the son of Eliel, the son of Toah,

**35** the son of Zuph, the son of Elkanah, the son of Mahath, the son of Amasai,

**36** the son of Elkanah, the son of Joel, the son of Azariah, the son of Zephaniah,

**37** the son of Tahath, the son of Assir, the son of Ebiasaph, the son of Korah,

**38** the son of Izhar, the son of Kohath, the son of Levi, the son of Israel.

**39** Heman's relative Asaph stood at his right side. Asaph was the son of Berachiah, the son of Shimea,

**40** the son of Michael, the son of Baaseiah, the son of Malchiah,

**41** the son of Ethni, the son of Zerah, the son of Adaiah,

**42** the son of Ethan, the son of Zimmah, the son of Shimei,

**43** the son of Jahath, the son of Gershom, the son of Levi.

**44** Their relatives, the descendants of Merari, stood at the left side. Ethan was there. He was the son of Kishi, the son of Abdi, the son of Malluch,

**45** the son of Hashabiah, the son of Amaziah, the son of Hilkiah,

**1** David talked with the commanders of 1,000 men and the commanders of 100 men. He talked with each leader.

He said, "If you think that this is good, and if the LORD our God agrees, then we will send messengers to all our relatives in the land of Israel. We will also send messengers to the priests and the Levites in their cities. We will tell them all to come to us.

**3** Then we will bring the ark (the holy box) of our God back to us. In the time of Saul, we did not ask God for help through the ark."

**4** All the people agreed to do this, because they all thought that it was the right thing to do.

**5** Thus David brought all the people of Israel together, from the Shihor River in Egypt to Lebo-hamath. They came to bring the ark of God from Kirjath-jearim.

**6** David and all Israel went up to Baalah, which is Kirjath-jearim in Judah. They went to bring up the ark of God, the LORD, from there. The LORD sits on his throne between the cherubim (the winged figures). The ark has his name.

**8** David and all Israel celebrated before God with all their strength. They sang and played harps, lyres, tambourines, cymbals, and trumpets.

**14** The ark of God stayed with the family of Obed-edom in his house for 3 months. The LORD blessed the family of Obed-edom and all that he had.

## Chapter 14

**1** Hiram king of Tyre sent messengers to David. He also sent cedar wood, stone workers, and carpenters to build a house for David.

**2** Then David knew that the LORD made him king over Israel with a strong kingdom. He knew that the LORD made his kingdom great for the sake of the people of Israel.

**3** David married more wives in Jerusalem. He became the father of more sons and daughters.

**4** These are the names of his children who were born in Jerusalem: Shammua, Shobab, Nathan, Solomon,

**5** Ibhar, Elishua, Elpalet,

**6** Nogah, Nepheg, Japhia,

**7** Elishama, Beeliada, and Eliphalet.

## Chapter 15

**1** David built houses for himself in the city of David. He prepared a place for the ark of God. He put up a tent for the ark.

**2** Then David said, "Only the Levites can carry the ark of God. The LORD chose them to carry the ark of the LORD and to serve him always."

**3** David brought all Israel together in Jerusalem. He wanted them to bring up the ark of the LORD to the place that he prepared for it.

**4** David called together the descendants of Aaron and the Levites.

**5** From the descendants of Kohath there were Uriel, the chief, and 120 of his relatives.

**6** From the descendants of Merari there were Asaiah, the chief, and 220 of his relatives.

**7** From the descendants of Gershom there were Joel, the chief, and 130 of his relatives.

**8** From the descendants of Elizaphan there were Shemaiah, the chief, and 200 of his relatives.

**9** From the descendants of Hebron there were Eliel, the chief, and 80 of his relatives.

**10** From the descendants of Uzziel there were Amminadab, the chief, and 112 of his relatives.

**11** David called Zadok and Abiathar, the priests. He also called the Levites Uriel, Asaiah, Joel, Shemaiah, Eliel, and Amminadab.

**18** With them were their relatives of the second rank: Zechariah, Ben, Jaaziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattithiah, Elipheleh, Mikneiah, Obed-edom, and Jeiel. These men were the gatekeepers.

**19** The singers Heman, Asaph, and Ethan had the work of playing cymbals of bronze.

**20** Zechariah, Aziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Maaseiah, and Benaiah played lyres in the high range (Alamoth).

**21** Mattithiah, Elipheleh, Mikneiah, Obed-edom, Jeiel, and Azaziah played harps in the low range (Sheminith). They led the music.

**22** Chenaniah, the leader of the Levites, was in charge of the singing. He taught the singing because he was skilled.

**24** The priests Shebaniah, Jehoshaphat, Nethaneel, Amasai, Zechariah, Benaiah, and Eliezer blew the trumpets in front of the ark of God. Obed-edom and Jehiah were also gatekeepers for the ark.

**25** Thus David, the leaders of Israel, and the commanders of 1,000 men went to bring up the ark of the covenant (the agreement) of the LORD. They brought the ark from the house of Obed-edom with joy.

**26** God helped the Levites who carried the ark of the covenant of the LORD. Thus they sacrificed 7 bulls and 7 rams.

**27** David wore a robe of fine linen. All the Levites who carried the ark wore robes of fine linen. The singers and Chenaniah, the leader of the music, also wore these robes. David also wore a linen ephod (a priest's vest).

**28** Thus all Israel brought up the ark of the covenant of the LORD. They shouted and blew horns and trumpets. They played cymbals, lyres, and harps loudly.

## Chapter 16

**1** They brought the ark of God and put it in the tent that David put up for it. Then they made burnt offerings and peace offerings before God.

**2** David finished the burnt offerings and the peace offerings. Then he blessed the people in the name of the LORD.

**3** He gave food to each person in Israel, to each man and each woman. He gave each person a loaf of bread, a piece of meat, and a cake of raisins.

**4** David chose some of the Levites to serve in front of the ark of the LORD. They were to celebrate, thank, and praise the LORD, the God of Israel.

**5** Asaph was the chief, and Zechariah was second to him. Then there were Jeiel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Mattithiah, Eliab, Benaiah, Obed-edom, and Jeiel. They played lyres and harps. Asaph played the cymbals loudly.

**6** The priests Benaiah and Jahaziel always blew trumpets in front of the ark of the covenant of God.

**7** On that day, David first gave this song of thanks to the LORD to Asaph and his relatives:

**8** Give thanks to the LORD. Call on his name. Tell the nations what he did.

**9** Sing to him. Sing praises to him. Tell about all his wonderful acts.

**10** Be proud of his holy name. Let the hearts of the people who look for the LORD be glad.

**11** Look to the LORD and his strength. Always look for him.

**12** Remember the wonderful things that he did. Remember his miracles and the judgments that he spoke.

**13** Remember these things, you descendants of Israel his servant. Remember them, you children of Jacob, his chosen people.

**14** He is the LORD our God. His judgments are in all the earth.

**15** Always remember his covenant. Remember his promise to 1,000 generations.

**16** He made this covenant with Abraham. He made a promise to Isaac with an oath.

**17** He made the covenant firm with Jacob as a law. He made it with Israel as a covenant that will never end.

**18** He said, "I will give you the land of Canaan. It will be your property."

**19** At that time you were few people. You were foreigners in that land.

**20** They went from nation to nation. They went from one kingdom to another.

**21** He did not let anyone hurt them. He spoke strongly against kings to protect them.

**22** He said, "Do not touch my anointed people. Do not hurt my prophets."

**23** Sing to the LORD, all the earth. Each day, tell about how he saves.

**24** Tell the nations about his glory. Tell all peoples about his wonderful acts.

**25** The LORD is great. He is worthy of much praise. People must fear him more than all gods.

**26** All the gods of the nations are only idols. But the LORD made the heavens.

**27** Glory and honor are with him. Strength and joy are in his place.

**28** Families of the nations, give praise to the LORD. Tell of the glory and the strength of the LORD.

**29** Give the LORD the glory that his name deserves. Bring an offering and come before him. Worship the LORD in the beauty of his holiness.

**30** All the earth, shake with fear before him. The world is firm. It will not move.

**31** Let the heavens be glad. Let the earth be happy. Let people say among the nations, "The LORD is King."

**32** Let the sea and all that is in it make a loud noise. Let the fields and all that is in them be happy.

**33** Then the trees of the forest will sing with joy before the LORD. They will sing because he comes to judge the earth.

**34** Give thanks to the LORD, because he is good. His love will continue always.

**35** Say, "God, you save us. Save us now. Bring us together and rescue us from the nations. Then we will give thanks to your holy name. We will be proud to praise you."

**36** Bless the LORD, the God of Israel, always and forever. Then all the people said, "Amen," and praised the LORD.

**37** David left Asaph and his relatives in front of the ark of the covenant of the LORD. They were to serve in front of the ark always, as the work of each day needed.

**38** He also left Obed-edom and his 68 relatives. Obed-edom the son of Jeduthun and Hosah were gatekeepers.

**39** David left Zadok the priest and his relatives the priests in front of the tabernacle of the LORD. The tabernacle was at the place of worship on the hill at Gibeon.

**41** Heman, Jeduthun, and the other chosen men were with them. Their names are on the list. They were to give thanks to the LORD, because his love continues always.

**42** Heman and Jeduthun had trumpets, cymbals, and other instruments for the songs of God. The sons of Jeduthun were gatekeepers.

**43** Then all the people went back to their homes. David went back to bless his family.

## Chapter 17

**1** David lived in his house. He said to Nathan the prophet, "Look, I live in a house of cedar wood. But the ark of the covenant of the LORD is in a tent."

**2** Nathan said to David, "Do all that you want to do, because God is with you."

**3** But that night the word of God came to Nathan. God said,

**4** "Go and tell David my servant, 'The LORD says this: You will not build a house for me to live in.

**5** I did not live in a house from the day that I brought Israel out of Egypt until today. I moved from tent to tent and from one place to another.

**6** I went with all the people of Israel to many places. I never spoke to any of the judges of Israel about a house. I told those judges to be the shepherds of my people. I did not say to them, "Why do you not build a house of cedar wood for me?"'

**7** Now tell this to my servant David: 'The LORD of armies says this: I took you from the fields, where you followed the sheep. I made you the ruler of my people Israel.

**8** I was with you in all places where you went. I destroyed all your enemies in front of you. I will make your name great, like the names of the great men on the earth.

**9** I will make a place for my people Israel. I will put them in that place like a tree. They will live in their own place, and no one will trouble them again. Evil people will not hurt them again, as they did at the start.

**10** They hurt my people at the time when I put judges over my people Israel. I will defeat all your enemies. I also tell you that the LORD will build a house for you. This house will be a family of kings.

**11** Your life will end and you will go to be with your ancestors. Then I will make one of your descendants king after you. He will be one of your sons. I will make his kingdom strong.

**12** He will build a house for me. I will make his throne strong forever.

**13** I will be his father, and he will be my son. I will not take my love away from him. I took it away from Saul, who ruled before you.

**14** I will put him in charge of my house and my kingdom forever. His throne will continue forever.'"

**15** Nathan told David all these words and all this vision.

**16** Then King David went in and sat before the LORD. David said, "LORD God, I am not important. My family is not important. But you brought me to this place.

**17** God, you think that this is a small thing. Now you also speak about the future of the family of your servant. LORD God, you treat me like an important man.

**18** I cannot say more to you about the honor that you give to me, your servant. You know your servant.

**19** LORD, you did all these great things because of your servant and because of your own wish. You made known all these great things.

**20** LORD, no one is like you. There is no God but you. All that we heard tells us this.

**21** No other nation on earth is like your people Israel. God went to free them to be his own people. You made your name great with great and terrible acts. You forced nations to leave before your people. You freed your people from Egypt.

**22** You made your people Israel your own people forever. LORD, you became their God.

**23** Now, LORD, keep your promise about your servant and his family forever. Do what you said.

**24** Let your promise be firm. Then people will make your name great forever. They will say, 'The LORD of armies is the God of Israel.' Let the family of your servant David continue before you.

**25** My God, you told your servant that you will build a family of kings for him. Thus I, your servant, have the courage to pray this prayer to you.

**26** LORD, you are God. You promised these good things to your servant.

**27** Now please bless the family of your servant. Let my family continue before you forever. LORD, you blessed it, and it will be blessed forever."

## Chapter 18

**1** After this, David attacked the Philistines and defeated them. He took Gath and its towns from the control of the Philistines.

**2** David also defeated Moab. The Moabites became servants of David and paid tax to him.

**3** David also defeated Hadarezer king of Zobah, near Hamath. Hadarezer went to make his control strong at the Euphrates River.

**4** David took from him 1,000 chariots, 7,000 horsemen, and 20,000 foot soldiers. David cut the leg muscles of all the chariot horses. But he kept enough horses for 100 chariots.

**5** The Syrians of Damascus came to help Hadarezer king of Zobah. David killed 22,000 of the Syrians.

**6** Then David put groups of soldiers in Syria near Damascus. The Syrians became servants of David and paid tax to him. The LORD gave David victory in all places where he went.

**7** David took the gold shields that the officers of Hadarezer carried. He brought the shields to Jerusalem.

**8** David also took very much bronze from Tibhath and Chun, cities of Hadarezer. Later, Solomon used this bronze to make the large bronze water tank (the sea), the pillars, and the bronze containers.

**9** Tou king of Hamath heard that David defeated all the army of Hadarezer king of Zobah.

**10** Thus Tou sent his son Hadoram to King David. Hadoram greeted David and congratulated him because David fought against Hadarezer and defeated him. Hadarezer often fought wars with Tou. Hadoram brought all types of things made of gold, silver, and bronze.

**11** King David gave these things to the LORD. He also gave the silver and the gold that he took from all these nations. He took it from Edom, Moab, the Ammonites, the Philistines, and Amalek.

**12** Abishai the son of Zeruiah killed 18,000 Edomites in the Valley of Salt.

**13** He put groups of soldiers in Edom. All the Edomites became servants of David. The LORD gave David victory in all places where he went.

**14** Thus David ruled over all Israel. He did what was fair and right for all his people.

**15** Joab the son of Zeruiah was in command of the army. Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud kept the records.

**16** Zadok the son of Ahitub and Abimelech the son of Abiathar were the priests. Shavsha was the secretary.

**17** Benaiah the son of Jehoiada was in command of the Cherethites and the Pelethites. The sons of David were the chief officials next to the king.

## Chapter 19

**1** After this, Nahash the king of the Ammonites died. His son became king after him.

**2** David said, "I will be kind to Hanun the son of Nahash, because his father was kind to me." Thus David sent messengers to comfort Hanun about the death of his father. The officials of David came to Hanun in the land of the Ammonites to comfort him.

**3** But the leaders of the Ammonites said to Hanun, "Do you think that David honors your father? Do you think that he sent these men to comfort you? No! His officials came to look at the land. They came to spy, and then to destroy it."

**4** Thus Hanun took the officials of David and shaved them. He cut off their clothes in the middle, at the hips. Then he sent them away.

**5** Some people went and told David what occurred to the men. David sent messengers to meet them, because the men were very ashamed. The king said, "Stay at Jericho until your beards grow again. Then come back."

**6** The Ammonites saw that David now hated them. Thus Hanun and the Ammonites sent 1,000 talents of silver to pay for chariots and horsemen. They paid men from Mesopotamia, Syria-maachah, and Zobah.

**7** They paid for 32,000 chariots. They also paid the king of Maachah and his army. These men came and made their camp near Medeba. The Ammonites came together from their cities and came to the battle.

**8** David heard about this. He sent Joab and all the army of strong soldiers.

**9** The Ammonites came out and got ready for battle at the gate of their city. The kings who came to help them stayed in the open field.

**10** Joab saw that the enemy was in front of him and behind him. Thus he chose some of the best soldiers of Israel. He put them in position against the Syrians.

**11** He put the rest of the army under the command of his brother Abishai. They got into position against the Ammonites.

**12** Joab said, "If the Syrians are too strong for me, then you must help me. If the Ammonites are too strong for you, then I will help you.

**13** Be strong. We must fight bravely for our people and for the cities of our God. The LORD will do what he thinks is good."

**14** Joab and his soldiers went forward to fight against the Syrians. The Syrians ran away from him.

**15** The Ammonites saw that the Syrians ran away. Thus they also ran away from Abishai, the brother of Joab. They went into their city. Then Joab went back to Jerusalem.

**16** The Syrians saw that Israel defeated them. Thus they sent messengers and brought the Syrians from the other side of the Euphrates River. Shophach, the commander of the army of Hadarezer, led them.

**17** Someone told David about this. David brought all Israel together. He went across the Jordan River and came to the Syrians. He put his army in position against them. Then the Syrians fought against David.

**18** But the Syrians ran away from Israel. David killed 7,000 Syrian chariot soldiers and 40,000 foot soldiers. He also killed Shophach, the commander of the army.

**19** The officials of Hadarezer saw that Israel defeated them. Thus they made peace with David and became his servants. After this, the Syrians did not help the Ammonites again.

## Chapter 20

**1** In the spring, when kings usually go to war, Joab led the army out. He destroyed the land of the Ammonites. He came to Rabbah and put an army around the city. But David stayed in Jerusalem. Joab attacked Rabbah and destroyed it.

**2** David took the crown from the head of their king. The crown weighed 1 talent of gold, and it had valuable stones in it. The people put the crown on the head of David. David also took very many valuable things from the city.

**3** He brought out the people of the city. He made them do hard work with saws, iron picks, and axes. David did this to all the cities of the Ammonites. Then David and all the army went back to Jerusalem.

**4** After this, a war started with the Philistines at Gezer. At that time Sibbechai the Hushathite killed Sippai. Sippai was a descendant of the giants. Thus the Philistines were defeated.

**5** There was war again with the Philistines. Elhanan the son of Jair killed Lahmi, the brother of Goliath from Gath. The handle of the spear of Lahmi was as large as the beam of a weaving machine.

**6** There was war again at Gath. There was a very tall man there. He had 24 fingers and toes, 6 on each hand and 6 on each foot. He also was a descendant of the giants.

**7** This man insulted Israel. Then Jonathan the son of Shimea, the brother of David, killed him.

**8** These men were descendants of the giants in Gath. David and his soldiers killed them.

## Chapter 21

**1** Satan went against Israel. He caused David to count the people of Israel.

**2** David said to Joab and the leaders of the people, "Go and count the people of Israel from Beer-sheba to Dan. Then bring the number to me. I want to know it."

**3** Joab said, "I want the LORD to make his people 100 times more than they are now. But my lord the king, all these people are your servants. Why do you want to do this? Why will you cause Israel to sin?"

**4** But the king did not change his order to Joab. Thus Joab left and went through all Israel. Then he came back to Jerusalem.

**5** Joab gave David the number of the people. In all Israel there were 1,100,000 men who could fight with the sword. In Judah there were 470,000 men who could fight with the sword.

**6** But Joab did not count the tribes of Levi and Benjamin, because he hated the order of the king.

**7** God was not pleased with this act. Thus he punished Israel.

**8** David said to God, "I did a very bad sin because I did this. Now please take away the guilt of your servant. I did a very foolish thing."

**9** The LORD spoke to Gad, the seer of David. He said,

**10** "Go and tell David, 'The LORD says this: I give you 3 choices. Choose one of them, and I will do it to you.'"

**11** Thus Gad came to David. He said to David, "The LORD says this: 'Choose one of these.

**12** You can have 3 years of famine. Or your enemies can defeat you for 3 months, and their swords will catch you. Or the LORD can send a disease on the land for 3 days. Then the angel of the LORD will destroy people in all the land of Israel.' Now decide what answer I must give to the LORD, who sent me."

**13** David said to Gad, "I am in great trouble. Let me fall into the hand of the LORD, because his mercy is very great. But do not let me fall into the hands of men."

**14** Thus the LORD sent a disease on Israel. 70,000 men of Israel died.

**15** God sent an angel to destroy Jerusalem. The angel started to destroy the city. Then the LORD looked and felt sorry about the disaster. He said to the angel who destroyed the people, "That is enough! Stop now." At that time, the angel of the LORD stood by the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.

**16** David looked up and saw the angel of the LORD. The angel stood between the earth and the sky. He had a sword in his hand. He held the sword out over Jerusalem. David and the leaders of Israel wore rough cloth (sackcloth) to show their sorrow. They bowed with their faces to the ground.

**17** David said to God, "I gave the order to count the people. I am the person who sinned and did this evil. These people are like sheep. They did nothing wrong. LORD my God, please punish me and my family. But do not send the disease on your people."

**18** Then the angel of the LORD told Gad to speak to David. Gad told David to go up and build an altar to the LORD at the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.

**19** David went up, because Gad told him to go in the name of the LORD.

**20** Ornan threshed wheat at that time. He turned and saw the angel. His 4 sons who were with him hid.

**21** David came to Ornan. Ornan looked and saw David. He went out of the threshing floor. He bowed down to David with his face to the ground.

**22** David said to Ornan, "Sell me the place of this threshing floor. I want to build an altar to the LORD on it. Sell it to me for the full price. Then the disease will stop among the people."

**23** Ornan said to David, "Take it. My lord the king can do what he thinks is good. Look, I will also give you the oxen for the burnt offerings. I will give you the threshing tools for wood. I will give you the wheat for the grain offering. I give it all to you."

**7** For the work of the house of God, they gave 5,000 talents and 10,000 darics of gold. They gave 10,000 talents of silver, 18,000 talents of bronze, and 100,000 talents of iron.

**8** The people who had valuable stones gave them to the treasury of the house of the LORD. Jehiel the Gershonite was in charge of the treasury.

**9** The people were happy because their leaders gave willingly. They gave to the LORD with all their hearts. King David also was very happy.

**10** David praised the LORD in front of all the people. David said, "LORD, God of Israel our father, we praise you forever and ever.

**11** LORD, greatness, power, glory, victory, and majesty are yours. All things in heaven and on earth are yours. LORD, the kingdom is yours. You are the ruler over all things.

**12** Wealth and honor come from you. You rule over all things. You have power and strength in your hand. You can make people great and strong.

**13** Now, our God, we thank you. We praise your glorious name.

**14** But I am not important, and my people are not important. We cannot give so much on our own. All things come from you. We only gave you what came from your hand.

**15** We are like foreigners and visitors before you, as all our ancestors were. Our time on earth is like a shadow. It does not continue.

**16** LORD our God, we prepared all these materials to build a house for your holy name. But all these things come from your hand. They are all yours.

**17** My God, I know that you examine the heart. You are pleased with honest people. I gave all these things willingly and with an honest heart. Now I saw your people here give willingly to you. I was happy to see this.

**18** LORD, God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, our ancestors, keep this wish in the hearts of your people always. Keep their hearts loyal to you.

**19** Give my son Solomon a loyal heart. Then he will obey your commands, your laws, and your rules. He will do all these things. He will build the temple that I prepared materials for."

**20** Then David said to all the people, "Praise the LORD your God." All the people praised the LORD, the God of their ancestors. They bowed down and worshipped the LORD. They also bowed down to the king.

**21** On the next day, they made sacrifices to the LORD. They made burnt offerings to the LORD of 1,000 bulls, 1,000 rams, and 1,000 lambs. They also gave drink offerings and many other sacrifices for all Israel.

**22** That day they ate and drank before the LORD with great joy. Then they made Solomon the son of David king for the second time. They anointed him before the LORD to be the ruler. They anointed Zadok to be the priest.

**23** Then Solomon sat on the throne of the LORD as king after his father David. Solomon was successful. All Israel obeyed him.

**24** All the leaders, the strong soldiers, and all the sons of King David promised to be loyal to King Solomon.

**25** The LORD made Solomon very great in front of all Israel. He gave Solomon more royal glory than any king of Israel before him.

**26** Thus David the son of Jesse ruled over all Israel.

**27** He ruled over Israel for 40 years. He ruled for 7 years in Hebron and for 33 years in Jerusalem.

**28** He died when he was very old. He had a long life, wealth, and honor. His son Solomon became king after him.

**29** The acts of King David, from the start to the end, are written in the records of Samuel the seer, the records of Nathan the prophet, and the records of Gad the seer.

**30** These records tell about all his rule and his power. They tell about the events that occurred to him, to Israel, and to all the kingdoms of the other countries.
