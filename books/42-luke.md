# Luke

## Chapter 1

**1** Many people tried to write an account of the things that happened among us. We are sure that these things are true.

**2** The people who saw these things from the start told them to us. These people also served the message of God.

**3** I also studied all these things carefully from the start. Thus I decided to write an account in order for you, most excellent Theophilus.

**4** I write so that you will know that the things which people taught you are true.

**5** When Herod was king of Judea, there was a priest with the name Zechariah. He was in the priest group of Abijah. His wife was from the family of Aaron. Her name was Elizabeth.

**6** Zechariah and Elizabeth were both righteous before God. They obeyed all the commands and laws of the Lord, and they did no wrong.

**7** But they had no child, because Elizabeth could not have children. Also, they were both very old.

**8** One day, the group of Zechariah had its time to serve in the temple. Zechariah did his work as a priest before God.

**9** The priests used lots to choose a priest, as was their custom. The lot chose Zechariah to go into the temple of the Lord and burn incense.

**10** At the time of the incense, all the people prayed outside.

**11** Then an angel of the Lord came to Zechariah. The angel stood on the right side of the altar of incense.

**12** Zechariah saw the angel. He became upset and very afraid.

**13** But the angel said to him, "Do not be afraid, Zechariah. God heard your prayer. Your wife Elizabeth will give birth to a son for you. You must give him the name John.

**14** You will have joy and be very happy. Many people will be glad because of his birth.

**15** He will be great before the Lord. He must not drink wine or strong drink. The Holy Spirit will fill him, even before his birth.

**16** He will cause many people of Israel to turn back to the Lord their God.

**17** He will go before the Lord with the spirit and power of Elijah. He will cause fathers to love their children again. He will cause people who disobey to become wise like righteous people. He will make a people ready for the Lord."

**18** Zechariah said to the angel, "How can I be sure of this? I am an old man, and my wife is also very old."

**19** The angel answered him, "I am Gabriel. I stand in front of God. God sent me to speak to you and to tell you this good news.

**20** You did not believe my words. Thus you will not be able to speak until the day that these things happen. My words will come true at the correct time."

**21** The people waited for Zechariah. They were full of wonder that he stayed in the temple for a long time.

**22** Then he came out, but he could not speak to them. They knew that he saw a vision in the temple. He made signs to them with his hands, but he could not speak.

**23** When his days of service in the temple were complete, he went back to his home.

**24** After this, his wife Elizabeth became pregnant. She stayed in her house for 5 months. She said,

**25** "The Lord did this for me. He was kind to me. He took away my shame among the people."

**26** In the sixth month of the pregnancy of Elizabeth, God sent the angel Gabriel to a town in Galilee with the name Nazareth.

**27** God sent the angel to a virgin. She had a promise to marry a man with the name Joseph, from the family of David. The name of the virgin was Mary.

**28** The angel came to her and said, "Greetings! God is very kind to you. The Lord is with you."

**29** Mary was very upset because of his words. She thought, "What do these words of hello mean?"

**30** The angel said to her, "Do not be afraid, Mary. God is happy with you.

**31** You will become pregnant and give birth to a son. You must give him the name Jesus.

**32** He will be great. People will call him the Son of the Most High God. The Lord God will give him the throne of his ancestor David.

**33** He will rule over the people of Jacob forever. His kingdom will never end."

**34** Mary said to the angel, "How will this happen? I am a virgin."

**35** The angel answered her, "The Holy Spirit will come on you. The power of the Most High God will cover you. Thus people will call the holy child the Son of God.

**36** Listen. Your relative Elizabeth is also pregnant with a son, although she is old. People said that she could not have children. But now she is in her sixth month.

**37** God can do all things."

**38** Mary said, "I am the servant of the Lord. Let it happen to me as you said." Then the angel went away from her.

**39** At that time, Mary got ready and went quickly to a town in the hill country of Judah.

**40** She went into the house of Zechariah and greeted Elizabeth.

**41** Elizabeth heard Mary say hello. Then the baby moved suddenly inside Elizabeth. The Holy Spirit filled Elizabeth.

**42** She spoke with a loud voice. She said, "God blesses you more than all other women. And God blesses the child that you will have.

**43** Why does the mother of my Lord come to me? I am not worthy of this honor.

**44** When I heard you say hello, the baby inside me moved with joy.

**45** You believed that the Lord will do the things that he told you. Thus God blesses you."

**46** Mary said, "With all my soul, I praise the Lord.

**47** My spirit is glad because of God, my Savior.

**48** He thought about me, his servant, although I am not important. From now on, all people will say that God blessed me.

**49** The Mighty God did great things for me. His name is holy.

**50** He shows mercy to the people who respect him, in all generations.

**51** He showed his strength with his arm. He scattered the people who are proud in their thoughts.

**52** He took powerful rulers off their thrones. He made humble people important.

**53** He gave good things to hungry people. He sent rich people away with nothing.

**54** He helped his servant Israel. He remembered to show mercy.

**55** He promised this to our ancestors, to Abraham and to his descendants forever."

**56** Mary stayed with Elizabeth for about 3 months. Then she went back to her home.

**57** The time came for Elizabeth to give birth. She gave birth to a son.

**58** Her neighbors and relatives heard that the Lord showed her great mercy. They were happy with her.

**59** On the eighth day, they came to circumcise the child. They wanted to give him the name Zechariah, the same name as his father.

**60** But his mother said, "No. His name will be John."

**61** They said to her, "No one in your family has this name."

**62** Then they made signs to his father. They asked him what name he wanted for the child.

**63** Zechariah asked for a tablet to write on. He wrote, "His name is John." All the people were full of wonder.

**64** Immediately, Zechariah could speak again. He spoke and praised God.

**65** All the neighbors became afraid. People talked about all these things in all the hill country of Judea.

**66** All the people who heard about these things thought about them. They said, "What will this child become?" The power of the Lord was with the child.

**67** The Holy Spirit filled Zechariah, the father of John. Zechariah spoke a message from God. He said,

**68** "Praise the Lord, the God of Israel. He came to help his people. He made them free.

**69** He gave us a powerful Savior from the family of his servant David.

**70** He promised this a long time ago through his holy prophets.

**71** He promised to save us from our enemies. He promised to save us from the power of all the people who hate us.

**72** He showed mercy to our ancestors. He remembered his holy agreement (covenant).

**73** He made a promise with an oath to our ancestor Abraham.

**74** He promised to save us from our enemies. Then we can serve him without fear.

**75** We will be holy and righteous before him for all the days of our lives.

**76** And you, my child, people will call you the prophet of the Most High God. You will go before the Lord to prepare the way for him.

**77** You will tell his people how God will save them. God will forgive their sins.

**78** Our God is very kind and merciful. Thus he will send a light from heaven to us, like the sunrise.

**79** This light will shine on the people who live in darkness and in fear of death. It will guide us on the way of peace."

**80** The child grew and became strong in spirit. He lived in the desert until the day that he showed himself to the people of Israel.

## Chapter 2

**1** At that time, Caesar Augustus gave an order. The order said that all the people in the Roman world must register.

**2** This was the first registration. It happened when Quirinius was the governor of Syria.

**3** All the people went to register, each person to his own town.

**4** Joseph also went from the town of Nazareth in Galilee to Judea. He went to Bethlehem, the town of David, because he was from the family of David.

**5** He went to register with Mary. Mary had a promise to marry him, and she was pregnant.

**6** While they were in Bethlehem, the time came for Mary to give birth.

**7** She gave birth to her first son. She put strips of cloth around him and put him in a feed box for animals. There was no room for them in the inn.

**8** Some shepherds were in the fields near Bethlehem. They watched their sheep during the night.

**9** An angel of the Lord came to them. The glory of the Lord shined around them. They were very afraid.

**10** The angel said to them, "Do not be afraid. I bring you good news. This news will give great joy to all people.

**11** Today, in the town of David, a Savior came into the world for you. He is Christ the Lord.

**12** This will be a sign for you. You will find a baby in strips of cloth. He will be in a feed box for animals."

**13** Suddenly, a large army of angels from heaven came with the angel. They praised God and said,

**14** "Glory to God in the highest heaven. On earth, let there be peace among the people who make God happy."

**15** The angels went away from the shepherds back into heaven. Then the shepherds said to each other, "We must go to Bethlehem now. We will see this thing that happened. The Lord told us about it."

**16** They went quickly and found Mary and Joseph. They also found the baby in the feed box.

**17** After the shepherds saw the baby, they told other people the message that the angel told them about this child.

**18** All the people who heard the shepherds were full of wonder at the things that the shepherds told them.

**19** But Mary remembered all these things. She thought about them carefully.

**20** The shepherds went back to their fields. They gave glory to God and praised him for all the things that they heard and saw. All the things were as the angel told them.

**21** On the eighth day, it was time to circumcise the child. They gave him the name Jesus. The angel gave him this name before he was in the body of his mother.

**22** The law of Moses gave a time for the purification of a mother. When this time ended, Joseph and Mary took Jesus to Jerusalem. They gave him to the Lord.

**23** The law of the Lord says, "Each first male child is holy to the Lord."

**24** They also gave a sacrifice, as the law of the Lord says: "2 doves or 2 young pigeons."

**25** A man with the name Simeon lived in Jerusalem. He was righteous and he obeyed God. He waited for God to comfort Israel. The Holy Spirit was on him.

**26** The Holy Spirit told Simeon that he will not die before he sees the Christ of the Lord.

**27** The Spirit led Simeon into the temple. The parents of Jesus brought the child in to do the things that the law said.

**28** Simeon took the child in his arms and praised God. He said,

**29** "Lord, now you can let your servant die in peace, as you promised.

**30** I saw with my eyes the Savior that you sent.

**31** You prepared him in front of all people.

**32** He is a light that will show your truth to the Gentiles. He will bring glory to your people Israel."

**33** The father and mother of Jesus were full of wonder at the things that Simeon said about him.

**34** Simeon blessed them. He said to Mary, the mother of Jesus, "God chose this child to cause many people in Israel to fall and to rise. He will be a sign from God, but many people will speak against him.

**35** Thus the thoughts of many people will become known. And a sword of sorrow will go through your soul also."

**36** A prophetess with the name Anna was also there. She was the daughter of Phanuel, from the tribe of Asher. She was very old. She lived with her husband for 7 years after their marriage.

**37** Then she was a widow until the age of 84. She did not go away from the temple. She worshiped God day and night. She fasted and prayed.

**38** At that time, Anna came to them. She thanked God. She spoke about the child to all the people who waited for God to make Jerusalem free.

**39** Joseph and Mary did all the things that the law of the Lord said. Then they went back to their own town, Nazareth, in Galilee.

**40** The child grew and became strong. He became full of wisdom. The grace of God was on him.

**41** Each year, the parents of Jesus went to Jerusalem for the Passover feast.

**42** When Jesus was 12 years old, they went to the feast, as was their custom.

**43** After the feast ended, his parents started to go home. But the boy Jesus stayed in Jerusalem. His parents did not know this.

**44** They thought that Jesus was with the other travelers. They traveled for 1 day. Then they looked for him among their relatives and friends.

**45** They did not find him. Thus they went back to Jerusalem to look for him.

**46** After 3 days, they found him in the temple. He sat among the teachers. He listened to them and asked them questions.

**47** All the people who heard him were full of wonder at his knowledge and his answers.

**48** His parents saw him and were very surprised. His mother said to him, "Son, why did you do this to us? Your father and I were very worried. We looked for you everywhere."

**49** Jesus said to them, "Why did you look for me? Did you not know that I must be in the house of my Father?"

**50** But they did not understand what he told them.

**51** Then Jesus went with them to Nazareth. He obeyed them. His mother remembered all these things carefully.

**52** Jesus grew in wisdom and in height. God and people liked him more and more.

## Chapter 3

**1** Tiberius Caesar ruled for 15 years. At that time, Pontius Pilate was the governor of Judea. Herod ruled Galilee. His brother Philip ruled Iturea and Trachonitis. Lysanias ruled Abilene.

**2** Annas and Caiaphas were the high priests. At that time, the word of God came to John, the son of Zechariah, in the desert.

**3** John went into all the area around the Jordan River. He told the people, "Change your hearts and lives, and receive baptism. Then God will forgive your sins."

**4** The prophet Isaiah wrote about John in his book: "A man shouts in the desert. He says, 'Prepare the way for the Lord. Make his roads straight.

**5** Workers will fill each valley. They will make each mountain and hill low. They will make the crooked roads straight. They will make the rough roads smooth.

**6** And all people will see how God saves.'"

**7** Many people came to John to receive baptism. John said to them, "You are like a family of snakes! Who told you to run away from the anger of God that will come?

**8** Show by your actions that you changed your hearts. Do not say to yourselves, 'Abraham is our father.' I tell you, God can make children for Abraham from these stones.

**9** The axe is now ready to cut the roots of the trees. Workers will cut down each tree that does not make good fruit. They will throw it into the fire."

**10** The people asked John, "Then what must we do?"

**11** John answered them, "If a man has 2 shirts, he must give 1 shirt to a man who has none. If a man has food, he must also share it."

**12** Tax collectors also came to receive baptism. They said to John, "Teacher, what must we do?"

**13** John said to them, "Do not take more money than the correct amount."

**14** Some soldiers also asked John, "And what must we do?" John said to them, "Do not take money from people by force. Do not accuse people falsely. Be happy with your pay."

**15** The people waited and hoped. They all asked themselves if John was the Christ.

**16** John answered all of them, "I baptize you with water. But a man who is more powerful than I will come. I am not good enough to untie his sandals. He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and with fire.

**17** He holds a tool in his hand to clean the grain. He will clean the floor where he separates the grain completely. He will put the wheat into his barn. But he will burn the useless parts in a fire that never stops."

**18** John said many other things to encourage the people. He told them the good news.

**19** John criticized Herod the ruler because Herod married Herodias, the wife of his brother. John also criticized Herod for all the other bad things that he did.

**20** Then Herod did another bad thing. He put John in prison.

**21** John baptized all the people. He also baptized Jesus. While Jesus prayed, heaven opened.

**22** The Holy Spirit came down on Jesus in the form of a dove. A voice came from heaven. The voice said, "You are my Son, whom I love. I am very pleased with you."

**23** Jesus was about 30 years old when he started his work. People thought that Jesus was the son of Joseph. Joseph was the son of Heli.

**24** Heli was the son of Matthat. Matthat was the son of Levi. Levi was the son of Melchi. Melchi was the son of Jannai. Jannai was the son of Joseph.

**25** Joseph was the son of Mattathias. Mattathias was the son of Amos. Amos was the son of Nahum. Nahum was the son of Esli. Esli was the son of Naggai.

**26** Naggai was the son of Maath. Maath was the son of Mattathias. Mattathias was the son of Semein. Semein was the son of Josech. Josech was the son of Joda.

**27** Joda was the son of Joanan. Joanan was the son of Rhesa. Rhesa was the son of Zerubbabel. Zerubbabel was the son of Shealtiel. Shealtiel was the son of Neri.

**28** Neri was the son of Melchi. Melchi was the son of Addi. Addi was the son of Cosam. Cosam was the son of Elmadam. Elmadam was the son of Er.

**29** Er was the son of Joshua. Joshua was the son of Eliezer. Eliezer was the son of Jorim. Jorim was the son of Matthat. Matthat was the son of Levi.

**30** Levi was the son of Simeon. Simeon was the son of Judah. Judah was the son of Joseph. Joseph was the son of Jonam. Jonam was the son of Eliakim.

**31** Eliakim was the son of Melea. Melea was the son of Menna. Menna was the son of Mattatha. Mattatha was the son of Nathan. Nathan was the son of David.

**32** David was the son of Jesse. Jesse was the son of Obed. Obed was the son of Boaz. Boaz was the son of Salmon. Salmon was the son of Nahshon.

**33** Nahshon was the son of Amminadab. Amminadab was the son of Ram. Ram was the son of Hezron. Hezron was the son of Perez. Perez was the son of Judah.

**34** Judah was the son of Jacob. Jacob was the son of Isaac. Isaac was the son of Abraham. Abraham was the son of Terah. Terah was the son of Nahor.

**35** Nahor was the son of Serug. Serug was the son of Reu. Reu was the son of Peleg. Peleg was the son of Eber. Eber was the son of Shelah.

**36** Shelah was the son of Cainan. Cainan was the son of Arphaxad. Arphaxad was the son of Shem. Shem was the son of Noah. Noah was the son of Lamech.

**37** Lamech was the son of Methuselah. Methuselah was the son of Enoch. Enoch was the son of Jared. Jared was the son of Mahalalel. Mahalalel was the son of Cainan.

**38** Cainan was the son of Enosh. Enosh was the son of Seth. Seth was the son of Adam. Adam was the son of God.

## Chapter 4

**1** Jesus was full of the Holy Spirit. He went back from the Jordan River. The Spirit led him into the desert.

**2** There the devil tempted him for 40 days. Jesus ate nothing during those days. When the 40 days ended, he was hungry.

**3** The devil said to Jesus, "If you are the Son of God, tell this stone to become bread."

**4** Jesus answered him, "The scripture says, 'People must not live only on bread. They must also live on each word of God.'"

**5** Then the devil took Jesus up to a high place. In a very short time, he showed Jesus all the kingdoms of the world.

**6** The devil said to him, "I will give you all this power and glory. God gave it to me, and I can give it to any person that I choose.

**7** If you worship me, then all of it will be yours."

**8** Jesus answered him, "Go away from me, Satan! The scripture says, 'Worship the Lord your God. Serve only him.'"

**9** Then the devil took Jesus to Jerusalem. He put Jesus on the highest point of the temple. He said to Jesus, "If you are the Son of God, jump down from here.

**10** The scripture says, 'God will tell his angels to keep you safe.

**11** They will hold you up with their hands. Thus you will not hit your foot against a stone.'"

**12** Jesus answered him, "The scripture also says, 'Do not test the Lord your God.'"

**13** The devil finished all his temptations. Then he went away from Jesus until a better time.

**14** Jesus went back to Galilee with the power of the Spirit. People in all the area around talked about him.

**15** He taught in their synagogues. All the people praised him.

**16** Jesus went to Nazareth, the town where he grew up. On the Sabbath day, he went into the synagogue, as was his custom. He stood up to read.

**17** A man gave him the book of the prophet Isaiah. Jesus opened the book and found the place where Isaiah wrote these words:

**18** "The Spirit of the Lord is on me. He chose me to tell the good news to poor people. He sent me to tell prisoners that they are free. He sent me to tell blind people that they will see again. He sent me to make free the people who suffer.

**19** He sent me to tell people that this is the time when the Lord will show his kindness."

**20** Jesus closed the book. He gave it back to the helper and sat down. All the people in the synagogue looked carefully at him.

**21** He started to speak to them. He said, "Today, as you heard, these words in the scripture came true."

**22** All the people spoke well of Jesus. They were full of wonder at the kind words that he said. They asked, "Is this man not the son of Joseph?"

**23** Jesus said to them, "I am sure that you will tell me this proverb: 'Doctor, heal yourself.' You will say, 'We heard about the things that you did in Capernaum. Do them here in your own town also.'"

**24** He said, "I tell you the truth. People do not accept a prophet in his own town.

**25** I tell you the truth. There were many widows in Israel in the time of Elijah. At that time, no rain fell for 3 years and 6 months. There was very little food in all the land.

**26** But God did not send Elijah to any of those widows. God sent him only to a widow in Zarephath, in the area of Sidon.

**27** There were also many people with leprosy in Israel in the time of the prophet Elisha. But Elisha did not heal any of them. He healed only Naaman, a man from Syria."

**28** All the people in the synagogue heard these things. They became very angry.

**29** They stood up and pushed Jesus out of the town. Their town was on a hill. They took him to the edge of the hill. They wanted to throw him down from the cliff.

**30** But Jesus walked through the middle of the crowd and went away.

**31** Then Jesus went down to Capernaum, a town in Galilee. He taught the people on the Sabbath day.

**32** The people were full of wonder at the things that he taught, because he spoke with authority.

**33** A man in the synagogue had an unclean spirit, a demon, in him. The man shouted with a loud voice.

**34** He said, "Leave us alone! What do you want with us, Jesus of Nazareth? Did you come to destroy us? I know who you are. You are the Holy One of God!"

**35** Jesus spoke strongly to the demon. He said, "Be quiet! Come out of the man!" The demon threw the man down in front of the people. Then the demon came out of him. The demon did not hurt the man.

**36** All the people were very surprised. They said to each other, "What are these words? He gives commands to unclean spirits with authority and power, and they come out!"

**37** The news about Jesus went into all the places in that area.

**38** Jesus left the synagogue and went into the house of Simon. The mother of the wife of Simon was sick with a high fever. The people asked Jesus to help her.

**39** Jesus stood over her. He told the fever to leave, and the fever left her. Immediately, she got up and started to serve them.

**40** When the sun went down, the people brought to Jesus all the sick people with different diseases. He put his hands on each sick person and healed them.

**41** Demons also came out of many people. The demons shouted, "You are the Christ, the Son of God!" But Jesus spoke strongly to them. He did not let them speak, because they knew that he was the Christ.

**42** At sunrise, Jesus left and went to a place where no people lived. The people looked for him. They found him and tried to stop him. They did not want him to go away from them.

**43** But Jesus said to them, "I must tell the good news about the kingdom of God in other towns also. God sent me for this purpose."

**44** Then Jesus told the message in the synagogues of Galilee.

## Chapter 5

**1** One day, Jesus stood near Lake Gennesaret. Many people pushed close to him to hear the word of God.

**2** Jesus saw 2 boats at the edge of the lake. The fishermen were out of the boats. They washed their nets.

**3** Jesus got into one of the boats. The boat was the boat of Simon. Jesus asked Simon to move the boat a short distance away from the land. Then Jesus sat down and taught the people from the boat.

**4** Jesus stopped his talk. Then he said to Simon, "Take the boat out into the deep water. Put your nets down into the water to catch fish."

**5** Simon answered, "Master, we worked hard all night and caught nothing. But because you tell me, I will put down the nets."

**6** The men did this. They caught very many fish. Their nets started to break.

**7** They made signs to their partners in the other boat. They asked them to come and help. The partners came and filled both boats with fish. The boats started to sink.

**8** Simon Peter saw this. He went down on his knees in front of Jesus. He said, "Go away from me, Lord, because I am a sinful man!"

**9** Simon and all the men with him were very surprised at the number of fish that they caught.

**10** James and John, the sons of Zebedee, were also very surprised. They were partners with Simon. Jesus said to Simon, "Do not be afraid. From now on, you will catch people."

**11** The men brought their boats to the land. They left all things and followed Jesus.

**12** Jesus was in one of the towns. A man with very bad leprosy was there. The man saw Jesus and put his face to the ground. He asked Jesus, "Lord, if you want, you can make me clean."

**13** Jesus put out his hand and touched the man. He said, "I want to. Be clean!" Immediately, the leprosy went away from the man.

**14** Jesus told him, "Do not tell anyone. But go and show yourself to the priest. Give the offering for people who become clean, as Moses commanded. This will show the people that you are well now."

**15** But the news about Jesus went out more and more. Large crowds came to hear him. They also came so that he will heal their diseases.

**16** But Jesus often went away to places where no people lived. There he prayed.

**17** One day, Jesus taught the people. Pharisees and teachers of the law sat near him. They came from each town of Galilee and Judea, and from Jerusalem. The power of the Lord was with Jesus to heal sick people.

**18** Some men carried a paralyzed man on a mat. They tried to bring him into the house and put him in front of Jesus.

**19** But they could not find a way in because of the crowd. Thus they went up on the roof. They removed some tiles and let the man down on his mat. They put him in the middle of the crowd, in front of Jesus.

**20** Jesus saw their faith. He said, "Man, I forgive your sins."

**21** The teachers of the law and the Pharisees started to think, "Who is this man? He speaks against God! Only God can forgive sins."

**22** Jesus knew their thoughts. He answered them, "Why do you think these things in your hearts?

**23** Which is easier to say: 'I forgive your sins,' or 'Get up and walk'?

**24** But I will show you that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins." Then he spoke to the paralyzed man. He said, "I tell you, get up. Take your mat and go home."

**25** Immediately, the man stood up in front of them. He picked up his mat and went home. He praised God.

**26** All the people were very surprised. They praised God. They were afraid and said, "Today we saw very unusual things."

**27** After this, Jesus went out. He saw a tax collector with the name Levi. Levi sat at his tax table. Jesus said to him, "Follow me."

**28** Levi got up, left all things, and followed Jesus.

**29** Then Levi gave a large dinner for Jesus at his house. Many tax collectors and other people ate with them.

**30** The Pharisees and their teachers of the law complained to the disciples of Jesus. They said, "Why do you eat and drink with tax collectors and sinners?"

**31** Jesus answered them, "Healthy people do not need a doctor. Only sick people need a doctor.

**32** I did not come to call righteous people. I came to call sinners to change their hearts and lives."

**33** They said to Jesus, "The disciples of John often fast and pray. The disciples of the Pharisees do the same. But your disciples eat and drink."

**34** Jesus said to them, "Can you make the friends of the bridegroom fast while the bridegroom is with them? No.

**35** But a time will come when people will take the bridegroom away from them. Then they will fast."

**36** Jesus also told them a parable (a story with a lesson). He said, "No one cuts a piece of cloth from a new coat to repair an old coat. If he does, he tears the new coat. Also, the new piece will not match the old coat.

**37** No one puts new wine into old wineskins. If he does, the new wine will break the skins. The wine will flow out, and the wineskins will become useless.

**38** You must put new wine into new wineskins. Then both stay good.

**39** A person who drinks old wine does not want new wine. He says, 'The old wine is better.'"

## Chapter 6

**1** On a Sabbath day, Jesus walked through some fields of grain. His disciples picked some grain. They rubbed the grain in their hands and ate it.

**2** Some Pharisees said, "Why do you do this? The law does not permit it on the Sabbath day."

**3** Jesus answered them, "Did you not read what David did? He and his men were hungry.

**4** David went into the house of God. He took the holy bread and ate it. He also gave some to his men. The law permits only the priests to eat this bread."

**5** Then Jesus said to them, "The Son of Man is Lord of the Sabbath."

**6** On another Sabbath day, Jesus went into the synagogue and taught. A man with a paralyzed right hand was there.

**7** The teachers of the law and the Pharisees watched Jesus carefully. They wanted to see if he will heal on the Sabbath day. They wanted a reason to accuse him.

**8** But Jesus knew their thoughts. He said to the man with the paralyzed hand, "Get up and stand here in front of the people." The man got up and stood there.

**9** Then Jesus said to them, "I ask you a question. On the Sabbath day, does the law permit us to do good or to do evil? Does it permit us to save a life or to destroy it?"

**10** Jesus looked around at all of them. Then he said to the man, "Hold out your hand." The man did this. His hand became healthy again, the same as his other hand.

**11** But the Pharisees and the teachers of the law became very angry. They talked to each other about what they can do to Jesus.

**12** At that time, Jesus went up on a mountain to pray. He prayed to God all night.

**13** In the morning, he called his disciples to him. He chose 12 of them. He gave them the name apostles.

**14** He chose Simon, whom he also gave the name Peter, and Andrew, the brother of Simon. He chose James, John, Philip, and Bartholomew.

**15** He chose Matthew, Thomas, James the son of Alphaeus, and Simon, whom people called the Zealot.

**16** He chose Judas the son of James, and Judas Iscariot. Judas Iscariot later betrayed Jesus.

**17** Jesus came down from the mountain with the apostles. He stood on a flat area. A large group of his disciples was there. A very large crowd of people was also there. They came from all of Judea, from Jerusalem, and from the coast near Tyre and Sidon. They came to hear Jesus and to become well from their diseases.

**18** Jesus also healed the people whom unclean spirits caused to suffer.

**19** All the people tried to touch Jesus, because power came out from him. This power healed all of them.

**20** Jesus looked at his disciples and said, "God blesses you who are poor. The kingdom of God is yours.

**21** God blesses you who are hungry now. You will have enough food. God blesses you who cry now. You will laugh.

**22** People will hate you because of the Son of Man. They will push you out of their group and insult you. They will say that you are evil. When they do these things, God blesses you.

**23** At that time, be glad and jump with joy. You will get a large reward in heaven. The ancestors of these people did the same things to the prophets.

**24** But it will be very bad for you who are rich. You already got your comfort.

**25** It will be very bad for you who have much food now. You will be hungry. It will be very bad for you who laugh now. You will be sad and cry.

**26** It will be very bad for you when all people speak well of you. Their ancestors spoke well of the false prophets in the same way.

**27** But I tell you who listen to me: Love your enemies. Do good to the people who hate you.

**28** Bless the people who curse you. Pray for the people who treat you badly.

**29** If a person hits you on one side of the face, let him also hit the other side. If a person takes your coat, let him also take your shirt.

**30** Give to each person who asks you for something. If a person takes your things, do not ask for them back.

**31** Do to other people the things that you want them to do to you.

**32** If you love only the people who love you, why must God praise you? Even sinners love the people who love them.

**33** If you do good only to the people who do good to you, why must God praise you? Even sinners do that.

**34** If you lend money only to the people who can pay you back, why must God praise you? Even sinners lend to sinners. They expect to get back the same amount.

**35** But love your enemies. Do good to them. Lend to them and do not expect to get anything back. Then you will get a large reward. You will be children of the Most High God, because he is kind to ungrateful and evil people.

**36** Be merciful, as your Father is merciful.

**37** Do not judge other people, and God will not judge you. Do not condemn other people, and God will not condemn you. Forgive other people, and God will forgive you.

**38** Give, and you will get much. People will give you a full measure. They will press it down and shake it. It will be so full that it flows over into your lap. God will measure to you with the same measure that you use for other people."

**39** Jesus told them a parable: "Can a blind man lead a blind man? No. Both men will fall into a hole.

**40** A student is not more important than his teacher. But when a student learns all things, he will be like his teacher.

**41** Why do you look at a small piece of dust in the eye of your brother? You do not see the large piece of wood in your own eye.

**42** You cannot say to your brother, 'Brother, let me take the dust out of your eye.' You do not see the wood in your own eye! You hypocrite! First take the wood out of your own eye. Then you will see clearly to take the dust out of the eye of your brother.

**43** A good tree does not make bad fruit. And a bad tree does not make good fruit.

**44** You know each tree by its fruit. People do not pick figs from thorn bushes. They do not pick grapes from bushes with thorns.

**45** A good man has good things in his heart. Thus he brings out good things. An evil man has evil things in his heart. Thus he brings out evil things. The mouth speaks the things that fill the heart.

**46** Why do you call me 'Lord, Lord,' but you do not do the things that I tell you?

**47** I will show you what a person is like when he comes to me, hears my words, and obeys them.

**48** He is like a man who builds a house. He digs deep and puts the foundation on rock. A flood comes and the water hits the house strongly. But the water cannot move the house, because the man built it well on the rock.

**49** But a person who hears my words and does not obey them is like a different man. This man builds a house on the ground with no foundation. The water hits the house, and immediately the house falls down. The destruction of that house is complete."

## Chapter 7

**1** Jesus finished all his words to the people. Then he went into Capernaum.

**2** A centurion (an army officer) lived there. He had a servant whom he loved very much. The servant was sick and almost dead.

**3** The centurion heard about Jesus. He sent some Jewish elders to Jesus. He asked Jesus to come and heal his servant.

**4** The elders came to Jesus. They asked him strongly to help. They said, "This man is worthy of your help.

**5** He loves our nation. He built our synagogue for us."

**6** Thus Jesus went with them. He was near the house when the centurion sent some friends to him. The friends said, "Lord, do not trouble yourself. I am not worthy for you to come into my house.

**7** Thus I did not think that I was worthy to come to you. But only say a word, and my servant will become well.

**8** I also have officers above me, and I have soldiers under me. I tell one soldier, 'Go,' and he goes. I tell another soldier, 'Come,' and he comes. I tell my servant, 'Do this,' and he does it."

**9** Jesus heard these things and felt wonder about the centurion. He turned to the crowd that followed him. He said, "I tell you, I did not find such great faith even in Israel."

**10** The friends went back to the house. They found that the servant was healthy.

**11** The next day, Jesus went to a town with the name Nain. His disciples and a large crowd went with him.

**12** Jesus came near the gate of the town. Some people carried out a dead man. He was the only son of his mother, and she was a widow. A large crowd from the town was with her.

**13** The Lord saw her and felt sorry for her. He said to her, "Do not cry."

**14** Jesus went to the frame that held the body, and he touched it. The men who carried it stopped. Jesus said, "Young man, I tell you, get up!"

**15** The dead man sat up and started to speak. Jesus gave him back to his mother.

**16** All the people were afraid. They praised God. They said, "A great prophet came among us! God came to help his people!"

**17** This news about Jesus went through all of Judea and all the area around it.

**18** The disciples of John told John about all these things.

**19** John called 2 of his disciples. He sent them to the Lord to ask, "Are you the man who will come? Or must we wait for another man?"

**20** The men came to Jesus. They said, "John the Baptist sent us to you. He asks, 'Are you the man who will come? Or must we wait for another man?'"

**21** At that time, Jesus healed many people of their sicknesses, diseases, and evil spirits. He gave sight to many blind people.

**22** Jesus answered them, "Go and tell John the things that you saw and heard. Blind people see. Lame people walk. People with leprosy become clean. Deaf people hear. Dead people come back to life. Poor people hear the good news.

**23** God blesses the person who does not lose faith because of me."

**24** The messengers of John went away. Then Jesus started to speak to the crowd about John. He said, "What did you go out into the desert to see? Did you go to see a reed that the wind moves? No.

**25** What did you go out to see? Did you go to see a man in fine clothes? No. People who wear beautiful clothes and live in luxury are in the palaces of kings.

**26** Then what did you go out to see? A prophet? Yes, and I tell you, John is more than a prophet.

**27** The scripture says this about John: 'Look, I will send my messenger in front of you. He will prepare the way for you.'

**28** I tell you, no person born of a woman is greater than John. But the least important person in the kingdom of God is greater than John."

**29** All the people heard this, even the tax collectors. They agreed that God was right, because John baptized them.

**30** But the Pharisees and the experts in the law refused the plan of God for them. They did not let John baptize them.

**31** Jesus said, "How can I describe the people of this generation? What are they like?

**32** They are like children who sit in the market and call to each other. They say, 'We played music on the flute for you, but you did not dance. We sang sad songs, but you did not cry.'

**33** John the Baptist came. He did not eat bread or drink wine. And you say, 'He has a demon in him.'

**34** The Son of Man came. He eats and drinks. And you say, 'Look at this man! He eats too much and drinks too much wine. He is a friend of tax collectors and sinners.'

**35** But the results of wisdom show that wisdom is correct."

**36** One of the Pharisees asked Jesus to eat with him. Jesus went into the house of the Pharisee and sat down at the table.

**37** A sinful woman lived in that town. She learned that Jesus ate at the house of the Pharisee. She brought a jar of perfume. The jar was of alabaster stone.

**38** She stood behind Jesus at his feet and cried. Her tears made his feet wet, and she dried them with her hair. She kissed his feet and put the perfume on them.

**39** The Pharisee who invited Jesus saw this. He thought, "If this man is a prophet, then he knows what type of woman touches him. She is a sinner."

**40** Jesus said to him, "Simon, I have something to tell you." Simon said, "Teacher, tell me."

**41** Jesus said, "2 men owed money to a lender. One man owed 500 silver coins. The other man owed 50 silver coins.

**42** Neither man could pay. Thus the lender cancelled the debts of both men. Tell me, which man will love the lender more?"

**43** Simon answered, "I think that it is the man with the larger debt." Jesus said to him, "Your answer is correct."

**44** Then Jesus turned to the woman. He said to Simon, "Do you see this woman? I came into your house. You did not give me water for my feet. But she washed my feet with her tears and dried them with her hair.

**45** You did not kiss me. But from the time I came in, she did not stop. She continued to kiss my feet.

**46** You did not put oil on my head. But she put perfume on my feet.

**47** Thus I tell you, God forgives her many sins. This is shown because she loved much. But a person who gets only a little forgiveness loves only a little."

**48** Then Jesus said to the woman, "I forgive your sins."

**49** The people who sat at the table with him started to say to each other, "Who is this man? He even forgives sins!"

**50** Jesus said to the woman, "Your faith saved you. Go in peace."

## Chapter 8

**1** After this, Jesus traveled through each town and village. He told the good news about the kingdom of God. The 12 apostles were with him.

**2** Some women were also with him. Jesus healed these women of evil spirits and diseases. One woman was Mary, whom people called Magdalene. Jesus forced 7 demons out of her.

**3** Joanna, the wife of Chuza, was also there. Chuza was the manager of the house of Herod. Susanna and many other women were also there. These women used their own money to help Jesus and the apostles.

**4** A large crowd came together. People came to Jesus from each town. Then Jesus told them a parable (a story with a lesson):

**5** "A farmer went out to plant his seed. While he threw the seed, some seed fell on the path. People walked on it, and the birds ate it.

**6** Some seed fell on rock. It started to grow, but then it died because it had no water.

**7** Some seed fell among thorn bushes. The thorns grew with the plants and stopped their growth.

**8** Other seed fell on good soil. It grew and made 100 times more grain." When Jesus said these things, he called out, "If you can hear, then listen!"

**9** His disciples asked him, "What does this parable mean?"

**10** Jesus said, "God lets you know the secrets of the kingdom of God. But I speak to other people in parables. Thus they look but they do not see. They hear but they do not understand.

**11** This is the meaning of the parable. The seed is the word of God.

**12** The seed on the path is like the people who hear the word. Then the devil comes and takes the word from their hearts. Thus they do not believe and God does not save them.

**13** The seed on the rock is like the people who hear the word and accept it with joy. But they have no root. They believe for a short time. But when a test comes, they fall away.

**14** The seed among the thorn bushes is like the people who hear the word. But as they live, worries, money, and pleasures of this life stop their growth. They do not make mature fruit.

**15** The seed on the good soil is like the people with honest and good hearts. They hear the word and keep it. They are patient and make fruit.

**16** No person lights a lamp and then covers it with a bowl. No person puts a lamp under a bed. A person puts a lamp on a stand. Then the people who come in can see the light.

**17** All hidden things will become known. All secret things will become known and people will see them.

**18** Thus be careful how you listen. A person who has something will get more. But a person who has nothing will lose even the thing that he thinks he has."

**19** The mother and brothers of Jesus came to see him. But they could not get near him because of the crowd.

**20** Someone told Jesus, "Your mother and your brothers stand outside. They want to see you."

**21** Jesus answered them, "My mother and my brothers are the people who hear the word of God and obey it."

**22** One day, Jesus got into a boat with his disciples. He said to them, "We will go across to the other side of the lake." Thus they started to go.

**23** While they sailed, Jesus went to sleep. A strong wind storm came down on the lake. Water filled the boat. They were in danger.

**24** The disciples went to Jesus and woke him. They said, "Master, Master, we will die!" Jesus got up. He told the wind and the waves to stop. The storm stopped, and the lake became calm.

**25** Jesus said to the disciples, "Where is your faith?" They were afraid and surprised. They said to each other, "Who is this man? He gives commands to the wind and the water, and they obey him!"

**26** They sailed to the area of the Gadarenes, across the lake from Galilee.

**27** Jesus got out of the boat onto the land. A man from the town came to him. Demons were in this man for a long time. He did not wear clothes. He did not live in a house, but in the tombs.

**28** The man saw Jesus and shouted. He fell down in front of Jesus. He said with a loud voice, "What do you want with me, Jesus, Son of the Most High God? Please, do not cause me pain!"

**29** He said this because Jesus told the unclean spirit to come out of the man. Many times, the spirit took control of him. People put chains on his hands and feet and guarded him. But he broke the chains. Then the demon forced him into the desert.

**30** Jesus asked him, "What is your name?" He said, "Legion." He said this because many demons were in him.

**31** The demons asked Jesus strongly not to send them into the bottomless pit.

**32** A large group of pigs ate food on the hill near there. The demons asked Jesus to let them go into the pigs. Jesus let them go.

**33** The demons came out of the man and went into the pigs. The pigs ran down the steep hill into the lake and drowned.

**34** The men who took care of the pigs saw what happened. They ran away. They told the people in the town and in the country.

**35** The people went out to see what happened. They came to Jesus. They found the man without the demons. He sat at the feet of Jesus. He wore clothes and his mind was normal. The people were afraid.

**36** The people who saw these things told the others how Jesus healed the man with the demons.

**37** All the people from the area of the Gadarenes asked Jesus to go away from them. They were very afraid. Thus Jesus got into the boat and went back.

**38** The man without the demons asked Jesus to let him go with him. But Jesus sent him away. Jesus said,

**39** "Go back to your home. Tell people the great things that God did for you." Thus the man went away. He told all the people in the town about the great things that Jesus did for him.

**40** Jesus went back across the lake. A crowd was glad to see him, because they all waited for him.

**41** A man with the name Jairus came. He was a leader of the synagogue. He fell down at the feet of Jesus. He asked Jesus to come to his house.

**42** He had only 1 daughter, about 12 years old. She was almost dead. While Jesus went with Jairus, the crowds pushed against him.

**43** A woman in the crowd had a flow of blood for 12 years. She spent all her money on doctors, but no doctor could heal her.

**44** She came behind Jesus and touched the edge of his coat. Immediately, her flow of blood stopped.

**45** Jesus said, "Who touched me?" All the people said that they did not touch him. Peter said, "Master, the crowds push against you from all sides."

**46** But Jesus said, "Someone touched me. I know that power went out from me."

**47** The woman knew that she could not hide. She came to Jesus and shook with fear. She fell down in front of him. In front of all the people, she told why she touched him. She told how she became well immediately.

**48** Jesus said to her, "Daughter, your faith healed you. Go in peace."

**49** While Jesus still spoke, a man came from the house of the synagogue leader. The man said, "Your daughter is dead. Do not trouble the Teacher any more."

**50** Jesus heard this. He said to Jairus, "Do not be afraid. Only believe, and she will become well."

**51** Jesus came to the house. He let only Peter, John, James, and the father and mother of the girl go in with him.

**52** All the people cried and were sad about the girl. But Jesus said, "Do not cry. She is not dead. She is only asleep."

**53** The people laughed at Jesus, because they knew that the girl was dead.

**54** But Jesus took her hand and called, "Child, get up!"

**55** Her spirit came back, and immediately she stood up. Jesus told them to give her food.

**56** Her parents were very surprised. But Jesus told them not to tell anyone about this thing.

## Chapter 9

**1** Jesus called the 12 disciples together. He gave them power and authority over all demons. He also gave them power to heal diseases.

**2** He sent them to tell people about the kingdom of God and to heal sick people.

**3** He said to them, "Take nothing for your journey. Do not take a stick, a bag, bread, or money. Do not take 2 shirts.

**4** When you go into a house, stay there until you leave that town.

**5** If the people of a town do not accept you, leave that town. Shake the dust off your feet. This will show that they did wrong."

**6** The disciples left. They went through the villages. They told the good news and healed people everywhere.

**7** Herod the ruler heard about all the things that Jesus did. Herod did not understand, because some people said, "John came back to life from the dead."

**8** Other people said, "Elijah came back." And other people said, "One of the old prophets came back to life."

**9** Herod said, "I cut off the head of John. Then who is this man? I hear these things about him." Herod tried to see Jesus.

**10** The apostles came back. They told Jesus all the things that they did. Jesus took them with him, and they went away alone to a town with the name Bethsaida.

**11** But the crowds learned about it and followed Jesus. Jesus welcomed them. He spoke to them about the kingdom of God. He healed the people who were ill.

**12** Late in the day, the 12 apostles came to Jesus. They said, "Send the crowd away. Then they can go to the villages and farms near here to find food and a place to sleep. No people live in this area."

**13** But Jesus said to them, "You give them something to eat." They said, "We have only 5 loaves of bread and 2 fish. We cannot feed all these people if we do not go and buy food."

**14** There were about 5,000 men there. Jesus said to his disciples, "Tell the people to sit down in groups of about 50 people."

**15** The disciples did this. All the people sat down.

**16** Jesus took the 5 loaves and the 2 fish. He looked up to heaven and blessed the food. He broke it into pieces. He gave the pieces to the disciples to give to the crowd.

**17** All the people ate and had enough food. The disciples picked up 12 baskets of the pieces that remained.

**18** One time, Jesus prayed alone. His disciples were with him. He asked them, "Who do the people say that I am?"

**19** They answered, "Some people say that you are John the Baptist. Other people say that you are Elijah. And other people say that one of the old prophets came back to life."

**20** Jesus said to them, "But who do you say that I am?" Peter answered, "You are the Christ from God."

**21** Jesus gave them a strong order. He told them not to tell this to anyone.

**22** He said, "The Son of Man must suffer many things. The elders, the chief priests, and the teachers of the law will reject him. They will kill him. But on the third day, God will bring him back to life."

**23** Then Jesus said to all the people, "If a person wants to follow me, he must forget about his own wants. He must take his cross each day and follow me.

**24** A person who wants to save his life will lose it. But a person who loses his life because of me will save it.

**25** A person can get all the world but lose his own self. Then he gets no benefit.

**26** A person can feel shame about me and my words. Then the Son of Man will feel shame about that person when he comes. He will come in his glory and in the glory of the Father and of the holy angels.

**27** I tell you the truth. Some people who stand here will not die before they see the kingdom of God."

**28** About 8 days after Jesus said these things, he took Peter, John, and James with him. He went up on a mountain to pray.

**29** While Jesus prayed, his face changed. His clothes became bright white.

**30** 2 men spoke with him. They were Moses and Elijah.

**31** They came in glory. They spoke about the death of Jesus. Jesus will complete his death in Jerusalem.

**32** Peter and the men with him were very sleepy. But they woke up and saw the glory of Jesus. They also saw the 2 men who stood with him.

**33** The 2 men started to leave Jesus. Then Peter said to Jesus, "Master, it is good that we are here. We will make 3 shelters, 1 for you, 1 for Moses, and 1 for Elijah." Peter did not know what he said.

**34** While Peter said these things, a cloud came and covered them. The disciples were afraid when the cloud covered them.

**35** A voice came from the cloud. The voice said, "This is my Son, whom I love. Listen to him!"

**36** After the voice stopped, only Jesus was there. The disciples did not speak about this. At that time, they told no one about the things that they saw.

**37** The next day, they came down from the mountain. A large crowd met Jesus.

**38** A man in the crowd shouted, "Teacher, please look at my son. He is my only child.

**39** A spirit takes control of him. Suddenly he shouts. The spirit shakes him violently, and foam comes from his mouth. The spirit hurts him and does not leave him.

**40** I asked your disciples to force the spirit out of him. But they could not."

**41** Jesus answered, "You people today have no faith, and you do wrong things. How long must I stay with you? How long must I be patient with you? Bring your son here."

**42** While the boy came to Jesus, the demon threw him down and shook him violently. But Jesus spoke strongly to the unclean spirit. He healed the boy and gave him back to his father.

**43** All the people felt wonder at the great power of God. All the people wondered at all the things that Jesus did. At that time, he spoke to his disciples.

**44** He said, "Listen carefully to these words and remember them. People will give the Son of Man to the power of other people."

**45** But the disciples did not understand these words. God hid the meaning from them. Thus they could not understand it. They were afraid to ask Jesus about it.

**46** The disciples started to argue about which of them was the most important.

**47** Jesus knew their thoughts. He took a small child and put the child next to him.

**48** He said to them, "If a person accepts this child in my name, that person accepts me. And if a person accepts me, that person accepts God, who sent me. The least important person among you is the most important person."

**49** John said, "Master, we saw a man who used your name to force demons out of people. We told him to stop, because he is not one of our group."

**50** Jesus said to him, "Do not stop him. A person who is not against you is for you."

**51** The time came near for God to take Jesus up to heaven. Jesus decided firmly to go to Jerusalem.

**52** He sent messengers in front of him. They went into a village in Samaria to make things ready for him.

**53** But the people there did not welcome Jesus, because he traveled to Jerusalem.

**54** The disciples James and John saw this. They said, "Lord, do you want us to tell fire to come down from heaven and destroy these people, as Elijah did?"

**55** But Jesus turned and spoke strongly to them. He said, "You do not know what type of spirit you have.

**56** The Son of Man did not come to destroy the lives of people. He came to save them." Then they went to another village.

**57** While they traveled on the road, a man said to Jesus, "Lord, I will follow you to all the places that you go."

**58** Jesus said to him, "Foxes have holes, and birds have nests. But the Son of Man has no place to rest his head."

**59** Jesus said to another man, "Follow me." But the man said, "Lord, first let me go and bury my father."

**60** Jesus said to him, "Let the spiritually dead people bury their own dead. But you must go and tell people about the kingdom of God."

**61** Another man said, "Lord, I will follow you. But first let me go and say goodbye to my family."

**62** Jesus said to him, "A person who starts to plow a field and then looks back is not ready for the kingdom of God."

## Chapter 10

**1** After this, the Lord chose 70 other followers. He sent them out in groups of 2. He sent them in front of him to each town and place where he will go.

**2** He said to them, "There is a large harvest, but there are only a few workers. Thus pray to the Lord of the harvest. Ask him to send out workers into his harvest.

**3** Go now. Listen. I send you out like lambs among wolves.

**4** Do not carry a money bag, a travel bag, or sandals. Do not stop to greet people on the road.

**5** When you go into a house, first say, 'Peace to this house.'

**6** If a person of peace lives there, your peace will stay with him. If not, your peace will come back to you.

**7** Stay in that house. Eat and drink the things that the people give you. A worker must get his pay. Do not move from house to house.

**8** When you go into a town and the people welcome you, eat the food that they give you.

**9** Heal the sick people in that town. Tell them, 'The kingdom of God is near to you.'

**10** But sometimes you will go into a town and the people will not welcome you. Then go out into the streets of that town and say,

**11** 'We wipe off against you even the dust of your town that is on our feet. But be sure of this: the kingdom of God is near.'

**12** I tell you, on the day of judgment, the punishment for Sodom will be less than the punishment for that town.

**13** It will be very bad for you, Chorazin! It will be very bad for you, Bethsaida! I did many miracles in you. If I did those miracles in Tyre and Sidon, the people there will change their hearts quickly. They will wear rough cloth and sit in ashes to show their sorrow.

**14** At the judgment, the punishment for Tyre and Sidon will be less than your punishment.

**15** And you, Capernaum, do you think that God will lift you up to heaven? No. God will throw you down to hell.

**16** A person who listens to you listens to me. A person who rejects you rejects me. And a person who rejects me rejects God, who sent me."

**17** The 70 followers came back with joy. They said, "Lord, even the demons obey us when we use your name!"

**18** Jesus said to them, "I saw Satan fall from heaven like lightning.

**19** Listen. I gave you authority to walk on snakes and scorpions. I gave you authority over all the power of the enemy. Nothing will hurt you.

**20** But do not be glad because the spirits obey you. Be glad because God wrote your names in heaven."

**21** At that time, the Holy Spirit filled Jesus with joy. Jesus said, "Father, Lord of heaven and earth, I thank you. You hid these things from wise and educated people. But you showed them to people who are like little children. Yes, Father, you wanted this to happen.

**22** My Father gave all things to me. No person knows who the Son is, only the Father. And no person knows who the Father is, only the Son. And the Son can choose to show the Father to other people."

**23** Then Jesus turned to his disciples. He said to them alone, "God blesses you because you see the things that you see.

**24** I tell you, many prophets and kings wanted to see the things that you see, but they did not see them. They wanted to hear the things that you hear, but they did not hear them."

**25** Then an expert in the law stood up to test Jesus. He said, "Teacher, what must I do to get eternal life?"

**26** Jesus said to him, "What does the law say? What do you read there?"

**27** The man answered, "Love the Lord your God with all your heart, all your soul, all your strength, and all your mind. Also, love your neighbor as you love yourself."

**28** Jesus said to him, "Your answer is correct. Do this, and you will live."

**29** But the man wanted to show that he was right. Thus he said to Jesus, "And who is my neighbor?"

**30** Jesus answered, "A man went down from Jerusalem to Jericho. Robbers attacked him. They took his clothes and hit him. Then they went away and left him almost dead.

**31** A priest went down that road at that time. He saw the man, but he went past on the other side of the road.

**32** A Levite also came to that place. He looked at the man. But he also went past on the other side of the road.

**33** Then a Samaritan traveled on that road and came to the man. He saw the man and felt sorry for him.

**34** He went to the man. He put oil and wine on the wounds of the man and put bandages on them. Then he put the man on his own donkey. He took him to an inn and took care of him.

**35** The next day, the Samaritan took out 2 silver coins. He gave them to the owner of the inn. He said, 'Take care of this man. If you spend more money, I will pay you when I come back.'"

**36** Then Jesus asked, "Which of these 3 men was a neighbor to the man whom the robbers attacked?"

**37** The expert in the law said, "The man who showed mercy to him." Then Jesus said to him, "Go and do the same."

**38** While Jesus and his disciples traveled, Jesus went into a village. A woman with the name Martha welcomed him into her house.

**39** Martha had a sister with the name Mary. Mary sat at the feet of the Lord and listened to his words.

**40** But Martha was busy with all the work. She came to Jesus and said, "Lord, do you not care that my sister left me to do all the work alone? Tell her to help me!"

**41** The Lord answered her, "Martha, Martha, you have much worry and trouble about many things.

**42** But only 1 thing is necessary. Mary chose the best thing. No person will take it away from her."

## Chapter 11

**1** One time, Jesus prayed in a place. When he finished, one of his disciples said to him, "Lord, teach us to pray, as John taught his disciples."

**2** Jesus said to them, "When you pray, say: 'Father in heaven, let all people respect your name as holy. Let your kingdom come. Let people on earth do what you want, as in heaven.

**3** Give us the food that we need each day.

**4** Forgive our sins, because we also forgive each person who does wrong to us. Do not lead us into temptation, but save us from evil.'"

**5** Then Jesus said to them, "Think about this. You go to your friend at midnight. You say to him, 'Friend, lend me 3 loaves of bread.

**6** A friend of mine came to my house from a journey. I have no food to give him.'

**7** Your friend answers from inside the house, 'Do not trouble me. The door is already locked. My children and I are in bed. I cannot get up and give you anything.'

**8** I tell you, maybe he will not get up because you are his friend. But he will get up because you continue to ask. Then he will give you all the bread that you need.

**9** Thus I tell you: Ask, and God will give to you. Look, and you will find. Knock, and God will open the door for you.

**10** Each person who asks will get. Each person who looks will find. And God will open the door for each person who knocks.

**11** You fathers, if your son asks for bread, will you give him a stone? If he asks for a fish, will you give him a snake?

**12** If he asks for an egg, will you give him a scorpion? No.

**13** You are evil, but you know how to give good gifts to your children. Thus your Father in heaven will give the Holy Spirit to the people who ask him. He will give much more than you give."

**14** One time, Jesus forced a demon out of a man. The demon caused the man to be unable to speak. When the demon went out, the man spoke. The crowd felt wonder.

**15** But some people said, "He forces out demons with the power of Beelzebul, the ruler of the demons."

**16** Other people wanted to test Jesus. They asked him for a sign from heaven.

**17** But Jesus knew their thoughts. He said to them, "A kingdom that fights against itself will come to ruin. A family that fights against itself will fall.

**18** If Satan fights against himself, then his kingdom cannot continue. You say that I force out demons with the power of Beelzebul.

**19** If I use the power of Beelzebul to force out demons, then whose power do your followers use? Thus your own followers will show that you are wrong.

**20** But I use the power of God to force out demons. Thus the kingdom of God came to you.

**21** A strong man with weapons guards his house. Then his property is safe.

**22** But a stronger man can attack him and defeat him. Then the stronger man takes away the weapons that the first man trusted. He takes the property of the first man and divides it.

**23** A person who is not with me is against me. A person who does not gather with me scatters.

**24** An unclean spirit comes out of a person. It goes through dry places and looks for a place to rest. It finds no place. Then it says, 'I will go back to the house that I left.'

**25** It goes back and finds the house clean and in good order.

**26** Then the spirit goes and gets 7 other spirits. These spirits are more evil than the first spirit. They go into the person and live there. Then the person is in a worse condition than before."

**27** While Jesus said these things, a woman in the crowd called out to him. She said, "God blesses the mother who gave birth to you and fed you."

**28** But Jesus said, "God blesses more the people who hear the word of God and obey it."

**29** The crowds became larger. Jesus started to say, "The people of this generation are evil. They ask for a sign. But God will give them no sign, only the sign of the prophet Jonah.

**30** Jonah was a sign to the people of Nineveh. In the same way, the Son of Man will be a sign to the people of this generation.

**31** On the day of judgment, the Queen of the South will stand up with the people of this generation. She will show that they are guilty. She came from very far away to hear the wisdom of Solomon. And now a man who is greater than Solomon is here.

**32** On the day of judgment, the people of Nineveh will stand up with this generation. They will show that this generation is guilty. The people of Nineveh changed their hearts when Jonah spoke to them. And now a man who is greater than Jonah is here.

**33** No person lights a lamp and puts it in a hidden place or under a bowl. He puts it on a stand. Then the people who come in can see the light.

**34** Your eye is the lamp of your body. When your eye is good, all your body is full of light. But when your eye is bad, your body is full of darkness.

**35** Thus be careful. Make sure that the light in you is not darkness.

**36** If all your body is full of light, with no dark part, then all of it will be bright. It will be as when a lamp shines its light on you."

**37** After Jesus spoke, a Pharisee asked him to eat with him. Jesus went in and sat down at the table.

**38** The Pharisee saw that Jesus did not wash first before the meal. The Pharisee felt wonder.

**39** The Lord said to him, "You Pharisees clean the outside of the cup and the plate. But inside, you are full of greed and evil.

**40** You foolish people! God made the outside. He also made the inside.

**41** Give to poor people the things that are inside the cup and plate. Then all things will be clean for you.

**42** But it will be very bad for you Pharisees! You give God 1 tenth of your mint, your rue, and all your other garden plants. But you do not do justice, and you do not love God. You must do these things, and also continue to do the other things.

**43** It will be very bad for you Pharisees! You love the most important seats in the synagogues. You love it when people greet you with respect in the markets.

**44** It will be very bad for you! You are like graves with no marks on them. People walk on them and do not know it."

**45** One of the experts in the law said to Jesus, "Teacher, when you say these things, you insult us also."

**46** Jesus said, "It will be very bad for you experts in the law also! You put heavy loads on people. The loads are very hard to carry. But you do not use even 1 finger to help them.

**47** It will be very bad for you! You build tombs for the prophets. But your ancestors killed those prophets.

**48** Thus you show that you agree with the things that your ancestors did. They killed the prophets, and you build their tombs.

**49** Thus God in his wisdom said, 'I will send prophets and apostles to them. They will kill some of them and they will treat others badly.'

**50** Thus God will punish the people of this generation for the deaths of all the prophets since the start of the world.

**51** He will punish them for the death of Abel and for all the deaths until the death of Zechariah. People killed Zechariah between the altar and the temple. I tell you the truth, God will punish this generation for all these deaths.

**52** It will be very bad for you experts in the law! You took away the key to knowledge. You did not go in yourselves. And you stopped the people who tried to go in."

**53** Jesus went out from there. Then the teachers of the law and the Pharisees started to attack him strongly with words. They asked him many questions.

**54** They tried to catch him. They wanted him to say something wrong so that they could accuse him.

## Chapter 12

**1** At that time, thousands of people came together. There were so many that they walked on each other. Jesus first spoke to his disciples. He said, "Be careful of the yeast of the Pharisees. This yeast is hypocrisy.

**2** All hidden things will become known. All secret things will become known.

**3** Thus people will hear in the light the things that you said in the dark. People will shout from the roofs the things that you said quietly in private rooms.

**4** My friends, I tell you, do not be afraid of people who kill the body. After that, they can do nothing more.

**5** I will show you whom you must fear. Fear God. After he kills, he has authority to throw you into hell. Yes, I tell you, fear him.

**6** People sell 5 sparrows for 2 small coins. But God does not forget even 1 of them.

**7** God even knows the number of the hairs on your head. Do not be afraid. You are worth more than many sparrows.

**8** I tell you, a person can say in front of other people that he believes in me. Then the Son of Man will say in front of the angels of God that this person is his.

**9** But a person can say in front of other people that he does not know me. Then the Son of Man will say in front of the angels of God that he does not know that person.

**10** God will forgive a person who says something against the Son of Man. But God will not forgive a person who speaks evil against the Holy Spirit.

**11** People will bring you to the synagogues and to the rulers and the officials. Do not worry about how you will defend yourselves or what you will say.

**12** At that time, the Holy Spirit will teach you the things that you must say."

**13** A man in the crowd said to Jesus, "Teacher, tell my brother to divide the property of our father with me."

**14** But Jesus said to him, "Man, no person made me a judge over you. No person told me to divide your property."

**15** Then Jesus said to them, "Be careful! Guard yourselves against all types of greed. The life of a person is not the same as the number of things that he has."

**16** Then Jesus told them a parable: "The land of a rich man grew very many crops.

**17** The rich man thought to himself, 'What will I do? I have no place to keep all my crops.'

**18** Then he said, 'I will do this. I will pull down my barns and build larger barns. There I will keep all my grain and my other things.

**19** Then I will say to myself, "You have many good things for many years. Rest, eat, drink, and be happy."'

**20** But God said to him, 'You foolish man! Tonight you will die. Then who will get the things that you prepared for yourself?'

**21** This is what will happen to a person who keeps riches for himself but is not rich toward God."

**22** Then Jesus said to his disciples, "Thus I tell you: Do not worry about your life, about the food that you will eat. Do not worry about your body, about the clothes that you will wear.

**23** Life is more important than food. The body is more important than clothes.

**24** Think about the ravens. They do not plant or harvest. They do not have storerooms or barns. But God feeds them. You are worth much more than birds!

**25** You cannot add 1 hour to your life when you worry.

**26** You cannot do this small thing. Thus why do you worry about the other things?

**27** Think about how the lilies grow. They do not work or make cloth. But I tell you, even Solomon in all his glory did not wear clothes as beautiful as 1 of these flowers.

**28** God puts beautiful clothes on the grass in the field. The grass lives today, and tomorrow people throw it into the fire. Thus God will give clothes to you much more. You have little faith!

**29** Do not always think about what you will eat or drink. Do not worry.

**30** All the nations of the world look for these things. But your Father knows that you need them.

**31** Instead, look for the kingdom of God. Then God will also give you these things.

**32** Do not be afraid, little flock. Your Father is happy to give you the kingdom.

**33** Sell your property and give the money to poor people. Get for yourselves money bags that do not become old. Get riches in heaven that will never end. No thief can come near them, and no moth can destroy them.

**34** Your heart will be in the same place as your riches.

**35** Be ready for work, with your clothes on. Keep your lamps lit.

**36** Be like servants who wait for their master to come back from a wedding feast. When he comes and knocks, they open the door for him immediately.

**37** The master will come and find his servants awake and ready. God blesses those servants. I tell you the truth, the master will put on work clothes. He will tell the servants to sit at the table. Then he will come and serve them.

**38** The master can come at midnight or later in the night. If he finds the servants ready, God blesses those servants.

**39** Know this: If the owner of a house knows the time when a thief will come, then he will watch. He will not let the thief break into his house.

**40** You also must be ready. The Son of Man will come at a time when you do not expect him."

**41** Peter said, "Lord, do you tell this parable to us, or to all the people?"

**42** The Lord said, "A wise and faithful manager is like this. His master puts him in charge of the other servants. The manager gives them their food at the correct time.

**43** When the master comes back, he finds that the manager does this work. God blesses that servant.

**44** I tell you the truth. The master will put that servant in charge of all his property.

**45** But maybe that servant will think, 'My master will not come back soon.' Then the servant starts to hit the other servants, both men and women. He eats and drinks and becomes drunk.

**46** Then the master of that servant will come on a day when the servant does not expect him. He will come at a time that the servant does not know. The master will punish him very severely. He will put him with the people who are not faithful.

**47** A servant can know what his master wants, but he does not get ready or do it. That servant will get many hits with a whip.

**48** But another servant does not know what his master wants. He does things that deserve punishment, but he will get only a few hits. God will ask for much from each person to whom he gave much. People will ask for more from a person to whom they gave more.

**49** I came to bring fire on the earth. I wish that the fire already burned!

**50** I must go through a baptism of pain. I am very troubled until it is complete.

**51** Do you think that I came to give peace on earth? No, I tell you. I came to cause people to divide.

**52** From now on, a family of 5 people will divide. 3 people will be against 2 people, and 2 people will be against 3 people.

**53** A father will be against his son, and a son will be against his father. A mother will be against her daughter, and a daughter will be against her mother. A mother-in-law will be against her daughter-in-law, and a daughter-in-law will be against her mother-in-law."

**54** Jesus also said to the crowd, "When you see a cloud in the west, immediately you say, 'Rain will come.' And it rains.

**55** When the south wind blows, you say, 'It will be hot.' And it is hot.

**56** You hypocrites! You can understand the signs of the earth and the sky. Why do you not understand the meaning of this time?

**57** Why do you not decide for yourselves what is right?

**58** Your enemy can take you to court. On the way, try hard to make peace with him. If you do not, he will take you to the judge. The judge will give you to the officer. The officer will put you in prison.

**59** I tell you, you will not get out of prison until you pay the last small coin."

## Chapter 13

**1** At that time, some people came to Jesus. They told him about the people from Galilee whom Pilate killed. Pilate mixed their blood with their sacrifices.

**2** Jesus said to them, "Do you think that these people from Galilee were worse sinners than all the other people of Galilee? Do you think so because they suffered these things?

**3** I tell you, no. But if you do not repent, all of you will die too.

**4** Think about the 18 people who died when the tower in Siloam fell on them. Do you think that they were worse sinners than all the other people in Jerusalem?

**5** I tell you, no. But if you do not repent, all of you will die too."

**6** Then Jesus told this parable: "A man had a fig tree in his vineyard. He came to look for fruit on the tree, but he found none.

**7** The man said to the worker of his vineyard, 'For 3 years I came to look for fruit on this fig tree. I found none. Cut the tree down. Why must the tree use the ground for no purpose?'

**8** The worker said to him, 'Sir, let the tree stay for 1 more year. I will dig around the tree and put manure on the ground.

**9** If the tree gives fruit next year, good. If not, then you can cut the tree down.'"

**10** On a Sabbath day, Jesus taught in one of the synagogues.

**11** A woman was there who had a spirit that made her ill for 18 years. Her back was bent, and she could not stand up straight.

**12** Jesus saw her and told her to come to him. He said to her, "Woman, you are free from your illness."

**13** Jesus put his hands on her. Immediately she stood up straight, and she praised God.

**14** The leader of the synagogue was angry because Jesus healed on the Sabbath. The leader said to the people, "There are 6 days for work. Come on those days to become well. Do not come on the Sabbath day."

**15** The Lord answered him, "You are hypocrites! On the Sabbath, each of you unties his ox or his donkey from the stall. Each of you takes the animal out to give it water.

**16** This woman is a daughter of Abraham. Satan kept her bound for 18 years. Thus it is correct to make her free from this bond on the Sabbath day."

**17** When Jesus said these things, all his enemies felt shame. But all the people were happy because of all the wonderful things that Jesus did.

**18** Then Jesus said, "What is the kingdom of God like? To what can I compare it?

**19** The kingdom of God is like a mustard seed. A man took the seed and planted it in his garden. The seed grew and became a tree. The birds made their nests in its branches."

**20** Again Jesus said, "To what can I compare the kingdom of God?

**21** The kingdom of God is like yeast. A woman put yeast into 3 large measures of flour. The yeast went through all the dough."

**22** Jesus went through the cities and villages and taught the people. He continued his journey to Jerusalem.

**23** A man asked him, "Lord, will God save only a small number of people?" Jesus said to the people,

**24** "Work hard to go in through the narrow door. I tell you, many people will try to go in, but they will not be able to go in.

**25** The master of the house will get up and close the door. Then you will stand outside and knock on the door. You will say, 'Lord, open the door for us.' But he will answer you, 'I do not know where you come from.'

**26** Then you will say, 'We ate and drank with you. You taught in our streets.'

**27** But he will say, 'I tell you, I do not know where you come from. Go away from me, all you people who do evil!'

**28** You will see Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and all the prophets in the kingdom of God. But you will be outside. Then you will cry and grind your teeth.

**29** People will come from the east and the west, and from the north and the south. They will sit down to eat in the kingdom of God.

**30** Some people who are last now will be first. And some people who are first now will be last."

**31** At that time, some Pharisees came to Jesus. They said to him, "Go away from this place, because Herod wants to kill you."

**32** Jesus said to them, "Go and tell that fox, 'Today and tomorrow I will push out demons and heal people. On the third day I will complete my work.'

**33** But I must continue my journey today, tomorrow, and the next day. A prophet must not die outside of Jerusalem.

**34** O Jerusalem, Jerusalem! You kill the prophets. You throw stones at the people whom God sends to you. Many times I wanted to gather your children together. I wanted to do this as a hen gathers her chicks under her wings. But you did not want this.

**35** Look, God will leave your house empty. I tell you, you will not see me again until you say, 'Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord.'"

**1** On a Sabbath day, Jesus went to eat in the house of a leader of the Pharisees. The people there watched Jesus carefully.

**2** In front of Jesus there was a man whose body was swollen with fluid.

**3** Jesus spoke to the experts in the law and to the Pharisees. He asked, "Does the law let a person heal on the Sabbath day, or not?"

**8** "When a person invites you to a wedding feast, do not sit in the best seat. Maybe the host also invited a person who is more important than you.

**9** Then the host will come and say to you, 'Give your seat to this man.' Then with shame you will go to the lowest seat.

**10** But when a person invites you, go and sit in the lowest seat. Then your host will come and say to you, 'Friend, go to a better seat.' Then all the other guests will see that you have honor.

**11** All people who make themselves important will become low. And all people who make themselves low will become important."

**14** Then you will be blessed, because they cannot pay you back. God will pay you back when the good people come back to life."

**16** Jesus said to him, "A man prepared a large supper, and he invited many people.

**17** At the time of the supper, he sent his servant to the guests. The servant said, 'Come, because everything is ready now.'

**18** But all the guests started to give excuses. The first guest said, 'I bought a field, and I must go and see it. Please accept my excuse.'

**19** Another guest said, 'I bought 5 pairs of oxen, and I will go and test them. Please accept my excuse.'

**20** Another guest said, 'I married a wife, and thus I cannot come.'

**21** The servant came back and told his master these things. The master of the house became angry. He said to his servant, 'Go out quickly into the streets and roads of the city. Bring in the poor people, the crippled people, the blind people, and the lame people.'

**22** The servant said, 'Master, I did what you told me. But there is still space.'

**23** The master said to the servant, 'Go out to the roads and the country paths. Tell the people to come in. I want my house to be full.

**24** I tell you, none of the men whom I invited will eat my supper.'"

**27** If a person does not carry his cross and follow me, he cannot be my disciple.

**29** If he puts down the base and cannot complete the tower, all the people who see it will laugh at him.

**30** They will say, 'This man started to build, but he could not complete the work.'

**31** Or think about a king who goes to war against another king. First he will sit down and think. He has 10,000 soldiers. He must know if he can fight against the other king who comes with 20,000 soldiers.

**32** If he cannot, he will send messengers to the other king while that king is still far away. The messengers will ask for peace.

**33** In the same way, each of you must give up all that you have. If not, you cannot be my disciple.

**34** Salt is good. But if salt loses its taste, you cannot make it salty again.

**35** It is not good for the soil or for the manure pile. People throw it out. If you have ears, listen!"

## Chapter 15

**1** All the tax collectors and sinners came near to Jesus to hear him.

**2** The Pharisees and the teachers of the law complained. They said, "This man accepts sinners and eats with them."

**3** Then Jesus told them this parable:

**4** "Think about a man who has 100 sheep and loses 1 of them. He will leave the 99 sheep in the wilderness. He will go and look for the lost sheep until he finds it.

**5** When he finds the sheep, he will put it on his shoulders with joy.

**6** He will go home and call his friends and neighbors together. He will say to them, 'Be happy with me, because I found my lost sheep.'

**7** I tell you, in the same way there will be joy in heaven over 1 sinner who repents. That joy will be more than the joy over 99 good people who do not need to repent.

**8** Or think about a woman who has 10 silver coins and loses 1 coin. She will light a lamp and clean the house. She will look carefully until she finds the coin.

**9** When she finds the coin, she will call her friends and neighbors together. She will say, 'Be happy with me, because I found the coin that I lost.'

**10** In the same way, I tell you, there is joy in front of the angels of God over 1 sinner who repents."

**11** Jesus said, "A man had 2 sons.

**12** The younger son said to his father, 'Father, give me my part of your property.' So the father divided his property between the 2 sons.

**13** After a few days, the younger son collected all his things. He went on a journey to a country far away. There he lived a wild life and wasted all his money.

**14** After he spent all his money, there was a very bad famine in that country. The son did not have the things that he needed.

**15** So he went to work for a man of that country. The man sent him into his fields to feed pigs.

**16** The son was so hungry that he wanted to eat the pods that the pigs ate. But no person gave him food.

**17** Then he started to think clearly. He said, 'All the workers of my father have more than sufficient food. But I die of hunger here!

**18** I will get up and go to my father. I will say to him, "Father, I sinned against heaven and against you.

**19** I am not good enough to be your son. Make me one of your workers."'

**20** So he got up and went to his father. While the son was still far away, his father saw him and felt pity for him. The father ran to his son. He put his arms around his son and kissed him.

**21** The son said to him, 'Father, I sinned against heaven and against you. I am not good enough to be your son.'

**22** But the father said to his servants, 'Quickly bring the best robe and put it on him. Put a ring on his hand and sandals on his feet.

**23** Bring the fat calf and kill it. We will eat and celebrate.

**24** This son of mine was dead, and now he is alive again. He went away from us, and now he came back.' And they started to celebrate.

**28** The older son became angry and did not want to go in. So his father came out and asked him to come in.

**29** But the son said to his father, 'Look, for many years I worked for you like a slave. I always obeyed your commands. But you never gave me even a young goat to celebrate with my friends.

**31** The father said to him, 'Son, you are always with me. All that I have is yours.

## Chapter 16

**3** The manager thought, 'What will I do? My master will take my job from me. I am not strong enough to dig. I have too much shame to beg.

**4** I know what I will do. Then, when I lose my job, people will accept me into their houses.'

**5** So the manager called each person who owed money to his master. He said to the first person, 'How much do you owe my master?'

**6** The man said, '100 measures of olive oil.' The manager said to him, 'Take your bill. Sit down quickly and write 50.'

**7** Then the manager said to another person, 'How much do you owe?' The man said, '100 measures of wheat.' The manager said to him, 'Take your bill and write 80.'

**8** The master praised the dishonest manager because the manager acted wisely. The people of this world are wiser with other people of their kind than the people of the light are.

**9** I tell you, use the money of this world to make friends for yourselves. Then, when the money is gone, they will accept you into the homes that last for all time.

**10** A person whom you can trust with a small thing, you can also trust with a large thing. A person who is dishonest with a small thing is also dishonest with a large thing.

**11** If you are not honest with the money of this world, then no person will trust you with the true riches.

**12** If you are not honest with the property of another person, then no person will give you property of your own.

**13** No servant can serve 2 masters. He will hate one and love the other. Or he will be loyal to one and not respect the other. You cannot serve God and money."

**15** Jesus said to them, "You try to make yourselves look good to people. But God knows your hearts. The things that people think are important are very bad to God.

**16** The law and the prophets were the message until John came. Since that time, people tell the good news of the kingdom of God. And all people try hard to go into it.

**17** It is easier for heaven and earth to disappear than for the smallest mark of the law to fail.

**18** A man who divorces his wife and marries another woman commits adultery. And a man who marries a divorced woman commits adultery.

**19** There was a rich man who wore purple clothes and fine linen. Every day he ate very expensive food.

**20** A poor man with the name Lazarus lay at the gate of the rich man. Lazarus had sores on all of his body.

**21** Lazarus wanted to eat the small pieces of food that fell from the table of the rich man. Also, the dogs came and licked his sores.

**22** Then the poor man died. The angels carried him to the side of Abraham. The rich man also died, and people buried him.

**23** The rich man was in hell and he felt much pain. He looked up and saw Abraham far away. He saw Lazarus at the side of Abraham.

**24** The rich man shouted, 'Father Abraham, have mercy on me! Send Lazarus to put the end of his finger in water and cool my tongue. I feel very much pain in this fire.'

**25** But Abraham said, 'Son, remember this. In your life you got good things, and Lazarus got bad things. Now Lazarus gets comfort here, and you feel pain.

**26** Also, there is a large deep space between us and you. People who want to go from here to you cannot go. And no person can come from there to us.'

**27** The rich man said, 'Then, father, please send Lazarus to the house of my father.

**28** I have 5 brothers. Lazarus must warn them. Then they will not also come to this place of pain.'

**29** But Abraham said, 'They have the writings of Moses and the prophets. Your brothers must listen to them.'

**30** The rich man said, 'No, father Abraham! But if a person from the dead goes to them, they will repent.'

**31** Abraham said to him, 'They do not listen to Moses and the prophets. Thus they will not believe a person who comes back from the dead.'"

**3** Be careful! If your brother sins against you, tell him that he is wrong. If he repents, forgive him.

**4** He can sin against you 7 times in a day. And he can come back to you 7 times and say, 'I repent.' You must forgive him each time."

**5** The apostles said to the Lord, "Make our faith larger."

**6** The Lord said, "Your faith can be as small as a mustard seed. Then you can say to this mulberry tree, 'Pull yourself out of the ground and go into the sea.' And the tree will obey you.

**7** Think about a servant of yours who plows a field or keeps the sheep. When he comes in from the field, you will not say to him, 'Come now and sit down to eat.'

**12** He went into a village. There 10 men who had leprosy came to meet him. They stood far away.

**15** One of the men saw that he became well. He came back and praised God with a loud voice.

**16** He fell down on his face at the feet of Jesus and thanked him. This man was a Samaritan.

**17** Jesus said, "I healed 10 men. Where are the other 9?

**18** Only this foreigner came back to praise God."

**20** The Pharisees asked Jesus when the kingdom of God will come. Jesus answered, "The kingdom of God does not come with signs that you can watch for.

**24** Lightning flashes and lights the sky from one side to the other. The Son of Man will be like that on his day.

**25** But first he must suffer many things. The people of this time will refuse him.

**26** The days of the Son of Man will be like the days of Noah.

**28** It will also be like the days of Lot. The people ate and drank. They bought and sold. They planted and built.

**29** But on the day when Lot went out of Sodom, fire and sulfur fell from the sky. The fire and sulfur killed all of the people.

**30** It will be the same on the day when the Son of Man shows himself.

**31** On that day, a person can possibly be on the roof of his house, and his things can be in the house. He must not go down to get them. Also, a person in the field must not go back.

**32** Remember the wife of Lot!

**33** If a person tries to save his life, he will lose it. If a person loses his life, he will keep it.

**34** I tell you, on that night 2 people will be in 1 bed. God will take one and leave the other.

**35** 2 women will grind grain together. God will take one and leave the other.

**36** 2 men will be in the field. God will take one and leave the other."

**37** The disciples asked him, "Where, Lord?" Jesus said to them, "Where there is a dead body, the vultures will gather."

**2** He said, "In a city there was a judge. He did not fear God, and he did not respect people.

**3** In that city there was a widow. She came to the judge many times. She said, 'Give me justice against my enemy.'

**4** For some time the judge refused. But then he thought, 'I do not fear God, and I do not respect people.

**5** But this widow continues to give me trouble. So I will give her justice. If not, she will make me tired with her visits.'"

**6** The Lord said, "Listen to what the unjust judge said.

**7** God will certainly give justice to his chosen people who call to him day and night. He will not wait a long time to help them.

**8** I tell you, he will give them justice quickly. But when the Son of Man comes, will he find faith on the earth?"

**9** Some people were sure that they were good, and they thought that other people had no value. Jesus told them this parable:

**10** "2 men went up to the temple to pray. One man was a Pharisee, and the other man was a tax collector.

**11** The Pharisee stood and prayed about himself. He said, 'God, I thank you that I am not like other people. I do not steal. I am not dishonest. I do not commit adultery. I am not like this tax collector.

**12** I fast 2 times each week. I give one tenth of all that I get.'

**13** But the tax collector stood far away. He did not even look up to heaven. He hit his chest and said, 'God, have mercy on me, because I am a sinner.'

**14** I tell you, the tax collector went home and God accepted him as righteous. God did not accept the Pharisee. All people who make themselves important will become low. And all people who make themselves low will become important."

**15** People also brought babies to Jesus. They wanted him to touch the babies. When the disciples saw this, they told the people to stop.

**16** But Jesus called the children to him. He said, "Let the little children come to me. Do not stop them. The kingdom of God belongs to people like these children.

**17** I tell you the truth: If a person does not accept the kingdom of God like a little child, he will never go into it."

**18** A leader asked Jesus, "Good Teacher, what must I do to get eternal life?"

**19** Jesus said to him, "Why do you call me good? Only God is good.

**20** You know the commandments: 'Do not commit adultery. Do not murder. Do not steal. Do not tell lies about other people. Respect your father and your mother.'"

**21** The man said, "I obeyed all these commandments since I was young."

**22** When Jesus heard this, he said to the man, "You must still do 1 thing. Sell all that you have and give the money to the poor people. Then you will have treasure in heaven. Then come and follow me."

**23** When the man heard this, he became very sad, because he was very rich.

**24** Jesus saw that the man was very sad. Jesus said, "It is very difficult for rich people to go into the kingdom of God!

**25** It is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle. It is more difficult for a rich man to go into the kingdom of God."

**26** The people who heard this said, "Then who can God save?"

**27** Jesus said, "Things that are not possible for people are possible for God."

**28** Peter said, "Look, we left all that we had, and we followed you."

**29** Jesus said to them, "I tell you the truth: Some people left their house, parents, brothers, wife, or children for the kingdom of God.

**30** These people will get much more in this life. And in the future world they will get eternal life."

**31** Jesus took the 12 disciples to one side. He said to them, "Look, we go up to Jerusalem. There all the things that the prophets wrote about the Son of Man will occur.

**33** They will hit him with whips and kill him. On the third day he will come back to life."

**35** Jesus came near to Jericho. A blind man sat at the side of the road and begged.

**37** The people told him, "Jesus of Nazareth goes past."

**38** The blind man shouted, "Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me!"

**39** The people at the front of the crowd told him to be quiet. But he shouted more loudly, "Son of David, have mercy on me!"

**40** Jesus stopped. He told the people to bring the man to him. When the man came near, Jesus asked him,

**41** "What do you want me to do for you?" The man said, "Lord, I want to see again."

**42** Jesus said to him, "See again. Your faith healed you."

**43** Immediately the man could see. He followed Jesus and praised God. All the people saw this, and they also praised God.

**13** Before he went, he called 10 of his servants. He gave each servant 1 coin of high value. He said to them, 'Use this money to do business until I come back.'

**14** But the people of his country hated him. They sent messengers after him. The messengers said, 'We do not want this man to be our king.'

**15** But the nobleman became king and came back home. Then he told the servants to come to him. He wanted to know how much money each servant made with the coin.

**16** The first servant came and said, 'Master, your 1 coin made 10 more coins.'

**17** The king said to him, 'Good work! You are a good servant. You were faithful with a very small thing. So I give you authority over 10 cities.'

**18** The second servant came and said, 'Master, your 1 coin made 5 more coins.'

**19** The king said to him, 'I give you authority over 5 cities.'

**20** Another servant came and said, 'Master, here is your coin. I kept it in a cloth.

**21** I was afraid of you, because you are a hard man. You take what you did not put in. You harvest what you did not plant.'

**22** The king said to him, 'You are a bad servant! I will judge you with your own words. You knew that I am a hard man. You knew that I take what I did not put in, and harvest what I did not plant.

**23** Then why did you not put my money in the bank? Then, when I came back, I could get my money with interest.'

**24** The king said to the people who stood there, 'Take the coin from him. Give it to the servant who has 10 coins.'

**25** They said to him, 'Master, he already has 10 coins!'

**31** If a person asks you, 'Why do you untie the donkey?' tell him, 'The Lord needs it.'"

**33** While they untied the young donkey, its owners said to them, "Why do you untie the donkey?"

**36** While Jesus went along, people put their coats on the road in front of him.

**37** Jesus came near to the road that goes down the Mount of Olives. There all the crowd of disciples started to be happy. They praised God with loud voices for all the miracles that they saw.

**45** Jesus went into the temple. He started to push out the people who sold things there.

**46** He said to them, "The scriptures say, 'My house will be a house of prayer.' But you made it a place where thieves hide."

**48** But they could not find a way to do it, because all the people listened carefully to him.

**3** Jesus answered them, "I will also ask you a question. Answer me.

**5** They talked about this among themselves. They said, "If we say, 'From heaven,' he will say, 'Then why did you not believe John?'

**6** But if we say, 'From people,' all the people will kill us with stones. The people are sure that John was a prophet."

**7** So they answered, "We do not know where it came from."

**8** Jesus said to them, "Then I will not tell you what authority I have to do these things."

**9** Then Jesus told the people this parable: "A man planted a vineyard. He rented it to some farmers. Then he went to another country for a long time.

**10** At harvest time, he sent a servant to the farmers. The servant had to get some of the fruit of the vineyard. But the farmers hit the servant and sent him away with nothing.

**11** The man sent another servant. The farmers also hit him and insulted him. They sent him away with nothing.

**12** The man sent a third servant. The farmers injured him and threw him out.

**13** Then the owner of the vineyard said, 'What will I do? I will send my son whom I love. Maybe they will respect him.'

**14** But when the farmers saw the son, they talked among themselves. They said, 'This is the son who will get the vineyard. Come, we will kill him. Then the vineyard will be ours.'

**15** So they threw the son out of the vineyard and killed him. What will the owner of the vineyard do to them?

**4** Judas went to the chief priests and the officers of the temple guards. He talked with them about how he could give Jesus to them.

**5** They were happy. They agreed to give him money.

**6** Judas agreed. He looked for an opportunity to give Jesus to them when there was no crowd.

**7** The day of Unleavened Bread came. On that day the people had to kill the lamb for the Passover.

**8** Jesus sent Peter and John. He said, "Go and prepare the Passover meal for us to eat."

**9** They asked him, "Where do you want us to prepare it?"

**10** Jesus said to them, "When you go into the city, a man will meet you. He will carry a jar of water. Follow him into the house that he goes into.

**11** Say to the owner of the house, 'The Teacher asks you: Where is the guest room where I will eat the Passover with my disciples?'

**12** The owner will show you a large upstairs room with furniture. Prepare the meal there."

**13** Peter and John went. They found everything as Jesus told them. They prepared the Passover meal.

**14** When the time came, Jesus sat at the table with the 12 apostles.

**15** He said to them, "I wanted very much to eat this Passover meal with you before I suffer.

**16** I tell you, I will not eat it again until it becomes complete in the kingdom of God."

**17** Then Jesus took a cup and gave thanks. He said, "Take this cup and share it among you.

**18** I tell you, I will not drink wine again until the kingdom of God comes."

**19** Then Jesus took bread and gave thanks. He broke the bread and gave it to them. He said, "This is my body, which I give for you. Do this to remember me."

**20** After the supper, he took the cup in the same way. He said, "This cup is the new covenant in my blood, which I pour out for you.

**21** But look! The person who will betray me is with me at this table.

**22** The Son of Man will go as God decided. But how terrible it will be for that man who betrays him!"

**23** The apostles started to ask each other which of them will do this.

**24** Then the apostles started to argue. They argued about which of them was the most important.

**25** Jesus said to them, "The kings of the Gentiles rule over their people. The people call those rulers 'friends of the people.'

**26** But you must not be like that. The most important person among you must be like the youngest. The leader must be like a servant.

**27** Who is more important, the person who sits at the table or the person who serves? It is the person who sits at the table. But I am among you as a person who serves.

**28** You stayed with me in my troubles.

**29** My Father gave me a kingdom. In the same way, I give you a kingdom.

**30** You will eat and drink at my table in my kingdom. You will sit on thrones and judge the 12 tribes of Israel."

**31** The Lord said, "Simon, Simon, listen! Satan asked to test all of you, as a farmer separates wheat from the bad parts.

**32** But I prayed for you, Simon, that your faith will not fail. When you come back to me, make your brothers strong."

**33** Peter said to him, "Lord, I am ready to go with you to prison and to death."

**34** Jesus said, "Peter, I tell you, the rooster will not crow today before you say 3 times that you do not know me."

**35** Then Jesus said to them, "I sent you out with no money, no bag, and no sandals. Did you need anything?" They said, "No, nothing."

**36** He said to them, "But now, if you have money, take it. Also take a bag. If you do not have a sword, sell your coat and buy one.

**37** I tell you, the scripture says, 'People thought that he was a criminal.' These words must come true about me. The things that the scriptures say about me will come true."

**38** The disciples said, "Lord, look, here are 2 swords." He said to them, "That is sufficient."

**39** Jesus went out of the city to the Mount of Olives, as he usually did. His disciples followed him.

**40** When they came to the place, he said to them, "Pray that you will not go into temptation."

**41** Jesus went away from them about the distance that a person can throw a stone. He kneeled down and prayed.

**42** He said, "Father, if you agree, take this cup of pain away from me. But do what you want, not what I want."

**43** An angel from heaven came to him and made him strong.

**44** Jesus was in very much pain, and he prayed more strongly. His sweat was like large drops of blood that fell to the ground.

**45** He got up from prayer and went back to the disciples. He found them asleep because they were very sad.

**46** He said to them, "Why do you sleep? Get up and pray that you will not go into temptation."

**47** While Jesus still spoke, a crowd came. Judas, 1 of the 12 apostles, led them. Judas came near to Jesus to kiss him.

**48** But Jesus said to him, "Judas, do you betray the Son of Man with a kiss?"

**49** The disciples near Jesus saw what was about to occur. They said, "Lord, must we attack with our swords?"

**50** One of them hit the servant of the high priest and cut off his right ear.

**51** But Jesus said, "Stop! No more of this." He touched the ear of the servant and healed him.

**52** The chief priests, the officers of the temple guards, and the elders came to Jesus. Jesus said to them, "Do you come with swords and clubs as if I am a criminal?

**53** Every day I was with you in the temple, and you did not arrest me. But this is your time, and the time when darkness has power."

**54** They arrested Jesus and took him to the house of the high priest. Peter followed far behind.

**55** The people made a fire in the middle of the yard and sat down together. Peter sat down among them.

**56** A servant girl saw Peter while he sat near the fire. She looked carefully at him and said, "This man was also with Jesus."

**57** But Peter said that this was not true. He said, "Woman, I do not know him."

**58** After a short time, another person saw Peter. He said, "You are also one of them." Peter said, "Man, I am not."

**59** About 1 hour later, another man said strongly, "This man was certainly with Jesus. He is from Galilee."

**60** But Peter said, "Man, I do not know what you talk about." Immediately, while Peter still spoke, a rooster crowed.

**61** The Lord turned and looked at Peter. Then Peter remembered the words of the Lord. Jesus told him, "Before the rooster crows today, you will say 3 times that you do not know me."

**62** Peter went outside and cried bitterly.

**63** The men who guarded Jesus laughed at him and hit him.

**64** They covered his eyes and hit him in the face. They said, "Prophesy! Tell us who hit you!"

**65** They said many other insults to him.

**66** In the morning, the elders of the people, the chief priests, and the teachers of the law met together. They took Jesus to their council.

**67** They said, "If you are the Christ, tell us." Jesus said to them, "If I tell you, you will not believe me.

**68** If I ask you a question, you will not answer me. And you will not let me go.

**69** But from now on, the Son of Man will sit at the right side of the powerful God."

**70** They all said, "Then are you the Son of God?" He said to them, "You say that I am."

**71** Then they said, "We do not need more witnesses. We heard him say it with his own mouth."

## Chapter 23

**1** Then all the members of the council got up. They took Jesus to Pilate.

**2** They started to accuse Jesus. They said, "We found that this man tells our nation to do wrong things. He tells people not to pay taxes to Caesar. He says that he is Christ, a king."

**3** Pilate asked Jesus, "Are you the King of the Jews?" Jesus answered him, "You say so."

**4** Then Pilate said to the chief priests and the crowd, "I do not find that this man is guilty of a crime."

**5** But they spoke more strongly. They said, "He causes trouble among the people with his words. He taught in all of Judea. He started in Galilee and now he is here."

**6** When Pilate heard the word "Galilee," he asked if Jesus was from Galilee.

**7** Pilate learned that Jesus was under the authority of Herod. So Pilate sent Jesus to Herod. At that time Herod was also in Jerusalem.

**8** When Herod saw Jesus, he was very happy. He heard many things about Jesus, and he wanted to see him for a long time. Herod hoped to see Jesus do a miracle.

**9** Herod asked Jesus many questions. But Jesus did not answer him.

**10** The chief priests and the teachers of the law stood there. They accused Jesus very strongly.

**11** Herod and his soldiers insulted Jesus and laughed at him. They put a beautiful robe on him. Then Herod sent Jesus back to Pilate.

**12** Before that day, Pilate and Herod were enemies. But on that day they became friends.

**13** Pilate called together the chief priests, the leaders, and the people.

**14** He said to them, "You brought this man to me. You said that he tells the people to do wrong things. I asked him questions in front of you. I did not find that he is guilty of the things that you say.

**15** Herod also did not find him guilty, because Herod sent him back to us. Look, this man did nothing that must have the punishment of death.

**16** So I will hit him with whips, and then I will let him go free."

**17** At each Passover feast, Pilate had to let 1 prisoner go free for the people.

**18** But all the people shouted together, "Kill this man! Let Barabbas go free for us!"

**19** Barabbas was in prison because he started a fight against the government in the city. He also committed murder.

**20** Pilate wanted to let Jesus go free. So he spoke to the people again.

**21** But they shouted, "Crucify him! Crucify him!"

**22** Pilate spoke to them a third time. He said, "Why? What crime did this man commit? I did not find a reason to kill him. So I will hit him with whips, and then I will let him go free."

**23** But the people continued to shout loudly. They demanded that Pilate crucify Jesus. Their shouts and the shouts of the chief priests won.

**24** Pilate decided to give the people what they demanded.

**25** Pilate let Barabbas go free. Barabbas was the man who was in prison for a fight against the government and for murder. The people asked for Barabbas. But Pilate gave Jesus to them to do what they wanted.

**26** The soldiers took Jesus away. On the road they took hold of a man with the name Simon, from Cyrene. Simon came into the city from the country. The soldiers put the cross on Simon. They made him carry it behind Jesus.

**27** A large crowd of people followed Jesus. In the crowd there were women who cried and were very sad for him.

**28** Jesus turned to the women and said, "Daughters of Jerusalem, do not cry for me. Cry for yourselves and for your children.

**29** A time will come when people will say, 'Blessed are the women who cannot have children. Blessed are the women who never had babies and never fed babies.'

**30** Then people will say to the mountains, 'Fall on us!' They will say to the hills, 'Cover us!'

**31** People do these things to me now, while times are good. So the things that will occur when times are bad will be worse."

**32** The soldiers also led 2 other men with Jesus to kill them. These men were criminals.

**33** They came to the place with the name Calvary. There they crucified Jesus and the criminals. One criminal was at the right side of Jesus, and the other criminal was at his left side.

**34** Jesus said, "Father, forgive them, because they do not know what they do." The soldiers threw lots to divide the clothes of Jesus among them.

**35** The people stood there and watched. The leaders laughed at Jesus. They said, "He saved other people. If he is the Christ, the chosen one of God, he must save himself."

**36** The soldiers also laughed at him. They came to him and gave him sour wine.

**37** They said, "If you are the king of the Jews, save yourself!"

**38** Above Jesus there was a sign. Someone wrote it in Greek, Latin, and Hebrew. The sign said: THIS IS THE KING OF THE JEWS.

**39** One of the criminals on a cross insulted Jesus. He said, "If you are the Christ, save yourself and us!"

**40** But the other criminal told him that he was wrong. He said, "You also have the same punishment. Do you not fear God?

**41** Our punishment is correct, because we get what our actions deserve. But this man did nothing wrong."

**42** Then he said to Jesus, "Lord, remember me when you come into your kingdom."

**43** Jesus said to him, "I tell you the truth: Today you will be with me in paradise."

**44** At about 12 noon, darkness came over all the land. The darkness stayed until 3 o'clock in the afternoon.

**45** The sun stopped. It gave no light. The curtain in the temple tore into 2 pieces.

**46** Jesus shouted with a loud voice, "Father, I put my spirit into your hands." After he said this, he died.

**47** The Roman officer saw what occurred. He praised God and said, "This man was certainly innocent."

**48** A crowd of people came together to watch. They saw what occurred. Then they hit their chests in sorrow and went home.

**49** All the friends of Jesus stood far away and watched these things. The women who followed him from Galilee also stood there.

**50** There was a man with the name Joseph. He was a member of the council. He was a good and righteous man.

**51** Joseph did not agree with the decision and the actions of the council. He came from Arimathea, a city of the Jews. He waited for the kingdom of God.

**52** Joseph went to Pilate and asked for the body of Jesus.

**53** He took the body down from the cross and wrapped it in linen cloth. He put the body in a tomb that workers cut in rock. No person was in this tomb before.

**54** That day was the Day of Preparation. The Sabbath was about to start.

**55** The women who came with Jesus from Galilee followed Joseph. They saw the tomb and how Joseph put the body of Jesus in it.

**56** Then they went home and prepared spices and perfumes. On the Sabbath day they rested, as the law of God commands.

## Chapter 24

**1** On the first day of the week, very early in the morning, the women went to the tomb. They took the spices that they prepared. Some other women went with them.

**2** They found that someone rolled the stone away from the tomb.

**3** They went into the tomb, but they did not find the body of the Lord Jesus.

**4** The women did not know what to think about this. Suddenly 2 men in very bright clothes stood next to them.

**5** The women were afraid and bowed their faces to the ground. The men said to them, "Why do you look for a person who lives among the dead?

**6** Jesus is not here. He came back to life! Remember what he told you when he was still in Galilee.

**7** He said, 'Sinful men must take the Son of Man and crucify him. On the third day he will come back to life.'"

**8** Then the women remembered the words of Jesus.

**9** They went back from the tomb. They told all these things to the 11 apostles and to all the other disciples.

**10** The women were Mary Magdalene, Joanna, Mary the mother of James, and some other women. They told these things to the apostles.

**11** But the apostles thought that the words of the women were nonsense. The apostles did not believe them.

**12** But Peter got up and ran to the tomb. He bent down and looked in. He saw only the linen cloths. Then he went home. He was very surprised at what occurred.

**13** On that same day, 2 of the disciples went to a village with the name Emmaus. This village was about 11 kilometers from Jerusalem.

**14** They talked together about all the things that occurred.

**15** While they talked and discussed these things, Jesus came near and walked with them.

**16** But something stopped their eyes, and they did not know who he was.

**17** Jesus said to them, "What do you talk about while you walk?" They stopped, and they looked sad.

**18** One of them, with the name Cleopas, answered him. He said, "Are you the only visitor in Jerusalem who does not know the things that occurred there in these days?"

**19** Jesus said to them, "What things?" They said to him, "The things about Jesus of Nazareth. He was a prophet. He had much power in what he did and said in front of God and all the people.

**20** Our chief priests and leaders gave him to the Romans. The Romans sentenced him to death and crucified him.

**21** We hoped that he was the man who will make Israel free. Also, today is the third day since these things occurred.

**22** And some women of our group surprised us. They went to the tomb early this morning.

**23** They did not find his body there. They came back and said that they saw angels in a vision. The angels said that Jesus was alive.

**24** Some of our group went to the tomb. They found it as the women said. But they did not see Jesus."

**25** Then Jesus said to them, "You are foolish people! You are very slow to believe all the things that the prophets said.

**26** The Christ had to suffer these things before he went into his glory."

**27** Then Jesus explained to them what all the scriptures said about him. He started with the books of Moses and continued through all the prophets.

**28** They came near the village where they went. Jesus acted as if he will go farther.

**29** But they asked him strongly to stay. They said, "Stay with us, because it is almost evening. The day is almost done." So Jesus went in to stay with them.

**30** Jesus sat at the table with them. He took the bread and blessed it. Then he broke it and gave it to them.

**31** Then their eyes opened, and they knew who he was. Then he disappeared.

**32** They said to each other, "When he talked with us on the road, our hearts felt hot inside us. We felt this when he explained the scriptures to us."

**33** They got up immediately and went back to Jerusalem. There they found the 11 apostles and the people with them together.

**34** These people said, "It is true! The Lord came back to life. He showed himself to Simon."

**35** Then the 2 disciples told what occurred on the road. They told how they knew Jesus when he broke the bread.

**36** While they talked about these things, Jesus himself stood among them. He said to them, "Peace to you."

**37** But they were very afraid. They thought that they saw a spirit.

**38** Jesus said to them, "Why are you troubled? Why do you have doubts in your hearts?

**39** Look at my hands and my feet. It is I! Touch me and see. A spirit does not have flesh and bones. But you see that I have them."

**40** After he said this, he showed them his hands and his feet.

**41** They were very happy and surprised, but they still did not believe it. Jesus said to them, "Do you have any food here?"

**42** They gave him a piece of cooked fish and some honeycomb.

**43** He took the food and ate it in front of them.

**44** Jesus said to them, "When I was with you, I told you these words. All the things that the law of Moses, the prophets, and the Psalms say about me must come true."

**45** Then he made their minds open to understand the scriptures.

**46** He said to them, "The scriptures say that the Christ must suffer. They say that he must come back to life from the dead on the third day.

**47** People must tell all nations to repent. They must tell all nations that God forgives sins through the name of the Christ. They must start in Jerusalem.

**48** You saw these things, and you will tell people about them.

**49** Look, I will send to you what my Father promised. But stay in the city until you get power from heaven."

**50** Jesus led them out of the city to near Bethany. He lifted up his hands and blessed them.

**51** While he blessed them, he went away from them. God took him up into heaven.

**52** They worshiped him. Then they went back to Jerusalem with much joy.

**53** They were always in the temple, and they praised God. Amen.
