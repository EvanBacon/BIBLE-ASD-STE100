# 2 John

## Chapter 1

**1** I, the elder, write to the lady whom God chose, and to her children. I truly love you. I am not the only person who loves you. All the people who know the truth also love you.

**2** We love you because of the truth. The truth lives in us, and it will be with us for all time.

**3** Grace, mercy, and peace will be with us. They come from God the Father and from the Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of the Father. They come with truth and love.

**4** I was very happy because I found some of your children. They live in the truth, as the Father commanded us.

**5** Now, lady, I ask you this: We must love each other. This is not a new commandment that I write to you. We had this commandment from the start.

**6** This is love: We live as God commands. This is the commandment that you heard from the start: You must live in love.

**7** Many people who deceive went out into the world. They do not confess that Jesus Christ came in a human body. Such a person is a deceiver and an antichrist.

**8** Be careful. Do not lose the things that we worked for. Make sure that you get your full reward.

**9** A person who goes too far does not stay in the message of Christ. That person does not have God. A person who stays in the message of Christ has the Father and the Son.

**10** A person can come to you who does not bring this message. Do not let him into your house. Do not welcome him.

**11** A person who welcomes him shares in his evil actions.

**12** I have many things to write to you. But I do not want to write them with paper and ink. I hope to visit you and speak to you face to face. Then our joy will be full.

**13** The children of your sister, whom God chose, send you their greetings. Amen.
