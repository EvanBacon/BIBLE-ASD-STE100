# Genesis

## Chapter 1

**1** At the start, God made the heavens and the earth.

**2** The earth had no shape and it was empty. Darkness was on the surface of the deep water. The Spirit of God moved above the water.

**3** God said, "Let there be light." Then there was light.

**4** God saw that the light was good. God divided the light from the darkness.

**5** God gave the light the name "day." He gave the darkness the name "night." The evening and the morning were the first day.

**6** God said, "Let there be an expanse in the middle of the water. Let the expanse divide the water from the water."

**7** God made the expanse. He divided the water below the expanse from the water above the expanse. The result was as God said.

**8** God gave the expanse the name "sky." The evening and the morning were the second day.

**9** God said, "Let the water below the sky come together into one place. Let the dry land appear." The result was as God said.

**10** God gave the dry land the name "earth." He gave the water that came together the name "seas." God saw that the result was good.

**11** God said, "Let the earth make plants grow. Let there be plants that make seeds. Let there be trees that make fruit with seeds in the fruit. Each plant and tree will make seeds of its own type." The result was as God said.

**12** The earth made plants grow. The plants made seeds of their own type. The trees made fruit with seeds of their own type. God saw that the result was good.

**13** The evening and the morning were the third day.

**14** God said, "Let there be lights in the expanse of the sky. Let the lights divide the day from the night. Let the lights be signs for the seasons, the days, and the years.

**15** Let the lights be in the expanse of the sky. Let them give light to the earth." The result was as God said.

**16** God made 2 large lights. The larger light controls the day. The smaller light controls the night. God also made the stars.

**17** God put the lights in the expanse of the sky. The lights give light to the earth.

**18** The lights control the day and the night. They divide the light from the darkness. God saw that the result was good.

**19** The evening and the morning were the fourth day.

**20** God said, "Let the water be full of live animals. Let birds fly above the earth, across the expanse of the sky."

**21** God made the large sea animals. He made all the live animals that move in the water, each of its own type. He made all the birds, each of its own type. God saw that the result was good.

**22** God blessed the animals. He said, "Have many young and increase in number. Fill the water in the seas. Let the birds increase in number on the earth."

**23** The evening and the morning were the fifth day.

**24** God said, "Let the earth make live animals, each of its own type. Let there be livestock, small animals that move on the ground, and wild animals, each of its own type." The result was as God said.

**25** God made the wild animals, each of its own type. He made the livestock, each of its own type. He made all the small animals that move on the ground, each of its own type. God saw that the result was good.

**26** God said, "We will make man in our image, similar to us. Man will control the fish of the sea and the birds of the sky. Man will control the livestock and all the earth. Man will control all the small animals that move on the earth."

**27** God made man in his own image. He made man in the image of God. He made them male and female.

**28** God blessed them. God said to them, "Have many children and increase in number. Fill the earth and control it. Control the fish of the sea and the birds of the sky. Control all the live animals that move on the earth."

**29** God said, "Look, I give you all the plants on the earth that make seeds. I give you all the trees that make fruit with seeds. The plants and the fruit will be your food.

**30** I give all the green plants as food to all the animals of the earth and to all the birds of the sky. I give the green plants to all the small animals that move on the earth. All these animals have life." The result was as God said.

**31** God saw all the things that he made. They were very good. The evening and the morning were the sixth day.

## Chapter 2

**1** Thus God completed the heavens and the earth and all the things in them.

**2** On the seventh day, God completed the work that he did. On the seventh day, he stopped all the work that he did.

**3** God blessed the seventh day and made the seventh day holy. On that day, God stopped all the work that he did when he made all things.

**4** This is the history of the heavens and the earth at the time when God made them. The LORD God made the earth and the heavens.

**5** At that time, no bush of the field was on the earth. No plant of the field grew yet. The LORD God did not cause rain to fall on the earth. There was no man to work the ground.

**6** But a mist went up from the earth. The mist put water on all the surface of the ground.

**7** The LORD God made man from the dust of the ground. He blew the breath of life into the nose of the man. Then the man became a live being.

**8** The LORD God put a garden in Eden, in the east. He put the man that he made in the garden.

**9** The LORD God caused all types of trees to grow from the ground. The trees were good to see and good for food. The tree of life was in the middle of the garden. The tree of the knowledge of good and evil was also there.

**10** A river went out from Eden to give water to the garden. From the garden, the river divided into 4 rivers.

**11** The name of the first river is Pishon. The Pishon flows around all the land of Havilah. There is gold in Havilah.

**12** The gold of that land is good. Bdellium and onyx stone are also in that land.

**13** The name of the second river is Gihon. The Gihon flows around all the land of Cush.

**14** The name of the third river is Tigris. The Tigris flows to the east of Assyria. The fourth river is the Euphrates.

**15** The LORD God took the man and put him in the Garden of Eden. The man had to work the garden and keep the garden safe.

**16** The LORD God gave a command to the man. He said, "You can eat from all the trees of the garden.

**17** But do not eat from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil. On the day that you eat from that tree, you will die."

**18** The LORD God said, "It is not good for the man to be alone. I will make a helper that is correct for him."

**19** The LORD God made all the animals of the field and all the birds of the sky from the ground. He brought them to the man to see what names the man will give them. The name that the man gave to each animal became the name of that animal.

**20** The man gave names to all the livestock, to the birds of the sky, and to all the animals of the field. But there was no helper that was correct for Adam.

**21** The LORD God caused the man to sleep deeply. While the man slept, the LORD God took one of his ribs. Then he closed the flesh at that place.

**22** The LORD God made a woman from the rib that he took from the man. He brought the woman to the man.

**23** The man said, "Now this is bone from my bones and flesh from my flesh. Her name will be 'woman,' because she came out of man."

**24** Thus a man will leave his father and his mother. He will join with his wife. They will become one flesh.

**25** The man and his wife were both naked. They did not feel shame.

## Chapter 3

**1** The serpent was more clever than all the wild animals that the LORD God made. The serpent said to the woman, "Did God really tell you not to eat from all the trees of the garden?"

**2** The woman said to the serpent, "We can eat the fruit of the trees of the garden.

**3** But God said, 'Do not eat the fruit of the tree in the middle of the garden. Do not touch that tree, or you will die.'"

**4** The serpent said to the woman, "You will not die.

**5** God knows that your eyes will open on the day you eat from that tree. You will be like God. You will know good and evil."

**6** The woman saw that the tree was good for food. She saw that the tree was pleasant to the eyes. She saw that the tree can make a person wise. She took some of its fruit and ate the fruit. She also gave some fruit to her husband, who was with her. He ate the fruit too.

**7** Then the eyes of the man and the woman opened. They knew that they were naked. They sewed fig leaves together. They made coverings for themselves.

**8** They heard the sound of the LORD God, who walked in the garden in the cool part of the day. The man and his wife hid from the LORD God among the trees of the garden.

**9** The LORD God called to the man. He said, "Where are you?"

**10** The man said, "I heard your sound in the garden. I was afraid because I was naked. Thus I hid."

**11** God said, "Who told you that you were naked? Did you eat from the tree that I told you not to eat from?"

**12** The man said, "You gave me the woman to be with me. She gave me fruit from the tree. Then I ate the fruit."

**13** The LORD God said to the woman, "What did you do?" The woman said, "The serpent deceived me. Then I ate the fruit."

**14** The LORD God said to the serpent, "You did this. Thus I curse you more than all the livestock and all the wild animals. You will move on your stomach. You will eat dust for all the days of your life.

**15** I will cause hate between you and the woman. I will cause hate between your offspring and her offspring. He will crush your head. You will bruise his heel."

**16** The LORD God said to the woman, "I will greatly increase your pain when you have children. You will have pain when you give birth to children. You will want your husband. He will control you."

**17** The LORD God said to Adam, "You obeyed your wife. You ate from the tree that I told you not to eat from. Thus I curse the ground because of you. You will work hard to get food from the ground for all the days of your life.

**18** The ground will grow thorns and thistles for you. You will eat the plants of the field.

**19** You will work hard and sweat to get your bread. You will do this work until you go back to the ground. I took you from the ground. You are dust, and you will go back to dust."

**20** The man gave his wife the name Eve. Eve became the mother of all the people who live.

**21** The LORD God made clothes of animal skins for Adam and his wife. He put the clothes on them.

**22** The LORD God said, "Look, the man became like one of us. He knows good and evil. He must not put out his hand and take fruit from the tree of life. If he eats that fruit, he will live forever."

**23** Thus the LORD God sent the man out of the Garden of Eden. The man had to work the ground. God took the man from that ground.

**24** God sent the man out. On the east side of the Garden of Eden, God put cherubim (angels). He also put a sword of fire there. The sword turned in all directions. The cherubim and the sword guarded the path to the tree of life.

## Chapter 4

**1** Adam had sexual relations with his wife Eve. She became pregnant and gave birth to Cain. She said, "With the help of the LORD, I got a man."

**2** Then Eve gave birth to Abel, the brother of Cain. Abel kept sheep. Cain worked the ground.

**3** After some time, Cain brought some of the fruit of the ground to the LORD. Cain gave the fruit as an offering.

**4** Abel also brought an offering. He brought some of the first lambs of his sheep and their fat. The LORD accepted Abel and his offering.

**5** But the LORD did not accept Cain and his offering. Cain became very angry. His face showed that he was sad.

**6** The LORD said to Cain, "Why are you angry? Why does your face show that you are sad?

**7** If you do good things, I will accept you. If you do not do good things, sin waits at your door. Sin wants to control you. But you must control sin."

**8** Cain talked to his brother Abel. When they were in the field, Cain attacked his brother Abel. Cain killed Abel.

**9** The LORD said to Cain, "Where is your brother Abel?" Cain said, "I do not know. Must I guard my brother?"

**10** The LORD said, "What did you do? The blood of your brother calls to me from the ground.

**11** Now I curse you and send you away from the ground. The ground opened its mouth. The ground received the blood of your brother from your hand.

**12** When you work the ground, the ground will not give you good crops again. You will move from place to place on the earth. You will have no home."

**13** Cain said to the LORD, "My punishment is too large for me.

**14** Today you send me away from the surface of the ground. I will hide from your face. I will move from place to place on the earth, and I will have no home. Each person who finds me will kill me."

**15** The LORD said to Cain, "No. If a person kills Cain, I will punish that person 7 times." The LORD put a mark on Cain. The mark told people not to kill Cain when they found him.

**16** Cain went away from the LORD. He lived in the land of Nod, to the east of Eden.

**17** Cain had sexual relations with his wife. She became pregnant and gave birth to Enoch. Cain built a city. He gave the city the name of his son, Enoch.

**18** Enoch became the father of Irad. Irad became the father of Mehujael. Mehujael became the father of Methushael. Methushael became the father of Lamech.

**19** Lamech married 2 wives. The name of one wife was Adah. The name of the other wife was Zillah.

**20** Adah gave birth to Jabal. Jabal was the father of the people who live in tents and keep livestock.

**21** The name of his brother was Jubal. Jubal was the father of all the people who play the harp and the flute.

**22** Zillah gave birth to Tubal-Cain. Tubal-Cain made all types of tools from bronze and iron. The sister of Tubal-Cain was Naamah.

**23** Lamech said to his wives, "Adah and Zillah, hear my voice. Wives of Lamech, listen to my words. I killed a man because he wounded me. I killed a young man because he hit me.

**24** If a person kills Cain, God will punish that person 7 times. If a person kills Lamech, God will punish that person 77 times."

**25** Adam had sexual relations with his wife again. She gave birth to a son. She gave the son the name Seth. She said, "God gave me another son in the place of Abel, because Cain killed Abel."

**26** Seth also became the father of a son. Seth gave the son the name Enosh. At that time, people started to call on the name of the LORD.

## Chapter 5

**1** This is the book of the history of Adam. On the day that God made man, God made man similar to God.

**2** God made them male and female. He blessed them. On the day that he made them, he gave them the name "Man."

**3** When Adam was 130 years old, he became the father of a son. The son was similar to Adam. Adam gave the son the name Seth.

**4** After Adam became the father of Seth, Adam lived 800 years. He became the father of more sons and daughters.

**5** Adam lived a total of 930 years. Then he died.

**6** When Seth was 105 years old, he became the father of Enosh.

**7** After Seth became the father of Enosh, Seth lived 807 years. He became the father of more sons and daughters.

**8** Seth lived a total of 912 years. Then he died.

**9** When Enosh was 90 years old, he became the father of Kenan.

**10** After Enosh became the father of Kenan, Enosh lived 815 years. He became the father of more sons and daughters.

**11** Enosh lived a total of 905 years. Then he died.

**12** When Kenan was 70 years old, he became the father of Mahalalel.

**13** After Kenan became the father of Mahalalel, Kenan lived 840 years. He became the father of more sons and daughters.

**14** Kenan lived a total of 910 years. Then he died.

**15** When Mahalalel was 65 years old, he became the father of Jared.

**16** After Mahalalel became the father of Jared, Mahalalel lived 830 years. He became the father of more sons and daughters.

**17** Mahalalel lived a total of 895 years. Then he died.

**18** When Jared was 162 years old, he became the father of Enoch.

**19** After Jared became the father of Enoch, Jared lived 800 years. He became the father of more sons and daughters.

**20** Jared lived a total of 962 years. Then he died.

**21** When Enoch was 65 years old, he became the father of Methuselah.

**22** After Enoch became the father of Methuselah, Enoch walked with God for 300 years. He became the father of more sons and daughters.

**23** Enoch lived a total of 365 years.

**24** Enoch walked with God. Then people did not see Enoch again, because God took him.

**25** When Methuselah was 187 years old, he became the father of Lamech.

**26** After Methuselah became the father of Lamech, Methuselah lived 782 years. He became the father of more sons and daughters.

**27** Methuselah lived a total of 969 years. Then he died.

**28** When Lamech was 182 years old, he became the father of a son.

**29** Lamech gave the son the name Noah. Lamech said, "This son will give us comfort in our work. We work hard with our hands because the LORD cursed the ground."

**30** After Lamech became the father of Noah, Lamech lived 595 years. He became the father of more sons and daughters.

**31** Lamech lived a total of 777 years. Then he died.

**32** When Noah was 500 years old, he became the father of Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

## Chapter 6

**1** The number of men started to increase on the surface of the earth. The men had daughters.

**2** The sons of God saw that the daughters of men were beautiful. The sons of God took as wives the women that they chose.

**3** The LORD said, "My Spirit will not stay with man forever, because man is flesh. The days of man will be 120 years."

**4** The Nephilim (giants) were on the earth in those days and also after that time. The sons of God had sexual relations with the daughters of men. The women gave birth to children. These children became the strong men of old times. They were men with famous names.

**5** The LORD saw that the evil of man on the earth was very large. He saw that all the thoughts of the heart of man were evil all the time.

**6** The LORD was sorry that he made man on the earth. His heart felt much pain.

**7** The LORD said, "I will remove the men that I made from the surface of the ground. I will remove men, livestock, small animals, and birds of the sky. I am sorry that I made them."

**8** But the LORD was happy with Noah.

**9** This is the history of Noah. Noah was a righteous man. He did no wrong among the people of his time. Noah walked with God.

**10** Noah became the father of 3 sons. Their names were Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

**11** God saw that the earth was corrupt. The earth was full of violence.

**12** God looked at the earth. He saw that the earth was corrupt. All the people on the earth made their way of life corrupt.

**13** God said to Noah, "I will put an end to all people. The earth is full of violence because of them. I will destroy the people and the earth.

**14** Make an ark (a large boat) of gopher wood. Make rooms in the ark. Put pitch (tar) on the inside and the outside of the ark.

**15** Make the ark with these dimensions. The length of the ark will be 300 cubits. The width will be 50 cubits. The height will be 30 cubits.

**16** Make a roof for the ark. Make a space of 1 cubit below the roof. Put a door in the side of the ark. Make the ark with a lower deck, a second deck, and a third deck.

**17** I will cause a flood of water on the earth. The flood will destroy all flesh under the sky that has the breath of life. All things on the earth will die.

**18** But I will make my agreement (covenant) with you. You will go into the ark with your sons, your wife, and the wives of your sons.

**19** Bring into the ark 2 animals of each type, a male and a female. Keep the animals alive with you.

**20** 2 of each type of bird will come to you. 2 of each type of livestock will come to you. 2 of each type of small animal that moves on the ground will come to you. Keep them alive.

**21** Take all types of food. Keep the food with you. The food will be for you and for the animals."

**22** Noah did all the things that God told him to do.

## Chapter 7

**1** The LORD said to Noah, "Go into the ark with all your family. I see that you are righteous among the people of this time.

**2** Take 7 pairs of each type of clean animal, a male and its female. Take 1 pair of each type of animal that is not clean, a male and its female.

**3** Also take 7 pairs of each type of bird of the sky, male and female. Then their offspring will stay alive on the surface of all the earth.

**4** After 7 days, I will cause rain to fall on the earth for 40 days and 40 nights. I will remove all the live things that I made from the surface of the ground."

**5** Noah did all the things that the LORD told him to do.

**6** Noah was 600 years old when the flood of water came on the earth.

**7** Noah went into the ark with his sons, his wife, and the wives of his sons. They went into the ark to escape the water of the flood.

**8** Clean animals and animals that were not clean came to Noah. Birds and all the small animals that move on the ground also came.

**9** They came in pairs, male and female, and went into the ark. They came as God told Noah.

**10** After 7 days, the water of the flood came on the earth.

**11** In the 600th year of the life of Noah, on the 17th day of the second month, all the springs of the deep water opened. On that day, the windows of the sky opened.

**12** Rain fell on the earth for 40 days and 40 nights.

**13** On that same day, Noah went into the ark with his sons Shem, Ham, and Japheth. The wife of Noah and the 3 wives of his sons also went in with them.

**14** Each type of wild animal and each type of livestock went in with them. Each type of small animal that moves on the earth went in. Each type of bird went in.

**15** Pairs of all flesh that has the breath of life went into the ark with Noah.

**16** A male and a female of all flesh went in, as God told Noah. Then the LORD closed the door behind Noah.

**17** The flood continued on the earth for 40 days. The water increased and lifted the ark. The ark went up high above the earth.

**18** The water became very strong and increased much on the earth. The ark moved on the surface of the water.

**19** The water became very, very strong on the earth. The water covered all the high mountains under all the sky.

**20** The water went up 15 cubits above the mountains. The water covered the mountains.

**21** All flesh that moved on the earth died. Birds, livestock, wild animals, and all the small animals died. All the people died.

**22** All the things on the dry land that had the breath of life in their nose died.

**23** God removed all the live things from the surface of the ground. He removed people, livestock, small animals, and birds of the sky from the earth. Only Noah and the people and animals with him in the ark stayed alive.

**24** The water stayed strong on the earth for 150 days.

## Chapter 8

**1** God remembered Noah. He remembered all the wild animals and all the livestock with Noah in the ark. God caused a wind to blow over the earth. The water started to decrease.

**2** The springs of the deep water and the windows of the sky closed. The rain from the sky stopped.

**3** The water went back from the earth slowly. After 150 days, the water decreased.

**4** On the 17th day of the seventh month, the ark stopped on the mountains of Ararat.

**5** The water continued to decrease until the tenth month. On the first day of the tenth month, people can see the tops of the mountains.

**6** After 40 days, Noah opened the window that he made in the ark.

**7** Noah sent out a raven. The raven flew here and there until the water on the earth became dry.

**8** Then Noah sent out a dove. He wanted to see if the water decreased from the surface of the ground.

**9** The dove found no place to put its feet, because water covered the surface of all the earth. The dove came back to Noah in the ark. Noah put out his hand and took the dove. He brought the dove back into the ark.

**10** Noah waited 7 more days. Then he sent the dove out of the ark again.

**11** The dove came back to Noah in the evening. The dove had a fresh olive leaf in its beak. Then Noah knew that the water decreased from the earth.

**12** Noah waited 7 more days. Then he sent out the dove again. The dove did not come back to him.

**13** In the 601st year of the life of Noah, on the first day of the first month, the water on the earth became dry. Noah removed the cover of the ark. He looked out and saw that the surface of the ground was dry.

**14** On the 27th day of the second month, the earth was fully dry.

**15** Then God spoke to Noah. God said these words:

**16** "Go out of the ark with your wife, your sons, and the wives of your sons.

**17** Bring out all the live animals of all flesh with you. Bring out the birds, the livestock, and all the small animals that move on the earth. Let them have many young and increase in number on the earth."

**18** Noah went out with his sons, his wife, and the wives of his sons.

**19** All the animals, all the small animals, and all the birds went out of the ark. All the things that move on the earth went out. They went out in their families.

**20** Noah built an altar to the LORD. He took some of each type of clean animal and each type of clean bird. He gave them as burnt offerings on the altar.

**21** The LORD smelled the pleasant smell. The LORD said in his heart, "I will not curse the ground again because of man. The thoughts of the heart of man are evil from his youth. I will not destroy all the live things again, as I did.

**22** While the earth continues, the time to plant seeds and the time to collect crops will not stop. Cold and heat will not stop. Summer and winter will not stop. Day and night will not stop."

## Chapter 9

**1** God blessed Noah and his sons. He said to them, "Have many children and increase in number. Fill the earth.

**2** All the animals of the earth and all the birds of the sky will fear you. All the small animals that move on the ground and all the fish of the sea will fear you. I give them into your control.

**3** All the animals that move and live will be food for you. Before, I gave you the green plants as food. Now I give you all things.

**4** But do not eat meat that has its life in it. The life of the meat is its blood.

**5** Your blood is your life. I will make the killer pay for your blood. I will make each animal that kills a person pay. I will make each person who kills another person pay.

**6** If a person kills a man, another man will kill that person. God made man in his own image.

**7** You must have many children and increase in number. Fill the earth and increase on the earth."

**8** Then God spoke to Noah and to his sons with him. God said,

**9** "Look, I make my agreement (covenant) with you and with your offspring after you.

**10** I also make my agreement with all the live things that are with you. I make it with the birds, the livestock, and all the wild animals. I make it with all the animals that came out of the ark.

**11** I make my agreement with you. A flood of water will not kill all flesh again. A flood will not destroy the earth again."

**12** God said, "I give you a sign of the agreement between me and you. The agreement is also with all the live things with you. The agreement is for all the generations in the future.

**13** I put my rainbow in the cloud. The rainbow will be the sign of the agreement between me and the earth.

**14** I will bring clouds over the earth. Then people will see the rainbow in the clouds.

**15** I will remember my agreement between me and you and all the live animals of all flesh. Water will not become a flood to destroy all flesh again.

**16** The rainbow will be in the cloud. I will look at the rainbow. Then I will remember the agreement that continues forever. The agreement is between God and all the live animals of all flesh on the earth."

**17** God said to Noah, "The rainbow is the sign of the agreement between me and all flesh on the earth."

**18** The sons of Noah who went out of the ark were Shem, Ham, and Japheth. Ham was the father of Canaan.

**19** These 3 men were the sons of Noah. All the people of the earth came from these 3 men.

**20** Noah started to work as a farmer. He planted a vineyard.

**21** Noah drank some of the wine and became drunk. He lay naked in his tent.

**22** Ham, the father of Canaan, saw that his father was naked. Ham went outside and told his 2 brothers.

**23** Shem and Japheth took a garment and put the garment on their shoulders. They walked backward and covered their naked father. They turned their faces away. They did not see their father naked.

**24** Noah woke up from the effect of the wine. He knew what his youngest son did to him.

**25** Noah said, "I curse Canaan. Canaan will be the lowest servant of his brothers."

**26** Noah also said, "Praise the LORD, the God of Shem. Canaan will be the servant of Shem.

**27** May God make the land of Japheth large. Japheth will live in the tents of Shem. Canaan will be the servant of Japheth."

**28** After the flood, Noah lived 350 years.

**29** Noah lived a total of 950 years. Then he died.

## Chapter 10

**1** This is the history of the sons of Noah. Their names were Shem, Ham, and Japheth. After the flood, they became the fathers of sons.

**2** The sons of Japheth were Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras.

**3** The sons of Gomer were Ashkenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah.

**4** The sons of Javan were Elishah, Tarshish, Kittim, and Dodanim.

**5** The people of the coastlands came from these sons. They divided into their lands. Each group had its own language, family, and nation.

**6** The sons of Ham were Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan.

**7** The sons of Cush were Seba, Havilah, Sabtah, Raamah, and Sabteca. The sons of Raamah were Sheba and Dedan.

**8** Cush became the father of Nimrod. Nimrod became the first powerful man on the earth.

**9** Nimrod was a powerful hunter in front of the LORD. Thus people say, "He is like Nimrod, a powerful hunter in front of the LORD."

**8** Then the king of Sodom, the king of Gomorrah, and the king of Admah went out. The king of Zeboiim and the king of Bela (Zoar) also went out. They prepared for battle in the valley of Siddim.

**9** They fought against Chedorlaomer king of Elam, Tidal king of Goiim, Amraphel king of Shinar, and Arioch king of Ellasar. Four kings fought against five kings.

**10** The valley of Siddim had many pits of tar. The kings of Sodom and Gomorrah ran away. Some of their men fell into the pits. The other men ran away to the mountains.

**11** The four kings took all the possessions of Sodom and Gomorrah. They took all the food. Then they went away.

**12** They also took Lot, the son of the brother of Abram. Lot lived in Sodom. They took Lot and his possessions and went away.

**13** A man escaped and came to Abram the Hebrew. The man told Abram what occurred. Abram lived near the oak trees of Mamre the Amorite. Mamre was the brother of Eshcol and Aner. These men had an agreement with Abram.

**16** Abram brought back all the possessions. He also brought back his relative Lot and the possessions of Lot. He brought back the women and the other people too.

**17** Abram defeated Chedorlaomer and the kings with him. Then Abram came back. The king of Sodom went out to meet him in the valley of Shaveh (the King's Valley).

**18** Melchizedek king of Salem brought out bread and wine. Melchizedek was the priest of God Most High.

**21** The king of Sodom said to Abram, "Give the people to me. Keep the possessions for yourself."

**23** I will not take a thread or a sandal strap. I will take nothing that is yours. Then you cannot say, 'I made Abram rich.'

**24** I will take nothing for myself. I will only accept the food that my young men ate. Aner, Eshcol, and Mamre went with me. Let them take their part."

**5** The LORD brought Abram outside. The LORD said, "Look up at the sky. Count the stars, if you can count them." Then he said, "Your descendants will be as many as the stars."

**7** The LORD said to Abram, "I am the LORD. I brought you out of Ur of the Chaldeans. I will give you this land as your possession."

## Chapter 17

**2** I will make my agreement (covenant) between me and you. I will give you very many descendants."

**3** Abram bowed down with his face to the ground. God spoke to him and said,

**5** Your name will not be Abram any more. Your name will be Abraham. I make you the father of many nations.

**6** I will give you very many descendants. I will make nations from you. Kings will come from you.

**7** I will make my agreement between me and you and your descendants. This agreement will continue through all their generations for all time. I will be your God and the God of your descendants.

**8** Now you live as a foreigner in the land of Canaan. I will give all the land of Canaan to you and your descendants. The land will be your possession for all time. I will be their God."

**10** This is my agreement between me and you and your descendants. You must obey this agreement. You must circumcise each male among you.

**11** You must cut away the foreskin of each male. This will be the sign of the agreement between me and you.

**25** His son Ishmael was 13 years old when he received circumcision.

**26** Abraham and his son Ishmael received circumcision on that same day.

**27** All the men of the household of Abraham received circumcision with him. This included the men born in his house. It also included the men that he bought with money from foreigners.

**14** Is any thing too difficult for the LORD? I will come back to you at the set time next year. Then Sarah will have a son."

**31** Abraham said, "Look, I speak to the Lord again. Possibly you will find 20 there." The LORD said, "I will not destroy the city because of the 20."

## Chapter 19

**2** Lot said, "My lords, please come into the house of your servant. Stay the night and wash your feet. Then you can get up early and continue your journey." The angels said, "No, we will stay the night in the street."

**4** Before they lay down, the men of the city came. All the men of Sodom, young and old, came from all parts of the city. They stood around the house.

**5** They called to Lot. They said, "Where are the men who came to you tonight? Bring them out to us. We want to have sexual relations with them."

**6** Lot went out to the men at the door. He closed the door behind him.

**7** He said, "Please, my brothers, do not do this wicked thing.

**8** Look, I have two daughters who are virgins. Let me bring them out to you. Do to them what you think is good. But do nothing to these men. They came into my house for protection."

**9** The men said, "Move away!" Then they said, "This man came here as a foreigner. Now he wants to be our judge! Now we will do worse things to you than to them." They pushed hard against Lot. They came near to break the door.

**11** Then the angels made the men at the door blind, the young and the old. So the men could not find the door.

**12** The two men said to Lot, "Do you have other relatives here? Take your sons-in-law, your sons, and your daughters. Take all your people in the city out of this place.

**31** The older daughter said to the younger daughter, "Our father is old. There is no man in the land to marry us. All the people of the earth marry, but we cannot.

**34** On the next day, the older daughter said to the younger daughter, "Look, last night I had sexual relations with my father. Tonight we will make him drink wine again. Then you go in and have sexual relations with him. Thus we will have children from our father."

**37** The older daughter gave birth to a son. She called him Moab. He is the father of the Moabites until today.

**38** The younger daughter also gave birth to a son. She called him Ben-Ammi. He is the father of the people of Ammon until today.

**3** Abraham gave the name Isaac to his son. Sarah gave birth to Isaac for him.

**4** When Isaac was 8 days old, Abraham circumcised him. Abraham did as God commanded him.

**5** Abraham was 100 years old when Sarah gave birth to his son Isaac.

**6** Sarah said, "God caused me to laugh. All the people who hear about my son will laugh with me."

**7** She also said, "Nobody could tell Abraham that Sarah will feed children with her milk. But I gave birth to a son for him when he was old."

**9** Hagar was the Egyptian woman who gave birth to a son for Abraham. Sarah saw that son laugh at Isaac.

**34** Abraham lived as a foreigner in the land of the Philistines for many days.

## Chapter 22

**3** Early in the morning, Abraham got up and put a saddle on his donkey. He took 2 of his young men and his son Isaac with him. He cut the wood for the burnt offering. Then he went to the place that God told him about.

**4** On the third day, Abraham looked up. He saw the place far away.

**5** Abraham said to his young men, "Stay here with the donkey. The boy and I will go over there. We will worship, and then we will come back to you."

**6** Abraham took the wood for the burnt offering and put the wood on his son Isaac. He held the fire and the knife in his hand. The two of them walked together.

**7** Isaac spoke to his father Abraham. He said, "My father." Abraham said, "I am here, my son." Isaac said, "Here are the fire and the wood. But where is the lamb for a burnt offering?"

**9** They came to the place that God told Abraham about. Abraham built an altar there and put the wood on the altar. He tied his son Isaac. He put Isaac on the altar, on top of the wood.

**10** "'You will live in the land of Goshen. You will be near me. Your children, your grandchildren, your flocks, your herds, and all that you have will be near me.'"

**11** "'I will give you food there, because 5 more years of famine will come. If you do not come, you, your family, and all that you have will become poor.'"

**12** "Look. Your eyes and the eyes of my brother Benjamin see that I speak to you with my mouth."

**13** "Tell my father about all my glory in Egypt. Tell him about all that you saw. Quickly bring my father down here."

**14** Joseph put his arms around the neck of his brother Benjamin and cried. Benjamin cried on the neck of Joseph.

**15** Joseph kissed all his brothers and cried over them. After that, his brothers spoke with him.

**16** The house of Pharaoh heard the news. People said, "The brothers of Joseph came." Pharaoh and his servants were happy.

**17** Pharaoh said to Joseph, "Tell your brothers: 'Do this. Put loads on your animals. Go to the land of Canaan.'"

**18** "'Get your father and your families and come to me. I will give you the good things of the land of Egypt. You will eat the best food of the land.'"

**19** "You must also tell them: 'Do this. Take wagons from the land of Egypt for your little children and your wives. Bring your father and come.'"

**20** "'Do not worry about your things. The good things of all the land of Egypt are yours.'"

**21** The sons of Israel did so. Joseph gave them wagons, as Pharaoh told him. He gave them food for the travel.

**22** He gave each of them a set of new clothes. But he gave Benjamin 300 pieces of silver and 5 sets of clothes.

**23** He sent these things to his father: 10 donkeys with loads of the good things of Egypt. He also sent 10 female donkeys with loads of grain, bread, and food for his father on the travel.

**24** Then he sent his brothers away, and they went. He said to them, "Do not argue on the travel."

**25** They went up out of Egypt. They came into the land of Canaan to Jacob their father.

**26** They told him, "Joseph is still alive. He is the ruler over all the land of Egypt." The heart of Jacob became numb. He did not believe them.

**27** They told him all the words that Joseph said to them. Jacob saw the wagons that Joseph sent to carry him. Then the spirit of Jacob their father became alive again.

**28** Israel said, "This is sufficient. My son Joseph is still alive. I will go and see him before I die."

## Chapter 46

**1** Israel went with all that he had. He came to Beersheba. He offered sacrifices to the God of his father Isaac.

**2** God spoke to Israel in visions in the night. He said, "Jacob, Jacob." Jacob said, "I am here."

**3** God said, "I am God, the God of your father. Do not be afraid to go down into Egypt. I will make you into a great nation there."

**4** "I will go down with you into Egypt. I will also surely bring you up again. Joseph will close your eyes with his hand."

**5** Jacob went from Beersheba. The sons of Israel carried Jacob their father, their little children, and their wives in the wagons. Pharaoh sent the wagons to carry Jacob.

**6** They took their livestock and their goods. They got these things in the land of Canaan. They came into Egypt. Jacob and all his descendants came with him.

**7** He brought his sons and his grandsons with him. He brought his daughters and his granddaughters. He brought all his descendants into Egypt.

**8** These are the names of the children of Israel who came into Egypt: Jacob and his sons. Reuben was the first son of Jacob.

**9** The sons of Reuben were Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi.

**10** The sons of Simeon were Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul. Shaul was the son of a Canaanite woman.

**11** The sons of Levi were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.

**12** The sons of Judah were Er, Onan, Shelah, Perez, and Zerah. But Er and Onan died in the land of Canaan. The sons of Perez were Hezron and Hamul.

**13** The sons of Issachar were Tola, Puvah, Iob, and Shimron.

**14** Zebulun had 3 sons: Sered, Elon, and Jahleel.

**15** These were the sons of Leah. She gave birth to them for Jacob in Paddan Aram. She also gave birth to his daughter Dinah. All his sons and daughters from Leah were 33 persons.

**16** The sons of Gad were Ziphion, Haggi, Shuni, Ezbon, Eri, Arodi, and Areli.

**17** The sons of Asher were Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, and Beriah. Serah was their sister. The sons of Beriah were Heber and Malchiel.

**18** These were the sons of Zilpah. Laban gave Zilpah to his daughter Leah. Zilpah gave birth to these children for Jacob. They were 16 persons.

**19** The sons of Rachel, the wife of Jacob, were Joseph and Benjamin.

**20** In the land of Egypt, Joseph became the father of Manasseh and Ephraim. Asenath gave birth to them. She was the daughter of Potiphera, the priest of On.

**21** The sons of Benjamin were Bela, Becher, Ashbel, Gera, Naaman, Ehi, Rosh, Muppim, Huppim, and Ard.

**22** These were the sons of Rachel. She gave birth to them for Jacob. They were 14 persons in all.

**23** The son of Dan was Hushim.

**24** The sons of Naphtali were Jahzeel, Guni, Jezer, and Shillem.

**25** These were the sons of Bilhah. Laban gave Bilhah to his daughter Rachel. Bilhah gave birth to these children for Jacob. They were 7 persons in all.

**26** All the persons who came with Jacob into Egypt were his descendants. The wives of the sons of Jacob are not in this count. They were 66 persons in all.

**27** Joseph had 2 sons in Egypt. All the persons of the house of Jacob who came into Egypt were 70.

**28** Jacob sent Judah in front of him to Joseph. Judah went to get directions to Goshen. Then they came into the land of Goshen.

**29** Joseph made his chariot ready. He went up to Goshen to meet his father Israel. Joseph came to his father and put his arms around his neck. He cried on the neck of his father for a long time.

**30** Israel said to Joseph, "Now I can die. I saw your face. You are still alive."

**31** Joseph said to his brothers and to the family of his father, "I will go up and speak to Pharaoh. I will say to him, 'My brothers and the family of my father came to me. They were in the land of Canaan.'"

**32** "'The men are shepherds. They keep livestock. They brought their flocks, their herds, and all that they have.'"

**33** "Pharaoh will call you. He will ask, 'What is your work?'"

**34** "You must say, 'Your servants kept livestock from our youth until now. We and our fathers did this work.' Then you can live in the land of Goshen. All shepherds are hateful to the Egyptians."

## Chapter 47

**1** Joseph went in and spoke to Pharaoh. He said, "My father and my brothers came from the land of Canaan. They brought their flocks, their herds, and all that they have. They are now in the land of Goshen."

**2** Joseph took 5 of his brothers with him. He brought them in front of Pharaoh.

**3** Pharaoh said to the brothers of Joseph, "What is your work?" They said to Pharaoh, "Your servants are shepherds. We and our fathers are shepherds."

**4** They also said to Pharaoh, "We came to live for a time in the land. There is no grass for the flocks of your servants. The famine is very bad in the land of Canaan. Please let your servants live in the land of Goshen."

**5** Pharaoh spoke to Joseph. He said, "Your father and your brothers came to you."

**6** "The land of Egypt is open to you. Let your father and your brothers live in the best part of the land. Let them live in the land of Goshen. If you know capable men among them, put them in control of my livestock."

**7** Joseph brought Jacob his father in front of Pharaoh. Jacob gave a blessing to Pharaoh.

**8** Pharaoh said to Jacob, "How old are you?"

**9** Jacob said to Pharaoh, "I travelled on the earth for 130 years. My years were few and bad. They are not as many as the years of my fathers on their travels."

**10** Jacob gave a blessing to Pharaoh. Then he went out from Pharaoh.

**11** Joseph gave a place to his father and his brothers. He gave them property in the land of Egypt, in the best part of the land. He gave them the land of Rameses, as Pharaoh told him.

**12** Joseph gave food to his father, his brothers, and all the family of his father. He gave food to each family for the number of its children.

**13** There was no bread in all the land. The famine was very bad. The land of Egypt and the land of Canaan became weak because of the famine.

**14** Joseph collected all the money in the land of Egypt and in the land of Canaan. The people gave the money for the grain that they bought. Joseph brought the money into the house of Pharaoh.

**15** The money in the land of Egypt and in the land of Canaan came to an end. All the Egyptians came to Joseph. They said, "Give us bread. Why must we die in front of you? We have no more money."

**16** Joseph said, "Give me your livestock. If you have no money, I will give you food for your livestock."

**17** They brought their livestock to Joseph. Joseph gave them bread for their horses, flocks, herds, and donkeys. He gave them bread for all their livestock for that year.

**18** That year came to an end. They came to him in the second year. They said to him, "We will not hide from my lord that all our money is gone. My lord has all our herds of animals. Nothing stays for my lord but our bodies and our land."

**19** "Why must we and our land die in front of your eyes? Buy us and our land for bread. We and our land will be slaves to Pharaoh. Give us seed so that we live and do not die. Then the land will not become empty."

**20** Joseph bought all the land of Egypt for Pharaoh. Each Egyptian sold his field, because the famine was very bad for them. Thus the land became the property of Pharaoh.

**21** Joseph moved the people into the cities, from one end of Egypt to the other end.

**22** But Joseph did not buy the land of the priests. The priests received food from Pharaoh. They ate the food that Pharaoh gave them. Thus they did not sell their land.

**23** Joseph said to the people, "Today I bought you and your land for Pharaoh. Here is seed for you. Plant the seed in the land."

**24** "At the harvest, you will give one fifth to Pharaoh. Four fifths will be yours. Use them as seed for the field. Use them as food for you, your families, and your little children."

**25** They said, "You saved our lives. Let us find grace in the eyes of my lord. We will be the slaves of Pharaoh."

**26** Joseph made a law about the land of Egypt. The law continues to this day. Pharaoh must get one fifth. Only the land of the priests did not become the property of Pharaoh.

**27** Israel lived in the land of Egypt, in the land of Goshen. They got property there. They had many children and became very many.

**28** Jacob lived in the land of Egypt for 17 years. The full age of Jacob was 147 years.

**29** The time came near for Israel to die. He called his son Joseph. He said to him, "If you love me, put your hand under my thigh. Promise to be kind and faithful to me. Do not bury me in Egypt."

**30** "When I die and go to my fathers, carry me out of Egypt. Bury me in their grave." Joseph said, "I will do as you say."

**31** Israel said, "Make a vow to me." Joseph made a vow to him. Then Israel bowed down at the head of his bed.

## Chapter 48

**1** After these things, someone told Joseph, "Your father is sick." Joseph took his two sons, Manasseh and Ephraim, with him.

**2** Someone told Jacob, "Your son Joseph comes to you." Israel used his strength and sat up on the bed.

**3** Jacob said to Joseph, "God Almighty appeared to me at Luz in the land of Canaan. He gave me a blessing."

**4** "He said to me, 'I will make you fruitful, and I will make you many. I will make you a group of nations. I will give this land to your descendants after you. The land will be their property for all time.'"

**5** "Your two sons, Ephraim and Manasseh, are now mine. You became their father in the land of Egypt before I came to you. They will be mine, as Reuben and Simeon are mine."

**6** "The children that you will have after them will be yours. They will receive their inheritance under the names of their brothers."

**7** "When I came from Paddan, Rachel died near me in the land of Canaan. She died on the road, a short distance from Ephrath. I buried her there on the road to Ephrath. Ephrath is Bethlehem."

**8** Israel saw the sons of Joseph. He said, "Who are these boys?"

**9** Joseph said to his father, "They are my sons. God gave them to me here." Israel said, "Please bring them to me. I will give them a blessing."

**10** The eyes of Israel were weak from old age. He could not see well. Joseph brought his sons near to him. Israel kissed them and put his arms around them.

**11** Israel said to Joseph, "I did not think that I will see your face again. But God also let me see your children."

**12** Joseph took the boys from between the knees of Israel. Then Joseph bowed down with his face to the earth.

**13** Joseph took his two sons. He put Ephraim at his right hand, at the left hand of Israel. He put Manasseh at his left hand, at the right hand of Israel. He brought them near to Israel.

**14** Israel put out his right hand and put it on the head of Ephraim. Ephraim was the younger son. Israel put his left hand on the head of Manasseh. Manasseh was the first son. Israel crossed his hands on purpose.

**15** Israel gave a blessing to Joseph. He said, "My fathers Abraham and Isaac walked in front of God. God was my shepherd all my life until today."

**16** "The angel saved me from all evil. Let the angel give a blessing to these boys. Let my name and the names of my fathers Abraham and Isaac continue in them. Let them become very many on the earth."

**17** Joseph saw that his father put his right hand on the head of Ephraim. Joseph did not like this. He held the hand of his father to move it from the head of Ephraim to the head of Manasseh.

**18** Joseph said to his father, "Not so, my father. This boy is the first son. Put your right hand on his head."

**19** His father did not agree. He said, "I know, my son, I know. He will also become a people. He will also become great. But his younger brother will be greater than he. The descendants of his younger brother will become many nations."

**20** Israel gave them a blessing that day. He said, "The people of Israel will use your names in blessings. They will say, 'God make you as Ephraim and as Manasseh.'" Thus Israel put Ephraim before Manasseh.

**21** Israel said to Joseph, "I will die soon. But God will be with you. He will bring you back to the land of your fathers."

**22** "I give you one more portion of land than your brothers. I took that land from the Amorites with my sword and my bow."

## Chapter 49

**1** Jacob called his sons. He said, "Come together. I will tell you what will occur to you in the days that will come."

**2** "Come together and listen, you sons of Jacob. Listen to Israel your father."

**3** "Reuben, you are my first son. You are my strength and the start of my power. You are the first in honor and the first in power."

**4** "But you are as unstable as water. You will not be first. You went up onto the bed of your father. You made my bed unclean."

**5** "Simeon and Levi are brothers. Their swords are weapons of violence."

**6** "My soul will not go into their secret meetings. My honor will not join their group. In their anger, they killed men. In their bad will, they made oxen lame."

**7** "I curse their anger, because their anger is fierce. I curse their fury, because their fury is cruel. I will divide them in Jacob. I will scatter them in Israel."

**8** "Judah, your brothers will praise you. Your hand will be on the neck of your enemies. The sons of your father will bow down to you."

**9** "Judah is a young lion. My son, you go up from the prey. He lies down like a lion. He is like a female lion. Who will make him get up?"

**10** "The scepter (the rod of a king) will not go away from Judah. The staff of a ruler will not go from between his feet. This will continue until Shiloh comes. The peoples will obey him."

**11** "He ties his young donkey to the vine. He ties the young of his donkey to the best vine. He washes his clothes in wine. He washes his robes in the blood of grapes."

**12** "His eyes will be red with wine. His teeth will be white with milk."

**13** "Zebulun will live at the shore of the sea. He will be a harbor for ships. His border will be near Sidon."

**14** "Issachar is a strong donkey. He lies down between two loads."

**15** "He saw that a place of rest was good. He saw that the land was pleasant. He put his shoulder under the load. He became a slave who does forced work."

**16** "Dan will judge his people as one of the tribes of Israel."

**17** "Dan will be a snake on the road. He will be a viper on the path. He bites the heels of the horse. Then the rider falls backward."

**18** "LORD, I wait for your salvation."

**19** "A group of attackers will attack Gad. But Gad will attack their heels."

**20** "Asher will have rich food. He will give food that is good for kings."

**21** "Naphtali is a free female deer. He says beautiful words."

**22** "Joseph is a fruitful vine. He is a fruitful vine near a spring. His branches go over the wall."

**23** "The archers attacked him angrily. They shot at him and hated him."

**24** "But his bow stayed strong. His arms stayed strong because of the hand of the Mighty One of Jacob. The Shepherd, the Stone of Israel, comes from there."

**25** "The God of your father will help you. The Almighty will give you blessings. He will give you blessings of the sky above and of the deep water below. He will give you blessings of the breasts and of the womb."

**26** "The blessings of your father are greater than the blessings of my ancestors. They go to the edge of the old hills. Let them be on the head of Joseph. Let them be on the head of the prince among his brothers."

**27** "Benjamin is a hungry wolf. In the morning, he eats the prey. In the evening, he divides the things that he took from his enemies."

**28** All these are the 12 tribes of Israel. Their father said these words to them. He gave each son the correct blessing for that son.

**29** Jacob gave orders to his sons. He said, "I will soon die and go to my people. Bury me with my fathers in the cave in the field of Ephron the Hittite."

**30** "The cave is in the field of Machpelah, near Mamre, in the land of Canaan. Abraham bought the cave with the field from Ephron the Hittite. He bought the cave as a burial place."

**31** "People buried Abraham and his wife Sarah there. People buried Isaac and his wife Rebekah there. I buried Leah there."

**32** "Abraham bought the field and the cave from the sons of Heth."

**33** Jacob completed his orders to his sons. He put his feet up into the bed. He breathed his last breath and died. He went to his people.

## Chapter 50

**1** Joseph fell on the face of his father. He cried over him and kissed him.

**2** Joseph told his servants, the physicians, to embalm his father (to prepare the body with spices to keep it). The physicians embalmed Israel.

**3** They used 40 days for this work, because that work takes 40 days. The Egyptians cried for Jacob for 70 days.

**4** The days of sorrow for Jacob came to an end. Joseph spoke to the house of Pharaoh. He said, "If I find grace in your eyes, please speak to Pharaoh for me. Tell him this:"

**5** "'My father made me make a vow. He said, "I will die soon. Bury me in the grave that I dug for me in the land of Canaan." Now please let me go up and bury my father. Then I will come back.'"

**6** Pharaoh said, "Go up and bury your father, as you made a vow to him."

**7** Joseph went up to bury his father. All the servants of Pharaoh went up with him. The elders of the house of Pharaoh and all the elders of the land of Egypt went with him.

**8** All the family of Joseph, his brothers, and the family of his father also went. They left only their little children, their flocks, and their herds in the land of Goshen.

**9** Chariots and horsemen also went up with him. The group was very large.

**10** They came to the floor of Atad, where people beat the grain, on the other side of the Jordan. There they cried very loudly and with much sorrow. Joseph mourned for his father for 7 days.

**11** The Canaanites who lived in the land saw the sorrow at the floor of Atad, where people beat the grain. They said, "The Egyptians have a time of very deep sorrow." Thus people gave the place the name Abel Mizraim. The place is on the other side of the Jordan.

**12** The sons of Jacob did for him as he told them.

**13** His sons carried him into the land of Canaan. They buried him in the cave of the field of Machpelah, near Mamre. Abraham bought the field from Ephron the Hittite as a burial place.

**14** After Joseph buried his father, he went back into Egypt. His brothers and all the persons who went up with him to bury his father also went back.

**15** The brothers of Joseph saw that their father was dead. They said, "Maybe Joseph will hate us. Maybe he will do to us all the evil that we did to him."

**16** They sent a message to Joseph. They said, "Your father gave this order before he died:"

**17** "'Tell Joseph: "Please forgive the sin of your brothers. They did evil to you. Please forgive them."' Now, please forgive the sin of the servants of the God of your father." Joseph cried when they spoke to him.

**18** His brothers also came and fell down in front of him. They said, "We are your slaves."

**19** Joseph said to them, "Do not be afraid. I am not in the place of God."

**20** "You wanted to do evil against me. But God used the evil for good. He did so to keep many people alive, as he does today."

**21** "Now do not be afraid. I will give food to you and your little children." He comforted them and spoke kindly to them.

**22** Joseph lived in Egypt with the family of his father. Joseph lived 110 years.

**23** Joseph saw the children of Ephraim to the third generation. He also took the children of Machir, the son of Manasseh, onto his knees at their birth.

**24** Joseph said to his brothers, "I will die soon. But God will surely help you. He will bring you up out of this land. He will bring you to the land that he promised to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob."

**25** Joseph made the children of Israel make a vow. He said, "God will surely help you. When he helps you, carry my bones up from here."

**26** Joseph died. He was 110 years old. They embalmed him. They put his body in a coffin in Egypt.
