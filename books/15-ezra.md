# Ezra

## Chapter 1

**1** In the first year of Cyrus king of Persia, the LORD caused the words of Jeremiah to come true. The LORD moved the spirit of Cyrus king of Persia. Thus Cyrus sent a message through all his kingdom. He also wrote the message down. The message said:

**2** "Cyrus king of Persia says this: The LORD, the God of heaven, gave me all the kingdoms of the earth. He told me to build a house for him at Jerusalem in Judah.

**3** Any person among you who is one of the people of the LORD can go. Let his God be with him. Let him go up to Jerusalem in Judah. Let him build the house of the LORD, the God of Israel. He is the God who is in Jerusalem.

**4** Some of his people stay in other places. Let the men of each place help those people with silver, gold, goods and animals. They must also give free gifts (freewill offerings) for the house of God in Jerusalem."

**5** Then the leaders of the families of Judah and Benjamin got ready to go. The priests and the Levites also got ready. All the people whose spirit God moved got ready. They prepared to go up and build the house of the LORD in Jerusalem.

**6** All their neighbors helped them. The neighbors gave them containers of silver, gold, goods, animals and valuable things. They gave these things in addition to all the freewill offerings.

**7** King Cyrus also brought out the containers from the house of the LORD. Nebuchadnezzar took these containers from Jerusalem. He put them in the house of his gods.

**8** Cyrus king of Persia told Mithredath the treasurer to bring out the containers. Mithredath counted them and gave them to Sheshbazzar, the leader of Judah.

**9** This is the number of the items: 30 gold dishes, 1,000 silver dishes, 29 knives,

**10** 30 gold bowls, 410 silver bowls of a second type, and 1,000 other containers.

**11** There were 5,400 gold and silver containers in total. Sheshbazzar brought all these items with him. He came with the captives who went up from Babylon to Jerusalem.

## Chapter 3

**1** The seventh month came, and the people of Israel were in their cities. Then all the people came together in Jerusalem as one person.

**2** Jeshua the son of Jozadak and his brothers the priests started to work. Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel and his brothers also worked. They built the altar of the God of Israel. They wanted to offer burnt offerings on the altar. The law of Moses, the man of God, tells them to do this.

**3** They put the altar on its foundation. They were afraid of the people of the countries around them. They offered burnt offerings to the LORD on the altar each morning and each evening.

**4** They also kept the Festival of Shelters (Tabernacles), as the law tells them. Each day they offered the correct number of burnt offerings for that day.

**5** After that, they offered the regular burnt offerings. They offered the offerings for the new moons and for all the holy festivals of the LORD. They also offered the freewill offerings that the people gave to the LORD.

**6** On the first day of the seventh month, they started to offer burnt offerings to the LORD. But they did not yet put down the foundation of the temple of the LORD.

**7** They gave money to the stone workers and to the carpenters. They gave food, drink and oil to the people of Sidon and Tyre. Then these people brought cedar trees from Lebanon by sea to Joppa. King Cyrus of Persia gave permission for this.

**8** They came to the house of God at Jerusalem. In the 2nd month of the 2nd year after that, Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel started the work. Jeshua the son of Jozadak, the other priests and the Levites helped. All the people who came back from captivity to Jerusalem also helped. They put the Levites who were 20 years old or more in control of the work on the house of the LORD.

**9** Jeshua, his sons and his brothers worked together with Kadmiel and his sons, the descendants of Judah. They controlled the workers on the house of God. The sons of Henadad and their sons and brothers, the Levites, also helped.

**10** The builders put down the foundation of the temple of the LORD. Then the priests stood in their special clothes with trumpets. The Levites, the sons of Asaph, stood with cymbals. They praised the LORD, as David king of Israel told them to do.

**11** They sang in groups. They praised the LORD and gave thanks to him. They sang, "He is good. His love for Israel continues always." All the people shouted loudly and praised the LORD. They shouted because the foundation of the house of the LORD was in its position.

**12** Many of the older priests, Levites and family leaders saw the first house. When they saw the foundation of this new house, they cried loudly. But many other people shouted with joy.

**13** The people could not hear the difference between the shouts of joy and the sounds of the people who cried. The people shouted very loudly. People far away heard the noise.

## Chapter 4

**1** The enemies of Judah and Benjamin heard that the former captives built a temple to the LORD, the God of Israel.

**2** Then the enemies came to Zerubbabel and to the family leaders. They said, "Let us build with you. We worship your God, as you do. We made sacrifices to him since the time of Esarhaddon king of Assyria. He brought us here."

**3** But Zerubbabel, Jeshua and the other family leaders of Israel answered them. They said, "You will not help us build a house for our God. We alone will build it for the LORD, the God of Israel. King Cyrus, the king of Persia, told us to do this."

**4** Then the people of the land tried to make the people of Judah weak. They tried to make the people of Judah afraid to build.

**5** They paid advisers to work against them and to stop their plans. They did this during all the rule of Cyrus king of Persia, until the rule of Darius king of Persia.

**6** At the start of the rule of Ahasuerus, the enemies wrote an accusation against the people of Judah and Jerusalem.

**7** In the time of Artaxerxes, Bishlam, Mithredath, Tabeel and their companions wrote a letter to Artaxerxes king of Persia. They wrote the letter in the Aramaic language. Someone also translated the letter.

**8** Rehum the commander and Shimshai the scribe wrote a letter against Jerusalem to King Artaxerxes. The letter said this:

**9** "From Rehum the commander, Shimshai the scribe and their companions. The companions are the Dinaites, the Apharsathchites, the Tarpelites, the Apharsites and the Archevites. They are also the Babylonians, the Susanchites, the Dehavites and the Elamites.

**10** They are also the other nations that the great and famous Ashurbanipal (Asnappar) took from their countries. He put them in the cities of Samaria and in the other places west of the Euphrates River."

**11** This is a copy of the letter that they sent to King Artaxerxes: "To King Artaxerxes, from your servants, the men west of the river.

**12** We tell the king this: The Jews who came up from you to us went to Jerusalem. They build that bad city again. That city always fights against kings. They finish the walls and repair the foundations.

**13** We tell the king this: If the people build this city and finish the walls, they will not pay taxes. Then the money that comes to the king will become less.

**14** We get our food from the palace of the king. We do not want to see the king lose his honor. Thus we send this message to the king.

**15** Look in the books of records of your fathers. In the records you will find that this city fights against kings and provinces. It causes much trouble. Long ago, the people of the city caused revolts. Because of this, a king destroyed the city.

**16** We tell the king this. Possibly the people will build this city again and finish its walls. If they do, you will lose your control of the land west of the river."

**17** Then the king sent an answer to Rehum the commander, Shimshai the scribe and their companions in Samaria and west of the river. He wrote, "Peace to you.

**18** My officials read the letter that you sent to us. They read it clearly to me.

**19** I gave an order, and my officials searched the records. They found that, long ago, this city fought against kings. Revolts and fights started there.

**20** Strong kings ruled over Jerusalem. These kings ruled over all the land west of the river. The people paid taxes to them.

**21** Now give an order to stop these men. They must not build this city until I give another order.

**22** Be careful to do this. Do not let more damage come to the kings."

**23** Rehum, Shimshai the scribe and their companions heard the letter of King Artaxerxes. Then they quickly went to the Jews in Jerusalem. They used force to make the Jews stop.

**24** Thus the work on the house of God in Jerusalem stopped. The work stayed stopped until the second year of the rule of Darius king of Persia.

## Chapter 5

**1** Then the prophets Haggai and Zechariah the son of Iddo spoke to the Jews in Judah and Jerusalem. They spoke in the name of the God of Israel, who was over them.

**2** Then Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel and Jeshua the son of Jozadak started to build the house of God in Jerusalem again. The prophets of God were with them and helped them.

**3** At that time Tattenai (Tatnai), the governor west of the river, came to them. Shethar-boznai and their companions also came. They asked the Jews, "Who gave you permission to build this house and to finish this wall?"

**4** They also asked, "What are the names of the men who build this building?"

**5** But God watched over the elders of the Jews. The officials did not stop the work until they sent a report to Darius. They waited for a letter from him about this matter.

**6** This is a copy of the letter that Tatnai, Shethar-boznai and their companions sent to King Darius. Their companions were the officials west of the river.

**7** The letter that they sent to him said this: "To King Darius. All peace to you.

**8** We tell the king this: We went to the province of Judah, to the house of the great God. The people build it with large stones. They put wood beams in the walls. The work goes quickly and well under their control.

**9** We asked the elders, 'Who gave you permission to build this house and to finish these walls?'

**10** We also asked their names. We wanted to write down the names of their leaders for you.

**11** They gave us this answer: 'We are the servants of the God of heaven and earth. We build again the house that a great king of Israel built and finished many years ago.

**12** But our fathers made the God of heaven angry. Thus he gave them to Nebuchadnezzar the Chaldean, king of Babylon. Nebuchadnezzar destroyed this house. He took the people away to Babylon.

**13** But in the first year of Cyrus king of Babylon, King Cyrus gave an order to build this house of God again.

**14** Nebuchadnezzar took the gold and silver containers of the house of God from the temple in Jerusalem. He put them in the temple in Babylon. King Cyrus took these containers out of the temple in Babylon. He gave them to a man with the name Sheshbazzar. Cyrus made Sheshbazzar governor.

**15** Cyrus told him, "Take these containers. Go and put them in the temple in Jerusalem. Build the house of God again on its site."

**16** Then Sheshbazzar came and put down the foundation of the house of God in Jerusalem. From that time until now, the people build it. But the work is not complete.'

**17** If the king agrees, let his officials search the record rooms of the king in Babylon. Let them find if King Cyrus gave an order to build this house of God in Jerusalem. Then let the king send us his decision about this matter."

## Chapter 6

**1** Then King Darius gave an order. His officials searched the record rooms in Babylon, where the treasures were.

**2** In the palace at Ecbatana (Achmetha), in the province of the Medes, they found a scroll. The scroll had this record on it:

**3** "In the first year of King Cyrus, the king gave an order about the house of God at Jerusalem. Let the people build the house again as a place to offer sacrifices. Let them put down strong foundations. The house must be 60 cubits high and 60 cubits wide.

**4** It must have 3 layers of large stones and 1 layer of new wood. The money for the work must come from the treasury of the king.

**5** Nebuchadnezzar took the gold and silver containers of the house of God from the temple in Jerusalem. He brought them to Babylon. Give these containers back. Take each container back to its place in the temple in Jerusalem. Put them in the house of God."

**6** Darius wrote, "Now, Tatnai, governor west of the river, Shethar-boznai and your companions, the officials west of the river: stay away from that place.

**7** Do not stop the work on this house of God. Let the governor of the Jews and the elders of the Jews build this house of God on its site.

**8** I also give an order about how you must help these elders of the Jews to build this house of God. Pay all the costs of these men from the money of the king. Use the taxes from the land west of the river. Pay them quickly, so that the work does not stop.

**9** Give the priests in Jerusalem all that they need each day without fail. Give them young bulls, rams and lambs for burnt offerings to the God of heaven. Give them wheat, salt, wine and oil, as the priests ask.

**10** Then they can offer sacrifices that please the God of heaven. They can pray for the life of the king and of his sons.

**11** I also give this order: If a person changes this order, pull a wood beam from his house. Put the beam up and hang that person on it. Make his house a pile of waste because of his crime.

**12** God caused his name to live in Jerusalem. Let God destroy any king or nation that tries to change this order or to destroy this house of God in Jerusalem. I, Darius, give this order. Obey it quickly."

**13** Then Tatnai, governor west of the river, Shethar-boznai and their companions quickly obeyed the order of King Darius.

**14** The elders of the Jews continued to build, and the work went well. The prophet Haggai and Zechariah the son of Iddo encouraged them with their messages. The Jews completed the building. They obeyed the command of the God of Israel. They also obeyed the orders of Cyrus, Darius and Artaxerxes, kings of Persia.

**15** The people completed this house on the third day of the month Adar. This was in the sixth year of the rule of King Darius.

**16** Then the people of Israel celebrated the dedication of this house of God with joy. The priests, the Levites and all the other former captives were there.

**17** For the dedication of this house of God, they offered 100 bulls, 200 rams and 400 lambs. As a sin offering for all Israel, they offered 12 male goats, 1 for each tribe of Israel.

**18** They put the priests in their groups and the Levites in their divisions for the service of God in Jerusalem. They did this as the book of Moses tells them.

**19** On the 14th day of the first month, the former captives kept the Passover.

**20** The priests and the Levites made themselves clean together. All of them were clean. The Levites killed the Passover lambs for all the former captives, for the priests and for themselves.

**21** The people of Israel who came back from captivity ate the Passover. Other people also ate with them. These people separated themselves from the unclean practices of the nations of the land. They wanted to worship the LORD, the God of Israel.

**22** For 7 days they kept the Festival of Bread Without Yeast with joy. The LORD made them happy. He changed the attitude of the king of Assyria toward them. Thus the king helped them in the work on the house of God, the God of Israel.

## Chapter 7

**1** After these things, during the rule of Artaxerxes king of Persia, there was a man with the name Ezra. Ezra was the son of Seraiah, the son of Azariah, the son of Hilkiah,

**2** the son of Shallum, the son of Zadok, the son of Ahitub,

**3** the son of Amariah, the son of Azariah, the son of Meraioth,

**4** the son of Zerahiah, the son of Uzzi, the son of Bukki,

**5** the son of Abishua, the son of Phinehas, the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the chief priest.

**6** This Ezra went up from Babylon. He was a scribe who knew the law of Moses very well. The LORD, the God of Israel, gave that law. The LORD his God helped Ezra. Thus the king gave Ezra all that he asked for.

**7** Some of the people of Israel also went up to Jerusalem with him. Some priests, Levites, singers, gatekeepers and Nethinims went. This was in the seventh year of King Artaxerxes.

**8** Ezra came to Jerusalem in the fifth month of the seventh year of the king.

**9** He started the journey from Babylon on the first day of the first month. He came to Jerusalem on the first day of the fifth month. The good hand of his God was on him.

**10** Ezra decided to study the law of the LORD and to obey it. He also decided to teach the rules and laws of the LORD in Israel.

**11** This is a copy of the letter that King Artaxerxes gave to Ezra. Ezra was the priest and the scribe. He was a scholar of the commands and the rules that the LORD gave to Israel.

**12** "From Artaxerxes, king of kings, to Ezra the priest, the scribe of the law of the God of heaven. Peace to you.

**13** I give this order: Any person in my kingdom from the people of Israel can go with you to Jerusalem. This includes the priests and the Levites. Each person who wants to go can go.

**14** The king and his 7 advisers send you. Find out about the conditions in Judah and Jerusalem. Use the law of your God, which is in your hand.

**15** Take the silver and gold that the king and his advisers freely give to the God of Israel. His home is in Jerusalem.

**16** Also take all the silver and gold that you can get in the province of Babylon. Take the freewill offerings of the people and of the priests for the house of their God in Jerusalem.

**17** With this money, quickly buy bulls, rams and lambs. Also buy their grain offerings and drink offerings. Offer them on the altar of the house of your God in Jerusalem.

**18** You and your brothers can use the rest of the silver and gold as you think best. Do what your God wants.

**19** The officials will give you containers for the service in the house of your God. Give all these containers to the God of Jerusalem.

**20** The house of your God will need other things. Pay for these things from the treasury of the king.

**21** I, King Artaxerxes, give an order to all the treasurers west of the river. Ezra the priest is the scribe of the law of the God of heaven. Quickly give him all that he asks for.

**22** Give him up to 100 talents of silver and 100 measures of wheat. Give him up to 100 baths of wine and 100 baths of oil. Give him as much salt as he needs.

**23** Carefully do all that the God of heaven commands for his house. Do not let God become angry with the kingdom of the king and his sons.

**24** We also tell you this: You must not make the priests, Levites, singers, gatekeepers, Nethinims or other workers of this house of God pay taxes.

**25** You, Ezra, have the wisdom of your God. Use that wisdom to choose officials and judges. They will judge all the people west of the river who know the laws of your God. Teach the laws to the people who do not know them.

**26** Some people will not obey the law of your God and the law of the king. Punish these people quickly. Kill them, or send them away, or take their property, or put them in prison."

**27** Ezra said, "Praise the LORD, the God of our fathers! He caused the king to want to make the house of the LORD in Jerusalem beautiful.

**28** He caused the king, his advisers and all his strong officials to be kind to me. The LORD my God helped me and made me strong. Thus I gathered leaders from Israel to go up with me."

## Chapter 8

**1** These are the family leaders who went up with me from Babylon during the rule of King Artaxerxes. These are their family records:

**2** From the descendants of Phinehas, Gershom. From the descendants of Ithamar, Daniel. From the descendants of David, Hattush.

**3** From the descendants of Shechaniah and Pharosh, Zechariah. With him there were 150 males in the family records.

**4** From the descendants of Pahath-moab, Elihoenai the son of Zerahiah, and 200 males with him.

**5** From the descendants of Shechaniah, the son of Jahaziel, and 300 males with him.

**6** From the descendants of Adin, Ebed the son of Jonathan, and 50 males with him.

**7** From the descendants of Elam, Jeshaiah the son of Athaliah, and 70 males with him.

**8** From the descendants of Shephatiah, Zebadiah the son of Michael, and 80 males with him.

**9** From the descendants of Joab, Obadiah the son of Jehiel, and 218 males with him.

**10** From the descendants of Shelomith, the son of Josiphiah, and 160 males with him.

**11** From the descendants of Bebai, Zechariah the son of Bebai, and 28 males with him.

**12** From the descendants of Azgad, Johanan the son of Hakkatan, and 110 males with him.

**13** From the last descendants of Adonikam, Eliphelet, Jeiel and Shemaiah, and 60 males with them.

**14** From the descendants of Bigvai, Uthai and Zabbud, and 70 males with them.

**15** I gathered them together at the river that flows to Ahava. We stayed there in tents for 3 days. I looked at the people and the priests. I found no Levites there.

**16** Then I sent for some leaders: Eliezer, Ariel, Shemaiah, Elnathan, Jarib, Elnathan, Nathan, Zechariah and Meshullam. I also sent for Joiarib and Elnathan, who were wise teachers.

**17** I sent them to Iddo, the leader at the place Casiphia. I told them what to say to Iddo and to his relatives, the Nethinims, at Casiphia. They had to ask for workers for the house of our God.

**18** The good hand of our God was on us. Thus they brought us a wise man from the descendants of Mahli, the son of Levi, the son of Israel. His name was Sherebiah. They also brought his sons and brothers, 18 men in total.

**19** They also brought Hashabiah and Jeshaiah from the descendants of Merari. They brought the brothers of Jeshaiah and their sons, 20 men in total.

**20** They also brought 220 Nethinims. David and the officials gave the Nethinims the work to help the Levites. We wrote down the name of each of these men.

**21** There, at the river of Ahava, I told the people to fast. We made ourselves humble in front of our God. We asked him for a safe journey for us, our children and all our property.

**22** I felt shame to ask the king for soldiers and horsemen to protect us from enemies on the road. We told the king, "The hand of our God is on all who look for him. He helps them. But his power and his anger are against all who leave him."

**23** Thus we fasted and asked our God to protect us. He answered our prayer.

**24** Then I chose 12 of the leaders of the priests: Sherebiah, Hashabiah and 10 of their brothers.

**25** I weighed the silver, the gold and the containers, and gave them to the priests. These items were the offering for the house of our God. The king, his advisers, his officials and all the Israelites there gave this offering.

**26** I weighed and gave to them 650 talents of silver. I also gave them silver containers that weighed 100 talents, and 100 talents of gold.

**27** I also gave them 20 gold bowls with a value of 1,000 darics. I gave them 2 containers of good bronze. These were as valuable as gold.

**28** I told them, "You are holy to the LORD. The containers are also holy. The silver and the gold are a freewill offering to the LORD, the God of your fathers.

**29** Guard these things carefully. Weigh them in the rooms of the house of the LORD in Jerusalem. Weigh them in front of the leaders of the priests, the Levites and the family leaders of Israel."

**30** Then the priests and the Levites took the silver, the gold and the containers that I weighed. They had to bring these things to the house of our God in Jerusalem.

**31** We left the river of Ahava on the 12th day of the first month to go to Jerusalem. The hand of our God was on us. He protected us from enemies and from robbers on the road.

**32** We came to Jerusalem and stayed there for 3 days.

**33** On the fourth day, in the house of our God, we weighed the silver, the gold and the containers. We gave them to Meremoth the son of Uriah the priest. Eleazar the son of Phinehas was with him. The Levites Jozabad the son of Jeshua and Noadiah the son of Binnui were also with them.

**34** They counted and weighed each item. They wrote down the total weight at that time.

**35** Then the former captives who came back offered burnt offerings to the God of Israel. They offered 12 bulls for all Israel, 96 rams and 77 lambs. They also offered 12 male goats as a sin offering. All these were a burnt offering to the LORD.

**36** They gave the orders of the king to the officials of the king and to the governors west of the river. Then these officials helped the people and the house of God.

## Chapter 9

**1** After these things, the leaders came to me. They said, "The people of Israel, the priests and the Levites did not separate themselves from the people of the lands. They do the detestable things that those people do. These people are the Canaanites, Hittites, Perizzites, Jebusites, Ammonites, Moabites, Egyptians and Amorites.

**2** The Israelites married some of the daughters of these people. They let their sons marry these women too. Thus the holy people mixed with the people of those lands. The leaders and officials were the first to do this wrong."

**3** When I heard this, I tore my clothes and my coat. I pulled out hair from my head and my beard. I sat down. I was very shocked.

**4** Then all the people who feared the words of the God of Israel came to me. They were afraid because of the sin of the former captives. I sat there shocked until the evening sacrifice.

**5** At the time of the evening sacrifice, I stood up from my sadness. I tore my clothes and my coat. Then I went down on my knees. I held out my hands to the LORD my God.

**6** I said, "My God, I feel shame. I cannot lift up my face to you, my God. Our sins are higher than our heads. Our guilt goes up to the heavens.

**7** Since the time of our fathers, our guilt is very great. Because of our sins, you gave us, our kings and our priests to the kings of other lands. They killed us with swords. They took us as captives. They took our property. They made us ashamed, as we are today.

**8** But now, for a short time, the LORD our God was kind to us. He let a small group of us escape. He gave us a safe place in his holy place. Our God gave us hope. He gave us a little new life while we are slaves.

**9** We are slaves. But our God did not leave us while we are slaves. He caused the kings of Persia to be kind to us. He gave us new life. Thus we can build the house of our God again and repair it. He gave us a wall of protection in Judah and Jerusalem.

**10** Now, our God, what can we say after this? We did not obey your commands.

**11** You gave these commands through your servants the prophets. They said, 'The land that you will take is unclean. The people of the lands made it unclean with their detestable practices. They filled the land with their dirt from one end to the other.

**12** Thus do not let your daughters marry their sons. Do not let your sons marry their daughters. Never try to make peace with them or help them become rich. Then you will be strong and eat the good things of the land. You will give the land to your children for all time.'

**13** All these bad things came to us because of our evil actions and our great guilt. But you, our God, punished us less than our sins deserve. You let some of us escape.

**14** Thus we must not again disobey your commands. We must not marry the people who do these detestable things. If we do, you will be angry with us. You will destroy us until no person stays alive or escapes.

**15** LORD God of Israel, you are righteous. You let a small group of us escape, as we are today. Look, we stand in front of you with our guilt. Because of this, no person can stand in front of you."

## Chapter 10

**1** Ezra prayed and confessed the sins. He cried and lay on the ground in front of the house of God. A very large group of Israelite men, women and children came to him. The people also cried very much.

**2** Shechaniah the son of Jehiel, from the descendants of Elam, spoke to Ezra. He said, "We were not loyal to our God. We married foreign women from the people of the land. But there is still hope for Israel.

**3** Let us make an agreement (covenant) with our God. We will send away all these wives and their children. We will do as my lord and the people who fear the command of our God advise. We will obey the law.

**4** Stand up. You must control this matter. We will help you. Be brave and do it."

**5** Then Ezra stood up. He made the leaders of the priests, the Levites and all Israel promise to do this. They made the promise.

**6** Then Ezra went away from the house of God. He went into the room of Jehohanan (Johanan) the son of Eliashib. There he did not eat food or drink water. He was very sad because of the sin of the former captives.

**7** Then the leaders sent a message through all Judah and Jerusalem to all the former captives. The message told them to come together in Jerusalem.

**8** The leaders and the elders decided this: If a person does not come within 3 days, they will take all his property. They will also send him out of the group of former captives.

**9** Within 3 days all the men of Judah and Benjamin came together in Jerusalem. It was the 20th day of the ninth month. All the people sat in the square in front of the house of God. They shook with fear because of this matter and because of the heavy rain.

**10** Ezra the priest stood up and spoke to them. He said, "You were not loyal to God. You married foreign women. Thus you made the guilt of Israel greater.

**11** Now confess your sin to the LORD, the God of your fathers. Do what he wants. Separate yourselves from the people of the land and from your foreign wives."

**12** All the people answered with a loud voice. They said, "You are right! We must do as you say.

**13** But there are many people here, and it is the season of heavy rain. We cannot stand outside. This work will take more than 1 or 2 days. Many of us did this sin.

**14** Let our leaders act for all the people. Let each man in our cities who married a foreign woman come at a specified time. Let the elders and judges of each city come with him. Then the strong anger of our God about this matter will go away from us."

**15** Only Jonathan the son of Asahel and Jahaziah the son of Tikvah disagreed with this plan. Meshullam and Shabbethai the Levite supported them.

**16** But the former captives did as they agreed. Ezra the priest chose some family leaders, 1 from each family. He chose each leader by name. On the first day of the tenth month, they sat down to examine the matter.
