# Daniel

## Chapter 1

**1** In the 3rd year of the rule of Jehoiakim king of Judah, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came to Jerusalem. His army put the city under siege.

**2** The Lord gave Jehoiakim king of Judah into the control of Nebuchadnezzar. The Lord also gave him some of the containers from the house of God. Nebuchadnezzar took the containers to the land of Shinar, to the temple of his god. He put the containers in the treasure house of his god.

**3** The king spoke to Ashpenaz, the chief of his officials. The king told him to bring some young men of Israel. These young men were from the royal family and from the noble families.

**4** The young men must have no defect in their bodies. They must be handsome, wise, and quick to learn. They must have knowledge and good insight. They must be able to serve in the palace of the king. Ashpenaz must teach them the books and the language of the Chaldeans.

**5** The king gave them a daily part of his food and of the wine that he drank. The officials must train them for 3 years. At the end of the 3 years, the young men must serve the king.

**6** Among these young men were Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah. They were from the tribe of Judah.

**7** The chief official gave them new names. He gave Daniel the name Belteshazzar. He gave Hananiah the name Shadrach. He gave Mishael the name Meshach. He gave Azariah the name Abednego.

**8** But Daniel decided that he will not make himself unclean with the food and wine of the king. Thus he asked the chief official for permission not to make himself unclean.

**9** God caused the chief official to show kindness and pity to Daniel.

**10** The chief official said to Daniel, "I am afraid of my lord the king. The king decided what food and drink you must have. If he sees that you look worse than the other young men of your age, he will kill me because of you."

**11** Then Daniel spoke to the guard. The chief official put this guard in charge of Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah.

**12** Daniel said, "Please, test your servants for 10 days. Give us only vegetables to eat and water to drink.

**13** Then compare our faces with the faces of the young men who eat the food of the king. After that, do to your servants what you think is correct."

**14** The guard agreed to this request. He tested them for 10 days.

**15** At the end of 10 days, they looked healthier and stronger than all the young men who ate the food of the king.

**16** Thus the guard took away their food and the wine that they had to drink. He gave them vegetables.

**17** God gave these 4 young men knowledge and skill in all types of books and wisdom. Daniel could also understand all types of visions and dreams.

**18** The king told the officials to bring in all the young men at the end of the time for their education. At that time the chief official brought them to Nebuchadnezzar.

**19** The king spoke with them. He found no one as good as Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah. Thus these 4 young men served the king.

**20** The king asked them about all matters of wisdom and insight. He found them 10 times better than all the magicians and astrologers in all his kingdom.

**21** Daniel stayed there until the 1st year of King Cyrus.

## Chapter 2

**1** In the 2nd year of his rule, Nebuchadnezzar had dreams. The dreams troubled his mind, and he could not sleep.

**2** The king told his servants to call the magicians, the astrologers, the sorcerers, and the Chaldeans. They must tell the king his dreams. They came and stood in front of the king.

**3** The king said to them, "I had a dream. My mind is not calm, and I want to know the dream."

**4** The Chaldeans spoke to the king in the Aramaic language. They said, "O king, live forever! Tell your servants the dream, and we will tell you the meaning."

**5** The king said to the Chaldeans, "I made a firm decision. You must tell me the dream and its meaning. If you do not, my men will cut you into pieces. They will make your houses into heaps of rubble.

**6** But if you tell me the dream and its meaning, I will give you gifts, rewards, and great honor. Thus tell me the dream and its meaning."

**7** They spoke again and said, "Let the king tell his servants the dream. Then we will tell the meaning."

**8** The king said, "I know that you try to get more time. You see that I made a firm decision.

**9** If you do not tell me the dream, you will all get the same punishment. You agreed together to tell me lies and false words. You hope that the situation will change. Thus tell me the dream. Then I will know that you can tell me its meaning."

**10** The Chaldeans said to the king, "No man on earth can do what the king asks. No great king or ruler ever asked such a thing from a magician, an astrologer, or a Chaldean.

**11** The king asks for a very difficult thing. No one can tell it to the king except the gods. And the gods do not live among humans."

**12** Because of this, the king became very angry. He gave an order to kill all the wise men of Babylon.

**13** The king sent out the order to kill the wise men. Men looked for Daniel and his friends to kill them.

**14** Arioch, the captain of the king's guard, went out to kill the wise men of Babylon. Daniel spoke to him with care and wisdom.

**15** Daniel asked Arioch, the captain of the king, "Why is the order from the king so severe?" Then Arioch told Daniel about the matter.

**16** Daniel went in to the king. He asked the king to give him time. Then he will tell the king the meaning of the dream.

**17** Then Daniel went to his house. He told his friends Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah about the matter.

**18** He told them to ask the God of heaven for mercy about this secret. Then the king will not kill Daniel and his friends with the other wise men of Babylon.

**19** During the night, God showed the secret to Daniel in a vision. Then Daniel praised the God of heaven.

**20** Daniel said, "Praise the name of God forever and ever. Wisdom and power are his.

**21** He changes the times and the seasons. He removes kings, and he puts kings into power. He gives wisdom to the wise. He gives knowledge to the people who have insight.

**22** He shows deep and secret things. He knows what is in the darkness. The light lives with him.

**23** God of my ancestors, I thank you and praise you. You gave me wisdom and power. You told me what we asked from you. You told us the matter of the king."

**24** Then Daniel went to Arioch. The king gave Arioch the order to kill the wise men of Babylon. Daniel said to him, "Do not kill the wise men of Babylon. Take me to the king. I will tell the king the meaning of his dream."

**25** Arioch quickly took Daniel to the king. Arioch said to the king, "I found a man from the captives of Judah. He will tell the king the meaning of the dream."

**26** The king said to Daniel, who had the name Belteshazzar, "Can you tell me the dream that I saw, and its meaning?"

**27** Daniel spoke to the king. He said, "No wise man, astrologer, magician, or fortune-teller can tell the king the secret that he asks about.

**28** But there is a God in heaven who shows secrets. He told King Nebuchadnezzar what will occur in the last days. These are your dream and the visions that you saw on your bed.

**29** O king, on your bed you thought about the future. God, who shows secrets, told you what will occur.

**30** God did not show me this secret because I am wiser than other people. He showed it to me so that I can tell the king its meaning. Then you will understand the thoughts of your mind.

**31** O king, you looked, and you saw a large statue. This large statue was very bright. It stood in front of you, and its appearance was terrible.

**32** The head of the statue was of pure gold. Its chest and its arms were of silver. Its stomach and its thighs were of bronze.

**33** Its legs were of iron. Its feet were partly of iron and partly of clay.

**34** While you looked, someone cut out a stone, but not with human hands. The stone hit the statue on its feet of iron and clay. The stone broke the feet into pieces.

**35** Then the iron, the clay, the bronze, the silver, and the gold broke into pieces at the same time. They became like the chaff on a floor where people beat grain in summer. The wind blew them away, and no trace stayed. The stone that hit the statue became a large mountain. The mountain filled all the earth.

**36** This was the dream. Now we will tell the king its meaning.

**37** O king, you are the king of kings. The God of heaven gave you a kingdom, power, strength, and glory.

**38** He gave you control over all people, wherever they live. He also gave you control over the wild animals and the birds. He made you ruler over all of them. You are the head of gold.

**39** After you, another kingdom will start. It will be less strong than your kingdom. Then a 3rd kingdom, of bronze, will rule over all the earth.

**40** The 4th kingdom will be as strong as iron. Iron breaks and crushes all things. As iron breaks things into pieces, this kingdom will break and crush all the other kingdoms.

**41** You saw that the feet and toes were partly of clay and partly of iron. Thus the 4th kingdom will have divisions. But some of the strength of iron will be in it, because you saw iron mixed with soft clay.

**42** The toes were partly of iron and partly of clay. Thus the kingdom will be partly strong and partly weak.

**43** You saw iron mixed with soft clay. Thus the people of that kingdom will mix together through marriage. But they will not stay united, because iron does not mix with clay.

**44** In the time of these kings, the God of heaven will set up a kingdom. No one will ever destroy that kingdom. No other nation will take control of it. It will break all these kingdoms and bring them to an end. But that kingdom will continue forever.

**45** You saw the stone that someone cut out of the mountain, but not with human hands. The stone broke the iron, the bronze, the clay, the silver, and the gold into pieces. The great God told the king what will occur in the future. The dream is true, and its meaning is correct."

**46** Then King Nebuchadnezzar bowed down with his face to the ground. He honored Daniel. He told his servants to give Daniel an offering and incense.

**47** The king said to Daniel, "It is true that your God is the God of gods and the Lord of kings. He shows secrets. I know this because you could tell me this secret."

**48** Then the king gave Daniel a high position and many large gifts. He made Daniel the ruler over all the province of Babylon. He also made Daniel the chief over all the wise men of Babylon.

**49** Daniel asked the king for a favor. Thus the king put Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego in charge of the work of the province of Babylon. But Daniel stayed at the court of the king.

## Chapter 3

**1** King Nebuchadnezzar made a statue of gold. The statue was 60 cubits high and 6 cubits wide. He set it up on the plain of Dura, in the province of Babylon.

**2** Then King Nebuchadnezzar sent messengers to gather all his officials. These were the princes, the governors, the captains, the judges, the treasurers, the advisors, the magistrates, and all the rulers of the provinces. They must come to the dedication of the statue that he set up.

**3** Then all these officials gathered for the dedication of the statue that King Nebuchadnezzar set up. They stood in front of the statue.

**4** Then a messenger shouted, "People of all nations and languages, the king gives you this order.

**5** When you hear the sound of the horn, flute, harp, lyre, harp, pipes, and all types of music, bow down. Worship the gold statue that King Nebuchadnezzar set up.

**6** If a person does not bow down and worship, soldiers will immediately throw that person into a very hot furnace."

**7** Thus all the people heard the sound of the horn, flute, harp, lyre, harp, and all types of music. Then the people of all nations and languages bowed down. They worshipped the gold statue that King Nebuchadnezzar set up.

**8** At that time some Chaldeans came to the king. They accused the Jews.

**9** They said to King Nebuchadnezzar, "O king, live forever!

**10** O king, you gave an order. Every person who hears the sound of the horn, flute, harp, lyre, harp, pipes, and all types of music must bow down. That person must worship the gold statue.

**11** If a person does not bow down and worship, soldiers must throw that person into a very hot furnace.

**12** But there are some Jews whom you put in charge of the work of the province of Babylon. They are Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. O king, these men do not obey you. They do not serve your gods. They do not worship the gold statue that you set up."

**13** Nebuchadnezzar became very angry. He ordered his servants to bring Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. Then the servants brought these men to the king.

**14** Nebuchadnezzar said to them, "Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, is it true that you do not serve my gods? Is it true that you do not worship the gold statue that I set up?

**15** Now when you hear the sound of the horn, flute, harp, lyre, harp, pipes, and all types of music, be ready. Bow down and worship the statue that I made. If you do this, good. But if you do not worship, soldiers will immediately throw you into a very hot furnace. Then which god can save you from my power?"

**16** Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego said to the king, "O Nebuchadnezzar, we do not need to defend ourselves to you in this matter.

**17** If you throw us into the furnace, our God can save us from the very hot furnace. The God whom we serve will save us from your power, O king.

**18** But if he does not save us, O king, know this. We will not serve your gods. We will not worship the gold statue that you set up."

**19** Then Nebuchadnezzar became very angry with Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. The expression on his face changed. He ordered his servants to make the furnace 7 times hotter than usual.

**20** He ordered some of the strongest soldiers in his army to tie up Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. He ordered them to throw the 3 men into the very hot furnace.

**21** The soldiers tied up these men in their coats, their trousers, their hats, and their other clothes. Then the soldiers threw them into the very hot furnace.

**22** The order of the king was urgent, and the furnace was very hot. Thus the flames killed the soldiers who carried Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego.

**23** These 3 men, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, fell into the very hot furnace. Their hands and feet were tied.

**24** Then King Nebuchadnezzar was very surprised. He quickly stood up. He asked his advisors, "Did we not throw 3 men into the fire, with their hands and feet tied?" They said to the king, "Yes, O king."

**25** The king said, "Look! I see 4 men. They are not tied, and they walk in the middle of the fire. The fire does not hurt them. The 4th man looks like a son of the gods."

**26** Then Nebuchadnezzar came near to the door of the very hot furnace. He said, "Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, servants of the Most High God, come out! Come here!" Then Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego came out of the fire.

**27** The princes, the governors, the captains, and the advisors of the king gathered around. They saw that the fire did not hurt the bodies of these men. The fire did not burn the hair on their heads. Their coats did not change. Their clothes did not smell of fire.

**28** Then Nebuchadnezzar said, "Praise the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego! He sent his angel and saved his servants. They trusted in him. They did not obey the order of the king. They were ready to die. They did not serve or worship a god other than their own God.

**29** Thus I give this order. Possibly people of some nation or language say bad things against the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. If they do, my men will cut them into pieces. My men will make their houses into heaps of rubble. No other god can save people in this way."

**30** Then the king gave Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego higher positions in the province of Babylon.

## Chapter 4

**1** King Nebuchadnezzar writes to the people of all nations and languages who live on all the earth: "I wish you much peace.

**2** I want to tell you about the signs and the miracles that the Most High God did for me.

**3** His signs are very great! His miracles are very powerful! His kingdom will continue forever. His rule continues from generation to generation.

**4** I, Nebuchadnezzar, was at rest in my house. I had a good life in my palace.

**5** Then I had a dream, and the dream made me afraid. While I was on my bed, the thoughts and the visions in my mind troubled me.

**6** Thus I gave an order to bring all the wise men of Babylon to me. They must tell me the meaning of the dream.

**7** Then the magicians, the astrologers, the Chaldeans, and the fortune-tellers came in. I told them the dream. But they could not tell me its meaning.

**8** At last Daniel came to me. I gave him the name Belteshazzar, from the name of my god. The spirit of the holy gods is in him. I told him the dream.

**9** I said, 'Belteshazzar, chief of the magicians, I know that the spirit of the holy gods is in you. No secret is too difficult for you. Tell me the visions of my dream and their meaning.

**10** These are the visions that I saw on my bed. I looked, and I saw a tree in the middle of the earth. The tree was very tall.

**11** The tree grew large and strong. Its top touched the sky. People could see it from the ends of the earth.

**12** Its leaves were beautiful, and it had much fruit. The tree had food for all. The wild animals rested in its shade. The birds lived in its branches. All animals got food from it.

**13** In the visions on my bed, I looked, and I saw a holy messenger. He came down from heaven.

**14** He shouted, "Cut down the tree. Cut off its branches. Remove its leaves and throw away its fruit. Let the animals go away from under the tree. Let the birds go away from its branches.

**15** But leave the stump and its roots in the ground. Put a band of iron and bronze around it, in the grass of the field. Let the dew from the sky make him wet. Let him live with the animals among the plants of the earth.

**16** Let his mind change from the mind of a man. Give him the mind of an animal. Let 7 periods of time go by for him.

**17** The holy messengers give this order. They make this decision. Thus all people who are alive will know that the Most High rules over the kingdoms of men. He gives the kingdoms to the persons whom he chooses. He puts the lowest of men in power over the kingdoms."

**18** I, King Nebuchadnezzar, saw this dream. Now, Belteshazzar, tell me its meaning. All the wise men of my kingdom cannot tell me its meaning. But you can, because the spirit of the holy gods is in you.'

**19** Then Daniel, who had the name Belteshazzar, was very surprised for a short time. His thoughts troubled him. The king said, 'Belteshazzar, do not let the dream or its meaning trouble you.' Belteshazzar said, 'My lord, I wish that the dream were for your enemies. I wish that its meaning were for the people who hate you.

**20** You saw a tree that grew large and strong. Its top touched the sky. People could see it from all the earth.

**21** Its leaves were beautiful, and it had much fruit. The tree had food for all. The wild animals lived under it. The birds lived in its branches.

**22** O king, you are that tree. You became large and strong. Your greatness grew and touched the sky. Your rule goes to the ends of the earth.

**23** The king saw a holy messenger who came down from heaven. The messenger said, "Cut down the tree and destroy it. But leave the stump and its roots in the ground. Put a band of iron and bronze around it, in the grass of the field. Let the dew from the sky make him wet. Let him live with the wild animals until 7 periods of time go by for him."

**24** O king, this is the meaning. The Most High made this decision against my lord the king.

**25** People will send you away from other people. You will live with the wild animals. You will eat grass like cattle. The dew from the sky will make you wet. 7 periods of time will go by for you. Then you will know that the Most High rules over the kingdoms of men. He gives the kingdoms to the persons whom he chooses.

**26** The messengers said to leave the stump and the roots of the tree. Thus your kingdom will come back to you. This will occur when you know that heaven rules.

**27** Thus, O king, please accept my advice. Stop your sins and do what is right. Stop your evil and show mercy to the poor. Then maybe your peace will continue for a longer time.'

**28** All these things occurred to King Nebuchadnezzar.

**29** After 12 months, the king walked on the roof of the royal palace of Babylon.

**30** The king said, 'Babylon is a great city! I built it with my great power as a royal city. It shows the honor of my glory.'

**31** While the king still spoke, a voice came from heaven. The voice said, 'King Nebuchadnezzar, hear this message. Your kingdom is not yours now.

**32** People will send you away from other people. You will live with the wild animals. You will eat grass like cattle. 7 periods of time will go by for you. Then you will know that the Most High rules over the kingdoms of men. He gives the kingdoms to the persons whom he chooses.'

**33** Immediately, these words came true for Nebuchadnezzar. People sent him away from other people. He ate grass like cattle. The dew from the sky made his body wet. His hair grew long like the feathers of an eagle. His nails grew like the claws of a bird.

**34** At the end of that time, I, Nebuchadnezzar, looked up to heaven. My mind became healthy again. I praised the Most High. I praised and honored the God who lives forever. His rule continues forever. His kingdom continues from generation to generation.

**35** All the people of the earth are as nothing to him. He does what he wants with the armies of heaven and with the people of the earth. No one can stop his hand. No one can say to him, 'What do you do?'

**36** At that time my mind became healthy again. My honor and glory came back to me, for the glory of my kingdom. My advisors and my nobles came to me. I got my kingdom back, and I became even greater than before.

**37** Now I, Nebuchadnezzar, praise and honor the King of heaven. All his work is true, and all his ways are fair. He can make proud people humble."

## Chapter 5

**1** King Belshazzar gave a large feast for 1,000 of his nobles. He drank wine with the 1,000 nobles.

**2** While Belshazzar drank the wine, he gave an order. He told his servants to bring the gold and silver cups that his father Nebuchadnezzar took from the temple in Jerusalem. The king, his nobles, his wives, and his concubines wanted to drink from these cups.

**3** Thus the servants brought the gold cups from the temple of the house of God in Jerusalem. The king, his nobles, his wives, and his concubines drank from them.

**4** They drank wine. They praised the gods of gold, silver, bronze, iron, wood, and stone.

**5** Immediately the fingers of a human hand came out. The fingers wrote on the plaster of the wall of the palace, near the lampstand. The king saw the hand while it wrote.

**6** Then the face of the king became pale. His thoughts made him afraid. His legs became weak, and his knees hit together.

**7** The king shouted for his servants to bring the astrologers, the Chaldeans, and the fortune-tellers. The king said to the wise men of Babylon, "I will reward the man who reads these words and tells me their meaning. I will give him purple clothes and a gold chain for his neck. He will be the 3rd ruler in the kingdom."

**8** Then all the wise men of the king came in. But they could not read the words. They could not tell the king their meaning.

**9** Then King Belshazzar became very afraid. His face became more pale. His nobles did not know what to do.

**10** The queen heard the words of the king and his nobles. She came into the banquet room. She said, "O king, live forever! Do not let your thoughts make you afraid. Do not let your face become pale.

**11** There is a man in your kingdom who has the spirit of the holy gods in him. In the time of your father, people saw that this man had insight, knowledge, and wisdom like the gods. Your father, King Nebuchadnezzar, made him chief of the magicians, astrologers, Chaldeans, and fortune-tellers.

**12** The king gave this man the name Belteshazzar, but his name is Daniel. He has a very good mind, knowledge, and insight. He can tell the meaning of dreams, explain riddles, and solve difficult problems. Call Daniel now, and he will tell you the meaning."

**13** Then the servants brought Daniel to the king. The king said to Daniel, "Are you Daniel? Are you one of the captives from Judah? My father the king brought you from Judah.

**14** I heard about you. People say that the spirit of the gods is in you. They say that you have insight, knowledge, and very good wisdom.

**15** The wise men and the astrologers came to me. I told them to read these words and tell me their meaning. But they could not tell me their meaning.

**16** But I heard about you. People say that you can tell meanings and solve difficult problems. If you can read the words and tell me their meaning, I will reward you. I will give you purple clothes and a gold chain for your neck. You will be the 3rd ruler in the kingdom."

**17** Then Daniel said to the king, "Keep your gifts for yourself. Give your rewards to another person. But I will read the words to the king. I will tell him their meaning.

**18** O king, the Most High God gave your father Nebuchadnezzar a kingdom, greatness, glory, and honor.

**19** God gave him much greatness. Thus the people of all nations and languages were very afraid of him. He killed the persons whom he wanted to kill. He let the persons whom he wanted to live stay alive. He gave high positions to some persons, and he made other persons low.

**20** But Nebuchadnezzar became proud and stubborn. Thus God removed him from his royal throne and took away his glory.

**21** People sent him away from other people. God gave him the mind of an animal. He lived with the wild donkeys. He ate grass like cattle. The dew from the sky made his body wet. Then he knew that the Most High God rules over the kingdoms of men. God puts in power the persons whom he chooses.

**22** You, Belshazzar, are his son. You knew all this, but you did not make yourself humble.

**23** You made yourself proud against the Lord of heaven. You told your servants to bring the cups from his temple to you. You, your nobles, your wives, and your concubines drank wine from them. You praised the gods of silver, gold, bronze, iron, wood, and stone. These gods cannot see, hear, or know. But you did not honor the God who holds your life in his hand. He controls all that you do.

**24** Thus God sent the hand, and the hand wrote these words.

**25** These are the words that the hand wrote: MENE, MENE, TEKEL, UPHARSIN.

**26** This is the meaning of the words. MENE: God counted the days of your kingdom and brought it to an end.

**27** TEKEL: God weighed you on the scales, and you do not have enough weight.

**28** PERES: God divided your kingdom and gave it to the Medes and the Persians."

**29** Then Belshazzar gave an order. His servants put purple clothes on Daniel and a gold chain around his neck. They announced that Daniel was the 3rd ruler in the kingdom.

**30** That night, men killed Belshazzar, the king of the Chaldeans.

**31** Darius the Mede took the kingdom. He was about 62 years old.

## Chapter 6

**1** Darius decided to put 120 officials over the kingdom. They must rule all the kingdom.

**2** He put 3 chief officials over the 120 officials. Daniel was 1 of the 3 chief officials. The officials must report to the chief officials. Thus the king will lose nothing.

**3** Daniel was better than the other chief officials and officials, because he had a very good spirit. The king planned to put Daniel over all the kingdom.

**4** Then the chief officials and the officials tried to find a reason to accuse Daniel about his work in the kingdom. But they could not find a reason or a fault. Daniel was honest. He made no mistakes, and he did nothing wrong.

**5** Then these men said, "We will not find a reason to accuse this Daniel. We can only find a reason in the law of his God."

**6** Then these chief officials and officials went together to the king. They said to him, "King Darius, live forever!

**7** All the chief officials of the kingdom, the governors, the officials, the advisors, and the captains agreed together. The king must make a law and give a firm order. For 30 days, no person can pray to any god or man except you, O king. If a person does this, soldiers will throw that person into the pit of lions.

**8** Now, O king, make the order and sign it. Then no person can change it. It will be a law of the Medes and the Persians, which no person can cancel."

**9** Thus King Darius signed the written order.

**10** Daniel knew that the king signed the order. But Daniel went to his house. In his upstairs room the windows were open toward Jerusalem. 3 times each day he went down on his knees. He prayed and gave thanks to his God, as he did before.

**11** Then these men came together. They found Daniel while he prayed and asked his God for help.

**12** Then they went to the king and spoke about the order of the king. They said, "You signed an order. For 30 days, no person can pray to any god or man except you, O king. If a person does this, soldiers will throw that person into the pit of lions. Is that correct?" The king said, "Yes, that is correct. It is a law of the Medes and the Persians, which no person can cancel."

**13** Then they said to the king, "Daniel is one of the captives from Judah. He does not obey you, O king. He does not obey the order that you signed. He prays 3 times each day."

**14** When the king heard these words, he was very sad. He decided to save Daniel. Until sunset he tried to find a way to save Daniel.

**15** Then these men came together to the king. They said to him, "O king, remember the law of the Medes and the Persians. No person can change an order or law that the king makes."

**16** Then the king gave an order. His servants brought Daniel and threw him into the pit of lions. The king said to Daniel, "You always serve your God. I hope that he will save you."

**17** The servants brought a stone and put it over the mouth of the pit. The king sealed the stone with his own ring and with the rings of his nobles. Thus no person could change what occurred to Daniel.

**18** Then the king went to his palace. He did not eat during the night. He did not let musicians come to him. He could not sleep.

**19** Very early in the morning, the king got up. He quickly went to the pit of lions.

**20** When he came near the pit, he called to Daniel with a sad voice. The king said, "Daniel, servant of the God who lives! You always serve your God. Could your God save you from the lions?"

**21** Then Daniel said to the king, "O king, live forever!

**22** My God sent his angel. The angel closed the mouths of the lions, and the lions did not hurt me. God found me innocent. And, O king, I did nothing wrong to you."

**23** Then the king was very glad. He told his servants to lift Daniel out of the pit. They lifted Daniel out of the pit. He had no injury, because he trusted in his God.

**24** Then the king gave an order. His servants brought the men who accused Daniel. They threw these men, their children, and their wives into the pit of lions. Before these people touched the bottom of the pit, the lions attacked them. The lions broke all their bones into pieces.

**25** Then King Darius wrote to the people of all nations and languages who live on all the earth: "I wish you much peace.

**26** I give this order. In all parts of my kingdom, people must fear and respect the God of Daniel. He is the God who lives, and he continues forever. No one will destroy his kingdom. His rule will never end.

**27** He saves and he rescues. He does signs and miracles in heaven and on earth. He saved Daniel from the power of the lions."

**28** Thus Daniel had success during the rule of Darius and during the rule of Cyrus the Persian.

## Chapter 7

**1** In the 1st year of Belshazzar king of Babylon, Daniel had a dream. He saw visions in his mind while he was on his bed. Then he wrote down the dream. He wrote the main points.

**2** Daniel said, "In my vision during the night, I looked. I saw the 4 winds of heaven. The winds stirred up the great sea.

**3** 4 large animals came up out of the sea. Each animal was different from the others.

**4** The 1st animal was like a lion, and it had the wings of an eagle. I watched until someone pulled off its wings. Someone lifted it up from the ground. It stood on 2 feet like a man. Someone gave it the mind of a man.

**5** Then I saw a 2nd animal. It was like a bear. It raised up 1 side of its body. It had 3 ribs in its mouth, between its teeth. A voice said to it, 'Get up! Eat much meat!'

**6** After this I looked, and I saw another animal. It was like a leopard. On its back it had 4 wings like the wings of a bird. This animal also had 4 heads. Someone gave it the power to rule.

**7** After this, in my visions in the night, I saw a 4th animal. It was terrible, dreadful, and very strong. It had large iron teeth. It ate and crushed its victims. It walked on the remains with its feet. It was different from all the animals before it. It had 10 horns.

**8** While I thought about the horns, I saw another horn. It was a small horn, and it grew up among the other horns. It pulled out 3 of the first horns by the roots. This small horn had eyes like the eyes of a man. It had a mouth that spoke proud words.

**9** I watched until someone put thrones in their positions. The Ancient of Days sat down. His clothes were as white as snow. The hair on his head was like pure wool. His throne was like flames. The wheels of his throne were like fire that burns.

**10** A river of fire came out from in front of him. Millions of servants served him. Hundreds of millions stood in front of him. The court sat down to judge, and someone opened the books.

**11** Then I continued to watch, because of the proud words that the horn spoke. I watched until someone killed the 4th animal. Someone destroyed its body and put it into the fire.

**12** Someone took the power to rule from the other animals. But they could live for a period of time.

**13** In my visions during the night, I looked. I saw a person like a son of man. He came with the clouds of heaven. He came to the Ancient of Days, and they brought him near to the Ancient of Days.

**14** The Ancient of Days gave him power to rule, glory, and a kingdom. The people of all nations and languages will serve him. His rule will continue forever, and it will not end. No one will ever destroy his kingdom.

**15** As for me, Daniel, my spirit was sad. The visions in my mind made me afraid.

**16** I went near to one of the persons who stood there. I asked him for the true meaning of all this. He told me the meaning of these things.

**17** He said, 'These 4 large animals are 4 kings. They will start to rule on the earth.

**18** But the holy people of the Most High will receive the kingdom. They will have the kingdom forever and ever.'

**19** Then I wanted to know the truth about the 4th animal. It was different from all the others and very dreadful. It had iron teeth and bronze claws. It ate and crushed its victims. It walked on the remains with its feet.

**20** I also wanted to know about the 10 horns on its head. And I wanted to know about the other horn that grew up. 3 horns fell in front of it. That horn had eyes and a mouth that spoke proud words. It looked stronger than the other horns.

**21** While I watched, this horn made war against the holy people. It defeated them.

**22** Then the Ancient of Days came. He judged in favor of the holy people of the Most High. The time came for the holy people to receive the kingdom.

**23** The person said, 'The 4th animal is a 4th kingdom on the earth. It will be different from all the other kingdoms. It will destroy all the earth. It will crush the earth and break it into pieces.

**24** The 10 horns are 10 kings who will come from this kingdom. After them another king will come. He will be different from the first kings. He will defeat 3 kings.

**25** He will speak against the Most High. He will cause much trouble for the holy people of the Most High. He will try to change the holy times and the laws. The holy people will be in his control for a time, 2 times, and half a time.

**26** But the court will sit to judge. The court will take away his power to rule. They will destroy his power completely and forever.

**27** Then the holy people of the Most High will receive the kingdom, the power to rule, and the greatness of all the kingdoms under heaven. The kingdom of the Most High will continue forever. All rulers will serve him and obey him.'

**28** This is the end of the matter. As for me, Daniel, my thoughts troubled me very much. My face became pale. But I kept the matter to myself."

## Chapter 8

**1** In the 3rd year of the rule of King Belshazzar, I, Daniel, saw a vision. This vision came after the vision that I saw before.

**2** In the vision, I saw that I was in the fortress city of Susa, in the province of Elam. In the vision, I was next to the Ulai Canal.

**3** I looked up, and I saw a ram. The ram stood next to the canal. It had 2 horns, and the 2 horns were long. But 1 horn was longer than the other. The longer horn grew up after the other.

**4** I saw that the ram attacked toward the west, the north, and the south. No animal could stand against him. No one could save an animal from his power. He did what he wanted, and he became great.

**5** While I thought about this, I saw a male goat. The goat came from the west, across all the earth, and did not touch the ground. The goat had a large horn between his eyes.

**6** He came to the ram with the 2 horns. I saw that ram, which stood next to the canal. The goat ran at the ram with all his angry power.

**7** I saw the goat attack the ram. The goat was very angry with the ram. He hit the ram and broke its 2 horns. The ram had no power to stand against him. The goat threw the ram down on the ground and walked on him. No one could save the ram from his power.

**8** The male goat became very great. But when he was strong, his large horn broke. In its place, 4 large horns grew up. They pointed to the 4 winds of heaven.

**9** From 1 of these horns a small horn grew. It became very great toward the south, the east, and the beautiful land.

**10** It became great, until it touched the army of heaven. It threw down to the ground some of the army and some of the stars. It walked on them.

**11** It made itself as great as the Prince of the army. It stopped the daily sacrifice to the Prince. It threw down the place of his sanctuary.

**12** Because of sin, the horn got control of the army of heaven and the daily sacrifice. The horn threw truth down to the ground. The horn did what it wanted, and it had success.

**13** Then I heard a holy one speak. Another holy one said to him, "How long will the events of this vision continue? How long will the daily sacrifice stop? How long will the sin that destroys continue? How long will people walk on the sanctuary and the army?"

**14** He said to me, "For 2,300 evenings and mornings. Then the sanctuary will become clean again."

**15** I, Daniel, saw the vision, and I tried to understand it. Then I saw a person who looked like a man. He stood in front of me.

**16** I heard the voice of a man from the Ulai Canal. The voice called, "Gabriel, tell this man the meaning of the vision."

**17** Gabriel came near to the place where I stood. When he came, I was afraid. I fell down with my face to the ground. He said to me, "Son of man, understand that the vision is about the time of the end."

**18** While he spoke to me, I was in a deep sleep, with my face on the ground. Then he touched me and lifted me to my feet.

**19** He said, "I will tell you what will occur later, at the time of the anger of God. The vision is about the time that God chose for the end.

**20** You saw the ram with 2 horns. The 2 horns are the kings of Media and Persia.

**21** The male goat is the king of Greece. The large horn between his eyes is the 1st king.

**22** The large horn broke, and 4 horns grew in its place. These 4 horns are 4 kingdoms. They will start from the nation of that 1st king. But they will not have his power.

**23** At the end of the time of these kingdoms, the sins of the people will be complete. Then a fierce king will start to rule. He will be very clever and will use tricks.

**24** He will become very powerful, but not by his own power. He will cause terrible destruction, and he will have success in what he does. He will destroy powerful men and the holy people.

**25** He will be clever, and he will use lies to have success. He will think that he is very great. He will destroy many people when they think that they are safe. He will even fight against the Prince of princes. But someone will destroy him, and not by human power.

**26** The vision of the evenings and the mornings that you heard about is true. But keep the vision secret, because it is about a time far in the future."

**27** I, Daniel, became weak, and I was sick for some days. Then I got up and did the work of the king. But the vision troubled me. No one could understand it.

## Chapter 9

**1** Darius the son of Ahasuerus was from the family of the Medes. He became king over the kingdom of the Chaldeans.

**2** In the 1st year of his rule, I, Daniel, studied the books. I understood the number of years from the word of the LORD to Jeremiah the prophet. Jerusalem will be in ruins for 70 years.

**3** Then I turned to the Lord God. I prayed and asked him for help. I did not eat food. I wore rough cloth (sackcloth) and put ashes on myself.

**4** I prayed to the LORD my God and told him our sins. I said, "O Lord, you are the great and powerful God. You keep your agreement (covenant) of love with the people who love you and obey your commands.

**5** We did wrong, and we did evil. We did bad things, and we fought against you. We turned away from your commands and your laws.

**6** We did not listen to your servants the prophets. They spoke in your name to our kings, our rulers, our ancestors, and all the people of the land.

**7** O Lord, you are right. But today we feel shame. The men of Judah, the people of Jerusalem, and all Israel feel shame. Some of them are near, and some are far away. You sent them into many countries because they were not loyal to you.

**8** O Lord, we feel shame. Our kings, our rulers, and our ancestors feel shame, because we did wrong against you.

**9** The Lord our God shows mercy and forgives, but we fought against him.

**10** We did not obey the LORD our God. We did not obey his laws. He gave us these laws through his servants the prophets.

**11** All Israel disobeyed your law. They turned away and did not obey you. Thus the curses came on us. These are the curses that Moses, the servant of God, wrote in his law. They came on us because we did wrong against God.

**12** God did what he said against us and against our rulers. He brought a terrible disaster on us. Nothing under heaven was as bad as the disaster on Jerusalem.

**13** All this disaster came on us, as the law of Moses said. But we did not pray to the LORD our God for his favor. We did not turn away from our sins. We did not try to understand your truth.

**14** Thus the LORD was ready to bring the disaster, and he brought it on us. The LORD our God is fair in all that he does. But we did not obey him.

**15** O Lord our God, you brought your people out of the land of Egypt with your great power. You made your name famous, and it is still famous today. We did wrong, and we did evil.

**16** O Lord, you always do what is right. Thus please stop your anger against Jerusalem, your city, your holy mountain. Because of our sins and the sins of our ancestors, all the people around us insult Jerusalem and your people.

**17** Now, our God, hear the prayer of your servant. Hear my request for help. Lord, for your own sake, show kindness to your sanctuary, which is in ruins.

**18** My God, listen and hear. Open your eyes and look at our ruins. Look at the city that has your name. We do not ask you because we are good. We ask you because your mercy is great.

**19** Lord, hear us! Lord, forgive us! Lord, listen and do something! My God, for your own sake, do not wait. Your city and your people have your name."

**20** I continued to speak and pray. I told God my sins and the sins of my people Israel. I asked the LORD my God to help his holy mountain.

**21** While I prayed, Gabriel came to me. I saw him in the vision before. He flew quickly to me. He came at about the time of the evening offering.

**22** He spoke with me. He said, "Daniel, I came now to give you wisdom and insight.

**23** When you started to pray, God gave an answer. I came to tell you the answer, because God loves you very much. Thus think about the message and understand the vision.

**24** God decided on 70 periods of 7 years for your people and your holy city. In this time he will stop sin and bring an end to evil. He will pay for sin. He will bring good that continues forever. He will make the vision and the prophecy come true. He will anoint the most holy place.

**25** Know and understand this. A command will go out to build Jerusalem again. From that command until the anointed ruler (Messiah) comes, there will be 7 periods of 7 years and 62 periods of 7 years. People will build Jerusalem again with streets and a wall. But it will be a time of trouble.

**26** After the 62 periods of 7 years, people will kill the Messiah, and he will have nothing. The army of a ruler who will come will destroy the city and the sanctuary. The end will come like a flood. War will continue until the end. God decided that there will be destruction.

**27** The ruler will make a strong agreement with many people for 1 period of 7 years. In the middle of that period, he will stop the sacrifices and the offerings. He will put a terrible thing in the temple that makes it unclean. This will continue until the end that God decided comes on the one who destroys."

## Chapter 10

**1** In the 3rd year of Cyrus king of Persia, God showed a message to Daniel. Daniel also had the name Belteshazzar. The message was true. It was about a large war. Daniel understood the message. He understood the vision.

**2** At that time I, Daniel, was sad for 3 full weeks.

**3** I ate no good food. I ate no meat, and I drank no wine. I did not put oil on myself until the 3 weeks were complete.

**4** On the 24th day of the 1st month, I stood next to the great river Tigris.

**5** I looked up, and I saw a man in linen clothes. He had a belt of pure gold from Uphaz around his waist.

**6** His body was like a precious stone. His face was as bright as lightning. His eyes were like torches of fire. His arms and his legs shone like polished bronze. His voice was like the sound of a large crowd.

**7** Only I, Daniel, saw the vision. The men with me did not see it. But they became very afraid. They ran away and hid themselves.

**8** Thus I stayed alone, and I saw this great vision. I had no strength. My face became very pale, and I was weak.

**9** Then I heard the sound of his words. When I heard his voice, I fell into a deep sleep with my face on the ground.

**10** Then a hand touched me. It lifted me up onto my hands and knees. I shook with fear.

**11** He said to me, "Daniel, God loves you very much. Understand the words that I tell you. Stand up, because God sent me to you." When he said this to me, I stood up, but I shook with fear.

**12** Then he said to me, "Do not be afraid, Daniel. You decided to understand and to make yourself humble in front of your God. From the 1st day, God heard your words. I came because of your words.

**13** But the prince of the kingdom of Persia fought against me for 21 days. Then Michael, one of the chief princes, came to help me. I stayed there with the kings of Persia.

**14** Now I came to tell you what will occur to your people in the future. The vision is about a time far in the future."

**15** While he spoke these words to me, I looked down to the ground. I could not speak.

**16** Then a person who looked like a man touched my lips. I opened my mouth and spoke. I said to the person who stood in front of me, "My lord, because of the vision, I feel much pain. I have no strength.

**17** I am your servant, my lord. How can I speak with you? I have no strength, and I can hardly breathe."

**18** Then the person who looked like a man touched me again. He gave me strength.

**19** He said, "God loves you very much. Do not be afraid. Peace be with you. Be strong! Be strong!" When he spoke to me, I became strong. I said, "My lord, speak, because you gave me strength."

**20** Then he said, "Do you know why I came to you? Now I will go back to fight against the prince of Persia. When I go, the prince of Greece will come.

**21** But first I will tell you what the Book of Truth says. No one helps me against these princes except Michael, your prince."

## Chapter 11

**1** "In the 1st year of Darius the Mede, I stood up to help Michael and to make him strong.

**2** Now I will tell you the truth. 3 more kings will rule in Persia. Then a 4th king will come. He will be much richer than all the other kings. He will become strong because of his riches. Then he will cause all people to fight against the kingdom of Greece.

**3** Then a powerful king will start to rule. He will rule a large empire, and he will do what he wants.

**4** But after he starts to rule, his kingdom will break apart. It will divide toward the 4 winds of heaven. His children will not get his kingdom. The kingdom will not have the power that he had. Other people will take his kingdom.

**5** The king of the south will become strong. But 1 of his commanders will become stronger than him. That commander will rule a large kingdom.

**6** After some years, the 2 kings will make an alliance. The daughter of the king of the south will go to the king of the north to make an agreement. But she will not keep her power, and the king will not keep his power. People will kill her, the men who brought her, her father, and the man who helped her.

**7** But a person from her family will become king in place of her father. He will come with an army. He will go into the fortress of the king of the north. He will fight against the king of the north and defeat him.

**8** He will take their gods, their metal statues, and their precious things of silver and gold to Egypt. For some years he will not attack the king of the north.

**9** Then the king of the north will attack the kingdom of the king of the south. But he will go back to his own land.

**10** The sons of the king of the north will prepare for war. They will gather a large army. The army will move forward like a flood and go through the land. Then it will go back and fight again, as far as the fortress of the king of the south.

**11** Then the king of the south will become very angry. He will go out and fight against the king of the north. The king of the north will gather a large army. But the king of the south will defeat that army.

**12** The king of the south will defeat the army, and he will become proud. He will kill tens of thousands of soldiers. But he will not stay strong.

**13** The king of the north will come back with a larger army than before. After some years he will attack with a large army and much equipment.

**14** At that time many people will fight against the king of the south. Violent men among your people will also start to fight. They will try to make the vision come true. But they will fail.

**15** Then the king of the north will come. He will build ramps against the walls and capture a strong city. The armies of the south will not be able to stop him. Even their best soldiers will not have the strength to stop him.

**16** The king of the north will do what he wants. No one will be able to stand against him. He will stay in the beautiful land, and he will have the power to destroy it.

**17** He will decide to come with the power of all his kingdom. He will make an agreement with the king of the south. He will give the king of the south a daughter as a wife, to destroy the kingdom. But his plan will not succeed, and it will not help him.

**18** After this he will turn to the coastlands and capture many of them. But a commander will stop his proud actions. The commander will make the proud actions come back on him.

**19** Then he will go back to the fortresses of his own land. But he will fall, and no one will see him again.

**20** Another king will take his place. That king will send a tax collector to keep the glory of the kingdom. But after a short time, someone will destroy that king. He will not die in anger or in battle.

**21** Then an evil man will take his place. People will not give him the honor of a king. He will come when the people feel safe. He will get the kingdom with lies and false praise.

**22** He will completely defeat large armies like a flood and destroy them. He will also destroy the prince of the covenant.

**23** He will make agreements with other nations, but he will use tricks. He will become powerful with only a small group of people.

**24** He will go into the richest parts of the provinces when the people feel safe. He will do things that his father and his ancestors did not do. He will give the plunder, the goods, and the riches to his followers. He will make plans to attack fortresses. But he will do this only for a time.

**25** He will gather his strength and his courage with a large army. He will attack the king of the south. The king of the south will fight with a very large and powerful army. But the king of the south will not win, because his enemies will make plans against him.

**26** The men who eat at his table will destroy him. His army will go away like a flood, and many soldiers will die.

**27** Both kings will want to do evil. They will sit at the same table and tell lies to each other. But their plans will not succeed, because the end will come at the time that God chose.

**28** The king of the north will go back to his land with great riches. But he will decide to act against the holy covenant. He will do what he wants, and then he will go back to his own land.

**29** At the time that God chose, he will attack the south again. But this time the result will not be as before.

**30** Ships from Cyprus will come against him. Thus he will become afraid and go back. He will be very angry with the holy covenant, and he will act against it. He will go back and help the people who leave the holy covenant.

**31** His soldiers will make the temple fortress unclean. They will stop the daily sacrifice. They will put the terrible thing that causes destruction in the temple.

**32** He will use false praise to make the people who disobey the covenant follow him. But the people who know their God will be strong and fight against him.

**33** The wise people among the nation will teach many people. But for some time, enemies will kill some of them with swords and fire. Enemies will take some of them as captives and steal their goods.

**34** When they fall, they will get a little help. Many people will join them but will not be honest.

**35** Some of the wise people will fall. This will test them, make them pure, and make them clean until the time of the end. The end will come at the time that God chose.

**36** The king will do what he wants. He will make himself more important than all the gods. He will say terrible things against the God of gods. He will have success until the time of the anger of God is complete. What God decided will occur.

**37** He will not respect the gods of his ancestors. He will not respect the god that women love. He will not respect any god. He will make himself more important than all of them.

**38** In place of these gods, he will honor a god of fortresses. His ancestors did not know this god. He will honor this god with gold, silver, precious stones, and expensive gifts.

**39** With the help of this foreign god, he will attack the strongest fortresses. He will give great honor to the people who accept him. He will make them rulers over many people. He will divide the land and give it to them as a reward.

**40** At the time of the end, the king of the south will attack him. The king of the north will attack the king of the south like a storm. He will come with chariots, horsemen, and many ships. He will go into many countries and move through them like a flood.

**42** He will use his power against many countries. Egypt will not escape.

**43** He will take control of the treasures of gold and silver and all the precious things of Egypt. The Libyans and the Ethiopians will obey him.

**44** But news from the east and the north will make him afraid. Thus he will go out with great anger to destroy and kill many people.

**45** He will put up his royal tents between the seas and the beautiful holy mountain. But he will come to his end, and no one will help him.

## Chapter 12
