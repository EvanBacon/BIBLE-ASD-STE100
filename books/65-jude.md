# Jude

## Chapter 1

**1** Jude writes this letter. I am a servant of Jesus Christ and the brother of James. I write to the people whom God called. God the Father loves them and makes them holy. Jesus Christ keeps them safe.

**2** I pray that God gives you more and more mercy, peace, and love.

**3** Dear friends, I wanted very much to write to you about the salvation that we all share. But I find it necessary to write to you about a different thing. I tell you to fight hard for the faith. God gave this faith to his holy people one time for all time.

**4** Some men came in secretly among you. Long ago, the scriptures wrote about the punishment of these men. These men do not respect God. They change the grace of our God into a reason for sexual sin. They refuse to accept Jesus Christ, our only Master and Lord.

**5** You already know all these things. But I want to cause you to remember them. The Lord saved his people out of the land of Egypt. But after that, he destroyed the people who did not believe.

**6** Some angels did not stay in their position of authority. They left their correct home. God keeps these angels in chains that last forever, in darkness. He keeps them there until the judgment on the great day.

**7** Also remember Sodom and Gomorrah and the cities near them. The people of those cities did sexual sins, as those angels did. They followed unnatural sexual desires. God punished them with fire that lasts forever. They are an example for all people.

**8** These men do the same things. They follow their dreams. They make their bodies unclean. They refuse authority. They speak bad words against the glorious beings.

**9** Michael, the chief angel, argued with the devil about the body of Moses. But Michael did not speak bad words of judgment against the devil. He said, "The Lord will correct you."

**10** But these men speak bad words against things that they do not understand. They are like animals that cannot think. They know some things by instinct. But these things destroy them.

**11** Terrible things will happen to them! They live as Cain lived. They hurry to do the wrong thing that Balaam did, because they want money. They will die, as Korah died when he fought against authority.

**12** These men are a danger at your meals of love. They eat with you without shame. They take care only of themselves. They are like clouds that the wind blows, but the clouds give no rain. They are like trees in autumn that give no fruit. Someone pulled out these trees by the roots. Thus they are completely dead.

**13** They are like the wild waves of the sea. The waves throw up foam, and their shameful acts are like this foam. They are like stars that move away from their correct path. God keeps the blackest darkness for them forever.

**14** Enoch was in the seventh generation from Adam. He spoke a prophecy about these men. He said, "Look! The Lord comes with many thousands of his holy ones.

**15** He comes to judge all people. He will show that all the godless people are guilty. They did godless acts in a godless manner. Godless sinners also spoke hard words against him. He will find them guilty for all these things."

**16** These men always complain and find fault with others. They follow their own bad desires. They speak proud words about themselves. They praise other people to get an advantage.

**17** But dear friends, remember the words that the apostles of our Lord Jesus Christ spoke before.

**18** They told you, "In the last time, there will be people who laugh at God. They will follow their own godless desires."

**19** These men cause divisions among you. They follow their natural desires. They do not have the Spirit.

**20** But you, dear friends, make yourselves strong in your most holy faith. Pray with the help of the Holy Spirit.

**21** Stay in the love of God. Wait for the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ. He will give you life that continues forever.

**22** Show mercy to some people. Know the difference between people.

**23** Save other people. Pull them out of the fire. Do this with fear. Hate even the clothes that their sinful bodies made dirty.

**24** God can keep you from a fall into sin. He can bring you in front of his glory with no fault and with much joy.

**25** He is the only wise God and our Savior. Glory, honor, power, and authority belong to him, now and forever. Amen.
