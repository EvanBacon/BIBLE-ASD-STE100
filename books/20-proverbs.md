# Proverbs

## Chapter 1

**1** These are the wise sayings (proverbs) of Solomon. Solomon was the son of David and the king of Israel.

**2** These proverbs help you to know wisdom and to accept correction. They help you to understand wise words.

**3** They help you to learn how to live wisely. They teach you to do what is right, fair and honest.

**4** They give good sense to simple people. They give knowledge and good judgment to young men.

**5** A wise man will listen and learn more. A man with understanding will get good advice.

**6** Then he will understand proverbs and their meanings. He will understand the words of wise people and their difficult sayings.

**7** Fear of the LORD is the start of knowledge. But fools hate wisdom and correction.

**8** My son, listen to the teaching of your father. Do not forget the teaching of your mother.

**9** Their teaching will be like a beautiful crown on your head. It will be like a chain of honor around your neck.

**10** My son, sinners will try to make you do wrong. Do not agree with them.

**11** They will say, "Come with us. We will hide and wait to kill someone. We will attack innocent people for no reason.

**12** We will destroy them completely, as the grave takes the dead. We will kill them as the dead go down into the pit.

**13** We will find all types of valuable things. We will fill our houses with the things that we steal.

**14** Join our group. We will share all the money."

**15** My son, do not go with them. Keep your feet away from their path.

**16** Their feet run to do evil. They go quickly to kill people.

**17** A bird can see a net. Then the net does not catch the bird.

**18** But these men wait to kill, and they kill themselves. They hide and wait for their own death.

**19** This is what occurs to all people who are greedy for money. Greed takes away the lives of the greedy people.

**20** Wisdom calls aloud outside. She raises her voice in the streets.

**21** She calls in the busy places. She speaks at the city gates. She says,

**22** "Simple people, how long will you love your foolish ways? How long will mockers enjoy their mockery? How long will fools hate knowledge?

**23** Listen when I correct you. I will give you my spirit. I will tell you my words.

**24** I called to you, but you refused to listen. I held out my hand to you, but no person paid attention.

**25** You ignored all my advice. You did not accept my correction.

**26** So I will laugh when disaster comes to you. I will mock you when fear comes to you.

**27** Fear will come on you like a storm. Destruction will come on you like a strong wind. Trouble and pain will come on you.

**28** Then they will call to me, but I will not answer. They will look for me, but they will not find me.

**29** They hated knowledge. They did not choose to fear the LORD.

**30** They did not accept my advice. They hated all my correction.

**31** Thus they will get the results of their own way. Their own plans will cause them much trouble.

**32** Simple people turn away from wisdom, and this kills them. Fools are too comfortable, and this destroys them.

**33** But the person who listens to me will live safely. He will be calm and he will not fear evil."

## Chapter 2

**1** My son, accept my words. Keep my commands in your memory.

**2** Listen carefully to wisdom. Use all your mind to get understanding.

**3** Call out for knowledge. Ask loudly for understanding.

**4** Look for wisdom as you look for silver. Search for it as you search for hidden treasure.

**5** If you do these things, then you will understand the fear of the LORD. You will find the knowledge of God.

**6** The LORD gives wisdom. Knowledge and understanding come from his mouth.

**7** He keeps good wisdom for honest people. He is a shield to the people who live correctly.

**8** He protects the paths of fair people. He keeps his faithful people safe.

**9** Then you will understand what is right, fair and honest. You will understand every good path.

**10** Wisdom will come into your heart. Knowledge will give pleasure to your soul.

**11** Then good judgment will protect you. Understanding will keep you safe.

**12** Wisdom will save you from the way of evil men. It will save you from men who speak lies.

**13** These men leave the correct paths. They walk in the ways of darkness.

**14** They are happy when they do evil. They enjoy the bad things that wicked people do.

**15** Their ways are not straight. Their paths are dishonest.

**16** Wisdom will also save you from the adulterous woman. It will save you from the foreign woman who speaks false sweet words.

**17** She left the husband of her youth. She forgot the agreement (covenant) that she made in front of her God.

**18** Her house leads down to death. Her paths lead to the dead.

**19** No man who goes to her comes back. No man finds the paths of life again.

**20** Thus you will walk in the way of good men. You will stay on the paths of honest people.

**21** Honest people will live in the land. People who do right will stay in the land.

**22** But God will remove wicked people from the land. He will pull out dishonest people from the land.

## Chapter 3

**1** My son, do not forget my teaching. Keep my commands in your heart.

**2** My commands will give you a long life. They will give you many years and peace.

**3** Do not let love and truth leave you. Tie them around your neck. Write them on your heart.

**4** Then God and people will like you. They will think well of you.

**5** Trust in the LORD with all your heart. Do not depend on your own understanding.

**6** Remember the LORD in all that you do. He will make your paths straight.

**7** Do not think that you are wise. Fear the LORD and turn away from evil.

**8** This will make your body healthy. It will make your bones strong.

**9** Honor the LORD with your wealth. Give him the first and best part of all your crops.

**10** Then your storehouses will be very full. Your wine containers will overflow with new wine.

**11** My son, do not hate the discipline of the LORD. Do not become angry when he corrects you.

**12** The LORD corrects the people that he loves. A father corrects the son that gives him pleasure.

**13** The man who finds wisdom is happy. The man who gets understanding is happy.

**14** Wisdom is better than silver. Its profit is better than gold.

**15** Wisdom is more valuable than rubies. All the things that you want are not equal to her.

**16** Wisdom holds a long life in her right hand. She holds wealth and honor in her left hand.

**17** Her ways give pleasure. All her paths give peace.

**18** Wisdom is a tree of life to the people who hold her. The people who keep her are happy.

**19** The LORD used wisdom to make the earth. He used understanding to put the heavens in their place.

**20** His knowledge caused the deep waters to break out. His knowledge causes the clouds to drop dew.

**21** My son, keep good wisdom and good judgment. Do not let them go away from your eyes.

**22** They will give life to your soul. They will be like a beautiful chain around your neck.

**23** Then you will walk on your path safely. Your foot will not trip.

**24** When you lie down, you will not be afraid. You will lie down and sleep well.

**25** Do not be afraid of sudden disaster. Do not be afraid when destruction comes on the wicked people.

**26** The LORD will be your hope. He will keep your foot from a trap.

**27** Do not keep good things from the people who must get them. Give good things when you can.

**28** Your neighbor can ask you for something that you have now. Then do not say, "Go away and come back. I will give it to you tomorrow."

**29** Do not plan evil against your neighbor. He lives near you and trusts you.

**30** Do not argue with a man for no reason. Do not argue with him if he did not hurt you.

**31** Do not envy a violent man. Do not do the things that he does.

**32** The LORD hates dishonest people. But he is a friend to honest people.

**33** The LORD curses the house of wicked people. But he blesses the home of good people.

**34** The LORD mocks proud mockers. But he is kind to humble people.

**35** Wise people will get honor. But fools will get shame.

## Chapter 4

**1** Children, listen to the teaching of your father. Pay attention so that you get understanding.

**2** I give you good teaching. Do not leave my instruction.

**3** I was a son to my father. I was young and the only child that my mother loved.

**4** My father taught me. He said, "Keep my words in your heart. Obey my commands and you will live.

**5** Get wisdom. Get understanding. Do not forget my words. Do not turn away from them.

**6** Do not leave wisdom, and she will protect you. Love her, and she will keep you safe.

**7** Wisdom is the most important thing. Thus, get wisdom. Use all that you have to get understanding.

**8** Think highly of wisdom, and she will make you great. Hold her close, and she will give you honor.

**9** She will put a beautiful crown on your head. She will give you a crown of glory."

**10** My son, listen and accept my words. Then you will live for many years.

**11** I taught you the way of wisdom. I led you on straight paths.

**12** When you walk, nothing will stop you. When you run, you will not trip.

**13** Hold on to correction. Do not let it go. Keep it, because it is your life.

**14** Do not go on the path of wicked people. Do not walk in the way of evil men.

**15** Stay away from their path. Do not go on it. Turn away from it and go past it.

**16** Wicked people cannot sleep until they do evil. They cannot sleep until they cause a person to fall.

**17** They eat food that they get by wicked acts. They drink wine that they get by violence.

**18** The path of good people is like the light of the sunrise. The light becomes brighter until it is full day.

**19** The way of wicked people is like deep darkness. They do not know what causes them to fall.

**20** My son, pay attention to my words. Listen carefully to what I say.

**21** Do not let my words go away from your eyes. Keep them deep in your heart.

**22** My words give life to the people who find them. They give health to the whole body.

**23** Guard your heart carefully, because your life comes from your heart.

**24** Do not let your mouth speak lies. Keep your lips away from dishonest words.

**25** Let your eyes look straight ahead. Look directly in front of you.

**26** Think carefully about the path for your feet. Then all your ways will be safe.

**27** Do not turn to the right or to the left. Keep your foot away from evil.

## Chapter 5

**1** My son, pay attention to my wisdom. Listen carefully to my understanding.

**2** Then you will keep good judgment. Your lips will keep knowledge.

**3** The lips of an adulterous woman are sweet like honey. Her words are smoother than oil.

**4** But at the end she is bitter like poison. She is as sharp as a sword with 2 edges.

**5** Her feet go down to death. Her steps go to the grave.

**6** She does not think about the path of life. Her ways go in different directions, and she does not know it.

**7** Thus, children, listen to me now. Do not turn away from my words.

**8** Stay far away from that woman. Do not go near the door of her house.

**9** If you go to her, you will give your honor to other people. You will give your years to cruel people.

**10** Strangers will take your wealth. Your work will make strangers rich.

**11** At the end of your life you will cry. Your body will be weak and sick.

**12** You will say, "I hated correction. My heart did not accept a warning.

**13** I did not obey my teachers. I did not listen to the people who taught me.

**14** I almost destroyed myself in front of all the people."

**15** Drink water from your own well. Drink the water that flows from your own spring. Be faithful to your wife.

**16** Do not let your springs flow into the streets. Do not let your water flow into public places.

**17** Your water is only for you. Do not share it with strangers.

**18** Let your spring be blessed. Be happy with the wife that you married when you were young.

**19** She is beautiful and graceful like a deer. Let her breasts satisfy you always. Let her love fill you with joy always.

**20** My son, do not let an adulterous woman fill you with desire. Do not hold the body of another man's wife.

**21** The LORD sees all that a person does. He examines all his paths.

**22** The sins of a wicked man will catch him. His sins will hold him like ropes.

**23** He will die because he did not accept correction. His great foolishness will cause him to go the wrong way.

## Chapter 6

**1** My son, maybe you promised to pay the debt of your friend. Maybe you made an agreement for a stranger.

**2** Then the words of your mouth caught you like a trap. Your own words caught you.

**3** My son, do this now to make yourself free. Your friend has power over you. Go to him and be humble. Ask him again and again to release you.

**4** Do not sleep. Do not rest your eyes.

**5** Make yourself free, as a deer escapes from the hunter. Make yourself free, as a bird escapes from a trap.

**6** Lazy person, go to the ant. Look at what it does and become wise.

**7** The ant has no leader, no supervisor and no ruler.

**8** But it gets its food ready in the summer. It collects its food at the harvest.

**9** Lazy person, how long will you sleep? When will you get up from your sleep?

**10** You say, "I will sleep a little more. I will rest a little more. I will fold my hands and rest a little more."

**11** Then poverty will come on you like a thief. Need will come on you like an armed man.

**12** A bad and wicked man always tells lies.

**13** He winks his eyes. He makes signs with his feet. He points with his fingers.

**14** His heart is dishonest. He always plans evil. He causes arguments.

**15** Thus disaster will come on him suddenly. He will be destroyed suddenly, and nothing will help him.

**16** The LORD hates 6 things. There are 7 things that he hates very much:

**17** proud eyes, a tongue that lies, hands that kill innocent people,

**18** a heart that plans wicked things, feet that run quickly to do evil,

**19** a false witness who tells lies, and a person who causes arguments among brothers.

**20** My son, obey the commands of your father. Do not forget the teaching of your mother.

**21** Always keep their words in your heart. Tie them around your neck.

**22** When you walk, their teaching will lead you. When you sleep, it will protect you. When you wake up, it will speak to you.

**23** The command is a lamp. The teaching is a light. Correction and discipline show you the way of life.

**24** They will keep you away from the evil woman. They will keep you from the sweet words of the adulterous woman.

**25** Do not let her beauty fill your heart with desire. Do not let her eyes catch you.

**26** A prostitute can make a man poor, so that he has only a piece of bread. Another man's wife hunts for your life.

**27** Can a man carry fire against his chest and not burn his clothes?

**28** Can a man walk on hot coals and not burn his feet?

**29** The same is true for the man who sleeps with his neighbor's wife. Every man who touches her will be punished.

**30** People do not hate a thief if he steals food when he is hungry.

**31** But if people catch the thief, he must pay back 7 times the value. He must give all the things in his house.

**32** But the man who commits adultery has no sense. The man who does this destroys himself.

**33** He will get wounds and shame. His shame will never go away.

**34** Jealousy makes a husband very angry. When the husband punishes him, he will show no pity.

**35** The husband will not accept payment. He will refuse all gifts, even many gifts.

## Chapter 7

**1** My son, obey my words. Keep my commands in your heart.

**2** Obey my commands and you will live. Protect my teaching as you protect your eyes.

**3** Tie my commands on your fingers. Write them on your heart.

**4** Say to wisdom, "You are my sister." Call understanding your close relative.

**5** They will keep you away from the adulterous woman. They will keep you away from the foreign woman who speaks false sweet words.

**6** I looked out through the window of my house.

**7** I saw a group of simple young men. Among them I saw a young man who had no sense.

**8** He went along the street near the corner where the woman lived. He walked toward her house.

**9** It was the evening, at sunset. Then the night became dark.

**10** Then a woman came out to meet him. She wore clothes like a prostitute, and she had a dishonest plan.

**11** She is loud and she does not obey. She does not stay at home.

**12** Sometimes she is in the street and sometimes in the market. She waits at every corner.

**13** She took hold of the young man and kissed him. With a bold face she said to him,

**14** "I made peace offerings today. I did the things that I promised to God.

**15** So I came out to meet you. I looked for you, and I found you.

**16** I put beautiful covers on my bed. I put colored linen from Egypt on it.

**17** I put perfume on my bed. I used myrrh, aloes and cinnamon.

**18** Come, we will make love until the morning. We will enjoy our love.

**19** My husband is not at home. He went on a long journey.

**20** He took a bag of money with him. He will not come home until the full moon."

**21** She used her many sweet words to persuade him. Her false words caused him to agree.

**22** He followed her immediately. He went like an ox that goes to be killed. He went like a deer that goes into a trap.

**23** He went until an arrow went into his body. He went like a bird that flies quickly into a trap. He did not know that the trap will kill him.

**24** Now, children, listen to me. Pay attention to my words.

**25** Do not let your heart turn to her ways. Do not go on her paths.

**26** She caused many men to fall. She killed many strong men.

**27** Her house is the road to the grave. It goes down to the rooms of death.

## Chapter 8

**1** Wisdom calls out. Understanding raises her voice.

**2** She stands on the high places beside the road. She stands where the paths meet.

**3** She calls out at the gates at the entrance to the city. She calls at the doors.

**4** She says, "People, I call to you. I speak to all humans.

**5** Simple people, learn to be careful. Fools, learn to have understanding.

**6** Listen, because I will tell you very good things. My lips will say what is right.

**7** My mouth speaks the truth. My lips hate wicked words.

**8** All my words are honest. None of my words are dishonest or false.

**9** All my words are clear to the people who understand. They are correct to the people who have knowledge.

**10** Accept my teaching instead of silver. Accept knowledge instead of the best gold.

**11** Wisdom is better than rubies. Nothing that you want is equal to her.

**12** I, Wisdom, live with good sense. I have knowledge and good judgment.

**13** The fear of the LORD is to hate evil. I hate pride and arrogance. I hate the evil way and dishonest words.

**14** I give advice and good wisdom. I have understanding and strength.

**15** Kings rule with my help. Rulers make fair laws with my help.

**16** Princes rule with my help. All nobles and judges of the earth rule with my help.

**17** I love the people who love me. The people who look for me will find me.

**18** I have wealth and honor. I give wealth that lasts, and I give justice.

**19** My gifts are better than gold, even the best gold. What I give is better than the best silver.

**20** I walk in the way of what is right. I walk on the paths of justice.

**21** I give wealth to the people who love me. I fill their storehouses.

**22** The LORD made me at the start of his work. He made me before his first acts.

**23** He put me in my place a very long time ago. He made me at the start, before he made the earth.

**24** He made me before there were oceans. He made me before there were springs full of water.

**25** He made me before he put the mountains in their places. He made me before the hills.

**26** He made me before he made the earth and the fields. He made me before he made the first dust of the world.

**27** I was there when he put the heavens in their place. I was there when he drew the horizon on the surface of the ocean.

**28** I was there when he put the clouds in the sky. I was there when he made the springs of the ocean strong.

**29** I was there when he gave the sea its limits, so that the waters obey his command. I was there when he marked the foundations of the earth.

**30** I was near him like a skilled worker. I gave him pleasure every day. I was always happy in front of him.

**31** I was happy with the whole world that he made. I enjoyed the human race.

**32** Now, children, listen to me. The people who obey my ways are happy.

**33** Listen to my teaching and be wise. Do not refuse my teaching.

**34** The person who listens to me is happy. He watches at my gates every day. He waits at my doors.

**35** The person who finds me finds life. The LORD will be kind to him.

**36** But the person who does not find me hurts himself. All the people who hate me love death."

## Chapter 9

**1** Wisdom built her house. She cut her 7 columns.

**2** She killed animals for a meal. She mixed her wine. She made her table ready.

**3** She sent out her female servants to invite people. She calls from the highest places in the city.

**4** She says, "Simple people, come in here." She says to people who have no sense,

**5** "Come, eat my food. Drink the wine that I mixed.

**6** Stop your foolish ways, and live. Walk in the way of understanding."

**7** If you correct a mocker, he will insult you. If you correct a wicked man, he will hurt you.

**8** Do not correct a mocker, because he will hate you. Correct a wise man, and he will love you.

**9** Teach a wise man, and he will become wiser. Teach a good man, and he will learn more.

**10** Fear of the LORD is the start of wisdom. Knowledge of the Holy One is understanding.

**11** Wisdom will give you more days. She will add years to your life.

**12** If you are wise, your wisdom will help you. If you mock, you alone will suffer.

**13** The foolish woman is loud. She has no sense, and she knows nothing.

**14** She sits at the door of her house. She sits on a seat in the high places of the city.

**15** She calls to the people who go past. She calls to the people who walk straight on their way.

**16** She says, "Simple people, come in here." She says to people who have no sense,

**17** "Stolen water is sweet. Food that you eat in secret gives much pleasure."

**18** But the simple man does not know that the dead are in her house. Her guests are in the deepest part of the grave.

## Chapter 10

**1** These are the proverbs of Solomon. A wise son makes his father happy. But a foolish son makes his mother sad.

**2** Wealth that a person gets by wicked acts has no value. But good acts save a person from death.

**3** The LORD does not let good people become hungry. But he stops wicked people from getting what they want.

**4** Lazy hands make a man poor. But hard work makes a man rich.

**5** A son who collects the crops in summer is wise. But a son who sleeps at the harvest causes shame.

**6** Blessings come to good people. But the words of wicked people hide violence.

**7** People remember good people with blessings. But people will soon forget wicked people.

**8** A wise person accepts commands. But a fool who talks too much will fall.

**9** The honest person lives safely. But people will discover the dishonest person.

**10** The person who winks to deceive causes trouble. A fool who talks too much will fall.

**11** The words of a good man are like a spring of life. But the words of wicked people hide violence.

**12** Hate causes arguments. But love forgives all wrongs.

**13** People find wisdom in the words of a person with understanding. But a person with no sense will get punishment with a stick.

**14** Wise people collect knowledge. But the words of a fool bring destruction near.

**15** The wealth of a rich man protects him like a strong city. Poverty destroys poor people.

**16** The pay of good people brings life. The pay of wicked people brings sin.

**17** The person who accepts correction is on the path to life. But the person who refuses correction goes the wrong way.

**18** The person who hides hate with lies is a fool. The person who tells false stories is a fool.

**19** When you speak too many words, sin comes easily. But the person who controls his words is wise.

**20** The words of a good person are like the best silver. But the thoughts of a wicked person have little value.

**21** The words of good people help many people. But fools die because they have no sense.

**22** The blessing of the LORD makes a person rich. And the LORD adds no sadness to it.

**23** A fool enjoys evil acts. But a man with understanding enjoys wisdom.

**24** The thing that a wicked person fears will come to him. But good people will get what they want.

**25** When the storm goes past, wicked people are gone. But good people stand firm for all time.

**26** Vinegar hurts the teeth and smoke hurts the eyes. A lazy person is the same to the people who send him.

**27** Fear of the LORD gives a long life. But the years of wicked people will be short.

**28** The hope of good people will bring joy. But the hope of wicked people will come to nothing.

**29** The way of the LORD protects honest people. But it destroys the people who do evil.

**30** Good people will always be safe. But wicked people will not stay in the land.

**31** The mouth of a good person speaks wisdom. But God will stop the tongue that tells lies.

**32** Good people know the correct words to say. But wicked people say dishonest things.

## Chapter 11

**1** The LORD hates false scales. But correct weights give him pleasure.

**2** When pride comes, shame also comes. But wisdom is with humble people.

**3** Honesty leads honest people. But dishonesty destroys wicked people.

**4** Wealth will not help on the day of God's anger. But good acts save a person from death.

**5** Good acts make the path of honest people straight. But wicked people fall because of their wicked acts.

**6** Good acts save honest people. But the desires of wicked people catch them.

**7** When a wicked man dies, his hope stops. All that he expected from his wealth comes to nothing.

**8** God saves good people from trouble. Then the trouble comes on wicked people instead.

**9** A person who does not love God destroys his neighbor with his words. But knowledge saves good people.

**10** When good people succeed, the city is happy. When wicked people die, the people shout with joy.

**11** The blessing of honest people makes a city great. But the words of wicked people destroy a city.

**12** A person with no sense insults his neighbor. But a person with understanding stays quiet.

**13** A gossip tells secrets. But a faithful person keeps a secret.

**14** A nation with no good leaders will fall. But many advisers make a nation safe.

**15** If you promise to pay the debt of a stranger, you will suffer. If you refuse to make such promises, you will be safe.

**16** A kind woman gets honor. Strong men get only wealth.

**17** A kind man helps himself. But a cruel man hurts himself.

**18** A wicked man gets pay that has no value. But a person who does good gets a true reward.

**19** A person who does good things will live. But a person who follows evil goes to his death.

**20** The LORD hates people with dishonest hearts. But people who live honestly give him pleasure.

**21** Be sure of this: wicked people will get punishment. But the children of good people will be safe.

**22** A beautiful woman without good sense is like a gold ring in the nose of a pig.

**23** The desire of good people brings only good things. But the hope of wicked people brings anger.

**24** One person gives freely, but he gets more. Another person keeps too much, but he becomes poor.

**25** A person who gives generously will have more. A person who helps others will get help.

**26** People curse the man who keeps his grain for a higher price. But they bless the man who sells his grain.

**27** The person who looks for good will get kindness. But evil will come to the person who looks for evil.

**28** The person who trusts in his wealth will fall. But good people will grow like green leaves.

**29** The person who causes trouble for his family will get nothing. The fool will become the servant of the wise person.

**30** The work of good people is like a tree that gives life. A wise person brings people to life.

**31** Good people get their reward on earth. Thus, wicked people and sinners will surely get their punishment.

## Chapter 12

**1** The person who loves correction loves knowledge. But the person who hates correction is stupid.

**2** The LORD is kind to a good man. But the LORD condemns a man who makes evil plans.

**3** Wicked acts do not make a person safe. But good people stand firm like a tree with deep roots.

**4** A good wife is like a crown to her husband. But a wife who causes shame is like a disease in his bones.

**5** The plans of good people are fair. But the advice of wicked people is false.

**6** The words of wicked people are like a trap that kills. But the words of honest people save them.

**7** Wicked people fall and are gone. But the families of good people will continue.

**8** People praise a man for his wisdom. But people hate a man with a dishonest mind.

**9** It is better to be a common man who has a servant. It is worse to act important and have no food.

**10** A good man takes care of his animals. But even the kindest acts of wicked people are cruel.

**11** The man who works his land will have much food. But the man who follows foolish plans has no sense.

**12** Wicked people want the things that evil men steal. But good people are like a tree with deep roots that gives fruit.

**13** The wicked person's sinful words trap him. But a good person escapes from trouble.

**14** Good words bring good things to a man. The work of a man's hands brings him reward.

**15** A fool thinks that his own way is correct. But a wise man listens to advice.

**16** A fool shows his anger immediately. But a wise man ignores an insult.

**17** An honest witness tells the truth. But a false witness tells lies.

**18** Some people speak careless words that cut like a sword. But the words of wise people heal.

**31** Do not look at the wine when it is red. Do not look at it when it shines in the cup and goes down easily.

**32** At the end, the wine bites like a snake. It hurts like a poisonous snake.

**33** Your eyes will see strange things. Your mind will think and say wrong things.

**34** You will be like a man who lies down in the middle of the sea. You will be like a man who lies at the top of the mast of a ship.

**35** You will say, "People hit me, but I did not feel pain. People beat me, but I did not know it. When will I wake up? I will look for more wine."

## Chapter 24

**1** Do not be jealous of evil men. Do not want to be with them.

**2** Evil men think about how to cause damage. Their words speak about trouble.

**3** A man builds a house with wisdom. He makes the house strong with understanding.

**4** With knowledge, he fills the rooms with all types of valuable and good things.

**5** A wise man is strong. A man with knowledge increases his strength.

**6** You must have wise advice before you go to war. With many advisers, you will win.

**7** Wisdom is too high for a fool. In the meeting place at the city gate, the fool does not speak.

**8** People will call a man who plans evil a troublemaker.

**9** The plans of a foolish man are sin. People hate a man who mocks others.

**10** If you become weak at the time of trouble, then your strength is small.

**11** Save the people whom others take away to death. Stop the people who go to kill them.

**12** You can say, "We did not know about it." But God examines the heart, and He knows. He keeps your life, and He knows. He will give to each person what that person's actions deserve.

**13** My son, eat honey because it is good. The honey from the comb is sweet to your taste.

**14** Wisdom is like honey for your soul. If you find wisdom, then you will have a good future. Your hope will not stop.

**15** Wicked man, do not wait to attack the house of a righteous man. Do not destroy his home.

**16** A righteous man can fall 7 times, but he gets up again. But disaster makes the wicked fall.

**17** Do not be glad when your enemy falls. Do not be happy when he trips.

**18** If you are glad, the LORD will see it and He will not be pleased. Then He can stop His anger against your enemy.

**19** Do not be worried because of evil men. Do not be jealous of the wicked.

**20** The evil man has no future. The life of the wicked will stop, as a lamp goes out.

**21** My son, fear the LORD and the king. Do not join with people who rebel.

**22** Disaster will come to them suddenly. Nobody knows what destruction the LORD and the king can cause.

**23** These are also words of wise people. It is not good to show special favor to some people when you judge.

**24** A man can say to a guilty person, "You are innocent." People will curse that man. Nations will hate him.

**25** But people who correct the guilty person will have good things. Good blessings will come to them.

**26** An honest answer is like a kiss on the lips.

**27** First, do your work outside and make your fields ready. After that, build your house.

**28** Do not speak against your neighbor without a reason. Do not use your words to deceive.

**29** Do not say, "I will do to him what he did to me. I will pay the man back for what he did."

**30** I went past the field of a lazy man. I went past the vineyard of a man without good sense.

**31** Thorns grew everywhere on the field. Weeds covered the ground. The stone wall was broken.

**32** I looked at it and thought about it. I learned a lesson from what I saw.

**33** You sleep a little and rest a little. You fold your hands a little to sleep.

**34** Then poverty will come to you suddenly, like a robber. Need will come to you like an armed man.

## Chapter 25

**1** These are also proverbs of Solomon. The men of Hezekiah, king of Judah, copied them.

**2** God gets honor when He keeps a thing secret. Kings get honor when they examine a matter.

**3** The sky is very high and the earth is very deep. Also, nobody can know the mind of kings.

**4** Remove the waste metal from the silver. Then the metalworker can make a good container.

**5** Remove the wicked from the presence of the king. Then righteousness will make his throne strong.

**6** Do not try to look important in front of the king. Do not stand in the place of great men.

**7** It is better if the king tells you, "Come up here." That is better than if he puts you lower in front of an important person.

**8** Do not go quickly to the court to argue. At the end, your neighbor can prove you are wrong. Then what will you do?

**9** Discuss your argument with your neighbor himself. Do not tell the secret of another person.

**10** If you tell it, then the person who hears it can bring shame on you. Your bad name will not go away.

**11** A word that is spoken at the correct time is beautiful. It is like gold apples in a silver frame.

**12** A wise correction to a person who listens is valuable. It is like a gold earring or a jewel of fine gold.

**13** A faithful messenger is like cold snow at the time of harvest. He refreshes the masters who send him.

**14** A man who boasts about a gift that he does not give is like clouds and wind without rain.

**15** With patience, a person can persuade a ruler. Gentle words can break strong opposition.

**16** If you find honey, eat only the quantity that you need. If you eat too much, you will vomit it.

**17** Do not go too often to the house of your neighbor. If you do, he will become tired of you and hate you.

**18** A man who tells lies against his neighbor in court is dangerous. He is like a club, a sword or a sharp arrow.

**19** To trust an unfaithful man at a time of trouble is bad. It is like a broken tooth or a lame foot.

**20** Do not sing happy songs to a person who is very sad. That is like taking away a coat on a cold day. It is like pouring vinegar on soda.

**21** If your enemy is hungry, give him bread to eat. If he is thirsty, give him water to drink.

**22** Your kindness will make him ashamed, like hot coals on his head. And the LORD will reward you.

**23** The north wind brings rain. Secret talk against other people brings angry faces.

**24** It is better to live in a corner of the roof than in a large house with a woman who argues.

**25** Good news from a far country is like cold water to a thirsty person.

**26** A righteous man who gives way to the wicked is like a dirty spring or a polluted well.

**27** It is not good to eat too much honey. It is not good to look for honor for yourself.

**28** A man who cannot control himself is like a city with broken walls.

## Chapter 26

**1** Snow is not correct in summer. Rain is not correct at harvest. Also, honor is not correct for a fool.

**2** A bird that flies around does not land. Also, a curse without a reason will not come to a person.

**3** A whip is for the horse. A bridle is for the donkey. A stick is for the back of a fool.

**4** Do not answer a fool in his foolish way. If you do, you will become like him.

**5** Answer a fool as his foolishness deserves. If you do not, he will think that he is wise.

**6** A man who sends a message with a fool causes damage to himself. It is like cutting off his own feet.

**7** The legs of a lame man hang weak. Also, a proverb in the mouth of fools has no strength.

**8** To give honor to a fool is like tying a stone in a sling.

**9** A proverb in the mouth of fools is like a thorn in the hand of a drunk man.

**10** An employer who hires a fool or a person who passes by is like an archer who injures everyone.

**11** A dog goes back to its vomit. Also, a fool does his foolish things again.

**12** Do you see a man who thinks that he is wise? There is more hope for a fool than for him.

**13** The lazy man says, "There is a lion on the road. A lion is in the streets."

**14** A door turns on its hinges. Also, a lazy man turns on his bed.

**15** The lazy man puts his hand in the dish. He is too lazy to bring the food back to his mouth.

**16** The lazy man thinks that he is wiser than 7 men who give good answers.

**17** A man goes by and gets involved in an argument of other people. He is like a man who takes a dog by the ears.

**18** A crazy man throws burning sticks, arrows and death.

**19** A man who deceives his neighbor and then says, "I only made a joke," is like that crazy man.

**20** Without wood, a fire goes out. Without a person who gossips, an argument stops.

**21** Coals keep a fire hot, and wood keeps a fire burning. Also, a man who argues causes more arguments.

**22** The words of a gossip are like tasty food. People take those words deep inside themselves.

**23** Kind words from an evil heart are like a cheap clay pot with a silver cover.

**24** A man who hates you hides his hate with his words. Inside, he keeps his deceit.

**25** When he speaks kindly, do not believe him. His heart is full of 7 bad things that God hates.

**26** He can hide his hate with deceit. But all the people will see his wickedness.

**27** A man who digs a pit will fall into it. A man who rolls a stone will be hurt by the stone.

**28** A person who lies hates the people whom he hurts. A person who flatters causes destruction.

## Chapter 27

**1** Do not boast about tomorrow. You do not know what will occur in one day.

**2** Let another man praise you, not your own mouth. Let a stranger praise you, not your own lips.

**3** A stone is heavy and sand is heavy. But the anger of a fool is heavier than both.

**4** Anger is cruel and rage is like a flood. But nobody can stand against jealousy.

**5** Open correction is better than hidden love.

**6** A friend can hurt you, but you can trust him. An enemy can kiss you many times, but he deceives you.

**7** A person who is full does not want honey. But a hungry person thinks that bitter food is sweet.

**8** A man who goes away from his home is like a bird that goes away from its nest.

**9** Oil and perfume make the heart glad. Also, the honest advice of a friend is sweet.

**10** Do not leave your friend or the friend of your father. When you have a disaster, do not go to the house of your brother. A neighbor who is near is better than a brother who is far away.

**11** My son, be wise and make me glad. Then I can answer the person who insults me.

**12** A careful man sees danger and hides. But a foolish man continues and he suffers.

**13** If a man promises to pay the debt of a stranger, take his coat. If he promises for a foreign woman, keep his coat as a guarantee.

**14** A man can bless his friend with a loud voice early in the morning. His friend will think that it is a curse.

**15** A wife who argues is like water that drops all the time on a rainy day.

**16** To control her is like trying to control the wind. It is like trying to hold oil in your hand.

**17** Iron makes iron sharp. Also, one man makes his friend better.

**18** A man who takes care of a fig tree will eat its fruit. A man who serves his master well will get honor.

**19** Water shows the reflection of a face. Also, the heart of a man shows what the man is.

**20** Death and destruction always want more. Also, the eyes of man always want more.

**21** People test silver and gold in a hot fire. People test a man by the praise that he gets.

**22** You can crush a fool with grain in a bowl. But you cannot remove his foolishness from him.

**23** Know the condition of your sheep carefully. Take good care of your cattle.

**24** Riches do not continue forever. A crown does not continue to all generations.

**25** The hay goes away and the new grass grows. People gather the plants from the mountains.

**26** The lambs will give wool for your clothes. The goats will give you the money to buy a field.

**27** You will have sufficient milk from the goats for your food. You will have food for your family and for your female servants.

## Chapter 28

**1** The wicked run away when nobody chases them. But the righteous are brave like a lion.

**2** When a country rebels, it has many rulers. But a man with understanding and knowledge keeps order for a long time.

**3** A poor man who oppresses the poor is like a hard rain that destroys the crops.

**4** People who do not obey the law praise the wicked. But people who obey the law fight against the wicked.

**5** Evil men do not understand justice. But people who look for the LORD understand all things.

**6** A poor man who lives honestly is better than a rich man who lives in a dishonest way.

**7** A son who obeys the law is wise. But a son who is a friend of greedy eaters brings shame to his father.

**8** A man can get rich with high interest and dishonest profit. But his money will go to a man who is kind to the poor.

**9** If a man does not listen to the law, then God hates his prayer.

**10** A man can cause the righteous to go on an evil road. That man will fall into his own pit. But the honest will get good things.

**11** A rich man thinks that he is wise. But a poor man with understanding sees the truth about him.

**12** When the righteous win, there is a great celebration. But when the wicked get power, people hide.

**13** A man who hides his sins will not have success. But a man who confesses his sins and stops them will get mercy.

**14** A man who always fears the LORD is happy. But a man who makes his heart hard will fall into trouble.

**15** A wicked ruler over poor people is like a roaring lion or an attacking bear.

**16** A ruler without understanding oppresses the people very much. But a ruler who hates greed will have a long life.

**17** A man who is guilty of murder will run until he dies. Do not help him.

**18** A man who lives honestly will be safe. But a man who lives in a dishonest way will fall suddenly.

**19** A man who works his land will have much bread. But a man who follows foolish plans will be very poor.

**20** A faithful man will get many blessings. But a man who tries to become rich quickly will get punishment.

**21** It is not good to show special favor to some people. But a man will do wrong for a piece of bread.

**22** A greedy man tries to become rich quickly. He does not know that poverty will come to him.

**23** A man who corrects another man will get more thanks at the end than a man who flatters.

**24** A man can rob his father or his mother and say, "That is not wrong." That man is like a person who destroys.

**25** A greedy man causes arguments. But a man who trusts the LORD will have many good things.

**26** A man who trusts his own mind is a fool. But a man who lives wisely will be safe.

**27** A man who gives to the poor will not need anything. But a man who does not see the poor will get many curses.

**28** When the wicked get power, people hide. But when the wicked die, the righteous increase.

## Chapter 29

**1** A man can refuse correction many times. Suddenly, that man will be destroyed. Nothing will help him.

**2** When the righteous have power, the people are glad. But when a wicked man rules, the people are sad.

**3** A man who loves wisdom makes his father glad. But a man who stays with prostitutes wastes his money.

**4** A king who gives justice makes the country strong. But a king who takes bribes destroys the country.

**5** A man who flatters his neighbor puts a trap for his feet.

**6** An evil man is caught by his own sin. But a righteous man sings and is glad.

**7** A righteous man knows the rights of the poor. But a wicked man does not care about the poor.

**8** Men who mock other people cause trouble in a city. But wise men stop anger.

**9** A wise man can take a fool to court. The fool gets angry or laughs, and there is no peace.

**10** Murderers hate an honest man. But the righteous try to protect his life.

**11** A fool shows all his anger. But a wise man controls his anger.

**12** If a ruler listens to lies, all his officials become wicked.

**13** The poor man and the cruel man are the same in one thing. The LORD gives light to the eyes of both.

**14** A king can judge the poor honestly. The rule of that king will continue forever.

**15** The stick and correction give wisdom. But a child who does what he wants brings shame to his mother.

**16** When the wicked increase, sin increases. But the righteous will see the fall of the wicked.

**17** Correct your son, and he will give you peace. He will give joy to you.

**18** When the people do not get a message from God, they do what they want. But a man who obeys the law is happy.

**19** Words alone will not correct a servant. He understands, but he does not obey.

**20** Do you see a man who speaks too quickly? There is more hope for a fool than for him.

**21** A man can give his servant everything from childhood. At the end, the servant will cause him grief.

**22** An angry man causes arguments. A man with a hot temper does much wrong.

**23** The pride of a man will make him low. But a humble man will get honor.

**24** A man who is a partner of a thief hates his own life. He hears the oath in court, but he does not tell the truth.

**25** Fear of people is like a trap. But a man who trusts the LORD will be safe.

**26** Many people want the favor of a ruler. But justice for a man comes from the LORD.

**27** The righteous hate a dishonest man. The wicked hate an honest man.

## Chapter 30

**1** These are the words of Agur, the son of Jakeh. This is his message. He spoke to Ithiel, to Ithiel and to Ucal.

**2** "I am more foolish than all other men. I do not have the understanding of a man.

**3** I did not learn wisdom. I do not have the knowledge of the Holy God.

**4** Who went up into heaven and came down? Who held the wind in his hands? Who wrapped the waters in a cloth? Who set all the ends of the earth? What is his name? What is the name of his son? Tell me if you know!

**5** Every word of God is true. He is a shield to the people who trust Him.

**6** Do not add to His words. If you do, He will correct you. He will show that you are a liar.

**7** God, I ask You for 2 things. Give them to me before I die.

**8** Keep lies and false words far from me. Do not make me poor or rich. Give me only the food that I need.

**9** If I have too much, I can refuse You and say, 'Who is the LORD?' If I am poor, I can steal. Then I will bring shame to the name of my God.

**10** Do not speak against a servant to his master. If you do, the servant will curse you, and you will be guilty.

**11** Some people curse their father. They do not bless their mother.

**12** Some people think that they are clean. But they did not wash away their dirt.

**13** Some people are very proud. They look down on other people.

**14** Some people have teeth like swords and jaws like knives. They destroy the poor on the earth and the needy among men.

**15** The leech has 2 daughters. Their names are 'Give' and 'Give.' 3 things are never satisfied. 4 things never say, 'That is sufficient.'

**16** These are the grave and a woman who cannot have children. They are also dry land that always needs water, and fire that never says, 'That is sufficient.'

**17** A person can mock his father and refuse to obey his mother. The ravens of the valley will pick out his eye. The young eagles will eat it.

**18** 3 things are too wonderful for me. 4 things I do not understand.

**19** These are the path of an eagle in the sky and the path of a snake on a rock. They are also the path of a ship on the sea and the way of a man with a young woman.

**20** This is the way of an unfaithful wife. She eats and wipes her mouth. Then she says, 'I did nothing wrong.'

**21** 3 things make the earth shake. 4 things the earth cannot carry.

**22** These are a servant who becomes king and a fool who has too much food.

**23** They are also a hated woman who gets married and a female servant who takes the place of her mistress.

**24** 4 things on the earth are small, but they are very wise.

**25** The ants are not strong. But they store their food in the summer.

**26** The rock badgers are not strong. But they make their homes in the rocks.

**27** The locusts have no king. But all of them go forward in groups.

**28** You can hold a lizard in your hands. But a lizard lives in the palaces of kings.

**29** 3 things walk with pride. 4 things move in a proud way.

**30** One is the lion. It is the strongest of the animals, and it does not turn away from anything.

**31** The others are the rooster, the male goat and a king with his army around him.

**32** You can be foolish and make yourself proud, or you can plan evil. Then stop and put your hand over your mouth.

**33** When you stir milk, you make butter. When you hit a nose, you cause blood. Also, when you cause anger, you cause arguments."

## Chapter 31

**1** These are the words of King Lemuel. His mother taught him this message.

**2** "My son, what must I tell you? You are the son that I gave birth to. You are the son that I prayed for.

**3** Do not give your strength to women. Do not give your life to the women who destroy kings.

**4** Lemuel, kings must not drink wine. Rulers must not want strong drink.

**5** If they drink, they can forget the law. They can take away the rights of the poor.

**6** Give strong drink to the man who is about to die. Give wine to the people who are very sad.

**7** Let them drink and forget their poverty. Let them not remember their trouble.

**8** Speak for the people who cannot speak for themselves. Speak for the rights of all helpless people.

**9** Speak and judge honestly. Defend the rights of the poor and needy."

**10** Who can find a good wife? She is worth much more than jewels.

**11** Her husband trusts her completely. He will always have sufficient money.

**12** She does good things for him, not bad things, all the days of her life.

**13** She finds wool and linen. She works with her hands willingly.

**14** She is like the ships of merchants. She brings her food from far away.

**15** She gets up while it is still night. She gives food to her family. She gives work to her female servants.

**16** She looks at a field and buys it. With the money that she earns, she plants a vineyard.

**17** She works with strength. Her arms are strong.

**18** She knows that her trade gives a good profit. Her lamp burns all night.

**19** She puts her hands on the spindle. She holds the thread in her hands.

**20** She gives help to the poor. She reaches out her hands to the needy.

**21** She is not afraid of the snow for her family. All her family wear warm, red clothes.

**22** She makes covers for her beds. She wears clothes of fine linen and purple cloth.

**23** People know her husband in the city gates. He sits among the leaders of the country.

**24** She makes linen clothes and sells them. She gives belts to the merchants.

**25** She is strong and people respect her. She laughs without fear of the future.

**26** She speaks with wisdom. She teaches with kindness.

**27** She watches carefully all the work of her family. She is never lazy.

**28** Her children stand up and call her blessed. Her husband also praises her.

**29** He says, "Many women do good things, but you are better than all of them."

**30** Charm can deceive, and beauty does not continue. But a woman who fears the LORD will get praise.

**31** Give her the reward that she earns. Let her work bring her praise in the city gates.
