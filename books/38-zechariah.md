# Zechariah

## Chapter 1

**1** In the eighth month of the second year of Darius, the LORD gave a message to the prophet Zechariah. Zechariah was the son of Berechiah, the son of Iddo. The message said:

**2** "The LORD was very angry with your ancestors.

**3** Thus tell the people this. The LORD of armies says, 'Come back to me.' The LORD of armies says, 'Then I will come back to you.'

**4** Do not be like your ancestors. The prophets before you called out to them. The prophets said, "The LORD of armies says this: Turn away from your evil ways and your evil actions." But your ancestors did not listen. They did not obey me,' says the LORD.

**5** 'Where are your ancestors now? Do the prophets live forever?

**6** I gave my words and my laws to my servants the prophets. My words and laws came true against your ancestors. Then your ancestors turned back. They said, "The LORD of armies did to us what he decided to do. He punished us because of our ways and our actions."'"

**7** It was the second year of Darius. On the 24th day of the eleventh month, the month Shebat, the LORD gave a message to the prophet Zechariah. Zechariah was the son of Berechiah, the son of Iddo. Zechariah said:

**8** In the night I saw a vision. A man sat on a red horse. He stood among the myrtle trees in a valley. Behind him were red horses, brown horses, and white horses.

**9** I asked, "My lord, what are these horses?" The angel who spoke with me said, "I will show you what they are."

**10** The man who stood among the myrtle trees answered. He said, "The LORD sent these riders to go across all the earth."

**11** The riders spoke to the angel of the LORD, who stood among the myrtle trees. They said, "We went across all the earth. All the earth is quiet and at peace."

**12** Then the angel of the LORD said, "LORD of armies, how long will you not show mercy to Jerusalem and the cities of Judah? You were angry with them for 70 years."

**13** The LORD answered the angel who spoke with me. The LORD spoke good words and words of comfort.

**14** Then the angel who spoke with me said to me, "Call out this message. 'The LORD of armies says this. I have a very strong love for Jerusalem and for Zion.

**15** I am very angry with the nations that feel safe. I was only a little angry with my people. But the nations made the punishment worse than I wanted.'

**16** Thus the LORD says this: 'I will come back to Jerusalem with mercy. The people will build my house in Jerusalem,' says the LORD of armies. 'A builder will put a line across Jerusalem to measure it.'

**17** Call out again: 'The LORD of armies says this: My cities will again have many good things. The LORD will again comfort Zion. He will again choose Jerusalem.'"

**18** Then I looked up and saw 4 horns.

**19** I said to the angel who spoke with me, "What are these horns?" He answered me, "These are the horns that sent Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem away into many countries."

**20** Then the LORD showed me 4 metal workers.

**21** I asked, "What will these men do?" He said, "These horns sent Judah away into many countries. Thus no person in Judah could lift up his head. But these workers came to make the horns afraid. They will throw down the horns of the nations. Those nations used their power against the land of Judah to send its people away."

## Chapter 2

**1** I looked up again and saw a man with a line in his hand. He used the line to measure things.

**2** I asked, "Where are you going?" He said to me, "I will measure Jerusalem. I will find its width and its length."

**3** Then the angel who spoke with me went out. Another angel came out to meet him.

**4** The second angel said to him, "Run and tell this young man: 'Jerusalem will be like towns with no walls. Jerusalem will have too many people and animals for walls.

**5** "I will be a wall of fire around Jerusalem," says the LORD. "I will be the glory in Jerusalem."'

**6** "Come! Run away from the land of the north," says the LORD. "I sent you away to all parts of the earth, as the 4 winds of the sky go out," says the LORD.

**7** "Come, Zion! Escape, you people who live in Babylon!"

**8** The LORD of armies says this: "After the glory, he sent me to the nations that took your possessions. A person who attacks you attacks the most valuable thing to the LORD.

**9** I will raise my hand against those nations. Their slaves will take their possessions. Then you will know that the LORD of armies sent me.

**10** Sing and be glad, people of Zion! I come, and I will live among you," says the LORD.

**11** "At that time, many nations will join the LORD. They will become my people. I will live among you. Then you will know that the LORD of armies sent me to you."

**12** The LORD will take Judah as his own part in the holy land. He will choose Jerusalem again.

**13** All people, be quiet in front of the LORD! He comes out of his holy home to act.

## Chapter 3

**1** Then the angel showed me Joshua the high priest. Joshua stood in front of the angel of the LORD. Satan stood at the right side of Joshua to accuse him.

**2** The LORD said to Satan, "The LORD tells you to stop, Satan! The LORD who chose Jerusalem tells you to stop! This man is like a stick that someone pulled out of the fire."

**3** Joshua wore very dirty clothes. He stood in front of the angel.

**4** The angel spoke to the others who stood in front of him. He said, "Take the dirty clothes off Joshua." Then he said to Joshua, "Look, I took your sin away from you. I will put fine clothes on you."

**5** Then I said, "Put a clean turban on his head." So they put a clean turban on his head and put clothes on him. The angel of the LORD stood near.

**6** Then the angel of the LORD spoke strongly to Joshua. He said:

**7** "The LORD of armies says this: 'Live as I tell you, and do the work that I give you. Then you will rule my house and keep my courtyards. I will let you go among these angels who stand here.

**8** Listen, Joshua the high priest! Listen, you and your friends who sit in front of you. These men are a sign of things that will come. I will bring my servant, the Branch.

**9** Look at the stone that I put in front of Joshua. The stone has 7 eyes. I will cut words into the stone,' says the LORD of armies. 'I will take away the sin of this land in one day.

**10** At that time,' says the LORD of armies, 'each one of you will invite his neighbor. You will sit together under your vine and under your fig tree.'"

## Chapter 4

**1** The angel who spoke with me came back. He woke me as a person wakes a man from sleep.

**2** He asked me, "What do you see?" I said, "I see a lampstand of gold. A bowl is on the top of the lampstand. The lampstand has 7 lamps on it. Each lamp has pipes that connect to it.

**3** Two olive trees are near the lampstand. One tree is on the right side of the bowl, and one tree is on the left side."

**4** I asked the angel who spoke with me, "My lord, what are these things?"

**5** The angel who spoke with me answered, "Do you not know what these things are?" I said, "No, my lord."

**6** Then he said to me, "This is the message of the LORD to Zerubbabel: 'You will not succeed by strength or by power. You will succeed by my Spirit,' says the LORD of armies.

**7** 'Large problems are like a large mountain in front of Zerubbabel. But the mountain will become flat ground. Zerubbabel will put the last stone on the temple. The people will shout, "God is kind to it! God is kind to it!"'"

**8** Then the LORD gave me another message. He said:

**9** "The hands of Zerubbabel put down the base of this temple. His hands will also complete the temple. Then you will know that the LORD of armies sent me to you.

**10** Some people thought that this small start was not important. But they will be glad when they see the plumb line in the hand of Zerubbabel. The 7 lamps are the eyes of the LORD. They look at all the earth."

**11** Then I asked the angel, "What are these 2 olive trees on the right side and the left side of the lampstand?"

**12** I asked him again, "What are these 2 olive branches? They pour golden oil through the 2 gold pipes."

**13** He said to me, "Do you not know what these things are?" I said, "No, my lord."

**14** He said, "These are the 2 men whom God chose and put oil on. They stand near the Lord of all the earth."

## Chapter 5

**1** Then I looked up again. I saw a scroll that flew in the air.

**2** The angel asked me, "What do you see?" I answered, "I see a scroll that flies. It is 20 cubits long and 10 cubits wide."

**3** Then he said to me, "This scroll is the curse that goes out over all the land. One side of the scroll says that every thief will go away from the land. The other side says that every person who makes false promises will go away.

**4** The LORD of armies says, 'I will send the curse out. It will go into the house of the thief. It will go into the house of the person who makes a false promise with my name. The curse will stay in his house. It will destroy the house, its wood, and its stones.'"

**5** Then the angel who spoke with me came forward. He said to me, "Look up and see this thing that comes out."

**6** I asked, "What is it?" He said, "It is a basket that people use to measure grain (an ephah). It shows the sin of the people in all the land."

**7** Then someone lifted a lead cover off the basket. A woman sat in the basket.

**8** The angel said, "This woman is evil." He pushed her back into the basket. He put the heavy lead cover on the top of the basket.

**9** Then I looked up and saw 2 women come out. They had wings like the wings of a stork. The wind was in their wings. They lifted the basket up between the earth and the sky.

**10** I asked the angel who spoke with me, "Where do these women take the basket?"

**11** He said to me, "They will build a house for the basket in the land of Shinar. When the house is ready, they will put the basket there on its base."

## Chapter 6

**1** Then I looked up again. I saw 4 chariots come out from between 2 mountains. The mountains were bronze.

**2** Red horses pulled the first chariot. Black horses pulled the second chariot.

**3** White horses pulled the third chariot. Strong horses with spots pulled the fourth chariot.

**4** I asked the angel who spoke with me, "My lord, what are these chariots?"

**5** The angel answered me, "These are the 4 spirits of heaven. They stood in front of the Lord of all the earth. Now they go out.

**6** The chariot with the black horses goes to the land of the north. The white horses go after them. The horses with spots go to the land of the south."

**7** The strong horses went out. They wanted to go across all the earth. The angel said, "Go across the earth!" So they went across all the earth.

**8** Then the angel called to me. He said, "Look, the horses that go to the land of the north made my Spirit calm there."

**9** Then the LORD gave me a message. He said:

**10** "Take gifts from the people who came back from Babylon. Take gifts from Heldai, Tobijah, and Jedaiah. Go on that same day to the house of Josiah the son of Zephaniah.

**11** Take the silver and gold and make a crown. Put the crown on the head of Joshua the son of Josedech, the high priest.

**12** Tell him, 'The LORD of armies says this: Look, here is the man whose name is the Branch. He will grow up from his place. He will build the temple of the LORD.

**13** He will build the temple of the LORD. He will receive royal honor. He will sit on his throne and rule. He will also be a priest on his throne. The king and the priest will agree and work together in peace.'

**14** The crown will stay in the temple of the LORD. It will help the people remember Helem, Tobijah, Jedaiah, and Hen the son of Zephaniah.

**15** People from far away will come and help to build the temple of the LORD. Then you will know that the LORD of armies sent me to you. This will occur if you carefully obey the voice of the LORD your God."

## Chapter 7

**1** In the fourth year of King Darius, the LORD gave a message to Zechariah. This occurred on the fourth day of the ninth month, the month Kislev.

**2** At that time, the people of Bethel sent Sharezer and Regem-Melech and their men. These men went to ask the LORD for his favor.

**3** They asked the priests of the house of the LORD of armies and the prophets a question. They said, "For many years we cried and did not eat in the fifth month. Must we continue to do this?"

**4** Then the LORD of armies gave me a message. He said:

**5** "Speak to all the people of the land and to the priests. Tell them, 'For 70 years you did not eat and you were sad in the fifth and seventh months. But did you really do this for me?

**6** When you ate and drank, you did it only for yourselves.

**7** The LORD spoke these words through the prophets before you. At that time Jerusalem and the cities around it had many people and many good things. People lived in the south and in the low hills. You must listen to those words.'"

**8** The LORD gave this message to Zechariah:

**9** "The LORD of armies says this: 'Judge honestly. Show kindness and mercy to each other.

**10** Do not be cruel to widows, to children with no father, to foreigners, or to poor people. Do not make evil plans against each other.'

**11** But the people did not listen. They turned their backs. They closed their ears so that they could not hear.

**12** They made their hearts as hard as stone. They did not listen to the law. They did not listen to the words that the LORD of armies sent by his Spirit through the prophets before them. Thus the LORD of armies became very angry.

**13** "I called to them, but they did not listen. Thus when they called to me, I did not listen," says the LORD of armies.

**14** "I sent them away to all the nations that they did not know. I scattered them as a storm wind scatters dust. Then the land became empty. No person went through it or came back. Thus the people made the good land empty."

## Chapter 8

**1** The LORD of armies gave me another message. He said:

**2** "The LORD of armies says this: 'I have a very strong love for Zion. I am very angry with her enemies because of this love.'

**3** The LORD says this: 'I will come back to Zion. I will live in Jerusalem. People will call Jerusalem the City of Truth. They will call the mountain of the LORD of armies the Holy Mountain.'

**4** The LORD of armies says this: 'Old men and old women will again sit in the streets of Jerusalem. Each one will hold a stick in his hand because of his old age.

**5** The streets of the city will be full of boys and girls who play there.'

**6** The LORD of armies says this: 'This will seem too wonderful to the people who remain at this time. But it is not too wonderful for me,' says the LORD of armies.

**7** The LORD of armies says this: 'I will save my people from the lands of the east and the lands of the west.

**8** I will bring them back. They will live in Jerusalem. They will be my people, and I will be their God. I will be faithful and good to them.'

**9** The LORD of armies says this: 'Be strong! At this time you hear the words of the prophets. The prophets spoke on the day when the people put down the base of the house of the LORD of armies. They spoke so that you will build the temple.

**10** Before that time, no person got pay for his work or for the work of his animals. Enemies caused trouble, and thus no person could travel safely. I made each man fight against his neighbor.

**11** But now I will not treat the people who remain as I did before,' says the LORD of armies.

**12** 'The seeds will grow well. The vine will give its fruit. The ground will give its crops. The sky will give its dew. I will give all these things to the people who remain.

**13** People of Judah and people of Israel, the nations used your name as a curse. But I will save you, and you will be a blessing. Do not be afraid. Be strong.'

**14** The LORD of armies says this: 'Your ancestors made me angry. Thus I decided to punish you, and I did not change my decision,' says the LORD of armies.

**15** 'Now I decided to do good to Jerusalem and to the people of Judah again. Do not be afraid.

**16** These are the things that you must do. Tell the truth to each other. In your courts, judge honestly and make decisions that bring peace.

**17** Do not make evil plans against your neighbor. Do not make false promises. I hate all these things,' says the LORD."

**18** The LORD of armies gave me another message. He said:

**19** "The LORD of armies says this: 'The people of Judah do not eat on special days in the fourth, fifth, seventh, and tenth months. These days will become times of joy and happy festivals. Thus love truth and peace.'

**20** The LORD of armies says this: 'People will come again from many cities.

**21** The people of one city will go to another city. They will say, "Come, we must go quickly to ask the LORD for his favor. We will look for the LORD of armies. I will go too."

**22** Many people and strong nations will come to Jerusalem. They will come to look for the LORD of armies and to ask for his favor.'

**23** The LORD of armies says this: 'At that time, 10 men from all the nations and languages will hold the clothes of one Jew. They will say, "We will go with you, because we heard that God is with you."'"

## Chapter 9

**1** This is a message from the LORD. The message is against the land of Hadrach and the city of Damascus. The eyes of all people and of all the tribes of Israel look to the LORD.

**2** The message is also against Hamath, which is near Damascus. It is against Tyre and Sidon, though their people think that they are very wise.

**3** Tyre built a strong wall around itself. The people of Tyre put together silver as common as dust. They put together gold as common as mud in the streets.

**4** But the Lord will take away the possessions of Tyre. He will destroy the power of Tyre on the sea. Fire will burn up the city.

**5** The city of Ashkelon will see this and be afraid. The city of Gaza will see this and feel very much pain. The city of Ekron will also feel pain, because its hope will fail. The king of Gaza will die. No people will live in Ashkelon.

**6** Foreigners will live in Ashdod. I will destroy the pride of the Philistines.

**7** I will take the blood out of their mouths. I will take the unclean food from between their teeth. The people who remain will belong to our God. They will become like a family group in Judah. The people of Ekron will become like the Jebusites.

**8** I will put my army around my house to protect it from enemy armies. No cruel ruler will attack my people again, because now I watch over them.

**9** Be very glad, people of Zion! Shout, people of Jerusalem! Look, your king comes to you. He is good and he brings victory. He is humble. He sits on a donkey, on a young male donkey.

**10** I will take the chariots away from Ephraim. I will take the war horses away from Jerusalem. I will break the bows of war. Your king will tell the nations about peace. He will rule from sea to sea. He will rule from the Euphrates River to the ends of the earth.

**11** Because of the blood of my agreement (covenant) with you, I will free your prisoners. I will take them out of the pit with no water.

**12** Prisoners who have hope, come back to your safe place. Today I tell you that I will give you 2 times more good things than you lost.

**13** I will use Judah as my bow. I will use Ephraim as my arrow. Zion, I will send your sons against the sons of Greece. I will make you like the sword of a strong soldier.

**14** The LORD will appear above his people. His arrow will go out as fast as lightning. The Lord GOD will blow the trumpet. He will go out in storms from the south.

**15** The LORD of armies will protect his people. They will destroy their enemies. They will throw down their enemies with stones from slings. They will drink and shout like men who drink wine. They will be full like the bowls that the priests use at the corners of the altar.

**16** At that time, the LORD their God will save his people as a shepherd saves his sheep. They will shine on his land like the jewels in a crown.

**17** They will be very good and very beautiful. Grain will make the young men strong. New wine will make the young women strong.

## Chapter 10

**1** Ask the LORD for rain in the spring. The LORD makes the storm clouds. He gives rain to all people. He makes the plants grow in the field for each person.

**2** The idols give false messages. The people who tell the future see false visions. They tell false dreams. Their comfort has no value. Thus the people go away like sheep. They have trouble, because they have no shepherd.

**3** "I am very angry with the shepherds. I will punish the leaders. The LORD of armies takes care of his flock, the people of Judah. He will make them like a proud horse in a battle.

**4** From Judah will come the cornerstone. From Judah will come the tent peg. From Judah will come the bow of war. From Judah will come every ruler.

**5** The people of Judah will be like strong soldiers in a battle. They will push their enemies down into the mud of the streets. They will fight, because the LORD is with them. They will put the enemy riders on horses to shame.

**6** I will make the people of Judah strong. I will save the people of Joseph. I will bring them back, because I have mercy on them. They will be as if I did not send them away. I am the LORD their God, and I will answer them.

**7** The people of Ephraim will be like strong soldiers. They will be glad like men who drink wine. Their children will see it and be glad. They will be happy because of the LORD.

**8** I will whistle to call them, and I will bring them together. I will free them. They will become many people again, as they were before.

**9** I sent them away among the nations. But in far countries they will remember me. They and their children will live, and they will come back.

**10** I will bring them back from the land of Egypt. I will bring them together from Assyria. I will bring them to the land of Gilead and to Lebanon. There will not be sufficient space for them all.

**11** They will go through the sea of trouble. The LORD will hit the waves of the sea. All the deep water of the Nile River will become dry. The LORD will throw down the pride of Assyria. He will take away the power of Egypt.

**12** I will make my people strong in the LORD. They will live as he tells them," says the LORD.

## Chapter 11

**1** Open your gates, Lebanon, so that fire can burn your cedar trees.

**2** Cry aloud, you cypress trees, because the cedar tree fell. Enemies destroyed the strong trees. Cry aloud, you oak trees of Bashan, because the thick forest fell.

**3** Listen to the shepherds who cry aloud. Their good fields are ruins. Listen to the young lions that roar. The thick plants near the Jordan River are ruins.

**4** The LORD my God says this: "Be the shepherd of the flock that the owners will kill.

**5** The people who buy the sheep kill them, and nobody punishes those people. The people who sell the sheep say, 'Praise the LORD, I am rich!' Their own shepherds do not feel sorry for them.

**6** I will not feel sorry for the people of the land again," says the LORD. "I will put each person into the power of his neighbor and of his king. They will destroy the land. I will not save the people from their power."

**7** So I became the shepherd of the flock that the owners will kill. I took care of the weakest sheep in the flock. I took 2 sticks. I called one stick Favor and the other stick Union. Then I took care of the flock.

**8** In one month I removed 3 shepherds. I became tired of the sheep, and the sheep hated me.

**9** Then I said, "I will not be your shepherd. Let the sheep that die, die. Let the enemy destroy the sheep that he wants to destroy. Let the sheep that remain eat each other."

**10** I took my stick named Favor and broke it. Thus I broke the agreement that I made with all the nations.

**11** I broke the agreement on that day. The weakest sheep watched me, and they knew that this was the message of the LORD.

**12** I said to them, "If you think that it is good, pay me. If not, do not pay me." So they paid me 30 pieces of silver.

**13** The LORD said to me, "Throw the silver to the potter. That is the fine price that they put on me!" So I took the 30 pieces of silver. I threw them to the potter in the house of the LORD.

**14** Then I broke my second stick, named Union. Thus I broke the family connection between Judah and Israel.

**15** Then the LORD said to me, "Again take the equipment of a foolish shepherd.

**16** I will put a new shepherd over the land. He will not take care of the sheep that go away. He will not look for the young sheep. He will not heal the hurt sheep. He will not feed the healthy sheep. But he will eat the meat of the fat sheep and tear off their feet.

**17** Terrible things will occur to the bad shepherd who leaves the flock! A sword will cut his arm and his right eye. His arm will become fully weak. His right eye will become fully blind."

## Chapter 12

**1** This is a message from the LORD about Israel. The LORD spread out the heavens. He made the base of the earth. He made the spirit in each person. The LORD says:

**2** "I will make Jerusalem like a cup of strong wine. All the nations around her will drink it and fall. They will attack Jerusalem and Judah.

**3** At that time, I will make Jerusalem like a very heavy stone for all the nations. All the nations of the earth will come together against Jerusalem. But all who try to lift her will hurt themselves badly.

**4** At that time," says the LORD, "I will make every horse afraid. I will make every rider crazy. I will watch over the people of Judah. But I will make the horses of the nations blind.

**5** Then the leaders of Judah will say to themselves, 'The people of Jerusalem are strong, because the LORD of armies is their God.'

**6** At that time, I will make the leaders of Judah like a fire in a pile of wood. They will be like a torch of fire among dry grain. They will destroy all the nations around them, on the right side and on the left side. But the people of Jerusalem will stay safe in their city.

**7** The LORD will save the homes of Judah first. Thus the family of David and the people of Jerusalem will not have more honor than the rest of Judah.

**8** At that time, the LORD will protect the people of Jerusalem. The weakest person among them will be strong like David. The family of David will be like God, like the angel of the LORD who goes in front of them.

**9** At that time, I will destroy all the nations that attack Jerusalem.

**10** I will give a spirit of kindness and prayer to the family of David and to the people of Jerusalem. They will look at me, the one whom they stabbed. They will cry for him, as a person cries for an only son. They will feel very sad for him, as a person feels sad for a first son who died.

**11** At that time, the people of Jerusalem will cry very much. It will be like the sorrow for Hadad Rimmon in the valley of Megiddo.

**12** All the land will cry, each family alone. The family of David will cry alone, and their wives will cry alone. The family of Nathan will cry alone, and their wives will cry alone.

**13** The family of Levi will cry alone, and their wives will cry alone. The family of Shimei will cry alone, and their wives will cry alone.

**14** All the other families will cry, each family alone, and their wives will cry alone."

## Chapter 13

**1** "At that time, a spring of water will open for the family of David and for the people of Jerusalem. The water will wash away their sin and their uncleanness.

**2** At that time," says the LORD of armies, "I will remove the names of the idols from the land. People will not remember the idols again. I will also remove the false prophets and the unclean spirit from the land.

**3** If a person continues to speak as a prophet, his own father and mother will speak to him. They will say, 'You must die, because you tell lies in the name of the LORD.' Then his own father and mother will stab him when he speaks as a prophet.

**4** At that time, each prophet will feel shame about his vision when he speaks. He will not wear a rough coat of hair to trick people.

**5** Each one will say, 'I am not a prophet. I am a farmer. I worked on a farm since I was young.'

**6** Then someone will ask him, 'What are these wounds on your body?' He will answer, 'My friends gave me these wounds in their house.'

**7** Sword, wake up and attack my shepherd! Attack the man who is near to me," says the LORD of armies. "Kill the shepherd, and the sheep will run away in all directions. I will turn my hand against the small sheep.

**8** In all the land," says the LORD, "2 of 3 parts of the people will die. But 1 part will remain in the land.

**9** I will put that third part through the fire. I will make them clean as a worker makes silver clean. I will test them as a worker tests gold. They will call to me, and I will answer them. I will say, 'They are my people.' They will say, 'The LORD is our God.'"

## Chapter 14

**1** A day of the LORD will come. On that day, your enemies will take your possessions and divide them in your city.

**2** I will bring all the nations together to fight against Jerusalem. They will capture the city. They will take the possessions from the houses. They will attack the women. Half of the people of the city will go away as prisoners. But the rest of the people will stay in the city.

**3** Then the LORD will go out and fight against those nations. He will fight as he fought in battles before.

**4** On that day, he will stand on the Mount of Olives, east of Jerusalem. The Mount of Olives will break into 2 parts, from east to west. A very large valley will form. Half of the mountain will move to the north, and half will move to the south.

**5** You will run away through this valley between the mountains, because it will go as far as Azel. You will run away as your ancestors ran from the earthquake in the time of Uzziah king of Judah. Then the LORD my God will come, and all his holy ones will come with him.

**6** On that day there will be no light, no cold, and no frost.

**7** It will be one special day that only the LORD knows. There will be no difference between day and night. When evening comes, there will still be light.

**8** On that day, fresh water will flow out from Jerusalem. Half of the water will flow east to the Dead Sea. Half will flow west to the Mediterranean Sea. The water will flow in summer and in winter.

**9** The LORD will be king over all the earth. On that day, the LORD will be the only LORD. His name will be the only name.

**10** All the land from Geba to Rimmon, south of Jerusalem, will become flat. But Jerusalem will stay high in its place. People will live in Jerusalem from the Benjamin Gate to the place of the First Gate and the Corner Gate. They will live there from the Tower of Hananel to the winepresses of the king.

**11** People will live in Jerusalem. God will not destroy the city again. Jerusalem will be safe.

**12** The LORD will send a terrible disease on all the nations that fought against Jerusalem. Their bodies will rot while they stand on their feet. Their eyes will rot in their heads. Their tongues will rot in their mouths.

**13** On that day, the LORD will make them very confused and afraid. Each man will hold his neighbor, and they will attack each other.

**14** The people of Judah will also fight at Jerusalem. They will collect the wealth of all the nations around them. They will collect very much gold, silver, and clothes.

**15** The same disease will kill the horses, the mules, the camels, and the donkeys. It will kill all the animals in the camps of the enemies.

**16** Some people from all the nations that attacked Jerusalem will remain alive. Each year they will go up to Jerusalem to worship the King, the LORD of armies. They will keep the Festival of Shelters (Tabernacles).

**17** Some families of the earth will not go up to Jerusalem to worship the King, the LORD of armies. The LORD will not send rain on them.

**18** The people of Egypt do not get rain. If they do not go up to Jerusalem, the LORD will send on them the same disease. He will send this disease on the nations that do not go up to keep the Festival of Shelters.

**19** This will be the punishment of Egypt. It will be the punishment of all the nations that do not go up to keep the Festival of Shelters.

**20** On that day, the bells of the horses will have these words on them: "HOLY TO THE LORD." The pots in the house of the LORD will be as holy as the bowls in front of the altar.

**21** Every pot in Jerusalem and in Judah will be holy to the LORD of armies. All the people who make sacrifices will come and use these pots to cook their meat. On that day, no trader will be in the house of the LORD of armies again.
