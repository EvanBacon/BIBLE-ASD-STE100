# Galatians

## Chapter 1

**1** This letter is from Paul, an apostle. People did not send me, and no person made me an apostle. Jesus Christ and God the Father made me an apostle. God raised Jesus from the dead.

**2** All the brothers who are with me also send this letter. We write to the churches in Galatia.

**3** We pray that God the Father and our Lord Jesus Christ will give you grace and peace.

**4** Christ gave himself for our sins. He did this to save us from this evil world. God our Father wanted Christ to do this.

**5** Give glory to God forever and ever. Amen.

**6** I am surprised that you leave God so quickly. God called you through the grace of Christ. But now you turn to a different gospel.

**7** That gospel is not really a gospel. But some people cause you trouble. They want to change the gospel of Christ.

**8** We preached the gospel to you. Possibly we, or an angel from heaven, will preach a different gospel to you. Then let God curse that person.

**9** We said this before, and now I say it again. If a person preaches to you a gospel that is different from the gospel that you received, let God curse that person.

**10** Do I try to get the approval of people, or the approval of God? Do I try to please people? If I continued to please people, I would not be a servant of Christ.

**11** Brothers, I want you to know this: The gospel that I preached does not come from people.

**12** I did not receive it from a person. No person taught it to me. Jesus Christ showed it to me in a revelation.

**13** You heard about my life in the past, when I followed the Jewish religion. I attacked the church of God very violently. I tried to destroy the church.

**14** In the Jewish religion I was better than many people of my age in my nation. I had a very strong devotion to the traditions of my ancestors.

**15** But God chose me before I was born. He called me through his grace. Then God decided

**16** to show his Son to me. God wanted me to preach about his Son among the Gentiles (people who are not Jews). At that time I did not ask any person for advice.

**17** I did not go to Jerusalem to see the people who were apostles before me. I went to Arabia. Then I went back to Damascus.

**18** After 3 years, I went to Jerusalem to meet Peter. I stayed with him for 15 days.

**19** I did not see any other apostle. I saw only James, the brother of the Lord.

**20** I tell you the truth. God knows that the things which I write to you are not lies.

**21** Then I went to the areas of Syria and Cilicia.

**22** The churches of Christ in Judea did not know me personally.

**23** They only heard this: "The man who attacked us in the past now preaches the faith. Before, he tried to destroy the faith."

**24** And they praised God because of me.

## Chapter 2

**1** 14 years later, I went to Jerusalem again with Barnabas. I also took Titus with me.

**2** I went because God showed me in a revelation that I must go. I met privately with the leaders of the church. I told them the gospel that I preach among the Gentiles. I wanted to make sure that my work, in the past and now, was not without a result.

**3** Titus was with me. He is a Greek. But the leaders did not force him to receive circumcision.

**4** Some false brothers came into our group secretly. They came to spy on the freedom that we have in Christ Jesus. They wanted to make us slaves again.

**5** We did not obey them, not even for 1 hour. We wanted the truth of the gospel to stay with you.

**6** Some men seemed to be important leaders. What they were before is not important to me, because God does not judge by appearance. These leaders added nothing to my message.

**7** But they saw that God gave me the work of preaching the gospel to the Gentiles. In the same way, God gave Peter the work of preaching to the Jews.

**8** God worked through Peter to make him an apostle to the Jews. God also worked through me to make me an apostle to the Gentiles.

**9** James, Cephas, and John seemed to be the most important leaders. They saw that God gave me his grace. Thus, they shook hands with Barnabas and me as partners. They agreed that we must go to the Gentiles. They agreed to go to the Jews.

**10** They asked us only to remember the poor. I was eager to do this.

**11** But when Peter came to Antioch, I spoke against him in front of the people. He was clearly wrong.

**12** Before some men came from James, Peter ate with the Gentiles. But when those men came, Peter stopped. He separated himself from the Gentiles. He was afraid of the Jews who said that all men must receive circumcision.

**13** The other Jewish believers also acted falsely with Peter. Even Barnabas followed their false actions.

**14** I saw that they did not obey the truth of the gospel. So I spoke to Peter in front of all the people. I said, "You are a Jew, but you live like a Gentile, not like a Jew. So why do you force the Gentiles to live like Jews?"

**15** We are Jews by birth. We are not Gentile sinners.

**16** But we know that a person does not become righteous before God by obeying the law. He becomes righteous by faith in Jesus Christ. So we also believed in Christ Jesus. We wanted God to make us righteous by faith in Christ, not by the law. No person will become righteous by obeying the law.

**17** We try to become righteous through Christ. But possibly people find that we are still sinners. Does that mean that Christ causes sin? No!

**18** If I build again the things that I destroyed, I show that I break the law.

**19** Through the law, I died to the law. Thus, I can live for God.

**20** I died with Christ on the cross. Now I do not live, but Christ lives in me. I live my life in this body by faith in the Son of God. He loved me and gave himself for me.

**21** I do not reject the grace of God. If people can become righteous through the law, then Christ died for no reason.

## Chapter 3

**1** You foolish people of Galatia! Who tricked you? We showed you clearly that Jesus Christ died on the cross.

**2** Tell me only this: Did you receive the Spirit because you obeyed the law? Or did you receive the Spirit because you heard the message and believed it?

**3** Are you so foolish? You started with the Spirit. Do you now try to become complete with your own human strength?

**4** You suffered many things. Did you suffer for no result? I hope not!

**5** God gives you the Spirit and does miracles among you. Does he do this because you obey the law? Or does he do it because you hear the message and believe it?

**6** Abraham believed God. Thus, God accepted Abraham as righteous.

**7** Thus, you know that the people who have faith are the children of Abraham.

**8** The scripture said before that God would make the Gentiles righteous through faith. So the scripture told the good news to Abraham before: "Through you, God will bless all nations."

**9** Thus, the people who have faith receive the blessing with Abraham, who had faith.

**10** All people who depend on obeying the law are under a curse. The scripture says, "God curses each person who does not obey all the things in the book of the law."

**11** It is clear that no person becomes righteous before God by the law. The scripture says, "The righteous person will live by faith."

**12** The law does not depend on faith. The scripture says, "The person who obeys these laws will live by them."

**13** Christ made us free from the curse of the law. He took the curse on himself for us. The scripture says, "God curses each person who hangs on a tree."

**14** Christ did this so that the blessing of Abraham would come to the Gentiles through Christ Jesus. Thus, we can receive the promised Spirit through faith.

**15** Brothers, I will use an example from daily life. When people agree to a covenant (agreement) and confirm it, no person can cancel it. And no person can add to it.

**16** God gave his promises to Abraham and to his descendant. The scripture does not say "descendants," as if it means many people. It says "your descendant," and this means 1 person. That person is Christ.

**17** This is what I mean: God made a covenant and confirmed it. The law came 430 years later. The law cannot cancel the covenant and stop the promise.

**18** If we get the inheritance through the law, then it does not come through the promise. But God gave the inheritance to Abraham through a promise.

**19** Then why did God give the law? God added the law because of sins. The law was to continue until the descendant came. God gave the promise to that descendant. God gave the law through angels and through a man who went between God and the people.

**20** A man who goes between 2 groups does not act for 1 group only. But God is one.

**21** Is the law against the promises of God? No! If God gave a law that could give life, then people would become righteous by the law.

**22** But the scripture says that all people are prisoners of sin. Thus, God gives the promise to the people who believe in Jesus Christ.

**23** Before faith came, the law kept us like prisoners. We were prisoners until God showed us the way of faith.

**24** Thus, the law was our guardian until Christ came. Then God could make us righteous through faith.

**25** Now that faith came, we do not need the guardian.

**26** All of you are children of God through faith in Christ Jesus.

**27** All of you who received baptism into Christ put on Christ, as you put on clothes.

**28** In Christ there is no Jew or Greek, no slave or free person, no male or female. All of you are one in Christ Jesus.

**29** If you belong to Christ, then you are the descendants of Abraham. You will get the things that God promised.

## Chapter 4

**1** I tell you this: While the heir is a child, he is the same as a slave. This is true even when he owns all the property.

**2** He must obey guardians and managers until the time that his father decided.

**3** It is the same with us. When we were children, we were slaves to the basic rules of this world.

**4** But at the correct time, God sent his Son. A woman gave birth to his Son. His Son lived under the law.

**5** God sent him to make free the people who were under the law. Thus, God could adopt us as his sons.

**6** You are his sons. Thus, God sent the Spirit of his Son into our hearts. The Spirit calls, "Abba, Father."

**7** Thus, you are not a slave now. You are a son. And because you are a son, God will make you his heir through Christ.

**8** Before, you did not know God. You were slaves to things that are not really gods.

**9** But now you know God. Or, it is better to say that God knows you. So why do you turn back to those weak and poor rules? Why do you want to be slaves to those rules again?

**10** You follow special days, months, seasons, and years.

**11** I am afraid for you. Possibly my work for you had no result.

**12** Brothers, I ask you to become as I am. I became as you are. You did not do wrong to me.

**13** You know that I first preached the gospel to you because I was sick.

**14** My sickness was a test for you. But you did not hate me or reject me. You received me as an angel of God. You received me as if I was Christ Jesus.

**15** You were very happy then. What happened to that joy? I am a witness that you would have removed your own eyes and given them to me, if possible.

**16** Did I become your enemy because I tell you the truth?

**17** Those people are very eager to get your support, but not for a good purpose. They want to separate you from us. Then you will be eager to support them.

**18** It is always good to be eager for a good purpose. Be eager all the time, not only when I am with you.

**19** My little children, I feel pain for you again, as a woman feels pain when she gives birth. I will feel this pain until Christ is completely formed in you.

**20** I want to be with you now and speak to you in a different way. I do not know what to do about you.

**21** You want to obey the law. Tell me, do you not listen to what the law says?

**22** The scripture says that Abraham had 2 sons. 1 son was from a slave woman. The other son was from a free woman.

**23** The son of the slave woman was born in the natural way. But the son of the free woman was born because of the promise of God.

**24** These things are an example. The 2 women are like 2 covenants. One covenant is from Mount Sinai. Its children are born as slaves. This covenant is Hagar.

**25** Hagar is like Mount Sinai in Arabia. She is like the city of Jerusalem now. Jerusalem and her children are slaves.

**26** But the Jerusalem in heaven is free. She is our mother.

**27** The scripture says, "Be happy, woman who cannot have children! Shout with joy, woman who did not feel the pains of birth! The woman who is alone will have more children than the woman who has a husband."

**28** Brothers, you are children of the promise, as Isaac was.

**29** At that time, the son who was born in the natural way attacked the son who was born by the Spirit. It is the same now.

**30** But what does the scripture say? "Send away the slave woman and her son. The son of the slave woman will not share the inheritance with the son of the free woman."

**31** Thus, brothers, we are not children of the slave woman. We are children of the free woman.

## Chapter 5

**1** Christ made us free. Thus, stand firmly in this freedom. Do not become slaves of the law again.

**2** Listen! I, Paul, tell you this: If you receive circumcision, then Christ will not help you at all.

**3** Again I tell each man who receives circumcision that he must obey all of the law.

**4** You try to become righteous by the law. Then you separated yourselves from Christ. You went away from grace.

**5** But through the Spirit, we wait by faith for the righteousness that we hope for.

**6** In Christ Jesus, circumcision has no value. And no circumcision has no value. The important thing is faith that shows itself through love.

**7** You lived very well. Who stopped you from obeying the truth?

**8** That persuasion does not come from God, who calls you.

**9** "A small quantity of yeast causes the whole quantity of dough to rise."

**10** I trust in the Lord that you will not think differently. The person who causes you trouble will get punishment, whoever he is.

**11** Brothers, if I still preach circumcision, then why do people still attack me? If I preach circumcision, then the cross no longer offends people.

**12** I wish that the people who cause you trouble would cut themselves off completely!

**13** Brothers, God called you to be free. But do not use your freedom as an opportunity to sin. Serve each other with love.

**14** All the law is in 1 command: "Love other people as you love yourself."

**15** But if you continue to fight and hurt each other, be careful. You will destroy each other.

**16** Thus, I tell you this: Let the Spirit control your life. Then you will not do the bad things that your human nature wants.

**17** The human nature wants things that are against the Spirit. And the Spirit wants things that are against the human nature. These 2 are enemies. Thus, you do not do the things that you want to do.

**18** But if the Spirit leads you, then you are not under the law.

**19** The actions of the human nature are clear. They are adultery, sexual sins, impurity, and shameful actions.

**20** They are worship of idols, witchcraft, hate, arguments, jealousy, and anger. They are fights, divisions, and false groups.

**21** They are envy, murder, drunkenness, wild parties, and other such things. I warn you now, as I warned you before. The people who do such things will not receive the kingdom of God.

**22** But the Spirit causes love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, and faithfulness.

**23** The Spirit also causes gentleness and self-control. No law is against such things.

**24** The people who belong to Christ killed their human nature on the cross. They killed its emotions and bad wishes.

**25** We live by the Spirit. Thus, we must also let the Spirit lead us.

**26** We must not be proud. We must not make each other angry. We must not be jealous of each other.

## Chapter 6

**1** Brothers, possibly a person does a sin. Then you who are spiritual must help that person to come back. Help him gently. Be careful, because you can also be tempted.

**2** Help each other with your difficulties. Thus, you will obey the law of Christ.

**3** If a person thinks that he is important when he is not, he tricks himself.

**4** Each person must examine his own work. Then he can be proud of his own work. He must not compare himself with other people.

**5** Each person must carry his own load.

**6** The person who learns the word of God must share all good things with his teacher.

**7** Do not let people trick you. No person can trick God. A person will harvest the things that he plants.

**8** A person who plants to please his human nature will harvest death from that nature. But a person who plants to please the Spirit will harvest eternal life from the Spirit.

**9** We must not become tired of doing good things. At the correct time we will harvest, if we do not stop.

**10** Thus, when we have the opportunity, we must do good things for all people. We must especially do good things for the family of believers.

**11** Look at the large letters that I use when I write to you with my own hand!

**12** Some people want to look good to other people. These people try to force you to receive circumcision. They do this only because they do not want people to attack them for the cross of Christ.

**13** The people who receive circumcision do not obey the law. But they want you to receive circumcision. Then they can be proud of your bodies.

**14** But I will be proud only of the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ. Through the cross, the world is dead to me, and I am dead to the world.

**15** In Christ Jesus, circumcision has no value. And no circumcision has no value. The important thing is to be a new creation.

**16** I pray for peace and mercy on all the people who follow this rule. I also pray for peace and mercy on the Israel of God.

**17** From now on, no person must cause me trouble. I have the marks of the Lord Jesus on my body.

**18** Brothers, I pray that the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ will be with your spirit. Amen.
